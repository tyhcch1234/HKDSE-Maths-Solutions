HKDSE 2017 Paper 1 · Q8 5 marks Rate, Ratio & Variations

It is given that $y$ varies inversely as $\sqrt{x}$. When $x=144$, $y=81$.

(a) Express $y$ in terms of $x$.

(b) If the value of $x$ is increased from $144$ to $324$, find the change in the value of $y$.

aExpress $y$ in terms of $x$

Inverse Variation \(y\propto\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \;\Longleftrightarrow\; y=\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{x}}\) The statement that \(y\) varies inversely as \(\sqrt{x}\) gives the model \(y=\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{x}}\) with \(k\neq0\).
Step 1 · Set up the variation model Since $y$ varies inversely as $\sqrt{x}$, $$y=\frac{k}{\sqrt{x}}$$ where $k$ is a non-zero constant.
“Varies inversely as $\sqrt{x}$” means $y \propto \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$, so write it as an equation with a constant $k$.
Finding the Constant of Variation \(k=xy\) Substituting \(x=144\) and \(y=81\) gives \(k=81\times\sqrt{144}=81\times12=972\).
Step 2 · Substitute the given data to find $k$ Put $x=144$, $y=81$: $$81=\frac{k}{\sqrt{144}}=\frac{k}{12}$$ $$k=81\times 12=972$$
$\sqrt{144}=12$ exactly — that is why this value of $x$ was chosen.
Step 3 · Write the answer for (a) Substitute $k=972$ back into the model: $$y=\frac{972}{\sqrt{x}}$$
Always replace $k$ with its numerical value to “express $y$ in terms of $x$”.

bChange in $y$ when $x$ goes from $144$ to $324$

Step 4 · Identify the initial value of $y$ At $x=144$, the value of $y$ is given as $$y_{\text{initial}}=81$$
This is the given condition from the question — no calculation needed here.
Step 5 · Compute the final value of $y$ Using the result of (a), at $x=324$: $$y_{\text{final}}=\frac{972}{\sqrt{324}}=\frac{972}{18}=54$$
$\sqrt{324}=18$; note $324=18^2$.
Change in a Quantity \(\text{change}=y_{\text{initial}}-y_{\text{final}}\) From \(81\) to \(54\) the value falls by \(27\), so \(y\) decreases by \(27\).
Step 6 · Find the change in $y$ $$\text{Change}=81-54=27$$ Since the result is positive, $y$ decreases by $27$.
The question asks for “the change”, so state the direction too: a decrease of $27$.
Answer: (a) $y=\dfrac{972}{\sqrt{x}}$   ·   (b) $y$ decreases by $27$ (from $81$ to $54$).
PRACTICESimilar question — try it yourself

Question. It is given that \(y\) varies inversely as \(\sqrt{x}\). When \(x=100\), \(y=45\). (a) Express \(y\) in terms of \(x\). (b) If the value of \(x\) is increased from \(100\) to \(225\), find the change in the value of \(y\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Since \(y\) varies inversely as \(\sqrt{x}\), write \(y=\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{x}}\) where \(k\neq0\).
Step 2. Put \(x=100\), \(y=45\): \(45=\dfrac{k}{10}\), so \(k=450\).
Step 3. Hence \(y=\dfrac{450}{\sqrt{x}}\).
Step 4. At \(x=225\): \(y=\dfrac{450}{15}=30\).
Step 5. The change is \(45-30=15\), so \(y\) decreases by \(15\).
Answer: (a) \(y=\dfrac{450}{\sqrt{x}}\)   (b) \(y\) decreases by \(15\) (from \(45\) to \(30\))
