Question

2017 MC #38. In the figure, $ABCD$ is a rectangle. If $E$ is a point lying on $AC$ such that $AE = 30\text{ cm}$, then $DE =$

A. $\;3\sqrt{65}\text{ cm}$
B. $\;5\sqrt{29}\text{ cm}$
C. $\;\sqrt{641}\text{ cm}$
D. $\;\sqrt{697}\text{ cm}$

Step-by-step Solution

Step 1 · Find the diagonal AC
Pythagoras Theorem \(AC^{2}=AB^{2}+BC^{2}\) \(\triangle ABC\) is right-angled at \(B\), so \(AC=\sqrt{28^{2}+21^{2}}=\sqrt{1225}=35\).

Since $ABCD$ is a rectangle, $\triangle ABC$ is right-angled at $B$,
with $AB = 28\text{ cm}$ and $BC = AD = 21\text{ cm}$.

$$AC=\sqrt{AB^{2}+BC^{2}}=\sqrt{28^{2}+21^{2}}=\sqrt{784+441}=\sqrt{1225}=35\text{ cm}$$

Key idea: the diagonal of a rectangle splits it into two congruent right-angled triangles, so Pythagoras' theorem applies directly.
Step 2 · Find cos∠CAD
Cosine Ratio in a Right Triangle \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}\) In \(\triangle ACD\), \(\cos\angle CAD=\dfrac{AD}{AC}=\dfrac{21}{35}=\dfrac{3}{5}\).

In the right-angled $\triangle ACD$, the angle $\angle CAD$ is at vertex $A$,
with adjacent side $AD$ and hypotenuse $AC$. So

$$\cos\angle CAD=\frac{AD}{AC}=\frac{21}{35}=\frac{3}{5}$$

Since $E$ lies on the ray $AC$,
we have $\angle DAE=\angle CAD$.

Step 3 · Apply the cosine formula in △ADE
Cosine Formula \(a^{2}=b^{2}+c^{2}-2bc\cos A\) In \(\triangle ADE\) the sides \(AD=21\), \(AE=30\) and the included angle at \(A\) are known, giving \(DE^{2}=585\).

In $\triangle ADE$ we know $AD = 21$, $AE = 30$ and $\cos\angle DAE=\dfrac{3}{5}$. The cosine formula gives

$$DE^{2}=AD^{2}+AE^{2}-2\cdot AD\cdot AE\cdot\cos\angle DAE$$

$$DE^{2}=21^{2}+30^{2}-2(21)(30)\left(\tfrac{3}{5}\right)$$

$$=441+900-756=585$$

Step 4 · Simplify the answer

$$DE=\sqrt{585}=\sqrt{9\cdot 65}=3\sqrt{65}\text{ cm}$$

Hence the answer is $\textbf{A}$.

Check: $\sqrt{585}\approx 24.19$ cm. Also note the other options correspond to $\sqrt{725}$, $\sqrt{641}$, $\sqrt{697}$ — none match.
Final Answer

$DE = 3\sqrt{65}\text{ cm}$, so the correct choice is A.

Question. In the rectangle \(ABCD\), \(AB=24\text{ cm}\) and \(BC=AD=18\text{ cm}\). \(E\) is a point lying on \(AC\) such that \(AE=20\text{ cm}\). Find \(DE\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(\triangle ABC\) is right-angled at \(B\), so \(AC=\sqrt{24^{2}+18^{2}}=\sqrt{576+324}=\sqrt{900}=30\text{ cm}\).
Step 2. In the right-angled \(\triangle ACD\), \(\cos\angle CAD=\dfrac{AD}{AC}=\dfrac{18}{30}=\dfrac{3}{5}\). As \(E\) lies on \(AC\), \(\angle DAE=\angle CAD\).
Step 3. By the cosine formula in \(\triangle ADE\): \(DE^{2}=18^{2}+20^{2}-2(18)(20)\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)=324+400-432=292\).
Step 4. \(DE=\sqrt{292}=\sqrt{4\cdot73}=2\sqrt{73}\text{ cm}\approx17.1\text{ cm}\).
Answer: \(DE=2\sqrt{73}\text{ cm}\approx17.1\text{ cm}\)
