Question

\[ \frac{(6x^7)^2}{4x^5} = \text{?} \]
A \(3x^4\)
B \(9x^4\)
C \(3x^9\)
D \(9x^9\)

📘 Step-by-step Solution

tap a step to reveal
1

Apply the power rule to the numerator

\((6x^7)^2 = 6^2 \cdot (x^7)^2 = 36x^{14}\)
Rule: \((ab)^n = a^n b^n\) and \((x^m)^n = x^{mn}\). Don't forget to square the coefficient \(6\)!
2

Rewrite the full expression

\[ \frac{(6x^7)^2}{4x^5} = \frac{36x^{14}}{4x^5} \]
After expanding the numerator, the expression becomes a single fraction with a monomial in the numerator and denominator.
3

Simplify the coefficients

\[ \frac{36}{4} = 9 \]
Divide the numbers: \(36 \div 4 = 9\). This becomes the new coefficient.
4

Apply quotient rule for the variable \(x\)

\[ \frac{x^{14}}{x^5} = x^{14-5} = x^9 \]
Rule: \(\dfrac{x^a}{x^b} = x^{a-b}\). Subtract the exponents: \(14 - 5 = 9\).
5

Combine to get the final answer

\[ \frac{(6x^7)^2}{4x^5} = 9x^9 \]
The coefficient is \(9\) and the variable part is \(x^9\). So the answer is \(9x^9\), which matches option D.
