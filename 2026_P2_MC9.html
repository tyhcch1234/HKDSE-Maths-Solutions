Linear Inequalities

Question

The solution of \(x-1>\dfrac{2x-9}{3}\) or \(3x+12\geq 0\) is

  • A. \(x\leq-4\)
  • B. \(x\geq-4\)
  • C. \(x<-6\)
  • D. \(x>-6\)

Solution

Solve the first inequality

Multiply by \(3>0\):

\[ x-1>\frac{2x-9}{3}\ \Leftrightarrow\ 3x-3>2x-9\ \Leftrightarrow\ x>-6 \]
Solve the second inequality \[ 3x+12\geq 0\ \Leftrightarrow\ 3x\geq-12\ \Leftrightarrow\ x\geq-4 \]
Take the union ("or")

The solution is \(x>-6\) or \(x\geq-4\). Since every \(x\geq-4\) already satisfies \(x>-6\), the union is simply

\[ x>-6 \]
Final Answer: D
