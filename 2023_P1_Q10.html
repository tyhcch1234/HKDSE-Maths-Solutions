HKDSE 2023 Paper 1 · Q10 · Coordinate Geometry

Perpendicular bisector & circle with diameter \(AB\)

\(\Gamma:\;3x+y-12=0\) Line \(AB\) Circle Points Centre \(C\)
[2023#10]
It is given that \(A\) and \(B\) are two distinct points in a rectangular coordinate plane. Let \(P\) be a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that \(P\) is equidistant from \(A\) and \(B\). Denote the locus of \(P\) by \(\Gamma\).

(a) Describe the geometric relationship between \(\Gamma\) and \(AB\).  [1 mark]

(b) Suppose that the coordinates of \(A\) are \((2,-4)\) and the equation of \(\Gamma\) is \(3x+y-12=0\). Find
  (i) the equation of the straight line which passes through \(A\) and \(B\),
  (ii) the equation of the circle with \(AB\) as a diameter.  [5 marks]

STEP 1 · Part (a) – Geometry of the locus

A moving point \(P\) equidistant from \(A\) and \(B\) satisfies \(PA=PB\), so \(P\) always lies on the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\).

$$\Gamma \text{ is the perpendicular bisector of } AB.$$
Since \(P\) is equidistant from \(A\) and \(B\), the locus is a straight line through the midpoint of \(AB\) perpendicular to \(AB\).
STEP 2 · Slopes of \(\Gamma\) and \(AB\)

Rewriting \(\Gamma:\;3x+y-12=0\) as \(y=-3x+12\), its slope is \(-3\).

$$\text{Slope of }\Gamma = -3$$

Since \(AB\perp\Gamma\), the slope of \(AB\) is the negative reciprocal:

$$\text{Slope of }AB=\frac{1}{3}$$
STEP 3 · (b)(i) Equation of \(AB\)

\(AB\) passes through \(A(2,-4)\) with slope \(\dfrac{1}{3}\):

$$y-(-4)=\frac{1}{3}(x-2)$$
$$3(y+4)=x-2 \;\Longrightarrow\; x-3y-14=0$$
ANSWER (b)(i)
$$x-3y-14=0$$
STEP 4 · (b)(ii) Centre of the circle

For a circle with diameter \(AB\), the centre is the midpoint of \(AB\), which lies on the intersection of \(AB\) and \(\Gamma\).

Solve simultaneously:

$$\begin{cases}3x+y-12=0\\[2pt] x-3y-14=0\end{cases} \;\Longrightarrow\; x=5,\;y=-3$$
$$\text{Centre }C=(5,-3)$$
STEP 5 · (b)(ii) Radius and equation of the circle

The radius is the distance from the centre to \(A(2,-4)\):

$$r=\sqrt{(2-5)^2+(-4+3)^2}=\sqrt{9+1}=\sqrt{10}$$

Hence the equation of the circle is

$$(x-5)^2+(y+3)^2=10$$
FINAL ANSWERS
(a) \(\Gamma\) is the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\).
(b)(i) \(x-3y-14=0\)
(b)(ii) \((x-5)^2+(y+3)^2=10\)
