Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Applications

T13 Q45

A ladder of length $12$ m leans against a wall at an angle of $15°$ to the horizontal. Find the horizontal distance from the foot of the ladder to the wall.

Solution

Model the ladder, wall, and ground as a right triangle. The ladder is the hypotenuse of length $12$ m, and the horizontal distance to the wall is adjacent to the $15^\circ$ angle.
Use $\cos 15^\circ=\frac{\text{horizontal distance}}{12}$.
Thus the horizontal distance is $12\cos 15^\circ$.
Since $\cos 15^\circ=\frac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}$, the distance is $12\cdot\frac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}=3(\sqrt6+\sqrt2)$ m.
This is approximately $3(2.449+1.414)=11.59$ m, which is less than $12$ m, so the result is reasonable.
The horizontal distance from the foot of the ladder to the wall is $3(\sqrt6+\sqrt2)$ m, approximately $11.59$ m.
