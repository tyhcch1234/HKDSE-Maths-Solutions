Question. In the figure, \(P\) and \(Q\) are points lying on \(OR\) while \(U\) and \(T\) are points lying on \(OS\) such that \(OP=PQ=QR\) and \(PU // QT // RS\). The ratio of the area of the trapezium \(PQTU\) to the area of the trapezium \(QRST\) is
  • A. \(1:2\)
  • B. \(2:3\)
  • C. \(3:5\)
  • D. \(4:9\)
STEP 1 · Similar triangles
Similar Triangles (AAA) \(\triangle OPU\sim\triangle OQT\sim\triangle ORS\) All three triangles share \(\angle O\) and have equal corresponding angles because \(PU // QT // RS\).
Since \(PU // QT // RS\), triangles \(\triangle OPU\), \(\triangle OQT\) and \(\triangle ORS\) share the common angle \(\angle O\) and have equal corresponding angles,
so they are similar (AAA): $$\triangle OPU \sim \triangle OQT \sim \triangle ORS.$$
Look at the two parallel segments \(PU\) and \(QT\) cutting the sides of \(\triangle ORS\).
STEP 2 · Ratio of sides
\(OP=PQ=QR\) gives \(OP:OQ:OR = 1:2:3\). Corresponding sides of similar triangles are proportional: $$PU:QT:RS = OP:OQ:OR = 1:2:3.$$
STEP 3 · Ratio of areas
Areas of Similar Figures \(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2}=\left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^{2}\) The sides are in the ratio \(1:2:3\), so the three triangle areas are in the ratio \(1:4:9\).
Areas of similar figures are in the ratio of the squares of corresponding sides: $${[}\,\triangle OPU\,]:[\,\triangle OQT\,]:[\,\triangle ORS\,] = 1^2:2^2:3^2 = 1:4:9.$$
STEP 4 · Area of trapezium PQTU
The trapezium \(PQTU\) is \(\triangle OQT\) minus \(\triangle OPU\): $$[\,PQTU\,] = 4-1 = 3 \text{ (parts)}.$$
STEP 5 · Area of trapezium QRST
The trapezium \(QRST\) is \(\triangle ORS\) minus \(\triangle OQT\): $$\frac{[\,PQTU\,]}{[\,QRST\,]} = \frac{4-1}{9-4} = \frac{3}{5}.$$
\(\;[\,PQTU\,]:[\,QRST\,] = 3:5\;\) → Answer: C

Question. In the figure, \(P\) and \(Q\) lie on \(OR\), \(U\) and \(T\) lie on \(OS\), with \(OP:PQ:QR=1:2:3\) and \(PU // QT // RS\). Find the ratio of the area of trapezium \(PQTU\) to the area of trapezium \(QRST\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(OP:OQ:OR=1:(1+2):(1+2+3)=1:3:6\).
Step 2. By AAA similarity the triangle areas are \(1^{2}:3^{2}:6^{2}=1:9:36\).
Step 3. \(\text{area of }PQTU=9-1=8\) parts and \(\text{area of }QRST=36-9=27\) parts.
Step 4. Hence \(\text{area of }PQTU:\text{area of }QRST=8:27\).
Answer: \(8:27\)
