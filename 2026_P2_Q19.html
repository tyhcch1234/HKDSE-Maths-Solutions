Area of a Right Triangle

Question

It is given that \(\triangle PQR\) is a right-angled triangle with \(\angle QPR=90^\circ\). Let the area of \(\triangle PQR\) be \(S\). If \(QR=p\), \(PR=q\) and \(\angle PQR=\theta\), which of the following must be true?

  • A. \(S=\dfrac{1}{2}pq\)
  • B. \(S=\dfrac{1}{2}pq\sin\theta\)
  • C. \(S=\dfrac{1}{2}pq\cos\theta\)
  • D. \(S=\dfrac{1}{2}pq\tan\theta\)

Solution

Identify the sides

The right angle is at \(P\), so the hypotenuse is \(QR=p\). The two legs are \(PQ\) and \(PR=q\).

Express \(PQ\) using \(\theta\)

In the right triangle, \(\theta=\angle PQR\) has adjacent leg \(PQ\) and hypotenuse \(QR\):

\[ \cos\theta=\frac{PQ}{QR}\ \Rightarrow\ PQ=p\cos\theta \]
Compute the area \[ S=\frac{1}{2}\times PQ\times PR=\frac{1}{2}(p\cos\theta)(q)=\frac{1}{2}pq\cos\theta \]

This matches option C.

Final Answer: C
