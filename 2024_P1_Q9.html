9. The table below shows the distribution of the numbers of keys owned by a group of housewives.

Number of keys345678
Number of housewives109434$k$

If a housewife is randomly selected from the group, then the probability that she owns more than 6 keys is $\dfrac{5}{18}$.

(a) Find $k$.
(b) Write down the mean, the mode and the median of the distribution.   (5 marks)

STEP 1Set up the probability condition.
“More than 6 keys” means $7$ or $8$ keys.
Number who own more than 6 keys $= 4 + k$.
Total number of housewives $= 10+9+4+3+4+k = 30+k$.
Hint: find the favourable cases and the total cases first.
STEP 2Form and solve the equation.
Cross-Multiplication of a Proportion \(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d}\Rightarrow ad=bc\) Applying it to \(\dfrac{3+k}{24+k}=\dfrac{2}{5}\) gives \(15+5k=48+2k\), so \(k=11\).
$$\frac{4+k}{30+k}=\frac{5}{18}$$ $$18(4+k)=5(30+k)$$ $$72+18k=150+5k$$ $$13k=78 \quad\Rightarrow\quad k=6$$
Hint: cross-multiply, then collect like terms.
STEP 3 (b)Find the mean.
Mean of Grouped Data \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}\) The \(35\) housewives own \(175\) keys in total, so \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{175}{35}=5\).
Total keys: $$3(10)+4(9)+5(4)+6(3)+7(4)+8(6)=30+36+20+18+28+48=180$$ Total housewives $= 30+6 = 36$. $$\text{Mean}=\frac{180}{36}=5$$
Hint: mean $=\dfrac{\sum f x}{\sum f}$.
STEP 4 (b)Find the mode.
Frequencies: $10,\;9,\;4,\;3,\;4,\;6$.
The largest frequency is $10$, which corresponds to $3$ keys. $$\text{Mode}=3$$
Hint: the mode is the data value with the highest frequency.
STEP 5 (b)Find the median.
Median from Cumulative Frequency \(\text{median}=\text{value at position }\dfrac{n+1}{2}\) With \(35\) housewives the \(18\)th value falls in the block \(16
There are $36$ housewives (even), so the median is the average of the $18$th and $19$th values.
Cumulative frequencies: $10,\;19,\;23,\;26,\;30,\;36$.
The $18$th and $19$th values both fall in the group with $4$ keys (cf. $10<18,19\le 19$). $$\text{Median}=4$$
Hint: locate the middle position using cumulative frequencies.
ANSWER (a) $k=6$
(b) Mean $=5$,   Mode $=3$,   Median $=4$

Question. The table shows the numbers of keys owned by a group of housewives: \(3,4,5,6,7\) keys with numbers \(9,7,5,3,k\) respectively. If a housewife is randomly selected, the probability that she owns more than \(5\) keys is \(\dfrac{2}{5}\).
(a) Find \(k\).
(b) Write down the mean, the mode and the median of the distribution.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. More than \(5\) keys means \(6\) or \(7\) keys, so the favourable number is \(3+k\) and the total is \(9+7+5+3+k=24+k\).
Step 2. \(\dfrac{3+k}{24+k}=\dfrac{2}{5}\Rightarrow 5(3+k)=2(24+k)\Rightarrow 15+5k=48+2k\Rightarrow k=11\).
Step 3. The total number of housewives is \(24+11=35\) and the total number of keys is \(3(9)+4(7)+5(5)+6(3)+7(11)=27+28+25+18+77=175\), so the mean is \(\dfrac{175}{35}=5\).
Step 4. The highest frequency is \(9\), at \(3\) keys, so the mode is \(3\).
Step 5. The cumulative frequencies are \(9,16,21,24,35\);
the \(\dfrac{35+1}{2}=18\)th value lies in the \(5\)-key group, so the median is \(5\).
Answer: (a) \(k=11\); (b) mean \(=5\), mode \(=3\), median \(=5\)
