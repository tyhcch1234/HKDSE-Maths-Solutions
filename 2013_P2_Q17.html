[2013 MC #17] In the figure, the solid consists of a right circular cone and a hemisphere with a common base. The base radius and the height of the circular cone are 3 cm and 4 cm respectively. Find the total surface area of the solid.
A. $30\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
B. $33\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
C. $48\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
D. $51\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
STEP 1 · Identify the surfaces The solid is a cone placed on a hemisphere with a common base.
Only curved surfaces are exposed — the common circular base is glued inside:
Curved surface of cone  
+ 
Curved surface of hemisphere.
STEP 2 · Slant height of the cone
Pythagoras’ Theorem\(l^{2}=r^{2}+h^{2}\)The slant edge, radius \(3\) and height \(4\) form a right triangle, giving \(l=5\ \text{cm}\).
The slant edge $l$ joins the apex to a point on the rim of the base: $$l=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{9+16}=\sqrt{25}=5\ \text{cm}$$
3-4-5 right triangle — the key to the whole question.
STEP 3 · Curved surface area of the cone
Curved Surface Area of a Cone\(\text{CSA}=\pi r l\)With \(r=3\) and \(l=5\) the cone contributes \(\pi(3)(5)=15\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
$$\text{CSA}_{\text{cone}}=\pi r l=\pi(3)(5)=15\pi\ \text{cm}^2$$
STEP 4 · Curved surface area of the hemisphere The curved surface of a hemisphere is half
Surface Area of a Sphere\(4\pi r^{2}\)A hemisphere exposes only half of that curved surface, so it contributes \(2\pi r^{2}=18\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
the surface of a whole sphere (radius $r=3$): $$\text{CSA}_{\text{hemi}}=\tfrac12\cdot 4\pi r^2=2\pi r^2=2\pi(3)^2=18\pi\ \text{cm}^2$$
STEP 5 · Total surface area $$\text{TSA}=15\pi+18\pi=33\pi\ \text{cm}^2$$
The flat circle of radius 3 does not contribute — it is an internal face.
Total surface area $=33\pi\ \text{cm}^2$  ⟶  Answer: B

Question. A solid is formed by placing a right circular cone of base radius \(5\ \text{cm}\) and height \(12\ \text{cm}\) on a hemisphere of radius \(5\ \text{cm}\), with the circular base glued. Find the total surface area of the solid.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Slant height: \(l=\sqrt{5^{2}+12^{2}}=\sqrt{169}=13\ \text{cm}\).
Step 2. Curved surface area of the cone \(=\pi(5)(13)=65\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Step 3. Curved surface area of the hemisphere \(=2\pi(5)^{2}=50\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Step 4. Total surface area \(=65\pi+50\pi=115\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\) — the glued circle is an internal face.
Answer: \(115\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\)
