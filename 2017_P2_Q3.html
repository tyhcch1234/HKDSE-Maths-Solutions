Step 1 — Given equation

[2017 MC #3]
If $\dfrac{a+4b}{2a}=2+\dfrac{b}{a}$, then $a=$
  • A. $\dfrac{2b}{3}$
  • B. $\dfrac{3b}{2}$
  • C. $\dfrac{5b}{6}$
  • D. $\dfrac{6b}{5}$
Step 1Write down the given equation. $$ \frac{a+4b}{2a}=2+\frac{b}{a} $$ Hint: The unknown is $a$; treat $b$ as a constant and clear the denominators.
Step 2Multiply both sides by $2a$ to clear the denominators.
Clearing Denominators \(\dfrac{p}{q}=r \;\Longrightarrow\; p=qr\) Multiplying \(\dfrac{a+4b}{2a}=2+\dfrac{b}{a}\) by \(2a\) clears both denominators in one step.
$$ a+4b = 2a\left(2+\frac{b}{a}\right) $$ $$ a+4b = 4a + \frac{2a\cdot b}{a} $$ $$ a+4b = 4a + 2b $$ Hint: $\dfrac{2a \cdot b}{a}$ simplifies to $2b$ since $a \neq 0$.
Step 3Collect the $a$-terms on one side and the $b$-terms on the other.
Collecting Like Terms \(pa-qa=(p-q)a\) The \(a\)-terms are brought to one side and the \(b\)-terms to the other, giving \(-3a=-2b\).
$$ a - 4a = 2b - 4b $$ $$ -3a = -2b $$ Hint: Move $4a$ to the left and $4b$ to the right, keeping balance.
Step 4Divide both sides by $-3$ to isolate $a$.
Division Property of Equations \(pa=qb \;\Longrightarrow\; a=\dfrac{qb}{p}\) Dividing \(-3a=-2b\) by \(-3\) isolates \(a=\dfrac{2b}{3}\).
$$ a = \frac{-2b}{-3} = \frac{2b}{3} $$ Hint: A negative divided by a negative gives a positive result.
Step 5Check against the options. Our result $a=\dfrac{2b}{3}$ matches option A. Hint: The other options come from common mistakes — e.g. forgetting to distribute $2a$ over both terms, or losing a sign.
Answer: A  —  $a=\dfrac{2b}{3}$

Question. If \(\dfrac{3a-2b}{4a}=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{b}{a}\), then \(a=\)
A. \(6b\)   B. \(\dfrac{b}{6}\)   C. \(2b\)   D. \(\dfrac{3b}{2}\)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Multiply both sides by \(4a\) (with \(a\neq0\)): \(3a-2b=2a+4b\).
Step 2. Collect like terms: \(3a-2a=4b+2b\), so \(a=6b\).
Step 3. Check with \(b=1\), \(a=6\): the left side is \(\dfrac{18-2}{24}=\dfrac{2}{3}\) and the right side is \(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{2}{3}\), so the answer is A.
Answer: A   \(a=6b\)
