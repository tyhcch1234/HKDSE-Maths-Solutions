Laws of Indices, Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

It is given that $\log_{5} y$ is a linear function of $\log_{25} x$. The intercepts on the vertical axis and on the horizontal axis of the graph of the linear function are $12$ and $2$ respectively. If $y = m x^{\,n}$, then $n =$

A. $-12$
B. $-4$
C. $-3$
D. $-1$
Step-by-step solution
1 Read the intercepts

Let the vertical axis be $\log_5 y$ and the horizontal axis be $\log_{25} x$. The graph is a straight line through

$\bigl(0,\;12\bigr)\quad\text{and}\quad\bigl(2,\;0\bigr).$

💡 “Intercept on the vertical axis = 12” means the point $(0,12)$ lies on the line; “on the horizontal axis = 2” gives $(2,0)$.
2 Find the slope

$$\text{Slope}=\frac{0-12}{2-0}=-6.$$

Hence the line is

$\log_5 y = -6\log_{25}x + 12.$

💡 Slope $=\dfrac{\Delta(\log_5 y)}{\Delta(\log_{25}x)}$. A negative slope tells us the power $n$ will be negative.
3 Rewrite the equation in powers of 5

Since $x = 25^{\log_{25}x} = \left(5^{2}\right)^{\log_{25}x} = 5^{\,2\log_{25}x}$, taking $\log_5$ of both sides gives $\log_{25}x = \dfrac{1}{2}\log_5 x$.

Substitute into $\log_5 y = -6\log_{25}x + 12$:

$\log_5 y = -6\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\log_5 x + 12 = -3\log_5 x + 12.$

💡 Change of base: $\log_{25}x = \dfrac{\log_5 x}{\log_5 25} = \dfrac{\log_5 x}{2}$. This turns the equation into logarithms with the same base 5.
4 Combine into a single logarithm

$$12 = \log_5 5^{12}, \qquad -3\log_5 x = \log_5 x^{-3},$$

so

$\log_5 y = \log_5 x^{-3} + \log_5 5^{12} = \log_5\!\left(5^{12}x^{-3}\right).$

💡 Use the laws of logarithms: $\log_5 A+\log_5 B=\log_5(AB)$ and $k\log_5 A=\log_5 A^{k}$.
5 Remove the logarithms and compare

Since $\log_5$ is one-to-one, $$y = 5^{12}x^{-3}.$$

Comparing with $y = m x^{\,n}$ gives $$m = 5^{12}, \qquad n = -3.$$

💡 Only $n$ is asked for — read off the exponent of $x$. The constant $5^{12}$ is $m$ (option C is the answer).
Answer:   $n = -3$   ⟶   Option C
