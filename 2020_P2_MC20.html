Q The Question 2020MC#20

In the figure, \(ABC\) is an equilateral triangle and \(CDE\) is an isosceles triangle with \(CD = CE\). If \(\angle DCE = 78^\circ\) and \(\angle ADC = \angle CAD = 40^\circ\), then \(\angle CBE =\)
A. 14°
B. 19°
C. 24°
D. 29°
Given: \(ABC\) equilateral, \(CD = CE\), \(\angle DCE = 78^\circ\), \(\angle ADC = \angle CAD = 40^\circ\).  Find: \(\angle CBE\).

b Part (b) — Find \(\angle CBE\)

Combine \(AC=CD\) with \(AC=BC=CD=CE\) to get four equal radii, then use “angle at the centre = 2 × angle at the circumference”.

∠ABE89.0°

0 / 4
STEP 1
\(ABC\) is equilateral, so \(AC = BC\) and \(\angle ABC = 60^\circ\). From part (a), \(AC = CD\), and we are given \(CD = CE\). Therefore \[ AC = BC = CD = CE \]
STEP 2
Hence \(A,\,B,\,D,\,E\) all lie on the circle with centre \(C\) and radius \(AC\) (all four are the same distance from \(C\)). So \(C\) is the centre of that circle.
STEP 3
The reflex angle at the centre subtending arc \(AE\): \[ \text{reflex } \angle ACE = \angle ACD + \angle DCE = 100^\circ + 78^\circ = 178^\circ \] By “angle at the centre = 2 × angle at the circumference”: \[ 2\angle ABE = 178^\circ \;\Rightarrow\; \angle ABE = 89^\circ \]
STEP 4
Since \(\angle ABC = 60^\circ\), \[ \angle CBE = \angle ABE - \angle ABC = 89^\circ - 60^\circ = 29^\circ \]

\(\angle CBE = 29^\circ\)  ⟶  Answer: D

✓ Summary of Answers

  • In \(\triangle ACD\): \(\angle ADC=\angle CAD=40^\circ \Rightarrow \boxed{AC=CD}\)
  • \(\angle ACD = 180^\circ-40^\circ-40^\circ = \boxed{100^\circ}\)
  • \(ABC\) equilateral \(\Rightarrow AC=BC\), \(\angle ABC=60^\circ\); so \(AC=BC=CD=CE\)
  • \(\therefore A,B,D,E\) lie on a circle centred at \(C\)
  • Reflex \(\angle ACE = 100^\circ+78^\circ=178^\circ \Rightarrow 2\angle ABE=178^\circ\)
  • \(\angle ABE = 89^\circ \Rightarrow \angle CBE = 89^\circ-60^\circ = \boxed{29^\circ}\)
  • Correct option: D. \(29^\circ\)
