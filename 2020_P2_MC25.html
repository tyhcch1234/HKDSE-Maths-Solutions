Coordinate Geometry — Locus of a Moving Point
Coordinate Geometry Locus Intersection of Lines Circle Fixed Distance

QQuestion

Let \(A\) be the point of intersection of the straight lines \[9x+4y-7=0 \quad\text{and}\quad 9x-4y+7=0.\] If \(P\) is a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that the distance between \(P\) and \(A\) is \(8\), then the locus of \(P\) is a

  1. circle. A
  2. triangle.
  3. quadrilateral.
  4. regular hexagon.

(1 mark, multiple choice)

aPart (a) — Find the fixed point \(A\)

\(A\) is the intersection of the two given lines. Solve the system: \[ \begin{cases} 9x+4y-7=0 \\ 9x-4y+7=0 \end{cases} \] Use the sliders below to move the lines and watch how the intersection point \(A\) behaves.

Step 1

Add the two equations to eliminate \(y\):

\[ (9x+4y-7)+(9x-4y+7)=0 \;\Rightarrow\; 18x=0 \;\Rightarrow\; x=0. \]
Hint: The \(y\)-terms are \(+4y\) and \(-4y\), so they cancel perfectly when adding.
Step 2

Substitute \(x=0\) into the first equation:

\[ 9(0)+4y-7=0 \;\Rightarrow\; 4y=7 \;\Rightarrow\; y=\tfrac{7}{4}. \] Check with the second equation: \(0-4\left(\tfrac{7}{4}\right)+7=-7+7=0\). ✓
Hint: The two lines are symmetric about the \(x\)-axis, so their intersection lies on the \(x\)-axis.
Step 3

Therefore the fixed point is

\[ A=\left(0,\; \tfrac{7}{4}\right). \]
Answer (a)\(A=\left(0,\dfrac{7}{4}\right)\)

bPart (b) — Identify the locus of \(P\)

The condition is \(AP = 8\), i.e. the distance from the moving point \(P(x,y)\) to the fixed point \(A\) is constant. Drag the slider to move \(P\) around and see the locus form.

Step 1

Let the moving point be \(P(x,y)\). The condition is

\[ AP = 8. \] Using the distance formula with \(A=\left(0,\tfrac{7}{4}\right)\): \[ \sqrt{x^2+\left(y-\tfrac{7}{4}\right)^2}=8. \]
Hint: "The distance between \(P\) and \(A\)" is exactly \(AP\).
Step 2

Square both sides:

\[ x^2+\left(y-\tfrac{7}{4}\right)^2 = 8^2 = 64. \]
Hint: Squaring removes the square root and preps the equation for a circle form.
Step 3

This is exactly the standard form of a circle:

\[ (x-0)^2+\left(y-\tfrac{7}{4}\right)^2 = 8^2, \] a circle centred at \(A=\left(0,\tfrac{7}{4}\right)\) with radius \(8\).
Hint: The standard circle equation is \((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2 = r^2\) with centre \((h,k)\) and radius \(r\).
Step 4

Since the locus is a circle, the answer is option A.

Answer (b)Locus is a circle. Correct option: A

ΣSummary — Answers at a Glance

  • Intersection point: \(A=\left(0,\dfrac{7}{4}\right)\)
  • Locus condition: \(AP = 8\) → circle centred at \(A\) with radius \(8\)
  • Locus equation: \(\;x^2+\left(y-\dfrac{7}{4}\right)^2=64\)
  • Correct MC option: A — circle
