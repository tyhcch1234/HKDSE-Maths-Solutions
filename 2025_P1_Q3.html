HKDSE 2025 · Paper 1 · Q3

Let $m$ and $n$ be two numbers. The sum of $2m$ and $3n$ is $999$ while the ratio of $m$ to $n$ is $8:7$.

Find $n$.  (3 marks)

Step-by-step solution

1

Translate the words into equations

“The sum of $2m$ and $3n$ is $999$”:

$$2m+3n=999 \tag{1}$$

“The ratio of $m$ to $n$ is $8:7$”:

$$\frac{m}{n}=\frac{8}{7} \;\Longrightarrow\; 7m=8n. \tag{2}$$

Hint: A ratio $a:b$ means $\dfrac{\text{first}}{\text{second}}=\dfrac{a}{b}$ — write it as a fraction before cross-multiplying.

2

Re-write the ratio for substitution

Ratio and Proportion \(p:q=5:6\ \Rightarrow\ p=\frac{5}{6}q\) A ratio gives one unknown in terms of the other, so the two-variable problem becomes one equation.

From (2), divide both sides by $7$ (or by $7n$):

$$m=\frac{8n}{7}.$$

This works because $n\neq 0$ (a ratio is defined only for non-zero terms).

Hint: Make one variable the subject so a single letter is left in equation (1).

3

Substitute into equation (1)

Substitution \(2\!\left(\frac{5}{6}q\right)+5q=1000\) Substitute the expression from the ratio into the given equation.

Replace $m$ by $\dfrac{8n}{7}$:

$$2\left(\frac{8n}{7}\right)+3n=999.$$

Now every term involves only $n$ — one linear equation in one unknown.

Hint: Always put brackets around the substituted expression to avoid sign or fraction mistakes.

4

Solve the linear equation

Solving a Linear Equation in One Unknown \(\frac{5q}{3}+5q=\frac{20q}{3}\) Combine the like terms over a common denominator before dividing.

$$\frac{16n}{7}+3n=999$$

Multiply every term by $7$ to clear the denominator:

$$16n+21n=6993$$

$$37n=6993$$

$$n=\frac{6993}{37}=189.$$

Hint: $37\times189 = 37(190-1)=7030-37=6993$ — a quick check of the division.

5

Check the answer (both conditions)

With $n=189$:   $m=\dfrac{8(189)}{7}=8\times27=216$.

Ratio check:   $m:n=216:189=216\div27 : 189\div27 = 8:7.$ ✓

Sum check:   $2m+3n=2(216)+3(189)=432+567=999.$ ✓

Hint: Substituting back into both original conditions guarantees no lost marks on the final answer.

Answer:  $n=189$  (with $m=216$)

Question. It is given that \(p:q=5:6\) and \(2p+5q=1000\). Find \(p\) and \(q\).

Show the worked solution
1. From the ratio, \(p=\dfrac{5}{6}q\).
2. Substitute into \(2p+5q=1000\): \(\dfrac{5q}{3}+5q=1000\).
3. \(\dfrac{20q}{3}=1000\), so \(q=150\).
4. Then \(p=\dfrac{5}{6}(150)=125\).
5. Check: \(2(125)+5(150)=250+750=1000\). Hence \(p=125,\ q=150\).
Answer: \(p=125,\ q=150\)
