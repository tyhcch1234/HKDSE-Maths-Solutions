Question. The graph in the figure shows the linear relation between $\log_3 x$ and $\log_3 y$. Which of the following must be true?

  • A. $x^2y^3 = 729$
  • B. $x^3y^2 = 729$
  • C. $x^2 + y^3 = 729$
  • D. $x^3 + y^2 = 729$
1
Read the two intercepts off the graph

The line cuts the horizontal axis ($\log_3 x$-axis) at $x=3$,
giving the point $(3,\,0)$.

The line cuts the vertical axis ($\log_3 y$-axis) at the value $2$,
giving the point $(0,\,2)$.

Hint: the vertical intercept is read on the $\log_3 y$-axis, so its value is $\log_3 y = 2$.
2
Find the slope of the line
Slope of a Line \(m=\dfrac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}\) Using the intercepts \((3,0)\) and \((0,2)\) on the \(\log_{3}x\) and \(\log_{3}y\) axes gives \(m=-\dfrac{2}{3}\).

Using the two points $(3,\,0)$ and $(0,\,2)$:

$$m=\frac{2-0}{0-3}=-\frac{2}{3}$$

Hint: slope $=\dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$ using the two intercepts.
3
Write the equation of the line

Using point–slope form with the point $(3,\,0)$:

$$\log_3 y - 0 = -\frac{2}{3}\left(\log_3 x - 3\right)$$

$$\log_3 y = -\frac{2}{3}\log_3 x + 2$$

(Check: putting $\log_3 x = 0$ gives $\log_3 y = 2$ ✔)

Hint: always use the vertical intercept to verify the constant term.
4
Multiply through to clear the fraction

Multiply both sides by $3$ and rearrange:

$$3\log_3 y = -2\log_3 x + 6$$

$$2\log_3 x + 3\log_3 y = 6$$

Hint: collect the log terms on one side; the constant $6$ becomes the right-hand side.
5
Apply the laws of logarithms
Laws of Logarithms \(k\log_{3}a=\log_{3}a^{k},\qquad \log_{3}a+\log_{3}b=\log_{3}(ab)\) The coefficients 2 and 3 become powers, and the sum becomes the logarithm of a product.

Power law: $k\log_3 a = \log_3 a^k$

$$\log_3 x^2 + \log_3 y^3 = 6$$

Product law: $\log_3 a + \log_3 b = \log_3(ab)$

$$\log_3\!\left(x^2y^3\right) = 6$$

Hint: combining coefficients into powers is the key move for these MC questions.
6
Remove the logarithm
Definition of Logarithm \(\log_{3}A=k\ \Longleftrightarrow\ A=3^{k}\) Since \(\log_{3}(x^{2}y^{3})=6\) and \(3^{6}=729\), the answer is \(x^{2}y^{3}=729\).

Since $3^6 = 729$,

$$x^2y^3 = 3^{6} = 729$$

Hint: if $\log_3 A = k$, then $A = 3^k$.
7
Match with the option list

$x^2y^3 = 729$ matches Option A.

  • A. $x^2y^3 = 729$  
  • B. $x^3y^2 = 729$
  • C. $x^2 + y^3 = 729$
  • D. $x^3 + y^2 = 729$
Hint: options C and D involve sums — logs never turn a sum into a product, so they cannot arise here.
Answer: A  —  $x^2y^3 = 729$

Question. The graph shows the linear relation between \(\log_{2}x\) and \(\log_{2}y\). The line cuts the \(\log_{2}x\)-axis at \(4\) and the \(\log_{2}y\)-axis at \(3\). Find the relation between \(x\) and \(y\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The intercepts give the points \((4,\,0)\) and \((0,\,3)\).
Step 2. Slope \(m=\dfrac{3-0}{0-4}=-\dfrac{3}{4}\), so \(\log_{2}y=-\dfrac{3}{4}\log_{2}x+3\).
Step 3. Multiply by \(4\): \(4\log_{2}y=-3\log_{2}x+12\), i.e. \(3\log_{2}x+4\log_{2}y=12\).
Step 4. By the laws of logarithms, \(\log_{2}(x^{3}y^{4})=12\).
Step 5. Since \(2^{12}=4096\), the relation is \(x^{3}y^{4}=4096\).
Answer: \(x^{3}y^{4}=4096\)
