HKDSE 2013 Paper 2 Q4 Estimation & Error Significant Figures Decimal Places

📐 Estimation, Approximation & Error

Which of the following is correct?

0.0504545 = ?
A. 0.051  (correct to 2 significant figures)
B. 0.0505  (correct to 3 decimal places)
C. 0.05045  (correct to 4 significant figures)
D. 0.05046  (correct to 5 decimal places)
💡

Key Idea

Significant figures start from the first non-zero digit. Decimal places count digits after the decimal point. Rounding depends on the next digit after the required position.

Write the number with place values:

0
5
0

5

Hint: The first non-zero digit is 5 in the thousandths place. That is the 1st significant figure.

A. 0.051 (2 sig. fig.)

The first two significant figures are 5 and 0. Look at the third significant figure: digit 4.

Since 4 < 5, we round down: 0.0504545 → 0.050, not 0.051.

❌ Incorrect.

B. 0.0505 (3 decimal places)

3 decimal places means keep 3 digits after the decimal point: 0.0504545.

The 4th decimal is 4 (< 5), so round down → 0.050, not 0.0505.

❌ Incorrect.

C. 0.05045 (4 significant figures)

First 4 significant figures: 5, 0, 4, 5 (starting from the first 5). The next digit is 4.

Since 4 < 5, we round down → 0.05045. ✅ This matches exactly.

✔ Correct.

D. 0.05046 (5 decimal places)

5 decimal places: 0.0504545 → look at the 6th decimal = 5.

Round up the 5th decimal: 5 → 6, giving 0.05046. But check: the original number is 0.0504545, not 0.050455. The 6th decimal is 4 (not 5) — wait, 0.0504545: digits after decimal: 0 (1st), 5 (2nd), 0 (3rd), 4 (4th), 5 (5th), 4 (6th), 5 (7th). So the 6th decimal is 4, which is less than 5. So we round down, not up. Thus 0.05045, not 0.05046.

❌ Incorrect.

The only correct approximation is C.

\(0.0504545 = 0.05045\) (correct to 4 significant figures).

✅ Answer: C
