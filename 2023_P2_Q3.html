Question & Worked Solution

[2023 MC #3]
Simplify: $\;4^{\,n+2}\,3^{\,2n+4} =$
  • A. $\;6^{\,2n+4}$
  • B. $\;6^{\,4n+8}$
  • C. $\;12^{\,2n+4}$
  • D. $\;12^{\,3n+6}$
Step 1Rewrite base 4 as a power of 2.
$$4^{\,n+2} = (2^{2})^{\,n+2}$$
Hint: $4 = 2^2$, so an expression with base 4 can always be turned into base 2.
Step 2Apply the power law $(a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn}$.
$$(2^{2})^{\,n+2}=2^{\,2(n+2)}=2^{\,2n+4}$$
Hint: multiply the two exponents together.
Power Law of Indices \((a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn}\) \((2^{2})^{n+2}=2^{2n+4}\), so base 4 becomes base 2.
Step 3Both factors now share the SAME exponent.
$$4^{\,n+2}3^{\,2n+4}=2^{\,2n+4}\,3^{\,2n+4}$$
Hint: $2n+4$ appears in both powers — this is the key observation.
Step 4Use $a^{k}b^{k}=(ab)^{k}$.
$$2^{\,2n+4}\,3^{\,2n+4}=(2\cdot 3)^{\,2n+4}=6^{\,2n+4}$$
Hint: equal exponents let you merge the bases by multiplying them.
Product Law with Equal Indices \(a^{k}b^{k}=(ab)^{k}\) Both factors now carry the exponent \(2n+4\), so \(2^{2n+4}3^{2n+4}=6^{2n+4}\).
Step 5Check the options.
$6^{\,2n+4}$ matches option A.
Hint: B doubles the exponent, C and D wrongly merge 2 and 3 into 12 while keeping the wrong exponent.
Answer: A  —  $6^{\,2n+4}$

Question. Simplify \(9^{\,n+1}\,2^{\,2n+2}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Write \(9=3^{2}\), so \(9^{\,n+1}=(3^{2})^{n+1}=3^{\,2n+2}\).
Step 2. The expression becomes \(3^{\,2n+2}\,2^{\,2n+2}\).
Step 3. Both powers share the exponent \(2n+2\), so \(3^{\,2n+2}2^{\,2n+2}=(3\times2)^{2n+2}=6^{\,2n+2}\).
Answer: \(6^{\,2n+2}\)
