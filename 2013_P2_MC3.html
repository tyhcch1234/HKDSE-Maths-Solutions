HKDSE Math 2013 Paper 2 Q3 4. Polynomials Factorization by Grouping

Factorize the expression

$$ h\ell - k\ell + hm - km - hn + kn = $$

A. \((h+k)(\ell - m + n)\)
B. \((h+k)(\ell + m - n)\)
C. \((h-k)(\ell - m + n)\)
D. \((h-k)(\ell + m - n)\)

Correct answer : D

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 of 4
1

Group the terms in pairs

Look for common factors in pairs of terms. Notice that the first four terms can be grouped as \((h\ell - k\ell)\) and \((hm - km)\), while the last two are \((-hn + kn)\).

$$ h\ell - k\ell + hm - km - hn + kn = (h\ell - k\ell) + (hm - km) + (-hn + kn) $$
💡 Hint
Grouping: We group terms that share a common variable factor. Here, \(h\ell\) and \(k\ell\) both contain \(\ell\); \(hm\) and \(km\) both contain \(m\); and \(hn\) and \(kn\) both contain \(n\). Note: the last two terms have a minus sign before \(hn\).
2

Factor out common factors from each pair

From \((h\ell - k\ell)\), factor out \(\ell\): \(\ell(h - k)\). From \((hm - km)\), factor out \(m\): \(m(h - k)\). From \((-hn + kn)\), factor out \(-n\): \(-n(h - k)\).

$$ = \ell(h - k) + m(h - k) - n(h - k) $$
💡 Hint
Factoring each pair:
\(h\ell - k\ell = \ell(h - k)\)
\(hm - km = m(h - k)\)
\(-hn + kn = -n(h - k)\)
Notice the common factor \((h - k)\) appears in all three terms.
3

Factor out the common binomial \((h - k)\)

All three terms contain \((h - k)\). Taking it out as a common factor gives:

$$ = (h - k)(\ell + m - n) $$
💡 Hint
Common factor: The expression is now \(\ell(h - k) + m(h - k) - n(h - k)\). Treat \((h - k)\) as a single factor \(X\). Then it becomes \(\ell X + m X - n X = X(\ell + m - n)\). Substituting back gives \((h - k)(\ell + m - n)\).
4

Match with the options

The fully factorized form is \((h - k)(\ell + m - n)\). Comparing with the given choices, this matches Option D.

$$ \boxed{D. \ (h-k)(\ell + m - n)} $$
