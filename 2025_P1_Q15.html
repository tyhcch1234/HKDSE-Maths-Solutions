HKDSE Math 2025 Paper 1 Q15

Probability · Combinatorics

Probability Combination \(C^{n}_{r}\) Sampling without replacement
📦

There are 8 white plates, 4 red plates and 1 black plate in a box. If 3 plates are randomly drawn from the box at the same time, find

  • (a) the probability that the colours of the 3 plates drawn are the same; (2 marks)
  • (b) the probability that the colours of the 3 plates drawn are all different. (2 marks)

Box content (visual)

W W W W W W W W R R R R B = 13 plates total

🔍 Interactive: same colour vs all different

\(30/143 \approx 20.98\%\)
\(16/143 \approx 11.19\%\)

Total probability space = 1. The remaining probability covers mixed colour combinations.

📝 Step-by-step solution

We choose 3 plates out of 13 without order (combination).

\[ \text{Total outcomes} = C^{13}_{3} = \frac{13 \times 12 \times 11}{3 \times 2 \times 1} = 286 \]

Hint: \(C^n_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}\)

We can only get 3 plates of the same colour if we pick 3 white plates or 3 red plates (there is only 1 black plate).

3 white plates: \(C^{8}_{3} = 56\)

3 red plates: \(C^{4}_{3} = 4\)

Total favourable = \(56 + 4 = 60\)

Probability = (favourable outcomes) / (total outcomes)

\[ P(\text{same colour}) = \frac{C^8_3 + C^4_3}{C^{13}_3} = \frac{56 + 4}{286} = \frac{60}{286} = \frac{30}{143} \]

Answer (a) = 30/143

To have all different colours, we must pick exactly one white, one red, and one black plate.

\[ \text{Favourable} = C^8_1 \times C^4_1 \times C^1_1 = 8 \times 4 \times 1 = 32 \]

Hint: multiply choices for independent colour selections.

\[ P(\text{all different}) = \frac{C^8_1 C^4_1 C^1_1}{C^{13}_3} = \frac{32}{286} = \frac{16}{143} \]

Answer (b) = 16/143

✅ Quick check

\( \frac{30}{143} + \frac{16}{143} = \frac{46}{143} \approx 0.3217 \). The remaining probability accounts for exactly two plates of one colour and one of another (e.g., 2W+1R, 2W+1B, 2R+1W, etc.).
