HKDSE 2025 · Paper 1 · Q15 · Probability

There are 8 white plates, 4 red plates and 1 black plate in a box. If 3 plates are randomly drawn from the box at the same time, find

(a) the probability that the colours of the 3 plates drawn are the same; (2 marks)

(b) the probability that the colours of the 3 plates drawn are all different. (2 marks)

1Identify the total number of plates and the sample space

White plates: $8$, Red plates: $4$, Black plates: $1$.

Total plates $= 8+4+1 = 13$.

Since 3 plates are drawn at the same time, order does not matter — we use combinations.

$$\text{Total outcomes} = C^{13}_{3} = \frac{13\times 12\times 11}{3\times 2\times 1} = 286$$

Hint: "At the same time" ⇒ use $C^n_r$, not permutations.

2Part (a) — Favourable outcomes for "same colour"

The 3 plates must all be white or all be red. (3 black plates are impossible — only 1 black plate exists.)

All white: $C^{8}_{3} = \dfrac{8\times 7\times 6}{3\times 2\times 1} = 56$
All red: $\;C^{4}_{3} = \dfrac{4\times 3\times 2}{3\times 2\times 1} = 4$
Total favourable $= 56 + 4 = 60$

Hint: The black plate can never give a "same colour" triple, so $C^1_3 = 0$.

3Part (a) — Compute the probability

$$P(\text{same colour}) = \frac{C^{8}_{3} + C^{4}_{3}}{C^{13}_{3}} = \frac{56+4}{286} = \frac{60}{286} = \frac{30}{143}$$

Hint: Always simplify the fraction at the end. Answer: $\dfrac{30}{143}$.

4Part (b) — Favourable outcomes for "all different"

To have 3 different colours we must take exactly 1 white, 1 red and 1 black plate.

$$C^{8}_{1}\times C^{4}_{1}\times C^{1}_{1} = 8 \times 4 \times 1 = 32$$

Hint: Choose one plate from each colour group — multiply the combinations (multiplication principle).

5Part (b) — Compute the probability

$$P(\text{all different}) = \frac{C^{8}_{1}C^{4}_{1}C^{1}_{1}}{C^{13}_{3}} = \frac{32}{286} = \frac{16}{143}$$

Hint: The denominator stays the same as in (a): $C^{13}_3 = 286$.

Answer:
(a) $\displaystyle \frac{30}{143}$
(b) $\displaystyle \frac{16}{143}$
