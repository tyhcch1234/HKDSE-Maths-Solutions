For \(90^\circ < \theta < 270^\circ\), how many roots does the equation \(\tan^3\theta = 2\tan\theta\) have?
  • A.  2
  • B.  3
  • C.  4
  • D.  5

Choose the correct option. Explore the interactive graphs below to see how the roots arise.

Step 11M
Move all terms to one side:
\[ \tan^3\theta - 2\tan\theta = 0 \]
Hint: Never divide by \(\tan\theta\) — you would lose the solutions where \(\tan\theta=0\).
Step 21A
Factor out the common factor \(\tan\theta\):
\[ \tan\theta\,(\tan^2\theta - 2) = 0 \] \[ \therefore \tan\theta = 0 \quad\text{or}\quad \tan^2\theta = 2 \]
Hint: A product is zero iff one of its factors is zero — split into two separate cases.
\(\tan\theta = 0\)  or  \(\tan\theta = \sqrt{2}\)  or  \(\tan\theta = -\sqrt{2}\)

Step 11M
Solve each case within \(90^\circ<\theta<270^\circ\).
Case 1: \(\tan\theta=0 \Rightarrow \theta = 0^\circ, 180^\circ, 360^\circ,\dots\)
Only \(\theta = 180^\circ\) lies inside the interval.
Hint: The tangent function has period \(180^\circ\), so within a \(180^\circ\) span each tangent value occurs at most once.
Step 21M
Case 2: \(\tan\theta=\sqrt{2} \Rightarrow \theta \approx 54.7^\circ\) (principal value).
Adding the period \(180^\circ\): \(\theta \approx 54.7^\circ + 180^\circ = 234.7^\circ\).
Case 3: \(\tan\theta=-\sqrt{2} \Rightarrow \theta \approx -54.7^\circ\) (principal value).
Adding the period \(180^\circ\): \(\theta \approx -54.7^\circ + 180^\circ = 125.3^\circ\).
Hint: The tangent graph is strictly increasing on each branch, so on each \(180^\circ\)-wide interval every value is taken exactly once.
Step 31A
Collect all solutions inside \(90^\circ<\theta<270^\circ\):
\[ \theta \approx 125.3^\circ,\quad \theta = 180^\circ,\quad \theta \approx 234.7^\circ \]
That is \(3\) roots in total.
Hint: Check the open endpoints carefully — \(90^\circ\) and \(270^\circ\) are not included.
Number of roots = \(3\)  →  Answer: B

Summary Answers at a Glance

  • Three tangent values: \(\tan\theta = 0,\ \sqrt{2},\ -\sqrt{2}\)
