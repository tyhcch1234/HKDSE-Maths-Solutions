Question

2023 #12
It is given that \(f(x)\) is partly constant and partly varies as \(x^2\). Suppose that \(f(10)=62\) and \(f(15)=122\).

(a) Find \(f(5)\). [3 marks]
(b) Suppose that \(U(0,u)\) and \(V(5,v)\) are points lying on the graph of \(y=f(x)\). The horizontal line passing through \(V\) cuts the \(y\)-axis at the point \(W\). Denote the circle which passes through \(U\), \(V\) and \(W\) by \(C\). Express the circumference of \(C\) in terms of \(\pi\). [4 marks]

Step-by-step Solution

Step 1 · Set up the function Since \(f\) is partly constant and partly varies as \(x^2\): \[ f(x)=p x^2+q \] where \(p\) and \(q\) are non-zero constants.
Use the two given data points to build two equations.
\[ f(10)=100p+q=62 \] \[ f(15)=225p+q=122 \]
Step 2 · Solve for p and q Subtract the two equations: \[ 125p = 60 \;\Rightarrow\; p=\frac{12}{25} \] Substitute back: \[ 100\left(\tfrac{12}{25}\right)+q=62 \;\Rightarrow\; q=14 \] Hence \[ f(x)=\frac{12}{25}x^2+14 \]
Step 3 · Find f(5) \[ f(5)=\frac{12}{25}(25)+14 = 12+14 = 26 \]
(a) \(f(5)=26\)
Step 4 · Locate U, V and W \(U(0,u)\) lies on the graph, so: \[ u=f(0)=14 \] \(V(5,v)\) lies on the graph, so: \[ v=f(5)=26 \] The horizontal line through \(V\) meets the \(y\)-axis at \(W\), so \(W=(0,26)\).
So \(UW = 26-14 = 12\) and \(VW = 5\).
Step 5 · Angle UWV is 90° \(U\) and \(W\) both lie on the \(y\)-axis (vertical segment \(UW\)). \(V\) and \(W\) lie on a horizontal line (segment \(VW\)). Hence \[ \angle UWV = 90^\circ \] By the converse of the angle in a semicircle, \(UV\) is a diameter of circle \(C\).
Step 6 · Circumference of C \[ UV = \sqrt{UW^2+VW^2} = \sqrt{12^2+5^2}=\sqrt{169}=13 \] Radius \(=\dfrac{UV}{2}=\dfrac{13}{2}\) \[ \text{Circumference} = \pi \cdot UV = \pi\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=13\pi \]
(b) The circumference of \(C\) is \(13\pi\).
