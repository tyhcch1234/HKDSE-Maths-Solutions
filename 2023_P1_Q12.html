Question 12

It is given that $f(x)$ is partly constant and partly varies as $x^2$. Suppose that $f(10)=62$ and $f(15)=122$.

(a) Find $f(5)$. [3 marks]

(b) Suppose that $U(0,u)$ and $V(5,v)$ are points lying on the graph of $y=f(x)$. The horizontal line passing through $V$ cuts the $y$-axis at the point $W$. Denote the circle which passes through $U$, $V$ and $W$ by $C$. Express the circumference of $C$ in terms of $\pi$. [4 marks]

1 Set up the partial variation

“Partly constant and partly varies as $x^2$” means

$$f(x) = a + b x^2$$

where $a,b$ are constants.

Hint: two unknown constants ⇒ two pieces of given data are enough to find them.
2 Solve for $a$ and $b$ (part a)
Elimination Method \(a+4b=\dfrac{5}{2},\quad a+36b=\dfrac{13}{2}\ \Longrightarrow\ 32b=4\) Subtracting the two data equations removes \(a\) and leaves \(b\), then \(a\) follows.

Using $f(10)=62$ and $f(15)=122$:

$$a + 100b = 62 \qquad (1)$$ $$a + 225b = 122 \qquad (2)$$

$(2)-(1)$: $\;125b = 60 \Rightarrow b = \dfrac{60}{125} = 0.48$.

Substitute into $(1)$: $a + 100(0.48)=62 \Rightarrow a = 62-48 = 14$.

$$f(x) = 14 + 0.48x^2$$

Hence $f(5) = 14 + 0.48(25) = 14 + 12 = 26$.

Answer for (a): $f(5)=26$ (1M for method, 1A for $a$, 1A for $b$, 1A for $f(5)$).
3 Coordinates of $U$, $V$ and $W$ (part b)

$U(0,u)$ with $u=f(0)=14 \Rightarrow U(0,14)$.

$V(5,v)$ with $v=f(5)=26 \Rightarrow V(5,26)$.

$W$ is on the $y$-axis on the same horizontal line as $V$, so $W(0,26)$.

Hint: “horizontal line through $V$ cuts the $y$-axis” ⇒ $W$ has the same $y$-coordinate as $V$.
4 Find the centre and radius of circle $C$
Equal Radii from the Centre \(OU^{2}=OV^{2}\ \Longrightarrow\ (x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=r^{2}\) Since \(U\) and \(W\) share the \(y\)-axis, the centre has \(y=4.25\), and \(OU=OV\) fixes \(x\).

Since $U,W$ lie on the $y$-axis, the perpendicular bisector of $UW$ is $y=\dfrac{14+26}{2}=20$.

Let the centre be $O_c(x_c,20)$. Using $OU = OV$:

$$x_c^2 + (20-14)^2 = (x_c-5)^2 + (20-26)^2$$ $$x_c^2 + 36 = x_c^2 -10x_c + 25 + 36 \Rightarrow 10x_c = 25 \Rightarrow x_c = 2.5$$

So $O_c(2.5,\,20)$ and

$$r^2 = 2.5^2 + 6^2 = 6.25 + 36 = 42.25 \Rightarrow r = 6.5$$
Hint: the centre lies on the perpendicular bisector of $UW$ because $OU=OW$.
5 Circumference of $C$
Circumference of a Circle \(C=2\pi r\) With \(r=3.75\) the circumference is \(2\pi(3.75)=7.5\pi\).
$$C_{\text{circumference}} = 2\pi r = 2\pi(6.5) = 13\pi$$
Answer for (b): $13\pi$.
✓ Answers

(a) $f(x)=14+0.48x^2$, so $f(5)=26$.

(b) The circumference of $C$ is $13\pi$.

Question. \(f(x)\) is partly constant and partly varies as \(x^{2}\). It is given that \(f(2)=2.5\) and \(f(6)=6.5\).
(a) Find \(f(4)\).
(b) The curve \(y=f(x)\) passes through \(U\), and the horizontal line through \(V(6,\,f(6))\) cuts the \(y\)-axis at \(W\). A circle passes through \(U\), \(V\) and \(W\). Find its centre, its radius and its circumference.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The relation is \(f(x)=a+bx^{2}\), so \(a+4b=2.5\) and \(a+36b=6.5\).
Step 2. Subtracting gives \(32b=4\), so \(b=\dfrac{1}{8}\) and \(a=2\); hence \(f(4)=2+2=4\).
Step 3. \(U(0,2)\), \(V(6,6.5)\) and \(W(0,6.5)\), so the perpendicular bisector of \(UW\) is \(y=4.25\).
Step 4. Let the centre be \((x_{c},4.25)\): \(x_{c}^{2}+2.25^{2}=(x_{c}-6)^{2}+2.25^{2}\), so \(x_{c}=3\).
Step 5. \(r=\sqrt{3^{2}+2.25^{2}}=\sqrt{14.0625}=3.75\), so the circumference is \(2\pi(3.75)=7.5\pi\).
Answer: (a) \(f(4)=4\) (b) centre \((3,4.25)\), \(r=3.75\), circumference \(7.5\pi\)
