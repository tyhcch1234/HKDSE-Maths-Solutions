2015 MC #20

In the figure, AD is a diameter of the circle ABCDE. If \(\angle BAD = 58^\circ\) and \(BC = CD\), then \(\angle AEC =\)

A. \(32^\circ\)
B. \(58^\circ\)
C. \(61^\circ\)
D. \(64^\circ\)
STEP 1 · Split the angle at A
Equal Chords Subtend Equal Angles \(BC=CD\ \Longrightarrow\ \angle BAC=\angle CAD\) The equal chords \(BC\) and \(CD\) split \(\angle BAD=58^{\circ}\) into two equal parts.

Equal chords \(BC = CD\) subtend equal angles at the circumference,
so \(\angle BAC = \angle CAD\).

$$\angle BAC + \angle CAD = \angle BAD = 58^\circ$$

$$\therefore \angle CAD = 58^\circ \div 2 = 29^\circ$$

STEP 2 · Angle in a semi-circle
Angle in a Semicircle \(AD\ \text{is a diameter}\ \Longrightarrow\ \angle ACD=90^{\circ}\) The angle subtended by the diameter \(AD\) at \(C\) is a right angle.

Since \(AD\) is a diameter, the angle subtended at the circumference is a right angle:

$$\angle ACD = 90^\circ \quad (\angle \text{ in semi-circle})$$

STEP 3 · Find \(\angle ADC\) in \(\triangle ACD\)

By the angle sum of a triangle,

$$\angle ADC = 180^\circ - \angle ACD - \angle CAD$$

$$= 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 29^\circ = 61^\circ$$

STEP 4 · Angles in the same segment
Angles in the Same Segment \(\angle AEC=\angle ADC\) Both angles stand on the chord \(AC\) in the same segment.

Both \(\angle AEC\) and \(\angle ADC\) stand on the chord \(AC\) in the same segment:

$$\angle AEC = \angle ADC = 61^\circ$$

ANSWER
C  ·  \(\angle AEC = 61^\circ\)

Question. In the figure, \(AD\) is a diameter of the circle and \(B\), \(C\) lie on the circle with \(BC=CD\). If \(\angle BAD=84^{\circ}\) and \(E\) is a point on the circle, find \(\angle AEC\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Equal chords subtend equal angles,
so \(\angle BAC=\angle CAD=84^{\circ}\div2=42^{\circ}\).
Step 2. Since \(AD\) is a diameter, \(\angle ACD=90^{\circ}\) (angle in a semicircle).
Step 3. In \(\triangle ACD\): \(\angle ADC=180^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-42^{\circ}=48^{\circ}\).
Step 4. Angles in the same segment: \(\angle AEC=\angle ADC=48^{\circ}\).
Answer: \(\angle AEC=48^{\circ}\)
Key facts used: equal chords \(\Rightarrow\) equal angles at the circumference; angle in a semi-circle \(=90^\circ\); angle sum of a triangle \(=180^\circ\); angles in the same segment are equal.
