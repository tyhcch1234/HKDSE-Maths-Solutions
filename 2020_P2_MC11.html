Variations: $w \propto u^3\sqrt{v}$

Topic 2 · Rate, Ratio and Variations  |  Multiple-Choice Question

Question

It is given that $w$ varies as the cube of $u$ and the square root of $v$.

When $u = 2$ and $v = 4$, $w = 8$.

When $u = 4$ and $v = 9$, $w =$

  • A. 96
  • B. 324
  • C. 384
  • D. 729
Joint Variation Proportionality Constant $k$ Substitution Indices & Surds

Step-by-Step Solution

0 / 5

"$w$ varies as the cube of $u$ and the square root of $v$" means $w$ is jointly proportional to $u^3$ and $\sqrt{v}$:

$$w = k\,u^{3}\sqrt{v}$$

where $k$ is a non-zero constant (the proportionality constant).

Hint "varying as" → direct variation. "the cube of $u$" → power $u^{3}$. "the square root of $v$" → power $v^{1/2}$. Multiply all parts together and put one constant $k$ in front.

Given: when $u = 2$, $v = 4$, $w = 8$. Put these into $w = k\,u^{3}\sqrt{v}$:

$$8 = k\,(2)^{3}\sqrt{4}$$
Hint $(2)^3 = 8$ and $\sqrt{4} = 2$, so the right-hand side becomes $k \cdot 8 \cdot 2 = 16k$.
$$8 = 16k \quad\Longrightarrow\quad k = \frac{8}{16} = \frac{1}{2}$$

So the full relationship is:

$$\boxed{\,w = \tfrac{1}{2}\,u^{3}\sqrt{v}\,}$$
Hint This step is essential — you must fix $k$ before you can answer anything about $w$. The constant $k$ never changes once found.
$$w = \tfrac{1}{2}\,(4)^{3}\sqrt{9}$$

Evaluate piece by piece:

$$(4)^{3} = 64,\qquad \sqrt{9} = 3$$
Hint Careful: it is $4^{3}$, not $4 \times 3$. Cubing means $4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64$.
$$w = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 64 \times 3 = \tfrac{64 \times 3}{2} = \frac{192}{2} = 96$$
Correct Choice
A. 96

Why not just "scale" the answer?

A tempting shortcut is to multiply $w$ by the change in $u$ and $\sqrt{v}$. Let's check whether that works.

Factor ×2×2.25×1.5×12
Because $w \propto u^{3}$, doubling $u$ multiplies $w$ by $2^{3} = 8$. Because $w \propto \sqrt{v}$, multiplying $v$ by $2.25$ multiplies $w$ by $\sqrt{2.25} = 1.5$. Together: $8 \times 1.5 = 12$, and $8 \times 12 = 96$. ✓
