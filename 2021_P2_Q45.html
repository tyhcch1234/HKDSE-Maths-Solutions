Arithmetic Sequence \(T(n)\) — Two Groups of 49 Terms

Step 1 — A.P. terms: \(T(n)=a+(n-1)d\). Group 1 = \(T(1),\dots,T(49)\); Group 2 = \(T(51),\dots,T(99)\).

HKDSE 2021 Paper 2 · Q45 · Statistics

Question

It is given that \(T(n)\) is the \(n\)th term of an arithmetic sequence. Let \(x_1\), \(y_1\) and \(z_1\) be the median, the range and the variance of the group of numbers \(\{\,T(1),T(2),T(3),\dots,T(49)\,\}\) respectively, while \(x_2\), \(y_2\) and \(z_2\) be the median, the range and the variance of the group of numbers \(\{\,T(51),T(52),T(53),\dots,T(99)\,\}\) respectively. Which of the following must be true?

I.  \(x_1 < x_2\)
II.  \(y_1 = y_2\)
III.  \(z_1 > z_2\)

A. I only
B. II only
C. I and III only
D. II and III only
Step 1Set up the arithmetic sequence

Let the first term be \(a\) and the common difference be \(d\). Then \[T(n)=a+(n-1)d.\] Group 1: \(T(1),\dots,T(49)\)  ·  Group 2: \(T(51),\dots,T(99)\).

Hint: every term of Group 2 is "50 terms further along" than the corresponding term of Group 1.
Step 2Compare the two groups term-by-term
General Term of an Arithmetic Sequence \(T(50+k)=T(k)+50d\) Every term of Group 2 is exactly \(50d\) larger than the matching term of Group 1.

Compare the \(k\)th term of each group: \[T(50+k)=a+(49+k)d=\underbrace{a+(k-1)d}_{T(k)}+50d .\] So each term of Group 2 is exactly \(50d\) larger than the matching term of Group 1.

Key relation: Group 2 = Group 1 “shifted up” by the constant \(50d\).
Step 3Median: \(x_2 = x_1 + 50d\) — statement I fails
Effect of Adding a Constant on the Median \(x_{2}=x_{1}+50d\) Adding the same constant to every datum adds it to the median, so the median changes by \(50d\).

Adding a constant to every datum adds that constant to the median. Hence \[x_2=x_1+50d.\] If \(d>0\) then \(x_1x_2\). So I is NOT necessarily true.

A "must be true" statement has to hold for every possible value of \(d\).
Step 4Range: \(y_2 = y_1\) — statement II holds
Range is Unchanged by Adding a Constant \(y_{2}=\max-\min=y_{1}\) Both groups are shifted by \(50d\), so the distance between largest and smallest is the same.

Group 2 has the same spread as Group 1 (all terms shifted by the same \(50d\)): \[y_2=\max-\min=T(99)-T(51)=48d=T(49)-T(1)=y_1.\] So \(y_1=y_2\) always true ✔.

Range depends only on how far apart the data are, not on where they sit.
Step 5Variance: \(z_2 = z_1\) — statement III fails

Adding a constant to every datum does not change the variance: \[z_2=z_1.\] Therefore \(z_1>z_2\) is false III is NOT true.

Variance measures spread about the mean; a shift of all data leaves spread unchanged.
Step 6Conclusion

Only statement II must be true.

Answer: B  (II only)

Question. The terms \(T(1),T(2),\dots,T(99)\) of an arithmetic sequence are divided into Group 1: \(T(1)\) to \(T(29)\), and Group 2: \(T(31)\) to \(T(59)\). Let \(x_{1},x_{2}\) be the medians, \(y_{1},y_{2}\) the ranges and \(z_{1},z_{2}\) the variances of the two groups. Which of the following must be true? I. \(x_{2}=x_{1}+30d\) II. \(y_{1}=y_{2}\) III. \(z_{1}>z_{2}\)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Each term of Group 2 is \(30\) terms further along, so \(T(30+k)=T(k)+30d\).
Step 2. I. Adding the constant \(30d\) to every datum adds \(30d\) to the median, so \(x_{2}=x_{1}+30d\). I is true.
Step 3. II. The range is \(\max-\min\): Group 2 has \(T(59)-T(31)=28d\), and Group 1 has \(T(29)-T(1)=28d\). So \(y_{1}=y_{2}\). II is true.
Step 4. III. Adding a constant does not change the spread,
so \(z_{2}=z_{1}\) and \(z_{1}>z_{2}\) is false. III is false.
Step 5. Only I and II must be true.
Answer: I and II only
