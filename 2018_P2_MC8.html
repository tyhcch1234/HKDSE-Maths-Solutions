HKDSE Math 2018 Paper 2 Q8 Polynomials Remainder Theorem

Let \( g(x) = x^8 + ax^7 + b \), where \( a \) and \( b \) are constants.

If \( g(x) \) is divisible by \( x - 1 \), find the remainder when \( g(x) \) is divided by \( x + 1 \).

A. \(0\)
B. \(2a\)
C. \(-2a\)
D. \(-2a + 2\)

Step-by-step solution

Since \(g(x)\) is divisible by \(x - 1\), the remainder when \(g(x)\) is divided by \(x - 1\) is \(0\).

By the Remainder Theorem: \(g(1) = 0\).

\( g(1) = (1)^8 + a(1)^7 + b = 1 + a + b = 0 \)

💡 Hint

When a polynomial is divided by \(x-c\), the remainder is \(p(c)\). So here \(c = 1\).

From Step 1: \(1 + a + b = 0\)

Rearrange to isolate \(b\):

\( b = -a - 1 \)

This relationship is crucial for eliminating \(b\) later.

By the Remainder Theorem, the remainder when \(g(x)\) is divided by \(x + 1\) is \(g(-1)\).

Compute \(g(-1)\):

\( g(-1) = (-1)^8 + a(-1)^7 + b \)
\( \quad = 1 + a(-1) + b \)
\( \quad = 1 - a + b \)

💡 Hint

Remember: \((-1)^8 = 1\) because 8 is even, and \((-1)^7 = -1\) because 7 is odd.

Replace \(b\) with \(-a - 1\) in the expression from Step 3:

\( g(-1) = 1 - a + (-a - 1) \)
\( \quad = 1 - a - a - 1 \)
\( \quad = -2a \)

✅ The remainder is \(-2a\).

This matches option C.
