Mensuration — areas of sectors and triangles in a circle

Circle Geometry Sector Area Equilateral Triangle Area of Shaded Region

QUESTION Multiple Choice [MC]

In the figure, \(AB\) is a diameter of the circle \(ABCD\). If \(AB = 12\text{ cm}\) and \(CD = 6\text{ cm}\), then the area of the shaded region is
A. \(\;(12\pi - 9)\ \text{cm}^2\)
B. \(\;(12\pi + 9)\ \text{cm}^2\)
C. \(\;(12\pi - 9\sqrt{3})\ \text{cm}^2\)
D. \(\;(12\pi + 9\sqrt{3})\ \text{cm}^2\)

PART A Radius & the key angle \(\angle COD\) 1M

6.0
6.0
Circle / diameter \(AB\) Chord \(CD\) Radii \(OA=OB=OC=OD\) \(\triangle OCD\) (isosceles)
Step 11M

Since \(AB\) is a diameter,

\[ r = \frac{AB}{2} = \frac{12}{2} = 6\ \text{cm}. \]

All radii are equal, so \(OC = OD = 6\ \text{cm}\), and also given \(CD = 6\ \text{cm}\).

Hint: The radius of a circle equals half its diameter. This is the first thing to spot whenever a diameter is given.
Step 21M

Now \(OC = OD = CD = 6\ \text{cm}\), so \(\triangle OCD\) is an equilateral triangle.

\[\therefore \angle COD = 60^\circ\]
Hint: Three equal sides ⇒ equilateral ⇒ every interior angle is \(60^\circ\).

PART B Find the central angles \(\angle AOD\) and \(\angle BOC\) 1M

Sector \(OBC\) Sector \(OAD\) \(\triangle OCD\) \(AB/\!/ CD\) line pair
Step 11M

\(AB\) is a straight line (a diameter), so the angles on one side of it add up to \(180^\circ\):

\[ \angle AOD + \angle DOC + \angle COB = 180^\circ \] \[ \angle AOD + 60^\circ + \angle BOC = 180^\circ \] \[ \angle AOD + \angle BOC = 120^\circ \]
Hint: Angles on a straight line sum to \(180^\circ\). Use this with the \(60^\circ\) from Part A.
Step 21M

By symmetry (the whole configuration is symmetric about the perpendicular from \(O\) to \(CD\)),

\[ \angle AOD = \angle BOC = \frac{120^\circ}{2} = 60^\circ. \]
\[\angle AOD = \angle BOC = 60^\circ \quad\text{and}\quad \angle COD = 60^\circ\]
Hint: All three central angles are \(60^\circ\). That means the three sectors are equal in area.

PART C Height \(OM\) of \(\triangle OCD\) 1M

6.0
6.0
\(\triangle OCD\) Height \(OM\) Base \(CD\)
Step 11M

In an isosceles triangle, the line from the apex \(O\) perpendicular to the base \(CD\) bisects the base. So

\[ CM = MD = \frac{CD}{2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3\ \text{cm}. \] \[ \angle COM = \frac{\angle COD}{2} = \frac{60^\circ}{2} = 30^\circ. \]
Hint: "Bisects the base and the apex angle" is a standard property of isosceles triangles.
Step 21M

In the right-angled triangle \(OCM\) (right angle at \(M\)):

\[ OM = OC\cos 30^\circ = 6 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 3\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}. \]
\[OM = 3\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}\]
Hint: Use \(\cos\) when you know the hypotenuse (\(OC = 6\)) and want the adjacent side (\(OM\)).

PART D Area of the shaded region → the answer 1M 1A

6.0
Sector \(OBC\) Sector \(OAD\) \(\triangle OCD\)
Step 11M

Decompose the shaded region into three parts:

\[ \text{Shaded} = \text{sector } OAD + \text{sector } OBC + \triangle OCD \]
Hint: Look at the picture — the shaded area is not a single simple shape. Split it into the two sectors below the horizontal diameter plus the triangle at the bottom.
Step 21M

Sector area formula: \(\dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\pi r^2\).

\[ \text{sector }OAD = \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ}\pi(6)^2 = \frac{1}{6}(36\pi) = 6\pi\ \text{cm}^2 \] \[ \text{sector }OBC = \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ}\pi(6)^2 = 6\pi\ \text{cm}^2 \]

Triangle area: \(\dfrac{1}{2}\times\text{base}\times\text{height}\).

\[ \triangle OCD = \frac{1}{2}\times 6 \times 3\sqrt{3} = 9\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^2 \]
Hint: Both sectors have central angle \(60^\circ = \frac16\) of a full turn, so each is one-sixth of the circle's area. \(\pi(6)^2 = 36\pi\), and \(\frac{36\pi}{6} = 6\pi\).
Step 31A

Add the three pieces:

\[ \text{Shaded area} = 6\pi + 6\pi + 9\sqrt{3} = (12\pi + 9\sqrt{3})\ \text{cm}^2 \]
\[\boxed{(12\pi + 9\sqrt{3})\ \text{cm}^2 \;\Rightarrow\; \textbf{Answer: D}}\]
Hint: Compare with the options. The correct one is D — the others miss the \(9\sqrt{3}\) or get its sign wrong.

Summary of Answers

  • Radius of the circle: r = 6 cm (since \(AB = 12\) cm is a diameter)
  • \(\triangle OCD\) is equilateral: ∠COD = 60°
  • Angles on the diameter: ∠AOD = ∠BOC = 60°
  • Each sector: 6π cm²  (\(\frac{60}{360}\times\pi\times 6^2\))
  • Height of \(\triangle OCD\): OM = 3√3 cm
  • Area of \(\triangle OCD\): 9√3 cm²
  • Shaded area = \(12\pi + 9\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^2\) → Option D
