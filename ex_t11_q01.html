T11 Q01

In a triangle, two of the angles are $55°$ and $65°$. Find the third angle.

Solution

The sum of the angles in a triangle is $180^\circ$.
Sum of the two given angles: $55^\circ + 65^\circ = 120^\circ$.
Third angle: $180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ$.
The third angle is $60^\circ$.
