Laws of Indices

Question [2013 #1]:

Simplify $\dfrac{x^{20}y^{13}}{(x^{5}y)^{6}}$ and express your answer with positive indices. [3 marks]

Step 1Expand the denominator using \((ab)^n=a^nb^n\).
Power of a Product \((ab)^{n}=a^{n}b^{n}\) Raise each factor inside the bracket to the power \(6\), including the invisible \(y^{1}\).

\[ (x^{5}y)^{6}=x^{5\times 6}\,y^{1\times 6}=x^{30}y^{6} \]

Power of a product: raise every factor inside the bracket to the power 6.
Step 2Rewrite the fraction.

\[ \frac{x^{20}y^{13}}{(x^{5}y)^{6}}=\frac{x^{20}y^{13}}{x^{30}y^{6}} \]

Now the numerator and denominator have the same bases \(x\) and \(y\).
Step 3Apply \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\) for each base.
Quotient Law of Indices \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\quad(a\neq 0)\) Same base, dividing: subtract the lower index from the higher one for \(x\) and for \(y\).

\[ x^{20-30}=x^{-10},\qquad y^{13-6}=y^{7} \]

Subtract the denominator index from the numerator index.
Step 4Combine and convert to positive indices using \(a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^{n}}\).
Negative Index Law \(a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^{n}}\) The index of \(x\) is negative, so move \(x^{10}\) to the denominator and make the index positive.

\[ x^{-10}y^{7}=\frac{y^{7}}{x^{10}} \]

A negative index moves the base to the opposite part of the fraction.
Answer: \(\displaystyle \frac{y^{7}}{x^{10}}\)

Question. Simplify \(\dfrac{a^{18}b^{7}}{(a^{4}b)^{5}}\) and express your answer with positive indices.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Expand the denominator: \((a^{4}b)^{5}=a^{4\times5}b^{5}=a^{20}b^{5}\).
Step 2. Rewrite: \(\dfrac{a^{18}b^{7}}{a^{20}b^{5}}\).
Step 3. Subtract the indices: \(a^{18-20}=a^{-2}\) and \(b^{7-5}=b^{2}\).
Step 4. Convert the negative index: \(a^{-2}b^{2}=\dfrac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}\)
