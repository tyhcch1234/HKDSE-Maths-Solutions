Mensuration — Areas of Triangles with a Common Height
Parallelogram Area Common Height Area Ratio Similar Triangles

Question Multiple Choice

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram. \(E\) and \(F\) are points lying on \(AB\) and \(CD\) respectively. \(AD\) produced meet at \(G\). It is given that \(DF:FC=3:4\) and \(AD:DG=1:1\). If the area of \(\Delta DFG\) is \(3\text{ cm}^2\), then the area of the parallelogram \(ABCD\) is

  1. \(12\text{ cm}^2\)
  2. \(14\text{ cm}^2\)
  3. \(18\text{ cm}^2\)
  4. \(21\text{ cm}^2\)

Part (a) — Relating \(\Delta ADF\) and \(\Delta DFG\) Common Height

First we compare \(\Delta ADF\) and \(\Delta DFG\). They share the same height from \(F\) to the line \(AG\) (i.e. the line containing \(AD\)), so their areas are in the ratio of their bases \(AD\) and \(DG\).

AD : DG = 1.00 : 1 Area \(\Delta ADF\) = 3.00 cm² Area \(\Delta DFG\) = 3.00 cm²
Step 11M

\(\Delta ADF\) and \(\Delta DFG\) have the same height (the perpendicular distance from \(F\) to line \(ADG\)).

Hint: When two triangles share the same height, their areas are proportional to their bases. Here the bases are \(AD\) and \(DG\).
Step 21A

\[\frac{\text{Area of }\Delta ADF}{\text{Area of }\Delta DFG}=\frac{AD}{DG}=\frac{1}{1}\]

So the area of \(\Delta ADF=3\text{ cm}^2\).

Result: Area of \(\Delta ADF=3\text{ cm}^2 \qquad (1)\)
0 / 2

Part (b)(i) — Relating \(\Delta ACD\) and \(\Delta ADF\) Common Height

Now compare \(\Delta ACD\) and \(\Delta ADF\). They share the same height from \(A\) to the line \(CD\) (the line containing \(DF\)), so their areas are in the ratio of their bases \(CD\) and \(DF\).

DF = 3 units FC = 4 units Area \(\Delta ACD\) = 7.00 cm²
Step 11M

\(\Delta ACD\) and \(\Delta ADF\) have the same height (from \(A\) to line \(CD\)). Therefore

\[\frac{\text{Area of }\Delta ACD}{\text{Area of }\Delta ADF}=\frac{CD}{DF}\]

Hint: \(CD=DF+FC\), so \(\dfrac{CD}{DF}=\dfrac{DF+FC}{DF}=1+\dfrac{FC}{DF}\).
Step 21A

Given \(DF:FC=3:4\), we have \(\dfrac{FC}{DF}=\dfrac{4}{3}\). Hence

\[\frac{\text{Area of }\Delta ACD}{\text{Area of }\Delta ADF}=1+\frac{4}{3}=\frac{7}{3}\]

Result: Area of \(\Delta ACD=\dfrac{7}{3}\times \text{Area of }\Delta ADF \qquad (2)\)
0 / 2

Part (b)(ii) — Relating \(ABCD\) and \(\Delta ACD\) Parallelogram Diagonal

The diagonal \(AC\) divides the parallelogram \(ABCD\) into two congruent triangles \(\Delta ACD\) and \(\Delta ABC\).

Area \(\Delta ACD\) = 7.00 cm² Area \(ABCD\) = 14.00 cm²
Step 11M

\(ABCD\) and \(\Delta ACD\) have a common base (\(CD\)) and the same height (the perpendicular distance between \(AB\) and \(CD\)).

Hint: A parallelogram can be split by a diagonal into two triangles of equal area.
Step 21A

\[\text{Area of } ABCD = 2\times \text{Area of }\Delta ACD \qquad (3)\]

Result: Area of \(ABCD=2\times \text{Area of }\Delta ACD\)
0 / 2

Part (b)(iii) — Final Area of \(ABCD\) Combine Results

Combine equations (1), (2) and (3) to find the area of the parallelogram.

Step 11M

Substitute (1) and (2) into (3):

\[\text{Area of }ABCD=2\times\frac{7}{3}\times \text{Area of }\Delta ADF\]

Hint: Use Area of \(\Delta ADF=3\) cm² from part (a).
Step 21A

\[\text{Area of }ABCD=2\times\frac{7}{3}\times 3=\frac{42}{3}=14\text{ cm}^2\]

Answer: \(14\text{ cm}^2\) — Option B
0 / 2

Summary of Results

  • Area of \(\Delta ADF=3\text{ cm}^2\) (since \(AD:DG=1:1\))
  • \(\dfrac{\text{Area of }\Delta ACD}{\text{Area of }\Delta ADF}=1+\dfrac{4}{3}=\dfrac{7}{3}\)
  • Area of \(ABCD=2\times\text{Area of }\Delta ACD\)
  • Area of \(ABCD=2\times\dfrac{7}{3}\times 3=\boxed{14\text{ cm}^2}\)
  • Correct option: B
