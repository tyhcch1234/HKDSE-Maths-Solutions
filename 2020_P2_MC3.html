HKDSE Math 2020 Paper 2 Q3

Polynomials & Algebraic Fractions

#Algebraic Fractions #Difference of Squares #LCM of Denominators #Simplification

Question

Simplify the following expression:

\[ \frac{5}{4k+3} \;-\; \frac{2}{4k-3} \]

Choose the correct answer:

A. \(\dfrac{12k-21}{16k^2-9}\)
B. \(\dfrac{12k+9}{16k^2-9}\)
C. \(\dfrac{14k-21}{16k^2-9}\)
D. \(\dfrac{14k+9}{16k^2-9}\)

Step-by-Step Guided Solution

Click each step to reveal the reasoning. Try to predict the next line before expanding!

The two denominators are \((4k+3)\) and \((4k-3)\). They share no common factor, so the least common denominator (LCD) is their product:

\[ \text{LCD} = (4k+3)(4k-3) \]
💡 Hint: This is the difference of squares pattern: \((a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2\). It will simplify later.

Multiply the numerator and denominator of each fraction by the missing factor:

\[ \frac{5}{4k+3} = \frac{5\,(4k-3)}{(4k+3)(4k-3)}, \qquad \frac{2}{4k-3} = \frac{2\,(4k+3)}{(4k-3)(4k+3)} \]
💡 Hint: For the first fraction, the missing factor is \((4k-3)\). For the second, it is \((4k+3)\). This keeps the fraction value unchanged.

Now both fractions have the same denominator, so subtract the numerators:

\[ \frac{5\,(4k-3) - 2\,(4k+3)}{(4k+3)(4k-3)} \]
💡 Hint: The minus sign applies to the entire numerator of the second fraction: \(-\big(2(4k+3)\big)\).

Distribute the numbers into the brackets:

\[ \frac{(20k-15) - (8k+6)}{(4k+3)(4k-3)} \]
💡 Hint: Be careful with signs when distributing the minus: \(-2(4k+3) = -8k - 6\).

Combine like terms in the numerator:

\[ (20k-15) - (8k+6) = 20k - 15 - 8k - 6 = 12k - 21 \]

For the denominator, apply the difference of squares:

\[ (4k+3)(4k-3) = (4k)^2 - 3^2 = 16k^2 - 9 \]
💡 Hint: \((a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2\). Here \(a = 4k\) and \(b = 3\), so \(a^2 = 16k^2\) and \(b^2 = 9\).
\[ \frac{12k-21}{16k^2-9} \]

Check: can \(12k-21\) be factorised further? \(12k-21 = 3(4k-7)\), but this does not cancel with the denominator. So this is the simplest form.

✅ Correct answer: A

Key Takeaways

  • • To subtract algebraic fractions, find the LCD first.
  • • Remember to distribute the negative sign across the entire numerator.
  • • Difference of squares: \((a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2\) is a powerful shortcut.
  • • Always check if the final numerator can be factorised and cancelled.
