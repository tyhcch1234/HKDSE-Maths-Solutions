[2018 MC #15]

In the figure, the volume of the solid right triangular prism is

  • A. 544 cm³
  • B. 600 cm³
  • C. 660 cm³
  • D. 720 cm³
Key idea: Volume of a prism = (area of uniform cross-section) × (length). The cross-section here is a right-angled triangle; use Pythagoras to find the missing leg.
Step 1 · Identify the cross-section & the length

The uniform cross-section is the right-angled triangle with legs $8$ cm and an unknown side, and hypotenuse $17$ cm.

The prism is extruded along its base of length $12$ cm, so the prism length is $12$ cm.

Step 2 · Find the unknown side by Pythagoras' Theorem

\[\text{unknown side}=\sqrt{17^{2}-8^{2}}\ \text{cm}\]

\[=\sqrt{289-64}=\sqrt{225}=15\ \text{cm}\]

Step 3 · Area of the triangular cross-section

\[\text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}\times 15\times 8=60\ \text{cm}^{2}\]

Using the two perpendicular legs as base and height of the right-angled triangle.

Step 4 · Volume of the prism

\[V=\text{Area of cross-section}\times \text{length}\]

\[=\frac{1}{2}(15)(8)\times 12=60\times 12=720\ \text{cm}^{3}\]

FINAL ANSWER
D.  720 cm³
