HKDSE 2024 · Paper 2 · Q31

Topic 4: Polynomials

The H.C.F. of $u^{2}v^{3}w$, $u^{3}vw^{2}$ and $u^{2}v^{3}w^{4}$ is

  • A. $uvw$
  • B. $u^{2}vw$
  • C. $u^{2}v^{3}w^{4}$
  • D. $u^{3}v^{3}w^{4}$
1 Read off the powers (indices) of each variable

Write the three expressions and list the exponent of $u$, $v$ and $w$ in each:

$u^{2}v^{3}w^{1}$  →  $u^{2}\,v^{3}\,w^{1}$
$u^{3}v^{1}w^{2}$  →  $u^{3}\,v^{1}\,w^{2}$
$u^{2}v^{3}w^{4}$  →  $u^{2}\,v^{3}\,w^{4}$

Remember: a variable with no visible index has index 1 (e.g. $v = v^{1}$).
Index Notation \(v=v^{1}\) A variable with no visible index has index \(1\), so the three expressions are \(u^{2}v^{3}w\), \(u^{3}vw^{2}\) and \(u^{2}v^{3}w^{4}\).
2 Take the smallest power of each variable

For an H.C.F. (highest common factor) of products, the common factor takes the lowest index of each variable:

$u:\ \min(2,\ 3,\ 2) = 2$
$v:\ \min(3,\ 1,\ 3) = 1$
$w:\ \min(1,\ 2,\ 4) = 1$

Why minimum? The H.C.F. must divide every expression, so it can contain $u$ at most 2 times, $v$ at most 1 time and $w$ at most 1 time.
H.C.F. of Algebraic Terms \(\text{H.C.F. takes the lowest index of each variable}\) \(\min(2,3,2)=2\) for \(u\), \(\min(3,1,3)=1\) for \(v\) and \(\min(1,2,4)=1\) for \(w\).
3 Combine to write the H.C.F.

Multiply the common lowest powers together:

$$\text{H.C.F.} = u^{2}\,v^{1}\,w^{1} = u^{2}vw$$

Check the options: A has index too small; C and D contain higher indices that do not divide all three expressions.
✓  Answer: B  —  $u^{2}vw$

Question. Find the H.C.F. of \(a^{3}b^{2}c\), \(a^{2}b^{2}c^{3}\) and \(a^{4}bc^{2}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Write the indices: \(a^{3}b^{2}c^{1}\), \(a^{2}b^{2}c^{3}\), \(a^{4}b^{1}c^{2}\).
Step 2. Lowest index of \(a\): \(\min(3,2,4)=2\); of \(b\): \(\min(2,2,1)=1\); of \(c\): \(\min(1,3,2)=1\).
Step 3. H.C.F. \(=a^{2}bc\).
Answer: \(a^{2}bc\)

Tip: For H.C.F. take the lowest powers; for L.C.M. take the highest powers.
