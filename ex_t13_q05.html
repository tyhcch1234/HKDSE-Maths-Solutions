Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Ratios

T13 Q05

Find the value of $\cos 60^\circ$.

Solution

Recall the exact trigonometric value $\cos 60^\circ$.
From the standard exact values, $\cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2}$.
$\cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2}$.
