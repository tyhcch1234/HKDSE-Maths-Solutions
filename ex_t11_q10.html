Geometry (106) · Sub-Topic: Angles in Triangles/Polygons

T11 Q10

A regular polygon has 12 sides. Find the size of each exterior angle.

Solution

For a regular polygon, the sum of the exterior angles is $360^\circ$.
Since the polygon is regular and has $12$ sides, all exterior angles are equal.
Each exterior angle is $360^\circ \div 12 = 30^\circ$.
The size of each exterior angle is $30^\circ$.
