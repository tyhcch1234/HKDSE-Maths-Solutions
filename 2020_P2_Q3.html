HKDSE 2020 Paper 2 · Q3 · Polynomials

Question. Find the value of \[ \frac{5}{4k+3} - \frac{2}{4k-3}. \]

  • A. $\dfrac{12k-21}{16k^{2}-9}$
  • B. $\dfrac{12k+9}{16k^{2}-9}$
  • C. $\dfrac{14k-21}{16k^{2}-9}$
  • D. $\dfrac{14k+9}{16k^{2}-9}$

Step-by-step solution

1Identify the two denominators

The two fractions have different denominators,
so we cannot subtract the numerators directly.
The denominators are the two conjugate-looking linear factors \(4k+3\) and \(4k-3\).

Hint: To subtract fractions,
first make the denominators the same.

2
Difference of Two Squares\((a+b)(a-b)=a^{2}-b^{2}\)The common denominator \((4k+3)(4k-3)\) expands to \(16k^{2}-9\).
Find the lowest common denominator

The L.C.D. is the product of the two denominators: \[ \text{L.C.D.} = (4k+3)(4k-3). \]

Expand it using the difference of squares identity \(a^{2}-b^{2}=(a+b)(a-b)\): \[ (4k+3)(4k-3) = (4k)^{2} - 3^{2} = 16k^{2}-9. \]

Hint: $(4k+3)$ and $(4k-3)$ have no common factor,
so the L.C.D. is simply their product.

3Rewrite each fraction over the common denominator

Multiply the numerator and denominator of each fraction by the missing factor:

\[ \frac{5}{4k+3} = \frac{5(4k-3)}{(4k+3)(4k-3)}, \qquad \frac{2}{4k-3} = \frac{2(4k+3)}{(4k+3)(4k-3)}. \]

Hint: Top and bottom of each fraction must be multiplied by the same thing.

4
Adding Algebraic Fractions\(\dfrac{a}{b}-\dfrac{c}{d}=\dfrac{ad-bc}{bd}\)Over the common denominator the numerator becomes \(5(4k-3)-2(4k+3)\).
Subtract the numerators

Now the denominators match,
so combine the numerators over the single common denominator — remember to distribute the minus sign to both terms of $2(4k+3)$:

\[ \frac{5(4k-3) - 2(4k+3)}{(4k+3)(4k-3)} = \frac{(20k-15) - (8k+6)}{(4k+3)(4k-3)}. \]

Hint: $-(8k+6) = -8k-6$,
not $-8k+6$. This is the most common trap.

5
Distributive Law for a Minus Sign\(-(8k+6)=-8k-6\)The minus sign changes both terms, giving \(20k-15-8k-6=12k-21\).
Simplify the numerator

Collect the like terms in the numerator: \[ (20k-15) - (8k+6) = 20k - 15 - 8k - 6 = 12k - 21. \]

Hint: $20k-8k = 12k$ and $-15-6 = -21$.

6Write the final answer

Put the simplified numerator over the expanded common denominator:

\[ \frac{5}{4k+3} - \frac{2}{4k-3} = \frac{12k-21}{16k^{2}-9}. \]

This matches option A.

Hint: Options B and D have a wrong numerator sign;
option C comes from a wrong constant term (using $5\cdot3-2\cdot3$ incorrectly).

Answer: A  —  $\dfrac{12k-21}{16k^{2}-9}$

Question. Find the value of \(\dfrac{3}{2m+5}-\dfrac{4}{2m-5}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The L.C.D. is \((2m+5)(2m-5)=4m^{2}-25\), by the difference of squares.
Step 2. Write over the common denominator: \(\dfrac{3(2m-5)-4(2m+5)}{4m^{2}-25}\).
Step 3. Expand the numerator: \(6m-15-8m-20\).
Step 4. Simplify: \(-2m-35\), so the value is \(\dfrac{-2m-35}{4m^{2}-25}=\dfrac{2m+35}{25-4m^{2}}\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{2m+35}{25-4m^{2}}\)
