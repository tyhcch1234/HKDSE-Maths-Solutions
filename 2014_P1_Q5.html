HKDSE Math 2014 Paper 1 Q5

Formulas Change of Subject Sequences & Change

Question

Consider the formula \(2(3m + n) = m + 7\).

(a) Make \(n\) the subject of the above formula.

(b) If the value of \(m\) is increased by \(2\), write down the change in the value of \(n\).

4 marks

Explore the Relationship

Drag the slider to change \(m\) and observe how \(n\) changes according to the formula.

\(m\) 3.0
\(n\) 2.0

Formula

\(2(3m + n) = m + 7\)

Rearranged for \(n\)

\(n = \frac{7 - 5m}{2}\)

When \(m\) increases by 2, \(n\) decreases by 5.

Step-by-Step Solution

Expand the left side of the equation:

\(2(3m + n) = m + 7\)

\(6m + 2n = m + 7\)

Hint: Distribute the 2 to both terms inside the bracket.

Move the \(m\) term to the right side and the constant to the left:

\(6m + 2n = m + 7\)

\(2n = m + 7 - 6m\)

\(2n = 7 - 5m\)

Hint: Subtract \(6m\) from both sides to isolate terms with \(n\).

Divide both sides by 2:

\(n = \frac{7 - 5m}{2}\)

Answer (a): \(n = \frac{7 - 5m}{2}\)

From part (a), \(n = \frac{7 - 5m}{2}\).

If \(m\) increases by 2, the new value of \(n\) becomes:

\(n_{\text{new}} = \frac{7 - 5(m + 2)}{2} = \frac{7 - 5m - 10}{2}\)

\(n_{\text{new}} = \frac{-3 - 5m}{2}\)

The change in \(n\) is:

\(n_{\text{new}} - n = \frac{-3 - 5m}{2} - \frac{7 - 5m}{2}\)

\(= \frac{-3 - 5m - 7 + 5m}{2}\)

\(= \frac{-10}{2} = -5\)

Answer (b): The decrease in the value of \(n\) is 5.

Quick Alternative for (b)

Since \(n = \frac{7 - 5m}{2} = \frac{7}{2} - \frac{5}{2}m\), the coefficient of \(m\) is \(-\frac{5}{2}\). So when \(m\) increases by 2, \(n\) changes by \(-\frac{5}{2} \times 2 = -5\). Hence \(n\) decreases by 5.
