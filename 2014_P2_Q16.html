Question

In the figure, ABCD is a square. BC is produced to G such that $\angle CDG = 25^\circ$. E is a point lying on AB such that $AE = CG$. If F is a point lying on BC such that $\angle CDF = 20^\circ$, then $\angle DFE =$

A. $60^\circ$
B. $65^\circ$
C. $70^\circ$
D. $73^\circ$
Step 1 · Identify the congruent triangles

In $\triangle ADE$ and $\triangle CDG$:

$AE = CG$ (given) $\angle DAE = \angle DCG = 90^\circ$ (properties of square) $AD = CD$ (sides of square)

$\therefore \triangle ADE \cong \triangle CDG$   (SAS)

The square gives the right angle and equal side, and $AE=CG$ is given — so SAS applies immediately.
Step 2 · Corresponding angles & sides

Since the triangles are congruent, corresponding parts are equal:

$\angle ADE = \angle CDG = 25^\circ$   (corr. $\angle$s, $\cong \triangle$s) $DE = DG$   (corr. sides, $\cong \triangle$s)
This is the key bridge: it transfers the $25^\circ$ given at $G$ up to vertex $D$, and gives equal slant sides.
Step 3 · Find the two angles at D

At vertex $D$ the right angle $\angle ADC = 90^\circ$ is split by $DE$ and $DF$:

$\angle EDF = \angle ADC - \angle ADE - \angle CDF$ $= 90^\circ - 25^\circ - 20^\circ = 45^\circ$

On the other side of $DC$:

$\angle GDF = \angle CDG + \angle CDF = 25^\circ + 20^\circ = 45^\circ$

Hence $\angle EDF = \angle GDF = 45^\circ$.

Step 4 · Second congruence (ΔDEF ≅ ΔDGF)

In $\triangle DEF$ and $\triangle DGF$:

$DE = DG$ (proved in Step 2) $\angle EDF = \angle GDF$ (proved in Step 3) $DF = DF$ (common side)

$\therefore \triangle DEF \cong \triangle DGF$   (SAS)

Step 5 · Conclude the required angle

Corresponding angles of the congruent triangles are equal:

$\angle DFE = \angle DFG$   (corr. $\angle$s, $\cong \triangle$s)

In $\triangle CDF$, since $\angle DCF = 90^\circ$ (square) and $\angle CDF = 20^\circ$:

$\angle CFD = 180^\circ - \angle DCF - \angle CDF$ $= 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 20^\circ = 70^\circ$

Since $B$, $F$, $C$, $G$ are collinear, $\angle DFG$ and $\angle CFD$ are the same angle, so

$\angle DFE = \angle DFG = 70^\circ$
Final Answer  ·  Option C $\angle DFE = 70^\circ$
