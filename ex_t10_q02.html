Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Area

T10 Q02

Find the area of a circle with radius $7$ cm. (Take $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.)

Solution

Use the formula for the area of a circle: $A=\pi r^2$.
Substitute $r=7$ and $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$: $A=\frac{22}{7}\times 7^2$.
Simplify: $A=\frac{22}{7}\times 49=22\times 7=154$.
So the area is $154\text{ cm}^2$.
The area of the circle is $154\text{ cm}^2$.
