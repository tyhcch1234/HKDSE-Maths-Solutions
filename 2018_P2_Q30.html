[2018 MC #30] — 15. Statistics

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of books read by 20 students in a year.
Stem (tens)  |  Leaf (units)
   2       |  1  2  2  8
   3       |  $a$  $a$
   4       |  0  2  4  5  5  7  8
   5       |  3
   6       |  $b$  $b$  9  9
   7       |  0  8

If the inter-quartile range of the above distribution is at most $25$, which of the following must be true?

I. $\;5 \le a \le 9$
II. $\;0 \le b \le 4$
III. $\;1 \le a - b \le 6$

  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III
Step 1Order the data &
find quartile positions.
Quartiles of a Data Set \(n=4k\ \Longrightarrow\ Q_{1}=x_{\left(\frac{n}{4}\right)},\quad Q_{3}=x_{\left(\frac{3n}{4}\right)}\) With \\(n=20\\), \\(Q_{1}\\) is the \\(5\\)th value and \\(Q_{3}\\) is the \\(15\\)th value of the ordered data.

The 20 values, already in ascending order of stem then leaf,
are read off directly.
With $n=20$:

$Q_1$ is the $\tfrac{n}{4}=\tfrac{20}{4}=5$th value, and $Q_3$ is the $\tfrac{3n}{4}=\tfrac{60}{4}=15$th value.

Hint: for $n$ divisible by 4, HKDSE takes $Q_1$ = the $\frac{n}{4}$th datum and $Q_3$ = the $\frac{3n}{4}$th datum.
Step 2Locate $Q_1$ and $Q_3$ in the table.

Counting positions 1 to 20:

Positions 1–4: $\;21,22,22,28$  
(stem 2)
Positions 5–6: $\;3a,3a$  
(stem 3)
Positions 7–13: $\;40,42,44,45,45,47,48$  
(stem 4)
Position 14: $\;53$  
(stem 5)
Positions 15–18: $\;6b,6b,69,69$  
(stem 6)
Positions 19–20: $\;70,78$  
(stem 7)

So the 5th value is $30+a$  
and 
the 15th value is $60+b$. Hence $Q_1 = 30+a$ and $Q_3 = 60+b$. Also notice $a$ occupies positions 5 and 6,
so we need $a \ge 0$ and (to keep order) $a \le 4$ is not required — only that $3a$ lies after $28$ would force $a\ge 3$,
but $a$ as leaf of stem 3 with two equal leaves is acceptable for any digit;
we keep $a\in\{0,\dots,9\}$.

Hint: $Q_1$ sits at the stem-3 row, $Q_3$ at the stem-6 row (because $b$ repeats twice).
Step 3Apply IQR $\le 25$.
Inter-Quartile Range \(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) \\(Q_{3}-Q_{1}=(60+b)-(30+a)=30+(b-a)\leq25\\), so \\(a-b\geq5\\).

$$Q_3 - Q_1 = (60+b)-(30+a) = 30 + (b-a) \le 25$$

$$\Rightarrow \; b - a \le -5 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a - b \ge 5.$$

Hint: the IQR is $Q_3 - Q_1$, i.e. $30$ minus the "extra" $a$, plus the "extra" $b$.
Step 4Test statement I: $5 \le a \le 9$.

From $a-b \ge 5$ and $b \ge 0$:  $a \ge b+5 \ge 5$.

Since $a$ is a digit, $a \le 9$ automatically.
So $5 \le a \le 9$: TRUE ✓

Hint: $b$ cannot be negative (it is a leaf digit), so $a$ must be at least 5.
Step 5Test statement II: $0 \le b \le 4$.

From $a-b \ge 5$ and $a \le 9$:  $b \le a - 5 \le 9-5 = 4$.

Also $b \ge 0$ (digit). So $0 \le b \le 4$: TRUE ✓

Hint: symmetry with Step 4 — now bound $b$ using the largest possible $a$.
Step 6Test statement III: $1 \le a - b \le 6$.
Counter-example \(\text{one counter-example}\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{the statement is not always true}\) Taking \\(a=9\\), \\(b=0\\) gives \\(a-b=9>6\\) while the IQR is still \\(21\\), so statement III fails.

We know $a - b \ge 5$. The maximum of $a-b$ is $9-0=9$, which gives no upper restriction from the IQR condition alone.

Check a counter-example: take $a = 9$ and $b = 0$. Then $a-b = 9$,
which violates $a-b \le 6$, yet $Q_3 - Q_1 = 30 + (0-9) = 21 \le 25$ is fine.
So statement III need not be true.

FALSE ✗

Hint: only $a-b \ge 5$ is forced — the IQR condition gives a lower bound, not an upper one.
Step 7Conclude.

Only statements I and II must be true → Option A.

Hint: "must be true" means true for every valid configuration — one counter-example kills a statement.
Answer: A (I and II only)

(b) Find the inter-quartile range.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. There are \\(n=16\\) data, so \\(Q_{1}\\) is the \\(4\\)th value,
the median is the average of the \\(8\\)th and \\(9\\)th values,
and \\(Q_{3}\\) is the \\(12\\)th value.
Step 2. The ordered data are \\(21,23,23,28,30,34,35,39,42,46,48,49,51,55,57,59\\), so \\(Q_{1}=28\\).
Step 3. Median \\(=\\dfrac{39+42}{2}=40.5\\).
Step 4. \\(Q_{3}=49\\).
Step 5. (b) \\(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}=49-28=21\\).
Answer: (a) \\(Q_{1}=28\\), median \\(=40.5\\), \\(Q_{3}=49\\) \quad (b) \\(21\)
