Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Area

T10 Q05

Find the area of a semicircle with radius $14$ cm, taking $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.

Solution

Area of a full circle is $\pi r^2$.
Substitute $r=14$ and $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$: area of full circle $=\frac{22}{7}\times 14^2=\frac{22}{7}\times 196=616\text{ cm}^2$.
A semicircle is half of a circle, so its area is $\frac{616}{2}=308\text{ cm}^2$.
The area of the semicircle is $308\text{ cm}^2$.
