Coordinate Geometry — Perpendicular Bisector & Circle Equation
Mid-point Perpendicular Bisector Circle Equation Diameter from General Form

Question [2015 #14]

Total: 9 marks  ·  (a) 6 marks  ·  (b) 3 marks

The coordinates of the points \(P\) and \(Q\) are \((4,-1)\) and \((-14,23)\) respectively.

(a)

  1. Let \(L\) be the perpendicular bisector of \(PQ\). Find the equation of \(L\).
  2. Suppose that \(G\) is a point lying on \(L\). Denote the \(x\)-coordinate of \(G\) by \(h\). Let \(C\) be the circle which is centred at \(G\) and passes through \(P\) and \(Q\). Prove that the equation of \(C\) is \[2x^{2}+2y^{2}-4hx-(3h+59)y+13h-93=0 .\]

[6 marks]

(b) The coordinates of the point \(R\) are \((26,43)\). Using (a)(ii), or otherwise, find the diameter of the circle which passes through \(P\), \(Q\) and \(R\).

[3 marks]

Part (a)(i) — Equation of the Perpendicular Bisector \(L\) 2 marks

M = (-5, 11)  |  slope PQ = -4/3  |  slope L = 3/4
Segment \(PQ\) Perpendicular bisector \(L\) Mid-point \(M\)
Step 11M
Find the mid-point \(M\) of \(PQ\): \[M=\left(\frac{4+(-14)}{2},\ \frac{-1+23}{2}\right)=(-5,\ 11)\]
Hint: The perpendicular bisector must pass through the mid-point of the segment.
Step 21M
Slope of \(PQ\) and of \(L\): \[m_{PQ}=\frac{23-(-1)}{-14-4}=\frac{24}{-18}=-\frac{4}{3}\] Since \(L\perp PQ\), \(\displaystyle m_L=-\frac{1}{m_{PQ}}=\frac{3}{4}\).
Hint: For perpendicular lines, the product of slopes is \(-1\).
Step 31A
Point–slope form through \(M(-5,11)\) with slope \(\tfrac34\): \[y-11=\frac{3}{4}\bigl(x-(-5)\bigr)\] \[4y-44=3x+15\ \Longrightarrow\ 3x-4y+59=0\]
Hint: Multiply through by 4 to clear the fraction, then rearrange into general form.
\[L:\ 3x-4y+59=0\]

Part (a)(ii) — Equation of the Circle \(C\) 4 marks

G = (-5.0, 11.0)  |  r = 15.00  |  r² = 225.00
Circle \(C\) Line \(L\) \(P\), \(Q\)
Step 11M
Since \(G\) lies on \(L\), and the \(x\)-coordinate of \(G\) is \(h\), substitute \((h,k)\) into \(3x-4y+59=0\): \[3h-4k+59=0\ \Longrightarrow\ k=\frac{3h+59}{4}\]
Hint: Let the \(y\)-coordinate of \(G\) be \(k\), then use the line equation.
Step 21M
The circle is centred at \(G(h,k)\) and passes through \(P(4,-1)\), so \(r^2 = (4-h)^2+(-1-k)^2\). Equation of \(C\): \[(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=(4-h)^2+(-1-k)^2\]
Hint: Standard form of a circle: \((x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2\).
Step 31M
Substitute \(k=\dfrac{3h+59}{4}\) and expand: \[x^2-2hx+h^2+y^2-2ky+k^2=(4-h)^2+(-1-k)^2\] \[x^2+y^2-2hx-2\!\left(\frac{3h+59}{4}\right)\!y+8h-2\!\left(\frac{3h+59}{4}\right)\!-17=0\]
Hint: Collect \(x\) and \(y\) terms on one side; the \(h^2, k^2\) cancel with part of the right-hand side.
Step 41A
Simplify, then multiply by 2: \[2x^2+2y^2-4hx-(3h+59)y+13h-93=0\]
Hint: Clear the denominators by multiplying by 2 to reach the required form.
\[C:\ 2x^{2}+2y^{2}-4hx-(3h+59)y+13h-93=0\]

Part (b) — Diameter of the Circle through \(P, Q, R\) 3 marks

Centre = (11, 23)  |  radius = 25.00  |  diameter = 50.00
Circle through \(P,Q,R\) \(P\), \(Q\), \(R\) Diameter
Step 11M
Let the circle through \(P,Q,R\) be \(C'\\) with centre on \(L\). Since \(R(26,43)\) lies on the circle, substitute \((x,y)=(26,43)\) into the equation from (a)(ii): \[2(26)^2+2(43)^2-4h(26)-(3h+59)(43)+13h-93=0\]
Hint: The centre of any circle through \(P\) and \(Q\) lies on the perpendicular bisector \(L\).
Step 21M
Solve the linear equation in \(h\): \[1352+3698-104h-129h-2537+13h-93=0\] \[-220h+2420=0\ \Longrightarrow\ h=11\] So the centre is \(\bigl(11,\ \tfrac{3(11)+59}{4}\bigr)=(11,23)\).
Hint: Use \(k=\dfrac{3h+59}{4}\) to find the \(y\)-coordinate of the centre.
Step 31A
With \(h=11\), the equation becomes \(x^2+y^2-22x-46y+25=0\). The diameter is \[\text{diameter}=2\sqrt{\left(\frac{22}{2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{46}{2}\right)^2-25}=2\sqrt{121+529-25}=2\sqrt{625}=50\]
Hint: For \(x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey+F=0\), \(r=\sqrt{\left(\tfrac D2\right)^2+\left(\tfrac E2\right)^2-F}\).
\[\text{Diameter}=50\]
0 / 3

Summary of Answers

  • (a)(i) \(M=(-5,11)\), \(m_{PQ}=-\tfrac43\), \(m_L=\tfrac34\)  ⟹  \(L:\ 3x-4y+59=0\).
  • (a)(ii) \(k=\dfrac{3h+59}{4}\). Circle equation: \[2x^{2}+2y^{2}-4hx-(3h+59)y+13h-93=0 .\]
  • (b) Centre of \(C'\\) is \((11,23)\). Equation \(x^{2}+y^{2}-22x-46y+25=0\). Diameter \(=2\sqrt{11^{2}+23^{2}-25}=2\sqrt{625}=50\).
