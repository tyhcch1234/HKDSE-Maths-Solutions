[2015#14] Coordinate Geometry Perpendicular bisector · Equation of a circle · Diameter

The coordinates of the points $P$ and $Q$ are $(4,-1)$ and $(-14,23)$ respectively.

(a) Let $L$ be the perpendicular bisector of $PQ$.

(i) Find the equation of $L$.

(ii) Suppose that $G$ is a point lying on $L$. Denote the $x$-coordinate of $G$ by $h$. Let $C$ be the circle which is centred at $G$ and passes through $P$ and $Q$. Prove that the equation of $C$ is
$2x^{2}+2y^{2}-4hx-(3h+59)y+13h-93=0$.

[6 marks]

(b) The coordinates of the point $R$ are $(26,43)$. Using (a)(ii), or otherwise, find the diameter of the circle which passes through $P$, $Q$ and $R$. [3 marks]

Step 1 · Mid-point & slope of PQ
$$M=\left(\frac{4+(-14)}{2},\ \frac{-1+23}{2}\right)=(-5,\,11)$$ $$\text{Slope of }PQ=\frac{23-(-1)}{-14-4}=\frac{24}{-18}=-\frac{4}{3}$$
Key idea: the perpendicular bisector $L$ passes through $M$ and is perpendicular to $PQ$, so its slope is the negative reciprocal of $-\tfrac43$.
Mid-point Formula \(M=\left(\dfrac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2},\ \dfrac{y_{1}+y_{2}}{2}\right)\) With \(P(4,-1)\) and \(Q(-14,23)\) this gives \(M(-5,\,11)\), the point that \(L\) must pass through.
Step 2 · Equation of the perpendicular bisector L
$$\text{Slope of }L=\frac{3}{4}$$ $$y-11=\frac{3}{4}\bigl(x-(-5)\bigr)$$ $$4y-44=3x+15 \;\Longrightarrow\; \boxed{3x-4y+59=0}$$
Perpendicular Lines \(m_{1}\,m_{2}=-1\) The slope of \(PQ\) is \(-\tfrac{4}{3}\), so the perpendicular bisector has slope \(\tfrac{3}{4}\).
Step 3 · (a)(ii) Coordinates of G

Since $G(h,k)$ lies on $L$: $\;3h-4k+59=0$, hence

$$k=\frac{3h+59}{4}$$
Note: $G$ is the common centre of all circles through $P$ and $Q$ — every such centre lies on $L$, so $C$ is fully described by the single parameter $h$.
Step 4 · Radius GP and the equation of the circle C

$C$ passes through $P(4,-1)$, so $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=(4-h)^2+(-1-k)^2$:

$$x^2+y^2-2hx-2\Bigl(\tfrac{3h+59}{4}\Bigr)y+8h-2\Bigl(\tfrac{3h+59}{4}\Bigr)-17=0$$ $$2x^2+2y^2-4hx-(3h+59)y+13h-93=0 \quad \blacksquare$$
Standard Equation of a Circle \((x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=r^{2}\) Here the centre is \(G\bigl(h,\tfrac{3h+59}{4}\bigr)\) and the radius is \(GP\), so expanding this form gives the equation of \(C\).
Step 5 · (b) Use R to find h

$R(26,43)$ lies on the circle through $P,Q,R$, so substitute $x=26,\ y=43$:

$$2(26)^2+2(43)^2-4h(26)-(3h+59)(43)+13h-93=0$$ $$h=11$$
Step 6 · Centre, radius and diameter

With $h=11$: $\;x^2+y^2-22x-46y+25=0$, so the centre is $(11,23)$ and

$$r=\sqrt{\left(\tfrac{22}{2}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{46}{2}\right)^2-25}=\sqrt{11^2+23^2-25}$$ $$r=\sqrt{625}=25$$ $$\text{Diameter}=2\times 25=50$$
Answer: (a)(i) $\;3x-4y+59=0$  ·  (b) the diameter is $50$ units.

Question. The coordinates of the points \(P\) and \(Q\) are \((1,-2)\) and \((9,4)\) respectively. \(L\) is the perpendicular bisector of \(PQ\), and \(G(h,k)\) is a point on \(L\). Let \(C\) be the circle centred at \(G\) which passes through \(P\) and \(Q\). (a) Find the equation of \(L\). (b) Show that the equation of \(C\) is \(3x^{2}+3y^{2}-6hx+(8h-46)y+22h-107=0\). (c) The coordinates of \(R\) are \((16,3)\). Find the diameter of the circle which passes through \(P\), \(Q\) and \(R\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. (a) The mid-point of \(PQ\) is \(M\left(\dfrac{1+9}{2},\ \dfrac{-2+4}{2}\right)=(5,\,1)\) and the slope of \(PQ\) is \(\dfrac{4-(-2)}{9-1}=\dfrac{3}{4}\).
Step 2. So the slope of \(L\) is \(-\dfrac{4}{3}\), and \(L\): \(y-1=-\dfrac{4}{3}(x-5)\), i.e. \(4x+3y-23=0\).
Step 3. (b) As \(G(h,k)\) lies on \(L\), \(4h+3k=23\), so \(k=\dfrac{23-4h}{3}\). The radius is \(GP\), hence \((x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=(1-h)^{2}+(-2-k)^{2}\), which expands to \(x^{2}+y^{2}-2hx-2ky+2h-4k-5=0\).
Step 4. Substituting \(k=\dfrac{23-4h}{3}\) and multiplying by \(3\) gives \(3x^{2}+3y^{2}-6hx+(8h-46)y+22h-107=0\).
Step 5. (c) Put \(x=16\), \(y=3\): \(3(256)+3(9)-96h+3(8h-46)+22h-107=550-50h=0\), so \(h=11\) and \(k=\dfrac{23-44}{3}=-7\).
Step 6. The centre is \((11,-7)\) and \(r=\sqrt{(1-11)^{2}+(-2+7)^{2}}=\sqrt{125}=5\sqrt{5}\), so the diameter is \(10\sqrt{5}\approx22.4\) units.
Answer: (a) \(4x+3y-23=0\) (b) shown (c) diameter \(=10\sqrt{5}\approx22.4\)
