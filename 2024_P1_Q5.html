Question  (4 marks)

Let $a$, $b$ and $c$ be non-zero numbers such that \[ 5a=6c \qquad \text{and} \qquad \frac{2b+7c}{b+c}=4 . \] Find $\dfrac{5a+8b}{2b+3c}$.

1 Turn the first condition into a ratio

From $5a=6c$ we get

\[ \frac{a}{c}=\frac{6}{5} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a:c = 6:5 . \]

Collect the two letters that appear in the equation and form a fraction; this gives the ratio of $a$ to $c$ directly.

2 Solve the second condition for $b:c$

Cross-multiplying $\dfrac{2b+7c}{b+c}=4$:

\[ 2b+7c = 4(b+c) = 4b+4c \] \[ 7c-4c = 4b-2b \] \[ 3c = 2b \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{b}{c}=\frac{3}{2} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad b:c=3:2 . \]

Expand, move every $b$ to one side and every $c$ to the other, then read off the ratio.

3 Make the two ratios compatible

The common letter $c$ must have the same number in both ratios. Write them with $c=10$:

\[ b:c = 3:2 = 15:10, \qquad a:c = 6:5 = 12:10 \] \[ \boxed{\,a:b:c = 12:15:10\,} \]

LCM of the two $c$-values ($2$ and $5$) is $10$, so scale each ratio by the appropriate factor.

4 Introduce a common multiplier $k$

For some non-zero constant $k$, take

\[ a = 12k, \qquad b = 15k, \qquad c = 10k . \]

Using one parameter $k$ keeps all three numbers in the correct proportion.

5 Substitute and evaluate

\[ \frac{5a+8b}{2b+3c} = \frac{5(12k)+8(15k)}{2(15k)+3(10k)} = \frac{60k+120k}{30k+30k} = \frac{180k}{60k} = 3 . \]

Every term carries $k$, so the unknown $k$ cancels — the answer is a pure number.

Answer: $\;\dfrac{5a+8b}{2b+3c} = 3$
