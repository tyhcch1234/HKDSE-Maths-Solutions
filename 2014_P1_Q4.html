📊 Distribution of calculators owned by students

Step 0 — Frequency distribution: 0 → 7 students, 1 → 14, 2 → 15, 3 → 4.

HKDSE 2014 · Paper 1 · Q4

The table below shows the distribution of the numbers of calculators owned by some students.

Number of calculators 0123
Number of students 714154

Find the median, the mode and the standard deviation of the above distribution. [3 marks]

STEP 1

Set up the working table

Total number of students: \[ N = 7 + 14 + 15 + 4 = \mathbf{40} \] Record $f$ (frequency),
the cumulative frequency and $fx$: \[ \begin{array}{c|c|c|c} x & f & \text{cum. }f & fx\\ \hline 0 & 7 & 7 & 0\\ 1 & 14 & 21 & 14\\ 2 & 15 & 36 & 30\\ 3 & 4 & 40 & 12 \end{array} \] \[ \sum f = 40,\qquad \sum fx = 0+14+30+12 = \mathbf{56} \]
Hint: always build a frequency table with columns $x,\ f,\ \text{cum.}f,\ fx$ — the cumulative frequency is needed for the median.
STEP 2

The median

Median of a Discrete Data Set \(\text{median}=\dfrac{x_{N/2}+x_{N/2+1}}{2}\quad(N\text{ even})\) Here \(N=40\), so average the 20th and 21st values, both equal to \(1\).
With $N = 40$ (even), the median is the mean of the $\tfrac{40}{2}=20$th and $21$st values.
From the cumulative frequency, the $20$th and $21$st values both fall in the group $x = 1$ (cum. $f$ goes $7 \to 21$). \[ \text{Median} = \frac{1+1}{2} = \mathbf{1} \]
Hint: cum. $f = 21 \ge 20$ but cum. $f$ before it is only $7 < 20$, so the 20th/21st values are both $1$.
STEP 3

The mode

The mode is the value with the highest frequency. \[ f(0)=7,\quad f(1)=14,\quad f(2)=\mathbf{15},\quad f(3)=4 \] The largest frequency is $15$,
which corresponds to $x = 2$. \[ \text{Mode} = \mathbf{2} \]
Hint: mode = the data value (not the frequency) that occurs most often.
STEP 4

The mean (needed for the s.d.)

Mean of a Frequency Distribution \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}\) Dividing the total \(\sum fx=56\) by the 40 students gives \(1.4\).
\[ \bar{x} = \frac{\sum fx}{\sum f} = \frac{56}{40} = 1.4 \] Now compute $\sum fx^2$: \[ 0^2(7)+1^2(14)+2^2(15)+3^2(4) = 0+14+60+36 = \mathbf{110} \]
Hint: for standard deviation we need both $\sum fx$ and $\sum fx^2$.
STEP 5

The standard deviation

Standard Deviation of a Frequency Distribution \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum fx^{2}}{\sum f}-\bar{x}^{2}}\) Use \(\sum fx^{2}=110\), \(\sum f=40\) and \(\bar{x}=1.4\) to obtain \(\sqrt{0.79}\).
Use the formula \[ \sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum fx^2}{\sum f} - \left(\frac{\sum fx}{\sum f}\right)^{2}} \] \[ \sigma = \sqrt{\frac{110}{40} - (1.4)^{2}} = \sqrt{2.75 - 1.96} = \sqrt{0.79} \] \[ \sigma \approx \mathbf{0.889} \;(\text{to 3 sig. fig.}) \]
Hint: square-root at the very end only; keep full accuracy in the intermediate steps.
ANSWER

Final answers

\[ \text{Median} = 1,\qquad \text{Mode} = 2,\qquad \sigma \approx 0.889 \]
Answer: median $=1$,
 
mode $=2$, 
standard deviation $\approx 0.889$

Question. The table shows the distribution of the number of books \(x\) read by \(20\) students in a month: \(x=1,2,3,4\) with frequencies \(3,8,6,3\) respectively. Find the median, the mode and the standard deviation of the distribution (correct to 3 significant figures). [3 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Cumulative frequencies: \(3,\ 11,\ 17,\ 20\), so \(N=20\).
Step 2. Median: the 10th and 11th values both lie in the group \(x=2\),
so the median is \(\dfrac{2+2}{2}=2\).
Step 3. Mode: the largest frequency is \(8\), which belongs to \(x=2\),
so the mode is \(2\).
Step 4. \(\sum fx=3+16+18+12=49\), so \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{49}{20}=2.45\);
also \(\sum fx^{2}=3+32+54+48=137\).
Step 5. \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{137}{20}-(2.45)^{2}}=\sqrt{6.85-6.0025}=\sqrt{0.8475}\approx 0.921\) (3 s.f.).
Answer: median \(=2\), mode \(=2\), \(\sigma\approx 0.921\)
