Statistics — median, mode and standard deviation from a frequency table

The Question

Number of Calculators Owned by 40 Students 3 marks

The table below shows the distribution of the numbers of calculators owned by some students.

Number of calculators0123
Number of students714154

Find the median, the mode and the standard deviation of the above distribution. 1A · 1A · 1A

Part (a)

Median & Mode from the Frequency Table 2A

We begin by reading the data: a total of \(N = 7+14+15+4 = 40\) students.

k = 40
Total students: 40
Median position: 20.5
Median value: 1
Mode value: 2
1Find the median — locate the middle value1A

There are \(N = 40\) students (an even number), so the median is the average of the 20th and 21st ordered values.

Cumulative frequency:

\[ 0\!:\!7 \quad\to\quad 1\!:\!7\!+\!14=21 \quad\to\quad 2\!:\!21\!+\!15=36 \quad\to\quad 3\!:\!36\!+\!4=40 \]

Both the 20th and 21st students fall in the group with cumulative frequency 21, i.e. the group owning 1 calculator.

\[ \text{Median}=\frac{1+1}{2}=1 \]
Hint: With an even number of data, always take \(\dfrac{20\text{th}+21\text{st}}{2}\). If both fall in the same class, the median is just that class value.
Median \(= 1\)
2Find the mode — the most frequent value1A

The mode is the value with the highest frequency. Comparing the frequencies:

\[ 7,\;\;14,\;\;\underline{15},\;\;4 \]

The largest frequency is \(15\), which corresponds to students owning 2 calculators.

Hint: Read the mode directly from the second row of the table — it is the value above the largest frequency.
Mode \(= 2\)

Part (b)

Standard Deviation 1A

Use the deviation method: \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum f x^2}{\sum f}-\left(\dfrac{\sum f x}{\sum f}\right)^{2}}\).

×1
\(n=\sum f\): 40
\(\sum f x\): 60
\(\sum f x^2\): 110
Mean \(\bar x\): 1.5
\(\sigma\): 0.889

Notice: scaling every frequency by the same factor m does not change the mean or the standard deviation — only the total count changes.

Summary of Answers

  • Total number of students: \(n = \sum f = 40\)
  • Median: 1  — the 20th and 21st values both equal 1
  • Mode: 2  — highest frequency is 15
  • Mean: \(\bar{x} = \dfrac{56}{40} = 1.4\)
  • Standard deviation: \(\sigma = \sqrt{\dfrac{110}{40}-1.4^{2}} \approx\) 0.889 (3 s.f.)
