Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q25

Find the volume of a cone with base radius $7$ cm and height $12$ cm, taking $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.

Solution

Volume $= \tfrac13\pi r^2 h = \tfrac13\times\tfrac{22}{7}\times49\times12 = 616$ cm³.
