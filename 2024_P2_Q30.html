Step 1 — the given data set

given numbers unknowns $m,n$ mode = 5 median
HKDSE 2024 · Paper 2 · Q30

Consider the following positive integers:

5 5 5 6 9 9 11 13 m n

Let $p$, $q$ and $r$ be the standard deviation, the mode and the median of the above positive integers respectively. If the mean of the above positive integers is $7$, which of the following must be true?

I.  $p > 3$
II.  $q = 5$
III.  $r < 7$

  • A.  I and II only
  • B.  I and III only
  • C.  II and III only
  • D.  I, II and III  
1Use the mean to find $m+n$

There are 10 numbers and the mean is 7, so the total is $10 \times 7 = 70$.

$$5+5+5+6+9+9+11+13+m+n = 70$$

Sum of the known values: $5+5+5+6+9+9+11+13 = 63$.

$$\therefore\; m+n = 70-63 = 7$$

First extract everything the mean tells you.
Mean of a Data Set \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum x}{n}\) Ten numbers with mean \(7\) give a total of \(70\), so \(m+n=70-63=7\).
2Determine the mode $q$

$m$ and $n$ are positive integers with $m+n=7$.

If the mode were not 5, some other value would have to occur at least 3 times. But 6, 9, 11 and 13 each appear fewer than 3 times already, and neither $m$ nor $n$ can equal 6, 9, 11 or 13 (as $m+n=7$), so they cannot create a new triple.

Since 5 already appears 3 times, the mode is $5$.

$$\therefore\; q = 5 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{II is true.}$$

Only one value can currently be the mode.
3List all possible $(m,n)$ and compute median & s.d.

With $m,n$ positive integers and $m+n=7$, the possibilities are $(m,n)=(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$. By symmetry of the set, we only need $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4)$.

For 10 values the median is the mean of the 5th and 6th.
Median of an Even Number of Data \(\text{median}=\tfrac12\left(x_{5}+x_{6}\right)\) With \(10\) values the median is the mean of the 5th and 6th ordered values.
4Test the three statements

I. $p>3$: $p = 3.3166,\ 3.1937,\ 3.1305$ — all are greater than 3. ✔

II. $q=5$: shown in Step 2. ✔

III. $r<7$: $r = 6,\ 5.5,\ 5.5$ — all are less than 7. ✔

Hence I, II and III are all true.

Check "must be true" for every allowed $(m,n)$.
Answer:  D  (I, II and III)

Question. Consider the positive integers \(4,4,4,5,8,8,10,12,m,n\). Let \(p\), \(q\) and \(r\) be their standard deviation, mode and median respectively. If the mean is \(6\), which of the following must be true? I. \(p>3\) II. \(q=4\) III. \(r<6\)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Total \(=10\times6=60\), and \(4+4+4+5+8+8+10+12=55\), so \(m+n=5\).
Step 2. The value \(4\) already appears three times and neither \(m\) nor \(n\) can reach it three times, so \(q=4\): II is true.
Step 3. For \((m,n)=(1,4)\) the ordered list is \(1,4,4,4,4,5,8,8,10,12\), median \(4.5<6\); for \((2,3)\) it is \(2,3,4,4,4,5,8,8,10,12\), median \(4.5<6\). III is true.
Step 4. Standard deviations: \(3.19\) for \((1,4)\) and \(3.66\) for \((2,3)\), both \(>3\), so I is true.
Answer: I, II and III
