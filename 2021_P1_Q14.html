Mensuration — Cylinder, Cones and Similar Solids

Topic 10 · Interactive solution with live figure

Figure — Cylinder $X$, Cones $Y$ & $Z$

Cylinder $X$ (r = 10 cm, h = 20 cm) Cone $Y$ (r = 10 cm, h = 24 cm) Cone $Z$ (r = 20 cm, h = 21 cm) Centre / radius marks

Question

The base radius of the solid right circular cylinder $X$ and the base radius of the solid right circular cone $Y$ are equal. The heights of $X$ and $Y$ are $20\text{ cm}$ and $24\text{ cm}$ respectively. The volume of the solid right circular cone $Z$ is equal to the sum of the volume of $X$ and the volume of $Y$. The base radius of $Z$ is equal to the base diameter of $X$. A craftsman finds that the volume of $Y$ is $800\pi\text{ cm}^3$.

(a) Find the base radius of $Y$. [2 marks]

(b) Are $Y$ and $Z$ similar? Explain your answer. [3 marks]

(c) The craftsman claims that the sum of the curved surface area of $X$ and the curved surface area of $Y$ is greater than the curved surface area of $Z$. Do you agree? Explain your answer. [3 marks]

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1 · (a) Base radius of cone $Y$
Volume of a Cone \(V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}h\) \(\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}(24)=800\pi\) gives \(r^{2}=100\), so \(r=10\) cm.

Let $r$ cm be the base radius of $Y$ (and hence also of $X$).

The volume of a right circular cone is $\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$, so

$$\frac{\pi r^2(24)}{3}=800\pi$$

$$\frac{24\pi r^2}{3}=800\pi \;\Rightarrow\; 8r^2=800 \;\Rightarrow\; r^2=100 \;\Rightarrow\; r=10$$

Cancel the common factor $\pi$ and divide both sides by 8. Since $r>0$, take the positive root.

Answer (a): The base radius of $Y$ is $10\text{ cm}$.
STEP 2 · (b) Volume of cylinder $X$ and cone $Z$
Volume of a Cylinder \(V=\pi r^{2}h\) \(V_X=\pi(10^{2})(20)=2000\pi\), so \(V_Z=2000\pi+800\pi=2800\pi\).

Base radius of $X$ is $r=10\text{ cm}$, height $=20\text{ cm}$:

$$V_X=\pi(10^2)(20)=2000\pi\text{ cm}^3$$

Given $V_Y=800\pi\text{ cm}^3$, the cone $Z$ has volume

$$V_Z=V_X+V_Y=2000\pi+800\pi=2800\pi\text{ cm}^3$$

The base radius of $Z$ equals the base diameter of $X$, i.e. $2\times 10=20\text{ cm}$ — so the radius ratio is $10:20=1:2$.

STEP 3 · (b) Testing similarity of $Y$ and $Z$
Volumes of Similar Solids \(\dfrac{V_{1}}{V_{2}}=\left(\dfrac{l_{1}}{l_{2}}\right)^{3}\) Radii are \(10:20\), so similar cones would need \(\dfrac{V_Y}{V_Z}=\dfrac{1}{8}\), but \(\dfrac{V_Y}{V_Z}=\dfrac{2}{7}\).

For similar solids, volumes scale as the cube of the linear ratio:

$$\left(\frac{\text{base radius of }Y}{\text{base radius of }Z}\right)^3=\left(\frac{10}{20}\right)^3=\left(\frac12\right)^3=\frac18$$

But the actual volume ratio is

$$\frac{V_Y}{V_Z}=\frac{800\pi}{2800\pi}=\frac{2}{7}\neq\frac18$$

Equivalently, comparing with $V_Z$, $$\frac{V_X \text{ ratio check}}{}\quad \frac{V_Y}{V_Z}\neq\left(\frac{r_Y}{r_Z}\right)^3.$$

Answer (b): Since $\dfrac{V_Y}{V_Z}\neq\left(\dfrac{r_Y}{r_Z}\right)^3$, $Y$ and $Z$ are not similar.
STEP 4 · (c) Curved surface areas of $X$ and $Y$

Cylinder $X$ (curved surface area $=2\pi r h$):

$$\text{CSA}_X=2\pi(10)(20)=400\pi\text{ cm}^2$$

Cone $Y$ (curved surface area $=\pi r l$, slant height $l$):

$$l_Y=\sqrt{10^2+24^2}=\sqrt{100+576}=\sqrt{676}=26$$

$$\text{CSA}_Y=\pi(10)(26)=260\pi\text{ cm}^2$$

The slant height is found by Pythagoras on the vertical height $24$ and radius $10$.

STEP 5 · (c) Height and curved surface area of $Z$

Let $h$ cm be the height of cone $Z$, whose radius is $20\text{ cm}$:

$$\frac{\pi(20^2)h}{3}=2800\pi \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{400h}{3}=2800 \;\Rightarrow\; h=21$$

Slant height of $Z$:

$$l_Z=\sqrt{20^2+21^2}=\sqrt{400+441}=\sqrt{841}=29$$

$$\text{CSA}_Z=\pi(20)(29)=580\pi\text{ cm}^2$$

STEP 6 · (c) Comparing the areas — conclusion

Sum of the curved surface areas of $X$ and $Y$:

$$400\pi+260\pi=660\pi\text{ cm}^2$$

Compare with $\text{CSA}_Z=580\pi\text{ cm}^2$:

$$660\pi > 580\pi$$

Answer (c): Since $660\pi\text{ cm}^2>580\pi\text{ cm}^2$, the claim is agreed.

Question. Xie is a solid right circular cylinder and Yiu is a solid right circular cone with the same base radius \(r\) cm. The heights of Xie and Yiu are \(8\text{ cm}\) and \(16\text{ cm}\) respectively, and the volume of Yiu is \(768\pi\text{ cm}^{3}\). The cone Zung has volume equal to the sum of the volumes of Xie and Yiu, and its base radius equals the base diameter of Xie.  [8 marks]
(a) Find the base radius of Yiu. [2 marks]
(b) Are Yiu and Zung similar? Explain your answer. [3 marks]
(c) Is the sum of the curved surface areas of Xie and Yiu greater than the curved surface area of Zung? Explain your answer. [3 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}(16)=768\pi\), so \(\dfrac{16r^{2}}{3}=768\), \(r^{2}=144\) and \(r=12\) cm.
Step 2. \(V_{\text{Xie}}=\pi(12^{2})(8)=1152\pi\), so \(V_{\text{Zung}}=1152\pi+768\pi=1920\pi\). Zung\u2019s radius is \(2\times12=24\) cm.
Step 3. Similar cones would need \(\dfrac{V_{\text{Yiu}}}{V_{\text{Zung}}}=\left(\dfrac{12}{24}\right)^{3}=\dfrac{1}{8}\), but \(\dfrac{768\pi}{1920\pi}=\dfrac{2}{5}\ne\dfrac{1}{8}\), so they are not similar.
Step 4. \(\text{CSA}_{\text{Xie}}=2\pi(12)(8)=192\pi\);
the slant height of Yiu is \(\sqrt{12^{2}+16^{2}}=20\), so \(\text{CSA}_{\text{Yiu}}=\pi(12)(20)=240\pi\).
Step 5. Zung: \(\dfrac{\pi(24^{2})h}{3}=1920\pi\) gives \(h=10\), and its slant height is \(\sqrt{24^{2}+10^{2}}=26\), so \(\text{CSA}_{\text{Zung}}=\pi(24)(26)=624\pi\).
Step 6. \(192\pi+240\pi=432\pi<624\pi\), so the claim (that the sum is greater) is not agreed.
Answer: (a) \(12\text{ cm}\)  (b) No, \(\dfrac{V_{\text{Yiu}}}{V_{\text{Zung}}}=\dfrac{2}{5}\ne\dfrac{1}{8}\)  (c) No — \(432\pi<624\pi\)
Exam tips: Always state the formula before substituting. For "similar solids" checks, compare the volume ratio with the cube of the linear ratio — surfaces would use the square. Keep a $\pi$ factor throughout to simplify cancelation, and give units ($\text{cm}$, $\text{cm}^2$, $\text{cm}^3$) in every final answer.
