HKDSE 2025 · Paper 2 · Q25 — Coordinate Geometry / Locus

Point A(a, 2a)
Origin O
Locus circle
Centre of locus (a,2a)
Radius √5·a

Denote the origin by $O$. The coordinates of the point $A$ are $(a,\,2a)$, where $a>0$. Let $P$ be a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that $AP = OA$. The locus of $P$ is a

A. point. B. circle. C. rhombus. D. straight line.
Figure: origin O, point A(a, 2a) with OA drawn.
STEP 1 · SET UP

Let the moving point be $P=(x,\,y)$. Also $O=(0,0)$ and $A=(a,\,2a)$ with $a>0$.

The condition is

$$AP = OA.$$
Hint: "locus" = set of ALL points satisfying a distance condition — so we write the condition algebraically and identify the shape.
STEP 2 · COMPUTE OA
Distance Formula \(OA=\sqrt{a^{2}+(2a)^{2}}\) The distance from the origin to \(A\) is the fixed length that \(AP\) must equal.

Distance $OA$ (from origin to $A$):

$$OA=\sqrt{(a-0)^2+(2a-0)^2}=\sqrt{a^2+4a^2}=\sqrt{5a^2}=\sqrt{5}\,a.$$
STEP 3 · WRITE AP = OA

Distance $AP$ (from $A$ to $P$):

$$\sqrt{(x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2}=\sqrt{a^2+(2a)^2}$$ $$\sqrt{(x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2}=\sqrt{5a^2}$$
STEP 4 · SQUARE & SIMPLIFY
Equation of a Circle \((x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=r^{2}\) A fixed distance from a fixed point is exactly the definition of a circle.

Squaring both sides:

$$(x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2=5a^2.$$

This is the equation of a circle with centre $(a,\,2a)$ and radius $\sqrt{5}\,a$.

STEP 5 · INTERPRET

The locus of $P$ is a circle centred at $A(a,2a)$ with radius $\sqrt{5}\,a$.

Note: the locus passes through $O$ (since $OA=\sqrt5 a$),
and $OA$ itself is a radius of that circle — visually the red segment $OA$ has the same length as the purple radius.

Answer: B. circle Locus: $(x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2=5a^2$

Question. Denote the origin by \(O\). The coordinates of the point \(A\) are \((3a,\,4a)\), where \(a>0\). Let \(P\) be a moving point such that \(AP=OA\). Find the equation of the locus of \(P\) and describe it.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(OA=\sqrt{(3a)^{2}+(4a)^{2}}=\sqrt{25a^{2}}=5a\).
Step 2. Let \(P=(x,y)\). The condition \(AP=OA\) gives \(\sqrt{(x-3a)^{2}+(y-4a)^{2}}=5a\).
Step 3. Squaring: \((x-3a)^{2}+(y-4a)^{2}=25a^{2}\).
Step 4. So the locus is a circle with centre \(A(3a,4a)\) and radius \(5a\);
it passes through \(O\) because \(OA=5a\) ✓
Answer: \((x-3a)^{2}+(y-4a)^{2}=25a^{2}\) — a circle with centre \((3a,4a)\) and radius \(5a\)
