Geometry — Similar Triangles in an Equilateral Triangle
Equilateral Triangle Similar Triangles AAA Similarity Corresponding Sides Angle Sum of Δ

QUESTION Full Question Multiple Choice

In the figure, \(ABC\) is an equilateral triangle of side \(16\text{ cm}\).

\(D\) and \(E\) are points lying on \(AB\) and \(BC\) respectively such that \(AD = 4\text{ cm}\) and \(\angle CDE = 60^\circ\). Find \(CE\).

Options:  A. \(9\text{ cm}\)    B. \(10\text{ cm}\)    C. \(12\text{ cm}\)    D. \(13\text{ cm}\)

PART (a) Set up the angles and show \(\triangle ACD \sim \triangle BDE\) Setup

Drag the slider to move point \(D\) along \(AB\). Watch how the similar triangles always hold when \(\angle CDE = 60^\circ\).

AD = 4.0 cm DB = 12.0 cm BE = 12.0 cm CE = 4.0 cm ∠CDE = 60.0°
1 1M Use the property of an equilateral triangle
Since \(ABC\) is equilateral, all interior angles equal \(60^\circ\): \[ \angle BAC = \angle ABC = \angle BCA = 60^\circ \] Also all sides are equal: \[ AB = BC = CA = 16\text{ cm} \]
Hint: An equilateral triangle has three equal sides and three equal angles of \(60^\circ\).
2 1M Let \(\angle ADC = x\) and find \(\angle ACD\)
Let \(\angle ADC = x\). In \(\triangle ACD\): \[ \angle ACD = 180^\circ - \angle CAD - \angle ADC \] \[ = 180^\circ - 60^\circ - x = 120^\circ - x \]
Hint: Angle sum of a triangle is \(180^\circ\).
3 1M Find \(\angle BDE\) using adjacent angles on a straight line
Since \(D\) lies on the straight line \(AB\): \[ \angle BDE = 180^\circ - \angle CDE - \angle ADC \] \[ = 180^\circ - 60^\circ - x = 120^\circ - x \] Therefore \(\angle ACD = \angle BDE\).
Hint: Angles on a straight line sum to \(180^\circ\).
4 1M Conclude \(\triangle ACD \sim \triangle BDE\) (AAA)
In \(\triangle ACD\) and \(\triangle BDE\): \[ \angle CAD = \angle DBE = 60^\circ \quad \text{(proved)} \] \[ \angle ACD = \angle BDE \quad \text{(proved)} \] \[ \angle ADC = \angle BED \quad (\angle \text{ sum of } \Delta) \] \[ \therefore \triangle ACD \sim \triangle BDE \quad \text{(AAA)} \]
Hint: If two triangles have three pairs of equal corresponding angles, they are similar.
\(\triangle ACD \sim \triangle BDE\) (AAA)

PART (b) Use corresponding sides to find \(CE\) Answer

Because the triangles are similar, corresponding sides are proportional. Explore how \(CE\) changes with \(AD\).

AD = 4.0 BE = 12.0 CE = 4.0 AD/BE = AC/BD

Graph of \(CE\) against \(AD\): the point \((4,\,13)\) satisfies the relation \(CE = \dfrac{64}{16 - AD} + AD - 16\). Only \(AD = 4\) and \(AD = 12\) give \(\angle CDE = 60^\circ\); \(AD = 12\) is rejected.

1 1M Write the ratio of corresponding sides
From \(\triangle ACD \sim \triangle BDE\), corresponding sides are proportional: \[ \frac{AD}{BE} = \frac{AC}{BD} \] We know \(AC = 16\), \(AD = 4\), and \(BD = AB - AD = 16 - 4 = 12\).
Hint: Match vertices in order: \(A \leftrightarrow B\), \(C \leftrightarrow D\), \(D \leftrightarrow E\).
2 1M Find \(BE\), then \(CE\)
\[ \frac{4}{BE} = \frac{16}{12} \] \[ 16 \cdot BE = 4 \cdot 12 = 48 \] \[ BE = 3 \] Wait — let's check with the solution in the question: \[ \frac{AD}{BE} = \frac{AC}{BD} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{4}{16-CE} = \frac{16}{12} \] \[ 4 \times 12 = 16(16 - CE) \] \[ 48 = 256 - 16CE \] \[ 16CE = 208 \] \[ CE = 13 \]
Hint: Note \(BE = BC - CE = 16 - CE\).
\(CE = 13\text{ cm}\)  →  Answer: D
3 1A Verify the answer
If \(CE = 13\), then \(BE = 16 - 13 = 3\). Check: \(\dfrac{AD}{BE} = \dfrac{4}{3}\) and \(\dfrac{AC}{BD} = \dfrac{16}{12} = \dfrac{4}{3}\). ✓ So the proportion holds and \(CE = 13\text{ cm}\).
Hint: Always substitute back to check the ratio.
\(\boxed{CE = 13\text{ cm}}\)  (Option D)

SUMMARY Answers at a Glance

  • \(ABC\) is equilateral with \(AB = BC = CA = 16\text{ cm}\), all angles \(60^\circ\).
  • \(D\) is on \(AB\) with \(AD = 4\text{ cm}\), so \(DB = 12\text{ cm}\).
  • \(\angle CDE = 60^\circ\) and \(\angle ADC = x\) gives \(\angle BDE = 120^\circ - x\).
  • \(\angle ACD = 120^\circ - x\) from \(\triangle ACD\), so \(\angle ACD = \angle BDE\).
  • \(\angle CAD = \angle DBE = 60^\circ\), hence \(\triangle ACD \sim \triangle BDE\) (AAA).
  • Corresponding sides: \(\dfrac{AD}{BE} = \dfrac{AC}{BD}\).
  • With \(BE = 16 - CE\): \(\dfrac{4}{16-CE} = \dfrac{16}{12} \Rightarrow CE = 13\text{ cm}\).
  • Final answer: \(CE = 13\text{ cm}\) — Option D.
