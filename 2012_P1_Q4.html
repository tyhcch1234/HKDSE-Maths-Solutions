Percentage Problem — Daily Wages

The daily wage of Ada is $20\%$ higher than that of Billy while the daily wage of Billy is $20\%$ lower than that of Christine. It is given that the daily wage of Billy is $\$480$.

(a) Find the daily wage of Ada.
(b) Who has the highest daily wage? Explain your answer. [4 marks]

Step-by-step Solution

1Understand the given information

Billy's daily wage is given directly:

$$\text{Billy} = \$480$$

Ada is $20\%$ higher than Billy → multiply by $(1+20\%)$.

Billy is $20\%$ lower than Christine → Billy is $80\%$ of Christine.

Hint: "higher/lower than" tells you which value is the original (the 100% base).
2(a) Daily wage of Ada
Percentage Increase \(\text{new value}=(1+r\%)\times\text{original value}\) A \(20\%\) increase is the multiplier \(1+0.2=1.2\); the base (100%) is Billy's wage.

$$\text{Ada} = 480 \times (1 + 20\%) = 480 \times 1.2$$

$$= \$576$$

Hint: a $20\%$ increase means multiplying by $1.2$. (1M + 1A)
3(b) Let $x$ be Christine's daily wage
Reverse Percentage (finding the base) \(\text{original}=\dfrac{\text{new value}}{1-r\%}\) Undo a decrease by dividing by the multiplier, not by multiplying. The base here is Christine's wage, not Billy's.

Billy is $20\%$ lower than Christine, so Billy is $80\%$ of Christine's wage:

$$x(1 - 20\%) = 480$$

Hint: the $100\%$ base is Christine's wage, not Billy's. (1M)
4Solve for $x$ (Christine)

$$x = \frac{480}{1 - 20\%} = \frac{480}{0.8}$$

$$x = 600$$

Hint: dividing by $0.8$ reverses the $20\%$ decrease. (1A)
5Compare and conclude

Ada $=\$576$,   Billy $=\$480$,   Christine $=\$600$.

Since $600 > 576 > 480$:

Christine has the highest daily wage. (1A)

Hint: a $20\%$ decrease on a bigger number gives a bigger drop, so the one who is "lower than" the biggest earner ends up largest.
Answer: (a) Ada's daily wage is $\$576$.   (b) Christine has the highest daily wage (Christine $=\$600$, Ada $=\$576$, Billy $=\$480$).

Question. The daily wage of Ada is \(20\%\) higher than that of Billy, while Billy's daily wage is \(25\%\) lower than that of Christine. Billy earns \(\$720\) per day.
(a) Find Ada's daily wage.
(b) Who earns the most? Explain.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Ada is \(20\%\) above Billy, so Ada \(=720\times(1+20\%)=720\times1.2=\$864\).
Step 2. Let \(x\) be Christine's wage.
Billy is \(25\%\) lower than Christine: \(x(1-25\%)=720\).
Step 3. Hence \(x=\dfrac{720}{0.75}=\$960\).
Step 4. Compare: Christine \(\$960\) > Ada \(\$864\) > Billy \(\$720\), so Christine earns the most. Note \(25\%\) of a larger base is a bigger amount than \(20\%\) of a smaller one.
Answer: (a) \(\$864\) (b) Christine (\$960), since \(960>864>720\).
