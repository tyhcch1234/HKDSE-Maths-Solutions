Question & solution

In Figure 1, \(AB\), \(BC\), \(CD\) and \(AD\) are chords of the circle. \(AC\) and \(BD\) intersect at \(E\). It is given that \(BE=8\text{ cm}\), \(CE=20\text{ cm}\) and \(DE=15\text{ cm}\).

(a) Write down a pair of similar triangles. Also find \(AE\).

(b) Suppose \(AB=10\text{ cm}\). Are \(AC\) and \(BD\) perpendicular? Explain.

STEP 1 · Similar triangles

Angles in the same segment are equal:

\[ \angle ABE=\angle DCE,\qquad \angle BAE=\angle CDE \]

and \(\angle AEB=\angle DEC\) (vertically opposite). So

\[ \triangle ABE \sim \triangle DCE \]
STEP 2 · Find AE

Corresponding sides are proportional:

\[ \frac{AE}{DE}=\frac{BE}{CE}\;\Rightarrow\; AE=\frac{BE\cdot DE}{CE}=\frac{8\times15}{20}=6\text{ cm} \]
Answer (a): \(AE=6\text{ cm}\)
STEP 3 · Perpendicular? (b)

In \(\triangle ABE\): \(AB=10\), \(AE=6\), \(BE=8\).

\[ 6^2+8^2=36+64=100=10^2 \]

So \(\angle AEB=90^\circ\) by the converse of Pythagoras' theorem, hence \(AC\perp BD\).

Answer (b): Yes, \(AC\perp BD\).
