$y \le 9$ $x-y-9 \le 0$ $x+y-9 \ge 0$ Region $R$

2017 MC #37 HKDSE Mathematics Paper 2

Topic: Inequalities and Linear Programming

Consider the following system of inequalities:

$$\begin{cases} y \le 9 \\ x-y-9 \le 0 \\ x+y-9 \ge 0 \end{cases}$$

Let $R$ be the region which represents the solution of the above system of inequalities. If $(x, y)$ is a point lying in $R$, then the greatest value of $x-2y+43$ is

A. 25
B. 43
C. 52
D. 61
1Understand the Feasible Region

The system defines three half-planes. Their intersection $R$ forms a triangle with vertices at the corner points of the boundary lines.

The boundary lines are:

$\quad y = 9$ (horizontal), $\quad x-y-9=0$ (i.e. $x-y=9$), $\quad x+y-9=0$ (i.e. $x+y=9$).

The feasible region $R$ is the shaded triangle in the figure.
2Find the Corner Points (Vertices)

Solve the boundary lines in pairs:

• $y=9$ and $x-y-9=0$:
$\quad x-9-9=0 \Rightarrow x=18$, so $(x,y)=(18,\,9)$.

• $x-y-9=0$ and $x+y-9=0$:
$\quad$ Add: $2x-18=0 \Rightarrow x=9$, then $y=0$, so $(x,y)=(9,\,0)$.

• $x+y-9=0$ and $y=9$:
$\quad x+9-9=0 \Rightarrow x=0$, so $(x,y)=(0,\,9)$.

These three corner points mark the vertices of triangle $R$.
3Objective Function: Evaluate at Corner Points

The objective function is $P = x - 2y + 43$. By the corner-point principle, its maximum on $R$ occurs at a corner point.

Corner point $(x,y)$$x-2y+43$Value
$(18,\,9)$$18-2(9)+43$$43$
$(9,\,0)$$9-2(0)+43$$52$
$(0,\,9)$$0-2(9)+43$$25$
Compute $P$ at each vertex and take the largest value.
4Determine the Greatest Value

Comparing the values: $43,\;52,\;25$.

The greatest value is $52$, attained at $(x,y)=(9,\,0)$.

The answer is option C.
Answer: C. 52
