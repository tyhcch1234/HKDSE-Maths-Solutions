Coordinate Geometry — Equation of a Circle

The equation of the circle $C$ is

$$5x^{2}+5y^{2}-30x+10y+6=0\,.$$

Which of the following is true?

  • A. The origin lies inside $C$.
  • B. $C$ lies in the second quadrant.
  • C. The circumference of $C$ is less than 20.
  • D. The coordinates of the centre of $C$ are $(15,-5)$.
STEP 1 · Divide by 5 to get standard form
General Form of the Equation of a Circle \(x^{2}+y^{2}+Dx+Ey+F=0\) Dividing \\(5x^{2}+5y^{2}-30x+10y+6=0\\) by \\(5\\) gives \\(D=-6\\), \\(E=2\\), \\(F=\\dfrac{6}{5}\\).

Divide the whole equation by the coefficient of $x^2$,
namely 5:

$$x^{2}+y^{2}-6x+2y+\frac{6}{5}=0$$

Now the circle is in the form $x^{2}+y^{2}+Dx+Ey+F=0$ with $D=-6$, $E=2$, $F=\dfrac{6}{5}$.

STEP 2 · Centre and radius

Centre:

$$\left(-\frac{D}{2},-\frac{E}{2}\right)=\left(-\frac{-6}{2},-\frac{2}{2}\right)=(3,-1)$$

Radius:

$$r=\sqrt{\left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{E}{2}\right)^{2}-F} =\sqrt{3^{2}+(-1)^{2}-\frac{6}{5}}=\sqrt{\frac{44}{5}}$$

So $r=\sqrt{8.8}\approx 2.9665$.

STEP 3 · Check option D

Option D claims the centre is $(15,-5)$.

But the centre is $(3,-1)$,
so D is false ✗.

In fact $15$ and $-5$ are $-\frac{D}{2}\times 5$ style misreadings — the correct centre is found by the formula in STEP 2.

STEP 4 · Check option A (distance to origin)
Distance Formula \(d=\sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}+(y_{2}-y_{1})^{2}}\) Comparing \\(d=\\sqrt{10}\\) from the centre \\((3,-1)\\) to the origin with \\(r=\\sqrt{8.8}\\) decides that the origin is outside \\(C\\).

Distance from the centre $(3,-1)$ to the origin $(0,0)$:

$$d=\sqrt{(3-0)^{2}+(-1-0)^{2}}=\sqrt{9+1}=\sqrt{10}\approx 3.1623$$

Compare with the radius $r=\sqrt{\frac{44}{5}}=\sqrt{8.8}\approx 2.9665$:

$$d=\sqrt{10}>\sqrt{8.8}=r$$

Since the origin is farther from the centre than the radius,
the origin lies outside $C$. A is false ✗

STEP 5 · Check option B (which quadrant?)

The centre is $(3,-1)$: $x>0$, $y<0$, so the centre lies in the fourth quadrant.

The circle spans $x$ from $3-r\approx 0.03$ to $3+r\approx 5.97$ and $y$ from $-1-r\approx -3.97$ to $-1+r\approx 1.97$ — it is mostly in the fourth quadrant and touches the first quadrant near the origin.

So $C$ does not lie in the second quadrant. B is false ✗

STEP 6 · Check option C (circumference)
Circumference of a Circle \(C=2\pi r\) With \\(r=\\sqrt{8.8}\\approx2.9665\\), the circumference is \\(2\\pi(2.9665)\\approx18.64<20\\).

Circumference:

$$2\pi r = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{44}{5}} \approx 2\pi(2.9665)\approx 18.6389$$

Since $18.6389<20$,
the circumference is less than 20. C is true ✓

Exam tip: For these "which is true" circle questions, always reduce to standard form first, read off the centre and radius, then test each option numerically (distance to a point vs radius; circumference vs $2\pi r$).

Final answer: C

Centre $=(3,-1)$, radius $=\sqrt{\tfrac{44}{5}}\approx 2.97$; circumference $\approx 18.64 < 20$.

A ✗ origin outside ( $\sqrt{10}>\sqrt{8.8}$ )  ·  B ✗ centre is in quadrant IV  ·  C ✓  ·  D ✗ centre is $(3,-1)$
