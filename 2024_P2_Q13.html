Percentages and Variation

It is given that $z$ varies directly as the square of $x$ and inversely as $y$. If $x$ is increased by $20\%$ and $y$ is decreased by $20\%$, then $z$

  • A. is increased by $20\%$.
  • B. is decreased by $20\%$.
  • C. is increased by $80\%$.
  • D. is decreased by $80\%$.

Solution

1Write the variation as an equation.
Joint Variation \(z=\dfrac{kx^{2}}{y}\) Direct variation with \(x^{2}\) puts \(x^{2}\) in the numerator and inverse variation with \(y\) puts \(y\) in the denominator.

"$z$ varies directly as the square of $x$" and "$z$ varies inversely as $y$" means

$$z=\frac{kx^{2}}{y},\qquad k \text{ a non-zero constant.}$$

Always write the proportionality statement as an equation first — introduce the constant $k$.

2Apply the percentage changes.

$x$ increases by $20\%$: new $x=(1+20\%)x=1.2x$.

$y$ decreases by $20\%$: new $y=(1-20\%)y=0.8y$.

A $20\%$ increase multiplies by $1.2$;
a $20\%$ decrease multiplies by $0.8$.

3Substitute to find the new value $z'$.

$$z'=\frac{k(1.2x)^{2}}{0.8y}=\frac{k\,(1.44x^{2})}{0.8y}=\frac{1.44}{0.8}\cdot\frac{kx^{2}}{y}=1.8z$$

Note $(1.2)^{2}=1.44$,
and $\dfrac{1.44}{0.8}=1.8$. The constant $k$ cancels out.

4Compute the percentage change in $z$.
Percentage Change \(\text{percentage change}=\dfrac{\text{new}-\text{old}}{\text{old}}\times100\%\) With \(z^{\prime}=1.0z\) the change is \(0\%\), i.e. \(z\) is unchanged.

$$\text{Percentage change}=\frac{z'-z}{z}\times100\%=\frac{1.8z-z}{z}\times100\%=\frac{0.8z}{z}\times100\%=80\%$$

Positive result ⇒ increase of $80\%$.

5Conclude.

$z$ is increased by $80\%$.

Choose the option matching this conclusion.

Answer: C  —  $z$ is increased by $80\%$.
