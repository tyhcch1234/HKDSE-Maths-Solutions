Question

The length of a side of a solid cube is 60 cm. The volume of a solid right circular cylinder is equal to the volume of the cube while the curved surface area of the circular cylinder is equal to the total surface area of the cube. Find the base radius of the circular cylinder.
A. 20 cm
B. 30 cm
C. 76 cm
D. 172 cm

Solution

STEP 1 · Define the unknowns

Let $r$ cm be the base radius and $h$ cm the height of the cylinder, and let the cube edge be $a=60$ cm.

Two unknowns $(r,h)$ ⟹ we need two equations.

STEP 2 · Equal volumes

Volume of cube $=a^3=60^3=216000\ \text{cm}^3$.

Volume of cylinder $=\pi r^2h$.

$$ \pi r^2 h = 216000 \qquad \cdots(1)$$
STEP 3 · Equal surface areas

Total surface area of cube $=6a^2=6(60)^2=21600\ \text{cm}^2$.

Curved surface area of cylinder $=2\pi r h$.

$$ 2\pi r h = 21600 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \pi r h = 10800 \qquad \cdots(2)$$
STEP 4 · Eliminate $h$ — divide (1) by (2) $$ \frac{\pi r^2 h}{\pi r h}=\frac{216000}{10800} $$ $$ r = 20 $$

The $\pi$ and $h$ cancel beautifully — no need to find $h$.

STEP 5 · Conclusion

Base radius $r=20\ \text{cm}$. (Check: (2) gives $h=\dfrac{10800}{20\pi}\approx171.9$ cm.)

ANSWER A. 20 cm
