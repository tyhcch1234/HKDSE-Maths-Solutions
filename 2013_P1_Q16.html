2013 #16 A box contains $5$ white cups and $11$ blue cups. If $6$ cups are randomly drawn from the box at the same time,

(a) find the probability that at least $4$ white cups are drawn; [2 marks]

(b) find the probability that at least $3$ blue cups are drawn. [2 marks]

1Set up the sample space. All choices of 6 cups out of 16 are equally likely — count them with $C$. Total number of ways $=\;C^{16}_{6}$
Combination \(C^{n}_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}\) Choosing 6 cups out of 16 without regard to order gives \(C^{16}_{6}=8008\) equally likely choices.
2(a) "At least 4 white" = exactly 4 white, or exactly 5 white. Split into cases; the rest of the 6 cups must be blue. $4$W $2$B: $C^{5}_{4}\,C^{11}_{2}$   and   $5$W $1$B: $C^{5}_{5}\,C^{11}_{1}$
3(a) Compute the probability. Add the two cases over the total (mark: 1M for the correct expression). $\displaystyle \frac{C^{5}_{4}C^{11}_{2}+C^{5}_{5}C^{11}_{1}}{C^{16}_{6}} =\frac{(5)(55)+(1)(11)}{8008} =\frac{275+11}{8008} =\frac{286}{8008} =\frac{1}{28}$  (1A)
Addition Rule for Mutually Exclusive Events \(P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)\) The cases "exactly 4 white" and "exactly 5 white" cannot happen together, so their counts are added.
4(b) "At least 3 blue" — use the complement. Fewer than 3 blue means at most 2 blue, i.e. at least 4 white. Use the result from (a). $P(\text{at least }3\text{ blue}) = 1 - P(\text{at most }2\text{ blue}) = 1 - P(\text{at least }4\text{ white})$  (1M)
Complement Rule \(P(A^{\prime})=1-P(A)\) "At least 3 blue" is the complement of "at most 2 blue", which is the event of part (a).
5(b) Final value. Since $P(\text{at least }4\text{ W}) = \frac{1}{28}$ from part (a). $\displaystyle 1-\frac{1}{28}=\frac{27}{28}$  (1A)
Answer: (a) $\dfrac{1}{28}$   ·   (b) $\dfrac{27}{28}$

Question. A box contains 12 cups, of which 4 are white and 8 are blue. 4 cups are chosen at random. (a) Find the probability that at least 3 of them are white. (b) Find the probability that at least 2 of them are blue.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Total number of choices \(=C^{12}_{4}=495\).
Step 2. At least 3 white means exactly 3 white or exactly 4 white: \(C^{4}_{3}C^{8}_{1}+C^{4}_{4}=(4)(8)+1=33\).
Step 3. (a) \(P(\text{at least }3\text{ white})=\dfrac{33}{495}=\dfrac{1}{15}\).
Step 4. Fewer than 2 blue means at most 1 blue, i.e. at least 3 white, so (b) \(P=1-\dfrac{1}{15}=\dfrac{14}{15}\).
Answer: (a) \(\dfrac{1}{15}\) (b) \(\dfrac{14}{15}\)
