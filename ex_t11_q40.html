T11 Q40

In the figure, $AB = DE$, $BC = EF$ and $AC = DF$. Name the condition that proves $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$.

Solution

Given $AB = DE$, $BC = EF$ and $AC = DF$, all three pairs of corresponding sides of $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle DEF$ are equal.
By the Side-Side-Side congruence condition, the two triangles are congruent.
Therefore, $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$ by SSS.
The condition that proves $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$ is SSS (Side-Side-Side).
