Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Distance/Slope

T12 Q12

Find the midpoint of the line segment joining $A(1,2)$ and $B(5,4)$.

Solution

Use the midpoint formula: $\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$.
Substitute $A(1,2)$ and $B(5,4)$: $\left(\frac{1+5}{2},\frac{2+4}{2}\right)$.
Simplify: $\left(\frac{6}{2},\frac{6}{2}\right)=(3,3)$.
The midpoint of the line segment joining $A(1,2)$ and $B(5,4)$ is $(3,3)$.
