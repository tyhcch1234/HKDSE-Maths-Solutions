HKDSE 2014 Paper 2 · Q43

Number of Teams Formed

Question. There are 13 boys and 17 girls in a class. If a team of 2 boys and 3 girls is selected from the class to participate in a voluntary service, how many different teams can be formed?

  • A.  38 896
  • B.  53 040
  • C.  142 506
  • D.  636 480
Step 1Identify the given information.
Class roster: $13$ boys and $17$ girls. We need a team with exactly $2$ boys and $3$ girls.
A "team" is an unordered selection — order within the team does not matter.
Step 2Choose the boys.
The number of ways to choose $2$ boys from $13$ is $$C_2^{13}={}^{13}C_{2}=\frac{13\times 12}{2}=78.$$
Use the combination formula $C_r^n=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}$.
Step 3Choose the girls.
The number of ways to choose $3$ girls from $17$ is $$C_3^{17}={}^{17}C_{3}=\frac{17\times 16\times 15}{3\times 2\times 1}=680.$$
Each choice of boys can be combined with any choice of girls.
Step 4Multiply (multiplication principle).
Total number of teams $$=C_2^{13}\times C_3^{17}=78\times 680=53\,040.$$
Independent selections multiply together.
Answer: B  —  $53\,040$
