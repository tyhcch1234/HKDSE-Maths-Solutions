Question

In the figure, the perimeter of the rectangle $ABCD$ is $170\ \text{cm}$. It is given that $EBF$ is a straight line and $\angle AEB=\angle BFC=90^\circ$. If $AE=24\ \text{cm}$ and $BC=34\ \text{cm}$, then $EF=$

  1. A. $45\ \text{cm}$
  2. B. $51\ \text{cm}$
  3. C. $61\ \text{cm}$
  4. D. $75\ \text{cm}$

Solution

STEP 1 · Find AB

$AB=CD$ and $AD=BC=34\ \text{cm}$   (properties of rectangle)

Perimeter:

$$AB+BC+CD+AD=170$$

$$AB+34+AB+34=170$$

$$2AB=102 \;\Rightarrow\; AB=51\ \text{cm}$$

STEP 2 · Yes — a right angle at E

Given $\angle AEB=90^\circ$, triangle $ABE$ is right-angled at $E$. By Pythagoras' theorem:

$$BE=\sqrt{AB^2-AE^2}=\sqrt{51^2-24^2}$$

$$=\sqrt{2601-576}=\sqrt{2025}=45\ \text{cm}$$

STEP 3 · Angle chasing ⇒ $\angle BAE=\angle CBF$

In $\triangle ABE$, $\angle BAE+\angle AEB=\angle ABF$   (exterior angle of triangle)

Since $EBF$ is a straight line and $ABCD$ is a rectangle $\angle ABC=90^\circ$:

$$\angle ABF=\angle ABC+\angle CBF=90^\circ+\angle CBF$$

So $\angle BAE+90^\circ=90^\circ+\angle CBF$, hence

$$\angle BAE=\angle CBF$$

STEP 4 · $\triangle ABE \sim \triangle BCF$ (AAA)

In $\triangle ABE$ and $\triangle BCF$:

$\angle AEB=\angle BFC=90^\circ$   (given)

$\angle BAE=\angle CBF$   (proved)

$\angle ABE=\angle BCF$   (angle sum of triangle)

$$\therefore \triangle ABE \sim \triangle BCF \quad \text{(AAA)}$$

STEP 5 · Find BF

Corresponding sides of similar triangles:

$$\frac{AE}{BF}=\frac{AB}{BC}$$

$$\frac{24}{BF}=\frac{51}{34}$$

$$51\,BF=24\times 34=816 \;\Rightarrow\; BF=\frac{816}{51}=16\ \text{cm}$$

STEP 6 · Hence EF

Since $E$, $B$, $F$ are collinear with $B$ between $E$ and $F$:

$$EF=BE+BF=45+16=61\ \text{cm}$$

Answer: C.   $EF=61\ \text{cm}$
Key idea: the Pythagorean step gives $BE$, and the AAA similarity of $\triangle ABE$ and $\triangle BCF$ gives $BF$. The whole question is a two-part “Pythagoras + similarity” chain.
