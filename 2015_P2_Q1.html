2015 MC #1
$(x+1)(x^{2}+x+1)=$
  • A. $x^{3}+1$
  • B. $(x+1)^{3}$
  • C. $x^{3}+x^{2}+x+1$
  • D. $x^{3}+2x^{2}+2x+1$
1Interpret the product
The expression is a single binomial $(x+1)$ multiplied by a trinomial $(x^{2}+x+1)$. Distribute every term of the first bracket over the second bracket: $$(x+1)(x^{2}+x+1)=x(x^{2}+x+1)+1(x^{2}+x+1)$$
Hint: think of it as two separate products, then add the results.
2Expand the $x$ part
Multiply $x$ by each term inside the trinomial: $$x(x^{2}+x+1)=x^{3}+x^{2}+x$$
Hint: $x \cdot x^{2}=x^{1+2}=x^{3}$.
3Expand the $1$ part
Multiplying by $1$ leaves each term unchanged: $$1(x^{2}+x+1)=x^{2}+x+1$$
Hint: $1$ is the multiplicative identity.
4Combine like terms
$$ \begin{aligned} (x+1)(x^{2}+x+1)&=(x^{3}+x^{2}+x)+(x^{2}+x+1)\\ &=x^{3}+(x^{2}+x^{2})+(x+x)+1\\ &=x^{3}+2x^{2}+2x+1 \end{aligned} $$
Hint: add coefficients of terms with the same power of $x$.
5Check against the options
Compare with each option:
  • A. $x^{3}+1$ — missing the $x^{2}$ and $x$ terms ✗
  • B. $(x+1)^{3}=x^{3}+3x^{2}+3x+1$ — coefficients 3, not 2 ✗
  • C. $x^{3}+x^{2}+x+1$ — only the “$x$ part” simplified ✗
  • D. $x^{3}+2x^{2}+2x+1$ — matches ✓
Hint: substituting $x=2$ gives $3\times 7=21$; only option D gives $8+8+4+1=21$.
Answer: D  —  $(x+1)(x^{2}+x+1)=x^{3}+2x^{2}+2x+1$
