Laws of Indices Negative Exponents Power of a Product

Simplify the following expression:

\[ (2n^3)^{-5} \]

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Identify the structure

The expression is \((2n^3)^{-5}\). We have a product inside the bracket: \(2 \times n^3\).

Hint: The power \(-5\) applies to everything inside the bracket. Use the rule \((ab)^m = a^m b^m\).

2

Apply the power to each factor

\[ (2n^3)^{-5} = 2^{-5} \cdot (n^3)^{-5} \]

Distribute the exponent \(-5\) to both the constant \(2\) and the variable factor \(n^3\).

3

Simplify the variable part

Use the power rule \((x^a)^b = x^{a \cdot b}\):

\[ (n^3)^{-5} = n^{3 \times (-5)} = n^{-15} \]

4

Simplify the constant part

Compute \(2^{-5}\):

\[ 2^{-5} = \frac{1}{2^5} = \frac{1}{32} \]

(Recall: \(2^5 = 32\))

5

Combine everything

\[ (2n^3)^{-5} = 2^{-5} \cdot n^{-15} = \frac{1}{32} \cdot \frac{1}{n^{15}} = \frac{1}{32n^{15}} \]

✅ Final answer: \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{32n^{15}}\) — Option B
