HKDSE Math 2025 Paper 1 Q6

4 marks
Inequalities Compound Inequality (“or”) Union of Solutions Greatest Integer

Consider the compound inequality

\[ (*) \qquad \frac{6x+1}{2} < x-8 \quad \text{or} \quad 3x \le -21 \]

(a) Solve (*). (3 marks)

(b) Write down the greatest integer satisfying (*). (1 mark)

📘 Step-by-step solution

1

Solve the first inequality 1M

Multiply both sides by the positive number 2 to clear the fraction, then collect the \(x\) terms. Multiplying by a positive number keeps the inequality sign unchanged.

\[ \frac{6x+1}{2} < x-8 \] \[ 6x+1 < 2x-16 \] \[ 6x-2x < -16-1 \] \[ 4x < -17 \] \[ x < -\frac{17}{4} \]
2

Solve the second inequality 1M

Divide both sides by 3. Since 3 is positive, the inequality sign stays the same.

\[ 3x \le -21 \] \[ x \le -7 \]
3

Combine using “or” 1A

For “or”, a value is valid if it satisfies either inequality. Compare \(-17/4 = -4.25\) and \(-7\): since \(-7 < -4.25\), every \(x \le -7\) already satisfies \(x < -17/4\).

We have:

\[ x < -\frac{17}{4} \quad \text{or} \quad x \le -7 \]

Since \(-17/4 = -4.25\) and \(-7 < -4.25\), the condition \(x \le -7\) is already included in \(x < -17/4\). Therefore the union simplifies to:

Solution of (*) is \(x < -\dfrac{17}{4}\).

4

Greatest integer satisfying (*) 1A

\(-17/4 = -4.25\). We need the greatest integer strictly less than \(-4.25\). The integers around it are \(-5, -4, -3, \dots\)

The solution is \(x < -4.25\).

Check nearby integers: \(-5 < -4.25\) ✓, but \(-4 > -4.25\) ✗ (since \(-4\) is not less than \(-4.25\)).

Therefore the greatest integer satisfying (*) is -5

✅ Final Answer

(a) Solution of (*)

\( x < -\dfrac{17}{4} \)

(b) Greatest integer

\( -5 \)
