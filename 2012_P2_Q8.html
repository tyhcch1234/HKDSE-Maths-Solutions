2012MC#8 Topic 1 · Percentages
In a company, $37.5\%$ of the employees are female. If $60\%$ of the male employees and $80\%$ of the female employees are married, then the percentage of married employees in the company is
  • A. $32.5\%$
  • B. $45\%$
  • C. $55\%$
  • D. $67.5\%$

Step-by-step Solution

Step 1 — Split the staff by gender Female $= 37.5\%$, so \[ \text{Male} = 100\% - 37.5\% = 62.5\%. \]
The two gender groups must add up to $100\%$.
Step 2 — Let $n$ = total employees (weighted average) Take a convenient total. With $n=1000$: \[ \text{Female} = 375,\qquad \text{Male} = 625. \]
Choosing $1000$ makes $37.5\%$ a whole number — the unknown $n$ cancels anyway.
Complement Rule for Percentages \(\text{Percentage of males}=100\%-\text{Percentage of females}\) With \(37.5\%\) of the members female, the male share is \(62.5\%\).
Step 3 — Count married employees \[ \text{Married men} = 625 \times 60\% = 375 \] \[ \text{Married women} = 375 \times 80\% = 300 \] \[ \text{Total married} = 375 + 300 = 675. \]
Apply each group's own married rate separately, then add.
Percentage of a Quantity \(\text{Part}=\text{Percentage}\times\text{Whole}\) Applying \(37.5\%\) and \(62.5\%\) to \(n=1000\) gives the two group sizes.
Step 4 — Weighted average formula In general, with $n$ employees: \[ \frac{n(1-37.5\%)\times 60\% + n\times 37.5\%\times 80\%}{n}\times 100\% \] \[ = \frac{0.625\times 0.60 + 0.375\times 0.80}{1}\times 100\% \] \[ = (0.375 + 0.30)\times 100\%. \]
This is a weighted average — the weights are the group sizes.
Overall Percentage \(\text{Overall}\ \%=\dfrac{\text{total in the group}}{\text{total number}}\times 100\%\) The married total \(675\) out of \(1000\) gives the required percentage.
Step 5 — Evaluate \[ 0.675 \times 100\% = 67.5\%. \]
Check: the answer $(67.5\%)$ lies between $60\%$ and $80\%$, closer to $60\%$ since males are the majority. ✓
Answer: D  (67.5%)

Question. In a club, \(45\%\) of the members are female and \(55\%\) are male. \(40\%\) of the female members and \(20\%\) of the male members wear glasses. Find the percentage of members who wear glasses.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Take \(n=100\) members: \(45\) female, \(55\) male.
Step 2. Females with glasses: \(40\%\times 45=18\).
Step 3. Males with glasses: \(20\%\times 55=11\).
Step 4. Total with glasses \(=18+11=29\) out of \(100\), so the percentage is \(29\%\).
Answer: \(29\%\)
