Question

In Figure 1, $A$ is a point lying inside the quadrilateral $BCDE$ such that $AC \parallel ED$ and $AD \parallel BC$. It is given that $\angle ABC = \angle AED$ and $AB = AE$.

(a) Prove that $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle AED$.

(b) If $\angle ABC = 39^\circ$ and $\angle DAE = 87^\circ$, find $\angle ACD$.   [5 marks]

Solution

STEP 0 · Set up the figure

Quadrilateral $BCDE$ with interior point $A$. Given $AC \parallel ED$, $AD \parallel BC$, $\angle ABC = \angle AED$ and $AB = AE$.

STEP 1 · Part (a): Angles from parallels

Since $AD \parallel BC$ (alternate angles, $AD \parallel BC$):

$$\angle ACB = \angle CAD$$

Since $AC \parallel ED$ (alternate angles, $AC \parallel ED$):

$$\angle CAD = \angle ADE$$

Therefore, by transitivity:

$$\angle ACB = \angle ADE$$

STEP 2 · Part (a): Congruence (AAS)

We now have:

$\angle ACB = \angle ADE$ (proved above)

$\angle ABC = \angle AED$ (given)

$AB = AE$ (given)

Hence by AAS:

$$\triangle ABC \cong \triangle AED$$

STEP 3 · Part (b): Find $\angle BAC$

From the congruence, corresponding angles are equal:

$$\angle BAC = \angle DAE = 87^\circ$$

STEP 4 · Part (b): Find $\angle ACB$

In $\triangle ABC$, angles sum to $180^\circ$:

$$\angle ACB = 180^\circ - \angle BAC - \angle ABC = 180^\circ - 87^\circ - 39^\circ = 54^\circ$$

STEP 5 · Part (b): Find $\angle CAD$

From Step 1, alternate angles give $\angle CAD = \angle ACB$:

$$\angle CAD = 54^\circ$$

Also, from the congruence $AC = AD$, so $\triangle ACD$ is isosceles.

STEP 6 · Part (b): Find $\angle ACD$

In isosceles $\triangle ACD$, base angles are equal: $\angle ACD = \angle ADC$.

$$\angle ACD = \frac{180^\circ - \angle CAD}{2} = \frac{180^\circ - 54^\circ}{2} = 63^\circ$$

Answer:

(a) $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle AED$ (AAS — proved in Steps 1–2).

(b) $\angle ACD = \boxed{63^\circ}$
