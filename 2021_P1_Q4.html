HKDSE Math 2021 Paper 1 Q4

Topic 9: Inequalities and Linear Programming

Inequalities Number Line Integer Solutions

Question

(a) Find the range of values of \(x\) which satisfy both

\[ \frac{7(x-2)}{5} + 11 > 3(x-1) \quad \text{and} \quad x+4 \ge 0 \]

(b) How many positive integers satisfy both inequalities in (a)?

[4 marks]

Step-by-Step Solution

Start with the first inequality:

\[ \frac{7(x-2)}{5} + 11 > 3(x-1) \]

Hint: Multiply both sides by 5 to eliminate the fraction. Remember to distribute correctly.

\[ 7(x-2) + 55 > 15(x-1) \]

Expand the brackets:

\[ 7x - 14 + 55 > 15x - 15 \]

Combine constants on the left side:

\[ 7x + 41 > 15x - 15 \]

Rearrange to group \(x\) terms on one side and constants on the other:

\[ 41 + 15 > 15x - 7x \] \[ 56 > 8x \]

Divide both sides by 8:

\[ x < 7 \]

Start with the second inequality:

\[ x + 4 \ge 0 \]

Subtract 4 from both sides:

\[ x \ge -4 \]

We have:

  • From first inequality: \( x < 7 \)
  • From second inequality: \( x \ge -4 \)

The solution satisfying both inequalities is the intersection:

\[ -4 \le x < 7 \]

We need to find positive integers \(x\) that satisfy \(-4 \le x < 7\).

Hint: Positive integers are \(1, 2, 3, \dots\)

The integers that satisfy the inequality are:

1 2 3 4 5 6

Counting them, there are 6 positive integers.
