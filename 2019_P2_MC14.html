In the figure, the 1st pattern consists of 6 dots. For any positive integer \(n\), the \((n+1)\)th pattern is formed by adding 4 dots to the \(n\)th pattern. Find the number of dots in the 9th pattern.

Drag to see more patterns
\(T(n)\) = 6 dots

Step-by-Step Solution

Let \(T(n)\) be the number of dots in the \(n\)th pattern.

Hint: We need to find \(T(9)\).

From the problem statement:

  • 1st pattern: \(T(1) = 6\)
  • Each next pattern adds 4 dots: \(T(n+1) = T(n) + 4\)

Hint: This is an arithmetic sequence with first term \(a = 6\) and common difference \(d = 4\).

We can find \(T(9)\) by repeatedly applying the recurrence:

\[ \begin{aligned} T(9) &= T(8) + 4 \\ &= T(7) + 4 + 4 \\ &\;\;\vdots \\ &= T(1) + \underbrace{4 + 4 + \cdots + 4}_{8 \text{ times}} \\ &= 6 + (8 \times 4) \\ &= 6 + 32 \\ &= 38 \end{aligned} \]

Alternative Hint: Use the arithmetic sequence formula: \(T(n) = a + (n-1)d\).

The number of dots in the 9th pattern is 38.

Correct answer: C. 38
