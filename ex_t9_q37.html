Inequalities and Linear Programming (46) · Sub-Topic: Quadratic Inequalities

T9 Q37

Solve the inequality \(2x^2 - x - 1 > 0\).

Solution

Factor the quadratic: $2x^2-x-1=(2x+1)(x-1)$.
Find the critical values by solving $(2x+1)(x-1)=0$, giving $x=-\frac{1}{2}$ and $x=1$.
Since the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, the quadratic is positive outside the interval between the two roots.
Therefore, the solution is $x<-\frac{1}{2}$ or $x>1$.
The solution is $x<-\frac{1}{2}$ or $x>1$.
