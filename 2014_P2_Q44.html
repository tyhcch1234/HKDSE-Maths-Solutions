In an examination, Peter gets 55 marks and his standard score is $-3$ while Mary gets 95 marks and her standard score is $2$. Find the mean of the examination scores.
  • A. 8 marks
  • B. 64 marks
  • C. 75 marks
  • D. 79 marks
Step 1Set up the unknowns.
Standard Score \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\) Each mark and its standard score give one equation in the mean \(\mu\) and standard deviation \(\sigma\).
Let $\mu$ be the mean and $\sigma$ the standard deviation of the examination scores.
Standard score (z-score) definition: $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$.
Step 2Equation for Peter.
$$-3=\frac{55-\mu}{\sigma}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mu-3\sigma=55 \quad\cdots(1)$$
Peter's mark is 3 standard deviations below the mean.
Step 3Equation for Mary.
$$2=\frac{95-\mu}{\sigma}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mu+2\sigma=95 \quad\cdots(2)$$
Mary's mark is 2 standard deviations above the mean.
Step 4Eliminate $\sigma$.
Elimination Method \(a_{1}\mu+b_{1}\sigma=c_{1},\quad a_{2}\mu+b_{2}\sigma=c_{2}\) Multiplying the equations by \(2\) and \(3\) makes the \(\sigma\) terms cancel when added.
$(1)\times 2+(2)\times 3:$
$$2(\mu-3\sigma)+3(\mu+2\sigma)=2(55)+3(95)$$
$$2\mu-6\sigma+3\mu+6\sigma=110+285$$
$$5\mu=395$$
Multiply so that the $\sigma$ terms cancel; then add.
Step 5Solve for the mean.
Solving Simultaneous Linear Equations \(\mu=\dfrac{b_{2}c_{1}-b_{1}c_{2}}{a_{1}b_{2}-a_{2}b_{1}}\) With the \(\sigma\) terms removed, \(5\mu=395\) gives the mean directly.
$$\mu=\frac{395}{5}=79$$
Also $\sigma=\dfrac{79-55}{3}=8$, which is consistent with (2): $79+2(8)=95$. ✓
The mean score is 79 marks, which is option D.
Answer: D. 79 marks

Question. In an examination, Peter gets \(48\) marks and his standard score is \(-2\), while Mary gets \(84\) marks and her standard score is \(1\). Find the mean of the examination scores.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let the mean be \(\mu\) and the standard deviation be \(\sigma\);
use \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\).
Step 2. Peter: \(-2=\dfrac{48-\mu}{\sigma}\), so \(\mu-2\sigma=48\) … (1).
Step 3. Mary: \(1=\dfrac{84-\mu}{\sigma}\), so \(\mu+\sigma=84\) … (2).
Step 4. (2) − (1): \(3\sigma=36\), so \(\sigma=12\).
Step 5. From (2): \(\mu=84-12=72\). Check with (1): \(72-2(12)=48\). ✓
Answer: \(72\) marks
