Question — Percentages

The marked price of a shirt is higher than its cost by $80. The shirt is sold at a discount of 10% on its marked price. After selling the shirt, the percentage profit is 30%. Find the marked price of the shirt. (4 marks)

1Define the unknown & express quantities.

Let the marked price be $\$x$.

Since the marked price is higher than the cost by $\$80$:

$$\text{Cost} = \$\,(x-80)$$

Hint: Turn the phrase “higher than its cost by $80$” into an equation with $x$.
2Apply the 10% discount to get the selling price.
Percentage Discount \(\text{selling price}=\text{marked price}\times(1-\text{discount rate})\) A \(10\%\) discount means the customer pays \(90\%\) of the marked price, i.e. \(0.9x\).

A discount of $10\%$ on the marked price means the customer pays $90\%$:

$$\text{Selling price} = (90\%)\,x = \$\,0.9x$$

Hint: discount $10\%$ ⇒ pay $100\%-10\%=90\%$.
3Write the 30% profit equation.
Percentage Profit \(\text{selling price}=\text{cost}\times(1+\text{profit rate})\) The \(30\%\) profit is calculated on the cost \(x-80\), giving \(1.3(x-80)\).

Percentage profit is based on cost, so:

$$\text{Selling price} = \text{Cost}\times(1+30\%)$$

$$0.9x = (x-80)(1+30\%)$$

$$0.9x = 1.3x - 104$$

Hint: $1.3\times 80 = 104$ when you expand the bracket.
4Solve for $x$.
Balancing Method for a Linear Equation \(ax+b=cx+d\ \Longrightarrow\ (a-c)x=d-b\) \(0.9x=1.3x-104\) gives \(104=0.4x\), so \(x=260\).

$$0.9x = 1.3x - 104$$

$$104 = 0.4x$$

$$x = 260$$

Hint: rearrange, then divide by the coefficient of $x$.
Answer: The marked price of the shirt is $\$260$.

Question. The marked price of a bag is \(\$x\), which is higher than its cost by \(\$60\). If the bag is sold at a discount of \(20\%\) on its marked price and the percentage profit is \(28\%\), find \(x\). A. \(\$140\) B. \(\$150\) C. \(\$160\) D. \(\$180\) [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The cost is \(\$(x-60)\) because the marked price is \(\$60\) above the cost.
Step 2. A \(20\%\) discount gives a selling price of \(0.8x\).
Step 3. A \(28\%\) profit on cost: \(0.8x=1.28(x-60)\), i.e. \(0.8x=1.28x-76.8\).
Step 4. \(76.8=0.48x\), so \(x=160\).
Step 5. Check: cost \(=\$100\), selling price \(=0.8\times160=\$128\), and \(\dfrac{128-100}{100}=28\%\) \u2713.
Answer: C. \(x=160\)
