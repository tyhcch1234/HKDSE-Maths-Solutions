Hint: Always start by clearly defining what you are looking for.

Let \(x\) be the marked price of the shirt.

The marked price is the price tag before any discount is applied.

Hint: The marked price is higher than the cost by $80. So cost = marked price − $80.

Since the marked price is higher than the cost by $80:

\[ \text{Cost} = (x - 80) \]

1M mark for correct expression.

Hint: A 10% discount means you pay 90% of the marked price. Selling price = 90% × marked price.

The shirt is sold at a 10% discount on the marked price:

\[ \text{Selling price} = (90\%)\,x = 0.9x \]

1M mark for correct selling price expression.

Hint: Percentage profit = \(\dfrac{\text{Selling price} - \text{Cost}}{\text{Cost}} \times 100\%\).
Here, profit is 30% of the cost.

Selling price = Cost × (1 + 30%):

\[ 0.9x = (x - 80)(1 + 30\%) \]

1M mark for correct equation.

Hint: Expand the brackets, collect like terms, and solve the linear equation.

\[ \begin{aligned} 0.9x &= (x - 80)(1 + 0.3) \\ 0.9x &= 1.3(x - 80) \\ 0.9x &= 1.3x - 104 \\ 104 &= 1.3x - 0.9x \\ 104 &= 0.4x \\ x &= \frac{104}{0.4} \\ x &= 260 \end{aligned} \]

1A mark for correct answer.

Thus, the marked price of the shirt is $260.
