Statistics — use the mean and mode to pin down the unknown values, then find the median

Mean Mode Median Ordered Data Unknown Values

Question MC

If the mean and the mode of the nine numbers \[ 14,\;6,\;4,\;5,\;7,\;5,\;x,\;y,\;z \] are \(8\) and \(14\) respectively, then the median of these nine numbers is
A. \(5\)    B. \(6\)    C. \(7\)    D. \(8\)

Part (a) — Use the mean to find \(x+y+z\) Mean

8
Step 1

Write down the definition of the mean for the nine numbers:

\[ \frac{14+6+4+5+7+5+x+y+z}{9}=8 \]
Hint: The mean is the total sum divided by the number of data values, here \(9\).
Step 2

Add the five known numbers and multiply both sides by \(9\):

\[ 41+x+y+z=72 \]
Hint: \(14+6+4+5+7+5=41\). Subtracting \(41\) from \(72\) isolates \(x+y+z\).
\[ x+y+z = 31 \]
0 / 2

Part (b) — Use the mode to fix \(x,y,z\) Mode

Step 1

Count the frequencies of the known numbers:

\[ 4{:}1,\quad 5{:}2,\quad 6{:}1,\quad 7{:}1,\quad 14{:}1 \]

The mode is \(14\), so \(14\) must occur more often than any other value.

Hint: The current highest frequency is \(2\) (the number \(5\)). For \(14\) to be the mode, it needs a frequency of at least \(3\).
Step 2

Since \(14\) appears once already, two of \(x,y,z\) must equal \(14\), and the remaining one is

\[ 31-14-14=3 \]
Hint: Using the result from part (a), \(x+y+z=31\). If two of them are \(14\), the last is \(31-28=3\).
\[ \{x,y,z\}=\{14,14,3\} \]
0 / 2

Part (c) — Order the data and read off the median Median

Step 1

List all nine numbers and arrange them in ascending order:

\[ 3,\;4,\;5,\;5,\;6,\;7,\;14,\;14,\;14 \]
Hint: Sorting the data first makes the median obvious — it is the middle value.
Step 2

There are \(9\) data values, so the median is the \(\frac{9+1}{2}=5^{\text{th}}\) value:

\[ \text{Median} = 6 \]
Hint: For \(n=9\) (odd), the median is the \(\frac{n+1}{2}\)-th value — the \(5^{\text{th}}\) in the ordered list. Count: \(3,4,5,5,\mathbf{6},7,14,14,14\).
Answer: B \(\;(6)\)
0 / 2

Summary of Answers

  • Part (a): \(\;x+y+z = 31\)
  • Part (b): \(\;\{x,y,z\}=\{14,14,3\}\) (two of them are \(14\))
  • Part (c): Ordered data \(3,4,5,5,6,7,14,14,14\)
  • Median of the nine numbers \(= 6\)
  • Correct option: B
