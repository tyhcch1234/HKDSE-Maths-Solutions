Inequalities and Linear Programming

Inequalities and Linear ProgrammingPassing rate 31%Difficulty · <50%8 marks

Question

(a) In Figure 7, the equation of the straight line \(L_1\) is \(6x+7y=900\) and the \(x\)-intercept of \(L_2\) is 180. \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) intersect at \((45,90)\). The shaded region (including the boundary) represents the solution of a system of inequalities. Find the system of inequalities. 4 marks

(b) A factory produces two types of wardrobes, \(X\) and \(Y\). Each \(X\) requires 6 man-hours assembly and 2 man-hours packing; each \(Y\) requires 7 man-hours assembly and 3 man-hours packing. In a month, the factory has 900 man-hours for assembly and 360 for packing. Profits for \(X\) and \(Y\) are \(\$440\) and \(\$665\). A worker claims the total profit can exceed \(\$80\,000\) that month. Do you agree? Explain. 4 marks

Solution

a(a) System of inequalities
\[m_{L_2}=\frac{90}{45-180}=-\frac23\ \Rightarrow\ L_2:2x+3y=360\]

1M finding the equation of \(L_2\).1A correct equation.

\[\begin{cases}6x+7y\le900\ 2x+3y\le360\ x\ge0\ y\ge0\end{cases}\]

1M writing the inequalities.1A correct system.

b(b) Maximum profit
\[P=440x+665y\]

1A correct profit function.

\[\text{vertices: }(0,0),(0,120),(45,90),(150,0)\]

1M identifying vertices.1M testing vertices.

\[P(0,120)=79\,800,\ P(45,90)=79\,650\ \Rightarrow\ \text{max}=\$79\,800<\$80\,000\]

Hence disagree.

1A correct conclusion.

Answer

Answer(a) \(6x+7y\le900,\ 2x+3y\le360,\ x,y\ge0\) · (b) disagree
