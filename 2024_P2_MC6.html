Question 6 — Polynomials

Polynomials Identities Equating Coefficients Ratios

Q Question

If \(a\), \(b\) and \(c\) are non-zero constants such that

\[ 4x^2 + 2ax + 3a \equiv x(4x + b) + 2c \]

then \(a : b : c =\)

💡 Key Concept

When two polynomials are identically equal (written with \(\equiv\)), the coefficients of like terms must match. This is called the equating of coefficients. Here, \(x\) is the variable, while \(a\), \(b\), \(c\) are unknown constants.
