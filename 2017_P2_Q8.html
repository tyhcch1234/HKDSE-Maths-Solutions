HKDSE 2017 Paper 2 — Question 8 (Polynomials)

If $m$ and $n$ are constants such that $$4x^{2}+m(x+1)+28 \equiv mx(x+3)+n(x-4),$$ then $n=$
A. $-8$
B. $-7$
C. $4$
D. $16$
1Expand both sides into standard form.
Expanding Brackets \(a(b+c)=ab+ac\) Expanding \(m(x+1)\) and \(n(x-4)\) lets both sides be compared term by term.
LHS $=4x^{2}+mx+m+28$
RHS $=mx^{2}+3mx+nx-4n = mx^{2}+(3m+n)x-4n$
Hint: collect like powers of $x$ — the $x^2$ term,
the $x$ term and the constant term.
2Compare the coefficient of $x^{2}$.
Comparing Coefficients of an Identity \(Ax^{2}+Bx+C \equiv ax^{2}+bx+c \Rightarrow A=a,\ B=b,\ C=c\) The \(x^{2}\) coefficients give \(4=m\), since the identity holds for every \(x\).
$$4x^{2} \;\equiv\; mx^{2} \quad\Rightarrow\quad m=4 \quad\cdots(1)$$
Hint: coefficients of like terms in an identity must be equal.
3Compare the constant terms.
$$m+28 \;\equiv\; -4n \quad\cdots(2)$$
Hint: the constant term on the left is $m+28$;
on the right it is $-4n$.
4Substitute $(1)$ into $(2)$ and solve for $n$.
Substitution into an Equation \(m+28=-4n,\quad m=4\) Putting \(m=4\) gives \(32=-4n\), so \(n=-8\).
$4+28=-4n$
$32=-4n$
$n=-8$
Hint: put $m=4$ straight into the constant-term equation — the $x$ term is not needed.
5Check the answer.
With $m=4,\;n=-8$:
LHS $=4x^{2}+4(x+1)+28 = 4x^{2}+4x+32$
RHS $=4x(x+3)-8(x-4)=4x^{2}+12x-8x+32=4x^{2}+4x+32$ ✓
Hint: both sides are identical for every $x$,
so the identity holds.
Answer: $n=-8$, i.e. option A.

Question. If \(3x^{2}+mx+(m+3)\equiv mx^{2}+(2m+n)x-2n\), find \(n\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Compare the coefficients of \(x^{2}\): \(3=m\).
Step 2. Compare the constant terms: \(m+3=-2n\), so \(6=-2n\) and \(n=-3\).
Step 3. Check the \(x\) term: \(2m+n=6-3=3=m\), which agrees.
Step 4. So the identity holds with \(m=3\) and \(n=-3\).
Answer: \(n=-3\)
