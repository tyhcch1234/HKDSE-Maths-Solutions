📘 Question Topic: Equations

The length and the breadth of a rectangle are 24 cm and \((13 + r)\) cm respectively. If the length of a diagonal of the rectangle is \((17 - 3r)\) cm, find the value of \(r\).

Only when \( r = -3 \) does the diagonal match the formula \( (17 - 3r) \).

🧩 Step-by-Step Solution

Draw the rectangle. The diagonal is the hypotenuse of the right-angled triangle formed by the length and breadth.

💡 Hint – Pythagoras' Theorem:
\( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \) where \(a\) and \(b\) are the two shorter sides, and \(c\) is the hypotenuse (diagonal).

Substitute the given values:

\[ 24^2 + (13 + r)^2 = (17 - 3r)^2 \]

Expand using \((a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2\) and \((a-b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2\).

\[ 576 + 169 + 26r + r^2 = 289 - 102r + 9r^2 \]

Combine like terms on the left:

\[ 745 + 26r + r^2 = 289 - 102r + 9r^2 \]

Move all terms to one side to get a quadratic equation \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\).

\[ 8r^2 - 128r - 456 = 0 \]

Divide every term by 8 to simplify:

\[ r^2 - 16r - 57 = 0 \]
💡 Hint – Factorisation:
Find two numbers that multiply to \(-57\) and add to \(-16\).
Those numbers are \(-19\) and \(+3\).
\[ (r + 3)(r - 19) = 0 \]

\[ r = -3 \quad \text{or} \quad r = 19 \]

A physical length must be positive. Test each root:

\( r = 19 \)
Breadth = \(13+19=32\) cm
Diagonal = \(17-3(19)=-40\) cm
✗ Negative diagonal → rejected
\( r = -3 \)
Breadth = \(13-3=10\) cm
Diagonal = \(17-3(-3)=26\) cm
✓ Both positive

Verify: \(24^2 + 10^2 = 576 + 100 = 676 = 26^2\) ✓

Therefore, \( r = -3 \).
🎯 Final Answer

\( r = -3 \)

The value \(r = 19\) is rejected because it gives a negative diagonal length.
