Equations — Forming & Solving a Quadratic

Question. The length and the breadth of a rectangle are $24\text{ cm}$ and $(13+r)\text{ cm}$ respectively. If the length of a diagonal of the rectangle is $(17-3r)\text{ cm}$, find $r$. [3 marks]

Step-by-step solution

1Set up the model
Draw the rectangle with length $24$, breadth $(13+r)$ and diagonal $(17-3r)$. The diagonal splits the rectangle into two right-angled triangles, so Pythagoras' Theorem applies.
Spot the right-angled triangle formed by two sides and the diagonal.
2Apply Pythagoras' Theorem  (1M)
\[24^{2}+(13+r)^{2}=(17-3r)^{2}\]
leg² + leg² = hypotenuse² — the diagonal is the hypotenuse.
3Expand both sides  (1M)
\[576+169+26r+r^{2}=289-102r+9r^{2}\] \[745+26r+r^{2}=289-102r+9r^{2}\]
Careful with signs when expanding $(17-3r)^2$.
4Collect terms into a quadratic
\[8r^{2}-128r-456=0\] \[r^{2}-16r-57=0\]
Bring everything to one side, then divide by the common factor $8$.
5Factorise
\[(r+3)(r-19)=0\] \[r=-3 \quad \text{or} \quad r=19\]
Look for two numbers multiplying to $-57$ and adding to $-16$.
6Check the condition
The diagonal length must be positive: \[17-3r>0\;\Rightarrow\;r<\tfrac{17}{3}\approx 5.67\] So $r=19$ is rejected, and $r=-3$ is accepted.
Always test whether each root keeps the diagonal (and breadth) positive.
7Final answer  (1A)
\[\boxed{r=-3}\] Check: breadth $=13+(-3)=10$ cm, diagonal $=17-3(-3)=26$ cm, and $24^{2}+10^{2}=676=26^{2}$. ✓
Answer: $r=-3$
