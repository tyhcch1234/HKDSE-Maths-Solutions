HKDSE 2026 · Paper 1 · Q16

Circle: Centre, Tangents & Triangle Angles

Question

It is given that \(P\), \(Q\) and \(R\) are three points in a rectangular coordinate plane. The coordinates of \(P\) and \(Q\) are \((7,8)\) and \((-13,20)\) respectively. The equation of the perpendicular bisector of \(PR\) is \(3x+5y-27=0\). Let \(C\) be the circle which passes through \(P\), \(Q\) and \(R\).

(a)   Find the equation of \(C\). (3 marks)

(b)   The tangent to \(C\) at \(P\) and the tangent to \(C\) at \(R\) intersect at the point \(H\). Is \(\triangle HPR\) a right-angled triangle? Explain your answer. (3 marks)

Solution

PART (a) — centre on two bisectors
The centre \(O\) of \(C\) is equidistant from \(P,Q,R\), so \(O\) lies on the perpendicular bisector of \(PQ\) and on the given bisector of \(PR\).
Midpoint of \(PQ = (-3,\,14)\); slope of \(PQ = \frac{20-8}{-13-7}=-\frac35\), so the bisector of \(PQ\) has slope \(\frac53\): \[ y-14=\frac53(x+3) \implies 5x-3y+57=0 \]
PART (a) — intersect the bisectors
\[ \begin{cases}3x+5y-27=0\\ 5x-3y+57=0\end{cases} \implies 34x+204=0 \implies x=-6,\; y=9 \] \[ O=(-6,\,9) \] Radius: \(OP^2=(7+6)^2+(8-9)^2=170\).
PART (a) — equation of C
\[ (x+6)^2+(y-9)^2=170 \]
PART (b) — find R
The bisector of \(PR\) has normal \((3,5)\), so chord \(PR\) is parallel to \((3,5)\). The line through \(P(7,8)\) in this direction: \((x,y)=(7+3t,\;8+5t)\). Substitute into \(C\): \[ (13+3t)^2+(5t-1)^2=170 \implies 34t^2+68t=0 \implies t=0 \text{ or } t=-2 \] \[ R=(1,\,-2) \]
PART (b) — check the angles
Tangents at \(P\) and \(R\) are \(\perp\) to \(OP\) and \(OR\), so in quadrilateral \(OPHR\), \(\angle P=\angle R=90^\circ\), giving \[ \angle PHR=180^\circ-\angle POR \] Now \(\overrightarrow{OP}=(13,-1)\), \(\overrightarrow{OR}=(7,-11)\): \[ \cos\angle POR=\frac{13(7)+(-1)(-11)}{\sqrt{170}\sqrt{170}}=\frac{102}{170}=\frac35 \] \[ \angle POR\approx 53.1^\circ \implies \angle PHR\approx126.9^\circ,\quad \angle HPR=\angle HRP\approx 26.6^\circ \] No angle is \(90^\circ\) (equivalently: \(H=(6.5,\,1.5)\), so \(HP^2+HR^2=42.5+42.5=85\neq PR^2=136\)).
(a) \((x+6)^2+(y-9)^2=170\)    (b) No — \(\angle PHR\approx126.9^\circ\) (and base angles \(\approx26.6^\circ\)), so \(\triangle HPR\) is not right-angled.
