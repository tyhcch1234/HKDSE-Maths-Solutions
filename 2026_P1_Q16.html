Circle: Centre, Tangents & Triangle Angles

Question

It is given that \(P\), \(Q\) and \(R\) are three points in a rectangular coordinate plane. The coordinates of \(P\) and \(Q\) are \((7,8)\) and \((-13,20)\) respectively. The equation of the perpendicular bisector of \(PR\) is \(3x+5y-27=0\). Let \(C\) be the circle which passes through \(P\), \(Q\) and \(R\).

(a) Find the equation of \(C\). (3 marks)

(b) The tangent to \(C\) at \(P\) and the tangent to \(C\) at \(R\) intersect at the point \(H\). Is \(\triangle HPR\) a right-angled triangle? Explain your answer. (3 marks)

Solution

Step 1 · Centre on two bisectors

The centre \(O\) of \(C\) is equidistant from \(P,Q,R\), so it lies on the perpendicular bisector of \(PQ\) and on the given bisector of \(PR\).

Midpoint of \(PQ=(-3,\,14)\); slope of \(PQ=\dfrac{20-8}{-13-7}=-\dfrac35\), so its bisector has slope \(\dfrac53\):

\[ y-14=\tfrac53(x+3)\ \Longrightarrow\ 5x-3y+57=0 \]
Step 2 · Intersect the bisectors → find O
\[ \begin{cases}3x+5y-27=0\\ 5x-3y+57=0\end{cases}\ \Longrightarrow\ 34x+204=0 \] \[ x=-6,\quad y=9 \qquad O=(-6,\,9) \]

Radius: \(OP^2=(7+6)^2+(8-9)^2=170\).

Step 3 · Equation of C
\[ (x+6)^2+(y-9)^2=170 \]
This is the answer to part (a).
Step 4 · Find R using the chord PR

The bisector of \(PR\) has normal \((3,5)\), so chord \(PR\) is parallel to \((3,5)\). Line through \(P(7,8)\):

\[ (x,y)=(7+3t,\ 8+5t) \]

Substitute into \(C\):

\[ (13+3t)^2+(5t-1)^2=170 \ \Longrightarrow\ 34t^2+68t=0 \] \[ t=0 \text{ or } t=-2 \qquad R=(1,\,-2) \]
Step 5 · Tangents & quadrilateral OPHR

Tangents at \(P\) and \(R\) are \(\perp\) to \(OP\) and \(OR\), so in quadrilateral \(OPHR\), \(\angle OPH=\angle ORH=90^\circ\), giving

\[ \angle PHR=180^\circ-\angle POR \]
Step 6 · Angle POR & final check

\(\overrightarrow{OP}=(13,-1)\), \(\overrightarrow{OR}=(7,-11)\):

\[ \cos\angle POR=\frac{13(7)+(-1)(-11)}{\sqrt{170}\sqrt{170}}=\frac{102}{170}=\frac35 \] \[ \angle POR\approx 53.1^\circ \] \[ \angle PHR\approx 126.9^\circ,\quad \angle HPR=\angle HRP\approx 26.6^\circ \]

No angle equals \(90^\circ\). Alternatively, \(H=(6.5,\,1.5)\), so \(HP^2+HR^2=42.5+42.5=85\neq PR^2=136\).

(a) $(x+6)^2+(y-9)^2=170$
(b) No — $\angle PHR\approx 126.9^\circ$ (base angles $\approx 26.6^\circ$), so $\triangle HPR$ is not right-angled.
