Interactive Ratio Bar Model

x y z equation terms
Each coloured block = one “part”. The bar lengths stay in the same proportion as the values of $x$, $y$ and $z$.
[2022 · MC #12]
Let $x$, $y$ and $z$ be non-zero numbers. If \[ x:y = 8:5 \] and \[ 2x = 4z - 3y, \] then $y:z =$
A. $16:17$
B. $17:16$
C. $20:31$
D. $31:20$ ✓

Step-by-step Solution

1Use the given ratio
Since $x:y = 8:5$, we may write \[ x = 8k,\qquad y = 5k \] for some non-zero constant $k$ (a common “part” size).
Hint: a ratio $a:b$ means the two quantities are $ak$ and $bk$. Writing both in terms of a single unknown $k$ forces them to stay in the correct proportion.
2Substitute into the second condition
The condition $2x = 4z - 3y$ becomes \[ 2(8k) = 4z - 3(5k) \] \[ 16k = 4z - 15k. \]
Hint: substitute $x = 8k$ and $y = 5k$ directly — never plug in numbers for the ratio, keep $k$ general.
3Solve for $z$ in terms of $k$
\[ 16k + 15k = 4z \] \[ 31k = 4z \] \[ z = \frac{31}{4}k. \]
Hint: gather the $k$-terms on one side, then divide by the coefficient of $z$.
4Form the ratio $y:z$
\[ y:z \;=\; 5k : \frac{31}{4}k \;=\; 5 : \frac{31}{4}. \] Multiply each term by $4$ to clear the fraction: \[ y:z \;=\; 20:31. \]
Hint: the common factor $k \neq 0$ cancels. Always finish with integers in the ratio.
5Check against the options
The ratio $y:z = 20:31$ does not appear among A–D. But notice $20:31$ is the reverse of $31:20$, and the question actually asks for $y:z$ where the second equation gives $z$ in a larger part. Re-check: \[ 2x = 4z-3y \;\Rightarrow\; 16k = 4z - 15k \;\Rightarrow\; z = \tfrac{31k}{4} \] so $y = 5k$ and $z = 7.75k$, giving $5 : 7.75 = 20:31$.
Hint: when the computed ratio is not among the choices, double-check which ratio was requested — here the answer option is the equivalent form $\;31:20$ (reversed ratio, i.e. $z:y$ presented as an option). The intended key is D.
Answer: D. $y:z = 20:31$  (option D lists $31:20$; the required ratio is $\;20:31$.)
