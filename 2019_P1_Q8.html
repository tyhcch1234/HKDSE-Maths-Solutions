Statistics — Pie chart, mode, weighted mean & probability

Pie Chart Mode Weighted Mean Probability Angle → Frequency

📋 Question 5 marks

The pie chart below shows the distribution of the number of rings owned by the girls in a group.

Angle sector 2: 144° → 40%
Angle sector 3: 54° → 15%
Angle sector 5: 72° → 20%
Angle sector 7: 90° → 25%
  1. Write down the mode of the distribution.1A
  2. Find the mean of the distribution.2M 1A
  3. If a girl is randomly selected from the group, find the probability that the selected girl owns more than 3 rings.1M 1A
[5 marks]

Part (a) — Mode of the distribution 1A

Frequencies: 2→144, 3→54, 5→72, 7→90
Largest frequency → 2 rings (144)
Step 11A

The mode is the data value with the highest frequency. From the pie chart, the sector for 2 rings has the largest angle, \(144^\circ\).

Compare the sector angles: the bigger the sector, the more girls own that many rings. \(144^\circ\) is the largest (it is actually \(40\%\) of the circle).
Mode \(= 2\)

Part (b) — Find the mean 2M 1A

Missing angle check: 360° − 54° − 90° − 144° = 72°
Number of girls (in "units"): 360
Step 11M

Work out the sector angle for "5 rings" (the sector whose angle is not written down).

\[360^\circ - 54^\circ - 90^\circ - 144^\circ = 72^\circ\]
The four sector angles must add up to the full circle \(360^\circ\).
Angle for "5 rings" \(= 72^\circ\)
Step 21M

A sector angle is proportional to the number of girls. Treat the angles as the frequencies.

\[\text{Mean} = \frac{2(144) + 3(54) + 5(72) + 7(90)}{360}\]
Multiply each data value (number of rings) by its frequency (sector angle), then divide by the total angle \(360^\circ\).
Step 31A

Evaluate carefully:

\[ \frac{288 + 162 + 360 + 630}{360} = \frac{1440}{360} = 4 \]
Check the arithmetic: \(2(144)=288\), \(3(54)=162\), \(5(72)=360\), \(7(90)=630\); and \(288+162+360+630=1440\).
Mean \(= 4\)

Part (c) — Probability of owning more than 3 rings 1M 1A

Favourable sector angles: 72° + 90° = 162°
Total angle: 360°
Step 11M

"More than 3 rings" means \(5\) or \(7\) rings. Add those sector angles:

\[72^\circ + 90^\circ = 162^\circ\]
The probability is (total favourable angle) ÷ (total angle \(360^\circ\)), since sector angle is proportional to frequency.
Step 21A

Form the probability and simplify:

\[ \frac{72+90}{360} = \frac{162}{360} = \frac{9}{20} \]
Divide top and bottom by \(18\): \(162 \div 18 = 9\), \(360 \div 18 = 20\).
Required probability \(= \dfrac{9}{20}\)

📌 Summary of Answers

  • (a) Mode \(= 2\)
  • (b) Mean \(= 4\)
  • (c) Probability \(= \dfrac{9}{20}\) (accept \(0.45\))
  • Key idea Sector angle \(\propto\) frequency; total angle \(= 360^\circ\)
