Pie chart — number of rings owned by the girls

2 rings (144°) 3 rings (54°) 5 rings (90°) 7 rings (72°)

Step 1 · Given: sector angles 144°, 54°, 90°; the last sector = 360° − 144° − 90° − 54°.

HKDSE 2019 Paper 1 Q8 · 15. Statistics

The pie chart below shows the distribution of the number of rings owned by the girls in a group.

(The pie chart in the left pane: the four sectors correspond to owning $2$, $3$, $5$ and $7$ rings respectively; the sector angles are $144^\circ$, $54^\circ$, $90^\circ$ and $72^\circ$.)

  1. Write down the mode of the distribution.
  2. Find the mean of the distribution.
  3. If a girl is randomly selected from the group, find the probability that the selected girl owns more than $3$ rings. [5 marks]
1 Read off the pie chart

The sector with the largest angle is the $144^\circ$ sector, and that sector is labelled "$2$". So more girls own $2$ rings than any other number.

Mode = the data value with the largest sector — the biggest angle wins.
2 Part (a) — the mode

The largest angle is $144^\circ$, which belongs to the "$2$" sector.

\[\text{Mode}=2 \qquad \text{(1A)}\]

Only the value "2" is needed — no working required for this 1 mark.
3 Part (b) — find the missing sector angle

Sector angles add up to $360^\circ$. The four labelled sectors use $144^\circ+54^\circ+90^\circ$, so the $7$-rings sector has angle

\[360^\circ-54^\circ-90^\circ-144^\circ=72^\circ .\]

Spot the right-angle symbol (the $90^\circ$ sector for "5") and subtract all known angles from $360^\circ$.
4 Part (b) — weighted mean using angles

A sector of angle $\theta^\circ$ represents a fraction $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$ of the whole group, so the mean is the angle-weighted average:

\[\text{Mean}=\frac{2(144)+3(54)+5(72)+7(90)}{360}\]

\[=\frac{288+162+360+630}{360}=\frac{1440}{360}=4 \qquad \text{(1M, 1A)}\]

Each value is multiplied by the angle of its sector (equivalently by the number of girls in that sector, since the group size cancels).
5 Part (c) — probability of "more than 3 rings"

"More than $3$ rings" means $5$ rings or $7$ rings, with total angle

\[72^\circ+90^\circ=162^\circ .\]

\[\text{Required probability}=\frac{72+90}{360}=\frac{162}{360}=\frac{9}{20}\qquad \text{(1M, 1A)}\]

Probability for pie charts: (angle of the favourable sectors) ÷ $360^\circ$ — the group size is never needed.
Answer:
(a) Mode $= 2$
(b) Mean $= 4$
(c) Probability $= \dfrac{72+90}{360}=\dfrac{9}{20}$
