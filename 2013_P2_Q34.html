HKDSE 2013 • Paper 2 • Q34
If $x-\log y=x^{2}-\log y^{2}-10=2$, then $y=$
  • A.  $100$.
  • B.  $2$ or $-4$.
  • C.  $\dfrac{1}{100}$ or $10000$.
  • D.  $\dfrac{1}{10000}$ or $100$.  ✔
STEP 1
The chain of equalities $x-\log y=x^{2}-\log y^{2}-10=2$ actually gives two equations: \[ \begin{cases} x-\log y=2 & \cdots(1)\\[2pt] x^{2}-\log y^{2}-10=2 & \cdots(2)\end{cases} \]
Break a "\(=A=B\)" chain into separate equations.
STEP 2
From (1): $x=2+\log y$ … (3)
Use the log law $\log y^{2}=2\log y$ in (2): \[ x^{2}-2\log y-12=0 \]
Make $x$ the subject first, then simplify log $y^2$.
Power Law of Logarithms \(\log y^{k}=k\log y\) The term \\(\log y^{2}\\) in the second equation becomes \\(2\log y\\), leaving \\(\log y\\) as the only unknown.
STEP 3
Substitute (3) into (2): \[(2+\log y)^{2}-2\log y-12=0\] \[4+4\log y+(\log y)^{2}-2\log y-12=0\] \[(\log y)^{2}+2\log y-8=0\]
Now the equation is purely in $\log y$.
STEP 4
Factorise: \[(\log y+4)(\log y-2)=0\] \[\log y=-4 \quad\text{or}\quad \log y=2\]
Let $u=\log y$, then $u^{2}+2u-8=(u+4)(u-2)$.
Zero Product Property \(uv=0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ u=0\ \text{or}\ v=0\) With \\(u=\log y\\), the factorised form \\((\log y+4)(\log y-2)=0\\) splits into two simple equations.
STEP 5
Since the base is $10$: \[ y=10^{-4}=\frac{1}{10000} \quad\text{or}\quad y=10^{2}=100 \]
$\log y=k \iff y=10^{k}$ (common log).
Definition of the Common Logarithm \(\log y=k\ \Longleftrightarrow\ y=10^{k}\) \\(\log\\) here is base \\(10\\), so \\(\log y=-4\\) gives \\(y=\dfrac{1}{10000}\\) and \\(\log y=2\\) gives \\(y=100\\).
ANSWER
Both values are positive, so both are valid.
ANSWER \[ y=\frac{1}{10000}\ \text{ or }\ 100 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \boxed{\text{D}} \]
