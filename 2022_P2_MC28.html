HKDSE Math 2022 Paper 2

Q28 · Probability

Probability Divisibility 3-digit numbers Favourable outcomes

📝 Question

\(5\blacklozenge2\) is a 3-digit number, where \(\blacklozenge\) is an integer from 0 to 9 inclusive. Find the probability that the 3-digit number is divisible by 7.

A. \(\frac{1}{5}\)
B. \(\frac{1}{7}\)
C. \(\frac{1}{9}\)
D. \(\frac{1}{10}\)

🧩 Step-by-step Solution

The digit \(\blacklozenge\) can be any integer from 0 to 9 inclusive.

So there are exactly 10 possible 3-digit numbers: \(502, 512, 522, \ldots, 592\).

Total outcomes = 10.

We need to find which value of \(\blacklozenge\) makes \(5\blacklozenge2\) divisible by 7.

💡 Hint

A number is divisible by 7 if the result of dividing it by 7 has no remainder. You can test each digit from 0 to 9 quickly.

Let's test systematically: \(502, 512, 522, 532, 542, 552, 562, 572, 582, 592\).

Dividing each by 7:

  • \(502 \div 7 = 71.71...\) ❌
  • \(512 \div 7 = 73.14...\) ❌
  • \(522 \div 7 = 74.57...\) ❌
  • \(532 \div 7 = 76\) ✅ Remainder 0
  • \(542 \div 7 \approx 77.43\) ❌
  • \(552 \div 7 \approx 78.86\) ❌
  • \(562 \div 7 \approx 80.29\) ❌
  • \(572 \div 7 \approx 81.71\) ❌
  • \(582 \div 7 \approx 83.14\) ❌
  • \(592 \div 7 \approx 84.57\) ❌

Only \(532\) is divisible by 7. So \(\blacklozenge = 3\).

There is exactly 1 favourable outcome: \(\blacklozenge = 3\).

Favourable outcomes = 1.

Using the probability formula:

Probability

\[ \frac{\text{Number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{Total number of possible outcomes}} = \frac{1}{10} \]

Therefore the required probability is \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{10}\).

Answer: D
