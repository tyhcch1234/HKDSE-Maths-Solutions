Inequality (1): $x \ge -1$ Inequality (2): $x > 3.25$ Combined range: $x > 3.25$ Least integer: $x = 4$

Question

(a) Find the range of values of $x$ which satisfy both

$$ \frac{7-3x}{5} \le 2(x+2) \qquad \text{and} \qquad 4x-13>0. $$

(b) Write down the least integer which satisfies both inequalities in (a).

[4 marks]

Solution

1 Solve the first inequality (clear the fraction)
$$ \frac{7-3x}{5} \le 2(x+2) $$ $$ 7-3x \le 10(x+2) $$
Hint: multiply both sides by $5$ (positive, so the inequality sign does not flip).
2 Expand and collect terms for the first inequality
Solving a Linear Inequality \(ax\le b \Rightarrow x\le\dfrac{b}{a}\quad(a>0)\) Dividing \(-13\le 13x\) by the positive \(13\) keeps the sign, giving \(x\ge -1\).
$$ 7-3x \le 10x + 20 $$ $$ -13 \le 13x $$ $$ x \ge -1 $$
Hint: move all $x$ terms to one side; dividing by $+13$ keeps the sign. 1A
3 Solve the second inequality
Solving \(ax>b\) \(ax>b \Rightarrow x>\dfrac{b}{a}\quad(a>0)\) Dividing \(4x>13\) by the positive \(4\) leaves \(x>\dfrac{13}{4}=3.25\).
$$ 4x-13 > 0 $$ $$ 4x > 13 $$ $$ x > \frac{13}{4} \;\; (=3.25) $$
Hint: divide by $4$ (positive) — the strict sign $>$ is kept. 1A
4 Combine both inequalities and find the least integer
Intersection of Two Solution Sets \(x\ge a \text{ and } x>b \iff x>\max(a,b)\) The stricter bound \(x>\dfrac{13}{4}\) wins over \(x\ge -1\), so the least integer is \(4\).
$$ x \ge -1 \quad \text{AND} \quad x > \frac{13}{4} $$ $$ \Rightarrow \; x > \frac{13}{4} $$

Since $13/4 = 3.25$,
the smallest integer strictly greater than $3.25$ is $4$.

Hint: take the intersection of the two ranges — the stricter bound wins. 1M 1A
Answer (a): $\displaystyle x > \frac{13}{4}$  ·  (b): the least integer is $4$.

Question. Solve \(\dfrac{7-2x}{4}\le 3(x+1)\) and \(2x-9>0\), and hence find the least integer satisfying both inequalities. [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Multiply the first inequality by \(4\) (positive, so the sign is kept): \(7-2x\le 12x+12\).
Step 2. Collect terms: \(7-12\le 12x+2x\), i.e. \(-5\le 14x\), so \(x\ge -\dfrac{5}{14}\).
Step 3. Second inequality: \(2x>9\), so \(x>4.5\).
Step 4. Both must hold, and \(x>4.5\) is the stricter bound.
Step 5. The least integer greater than \(4.5\) is \(5\).
Answer: \(x>4.5\); the least integer is \(5\)
