Sequences

Question

The sum of the first \(n\) terms of a sequence is \(3^n-1\). Which of the following are true?

I. The 2nd term of the sequence is \(8\).
II. The sequence is a geometric sequence.
III. All the terms of the sequence are even numbers.

  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III

Solution

General term
General Term from the Sum of the First n Terms \(a_{n}=S_{n}-S_{n-1}\quad (n\ge 2)\) Subtracting \(S_{n-1}=3^{n-1}-1\) from \(S_{n}=3^{n}-1\) leaves \(3^{n-1}(3-1)=2\cdot 3^{n-1}\), the general term.
\(a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}=(3^n-1)-(3^{n-1}-1)=3^{n-1}(3-1)=2\cdot3^{n-1}\).
Terms Sequence \(=2,6,18,54,\dots\). The 2nd term is \(6\), not \(8\). (I false)
Geometric?
Common Ratio of a Geometric Sequence \(r=\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}\) Here \(r=\dfrac{6}{2}=\dfrac{18}{6}=3\), a constant ratio, so the sequence is geometric.
Common ratio \(=\dfrac{6}{2}=\dfrac{18}{6}=3\), so it is geometric. (II true)
Even?
Divisibility of a Product \(2\cdot 3^{n-1}=2\times\left(\text{integer}\right)\) Every term carries the factor \(2\), so each term is even — statement III holds for all \(n\ge 1\).
Each term \(=2\cdot3^{n-1}\) is even. (III true)
Conclusion II and III only.
Answer: C — II and III only

Question. Let \(S_{n}=4^{n}-1\) be the sum of the first \(n\) terms of a sequence.
(a) Find the general term \(a_{n}\).
(b) Show that the sequence is geometric and state its common ratio.
(c) Show that every term is divisible by \(3\). (4 marks)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. For \(n\ge 2\), \(a_{n}=S_{n}-S_{n-1}=\left(4^{n}-1\right)-\left(4^{n-1}-1\right)=4^{n-1}(4-1)=3\cdot 4^{n-1}\).
Step 2. Also \(a_{1}=S_{1}=4-1=3\), which agrees with the formula, so \(a_{n}=3\cdot 4^{n-1}\) for all \(n\ge 1\). The terms are \(3,12,48,192,\dots\)
Step 3. Ratio test: \(\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}=\dfrac{3\cdot 4^{n}}{3\cdot 4^{n-1}}=4\), a constant, so the sequence is geometric with common ratio \(4\).
Step 4. Each term is \(3\times 4^{n-1}\), a multiple of \(3\), so every term is divisible by \(3\).
Answer: (a) \(a_{n}=3\cdot 4^{n-1}\); (b) common ratio \(4\); (c) every term is \(3\times 4^{n-1}\), hence divisible by \(3\)
