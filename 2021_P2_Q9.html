HKDSE 2021 Paper 2 · Q9

Percentages — weighted average of overweight percentages

In a school, $33\%$ of the students are overweight. It is given that $60\%$ of the students in the school are girls and $45\%$ of the girls are overweight. If $x\%$ of the boys in the school are overweight, then $x =$

  • A. 15.
  • B. 18.
  • C. 25.
  • D. 55.

Step-by-step solution

1Identify the given information

The whole school can be split into two groups only: girls and boys.

Girls $= 60\%$ of all students, so boys $= 1 - 60\% = 40\%$ of all students.

Overweight girls $= 45\%$ of the girls; let $x\%$ of the boys be overweight.

A whole is $100\%$; the two groups must add up to it.
2Use the weighted-average (all-students) idea

Total overweight $=$ overweight girls $+$ overweight boys:

$$(60\%)(45\%) + (40\%)(x\%) = 33\%$$

The school-wide $33\%$ is a percentage of all students, so each group’s share must be weighted by its fraction of the whole.
Weighted Average \(\bar{p}=w_{1}p_{1}+w_{2}p_{2}\) The school-wide \(33\%\) must equal \(60\%\times45\%\) from the girls plus \(40\%\times x\%\) from the boys.
3Simplify both terms

$$(60\%)(45\%) = 0.60 \times 0.45 = 0.27 = 27\%$$

$$(40\%)(x\%) = 0.4 \times \frac{x}{100}$$

So the equation becomes:

$$27\% + (40\%)(x\%) = 33\%$$

Multiplying two percentages: change each to a decimal, then multiply.
Converting Percentages to Decimals \(a\%\times b\%=\dfrac{ab}{10000}\) \((60\%)(45\%)\) must be multiplied as decimals, giving \(0.27=27\%\).
4Solve for $x$

$$(40\%)(x\%) = 33\% - 27\% = 6\%$$

$$0.4 \times \frac{x}{100} = 0.06 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{0.4x}{100}=0.06$$

$$0.4x = 6 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 15$$

Subtract the girls’ contribution first, then divide by the boys’ share $40\%$.
5Check the answer

$$27\% + (40\%)(15\%) = 27\% + 6\% = 33\% \;\checkmark$$

The boys’ overweight rate ($15\%$) is lower than the girls’ ($45\%$), which is consistent with the school average ($33\%$) lying between the two, closer to the girls’ value since girls are the majority.

Always verify: the weighted average must lie between the two group percentages.
Answer: \(x = 15\) → Option A.

Question. In a school, \(60\%\) of the students are girls. \(50\%\) of the girls are overweight, and \(32\%\) of all the students are overweight. Find the percentage of the boys who are overweight.

Show the worked solution

Step 2. Overweight students: \((60\%)(50\%)+(40\%)(x\%)=32\%\).
