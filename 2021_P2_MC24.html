Question 24 Multiple choice — find the value of \(\dfrac{AB}{CD}\)

Step-by-step derivation Reveal one step at a time

Step 1 1M Look at the right-angled triangle \(ABC\).

In \(\triangle ABC\), the right angle is at \(B\), so \(AB\) is the opposite side of \(\angle ACB=\phi\) and \(AC\) is the hypotenuse.

\[ \sin\phi = \frac{AB}{AC} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad AB = AC\sin\phi \tag{1} \]
Hint: The right angle at \(B\) tells us to use \(\triangle ABC\) with \(\sin\phi = \dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}} = \dfrac{AB}{AC}\).
Step 2 1M Look at the right-angled triangle \(ACD\).

In \(\triangle ACD\), the right angle is at \(C\), so \(AC\) is the side adjacent to \(\angle ADC=\theta\) and \(CD\) is the opposite side.

\[ \tan\theta = \frac{AC}{CD} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad CD = \frac{AC}{\tan\theta} \tag{2} \]
Hint: The right angle at \(C\) is between \(AC\) and \(CD\). With \(\angle ADC=\theta\), \(\tan\theta = \dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}} = \dfrac{AC}{CD}\).
Step 3 1M Divide (1) by (2) to eliminate \(AC\).
\[ \frac{AB}{CD} = \frac{AC\sin\phi}{\;AC/\tan\theta\;} = \sin\phi\cdot\tan\theta \]
Hint: Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal: \(\dfrac{AC\sin\phi}{AC/\tan\theta}=AC\sin\phi \times \dfrac{\tan\theta}{AC}\). The \(AC\) cancels.
Step 4 1A Simplify and match the answer.
\[ \frac{AB}{CD} = \tan\theta\,\sin\phi \]
Hint: The result only involves \(\theta\) and \(\phi\) — exactly the given angles. Check each option: only option D equals \(\tan\theta\sin\phi\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{AB}{CD} = \tan\theta\,\sin\phi\) \(\Rightarrow\) Option D.

Summary of Answers

  • Key relation 1: \(AB = AC\sin\phi\)
  • Key relation 2: \(CD = \dfrac{AC}{\tan\theta}\)
  • Ratio: \(\dfrac{AB}{CD} = \dfrac{AC\sin\phi}{AC/\tan\theta} = \tan\theta\,\sin\phi\)
  • Final answer: Option D — \(\tan\theta\sin\phi\)
