[2015MC#10]

A sum of $\$50\,000$ is deposited at an interest rate of $6\%$ per annum for $3$ years, compounded quarterly. Find the amount correct to the nearest dollar.

  • A. $\$59\,000$
  • B. $\$59\,551$
  • C. $\$59\,755$
  • D. $\$59\,781$
1Identify the given values

Principal $P = \$50\,000$, nominal rate $r = 6\%$ p.a., time $t = 3$ years, compounding frequency $n = 4$ (quarterly).

“Compounded quarterly” means interest is added 4 times a year.
2Rate per period and number of periods

Interest rate per quarter: $\dfrac{6\%}{4} = 1.5\% = 0.015$
Number of quarters in 3 years: $3 \times 4 = 12$

Divide the annual rate by the frequency; multiply the years by the frequency.
Rate per Compounding Period \(i=\dfrac{r}{n},\qquad N=nt\) Quarterly compounding gives \(i=\dfrac{6\%}{4}=1.5\%\) per quarter and \(N=3\times4=12\) quarters.
3Apply the compound interest formula

$$A = P\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$ $$A = 50\,000\left(1 + \frac{6\%}{4}\right)^{3\times 4} = 50\,000\,(1.015)^{12}$$

Substitute the values from Steps 1 and 2 into the formula.
Compound Interest Formula \(A=P\left(1+\dfrac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}\) With \(P=50\,000\), \(r=6\%\), \(n=4\) and \(t=3\), the amount is \(50\,000(1.015)^{12}\).
4Evaluate

$$A = 50\,000 \times 1.015^{12} = 59\,780.90857\ldots$$

Keep full accuracy until the final rounding step.
5Round to the nearest dollar

$$A \approx \$59\,781$$

The first decimal digit is 9, so round the dollar figure up.
ANSWER
$A = \$59\,781$  —  option D

Question. A sum of \(\$80\,000\) is deposited at \(4\%\) p.a. compounded monthly for \(2\) years. Find the amount correct to the nearest dollar.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Principal \(P=80\,000\), nominal rate \(r=4\%\) p.a., \(n=12\) (monthly) and \(t=2\) years.
Step 2. Rate per month: \(i=\dfrac{4\%}{12}\approx0.3333\%=0.003333\ldots\); number of periods: \(N=12\times2=24\).
Step 3. \(A=P\left(1+\dfrac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}=80\,000\left(1+\dfrac{0.04}{12}\right)^{24}\).
Step 4. \(A=80\,000(1.003333\ldots)^{24}\approx86\,651.4367\).
Step 5. To the nearest dollar, \(A\approx\$86\,651\).
Answer: \(\$86\,651\)
