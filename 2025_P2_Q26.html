Question & Step-by-step Solution

[2025MC#26]

The straight lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ are perpendicular to each other. The equations of $L_1$ and $L_2$ are $3x+4y-20=0$ and $mx+ny-20=0$ respectively, where $m$ and $n$ are constants. Suppose that $L_1$ and $L_2$ cut the $x$-axis at the points $A$ and $B$ respectively. Let $C$ be the point of intersection of $L_1$ and $L_2$. It is given that $C$ lies above the $x$-axis. If the area of $\triangle ABC$ is $6$, find $n$.

A. −12
B. −9
C. 9
D. 12
Hint: Put both lines in slope–intercept form to read off the two $x$-intercepts. The perpendicular condition gives a link between $m$ and $n$; the triangle area gives a second equation.
Step 1 · Slope–intercept form

From $3x+4y-20=0$: $\;4y=-3x+20$

$$L_1:\; y=-\tfrac{3}{4}x+5 \qquad x\text{-intercept}=\frac{20}{3}$$

From $mx+ny-20=0$: $\;ny=-mx+20$

$$L_2:\; y=-\tfrac{m}{n}x+\frac{20}{n} \qquad x\text{-intercept}=\frac{20}{m}$$

So $A=\left(\frac{20}{3},0\right)$ and $B=\left(\frac{20}{m},0\right)$.

Step 2 · Perpendicular condition

Since $L_1 \perp L_2$, the product of slopes is $-1$:

$$\left(-\frac{3}{4}\right)\times\left(-\frac{m}{n}\right)=-1$$

$$\frac{3m}{4n}=-1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=-\frac{3}{4}m \quad (\ast)$$

Condition for Perpendicular Lines \(m_{1}m_{2}=-1\) The product of the two slopes being \(-1\) links \(m\) and \(n\) before any lengths are used.
Step 3 · Use $\triangle ADO$ as a reference

Let $D$ be the $y$-intercept of $L_1$: $D=(0,5)$, and $O=(0,0)$.

$$AD=\sqrt{5^2+\left(\frac{20}{3}\right)^2}=\sqrt{25+\frac{400}{9}}=\sqrt{\frac{625}{9}}=\frac{25}{3}$$

$$\text{Area of }\triangle ADO=\frac{1}{2}\times 5\times\frac{20}{3}=\frac{50}{3}$$

Distance Formula \(AD=\sqrt{5^{2}+\left(\frac{20}{3}\right)^{2}}\) The intercepts give a right-angled triangle, so its hypotenuse is found by the distance formula.
Step 4 · Similar triangles

Both triangles have a right angle (perpendicular lines / axes) at the relevant vertex, so

$$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle ADO$$

Hence the ratio of areas equals the square of the ratio of corresponding sides:

$$\left(\frac{AB}{AD}\right)^2=\frac{\text{area of }\triangle ABC}{\text{area of }\triangle ADO}$$

Areas of Similar Triangles \(\dfrac{\text{area of }ABC}{\text{area of }ADO}=\left(\dfrac{AB}{AD}\right)^{2}\) The ratio of the areas is the square of the ratio of corresponding sides.
Step 5 · Solve for $m$

$$\left(\frac{AB}{25/3}\right)^2=\frac{6}{50/3}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left(\frac{3AB}{25}\right)^2=\frac{18}{50}=\frac{9}{25}$$

$$\frac{3AB}{25}=\frac{3}{5}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad AB=5$$

With $A$ to the right of $B$ (as in the figure): $\dfrac{20}{3}-\dfrac{20}{m}=5$

$$\frac{20}{m}=\frac{20}{3}-5=\frac{5}{3}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=12$$

Step 6 · Find $n$

From $(\ast)$:

$$n=-\frac{3}{4}m=-\frac{3}{4}(12)=-9$$

Check: $C$ lies above the $x$-axis, consistent with the given condition.
