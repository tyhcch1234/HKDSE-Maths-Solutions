Coordinate Geometry — Perpendicular Lines, Intercepts & Areas of Similar Triangles

Perpendicular Lines x-intercepts Similar Triangles Area Ratio Slope-Intercept Form

Question Multiple Choice

Four options · choose the correct value of \(n\)

The straight lines \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) are perpendicular to each other. The equations of \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) are \[3x+4y-20=0 \quad\text{and}\quad mx+ny-20=0\] respectively, where \(m\) and \(n\) are constants. Suppose that \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) cut the \(x\)-axis at the points \(A\) and \(B\) respectively. Let \(C\) be the point of intersection of \(L_1\) and \(L_2\). It is given that \(C\) lies above the \(x\)-axis. If the area of \(\triangle ABC\) is \(6\), find \(n\).
A.  \(-12\)
B.  \(-9\)
C.  \(9\)
D.  \(12\)  ✗

Part (a) — Slopes & Intercepts Setup

Rewrite both lines in slope-intercept form and read off the intercepts.

Slope of \(L_1\): \(-\frac34=-0.75\) x-intercept \(A=\left(\frac{20}{3},\,0\right)\) y-intercept of \(L_1\): \(D=(0,\,5)\)
\(m=12\)
Step 1
Rearrange \(L_1:\;3x+4y-20=0\) into slope-intercept form: \[y=-\frac34 x+5\] The \(x\)-intercept of \(L_1\) is \(A=\left(\frac{20}{3},\,0\right)\) and its \(y\)-intercept is \(D=(0,\,5)\).
Hint: For an \(x\)-intercept, set \(y=0\). For a \(y\)-intercept, set \(x=0\).
Answer: \(L_1:\;y=-\frac34x+5,\quad A=\left(\frac{20}{3},0\right),\quad D=(0,5)\)
Step 2
Rearrange \(L_2:\;mx+ny-20=0\). Treating \(m,n\neq0\): \[y=-\frac{m}{n}x+\frac{20}{n}\] Its \(x\)-intercept is \(B=\left(\frac{20}{m},\,0\right)\), and the \(y\)-intercept is \(\left(0,\,\frac{20}{n}\right)\).
Hint: Set \(y=0\) ⟹ \(x=\frac{20}{m}\); set \(x=0\) ⟹ \(y=\frac{20}{n}\).
Answer: \(L_2:\;y=-\frac{m}{n}x+\frac{20}{n},\quad B=\left(\frac{20}{m},0\right)\)
0 / 2

Part (b) — Perpendicularity gives \(n=-\frac34 m\) Condition

Use the perpendicular condition \(m_1 m_2=-1\).

Slope \(L_1\) : \(-\frac34\) Slope \(L_2\) : \(+\frac43\) Product : \(-\frac34\times\frac43=-1\)
Step 3
Since \(L_1\perp L_2\), the product of their slopes equals \(-1\): \[\left(-\frac34\right)\left(-\frac mn\right)=-1\] \[\frac{3m}{4n}=-1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=-\frac34 m \quad (\star)\] For \(C\) to lie above the \(x\)-axis we need \(m>0\) and \(n<0\).
Hint: Perpendicular lines satisfy \(m_1 m_2=-1\). The sign of \(n\) follows from \(n=-\frac34m\) since the intersection point must be above the \(x\)-axis.
Answer: \(n=-\frac34 m\)

Part (c) — Similar Triangles & Area Ratio Key Idea

Compare \(\triangle ABC\) with the larger right triangle \(\triangle ADO\) using the area ratio.

\(AD=\frac{25}{3}\) Area of \(\triangle ADO=\frac{50}{3}\) \(AB=5\)
Step 4
Let \(D\) be the \(y\)-intercept of \(L_1\), i.e. \(D=(0,5)\). Then: \[AD=\sqrt{\left(\frac{20}{3}\right)^2+5^2}=\sqrt{\frac{400}{9}+\frac{225}{9}}=\sqrt{\frac{625}{9}}=\frac{25}{3}\] \[\text{Area of }\triangle ADO=\frac12\times 5\times\frac{20}{3}=\frac{50}{3}\]
Hint: \(\triangle ADO\) is right-angled at \(O\), so its area is \(\frac12\,\bigl|OA\bigr|\cdot\bigl|OD\bigr|\).
Answer: \(AD=\frac{25}{3},\qquad [\triangle ADO]=\frac{50}{3}\)
Step 5
\(\triangle ABC\) is similar to \(\triangle ADO\) because:
  • \(\angle A\) is common;
  • the right angle at \(C\) corresponds to the right angle at \(O\).
Therefore the ratio of the areas equals the square of the ratio of corresponding sides: \[\left(\frac{AB}{AD}\right)^2=\frac{[\triangle ABC]}{[\triangle ADO]}=\frac{6}{50/3}\] \[\left(\frac{AB}{25/3}\right)^2=\frac{18}{50}=\frac{9}{25}\]
Hint: For similar figures, \(\dfrac{\text{area}_1}{\text{area}_2}=\left(\dfrac{\text{side}_1}{\text{side}_2}\right)^2\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{AB}{AD}=\dfrac{3}{5}\)
Step 6
From \(\dfrac{AB}{AD}=\dfrac35\), with \(AD=\dfrac{25}{3}\): \[AB=\frac35\times\frac{25}{3}=5\] But \(AB=\left|\frac{20}{3}-\frac{20}{m}\right|\). Since \(C\) lies above the \(x\)-axis we take \(m>0\) and \(B\) right of \(O\), so: \[\frac{20}{3}-\frac{20}{m}=5\] \[\frac{20}{m}=\frac{20}{3}-5=\frac{5}{3}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=12\]
Hint: Solve \(\frac{20}{3}-\frac{20}{m}=5\) for \(m\).
Answer: \(m=12\)
Step 7
Substitute \(m=12\) into \((\star)\): \[n=-\frac34 m=-\frac34(12)=-9\]
Hint: Check: for \(m=12,\ n=-9\), \(L_2:12x-9y-20=0\), i.e. \(4x-3y=\frac{20}{3}\), with slope \(\frac43\) ⟂ slope of \(L_1=-\frac34\).
Answer: \(n=-9\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\) Option B

Summary of Results

  • \(L_1:3x+4y-20=0\;\Rightarrow\;y=-\frac34x+5,\quad A=\left(\frac{20}{3},0\right)\)
  • \(L_2:mx+ny-20=0\;\Rightarrow\;y=-\frac{m}{n}x+\frac{20}{n},\quad B=\left(\frac{20}{m},0\right)\)
  • Perpendicular condition: \(-\frac34\times-\frac mn=-1\;\Rightarrow\;n=-\frac34 m\)
  • \(D=(0,5)\), \(AD=\frac{25}{3}\), \([\triangle ADO]=\frac{50}{3}\)
  • \(\triangle ABC\sim\triangle ADO\;\Rightarrow\;\left(\frac{AB}{AD}\right)^2=\frac{6}{50/3}=\frac{9}{25}\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{AB}{AD}=\frac35\)
  • \(AB=\frac{20}{3}-\frac{20}{m}=5\;\Rightarrow\;m=12\)
  • \(n=-\frac34(12)=-9\)  →  Answer: Option B
