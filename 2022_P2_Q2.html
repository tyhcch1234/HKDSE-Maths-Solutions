Step 1: Write 81 and 27 as powers of 3

Numerator $81=3^4$ Denominator $27=3^3$ Final answer
HKDSE 2022 Paper 2 · Q2

Simplify:

$\dfrac{81^{\,2n+3}}{(27^{\,n+1})^{2}}=$

  • A. $\;3$
  • B. $\;3^{\,2n+6}$
  • C. $\;3^{\,4n+8}$
  • D. $\;3^{\,10n+14}$

Step-by-step Solution

1
Expressing Numbers as Powers of a Common Base \(16=2^{4},\qquad 8=2^{3}\) Writing every number as a power of the same base is what allows the index laws to be applied.
Express every base as a power of 3

Since $81 = 3^{4}$ and $27 = 3^{3}$,
rewrite the expression: $$\frac{81^{\,2n+3}}{(27^{\,n+1})^{2}}=\frac{(3^{4})^{2n+3}}{\left[(3^{3})^{n+1}\right]^{2}}$$

Hint: convert the numbers into powers of the same base first — this is the key idea for indices questions.
2
Power of a Power \(\left(a^{m}\right)^{n}=a^{mn}\) The numerator becomes \(2^{12n-4}\) and the denominator \(\left(2^{3n+6}\right)^{2}=2^{6n+12}\).
Apply the power law $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$

Numerator: $\;(3^{4})^{2n+3}=3^{\,4(2n+3)}=3^{\,8n+12}$

Denominator: $\;\left[(3^{3})^{n+1}\right]^{2}=3^{\,3\cdot 2(n+1)}=3^{\,6n+6}$

Hint: multiply the outer exponent into the bracket, then multiply again by the power of 2.
3
Quotient Law of Indices \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\) Subtracting the exponents gives \(2^{(12n-4)-(6n+12)}=2^{6n-16}\).
Apply the quotient law $\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{\,m-n}$

$$\frac{3^{\,8n+12}}{3^{\,6n+6}}=3^{\,(8n+12)-(6n+6)}$$

Hint: same base ⇒ subtract the exponents.
4Simplify the exponent

$(8n+12)-(6n+6)=8n+12-6n-6=2n+6$

$$\therefore \frac{81^{\,2n+3}}{(27^{\,n+1})^{2}}=3^{\,2n+6}$$

Hint: collect like terms carefully — watch the sign when removing the bracket.
Answer: B  —  $3^{\,2n+6}$

Question. Simplify \(\dfrac{16^{\,3n-1}}{\left(8^{\,n+2}\right)^{2}}\), where \(n\) is a constant.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Write \(16=2^{4}\) and \(8=2^{3}\): the expression is \(\dfrac{\left(2^{4}\right)^{3n-1}}{\left[\left(2^{3}\right)^{n+2}\right]^{2}}\).
Step 2. Power of a power: numerator \(=2^{4(3n-1)}=2^{12n-4}\), denominator \(=\left(2^{3n+6}\right)^{2}=2^{6n+12}\).
Step 3. Quotient law: \(\dfrac{2^{12n-4}}{2^{6n+12}}=2^{(12n-4)-(6n+12)}\).
Step 4. Simplify the exponent: \(12n-4-6n-12=6n-16\), so the expression is \(2^{\,6n-16}\).
Answer: \(2^{\,6n-16}\)
