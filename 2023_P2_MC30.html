Statistics — Combined Mean of Two Groups (Weighted Mean)
Weighted Mean Combined Mean Statistics Multiple Choice

Q30Question

There are \(14\) full-time employees and \(56\) part-time employees in a company. The mean salary of the full-time employees is \(\$31\,530\) while the mean salary of the part-time employees is \(\$21\,525\). Find the mean salary of these employees of the company.
A. \(\$23\,526\)
B. \(\$25\,527\)
C. \(\$27\,528\)
D. \(\$29\,529\)

Part (a)Find the total salary of each group

The mean salary of a group tells us the salary per person. To combine the two groups we must first recover the total salary of each group.

Step 11MTotal salary of full-time employees

Multiply the number of employees by the mean salary:

\[ \text{Total (full-time)} = 14 \times 31\,530 \] \[ = 441\,420 \]
Hint: Mean = Total ÷ Number, so Total = Mean × Number. This single relationship drives the whole question.
Step 21MTotal salary of part-time employees
\[ \text{Total (part-time)} = 56 \times 21\,525 \] \[ = 1\,205\,400 \]
Hint: The part-time group is \(4\) times larger, so its total salary dominates the final answer — expect the result to be closer to \(\$21\,525\).
Step 31ATotal salary of the company
\[ \text{Grand total} = 441\,420 + 1\,205\,400 = 1\,646\,820 \] \[ \text{Total employees} = 14 + 56 = 70 \]
Total salary \(=\$1\,646\,820\), total employees \(=70\).

Part (b)Compute the combined mean salary

Now divide the grand total salary by the total number of employees. This is the weighted mean of the two group means.

Interactive: Weighted mean vs the two group means
0 / 3
Step 11MSet up the combined mean
\[ \bar{x} = \frac{\text{Total salary}}{\text{Total number of employees}} = \frac{31\,530 \times 14 + 21\,525 \times 56}{14 + 56} \]
Hint: Never average the two means directly as \(\frac{31\,530+21\,525}{2}\) — the groups have different sizes! The sizes act as weights.
Step 21MSimplify the fraction
\[ \bar{x} = \frac{441\,420 + 1\,205\,400}{70} = \frac{1\,646\,820}{70} = 23\,526 \]
Hint: Dividing by \(70\) is like finding the "fair share" per employee across the whole company.
Step 31AConclusion and answer check

The mean salary of the employees of the company is \(\$23\,526\), which matches option A.

As a sanity check, the answer lies between \(21\,525\) and \(31\,530\), and is much closer to \(21\,525\) because part-time employees are the majority.

Answer: A. \(\$23\,526\)

Summary of Answers

  • Total salary (full-time) \(= 14 \times 31\,530 = \$441\,420\)
  • Total salary (part-time) \(= 56 \times 21\,525 = \$1\,205\,400\)
  • Total employees \(= 14 + 56 = 70\)
  • Combined mean \(= \dfrac{441\,420+1\,205\,400}{70} = \dfrac{1\,646\,820}{70}\)
  • Mean salary \(= \$23\,526\)  →  Option A
