Probability: Drawing Cards

Question

A bag contains five cards numbered \(2,2,3,4\) and \(6\) respectively. Two cards are randomly drawn from the bag at the same time. Find the probability that the sum of the numbers drawn is greater than \(7\).

  • A. \(\tfrac12\)
  • B. \(\tfrac15\)
  • C. \(\tfrac25\)
  • D. \(\tfrac{3}{10}\)

Solution

Total
Combinations \(C_{2}^{5}=\dfrac{5\times 4}{2}=10\) Choosing \(2\) cards from \(5\) gives \(10\) equally likely pairs.
\(C(5,2)=10\) equally likely pairs.
Favourable
Counting Favourable Outcomes \(\text{sum}>7\) Only \((2_a,6),(2_b,6),(3,6),(4,6)\) exceed \(7\), i.e. \(4\) pairs.
Pairs with sum \(>7\): \((2_a,6),(2_b,6),(3,6),(4,6)\), i.e. sums \(8,8,9,10\) \(\Rightarrow 4\) pairs.
Probability
Probability of an Event \(P=\dfrac{\text{favourable}}{\text{total}}\) \(\dfrac{4}{10}=\dfrac{2}{5}\).
\(\dfrac{4}{10}=\dfrac25\).
Answer: C — 2/5
