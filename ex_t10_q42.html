Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Similar Solids

T10 Q42

Two similar solid cones have heights $4 \text{ cm}$ and $12 \text{ cm}$. Find the ratio of their corresponding linear dimensions.

Solution

For similar solids, corresponding linear dimensions are in the same ratio as their heights.
Use the given heights: $4:12$.
Simplify the ratio by dividing both terms by $4$: $1:3$.
The ratio of their corresponding linear dimensions is $1:3$.
