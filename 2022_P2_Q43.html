HKDSE Math 2022 Paper 2 Q43 Probability Geometric Series

Question

A box contains 2 white balls, 2 yellow balls and 3 red balls. A boy and a girl take turns to draw one ball randomly from the box with replacement until one of them draws a white ball or a yellow ball. The boy draws a ball first. Find the probability that the girl draws a white ball.

2W · 2Y · 3R (total 7 balls)
A. \(\frac{3}{10}\)
B. \(\frac{3}{20}\)
C. \(\frac{7}{20}\)
D. \(\frac{17}{20}\)

Key Idea

  • Draws are with replacement → independent, probabilities stay the same each turn.
  • The game stops when a white or yellow ball is drawn.
  • A girl wins a round only if the boy draws red (game continues) and then the girl draws white.
  • This repeats with a constant “continue factor” → Geometric Series.

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Understand the stopping rule

Each turn, one ball is drawn and replaced. The game ends as soon as a white or yellow ball is drawn.

So a turn continues only when a red ball is drawn.

\(P(\text{white}) = \frac{2}{7}\) \(P(\text{yellow}) = \frac{2}{7}\) \(P(\text{red}) = \frac{3}{7}\)

Hint: “With replacement” means the denominator stays 7 every turn.

2

Describe the event “girl draws a white ball”

The girl draws on turns 2, 4, 6, … (every even turn). For the girl to draw a white ball:

  • The boy must draw red on all his previous turns (otherwise the game would already have ended).
  • The girl finally draws a white ball on her turn. (Any white, not necessarily the first — but the first white on a girl’s turn ends the game.)

So the girl wins on turn \(2k\) if:

Boy turns \(1,3,\dots,2k-1\) draw red, and girl turn \(2k\) draws white.

3

Probability of winning on the first girl turn (\(k=1\))

Boy draws red (prob \(\frac{3}{7}\)), then girl draws white (prob \(\frac{2}{7}\)).

\[ P_1 = \frac{3}{7} \cdot \frac{2}{7} = \frac{6}{49} \]

Hint: Red is the “continue” ball for the boy; white is the “win” ball for the girl.

4

Probability of winning on the second girl turn (\(k=2\))

Now the boy and girl each draw red on their first turn, then boy draws red again, then girl draws white.

\[ P_2 = \left(\frac{3}{7}\cdot\frac{3}{7}\right) \cdot \frac{3}{7}\cdot\frac{2}{7} = \left(\frac{3}{7}\right)^3 \cdot \frac{2}{7} \]

Three continue events before the winning white.

5

General term on turn \(2k\)

For the girl to win on her \(k\)-th turn, there must be \(2k-1\) red balls in a row (boy \(k\) times, girl \(k-1\) times), then a white ball.

\[ P_k = \left(\frac{3}{7}\right)^{2k-1} \cdot \frac{2}{7} \]

Hint: Each girl turn adds two red draws before the next possible win, so the power increases by 2 each time.

6

Sum the infinite geometric series

Total probability:

\[ P = \frac{3}{7}\cdot\frac{2}{7} + \left(\frac{3}{7}\right)^3\cdot\frac{2}{7} + \left(\frac{3}{7}\right)^5\cdot\frac{2}{7} + \cdots \]

Factor out the first term and identify the ratio:

\[ = \frac{3}{7}\cdot\frac{2}{7} \left[ 1 + \left(\frac{3}{7}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{3}{7}\right)^4 + \cdots \right] \]

It is a geometric series with first term \(1\), common ratio \(r = \left(\frac{3}{7}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{49}\).

\[ S = \frac{1}{1-\frac{9}{49}} = \frac{1}{\frac{40}{49}} = \frac{49}{40} \]
7

Final answer

\[ P = \frac{3}{7}\cdot\frac{2}{7} \cdot \frac{49}{40} = \frac{6}{49}\cdot\frac{49}{40} = \frac{6}{40} = \frac{3}{20} \]

✅ The required probability is \(\displaystyle \frac{3}{20}\).

Correct option: B
