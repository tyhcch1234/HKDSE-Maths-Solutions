2013MC#26 14. Probability

Two numbers are randomly drawn at the same time from seven cards numbered $1,\ 2,\ 3,\ 4,\ 5,\ 6$ and $7$ respectively. Find the probability that the product of the numbers drawn is an odd number.

A. $\dfrac{2}{7}$
B. $\dfrac{4}{7}$
C. $\dfrac{12}{49}$
D. $\dfrac{16}{49}$

Step-by-step Solution

1 List the sample space

Two cards are drawn at the same time (order does not matter), so each outcome is an unordered pair $\{a,b\}$ with $a\ne b$.

$\{1,2\}\ \{1,3\}\ \{1,4\}\ \{1,5\}\ \{1,6\}\ \{1,7\}$
$\{2,3\}\ \{2,4\}\ \{2,5\}\ \{2,6\}\ \{2,7\}$
$\{3,4\}\ \{3,5\}\ \{3,6\}\ \{3,7\}$
$\{4,5\}\ \{4,6\}\ \{4,7\}$
$\{5,6\}\ \{5,7\}$
$\{6,7\}$

Hint: "At the same time" means unordered — do not use $7\times 7$.
2 Count the total number of outcomes

Choosing $2$ cards from $7$:

$${}^{7}C_{2}=\frac{7\times 6}{2}=21$$

(Equivalently: $6+5+4+3+2+1=21$ from the list above.)

Hint: $n(\text{total}) = 21$ is the denominator.
3 Translate the condition

The product of two integers is odd exactly when both numbers are odd.

Odd cards available: $1,\ 3,\ 5,\ 7$  →  $4$ cards.

Even cards: $2,\ 4,\ 6$  →  $3$ cards (any even card makes the product even).

Hint: odd × odd = odd; odd × even = even; even × even = even.
4 Count the favourable outcomes

Favourable pairs = choose $2$ odd cards from $1,3,5,7$:

$\{1,3\}\ \{1,5\}\ \{1,7\}\ \{3,5\}\ \{3,7\}\ \{5,7\}$

$${}^{4}C_{2}=\frac{4\times 3}{2}=6$$

Hint: $n(\text{favourable}) = 6$.
5 Compute the probability

$$P=\frac{n(\text{favourable})}{n(\text{total})}=\frac{6}{21}=\frac{2}{7}$$

Check: $\dfrac{2}{7}\approx0.2857$, matching option A.

Hint: Always simplify the fraction to match the given options.
Answer: A  ·  $\dfrac{2}{7}$
