HKDSE 2015 Paper 2 Q10 Percentages Compound Interest

Compound Interest

A sum of $50 000 is deposited at an interest rate of 6% per annum for 3 years, compounded quarterly. Find the amount correct to the nearest dollar.

A. $59 000
B. $59 551
C. $59 755
D. $59 781
🔑
Key concept: Compound interest formula \[ A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} \] where \(P\) = principal, \(r\) = annual rate, \(n\) = compounding periods per year, \(t\) = years.

🧩 Step-by-step solution

1

Identify the variables

\(P = 50\,000\) (principal)

\(r = 6\% = 0.06\) per annum

\(t = 3\) years

compounded quarterly → \(n = 4\) times per year

💡 Hint: “Quarterly” means interest is added 4 times a year. Each period has rate \(r/n = 0.06/4 = 0.015\) (1.5%).
2

Write the compound interest formula

\[ A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} \]

Substitute \(P=50000\), \(r=0.06\), \(n=4\), \(t=3\).

\[ A = 50000 \times \left(1 + \frac{0.06}{4}\right)^{4 \times 3} \]
3

Simplify the bracket and exponent

\(1 + 0.06/4 = 1 + 0.015 = 1.015\)

Exponent \(nt = 4 \times 3 = 12\)

\[ A = 50000 \times (1.015)^{12} \]
4

Compute the value

\((1.015)^{12} \approx 1.195618171\)

\[ A = 50000 \times 1.195618171 = 59780.90857 \]

(using full calculator precision)

5

Round to nearest dollar

\[ A \approx \$59\,781 \]

Which matches option D.

Step 1 of 5
✓

Correct answer: D ($59 781)

Always check whether interest is compounded yearly, half-yearly, quarterly, or monthly. Adjust \(n\) accordingly and ensure the exponent is \(nt\).
