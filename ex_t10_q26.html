Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q26

A rectangular box has dimensions $3$ m, $4$ m and $2$ m. Find its volume.

Solution

The volume of a rectangular box is given by $V = l \times w \times h$.
Substitute $l=3$, $w=4$ and $h=2$: $V = 3 \times 4 \times 2$.
Calculate: $V = 24$.
Include the unit: $V = 24 \text{ m}^3$.
The volume of the rectangular box is $24 \text{ m}^3$.
