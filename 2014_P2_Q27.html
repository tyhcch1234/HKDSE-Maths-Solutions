Yellow balls ($m$) Black balls (20)

Step 1 — The box holds $m$ yellow balls and 20 black balls.

HKDSE 2014 Paper 2 — Q27

QUESTION

A box contains $m$ yellow balls and 20 black balls. If a ball is randomly drawn from the box, then the probability of drawing a yellow ball is $\dfrac{1}{m}$. Find the value of $m$.

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 15
  4. 25
1Set up the total number of balls.

Number of yellow balls $= m$
Number of black balls $= 20$
Total number of balls in the box $= m + 20$

Always write the total first — probability is the favourable count divided by the total count.
2Write the probability of a yellow ball.

$P(\text{yellow}) = \dfrac{\text{number of yellow balls}}{\text{total number of balls}}$ $= \dfrac{m}{m+20}$

This must equal the given value $\dfrac{1}{m}$.
3Form and simplify the equation.

$\dfrac{m}{m+20} = \dfrac{1}{m}$

Cross-multiply: $m \times m = 1 \times (m+20)$

$m^{2} = m + 20$

Cross-multiplication is allowed since $m \neq 0$ and $m + 20 \neq 0$.
4Solve the quadratic equation.

$m^{2} - m - 20 = 0$

$(m - 5)(m + 4) = 0$

$m = 5$   or   $m = -4$

Factorise by finding two numbers multiplying to $-20$ and adding to $-1$.
5Reject the invalid root.

Since $m$ is the number of yellow balls, $m$ must be a positive integer.

So $m = -4$ is rejected, leaving $m = 5$.

Check: $P(\text{yellow}) = \dfrac{5}{5+20} = \dfrac{5}{25} = \dfrac{1}{5}$ ✓

A count of balls can never be negative — always check that the root is physically reasonable.
Answer: B  ( $m = 5$ )
