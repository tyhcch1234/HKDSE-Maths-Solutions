Probability · Solving for m

Probability Algebra Quadratic Equation

Q Question

A box contains m yellow balls and 20 black balls. If a ball is randomly drawn from the box, then the probability of drawing a yellow ball is \(\dfrac{1}{m}\) . Find the value of m.

A. 4
B. 5
C. 15
D. 25

👀 Visualise the box

Number of yellow balls m can be changed with the slider.

m = 10
Total balls: 30
P(yellow) = \(\frac{10}{30} = 0.333\)
Target \(\frac{1}{m}\): \(\frac{1}{10} = 0.100\)

📝 Step-by-step Solution

1

Set up the probability equation

Probability of drawing a yellow ball = (number of yellow balls) / (total number of balls).

\[ \frac{m}{m + 20} = \frac{1}{m} \]
💡 Hint

Total balls = \(m\) yellow + \(20\) black = \(m + 20\).
The probability is given as \(\frac{1}{m}\).

2

Cross-multiply

Multiply both sides by \(m(m + 20)\) (valid since \(m > 0\) and \(m + 20 > 0\)).

\[ m \cdot m = 1 \cdot (m + 20) \] \[ m^2 = m + 20 \]
💡 Hint

Cross-multiplication: if \(\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}\) then \(a \cdot d = b \cdot c\).

3

Rearrange into a quadratic equation

\[ m^2 - m - 20 = 0 \]
💡 Hint

Subtract \(m + 20\) from both sides.

4

Factorise

\[ (m - 5)(m + 4) = 0 \]

So \(m = 5\) or \(m = -4\).

💡 Hint

Find two numbers that multiply to \(-20\) and add to \(-1\): they are \(-5\) and \(+4\).

5

Reject the invalid value

Since \(m\) represents the number of balls, it must be a positive integer.

Therefore \(m = -4\) is rejected.

\[ m = 5 \]

✓ Correct answer: B. 5

✅ Check your answer

If \(m = 5\), number of yellow balls = 5, black balls = 20, total = 25.
Probability = \(\frac{5}{25} = \frac{1}{5}\). This matches \(\frac{1}{m} = \frac{1}{5}\).
