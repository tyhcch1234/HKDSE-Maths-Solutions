Question

The base of a right pyramid is a rectangle. The length and the breadth of the base are \(18\text{ cm}\) and \(16\text{ cm}\) respectively. The height of the pyramid is \(12\text{ cm}\). The pyramid is divided into a pyramid \(X\) and a frustum \(Y\) by a plane parallel to its base. If the breadth of the base of \(X\) is \(12\text{ cm}\), then the volume of \(Y\) is

A\(486\ \text{cm}^3\)
B\(666\ \text{cm}^3\)
C\(688\ \text{cm}^3\)
D\(944\ \text{cm}^3\)

Rectangular-based pyramid divided by a plane parallel to its base (not to scale).

Solution

Step 1 · Whole pyramid volume

Use the pyramid volume formula:

\[ V=\frac{1}{3}\times(\text{area of base})\times\text{height} =\frac{1}{3}(18)(16)(12)=1152\ \text{cm}^3 \]
Step 2 · Similar pyramids

The cutting plane is parallel to the base, so pyramid \(X\) is similar to the whole pyramid. The linear scale factor is the ratio of the breadths:

\[ \frac{12}{16}=\frac{3}{4} \]
Lengths in similar solids scale by the linear ratio \(k\).
Step 3 · Volume of \(X\)

Volumes scale as the cube of the linear ratio:

\[ V_X=\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{3}\times 1152 =\frac{27}{64}\times 1152 = 486\ \text{cm}^3 \]
Step 4 · Volume of the frustum \(Y\)

The frustum is the whole pyramid minus the top pyramid \(X\):

\[ V_Y = 1152 - 486 = 666\ \text{cm}^3 \]
Always check: \(V_Y\) must equal the whole volume minus \(V_X\).
Final Answer: B · \(666\ \text{cm}^3\)
