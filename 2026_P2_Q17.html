Volume of a Frustum

Question

The base of a right pyramid is a rectangle. The length and the breadth of the base are \(18\) cm and \(16\) cm respectively. The height of the pyramid is \(12\) cm. The pyramid is divided into a pyramid \(X\) and a frustum \(Y\) by a plane parallel to its base. If the breadth of the base of \(X\) is \(12\) cm, then the volume of \(Y\) is

  • A. \(486\) cm\(^3\)
  • B. \(666\) cm\(^3\)
  • C. \(688\) cm\(^3\)
  • D. \(944\) cm\(^3\)

Solution

Volume of the whole pyramid \[ V=\frac{1}{3}\times(\text{area of base})\times\text{height} =\frac{1}{3}(18)(16)(12)=1152\ \text{cm}^3 \]
Pyramid \(X\) is similar to the whole pyramid

The cutting plane is parallel to the base, so \(X\) is a similar pyramid. Its linear scale factor is

\[ \frac{12}{16}=\frac{3}{4} \]

(breadth of \(X\)'s base over breadth of the original base).

Volume of \(X\)

Volumes scale as the cube of the linear ratio:

\[ V_X=\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^3\times1152=\frac{27}{64}\times1152=486\ \text{cm}^3 \]
Volume of the frustum \(Y\) \[ V_Y=1152-486=666\ \text{cm}^3 \]
Final Answer: B
