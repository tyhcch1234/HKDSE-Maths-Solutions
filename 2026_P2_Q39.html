QUESTION

The figure shows the quadrilateral \(STUV\), where \(ST=8\) cm and \(SV=5\) cm. If \(\angle UTV=30^\circ\) and \(\angle TSU=\angle TVU=90^\circ\), find \(\angle SUV\) correct to the nearest degree.

A. \(22^\circ\)
B. \(28^\circ\)
C. \(30^\circ\)
D. \(38^\circ\)
STEP 1 · SET UP COORDINATES

Put \(S=(0,0)\). Since \(ST=8\) and \(\angle TSU=90^\circ\), take \(T=(0,8)\) and \(U=(u,0)\) on the positive \(x\)-axis.

Then \(ST=8\) is the vertical leg and \(SU=u\) is the horizontal leg.

STEP 2 · RIGHT ANGLE AT V

\(\angle TVU=90^\circ\) means \(V\) lies on the circle with diameter \(TU\) (Thales' theorem).

In \(\triangle TUV\), \(\angle UTV=30^\circ\), so \(V\) sits at the \(30^\circ\) position measured from \(T\) along this circle.

STEP 3 · USE \(SV=5\)

Writing \(V\) from the \(30^\circ\) construction and imposing \(|SV|=5\) gives

\[ u=6\sqrt{3},\qquad V=\left(\tfrac{5\sqrt3}{2},\,-\tfrac52\right). \]

So \(SU=6\sqrt3\) cm.

STEP 4 · ANGLE AT U

Vectors from \(U=(6\sqrt3,0)\):

\[ \overrightarrow{US}=(-6\sqrt3,\,0),\qquad \overrightarrow{UV}=\left(-\tfrac{7\sqrt3}{2},\,-\tfrac52\right). \]

\[ \cos\angle SUV=\frac{\overrightarrow{US}\cdot\overrightarrow{UV}}{|US|\,|UV|} =\frac{63}{6\sqrt3\cdot\sqrt{43}}\approx 0.924. \]

STEP 5 · RESULT

\[ \angle SUV\approx 22.4^\circ \approx 22^\circ \text{ (nearest degree)}. \]

Hint: \(\angle TVU=90^\circ\) is the key — it forces \(V\) onto the circle with diameter \(TU\), turning a hard "quadrilateral" problem into two right triangles you can coordinate.
FINAL ANSWER A  —  \(22^\circ\)
