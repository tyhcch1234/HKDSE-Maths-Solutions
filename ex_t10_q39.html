Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Surface Area

T10 Q39

A rectangular box has dimensions $2$ cm, $3$ cm and $6$ cm. Find its total surface area.

Solution

For a rectangular box, total surface area is $2(lw+lh+wh)$.
Substitute $l=2$, $w=3$ and $h=6$: $2(2\times3+2\times6+3\times6)$.
Simplify inside the brackets: $2(6+12+18)=2(36)$.
Calculate: $2\times36=72$.
The total surface area is $72\mathrm{cm}^2$.
