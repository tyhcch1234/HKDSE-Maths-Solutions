Mensuration — Volume of a Frustum of a Right Circular Cone

Similar Solids Volume of Cone Frustum Ratio of Volumes

QQuestion

The height and the base radius of a right circular cone are \(12\text{ cm}\) and \(9\text{ cm}\) respectively. The figure shows a frustum which is made by cutting off the upper part of the circular cone. The height of the frustum is \(8\text{ cm}\). Find the volume of the frustum.

A. \(210\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
B. \(312\pi\text{ cm}^3\) ✓
C. \(324\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
D. \(936\pi\text{ cm}^3\)

Original Figure

aSet Up the GeometryUnderstanding

Original cone (h=12, r=9) Removed upper cone (h=4, r=3) Frustum (h=8)
8 cm
cone height = 12 cm, base radius = 9 cm
Step 1

Whole cone: height \(H = 12\text{ cm}\), base radius \(R = 9\text{ cm}\).

The frustum is the whole cone minus the small upper cone. Since the frustum height is \(8\text{ cm}\), the removed cone has height

\[h = 12 - 8 = 4\text{ cm}\]
Hint: Always subtract heights from the full cone to find the removed cone's height.
Step 2

By similar triangles (or similar solids), the small cone and the large cone have proportional radii:

\[\frac{r}{R} = \frac{h}{H}\] \[\frac{r}{9} = \frac{4}{12} = \frac{1}{3}\]
Hint: The two cones share the same apex angle, so every length scales by the ratio \(h : H\).
Radius of the upper cone: \(r = 3\text{ cm}\)
0 / 2

bCompute the Frustum VolumeSolution

k = 0.333
V(frustum) = V(big) − V(small)
Step 1

Volume of the whole cone:

\[V_{\text{big}} = \frac{1}{3}\pi R^2 H = \frac{1}{3}\pi (9)^2 (12) = 324\pi\text{ cm}^3\]
Hint: Use \(V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h\) where \(r\) is the base radius and \(h\) the height.
Step 2

Volume of the removed upper cone (height \(4\text{ cm}\), radius \(3\text{ cm}\)):

\[V_{\text{small}} = \frac{1}{3}\pi (3)^2 (4) = 12\pi\text{ cm}^3\]
Hint: Notice \(V_{\text{small}} = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^3 V_{\text{big}} = \frac{1}{27} \cdot 324\pi = 12\pi\). Volume scales as \(k^3\).
Step 3

Therefore the frustum volume is:

\[V = V_{\text{big}} - V_{\text{small}} = 324\pi - 12\pi = 312\pi\text{ cm}^3\]
Hint: The answer matches option B.
Volume of the frustum \(= 312\pi\text{ cm}^3\)  (Option B)
0 / 3

✓Summary of Answers

  • Height of the removed upper cone: \(h = 12 - 8 = \mathbf{4\text{ cm}}\)
  • Base radius of the upper cone (similar triangles): \(r = 9 \times \frac{4}{12} = \mathbf{3\text{ cm}}\)
  • Volume of the whole cone: \(\frac{1}{3}\pi(9)^2(12) = \mathbf{324\pi\text{ cm}^3}\)
  • Volume of the upper cone: \(\frac{1}{3}\pi(3)^2(4) = \mathbf{12\pi\text{ cm}^3}\)
  • Volume of the frustum: \(324\pi - 12\pi = \mathbf{312\pi\text{ cm}^3}\)
  • Correct option: B
