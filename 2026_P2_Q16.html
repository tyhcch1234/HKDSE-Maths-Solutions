The perimeter and the area of a sector are \(k\) cm and \(3k\text{ cm}^2\) respectively. If the radius of the sector is \(14\text{ cm}\), then the arc length of the sector is

A \(19\text{ cm}\)
B \(21\text{ cm}\)
C \(28\text{ cm}\)
D \(49\text{ cm}\)
Step 1 · Express the perimeter The perimeter of a sector is made of two radii plus the arc length
Perimeter of a Sector \(P=2r+\text{arc}\) Two radii of \(14\text{ cm}\) plus the arc length give \(k=28+\text{arc}\).
. So \[ k = 2(14) + \text{arc} = 28 + \text{arc}. \]
Step 2 · Express the area
Area of a Sector \(A=\tfrac{1}{2}\times\text{arc}\times r\) With \(r=14\), the given area \(3k\) equals \(7\,\text{arc}\).
The area of a sector is \(\tfrac12 \times \text{arc} \times r\): \[ 3k = \frac{1}{2}\times \text{arc}\times 14 = 7\times \text{arc}. \]
Step 3 · Solve for the arc length
Solving by Substitution \(7\,\text{arc}=3(28+\text{arc})\) This gives \(4\,\text{arc}=84\), so the arc length is \(21\text{ cm}\).
Substitute \(k = 28 + \text{arc}\) into \(3k = 7\,\text{arc}\): \[ 7\,\text{arc} = 3k = 3(28 + \text{arc}) \] \[ 7\,\text{arc} = 84 + 3\,\text{arc} \;\Rightarrow\; 4\,\text{arc} = 84 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{arc} = 21\text{ cm}. \]
Final Answer: B — \(21\text{ cm}\)
