Translation & Perpendicular Vectors

Question

The coordinates of the points \(P\) and \(Q\) are \((-6,5)\) and \((2,1)\) respectively. \(P\) is translated downwards by \(r\) units to \(R\).
(a) Write down, in terms of \(r\), the coordinates of \(R\).
(b) Find \(r\) such that \(\angle PQR=90^{\circ}\). (3 marks)

Solution

PART (a)
A translation downwards by r units keeps the x-coordinate unchanged and decreases the y-coordinate by r: \[ R=(-6,\;5-r) \]
PART (b) — set up perpendicular vectors
\(\angle PQR=90^\circ\) means the vectors QP and QR (tail at Q) are perpendicular. \[ \overrightarrow{QP}=P-Q=(-6-2,\;5-1)=(-8,\;4) \] \[ \overrightarrow{QR}=R-Q=(-6-2,\;(5-r)-1)=(-8,\;4-r) \]
PART (b) — dot product = 0
Perpendicular vectors have zero scalar (dot) product: \[ \overrightarrow{QP}\cdot\overrightarrow{QR}=(-8)(-8)+4(4-r)=0 \] \[ 64+16-4r=0 \implies 80=4r \implies r=20 \]
Check: with \(r=20\), \(R=(-6,-15)\) and \(\overrightarrow{QR}=(-8,-16)\). Then \(\overrightarrow{QP}\cdot\overrightarrow{QR}=64+4(-16)=0\). ✓
(a) \(R=(-6,\;5-r)\)    (b) \(r=20\)
