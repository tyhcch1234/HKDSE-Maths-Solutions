Question. The coordinates of the points \(P\) and \(Q\) are \((-6,\,5)\) and \((2,\,1)\) respectively. \(P\) is translated downwards by \(r\) units to \(R\).

(a) Write down, in terms of \(r\), the coordinates of \(R\).
(b) Find \(r\) such that \(\angle PQR=90^{\circ}\). (3 marks)

STEP 1 · TRANSLATION DOWNWARDS → PART (a)
Translation \((x,y)\to(x,\ y-r)\quad(\text{down }r\text{ units})\) The \(x\)-coordinate stays \(-6\), so \(R=(-6,\,5-r)\).

A translation downwards by \(r\) units leaves the \(x\)-coordinate unchanged and decreases the \(y\)-coordinate by \(r\):

\[ R=(-6,\;5-r) \]
STEP 2 · COMPARE THE GRADIENTS AT Q
Gradient of a Segment \(m=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\) \(QP\) rises \(4\) over a run of \(-8\), so \(m_{QP}=-\tfrac12\); \(QR\) has the same run \(-8\) and rise \(4-r\).

\(\angle PQR=90^{\circ}\) means the segment \(QP\) and the segment \(QR\) are perpendicular,
so the product of their gradients is \(-1\).

From \(Q(2,\,1)\) to \(P(-6,\,5)\) the \(x\)-coordinate changes by \(x_P-x_Q=-6-2=-8\) and the \(y\)-coordinate by \(y_P-y_Q=5-1=4\):

\[ m_{QP}=\frac{y_P-y_Q}{x_P-x_Q}=\frac{5-1}{-6-2}=\frac{4}{-8}=-\frac12 \]

From \(Q(2,\,1)\) to \(R(-6,\,5-r)\) the changes are \(x_R-x_Q=-6-2=-8\) and \(y_R-y_Q=(5-r)-1=4-r\):

\[ m_{QR}=\frac{(5-r)-1}{-6-2}=\frac{4-r}{-8} \]
STEP 3 · SET THE PRODUCT OF GRADIENTS EQUAL TO −1
PERPENDICULAR LINES \(m_1m_2=-1\) \(\dfrac{4-r}{16}=-1\), so \(4-r=-16\) and \(r=20\).

Perpendicular segments satisfy \(m_1m_2=-1\):

\[ \left(-\frac12\right)\left(\frac{4-r}{-8}\right)=\frac{4-r}{16}=-1 \] \[ 4-r=-16 \;\Longrightarrow\; r=20 \]
STEP 4 · CHECK

With \(r=20\), \(R=(-6,\,-15)\), so

\[ m_{QR}=\frac{-15-1}{-6-2}=\frac{-16}{-8}=2,\qquad m_{QP}\,m_{QR}=\left(-\frac12\right)(2)=-1 \;\checkmark \]

Pythagoras confirms the right angle: \(PQ^2=(-8)^2+4^2=80\), \(QR^2=(-8)^2+(-16)^2=320\) and \(PR^2=0^2+(-20)^2=400\),
with \(80+320=400\).

\(QP\) is shallow (gradient \(-\tfrac12\)) and \(QR\) is steep (gradient \(2\)); the diagram shows the right angle at \(Q\).
(a) \(\;R=(-6,\;5-r)\)     (b) \(\;r=20\)

Question. \(P(4,3)\) and \(Q(0,1)\). The point \(P\) is translated \(r\) units downwards to \(R\) such that \(\angle PQR=90^\circ\). Find \(r\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Translation downwards: \(R=(4,\,3-r)\).
Step 2. Gradient of \(QP\): \(m_{QP}=\dfrac{3-1}{4-0}=\dfrac12\).
Step 3. Gradient of \(QR\): \(m_{QR}=\dfrac{(3-r)-1}{4-0}=\dfrac{2-r}{4}\), so \(m_{QP}m_{QR}=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{2-r}{4}=\dfrac{2-r}{8}=-1\).
Step 4. \(2-r=-8\Rightarrow r=10\). Check: \(m_{QR}=\dfrac{2-10}{4}=-2\) and \(\left(\tfrac12\right)(-2)=-1\).
Answer: \(r=10\)
