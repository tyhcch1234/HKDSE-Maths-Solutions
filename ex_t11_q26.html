T11 Q26

In the figure, a tangent touches the circle at a point, and a chord is drawn from that point. If the angle between the tangent and the chord is $38°$, find the angle in the alternate segment.

Solution

The angle between the tangent and the chord is given as $38^\circ$.
By the alternate segment theorem, the angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle in the alternate segment subtended by the chord.
Therefore, the angle in the alternate segment is equal to $38^\circ$.
The angle in the alternate segment is $38^\circ$.
