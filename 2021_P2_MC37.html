HKDSE Math 2021 Paper 2 Q37

Quadratic Inequality

Let $k$ be a constant. Find the range of values of $k$ such that $x^2+kx+k+8\ge0$ for any real number $x$.

A. $-8\le k\le4$
B. $-4\le k\le8$
C. $k\le-8$ or $k\ge4$
D. $k\le-4$ or $k\ge8$

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — Recognise the condition

For a quadratic $ax^2+bx+c$ to be $\ge0$ for all real $x$, the coefficient of $x^2$ must be positive and the discriminant must be $\le0$. Here $a=1>0$, so only the discriminant condition is needed.

Step 2 — Write the discriminant

With $a=1$, $b=k$ and $c=k+8$:

$\Delta=b^2-4ac=k^2-4(1)(k+8)=k^2-4k-32$

Step 3 — Factorise and solve $\Delta\le0$

$k^2-4k-32=(k-8)(k+4)\le0$

The critical values are $k=-4$ and $k=8$. The product is $\le0$ between them:

$-4\le k\le8$

Answer: B. $-4\le k\le8$
