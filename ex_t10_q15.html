Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Area

T10 Q15

A right-angled triangle has legs of length $3$ cm and $4$ cm. Find its area.

Solution

For a right-angled triangle, the two legs are perpendicular, so they can be used as the base and height.
Area formula: $A=\frac{1}{2}bh$.
Substitute $b=3$ cm and $h=4$ cm: $A=\frac{1}{2}(3)(4)$.
Compute: $A=\frac{1}{2}\times 12=6$.
The area of the right-angled triangle is $6$ cm$^2$.
