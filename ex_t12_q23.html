Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Equation of Straight Line

T12 Q23

Find the equation of the vertical line passing through the point $(-2, 3)$.

Solution

A vertical line has an equation of the form $x = c$.
Since the line passes through $(-2, 3)$, the $x$-coordinate is $-2$.
Therefore, $c = -2$, so the equation is $x = -2$.
The equation of the vertical line is $x = -2$.
