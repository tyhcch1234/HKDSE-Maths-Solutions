T11 Q34

In $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle DEF$, $AB=DE$, $AC=DF$, and $\angle BAC=\angle EDF$. Which congruence condition can be used to prove that $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$?

Solution

SAS (two sides and included angle).
