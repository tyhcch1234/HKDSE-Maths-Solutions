Result terms
[2021MC#3]
$$(u+v)(u-v)(u-1)=$$
  • A. $u^3+u^2+uv^2+v^2$
  • B. $u^3+u^2-uv^2+v^2$
  • C. $u^3-u^2+uv^2+v^2$
  • D. $u^3-u^2-uv^2+v^2$
1
Difference of Two Squares \((a+b)(a-b)=a^{2}-b^{2}\) \((u+v)(u-v)=u^{2}-v^{2}\), so the cross term \(uv\) disappears.
Spot the difference of two squares
The first two factors form the pattern $(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2$ with $a=u$, $b=v$: $$(u+v)(u-v)=u^2-v^2$$
Hint: always pair the two factors that match the $(a+b)(a-b)$ identity first — it kills the cross term $uv$.
2Rewrite the whole product
$$(u+v)(u-v)(u-1)=(u^2-v^2)(u-1)$$ Now expand a product of a binomial and a binomial.
Now distribute each term of $(u^2-v^2)$ over $(u-1)$.
3
Distributive Law \(a(b+c)=ab+ac\) \((u^{2}-v^{2})(u-1)=u^{2}(u-1)-v^{2}(u-1)=u^{3}-u^{2}-uv^{2}+v^{2}\).
Distribute $u^2$ and $-v^2$
$$(u^2-v^2)(u-1)=u^2(u-1)\;-\;v^2(u-1)$$ $$=u^3-u^2-uv^2+v^2$$
Watch the sign: $-v^2 \times (-1) = +v^2$.
4Match the terms with the options
$$u^3-u^2-uv^2+v^2$$ Compare with $u^3 \pm u^2 \pm uv^2 \pm v^2$: only option D has $-u^2$ and $-uv^2$ with $+v^2$.
Check the four signs one by one: $+u^3,\ -u^2,\ -uv^2,\ +v^2$.
Answer: D  —  $u^3-u^2-uv^2+v^2$

Question. \((p+q)(p-q)(p+2)=\)
A. \(p^{3}+2p^{2}-pq^{2}+2q^{2}\)    B. \(p^{3}+2p^{2}-pq^{2}-2q^{2}\)    C. \(p^{3}-2p^{2}-pq^{2}-2q^{2}\)    D. \(p^{3}+2p^{2}+pq^{2}+2q^{2}\)  (1 mark)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The first two factors form a difference of two squares: \((p+q)(p-q)=p^{2}-q^{2}\).
Step 2. Subtract over the remaining factor: \((p^{2}-q^{2})(p+2)=p^{2}(p+2)-q^{2}(p+2)\).
Step 3. Expand: \(p^{3}+2p^{2}-pq^{2}-2q^{2}\). The signs are \(+p^{3},\ +2p^{2},\ -pq^{2},\ -2q^{2}\), which is option B.
Answer: B. \(p^{3}+2p^{2}-pq^{2}-2q^{2}\)
