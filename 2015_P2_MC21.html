QQuestion Multiple Choice

The diameters \(AC\) and \(BD\) of the circle \(ABCD\) intersect at the point \(E\). If \(\angle AEB = 90^\circ\) and \(AC = 24\) cm, then the area of \(\triangle AEB\) is
  • A. \(41\text{ cm}^2\)
  • B. \(72\text{ cm}^2\)
  • C. \(144\text{ cm}^2\)
  • D. \(288\text{ cm}^2\)

The four points \(A, B, C, D\) are on the circle. Both chords \(AC\) and \(BD\) pass through the centre \(E\), so each is a diameter of the circle.

1Step 1 — Why \(E\) is the Centre & Find the Radius

Move the slider to change the angle \(\angle AEB\) and watch how the two diameters always cross at the centre \(E\) of the circle.

90°
AC = 24 cm
radius \(AE = BE =\) 12 cm
\(\angle AEB =\) 90°
Step 1

\(AC\) and \(BD\) are diameters of the circle \(ABCD\). Any two diameters of a circle meet at the centre of that circle.

Hint: A diameter is a chord passing through the centre. Since both \(AC\) and \(BD\) are diameters, their intersection point \(E\) must be the centre.
\(\therefore\ E\) is the centre of the circle.
Step 2

Since \(E\) is the centre, each diameter has length twice the radius:

\[ \text{radius} \;=\; \frac{AC}{2} \;=\; \frac{24}{2} \;=\; 12 \text{ cm} \]

So \(AE = BE = 12\) cm (both are radii).

Hint: Radii of the same circle are equal — that is why \(AE = BE = 12\) cm.
\(AE = BE = 12\text{ cm}\)

2Step 2 — Area of the Right-angled \(\triangle AEB\)

\(\angle AEB = 90^\circ\), so \(\triangle AEB\) is right-angled at \(E\), with legs \(AE\) and \(BE\). Drag the slider to see how the area changes when the angle is not \(90^\circ\), and confirm that the maximum area occurs at \(90^\circ\).

90°
\(AE = BE =\) 12 cm
\(\theta =\) 90°
Area \(= \frac{1}{2}(12)(12)\sin\theta =\) 72 cm²
Step 1

The area of a triangle with two sides \(a,b\) and included angle \(\theta\) is

\[ \text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}ab\sin\theta . \]

For \(\triangle AEB\): \(a = AE = 12\), \(b = BE = 12\), \(\theta = \angle AEB = 90^\circ\).

Hint: Because \(\theta = 90^\circ\), the two sides are perpendicular, so \(AE\) and \(BE\) are the base and the perpendicular height.
Step 2

Since \(\sin 90^\circ = 1\),

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Area of } \triangle AEB &= \frac{1}{2}\,AE \cdot BE \cdot \sin 90^\circ \\[4pt] &= \frac{1}{2}(12)(12)(1) \\[4pt] &= 72 \text{ cm}^2 . \end{aligned} \]
Hint: Compare with the slider: at \(90^\circ\) the product \(\sin\theta\) is largest, giving the maximum area \(72\text{ cm}^2\).
\(\text{Area of } \triangle AEB = \mathbf{72 \text{ cm}^2}\)  \(\Rightarrow\) answer: B

✓Summary of the Solution

  • \(AC\) and \(BD\) are diameters, so they intersect at the centre \(E\).
  • Radius \(= \dfrac{AC}{2} = \dfrac{24}{2} = \mathbf{12\text{ cm}}\), so \(AE = BE = 12\text{ cm}\).
  • \(\angle AEB = 90^\circ\) makes \(\triangle AEB\) right-angled at \(E\).
  • Area \(= \dfrac{1}{2}\times AE \times BE = \dfrac{1}{2}(12)(12) = \mathbf{72\text{ cm}^2}\).
Final answer: \(72\text{ cm}^2\)  —  Option B
