Let \(g(x)=(x+1)(x+a)\), where \(a\) is a constant. If \(g(1)=g(2)\), then \(g(-4)=\)

A. \(-4\)     B. \(0\)     C. \(12\)     D. \(24\)

We must first use the condition \(g(1)=g(2)\) to find \(a\), then substitute \(x=-4\).

Summary of Answers

  • From \(g(1)=g(2)\): \(\;2(1+a)=3(2+a)\)
  • Solving: \(\;a=\boxed{-4}\)
  • Function becomes: \(\;g(x)=(x+1)(x-4)\)
  • \(g(-4)=(-4+1)(-4-4)=(-3)(-8)=\boxed{24}\)
  • Final answer: D
