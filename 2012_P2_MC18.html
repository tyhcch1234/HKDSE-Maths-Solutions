Step 1 1M
In the right-angled triangle \(\triangle BCD\), the angle at \(C\) is \(\alpha\) and the hypotenuse is \(BC = \ell\). The side opposite to \(\alpha\) is \(BD\).
Hint: \(\sin\theta = \dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}\). Here the opposite side to \(\angle C\) is \(BD\).
Step 2 1A
\[ \sin\alpha = \frac{BD}{BC} = \frac{BD}{\ell} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad BD = \ell\sin\alpha \]
Hint: Rearranging gives \(BD\) in terms of \(\ell\) and \(\alpha\). This will be the link between the two right-angled triangles.
\(\boxed{BD = \ell\sin\alpha}\)

Step 1 1M
In the right-angled triangle \(\triangle ABD\), the angle at \(B\) is \(\beta\) and the hypotenuse is \(AB\). The side adjacent to \(\beta\) is \(BD\).
Hint: \(\cos\theta = \dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}\). Here adjacent \(= BD\) and hypotenuse \(= AB\).
Step 2 1A
\[ \cos\beta = \frac{BD}{AB} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad AB = \frac{BD}{\cos\beta} \]
Hint: This rearranges the cosine ratio to make \(AB\) the subject.
Step 3 1A
Substitute \(BD = \ell\sin\alpha\) from Part (a): \[ AB = \frac{BD}{\cos\beta} = \frac{\ell\sin\alpha}{\cos\beta} \]
Hint: Combine the two right-angled triangles through the shared side \(BD\). This matches option A.
\(\boxed{AB = \dfrac{\ell\sin\alpha}{\cos\beta}}\)  —  Answer: A
