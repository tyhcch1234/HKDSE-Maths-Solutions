HKDSE 2023 Paper 2 · Q17 · Mensuration

Parallelogram PQRS Diagonals PR, SX Point X on PQ Intersection Y Area 32 cm² / 58 cm²
2023MC#17

In the figure, \(PQRS\) is a parallelogram. Let \(X\) be a point lying on \(PQ\). Denote the point of intersection of \(PR\) and \(SX\) by \(Y\). If the area of \(\Delta PXY\) and the area of the quadrilateral \(QRYX\) are \(32\text{ cm}^2\) and \(58\text{ cm}^2\) respectively, then the area of \(\Delta RSY\) is

  • A. \(40\text{ cm}^2\)
  • B. \(50\text{ cm}^2\)
  • C. \(58\text{ cm}^2\)
  • D. \(72\text{ cm}^2\)
Step 1 · Area of \(\Delta PQR\)

Split \(\Delta PQR\) into \(\Delta PXY\) and quadrilateral \(QRYX\):

\[ \text{Area of }\Delta PQR = 32 + 58 = 90\text{ cm}^2 \]

Tip: The diagonal \(PR\) cuts the parallelogram into two congruent triangles.

Step 2 · Half of the parallelogram

Diagonal \(PR\) bisects the parallelogram, so \(\Delta PQR \cong \Delta RSP\).

\[ \text{Area of }\Delta RSP = \text{Area of }\Delta PQR = 90\text{ cm}^2 \]

Step 3 · Let \(x\) = area of \(\Delta RSY\)

On the \(S\)-side of the parallelogram:

\[ \text{Area of }\Delta PSY = \text{Area of }\Delta RSP - \text{Area of }\Delta RSY = 90 - x \]

Use \(x\) for the unknown area of \(\Delta RSY\).

Step 4 · Common-height ratios

\(\Delta PXY\) and \(\Delta PSY\) share the same height from \(P\) to line \(SX\):

\[ \frac{XY}{SY} = \frac{[\Delta PXY]}{[\Delta PSY]} = \frac{32}{90-x} \]

\(\Delta PSY\) and \(\Delta RSY\) share the same height from \(S\) to line \(PR\):

\[ \frac{PY}{RY} = \frac{[\Delta PSY]}{[\Delta RSY]} = \frac{90-x}{x} \]

Step 5 · Prove \(\Delta PXY \sim \Delta RSY\)

\[ \angle PYX = \angle RYS \quad (\text{vert. opp. } \angle s) \]

\[ \angle PXY = \angle RSY \quad (\text{alt. } \angle s,\ PQ \parallel SR) \]

\[ \angle XPY = \angle SRY \quad (\text{alt. } \angle s,\ PQ \parallel SR) \]

\[ \therefore\ \Delta PXY \sim \Delta RSY \quad \text{(AAA)} \]

Step 6 · Set up the equation

Corresponding sides of similar triangles are proportional:

\[ \frac{XY}{SY} = \frac{PY}{RY} \]

\[ \frac{32}{90-x} = \frac{90-x}{x} \]

\[ 32x = (90-x)^2 \]

\[ 32x = 8100 - 180x + x^2 \]

\[ x^2 - 212x + 8100 = 0 \]

Step 7 · Solve & reject

\[ (x-50)(x-162) = 0 \]

\[ x = 50 \quad \text{or} \quad x = 162 \text{ (rejected)} \]

Reject \(x=162\) because the area of \(\Delta RSY\) must be less than \(90\text{ cm}^2\) (area of \(\Delta RSP\)).

Answer: B. \(50\text{ cm}^2\)
