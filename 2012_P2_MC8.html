In a company, 37.5% of the employees are female. If 60% of the male employees and 80% of the female employees are married, then the percentage of married employees in the company is

A 32.5%
B 45%
C 55%
D 67.5%
1

Let total number of employees be a convenient number

Hint: Choose \( n = 100 \) (or any \( n \)). The percentages are independent of the total number of employees, so we can pick \( n = 100 \) for easy calculation.

Let \( n = 100 \) employees in the company.

2

Find number of male and female employees

Female

37.5% of 100 = 37.5

Male

(100 – 37.5)% = 62.5

Check: \(37.5 + 62.5 = 100\)

3

Compute married employees in each group

Hint: "60% of the male employees are married" means \(0.60 \times\) (number of males). Similarly for females.

Married males

60% × 62.5 = 37.5

Married females

80% × 37.5 = 30

4

5

Final answer

Percentage of married employees = \(\frac{37.5 + 30}{100} \times 100\% = 67.5\%\)

\( \boxed{67.5\%} \)   → option D

This is a weighted average: \((62.5\% \times 60\%) + (37.5\% \times 80\%) = 67.5\%\).

⚠️
Common mistake: Averaging the two percentages directly: \((60 + 80)/2 = 70\%\) — wrong, because the groups have different sizes. Always use a weighted average.
