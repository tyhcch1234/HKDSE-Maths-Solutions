Rate, Ratio and Variations

Joint variation: z varies as the square of x and the cube root of y.

Variation Joint Variation Constant k Substitution
Question

It is given that \(z\) varies as the square of \(x\) and the cube root of \(y\). When \(x = 12\) and \(y = 64\), \(z = 36\). When \(x = 16\) and \(y = 729\), \(z =\)

🎯 What is being asked?
Find the new value of \(z\) when \(x = 16\) and \(y = 729\).
  • A. 108
  • B. 144
  • C. 162
  • D. 216

🧭 Step-by-Step Solution

Tap each step to reveal the reasoning. Try to predict before opening!

1
Translate the sentence into an equation
▶

"\(z\) varies as the square of \(x\) and the cube root of \(y\)" means \(z\) is jointly proportional to \(x^2\) and \(\sqrt[3]{y}\).

\[ z = k\,x^{2}\sqrt[3]{y} \]

where \(k\) is a non-zero constant.

💡 Hint: "Varies as" → direct proportion. Combine all parts with a single constant \(k\).
2
Substitute the first set of values to find k
▶

Given \(x = 12,\; y = 64,\; z = 36\):

\[ 36 = k\,(12)^{2}\sqrt[3]{64} \]

Evaluate the powers first:

\[ 12^{2} = 144, \qquad \sqrt[3]{64} = 4 \]
\[ 36 = k \cdot 144 \cdot 4 = 576k \]
💡 Hint: \(64 = 4^3\), so \(\sqrt[3]{64} = 4\).
3
Solve for the constant k
▶
\[ k = \frac{36}{576} = \frac{1}{16} \]

So the variation formula becomes:

\[ z = \frac{1}{16}\,x^{2}\sqrt[3]{y} \]
💡 Hint: Simplify \(36/576\) by dividing numerator and denominator by 36.
4
Substitute the new values x = 16, y = 729
▶
\[ z = \frac{1}{16}\,(16)^{2}\sqrt[3]{729} \]

Evaluate carefully:

\[ 16^{2} = 256, \qquad \sqrt[3]{729} = 9 \]
\[ z = \frac{1}{16}\cdot 256 \cdot 9 = 16 \cdot 9 = 144 \]
💡 Hint: \(729 = 9^3\), so \(\sqrt[3]{729} = 9\). Also \(\frac{256}{16} = 16\).

📝 Key Takeaways

Phrase Equation
varies as / directly proportional to \(z = kx\)
varies as the square of x \(z = kx^{2}\)
varies as the cube root of y \(z = k\sqrt[3]{y}\)
joint variation (both) \(z = kx^{2}\sqrt[3]{y}\)

⚠️ Common mistake: forgetting to take the cube root of \(y\), or mixing up which power applies to which variable.

✅ Final Answer

\(z = 144\)   →   Option B
