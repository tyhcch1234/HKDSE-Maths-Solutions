Mensuration — Area of a major segment formed by a chord

Question Multiple Choice

The diameter of a circle is \(10\text{ cm}\). The circle is divided into a major segment and a minor segment by a chord of length \(8\text{ cm}\). Find the area of the major segment correct to the nearest \(\text{cm}^2\).
A. \(11\text{ cm}^2\)
B. \(23\text{ cm}^2\)
C. \(55\text{ cm}^2\)
D. \(67\text{ cm}^2\)

Step 1 — Locate the distance from the centre to the chord Geometry

Let \(O\) be the centre, \(A,B\) the chord ends, and \(M\) the mid-point of \(AB\). Since \(OA=OB=r\), the radius \(OM\) bisects the chord at right angles.

Radius \(r\)5.00 cm
Half-chord \(AM\)4.00 cm
Distance \(OM\)3.00 cm
\(\angle AOM\)53.13°
0 / 2
Step 1a

Radius is half the diameter:

\[ r = OA = OB = \frac{10}{2} = 5\text{ cm} \]

Since \(OM \perp AB\) and \(M\) is the mid-point of \(AB\):

\[ AM = \frac{AB}{2} = \frac{8}{2} = 4\text{ cm} \]
Hint: A perpendicular from the centre to a chord always bisects that chord (property of the circle).
Step 1b

In the right-angled triangle \(AOM\) (right angle at \(M\)):

\[ OM = \sqrt{OA^2 - AM^2} = \sqrt{5^2 - 4^2} = \sqrt{9} = 3\text{ cm} \]

And for the half-angle:

\[ \sin \angle AOM = \frac{AM}{OA} = \frac{4}{5} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \angle AOM = \sin^{-1}\frac{4}{5} \approx 53.13^\circ \]
Answer so far: \(OM = 3\text{ cm}\), \(\angle AOM = \sin^{-1}\frac45 \approx 53.13^\circ\)

Step 2 — Angle at the centre & the major sector Angles

The full central angle subtended by chord \(AB\) is \(\angle AOB = 2\angle AOM\). The major sector is the sector on the reflex side of \(AB\), with angle \(360^\circ - \angle AOB\).

\(\angle AOB\)106.26°
Reflex angle253.74°
0 / 1
Step 2

Since \(OM\) is the perpendicular bisector of chord \(AB\), it also bisects \(\angle AOB\):

\[ \angle AOB = 2\,\angle AOM = 2\sin^{-1}\tfrac{4}{5} \approx 106.26^\circ \]

The major segment corresponds to the reflex angle at the centre:

\[ \text{Reflex } \angle AOB = 360^\circ - 2\sin^{-1}\tfrac{4}{5} \approx 253.74^\circ \]
Hint: The chord cuts the circle into two arcs. The longer (major) arc subtends the reflex angle, hence the "major segment" uses that reflex angle.

Step 3

Major sector area (using the reflex angle in degrees):

\[ A_{\text{sector}} = \pi r^2 \times \frac{360^\circ - 2\sin^{-1}\frac45}{360^\circ} = \pi(5)^2 \times \frac{360^\circ - 2\sin^{-1}\frac45}{360^\circ} \]

Triangle \(AOB\) area:

\[ A_{\triangle} = \frac{1}{2} \times AB \times OM = \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 3 = 12\text{ cm}^2 \]

Combine:

\[ A_{\text{major}} = 25\pi \cdot \frac{360^\circ - 2\sin^{-1}\frac45}{360^\circ} + \frac{1}{2}(8)(3) \approx 55.3574 + 12 = 67.3574 \] \[ \boxed{A_{\text{major}} \approx 67\text{ cm}^2} \]
Hint: The major segment is larger than a semicircle. It equals the major sector plus the triangle \(AOB\), because the triangle lies inside the major sector region.
Correct option: D. \(67\text{ cm}^2\)

Summary of Results

  • Radius: \(r = 5\text{ cm}\), half-chord \(AM = 4\text{ cm}\), so \(OM = \sqrt{5^2-4^2}=3\text{ cm}\).
  • Half-angle: \(\angle AOM = \sin^{-1}\frac{4}{5}\); full angle \(\angle AOB = 2\sin^{-1}\frac45\).
  • Reflex angle of the major sector: \(360^\circ - 2\sin^{-1}\frac45\).
  • Major sector area \(\approx 55.36\text{ cm}^2\); triangle \(AOB\) area \(=12\text{ cm}^2\).
  • Major segment \(= 55.36 + 12 \approx 67.36\text{ cm}^2\).
Answer: \(\;67\text{ cm}^2\;\) — Option D
