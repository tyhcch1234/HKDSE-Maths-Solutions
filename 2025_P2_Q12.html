HKDSE 2025 Paper 2 · Q12

Rate, Ratio and Variations

It is given that $z$ varies directly as the cube of $x$ and inversely as the square of $y$. When $x=3$ and $y=6$, $z=3$. When $x=5$ and $y=2$, $z=?$

  • A. 5
  • B. 25
  • C. 125
  • D. 243
1

Translate the words into an equation

$z$ varies directly as the cube of $x$  $\Rightarrow$  factor $x^{3}$ in the numerator.

$z$ varies inversely as the square of $y$  $\Rightarrow$  factor $y^{2}$ in the denominator.

$$z=\frac{kx^{3}}{y^{2}} \qquad (k \text{ is the variation constant})$$

Hint: “directly” → multiply on top; “inversely” → divide (bottom). The single unknown $k$ is fixed for all values.

Direct and Inverse Variation \(z=\dfrac{kx^{3}}{y^{2}}\) The cube of \(x\) sits in the numerator and the square of \(y\) in the denominator.
2

Use the given data to find $k$

Put $x=3$, $y=6$, $z=3$ into the equation:

$$3=\frac{k(3)^{3}}{6^{2}}=\frac{27k}{36}=\frac{3k}{4}$$

$$3\times 4=3k \;\Rightarrow\; k=4$$

Hint: $3^{3}=27$ and $6^{2}=36$; simplify $\dfrac{27}{36}=\dfrac{3}{4}$ first, then solve for $k$.

Finding the Variation Constant \(k=\dfrac{zy^{2}}{x^{3}}\) With \(x=3\), \(y=6\) and \(z=3\): \(k=\dfrac{3\times36}{27}=4\).
3

Write the formula with $k$ known, then substitute

With $k=4$ the relation becomes

$$z=\frac{4x^{3}}{y^{2}}$$

Now $x=5$ and $y=2$:

$$z=\frac{4(5)^{3}}{2^{2}}=\frac{4\times 125}{4}=125$$

Hint: the factor $4$ in the numerator cancels the $2^{2}=4$ in the denominator — a handy shortcut.

4

Check and choose the option

$z=125$, which matches option C.

Quick sanity check: tripling $x$ multiplies $z$ by $3^{3}=27$; the change of $y$ from $6$ to $2$ multiplies $z$ by $\left(\tfrac{6}{2}\right)^{2}=9$. Ratio from $z=3$: $3\times 27 \div 9 = 9$? Careful — this comparison only holds for a fixed constant, so always substitute into $z=\dfrac{4x^3}{y^2}$ directly.

Answer:  C. 125

Question. It is given that \(z\) varies directly as the cube of \(x\) and inversely as the square of \(y\). When \(x=2\) and \(y=3\), \(z=8\). (a) Find the value of \(z\) when \(x=4\) and \(y=6\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Write the relation as \(z=\dfrac{kx^{3}}{y^{2}}\).
Step 2. Substitute \(x=2\), \(y=3\), \(z=8\): \(8=\dfrac{k(8)}{9}\), so \(k=9\).
Step 3. Thus \(z=\dfrac{9x^{3}}{y^{2}}\).
Step 4. When \(x=4\), \(y=6\): \(z=\dfrac{9(64)}{36}=16\).
Answer: \(z=16\)
