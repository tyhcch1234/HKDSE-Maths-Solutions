3-D Trigonometry in a Folded Parallelogram Card

Sine Formula Cosine Formula Parallelogram Angle between Two Planes Perpendicular Foot

Question

Read the whole question carefully before unfolding the steps.

(a) In Figure 3(a), \(ABCD\) is a paper card in the shape of a parallelogram. It is given that \(AB = 60\) cm, \(\angle ABD = 20^\circ\) and \(\angle BAD = 120^\circ\).
Find the length of \(AD\). 2 marks

(b) The paper card in Figure 3(a) is folded along \(BD\) such that the distance between \(A\) and \(C\) is \(40\) cm. (see Figure 3(b)).

  1. Find \(\angle ABC\). 5 marks
  2. Find the angle between the plane \(ABD\) and the plane \(BCD\). included in (b)(ii)

Part (a) — Find \(AD\) Sine Formula

Use the triangle \(ABD\) and the sine formula.

Step 11M

In \(\triangle ABD\), the third angle is

\[ \angle ADB = 180^\circ - 120^\circ - 20^\circ = 40^\circ. \]
Hint: Angles in a triangle add up to \(180^\circ\).
Step 21M

Apply the sine formula to \(\triangle ABD\):

\[ \frac{AD}{\sin \angle ABD} = \frac{AB}{\sin \angle ADB} \] \[ \frac{AD}{\sin 20^\circ} = \frac{60}{\sin 40^\circ} \]
Hint: Pair each side with the angle opposite it. \(AD\) is opposite \(\angle ABD = 20^\circ\), \(AB\) is opposite \(\angle ADB = 40^\circ\).
Step 31A

Solve for \(AD\):

\[ AD = \frac{60 \sin 20^\circ}{\sin 40^\circ} \approx 31.92533317 \text{ cm} \]
\(AD \approx 31.9\) cm (correct to 3 s.f.)
Hint: Keep the full calculator value — it will be reused in part (b)(i).

Part (b)(i) — Find \(\angle ABC\) Cosine Formula

After folding, \(AC = 40\) cm. Use \(\triangle ABC\).

Step 11M

Since \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram, opposite sides are equal:

\[ AB = CD = 60 \text{ cm}, \qquad BC = AD \approx 31.92533317 \text{ cm}. \]
Hint: The fold does not change any edge length of the card.
Step 21M

In \(\triangle ABC\), apply the cosine formula:

\[ \cos \angle ABC = \frac{AB^2 + BC^2 - AC^2}{2(AB)(BC)} \] \[ \cos \angle ABC = \frac{60^2 + (31.92533317)^2 - 40^2}{2(60)(31.92533317)} \]
Hint: The angle \(\angle ABC\) is the one between sides \(AB\) and \(BC\), so it appears in the numerator as \(-AC^2\) (opposite side).
Step 31A

Evaluate:

\[ \cos \angle ABC \approx 0.78705183 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \angle ABC \approx 37.99207534^\circ \]
\(\angle ABC \approx 38.0^\circ\) (correct to 3 s.f.)
Hint: \(AC = 40\) cm is the folded distance, so it is shorter than \(AB + BC\).

Part (b)(ii) — Angle between planes \(ABD\) and \(BCD\) 3-D / Perpendicular Foot

The required angle is the angle between the two triangular halves of the folded card.

Step 11M

In Figure 3(a), \(AP\) produced meets \(CD\) at \(Q\), where \(P\) is the foot of the perpendicular from \(A\) to \(BD\). Note that the required angle is \(\angle APQ\) in Figure 3(b).

Hint: A plane angle between two planes is the angle between the two lines drawn in each plane perpendicular to the common edge \(BD\).
Step 21M

In \(\triangle ABD\), compute \(AP\) and \(DP\):

\[ AP = AD \sin \angle ADP \approx 31.92533317 \sin(180^\circ - 120^\circ - 20^\circ) \approx 20.5212086 \text{ cm} \] \[ DP^2 = AD^2 - AP^2 \approx (31.92533317)^2 - (20.5212086)^2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad DP \approx 24.45622407 \text{ cm} \]
Hint: \(AP \perp BD\), so \(\triangle APD\) is right-angled at \(P\).
Step 31M

Compute \(PQ\) and \(DQ\):

\[ PQ = DP \tan \angle PDQ \approx 24.45622407 \tan 20^\circ \approx 8.901337605 \text{ cm} \] \[ DQ^2 = DP^2 + PQ^2 \approx (24.45622407)^2 + (8.901337605)^2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad DQ \approx 26.02577006 \text{ cm} \]
Hint: \(\angle PDQ = \angle ADB = 40^\circ\)? Careful — here \(\angle PDQ\) is the angle at \(D\) inside \(\triangle DPQ\), which equals \(20^\circ\) because \(PQ /\!/ AP\) after folding preserves right angles in the base plane.
Step 41M

Note that \(\angle ADC = \angle ABC = 37.99207534^\circ\). Apply the cosine formula in \(\triangle ADC\):

\[ AQ^2 = AD^2 + DQ^2 - 2(AD)(DQ)\cos \angle ADC \] \[ AQ^2 \approx (31.92533317)^2 + (26.02577006)^2 - 2(31.92533317)(26.02577006)\cos 37.99207534^\circ \] \[ AQ \approx 19.67076991 \text{ cm} \]
Hint: The fold preserves the length \(DQ\) and \(AD\), but the angle between them changes to \(\angle ADC\).
Step 51A

Finally apply the cosine formula in \(\triangle APQ\):

\[ \cos \angle APQ = \frac{AP^2 + PQ^2 - AQ^2}{2(AP)(PQ)} \] \[ \cos \angle APQ \approx \frac{(20.5212086)^2 + (8.901337605)^2 - (19.67076991)^2}{2(20.5212086)(8.901337605)} \] \[ \angle APQ \approx 71.91411397^\circ \]
The angle between plane \(ABD\) and plane \(BCD\) is \(\approx 71.9^\circ\) (correct to 3 s.f.).
Hint: This is a 3-D angle. Check your calculator is in degree mode.

Summary of Answers

  • (a) \(AD = \dfrac{60\sin 20^\circ}{\sin 40^\circ} \approx \mathbf{31.9\ \text{cm}}\)
  • (b)(i) \(\angle ABC \approx \mathbf{38.0^\circ}\)
  • (b)(ii) Angle between planes \(ABD\) and \(BCD\) \(\approx \mathbf{71.9^\circ}\)
  • Key relations: \(\angle ADB = 40^\circ\), \(BC = AD\), \(AC = 40\) cm, \(AP \perp BD\), \(PQ /\!/ AP\).
