HKDSE Math 2019 Paper 2 Q42

Topic 14 · Probability

Probability Combination Complement Rule

There are 2 green cups, 8 blue cups and 9 red cups in a bag. If 6 cups are randomly drawn from the bag at the same time, find the probability that at least 1 blue cup is drawn.

A

$\frac{31}{57}$

B

$\frac{44}{323}$

C

$\frac{635}{646}$

D

$\frac{968}{969}$

🎨 Visual – What's inside the bag?

2 green

8 blue

9 red

Total = 2 + 8 + 9 = 19 cups

📘 Step‑by‑Step Solution

“At least 1 blue cup” means 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 or ... actually up to 8 blue cups (since we can't draw more blue cups than exist).

Instead of adding all those cases, we use the complement rule:

P(at least 1 blue) = 1 − P(no blue cups)

💡 This is much faster – only one case to compute!

Total cups: $2 + 8 + 9 = 19$.

Number of ways to choose 6 out of 19 (order doesn’t matter):

$$ C^{19}_{6} = 27132 $$

💡 Combination formula: $C^{n}_{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$

If no blue cup is drawn, we must pick all 6 cups from the non‑blue cups.

Number of non‑blue cups: green + red = $2 + 9 = 11$.

Number of ways to choose 6 from 11:

$$ C^{11}_{6} = 462 $$

💡 We are selecting only from the 11 non‑blue cups.

$$ P(\text{0 blue}) = \frac{\text{favourable}}{\text{total}} = \frac{C^{11}_{6}}{C^{19}_{6}} = \frac{462}{27132} $$

Simplify the fraction:

$$ \frac{462 \div 42}{27132 \div 42} = \frac{11}{646} $$

💡 Divide numerator and denominator by 42.

$$ P(\text{at least 1 blue}) = 1 - P(\text{0 blue}) $$

$$ = 1 - \frac{11}{646} = \frac{646 - 11}{646} = \frac{635}{646} $$

Answer: C

💡 Key Insight

When a question says “at least one”, always think of the complement. Calculating P(no blue) is just one simple combination, whereas adding all possibilities (1 blue, 2 blue, …) would take much longer.
