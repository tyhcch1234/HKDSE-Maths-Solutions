2019 Paper 2 · Q42
There are 2 green cups, 8 blue cups and 9 red cups in a bag. If 6 cups are randomly drawn from the bag at the same time, find the probability that at least 1 blue cup is drawn.
A. $\dfrac{31}{57}$
B. $\dfrac{44}{323}$
C. $\dfrac{635}{646}$
D. $\dfrac{968}{969}$

Step-by-step Solution

1Read off the contents of the bag.

Green $=2$,
Blue $=8$, Red $=9$. Total number of cups: \[ 2+8+9 = 19 \] Number of cups that are not blue: \[ 2+9 = 11 \]

Hint: separate “blue” from “non-blue” — the complementary event is much easier here.
2Use the complement: $P(\text{at least 1 blue}) = 1 - P(\text{0 blue})$.
Complement Rule \(P(A^{\prime})=1-P(A)\) "At least 1 blue" and "0 blue" are complementary, so only \(P(0\text{ blue})\) must be found.

“At least 1 blue” and “0 blue” are complementary events,
so \[ P(\text{at least 1 blue}) = 1 - P(\text{0 blue}). \]

Hint: “at least one” almost always suggests the complementary event “none”.
3Count total ways to choose 6 cups from 19.
Combination \(C^{n}_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}\) Drawing 6 cups at the same time ignores order, so the total is \(C^{19}_{6}\).

Order does not matter (drawn at the same time),
so combinations: \[ {}^{19}C_{6} \]

Hint: “drawn at the same time” $\Rightarrow$ use $C^n_r$,
not permutations.
4Count favourable ways with 0 blue cups (all 6 from the 11 non-blue).

Choose 6 cups from the $2+9=11$ non-blue cups: \[ {}^{11}C_{6} \]

Hint: 0 blue means every chosen cup is green or red.
5Compute $P(\text{0 blue})$.
Probability of an Event \(P(E)=\dfrac{n(E)}{n(S)}\) The favourable choices are the \(C^{11}_{6}\) ways of taking all 6 cups from the 11 non-blue cups.

\[ P(\text{0 blue}) = \frac{{}^{11}C_{6}}{{}^{19}C_{6}} = \frac{462}{27132} = \frac{11}{646} \]

Hint: ${}^{11}C_{6}=462$, ${}^{19}C_{6}=27132$;
reduce by dividing by 42.
6Subtract from 1 to get the required probability.

\[ P(\text{at least 1 blue}) = 1 - \frac{11}{646} = \frac{646-11}{646} = \frac{635}{646} \]

Hint: give the fraction a common denominator,
then simplify if possible.
Answer: C. $\dfrac{635}{646}$

Question. A bag contains 3 green cups, 6 blue cups and 9 red cups. If 4 cups are drawn at random at the same time, find the probability that at least 1 blue cup is drawn.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Total number of cups \(=3+6+9=18\);
the number of non-blue cups is \(3+9=12\).
Step 2. \(P(\text{at least 1 blue})=1-P(\text{0 blue})\).
Step 3. Total ways \(=C^{18}_{4}=3060\) and ways with 0 blue \(=C^{12}_{4}=495\).
Step 4. \(P(\text{0 blue})=\dfrac{495}{3060}=\dfrac{11}{68}\), so the required probability is \(1-\dfrac{11}{68}=\dfrac{57}{68}\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{57}{68}\)

Alternative check: $P(\text{at least 1 blue}) = P(1B)+P(2B)+\cdots+P(6B)$ $= \dfrac{{}^{8}C_{1}{}^{11}C_{5}+{}^{8}C_{2}{}^{11}C_{4}+\cdots+{}^{8}C_{6}{}^{11}C_{0}}{{}^{19}C_{6}} = \dfrac{635}{646}$.
