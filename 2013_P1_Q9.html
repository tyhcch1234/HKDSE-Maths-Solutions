Question

The bar chart below shows the distribution of the numbers of family members of the employees of company $D$.

Frequencies read from the chart: $x=1:4$, $x=2:16$, $x=3:20$, $x=4:16$, $x=5:8$, $x=6:4$, $x=7:2$ (total $=70$ employees).

(a) Find the mean, the inter-quartile range and the standard deviation of the above distribution.

(b) An employee leaves company $D$. The number of family members of this employee is $7$. Find the change in the standard deviation of the numbers of family members of the employees of company $D$ due to the leaving of this employee. [5 marks]

Step-by-step solution

Step 1Read the frequencies.
family members $x$1234567
frequency $f$4162016842
Hint: total number of employees $=\sum f = 4+16+20+16+8+4+2 = 70$.
Step 2Find the mean.
$\bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}=\dfrac{1(4)+2(16)+3(20)+4(16)+5(8)+6(4)+7(2)}{70}$
$=\dfrac{4+32+60+64+40+24+14}{70}=\dfrac{238}{70}=3.4$
Hint: multiply each value by its frequency, add, then divide by 70. Note $\bar{x}=3.4$, not $3.5$.
Mean of a Frequency Distribution \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}\) Each value \(x\) is weighted by its frequency \(f\), and here \(\sum f=70\).
Step 3Find the inter-quartile range.
Lower quartile $Q_1$: $70\times 25\% = 17.5$ → 18th value. Cumulative: $4,20,40,\dots$, so $Q_1 = 2$.
Upper quartile $Q_3$: $70\times 75\% = 52.5$ → 53rd value. Cumulative: $4,20,40,56,\dots$, so $Q_3 = 4$.
$\text{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1=4-2=2$
Hint: the $25\%$ and $75\%$ positions fall in the $x=2$ and $x=4$ groups respectively.
Inter-Quartile Range \(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) The \(25\%\) and \(75\%\) positions fall in the \(x=2\) and \(x=4\) groups.
Step 4Find the standard deviation.
$\sum fx^2 = 1^2(4)+2^2(16)+3^2(20)+4^2(16)+5^2(8)+6^2(4)+7^2(2) = 4+64+180+256+200+144+98 = 946$
$\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum fx^2}{\sum f}-\bar{x}^{2}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{946}{70}-(3.4)^{2}}$
$=\sqrt{13.514285\ldots-11.56}=\sqrt{1.954285\ldots}\approx 1.40$
Hint: $\bar{x}=3.4$ so $\bar{x}^2=11.56$, and $\sqrt{1.954285\ldots}=1.39796\ldots$, i.e. $1.40$ (3 s.f.).
Standard Deviation of a Frequency Distribution \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum fx^{2}}{\sum f}-\bar{x}^{2}}\) With \(\sum fx^{2}=946\), \(\sum f=70\) and \(\bar{x}=3.4\), the spread follows directly.
Step 5(b) New statistics after one employee with 7 family members leaves.
New total $=70-1=69$, new $\sum fx = 238-7=231$, new $\sum fx^2 = 946-49=897$.
New mean $\bar{x}'=\dfrac{231}{69}=\dfrac{77}{23}\approx 3.3478$
New $\sigma' = \sqrt{\dfrac{897}{69}-\left(\dfrac{231}{69}\right)^{2}}$
Step 6Compute the change and the answer.
$\sigma' = \sqrt{13-11.207930\ldots}=\sqrt{1.792070\ldots}=1.338679\ldots$
Decrease $=1.397958\ldots-1.338679\ldots=0.059279\ldots$
$\approx 0.0593$ (to 3 significant figures)
Hint: the value 7 lies far above the mean $3.4$, so removing it slightly decreases the spread.
Answers: (a) mean $=3.4$, IQR $=2$, SD $\approx 1.40$; (b) the standard deviation decreases by $0.0593$ (from $1.3980$ to $1.3387$).

Question. The numbers of members in 40 households are summarised as follows: \(x=1,2,3,4,5,6\) with frequencies \(2,6,12,12,6,2\). Find the mean, the inter-quartile range and the standard deviation of the distribution.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(\sum f=2+6+12+12+6+2=40\).
Step 2. \(\sum fx=1(2)+2(6)+3(12)+4(12)+5(6)+6(2)=2+12+36+48+30+12=140\), so \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{140}{40}=3.5\).
Step 3. \(Q_{1}\) is the 10th value (cumulative \(2,8,20,\dots\)), so \(Q_{1}=3\); \(Q_{3}\) is the 30th value, so \(Q_{3}=4\). Hence \(\text{IQR}=4-3=1\).
Step 4. \(\sum fx^{2}=1(2)+4(6)+9(12)+16(12)+25(6)+36(2)=2+24+108+192+150+72=548\).
Step 5. \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{548}{40}-3.5^{2}}=\sqrt{13.7-12.25}=\sqrt{1.45}\approx1.20\).
Answer: mean \(=3.5\), \(\text{IQR}=1\), \(\sigma\approx1.20\)
