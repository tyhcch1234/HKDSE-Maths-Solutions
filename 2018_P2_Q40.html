[2018 MC #40]

It is given that $a$ is a positive constant. The straight line $2x+5y=a$ cuts the $x$-axis and the $y$-axis at the points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. Let $R$ be a point lying on the $y$-axis such that the $x$-coordinate of the orthocentre of $\Delta PQR$ is $10$. Find the $y$-coordinate of $R$.

A. $-25$
B. $-4$
C. $4$
D. $25$
STEP 1 · Find $P$, $Q$ and the slope of $PQ$

On the $x$-axis ($y=0$):  $2x = a \Rightarrow P=\left(\dfrac{a}{2},\,0\right)$.

On the $y$-axis ($x=0$):  $5y = a \Rightarrow Q=\left(0,\,\dfrac{a}{5}\right)$.

Slope of $PQ$:  $\dfrac{\frac{a}{5}-0}{0-\frac{a}{2}} = -\dfrac{2}{5}$.

The line $2x+5y=a$ has slope $-\dfrac{2}{5}$ — confirmed.

STEP 2 · Locate the orthocentre $H$ using the altitude from $P$

Let $H=(h,k)$. Because $R$ and $Q$ both lie on the $y$-axis, side $QR$ is vertical. The altitude from $P$ is therefore horizontal, i.e. $y = $ (y-coordinate of $P$) $= 0$.

Since $H$ lies on this altitude:  $k = 0$.

So $H=(h,0)$, and we are told $h=10$, hence $H=(10,0)$.

Key insight: $QR$ vertical $\Rightarrow$ altitude from $P$ is the horizontal line through $P$.
STEP 3 · Use the altitude from $R$ (perpendicular to $PQ$)

Let $R=(0,\,r)$. Since $H$ is the orthocentre, $RH \perp PQ$.

Slope of $RH$:  $\dfrac{r-0}{0-10} = -\dfrac{r}{10}$.

Perpendicular condition:  $\left(-\dfrac{r}{10}\right)\left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right) = -1$.

STEP 4 · Solve for $r$

$\dfrac{2r}{50} = -1 \Rightarrow \dfrac{r}{25} = -1 \Rightarrow r = -25$.

So $R=(0,\,-25)$, and the $y$-coordinate of $R$ is $-25$.

STEP 5 · Conclusion

The answer is $-25$, matching option A.

Answer: A  ($-25$)
