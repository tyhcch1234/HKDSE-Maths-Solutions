Complex Numbers

Question

If \(\beta\) and \(\dfrac{i^4+2i^3+3i^2+4i}{\beta i-1}\) are real numbers, then \(\beta=\)

  • A. \(-2\)
  • B. \(-1\)
  • C. \(1\)
  • D. \(2\)

Solution

Numerator \(i^4+2i^3+3i^2+4i=1-2i-3+4i=-2+2i\).
Powers of the Imaginary Unit \(i^{2}=-1,\quad i^{3}=-i,\quad i^{4}=1\) Reducing each power gives \(1-2i-2+4i=-1+2i\) for the numerator.
Rationalise \(\dfrac{-2+2i}{-1+\beta i}\cdot\dfrac{-1-\beta i}{-1-\beta i}=\dfrac{(2+2\beta)+(2\beta-2)i}{1+\beta^2}\).
Rationalising a Complex Denominator \(\dfrac{a+bi}{c+\beta i}\cdot\dfrac{c-\beta i}{c-\beta i}=\dfrac{(ac+b\beta)+(bc-a\beta)i}{c^{2}+\beta^{2}}\) Multiplying by the conjugate makes the denominator \(c^{2}+\beta^{2}\) real; the imaginary part of the quotient is then \(\dfrac{bc-a\beta}{c^{2}+\beta^{2}}\).
Imaginary part = 0 \(2\beta-2=0\Rightarrow \beta=1\).
A Complex Number is Real \(\operatorname{Im}(z)=0\iff bc-a\beta=0\) With \(a=-1,\ b=2,\ c=-3\): \(-6+\beta=0\), so \(\beta=6\).
Answer: C — 1

Question. It is given that \(\dfrac{i^{4}+2i^{3}+2i^{2}+4i}{-3+\beta i}\) is a real number, where \(\beta\) is a real constant. Find \(\beta\). (3 marks)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Simplify the numerator using \(i^{2}=-1\), \(i^{3}=-i\), \(i^{4}=1\): \(1-2i-2+4i=-1+2i\).
Step 2. Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate \(-3-\beta i\): the denominator becomes \(9+\beta^{2}\), which is real.
Step 3. Numerator: \((-1+2i)(-3-\beta i)=3+\beta i-6i-2\beta i^{2}=(3+2\beta)+(\beta-6)i\).
Step 4. For the quotient to be real, the imaginary part must vanish: \(\beta-6=0\), so \(\beta=6\).
Step 5. Check: \(\dfrac{-1+2i}{-3+6i}=\dfrac{15}{45}+\dfrac{0}{45}i=\dfrac{1}{3}\), which is real.
Answer: \(\beta=6\)
