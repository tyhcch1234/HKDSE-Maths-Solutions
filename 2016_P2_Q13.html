Question

The cost of flour of brand X is \$42 / kg. If 3 kg of flour of brand X and 2 kg of flour of brand Y are mixed so that the cost of the mixture is \$36 / kg, find the cost of flour of brand Y.

  • A. \$27 / kg
  • B. \$30 / kg
  • C. \$32 / kg
  • D. \$39 / kg

Step-by-step Solution

1Given information.
$3\text{ kg of }X\ (\$42/\text{kg}) \;
+\;
2\text{ kg of }Y\ (\$y/\text{kg})$
Total mass of the mixture $= 3 + 2 = 5$ kg.
2Introduce the unknown.
$\text{Let } y \ (\$/\text{kg}) \text{ be the cost of flour of brand } Y.$
The unknown is the unit price of brand Y.
3Set up the weighted-average equation.
Weighted Average \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{w_{1}x_{1}+w_{2}x_{2}}{w_{1}+w_{2}}\) The cost per kg of the mixture is the total cost of the two brands divided by the total mass \(5\) kg.
$\dfrac{42 \times 3 \;+\; y \times 2}{3 + 2} = 36$
Total cost ÷ total mass = cost per kg of the mixture.
4Simplify the numerator.
Clearing a Denominator \(\dfrac{A}{B}=C\ \Longrightarrow\ A=BC\quad(B\neq 0)\) Multiplying both sides by \(5\) turns \(\dfrac{126+2y}{5}=36\) into \(126+2y=180\).
$\dfrac{126 + 2y}{5} = 36 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 126 + 2y = 180$
Multiply both sides by 5 to clear the fraction.
5Solve for $y$.
$2y = 180 - 126 = 54 \;\Longrightarrow\; y = \dfrac{54}{2} = 27$
Divide both sides by 2.
6Check the answer.
Checking a Solution by Substitution \(\dfrac{42(3)+27(2)}{5}=36\) Substituting \(y=27\) back reproduces the given average of \(\$36\) per kg.
$\dfrac{42(3) + 27(2)}{5} = \dfrac{126 + 54}{5} = \dfrac{180}{5} = 36 \ \checkmark$
The mixture costs exactly $36 per kg, matching the question.
ANSWER Option A — the cost of flour of brand Y is \$27 / kg.
