Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Circle Equation

T12 Q34

Find the equation of the circle with centre \((1,-2)\) and radius \(3\).

Solution

Use the standard form of a circle: $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$, where $(h,k)$ is the centre and $r$ is the radius.
Substitute the centre $(h,k)=(1,-2)$ and radius $r=3$.
This gives $(x-1)^2+(y+(-2))^2=3^2$.
Simplify: $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=9$.
Expand if desired: $x^2-2x+1+y^2+4y+4=9$, so $x^2+y^2-2x+4y-4=0$.
The equation of the circle is $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=9$, or equivalently $x^2+y^2-2x+4y-4=0$.
