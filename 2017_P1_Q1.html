HKDSE 2017 · Paper 1 · Q1 [3 marks] Formulas & Sequences

Question. Make \(y\) the subject of the formula

\[ k=\frac{3x-y}{y}. \]
Step 1
\[ k=\frac{3x-y}{y} \]
Check: \(y\) is in the denominator. Our first goal is to clear the fraction so that \(y\) is no longer stuck underneath.
Step 2
\[ ky=\left(\frac{3x-y}{y}\right)y \quad\Longrightarrow\quad ky=3x-y \]
Hint: Multiply both sides by \(y\). On the right, the \(y\)'s cancel, leaving \(3x-y\). 1M
Step 3
\[ ky+y=3x \]
Hint: Move the term \(-y\) across the equals sign (add \(y\) to both sides), so every term containing \(y\) is now on the left. 1M
Step 4
\[ y(k+1)=3x \]
Hint: Factorise the left-hand side — take out the common factor \(y\). Now \(y\) appears only once.
Step 5
\[ y=\frac{3x}{k+1} \]
Hint: Divide both sides by \((k+1)\). Provided \(k\neq -1\), this is the final answer. 1A
Answer
\[ \boxed{\,y=\frac{3x}{k+1}\,} \]
