Factorize

(a)   $\alpha^{2}+\alpha-6$ ,

(b)   $\alpha^{4}+\alpha^{3}-6\alpha^{2}$ .

STEP 1Identify the type of each expression.

(a) $\alpha^{2}+\alpha-6$ is a quadratic in $\alpha$ with leading coefficient $1$.

(b) $\alpha^{4}+\alpha^{3}-6\alpha^{2}$ is a degree-4 polynomial, but every term contains $\alpha$.

Hint: for (b), look for the highest common factor of the three terms before anything else.

STEP 2 (a)Factorize the quadratic by the cross method.
Cross Method \(\alpha^{2}+(p+q)\alpha+pq=(\alpha+p)(\alpha+q)\) The pair \(+3\) and \(-2\) has sum \(+1\) and product \(-6\), so the quadratic factorises.

We need two numbers whose product is $-6$ and whose sum is $+1$.

The pair is $+3$ and $-2$:

$$\alpha^{2}+\alpha-6=(\alpha+3)(\alpha-2)$$

Check: $3\times(-2)=-6$ ✓ and $3+(-2)=+1$ ✓   (1A)

STEP 3 (b)Take out the common factor $\alpha^{2}$.
Common Factor Law \(ab+ac=a(b+c)\) Every term of \(\alpha^{4}+\alpha^{3}-6\alpha^{2}\) contains \(\alpha^{2}\), so it can be taken out.

Since $\alpha^{4}=\alpha^{2}\cdot\alpha^{2}$, $\alpha^{3}=\alpha^{2}\cdot\alpha$ and $-6\alpha^{2}=-6\cdot\alpha^{2}$:

$$\alpha^{4}+\alpha^{3}-6\alpha^{2}=\alpha^{2}\big(\alpha^{2}+\alpha-6\big)$$

This is the key move — it converts (b) into (a). (1M)

STEP 4 (b)Substitute the result of (a).

$$\alpha^{2}\big(\alpha^{2}+\alpha-6\big)=\alpha^{2}(\alpha+3)(\alpha-2)$$

Apply part (a) inside the bracket and simplify. (1A)

CHECKVerify by expansion.

$\alpha^{2}(\alpha+3)(\alpha-2)=\alpha^{2}(\alpha^{2}+\alpha-6)=\alpha^{4}+\alpha^{3}-6\alpha^{2}$ ✓

A quick expansion confirms no term was lost or gained.

Answer:
(a) $\;(\alpha+3)(\alpha-2)$
(b) $\;\alpha^{2}(\alpha+3)(\alpha-2)$

Question. Factorize
(a) \(\beta^{2}-\beta-12\),
(b) \(\beta^{4}-\beta^{3}-12\beta^{2}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. (a) Two numbers with product \(-12\) and sum \(-1\) are \(-4\) and \(3\), so \(\beta^{2}-\beta-12=(\beta-4)(\beta+3)\).
Step 2. (b) Take out the common factor \(\beta^{2}\): \(\beta^{4}-\beta^{3}-12\beta^{2}=\beta^{2}\bigl(\beta^{2}-\beta-12\bigr)\).
Step 3. (b) Substitute the result of (a): \(\beta^{2}(\beta-4)(\beta+3)\).
Step 4. Check: \(\beta^{2}(\beta^{2}-\beta-12)=\beta^{4}-\beta^{3}-12\beta^{2}\) ✓
Answer: (a) \((\beta-4)(\beta+3)\) (b) \(\beta^{2}(\beta-4)(\beta+3)\)
