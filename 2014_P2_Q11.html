HKDSE 2014 Paper 2 · Q11

Topic 3: Estimation, Approximation and Error

The width and the length of a thin rectangular metal sheet are measured as 8 cm and 10 cm correct to the nearest cm respectively. Let $x\ \mathrm{cm}^2$ be the actual area of the metal sheet. Find the range of values of $x$.

  • A.   $71.25 < x \le 89.25$
  • B.   $79.5 \le x < 80.5$
  • C.   $79.5 < x \le 80.5$
  • D.   $71.25 \le x < 89.25$

1 Interpret the measurement & the error

Maximum Absolute Error \(\text{maximum absolute error}=\dfrac{1}{2}\times\text{unit of measurement}\) “Correct to the nearest cm” has unit \(1\) cm, so the maximum error is \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) cm \(=0.5\) cm.

“Correct to the nearest cm” means the maximum error is $\dfrac{1}{2}\ \mathrm{cm} = 0.5\ \mathrm{cm}$.

So each measured value $M$ represents the interval \[ M - 0.5 \;\le\; \text{actual} \;<\; M + 0.5. \]

Hint: the lower end is included ($\le$) and the upper end is excluded ($<$), because a value of exactly $M+0.5$ would round up to the next centimetre.

2 Write the bounds for width and length

Measurement Interval \(M-\dfrac{u}{2}\le \text{actual} Measured width \(8\) cm gives \(7.5\le w<8.5\), and length \(10\) cm gives \(9.5\le l<10.5\).

Let $w$ cm be the actual width and $l$ cm the actual length.

\[ 7.5 \;\le\; w \;<\; 8.5 \] \[ 9.5 \;\le\; l \;<\; 10.5 \]

Hint: measured width $8 \Rightarrow 8\pm0.5$; measured length $10 \Rightarrow 10\pm0.5$.

3 Relate the area to $w$ and $l$

Area of a Rectangle \(A=lw\) The sheet is a rectangle, so the area \(x\) is the product \(w\times l\) of the two measured quantities.

The sheet is a rectangle, so

\[ x \;=\; w \times l. \]

All quantities are positive, so multiplying the lower ends together gives the smallest possible area, and multiplying the upper ends together gives the largest possible area.

Hint: for positive numbers, the product of intervals keeps the same order — no sign flipping.

4 Multiply the bounds to get the area range

Lower end:

\[ 7.5 \times 9.5 \;=\; 71.25 \]

Upper end:

\[ 8.5 \times 10.5 \;=\; 89.25 \]

Therefore

\[ 71.25 \;\le\; x \;<\; 89.25. \]
Careful! At the lower end, $w=7.5$ and $l=9.5$ are allowed (equality included). At the upper end, $w=8.5$ and $l=10.5$ are not allowed (strict inequality). So the lower bound uses $\le$ and the upper bound uses $<$.

5 Check against the options

Our result $71.25 \le x < 89.25$ matches option D.

Hint: option A flips the inequality signs (a very common trap), and options B / C confuse the area range with a simple ±0.5 range around $80$.

✓ Answer:   D.   $71.25 \;\le\; x \;<\; 89.25$
