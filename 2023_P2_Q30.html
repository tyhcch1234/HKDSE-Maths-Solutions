Visualising the Weighted Mean

Step 1 — Identify the two groups of employees.

Full-time Part-time Overall mean
HKDSE 2023 · Paper 2 · Q30 · Statistics

There are 14 full-time employees and 56 part-time employees in a company. The mean salary of the full-time employees is \$31 530 while the mean salary of the part-time employees is \$21 525. Find the mean salary of these employees of the company.

A. \$23 526 B. \$25 527 C. \$27 528 D. \$29 529

Step-by-step solution

1Read off the given information.

Full-time: number $n_f = 14$, mean salary $\bar{x}_f = 31530$.
Part-time: number $n_p = 56$, mean salary $\bar{x}_p = 21525$.
Total number of employees $= 14 + 56 = 70$.

The mean of a whole population is a weighted mean — you cannot simply average the two means.
2Convert each mean back to a total.

Total salary of full-time employees $= 31530 \times 14 = 441\,420$.
Total salary of part-time employees $= 21525 \times 56 = 1\,205\,400$.

Since $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\text{sum}}{n}$, we have $\text{sum} = \bar{x}\times n$.
3Add the totals.

Total salary of all employees $= 441\,420 + 1\,205\,400 = 1\,646\,820$.

This is the numerator of the combined mean.
4Divide by the total number of employees.

\[ \bar{x} = \frac{31530\cdot 14 + 21525\cdot 56}{14+56} = \frac{1\,646\,820}{70} = 23\,526 \]

Because there are far more part-time employees, the combined mean is pulled towards \$21 525.
Answer: A. \$23 526
