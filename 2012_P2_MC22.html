Angles & Symmetry of a Regular 12-sided Polygon

QuestionMultiple Choice

Which of the following statements about a regular 12-sided polygon are true?
I. Each exterior angle is \(30^\circ\).
II. Each interior angle is \(150^\circ\).
III. The number of axes of reflectional symmetry is 6.
  1. A. I and II only
  2. B. I and III only
  3. C. II and III only
  4. D. I, II and III

Part (a) — Statement I: Each exterior angle is \(30^\circ\)

Drag the slider to change the number of sides \(n\) of a regular polygon and watch the exterior angle change. For a regular polygon, the exterior angles always add up to a full turn.

n = 12
Each exterior angle = 30.0°
Step 11M

Use the key fact: the sum of the exterior angles of any convex polygon is one complete turn.

\[\text{Sum of exterior angles} = 360^\circ\]
Hint: Imagine walking around the polygon — you turn through all the exterior angles once, so the total turn is \(360^\circ\).
Step 21A

In a regular \(n\)-sided polygon all exterior angles are equal, so divide by \(n\). For \(n = 12\):

\[\text{Each exterior angle} = \frac{360^\circ}{12} = 30^\circ\]
Hint: "Regular" means all sides and all angles are equal — that is why we may simply divide by 12.
Statement I is TRUE ✓ — each exterior angle is \(30^\circ\).

Part (b) — Statement II: Each interior angle is \(150^\circ\)

At every vertex, the interior angle and its adjacent exterior angle form a straight line (angles on a straight line). Move the slider to see the pair of angles at the highlighted vertex.

n = 12
Interior = 150.0° , Exterior = 30.0°
Step 11M

At any vertex of a polygon, the interior angle and the adjacent exterior angle lie on a straight line, so they are supplementary:

\[\text{Interior} + \text{Exterior} = 180^\circ\]
Hint: The exterior angle is formed by extending one side; together with the interior angle it makes a straight angle.
Step 21A

From Part (a), the exterior angle of the regular 12-gon is \(30^\circ\). Hence:

\[\text{Each interior angle} = 180^\circ - 30^\circ = 150^\circ\]
Hint: Alternative route: \(\dfrac{(n-2)\times 180^\circ}{n} = \dfrac{10 \times 180^\circ}{12} = 150^\circ\) — both methods agree.
Statement II is TRUE ✓ — each interior angle is \(150^\circ\).

Part (c) — Statement III: Number of axes of reflectional symmetry is 6

Toggle the axes below. A regular \(n\)-gon has an axis of reflectional symmetry through every vertex and through every midpoint of a side — so there are far more than 6 for a 12-gon.

n = 12
Total axes of symmetry = 12
Step 11M

Count the lines of symmetry of a regular polygon systematically. For a regular \(n\)-gon there are two families:

  • Lines through a vertex and the centre (the vertex axes).
  • Lines through the midpoint of a side and the centre (the edge axes).
Hint: If \(n\) is even, the vertex axes and edge axes are distinct lines; if \(n\) is odd, a line through a vertex also passes through the midpoint of the opposite side.
Step 21M

For \(n=12\) (even): there are \(12\) vertex axes and \(12\) edge axes, all distinct. But each family already gives \(n\) lines, so:

\[\text{Total axes of symmetry} = n = 12\]
Hint: In general, a regular \(n\)-gon has exactly \(n\) axes of reflectional symmetry — never \(n/2\).
Step 31A

Statement III claims the number of axes is 6, but we found 12. Therefore:

Statement III is FALSE ✗ — the number of axes of reflectional symmetry is \(12\), not \(6\).
0 / 3

Summary & Answer

  • Statement I: Each exterior angle \(= \dfrac{360^\circ}{12} = 30^\circ\) ✓ TRUE
  • Statement II: Each interior angle \(= 180^\circ - 30^\circ = 150^\circ\) ✓ TRUE
  • Statement III: Number of axes of reflectional symmetry \(= 12 \neq 6\) ✗ FALSE
Only I and II are true → Answer: A
