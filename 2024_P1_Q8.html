Q8 The Question 5 marks

In the figure, \(E\) is the point of intersection of \(AC\) and \(BD\). It is given that \(\angle ACB=\angle ADB=90^\circ\) and \(BC=AD\).

(a) Prove that \(\Delta ABC \cong \Delta BAD\).  (2 marks)

(b) If \(AD=12\text{ cm}\) and \(DE=9\text{ cm}\), find the area of the pentagon \(ABCDE\).  (3 marks)

a Part (a) — Proving \(\Delta ABC \cong \Delta BAD\) 2 marks

AD = 12.0 cm · BC = 12.0 cm
0 / 4 steps
Step 11M

List the given information. Both right angles and one pair of equal legs are given:

\[ \angle ACB=\angle ADB=90^\circ \quad (\text{given}) \] \[ BC=AD \quad (\text{given}) \]
Hint: Tick the boxes on the diagram to highlight each given fact one at a time.
Step 21M

Identify the common side shared by the two triangles:

\[ AB=AB \quad (\text{common side}) \]
Hint: \(AB\) is the hypotenuse of both right-angled triangles — it belongs to the pair.
Step 31A

Combine the three facts. We have a right angle, a hypotenuse and another side in each triangle:

\[ \text{Right angle} + \text{Hypotenuse} + \text{Side} \Rightarrow \text{RHS condition} \]
Hint: RHS = Right angle, Hypotenuse, Side. It only applies to right-angled triangles — exactly our case here.
Step 41A

Conclude the congruence with full reasons:

\[ \Delta ABC \cong \Delta BAD \quad (\text{RHS}) \]
✔ \(\Delta ABC \cong \Delta BAD\)  (RHS)
Hint: The order of letters matters: \(A\leftrightarrow B\), \(B\leftrightarrow A\), \(C\leftrightarrow D\).

b Part (b) — Area of pentagon \(ABCDE\) 3 marks

AE = 15.0 cm · BE = 15.0 cm · CE = 9.0 cm
0 / 4 steps
Step 11M

In the right-angled triangle \(ADE\), apply Pythagoras' theorem (note \(AE\) is the hypotenuse):

\[ AE=\sqrt{AD^2+DE^2}=\sqrt{12^2+9^2}=\sqrt{144+81}=\sqrt{225}=15\text{ cm} \]
Hint: The right angle is at \(D\) because \(AD\perp DB\) (same given right angle \(\angle ADB=90^\circ\)).
Step 21M

From part (a), \(\Delta ABC \cong \Delta BAD\), so corresponding angles are equal:

\[ \angle ABE=\angle BAE \]

Hence \(\Delta ABE\) is isosceles with \(AE=BE\):

\[ BE=AE=15\text{ cm} \]
Hint: Equal angles \(\Rightarrow\) equal opposite sides. \(\angle BAE\) is opposite \(BE\), \(\angle ABE\) is opposite \(AE\).
Step 31M

Also by the congruence, \(AC=BD\). Since \(E\) is the intersection point, the remaining short pieces are equal too:

\[ CE=DE=9\text{ cm} \] \[ BD = BE+DE = 15+9 = 24\text{ cm} \]
Hint: \(AC = AE+CE\) and \(BD = BE+DE\). Once \(AE=BE\) and \(AC=BD\), subtract to get \(CE=DE\).
Step 41A

Split the pentagon along \(BD\) into \(\Delta ABD\) and \(\Delta BCD\), then sum the areas:

\[ \text{Area}=\tfrac12(AD)(BD)+\tfrac12(BC)(CE) \] \[ =\tfrac12(12)(9+15)+\tfrac12(12)(9) \] \[ =144+54=198\text{ cm}^2 \]
✔ Area of pentagon \(ABCDE=198\text{ cm}^2\)
Hint: Both triangles are right-angled: \(\angle ADB=90^\circ\) for \(\Delta ABD\), and \(\angle BCE=90^\circ\) because \(\angle ACB=90^\circ\).

Σ Final Answers at a Glance

  • Part (a): \(\angle ACB=\angle ADB=90^\circ\) (given), \(BC=AD\) (given), \(AB=AB\) (common side) \(\Rightarrow \Delta ABC \cong \Delta BAD\) (RHS)
  • Part (b) — key lengths: \(AE=15\text{ cm}\), \(\;BE=15\text{ cm}\), \(\;CE=DE=9\text{ cm}\), \(\;BD=24\text{ cm}\)
  • Part (b) — answer: Area of pentagon \(ABCDE = \dfrac12(12)(24)+\dfrac12(12)(9)=\mathbf{198\text{ cm}^2}\)
  • Core idea: Congruent right triangles give equal corresponding sides, which converts the pentagon into two right triangles whose areas add up.
