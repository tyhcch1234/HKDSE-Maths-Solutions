Coordinate Geometry — intersection of a circle and a straight line

QUESTION Full Question Multiple Choice

Find the range of values of \(c\) such that the circle \[x^{2}+y^{2}-6x+cy-7=0\] and the straight line \[x-y+9=0\] intersect.

A. \(-56 \le c \le 8\)
B. \(-8 \le c \le 56\)
C. \(c \le -56\) or \(c \ge 8\)
D. \(c \le -8\) or \(c \ge 56\) ✓

PART (a) Solve the line for \(y\) and substitute into the circle

The equation \(x-y+9=0\) is a straight line. Rearranging gives \(y=x+9\), which we can substitute into the circle equation to eliminate one variable.

Step 1
Make \(y\) the subject of the line equation: \[x-y+9=0 \;\Longrightarrow\; y=x+9\]
Hint: Substituting one equation into the other turns the intersection problem into a single-variable quadratic equation.
Step 2
Substitute \(y=x+9\) into \(x^{2}+y^{2}-6x+cy-7=0\): \[x^{2}+(x+9)^{2}-6x+c(x+9)-7=0\] Expand \((x+9)^{2}=x^{2}+18x+81\) and \(c(x+9)=cx+9c\): \[x^{2}+x^{2}+18x+81-6x+cx+9c-7=0\] \[2x^{2}+(18-6+c)x+(81-7+9c)=0\] \[2x^{2}+(c+12)x+(9c+74)=0\]
Hint: Collect like terms carefully. The coefficient of \(x^{2}\) is \(2\), of \(x\) is \(18-6+c\), and the constant term is \(81-7+9c\).
Resulting quadratic: \[2x^{2}+(c+12)x+(9c+74)=0\]
Step 3
For the line to intersect the circle, this quadratic in \(x\) must have at least one real root, i.e. \[\Delta=(c+12)^{2}-4(2)(9c+74)\ge 0\] We will expand and simplify this inequality in Part (b).
Hint: "Intersect" includes both tangent (Δ = 0, one point) and secant (Δ > 0, two points).

PART (b) Expand Δ ≥ 0 and solve the quadratic inequality

Step 1
Expand the discriminant: \[(c+12)^{2}-4(2)(9c+74)\ge 0\] \[c^{2}+24c+144-8(9c+74)\ge 0\] \[c^{2}+24c+144-72c-592\ge 0\] \[c^{2}-48c-448\ge 0\]
Hint: \((c+12)^{2}=c^{2}+24c+144\) and \(4(2)(9c+74)=8(9c+74)=72c+592\).
Step 2
Factorise \(c^{2}-48c-448\). We need two numbers whose product is \(-448\) and sum is \(-48\): \[c^{2}-48c-448=(c+8)(c-56)\] Check: \((-56)\times 8=-448\) and \(-56+8=-48\). ✓
Hint: If factorising is tricky, find the roots using the quadratic formula, then write the factorised form.
Step 3
Solve \((c+8)(c-56)\ge 0\). The critical values are \(c=-8\) and \(c=56\). A product of two factors is non-negative when both are non-negative, or both are non-positive: \[c\le -8 \quad\text{or}\quad c\ge 56\]
Hint: For a "≥ 0" quadratic inequality with the parabola opening upward, take the values outside the two roots.
Answer: \(c\le -8\) or \(c\ge 56\), which corresponds to option D.

Final Answers at a Glance

  • Line form \(y=x+9\)
  • Substitution \(2x^{2}+(c+12)x+(9c+74)=0\)
  • Δ ≥ 0 \((c+12)^{2}-4(2)(9c+74)\ge 0\)
  • Simplified \(c^{2}-48c-448\ge 0\)
  • Factorised \((c+8)(c-56)\ge 0\)
Answer: \(c\le -8\) or \(c\ge 56\)  →  Option D
