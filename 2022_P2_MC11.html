Question

A sum of \$88 000 is deposited at an interest rate of 6% per annum for 4 years, compounded monthly. Find the interest, correct to the nearest dollar.

A. $21 120
B. $23 098
C. $23 803
D. $23 825

Step-by-step solution

\[ A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} \]
  • P = principal (initial deposit)
  • r = annual interest rate (as a decimal)
  • n = number of compounding periods per year
  • t = time in years
  • A = amount after \(t\) years
💡 Hint: "compounded monthly" means interest is added 12 times a year, so \(n = 12\).
  • \(P = 88\,000\)
  • \(r = 6\% = 0.06\)
  • \(n = 12\) (compounded monthly)
  • \(t = 4\) years
💡 Hint: Always convert the percentage to a decimal before substituting.

Monthly interest rate:

\[ \frac{r}{n} = \frac{6\%}{12} = 0.5\% = 0.005 \]

Total number of compounding periods:

\[ nt = 12 \times 4 = 48 \]
\[ A = 88\,000\left(1 + \frac{6\%}{12}\right)^{48} \]

Evaluating:

\[ A \approx 111\,803.04618 \]
\[ \text{Interest} = 88\,000\left[\left(1 + \frac{6\%}{12}\right)^{48} - 1\right] \]
\[ \approx 23\,803.04618 \approx \$23\,803 \]
✅ Correct answer: C. $23 803

$21 120 — uses \(88\,000 \times 6\% \times 4\) (simple interest). ✗

$23 098 — uses a slightly different rounding or \(4\) instead of \(48\). ✗

$23 803 — matches our calculation. ✓

$23 825 — uses \((1 + 0.06/12)^{48}\) incorrectly (e.g. rounded rate). ✗

✏️ Exam tip

The distractor $21 120 is the simple-interest value. Always check whether the question says “compounded monthly / quarterly / half-yearly” — this changes both the rate per period and the number of periods.
