Question
For $0^\circ<\theta\le 360^\circ$, how many roots does the equation $$4\cos^{2}\theta-3\cos\theta-1=0$$ have?
  • A. 3
  • B. 4
  • C. 5
  • D. 6
Step 1 · Factorise the quadratic in cos θ Treat $4\cos^2\theta-3\cos\theta-1=0$ as a quadratic in $\cos\theta$: $$4\cos^{2}\theta-3\cos\theta-1=0$$ $$(4\cos\theta+1)(\cos\theta-1)=0$$
Try $\cos\theta=1$: $4-3-1=0$ ✓ — so $(\cos\theta-1)$ is a factor.
Zero Product Property \(ab=0\iff a=0\text{ or }b=0\) After factorising the quadratic in \(\cos heta\), each factor can be set to zero.
Step 2 · Solve each factor $$4\cos\theta+1=0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \cos\theta=-\tfrac14$$ $$\cos\theta-1=0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \cos\theta=1$$
Step 3 · Count roots of cos θ = 1 Within $0^\circ<\theta\le 360^\circ$: $\cos\theta=1$ gives $$\theta=360^\circ \quad(\text{1 root; } \theta=0^\circ \text{ excluded})$$
Periodicity of Trigonometric Functions \(\cos(\theta+360^\circ)=\cos\theta\) Add or subtract \(360^\circ\) to collect every solution inside the required interval.
Step 4 · Count roots of cos θ = −1/4 Since $\cos\theta<0$, $\theta$ lies in Quadrants II and III.
In Quadrant II: $\theta=180^\circ-75.5^\circ\approx 104.5^\circ$ → $\theta\approx 105^\circ$
In Quadrant III: $\theta=180^\circ+75.5^\circ\approx 255.5^\circ$ → $\theta\approx 256^\circ$
Two more roots — one in each of the two quadrants where cosine is negative.
Quadrant Rule (CAST) \(\cos\theta<0\Rightarrow\theta\text{ lies in Q II or Q III}\) The sign of the trigonometric ratio decides which two quadrants contain the solutions.
Step 5 · Total number of roots $$105^\circ,\ 256^\circ,\ 360^\circ$$ Total $= 1 + 2 = 3$ roots.
Answer: A. 3
θ = 105°, 256°, 360°

Question. Solve \(2\sin^2\theta-\sin\theta-1=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).

Show the worked solution
1. Factorise: \(2\sin^2\theta-\sin\theta-1=(2\sin\theta+1)(\sin\theta-1)=0\).
2. So \(\sin\theta=-\dfrac{1}{2}\) or \(\sin\theta=1\).
3. \(\sin\theta=1\) gives \(\theta=90^\circ\).
4. \(\sin\theta=-\frac{1}{2}\) with \(\theta\) in Q III or Q IV gives \(210^\circ\) and \(330^\circ\).
5. Hence \(\theta=90^\circ,\ 210^\circ,\ 330^\circ\).
Answer: \(\theta=90^\circ,\ 210^\circ,\ 330^\circ\)
