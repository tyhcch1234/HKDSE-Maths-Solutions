Coordinate Geometry — the locus of a point equidistant from two fixed points

Distance Formula Locus Equation of a Circle Perpendicular Bisector

Question

Multiple-choice question from HKDSE 2021 Paper 2, Question 25.

The coordinates of the points \(M\) and \(N\) are \((5,7)\) and \((6,8)\) respectively. Let \(P\) be a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that \(PM = MN\). Find the equation of the locus of \(P\).
A. \(x - y + 2 = 0\)
B. \(x + y - 13 = 0\)
C. \(x^{2} + y^{2} - 10x - 14y + 72 = 0\)
D. \(x^{2} + y^{2} - 12x - 16y + 98 = 0\)

Part (a) — Interpret the condition \(PM = MN\)

Let \(P=(u,v)\). Start by translating the geometric condition into algebra.

115°
\(MN = \) 1.414 \(PM = \) 1.414 \(P = (4.44, 8.28)\)
Step 11M

Let the moving point be \(P=(u,v)\). The condition of the locus is

\[ PM = MN. \]
Hint: Squaring both sides removes the square roots and gives an equivalent condition, since both distances are non-negative.
Step 21M

Compute the fixed distance \(MN\):

\[ MN^{2} = (6-5)^{2} + (8-7)^{2} = 1^{2}+1^{2} = 2, \qquad MN=\sqrt{2}. \]
Hint: \(MN\) is a constant, so \(PM = MN\) means \(P\) is always the same distance \(\sqrt{2}\) from the fixed point \(M\) — a circle!
Answer: \(MN = \sqrt{2}\), so the locus of \(P\) is a circle with centre \(M(5,7)\) and radius \(\sqrt{2}\).

Part (b)(i) — Set up the equation of the locus

Write the distance condition in terms of \(u\) and \(v\).

200°
Condition: \(PM = MN\) Centre \(M(5,7)\), radius \(r=\sqrt{2}\approx 1.414\)
Step 11M

The condition \(PM = MN\) can be written using the distance formula. Squaring both sides:

\[ PM^{2} = MN^{2}. \]
Hint: \(PM^{2} = (u-5)^{2}+(v-7)^{2}\), while \(MN^{2}=2\) from Part (a).
Step 21A

Substitute the coordinates of \(M(5,7)\) and \(N(6,8)\):

\[ (u-5)^{2}+(v-7)^{2} = (6-5)^{2}+(8-7)^{2}. \] \[ (5-u)^{2}+(7-v)^{2} = 2. \]
Hint: Either order \((u-5)^2 = (5-u)^2\) gives the same value — the textbook solution uses \((5-u)^2+(7-v)^2=2\).
Equation so far: \((u-5)^2 + (v-7)^2 = 2\).

Part (b)(ii) — Expand and simplify to the general form

Expand the squares and collect terms to match one of the options.

Step 11M

Expand each squared bracket:

\[ (25 - 10u + u^{2}) + (49 - 14v + v^{2}) = 2. \]
Hint: \((5-u)^2 = 25-10u+u^2\) and \((7-v)^2 = 49-14v+v^2\).
Step 21A

Collect like terms and bring everything to one side:

\[ 25 + 49 - 2 - 10u - 14v + u^{2} + v^{2} = 0, \] \[ u^{2} + v^{2} - 10u - 14v + 72 = 0. \]
Hint: Compare with the options. Option C is exactly \(x^{2}+y^{2}-10x-14y+72=0\).
Answer: The equation of the locus of \(P\) is \[ x^{2}+y^{2}-10x-14y+72=0. \] This is option C.

Summary of Answers

  • Fixed length: \(MN = \sqrt{2}\), so \(PM^{2}=MN^{2}=2\).
  • Locus condition: \((u-5)^{2}+(v-7)^{2}=2\) — a circle of centre \(M(5,7)\) and radius \(\sqrt{2}\).
  • Expanded locus: \(x^{2}+y^{2}-10x-14y+72=0\).
  • Correct option: C.
