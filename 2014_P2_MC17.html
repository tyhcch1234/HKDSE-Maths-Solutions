Mensuration — Area of a Trapezium via Similar Triangles

Similar Triangles Area Ratio Parallel Lines Trapezium Area

Question Multiple Choice

In the figure, \(B\) is a point lying on \(AC\) such that \(AB : BC = 3 : 2\). It is given that \(AE /\!/ BD\). If the area of \(\triangle BCD\) and the area of \(\triangle CDE\) are \(4\text{ cm}^2\) and \(8\text{ cm}^2\) respectively, then the area of the trapezium \(ABDE\) is

A. \(18\text{ cm}^2\)
B. \(21\text{ cm}^2\)
C. \(24\text{ cm}^2\)
D. \(33\text{ cm}^2\)

Part (a) — Similar Triangles & Area Ratio Key Move

Produce \(CD\) to meet \(AE\) at \(H\). Drag the slider to see how the extended line creates the similar triangles \(\triangle ACH\) and \(\triangle BCD\).

\(AB:BC = 3:2\) → \(AC:BC =\) 5:2
Area ratio \([ACH]:[BCD] =\) 25:4
\([ACH] =\) 4 cm²
Step 11MProduce \(CD\) to meet \(AE\) at \(H\)

Extend line \(CD\) until it intersects line \(AE\) at point \(H\). Now \(AE /\!/ BD\) and \(AH\) is a transversal.

Hint: Since \(AE /\!/ BD\) (corresponding angles), \(\angle CAH = \angle CBD\) and \(\angle CHA = \angle CDB\).
Step 21AEstablish \(\triangle ACH \sim \triangle BCD\) (AAA)

Also, \(\angle ACH = \angle BCD\) (common angle). Therefore \(\triangle ACH \sim \triangle BCD\) by AAA similarity.

Hint: Identify all three pairs of equal angles — two from parallel lines \(AE /\!/ BD\), one common angle at \(C\).
Step 31MFind the area ratio

Since \(AB:BC = 3:2\), we have \(AC:BC = 5:2\). For similar figures, the ratio of areas equals the square of the ratio of corresponding sides.

\[\frac{[ACH]}{[BCD]} = \left(\frac{AC}{BC}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{5}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{25}{4}\]
Hint: Do not use the linear ratio directly on areas — always square it first.
Step 41ACompute \([ACH]\)
\[[ACH] = 4 \times \frac{25}{4} = 25\text{ cm}^2\]
Answer: The area of \(\triangle ACH\) is \(25\text{ cm}^2\).
0 / 4

Part (b) — Find the Area of \(\triangle DHE\) Segment Ratio

Because \(HC:DC = AC:BC = 5:2\), we get \(HD:DC = 3:2\). Triangles \(DHE\) and \(CDE\) share the same height from \(E\), so their areas are in the ratio \(HD:DC\).

\(HD:DC =\) 3:2
\([DHE] : [CDE] =\) 3:2
\([DHE] =\) 12 cm²
Step 11MFind the ratio \(HD:DC\)

From step (a), \(HC:DC = AC:BC = 5:2\) (corresponding sides of similar triangles). Therefore

\[HC - DC = 5k - 2k = 3k \quad\Rightarrow\quad HD:DC = 3:2\]
Hint: \(HD = HC - DC\). Write the sides in parts using a common multiplier \(k\).
Step 21MSame height → area ratio equals base ratio

Since \(\triangle DHE\) and \(\triangle CDE\) share the same perpendicular height from vertex \(E\) to the line \(HC\),

\[\frac{[DHE]}{[CDE]} = \frac{HD}{DC} = \frac{3}{2}\]
Hint: When two triangles share the same height, their areas are proportional to their bases.
Step 31ACompute \([DHE]\)
\[[DHE] = 8 \times \frac{3}{2} = 12\text{ cm}^2\]
Answer: The area of \(\triangle DHE\) is \(12\text{ cm}^2\).
0 / 3

Part (c) — Area of Trapezium \(ABDE\) Final Answer

The trapezium \(ABDE\) can be split into \(\triangle BDH\) (part of \(\triangle ACH\) minus \(\triangle BCD\)) plus \(\triangle DHE\).

\([ABDH] = [ACH] - [BCD] = 25 - 4 =\) 21 cm²
\([ABDE] = [ABDH] + [DHE] = 21 + 12 =\) 33 cm²
Step 11MDecompose the trapezium

Note that quadrilateral \(ABDH\) together with \(\triangle DHE\) forms the trapezium \(ABDE\):

\[[ABDE] = [ABDH] + [DHE]\]
Hint: \(H\) lies on line \(AE\), so \(AH\) and \(DE\) together form the boundary. \(ABDH\) is the part of \(\triangle ACH\) left after removing \(\triangle BCD\).
Step 21MFind \([ABDH]\)
\[[ABDH] = [ACH] - [BCD] = 25 - 4 = 21\text{ cm}^2\]
Hint: Subtract the smaller triangle from the larger similar triangle.
Step 31AAdd the two parts
\[[ABDE] = 21 + 12 = 33\text{ cm}^2\]
Answer: The area of trapezium \(ABDE\) is \(33\text{ cm}^2\) → Option D.

Summary of Answers

  • \(AB:BC = 3:2 \;\Rightarrow\; AC:BC = 5:2\)
  • \(\triangle ACH \sim \triangle BCD\) (AAA) \(\Rightarrow \dfrac{[ACH]}{[BCD]} = \left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{25}{4}\)
  • \([ACH] = 4 \times \dfrac{25}{4} = 25\text{ cm}^2\)
  • \(HD:DC = 3:2 \;\Rightarrow\; [DHE] = 8 \times \dfrac{3}{2} = 12\text{ cm}^2\)
  • \([ABDH] = 25 - 4 = 21\text{ cm}^2\)
  • \([ABDE] = 21 + 12 = 33\text{ cm}^2\) → Answer: D
