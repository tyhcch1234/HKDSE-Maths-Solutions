The diameters $AC$ and $BD$ of the circle $ABCD$ intersect at the point $E$. If $\angle AEB = 90^\circ$ and $AC = 24$ cm, then the area of $\triangle AEB$ is
A. 41 cm²
B. 72 cm²
C. 144 cm²
D. 288 cm²
Step 1 · Diameters meet at the centre
Diameters Pass through the Centre \(AC,\ BD\ \text{are diameters}\ \Longrightarrow\ AC\cap BD=O\) Both diameters pass through the centre, so their intersection \(E\) is the centre.
Every diameter of a circle passes through the centre,
so the two diameters $AC$ and $BD$ meet at the centre.
Since they intersect at $E$, $$\therefore E \text{ is the centre of the circle.}$$
Step 2 · Find the radius
Radius and Diameter \(r=\dfrac{d}{2}\) The diameter \(AC=24\ \text{cm}\) gives a radius of \(12\ \text{cm}\).
$AC$ is a diameter of length $24$ cm, so the radius is $$\text{radius} = AC \div 2 = 24 \div 2 = 12 \text{ cm}.$$
Step 3 · A, E, B are radius lengths $A$ and $B$ lie on the circle and $E$ is the centre,
so $$AE = EB = 12 \text{ cm}.$$
Step 4 · Area of ΔAEB
Area of a Triangle \(\text{Area}=\tfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C\) With \(AE=EB=12\) and \(\angle AEB=90^{\circ}\), the area is \(\tfrac{1}{2}(12)(12)\).
The legs $AE$ and $EB$ are perpendicular ($\angle AEB = 90^\circ$): $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\cdot AE \cdot EB = \frac{1}{2}(12)(12) = 72 \text{ cm}^2.$$
Answer: B  ·  72 cm²

Question. The diameters \(AC\) and \(BD\) of a circle intersect at \(E\). If \(\angle AEB=90^{\circ}\) and \(AC=20\ \text{cm}\), find the area of \(\triangle AEB\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Both \(AC\) and \(BD\) are diameters, so they meet at the centre: \(E\) is the centre of the circle.
Step 2. The radius is \(AC\div2=20\div2=10\ \text{cm}\).
Step 3. \(A\) and \(B\) lie on the circle, so \(AE=EB=10\ \text{cm}\).
Step 4. \(\text{Area}=\tfrac{1}{2}\times AE\times EB=\tfrac{1}{2}(10)(10)=50\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Answer: \(50\ \text{cm}^{2}\)
Key idea: a diameter always passes through the centre ⇒ $AE=EB=$ radius $=12$ cm. With the right angle at $E$, $\triangle AEB$ is a right-angled triangle with legs of 12 cm.
