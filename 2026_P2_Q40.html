Right Prism \(ABCDEF\) — Angle Between Planes

HKDSE 2026 · Paper 2 · MC40

Interactive Figure

Question
The figure shows the right prism \(ABCDEF\), where \(AB:AC:AE=1:1:2\). Let \(\theta\) be the angle between the plane \(ABC\) and the plane \(BCE\). If \(\angle BAC=90^\circ\), find \(\sin\theta\).
A. \(\tfrac13\)
B. \(\tfrac23\)
C. \(\tfrac{\sqrt2}{4}\)
D. \(\tfrac{2\sqrt2}{3}\)
Step 1 · Scale the prism

Take \(AB=1,\ AC=1,\ AE=2\). The right prism has vertical edge \(AE\) standing on the base \(BCFE\).

Key idea: because the ratio is \(1:1:2\), we can freely choose the unit length.
Step 2 · Assign coordinates

Put the base plane \(z=0\), with \(A\), \(B\), \(C\) on the top face \(z=2\):

\[A=(0,0,2),\quad B=(1,0,2),\quad C=(0,1,2),\quad E=(0,0,0).\]

Step 3 · Normal to plane \(ABC\)

The plane \(ABC\) is the top face (horizontal), so

\[n_1=(0,0,1).\]

Step 4 · Normal to plane \(BCE\)

Two vectors in plane \(BCE\):

\[\overrightarrow{BC}=(-1,1,0),\quad \overrightarrow{BE}=(-1,0,-2).\]

\[n_2=\overrightarrow{BC}\times\overrightarrow{BE}=(-2,-2,1).\]

Step 5 · Compute the angle

\[\cos\theta=\frac{|n_1\cdot n_2|}{|n_1|\,|n_2|} =\frac{1}{1\cdot\sqrt{4+4+1}}=\frac13.\]

\[\sin\theta=\sqrt{1-\tfrac19}=\sqrt{\tfrac89}=\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}.\]

Answer D — \(\dfrac{2\sqrt2}{3}\)
