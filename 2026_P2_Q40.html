Angle Between Planes

Question

The figure shows the right prism \(ABCDEF\), where \(AB:AC:AE=1:1:2\). Let \(\theta\) be the angle between the plane \(ABC\) and the plane \(BCE\). If \(\angle BAC=90^\circ\), find \(\sin\theta\).

  • A. \(\tfrac13\)
  • B. \(\tfrac23\)
  • C. \(\tfrac{\sqrt2}{4}\)
  • D. \(\tfrac{2\sqrt2}{3}\)

Original Figure

Solution

Scale Let \(AB=1,\ AC=1,\ AE=2\) (vertical edge below \(A\)).
Coordinates \(A=(0,0,2),\ B=(1,0,2),\ C=(0,1,2),\ E=(0,0,0)\).
Normal to ABC \(n_1=(0,0,1)\).
Normal to BCE \(BC=(-1,1,0),\ BE=(-1,0,-2)\Rightarrow n_2=BC\times BE=(-2,-2,1)\).
Angle \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{|n_1\cdot n_2|}{|n_1||n_2|}=\dfrac{1}{3}\Rightarrow \sin\theta=\sqrt{1-\tfrac19}=\dfrac{2\sqrt2}{3}\).
Answer: D — 2√2/3
