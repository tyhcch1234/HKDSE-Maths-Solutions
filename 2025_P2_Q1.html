[2025MC#1]  Question 1

Simplify:

\[\frac{(27x)^5}{(3x^{-2})^4}=\;?\]

  • A. $3^{2}x^{3}$
  • B. $3^{4}x^{3}$
  • C. $3^{11}x^{13}$
  • D. $3^{14}x^{13}$
1Rewrite 27 and the denominator as powers
Converting to a Prime Base \(27=3^{3},\qquad x^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}\) Both \(27\) and the denominator are rewritten with base \(3\) before the indices are combined.

\[27 = 3^{3},\qquad x^{-2}=\frac{1}{x^{2}}\] \[\frac{(27x)^5}{(3x^{-2})^4}=\frac{(3^{3}x)^{5}}{(3x^{-2})^{4}}\]

Hint: always convert numbers into a common prime base first.

2Apply the power law $(ab)^n=a^{n}b^{n}$
Power of a Product \((ab)^{n}=a^{n}b^{n}\) \((3^{3}x)^{5}=3^{15}x^{5}\) and \((3x^{-2})^{4}=3^{4}x^{-8}\).

\[(3^{3}x)^{5}=3^{15}x^{5},\qquad (3x^{-2})^{4}=3^{4}x^{-8}\]

Hint: multiply the exponents — the power applies to every factor inside the bracket.

3Combine the two fractions
Quotient Law of Indices \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\) Subtracting gives \(3^{15-4}=3^{11}\) and \(x^{5-(-8)}=x^{13}\).

\[=\frac{3^{15}x^{5}}{3^{4}x^{-8}} =3^{15-4}\,x^{5-(-8)} =3^{11}x^{13}\]

Hint: $\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}$ — divide the 3-parts and the x-parts separately.

4Check the exponents and match the option

Exponent of 3: $15-4=11$  
(blue)
Exponent of $x$: $5-(-8)=5+8=13$  
(red)

Hint: subtracting a negative exponent means adding.

Answer \[\frac{(27x)^5}{(3x^{-2})^4}=3^{11}x^{13}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \textbf{C}\]

Question. Simplify \(\dfrac{\left(8x^{3}\right)^{2}}{\left(2x^{-1}\right)^{3}}\), expressing the answer with positive indices.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(8=2^{3}\), so \(\left(8x^{3}\right)^{2}=\left(2^{3}x^{3}\right)^{2}=2^{6}x^{6}=64x^{6}\).
Step 2. \(\left(2x^{-1}\right)^{3}=2^{3}x^{-3}=8x^{-3}\).
Step 3. \(\dfrac{64x^{6}}{8x^{-3}}=2^{6-3}x^{6-(-3)}=2^{3}x^{9}\).
Step 4. So the answer is \(8x^{9}\).
Answer: \(8x^{9}\)
