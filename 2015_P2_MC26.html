Coordinate Geometry — Equation of a Circle from its Centre and a Point
Circle Equation Centre & Radius Distance Formula Expanding Squares

Q26 The Question Multiple Choice

A circle \(C\) passes through the point \((0,3)\). If the coordinates of the centre of \(C\) are \((-4,3)\), then the equation of \(C\) is

A. \(x^2+y^2-8x+6y+9=0\)
B. \(x^2+y^2-8x+6y+16=0\)
C. \(x^2+y^2+8x-6y+9=0\) ✓
D. \(x^2+y^2+8x-6y+16=0\)

Step 1 Find the radius \(r\) from the centre and a point on the circle

The circle is completely determined by its centre \((-4,3)\) and the fact that it passes through \(P(0,3)\). The radius is simply the distance from the centre to \(P\).

Step 1

Use the distance formula between the centre \(O(-4,3)\) and the point \(P(0,3)\):

\[ r = \sqrt{(0-(-4))^2 + (3-3)^2} \]
Hint: The two \(y\)-coordinates are equal (\(3\) and \(3\)), so the vertical difference is \(0\). The radius is the horizontal distance between \(x=-4\) and \(x=0\).
Step 2

Evaluate:

\[ r = \sqrt{4^2 + 0^2} = \sqrt{16} = 4 \]
Hint: A common slip is to compute \(\sqrt{(0-(-4))^2+(3-3)^2}\) as \(16\). Remember: you must take the square root, so \(r=4\), not \(16\).
The radius of \(C\) is \(\;r=4\).
0 / 2

Step 2 Write the standard form and expand it

The standard (centre–radius) form of a circle with centre \((h,k)\) and radius \(r\) is

\[ (x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2. \]
Step 1

Substitute \(h=-4,\; k=3,\; r=4\) into the standard form:

\[ (x-(-4))^2+(y-3)^2=4^2 \] \[ \Rightarrow\quad (x+4)^2+(y-3)^2=16 \]
Hint: Remember \((x-h)^2=(x-(-4))^2=(x+4)^2\). Dropping the minus sign here is the most common error and leads to options A or B.
Step 2

Expand the two squares:

\[ (x^2+8x+16)+(y^2-6y+9)=16 \] \[ x^2+y^2+8x-6y+16+9-16=0 \]
Hint: Expand \((x+4)^2=x^2+8x+16\) and \((y-3)^2=y^2-6y+9\). Do not forget to move the \(16\) on the right-hand side across the equals sign.
Step 3

Simplify the constant terms: \(16+9-16=9\).

\[ \boxed{\,x^2+y^2+8x-6y+9=0\,} \]
Hint: Check the signs: the coefficient of \(x\) equals \(-2h=+8\) and the coefficient of \(y\) equals \(-2k=-6\). Option C matches exactly.
The equation of \(C\) is \(\;x^2+y^2+8x-6y+9=0\).  (Answer C)
0 / 3

Summary of Answers

  • Centre of \(C\): \(O(-4,\,3)\)
  • Point on \(C\): \(P(0,\,3)\)
  • Radius: \(r=\sqrt{(0+4)^2+(3-3)^2}=4\)
  • Standard form: \((x+4)^2+(y-3)^2=16\)
  • Expanded form: \(x^2+y^2+8x-6y+9=0\)
  • Correct option: C
