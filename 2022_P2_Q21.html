QUESTION 21

In the figure, $O$ is the centre of the circle $ABC$. If $\angle ABO = 36^\circ$ and $\angle BOC = 164^\circ$, then $\angle ACO =$

A. $41^\circ$
B. $46^\circ$
C. $52^\circ$
D. $64^\circ$
💡 Hint: $OA=OB=OC$ (radii). So $\triangle ABO$ and $\triangle ACO$ are isosceles, and the angle at the centre $\angle BOC = 2\,\angle BAC$.
STEP 1 · Mark the radii & equal sides

Join $OA$, $OB$, $OC$. All three are radii, so $$OA = OB = OC .$$

This gives two isosceles triangles sharing the vertex $A$.

STEP 2 · Isosceles $\triangle ABO$

In $\triangle ABO$, $OA = OB$ (radii), so base angles are equal:

$$\angle BAO = \angle ABO = 36^\circ .$$

STEP 3 · Let $\angle ACO = x$

In $\triangle ACO$, $OA = OC$ (radii), so again base angles are equal:

$$\angle CAO = \angle ACO = x .$$

STEP 4 · Angle at the centre = twice angle at circumference

$\angle BOC$ is the angle at the centre subtended by arc $BC$, and $\angle BAC$ is the angle at the circumference standing on the same arc:

$$\angle BOC = 2\,\angle BAC .$$

But $\angle BAC = \angle BAO + \angle OAC = 36^\circ + x$.

STEP 5 · Solve for $x$

$$164^\circ = 2(36^\circ + x)$$

$$82^\circ = 36^\circ + x$$

$$x = 82^\circ - 36^\circ = 46^\circ .$$

STEP 6 · Alternative check (angle sum of $ABOC$)

$\angle BAC = 164^\circ \div 2 = 82^\circ$.

Reflex $\angle BOC = 360^\circ - 164^\circ = 196^\circ$ (angles at a point).

In quadrilateral $ABOC$ (angle sum of polygon):

$$\angle ACO = 360^\circ - 82^\circ - 36^\circ - 196^\circ = 46^\circ .$$

Same answer — consistent ✓

Answer: $\angle ACO = 46^\circ$  →  option B.
