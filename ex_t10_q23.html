Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q23

A cylinder has a base radius of $7$ cm and a height of $11$ cm. Find its volume, taking $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.

Solution

The volume of a cylinder is $V=\pi r^2 h$.
Substitute $r=7$, $h=11$ and $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$: $V=\frac{22}{7}\times 7^2\times 11$.
Simplify: $V=\frac{22}{7}\times 49\times 11=22\times 7\times 11=1694$.
The volume of the cylinder is $1694\text{ cm}^3$.
