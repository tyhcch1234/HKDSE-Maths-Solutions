HKDSE 2024 · Paper 2 · Q8

Let $g(x)=(x+1)(x+a)$, where $a$ is a constant. If $g(1)=g(2)$, then $g(-4)=$

A. $-4$ B. $0$ C. $12$ D. $24$
1 Write down what is given. $g(x)=(x+1)(x+a)$, and $a$ is a constant.
The condition is $g(1)=g(2)$.
Hint: two unknowns appear ( $x$ and $a$ ),
but $g(1)=g(2)$ gives one equation in $a$ alone.
2
Substitution into a Function \(g(1)=(1+2)(1+a),\qquad g(4)=(4+2)(4+a)\) Each substitution turns the condition \(g(1)=g(2)\) type statement into an equation in \(a\) alone.
Substitute $x=1$ and $x=2$. $$g(1)=(1+1)(1+a)=2(1+a)$$ $$g(2)=(2+1)(2+a)=3(2+a)$$
Careful: the factor $(2+1)=3$ is what makes the two sides unequal in size.
3
Solving a Linear Equation \(A(1+a)=B(1+b)\ \text{type}\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{expand and collect }a\) Expanding both sides and collecting the \(a\)-terms isolates the unknown constant.
Solve the linear equation for $a$. $$2(1+a)=3(2+a)$$ $$2+2a=6+3a$$ $$2-6=3a-2a$$ $$a=-4$$
Check: $g(1)=2(1-4)=-6$ and $g(2)=3(2-4)=-6$. ✔
4 Now compute $g(-4)$. With $a=-4$,   $g(x)=(x+1)(x-4)$. $$g(-4)=(-4+1)\big[-4+(-4)\big]=(-3)(-8)=24$$
Evaluate the bracket $-4+(-4)=-8$ first,
then multiply.
Answer: D. 24

Question. Let \(g(x)=(x+2)(x+a)\), where \(a\) is a constant. If \(g(2)=g(4)\), find \(g(-3)\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(g(2)=(2+2)(2+a)=4(2+a)\) and \(g(4)=(4+2)(4+a)=6(4+a)\).
Step 2. The condition \(g(2)=g(4)\) gives \(8+4a=24+6a\).
Step 3. Collect terms: \(8-24=6a-4a\), so \(-16=2a\) and \(a=-8\).
Step 4. With \(a=-8\), \(g(x)=(x+2)(x-8)\), so \(g(-3)=(-3+2)(-3-8)=(-1)(-11)=11\).
Step 5. Check the condition: \(g(2)=4(-6)=-24\) and \(g(4)=6(-4)=-24\) ✓.
Answer: \(g(-3)=11\)
