Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q27

A cylinder has a base radius of 4 cm and a height of 10 cm. Find its volume in terms of $\pi$.

Solution

The volume of a cylinder is given by $V=\pi r^2 h$.
Substitute $r=4$ and $h=10$: $V=\pi(4)^2(10)$.
Simplify: $V=\pi(16)(10)=160\pi$.
Include the unit: $160\pi\text{ cm}^3$.
The volume of the cylinder is $160\pi\text{ cm}^3$.
