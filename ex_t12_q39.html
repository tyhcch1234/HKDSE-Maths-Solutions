Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Circle Equation

T12 Q39

A circle has the equation $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y = 0$. Find its radius.

Solution

Complete the square for $x$: $x^2-6x=(x-3)^2-9$.
Complete the square for $y$: $y^2+8y=(y+4)^2-16$.
Substitute into the equation: $(x-3)^2-9+(y+4)^2-16=0$.
Rearrange: $(x-3)^2+(y+4)^2=25$.
Compare with $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$, so $r^2=25$.
Thus $r=\sqrt{25}=5$.
The radius of the circle is $5$.
