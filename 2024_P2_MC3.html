HKDSE Math 2024 Paper 2 · Question 3

Change of Subject of a Formula

Topic 5 — Formulas and Sequences

QQuestion

If \[ k=\frac{5}{2m}+n, \] then \(m=\)
A.\(\dfrac{5}{2(k-n)}\)
B.\(\dfrac{5}{2(n-k)}\)
C.\(\dfrac{2(k-n)}{5}\)
D.\(\dfrac{2(n-k)}{5}\)
Change of Subject Algebraic Manipulation Reciprocals Linear Equation

1Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1 · Identify the target

The subject of the formula is \(k\), but we want to make \(m\) the subject. So we must "free" the \(m\) that is trapped in the denominator.

\[ k=\frac{5}{2m}+n \]
Idea: Treat the whole term \(\dfrac{5}{2m}\) as one unknown block. Just like \(x+n=k\), we remove \(n\) first by subtracting it from both sides.
Step 2 · Isolate the fraction

Subtract \(n\) from both sides to leave the fraction alone on the right.

\[ k-n=\frac{5}{2m} \]
Careful: \(k-n\) and \(n-k\) are opposite in sign. Here we get exactly \(k-n\), not \(n-k\). Keep this in mind when checking the options.
Step 3 · Flip both sides (reciprocal)

Because \(m\) sits in the denominator of the right-hand side, taking the reciprocal of both sides moves the \(2m\) up to the numerator.

\[ \frac{1}{k-n}=\frac{2m}{5} \]
Principle: If \(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d}\), then \(\dfrac{b}{a}=\dfrac{d}{c}\) (provided \(a,c\neq 0\)). Flipping is allowed only when each side is a single fraction — that is why Step 2 must come first.
Step 4 · Multiply by 5 and divide by 2

Multiply both sides by \(5\), then divide both sides by \(2\).

\[ 2m=\frac{5}{k-n} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad m=\frac{5}{2(k-n)} \]
Watch the brackets! When \(2\) is placed in the denominator, both \(k\) and \(n\) must be inside the bracket: \[ m=\frac{5}{2(k-n)}\ \neq\ \frac{5}{2k-n} \]
Step 5 · Compare with the options

Our result is \[ m=\frac{5}{2(k-n)} \] which matches option A.

Common trap: option B, \(\dfrac{5}{2(n-k)}\), looks almost identical but the bracket is \((n-k)\), the negative of our answer. Substituting numbers (e.g. \(k=4,\ n=1\)) shows A gives \(m=5/6\) while B gives \(m=-5/6\).
