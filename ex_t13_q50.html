Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Applications

T13 Q50

A vertical tower stands on horizontal ground. From a point 30 m away from the base of the tower, the angle of elevation to the top of the tower is $45^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.

Solution

Let the height of the tower be $h$ metres.
In the right-angled triangle formed, $\tan 45^\circ = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}} = \frac{h}{30}$.
Since $\tan 45^\circ = 1$, we have $1 = \frac{h}{30}$.
Thus $h = 30$.
The height of the tower is $30\text{ m}$.
