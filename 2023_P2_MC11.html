Q Question

The marked price of a jacket is 60% above its cost. A profit of $104 is made by selling the jacket at a discount of 25% on its marked price. Find the cost of the jacket.

A $416
B $520
C $728
D $832

S Step-by-Step Solution

Let \( C \) be the cost of the jacket.

💡 Hint: We are asked to find the cost, so making it the variable is the natural first move.

The marked price is 60% above the cost.

So, marked price = \( C \times (1 + 60\%) = 1.6C \).

💡 Hint: “60% above” means the cost plus 60% of the cost, i.e. multiply by \( 1 + 0.6 = 1.6 \). Avoid the common mistake of using \( 0.6C \) only.

A 25% discount is applied to the marked price.

Selling price = \( 1.6C \times (1 - 25\%) = 1.6C \times 0.75 = 1.2C \).

💡 Hint: A 25% discount means you pay 75% of the marked price, so multiply by \( 0.75 \).

Profit = Selling price − Cost = 104.

\( 1.2C - C = 104 \)

\( 0.2C = 104 \)

💡 Hint: Profit is always selling price minus cost. Since selling price > cost, the profit is positive.

\( 0.2C = 104 \)

\( C = \dfrac{104}{0.2} = \dfrac{104 \times 10}{2} = 520 \)

✅ Answer: \( C = \$520 \) (Option B)

📝 Quick Recap

  • Let the cost be the variable.
  • Marked price = cost × (1 + 60%) = 1.6 × cost.
  • Selling price = marked price × (1 − 25%) = 0.75 × marked price.
  • Profit = selling price − cost = 104.
  • Solve the linear equation to find the cost.
