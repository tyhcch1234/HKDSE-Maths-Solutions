HKDSE 2017 Paper 1 — Question 4

There are only two kinds of admission tickets for a theatre: regular tickets and concessionary tickets. The prices of a regular ticket and a concessionary ticket are \(\$126\) and \(\$78\) respectively. On a certain day, the number of regular tickets sold is 5 times the number of concessionary tickets sold and the sum of money for the admission tickets sold is \(\$50\,976\). Find the total number of admission tickets sold that day. [4 marks]

1

Define the unknown 1A

Let \(x\) be the number of concessionary tickets sold that day.

Since the number of regular tickets sold is \(5\) times that number,

\[\text{regular tickets} = 5x.\]

Hint: choose the smaller quantity as \(x\) so the other becomes a tidy multiple.
2

Form the money equation 1M + 1A

Total Value = Price x Quantity \(\text{total money}=\sum(\text{price of one item})\times(\text{number of items})\) The regular tickets bring \(5x\times126\) dollars and the concessionary ones \(x\times78\) dollars, adding to \(\$50\,976\).

Money from regular tickets \(= 5x \times 126\), and money from concessionary tickets \(= x \times 78\).

The total money is \(\$50\,976\), so

\[5x(126) + x(78) = 50\,976.\]

Hint: each term is (number of tickets) × (price of one ticket).
3

Solve for \(x\)

Solving a Linear Equation \(ax=b \;\Longrightarrow\; x=\dfrac{b}{a}\quad(a\neq0)\) Collecting terms gives \(708x=50\,976\), so \(x=\dfrac{50\,976}{708}=72\).

\[630x + 78x = 50\,976\]

\[708x = 50\,976\]

\[x = \frac{50\,976}{708} = 72\]

Hint: combine like terms first, then divide.
4

Find the total number of tickets 1A

Total of Two Groups \(\text{total}=5x+x=6x\) The question asks for regular plus concessionary tickets, i.e. \(6\times72=432\), not just \(x\).

Total tickets \(=\) regular \(+\) concessionary

\[= 5x + x = 6x\]

\[= 6(72) = 432\]

Hint: the question asks for the total, not just \(x\). Don't stop at 72!
Answer: The total number of admission tickets sold that day is 432.

Question. There are only two kinds of tickets for a concert: adult tickets and student tickets. The prices of an adult ticket and a student ticket are \(\$95\) and \(\$60\) respectively. On a certain day, the number of adult tickets sold is \(3\) times the number of student tickets sold and the sum of money for the tickets sold is \(\$41\,400\). Find the total number of tickets sold that day. [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \(x\) be the number of student tickets sold. Then the number of adult tickets is \(3x\).
Step 2. Money from adult tickets \(=3x\times95\) and from student tickets \(=x\times60\), so \(3x(95)+x(60)=41\,400\).
Step 3. \(285x+60x=41\,400\), i.e. \(345x=41\,400\), so \(x=\dfrac{41\,400}{345}=120\).
Step 4. Total number of tickets \(=3x+x=4x=4(120)=480\).
Answer: \(480\) tickets
