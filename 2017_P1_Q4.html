Regular & Concessionary Tickets

Forming & solving a linear equation in one unknown · 4 marks

Equations Linear Equation Word Problem Setting up Unknowns

Question

4 marks

There are only two kinds of admission tickets for a theatre: regular tickets and concessionary tickets. The prices of a regular ticket and a concessionary ticket are $126 and $78 respectively. On a certain day, the number of regular tickets sold is 5 times the number of concessionary tickets sold and the sum of money for the admission tickets sold is $50 976. Find the total number of admission tickets sold that day.

Step-by-Step Guided Solution

Start with the smaller / base quantity — the number of concessionary tickets. Let it be \(x\).

Let \(x\) be the number of concessionary tickets sold that day.
Why pick this one? Because the question says the regular tickets sold are 5 times the concessionary tickets. Choosing the base quantity as \(x\) means the other quantity becomes a simple multiple, \(5x\) — no fractions involved.

"The number of regular tickets sold is 5 times the number of concessionary tickets sold."

Number of regular tickets \(= 5x\)

✅ This line alone usually earns 1A (1 accuracy mark) in the marking scheme.

Use the relationship: total money = (price × quantity) for each type, added together.

\(\underbrace{5x\,(126)}_{\text{regular money}} + \underbrace{x\,(78)}_{\text{concessionary money}} = 50\,976\)
Formula in general form: \[ \text{Regular price}\times(\text{no. of regular}) + \text{Concessionary price}\times(\text{no. of concessionary}) = \text{Total money} \] Here: \(126 \times 5x \;+\; 78 \times x \;=\; 50\,976\). The numbers \(126\) and \(78\) are the ticket prices; \(5x\) and \(x\) are the numbers of tickets.

✅ Correctly forming this equation usually earns 1M + 1A.

Multiply out the brackets, combine like terms, then divide.

\(630x + 78x = 50\,976\)
\(708x = 50\,976\)
\(x = \dfrac{50\,976}{708} = 72\)
Arithmetic check: \(5x(126) = 630x\) and \(x(78)=78x\), so \(630x+78x=708x\). Then \(50\,976 \div 708\): since \(700 \times 72 = 50\,400\) and \(8 \times 72 = 576\), the sum is \(50\,976\). ✔

Be careful! The question asks for the total, not just \(x\).

Total \(= \underbrace{5x}_{\text{regular}} + \underbrace{x}_{\text{concessionary}} = 6x\)
\(= 6(72) = 432\)
Common trap: Many students stop at \(x=72\) and lose the last mark ("1A" here). Always re-read what the question actually asks for — here, the total number of admission tickets sold. Since regular : concessionary \(= 5:1\), the total is \(5+1=6\) parts \(= 6x\).
Thus, the total number of admission tickets sold that day is 432.
Marking scheme recap: 1A for stating \(5x\), 1M+1A for the equation, 1A for the final answer 432. Always finish with a sentence that echoes the question.
