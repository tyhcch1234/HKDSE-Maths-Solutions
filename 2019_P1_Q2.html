Simplify algebraic fractions Polynomials

Simplify \(\dfrac{3}{7x-6}-\dfrac{2}{5x-4}\).

[3 marks]

1Read off the two denominators.

The two fractions have different denominators: \[ \frac{3}{\,7x-6\,}\quad\text{and}\quad\frac{2}{\,5x-4\,} \] Since \(7x-6\) and \(5x-4\) are not equal and have no common factor, we cannot subtract them directly.

Hint: two fractions can only be added/subtracted at once when the denominators are the same.

2Build the common denominator (LCM).

The lowest common multiple of \((7x-6)\) and \((5x-4)\) is their product: \[ \text{LCM}=(7x-6)(5x-4) \] Multiply the first fraction by \(\dfrac{5x-4}{5x-4}\) and the second by \(\dfrac{7x-6}{7x-6}\): \[ \frac{3}{7x-6}-\frac{2}{5x-4} =\frac{3(5x-4)}{(7x-6)(5x-4)}-\frac{2(7x-6)}{(7x-6)(5x-4)} \]

Hint: multiplying numerator and denominator by the same expression does not change the value of a fraction.

3Combine into a single fraction and expand the numerators.

\[ =\frac{3(5x-4)-2(7x-6)}{(7x-6)(5x-4)} =\frac{15x-12-14x+12}{(7x-6)(5x-4)} \] Note the sign: subtract the whole numerator \(2(7x-6)\), so both terms change sign.

Hint: \(3(5x-4)=15x-12\) and \(-2(7x-6)=-14x+12\).

4Simplify the numerator.

\[ \frac{15x-12-14x+12}{(7x-6)(5x-4)} =\frac{(15x-14x)+(-12+12)}{(7x-6)(5x-4)} =\frac{x}{(7x-6)(5x-4)} \] The constants \(-12\) and \(+12\) cancel completely, leaving \(x\).

Hint: collect like terms: \(15x-14x=x\) and \(-12+12=0\).

5Check and state the answer.

The numerator \(x\) and the denominator \((7x-6)(5x-4)\) share no common factor (the numerator does not contain the factors \(7x-6\) or \(5x-4\)), so the fraction is already in simplest form.

Answer: \[ \boxed{\;\frac{x}{(7x-6)(5x-4)}\;} \] (equivalently \(\dfrac{x}{35x^{2}-58x+24}\) if expanded)
