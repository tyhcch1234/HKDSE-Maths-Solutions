Passenger balance model

Male passengers $x$ Female passengers $y$ Balance / check Inactive

HKDSE 2023 · Paper 1 · Q5

On a ferry, the number of female passengers is 40% more than the number of male passengers. If 24 female passengers leave the ferry, then the number of male passengers is 40% more than the number of female passengers.

Find the number of male passengers on the ferry.

[4 marks]

1 Introduce the unknowns

Let $x$ be the number of male passengers
and $y$ be the original number of female passengers on the ferry.

Use two variables — one for each gender — so each condition becomes a simple equation.
2 Translate the first condition
Sharing in a Given Ratio \(\text{share}=\dfrac{\text{term of the ratio}}{\text{sum of the terms}}\times\text{total}\) With \(35\) males in the \(5:7\) ratio, one share is \(35\div5=7\) people.

“Female passengers are 40% more than male passengers”:

\[ y = (1+40\%)\,x = 1.4x \qquad \text{……(1)} \]
“40% more than” means $\times 1.4$,
i.e. the original amount plus 40% of it.
3 Translate the second condition
Simplifying a Ratio \(a:b=\dfrac{a}{k}:\dfrac{b}{k}\quad(k=\text{H.C.F. of }a\text{ and }b)\) The totals \(35\) and \(49\) share the factor \(7\), which cancels to give the simplest form.

After 24 female passengers leave, females become $y-24$,
and males are 40% more:

\[ x = (1+40\%)(y-24) = 1.4(y-24) \qquad \text{……(2)} \]
Only the female count changes — the male count $x$ stays the same throughout.
4 Solve the simultaneous equations
Equivalent Ratios \(a:b=c:d\ \Longleftrightarrow\ ad=bc\) The two ratios are compared by cross multiplying the numbers of males and females.

Substitute (1) into (2):

\[ x = 1.4\bigl(1.4x-24\bigr) \]
\[ x = 1.96x - 33.6 \]
\[ 0.96x = 33.6 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 35 \]

Then $y = 1.4(35) = 49$.

Expand,
collect $x$ on one side, then divide by the coefficient of $x$.
5 Check and state the answer

Check: $49 = 1.4 \times 35$ ✓ and $35 = 1.4 \times (49-24) = 1.4 \times 25 = 35$ ✓

Always substitute back to confirm both conditions hold.
The number of male passengers on the ferry is $\;35$. (Original female passengers: 49; after 24 leave: 25, and $1.4\times 25 = 35$.)

Question. In a club the ratio of the number of boys to the number of girls is \(3:4\). There are \(36\) boys.
(a) Find the number of girls.
(b) If \(12\) girls then leave the club, find the new ratio of boys to girls.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Boys : girls \(=3:4\), so one share is \(36\div3=12\) members.
Step 2. Girls \(=4\times12=48\).
Step 3. After \(12\) girls leave, girls \(=48-12=36\) while the boys stay at \(36\).
Step 4. New ratio \(=36:36=1:1\).
Answer: (a) \(48\) girls (b) \(1:1\)
