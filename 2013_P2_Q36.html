The real part of $\;i + 2i^{2} + 3i^{3} + 4i^{4}\;$ is
  • A. $2$
  • B. $-2$
  • C. $6$
  • D. $-6$
Cycle of Powers of i \(i^{4k}=1,\quad i^{4k+1}=i,\quad i^{4k+2}=-1,\quad i^{4k+3}=-i\) Every power of \\(i\\) here reduces to one of \\(i,-1,-i,1\\) because the pattern repeats every four steps.
1
Recall the cycle of powers of $i$

$$i^{1}=i,\qquad i^{2}=-1,\qquad i^{3}=-i,\qquad i^{4}=1.$$

The values repeat with period $4$,
so $i^{n}=i^{\,n \bmod 4}$.

Hint: Remembering the 4-cycle $i,-1,-i,1$ is faster than expanding each power.
2
Reduce each term

$$ i = i,\quad 2i^{2} = 2(-1) = -2,\quad 3i^{3} = 3(-i) = -3i,\quad 4i^{4} = 4(1) = 4. $$

Hint: Only real terms can change the real part. Keep the imaginary terms but label them clearly.
Addition of Complex Numbers \((a+bi)+(c+di)=(a+c)+(b+d)i\) The real terms \\((-2)+4\\) and the imaginary terms \\((1-3)i\\) are collected separately.
3
Collect real and imaginary parts

$$ i + 2i^{2} + 3i^{3} + 4i^{4} = \underbrace{(-2)+4}_{\text{real}} + \underbrace{(1-3)i}_{\text{imaginary}} = 2 - 2i. $$

Hint: Group the real numbers together and the $i$-terms together.
Real Part of a Complex Number \(\operatorname{Re}(a+bi)=a\) For \\(2-2i\\) the real part is the term without \\(i\\), namely \\(2\\).
4
Read off the real part

$$\operatorname{Re}(2-2i)=2.$$

Hint: For $a+bi$ with $a,b\in\mathbb{R}$, the real part is $a$.
Answer: A. $2$

Question. Find the real part of \\(i+3i^{2}+5i^{3}+7i^{4}+9i^{5}\\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Use the cycle \\(i^{2}=-1\\), \\(i^{3}=-i\\), \\(i^{4}=1\\), \\(i^{5}=i\\).
Step 2. So the sum is \\(i+3(-1)+5(-i)+7(1)+9(i)\\).
Step 3. Collect real and imaginary parts: \\((-3+7)+(1-5+9)i=4+5i\\).
Step 4. Hence \\(\operatorname{Re}(4+5i)=4\\).
Answer: \(4\)
