Rectangle perimeter, right angles and similar triangles

Rectangle Properties Pythagoras' Theorem Similar Triangles (AAA) Corresponding Sides

Question Full Question 1A + 1M + 1M

In the figure, the perimeter of the rectangle \(ABCD\) is \(170\text{ cm}\). It is given that \(EBF\) is a straight line and \(\angle AEB=\angle BFC=90^\circ\). If \(AE=24\text{ cm}\) and \(BC=34\text{ cm}\), then \(EF=\)

Perimeter of rectangle \(ABCD\;=\;AB+BC+CD+DA\;=\;170\text{ cm}\)

\(EBF\) is a straight line, \(\angle AEB=\angle BFC=90^\circ\),

\(AE=24\text{ cm}\), \(BC=34\text{ cm}\). Find \(EF\).

A. \(45\text{ cm}\)
B. \(51\text{ cm}\)
C. \(61\text{ cm}\)
D. \(75\text{ cm}\)

Original Figure

Part (a) Find \(AB\) from the perimeter 1M

Use the property that opposite sides of a rectangle are equal, then set up the perimeter equation.

170
1 Use rectangle properties Reason
\[ AB=CD,\qquad AD=BC=34\text{ cm}\quad(\text{prop. of rectangle}) \]
Hint: A rectangle has two pairs of equal opposite sides. So \(CD\) is the same length as \(AB\), and \(AD\) is the same as \(BC=34\).
2 Form the perimeter equation 1M
\[ AB+BC+CD+AD=170 \]
\[ AB+34+AB+34=170 \]
Hint: Substitute \(CD=AB\) and \(AD=BC=34\), then collect like terms: \(2AB+68=170\).
3 Solve for \(AB\) 1A
\[ 2AB=170-68=102 \qquad\Rightarrow\qquad AB=\frac{102}{2}=51\text{ cm} \]
\(AB=CD=51\text{ cm}\)
Hint: \(AB\) is not the answer to the question — it is a stepping stone. Keep \(51\) ready for Part (b).

Part (b)(i) Find \(BE\) with Pythagoras 1M

Triangle \(ABE\) is right-angled at \(E\). Note that \(AE\) is a leg and \(AB\) is the hypotenuse.

24
1 Identify the right triangle Reason
\[ \angle AEB=90^\circ \quad\Rightarrow\quad \triangle ABE \text{ is right-angled at } E \]
Hint: Because \(AB\) is a side of the rectangle, \(AB\) takes the role of the hypotenuse in \(\triangle ABE\).
2 Apply Pythagoras' Theorem 1M
\[ BE=\sqrt{AB^{2}-AE^{2}}=\sqrt{51^{2}-24^{2}} \]
\[ =\sqrt{2601-576}=\sqrt{2025}=45\text{ cm} \]
\(BE=45\text{ cm}\)
Hint: \(51^2=2601\) and \(24^2=576\). Notice \(45^2=2025\), so \(\sqrt{2025}=45\) exactly — a Pythagorean triple \((24,45,51)\).

Part (b)(ii) Prove \(\triangle ABE\sim\triangle BCF\) 1M

To prove two triangles similar, find three equal angles (AAA). Use the exterior angle of a triangle and the straight line \(EBF\).

1 Find \(\angle BAE\) using the exterior angle of \(\triangle ABE\) Reason
\[ \angle BAE+\angle AEB=\angle ABF \quad(\text{ext. }\angle\text{ of }\triangle) \]
\[ \angle ABF=\angle ABC+\angle CBF \]
\[ \angle BAE+90^\circ=90^\circ+\angle CBF \]
\[ \angle BAE=\angle CBF \]
Hint: Since \(EBF\) is a straight line, \(\angle ABF\) splits at \(B\) into \(\angle ABC=90^\circ\) and \(\angle CBF\). Now cancel \(90^\circ\) on both sides.
2 Collect the three pairs of equal angles 1M
\[ \angle AEB=\angle BFC \quad(\text{given}) \]
\[ \angle BAE=\angle CBF \quad(\text{proved}) \]
\[ \angle ABE=\angle BCF \quad(\angle\text{ sum of }\triangle) \]
\[ \therefore\; \triangle ABE \sim \triangle BCF \quad(\text{AAA}) \]
\(\triangle ABE\sim\triangle BCF\) (AAA)
Hint: Once two angle pairs are equal, the third pair must automatically be equal, because angles in a triangle sum to \(180^\circ\). So the AAA condition is satisfied.

Part (b)(iii) Find \(EF\) 1M

Use corresponding sides of similar triangles to get \(BF\), then add \(BE+BF\) along the straight line \(EBF\).

1 Write the ratio of corresponding sides Reason
\[ \frac{AE}{BF}=\frac{AB}{BC} \quad(\text{corr. sides, }\sim\triangle\text{s}) \]
Hint: Match carefully: \(AE\) (in \(\triangle ABE\)) corresponds to \(BF\) (in \(\triangle BCF\)); \(AB\) corresponds to \(BC\).
2 Substitute and solve for \(BF\) 1M
\[ \frac{24}{BF}=\frac{51}{34} \]
\[ BF=\frac{24\times 34}{51}=16\text{ cm} \]
Hint: \(24\times34=816\) and \(816\div51=16\). So \(BF=16\text{ cm}\).
3 Add the two segments on the straight line 1A
\[ EF=BE+BF=45+16=61\text{ cm} \]
\(EF=61\text{ cm}\) → Option C
Hint: \(B\) lies between \(E\) and \(F\) because \(EBF\) is a straight line, so the two segments simply add.

Summary of Answers

  • Part (a): \(AB=CD=51\text{ cm}\) (from perimeter \(170\text{ cm}\))
  • Part (b)(i): \(BE=\sqrt{51^2-24^2}=45\text{ cm}\)
  • Part (b)(ii): \(\triangle ABE\sim\triangle BCF\) (AAA)
  • Part (b)(iii): \(BF=16\text{ cm}\), so \(EF=45+16=61\text{ cm}\)
Final answer: \(EF=61\text{ cm}\)  →  Option C
