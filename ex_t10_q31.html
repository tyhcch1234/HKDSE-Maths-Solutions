Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Surface Area

T10 Q31

A cube has side length $5$ cm. Find its total surface area.

Solution

The area of one face is $5^2=25\text{ cm}^2$.
A cube has $6$ congruent faces.
Total surface area $=6\times25=150\text{ cm}^2$.
The total surface area of the cube is $150\text{ cm}^2$.
