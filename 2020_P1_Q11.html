It is given that the range of the above distribution is the triple of its inter-quartile range.

(a) Find \(w\). 4 marks

Step 11M

Write down the range in terms of \(w\). The smallest weight is \(11\) and the largest weight is \(50+w\). Hence

\[ \text{Range} \;=\;(50+w)-11 \;=\;(w+39)\ \text{grams} \]
Hint: The range is “largest − smallest”. The stem \(5\) with leaf \(w\) gives the weight \(50+w\), which is the largest since \(w\ge 0\).
Step 21M

Count the data and find the inter-quartile range. There are \(6+7+5+1+2=21\) data values. Using the \(n=21\) rule,

\[ Q_1=\text{5th value}=23,\quad Q_3=\text{16th value}=38 \] \[ \text{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1=38-23=15\ \text{grams} \]
Hint: Count leaves row by row: \(11,12,13,13\) (4 values), then \(23,\dots\) — the 5th value is \(23\). Continue counting to reach the 16th value, which is \(38\).
Step 31M

Apply the given condition: range = 3 × inter-quartile range.

\[ w+39 = 3(15) \] \[ w+39=45 \]
Hint: The wording “the range … is the triple of its inter-quartile range” means \(\text{Range}=3\times\text{IQR}\).
Step 41A

Solving,

\[ w = 45-39 = 6 \]
Answer: \(\boxed{w=6}\)  (so the largest weight is \(56\) grams).

Step 11M

Identify the mode. From the stem-and-leaf diagram (with \(w=6\)), the weight \(38\) appears three times — more often than any other weight. Hence the mode is \(38\) grams.

Hint: The mode is the value occurring most frequently. Look at stem \(3\): the leaves are \(1,6,7,8,8,8\), so \(38\) occurs three times.
Step 21M

Count the favourable outcomes and the total. There are \(6+7+5+1+1=20\) letters in total. The weights not less than \(38\) g are \(38\) (three letters) and \(42,50,56\) (one letter each), giving \(6\) favourable letters.

\[ P = \frac{6}{20} \]
Hint: “Not less than the mode” means weight \(\ge 38\). Count every leaf with value \(38\) or more across all stems.
Step 31A

Simplifying the fraction,

\[ P=\frac{6}{20}=\frac{3}{10} \]
Answer: The required probability is \(\boxed{\dfrac{3}{10}}\) (or \(0.3\)).

✓ Final Answers at a Glance

  • (a) Range \(=(w+39)\) g,   IQR \(=15\) g,   \(w+39=3(15)\)  \(\Rightarrow\)  \(w=6\)
  • (b) Mode \(=38\) g;   favourable letters \(=6\) out of \(20\);   probability \(=\dfrac{6}{20}=\) \(\dfrac{3}{10}\)
  • Key idea: Range \(=\) max \(–\) min; IQR \(=Q_3-Q_1\); mode \(=\) most frequent value.
  • Data (with \(w=6\)): 11, 12, 13, 13, 23, 23, 24, 25, 26, 29, 29, 31, 36, 37, 38, 38, 38, 42, 50, 56
