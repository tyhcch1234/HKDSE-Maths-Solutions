HKDSE 2023 Paper 2 Q43

When a boy throws a dart, the probability that he hits the target is $0.6$. If this boy throws the dart $4$ times, find the probability that he hits the target at least $2$ times.

A. $0.5248$
B. $0.7056$
C. $0.8208$
D. $0.8464$

Step-by-step Solution

Step 1Identify the model — binomial, $n=4$, $p=0.6$

Each throw is an independent trial with two outcomes: hit (H, probability $p = 0.6$) or miss (M, probability $q = 1 - 0.6 = 0.4$). There are $n = 4$ throws.

Hint: fixed number of independent throws, constant hit probability ⇒ use the binomial distribution / complementary counting.
Step 2Use the complement

"At least $2$ hits" is the complement of "at most $1$ hit", i.e. exactly $0$ hits or exactly $1$ hit.

$$\text{required } P = 1 - P(\text{0 hits}) - P(\text{1 hit})$$

Hint: $P(\text{at least }2) = 1 - P(\text{0 or 1 hit})$. This avoids adding eight separate cases.
Complement Rule \(P(E)=1-P(E')\) 'At least \(2\) hits' is the complement of 'at most \(1\) hit', so subtract \(P(0)\) and \(P(1)\) from \(1\).
Step 3Probability of exactly $0$ hits

All four throws must miss:

$$P(\text{0 hits}) = (0.4)^4 = (1-0.6)^4 = 0.0256$$

Hint: multiply the miss probability $0.4$ four times — one path M–M–M–M in the tree.
Multiplication Rule for Independent Events \(P(\text{all miss})=q^{n}=(0.4)^{4}\) All four throws must miss, and each miss has probability \(1-0.6=0.4\).
Step 4Probability of exactly $1$ hit

The hit can occur on any one of the $4$ throws. Each such sequence has probability $(0.6)(0.4)^3$, and there are $4$ of them, so

$${}^{4}C_{1}(0.6)(0.4)^3 = 4 \times 0.6 \times 0.064 = 0.1536$$

Hint: in the tree, exactly $4$ paths show precisely one orange (Miss-free) branch — H–M–M–M, M–H–M–M, M–M–H–M, M–M–M–H.
Binomial Probability \(P(X=1)=C_{1}^{4}p\,q^{3}\) The single hit can occur on any of the \(4\) throws, so \(4\times 0.6\times(0.4)^{3}=0.1536\).
Step 5Subtract from $1$

$$ \begin{aligned} P(\text{at least 2 hits}) &= 1 - (0.4)^4 - 4(0.6)(0.4)^3 \\[4pt] &= 1 - 0.0256 - 0.1536 \\[4pt] &= 1 - 0.1792 \\[4pt] &= 0.8208 \end{aligned} $$

Hint: $0.0256 + 0.1536 = 0.1792$, so the answer is $1 - 0.1792$.
Step 6Check with the binomial formula

Directly summing $k=2,3,4$:

$$\sum_{k=2}^{4}{}^{4}C_{k}(0.6)^k(0.4)^{4-k} = 0.3456 + 0.3456 + 0.1296 = 0.8208 \;\checkmark$$

Hint: both routes agree, confirming option C.
Answer: $0.8208$   ⟶   Option C

Question. When a boy throws a dart, the probability that he hits the target is \(0.7\). If he throws the dart \(3\) times, find the probability that he hits the target at least \(2\) times.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. 'At least \(2\) hits' is the complement of 'at most \(1\) hit', so the required probability is \(1-P(0)-P(1)\).
Step 2. \(P(0\text{ hits})=(0.3)^{3}=0.027\).
Step 3. \(P(1\text{ hit})=C_{1}^{3}(0.7)(0.3)^{2}=3\times 0.7\times 0.09=0.189\).
Step 4. Required probability \(=1-0.027-0.189=0.784\).
Step 5. Check by adding the cases \(k=2,3\): \(3(0.7)^{2}(0.3)+(0.7)^{3}=0.441+0.343=0.784\) ✓.
Answer: \(0.784\)
