HKDSE 2012 · Paper 2 · Q18 — Trigonometry (Right Triangles)

△ABC (sides) BD ⟂ AC △BCD (angle α) △ABD (angle β) Right angle at D

Question

In the figure, $D$ is a point lying on $AC$ such that $BD$ is perpendicular to $AC$. If $BC = \ell$, then $AB =$ A. $\dfrac{\ell \sin\alpha}{\cos\beta}$ B. $\dfrac{\ell \sin\beta}{\cos\alpha}$ C. $\dfrac{\ell \cos\alpha}{\sin\beta}$ D. $\dfrac{\ell \cos\beta}{\sin\alpha}$

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1 · Read the figure & mark the right angles $BD \perp AC$, so $D$ splits $\triangle ABC$ into two right-angled triangles: $\triangle BCD$ (right angle at $D$, angle $\alpha$ at $C$) and $\triangle ABD$ (right angle at $D$, angle $\beta$ at $B$).
Key data: $BC=\ell$, $\angle BCD=\alpha$, $\angle ABD=\beta$.
STEP 2 · Find the common side $BD$ in $\triangle BCD$ In right-angled $\triangle BCD$ (hypotenuse $BC=\ell$), the opposite side of $\alpha$ is $BD$: $$\sin\alpha=\frac{BD}{BC}=\frac{BD}{\ell}$$ $$\boxed{BD=\ell\sin\alpha}$$
STEP 3 · Find $AB$ in $\triangle ABD$ In right-angled $\triangle ABD$, the angle at $B$ is $\beta$ and $BD$ is the adjacent side to $\beta$, while $AB$ is the hypotenuse: $$\cos\beta=\frac{BD}{AB}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad AB=\frac{BD}{\cos\beta}$$
STEP 4 · Substitute and conclude Substitute $BD=\ell\sin\alpha$ from Step 2: $$AB=\frac{\ell\sin\alpha}{\cos\beta}$$
This matches option A.
✅ Final answer:   A.   $AB=\dfrac{\ell\sin\alpha}{\cos\beta}$
