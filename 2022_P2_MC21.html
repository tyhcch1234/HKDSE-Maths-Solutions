Step 11M

Let \(\angle ACO = x\).

Since \(OA = OC\) (radii of the same circle), \(\triangle AOC\) is isosceles. Therefore the base angles are equal:

\[ \angle CAO = \angle ACO = x \quad (\text{base } \angle\text{s, isos. } \triangle) \]
Hint: Radii of the same circle are always equal — this is the trigger to look for isosceles triangles.
Step 21M

Similarly, \(OA = OB\) (radii), so \(\triangle AOB\) is isosceles and

\[ \angle BAO = \angle ABO = 36^\circ \quad (\text{base } \angle\text{s, isos. } \triangle) \]
Hint: Both \(\triangle AOC\) and \(\triangle AOB\) are isosceles — each gives one useful pair of equal angles.
Step 31A

The whole angle at the circumference subtended by arc \(BC\) is

\[ \angle BAC = \angle BAO + \angle CAO = 36^\circ + x. \]

By the angle-at-the-centre theorem,

\[ \angle BOC = 2\,\angle BAC. \]

Substitute \(\angle BOC = 164^\circ\):

\[ 164^\circ = 2(36^\circ + x) \] \[ 82^\circ = 36^\circ + x \;\Rightarrow\; x = 46^\circ. \]
Hint: “Angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference” requires both angles to stand on the same arc \(BC\) — here \(\angle BOC\) (centre) and \(\angle BAC\) (circumference).
Answer: \(\angle ACO = 46^\circ\)  →  Option B

Step 11M

By the angle at the centre theorem,

\[ \angle BAC = \frac{\angle BOC}{2} = \frac{164^\circ}{2} = 82^\circ. \]

But note the quadrilateral \(BOCA\) is formed with reflex \(\angle BOC\) at \(O\), because \(B\) and \(C\) are joined through the reflex side (the interior of the polygon goes the long way round).

Hint: \(\angle BAC\) is measured on the minor arc \(BC\); the centre angle is the corresponding \(\angle BOC = 164^\circ\).
Step 21M

The reflex angle at the centre is

\[ \text{reflex } \angle BOC = 360^\circ - 164^\circ = 196^\circ \quad (\angle\text{s at a pt.}) \]
Hint: Angles at a point add to \(360^\circ\). This reflex angle is the interior angle of quadrilateral \(BOCA\) at \(O\).
Step 31A

In quadrilateral \(BOCA\), the interior angles sum to \(360^\circ\):

\[ \angle ACO + \angle BAC + \angle ABO + \text{reflex } \angle BOC = 360^\circ \] \[ \angle ACO + 82^\circ + 36^\circ + 196^\circ = 360^\circ \] \[ \angle ACO = 360^\circ - 82^\circ - 36^\circ - 196^\circ = 46^\circ. \]
Hint: At vertex \(B\), the interior angle of the quadrilateral is exactly \(\angle ABO = 36^\circ\) (since \(BC\) and \(BA\) are the sides). At vertex \(A\) it is \(\angle BAC = 82^\circ\).
Answer: \(\angle ACO = 46^\circ\)  →  Option B

✓ Summary of Answers

  • Given: \(\angle ABO = 36^\circ\), \(\angle BOC = 164^\circ\), \(O\) is the centre of circle \(ABC\).
  • Method 1: \(OA = OB = OC\) (radii) ⇒ base angles equal; \(\angle BAC = 36^\circ + x\); then \(164^\circ = 2(36^\circ + x)\).
  • Method 2: \(\angle BAC = 164^\circ \div 2 = 82^\circ\); reflex \(\angle BOC = 196^\circ\); angle sum of quadrilateral \(BOCA\).
