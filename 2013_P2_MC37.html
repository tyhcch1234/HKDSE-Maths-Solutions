Question:

Consider the following system of inequalities:

\[ \begin{cases} x \ge 0 \\ y \ge 0 \\ x + y \le 7 \\ 2x + y \le 10 \end{cases} \]

Let \(D\) be the region which represents the solution of the above system of inequalities.

If \((x, y)\) is a point lying in \(D\), then the greatest value of \(3y - 4x + 15\) is

A. 3
B. 17
C. 22
D. 30

Answer: C (22)

Key Takeaway

In linear programming, the maximum or minimum of a linear objective function over a convex polygonal region always occurs at one of the corner points (vertices) of the feasible region.
