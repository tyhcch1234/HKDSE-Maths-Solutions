[2024#2] 5. Formulas and Sequences

Make \(x\) the subject of the formula

\[ \frac{Ax+C}{B}=3x. \]

(3 marks)

1Clear the denominator
Multiply both sides by \(B\): \[ \frac{Ax+C}{B}\times B = 3x \times B \] \[ Ax + C = 3Bx \]
Hint: to remove a fraction, multiply every term on both sides by its denominator.
Clearing the Denominator \(\dfrac{P}{B}=Q\iff P=BQ\) Multiplying the whole equation by \(B\) turns it into \(Ax+C=3Bx\).
2Collect all \(x\)-terms on one side
Bring the \(Ax\) term to the right: \[ C = 3Bx - Ax \] Factor out \(x\): \[ C = x(3B - A) \]
Hint: factorise — \(x\) is the common factor on the right.
Factorising out a Common Factor \(3Bx-Ax=x(3B-A)\) The unknown \(x\) is common to both terms, so it can be taken out as a single bracket.
3Divide to isolate \(x\)
Divide both sides by \((3B - A)\): \[ x = \frac{C}{3B-A} \]
Hint: keep the denominator as a single bracket \((3B-A)\).
Changing the Subject of a Formula \(x(3B-A)=C\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{C}{3B-A}\) Dividing by the bracket isolates \(x\); the denominator must stay as one bracket.
Answer: \[ x = \frac{C}{3B-A} \]

Question. Make \(x\) the subject of the formula \(\dfrac{Ax-C}{B}=5x\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Multiply both sides by \(B\): \(Ax-C=5Bx\).
Step 2. Collect the \(x\)-terms on one side: \(-C=5Bx-Ax\).
Step 3. Factorise: \(-C=x(5B-A)\).
Step 4. Divide by the bracket: \(x=\dfrac{-C}{5B-A}=\dfrac{C}{A-5B}\).
Answer: \(x=\dfrac{C}{A-5B}\)
