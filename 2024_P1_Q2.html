Q2

Make \(x\) the subject of the formula

\[ \frac{Ax + C}{B} = 3x. \]

(3 marks)

Algebra Subject of Formula Linear Equations Rearranging

Step-by-step Solution

\[ \frac{Ax + C}{B} \times B = 3x \times B \] \[ Ax + C = 3Bx \]
Hint: To remove the fraction, multiply every term on both sides by the denominator \(B\). This keeps the equation balanced.
\[ Ax - 3Bx = -C \]

(Subtract \(3Bx\) and \(C\) from both sides)

Hint: Move all \(x\)-terms to the left-hand side and the constant term \(C\) to the right-hand side. Remember to change signs when moving across the equals sign.
\[ x(A - 3B) = -C \]
Hint: Both terms on the left contain \(x\). Factor out \(x\) to get \(x(A - 3B)\).
\[ x = \frac{-C}{A - 3B} = \frac{C}{3B - A} \]
Hint: Divide both sides by \((A - 3B)\). Then multiply numerator and denominator by \(-1\) to get the standard form.
✅ Final Answer: \[ x = \frac{C}{3B - A} \]

💡 Key Takeaway

To make \(x\) the subject: 1) clear fractions → 2) move all \(x\)-terms together → 3) factorise \(x\) → 4) divide. Always check by substituting back.
