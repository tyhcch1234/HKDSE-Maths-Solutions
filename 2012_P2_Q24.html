Coordinate Geometry — Locus of a Moving Point

Question

[2012MC#24] If \(P\) is a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that the distance between \(P\) and the point \((20, 12)\) is equal to \(5\), then the locus of \(P\) is a

  • A. circle.
  • B. square.
  • C. parabola.
  • D. triangle.

Part (a) — Explore the Moving Point \(P\) Interactive

Drag the slider to move \(P\) around the fixed point \(C(20,12)\). Watch the distance \(CP\) — it never changes.

Step 1

Let the fixed point be \(C(20,12)\). The condition says the distance from \(P\) to \(C\) is always \(5\).

Hint: A fixed distance from a fixed point is the definition of a circle — the fixed point is the centre.
Step 2

Therefore, the locus of \(P\) is the set of all points \(5\) units from \(C(20,12)\) — a circle with centre \((20,12)\) and radius \(5\).

Answer: A. circle
0 / 2

Part (b) — Verify Algebraically with the Distance Formula Interactive

Use the distance formula on a general point \(P=(x,y)\). Adjust the radius \(r\) to see how the equation changes.

Step 1

Let \(P=(x,y)\). Apply the distance formula between \(P(x,y)\) and \(C(20,12)\):

\[ CP = \sqrt{(x-20)^2 + (y-12)^2} \]
Hint: Distance formula: \(d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}\).
Step 2

The condition \(CP = 5\) gives:

\[ \sqrt{(x-20)^2 + (y-12)^2} = 5 \]
Hint: Square both sides to get rid of the square root.
Step 3

Squaring both sides:

\[ (x-20)^2 + (y-12)^2 = 25 \]

This is the standard equation of a circle with centre \((20,12)\) and radius \(5\). So the locus is a circle.

Answer: A. circle
0 / 3

Part (c) — Eliminate the Other Options Reasoning

Toggle each candidate shape to compare with the traced locus of \(P\).

Only the circle keeps \(CP = 5\) for every point.
Step 11R

Square / Triangle: These have vertices. Points on the edges have different distances from the centre, so the condition \(CP=5\) fails.

Hint: A locus must include all points satisfying the condition, not just some.
Step 21R

Parabola: A parabola is defined by equal distances to a fixed point (focus) and a fixed line (directrix). Here we only have a fixed point and a fixed distance — that's a circle, not a parabola.

Hint: Circle = fixed distance from a point. Parabola = fixed distance to a point equals distance to a line.
Step 3

Therefore, the correct option is A.

Answer: A. circle
0 / 3

📌 Summary of Answers

  • Fixed point: \(C(20,12)\)
  • Fixed distance (radius): \(5\)
  • Locus equation: \((x-20)^2 + (y-12)^2 = 25\)
  • Shape of locus: A circle with centre \((20,12)\) and radius \(5\)
  • Final answer: A
