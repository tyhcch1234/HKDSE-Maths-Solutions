Locus (circle, r = 5) Radius from A(20,12) Fixed point A(20,12) Moving point P Centre coordinates
If $P$ is a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that the distance between $P$ and the point $(20,\,12)$ is equal to $5$, then the locus of $P$ is a
  • A. circle.
  • B. square.
  • C. parabola.
  • D. triangle.
STEP 1 · NAME THE POINT AND ITS COORDINATES

Let the fixed point be $A(20,\,12)$ and let the moving point be $P(x,\,y)$, where $x$ and $y$ vary.

The condition given is: the distance $AP$ is always equal to $5$.

STEP 2 · WRITE THE DISTANCE FORMULA

By the distance formula (a direct use of Pythagoras' theorem):

$$AP=\sqrt{(x-20)^2+(y-12)^2}$$

Since $AP=5$, we have the equation of the locus:

$$\sqrt{(x-20)^2+(y-12)^2}=5$$

STEP 3 · SQUARE BOTH SIDES

Squaring both sides removes the root:

$$(x-20)^2+(y-12)^2=25$$

This is exactly the standard form of a circle.

Hint: The general circle with centre $(h,k)$ and radius $r$ is $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$. Compare with the equation above.
STEP 4 · IDENTIFY THE LOCUS

The equation $(x-20)^2+(y-12)^2=5^2$ represents a circle with:

Centre $= (20,\,12)$  and  Radius $= 5$.

Therefore the locus of $P$ is a circle.

Answer: A — circle
Locus: $(x-20)^2+(y-12)^2=25$, a circle of radius $5$ centred at $(20,12)$.
