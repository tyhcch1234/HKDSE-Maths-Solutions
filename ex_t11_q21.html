Geometry (106) · Sub-Topic: Circle Properties

T11 Q21

The angle at the centre of a circle is twice the angle at the circumference subtended by the same arc. If the centre angle is \(2x\) and the circumference angle is \(x+30\), write an equation relating them.

Solution

By the circle theorem, the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference: $\text{centre angle}=2\times\text{circumference angle}$.
Substitute the given angles: $2x=2(x+30)$.
Expand the right-hand side: $2x=2x+60$.
Subtract $2x$ from both sides: $0=60$, which is impossible.
Therefore the given angle expressions are inconsistent, so there is no value of $x$ satisfying both conditions.
The relation is $2x=2(x+30)$; solving it gives $0=60$, so there is no solution for $x$.
