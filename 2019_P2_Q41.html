HKDSE 2019 · Paper 2 · MC41

Orthocentre, Centroid and In-centre of a Right Triangle

Question

If \(\triangle ABC\) is a right-angled triangle with \(\angle ABC = 90^\circ\), which of the following is/are true?

  • I.  The orthocentre of \(\triangle ABC\) lies on \(AC\).
  • II.  The centroid of \(\triangle ABC\) lies inside \(\triangle ABC\).
  • III.  The in-centre of \(\triangle ABC\) lies outside \(\triangle ABC\).
  1. I only
  2. II only
  3. I and III only
  4. II and III only

Solution

Step 1 · Check statement I (orthocentre)

The altitude from \(A\) is the side \(AB\) (since \(AB \perp BC\)), and the altitude from \(B\) is the side \(BC\) (since \(BC \perp AB\)). These two altitudes meet at the right-angle vertex \(B\).

So the orthocentre is at vertex \(B\), not on \(AC\). Statement I is false.

Step 2 · Check statement II (centroid)

The centroid is the intersection of the medians. For every triangle — acute, right or obtuse — the centroid lies inside the triangle. Statement II is true.

Step 3 · Check statement III (in-centre)

The in-centre is the intersection of the internal angle bisectors; it is the centre of the inscribed circle, which fits inside the triangle. For every triangle the in-centre lies inside the triangle. Statement III is false.

Recall: in an obtuse triangle the orthocentre and circumcentre lie outside, but the centroid and in-centre always lie inside.
Step 4 · Conclusion

Only statement II is true.

Final Answer: B. II only
