HKDSE 2015 · Paper 2 · Q26 — Coordinate Geometry

Circle equation from its centre and a point on the circle

Centre \(C(-4,3)\) Circle \(C\) Radius Point \((0,3)\) Axes

Question

[2015MC#26]

A circle \(C\) passes through the point \((0,\,3)\). If the coordinates of the centre of \(C\) are \((-4,\,3)\), then the equation of \(C\) is

A.\(x^{2}+y^{2}-8x+6y+9=0\)
B.\(x^{2}+y^{2}-8x+6y+16=0\)
C.\(x^{2}+y^{2}+8x-6y+9=0\)
D.\(x^{2}+y^{2}+8x-6y+16=0\)

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 · Identify the data

Centre of the circle: \(\;O(-4,\,3)\).

The circle passes through the point \(\;P(0,\,3)\),
so \(P\) lies on the circle.

The radius \(r\) is the distance from the centre to any point on the circle,
in particular to \(P\).

Step 2 · Find the radius
Distance Formula \(r=\sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}+(y_{2}-y_{1})^{2}}\) The radius is the distance from \((-4,3)\) to the point \((0,3)\) on the circle.

Use the distance formula between \(O(-4,3)\) and \(P(0,3)\):

$$r=\sqrt{(0-(-4))^{2}+(3-3)^{2}}=\sqrt{4^{2}+0^{2}}=4$$

So the radius is \(r=4\).

Step 3 · Write the circle equation
Equation of a Circle \((x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=r^{2}\) With centre \((-4,3)\) and \(r=4\) the equation is \((x+4)^{2}+(y-3)^{2}=16\).

For a circle with centre \((h,k)\) and radius \(r\):

$$(x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=r^{2}$$

Substitute \((h,k)=(-4,\,3)\) and \(r^{2}=16\):

$$(x-(-4))^{2}+(y-3)^{2}=16$$

$$(x+4)^{2}+(y-3)^{2}=16$$

Step 4 · Expand to general form
General Form of a Circle \(x^{2}+y^{2}+Dx+Ey+F=0,\qquad \text{centre}=\left(-\dfrac{D}{2},-\dfrac{E}{2}\right)\) Expanding and collecting constants gives \(x^{2}+y^{2}+8x-6y+9=0\), whose centre checks back as \((-4,3)\).

$$(x^{2}+8x+16)+(y^{2}-6y+9)=16$$

$$x^{2}+y^{2}+8x-6y+(16+9-16)=0$$

$$x^{2}+y^{2}+8x-6y+9=0$$

Check with the centre: for \(x^{2}+y^{2}+Dx+Ey+F=0\), the centre is \(\left(-\tfrac{D}{2},-\tfrac{E}{2}\right)=\left(-\tfrac{8}{2},-\tfrac{-6}{2}\right)=(-4,\,3)\) ✓
Step 5 · Conclusion

The required equation is \(x^{2}+y^{2}+8x-6y+9=0\), which matches option C.

Final answer
C  
·  \(x^{2}+y^{2}+8x-6y+9=0\)

Question. A circle \(C\) passes through the point \((3,\,4)\) and its centre is \((3,\,-2)\). Find the equation of \(C\) in the general form \(x^{2}+y^{2}+Dx+Ey+F=0\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The radius is the distance from \((3,-2)\) to \((3,4)\): \(r=\sqrt{(3-3)^{2}+(4+2)^{2}}=\sqrt{36}=6\).
Step 2. In centre-radius form: \((x-3)^{2}+(y+2)^{2}=36\).
Step 3. Expanding: \(x^{2}-6x+9+y^{2}+4y+4=36\).
Step 4. So \(x^{2}+y^{2}-6x+4y+(9+4-36)=0\), i.e. \(x^{2}+y^{2}-6x+4y-23=0\).
Answer: \(x^{2}+y^{2}-6x+4y-23=0\)
