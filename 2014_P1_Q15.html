HKDSE Mathematics 2014 Paper 1

Question 15 – Log-Log Linear Relation

Laws of Indices Exponential & Log Functions Change of Base

Q Question

The graph in Figure 4 shows the linear relation between \( \log_4 x \) and \( \log_8 y \). The slope and the intercept on the horizontal axis of the graph are \( -\dfrac{1}{3} \) and \( 3 \) respectively. Express the relation between \( x \) and \( y \) in the form \( y = A x^k \), where \( A \) and \( k \) are constants. [3 marks]

0 / 6
1

Use the slope–intercept form

The graph is a straight line with slope \( m = -\frac{1}{3} \) and horizontal-axis intercept at \( \log_4 x = 3 \). Its equation is:

\[ \log_8 y - 0 = -\frac{1}{3}\left(\log_4 x - 3\right) \]
💡 Hint: Point–slope form: \( y - y_1 = m(x - x_1) \), with the point \( (3,\ 0) \) lying on the line.
2

Expand and simplify

Expanding the right-hand side gives:

\[ \log_8 y = -\frac{1}{3}\log_4 x + 1 \]
💡 Hint: \( -\frac{1}{3}(-3) = 1 \). Keep the coefficient of \( \log_4 x \) as \( -\frac{1}{3} \).
3

Convert coefficients into logs

Use the power rule \( a\log_b M = \log_b M^a \) and \( 1 = \log_4 4 \):

\[ \log_8 y = \log_4 x^{-\frac{1}{3}} + \log_4 4 \]
💡 Hint: \( -\frac{1}{3}\log_4 x = \log_4 x^{-1/3} \). Also, \( 1 = \log_4 4 \) because \( 4^1 = 4 \).
4

Combine the logs

Apply the product rule \( \log_b M + \log_b N = \log_b (MN) \):

\[ \log_8 y = \log_4\!\left(4x^{-\frac{1}{3}}\right) \]
💡 Hint: The two logs on the right have the same base (\( 4 \)), so they can be combined into one.
5

Change the base to 2

The two sides still have different bases (\(8\) and \(4\)). Change both to base \(2\) using \( \log_b M = \frac{\log_2 M}{\log_2 b} \):

\[ \frac{\log_2 y}{\log_2 8} = \frac{\log_2\!\left(4x^{-\frac{1}{3}}\right)}{\log_2 4} \] \[ \frac{\log_2 y}{3} = \frac{\log_2\!\left(4x^{-\frac{1}{3}}\right)}{2} \]
💡 Hint: \( \log_2 8 = 3 \) and \( \log_2 4 = 2 \) because \( 8 = 2^3 \) and \( 4 = 2^2 \).
✓

Final answer

Multiply both sides by \( 3 \), then apply the power rule and simplify:

\[ \log_2 y = \frac{3}{2}\log_2\!\left(4x^{-\frac{1}{3}}\right) \] \[ \log_2 y = \log_2\!\left(4x^{-\frac{1}{3}}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} \] \[ \log_2 y = \log_2\!\left(4^{\frac{3}{2}} x^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right) \] \[ \log_2 y = \log_2\!\left(8x^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right) \]

Since log is one-to-one:

\[ \boxed{y = 8x^{-\frac{1}{2}}} \]
💡 Hint: \( 4^{3/2} = (2^2)^{3/2} = 2^3 = 8 \) and \( \left(x^{-1/3}\right)^{3/2} = x^{-1/2} \).
