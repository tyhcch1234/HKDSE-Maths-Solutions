HKDSE 2017 P2 Q14Mensuration — Triangle & Altitude

In the figure, $D$ is a point lying on $AC$ such that $BD$ is perpendicular to $AC$. It is given that $AC = 14\text{ cm}$ and $BD = 12\text{ cm}$. If the area of $\Delta ABD$ is greater than the area of $\Delta BCD$ by $24\text{ cm}^2$, then the perimeter of $\Delta ABC$ is

  • A.30 cm
  • B.42 cm
  • C.54 cm
  • D.84 cm
Hint: Both $\Delta ABD$ and $\Delta BCD$ have the same height $BD = 12$ cm. So the area difference only depends on the base difference $AD - CD$. That gives one equation for $AD$.
STEP 1 · Set up the area difference

Let $AD = x$ cm. Since $D$ lies on $AC$ and $AC = 14$, we have $CD = 14 - x$.

$$\text{Area of }\Delta ABD = \tfrac12 \cdot AD \cdot BD = \tfrac12(x)(12) = 6x$$

$$\text{Area of }\Delta BCD = \tfrac12 \cdot CD \cdot BD = \tfrac12(14-x)(12) = 6(14-x)$$

Both triangles use the same altitude $BD$ measured from $B$ down to the base $AC$.

STEP 2 · Solve for $AD$ and $CD$

Given the difference is $24\text{ cm}^2$:

$$\tfrac12(x)(12) - \tfrac12(14-x)(12) = 24$$

$$6x - 6(14-x) = 24$$

$$6x - 84 + 6x = 24 \;\Rightarrow\; 12x = 108 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 9$$

So $AD = 9$ cm and $CD = 14 - 9 = \mathbf{5}$ cm.

STEP 3 · Find $AB$ (Pythagoras in $\Delta ABD$)

$\Delta ABD$ is right-angled at $D$, so

$$AB = \sqrt{AD^2 + BD^2} = \sqrt{9^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{81 + 144} = \sqrt{225} = 15$$

So $AB = \mathbf{15}$ cm.

STEP 4 · Find $BC$ (Pythagoras in $\Delta BCD$)

$\Delta BCD$ is right-angled at $D$, so

$$BC = \sqrt{CD^2 + BD^2} = \sqrt{5^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{25 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13$$

So $BC = \mathbf{13}$ cm.

STEP 5 · Perimeter of $\Delta ABC$

$$AB + BC + CA = 15 + 13 + 14 = 42\text{ cm}$$

The answer is option B (42 cm).

FINAL ANSWER
$42$ cm  →  Option B
