T11 Q32

In the figure, $AB = DE$, $BC = EF$, and $AC = DF$. Name the condition that proves $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$.

Solution

List the given equal corresponding sides: $AB=DE$, $BC=EF$, and $AC=DF$.
All three pairs of corresponding sides of $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle DEF$ are equal.
Therefore, the congruence condition is Side-Side-Side (SSS).
The condition is SSS, so $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$ by SSS.
