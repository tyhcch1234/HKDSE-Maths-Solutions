T11 Q39

Two similar triangles have areas in the ratio $1:4$. Find the ratio of their corresponding side lengths.

Solution

For similar triangles, the ratio of areas equals the square of the ratio of corresponding side lengths.
Given area ratio $1:4$, take the square root: $\sqrt{1}:\sqrt{4}=1:2$.
Therefore, the ratio of corresponding side lengths is $1:2$.
The ratio of their corresponding side lengths is $1:2$.
