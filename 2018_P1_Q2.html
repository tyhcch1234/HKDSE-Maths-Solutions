[2018 #2] [3 marks]

Simplify $\dfrac{xy^{7}}{\left(x^{-2}y^{3}\right)^{4}}$ and express your answer with positive indices.

1Expand the bracket in the denominator
Power of a Product Law \((a^{m}b^{n})^{k}=a^{mk}b^{nk}\) The outer power 4 multiplies both exponents: \(x^{-2\times4}y^{3\times4}\).

Apply the power of a product law: $(ab)^{n}=a^{n}b^{n}$ and $(a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn}$.

$$\left(x^{-2}y^{3}\right)^{4}=x^{-2\times 4}\,y^{3\times 4}=x^{-8}y^{12}$$

Hint: multiply the outer power into every factor inside the bracket — don't forget the negative sign on $x$.
2Write the expression as one fraction
Quotient Law of Indices \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\) Each base is handled separately: \(x^{1-(-8)}\) and \(y^{7-12}\).

$$\frac{xy^{7}}{x^{-8}y^{12}}=x^{1-(-8)}\,y^{7-12}$$

Using the quotient law $\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}$ for each base separately.

Hint: subtract the denominator index from the numerator index, base by base.
3Simplify the indices

$$x^{1+8}\,y^{7-12}=x^{9}y^{-5}$$

Hint: $1-(-8)=1+8=9$ and $7-12=-5$. Leave the negative index for now.
4Rewrite with positive indices
Negative Index Law \(a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^{n}}\) The negative index \(y^{-5}\) must be rewritten as \(\dfrac{1}{y^{5}}\).

Use $a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^{n}}$ to remove the negative index:

$$x^{9}y^{-5}=\frac{x^{9}}{y^{5}}$$

Hint: the question requires positive indices — a negative index in the final answer loses the accuracy mark.
Answer $\dfrac{x^{9}}{y^{5}}$

Question. Simplify \(\dfrac{\left(x^{3}y^{-2}\right)^{3}}{x^{5}y^{-4}}\) and express your answer with positive indices.

Step 2. \(\dfrac{x^{9}y^{-6}}{x^{5}y^{-4}}=x^{9-5}y^{-6-(-4)}=x^{4}y^{-2}\).
