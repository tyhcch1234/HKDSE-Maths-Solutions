Let \(c\) be a constant and let \[ f(x)=x^{3}+c\,x^{2}+c . \] If \(f(c)+f(-c)=\;?\)

Part (a) Evaluate \(f(c)\) and \(f(-c)\)

Notice: \(f(-c)\) and \(f(c)\) are both contained in the amber even part (they share the same value \(c^{3}+c\)), while the blue odd part \(x^{3}\) flips sign.

1 Write down the function Given
\[ f(x)=x^{3}+c x^{2}+c \]
Hint: Treat \(c\) as a fixed number (a constant), so \(c^3\) stays as it is — do not differentiate.
2 Substitute \(x=c\)
\[ f(c)=(c)^{3}+c(c)^{2}+c \]
Hint: \(c(c)^{2}=c\cdot c^{2}=c^{3}\). Combine like terms carefully.
\[ f(c)=c^{3}+c^{3}+c=2c^{3}+c \]
3 Substitute \(x=-c\)
\[ f(-c)=(-c)^{3}+c(-c)^{2}+c \]
Hint: \((-c)^{3}=-c^{3}\) (odd power changes sign), while \((-c)^{2}=c^{2}\) (even power stays positive). Then \(c\cdot c^{2}=c^{3}\).
\[ f(-c)=-c^{3}+c^{3}+c=c \]

Part (b) Add them: \(f(c)+f(-c)\)

Now combine the two results and simplify.

\(c^3+c\) (even part) = —
\(c^3\) (odd part of \(f(c)\)) = —
\(f(c)+f(-c)\) = —
\(2c^{3}+2c\) = —

The \(c^{3}\) terms from \(f(c)\) and \(f(-c)\) cancel? No — \(f(-c)\) has no \(c^{3}\) left; the two sums give \(2c^{3}+2c\). The stacked bars always match the algebra, whatever \(c\) is.

1 Add the two values
\[ f(c)+f(-c)=\bigl(2c^{3}+c\bigr)+(c) \]
Hint: Use the results from Part (a): \(f(c)=2c^{3}+c\) and \(f(-c)=c\).
2 Collect like terms
\[ =2c^{3}+c+c=2c^{3}+2c \]
Hint: \(c+c=2c\). A handy check: the answer must stay valid for \(c=0\), where both sides give \(0\).
\[ f(c)+f(-c)=2c^{3}+2c \]
3 Choose the option
\[ 2c^{3}+2c \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \textbf{C} \]
Hint: Option B is \(-2c^{3}+2c\) — the sign trap from forgetting \((-c)^{3}=-c^{3}\). Option D drops the odd part entirely.
Correct answer: C   \(2c^{3}+2c\)

Summary of Answers

  • \(f(c)=2c^{3}+c\)
  • \(f(-c)=c\)
  • \(f(c)+f(-c)=2c^{3}+2c\)  →  Answer C
  • Key idea: the odd part \(x^{3}\) keeps the sign of \(c\), the even part \(c x^{2}+c\) always gives \(c^{3}+c\).
