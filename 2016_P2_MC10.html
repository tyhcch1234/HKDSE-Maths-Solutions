QUESTION [2016MC#10]

The monthly salary of Donald is 25% higher than that of Peter while the monthly salary of Peter is 25% lower than that of Teresa. It is given that the monthly salary of Donald is $33,360. The monthly salary of Teresa is

A. $31,275
B. $33,360
C. $35,584
D. $52,125

Step-by-Step Guided Solution

1

Understand the relationship between Donald and Peter

Hint: "25% higher" means the base is Peter's salary. The multiplier is $(1 + 25\%) = 1.25$.

Let Peter's monthly salary be $P$.

Donald's salary is 25% higher than Peter's, so:

$\text{Donald} = P \times (1 + 25\%) = 1.25P$

We know Donald's salary is \$33,360.

2

Find Peter's salary

Hint: Rearrange $1.25P = 33360$ to solve for $P$.

From Step 1: $1.25P = 33360$

Divide both sides by $1.25$:

$P = \frac{33360}{1.25} = 33360 \div 1.25 = 26688$

So Peter's monthly salary is \$26,688.

3

Understand the relationship between Peter and Teresa

Hint: "25% lower than Teresa" means the base is Teresa's salary. The multiplier is $(1 - 25\%) = 0.75$.

Let Teresa's monthly salary be $T$.

Peter's salary is 25% lower than Teresa's, so:

$\text{Peter} = T \times (1 - 25\%) = 0.75T$

We found Peter's salary in Step 2: $P = 26688$.

4

Find Teresa's salary (Final Answer)

Hint: $0.75T = 26688$. Divide both sides by $0.75$ to isolate $T$.

From Step 3: $0.75T = 26688$

Divide both sides by $0.75$:

$T = \frac{26688}{0.75} = 26688 \div 0.75 = 35584$

Teresa's monthly salary = $35,584 → Option C

Now you can reveal the official answer.

Correct Answer: C — $35,584

Official solution: $33360 \div 1.25 \div 0.75 = 35584$
