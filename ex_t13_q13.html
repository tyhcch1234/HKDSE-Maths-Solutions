Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Ratios

T13 Q13

Find the value of $\sin 45^\circ$.

Solution

Consider a $45^\circ-45^\circ-90^\circ$ right triangle.
Let each leg have length $1$. By Pythagoras' theorem, the hypotenuse is $\sqrt{1^2+1^2}=\sqrt{2}$.
For a $45^\circ$ angle, $\sin 45^\circ=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$.
Thus, $\sin 45^\circ=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
Therefore, $\sin 45^\circ=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
