QQuestion

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a square. \(E\) is a point lying on \(AB\) produced such that \(BE = 4\text{ cm}\). \(BC\) and \(DE\) intersect at the point \(F\). If \(EF = 5\text{ cm}\), then \(DF = \)

A. \(20\text{ cm}\)
B. \(15\text{ cm}\)
C. \(12\text{ cm}\)
D. \(\text{?}\)

(The four picture options \(20\text{ cm}\), \(15\text{ cm}\), \(12\text{ cm}\) and one further length are the choices; here we derive the correct one.)

12 cm
EF = 5 cm (given)
1Read the figure: what is given

\(ABCD\) is a square, so all sides are equal and every angle is \(90^\circ\). \(E\) lies on \(AB\) produced, i.e. beyond \(B\), with \(BE = 4\text{ cm}\). \(BC\) and \(DE\) cross at \(F\), and \(EF = 5\text{ cm}\).

Let \(AB = BC = CD = DA = s\). Notice the right angle at \(B\) (in \(\triangle EBF\)) and the right angle at \(A\) (in \(\triangle EAD\)).

Hint: An angle at \(B\) is \(90^\circ\) because the square corner meets the extension line \(EB\), and \(BC \perp AB\).
2Prove \(\triangle EBF \sim \triangle EAD\)

Compare \(\triangle EBF\) and \(\triangle EAD\):

  • \(\angle BEF = \angle AED\) (common angle at \(E\));
  • \(\angle EBF = \angle EAD = 90^\circ\) (square corner and the extension).

Hence the two triangles are equiangular, so \(\triangle EBF \sim \triangle EAD\) (AAA).

Hint: Write the vertices in matching order — the vertex \(E\) matches \(E\), \(B\) matches \(A\), \(F\) matches \(D\).
3Set up the ratio of corresponding sides

Corresponding sides are proportional:

\[ \frac{EF}{ED} = \frac{EB}{EA} = \frac{BF}{AD} \]

Use the pieces we know: \(EB = 4\), \(EA = EB + BA = 4 + s\), and \(AD = s\). Let \(DF = x\), so \(ED = EF + FD = 5 + x\).

Hint: Keep \(E\!-\!B\!-\!A\) and \(E\!-\!F\!-\!D\) collinear in mind — both \(EF,ED\) and \(EB,EA\) lie along the same rays.

bPart (b) — Solve for \(DF\)

Live solution. The slider scans candidate values of \(DF = x\); the readout shows how the two sides of the proportion \(\frac{EF}{ED}=\frac{EB}{EA}\) change. When they match, the figure is consistent — at \(x = 15\).

s = 12 cm
EF/ED  vs  EB/EA
1Write the proportion with \(s\) and \(x\)

Substituting \(EF = 5\), \(ED = 5 + x\), \(EB = 4\), \(EA = 4 + s\):

\[ \frac{5}{5+x} = \frac{4}{4+s} \]
Hint: At this stage we have two unknowns \(s\) and \(x\) — but the third ratio \(\dfrac{BF}{AD}\) will help us close the gap.
2Extract \(s\) in terms of \(x\)

Cross-multiply the proportion:

\[ 5(4+s) = 4(5+x) \Rightarrow 20 + 5s = 20 + 4x \Rightarrow s = \frac{4x}{5} \]
Hint: The constants \(20\) cancel — that's the whole trick of this question.
3Use the ratio involving \(BF\)

From the same similarity, \(\dfrac{BF}{AD} = \dfrac{EB}{EA} = \dfrac{4}{4+s}\). With \(AD = s\):

\[ BF = s \cdot \frac{4}{4+s} \]

But \(BF = BC - FC = s - FC\), and \(\triangle EBF\) is right-angled at \(B\), so Pythagoras gives \(BF^2 + 4^2 = 5^2\):

\[ BF^2 = 25 - 16 = 9 \Rightarrow BF = 3 \text{ cm} \]
Hint: In \(\triangle EBF\): hypotenuse \(EF = 5\), leg \(EB = 4\), so it is a 3‑4‑5 triangle. In HKDSE figures, looking for \(3\!-\!4\!-\!5\) is often the fastest route.
4Solve the simple linear equation

Now use \(BF = 3\) together with \(\dfrac{EB}{EA} = \dfrac{BF}{AD}\):

\[ \frac{4}{4+s} = \frac{3}{s} \Rightarrow 4s = 12 + 3s \Rightarrow s = 12 \]

Then substitute back into \(s = \dfrac{4x}{5}\):

\[ 12 = \frac{4x}{5} \Rightarrow 4x = 60 \Rightarrow x = 15 \]
\(DF = 15\text{ cm}\)  →  answer B

cPart (c) — Verify numerically

Check the answer with the scale drawing. If \(DF = 15\) cm and \(s = 12\) cm, then \(DE = 5 + 15 = 20\), \(EA = 4 + 12 = 16\), \(AD = 12\).

\[ \frac{EF}{ED} = \frac{5}{20} = \frac{1}{4},\qquad \frac{EB}{EA} = \frac{4}{16} = \frac{1}{4}\quad\checkmark \]

And \(BF = s \cdot \tfrac{EB}{EA} = 12 \cdot \tfrac14 = 3\), consistent with the \(3\!-\!4\!-\!5\) side \(EF = 5\) (since \(3^2 + 4^2 = 25\)).

Step-back insight: The whole question collapses once you spot the 3‑4‑5 right triangle at \(B\): \(BE = 4\), \(EF = 5\), so \(BF = 3\). Then the same similarity \(\dfrac{EB}{EA}=\dfrac{BF}{AD}\) gives \(s\), and \(\dfrac{EF}{ED}=\dfrac{EB}{EA}\) gives \(DF\). No trig needed.

✓Summary of Answers

  • Square side length: \(s = AB = BC = CD = DA = 12\text{ cm}\).
  • Key similar pair: \(\triangle EBF \sim \triangle EAD\) (AAA).
  • Slanted segment lengths: \(ED = 20\text{ cm}\), \(EA = 16\text{ cm}\), \(BF = 3\text{ cm}\).
  • Required quantity: \(DF = ED - EF = 20 - 5 = \mathbf{15\text{ cm}}\).
  • Correct option: B.
