HKDSE 2026 · Paper 2 · MC29

Box-and-Whisker Diagram

Question

Which of the following can be obtained from any box-and-whisker diagram?

I. Mean
II. Lower quartile
III. Inter-quartile range

  • A. I only
  • B. II only
  • C. I and III only
  • D. II and III only

Solution

What it shows
Five-Number Summary \(\min,\ Q_{1},\ \text{median},\ Q_{3},\ \max\) A box-and-whisker diagram marks exactly these five values.
A box-and-whisker diagram gives the minimum, lower quartile \(Q_1\),
median, upper quartile \(Q_3\) and maximum.
Read off
Inter-Quartile Range \(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) The box edges are \(Q_{1}\) and \(Q_{3}\), so the IQR is determined.
The lower quartile (II) is shown directly, and IQR \(=Q_3-Q_1\) (III) follows.
Mean
Mean is Not Shown on a Box Plot \(\text{mean}\ne\text{median in general}\) The mean uses every datum, so the five-number summary cannot give it.
The mean (I) cannot be determined from the diagram.
Conclusion Hence II and III only.
Answer: D — II and III only

Question. The box-and-whisker diagram shows the distribution of the ages of a group of teachers. Which of the following can be determined from the diagram? I. the mean age II. the range of the ages III. the inter-quartile range of the ages

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The diagram marks the minimum, \(Q_{1}\), the median, \(Q_{3}\) and the maximum.
Step 2. The range \(=\text{maximum}-\text{minimum}\) (II) and the IQR \(=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) (III) can both be read off.
Step 3. The mean (I) depends on the sum of all the data,
which the diagram does not show.
Step 4. Hence II and III only.
Answer: II and III only
