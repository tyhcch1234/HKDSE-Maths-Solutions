Inequalities and Linear Programming (46) · Sub-Topic: Linear Programming

T9 Q45

Sketch the region that satisfies both inequalities $y \ge x$ and $y \le 4$.

Solution

Draw the boundary line $y=x$ as a solid line because the inequality is $y\ge x$.
Draw the boundary line $y=4$ as a solid horizontal line because the inequality is $y\le 4$.
Test a point such as $(0,0)$: $0\ge 0$ and $0\le 4$, so shade the side containing $(0,0)$.
The required region is the intersection of the half-plane above/on $y=x$ and the half-plane below/on $y=4$, equivalently $x\le y\le 4$.
The two boundary lines meet at $(4,4)$, and the shaded region is the unbounded wedge extending down-left from $(4,4)$, including both boundary lines.
The required region is the unbounded region satisfying $x\le y\le 4$, bounded by the solid lines $y=x$ and $y=4$, with vertex $(4,4)$ and both boundary lines included.
