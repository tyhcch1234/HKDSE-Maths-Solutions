Circle Geometry — Tangent, Radii & Isosceles Triangles

Tangent ⟂ Radius Isosceles Triangle Base Angles Angle Sum

QQuestion MC

In the figure, \(O\) is the centre of the circle \(ABC\). \(DE\) is the tangent to the circle at \(A\). If \(\angle BAD = 68^\circ\) and \(\angle BCO = 26^\circ\), then \(\angle ABC =\)

A. \(42^\circ\)
B. \(48^\circ\)
C. \(54^\circ\)
D. \(64^\circ\)

Given: \(\angle BAD = 68^\circ\), \(\angle BCO = 26^\circ\). Find \(\angle ABC\).

aUse the tangent–radius property to find \(\angle BAO\)

\(\angle DAO = 90.0°\) \(\angle BAD = 68.0°\) \(\angle BAO = 22.0°\)
Tangent \(DE\) Radius \(OA\) Chord \(AB\) Angle arc
Step 1

Since \(DE\) is the tangent at \(A\) and \(OA\) is a radius, the tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact:

\[ \angle DAO = 90^\circ \qquad (\text{tangent} \perp \text{radius}) \]
💡 The tangent–radius theorem is the key fact that lets you “create” a right angle at \(A\).
Step 2

From the figure, \(B\) lies inside \(\angle DAO\), so:

\[ \angle BAO = \angle DAO - \angle BAD = 90^\circ - 68^\circ = 22^\circ \]
Answer for part (a): \(\angle BAO = 22^\circ\)
0 / 2

bUse isosceles triangles \(ABO\) and \(BCO\) to find \(\angle ABC\)

\(\angle BAO = 22°\) \(\angle ABO = 22°\) \(\angle BCO = 26°\) \(\angle CBO = 26°\) \(\angle ABC = 48°\)
Radius \(OB, OA, OC\) \(\triangle ABO\) \(\triangle BCO\)
Step 1

In \(\triangle ABO\), \(OA = OB\) because both are radii of the same circle. Hence \(\triangle ABO\) is isosceles and its base angles are equal:

\[ \angle ABO = \angle BAO = 22^\circ \qquad (\text{base } \angle\text{s, isos. } \triangle) \]
💡 Radii of the same circle are equal — this instantly gives an isosceles triangle whenever two radii meet at the centre.
Step 2

In \(\triangle BCO\), \(OB = OC\) (radii), so \(\triangle BCO\) is also isosceles:

\[ \angle CBO = \angle BCO = 26^\circ \qquad (\text{base } \angle\text{s, isos. } \triangle) \]
Step 3

The angle \(\angle ABC\) is the sum of the two angles at \(B\):

\[ \angle ABC = \angle ABO + \angle CBO = 22^\circ + 26^\circ = 48^\circ \]
Answer: \(\angle ABC = 48^\circ\) → B
💡 Check: \(48^\circ\) matches option B exactly.
0 / 3

Summary of Answers

  • \(\angle DAO = 90^\circ\) (tangent ⟂ radius)
  • \(\angle BAO = 90^\circ - 68^\circ = \mathbf{22^\circ}\)
  • \(\angle ABO = \angle BAO = 22^\circ\) (base angles, isosceles \(\triangle ABO\))
  • \(\angle CBO = \angle BCO = 26^\circ\) (base angles, isosceles \(\triangle BCO\))
  • \(\angle ABC = 22^\circ + 26^\circ = \mathbf{48^\circ}\)
