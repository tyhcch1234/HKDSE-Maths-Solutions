HKDSE 2013 Paper 2 — Question 1

Simplify \(\left(27\cdot 9^{\,n+1}\right)^{3}\).

A.  \(3^{\,6n+12}\)
B.  \(3^{\,6n+15}\)
C.  \(3^{\,9n+12}\)
D.  \(3^{\,9n+18}\)

Solution

1Rewrite every base as a power of 3.
\[27=3^{3},\qquad 9=3^{2}\]
Hint: choose a common base — here everything is a power of 3.
2Convert the product inside the bracket.
\[27\cdot 9^{\,n+1}=3^{3}\cdot\left(3^{2}\right)^{\,n+1}\]
Hint: \((a^{m})^{k}=a^{mk}\) — multiply the exponents.
Power Law of Indices \((a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn}\) \(9^{n+1}=(3^{2})^{n+1}=3^{2n+2}\), so the bracket becomes a single power of 3.
3Simplify inside the bracket.
\[3^{3}\cdot 3^{\,2(n+1)}=3^{\,3+2(n+1)}=3^{\,2n+5}\]
Hint: \(a^{m}\cdot a^{k}=a^{m+k}\) — add the exponents.
Product Law of Indices \(a^{m}\times a^{n}=a^{m+n}\) Multiplying \(3^{3}\) by \(3^{2n+2}\) adds the exponents to give \(3^{2n+5}\).
4Raise the whole bracket to the power 3.
\[\left(3^{\,2n+5}\right)^{3}=3^{\,3(2n+5)}=3^{\,6n+15}\]
Hint: multiply the outer exponent into the inner exponent.
5Match with the options.
\[3^{\,6n+15}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \textbf{B}\]
Hint: option A uses \(3^{6n+12}\) — a sign slip in \(3+2(n+1)\).
Answer:  B  \(\left(3^{\,6n+15}\right)\)

Question. Express \(\left(9\cdot27^{\,n+1}\right)^{2}\) in terms of \(3\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(9=3^{2}\) and \(27=3^{3}\).
Step 2. \(9\cdot27^{\,n+1}=3^{2}\cdot3^{3(n+1)}=3^{2+3n+3}=3^{3n+5}\).
Step 3. \(\left(3^{3n+5}\right)^{2}=3^{2(3n+5)}=3^{6n+10}\).
Answer: \(3^{6n+10}\)
