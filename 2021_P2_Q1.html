HKDSE 2021 Paper 2 — Q1

Simplify  $\dfrac{(2^{n})(8^{3n})}{64^{n}}$

  • A.  $4^{n}$
  • B.  $4^{2n}$
  • C.  $4^{-3n}$
  • D.  $4^{-4n}$
1

Rewrite every number with the SAME base

Notice that $8 = 2^{3}$ and $64 = 2^{6}$. Converting everything to base $2$ lets us combine the indices. \[ 2^{n}\cdot 8^{3n} = 2^{n}\left(2^{3}\right)^{3n}, \qquad 64^{n} = \left(2^{6}\right)^{n} \]
Hint: a common base is the key to every index simplification.
2

Apply the power law $(a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn}$

\[ \left(2^{3}\right)^{3n} = 2^{9n}, \qquad \left(2^{6}\right)^{n} = 2^{6n} \] \[ \Rightarrow \frac{2^{n}\cdot 2^{9n}}{2^{6n}} \]
Multiply the inside exponent by the outside exponent.
3

Apply the product law on the numerator

\[ 2^{n}\cdot 2^{9n} = 2^{\,n+9n} = 2^{10n} \] \[ \Rightarrow \frac{2^{10n}}{2^{6n}} \]
When multiplying powers of the same base, add the indices.
4

Apply the quotient law $a^{m}\div a^{n}=a^{m-n}$

\[ \frac{2^{10n}}{2^{6n}} = 2^{\,10n-6n} = 2^{4n} \]
When dividing powers of the same base, subtract the indices.
5

Convert to base $4$ to match the options

Since $4 = 2^{2}$, \[ 2^{4n} = \left(2^{2}\right)^{2n} = 4^{2n} \]
The options are in base 4, so re-express the answer as a power of 4.
6

Check against the options

$4^{2n}$ matches option B.
Quick sanity check with $n=1$: original $=\frac{2\cdot 8^{3}}{64}=\frac{2\cdot 512}{64}=16=4^{2}$. ✓
Answer: B   $4^{2n}$
