HKDSE 2015 · Paper 1 · Q17

Sequences and Logarithms

Question

For any positive integer \(n\), let \(A(n) = 4n - 5\) and \(B(n) = 10^{\,4n-5}\).

  1. Express \(A(1)+A(2)+A(3)+\cdots+A(n)\) in terms of \(n\). (2 marks)
  2. Find the greatest value of \(n\) such that \(\log\big(B(1)B(2)B(3)\cdots B(n)\big) \le 8000\). (3 marks)

Solution

Step 1 · Part (a): sum the linear terms

Since \(A(k) = 4k-5\),

\[ A(1)+A(2)+\cdots+A(n) = \sum_{k=1}^{n}(4k-5) = 4\sum_{k=1}^{n}k - 5\sum_{k=1}^{n}1. \]
Step 2 · Part (a): simplify \[ = 4\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}{2} - 5n = 2n(n+1) - 5n = 2n^2 - 3n. \]
Step 3 · Part (b): turn the product into a sum of exponents \[ B(1)B(2)\cdots B(n) = 10^{(4\cdot1-5)}\cdot 10^{(4\cdot2-5)}\cdots 10^{(4n-5)} = 10^{\,\sum_{k=1}^{n}(4k-5)}. \]
Step 4 · Take common logarithms \[ \log\big(B(1)\cdots B(n)\big) = \sum_{k=1}^{n}(4k-5) = 2n^2 - 3n, \]

using the result of part (a).

Step 5 · Solve the inequality \[ 2n^2 - 3n \le 8000 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 2n^2 - 3n - 8000 \le 0. \]

The equation \(2n^2-3n-8000 = 0\) has roots

\[ n = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{9 + 4\cdot2\cdot8000}}{4} = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{64009}}{4} = \frac{3 \pm 253}{4}, \]

so \(n = 64\) or \(n = -62.5\). Since the parabola opens upward, \(2n^2-3n-8000\le 0\) for \(-62.5 \le n \le 64\).

For positive integers, \(1 \le n \le 64\). Check: \(2(64)^2 - 3(64) = 8192 - 192 = 8000\). ✓

Final Answer: (a) \(2n^2 - 3n\)   (b) The greatest value of \(n\) is \(64\).
