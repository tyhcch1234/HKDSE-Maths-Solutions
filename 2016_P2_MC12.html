Topic 2 · Rate, Ratio and Variations

Variation Direct Variation Inverse Variation Percentage Change

The Question

It is given that $z$ varies directly as $\sqrt{x}$ and inversely as $y$.

If $x$ is decreased by $36\%$ and $y$ is increased by $60\%$, then $z$

Step-by-Step Solution

“$z$ varies directly as $\sqrt{x}$” means $z \propto \sqrt{x}$.

“$z$ varies inversely as $y$” means $z \propto \dfrac{1}{y}$.

Combining both, we introduce a non-zero constant $k$:

$$z = k\,\frac{\sqrt{x}}{y}$$
Hint 💡 — In a joint variation, direct variation goes on the numerator and inverse variation goes on the denominator. The constant $k$ absorbs all the “proportionality”.

“$x$ is decreased by $36\%$”: the new $x$ is

$$x' = (1 - 0.36)\,x = 0.64\,x$$

“$y$ is increased by $60\%$”: the new $y$ is

$$y' = (1 + 0.60)\,y = 1.6\,y$$
Hint 💡 — A “$p\%$ change” multiplies the original quantity by $(1 \pm \tfrac{p}{100})$. Decrease $\Rightarrow$ minus; Increase $\Rightarrow$ plus.

Write the new value of $z$ as

$$z' = (1 + p)\,z$$

We want to find $p$. Since $k$ is a constant, it cancels when we form a ratio:

$$\frac{z'}{z} = \frac{k\sqrt{x'}/y'}{k\sqrt{x}/y}$$

Replace $x'$ with $0.64x$ and $y'$ with $1.6y$:

$$\frac{(1+p)z}{z} = \frac{\sqrt{0.64\,x}\;/\;(1.6\,y)}{\sqrt{x}\;/\;y}$$

The $z$ on the left cancels, and the $x$ and $y$ on the right also cancel:

$$1+p = \frac{\sqrt{0.64}}{1.6}$$
$$\sqrt{0.64} = 0.8,\qquad \frac{0.8}{1.6} = 0.5$$

So $$1 + p = 0.5$$

$$p = 0.5 - 1 = -0.5$$

Hint 💡 — A negative $p$ means a decrease. $p = -0.5$ $\Rightarrow$ $50\%$ decrease.

$z$ is decreased by $50\%$.

✓ The answer is option D
