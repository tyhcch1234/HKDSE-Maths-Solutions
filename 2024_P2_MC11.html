Percentages
Percentages Word Problem MC

Question

In a school, 40% of the students are girls and β% of the girls are foreign students. It is given that 30% of the boys in the school are foreign students. In the school, the number of foreign students and the number of girls are equal. Find β.

A
20
B
45
C ✓
55
D
80

🧩 Step-by-Step Solution

Click each step to expand the explanation.

Let \(x\) be the total number of students in the school.

Since \(40\%\) of the students are girls, the number of girls is:

\(40\% \times x = 0.4x\)

Therefore the number of boys is:

\(x - 0.4x = 0.6x = (1 - 40\%)\times x\)

💡 Hint: Choosing \(x\) as the total number of students makes all percentages easy to convert into actual counts. The percentages \(40\%\) and \(60\%\) refer to the same total \(x\).

β% of the girls are foreign students.

Number of foreign girls = \(40\% \times x \times β\%\)

\(= 0.4x \times \dfrac{β}{100} = 0.4x \cdot \dfrac{β}{100}\)

💡 Hint: We take β% of the girls, not of the total. So we multiply the number of girls \(0.4x\) by \(\frac{β}{100}\).

30% of the boys are foreign students.

Number of boys = \(x(1 - 40\%) = 0.6x\)

Number of foreign boys = \(0.6x \times 30\% = 0.6x \times 0.3 = 0.18x\)

\(= x(1 - 40\%) \times 30\%\)

💡 Hint: The boys are \(60\%\) of the total. So number of boys = \(0.6x\). Take \(30\%\) of that to get foreign boys.

The number of foreign students equals the number of girls:

\[ \underbrace{0.4x \cdot \frac{β}{100}}_{\text{foreign girls}} + \underbrace{0.6x \cdot 0.3}_{\text{foreign boys}} = \underbrace{0.4x}_{\text{girls}} \]

This matches the given condition: the number of foreign students and the number of girls are equal.

💡 Hint: Foreign students = foreign girls + foreign boys. The problem tells us this equals the number of girls \(0.4x\).

Divide both sides by \(x\) (non-zero):

\(0.4 \cdot \frac{β}{100} + 0.18 = 0.4\)

Multiply the whole equation by 100:

\(0.4β + 18 = 40\)

\(0.4β = 22\)

\(β = \frac{22}{0.4} = 55\)

✅ Answer: C (β = 55)
