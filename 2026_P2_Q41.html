HKDSE 2026 · Paper 2 · MC41

Trigonometric Equation

Question

For \(90^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\), how many roots does the equation \(6\sin^4\theta-5\sin^2\theta+1=0\) have?

  • A. \(2\)
  • B. \(4\)
  • C. \(6\)
  • D. \(8\)

Solution

Substitute Let \(u=\sin^2\theta\): \(6u^2-5u+1=0\Rightarrow(2u-1)(3u-1)=0\Rightarrow u=\tfrac12\) or \(\tfrac13\).
Case u=1/2 \(\sin\theta=\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\Rightarrow\theta=135^\circ,225^\circ,315^\circ\) (3 roots).
Case u=1/3 \(\sin\theta=\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\Rightarrow\theta\approx144.7^\circ,215.3^\circ,324.7^\circ\) (3 roots).
Total 6 roots.
Answer: C — 6
