Stem-and-Leaf: Quartiles, Range & Probability

Question

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of driving hours of a group of taxi drivers in a week.

Stem (tens) Leaf (units)
25   6   9
32   3   3   6   6   7   8
40   0   2   2   5   5   7   7   7
5\(w\)

The ratio of the range to the inter-quartile range of the distribution is \(7:3\).

(a)   Find \(w\). (3 marks)

(i)  Write down the mode of the distribution.

(ii)  If two taxi drivers are randomly selected from the group, find the probability that the mean of their numbers of driving hours in the week exceeds the mode of the distribution. (3 marks)

Solution

STEP 1 — list the 21 values (with w = 3, max = 53)
25, 26, 29, 32, 33, 33, 36, 36, 37, 38, 40, 40, 42, 42, 45, 45, 45, 47, 47, 47, (50 + w). The smallest value is 25, so the range is \((50+w)-25 = 25+w\).
STEP 2 — find the quartiles
\(n=21\), so the median is the 11th value \(= 40\).
\(Q_1\) = mean of the 5th and 6th values of the lower half \(= (33+33)/2 = 33\).
\(Q_3\) = mean of the 5th and 6th values of the upper half \(= (45+45)/2 = 45\).
IQR \(= Q_3 - Q_1 = 45-33 = 12\).
PART (a) — use the ratio 7 : 3
\[ \frac{\text{Range}}{\text{IQR}}=\frac{7}{3} \implies \frac{25+w}{12}=\frac{7}{3} \] \[ 25+w=28 \implies w=3 \]
PART (b)(i) — mode
Both 45 and 47 appear three times (the highest frequency), so the distribution is bimodal: the modes are \(45\) and \(47\).
PART (b)(ii) — probability
Total ways to choose 2 drivers: \({}^{21}C_{2}=210\).
Let the two values be \(x\) and \(y\); we need \(x+y > 2\times47=94\) (the mean exceeds the mode, i.e. exceeds both modes).
Only pairs containing 53 can qualify (47 + 47 = 94 is not > 94): 53 with 42, 45 or 47: \[ 1\times(2+3+3)=8 \text{ pairs}. \] \[ P=\frac{8}{210}=\frac{4}{105} \]
Note: if "the mode" is taken to be 45 only (mean > 45, i.e. sum > 90), the favourable pairs are: 53 with {40,42,45,47} (10 pairs), 47 with 45 (9 pairs), and 47 with 47 (3 pairs), giving \(22/210 = 11/105\).
(a) \(w=3\)    (b)(i) 45 and 47 (bimodal)    (b)(ii) \(P=\dfrac{4}{105}\) (exceeding both modes; \(11/105\) if "mode" means 45)
