Tangent, Equal Chords & the Alternate Segment Theorem

Question

2018 MC #39

In the figure, TA is the tangent to the circle ABCD at the point A. CD produced and TA produced meet at the point E. It is given that AB = CD, ∠BAT = 24° and ∠AED = 72°. Find ∠ABC.

A · 66°
B · 60°
C · 72°
D · 78°

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 · Name the unknown

Let

$$\angle ABC = x$$

Everything is then expressed in terms of the single unknown $x$.

Step 2 · Tangent–chord (alternate segment) at A
Tangent–Chord Angle (Alternate Segment) \(\text{angle between tangent and chord}=\text{angle in the alternate segment}\) Since \\(\\angle BAT=24^\circ\\), the angle \\(\\angle ACB\\) subtended by chord \\(AB\\) is also \\(24^\circ\\).

The tangent $TA$ meets the chord $AC$ at $A$:

$$\angle ACB=\angle BAT=24^\circ \quad (\angle\text{ in alt. segment})$$

The angle between tangent $TA$ and chord $AC$ equals the angle in the opposite (alternate) segment, subtended by $AC$ — that is $\angle ABC$… careful: it matches the angle in the other segment, i.e. $\angle ACB$ for chord $AB$. Here the tangent–chord pair is $(TA,\,AC)$ giving $\angle ACB$ via chord $AB$; the standard statement is $\angle ACB=\angle BAT$.

Step 3 · Equal chords give equal angles
Equal Chords Subtend Equal Angles \(AB=CD\ \Longrightarrow\ \angle ACB=\angle CBD\) The equal chords \\(AB\\) and \\(CD\\) subtend equal angles at the circumference, so \\(\\angle CBD=24^\circ\\).

Since $AB=CD$ (equal chords), the angles they subtend at the circumference are equal:

$$\angle CBD=\angle ACB=24^\circ \quad (\text{equal chords, equal }\angle\text{ in same segment})$$

Step 4 · Split ∠ABC and transfer with the same segment

Because $BD$ lies inside $\angle ABC$:

$$\angle ABD=\angle ABC-\angle CBD=x-24^\circ$$

Angles in the same segment standing on chord $AD$:

$$\angle ACD=\angle ABD=x-24^\circ$$

Step 5 · Tangent–chord again at A (for chord AC)

The angle between tangent $AE$ ($=TA$) and chord $AC$ equals the angle in the alternate segment:

$$\angle CAE=\angle ABC=x \quad (\angle\text{ in alt. segment})$$

Step 6 · Angle sum in △ACE
Angle Sum of a Triangle \(\angle A+\angle B+\angle C=180^\circ\) In \\(\\triangle ACE\\): \\(72^\circ+(x-24^\circ)+x=180^\circ\\), which gives \\(x=66^\circ\\).

In $\triangle ACE$:

$$\angle AEC+\angle ACE+\angle CAE=180^\circ \quad (\angle\text{ sum of }\triangle)$$

Now $\angle AEC=\angle AED=72^\circ$ (the same angle at $E$) and $\angle ACE=\angle ACD=x-24^\circ$, $\angle CAE=x$:

$$72^\circ+(x-24^\circ)+x=180^\circ$$

Step 7 · Solve

$$72+x-24+x=180$$

$$2x+48=180 \;\Rightarrow\; 2x=132$$

$$\boxed{x=66^\circ}$$

Key idea: the tangent at $A$ lets you convert ∠BAT = 24° into an angle on the circle, and $AB=CD$ links the two chords. Then everything collapses into the triangle $ACE$ with ∠AED = 72°.
Answer:  ∠ABC = 66° (Option A)
