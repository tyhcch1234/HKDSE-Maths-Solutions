Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q21

A rectangular prism has a base area of $20\text{ cm}^2$ and a height of $8\text{ cm}$. Find its volume.

Solution

The volume of a rectangular prism is given by $V = \text{base area} \times \text{height}$.
Substitute the given values: $V = 20 \times 8$.
Calculate: $V = 160$.
The volume of the rectangular prism is $160\text{ cm}^3$.
