QQuestion

Let \(g(x)=13-5x^{2}\). If \(\alpha\) is a constant, find \(g(1-3\alpha)\).
A. \(\;8-45\alpha^{2}\)
B. \(\;8+45\alpha^{2}\)
C. \(\;8+30\alpha-45\alpha^{2}\)
D. \(\;8+30\alpha+45\alpha^{2}\)

1Understanding the Substitution \(x \mapsto 1-3\alpha\)

Step 1 Identify the input of \(g\) 1M
We are asked for \(g(1-3\alpha)\). Comparing with \(g(x)\), the input is \[ x = 1-3\alpha . \]
Hint: In \(g(\;\;)\), whatever is inside the bracket is the input. Here the bracket contains \(1-3\alpha\), so that whole expression plays the role of \(x\).
Step 2 Substitute into every \(x\) 1M
Replace \(x\) by \(1-3\alpha\) in \(g(x)=13-5x^{2}\): \[ g(1-3\alpha)=13-5(1-3\alpha)^{2}. \]
Hint: The bracket \((1-3\alpha)\) must be squared as a whole. A very common mistake is writing \(13-5(1-9\alpha^2)\) — forgetting the middle term.
Step 3 Expand the square correctly 1M
\[ (1-3\alpha)^{2}=1^{2}-2(1)(3\alpha)+(3\alpha)^{2}=1-6\alpha+9\alpha^{2}. \]
Hint: Using the identity \((a-b)^{2}=a^{2}-2ab+b^{2}\) with \(a=1,\;b=3\alpha\) guarantees you keep the middle term \(-6\alpha\).
\((1-3\alpha)^{2}=1-6\alpha+9\alpha^{2}\)

Step 1 Distribute the \(-5\) 1M
\[ 13-5(1-6\alpha+9\alpha^{2}) = 13-5+30\alpha-45\alpha^{2}. \]
Hint: \(-5\times(-6\alpha)=+30\alpha\) and \(-5\times(9\alpha^{2})=-45\alpha^{2}\). Watch the signs!
Step 2 Collect constants 1M
\[ 13-5=8, \] so \[ g(1-3\alpha)=8+30\alpha-45\alpha^{2}. \]
Hint: Arrange in descending powers of \(\alpha\): the \(\alpha^{2}\) term comes first, then \(\alpha\), then the constant.
Step 3 Match the option 1A
Comparing \(8+30\alpha-45\alpha^{2}\) with the options, it matches Option C.
Hint: Option D has \(+45\alpha^{2}\); Option A is missing the middle term. Only C has the correct signs on both terms.
\[ \boxed{g(1-3\alpha)=8+30\alpha-45\alpha^{2}} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \textbf{C} \]

Summary of Answers

  • Input of \(g\): \(\;x=1-3\alpha\)
  • Substitution: \(\;g(1-3\alpha)=13-5(1-3\alpha)^{2}\)
  • Expansion: \(\;(1-3\alpha)^{2}=1-6\alpha+9\alpha^{2}\)
  • Simplification: \(\;13-5+30\alpha-45\alpha^{2}=8+30\alpha-45\alpha^{2}\)
Final answer: \(g(1-3\alpha)=8+30\alpha-45\alpha^{2}\)  →  Option C
