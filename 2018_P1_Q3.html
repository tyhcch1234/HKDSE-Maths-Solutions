Estimation, Approximation & Error

Rounding numbers to the nearest integer, decimal place, and significant figures.

Rounding Significant Figures Decimal Places Approximation

Question

(a) Round up 265.473 to the nearest integer.

(b) Round down 265.473 to 1 decimal place.

(c) Round off 265.473 to 2 significant figures.

Note the different rounding directions: up / down / off.

Step-by-Step Solution

a

Round up 265.473 to the nearest integer.

"Round up" means always take the ceiling value — move to the next integer greater than or equal to the number.

Hint: The integer part of 265.473 is 265. Since there is a non-zero decimal part, rounding up gives 266.

Answer:

266

Since 265.473 is greater than 265, rounding up to the nearest integer gives 266.

b

Round down 265.473 to 1 decimal place.

"Round down" means always take the floor value at the required decimal place — simply truncate without looking at the next digit.

Hint: 1 decimal place means keep one digit after the decimal point. Look at 265.4 — the next digit is 7, but since we round down, we do not increase the 4.

Answer:

265.4

Truncating at 1 d.p. gives 265.4 (we ignore the 7 in the hundredths place).

c

Round off 265.473 to 2 significant figures.

"Round off" means standard rounding: look at the next digit to decide whether to round up or keep the same.

Hint: The first two significant figures are 2 and 6 (i.e. 26...). The next digit is 5, so we round the second significant figure up. Remember to keep the place value with zeros: 270.

Answer:

270

The first two significant figures are 2 and 6. Since the next digit is 5, we round up: 26 → 27, giving 270 (2 s.f.).
