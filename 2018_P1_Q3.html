HKDSE 2018 · Paper 1 · Q3
  1. Round up $265.473$ to the nearest integer.
  2. Round down $265.473$ to 1 decimal place.
  3. Round off $265.473$ to 2 significant figures.
[3 marks]
Step 1 · Given value

The number is $265.473$. Its decimal structure is:

$$265.473 \;=\; \underbrace{2}_{\text{hundreds}}\underbrace{6}_{\text{tens}}\underbrace{5}_{\text{units}}.\underbrace{4}_{\text{tenths}}\underbrace{7}_{\text{hundredths}}\underbrace{3}_{\text{thousandths}}$$
Identify the place value of each digit before rounding.
Step 2 (a) · Round UP to the nearest integer
Rounding Up \(x\ \text{rounded up}=\lceil x\rceil\) Any non-zero decimal part forces \(265.473\) up to the next integer, \(266\).

“Round up” means move away from zero to the next integer,
whatever the decimal part is.

The integer part is $265$ and the decimal part is not zero,
so we move up:

$$265.473 \;\longrightarrow\; 266$$
Note: this is not the usual “round off”. Even $265.001$ would round up to $266$.
Step 3 (b) · Round DOWN to 1 d.p.
Rounding Down (Truncation) \(x\ \text{rounded down}=\lfloor 10^{d}x\rfloor/10^{d}\) Rounding down simply cuts off the digits after the tenths place, giving \(265.4\).

“Round down” = truncate at the required place, ignoring the following digits.

1 decimal place means keep the tenths digit $4$ and drop all digits after it:

$$265.4\underline{73} \;\longrightarrow\; 265.4$$
Do NOT look at the $7$ — rounding down simply cuts off.
Step 4 (c) · Round OFF to 2 s.f.
Rounding Off to Significant Figures \(\text{round the digit by the next digit: } \ge 5 \Rightarrow \text{add }1\) The third significant digit is \(5\), so \(26\) rounds up to \(27\) and becomes \(270\).

“Round off” = the normal rounding rule: check the digit after the required place.

2 significant figures: the first two significant digits are $2$ and $6$,
so the rounding digit is the $5$ (the third digit). Since $5 \ge 5$,
we round up.

$$26\underline{5}.473 \;\longrightarrow\; 26 \to 27 \;\longrightarrow\; 270$$
After rounding up $26 \to 27$, the place value of the $2$ must be kept, so replace the ones digit by $0$: $270$.
Answer:  (a) $266$    (b) $265.4$    (c) $270$

Question. For the number \(47.286\), find (a) the value rounded up to the nearest integer,
(b) the value rounded down to 2 decimal places,
(c) the value rounded off to 3 significant figures.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The integer part is \(47\) and the decimal part is not zero,
so rounded up the value is \(48\).
Step 2. Rounding down to 2 d.p. truncates after the hundredths digit: \(47.28\).
Step 3. To 3 significant figures the first three digits are \(4,7,2\);
the next digit is \(8\ge5\), so \(47.2\) rounds up to \(47.3\).
Answer: (a) \(48\) (b) \(47.28\) (c) \(47.3\)
