Question. In the figure, $ABCD$ is a rectangle. Let $E$ be a point lying on $AC$ such that $BE$ is perpendicular to $AC$. $BE$ is produced to the point $F$ such that $CF = AD$. Denote the point of intersection of $BF$ and $CD$ by $G$. Which of the following are true?
  • I. $\triangle BCE \sim \triangle CGE$
  • II. $BC^2 = BE \times BG$
  • III. $CF$ is perpendicular to $BF$
A. I and II only
B. I and III only
C. II and III only
D. I, II and III
STEP 1 · Set up the geometry

Take $ABCD$ as a rectangle with $AB$ horizontal, $BC$ vertical, and $\angle ABC = 90^\circ$. The diagonal $AC$ has slope $m_{AC} = -\,BC/AB$.

Since $BE \perp AC$ at $E$, we have $$m_{BE} = \frac{AB}{BC}.$$ So the line $BF$ (same line as $BE$, produced) makes a fixed angle with $BC$.

Key fact: $BF \perp AC$, so every triangle using a side of $BF$ together with a side of $AC$ contains a right angle.
STEP 2 · Statement I — similar triangles

In $\triangle BCE$ and $\triangle CGE$:

$\angle CEB = 90^\circ$ (given $BE \perp AC$) and $\angle CGE = 90^\circ$ — because $G$ lies on $CD$, and $CD // AB$, so $BF \perp CD$ at $G$.

Also $\angle CBE = \angle ECG$ (same angle between line $BF$ and line $AC$ for the two triangles, as $E$ lies on $AC$, $B,E,F$ are collinear, and $G$ lies on $CD$).

Hence by AA, $$\triangle BCE \sim \triangle CGE.$$ So Statement I is TRUE.

AA Similarity Test \(\angle A=\angle D,\ \angle B=\angle E\ \Longrightarrow\ \triangle ABC\sim\triangle DEF\) Both \(\triangle BCE\) and \(\triangle CGE\) have a right angle and the shared angle at \(C\).
STEP 3 · Statement II — length relation

From $\triangle BCE \sim \triangle CGE$ (Step 2), corresponding sides give $$\frac{BC}{CE} = \frac{CG}{GE}.$$ Now note that in the right-angled figure, $GE$ is the perpendicular distance setting the right-angle condition, and $EC \cdot AC = BC^2$ when $BE \perp AC$ in a rectangle:

$$\triangle BCE \sim \triangle ABC \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{BC}{AC} = \frac{BE}{AB} = \frac{CE}{BC} \;\Rightarrow\; BC^2 = AC \cdot CE.$$ Also $BF \perp AC$ gives, in right triangle $BCG$, $$BC^2 = BE \cdot BG.$$ So Statement II is TRUE.

Corresponding Sides of Similar Triangles \(\dfrac{BC}{CE}=\dfrac{CG}{GE}=\dfrac{BE}{CG}\) The similarity \(\triangle BCE\sim\triangle CGE\) gives these ratios of corresponding sides.
STEP 4 · Statement III — perpendicularity

$CF = AD = BC$ (opposite sides of the rectangle are equal). So $\triangle BCF$ is isosceles with $BC = CF$.

Also $\angle CBF = \angle CEG = 90^\circ - \angle BCA$ … but more directly: since $BC = CF$ and $BF \perp AC$, triangle $BCF$ is isosceles with equal legs $BC$ and $CF$ meeting the same base $BF$;

the base angles are equal, and because $\angle BCF$ is the angle between $CD$ and $CA$ measured at $C$, the apex angle gives $$\angle CFB = 90^\circ.$$ Hence $CF \perp BF$, so Statement III is TRUE.

Base Angles of an Isosceles Triangle \(BC=CF\ \Longrightarrow\ \angle CBF=\angle CFB\) Since \(CF=AD=BC\), \(\triangle BCF\) is isosceles and its base angles are equal.
STEP 5 · Conclusion

All three statements I, II and III are true.

Answer: D (I, II and III)
I: $\triangle BCE \sim \triangle CGE$ (AA)  ·  II: $BC^2 = BE \times BG$ (right-triangle relation)  ·  III: $CF \perp BF$ (since $BC = CF$).

Question. In the rectangle \(ABCD\), \(AB=9\ \text{cm}\) and \(BC=12\ \text{cm}\). \(E\) lies on \(AC\) such that \(BE\perp AC\). Find \(AC\), \(BE\) and \(CE\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(\angle ABC=90^{\circ}\), so \(AC=\sqrt{9^{2}+12^{2}}=\sqrt{225}=15\ \text{cm}\).
Step 2. The area of \(\triangle ABC\) gives \(\tfrac12(9)(12)=\tfrac12(15)(BE)\), so \(BE=\dfrac{108}{15}=7.2\ \text{cm}\).
Step 3. Since \(BE\perp AC\), \(BC^{2}=AC\cdot CE\), so \(CE=\dfrac{144}{15}=9.6\ \text{cm}\).
Step 4. Check: \(BE^{2}+CE^{2}=51.84+92.16=144=BC^{2}\) ✓
Answer: \(AC=15\ \text{cm}\), \(BE=7.2\ \text{cm}\), \(CE=9.6\ \text{cm}\)
