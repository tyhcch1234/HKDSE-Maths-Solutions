[2013 MC #40]

If the height of a regular tetrahedron is \(2\text{ cm}\), then the volume of the tetrahedron is

  1. \(2\text{ cm}^3\)
  2. \(\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}^3\)
  3. \(\sqrt{6}\text{ cm}^3\)
  4. \(3\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}^3\)

Multiple choice · 1 answer

aPart (a) — Relate the side \(l\) to the height

Find the median \(AM\) of the equilateral face \(ABC\), then relate \(l\) to the height \(h\).

Side \(l\) = — Face median \(AM\) = — Height \(h\) = — \(\cos\angle AMD\) = —
1Let \(l\) be the side length

Since the tetrahedron is regular, every face is an equilateral triangle of side \(l\). Let \(M\) be the mid-point of \(BC\). Then \(AM\) is a median of \(\triangle ABC\), so it is also an altitude:

\[ AM = AB\sin\angle ABC = l\sin 60^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l \]
Hint: In an equilateral triangle of side \(l\), the median/altitude always equals \(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l\).
2Work in the plane \(\triangle ADM\)

\(DM\) is likewise a median of the equilateral face \(BCD\) (since \(M\) is the mid-point of \(BC\)), so

\[ DM = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l \]

Let \(H\) be the foot of the perpendicular from \(A\) to the plane \(BCD\). Because the tetrahedron is regular, \(H\) is the centroid of \(\triangle BCD\), and \(H\) lies on the median \(DM\). Also \(AH \perp\) plane \(BCD\), so \(AH \perp DM\).

\[ AH = h = 2\text{ cm} \]
Hint: Only the triangle \(ADM\) matters — the whole 3-D problem reduces to a plane triangle once we drop the height.
3Use the cosine formula in \(\triangle ADM\)

The three sides of \(\triangle ADM\) are \(AD = l\), \(AM = DM = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l\). The angle \(\angle AMD\) is the one between a face-median and the base median:

\[ \cos\angle AMD = \frac{AM^2 + DM^2 - AD^2}{2\cdot AM\cdot DM} = \frac{\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l\right)^2 - l^2}{2\cdot\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l\cdot\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l} = \frac{1}{3} \]
Hint: Notice \(AD\) is the side opposite \(\angle AMD\). The numbers \(\frac{3}{4}l^2 + \frac{3}{4}l^2 - l^2 = \frac{1}{2}l^2\) give \(\cos\angle AMD = \frac{1}{3}\).
4Get \(l\) in terms of \(h\)

From \(\cos\angle AMD = \frac{1}{3}\) in \(\triangle ADM\) (right-angled at \(H\) after dropping the height):

\[ \sin\angle AMD = \sqrt{1-\cos^2\angle AMD} = \sqrt{1-\left(\tfrac{1}{3}\right)^2} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3} \] \[ AH = AM\sin\angle AMD = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l \cdot \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}l \] Setting \(AH=2\): \[ \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}l = 2 \;\Longrightarrow\; l = \frac{6}{\sqrt{6}} = \sqrt{6}\text{ cm} \]
Answer (a): \(l = \sqrt{6}\text{ cm}\)

bPart (b) — Base area and Volume

Compute the area of the equilateral base \(BCD\), then apply the pyramid volume formula.

\(l\) = √6 ≈ 2.449 cm Base area \(= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}l^2\) = — Height \(h\) = 2.00 cm Volume \(= \frac{1}{3}\cdot\text{base}\cdot h\) = —
1Area of an equilateral triangle

The base \(BCD\) is equilateral with side \(l=\sqrt{6}\). Using \(\text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}bc\sin A\) (or the standard formula):

\[ \text{Area of } \triangle BCD = \frac{1}{2}\cdot BC\cdot DM = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\sqrt{6}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cdot\sqrt{6} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: Equivalently, area \(=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}l^2 = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\times 6 = \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{ cm}^2\).
2Volume of the tetrahedron

A tetrahedron is a pyramid; volume \(=\frac{1}{3}\times(\text{base area})\times(\text{height})\).

\[ V = \frac{1}{3}\times \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\times 2 = \sqrt{3}\text{ cm}^3 \]
Answer (b): \(V = \sqrt{3}\text{ cm}^3\) → the correct option is B.

Alternative: You can also use Heron's formula on \(\triangle BCD\) (all sides \(\sqrt{6}\)) to get the same base area \(\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\).

Summary of Answers

  • Side length of the tetrahedron: \(l = \sqrt{6}\text{ cm}\)
  • Face median used: \(AM = DM = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}l\)
  • Key angle: \(\cos\angle AMD = \frac{1}{3}\), so \(\sin\angle AMD = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\)
  • Height relation: \(h = AH = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}l\)
  • Base area: \(\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{ cm}^2\)
  • Volume: \(V = \frac{1}{3}\times\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\times 2 = \sqrt{3}\text{ cm}^3\)
  • Correct option: B
