Question [2015MC#25]

In the figure, the equations of the straight lines \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) are \[ ax = 1 \quad\text{and}\quad bx + cy = 1 \] respectively. Which of the following are true?

I. \(\;a < 0\)
II. \(\;a < b\)
III. \(\;c > 0\)

A. I and II only
B. I and III only
C. II and III only
D. I, II and III

STEP 1 · Read the intercepts from the figure
Intercepts of a Line \(Ax+By=C:\quad x\text{-intercept}=\dfrac{C}{A},\quad y\text{-intercept}=\dfrac{C}{B}\) For \(ax=1\) and \(bx+cy=1\) the intercepts are \(\dfrac{1}{a}\), \(\dfrac{1}{b}\) and \(\dfrac{1}{c}\).

\(L_1:\;ax=1\) is a vertical line, so its \(x\)-intercept is \(x=\dfrac{1}{a}\).

\(L_2:\;bx+cy=1\) has \(x\)-intercept \(x=\dfrac{1}{b}\) (set \(y=0\)) and \(y\)-intercept \(y=\dfrac{1}{c}\) (set \(x=0\)).

From the figure: \(L_1\) is on the left of the \(y\)-axis, \(L_2\)'s \(x\)-intercept is on the right of the \(y\)-axis but right of \(L_1\), and \(L_2\) crosses the positive \(y\)-axis.
STEP 2 · Check statement I: \(\;a < 0\)
Sign of a Reciprocal \(\dfrac{1}{a}<0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ a<0\) \(L_{1}\) meets the negative \(x\)-axis, so \(\dfrac{1}{a}<0\) forces \(a<0\).

\(L_1\) has a negative \(x\)-intercept:

\[ \frac{1}{a} < 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; a < 0 \quad(\text{since } a^2>0) \]

✓ Statement I is TRUE.

STEP 3 · Test statement II: \(\;a < b\)

\(L_2\)'s \(x\)-intercept is positive,
so \(\dfrac{1}{b}>0\Rightarrow b>0\). So \(b\) is positive while \(a\) is negative.

Geometrically the \(x\)-intercept of \(L_1\) is smaller than that of \(L_2\):

\[ \frac{1}{a} < \frac{1}{b} \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{b}<0 \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{b-a}{ab}<0 \]

Since \(a<0\) and \(b>0\), \(ab<0\),
hence \(b-a>0\Rightarrow b>a\), i.e. \(\;a<b\).

✓ Statement II is TRUE.

STEP 4 · Check statement III: \(\;c > 0\)

\(L_2\) has a positive \(y\)-intercept:

\[ \frac{1}{c} > 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; c > 0 \quad(\text{since } c^2>0) \]

✓ Statement III is TRUE.

STEP 5 · Conclusion

All three statements I, II and III are true.

Only option D says "I, II and III".
Answer

D  (I, II and III)

\(a<0\), \(\;a<b\), \(\;c>0\) — all confirmed by the intercepts.
