Tangent, Chords & Angles in a Circle

In the figure, $TA$ is the tangent to the circle $ABCDE$ at the point $A$. If $\angle BAD = 64^\circ$, $\angle EAT = 38^\circ$ and $\angle DCE = 22^\circ$, then $\angle ADB =$

A. $52^\circ$    B. $56^\circ$    C. $60^\circ$    D. $68^\circ$

Step 1 · Tangent–chord angle at A
Tangent-Chord Angle (Alternate Segment Theorem) \(\angle(\text{tangent},\ \text{chord})=\angle\ \text{in the alternate segment}\) \(TA\) is tangent at \(A\) and \(AB\) is a chord, so \(\angle ABE=\angle EAT=38^{\circ}\).

$TA$ is a tangent at $A$ and $AB$ is a chord, so the angle between the tangent and the chord equals the angle in the alternate segment:

$$\angle ABE = \angle EAT = 38^\circ \quad (\angle \text{ in alt. segment})$$

Step 2 · Angles in the same segment
Angles in the Same Segment \(\angle DBE=\angle DCE\) Both angles stand on the chord \(DE\) from the same side of it.

$\angle DCE$ and $\angle DBE$ subtend the same arc $DE$ from the same side, so they are equal:

$$\angle DBE = \angle DCE = 22^\circ \quad (\angle\text{s in the same segment})$$

Step 3 · Split angle ABD

Since $E$ lies inside the angle $ABD$ (ray $BE$ lies between $BA$ and $BD$):

$$\angle ABD = \angle ABE + \angle DBE = 38^\circ + 22^\circ = 60^\circ$$

Step 4 · Angle sum of triangle ABD
Angle Sum of a Triangle \(\angle A+\angle B+\angle C=180^{\circ}\) In \(\triangle ABD\) the third angle is \(180^{\circ}-64^{\circ}-60^{\circ}\).

In $\triangle ABD$, the three angles of a triangle add up to $180^\circ$:

$$\angle ADB = 180^\circ - \angle BAD - \angle ABD$$ $$= 180^\circ - 64^\circ - 60^\circ = 56^\circ$$

💡 Exam tip: The key is to "travel" the angle from $A$ (tangent) to $B$ using the alternate segment theorem, then combine with the same-segment angle at $B$ before using the triangle sum in $\triangle ABD$.
Answer: B. $56^\circ$
$\angle ADB = 180^\circ - 64^\circ - (38^\circ + 22^\circ) = 56^\circ$

Question. \(TA\) is a tangent to a circle at \(A\), and \(E\) lies inside \(\angle ABD\) with \(B\), \(C\), \(D\), \(E\) on the circle. Given \(\angle EAT=44^{\circ}\), \(\angle DCE=25^{\circ}\) and \(\angle BAD=70^{\circ}\), find \(\angle ADB\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Tangent-chord angle: \(\angle ABE=\angle EAT=44^{\circ}\).
Step 2. Angles in the same segment: \(\angle DBE=\angle DCE=25^{\circ}\).
Step 3. So \(\angle ABD=44^{\circ}+25^{\circ}=69^{\circ}\).
Step 4. In \(\triangle ABD\): \(\angle ADB=180^{\circ}-70^{\circ}-69^{\circ}=41^{\circ}\).
Answer: \(\angle ADB=41^{\circ}\)
