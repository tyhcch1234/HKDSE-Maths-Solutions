In the figure, ABCD is a parallelogram and AEFG is a square. It is given that $$BE:EF:FC = 2:7:3,$$ and BD cuts AE and FG at the points X and Y respectively. If the area of $\triangle ABX$ is $24\text{ cm}^2$, then the area of the quadrilateral CDYF is
A. $54\text{ cm}^2$
B. $77\text{ cm}^2$
C. $81\text{ cm}^2$
D. $87\text{ cm}^2$
STEP 1 · Set up the side ratio

Let $BC = EF = DA = 2+7+3 = 12$ units (equal opposite sides of the parallelogram,
and $EF$ is a side of the square sitting on $BC$).

So $$DA:BE=BC:BE=12:2=6:1.$$

STEP 2 · Similar triangles $\triangle ADX \sim \triangle EBX$

$\angle ADX=\angle EBX$ (alt. $\angle$s, $AD // BC$)

$\angle AXD=\angle EXB$ (vert. opp. $\angle$s)

$\angle DAX=\angle BEX$ ($\angle$ sum of $\triangle$)

$\therefore\ \triangle ADX\sim\triangle EBX$ (AAA). Hence the ratio of corresponding sides is $$DX:BX = DA:BE = 6:1.$$

STEP 3 · Area of $\triangle ADX$ from a common height
Triangles with the Same Height \(\dfrac{\text{area of }\triangle ADX}{\text{area of }\triangle ABX}=\dfrac{DX}{BX}\) Both triangles share the height from \(A\) to \(DB\), so their areas follow the bases \(DX:BX=6:1\).

$\triangle ADX$ and $\triangle ABX$ share the same height from $A$ to line $DB$,
so their areas are in the ratio of their bases on that line:

$$\frac{\text{area of }\triangle ADX}{\text{area of }\triangle ABX}=\frac{DX}{BX}=6$$

With $\text{area of }\triangle ABX=24\text{ cm}^2$: $$\text{area of }\triangle ADX=24\times 6=144\text{ cm}^2.$$

STEP 4 · Area of $\triangle DAB$

$\triangle DAB$ is split by $AX$ into the two parts above, so $$\text{area of }\triangle DAB=\text{area of }\triangle ABX+\text{area of }\triangle ADX=24+144=168\text{ cm}^2.$$

STEP 5 · Similar triangles $\triangle FBX \sim \triangle ADX$ … find $\text{area of }\triangle FBY$
Area Ratio of Similar Triangles \(\dfrac{A_{1}}{A_{2}}=\left(\dfrac{\ell_{1}}{\ell_{2}}\right)^{2}\) With \(BF=9\) and \(DA=12\), the ratio is \(\left(\dfrac{9}{12}\right)^{2}=\dfrac{9}{16}\), so \(144\to 81\).

Since $AD // GF$, the alternate angles give $\triangle ADX\sim\triangle FBY$ (AAA), and $$\frac{\text{area of }\triangle FBY}{\text{area of }\triangle ADX}=\left(\frac{BF}{DA}\right)^2=\left(\frac{9}{12}\right)^2=\frac{9}{16}.$$

(Here $BF=BE+EF=2+7=9$ units.) Hence $$\text{area of }\triangle FBY=144\times\frac{9}{16}=81\text{ cm}^2.$$

STEP 6 · Solve for the quadrilateral $CDYF$
Diagonal of a Parallelogram \(\text{area of }\triangle BCD=\text{area of }\triangle DAB\) The diagonal \(DB\) splits the parallelogram into two congruent triangles of area \(168\text{ cm}^{2}\).

$\triangle BCD$ is made of the quadrilateral $CDYF$ together with $\triangle FBY$: $$\text{area of }\triangle BCD=[\text{quad. }CDYF]+81=(x+81)\text{ cm}^2.$$

But $\triangle BCD$ and $\triangle DAB$ are congruent (a diagonal splits a parallelogram into two congruent triangles),
so $$x+81=168 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=87.$$

Answer: D  ·  $87\text{ cm}^2$ Key idea: use the $12:2=6:1$ side ratio, split areas by a shared height, and use the $(9/12)^2$ area ratio of the similar triangles.

Question. In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram and \(AEFG\) is a square. It is given that \(BE:EF:FC=3:6:3\), and \(BD\) cuts \(AE\) and \(FG\) at \(X\) and \(Y\) respectively. If the area of \(\triangle ABX\) is \(24\text{ cm}^{2}\), find the area of the quadrilateral \(CDYF\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Take \(BC=EF=DA=3+6+3=12\) units, so \(DA:BE=12:3=4:1\).
Step 2. \(\triangle ADX\sim\triangle EBX\) (AAA), so \(DX:BX=DA:BE=4:1\).
Step 3. \(\triangle ADX\) and \(\triangle ABX\) share the height from \(A\) to \(DB\), so \(\text{area of }\triangle ADX=4\times 24=96\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Step 4. Hence \(\text{area of }\triangle DAB=24+96=120\text{ cm}^{2}\), and \(\text{area of }\triangle BCD=120\text{ cm}^{2}\) (diagonal of a parallelogram).
Step 5. \(BF=BE+EF=9\) units, and \(\triangle FBY\sim\triangle ADX\) gives \(\text{area of }\triangle FBY=96\times\left(\dfrac{9}{12}\right)^{2}=54\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Step 6. So \([\text{quad. }CDYF]=120-54=66\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Answer: \(66\text{ cm}^{2}\)
