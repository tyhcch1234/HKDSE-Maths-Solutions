HKDSE Math 2015 Paper 2 Q27 MC

14. Probability — Expected Gain

Probability Expected Value Two Dice Sample Space
Question: Two fair dice are thrown in a game. If the sum of the two numbers thrown is 7, $\$36$ is gained; otherwise, $\$6$ is gained. Find the expected gain of the game.
A. $\$11$
B. $\$12$
C. $\$30$
D. $\$31$

📘 Step-by-Step Solution

1 Identify the Sample Space ▼

Two fair dice are thrown. Each die has 6 faces, so the total number of equally likely outcomes is:

$6 \times 6 = 36$
💡 Hint: A "sample space" is the set of all possible outcomes. For two dice, we can list them as ordered pairs $(a, b)$ where $a$ is the first die and $b$ is the second die.
2 Define the Events $A$ and $B$ ▼

Let $A$ be the event that the sum of the two numbers is 7.

Let $B$ be the event that the sum is not 7.

💡 Hint: Events $A$ and $B$ are complementary, meaning $P(A) + P(B) = 1$.
3 Find $P(A)$ — Probability of Sum = 7 ▼

The favourable outcomes where the sum equals 7 are:

$(1,6),\ (2,5),\ (3,4),\ (4,3),\ (5,2),\ (6,1)$

There are 6 such outcomes.

$P(A) = \dfrac{6}{36} = \dfrac{1}{6}$
💡 Hint: The probability of event $A$ = $\dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total number of outcomes}}$.
4 Find $P(B)$ — Probability of Sum ≠ 7 ▼

Since $B$ is the complement of $A$:

$P(B) = 1 - P(A) = 1 - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{5}{6}$

Equivalently: $P(B) = \dfrac{36 - 6}{36} = \dfrac{30}{36} = \dfrac{5}{6}$.

5 Apply the Expected Value Formula ▼

The expected gain is the sum of each outcome multiplied by its probability:

$E = 36 \times P(A) + 6 \times P(B)$
💡 Hint: $\text{Expected Value} = \sum (\text{value} \times \text{probability})$. Here the "values" are the gains: $\$36$ when sum = 7, and $\$6$ otherwise.
6 Compute the Expected Gain ▼
$E = 36 \times \dfrac{1}{6} + 6 \times \dfrac{5}{6}$
$E = 6 + 5 = 11$
✅ Expected Gain $= \$11$  →  Answer: A

(This works because $36 \times \frac{1}{6} = 6$ and $6 \times \frac{5}{6} = 5$, giving $6 + 5 = 11$.)
