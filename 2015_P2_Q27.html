[2015MC#27] Probability & Expected Value

Two fair dice are thrown in a game. If the sum of the two numbers thrown is $7$, $\$36$ will be gained; otherwise, $\$6$ will be gained. Find the expected gain of the game.
  • A. $\$11$
  • B. $\$12$
  • C. $\$30$
  • D. $\$31$
1Define the events Let $A$ be the event that the sum of the two numbers thrown is $7$; let $B$ be the event that the sum is not $7$ (i.e. $B=A'$).
Hint: the two events $A$ and $B$ are complementary and mutually exclusive.
2Count the total outcomes Each die has $6$ equally likely faces, so the total number of possible outcomes is \[ n(S)=6\times 6 = 36. \] The $36$ outcomes are exactly the cells of the $6\times6$ grid on the left.
Hint: always start a dice problem by counting $n(S)$.
3Find $P(A)$ — sum is 7
Probability of an Event \(P(A)=\dfrac{n(A)}{n(S)}\) Six of the \(36\) outcomes give a sum of \(7\), so \(P(A)=\dfrac{1}{6}\).
The favourable outcomes are the six blue diagonal cells: \[(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1).\] \[ P(A)=\frac{6}{36}=\frac{1}{6}. \]
Hint: exactly $6$ outcomes give a sum of $7$ — one per choice of the first die.
4Find $P(B)$ — sum is not 7
Complement Rule \(P(A^{\prime})=1-P(A)\) Since \(B\) is the complement of \(A\), \(P(B)=1-\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{5}{6}\).
The remaining $36-6=30$ outcomes (grey cells) belong to $B$: \[ P(B)=\frac{36-6}{36}=\frac{30}{36}=\frac{5}{6}. \] Check: $\frac{1}{6}+\frac{5}{6}=1$. ✓
Hint: use the complement rule $P(B)=1-P(A)$.
5Compute the expected gain
Expected Value \(E(X)=\sum x_{i}\,P(X=x_{i})\) The expected gain is \(36\times\dfrac{1}{6}+6\times\dfrac{5}{6}=11\).
By the definition of expected value, \[ \begin{aligned} E &= 36\times P(A) + 6\times P(B)\\[2pt] &= 36\times\frac{1}{6} + 6\times\frac{5}{6}\\[2pt] &= 6 + 5\\[2pt] &= 11. \end{aligned} \] Hence the expected gain is $\$11$.
Hint: $\text{expected gain}=\sum (\text{gain}\times\text{probability of that gain})$.
Answer: A — the expected gain is $\$11$.

Question. Two fair dice are thrown in a game. If the sum of the two numbers thrown is \(7\), \(\$48\) will be gained; otherwise, \(\$6\) will be gained. Find the expected gain of the game.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \(A\) be the event that the sum is \(7\);
there are \(36\) equally likely outcomes in total.
Step 2. The six outcomes \((1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)\) give a sum of \(7\), so \(P(A)=\dfrac{6}{36}=\dfrac{1}{6}\).
Step 3. The complement has probability \(1-\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{5}{6}\).
Step 4. \(E=48\times\dfrac{1}{6}+6\times\dfrac{5}{6}=8+5=13\).
Answer: \(\$13\)
