Geometry — Congruent Triangles, Similar Triangles & Perimeter

Q Question 5 marks

In the figure, \(ST\) and \(UV\) intersect at the point \(W\). It is given that \(SU \;/\!/\; VT\) and \(W\) is the mid-point of \(ST\).

(a) Prove that \(\triangle SUW \cong \triangle TVW\). 2 marks

(b) Let \(X\) be a point lying on \(TW\) such that \(\triangle SUW \sim \triangle VXW\). If \(SU = 57\text{ cm}\), \(SW = 63\text{ cm}\) and \(UW = 21\text{ cm}\), find the perimeter of \(\triangle TVX\). 3 marks

(The diagram is not drawn to scale.)

a Part (a) — Prove \(\triangle SUW \cong \triangle TVW\)

We prove the two triangles are congruent by AAS (two angles and a non-included side).

0 / 3
Step 1given
\[ SW = TW \]
Reason: \(W\) is the mid-point of \(ST\), so it divides \(ST\) into two equal parts.
Step 21M
\[ \angle SWU = \angle TWV \quad (\text{vert. opp. } \angle s) \] \[ \angle WSU = \angle VTW \quad (\text{alt. } \angle s,\ SU \;/\!/\; VT) \]
Reason: \(ST\) and \(UV\) cross at \(W\), so the angles at \(W\) are vertically opposite. Since \(SU \;/\!/\; VT\) and \(ST\) is a transversal, the marked alternate angles are equal.
Step 31A
\[ \therefore \triangle SUW \cong \triangle TVW \quad (\text{AAS}) \]
\(\triangle SUW \cong \triangle TVW\)  (AAS)

b Part (b) — Perimeter of \(\triangle TVX\)

Here \(SU = 57\) cm, \(SW = 63\) cm and \(UW = 21\) cm. The point \(X\) lies on \(TW\) such that \(\triangle SUW \sim \triangle VXW\).

\(X\) lies on \(TW\); the two shaded triangles are similar.

0 / 4
Step 11M

From part (a), corresponding sides of congruent triangles are equal:

\[ UW = VW ,\qquad TW = SW = 63 ,\qquad TV = SU = 57 . \]

Hence \(VW = UW = 21\) cm.

Reason: \(\triangle SUW \cong \triangle TVW\), so the sides matched by the congruence are equal: \(SW \leftrightarrow TW,\ UW \leftrightarrow VW,\ SU \leftrightarrow TV\).
Step 21M

Since \(\triangle SUW \sim \triangle VXW\), corresponding sides are in the same ratio:

\[ \frac{SU}{VX} = \frac{SW}{VW} = \frac{UW}{XW} . \] \[ \frac{57}{VX} = \frac{63}{21} = 3 \quad\Rightarrow\quad VX = \frac{57}{3} = 19 \text{ cm}. \] \[ \frac{UW}{XW} = \frac{21}{XW} = 3 \quad\Rightarrow\quad XW = \frac{21}{3} = 7 \text{ cm}. \]
Reason: The ratio \(\frac{SW}{VW} = \frac{63}{21} = 3\) is the scale factor between the two similar triangles.
Step 31M
\[ TX = TW - XW = 63 - 7 = 56 \text{ cm}. \]
Reason: \(X\) lies on \(TW\), so \(TW = TX + XW\).
Step 41A
\[ \text{Perimeter of } \triangle TVX = TV + VX + TX = 57 + 19 + 56 = 132 \text{ cm}. \]
Perimeter of \(\triangle TVX = 132\text{ cm}\)

✓ Summary of Answers

  • (a) \(\triangle SUW \cong \triangle TVW\)  (AAS: vertically opposite \(\angle s\), given \(SW = TW\), alternate \(\angle s\) with \(SU \;/\!/\; VT\))
  • (b) From (a): \(UW = VW = 21\) cm, \(TW = SW = 63\) cm, \(TV = SU = 57\) cm.
  • (b) From similarity: \(VX = 19\) cm, \(XW = 7\) cm, so \(TX = 63 - 7 = 56\) cm.
  • (b) Perimeter of \(\triangle TVX = 57 + 19 + 56 = \mathbf{132\text{ cm}}\).
