Geometry — Tangent, Alternate Segment & Angle at the Centre

Tangent Properties Angle in Alternate Segment Angle at Centre = 2 × Circumference Isosceles Triangle Angles in the Same Segment

QQuestion

In the figure, \(PQ\) is the tangent to the circle \(ABC\) at \(O\), where \(O\) is the centre of the semicircle \(PBQ\). It is given that \(BCP\) is a straight line. If \(\angle BPQ = 12^\circ\), then \(\angle BAC =\)

A. \(18^\circ\)     B. \(24^\circ\)     C. \(36^\circ\)     D. \(54^\circ\)

Original Figure

1Part (1) — Angle at the Centre: find \(\angle BOQ\)

The semicircle has centre \(O\) on the tangent \(PQ\). The angle \(\angle BPQ\) is an angle in the alternate segment standing on chord \(BO\), while \(\angle BOQ\) is the angle at the centre standing on the same arc \(BO\).

\(\angle BPQ =\) 12° arc \(BO\) central angle \(\angle BOQ =\) 24° relationship: \(\angle BOQ = 2\angle BPQ\)
12°
STEP 1

Since \(PQ\) is the tangent at \(O\) and \(B\) lies on the semicircle, \(OB \perp PQ\). Thus \(\triangle OBQ\) is a right triangle with the right angle at \(O\).

\[ \angle BOQ = 2\angle BPQ \quad(\angle \text{ at centre} = 2\times \angle \text{ at circumference}) \]
Hint: The angle at the centre standing on an arc is always twice the angle at the circumference standing on the same arc.
STEP 2
\[ \angle BOQ = 2\times 12^\circ = 24^\circ \]
\(\angle BOQ = 24^\circ\)
0 / 2

2Part (2) — Alternate Segment: find \(\angle OAB\)

Now use the tangent \(PO\) together with chord \(AO\). The angle between the tangent and the chord equals the angle in the alternate segment.

\(\angle BOQ =\) 24° \(\angle OAB =\) 24° (alternate segment) \(\angle OAB = \angle BOQ\)
12°
STEP 1

Chord \(AO\) cuts off the alternate segment containing \(Q\). By the alternate segment theorem:

\[ \angle OAB = \angle BOQ \]
Hint: The angle between tangent \(PQ\) and chord \(OB\) (\(\angle BOQ\)) equals the angle that chord \(OB\) subtends in the alternate segment, i.e. \(\angle OAB\).
STEP 2
\[ \angle OAB = \angle BOQ = 24^\circ \]
\(\angle OAB = 24^\circ\)
0 / 2

3Part (3) — Isosceles Triangle & Same Segment: find \(\angle OAC\)

Because \(OB\) and \(OP\) are radii of the semicircle, \(\triangle OBP\) is isosceles. This gives \(\angle OBP = \angle OPB\), then use the same-segment theorem to transfer the angle to \(\angle OAC\).

\(OB = OP\) (radii) → \(\angle OBP = \angle OPB =\) 12° \(\angle OAC = \angle OBC =\) 12° (same segment) \(\angle BAC = \angle OAB + \angle OAC =\) 36°
12°
STEP 1

Since \(OB\) and \(OP\) are radii of the semicircle, \(OB = OP\). Therefore \(\triangle OBP\) is isosceles with base \(BP\).

\[ \angle OBP = \angle OPB \quad(\text{base } \angle\text{s, isos. } \triangle) \]
\[ \angle OBP = \angle OPB = \angle BPQ = 12^\circ \]
Hint: \(P, Q\) lie on the straight line through \(O\), so \(\angle OPB\) is the same angle as \(\angle BPQ\).
STEP 2

\(\angle OAC\) and \(\angle OBC\) are angles in the same segment standing on chord \(OC\) of circle \(ABC\).

\[ \angle OAC = \angle OBC \quad(\angle\text{s in the same segment}) \]
Hint: Angles subtended by the same chord at the circumference are equal.
STEP 3

Since \(B, C, P\) is a straight line, \(\angle OBC = \angle OBP = 12^\circ\). Hence:

\[ \angle OAC = \angle OBC = \angle OBP = 12^\circ \]
\(\angle OAC = 12^\circ\)
STEP 4

Finally, split \(\angle BAC\) at the chord \(AO\):

\[ \angle BAC = \angle OAB + \angle OAC = 24^\circ + 12^\circ = 36^\circ \]
\(\angle BAC = 36^\circ\) → the answer is C
0 / 4

ΣSummary of the Solution

  • Angle at centre: \(\angle BOQ = 2\angle BPQ = 2(12^\circ) = 24^\circ\)
  • Alternate segment: \(\angle OAB = \angle BOQ = 24^\circ\)
  • Isosceles triangle: \(OB = OP \Rightarrow \angle OBP = \angle OPB = 12^\circ\)
  • Same segment: \(\angle OAC = \angle OBC = \angle OBP = 12^\circ\)
  • Final answer: \(\angle BAC = 24^\circ + 12^\circ = 36^\circ\) → Option C
