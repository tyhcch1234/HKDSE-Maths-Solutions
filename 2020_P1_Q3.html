Question

Given the number 534.7698:

(a) Round up 534.7698 to the nearest hundred.

(b) Round down 534.7698 to 2 decimal places.

(c) Round off 534.7698 to 2 significant figures.

[3 marks]

Step-by-Step Guided Solution
a
Part (a) — Round up to the nearest hundred
▼

“Nearest hundred” means we look for the multiples of 100 closest to 534.7698.

The two neighbouring hundreds are:

\[\underline{500} \quad \longleftarrow \quad 534.7698 \quad \longrightarrow \quad \underline{600}\]

The number 534.7698 lies between 500 and 600. Its position is closer to 500, but the question asks us to round up.

Round up means we always choose the larger hundred — i.e. 600.

💡 Hint
“Round up” is a directed rounding — it ignores whether the number is closer to the lower or upper value. It always moves to the next higher place value.

Answer: 600

b
Part (b) — Round down to 2 decimal places
▼

“2 decimal places” means we keep exactly two digits after the decimal point.

\[534.7\underline{6}\,98\]

Look at the digit in the third decimal place, which is 9. Normally, 9 ≥ 5 would cause rounding up. However, the question asks us to round down.

Round down (truncation) means we simply drop all digits after the second decimal place, regardless of the next digit.

💡 Hint
For “round down”, the digit after the target place never changes the kept digits — it is discarded without adjustment.

Answer: 534.76

c
Part (c) — Round off to 2 significant figures
▼

Significant figures start from the first non-zero digit.

The first two significant figures of 534.7698 are 5 and 3:

\[\underline{5}\;\underline{3}\;4.7698\]

Now look at the third significant figure (the digit 4). Since 4 < 5, we round down — the second significant figure stays the same.

Replace all digits after the second significant figure with zeros to preserve the place value:

\[53\underline{0}\]
💡 Hint
“Round off” is the standard rounding rule: if the next digit is 5 or more, round up; otherwise round down. Here the next digit is 4, so we keep 3 and fill with 0.

Answer: 530

Part Instruction Answer
(a) Round up to nearest hundred 600
(b) Round down to 2 decimal places 534.76
(c) Round off to 2 significant figures 530
🎯
Key Takeaway

In HKDSE, you must carefully distinguish between the three instructions:

Round up — always move to the next higher value at the given place.
Round down — simply truncate (cut off) at the given place, ignoring the following digit.
Round off — standard rounding: look at the next digit; if it is 5 or more, round up; otherwise round down.
