Question

The rectangular coordinates of the point \(P\) are \(\left(\sqrt{2},\,-\sqrt{2}\right)\). If \(P\) is rotated anticlockwise about the origin through \(90^{\circ}\), then the polar coordinates of its image are

A\(\left(\sqrt{2},\,45^{\circ}\right)\)
B\(\left(\sqrt{2},\,225^{\circ}\right)\)
C\(\left(2,\,45^{\circ}\right)\)
D\(\left(2,\,225^{\circ}\right)\)
STEP 1 · Polar form of \(P\)
Polar Coordinates \(r=\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}},\qquad \theta=\tan^{-1}\dfrac{y}{x}\) The distance from the origin and the angle from the positive \(x\)-axis locate the point.

Compute the distance from the origin:

\[ r = \sqrt{(\sqrt{2})^{2}+(-\sqrt{2})^{2}} = \sqrt{2+2} = 2. \]

\(P\) lies in the fourth quadrant with reference angle \(45^{\circ}\),
so its polar angle is \(360^{\circ}-45^{\circ}=315^{\circ}\). Hence \(P=(2,\,315^{\circ})\).

STEP 2 · Rotate anticlockwise by \(90^{\circ}\)
Rotation about the Origin \((r,\ \theta)\ \to\ (r,\ \theta+90^\circ)\) A quarter turn anticlockwise adds \(90^\circ\) to the polar angle and leaves \(r\) unchanged.

An anticlockwise rotation adds to the angle:

\[ 315^{\circ} + 90^{\circ} = 405^{\circ} \equiv 405^{\circ} - 360^{\circ} = 45^{\circ}. \]

The distance from the origin is unchanged (\(r=2\)),
so the image is at \(\left(2,\,45^{\circ}\right)\).

💡 Quick check: rotating \((\sqrt2,-\sqrt2)\) by \(90^{\circ}\) anticlockwise gives \(\left(\sqrt2,\sqrt2\right)\) — which is indeed \(r=2\) at \(45^{\circ}\).
Final Answer C. \(\left(2,\,45^{\circ}\right)\)

Question. The point \(Q(-\sqrt{3},\,1)\) is rotated clockwise through \(90^\circ\) about the origin. Find the polar coordinates of the image.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(r=\sqrt{(-\sqrt{3})^{2}+1^{2}}=\sqrt{4}=2\);
the reference angle is \(\tan^{-1}\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}=30^\circ\), and \(Q\) is in the second quadrant,
so \(\theta=180^\circ-30^\circ=150^\circ\).
Step 2. A clockwise rotation subtracts \(90^\circ\): \(150^\circ-90^\circ=60^\circ\).
Step 3. The distance from the origin does not change,
so the image is \((2,\,60^\circ)\).
Step 4. Check: rotating \((x,y)\) clockwise by \(90^\circ\) gives \((y,\,-x)=(1,\,\sqrt{3})\), which has \(r=2\) and angle \(60^\circ\) ✓
Answer: \((2,\,60^\circ)\)
