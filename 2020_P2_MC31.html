HKDSE 2020 · Paper 2 · MC31

Base-16 (Hexadecimal) Numbers

Question

\( \texttt{B000000000000030}_{16} = \)

  1. \( 10\times2^{60}+48 \)
  2. \( 11\times2^{60}+48 \)
  3. \( 10\times2^{64}+768 \)
  4. \( 11\times2^{64}+768 \)

Solution

STEP 1 · Read the base-16 digits \( \texttt{B000000000000030}_{16} \) is a 16-digit hexadecimal number: the leading digit \( \texttt{B} \) sits in the \( 16^{15} \) place, and the last two digits are \( \texttt{30} \). \[ \texttt{B000000000000030}_{16} = \texttt{B}\times16^{15} + 3\times16^{1} + 0\times16^{0}. \]
STEP 2 · Convert the leading part In hexadecimal, \( \texttt{B} = 11 \) in decimal, and \( 16^{15} = (2^{4})^{15} = 2^{60} \), so \[ \texttt{B}\times16^{15} = 11\times2^{60}. \]
STEP 3 · Convert the trailing part \[ \texttt{30}_{16} = 3\times16 + 0 = 48. \]
STEP 4 · Combine \[ \texttt{B000000000000030}_{16} = 11\times2^{60} + 48. \]
Final Answer: B. \( 11\times2^{60}+48 \)
