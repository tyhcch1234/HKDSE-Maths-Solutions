HKDSE Math 2025 Paper 1 Q11 Variation Quadratic Discriminant

Partial Variation & Two Distinct Real Roots

Core concept: Expressing a quadratic from partial variation, then using the discriminant.

Q Question

It is given that \(p(x)\) partly varies as \(x\) and partly varies as \(x^2\). Suppose that \(p(7) = 56\) and \(p(9) = 216\).

  1. (a) Find \(p(x)\). (3 marks)
  2. (b) Let \(c\) be a real constant. Find the range of values of \(c\) such that the equation \(p(x) = c\) has two distinct real roots. (3 marks)

Step-by-Step Solution

"Partly varies as \(x\)" means one term is \(a x\). "Partly varies as \(x^2\)" means another term is \(b x^2\).

So we can write:

\[ p(x) = a x + b x^2 \]

Hint: \(a\) and \(b\) are non-zero constants. We need two conditions to find them.

Given \(p(7) = 56\):

\(7a + 49b = 56\)   …… (1)

Given \(p(9) = 216\):

\(9a + 81b = 216\)   …… (2)

Hint: Substitute \(x = 7\) and \(x = 9\) into \(p(x) = a x + b x^2\).

From (1): divide by 7 → \(a + 7b = 8\)   …… (1')

From (2): divide by 9 → \(a + 9b = 24\)   …… (2')

Subtract (1') from (2'): \((a+9b) - (a+7b) = 24 - 8\)

\(2b = 16 \Rightarrow b = 8\)

Substitute \(b = 8\) into (1'): \(a + 7(8) = 8\)

\(a + 56 = 8 \Rightarrow a = -48\)

Substitute \(a = -48\) and \(b = 8\) into \(p(x) = a x + b x^2\):

\(\boxed{p(x) = -48x + 8x^2}\)

We need \(p(x) = c\) to have two distinct real roots.

\(-48x + 8x^2 = c\)

Rearrange into standard quadratic form:

\(8x^2 - 48x - c = 0\)

For \(8x^2 - 48x - c = 0\) to have two distinct real roots, the discriminant must be positive:

\(\Delta = (-48)^2 - 4(8)(-c) > 0\)

Simplify:

\(2304 + 32c > 0\)
\(32c > -2304\)
\(c > -72\)
\(\boxed{c > -72}\)

Visualise \(p(x) = c\)

Drag the slider to change \(c\). The horizontal line \(y = c\) intersects the parabola \(y = -48x + 8x^2\). Two intersections mean two distinct real roots.

Key Takeaways

  • • "Partly varies as \(x\) and partly varies as \(x^2\)" → \(p(x) = ax + bx^2\).
  • • Two conditions give two equations → solve for \(a\) and \(b\).
  • • For two distinct real roots of a quadratic, require discriminant \(\Delta > 0\).
  • • The minimum value of \(p(x) = 8x^2 - 48x\) is \(-72\) at \(x=3\), so \(c > -72\) gives two intersections.
