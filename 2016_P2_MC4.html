Key Concept

Significant figures (s.f.) count meaningful digits from the first non-zero digit. Decimal places (d.p.) count digits after the decimal point. For numbers less than 1, leading zeros are not significant.

Step-by-Step Solution

Look at the number:

0.0765403

Leading zeros (0.) are placeholders — they are not counted.

The first non-zero digit is 7 at the 2nd decimal place.

Significant digits in order:

7 6 5 4 0 3

That's 6 significant figures in total.

Keep the first 4 significant digits and look at the 5th significant digit.

The 5th significant digit is 0 (less than 5), so we round down (keep as is):

0.0765403 → 0.07654

✓ Option C says $0.07654$, which is correct!
