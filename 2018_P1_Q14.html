Mensuration — Cylinder poured into an inverted cone

Question

A right circular cylindrical container of base radius $8$ cm and height $64$ cm and an inverted right circular conical vessel of base radius $20$ cm and height $60$ cm are held vertically. The container is fully filled with water. The water in the container is now poured into the vessel.
(a) Find the volume of water in the vessel in terms of $\pi$. [2 marks]
(b) Find the depth of water in the vessel. [4 marks]
(c) If a solid metal sphere of radius $14$ cm is then put into the vessel and the sphere is totally immersed in the water, will the water overflow? Explain your answer. [3 marks]

Step-by-step Solution

Step 1 · Volume of water = volume of cylinder The water completely fills the cylinder, so its volume is $$V=\pi(8)^2(64)=4096\pi\ \text{cm}^3 .$$
Hint: the water keeps the same volume when poured — pouring never changes volume.
Step 2 · (a) Answer The volume of water in the vessel is exactly the cylinder's volume: $$\boxed{\;4096\pi\ \text{cm}^3\;}$$
Answer (a): $4096\pi$ cm³.
Step 3 · Set up the cone geometry for (b) Let the water depth be $h$ cm. The cone has base radius $20$ cm and height $60$ cm, so by similar triangles the radius of the water surface is $$r=\frac{20}{60}\,h=\frac{h}{3}\ \text{cm}.$$ The water forms a smaller cone of height $h$ and radius $h/3$, so $$\frac{1}{3}\pi\left(\frac{h}{3}\right)^{2}h=4096\pi .$$
Step 4 · (b) Solve for the depth $$\frac{1}{3}\pi\cdot\frac{h^{2}}{9}\cdot h=4096\pi \;\Longrightarrow\;\frac{h^{3}}{27}=4096 \;\Longrightarrow\;h^{3}=110592$$ $$h=\sqrt[3]{110592}=48 .$$
Check: $\dfrac{1}{3}\pi(16)^2(48)=4096\pi$ ✓ (radius $=48/3=16$ cm, which is less than $20$ cm — the water still fits in the cone).
Step 5 · (c) Empty space left in the cone Volume of the whole conical vessel: $$\frac{1}{3}\pi(20)^{2}(60)=8000\pi\ \text{cm}^3 .$$ Volume not occupied by water: $$8000\pi-4096\pi=3904\pi\ \text{cm}^3 .$$
Step 6 · (c) Volume of the metal sphere $$\text{Volume of sphere}=\frac{4}{3}\pi(14)^{3}=\frac{10976}{3}\pi\ \text{cm}^3 \approx 3658.7\pi\ \text{cm}^3 .$$ Compare with the empty space: $$\frac{10976}{3}\pi < 3904\pi .$$
Step 7 · Conclusion for (c) Since the volume of the sphere is less than the volume of the empty space above the water, the sphere can be totally immersed without the water rising above the rim. $$\therefore\ \text{The water will not overflow.}$$
Answers:
(a) $4096\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
(b) Depth of water $=48$ cm
(c) No, the water will not overflow, because $\dfrac{10976}{3}\pi < 3904\pi$.
