Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Similar Solids

T10 Q50

Two similar solid cones have heights $5\text{ cm}$ and $15\text{ cm}$. Find the ratio of their corresponding linear dimensions in simplest form.

Solution

Linear ratio $= 5:15 = 1:3$.
Volume ratio $= 1:27$.
