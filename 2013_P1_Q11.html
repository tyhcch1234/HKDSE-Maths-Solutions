HKDSE 2013 Paper 1 Q11 Rate, Ratio & Variation 6 marks

Weight & Perimeter of a Tray

The weight of a tray of perimeter ℓ metres is W grams. It is given that W is the sum of two parts, one part varies directly as ℓ and the other part varies directly as ℓ². When ℓ = 1, W = 181 and when ℓ = 2, W = 402.

(a) Find the weight of a tray of perimeter 1.2 metres. [4 marks]
(b) If the weight of a tray is 594 grams, find the perimeter of the tray. [2 marks]

Step-by-step Solution

1

Set up the variation equation

Hint: "varies directly" means one quantity = constant × other. Combine the two parts as a sum.

Since \(W\) is the sum of a part varying directly as \(\ell\) and another varying directly as \(\ell^2\), we have

\[ W = h\ell + k\ell^2 \]

where \(h\) and \(k\) are non-zero constants.

2

Use the given data to form equations

Hint: Substitute \(\ell = 1, W = 181\) and \(\ell = 2, W = 402\) into \(W = h\ell + k\ell^2\).

When \(\ell = 1\), \(W = 181\):

\[ h(1) + k(1)^2 = 181 \quad \Rightarrow \quad h + k = 181 \tag{1} \]

When \(\ell = 2\), \(W = 402\):

\[ h(2) + k(2)^2 = 402 \quad \Rightarrow \quad 2h + 4k = 402 \tag{2} \]

3

Solve for \(h\) and \(k\)

Hint: From (1), \(h = 181 - k\). Substitute into (2) to find \(k\), then \(h\).

From (1): \(h = 181 - k\).

Substitute into (2):

\[ 2(181 - k) + 4k = 402 \]

\[ 362 - 2k + 4k = 402 \]

\[ 2k = 40 \quad \Rightarrow \quad k = 20 \]

Then \(h = 181 - 20 = 161\).

Therefore \(W = 161\ell + 20\ell^2\).

4

(a) Find \(W\) when \(\ell = 1.2\)

Hint: Substitute \(\ell = 1.2\) into \(W = 161\ell + 20\ell^2\).

\[ W = 161(1.2) + 20(1.2)^2 \]

\[ = 193.2 + 20(1.44) \]

\[ = 193.2 + 28.8 \]

\[ = 222 \]

Therefore the weight is \(222\) grams.

5

(b) Form the equation for \(W = 594\)

Hint: Set \(W = 594\) in the variation model: \(161\ell + 20\ell^2 = 594\).

Set \(W = 594\):

\[ 161\ell + 20\ell^2 = 594 \]

Rearrange into standard quadratic form:

\[ 20\ell^2 + 161\ell - 594 = 0 \]

6

Solve the quadratic equation

Hint: Try factorisation. The discriminant is \(161^2 - 4(20)(-594) = 25921 + 47520 = 73441 = 271^2\).

Factorise \(20\ell^2 + 161\ell - 594 = 0\):

\[ (4\ell - 11)(5\ell + 54) = 0 \]

So \(\ell = \dfrac{11}{4}\) or \(\ell = -\dfrac{54}{5}\).

Since perimeter must be positive, reject the negative root. Thus

\[ \ell = \frac{11}{4} = 2.75 \]

Therefore the perimeter of the tray is \(2.75\) metres (or \( \frac{11}{4} \) m).
