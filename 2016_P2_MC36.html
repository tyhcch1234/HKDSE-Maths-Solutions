QUESTION
Geometric Sequence Common Ratio Sum of first n terms

Let \(a_n\) be the \(n\)th term of a geometric sequence. If \(a_3 = 21\) and \(a_7 = 189\), which of the following must be true?

I. The common ratio of the sequence is less than \(1\).
II. Some of the terms of the sequence are irrational numbers.
III. The sum of the first \(99\) terms of the sequence is greater than \(3 \times 10^{24}\).
A I only
B II only
C I and III only
D II and III only

Check Each Statement

I

The common ratio of the sequence is less than \(1\).

✗ FALSE We found \(r = \sqrt{3}\) or \(-\sqrt{3}\). Both have \(|r| \approx 1.732 > 1\).
II

Some of the terms of the sequence are irrational numbers.

✓ TRUE When \(r = \sqrt{3}\), \(a_n = 7(\sqrt{3})^{n-1}\). For example, \(a_2 = 7\sqrt{3}\) is irrational.
III

The sum of the first \(99\) terms of the sequence is greater than \(3 \times 10^{24}\).

? NOT ALWAYS For \(r = \sqrt{3}\), \(S_{99} \approx 3.96 \times 10^{24} > 3\times10^{24}\) ✓. But for \(r = -\sqrt{3}\), \(S_{99} \approx 1.06 \times 10^{24} < 3\times10^{24}\) ✗. So it does not must be true.

Answer: B (II only)

Only statement II is true for all possible sequences satisfying \(a_3=21, a_7=189\).

Key Takeaway

  • ▸ When an even power leads to \(r^2 = k\), there are two possible values for \(r\): \(+\sqrt{k}\) and \(-\sqrt{k}\).
  • ▸ Always test statements against all possible cases before concluding "must be true".
  • ▸ For sequences with irrational ratios, terms and sums quickly become large irrational numbers.
