Principal $P=88\,000$ Amount $A$ Interest $=A-P$ Monthly compounding
HKDSE 2022 Paper 2 · Q11 · Percentages

A sum of $\$88\,000$ is deposited at an interest rate of $6\%$ per annum for $4$ years, compounded monthly. Find the interest correct to the nearest dollar.

  • A. $\$21\,120$
  • B. $\$23\,098$
  • C. $\$23\,803$
  • D. $\$23\,825$

Step-by-step solution

1Write down what is given

Principal $P=\$88\,000$, nominal annual rate $r=6\%$, time $t=4$ years, compounded monthly.

List $P$, $r$ and $t$ first — most mistakes come from mixing up annual and monthly quantities.
2Convert to the monthly rate

Since interest is compounded monthly, the annual rate must be divided into $12$ equal parts:

$$\text{monthly rate}=\frac{6\%}{12}=0.5\%=0.005$$

The rate, the number of periods and the compounding frequency must all use the same time unit (here: months).
3Count the number of periods

$$n = 4 \text{ years} \times 12 = 48 \text{ months}$$

A period is one month, so there are $48$ compounding periods in total.
4Apply the compound-interest formula

$$A=P(1+i)^n = 88\,000\left(1+\frac{6\%}{12}\right)^{12\times 4}$$

$$A = 88\,000(1.005)^{48}$$

$A$ is the final amount (principal + interest), not the interest itself.
5Compute the amount

$$(1.005)^{48}\approx 1.2704892$$

$$A \approx 88\,000 \times 1.2704892 \approx 111\,803.046$$

Keep plenty of decimal places now; round only at the very end.
6Find the interest = amount − principal

$$\text{Interest}=A-P \approx 111\,803.046-88\,000=23\,803.046$$

$$= 88\,000\left[(1+6\%\div 12)^{4\times 12}-1\right]$$

A shortcut: factor out $P$ to get $P\big[(1+i)^n-1\big]$ directly in one line.
7Round and check the options

$$\text{Interest}\approx \$23\,803 \ \text{(to the nearest dollar)}$$

Compare with the options: A gives simple interest only ($88\,000\times0.06\times4=21\,120$); B and D are rounding / frequency errors. C matches.

Distractor A ($88000(0.06)(4)$) is simple interest — a common trap.

Answer: C — \(\$23\,803\)
