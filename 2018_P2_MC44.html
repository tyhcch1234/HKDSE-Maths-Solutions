Standard score — using z-scores and the mean / standard deviation

Statistics Standard Score Mean & Standard Deviation Simultaneous Equations

Question Multiple Choice

In a test, the mean of the test scores is \(68\) marks. Peter gets \(46\) marks in the test and his standard score is \(-2.2\). If Susan gets \(52\) marks in the test, then her standard score is

  • A. \(-2.5\)
  • B. \(-1.6\)
  • C. \(-0.6\)
  • D. \(1.6\)
Key idea: the standard score (z-score) is \[ z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}, \] where \(x\) is the raw score, \(\mu\) the mean, and \(\sigma\) the standard deviation.

Part (a) Find the standard deviation σ

Peter's standard score is \(-2.2\) and his raw score is \(46\), with mean \(68\). Use this to find \(\sigma\).

Step 11M

Write the standard-score (z-score) formula and substitute Peter's values:

\[ -2.2=\frac{46-68}{\sigma} \]
Hint: Peter's score is below the mean (\(46<68\)), so a negative z-score is expected — the sign checks out.
Step 21M

Simplify the numerator \(46-68=-22\) and solve for \(\sigma\):

\[ -2.2=\frac{-22}{\sigma}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \sigma=\frac{-22}{-2.2}=10 \]
Answer: \(\sigma=10\) marks.
Hint: Move the slider above to \(\sigma=10\) — the marker for Peter should sit exactly at \(-2.2\) standard units.

Part (b) Find Susan's standard score

Susan scored \(52\) marks. With \(\mu=68\) and \(\sigma=10\), compute her standard score.

Step 11M

Substitute Susan's score into the standard score formula:

\[ z_S=\frac{52-68}{10} \]
Hint: \(52-68=-16\); since \(52<68\), Susan's score is also below the mean, so her z-score must be negative.
Step 21A

Compute the value:

\[ z_S=\frac{-16}{10}=-1.6 \]
Answer: Susan's standard score is \(-1.6\).
Hint: This corresponds to option B. Compare with Peter (\(-2.2\)): Susan scored higher (\(52>46\)), so her z-score is closer to 0 than Peter's. ✓

Extra Insight Why both equations?

Alternatively, treat \(\sigma\) and \(s\) (Susan's standard score) as two unknowns and solve simultaneously — exactly the method shown in the solution:

\[ \begin{cases} 68-2.2\sigma=46 & \text{......(1)}\\[4pt] 68+s\sigma=52 & \text{......(2)} \end{cases} \]
Step 1—

From (1): \(2.2\sigma=22\Rightarrow \sigma=10\). Substitute into (2):

\[ 68+10s=52 \Rightarrow 10s=-16 \Rightarrow s=-1.6 \]
Both routes agree: \(\sigma=10\) and \(s=-1.6\).
Hint: The green line and blue line above cross at exactly \(\sigma=10,\ s=-1.6\) — the unique solution of the system.

Summary of Answers

  • Standard score formula: \(\displaystyle z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\) (here \(\mu=68\)).
  • From Peter: \(\sigma=\dfrac{46-68}{-2.2}=\dfrac{-22}{-2.2}=\mathbf{10}\) marks.
  • For Susan (\(52\) marks): \(\displaystyle z_S=\frac{52-68}{10}=\mathbf{-1.6}\).
  • Correct option: B. −1.6
