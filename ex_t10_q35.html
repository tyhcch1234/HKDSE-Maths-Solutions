Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Surface Area

T10 Q35

A cone has a base radius of $7$ cm and a slant height of $25$ cm. Find its curved surface area. (Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.)

Solution

The curved surface area of a cone is $A=\pi r l$.
Substitute $r=7$ cm, $l=25$ cm and $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$: $A=\frac{22}{7}\times 7\times 25$.
Simplify: $A=22\times 25=550$.
So the curved surface area is $550\text{ cm}^2$.
The curved surface area of the cone is $550\text{ cm}^2$.
