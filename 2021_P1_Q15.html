HKDSE Math 2021 Paper 1 Q15

Probability Permutations Combinations

Q Question

A queue is randomly formed by 7 teachers and 3 students.

  1. (a) How many different queues can be formed? [1 mark]
  2. (b) Find the probability that no students are next to each other in the queue. [3 marks]

a Number of Different Queues

Step 1: Treat all 10 people as distinct.

There are 7 teachers and 3 students, but each person is unique (e.g., Teacher A, Teacher B, Student X, etc.).

💡 Hint

The number of ways to arrange \(n\) distinct objects in a row is \(n!\).

\(P^n_n = n!\)

Step 2: Apply the formula for permutations.

Total number of arrangements = \(P^{10}_{10} = 10!\)

\(10! = 3\,628\,800\)

b Probability No Students Are Next to Each Other

Step 1: Arrange the 7 teachers first.

The 7 teachers can be arranged in \(7!\) ways.

T T T T T T T

Step 2: Identify the gaps between teachers.

With 7 teachers in a row, there are 8 gaps to place the 3 students so that no two students are adjacent:

_ T _ T _ T _ T _ T _ T _ T _

(8 gaps including the ends)

💡 Hint

Choose 3 gaps out of the 8 to place the students: \(C^8_3\)

Then arrange the 3 students among themselves: \(3!\)

Step 3: Calculate favourable arrangements.

Favourable ways = \(7! \times C^8_3 \times 3!\)

= \(5040 \times 56 \times 6 = 1\,693\,440\)

Step 4: Probability.

\(\displaystyle P = \frac{7! \times C^8_3 \times 3!}{10!}\)

\(\displaystyle = \frac{1\,693\,440}{3\,628\,800} = \frac{7}{15}\)

FINAL ANSWER

\(\dfrac{7}{15}\)

📘 Key Concepts

  • Permutations (\(n!\)): Arranging distinct objects in order.
  • Combinations (\(C^n_r\)): Choosing r items from n without order.
  • Gap Method: Placing separators (students) into gaps between fixed items (teachers) to avoid adjacency.
  • Probability = Favourable / Total
