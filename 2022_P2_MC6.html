HKDSE Math 2022 Paper 2 Q6

Estimation & Error

It is given that \(x\) is a real number. If \(x\) is rounded down to 3 significant figures, the result is 345. Find the range of values of \(x\).

Significant figures Rounding down (floor) Error & bounds

1 Guided Solution

Rounded down (floor rounding) means we drop the extra digits without increasing the last kept digit. It is the same as finding the largest number with 3 significant figures that is less than or equal to \(x\).

💡 Hint: 3 significant figures means we look at the first three non-zero digits from the left. For the result 345, the digit in the units place is the 3rd sig. fig.

Because we round down, the number \(x\) must be at least 345. If \(x\) were smaller than 345 (e.g., 344.9), rounding down to 3 sig. fig. would give 344, not 345.

So: \[ x \ge 345 \]

The next 3-significant-figure number after 345 is 346. If \(x\) is 346 or more, rounding down to 3 sig. fig. would give 346 (or higher), not 345.

Therefore \(x\) must be strictly less than 346: \[ x < 346 \]

🔍 Important: The boundary 346 is not included because rounding down 346 gives 346, not 345. The symbol is \(<\), not \(\le\).

From Step 2 and Step 3, the range of \(x\) is: \[ 345 \le x < 346 \]

This matches option B.
