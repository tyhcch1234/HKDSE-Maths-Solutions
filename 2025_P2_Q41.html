2025 P2 Q41 In-centre of a Right Triangle — Area of $\Delta OUV$

Interactive Diagram

In-circle (radius 6) In-centre I(6, 6) Tangent line VU Tangency pts A, B, C Right angle at O
Denote the origin by $O$. The coordinates of the point $I$ are $(6,\,6)$. Let $V$ be a point lying on the positive $y$-axis such that the $y$-coordinate of the in-centre of $\Delta OUV$ is $6$. Find the area of $\Delta OUV$.
A. $70$
B. $87$
C. $210$
D. $250$
STEP 1 · Set up the right triangle

$O=(0,0)$ is the origin with a right angle at $O$. $V$ lies on the positive $y$-axis, so $V=(0,k)$ with $k>0$.

$U$ lies on the positive $x$-axis and the in-centre is $I=(6,6)$. Since the in-circle is tangent to the $x$-axis at $C=(6,0)$, the tangent point is directly below $I$.

STEP 2 · Equal tangents from a vertex

Let $A$, $B$, $C$ be the tangent points on $OV$, $VU$, $OU$ respectively. Equal tangents from a common vertex give:

$$VA = VB = k-6,\qquad UB = UC = 14.$$

Hence the tangent length along the hypotenuse $VU$ is $(k-6)+14 = k+8$.

Equal Tangents from an External Point \(VA=VB\ \text{and}\ UB=UC\) From \(V\) the tangent lengths are \(k-6\); from \(U\) they are \(14\), so \(OU=20\).
STEP 3 · Right-angle condition

The tangent line $VU$ is perpendicular to the radius $IB$. The distance from $I$ to the line through $V(0,k)$ and $U(u,0)$ equals $6$.

Let $OU = u$. The slope of $VU$ is $-k/u$, so $ku = 6u + 6k$, giving the tangent-line relation.

STEP 4 · Solve for the tangent length

From the hypotenuse length: $VU = k+8$. Also the leg $UB = u-6$ equals $14$, so $u=20$.

Apply Pythagoras to the right triangle with legs $OU = 20$ and $OV = k$:

$$k^2 + 20^2 = (k+8)^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; 400 = 16k + 64 \;\Longrightarrow\; k = 21.$$

Pythagoras Theorem \(k^{2}+u^{2}=(k+u-2r)^{2}\) With \(u=20\) and \(r=6\): \(k^{2}+400=(k+8)^{2}\), so \(k=21\).
STEP 5 · Compute the area

With legs $OU = 20$ and $OV = 21$ (right angle at $O$):

$$[\Delta OUV] = \tfrac{1}{2}\times 20 \times 21 = 210.$$

Answer: C.  210

Question. In the figure, \(\angle O=90^\circ\) and the in-circle of \(\triangle OUV\) has centre \(I=(4,4)\), touching \(OU\) at \(C=(4,0)\). If \(OU=12\), find \(OV\) and the area of \(\triangle OUV\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Equal tangents from \(O\): \(OC=OA=4\), so \(UC=12-4=8\).
Step 2. Equal tangents from \(U\): \(UB=UC=8\); from \(V\): \(VA=VB=k-4\) where \(OV=k\).
Step 3. \(VU=(k-4)+8=k+4\); Pythagoras: \(k^{2}+144=(k+4)^{2}\Rightarrow 8k=128\Rightarrow k=16\).
Step 4. Area \(=\tfrac12(12)(16)=96\).
Answer: \(OV=16\), area \(=96\)
Key insight: For a right triangle with legs on the axes, the in-centre is at $(r,\,r)$. Equal-tangent segments from the right-angle vertex give the tangency length along the hypotenuse as $(k-r)+(u-r)$.
