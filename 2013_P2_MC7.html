Quadratic graphs — finding the axis of symmetry from intercepts

Quadratic Function y-intercept x-intercept Axis of Symmetry Multiple Choice

Question MCQ

The figure shows the graph of \[ y = -2x^{2} + ax + b, \] where \(a\) and \(b\) are constants. The graph cuts the negative \(y\)-axis at \(-10\) and cuts the positive \(x\)-axis at \(1\).

The equation of the axis of symmetry of the graph is

A. \(x = 2\)
B. \(x = 3\)
C. \(x = 5\)
D. \(y = 8\)

Part (a) — Find the constant \(b\) from the \(y\)-intercept 1M

\(y\)-intercept = \((0,\,-10)\) — the given condition
Step 11M

Put \(x = 0\) into \(y = -2x^{2} + ax + b\):

\[ y = -2(0)^{2} + a(0) + b = b. \]

So the \(y\)-intercept of the graph is \((0,\;b)\).

Hint: The constant term \(b\) of a quadratic \(y = cx^{2}+ax+b\) is the \(y\)-intercept value. No calculation is needed beyond reading the graph.
Step 21A

The figure shows the graph meets the negative \(y\)-axis at \(-10\), so

\[ b = -10. \]
Answer (a): \(\;b = -10\)
Hint: Always read intercepts directly from the graph — the \(y\)-intercept is where the curve crosses the vertical axis, i.e. at \(x=0\).

Part (b) — Find the constant \(a\) from the \(x\)-intercept 1M

At \(x=1\): \(\;y = -2 + a - 10 = a - 12\)
Need \(y=0\) at \(x=1\) \(\Rightarrow a = 12\)
Step 11M

The figure shows one \(x\)-intercept of the graph is \(1\), i.e. the point \((1,\,0)\) lies on the curve. Substitute \(x=1\), \(y=0\) together with \(b=-10\):

\[ 0 = -2(1)^{2} + a(1) - 10 \] \[ 0 = -2 + a - 10. \]
Hint: A point lying on the graph must satisfy the equation of the graph. " \(x\)-intercept is 1 " means \((1,0)\) is on the curve.
Step 21A

Simplify and solve for \(a\):

\[ 0 = a - 12 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a = 12. \]
Answer (b): \(\;a = 12\)  so the graph is \(\;y = -2x^{2} + 12x - 10\)
Hint: After both constants are found, always write down the full equation — it makes the next part much easier.

Part (c) — Find the axis of symmetry 1A

Roots: \(x = 1\) and \(x = 5\)
Axis of symmetry: \(x = \dfrac{1+5}{2} = 3\)
Vertex: \((3,\;8)\) — maximum point
Step 11M

For \(y = -2x^{2} + 12x - 10\), the axis of symmetry of a quadratic \(y = cx^{2} + ax + b\) is

\[ x = -\frac{a}{2c} = -\frac{12}{2(-2)}. \]
Hint: This is the standard formula — it also equals the \(x\)-coordinate of the vertex.
Step 21A

Evaluate:

\[ x = -\frac{12}{-4} = 3. \]

Alternative (midpoint of roots): Solve \(-2x^{2}+12x-10=0\), i.e. \(x^{2}-6x+5=0\), giving \(x = 1\) or \(x = 5\). The axis of symmetry passes through the midpoint of the two \(x\)-intercepts:

\[ x = \frac{1 + 5}{2} = 3. \]
Answer (c): \(\;x = 3\) \(\Rightarrow\) Option B
Hint: By symmetry the vertex always sits exactly halfway between the two \(x\)-intercepts — a handy shortcut in MC questions.

Summary of Answers

  • \(y\)-intercept is \((0,\,-10)\)  \(\Rightarrow\)  \(b = -10\)
  • \((1,\,0)\) lies on the curve  \(\Rightarrow\)  \(a = 12\)
  • Full equation: \(y = -2x^{2} + 12x - 10\)
  • Axis of symmetry: \(x = -\dfrac{a}{2c} = -\dfrac{12}{2(-2)} = \) \(3\)
  • Correct option: B. \(x = 3\)
