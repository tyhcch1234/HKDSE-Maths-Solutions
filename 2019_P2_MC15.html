Mensuration — Total Surface Area of a Right Pyramid

Right Pyramid Square Base Slant Height Pythagoras' Theorem Total Surface Area

Q15 The Question

Multiple-choice — 4 options

The base of a solid right pyramid is a square of side \(18 \text{ cm}\). If the height of the pyramid is \(12 \text{ cm}\), then the total surface area of the pyramid is

  1. \(432 \text{ cm}^2\)
  2. \(540 \text{ cm}^2\)
  3. \(756 \text{ cm}^2\)
  4. \(864 \text{ cm}^2\)

Part (a) Find the slant height of a lateral face

Each lateral face is an isosceles triangle. We need its vertical height (the slant height of the pyramid).

\(l = \sqrt{(s/2)^2 + h^2} =\) 15.00 cm
1Locate the right triangle inside the pyramid

Let \(O\) be the centre of the square base, \(M\) the midpoint of one base edge, and \(V\) the apex. Then \(VO\) is the height of the pyramid and \(VM\) is the slant height of a lateral face.

\[ VO = 12 \text{ cm}, \qquad OM = \tfrac{1}{2}\times 18 = 9 \text{ cm} \]

Since \(VO \perp\) base and \(OM \perp\) the edge, \(\angle VOM = 90^\circ\).

Hint: The midpoint of a base edge is the foot of the perpendicular from the centre — this gives a right-angled triangle \(VOM\).
2Apply Pythagoras' Theorem
\[ VM^2 = VO^2 + OM^2 = 12^2 + 9^2 = 144 + 81 = 225 \] \[ \boxed{VM = 15 \text{ cm}} \]
Hint: The slant height is always longer than the pyramid's height, since it is the hypotenuse.
Slant height of each lateral face \(= 15 \text{ cm}\)
0 / 2

Part (b) Compute the total surface area

Total surface area = square base + 4 congruent triangular lateral faces.

TSA = \(s^2 + 2s\sqrt{(s/2)^2+h^2} =\) 864 cm²
1Area of the square base
\[ A_{\text{base}} = 18^2 = 324 \text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: The base is a square of side \(18\), so its area is side × side.
Base area \(= 324 \text{ cm}^2\)
2Area of the four lateral faces

One lateral face is a triangle with base \(18 \text{ cm}\) and height (slant height) \(15 \text{ cm}\):

\[ A_{\text{one face}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\times 18 \times 15 = 135 \text{ cm}^2 \] \[ A_{\text{4 faces}} = 4 \times 135 = 540 \text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: The height of each triangular face is the slant height \(15\), NOT the pyramid's height \(12\).
3Add base + lateral faces
\[ \text{TSA} = 324 + 540 = 864 \text{ cm}^2 \] \[ \text{TSA} = (18)^2 + 4\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}(18)(15) = 864 \text{ cm}^2 \]
Total surface area \(= 864 \text{ cm}^2\)  →  Answer: D
Hint: Always check your lateral face uses the slant height, not the vertical height. Option A (432) comes from forgetting the base; option C (756) from omitting one face.
0 / 3

Summary — Final Answers

  • Base side: \(s = 18 \text{ cm}\)  |  Height: \(h = 12 \text{ cm}\)
  • Half-base offset: \(\tfrac{s}{2} = 9 \text{ cm}\)
  • Slant height: \[ l = \sqrt{9^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{225} = 15 \text{ cm} \]
  • Base area: \(18^2 = 324 \text{ cm}^2\)
  • Lateral surface area: \(4 \times \tfrac{1}{2}(18)(15) = 540 \text{ cm}^2\)
  • Total surface area: \(324 + 540 = 864 \text{ cm}^2\)
  • Correct option: D — 864 cm²
