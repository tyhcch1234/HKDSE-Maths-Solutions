Step 1 — Read the structure of the number: 9 ★ ◆

HKDSE 2012 Paper 2 · Q27

\(9\,★\,◆\) is a 3-digit number, where \(★\) and \(◆\) are integers from \(0\) to \(9\) inclusive. Find the probability that the 3-digit number is divisible by \(5\).

  • A. \(\dfrac{1}{5}\)
  • B. \(\dfrac{7}{33}\)
  • C. \(\dfrac{20}{99}\)
  • D. \(\dfrac{19}{100}\)
Step-by-step solution
1Total number of possible numbers

The hundreds digit is fixed as \(9\). The tens digit \(★\) can be any of the \(10\) digits \(0,1,\dots,9\), and the units digit \(◆\) can also be any of the \(10\) digits \(0,1,\dots,9\).

\[ \text{Total outcomes} = 10 \times 10 = 100 \]

Hint: the two unknown positions are independent, so multiply their counts (fundamental counting principle).

2Condition for divisibility by 5

A number is divisible by \(5\) exactly when its units digit is \(0\) or \(5\).

So we need \(◆ \in \{0,\,5\}\) — the value of \(★\) is free.

Hint: divisibility by 5 depends only on the last digit, regardless of \(★\).

3Count the favourable outcomes

Tens digit: \(10\) choices. Units digit: \(2\) choices (\(0\) or \(5\)).

\[ \text{Favourable outcomes} = 10 \times 2 = 20 \]

Hint: \(★\) is unchanged (10 ways); only \(◆\) is restricted to 2 values.

4Compute the probability

\[ P = \frac{\text{favourable}}{\text{total}} = \frac{20}{100} = \frac{1}{5} \]

Hint: always simplify the fraction to compare with the options.

Answer: A  ·  \(\dfrac{1}{5}\)
