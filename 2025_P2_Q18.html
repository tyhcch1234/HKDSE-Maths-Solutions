Triangle WXY Line XZ (60 cm) Right angle at Z Equal angles Points W, X, Y, Z
Question
The figure shows the triangle $WXY$. Let $Z$ be a point lying on $WY$ such that $XZ \perp WY$. If $WX = 65\text{ cm}$, $XZ = 60\text{ cm}$ and $WZ = 25\text{ cm}$, then $XY =$
A. $120$ cm    B. $144$ cm    C. $156$ cm    D. $169$ cm
Step 1 · Right angle at Z

Check the triangle $XZW$ with sides $XZ=60$, $WZ=25$, $WX=65$:

$$XZ^2+WZ^2=60^2+25^2=3600+625=4225=65^2=WX^2$$

By the converse of Pythagoras' Theorem,

$$\angle XZW = 90^\circ$$

So $XZ \perp WY$ and $\angle XZY = 90^\circ$ as well.

Step 2 · Show similarity

Consider $\triangle WYX$ and $\triangle XYZ$:

$$\angle XYW = \angle ZYX \quad (\text{common angle})$$ $$\frac{WY}{XY}=\frac{XY}{YZ} \quad \text{?}$$

Better: compare $\triangle WXY$ and $\triangle XZY$ — both have a $90^\circ$ angle at $Z$ / right angle.

Use the shared angle at $Y$: $\triangle WXY \sim \triangle XZY$ (AA).

AA Similarity \(\triangle WXY\sim\triangle XZY\) The two triangles share the angle at \(Y\) and both have a right angle, so two angles match.
Step 3 · Ratio from similarity

From $\triangle WXY \sim \triangle XZY$, matching sides:

$$\frac{XY}{YZ}=\frac{WX}{XZ}=\frac{65}{60}=\frac{13}{12}$$

Hence

$$YZ=\frac{12}{13}\,XY$$
Step 4 · Pythagoras in XZY

In right-angled triangle $XZY$ ($\angle XZY = 90^\circ$):

$$XZ^2+YZ^2=XY^2$$ $$60^2+\left(\tfrac{12}{13}XY\right)^2=XY^2$$ $$3600=XY^2-\frac{144}{169}XY^2=\frac{25}{169}XY^2$$ $$XY^2=\frac{3600\times169}{25}=144\times169$$ $$XY=\sqrt{144\times169}=12\times13=156$$
Pythagoras' Theorem \(XZ^{2}+YZ^{2}=XY^{2}\) In the right-angled \(\triangle XZY\), the hypotenuse is \(XY\).
Step 5 · Conclusion

$XY = 156\text{ cm}$, which matches option C.

Answer: C. 156 cm
Tip: After spotting a 25-60-65 triangle (a 5-12-13 triple scaled by 5), the right angle at $Z$ lets you relate $\triangle XYZ$ to the large triangle via AA similarity, avoiding messy algebra.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Both \(\triangle WXY\) and \(\triangle XZY\) have a right angle at \(Z\), and they share the angle at \(Y\), so \(\triangle WXY\sim\triangle XZY\) (AA).
Step 2. Corresponding sides: \(\dfrac{XY}{YZ}=\dfrac{WX}{XZ}=\dfrac{130}{120}=\dfrac{13}{12}\), so \(YZ=\dfrac{12}{13}XY\).
Step 3. Pythagoras in \(\triangle XZY\): \(120^{2}+\left(\dfrac{12}{13}XY\right)^{2}=XY^{2}\), so \(14\,400=\dfrac{25}{169}XY^{2}\).
Step 4. \(XY^{2}=\dfrac{14\,400\times 169}{25}=576\times 169\), hence \(XY=24\times 13=312\text{ cm}\).
Step 5. Check: \(YZ=\dfrac{12}{13}(312)=288\), and \(120^{2}+288^{2}=14\,400+82\,944=97\,344=312^{2}\) ✓
Answer: \(XY=312\text{ cm}\)
