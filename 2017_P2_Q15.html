HKDSE 2017 · P2 Q15Mensuration — Cylinder vs Cone

Cylinder (radius $r_1$, height $h_1$) Cone (radius $r_2$, height $h_2$) Dimensions Base circles
Question

The base radius of a right circular cone is 2 times the base radius of a right circular cylinder, while the height of the circular cylinder is 3 times the height of the circular cone. If the volume of the circular cone is $36\pi\text{ cm}^3$, then the volume of the circular cylinder is

  • A.  $27\pi\text{ cm}^3$
  • B.  $48\pi\text{ cm}^3$
  • C.  $81\pi\text{ cm}^3$
  • D.  $144\pi\text{ cm}^3$
Step 1 · Name the unknowns

Let the cylinder have base radius $r_1$ and height $h_1$.

Let the cone have base radius $r_2$ and height $h_2$.

From the question:

$$r_2 = 2r_1,\qquad h_1 = 3h_2$$

and the cone volume is $V_{\text{cone}} = 36\pi\text{ cm}^3$.

Step 2 · Write the volume formulas

Volume of a cylinder:

$$V_{\text{cyl}} = \pi r_1^{\,2} h_1$$

Volume of a cone:

$$V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3}\pi r_2^{\,2} h_2$$
Step 3 · Find the ratio of the volumes

Divide the cylinder volume by the cone volume:

$$\frac{V_{\text{cyl}}}{V_{\text{cone}}}=\frac{\pi r_1^{\,2}h_1}{\tfrac13\pi r_2^{\,2}h_2}=3\left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{h_1}{h_2}\right)$$

Substitute $\dfrac{r_1}{r_2}=\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac{h_1}{h_2}=3$:

$$\frac{V_{\text{cyl}}}{V_{\text{cone}}}=3\left(\frac12\right)^{2}(3)=3\cdot\frac14\cdot 3=\frac{9}{4}$$
Step 4 · Compute the cylinder volume

Since $V_{\text{cone}}=36\pi$:

$$V_{\text{cyl}}=\frac{9}{4}\times 36\pi = 81\pi\text{ cm}^3$$
💡 Quick check: the cone's radius is twice the cylinder's, but its height is only one third. The factor $\tfrac13$ in the cone formula works in the cone's favour, so the cylinder ends up larger — $81\pi > 36\pi$. ✔
ANSWER C.   $81\pi\text{ cm}^3$
