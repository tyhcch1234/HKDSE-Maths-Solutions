Geometry (106) · Sub-Topic: Angles in Triangles/Polygons

T11 Q06

A polygon has all its exterior angles equal, and each exterior angle is \(40^\circ\). Find the number of sides of the polygon.

Solution

The sum of the exterior angles of a convex polygon is $360^\circ$.
Let the polygon have $n$ sides. Since all exterior angles are equal and each is $40^\circ$, we have $40n=360$.
Solving gives $n=\frac{360}{40}=9$.
The polygon has $9$ sides.
