Diagram — 7 officers create 8 gaps

Step 1: 7 officers (O) in a row → 8 possible gaps for the managers.

PROBABILITY · COUNTING

A queue is formed by 2 managers and 7 officers. If no managers are next to each other, how many different queues can be formed?

  • A. 80640
  • B. 141120
  • C. 282240  
  • D. 362880
STEP 1 Arrange the 7 officers first.
Multiplication Principle \(\text{number of ways}=m\times n\) Arrange the first group, then place the second group into the gaps; the two stages are multiplied.
Treat the 7 officers (all distinct) as one block and order them along the queue.
$7! = 5040$ ways
Hint: handle the people with no restriction first — they create the “gaps”.
STEP 2 Count the gaps between the officers.
Gap Method \(n\text{ objects in a row create }n+1\text{ gaps}\) To keep two people apart, seat everyone else first and then choose separate gaps.
Once the 7 officers stand in a row, they form
$7+1 = 8$ gaps (including both ends of the queue).
Hint: 7 officers → 6 internal gaps + 2 end gaps = 8 gaps.
STEP 3 Place the 2 managers into two different gaps.
Permutation of Distinct Objects \({}^nP_r=\frac{n!}{(n-r)!}\) The chosen gaps are different positions, so order matters and a permutation is used.
Choosing two distinct gaps guarantees the managers are separated by at least one officer.
$P_2^{8} = 8 \times 7 = 56$ ways
Hint: use permutation (not combination) because the order of the two managers matters.
STEP 4 Multiply the two independent choices.
$P_2^{8} \times 7! = 56 \times 5040 = 282\,240$
Hint: independent stages → multiply.
Answer: C  (282 240)
PRACTICESimilar question — try it yourself

Question. Six boys and two girls are to be seated in a row of 8 seats. In how many ways can they be seated if the two girls must not sit next to each other?

Show the worked solution
1. Seat the 6 boys first: \(6!=720\) ways, creating \(7\) gaps.
2. Choose 2 of the 7 gaps for the girls: \({}^7P_2=7\times6=42\) ways.
3. By the multiplication principle the number of arrangements is \(720\times42\).
4. Hence there are \(30\,240\) ways.
Answer: \(30\,240\)
