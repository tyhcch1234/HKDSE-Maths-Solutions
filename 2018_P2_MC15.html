Mensuration — Volume of a Solid Right Triangular Prism

Prism Volume Pythagoras' Theorem Right Triangle Area Uniform Cross-Section

QQuestion

[2018MC#15] In the figure, the volume of the solid right triangular prism is

  1. \(544\ \text{cm}^3\)
  2. \(600\ \text{cm}^3\)
  3. \(660\ \text{cm}^3\)
  4. \(720\ \text{cm}^3\)

aUnderstanding the Prism & Its Cross-Section

A right prism has a uniform cross-section along its length. The volume is \[ V = (\text{area of cross-section}) \times (\text{length}). \] Here the cross-section is a right-angled triangle. We know one leg is \(8\ \text{cm}\), the hypotenuse is \(17\ \text{cm}\), and the prism length is \(12\ \text{cm}\).

Step 1 1M
Recognize the shape: a right triangular prism has a right-angled triangle as its uniform cross-section. Let the unknown leg be \(b\).
Hint: The "length" \(12\ \text{cm}\) is perpendicular to the triangular faces — it is the distance between the two parallel triangular ends.
Step 2 1M
Apply Pythagoras' theorem to the right triangle: \[ 8^2 + b^2 = 17^2 \] \[ b^2 = 289 - 64 = 225 \quad\Rightarrow\quad b = \sqrt{225} = 15\ \text{cm}. \]
Hint: Remember \(17\) is the hypotenuse (the longest side), so it must be paired with the \(8\) leg, not the unknown leg.
Unknown leg \(= 15\ \text{cm}\)
Step 3 1A
Area of the right triangular cross-section: \[ A = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 15 = 60\ \text{cm}^2. \]
Hint: In a right triangle, the two legs are perpendicular, so they can serve directly as base and height.
Cross-section area \(= 60\ \text{cm}^2\)

bComputing the Prism Volume

Now multiply the cross-section area by the prism length to find the volume. \[ V = A \times L = 60 \times 12. \]

Step 1 1M
Volume of a prism formula: \[ V = (\text{area of cross-section}) \times (\text{length}). \] Substitute \(A = 60\ \text{cm}^2\) and length \(= 12\ \text{cm}\): \[ V = 60 \times 12. \]
Hint: The cross-section here is the triangular face. The length is the perpendicular distance between the two triangular ends.
Step 2 1A
\[ V = 60 \times 12 = 720\ \text{cm}^3. \]
Hint: Match the options — \(720\ \text{cm}^3\) is option D.
Volume \(= 720\ \text{cm}^3\) ⇒ Answer: D

✓Summary of Answers

  • Right triangle legs: \(8\ \text{cm}\) and \(15\ \text{cm}\) (from \(\sqrt{17^2 - 8^2}\)).
  • Cross-section area: \(\tfrac{1}{2}(8)(15) =\) \(60\ \text{cm}^2\).
  • Prism length: \(12\ \text{cm}\).
  • Volume: \(60 \times 12 =\) \(720\ \text{cm}^3\).
  • Correct MC option: D.
