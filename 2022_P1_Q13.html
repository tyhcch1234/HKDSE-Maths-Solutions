Step 1 — compare surface areas to get the volume ratio.

HKDSE 2022 · P1 Q13Mensuration

There are two solid metal spheres. The ratio of the surface area of the smaller sphere to the surface area of the larger sphere is 4 : 9. The radius of the larger sphere is 9 cm.

(a) Express, in terms of $\pi$, the volume of the smaller sphere. [3 marks]

(b) The two spheres are melted and recast into two solid right circular cones. Denote these two circular cones by $A$ and $B$. It is given that the height and the base radius of $A$ are $10\text{ cm}$ and $6\text{ cm}$ respectively. A student finds that the base radius of $B$ is $12\text{ cm}$. The student claims that $A$ and $B$ are similar. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer. [4 marks]

Step 1 · Radius ratio from surface areas

For similar solids, surface area $\propto (\text{linear size})^2$: $$\frac{4\pi r_s^{2}}{4\pi r_L^{2}}=\frac{4}{9}\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{r_s}{r_L}=\frac{2}{3}$$ With $r_L=9$ cm: $$r_s=\frac{2}{3}\times 9 = 6\text{ cm}$$
Ratio of volumes of similar solids $=$ (ratio of radii)$^3$.

Step 2 · Volume of the smaller sphere

$$V_s=\frac{4}{3}\pi r_s^{3}=\frac{4}{3}\pi (6)^{3}=\frac{4}{3}\pi(216)$$ $$V_s=288\pi\text{ cm}^3$$ (Also, $V_L=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(9)^3=972\pi$ cm$^3$, and $288:972 = 8:27$ as expected.)

Step 3 · Volume of cone $A$

Cone $A$: base radius $6$ cm, height $10$ cm. $$V_A=\frac{1}{3}\pi (6)^{2}(10)=\frac{1}{3}\pi(36)(10)=120\pi\text{ cm}^3$$

Step 4 · Volume of cone $B$ (metal is conserved)

Total metal $=$ (small sphere) $+$ (large sphere) $-$ (cone $A$): $$V_B=288\pi+\frac{4}{3}\pi(9)^{3}-120\pi$$ $$=288\pi+972\pi-120\pi=1140\pi\text{ cm}^3$$

Step 5 · Test the similarity claim

If $A$ and $B$ were similar, the ratio of their volumes would equal $$\left(\frac{\text{base radius of }B}{\text{base radius of }A}\right)^3=\left(\frac{12}{6}\right)^{3}=8$$ But the actual volume ratio is $$\frac{V_B}{V_A}=\frac{1140\pi}{120\pi}=\frac{19}{2}=9.5$$
Since $\frac{19}{2}\neq 8$, the two cones are not similar.

Step 6 · Conclusion

For similar right circular cones, $r_B/r_A = h_B/h_A$ must hold, i.e. $$h_B=\frac{12}{6}\times 10 = 20\text{ cm}$$ Then $V_B$ would be $\left(\frac{12}{6}\right)^3 V_A = 8(120\pi)=960\pi \neq 1140\pi$. So the volumes are inconsistent with similarity.
Answers:
(a) $288\pi\text{ cm}^3$
(b) The claim is not correct. The volume ratio is $\dfrac{19}{2}$ while the cube of the base-radius ratio is $8$; since $9.5 \neq 8$, cones $A$ and $B$ are not similar.
