Coordinate Geometry — Reflection & Rotation of a Point
Reflection in y-axis Rotation 90° about Origin Coordinates Transformation Multiple Choice

Question Full Text

Read carefully — the point is transformed twice.

The coordinates of the point \(P\) are \((7,\,-5)\).  \(P\) is reflected with respect to the \(y\)-axis to the point \(Q\).  \(Q\) is then rotated clockwise about the origin through \(90^\circ\) to the point \(R\).  Find the \(x\)-coordinate of \(R\).
  1. \(-7\)
  2. \(-5\)
  3. \(5\)
  4. \(7\)
The correct option is B  —  \(x\)-coordinate of \(R\) is \(-5\).

Part (a) Reflection in the y-axis

Find the coordinates of \(Q\), the image of \(P(7,-5)\) under reflection in the \(y\)-axis.

P = (7, -5) Q = (-7, -5) distance from y-axis = 7
Step 11M

Recall the reflection rule for the \(y\)-axis: a point \((x,y)\) maps to \((-x,\,y)\). The \(y\)-coordinate is unchanged; the \(x\)-coordinate changes sign.

Hint: The \(y\)-axis is the "mirror". A point and its mirror image are always the same distance from the mirror line, on opposite sides.
Step 21A

Apply the rule to \(P(7,-5)\):

\[ Q = (\,-7,\; -5\,) \]
Hint: Check with the diagram — \(P\) is \(7\) units to the right of the \(y\)-axis, so \(Q\) must be \(7\) units to the left. Both sit on the horizontal line \(y=-5\).
\(Q = (-7,\,-5)\)

Part (b) Clockwise 90° rotation about O

Find the coordinates of \(R\), the image of \(Q\) after a clockwise rotation of \(90^\circ\) about the origin.

The button rotates \(Q\) clockwise by \(90^\circ\). Use the slider to see where \(Q\) lands at any angle — the landing spot at \(90^\circ\) reveals \(R\).
Q = (-7, -5) R (at 90°) = (-5, 7) x-coordinate of R = -5 |OQ| = 8.60
Step 11M

Recall the rotation rule about the origin. A clockwise rotation of \(90^\circ\) sends \((x,y)\) to \((y,\,-x)\).

Hint: Why? Clockwise \(90^\circ\) swaps the coordinates and flips the sign of the new \(y\)-coordinate. Test it on \((1,0)\): a clockwise quarter-turn gives \((0,-1)\), and \((y,-x)=(0,-1)\). ✓
Step 21M

Apply to \(Q(-7,-5)\) with \(x=-7,\;y=-5\):

\[ R = (\,y,\; -x\,) = (\,-5,\; -(-7)\,) = (\,-5,\;7\,) \]
Hint: Use the slider in the diagram — every intermediate angle keeps \(OQ = OR\), and at \(90^\circ\) the point lands exactly at \((-5,7)\).
Step 31A

The question asks only for the \(x\)-coordinate of \(R\):

\[ x_R = -5 \]
Hint: Common trap — a rotation of \(90^\circ\) clockwise sends \(x\) to the old \(y\) value. Since \(y_Q=-5\), the new \(x\) is \(-5\), i.e. option B.
\(R = (-5,\,7)\), so the \(x\)-coordinate of \(R\) is \(\boxed{-5}\)  (option B).

Summary At a glance

  • P\((7,\,-5)\) — the starting point.
  • Q\((-7,\,-5)\) — reflection of \(P\) in the \(y\)-axis: \((x,y)\to(-x,y)\).
  • R\((-5,\,7)\) — \(Q\) rotated \(90^\circ\) clockwise: \((x,y)\to(y,-x)\).
  • Answer\(x\)-coordinate of \(R\) = \(-5\), i.e. option B.
A. \(-7\)
B. \(-5\)  ✓
