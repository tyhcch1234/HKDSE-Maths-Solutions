Question Full Statement

In the figure, the equation of the straight line \(L\) is \[ ax+by+15=0 . \] Which of the following are true?

I.   \(a>b\)
II.   \(a>-3\)
III.   \(b>-5\)

A. I and II only    B. I and III only    C. II and III only    D. I, II and III

(The figure shows \(L\) with positive \(x\)-intercept and positive \(y\)-intercept; the \(x\)-intercept is larger than the \(y\)-intercept.)

Step 11M Intercepts of \(L\)

Put \(y=0\):   \(ax+15=0\Rightarrow\) \(x\)-intercept \(=-\dfrac{15}{a}\).

Put \(x=0\):   \(by+15=0\Rightarrow\) \(y\)-intercept \(=-\dfrac{15}{b}\).

Hint: Always isolate the intercept by setting the other variable to zero.
Step 21M Signs of \(a\) and \(b\)

Both intercepts are positive: \[ -\frac{15}{a}>0 \quad\text{and}\quad -\frac{15}{b}>0 .\] Since \(15>0\), this forces \[ a<0 \quad\text{and}\quad b<0 .\]

Hint: \(-\dfrac{15}{a}>0\iff \dfrac{15}{a}<0\), so \(a\) must be negative.
Step 31M Compare the two intercepts

The \(x\)-intercept is larger than the \(y\)-intercept: \[ -\frac{15}{a} > -\frac{15}{b} .\] Since \(ab>0\) (both negative), multiplying both sides by \(ab>0\) preserves the inequality sign: \[ -15b > -15a .\] Dividing by \(-15<0\) reverses the sign: \[ bb .\]

Hint: Multiplying an inequality by an unknown sign is dangerous — here you know \(ab>0\), so it is safe.
Conclusion: Statement I is TRUE.

Reducing the \(x\)-intercept below 5 makes statement II fail — find the boundary value of \(a\).

Step 11M Condition from the figure

The figure shows the \(x\)-intercept of \(L\) is larger than \(5\): \[ -\frac{15}{a}>5 .\]

Hint: "Larger than 5" means the intercept lies strictly to the right of \(x=5\).
Step 21M Solve the inequality

Since \(a<0\), multiply both sides of \(-\dfrac{15}{a}>5\) by \(a<0\) and reverse the sign: \[ -15 < 5a .\] Dividing by \(5>0\): \[ -3 < a \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a>-3 .\]

Hint: Alternative: \(-\dfrac{15}{a}>5 \iff \dfrac{15}{-a}>5 \iff \dfrac{15}{5}>-a \iff 3>-a \iff a>-3\).
Conclusion: Statement II is TRUE. Indeed \(a\) can be \(-2,-1,\dots\) but never \(-3\) exactly (at \(a=-3\) the intercept is exactly \(5\), not larger).

The figure shows the \(y\)-intercept is smaller than 3 — the opposite direction from the claim.

Step 11M Condition from the figure

The figure shows the \(y\)-intercept of \(L\) is smaller than \(3\): \[ -\frac{15}{b}<3 .\]

Hint: "Smaller than 3" means the intercept lies strictly below \(y=3\), i.e. the figure marks 3 above the intercept.
Step 21M Solve the inequality

Since \(b<0\), multiply both sides by \(b<0\) and reverse the sign: \[ -15 > 3b .\] Dividing by \(3>0\): \[ -5 > b \quad\Longrightarrow\quad b<-5 .\]

Hint: The true range is \(b<-5\). Statement III claims \(b>-5\) — the exact opposite!
Conclusion: Statement III is FALSE. For example \(b=-6\) gives intercept \(2.5<3\), so the figure holds, but \(b>-5\) fails.

Summary Final Answers at a Glance

  • Intercepts:   \(x\)-intercept \(=-\dfrac{15}{a}\),   \(y\)-intercept \(=-\dfrac{15}{b}\).
  • From positive intercepts:   \(a<0\) and \(b<0\).
  • I. \(a>b\) — TRUE (since \(-\frac{15}{a}>-\frac{15}{b}\) and \(ab>0\)).
  • II. \(a>-3\) — TRUE (since \(-\frac{15}{a}>5\) and \(a<0\)).
  • III. \(b>-5\) — FALSE (in fact \(-\frac{15}{b}<3 \Rightarrow b<-5\)).
  • Correct option: A (I and II only).
