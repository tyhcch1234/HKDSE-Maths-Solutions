Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Area

T10 Q10

Find the area of a triangle with base $8$ m and height $5$ m.

Solution

Use the triangle area formula: $A=\frac{1}{2}\times \text{base}\times \text{height}$。
Substitute the given values: $A=\frac{1}{2}\times 8\times 5$。
Calculate: $A=4\times 5=20$。
The units are square metres, so $A=20\text{ m}^2$。
The area of the triangle is $20\text{ m}^2$。
