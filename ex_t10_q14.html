Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Area

T10 Q14

A rectangle has a length of $7$ cm and a width of $5$ cm. Find its area.

Solution

The area of a rectangle is given by \(\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width}\).
Substitute the given values: \(\text{Area} = 7 \times 5\).
Multiply: \(7 \times 5 = 35\).
The area of the rectangle is \(35\text{ cm}^2\).
