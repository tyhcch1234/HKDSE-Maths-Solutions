Geometry (106) · Sub-Topic: Circle Properties

T11 Q28

In a cyclic quadrilateral, one angle is \( 75^\circ \). What is the size of the opposite angle?

Solution

In a cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles are supplementary, so their sum is $180^\circ$.
Let the opposite angle be $x$. Then $x + 75^\circ = 180^\circ$.
Subtract $75^\circ$ from both sides: $x = 180^\circ - 75^\circ = 105^\circ$.
The size of the opposite angle is $105^\circ$.
