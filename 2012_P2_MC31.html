HKDSE Math 2012 Paper 2 Q31

H.C.F. & L.C.M.

The H.C.F. and the L.C.M. of three expressions are $ab^2$ and $4a^4b^5c^6$ respectively. If the first expression and the second expression are $2a^2b^4c$ and $4a^4b^2c^6$ respectively, then the third expression is

A. $ab^2$
B. $ab^5$
C. $2ab^2c$
D. $2ab^5c$

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — Write the H.C.F. and L.C.M. in prime-factor form

Include the prime factor $2$ explicitly so the three expressions can be compared factor by factor:

$\text{H.C.F.}=ab^2=2^0\,a^1\,b^2\,c^0$

$\text{L.C.M.}=4a^4b^5c^6=2^2\,a^4\,b^5\,c^6$

Step 2 — Factorise the two given expressions

$E_1=2a^2b^4c=2^1\,a^2\,b^4\,c^1$

$E_2=4a^4b^2c^6=2^2\,a^4\,b^2\,c^6$

Step 3 — Use the H.C.F. (lowest powers)

The H.C.F. takes the smallest power of each factor among all three expressions. Let the third expression be $E_3=2^p\,a^q\,b^r\,c^s$.

  • For $2$: $\min(1,2,p)=0 \Rightarrow p=0$.
  • For $a$: $\min(2,4,q)=1 \Rightarrow q=1$.
  • For $b$: $\min(4,2,r)=2 \Rightarrow r\ge 2$.
  • For $c$: $\min(1,6,s)=0 \Rightarrow s=0$.

Step 4 — Use the L.C.M. (highest powers)

The L.C.M. takes the largest power of each factor among all three expressions:

  • For $2$: $\max(1,2,p)=2$, already satisfied by $p=0$.
  • For $a$: $\max(2,4,q)=4$, already satisfied by $q=1$.
  • For $b$: $\max(4,2,r)=5 \Rightarrow r=5$.
  • For $c$: $\max(1,6,s)=6$, already satisfied by $s=0$.

Step 5 — Assemble the third expression

$E_3=2^0\,a^1\,b^5\,c^0=ab^5$

Answer: B. $ab^5$
