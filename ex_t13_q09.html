Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Ratios

T13 Q09

In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite to angle $\theta$ is $5$ and the hypotenuse is $13$. Find $\cos\theta$.

Solution

$5$-$12$-$13$ triangle: $\cos\theta = \tfrac{12}{13}$.
