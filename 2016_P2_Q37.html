HKDSE Math 2016 Paper 2 Q37

Trigonometry

Let $a$ and $b$ be constants. The figure shows the graph of $y=a\cos 2x^{\circ}$, where the maximum value of the curve is $2$ and the point marked $b$ on the $x$-axis is the first positive $x$-value at which the curve attains this maximum.

A. $a=-2$, $b=90$
B. $a=-2$, $b=360$
C. $a=2$, $b=90$
D. $a=2$, $b=360$

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — Find the amplitude $a$

The maximum value of $y=a\cos 2x^{\circ}$ is $|a|$. Since the maximum shown is $2$,

$|a|=2$

Step 2 — Determine the sign of $a$

At $x=0$, $\cos 0^{\circ}=1$, so $y=a$. The curve has a minimum (turning point below the $x$-axis) at $x=0$, hence $a<0$.

$a=-2$

Step 3 — Find $b$ (first positive maximum)

With $a=-2$, the maximum $y=2$ occurs when $\cos 2x^{\circ}=-1$:

$2x^{\circ}=180^{\circ}\Rightarrow x=90$

This is the first positive $x$-value at which the maximum occurs, so $b=90$.

Answer: A. $a=-2$, $b=90$
