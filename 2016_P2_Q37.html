Topic 13 · Trigonometry — graph of $y=a\cos 2x^\circ$

Interactive Graph

curve $y=a\cos 2x^\circ$ maximum point $(b,\,2)$ axes / $x$-intercepts period marks

Question

Let $a$ and $b$ be constants. If the figure shows the graph of $y=a\cos 2x^\circ$, then
A.  $a=-2$ and $b=90$
B.  $a=-2$ and $b=360$
C.  $a=2$ and $b=90$
D.  $a=2$ and $b=360$

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1 · Read the graph

At $x=0$ the curve passes through the origin level, rises to a maximum $y=2$ at $x=b$, then returns. So the curve is a cosine-type wave sitting on the $x$-axis with maximum value $+2$ at $x=b$.

From the figure: the maximum is reached at $x=b$, and the first $x$-intercept after that is at $x=2b$.

Hint 1: The graph is upside-down relative to $y=\cos x^\circ$? No — it starts low at the $y$-axis and rises, so the standard cosine is flipped (or shifted). Check the value at $x=0$.
STEP 2 · Find $b$ — the position of the maximum

For $y=\cos 2x^\circ$, the maximum value $+1$ occurs when $2x^\circ=0^\circ,\,360^\circ,\dots$, i.e. $x=0,\,180^\circ,\dots$

But this graph attains its maximum at $x=b$ and the curve crosses the $x$-axis at $x=2b$ (the first intercept). Since the full wave has period $180^\circ$, the first minimum/zero pattern gives

$$2b = 180 \quad\Rightarrow\quad b=90$$

Hint 2: One complete cycle of $\cos 2x^\circ$ has period $360^\circ/2 = 180^\circ$. The peak is halfway, at $90^\circ$.
STEP 3 · Find $a$ — the amplitude and sign

Substitute the peak point $(x,y)=(b,\,2)=(90,\,2)$ into $y=a\cos 2x^\circ$:

$$2 = a\cos(2\cdot 90^\circ)=a\cos 180^\circ = -a$$

Hence $a=-2$.

Check: at $x=0$, $y=a\cos 0^\circ = a = -2$, which matches the graph (the curve at the $y$-axis is below the $x$-axis).

Hint 3: $\cos 180^\circ=-1$, so a negative $a$ is needed to produce a positive peak at $x=90^\circ$.
STEP 4 · Verify the shape

With $a=-2,\;b=90$: $y=-2\cos 2x^\circ$.

Period $=180^\circ$. Maximum $=+2$ at $x=90^\circ,\,270^\circ,\dots$ Minimum $=-2$ at $x=0^\circ,\,180^\circ,\dots$ $x$-intercepts at $x=45^\circ,\,135^\circ,\dots$ — exactly the wave drawn.

The graph correctly shows maximum $2$ (so $|a|=2$) and the first peak at $x=90$ (so $b=90$).
Answer: C  —  $a=-2$ and $b=90$
