The box-and-whisker diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of training hours of some engineers in a year. Find the upper quartile of the distribution.

A. \(20\)
B. \(40\)
C. \(60\)
D. \(70\)

The upper quartile \(Q_3\) is the right-hand edge of the box (the side closer to the maximum). From the diagram its position falls on \(60\).

Hint: The upper quartile separates the top \(25\%\) of the data, so it is always at the upper (right) end of the box — not at the whisker tip.

Hence the upper quartile is \(60\). Comparing with the options:

Answer: \[ Q_3 = 60 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{\text{C}} \]

Part (b) — What "Upper Quartile" Really Means

\(Q_3\) cuts off the top \(25\%\) of the data. Drag the slider to move a split line and watch the percentages below (and above) it change. At \(Q_3 = 60\) the split is exactly \(75\% / 25\%\).

The three quartiles divide the ordered data into four equal parts:

\[ \underbrace{\;\;25\%\;\;}_{10\to Q_1=20}\;\; \underbrace{\;\;25\%\;\;}_{20\to 40}\;\; \underbrace{\;\;25\%\;\;}_{40\to Q_3=60}\;\; \underbrace{\;\;25\%\;\;}_{60\to 80} \]
Hint: The right-hand edge of the box (\(Q_3\)) is where \(75\%\) of the data lies below it and \(25\%\) lies above it.
21A

So the value at the right edge of the box is the required upper quartile.

Upper quartile \(= Q_3 = \boxed{60}\) (option C)

\(20\) is the lower quartile \(Q_1\) (left edge of the box) — that separates the bottom \(25\%\), not the top \(25\%\).

Hint: Don't confuse the two ends of the box. Low value \(\to Q_1\), high value \(\to Q_3\).
21M

\(40\) is the median (the vertical line inside the box) — it splits the data into a lower \(50\%\) and an upper \(50\%\).

\(70\) is not a landmark at all: it lies on the right whisker between \(Q_3=60\) and \(\max=80\).

Hint: Only five values are marked: min, \(Q_1\), median, \(Q_3\), max. Any other value read from the axis is just an interior data value.
31A

Therefore the only correct choice is the right-hand box edge.

Answer: \(\boxed{\text{C}\;\;60}\)

Summary — At a Glance

  • Read landmarks off the diagram: \(\min=10,\; Q_1=20,\; \text{median}=40,\; Q_3=60,\; \max=80\).
  • The upper quartile is the right-hand edge of the box.
  • It marks the \(75\%\) point, leaving the top \(25\%\) of the data above it.
  • \(20\) is \(Q_1\), \(40\) is the median, \(70\) is just a point on the right whisker.
  • Final answer: C — \(60\)
