Question. Simplify the following expression.

$(4x+y)^2-(4x-y)^2 =$
  • A. $0$
  • B. $2y^2$
  • C. $8xy$
  • D. $16xy$
1Spot the pattern

The expression has the form $A^2-B^2$, where $A=4x+y$ and $B=4x-y$. This is the difference of two squares.

$A^2-B^2=(A+B)(A-B)$

Hint: Recognising $A^2-B^2$ avoids expanding both brackets separately.
Difference of Two Squares \(A^{2}-B^{2}=(A+B)(A-B)\) With \(A=4x+y\) and \(B=4x-y\) the expression collapses to a product of two simple factors.
Simplifying \(A+B\) and \(A-B\) \(A+B=8x,\qquad A-B=2y\) The \(x\) terms cancel in \(A-B\) and the \(y\) terms cancel in \(A+B\).
2Compute $A+B$ and $A-B$

$A+B=(4x+y)+(4x-y)=8x$

$A-B=(4x+y)-(4x-y)=4x+y-4x+y=2y$

Hint: The $x$ terms cancel in $A-B$; the $y$ terms cancel in $A+B$. This is the heart of the trick.
3Multiply the factors

$(4x+y)^2-(4x-y)^2=(8x)(2y)$

$=16xy$

Hint: $8\times 2=16$, and $x\cdot y=xy$.
4Check by direct expansion

$(4x+y)^2=16x^2+8xy+y^2$

$(4x-y)^2=16x^2-8xy+y^2$

Subtracting: $8xy-(-8xy)=16xy$ ✓

Hint: Both expansions agree — the answer is confirmed.
Answer: D  —  $(4x+y)^2-(4x-y)^2 = 16xy$

Question. Simplify \((3p+2q)^{2}-(3p-2q)^{2}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. This is \(A^{2}-B^{2}\) with \(A=3p+2q\) and \(B=3p-2q\).
Step 2. \(A+B=(3p+2q)+(3p-2q)=6p\) and \(A-B=(3p+2q)-(3p-2q)=4q\).
Step 3. Multiply: \(6p\times4q=24pq\).
Step 4. Check by expanding: \((9p^{2}+12pq+4q^{2})-(9p^{2}-12pq+4q^{2})=24pq\) ✓.
Answer: \(24pq\)
