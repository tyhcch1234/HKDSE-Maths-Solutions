Question MULTIPLE CHOICE

If an interior angle of a regular polygon is \(5\) times an exterior angle of the polygon, which of the following is / are true?

I. Each interior angle of the polygon is \(150^\circ\).
II. The number of diagonals of the polygon is \(6\).
III. The number of folds of rotational symmetry of the polygon is \(6\).

A. I only     B. II only     C. I and III only     D. II and III only

Part (a) — Find the number of sides \(n\) SETUP

An interior angle is \(5\times\) an exterior angle. Use the angle sums to find \(n\), then check statement I.

Step 11M
Let \(n\) be the number of sides. For any \(n\)-gon, \[\text{Sum of interior angles}=(n-2)\times180^\circ,\qquad \text{Sum of exterior angles}=360^\circ.\]
Hint: For a regular polygon, all \(n\) exterior angles are equal, so one exterior angle \(=\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}\), and one interior angle \(=\dfrac{(n-2)180^\circ}{n}\).
Step 21M1A
An interior angle is \(5\) times an exterior angle. Using a single pair: \[\frac{(n-2)180^\circ}{n}=5\cdot\frac{360^\circ}{n}.\] Multiply both sides by \(n\): \[(n-2)180=1800 \;\Rightarrow\; n-2=10 \;\Rightarrow\; n=12.\] (Equivalently: sum of interior angles \(=5\times\) sum of exterior angles \(\Rightarrow (n-2)180=5\times360\).)
Hint: Once \(n\) is fixed, every angle and every count (diagonals, symmetry) is determined — which is exactly what statements I, II, III ask about.
\(n=12\), i.e. the polygon is a regular 12-gon (dodecagon).
Step 31A
Check statement I: each interior angle \(=\dfrac{(12-2)\times180^\circ}{12}=\dfrac{1800^\circ}{12}=150^\circ\).
Hint: Also check with the exterior angle: \(360^\circ/12=30^\circ\) and \(180^\circ-30^\circ=150^\circ\). ✔
Statement I is TRUE: each interior angle \(=150^\circ\).

Part (b) — Diagonals & rotational symmetry CHECK II & III

Now examine the two counting statements for the regular 12-gon found above.

Step 11M
Statement II — diagonals. Fix a vertex \(P\) of the 12-gon. The segment joining \(P\) to another vertex is a side if that vertex is adjacent to \(P\) (2 of them), and \(P\) itself does not form a segment. So \[\text{diagonals with an end-point at }P=12-2-1=9.\]
Hint: Don't forget to subtract \(P\) itself — you cannot join a vertex to itself.
Step 21M1A
Count end-points over all 12 vertices: \(9\times12=108\). Each diagonal has 2 end-points, so each diagonal has been counted twice: \[\text{number of diagonals}=\frac{9\times12}{2}=54.\]
Hint: General formula: \(\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}\). Here \(\dfrac{12\times9}{2}=54\).
Statement II is FALSE: there are \(54\) diagonals, not \(6\).
Step 31A
Statement III — rotational symmetry. A regular \(n\)-gon maps onto itself when rotated about its centre by any multiple of \(\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}\), giving exactly \(n\) rotational positions (including the \(0^\circ\) identity). So the number of folds of rotational symmetry is \(n=12\).
Hint: “Folds of rotational symmetry” = number of distinct rotations (about the centre) that carry the polygon onto itself = \(n\), not \(\dfrac{n}{2}\). (Only reflectional symmetry has \(n\) lines, which is a different count.)
Statement III is FALSE: the number of folds of rotational symmetry is \(12\), not \(6\).
Step 41A
Collect the truth values:
  • I: TRUE
  • II: FALSE (54 ≠ 6)
  • III: FALSE (12 ≠ 6)
Hence only I is true.
Correct choice: A. I only

Summary — Answers at a glance

  • Interior angle \(=5\times\) exterior angle \(\Rightarrow (n-2)180=5\times360 \Rightarrow n=12\).
  • I. Each interior angle \(=\dfrac{10\times180}{12}=150^\circ\) → TRUE
  • II. Diagonals \(=\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}=\dfrac{12\times9}{2}=54\) → FALSE
  • III. Folds of rotational symmetry \(=n=12\) → FALSE
Answer: A. I only
