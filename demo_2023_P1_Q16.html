Question & solution

(b) Denote the circle \(x^2+y^2-6x-12y+20=0\) by \(C\). Find the constant \(m\) such that the straight line \(y=mx\) cuts \(C\) at the points \(Q\) and \(R\) with \(OQ:QR=1:4\), where \(O\) is the origin.

STEP 1 · Centre & radius of C
\[ x^2+y^2-6x-12y+20=0 \;\Rightarrow\; (x-3)^2+(y-6)^2=25 \]

Centre \((3,6)\), radius \(5\).

STEP 2 · Substitute y = mx
\[ x^2+(mx)^2-6x-12(mx)+20=0 \;\Rightarrow\; (m^2+1)x^2-(12m+6)x+20=0 \]

Let the \(x\)-coordinates of \(Q\) and \(R\) be \(t\) and \(5t\) (since \(OR=OQ+QR=5\,OQ\)).

Why \(5t\)? \(OQ:QR=1:4\) means \(QR=4\,OQ\), so \(OR=OQ+QR=5\,OQ\). On the line through \(O\), distance scales with \(x\).
STEP 3 · Sum & product of roots
\[ t+5t=\frac{12m+6}{m^2+1},\qquad t\cdot 5t=\frac{20}{m^2+1} \]

Using \(5a^2=36b\) from part (a) with \(a=-\,\dfrac{12m+6}{m^2+1}\) and \(b=\dfrac{20}{m^2+1}\):

\[ 5\left(\frac{12m+6}{m^2+1}\right)^2 = 36\left(\frac{20}{m^2+1}\right) \]
STEP 4 · Solve for m
\[ (12m+6)^2=144(m^2+1)\;\Rightarrow\;(m+\tfrac12)^2=m^2+1\;\Rightarrow\;m=\frac34 \]
Answer: \(m=\dfrac{3}{4}\)
