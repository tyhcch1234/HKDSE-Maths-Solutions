2021 Paper 1 — Question 5

Algebra Linear Equations Word Problems

Q Question Overview

The number of stickers owned by a boy is 3 times that owned by a girl. If the boy gives 20 of his stickers to the girl, then the number of stickers owned by the girl is 2 times that owned by the boy. Find the total number of stickers owned by the boy and the girl.

Key Idea: Let one unknown represent the girl's stickers. Express the boy's stickers in terms of that unknown, then form an equation from the transfer condition.

[4 marks]

👁 Visual: Sticker Transfer

Before the transfer, the boy has 3 times the girl's amount. After giving 20 stickers, the girl has 2 times the boy's remaining amount.

Before Transfer

Boy: 3x
Girl: x

After Giving 20

Boy: 3x − 20
Girl: x + 20

✎ Step-by-Step Solution

1

Define the unknown

Let \(x\) be the number of stickers owned by the girl.

We start with the smaller amount so the other can be expressed as a multiple (3x).
2

Express the boy's stickers

The boy has 3 times the girl's amount, so the boy has \(3x\) stickers.

Key phrase: "3 times that owned by a girl" → multiply by 3.
3

Show the transfer

Boy gives 20 away: boy now has \(3x - 20\). Girl receives 20: girl now has \(x + 20\).

The total number of stickers is unchanged: \((3x) + x = (3x - 20) + (x + 20)\).
4

Form the equation

After transfer, the girl has 2 times the boy's stickers:

\(2(3x - 20) = x + 20\)
"Girl is 2 times the boy" → Girl's amount = 2 × (Boy's amount). So \(x+20 = 2(3x-20)\), which is the same equation.
5

Solve for \(x\)

Expand and solve the linear equation:

\(6x - 40 = x + 20\)

\(6x - x = 20 + 40\)

\(5x = 60\)

\(x = 12\)

Combine like terms: bring \(x\) to the left, constants to the right.
6

Find the total

Girl has \(x = 12\). Boy has \(3x = 36\).

Total \(= 12 + 36 = 48\).

Total stickers = 48
Alternatively, total \(= (3x) + x = 4x = 4 \times 12 = 48\).

Final Answer

48 stickers
