In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a rhombus. \(C\) is the centre of the circle \(BDE\) and \(ADE\) is a straight line. \(BE\) and \(CD\) intersect at \(F\). If \(\angle ADC = 118^\circ\), then \(\angle DFE =\)

Options:  A. \(62^\circ\)   B. \(78^\circ\)   C. \(85^\circ\)   D. \(87^\circ\)

Step A — Find \(\angle BCD\)

Step 1

In a rhombus \(ABCD\), opposite sides are parallel: \(BC /\!/ AD\) and \(AB /\!/ CD\).

Hint: Look at \(BC /\!/ AD\). The line \(CD\) is a transversal.
Step 2

Since \(BC /\!/ AD\) and \(CD\) is a transversal, the interior angles on the same side are supplementary.

\[ \angle BCD + \angle ADC = 180^\circ \]
Hint: Interior angles on the same side of a transversal (co-interior angles) add to \(180^\circ\).
Step 3

Substitute \(\angle ADC = 118^\circ\):

\[ \angle BCD = 180^\circ - 118^\circ = 62^\circ \]
\(\angle BCD = 62^\circ\)
0 / 3

Step B — Find \(\angle BED\) (Angle at Centre)

Step 1

\(C\) is the centre of the circle through \(B\), \(D\) and \(E\). So \(\angle BCD\) is the angle at the centre standing on arc \(BD\).

Hint: The angle at the centre subtended by a chord is twice any inscribed angle on the same arc.
Step 2

\(\angle BED\) is the inscribed angle standing on the same arc \(BD\), so:

\[ \angle BED = \tfrac{1}{2}\,\angle BCD \]
Hint: “Angle at centre = \(2 \times\) angle at circumference.”
Step 3

Substitute \(\angle BCD = 62^\circ\):

\[ \angle BED = 62^\circ \div 2 = 31^\circ \]
\(\angle BED = 31^\circ\)
0 / 3

Step C — Find \(\angle DFE\)

Step 1

Since \(ADE\) is a straight line, \(\angle ADF\) lies on this straight line. Using the exterior angle of \(\triangle DEF\):

\[ \angle DFE = \angle ADF - \angle DEF \]
Hint: The exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles. Rearrange it for \(\angle DFE\).
Step 2

Note \(\angle ADF = \angle ADC = 118^\circ\) and \(\angle DEF = \angle BED = 31^\circ\).

\[ \angle DFE = 118^\circ - 31^\circ \]
Step 3
\[ \angle DFE = 87^\circ \]
\(\angle DFE = 87^\circ\) → Option D
0 / 3

Summary of Answers

  • \(\angle BCD = 62^\circ\) — co-interior angles with \(\angle ADC = 118^\circ\), since \(BC /\!/ AD\).
  • \(\angle BED = 31^\circ\) — angle at centre \(C\) is twice the angle at circumference \(E\).
  • \(\angle DFE = 87^\circ\) — exterior angle of \(\triangle DEF\).
  • Correct option: D
