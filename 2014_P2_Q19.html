HKDSE 2014 · Paper 2 · Q19 · Trigonometry

Unit circle Angle θ Rotation 90°+θ Rotation 360°−θ cos / sin values
[2014MC#19]
$(\cos(90^\circ+\theta)+1)(\sin(360^\circ-\theta)-1) = $
A. $\;-\cos^2\theta$
B. $\;-\sin^2\theta$
C. $\;\cos^2\theta$
D. $\;\sin^2\theta$
Step 1 · Recall allied-angle identities

For any angle $\theta$, the CAST / allied-angle rules give:

$$\cos(90^\circ+\theta) = -\sin\theta$$

$$\sin(360^\circ-\theta) = -\sin\theta$$

Watch the diagram: the green point (90°+θ) has its x-coordinate equal to −sin θ, and the purple point (360°−θ) has y-coordinate −sin θ.
Step 2 · Substitute into the expression

Replace each factor:

$$\big(\cos(90^\circ+\theta)+1\big)\big(\sin(360^\circ-\theta)-1\big)$$

$$= (-\sin\theta+1)(-\sin\theta-1)$$

Step 3 · Expand the product

This is a difference of squares pattern $(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2$ with $a=-\sin\theta$, $b=1$:

$$(-\!\sin\theta+1)(-\!\sin\theta-1)=(-\!\sin\theta)^2-1^2$$

$$= \sin^2\theta-1$$

Step 4 · Use the Pythagorean identity

The identity $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$ gives $\sin^2\theta-1=-\cos^2\theta$:

$$= -(1-\sin^2\theta) = -\cos^2\theta$$

Step 5 · Conclusion

The expression simplifies to $\;-\cos^2\theta$.

Answer: A  ·  $-\cos^2\theta$
