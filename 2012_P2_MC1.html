Laws of Indices Power of a Product Quotient Rule Multiple-Choice

Q The Question

Simplify:

\[ \frac{(2x^{4})^{3}}{2x^{5}} = \;? \]

§ Index Laws You Need

×
Product Rule\(a^{m}\cdot a^{n}=a^{m+n}\)
÷
Quotient Rule\(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\)
( )ⁿ
Power of a Power / Product\((ab)^{n}=a^{n}b^{n},\quad (a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn}\)

→ Step-by-Step Solution

1 Expand the numerator's power

The numerator is \((2x^{4})^{3}\). Apply the power of a product rule: raise every factor inside the bracket to the power outside.

\[ (2x^{4})^{3} = 2^{3}\cdot (x^{4})^{3} \]
💡 Hint \((ab)^{n}=a^{n}b^{n}\). Here \(a=2\), \(b=x^{4}\), \(n=3\).
2 Simplify the numerator

Compute each part:

\[ 2^{3}=8 \qquad (x^{4})^{3}=x^{4\times 3}=x^{12} \]

So the numerator becomes:

\[ (2x^{4})^{3} = 8x^{12} \]
💡 Hint Power of a power: \((a^{m})^{n}=a^{m\times n}\). Keep the base \(x\), multiply the exponents: \(4\times 3=12\).
3 Rewrite as a single fraction

Substitute the simplified numerator into the expression:

\[ \frac{(2x^{4})^{3}}{2x^{5}} = \frac{8x^{12}}{2x^{5}} \]

Now separate the numbers from the powers of \(x\):

\[ = \frac{8}{2}\cdot \frac{x^{12}}{x^{5}} \]
4 Divide the coefficients

Handle the numbers first:

\[ \frac{8}{2} = 4 \]
💡 Hint It is easier to simplify the coefficient \(8\div 2\) and the indices \(x^{12}\div x^{5}\) separately, then multiply the results back together.
5 Apply the quotient rule to the powers

For the same base \(x\), subtract the indices:

\[ \frac{x^{12}}{x^{5}} = x^{12-5} = x^{7} \]
💡 Hint \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\). Keep the base \(x\), subtract the exponents: \(12-5=7\).
6 Combine and conclude

Multiply the simplified parts together:

\[ \frac{(2x^{4})^{3}}{2x^{5}} = 4\cdot x^{7} = 4x^{7} \]

Comparing with the options, the answer is C.

💡 Why not the others?
  • A \(3x^{2}\): wrong coefficient and wrong index (subtracting wrongly).
  • B \(3x^{7}\): correct index, but the coefficient \(8\div 2=4\), not \(3\).
  • D \(4x^{59}\): \((x^{4})^{3}\) was mistaken as \(x^{4+3}\) style addition — indices should be multiplied here, giving \(x^{12}\), not \(x^{64}\).
