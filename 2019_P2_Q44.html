HKDSE 2019 · Paper 2 · Q44

Standard score & examination marks

In an examination, the standard deviation of the examination scores is $8$ marks. The examination score of Mary is $69$ marks and her standard score is $0.5$. If the standard score of John in the examination is $-1.5$, then his examination score is

  • A. 45 marks
  • B. 53 marks
  • C. 65 marks
  • D. 77 marks

Step-by-step solution

1Set up the unknown mean μ

Let $\mu$ = mean score, $\sigma = 8$ = standard deviation, and $y$ = John's score.
The standard score (z-score) of a mark $x$ is $\;z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$.

Hint: the z-score measures how many standard deviations a mark is from the mean.

Standard Score \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ x=\mu+z\sigma\) Mary has \(z=0.5\) with \(\sigma=8\), and John has \(z=-1.5\) with the same standard deviation.
2Mary's equation → find μ

Mary: $x=69$, $z=0.5$: $$\mu+0.5\times 8 = 69 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \mu+4=69$$ $$\mu=65$$

Hint: multiply $z\sigma$ and readjust — every z-score equation is linear in $x$.

Solving a Linear Equation \(\mu+4=69\ \Longrightarrow\ \mu=65\) Mary’s score equation is linear in the unknown mean \(\mu\).
3Write John's equation

John: $z=-1.5$, $\sigma=8$: $$\mu + (-1.5)\times 8 = y$$ $$\mu - 12 = y$$

Hint: same formula $x=\mu+z\sigma$, just with a negative z.

4Subtract to get John's score

With $\mu=65$: $y = 65 - 12 = 53$.

(Equivalently, the difference in scores is $(0.5-(-1.5))\times 8 = 2\times 8 = 16$, so $y = 69 - 16 = 53$.)

Hint: the gap between two marks equals (difference of z-scores) × σ.

Difference of Standard Scores \(x_{1}-x_{2}=(z_{1}-z_{2})\,\sigma\) The gap between the two marks is \((0.5-(-1.5))\times8=16\).
Answer: B. 53 marks

Question. In a test the standard deviation of the marks is \(10\). Mary's mark of \(74\) has standard score \(0.8\), while John's standard score is \(-1.2\). Find John's mark.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Using \(x=\mu+z\sigma\) for Mary: \(74=\mu+0.8(10)=\mu+8\), so \(\mu=66\).
Step 2. For John: \(x=66+(-1.2)(10)=66-12=54\).
Step 3. Check: the marks differ by \((0.8-(-1.2))\times10=20\), and \(74-20=54\) ✓
Answer: John's mark is \(54\).
