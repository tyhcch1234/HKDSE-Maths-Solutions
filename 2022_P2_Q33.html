Question

If \(\log_4 y = 2x-1\) and \((\log_4 y)^2 = 20x-31\), then \(\log_2 y =\)

A. 1 or 2.
B. 2 or 4.
C. 3 or 7.
D. 6 or 14.

Step-by-step solution

Step 1
Write the system with \(u=\log_4 y\): \[ \begin{cases} u = 2x-1 & \text{...(1)}\\[2pt] u^2 = 20x-31 & \text{...(2)}\end{cases} \]
Substitution works because (1) already gives \(x\) in terms of \(u\) (equivalently \(u\) in terms of \(x\)).
Step 2
Method of Substitution \(\text{one equation gives }x\ \text{in terms of }u,\ \text{then substitute}\) From \(u=2x-1\) we get \(x=\dfrac{u+1}{2}\), which removes \(x\) from the second equation.
From (1), \(x = \dfrac{u+1}{2}\). Substitute into (2): \[ u^2 = 20\left(\frac{u+1}{2}\right) - 31 = 10(u+1)-31 \]
This is the same as forming \((2)-10\times(1)\) to eliminate \(x\).
Step 3
Solving a Quadratic Equation by Factorisation \(x^{2}-(p+q)x+pq=(x-p)(x-q)\) \(u^{2}-10u+21=0\) factorises as \((u-3)(u-7)=0\), so \(u=3\) or \(7\).
Simplify to a quadratic in \(u\): \[ u^2 - 10u + 21 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; (u-3)(u-7)=0 \] \[ u = 3 \quad\text{or}\quad u = 7,\quad\text{i.e.}\quad \log_4 y = 3 \text{ or } 7 \]
Factorise directly — 3 × 7 = 21 and 3 + 7 = 10.
Step 4
Convert base 4 to base 2: \(\log_4 y = \dfrac{\log_2 y}{\log_2 4} = \dfrac{\log_2 y}{2}\).
Change of Base for a Power Base \(\log_{a^{n}}M=\dfrac{1}{n}\log_{a}M\) \(\log_{4}y=\dfrac{\log_{2}y}{2}\), so \(\log_{4}y=3\) or \(7\) gives \(\log_{2}y=6\) or \(14\).
So \(\log_4 y = 3 \Rightarrow \log_2 y = 6\), and \(\log_4 y = 7 \Rightarrow \log_2 y = 14\).
Change-of-base: \(\log_{a^n} M = \frac1n \log_a M\).
Answer: D  —  \(\log_2 y = 6\) or \(14\) \(\log_4 y = 3\) or \(7\) ⟺ \(\log_2 y = 6\) or \(14\).

Question. If \(\log_{4}y=3x-2\) and \(\left(\log_{4}y\right)^{2}=15x-14\), then \(\log_{2}y=\) A. \(2\) or \(4\) B. \(2\) or \(8\) C. \(4\) or \(8\) D. \(1\) or \(4\) [1 mark]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Put \(u=\log_{4}y\), so \(u=3x-2\) and \(u^{2}=15x-14\).
Step 2. From \(u=3x-2\): \(x=\dfrac{u+2}{3}\). Substitute: \(u^{2}=15\left(\dfrac{u+2}{3}\right)-14=5(u+2)-14\).
Step 3. \(u^{2}=5u-4\), i.e. \(u^{2}-5u+4=0\), so \((u-1)(u-4)=0\) and \(u=1\) or \(4\).
Step 4. \(\log_{4}y=\dfrac{\log_{2}y}{2}\), so \(\log_{2}y=2u=2\) or \(8\).
Answer: B. \(2\) or \(8\)
