Permutations Complementary Counting Factorials

A queue is formed by 6 boys and 2 girls. If no girls are next to each other, how many different queues can be formed?

A 1 440
B 10 080
C 30 240
D 35 280

🧩 Guided Solution

1

Understand the total without restriction

If we ignore the "no girls next to each other" rule, we just arrange 8 distinct people in a row.

Total arrangements = \(8! = 40\,320\)
2

Use complementary counting

It's easier to count the opposite case: queues where the girls are next to each other.

💡 Hint: Desired = Total − (girls together)
3

Count queues where the two girls are together

Treat the 2 girls as a single "block". Then we arrange this block together with the 6 boys.

  • Number of objects to arrange: \(6 + 1 = 7\)
  • Arrange 7 objects: \(7!\)
  • The two girls inside the block can swap: \(2!\)
Girls together = \(2! \times 7! = 2 \times 5\,040 = 10\,080\)
4

Compute the answer

Required number of queues

\(8! - 2! \cdot 7! = 40\,320 - 10\,080 = 30\,240\)

Answer: C · 30 240
