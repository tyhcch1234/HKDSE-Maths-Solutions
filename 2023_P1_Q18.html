HKDSE Math 2023 Paper 1 Q18 Geometric Sequence Arithmetic Sequence Logarithms

Sequences & Logarithms

Suppose that $\alpha, 7, \beta$ is a geometric sequence, where $1 < \alpha < \beta$.

📋 Question

(a) Express $\log_7 \alpha$ in terms of $\log_7 \beta$. [3 marks]

(b) If $\log_\beta \alpha$, $\log_7 \beta$, $\log_\alpha \beta$ is an arithmetic sequence, find the common difference of the arithmetic sequence. [5 marks]

🧩 Part (a) – Express $\log_7 \alpha$

1

Geometric mean property

In a geometric sequence $\alpha, 7, \beta$, the middle term is the geometric mean of the first and third terms.

$7 = \sqrt{\alpha \beta} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \alpha\beta = 49$
💡 Hint: Why?
In a GP, the ratio between consecutive terms is constant: $\frac{7}{\alpha} = \frac{\beta}{7}$. Cross-multiplying gives $49 = \alpha\beta$.
2

Take log base 7

Apply $\log_7$ to both sides of $\alpha\beta = 49$.

$\log_7(\alpha\beta) = \log_7 49$
3

Use logarithm laws

Use the product rule: $\log_7 \alpha + \log_7 \beta = \log_7(7^2) = 2$.

$\log_7 \alpha + \log_7 \beta = 2$

Rearranging:

$\log_7 \alpha = 2 - \log_7 \beta$
✅ Answer to (a): $\log_7 \alpha = 2 - \log_7 \beta$

🧮 Part (b) – Common difference of the arithmetic sequence

📈 Visual: Arithmetic progression of log terms

$\log_\beta \alpha$
→ + d →
$\log_7 \beta$
→ + d →
$\log_\alpha \beta$
1

Introduce variable

Let $x = \log_7 \beta$. From part (a), we have $\log_7 \alpha = 2 - x$.

💡 Hint
Since $1 < \alpha < \beta$, we know $\log_7 \beta > \log_7 \alpha$, and both are positive. Also $\alpha \beta = 49$ so $\beta = 49/\alpha$.
2

Express the three terms in terms of $x$

Using the change of base formula $\log_b a = \frac{\log_7 a}{\log_7 b}$:

Term 1: $\log_\beta \alpha = \frac{\log_7 \alpha}{\log_7 \beta} = \frac{2 - x}{x}$

Term 2: $\log_7 \beta = x$

Term 3: $\log_\alpha \beta = \frac{\log_7 \beta}{\log_7 \alpha} = \frac{x}{2 - x}$

3

Arithmetic sequence condition

The middle term is the average of the first and third terms:

$2x = \frac{2 - x}{x} + \frac{x}{2 - x}$
💡 Hint: Why this equation?
If $a, b, c$ form an arithmetic sequence, then $2b = a + c$. Here the terms are our three log expressions.
4

Solve for $x$

Multiply both sides by $x(2-x)$:

$2x \cdot x(2-x) = (2-x)^2 + x^2$

$2x^2(2-x) = (2-x)^2 + x^2$

$4x^2 - 2x^3 = 4 - 4x + x^2 + x^2$

$4x^2 - 2x^3 = 4 - 4x + 2x^2$

$2x^3 - 2x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0$

$x^3 - x^2 - 2x + 2 = 0$

5

Factorise the cubic

Group terms:

$x^2(x-1) - 2(x-1) = 0$

$(x-1)(x^2 - 2) = 0$

Since $1 < \alpha < \beta$, we have $\log_7 \beta = x > \log_7 \alpha = 2 - x$, so $x > 1$.

$x = \sqrt{2}$ (rejecting $x=1$ and $x=-\sqrt{2}$)
6

Find the common difference $d$

The common difference is $d = \log_7 \beta - \log_\beta \alpha$:

$d = \sqrt{2} - \frac{2 - \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}}$

$= \sqrt{2} - \left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{2}} - 1\right)$

$= \sqrt{2} - (\sqrt{2} - 1)$

$= 1$

✅ Answer to (b): The common difference is $1$.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • In a 3-term GP, the middle term is the geometric mean: $b^2 = ac$.
  • Change of base: $\log_b a = \dfrac{\log_c a}{\log_c b}$.
  • In an AP, $2b = a + c$ (the middle term is the average).
  • Always check the domain and given inequalities to discard invalid roots.
