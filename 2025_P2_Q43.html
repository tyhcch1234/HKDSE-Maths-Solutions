HKDSE 2025 Paper 2 — Q43 · Probability

There are 9 cans of apple juice and 4 cans of grape juice in a bag. If 6 cans are randomly chosen from the bag at the same time, find the probability that at most 3 cans of grape juice are chosen.

  • A. $\dfrac{9}{13}$
  • B. $\dfrac{133}{143}$
  • C. $\dfrac{140}{143}$
  • D. $\dfrac{714}{715}$

Step-by-step Solution

1Total number of cans

Apple juice: $9$ cans  ·  Grape juice: $4$ cans.

Total $= 9 + 4 = 13$ cans. We choose $6$ cans at the same time.

Hint: the total count is the denominator of every combination.
2Total number of ways to choose 6 cans

$${}^{13}C_{6} = 1716$$

Every equally-likely selection of $6$ cans is one outcome.

Hint: "chosen at the same time" means order does not matter — use ${}^{n}C_{r}$.
3Recognise the complement event

We want $P(\text{grape} \le 3)$.

The only case NOT included is when all 4 grape cans are chosen, i.e. the event "4 cans of grape juice" (so the other $6-4=2$ cans are apple juice).

Hint: "at most 3" excludes exactly one easy case — use the complement.
4Count the complement: "4 cans of grape juice"

Choose all $4$ grape cans: ${}^{4}C_{4} = 1$ way.

Choose the remaining $2$ cans from the $9$ apple cans: ${}^{9}C_{2} = 36$ ways.

$${}^{4}C_{4}{}^{9}C_{2} = 1 \times 36 = 36$$

Hint: split the selection into grape part × apple part.
5Probability of the complement

$$P(\text{4 grape}) = \frac{{}^{4}C_{4}{}^{9}C_{2}}{{}^{13}C_{6}} = \frac{36}{1716} = \frac{3}{143}$$

Hint: simplify by dividing numerator and denominator by $12$.
6Apply the complement rule

$$P(\text{at most 3 grape}) = 1 - P(\text{4 grape})$$ $$= 1 - \frac{3}{143} = \frac{140}{143}$$

Hint: $1 - \frac{3}{143} = \frac{143-3}{143} = \frac{140}{143}$.
7Conclusion

The required probability is $\dfrac{140}{143}$, which matches option C.

Hint: check that the answer is less than 1 and close to 1 (since the complement is tiny).
Answer: C  ·  $\dfrac{140}{143}$
