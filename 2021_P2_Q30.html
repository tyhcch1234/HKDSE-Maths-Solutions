Statistics — Combined Mean of Two Groups
Mean Sum of Data Two Groups MC

Question Full Statement

The mean of \(70\) integers is \(32\). If the mean of \(30\) of these \(70\) integers is \(24\), then the mean of the remaining \(40\) integers is

  • A. \(38\)
  • B. \(40\)
  • C. \(43\)
  • D. \(74\)
    • >

Part (a) Model the Two Groups Setup

We split the 70 integers into two groups: a Group 1 of 30 integers (mean 24) and a Group 2 of 40 integers (mean \(x\), unknown). The overall mean of all 70 is 32.

Sum of Group 1 = \(30 \times 24 = 720\) Sum of Group 2 = \(40 \times 40 = 1600\) Combined mean = \((720+1600)/70 = 33.1\)
Hint: Move the slider. Notice the combined mean only equals \(32\) when the slider reaches a specific value — that value is the answer.

Part (b) Set Up and Solve the Equation

Let \(x\) be the mean of the remaining 40 integers. Use total sum = sum of Group 1 + sum of Group 2.

Step 1 Total sum of all 70 integers
\[\text{Total sum}=70\times 32 = 2240.\]
Hint: \(\text{Sum} = \text{mean} \times \text{number of items}\). Always start by converting every mean into a sum.
Step 2 Sum of the group of 30
\[\text{Sum of 30 integers}=30\times 24 = 720.\]
Hint: This group contributes \(720\) to the total.
Step 3 Form the equation for the remaining 40
Let \(x\) be the mean of the remaining \(40\) integers. Their sum is \(40x\), so \[ 30\times 24 + 40x = 70\times 32 \] \[ 720 + 40x = 2240 \]
Hint: Group 1 sum + Group 2 sum = overall sum. That single equation contains all the information given.
Step 4 Solve for \(x\)
\[ 40x = 2240 - 720 = 1520 \] \[ x = \frac{1520}{40} = 38 \]
Answer: The mean of the remaining 40 integers is \(x = 38\). So the correct option is A.

Part (c) Visual Check with a Balance Diagram Concept

We can picture the combined mean as the balancing point between the two group means. For the combined mean to be \(32\), the group means must sit on opposite sides of 32: \(24 < 32\) and \(x > 32\).

Distance from 32 to 24 = \(8\) Weighted pull: \(30 \times 8 = 240\) Distance from 32 to 38 = \(6\); \(40 \times 6 = 240\)
Hint: The two groups "pull" the mean in proportion to their sizes. Because Group 2 is larger (40 vs 30), its mean \(38\) is closer to \(32\) than \(24\) is.

Answers at a Glance

  • Total sum of 70 integers: \(70 \times 32 = 2240\)
  • Sum of the 30 integers: \(30 \times 24 = 720\)
  • Equation: \(720 + 40x = 2240\)
  • Remaining sum: \(2240 - 720 = 1520\)
  • Required mean: \(x = \dfrac{1520}{40} = 38\)
  • Correct option: A
