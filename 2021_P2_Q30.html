[2021 MC #30]  ·  15. Statistics

The mean of $70$ integers is $32$. If the mean of $30$ of these $70$ integers is $24$, then the mean of the remaining $40$ integers is

  • A. $38$
  • B. $40$
  • C. $43$
  • D. $74$
Step 1Translate the given information.

Total number of integers $=70$, overall mean $=32$.
Group A: $30$ integers with mean $24$.   Group B: the remaining $40$ integers, mean $=x$ (unknown).

Hint: rearrange the mean formula — $\text{sum} = \text{mean} \times \text{number of items}$.
Step 2Find the total sum of all 70 integers.

$\text{Total sum} = 32 \times 70 = 2240$

Hint: the overall mean tells you the "weighted" total directly.
Step 3Find the sum of the 30 integers.

$\text{Sum of 30 integers} = 24 \times 30 = 720$

Hint: the remaining sum is the difference of the two sums above.
Step 4Set up and solve the equation for $x$.

$$24 \times 30 + x \times 40 = 32 \times 70$$ $$720 + 40x = 2240$$ $$40x = 1520$$ $$x = 38$$

Hint: this is the official method — write one equation combining the two groups.
Answer: x = 38  (Option A) A
