HKDSE 2017 Paper 2 · Q42 · Probability (Combinations)

Students (13) Teachers (6) Chosen for team
2017MC#42

There are $13$ students and $6$ teachers in a committee. If $5$ students and $4$ teachers are selected from the committee to form a team, how many different teams can be formed?

A.  4 290
B.  19 305
C.  92 378
D.  55 598 400
1Understand the task
Combination \({}^{n}C_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}\) A team is a set of people, so order does not matter and combinations (not permutations) are used.

We must pick 5 students out of 13 AND 4 teachers out of 6. The two choices are independent, so we multiply the two counts.

Order inside the team does not matter — so we use combinations, not permutations.

Hint: "select / choose / form a team" ⇒ use $C^n_r = \dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}$.

2Count the student choices

Choose $5$ students from $13$:

$$C^{13}_{5}=\frac{13!}{5!\,8!}=\frac{13\times12\times11\times10\times9}{5\times4\times3\times2\times1}=1287$$

Hint: cancel the numerator and denominator to avoid huge factorials.

3Count the teacher choices

Choose $4$ teachers from $6$:

$$C^{6}_{4}=\frac{6!}{4!\,2!}=\frac{6\times5}{2\times1}=15$$

Hint: $C^{6}_{4}=C^{6}_{2}=15$ — picking 4 to include = picking 2 to leave out.

4Multiply (multiplication principle)
Multiplication Principle of Counting \(N={}^{13}C_{5}\times{}^{6}C_{4}=1287\times15\) The choice of students and the choice of teachers are independent, so the two counts are multiplied.

Each student group can team up with any teacher group:

$$C^{13}_{5}\times C^{6}_{4}=1287\times15=19305$$

Hint: multiply when the choices are made together ("5 students and 4 teachers").

5Check & conclude

Total team size $=5+4=9$, as required — no over-counting since order is irrelevant.

$1287\times15 = 1287\times10 + 1287\times5 = 12870+6435 = 19305$ ✓

Hint: distractors like $C^{19}_{9}=92378$ ignore the fixed student/teacher split.

Answer: B  —  $19\,305$ teams

Question. A committee is formed by choosing \(4\) students from \(15\) students and \(3\) teachers from \(7\) teachers. How many different committees can be formed?

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Choose \(4\) students from \(15\): \({}^{15}C_{4}=\dfrac{15\times14\times13\times12}{4\times3\times2\times1}=1365\).
Step 2. Choose \(3\) teachers from \(7\): \({}^{7}C_{3}=\dfrac{7\times6\times5}{3\times2\times1}=35\).
Step 3. The two choices are independent,
so multiply: \(1365\times35\).
Step 4. \(1365\times35=47\,775\) committees.
Answer: \(47\,775\)
