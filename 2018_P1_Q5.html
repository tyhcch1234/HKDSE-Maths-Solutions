Topic 4 — Polynomials · Factorization by common factor & grouping

given / factor 1 common factor grouped pair A grouped pair B difference of squares

Step 0 — overview: the four-term expression to be factorized.

[2018 #5]

Factorize

(a) $9r^{3}-18r^{2}s$ ,
(b) $9r^{3}-18r^{2}s-rs^{2}+2s^{3}$ .   [4 marks]
Step 1 · Part (a)
Take out the common factor of $9r^{3}$ and $18r^{2}s$.
$9r^{3}-18r^{2}s \;=\; 9r^{2}\cdot r-9r^{2}\cdot 2s \;=\; \mathbf{9r^{2}(r-2s)}$
Hint: $9$ is the HCF of $9$ and $18$; $r^{2}$ is the lowest power of $r$. (1A)
Step 2 · Plan for Part (b)
Part (b) is 4 terms — group them into two pairs. Notice the first pair is exactly the expression in (a):
$9r^{3}-18r^{2}s-rs^{2}+2s^{3} \;=\; \underbrace{(9r^{3}-18r^{2}s)}_{\text{pair A}}+\underbrace{(-rs^{2}+2s^{3})}_{\text{pair B}}$
Hint: a 4-term cubic usually factorizes by grouping in pairs.
Step 3 · Factorize pair A
Applying the result of (a) to the first pair:
$9r^{3}-18r^{2}s \;=\; 9r^{2}(r-2s)$
Hint: reuse the answer from part (a). (1M)
Step 4 · Factorize pair B
Take the common factor $-s^{2}$ out of the second pair:
$-rs^{2}+2s^{3} \;=\; -s^{2}(r-2s)$
Hint: mind the minus sign — it makes the bracket match pair A.
Step 5 · Combine & extract $(r-2s)$
Both groups now share the factor $(r-2s)$:
$9r^{3}-18r^{2}s-rs^{2}+2s^{3}$
$\;=\; 9r^{2}(r-2s)-s^{2}(r-2s)$
$\;=\; (r-2s)\,(9r^{2}-s^{2})$
Hint: this is the key grouping step. (1M)
Step 6 · Difference of squares
$9r^{2}-s^{2}=(3r)^{2}-s^{2}=(3r+s)(3r-s)$
$\therefore\; 9r^{3}-18r^{2}s-rs^{2}+2s^{3}\;=\;(r-2s)(3r+s)(3r-s)$
Hint: apply $a^{2}-b^{2}=(a+b)(a-b)$ with $a=3r,\;b=s$. (1A)
Answers: (a) $9r^{2}(r-2s)$   ·   (b) $(r-2s)(3r+s)(3r-s)$
