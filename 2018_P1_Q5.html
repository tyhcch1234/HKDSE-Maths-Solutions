HKDSE Math 2018 Paper 1 Q5

Topic 4: Polynomials — Factorization

Factorization Common Factor Difference of Squares Grouping Terms

The Question

Factorize each of the following:

(a)

\(9r^3 - 18r^2 s\)

(b)

\(9r^3 - 18r^2 s - rs^2 + 2s^3\)

[4 marks]

Step 0 of 6 Ready
1

Part (a): Find the common factor

Look at \(9r^3 - 18r^2 s\). We look for the highest common factor (HCF) of the two terms.

HCF of coefficients
\(\text{HCF}(9, 18) = 9\)
HCF of variables
\(r^3\) and \(r^2 s\) share \(r^2\)

So the common factor is \(9r^2\).

2

Part (a): Factor out \(9r^2\)

Divide each term by \(9r^2\) and put it outside a bracket.

\(9r^3 \div 9r^2 = r\)
\(18r^2 s \div 9r^2 = 2s\)
\(\therefore 9r^3 - 18r^2 s = 9r^2(r - 2s)\)

✓ Answer for (a)

3

Part (b): Split into two groups

Rewrite the expression grouping the first two terms and the last two terms.

\(9r^3 - 18r^2 s - rs^2 + 2s^3\)
\(= \underbrace{(9r^3 - 18r^2 s)}_{\text{Group 1}} + \underbrace{(-rs^2 + 2s^3)}_{\text{Group 2}}\)
💡 Hint: Notice that Part (a) already helped you factor Group 1.
4

Part (b): Factor each group

Group 1 (from Part a)
\(9r^3 - 18r^2 s = 9r^2(r - 2s)\)
Group 2
\(-rs^2 + 2s^3 = -s^2(r - 2s)\)

So the expression becomes:

\(9r^2(r - 2s) - s^2(r - 2s)\)
5

Part (b): Take out the common bracket

Both terms share the factor \((r - 2s)\). Factor it out.

\(= (r - 2s)(9r^2 - s^2)\)
💡 Hint: \(9r^2 - s^2\) is a difference of two squares.
6

Part (b): Apply difference of squares

Use the identity \(a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)\).

\(9r^2 = (3r)^2\)   and   \(s^2 = (s)^2\)
\(9r^2 - s^2 = (3r + s)(3r - s)\)
\(\therefore 9r^3 - 18r^2 s - rs^2 + 2s^3 = (r - 2s)(3r + s)(3r - s)\)

✓ Final answer for (b)
