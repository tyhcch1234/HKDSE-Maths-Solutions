Q3
If $a$ and $b$ are constants such that \[ (x+8)(x+a)+b \equiv x^2+5a(x+3), \] then $b = ?$
A. $-14$
B. $-2$
C. $2$
D. $14$

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Expand the left-hand side

Expand $(x+8)(x+a)$:

\[ (x+8)(x+a) = x^2 + ax + 8x + 8a \]
💡 Hint

Use the distributive law: multiply each term in the first bracket by each term in the second bracket.

2

Write the full left-hand side

Now include $+b$ and group the $x$-terms:

\[ (x+8)(x+a)+b = x^2 + (a+8)x + (8a+b) \]
💡 Hint

Collect like terms: $ax + 8x = (a+8)x$. The constant terms are $8a$ and $b$.

3

Expand the right-hand side

Expand $5a(x+3)$:

\[ x^2 + 5a(x+3) = x^2 + 5ax + 15a \]
4

Compare the coefficient of $x$

Since the identity holds for all $x$, the coefficients of $x$ must match:

\[ a + 8 = 5a \]
💡 Hint

Left $x$-coefficient: $(a+8)$. Right $x$-coefficient: $5a$.

5

Solve for $a$

\[ \begin{aligned} a + 8 &= 5a \\ 8 &= 4a \\ a &= 2 \end{aligned} \]
6

Compare the constant term

Now equate the constant terms:

\[ 8a + b = 15a \]
💡 Hint

Left constant: $8a + b$. Right constant: $15a$.

Substitute $a=2$ and find $b$

\[ \begin{aligned} 8(2) + b &= 15(2) \\ 16 + b &= 30 \\ b &= 14 \end{aligned} \]

Answer: D. 14

📌 Key Takeaway

  • • An identity is true for all values of the variable. So coefficients of the same power of $x$ must be equal.
  • • First solve for the simpler unknown by comparing $x$-coefficients, then substitute into the constant equation.
  • • Always double-check: substitute $a=2,\,b=14$ back into the original identity.
