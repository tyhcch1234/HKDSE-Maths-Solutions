2024MC#15 · Mensuration

If the arc length and the area of a sector are $8\pi\text{ cm}$ and $80\pi\text{ cm}^2$ respectively, then the angle of the sector is

A. $36^\circ$
B. $45^\circ$
C. $60^\circ$
D. $72^\circ$
Step 1 · Let the unknowns
Arc Length of a Sector \(\text{arc length}=2\pi r\times\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}\) With arc length \(8\pi\) this gives \(r\cdot\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=4\).

Let $r$ cm be the radius and $\theta$ be the angle of the sector.

Recall the two sector formulas: $$\text{Arc length}=2\pi r\times\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}$$ $$\text{Area of sector}=\pi r^2\times\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}$$

Step 2 · Set up from the area
Area of a Sector \(\text{area}=\pi r^{2}\times\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}\) With area \(80\pi\) this gives \(r^{2}\cdot\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=80\).

Given area $=80\pi\text{ cm}^2$: $$\pi r^2\times\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}=80\pi$$ $$r^2\cdot\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}=80 \quad\cdots (1)$$

Step 3 · Set up from the arc length

Given arc length $=8\pi\text{ cm}$: $$2\pi r\times\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}=8\pi$$ $$r\cdot\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}=4 \quad\cdots (2)$$

Step 4 · Solve the system
Elimination by Division \(\dfrac{r^{2}c}{rc}=r\) Dividing the area equation by the arc-length equation cancels the angle factor and leaves \(r=20\).

Divide (1) by (2): $$\frac{r^2\cdot\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}}{r\cdot\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}}=\frac{80}{4} \;\Longrightarrow\; r=20$$

Substitute $r=20$ into (2): $$20\times\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}=4 \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{\theta}{360^\circ}=\frac{1}{5} \;\Longrightarrow\; \theta=72^\circ$$

Step 5 · Check & conclude

With $r=20$, $\theta=72^\circ$:
Arc $=2\pi(20)\frac{72}{360}=40\pi\cdot\frac15=8\pi$ ✓
Area $=\pi(20)^2\frac{72}{360}=400\pi\cdot\frac15=80\pi$ ✓

💡 Notice: $\text{Area}=\tfrac12\times(\text{arc length})\times r$ — a fast cross-check!
Answer
D. $\;72^\circ$

Question. If the arc length and the area of a sector are \(12\pi\text{ cm}\) and \(108\pi\text{ cm}^{2}\) respectively, find the angle of the sector.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let the radius be \(r\) and the angle be \(\theta\). Then \(2\pi r\times\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=12\pi\), i.e. \(r\cdot\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=6\).
Step 2. Also \(\pi r^{2}\times\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=108\pi\), i.e. \(r^{2}\cdot\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=108\).
Step 3. Dividing the second equation by the first gives \(r=\dfrac{108}{6}=18\).
Step 4. Substitute into the first equation: \(18\cdot\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=6\), so \(\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=\dfrac{1}{3}\) and \(\theta=120^{\circ}\).
Step 5. Check: arc \(=2\pi(18)\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)=12\pi\) ✓ and area \(=\pi(18)^{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)=108\pi\) ✓.
Answer: \(120^{\circ}\)
