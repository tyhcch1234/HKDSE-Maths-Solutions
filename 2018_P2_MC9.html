Q

A sum of $100,000 is deposited at an interest rate of 2% per annum for 3 years, compounded monthly. Find the interest to the nearest dollar.

A $6,000
B $6,121
C $6,176
D $6,178

Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1 of 5
1

Identify the Compound Interest Formula

For compound interest, the total amount \(A\) after \(t\) years is: \[ A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{n \cdot t} \] where \(P\) is the principal, \(r\) is the annual interest rate (decimal), \(n\) is the number of compounding periods per year, and \(t\) is the time in years.

💡 Hint: The interest earned is the total amount minus the principal.

2

List the Given Values

  • • Principal \(P = 100\,000\)
  • • Annual rate \(r = 2\% = 0.02\)
  • • Compounded monthly \(\Rightarrow n = 12\)
  • • Time \(t = 3\) years

💡 Hint: Monthly compounding means the interest is calculated 12 times per year.

3

Substitute into the Formula

\[ A = 100\,000 \left(1 + \frac{0.02}{12}\right)^{12 \times 3} \] Simplify the exponent and the bracket: \[ A = 100\,000 \left(1 + \frac{0.02}{12}\right)^{36} \]

💡 Hint: Do not round the bracket value too early — keep precision until the final step.

4

Calculate the Total Amount

\[ A = 100\,000 \times (1.001666\ldots)^{36} \] \[ A \approx 100\,000 \times 1.06178351496 \] \[ A \approx 106\,178.351496 \]

💡 Hint: The total amount is slightly more than $106,000 — this makes sense as interest accumulates.

5

Find the Interest

Interest \(= A - P\) \[ = 106\,178.351496 - 100\,000 \] \[ = 6\,178.351496 \] Rounded to the nearest dollar: \[ \boxed{\$6\,178} \] This matches option D.

✅ The correct answer is D — $6,178.
