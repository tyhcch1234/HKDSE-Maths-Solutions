Question

The monthly salary of Donald is $25\%$ higher than that of Peter while the monthly salary of Peter is $25\%$ lower than that of Teresa. It is given that the monthly salary of Donald is $\$33\,360$. The monthly salary of Teresa is

  • A. $\$31\,275$
  • B. $\$33\,360$
  • C. $\$35\,584$
  • D. $\$52\,125$
Step 1 — Read the percentages
Percentage Change \(\text{new value}=\text{original value}\times(1\pm r\%)\) A \(25\%\) rise gives \(1.25P\), and a \(25\%\) fall gives \(0.75T\).

Let Peter’s monthly salary be $P$ and Teresa’s be $T$.

“Donald is $25\%$ higher than Peter”:

$$\text{Donald}=P\times(1+25\%)=1.25P$$

“Peter is $25\%$ lower than Teresa”:

$$P=T\times(1-25\%)=0.75T$$

Tip: “$A$ is $r\%$ higher than $B$” ⇒ $A=(1+r\%)B$. “$A$ is $r\%$ lower than $B$” ⇒ $A=(1-r\%)B$.
Step 2 — Find Peter’s salary from Donald’s

Donald $=\$33\,360$ and $\text{Donald}=1.25P$, so

$$P=\frac{33\,360}{1+25\%}=\frac{33\,360}{1.25}=\$26\,688$$

Divide (not multiply) because we are going backwards from the enlarged value.
Step 3 — Find Teresa’s salary from Peter’s
Reverse Percentage (finding the base) \(\text{original value}=\dfrac{\text{new value}}{1\pm r\%}\) Peter earns \(75\%\) of Teresa, so divide \(\$26\,688\) by \(0.75\) instead of multiplying.

Peter’s salary $=\$26\,688$ and $P=0.75T$, so

$$T=\frac{26\,688}{1-25\%}=\frac{26\,688}{0.75}=\$35\,584$$

Again divide, since Peter’s salary is the reduced value of Teresa’s.
Step 4 — One-line check
Combined Percentage Multipliers \(\text{overall multiplier}=(1+r\%)(1-s\%)\) Here \(1.25\times0.75=0.9375\neq1\), so Donald earns \(93.75\%\) of Teresa.

Combine both steps into a single expression:

$$T=\frac{33\,360}{(1+25\%)(1-25\%)} =\frac{33\,360}{1.25\times0.75} =\frac{33\,360}{0.9375}=\$35\,584$$

Key insight: $1.25\times0.75\neq1$, so Donald’s salary is not equal to Teresa’s. Donald $=93.75\%$ of Teresa.
Step 5 — Eliminate the traps

Donald’s salary ($\$33\,360$) is about $93.75\%$ of Teresa’s, so Teresa must earn more than Donald.

Options A and B are $\le \$33\,360$ — impossible.

Option D ($\$52\,125$) would make Peter $=\$52\,125\times0.75=\$39\,094$, whose $+25\%$ is $\$48\,867\neq\$33\,360$ — too big.

Quick sanity test: the answer must satisfy $T\times0.75\times1.25=\$33\,360$.
Step 6 — Conclusion

Hence the monthly salary of Teresa is $\$35\,584$.

This is the compound-percentage trap: $+25\%$ then $-25\%$ does not return to the original value.
Answer: C. $\$35\,584$

Question. The monthly salary of Alan is \(20\%\) higher than that of Ben while the monthly salary of Ben is \(20\%\) lower than that of Carl. It is given that Alan earns \(\$28\,800\). Carl earns A. \(\$27\,648\) B. \(\$28\,800\) C. \(\$30\,000\) D. \(\$45\,000\) [1 mark]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Write the two statements as multipliers: \(\text{Alan}=1.2\,\text{Ben}\) and \(\text{Ben}=0.8\,\text{Carl}\).
Step 2. From Alan \(=\$28\,800\): \(\text{Ben}=\dfrac{28\,800}{1.2}=\$24\,000\).
Step 3. From Ben \(=\$24\,000\): \(\text{Carl}=\dfrac{24\,000}{0.8}=\$30\,000\).
Step 4. Check with one multiplier: \(30\,000\times0.8\times1.2=28\,800\) \u2713.
Note \(1.2\times0.8=0.96\neq1\), so Carl does not earn the same as Alan.
Answer: C. \(\$30\,000\)
