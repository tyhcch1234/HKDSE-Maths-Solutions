HKDSE 2024 · Paper 1 · Q7

Polar Coordinates & Triangle

Question

In a polar coordinate system, \( O \) is the pole. The polar coordinates of the points \( P \), \( Q \) and \( R \) are \( \left(11,59^{\circ}\right) \), \( \left(60,149^{\circ}\right) \) and \( \left(144,239^{\circ}\right) \) respectively.

  1. Find \( \angle POQ \).
  2. Are \( P \), \( O \) and \( R \) collinear? Explain your answer.
  3. Find the perimeter of \( \triangle PQR \). (4 marks)

Solution

STEP 1 · Part (a) \[ \angle POQ = 149^{\circ} - 59^{\circ} = 90^{\circ}. \]
STEP 2 · Part (b) — collinearity The position vectors of \( P \) and \( R \) make angles \( 59^{\circ} \) and \( 239^{\circ} \) with the initial line, and \[ 239^{\circ} - 59^{\circ} = 180^{\circ}. \] So \( OP \) and \( OR \) lie on the same straight line through \( O \) in opposite directions. Hence \( P \), \( O \) and \( R \) are collinear (with \( O \) between \( P \) and \( R \)).
STEP 3 · Part (c) — side \( PQ \) Since \( \angle POQ = 90^{\circ} \), \( \triangle POQ \) is right-angled at \( O \): \[ PQ = \sqrt{11^{2}+60^{2}} = \sqrt{121+3600} = \sqrt{3721} = 61. \]
STEP 4 · Part (c) — side \( QR \) \[ \angle QOR = 239^{\circ} - 149^{\circ} = 90^{\circ}, \] so \( \triangle QOR \) is right-angled at \( O \): \[ QR = \sqrt{60^{2}+144^{2}} = \sqrt{3600+20736} = \sqrt{24336} = 156. \]
STEP 5 · Part (c) — side \( PR \) and the perimeter From (b), \( O \) lies between \( P \) and \( R \), so \[ PR = OP + OR = 11 + 144 = 155. \] The perimeter of \( \triangle PQR \) is \[ PQ + QR + PR = 61 + 156 + 155 = 372. \]
Final Answer: (a) \( 90^{\circ} \)  |  (b) Yes — \( 239^{\circ}-59^{\circ}=180^{\circ} \), so \( P,O,R \) lie on one straight line  |  (c) \( 372 \)
