HKDSE Math 2020 Paper 2 Q28

Probability

Two numbers are randomly drawn at the same time from four cards numbered 3, 5, 7 and 9 respectively. Find the probability that the product of the numbers drawn is greater than 35.

A. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
B. $\dfrac{1}{3}$
C. $\dfrac{2}{3}$
D. $\dfrac{3}{8}$

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — Count the total number of outcomes

Two cards are drawn at the same time from 4 cards, so the order does not matter:

$C^{4}_{2}=\dfrac{4\times3}{2}=6$ outcomes

Step 2 — List all possible products

$3\times5=15$ $3\times7=21$ $3\times9=27$ $5\times7=35$ $5\times9=45$ $7\times9=63$

Step 3 — Identify products greater than $35$

The products exceeding $35$ are $45$ and $63$ (note that $35$ is not greater than $35$). So there are $2$ favourable outcomes.

Step 4 — Compute the probability

$P(\text{product}>35)=\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}$

Answer: B. $\dfrac{1}{3}$
