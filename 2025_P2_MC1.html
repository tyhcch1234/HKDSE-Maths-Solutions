HKDSE Math 2025 Paper 2 Q1 Laws of Indices Exponential Functions

Simplify the expression

Topic 8: Laws of Indices, Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

Simplify:

$$\frac{(27x)^5}{(3x^{-2})^4} = \text{?}$$
A. \(3^2 x^3\)
B. \(3^4 x^3\)
C. \(3^{11} x^{13}\)
D. \(3^{14} x^{13}\)
Power of a product Power of a quotient Negative indices

We know that \(27 = 3^3\). So we can replace 27:

$$\frac{(3^3 x)^5}{(3x^{-2})^4}$$
💡 Hint: Express all numbers as powers of the same base (here, base 3) to simplify later.

Use \((ab)^n = a^n b^n\) on both numerator and denominator:

$$\frac{(3^3)^5 \cdot x^5}{3^4 \cdot (x^{-2})^4}$$
💡 Hint: Distribute the exponent to each factor inside the parentheses.

Simplify \((3^3)^5 = 3^{15}\) and \((x^{-2})^4 = x^{-8}\):

$$\frac{3^{15} \cdot x^5}{3^4 \cdot x^{-8}}$$
💡 Hint: \((a^m)^n = a^{m \times n}\). Multiply exponents.

Group the same bases together:

$$\frac{3^{15}}{3^4} \cdot \frac{x^5}{x^{-8}}$$
💡 Hint: \(\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}\). Subtract exponents when dividing same base.

Compute the exponent for base 3:

$$3^{15-4} = 3^{11}$$

Compute the exponent for base x:

$$x^{5 - (-8)} = x^{5 + 8} = x^{13}$$
💡 Hint: Subtracting a negative is the same as adding. \(5 - (-8) = 5 + 8 = 13\).

Combine the results:

$$3^{11} x^{13}$$

Therefore the answer is C.
