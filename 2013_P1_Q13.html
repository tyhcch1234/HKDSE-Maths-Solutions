Mensuration — Similar Solids, Volume Conservation & Scale Factors

Volume of Cylinder Similar Figures Ratio of Areas & Volumes Recasting Solids

Question 5+2 marks

In a workshop, 2 identical solid metal right circular cylinders of base radius \(R\) cm are melted and recast into 27 smaller identical solid right circular cylinders of base radius \(r\) cm and height \(10\) cm.

It is given that the base area of a larger circular cylinder is 9 times that of a smaller one.

(a) Find
(i) \(r:R\),
(ii) the height of a larger circular cylinder. 5 marks
(b) A craftsman claims that a smaller circular cylinder and a larger circular cylinder are similar. Do you agree? Explain your answer. 2 marks

Step 11M
It is given that the base area of a larger cylinder is 9 times that of a smaller one. Since the base is a circle of radius \(R\) and \(r\) respectively, \[\frac{\pi r^{2}}{\pi R^{2}}=\frac{1}{9}.\]
Hint: "Area of large = 9 × area of small" means the ratio small : large is \(1:9\), so put the small area on top.
Step 2—
Cancel \(\pi\) and simplify: \[\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{2}=\frac{1}{9}.\] Taking the positive square root (lengths are positive), \[\frac{r}{R}=\frac{1}{3}.\]
Hint: Areas scale as the square of lengths, so to get the length ratio you take the square root of the area ratio. \(\sqrt{1/9}=1/3\).
Step 31A
Therefore \[r:R = 1:3.\]
Hint: Ratio concerns comparison, so write it in the form \(a:b\) with integers, not as a fraction.
Answer (a)(i): \(r:R = 1:3\)

Step 11M
Let \(h\) cm be the height of a larger circular cylinder. Since the metal is melted and recast, no volume is lost: \[2\pi R^{2}h = 27\left(\pi r^{2}(10)\right).\]
Hint: Volume of a cylinder \(=\pi(\text{base radius})^{2}\times\text{height}\). Left side = 2 big cylinders, right side = 27 small cylinders (each of height 10 cm).
Step 21M
Make \(h\) the subject (divide both sides by \(2\pi R^{2}\)): \[h=\frac{270}{2}\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{2} \qquad\left(\text{since } 27\times 10 = 270 \text{ and } \frac{\pi r^{2}}{\pi R^{2}}=\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{2}\right).\]
Hint: Group the numbers together first: \(\dfrac{27\times 10}{2}=135\), then multiply by \(\left(\dfrac{r}{R}\right)^{2}\).
Step 31M
From (a)(i), \(\dfrac{r}{R}=\dfrac{1}{3}\). Substituting, \[h=\frac{270}{2}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2} =\frac{270}{2}\times\frac{1}{9}=15.\]
Hint: \(\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2}=\frac{1}{9}\). So \(h=\frac{270}{2}\times\frac{1}{9}=\frac{270}{18}=15\).
Step 41A
Thus, the height of a larger circular cylinder is \(15\) cm.
Hint: Always state the answer with a unit, and add a concluding sentence for the final mark.
Answer (a)(ii): The height of a larger circular cylinder is \(15\text{ cm}\).

Step 11M
Compare the corresponding lengths of the two cylinders. For the base radii: \[\frac{\text{base radius of a smaller cylinder}}{\text{base radius of a larger cylinder}}=\frac{r}{R}=\frac{1}{3}.\] But for the heights (small : large): \[\frac{\text{height of a smaller cylinder}}{\text{height of a larger cylinder}}=\frac{10}{15}=\frac{2}{3}.\]
Hint: Two solids are similar only when every pair of corresponding lengths has the same ratio. Check the radii and the heights separately.
Step 2—
Since \[\frac{1}{3}\neq\frac{2}{3},\] the two ratios are different, so the corresponding lengths are not in a common proportion.
Hint: \(\frac{1}{3}\approx0.333\) while \(\frac{2}{3}\approx0.667\) — clearly unequal, so the shapes are not scaled copies of one another.
Step 31A
Therefore the two circular cylinders are not similar, and the craftsman's claim is disagreed with.
Hint: A conclusion plus a clear "I do not agree" statement earns the final A mark.
Answer (b): Disagree — \(\dfrac{r}{R}=\dfrac{1}{3}\) but \(\dfrac{10}{15}=\dfrac{2}{3}\), so the cylinders are not similar.

Summary of Answers

  • (a)(i) Base area ratio \(=1:9 \Rightarrow \left(\dfrac{r}{R}\right)^{2}=\dfrac{1}{9} \Rightarrow\) \(r:R = 1:3\)
  • (a)(ii) Volume conserved: \(2\pi R^{2}h = 27\pi r^{2}(10) \Rightarrow\) \(h = 15\text{ cm}\)
  • (b) Radii ratio \(=\dfrac{1}{3}\) but heights ratio \(=\dfrac{10}{15}=\dfrac{2}{3}\), which are unequal. Disagree — the cylinders are not similar.
