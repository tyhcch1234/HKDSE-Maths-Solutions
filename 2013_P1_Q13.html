QUESTION 13

In a workshop, 2 identical solid metal right circular cylinders of base radius \(R\) cm are melted and recast into 27 smaller identical solid right circular cylinders of base radius \(r\) cm and height \(10\) cm.

It is given that the base area of a larger circular cylinder is 9 times that of a smaller one.

(a) Find

  1. (i) \(r:R\),
  2. (ii) the height of a larger circular cylinder. [5 marks]

(b) A craftsman claims that a smaller circular cylinder and a larger circular cylinder are similar. Do you agree? Explain your answer. [2 marks]

STEP 1 · (a)(i) — Relate the radii
Ratio of Areas of Similar Figures \(\dfrac{A_{1}}{A_{2}}=\left(\dfrac{l_{1}}{l_{2}}\right)^{2}\) The base areas are in the ratio \(9:1\), so the radii are in the ratio \(3:1\).

The base area of a larger cylinder is 9 times that of a smaller one. Since a base is a circle of area \(\pi\rho^2\):

$$\frac{\pi R^{2}}{\pi r^{2}}=\frac{9}{1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left(\frac{R}{r}\right)^{2}=9$$

Taking the positive square root (lengths are positive):

$$\frac{R}{r}=3\;\Longrightarrow\; r:R=1:3$$
💡 Ratio of areas is the square of the ratio of lengths.
STEP 2 · (a)(ii) — Volume conservation
Volume of a Cylinder \(V=\pi r^{2}h\) Melting and recasting keeps the volume, so \(2\pi R^{2}h=27\pi r^{2}(10)\).

Melting and recasting keeps the total volume unchanged. Let \(h\) cm be the height of a larger cylinder.

Volume of 2 larger cylinders \(=\) Volume of 27 smaller cylinders:

$$2\pi R^{2}h=27\pi r^{2}(10)$$
💡 This is a required statement — the total volume is conserved.
STEP 3 · (a)(ii) — Solve for h

Rearrange and substitute \(\dfrac{r}{R}=\dfrac{1}{3}\):

$$h=\frac{270}{2}\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{2}=\frac{270}{2}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2}=135\times\frac{1}{9}=15$$

So the height of a larger circular cylinder is \(\mathbf{15\text{ cm}}\).

STEP 4 · (b) — Test for similarity
Similar Solids \(\dfrac{V_{1}}{V_{2}}=\left(\dfrac{l_{1}}{l_{2}}\right)^{3}\) Two solids are similar only if every pair of corresponding lengths is in the same ratio.

Two solids are similar only if every corresponding length is in the same ratio.

Ratio of base radii:

$$\frac{r}{R}=\frac{1}{3}$$

Ratio of heights (smaller : larger):

$$\frac{10}{15}=\frac{2}{3}$$

Since \(\dfrac{1}{3}\neq\dfrac{2}{3}\), the two cylinders are not similar.

STEP 5 · Conclusion (b)

The radius ratio \((1:3)\) differs from the height ratio \((2:3)\), so the two circular cylinders are not similar. The craftsman's claim is disagreed.

💡 Award marks: comparing the two ratios ≠ (1M) and a clear conclusion (1A).
✅ Final Answers
  • (a)(i) \(\;r:R=1:3\)
  • (a)(ii) Height of a larger cylinder \(=15\text{ cm}\)
  • (b) Not similar — \(\dfrac{r}{R}=\dfrac{1}{3}\neq\dfrac{10}{15}\). Claim disagreed.

Question. 4 identical solid metal cones of base radius \(R\ \text{cm}\) are melted and recast into 32 smaller identical solid cones of base radius \(r\ \text{cm}\) and height \(6\ \text{cm}\). The base area of a larger cone is 16 times that of a smaller one.
(a) Find \(R:r\) and the height of a larger cone.
(b) A craftsman claims the two cones are similar. Do you agree? Explain.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(\dfrac{\pi R^{2}}{\pi r^{2}}=16\), so \(\dfrac{R}{r}=4\) and \(R:r=4:1\).
Step 2. Volume is conserved: \(4\times\tfrac{1}{3}\pi R^{2}H=32\times\tfrac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}(6)\).
Step 3. So \(4R^{2}H=192r^{2}\) and \(H=48\left(\dfrac{r}{R}\right)^{2}=48\times\dfrac{1}{16}=3\ \text{cm}\).
Step 4. The radius ratio is \(1:4\) but the height ratio is \(6:3=2:1\);
since \(1:4\neq2:1\), the cones are not similar.
Answer: (a) \(R:r=4:1\), height \(=3\ \text{cm}\) (b) No, \(1:4\neq2:1\).
