HKDSE 2014 · Paper 2 Q13 Variations Rate, Ratio & Variations

Variations: Inverse & Direct Proportion

If \(z\) varies inversely as \(x\) and directly as the cube of \(y\), which of the following must be constant?

A. \(xy^3z\)
B. \(x^3yz^3\)
C. \(\dfrac{y^3}{xz}\)
D. \(\dfrac{y}{x^3z^3}\)
Your pick:

Step-by-Step Guided Solution

1

Translate the words into an equation

“\(z\) varies inversely as \(x\)” means \(z \propto \frac{1}{x}\). “\(z\) varies directly as the cube of \(y\)” means \(z \propto y^3\).

\[ z = k \cdot \frac{y^3}{x} \]

where \(k \neq 0\) is a constant.

💡 Hint

Always introduce a constant \(k\) (the variation constant) to convert a proportional statement into an equation.

2

Play with the equation to isolate the constant

Start from \(z = \dfrac{k y^3}{x}\) and rearrange to find an expression equal to \(k\).

\[ xz = k y^3 \] \[ \frac{xz}{y^3} = k \]

Equivalently, \(\dfrac{y^3}{xz} = \dfrac{1}{k}\).

3

Match the result with the options

Since \(k\) is a non-zero constant, \(\dfrac{1}{k}\) is also a constant. Therefore \(\dfrac{y^3}{xz}\) must be constant.

✅ Option C: \(\dfrac{y^3}{xz}\)
4

Verify the other options (optional)

Show why A, B, D may not be constant
  • A. \(xy^3z = xy^3 \cdot \frac{k y^3}{x} = k y^6\) → depends on \(y\).
  • B. \(x^3yz^3 = x^3 y \left(\frac{k y^3}{x}\right)^3 = k^3 y^{10}\) → depends on \(y\).
  • D. \(\frac{y}{x^3z^3} = \frac{y}{x^3 \left(\frac{k y^3}{x}\right)^3} = \frac{1}{k^3 y^8}\) → depends on \(y\).

📌 Key Takeaway

For variation problems, always write the equation with a constant \(k\). Then rearrange the expression so that \(k\) (or \(1/k\)) is isolated — that expression must be constant.
