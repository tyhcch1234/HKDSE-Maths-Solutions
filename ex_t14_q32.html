T14 Q32

A bag contains 3 blue balls and 2 red balls. Two balls are drawn at random without replacement. Find the probability that both balls drawn are blue.

Solution

First draw: there are $3$ blue balls out of $5$ total, so $P(\text{first blue})=\frac{3}{5}$.
Given the first ball is blue, without replacement there are $2$ blue balls left out of $4$ total, so $P(\text{second blue}\mid\text{first blue})=\frac{2}{4}$.
For both balls to be blue, multiply along the tree branch: $P(\text{both blue})=\frac{3}{5}\times\frac{2}{4}=\frac{6}{20}=\frac{3}{10}$.
The probability that both balls drawn are blue is $\frac{3}{10}$.
