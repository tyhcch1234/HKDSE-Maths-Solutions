Circle (circumcircle) Line \(4x+16y=17a\) Triangle \(OAB\) Centre Points
[2022MC#41] 12. Coordinate Geometry

Let \(O\) be the origin. The coordinates of the points \(A\) and \(B\) are \((a,\,0)\) and \((0,\,b)\) respectively, where \(a\) and \(b\) are positive numbers. If the circumcentre of \(\Delta OAB\) lies on the straight line \(4x+16y=17a\), then \(a:b =\)

A. \(8:15\)
B. \(15:8\)
C. \(16:47\)
D. \(47:16\)
Step 1 · Recognise the right angle

Since \(A=(a,0)\) lies on the \(x\)-axis and \(B=(0,b)\) lies on the \(y\)-axis, the axes are perpendicular. Hence \(\angle AOB = 90^\circ\).

Step 2 · Locate the circumcentre

By the converse of the angle in a semicircle, if \(\angle AOB = 90^\circ\), then \(AB\) is a diameter of the circumcircle of \(\Delta OAB\).

Therefore the circumcentre is the midpoint of \(AB\):

\[ M = \left(\frac{a+0}{2},\ \frac{0+b}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{a}{2},\ \frac{b}{2}\right) \]

💡 A right-angled triangle always has its hypotenuse as the diameter of its circumcircle — so the centre is simply the midpoint of the hypotenuse.
Step 3 · Substitute into the given line

The circumcentre \(M\) lies on \(4x+16y=17a\). Substitute \(x=\dfrac{a}{2}\) and \(y=\dfrac{b}{2}\):

\[ 4\left(\frac{a}{2}\right) + 16\left(\frac{b}{2}\right) = 17a \]

\[ 2a + 8b = 17a \]

\[ 8b = 15a \]

\[ \frac{a}{b} = \frac{8}{15} \quad\Rightarrow\quad a:b = 8:15 \]

Answer \[\boxed{a:b = 8:15} \quad \text{(Option A)}\]
