HKDSE 2020 Paper 2 — Question 36

Topic 9 · Inequalities and Linear Programming

Feasible region \(D\) with its four corner points

feasible region \(D\) \(x=0,\;x=2\) \(2x+y+3=0\) \(x+y+1=0\)
Question

Consider the following system of inequalities:

$0 \le x \le 2$
$2x + y + 3 \ge 0$
$x + y + 1 \le 0$

Let $D$ be the region which represents the solution of the above system of inequalities. Find the constant $k$ such that the least value of $4x+3y+k$ is $24$, where $(x,y)$ is a point lying in $D$.

A. 25    B. 27    C. 37    D. 53

STEP 1
Draw the three boundary lines and shade \(D\).
\(x=0\) (the \(y\)-axis, solid), \(x=2\) (vertical line, solid),
\(2x+y+3=0 \Rightarrow y=-2x-3\) (dashed),
\(x+y+1=0 \Rightarrow y=-x-1\) (dashed).
The region \(D\) lies in \(0\le x\le2\), above \(2x+y+3=0\) and below \(x+y+1=0\).
STEP 2
Finding a Vertex of a Region \(\text{vertex}=\text{intersection of two boundary lines}\) Each corner of \(D\) is obtained by solving two of the boundary equations together.
Find the four corner (vertex) points of \(D\).
Solve \(x=0\) with \(x+y+1=0\):  \((x,y)=(0,-1)\)
Solve \(x+y+1=0\) with \(x=2\):  \((x,y)=(2,-3)\)
Solve \(x=2\) with \(2x+y+3=0\):  \((x,y)=(2,-7)\)
Solve \(2x+y+3=0\) with \(x=0\):  \((x,y)=(0,-3)\)
Substitute one equation into the other — each corner is the intersection of two boundaries.
STEP 3
Vertex Theorem for Linear Programming \(f(x,y)=ax+by+k\ \text{takes its extreme values at a vertex of the region}\) So the least value of \(4x+3y+k\) on the quadrilateral \(D\) is found among the four corner values.
Evaluate \(4x+3y+k\) at each corner point.
At \((0,-1)\):  \(4(0)+3(-1)+k = -3+k\)
At \((2,-3)\):  \(4(2)+3(-3)+k = -1+k\)
At \((2,-7)\):  \(4(2)+3(-7)+k = -13+k\)
At \((0,-3)\):  \(4(0)+3(-3)+k = -9+k\)
For a linear objective function on a polygon, the extreme values must occur at a corner point.
STEP 4
Identify the least value.
Comparing \(-3+k,\;-1+k,\;-13+k,\;-9+k\), the smallest is \[ -13+k .\]
Only the constant \(k\) is common, so the smallest expression is the one with the smallest \(x,y\) contribution: \((2,-7)\).
STEP 5
Balancing Method for a Linear Equation \(ax+b=c\ \Longrightarrow\ x=\dfrac{c-b}{a}\quad(a\neq0)\) From \(-13+k=24\) we get \(k=37\) by adding \(13\) to both sides.
Set the least value equal to \(24\) and solve for \(k\).
\[ -13+k = 24 \] \[ k = 37 \]
The answer matches option C.
Answer: \(k = 37\)  (Option C) ✓

Question. The region \(D\) is defined by \(0\le x\le3\), \(3x+y+4\ge0\) and \(x+y+2\le0\). If the least value of \(5x+2y+k\) over \(D\) is \(18\), then \(k=\) A. \(7\) B. \(18\) C. \(29\) D. \(40\) [1 mark]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The boundaries are \(x=0\), \(x=3\), \(y=-3x-4\) and \(y=-x-2\).
Step 2. Corners: \(x=0\) with \(y=-x-2\) gives \((0,-2)\); \(y=-x-2\) with \(x=3\) gives \((3,-5)\); \(x=3\) with \(y=-3x-4\) gives \((3,-13)\); \(y=-3x-4\) with \(x=0\) gives \((0,-4)\).
Step 3. Values of \(5x+2y+k\): at \((0,-2)\) it is \(-4+k\); at \((3,-5)\) it is \(5+k\); at \((3,-13)\) it is \(-11+k\); at \((0,-4)\) it is \(-8+k\).
Step 4. The least of these is \(-11+k\), attained at \((3,-13)\).
Step 5. \(-11+k=18\), so \(k=29\).
Answer: C. \(k=29\)
