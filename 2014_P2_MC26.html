Coordinate Geometry of the Circle — using the slope of a diameter
Equation of a Circle Centre of a Circle Slope of a Line Diameter

QUESTION5 marks

If a diameter of the circle \[ x^2+y^2-8x+ky-214=0 \] passes through the point \((6,\,-5)\) and the slope of the diameter is \(-4\), then \(k=\)

A.  \(-6\)
B.  \(-4\)
C.  \(13\)
D.  \(70\)

STEP AWriting the circle in centre–radius form

The general circle \(x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey+F=0\) has centre \(\left(-\tfrac{D}{2},\,-\tfrac{E}{2}\right)\).

Here \(D=-8\), so the \(x\)-coordinate of the centre is \(4\). But the \(y\)-coordinate contains the unknown \(k\): the centre is \[ C\left(4,\;-\frac{k}{2}\right). \]

Drag the slider: the centre \(C\!\left(4,-\frac{k}{2}\right)\) slides vertically and the whole circle moves with it. Notice the \(x\)-coordinate of the centre is always \(4\), whatever \(k\) is.

STEP BApplying the slope condition

A diameter of a circle always passes through the centre. So the segment joining the centre \(C\) and the given point \(P(6,-5)\) is (part of) a diameter, with slope \(-4\).

The green line through \(P\) has slope exactly \(-4\). The only position of \(C\) that lies on it is the solution — slide \(k\) until the blue diameter lies along the green line.

STEP CSolving for \(k\)

The finished picture for \(k=-6\): centre \(C(4,3)\), the diameter \(CP\) through \(P(6,-5)\) with slope \(-4\), and the full circle of radius \(\sqrt{239}\approx 15.46\).

Step 11MFind the centre of the circle

Complete the square in \(x\) and in \(y\) (leaving the \(y\)-part in terms of \(k\)):

\[ x^2-8x+y^2+ky=214 \] \[ (x-4)^2-16+\left(y+\frac{k}{2}\right)^2-\frac{k^2}{4}=214 \]

Hence the centre is

\[ C\left(4,\;-\frac{k}{2}\right). \]
Hint: The \(x\)-coordinate of the centre is fixed at \(-\frac{-8}{2}=4\). Only the \(y\)-coordinate depends on \(k\).
Step 21MWrite the slope of \(CP\)

The diameter passes through \(P(6,-5)\), so \(C\) and \(P\) lie on the diameter. Using the slope formula:

\[ \text{slope of }CP=\frac{\left(-\frac{k}{2}\right)-(-5)}{4-6}=\frac{5-\frac{k}{2}}{-2}. \]
Hint: Slope \(=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\). Keep the order of the points the same in numerator and denominator. Here \(x_2-x_1=4-6=-2\).
Step 31ASet the slope equal to \(-4\) and solve
\[ \frac{\left(-\frac{k}{2}\right)-(-5)}{4-6}=-4 \] \[ \frac{5-\frac{k}{2}}{-2}=-4 \] \[ 5-\frac{k}{2}=8 \] \[ -\frac{k}{2}=3 \] \[ k=-6. \]
Hint: Multiply both sides by \(-2\) first — it turns the fraction into a simple linear equation. Always substitute \(k=-6\) back to check: \(-\frac{k}{2}=3\), so the centre is \((4,3)\) and slope \(=\frac{3-(-5)}{4-6}=\frac{8}{-2}=-4\). ✓
Answer: \(k=-6\), i.e. option A.
