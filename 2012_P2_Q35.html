HKDSE 2012 P2Question 35

35. If $\beta$ is a real number, then \[ i^{\,2}(\beta i - 3) = \]
  • A. $\beta + 3i$
  • B. $\beta - 3i$
  • C. $-\beta + 3i$
  • D. $-\beta - 3i$
Step 1 · Read the given We are asked to simplify $$i^{2}(\beta i - 3)$$ where $\beta$ is a real number. Recall that $i$ is the imaginary unit with \[ i^2 = -1. \] Hint: Replace $i^2$ by $-1$ before distributing.
Step 2 · Substitute $i^2=-1$ \[ i^{2}(\beta i - 3) = (-1)(\beta i - 3). \] Hint: The whole bracket is multiplied by $-1$.
Step 3 · Distribute the $-1$ \[ (-1)(\beta i - 3) = -\beta i + 3. \] Hint: Watch the signs: $-(-3)=+3$ and $-(\beta i)=-\beta i$.
Step 4 · Convert $i^2$ into a real part Alternatively, expand first: \[ i^{2}\beta i - 3i^{2} = \beta\, i^{3} - 3i^{2}. \] Since $i^{3}=i^{2}\cdot i = -i$ and $i^{2}=-1$, \[ \beta(-i) - 3(-1) = -\beta i + 3. \] Hint: Both methods must give the same result.
Step 5 · Write in $a+bi$ form \[ -\beta i + 3 = 3 + (-\beta)i, \] which is the standard form with real part $3$ and imaginary part $-\beta$. Hint: Re-order as real part $+$ imaginary part.
Answer:  $-\beta + 3i$  — option C.
