HKDSE 2012 Paper 2 · Q41 Geometry

Question

In the figure, PQ is the tangent to the circle ABC at O, where O is the centre of the semicircle PBQ. It is given that BCP is a straight line. If $\angle BPQ = 12^\circ$, then $\angle BAC =$
A. $18^\circ$    B. $24^\circ$    C. $54^\circ$    D. $36^\circ$

Solution

STEP 1 · Angle at the centre = 2 × angle at the circumference

$PQ$ is a diameter of the semicircle and $B$ lies on it, so $\angle PBQ = 90^\circ$. In $\triangle BPQ$:

$$\angle BQP = 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 12^\circ = 78^\circ$$

Since $OB = OQ$ (radii), $\angle OBQ = \angle OQB = 78^\circ$, hence

$$\angle BOQ = 180^\circ - 78^\circ - 78^\circ = 24^\circ$$

Equivalently: the angle at the centre $\angle BOQ$ subtends arc $BQ$; the angle at the circumference $\angle BPQ = 12^\circ$ subtends the same arc, so $\angle BOQ = 2\times 12^\circ = 24^\circ$.

Angle at the Centre \(\angle AOB=2\,\angle ACB\quad(\text{same arc }AB)\) The angle at the centre \(\angle BOQ\) and the angle at the circumference \(\angle BPQ=12^{\circ}\) stand on the same arc \(BQ\).
STEP 2 · Angle in alternate segment

$PQ$ is the tangent at $O$ to the circle $ABC$, and $OB$ is a chord. The angle between the tangent $PQ$ and the chord $OB$ equals the angle in the alternate segment:

$$\angle OAB = \angle BOQ = 24^\circ$$

Here $\angle BOQ$ is the angle the chord $OB$ makes with the tangent $PQ$ at $O$ (the arc $OB$ on the far side carries the point $A$).

Tangent-Chord Angle (Alternate Segment Theorem) \(\angle(\text{tangent }PQ,\ \text{chord }OB)=\angle OAB\) The angle between the tangent \(PQ\) at \(O\) and the chord \(OB\) equals the angle that \(OB\) subtends at \(A\).
STEP 3 · Isosceles triangle & equal base angles

$OB = OP$ (radii of the same circle), so $\triangle OBP$ is isosceles and

$$\angle OBP = \angle OPB = 12^\circ$$

Since $B,\,C,\,P$ are collinear, $\angle OBC = \angle OBP = 12^\circ$.

STEP 4 · Angles in the same segment

Chords $OC$—well, look at the circle $ABC$: $\angle OAC$ and $\angle OBC$ are angles in the same segment standing on the chord $OC$, therefore

$$\angle OAC = \angle OBC = 12^\circ$$

Angles in the Same Segment \(\angle OAC=\angle OBC\quad(\text{same chord }OC)\) Both angles stand on the chord \(OC\) of the circle \(ABC\), so they are equal.
STEP 5 · Add the two parts of $\angle BAC$

The ray $AO$ divides $\angle BAC$ into $\angle OAB$ and $\angle OAC$, so

$$\angle BAC = \angle OAB + \angle OAC = 24^\circ + 12^\circ = 36^\circ$$

Therefore the answer is option D.

💡 Tip: In tangent problems, always look for (i) radius ⊥ tangent, (ii) the alternate segment theorem, and (iii) radii forming isosceles triangles.
ANSWER
D  ·  $36^\circ$

Show the worked solution
