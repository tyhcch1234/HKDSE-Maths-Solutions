[2016#11] An inverted right circular conical vessel contains some milk. The vessel is held vertically. The depth of milk in the vessel is 12 cm. Peter then pours \(444\pi\) cm\(^3\) of milk into the vessel without overflowing. He now finds that the depth of milk in the vessel is 16 cm.

(a) Express the final volume of milk in the vessel in terms of \(\pi\). [3 marks]
(b) Peter claims that the final area of the wet curved surface of the vessel is at least \(800\) cm\(^2\). Do you agree? Explain your answer. [3 marks]
STEP 1 · VOLUME RATIO
Let \(V\) cm\(^3\) be the final volume of milk.
For an inverted cone, volume is proportional to the cube of the depth: \[ \frac{V-444\pi}{V}=\left(\frac{12}{16}\right)^3 \] \[ \frac{V-444\pi}{V}=\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^3=\frac{27}{64} \]
Hint: Similar cones → ratio of volumes = (ratio of heights)\(^3\).
STEP 2 · SOLVE FOR V
\[ 64(V-444\pi)=27V \] \[ 64V-28416\pi=27V \] \[ 37V=28416\pi \] \[ V=768\pi \]
Final volume \(=768\pi\) cm\(^3\)
STEP 3 · FIND RADIUS r
Let \(r\) cm be the final radius of the milk surface. \[ \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2(16)=768\pi \] \[ \frac{16}{3}r^2=768 \] \[ r^2=144 \quad\Rightarrow\quad r=12 \]
Final radius \(r=12\) cm
STEP 4 · WET CURVED SURFACE AREA
Slant height of the wet part: \(\sqrt{12^2+16^2}=\sqrt{400}=20\) cm. \[ \text{Curved surface area}=\pi r l=\pi(12)(20) \] \[ =240\pi\approx753.98\text{ cm}^2 \]
The wet curved surface is a cone of radius \(12\) and slant height \(20\).
STEP 5 · CONCLUSION
\[ 240\pi\approx753.98<800 \]
Peter's claim is disagreed — the area is less than \(800\) cm\(^2\).
