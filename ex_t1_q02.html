Percentages (30) · Sub-Topic: Interest

T1 Q02

Find the amount if \$8,000 is deposited at 2.5% p.a. simple interest for 4 years.

Solution

Use the simple interest amount formula $A = P(1 + rt)$.
Substitute $P = 8000$, $r = 2.5\% = 0.025$, and $t = 4$: $A = 8000(1 + 0.025 \times 4)$.
Calculate $0.025 \times 4 = 0.1$, so $A = 8000(1 + 0.1) = 8000 \times 1.1$.
$A = 8800$.
The amount is $\$8800$.
