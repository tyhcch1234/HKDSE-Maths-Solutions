Probability of Drawing Two Cards

Topic 14 — Probability  ·  3 marks

#Probability #EquallyLikelyOutcomes #SystematicListing #FavourableOutcomes

Question

Bag A contains four cards numbered 1, 3, 5 and 7 respectively, while bag B contains five cards numbered 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 respectively. If one card is randomly drawn from each bag, find the probability that the sum of the two numbers drawn is less than 9.

Bag A — 4 cards
Bag B — 5 cards

Since one card is drawn from each bag independently, every pair (one from A, one from B) is equally likely.

Strategy at a glance

\[ P(\text{event})=\frac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total number of outcomes}} \]
  • Denominator: total pairs = (number of cards in A) × (number of cards in B).
  • Numerator: count the pairs whose sum is less than 9.
  • Build a table so that no pair is missed and none is counted twice.
