Topic: Percentages (Weighted Averages)

Percentages Weighted Average Algebra Multiple Choice
Q9

In a school, 33% of the students are overweight. It is given that 60% of the students in the school are girls and 45% of the girls are overweight. If $x$% of the boys in the school are overweight, then $x =$

A. 15
B. 18
C. 25
D. 55

💡 Core Idea

The overall percentage of overweight students is a weighted average of the girls' and boys' percentages. The weights are the proportions of girls and boys in the school.

$$\text{Overall } \% = (\text{Weight of girls} \times \text{Girls' } \%) + (\text{Weight of boys} \times \text{Boys' } \%)$$

Notice how the overall 33% sits between the girls' 45% and boys' $x$%.

🧩 Step-by-Step Solution

Since 60% of the students are girls, the remaining are boys:

$100\% - 60\% = 40\%$

💡 Hint: The two groups (girls and boys) must add up to 100% of the school population.

60% of students are girls, and 45% of those girls are overweight:

$60\% \times 45\% = 27\%$

💡 Meaning: 27% of the whole school are overweight girls.

Total overweight = 33%, of which 27% are girls:

$33\% - 27\% = 6\%$

💡 Meaning: 6% of the whole school are overweight boys.

Boys are 40% of the school, and $x$% of boys are overweight. The overweight boys make up 6% of the school:

$40\% \times x\% = 6\%$

$0.4x = 6$

$x = \dfrac{6}{0.4} = 15$

$x = 15$

Therefore, the answer is A. 15.

Check: $60\%(45\%) + 40\%(15\%) = 27\% + 6\% = 33\%$ ✓

⚡ The Standard Formula (Exam Shortcut)

$$(60\%)(45\%) + (1 - 60\%)(x\%) = 33\%$$

This is exactly the weighted average equation — the most efficient way to answer under exam pressure.
