Logarithmic Linearisation

It is given that $\log_a y$ is a linear function of $x$, where $0 < a < 1$. The intercepts on the vertical axis and on the horizontal axis of the graph of the linear function are 6 and 3 respectively. If $y = m n^x$, which of the following is/are true?

I.   $m < 1$
II.   $n < 1$
III.   $m n^3 = 1$
A. I only
B. II only
C. I and III only
D. II and III only

🧩 Step-by-Step Solution

1

Transform the equation to a linear form.

Given $y = m n^x$, take $\log_a$ of both sides: \[ \log_a y = \log_a (m n^x) = \log_a m + x \log_a n \] This is linear in $x$: $\log_a y = (\log_a n)x + \log_a m$.
💡 Hint: $\log_a (UV) = \log_a U + \log_a V$ and $\log_a (n^x) = x \log_a n$.
2

Identify slope and vertical intercept.

From the graph, the vertical intercept is $6$ (when $x=0$), and the horizontal intercept is $3$ (when $x=3$, $\log_a y = 0$). \[ \begin{aligned} \text{Vertical intercept} &= \log_a m = 6 \\ \text{Horizontal intercept: } \log_a y &= 0 \text{ at } x=3 \\ \text{Slope} &= \frac{0 - 6}{3 - 0} = -2 = \log_a n \end{aligned} \]
💡 Hint: Slope $= \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{0-6}{3-0} = -2$. Also, $\log_a m = 6$.
3

Find expressions for $m$ and $n$.

\[ \log_a m = 6 \quad \Rightarrow \quad m = a^6 \] \[ \log_a n = -2 \quad \Rightarrow \quad n = a^{-2} \] Since $0 < a < 1$, $a^6$ is positive and less than $1$, and $a^{-2}$ is greater than $1$.
💡 Hint: If $\log_a M = k$, then $M = a^k$. Remember $a$ is between $0$ and $1$.

Final Answer: C (I and III only)

Statement I and III are true; II is false.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • To linearise $y = m n^x$, take $\log_a$ of both sides: $\log_a y = \log_a m + x \log_a n$.
  • In the graph of $\log_a y$ vs $x$, the vertical intercept is $\log_a m$, and the slope is $\log_a n$.
  • When $0 < a < 1$, $a^{\text{positive}}$ is small (<1), and $a^{\text{negative}}$ is large (>1).
  • Always verify each statement using the derived expressions and the given range of $a$.
