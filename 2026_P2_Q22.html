Question

According to the figure, which of the following must be true?

  • A\(p=r\)
  • B\(q=90^\circ\)
  • C\(p+r=q\)
  • D\(p+q+r=180^\circ\)

In the figure, the shape is a pentagon with right angles at the top-left and bottom-left corners. Its right side is indented: \(p\) and \(r\) are the interior angles at the top-right and bottom-right vertices, and \(q\) is the notch angle at the concave (indented) vertex, measured outside the indented region.

Original-style figure: convex quadrilateral A–B–C–D plus the concave vertex E.
Step-by-step Solution
Step 1 · Angle sum of a pentagon
Interior Angle Sum of a Polygon \((n-2)\times 180^{\circ}\) A pentagon has \(n=5\), so its interior angles total \(540^{\circ}\).

The pentagon has \(5\) sides, so its interior angles sum to

\[ (5-2)\times 180^\circ = 3\times 180^\circ = 540^\circ . \]
Hint: an \(n\)-gon always has interior angle sum \((n-2)\times 180^\circ\).
Step 2 · The concave vertex
Reflex Angle at a Concave Vertex \(\text{interior angle}=360^{\circ}-q\) \(q\) is measured outside the polygon, so the interior angle at the notch is the reflex angle.

The vertex carrying the notch angle \(q\) is concave.
The angle inside the pentagon there is the reflex angle

\[ (\text{interior angle at the notch}) = 360^\circ - q . \]
Hint: \(q\) is measured on the removed side of the indentation; the polygon's own interior angle wraps the other way round that point.
Step 3 · Set up the angle-sum equation

The two left corners are right angles,
so the five interior angles are \(90^\circ,\ p,\ 360^\circ-q,\ r,\ 90^\circ\). Hence

\[ 90^\circ + p + (360^\circ - q) + r + 90^\circ = 540^\circ . \]
Step 4 · Simplify
Simplifying an Angle-Sum Equation \(90^{\circ}+p+(360^{\circ}-q)+r+90^{\circ}=540^{\circ}\) The two right angles cancel the extra \(360^{\circ}\), leaving \(p+r=q\).
\[ p + 440^\circ - q + r = 540^\circ \] \[ p + r - q = 100^\circ ? \]

Careful — check the arithmetic again:

\[ 90^\circ+90^\circ+360^\circ = 540^\circ \] \[ \Rightarrow\ p - q + r + 540^\circ = 540^\circ \] \[ \Rightarrow\ p + r = q . \]
Hint: the two \(90^\circ\)'s exactly cancel the extra \(360^\circ\), leaving the neat relation \(p+r=q\).
Step 5 · Eliminate the other options

Nothing forces the notch to be symmetric, so \(p=r\) is not forced — (A) is false.

The notch could be shallow or deep,
so \(q\) need not be \(90^\circ\) — (B) is false.

And since \(p+r=q\),
the sum \(p+q+r = 2q\) can vary, so (D) is false.

Final Answer C \(p + r = q\)

Question. In the figure, \(ABCDEF\) is a concave hexagon with \(\angle A=\angle B=90^{\circ}\). At the notch \(C\) the angle measured outside the polygon is \(q\), and the other interior angles are \(p\), \(r\) and \(s\). Which of the following must be true? A. \(p+r+s=q\) B. \(p+r+s=q+180^{\circ}\) C. \(p+r+s+q=180^{\circ}\) D. \(p+r+s-q=540^{\circ}\)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. A hexagon has \(6\) sides, so its interior angles sum to \((6-2)\times 180^{\circ}=720^{\circ}\).
Step 2. The interior angle at the notch is the reflex angle \(360^{\circ}-q\).
Step 3. So \(90^{\circ}+90^{\circ}+(360^{\circ}-q)+p+r+s=720^{\circ}\).
Step 4. Simplifying: \(p+r+s+540^{\circ}-q=720^{\circ}\), i.e. \(p+r+s=q+180^{\circ}\).
Step 5. Hence the answer is B.
Answer: B
Key idea: a concave vertex contributes \(360^\circ-q\) (the reflex interior angle) to the polygon's angle sum.
