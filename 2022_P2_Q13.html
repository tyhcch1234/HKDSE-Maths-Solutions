HKDSE 2022 Paper 2 · Q13

If $u$ varies directly as the square root of $v$ and inversely as $w$, which of the following are true?

  1. $u^2$ varies directly as $v$ and inversely as the square of $w$.
  2. $v$ varies directly as $w$ and inversely as the square root of $u$.
  3. $w$ varies directly as the square root of $v$ and inversely as $u$.
  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III

Step-by-step solution

1Translate the words into an equation
Direct and Inverse Variation \(u\propto\dfrac{\sqrt{v}}{w}\ \Rightarrow\ u=\dfrac{k\sqrt{v}}{w}\) The words give \(u=\dfrac{k\sqrt{v}}{w}\) with \(k\neq0\); every statement follows from this equation.

“$u$ varies directly as $\sqrt{v}$ and inversely as $w$” means $u \propto \dfrac{\sqrt{v}}{w}$,
so we may write

\[ u=\frac{k\sqrt{v}}{w},\qquad k\ne 0 \text{ is a constant.} \]

Everything follows from this single equation — keep $k$ as the “constant of variation”.
2Test statement I 
: $u^2$ vs $v$ and $w^2$
Squaring a Variation Equation \(u^{2}=\dfrac{k^{2}v}{w^{2}}\) Squaring turns \(\sqrt{v}\) into \(v\) and \(w\) into \(w^{2}\), so \(u^{2}\propto\dfrac{v}{w^{2}}\); I is true.

Square the equation:

\[ u^2=\frac{k^2 v}{w^2}. \]

Here $k^2$ is a non-zero constant,
so $u^2$ varies directly as $v$ and inversely as $w^2$.

Statement I is TRUE.

Squaring turns $\sqrt{v}$ into $v$ and $w$ into $w^2$ — the powers double (1/2 → 1, 1 → 2).
3Test statement II 
: $v$ vs $w$ and $\sqrt{u}$

Rearrange for $v$: from $u=\dfrac{k\sqrt{v}}{w}$ we get $\sqrt{v}=\dfrac{uw}{k}$,
hence

\[ v=\frac{u^2w^2}{k^2}. \]

So $v \propto u^2w^2$ — it varies directly as $w^2$ (not $w$) and directly as $u^2$ (not inversely as $\sqrt{u}$).

Statement II is FALSE. (Note $v=\dfrac{w^2}{\left(k^2/u^2\right)}$,
not $v=\dfrac{w}{\sqrt u}$ times a constant.)

Check the form: $\dfrac{\sqrt{v/u}}{w}$ would have to be constant, but it equals $\dfrac{1}{k}\cdot\dfrac{u}{w^2}\cdot w = \dfrac{u}{kw}$, which is not constant.
4Test statement III 
: $w$ vs $\sqrt{v}$ and $u$
Rearranging a Variation Equation \(w=\dfrac{k\sqrt{v}}{u}\) Solving \(u=\dfrac{k\sqrt{v}}{w}\) for \(w\) shows \(w\) varies directly as \(\sqrt{v}\) and inversely as \(u\).

Rearrange for $w$: from $u=\dfrac{k\sqrt{v}}{w}$,

\[ w=\frac{k\sqrt{v}}{u}. \]

So $w$ varies directly as $\sqrt v$ and inversely as $u$.

Statement III is TRUE.

Alternatively, the ratio $\dfrac{w\sqrt u}{\sqrt v}$ is not constant, but $\dfrac{wu}{\sqrt v}=k$ is — the product form confirms III.
5Conclusion

Only statements I and III are true.

Answer: B  (I and III only)
Quick check with $k=1,\ v=4,\ w=2$: then $u=1$. I: $u^2=1$ and $\frac{v}{w^2}=1$ ✓. III: $w=2$ and $\frac{\sqrt v}{u}=2$ ✓. II: $\frac{vw}{\sqrt u}=8$ is not constant ✗.

Question. It is given that \(u\) varies directly as \(v^{2}\) and inversely as \(\sqrt{w}\). Which of the following are true? I. \(u^{2}\) varies directly as \(v^{4}\) and inversely as \(w\). II. \(v\) varies directly as \(\sqrt{uw}\). III. \(w\) varies directly as \(v^{4}\) and inversely as \(u^{2}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(u=\dfrac{kv^{2}}{\sqrt{w}}\).
Step 2. I: \(u^{2}=\dfrac{k^{2}v^{4}}{w}\), so \(u^{2}\propto\dfrac{v^{4}}{w}\) — true.
Step 3. II: \(\sqrt{w}=\dfrac{kv^{2}}{u}\), so \(w=\dfrac{k^{2}v^{4}}{u^{2}}\), giving \(v\propto(uw)^{1/4}\), not \(\sqrt{uw}\) — false.
Step 4. III: from \(w=\dfrac{k^{2}v^{4}}{u^{2}}\), \(w\propto v^{4}\) and \(w\propto\dfrac{1}{u^{2}}\) — true.
Answer: I and III only
