[2016MC#18] The figure shows a right prism. Find the volume of the prism.

A. 216 cm\(^3\)
B. 240 cm\(^3\)
C. 300 cm\(^3\)
D. 328 cm\(^3\)
STEP 1 · SET UP THE UNKNOWN

The cross-section is a trapezium lying flat, so the prism has length \(6\text{ cm}\).

Let \(x\) cm be the lower base of the trapezium. Then the upper base is \(4\text{ cm}\) and the height of the trapezium is \(5\text{ cm}\).

A slant edge of the trapezium makes a right triangle with legs \(5\) cm (height) and \((x-4)\) cm (horizontal gap), and hypotenuse \(13\) cm.

STEP 2 · PYTHAGORAS

Apply Pythagoras' theorem to the small right triangle:

\[(x-4)^2 + 5^2 = 13^2\] \[(x-4)^2 = 169 - 25 = 144\] \[x-4 = \pm 12\] \[x = 16 \quad \text{or} \quad x = -8 \ (\text{rejected})\]

Since a length must be positive, the lower base is \(x = 16\text{ cm}\).

💡 Hint: reject the negative root — it would give a base shorter than the top, contradicting the figure.
STEP 3 · AREA OF THE TRAPEZIUM

Area of a trapezium \(=\dfrac{1}{2}(\text{sum of parallel sides})\times\text{height}\):

\[\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\,(4 + 16)\times 5 = \frac{1}{2}\,(20)(5) = 50\text{ cm}^2\]
STEP 4 · VOLUME OF THE PRISM

For a prism, \(\text{Volume} = (\text{cross-sectional area})\times\text{length}\):

\[\text{Volume} = 50 \times 6 = 300\text{ cm}^3\]
FINAL ANSWER
Volume \(= 50\times 6 =\) 300 cm³ → Option C
