Topic 4: Polynomials — Factorization

Q Question

Factorize

(a) \(\;x^{3}+x^{2}y-7x^{2}\)

(b) \(\;x^{3}+x^{2}y-7x^{2}-x-y+7\;\) [4 marks]

🔍 Visualize: Can you spot the common factor?

Click each term to highlight its structure.

S Step-by-Step Solution

Part (a): \(x^{3}+x^{2}y-7x^{2}\)

Step 1 — Identify the highest common factor (HCF)

Look at all three terms: \(x^{3},\; x^{2}y,\; -7x^{2}\).

  • Each term contains \(x^{2}\).
  • The coefficients \(1, 1, -7\) have no common factor other than \(1\).
  • \(y\) only appears in one term.

∴ HCF = \(x^{2}\)

Step 2 — Factor out \(x^{2}\)

\[ x^{3}+x^{2}y-7x^{2} = x^{2}(x + y - 7) \]

Check: \(x^{2}\cdot x = x^{3}\), \(x^{2}\cdot y = x^{2}y\), \(x^{2}\cdot(-7)=-7x^{2}\). ✓

Answer (a): \(\;x^{2}(x+y-7)\)  1A

Part (b): \(x^{3}+x^{2}y-7x^{2}-x-y+7\)

Step 3 — Group the terms

Compare part (b) with part (a): the first three terms form the expression from part (a), and the last three terms can be grouped together.

\[ (\underbrace{x^{3}+x^{2}y-7x^{2}}_{\text{group 1}}) + (\underbrace{-x-y+7}_{\text{group 2}}) \]

Step 4 — Factor each group

From part (a): \(x^{3}+x^{2}y-7x^{2}=x^{2}(x+y-7)\)

For the second group, factor out \(-1\): \(-x-y+7 = -(x+y-7)\)

So: \[ x^{2}(x+y-7) - (x+y-7) \]

Hint: Always check if the leftover brackets match — they do here!

Step 5 — Factor out the common bracket

Both terms now contain \((x+y-7)\):

\[ x^{2}(x+y-7) - 1\cdot(x+y-7) = (x^{2}-1)(x+y-7) \]

Intermediate result: \((x^{2}-1)(x+y-7)\)  1M

Step 6 — Fully factorize using difference of squares

Apply \(a^{2}-b^{2}=(a-b)(a+b)\) with \(a=x,\;b=1\):

\[ x^{2}-1 = (x-1)(x+1) \]

Therefore:

\[ (x-1)(x+1)(x+y-7) \]

Answer (b)  1A

📐 Useful Formulas

  • Common factor: \(ab+ac = a(b+c)\)
  • Difference of squares: \(a^{2}-b^{2} = (a-b)(a+b)\)
  • Grouping strategy: When four or more terms appear, try grouping and looking for a repeated bracket.

✅ Final Answers

(a)

\(x^{2}(x+y-7)\)

(b)

\((x-1)(x+1)(x+y-7)\)
