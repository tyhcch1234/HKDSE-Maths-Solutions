HKDSE Mathematics 2025 · Paper 2 · Question 10 — Percentages (Compound Interest)

Deposit $40,000 · 3% p.a. · compounded half-yearly · 5 years

Principal $40,000 Growth per half-year Final amount
[2025 MC #10]

A sum of \(\$40\,000\) is deposited at an interest rate of \(3\%\) per annum for \(5\) years, compounded half-yearly. Find the amount correct to the nearest dollar.

A. \(\$46\,000\)
B. \(\$46\,371\)
C. \(\$46\,422\)
D. \(\$46\,465\)

Step 1Identify the compounding parameters.

Principal \(P = \$40\,000\). Nominal rate \(r = 3\%\) per annum, compounded half-yearly, so the rate per period is \[ \frac{r}{2} = \frac{3\%}{2} = 1.5\% \ \text{per half-year}. \]

Number of periods: \(5\) years \(\times\,2\) periods per year \(= 10\) half-years.

Hint: compounding frequency changes BOTH the rate per period and the number of periods.

Step 2Write the compound interest formula.

\[ A = P\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{n t} \]

where \(n\) is the number of compounding periods per year and \(t\) is the time in years. Here \(n=2,\ t=5\).

Hint: this is exactly the form \((1+\tfrac{3\%}{2})^{5\times 2}\) shown in the official solution.

Step 3Substitute the values.

\[ A = 40\,000\left(1+\frac{3\%}{2}\right)^{5\times 2} = 40\,000\,(1.015)^{10} \]

Hint: convert the percentage to a decimal: \(1.5\% = 0.015\).

Step 4Evaluate the power and multiply.

\[ (1.015)^{10} \approx 1.1605408 \]

\[ A \approx 40\,000 \times 1.1605408 \approx 46\,421.63 \]

Hint: keep full precision until the final rounding step.

Step 5Round to the nearest dollar.

\[ A \approx \$46\,421.63 \;\longrightarrow\; \$46\,422 \]

Hint: compare with the options — only \(C\) matches. (Simple interest, \(\$46\,000\), is a classic distractor.)

Answer: C — \(\$46\,422\) (correct to the nearest dollar)
