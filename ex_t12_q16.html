Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Equation of Straight Line

T12 Q16

Find the equation of the straight line passing through the point $(1, 3)$ with slope $2$.

Solution

Use the point-slope form: $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$.
Substitute $(x_1,y_1)=(1,3)$ and $m=2$: $y-3=2(x-1)$.
Expand the right-hand side: $y-3=2x-2$.
Add $3$ to both sides: $y=2x+1$.
The equation of the straight line is $y=2x+1$.
