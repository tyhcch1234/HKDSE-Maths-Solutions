2022 #4 Factorize [4 marks]

4. Factorize

(a) $\;9c^{2}-6c+1\,,$
(b) $\;(4c+d)^{2}-9c^{2}+6c-1\,.$
1Part (a) — recognise a perfect square
Perfect Square Trinomial \(a^{2}-2ab+b^{2}=(a-b)^{2}\) Here \(a=3c\) and \(b=1\), since \(9c^{2}=(3c)^{2}\), \(6c=2(3c)(1)\) and \(1=1^{2}\).
$9c^{2}-6c+1 = (3c)^{2}-2(3c)(1)+(1)^{2}$
Match the pattern $a^{2}-2ab+b^{2}$ with $a=3c$ and $b=1$ (note $9c^2=(3c)^2$ and $1=1^2$).
2Apply $a^{2}-2ab+b^{2}=(a-b)^{2}$
$\therefore\; 9c^{2}-6c+1=(3c-1)^{2}$   (1A)
A perfect square trinomial always gives a single squared bracket.
3Part (b) — spot the hidden part (a)
$(4c+d)^{2}-9c^{2}+6c-1=(4c+d)^{2}-(\underbrace{9c^{2}-6c+1}_{\text{from (a)}})$
$\phantom{(4c+d)^{2}-9c^{2}+6c-1}=(4c+d)^{2}-(3c-1)^{2}$   (1M)
Factor $-1$ out of the last three terms so the bracket equals the trinomial from part (a).
4Difference of two squares $A^{2}-B^{2}=(A+B)(A-B)$
Difference of Two Squares \(A^{2}-B^{2}=(A+B)(A-B)\) With \(A=4c+d\) and \(B=3c-1\) the expression splits into the two brackets, which are then simplified.
$=\big[(4c+d)+(3c-1)\big]\big[(4c+d)-(3c-1)\big]$   (1M)
Simplify each bracket separately — the middle signs are opposite.
5Simplify each factor
$=(7c+d-1)(c+d+1)$   (1A)
Collect like terms: $4c+3c=7c$ and $4c-3c=c$; the constant becomes $\mp1$.
Answer:
(a) $\;9c^{2}-6c+1=(3c-1)^{2}$
(b) $\;(4c+d)^{2}-9c^{2}+6c-1=(7c+d-1)(c+d+1)$

Question.
(a) Factorise \(4p^{2}+12p+9\).
(b) Hence factorise \((3p-q)^{2}-4p^{2}-12p-9\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. In part (a), \(4p^{2}=(2p)^{2}\), \(9=3^{2}\) and \(12p=2(2p)(3)\), so \(4p^{2}+12p+9=(2p+3)^{2}\).
Step 2. For part (b), factor \(-1\) out of the last three terms: \((3p-q)^{2}-\left(4p^{2}+12p+9\right)=(3p-q)^{2}-(2p+3)^{2}\).
Step 3. This is a difference of two squares: \(\big[(3p-q)+(2p+3)\big]\big[(3p-q)-(2p+3)\big]\).
Step 4. Simplify each bracket: \((5p-q+3)\) and \((p-q-3)\).
Step 5. So \((3p-q)^{2}-4p^{2}-12p-9=(5p-q+3)(p-q-3)\). Check by expanding \(p=1,\ q=0\): LHS \(=9-25=-16\) and RHS \(=8\times(-2)=-16\) ✓.
Answer: (a) \((2p+3)^{2}\); (b) \((5p-q+3)(p-q-3)\)
