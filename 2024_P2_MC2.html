Question

\[ \frac{(2a)^3}{(4a^{-5})^{-1}} = \text{?} \]
A. \(2a^8\)
B. \(32a^8\)
C. \(\dfrac{2}{a^2}\)
D. \(\dfrac{32}{a^2}\)

Correct answer: D

Step-by-step solution

1

Simplify the denominator – power of a product

Rule: \((xy)^n = x^n y^n\)

Apply the exponent \(-1\) to both \(4\) and \(a^{-5}\):

\[ (4a^{-5})^{-1} = 4^{-1} \cdot (a^{-5})^{-1} \]
Hint: The outer exponent \(-1\) multiplies with the inner exponent \(-5\). So \((a^{-5})^{-1} = a^{(-5)\times(-1)} = a^{5}\).
2

Simplify the denominator – power of a power

Rule: \((x^m)^n = x^{m \times n}\)

Now evaluate \((a^{-5})^{-1}\) and rewrite \(4^{-1}\):

\[ (4a^{-5})^{-1} = 4^{-1} \cdot a^{5} = \frac{1}{4} \cdot a^5 \]
Hint: \(4^{-1} = \frac{1}{4}\). So the denominator becomes \(\frac{a^5}{4}\).
3

Simplify the numerator – power of a product

Rule: \((xy)^n = x^n y^n\)

Apply exponent \(3\) to \(2a\):

\[ (2a)^3 = 2^3 \cdot a^3 = 8a^3 \]
Hint: \(2^3 = 8\).
4

Combine numerator and denominator

Original expression becomes:

\[ \frac{8a^3}{\frac{a^5}{4}} \]
Hint: Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.
5

Apply division of indices

Rule: \(\frac{x^m}{x^n} = x^{m-n}\)

Multiply numerator by reciprocal of denominator:

\[ 8a^3 \div \frac{a^5}{4} = 8a^3 \cdot \frac{4}{a^5} = \frac{8 \cdot 4 \cdot a^3}{a^5} \]
6

Simplify the coefficient and exponent

Multiply the numbers: \(8 \times 4 = 32\).

Simplify \(a^3 / a^5 = a^{3-5} = a^{-2}\):

\[ \frac{32 \cdot a^3}{a^5} = 32 \cdot a^{3-5} = 32 \cdot a^{-2} \]
✓

Final answer in positive index form

Rule: \(x^{-n} = \frac{1}{x^n}\)

Rewrite \(32 a^{-2}\):

\[ 32 \cdot a^{-2} = \frac{32}{a^2} \]
So the answer is D. \(\frac{32}{a^2}\)
