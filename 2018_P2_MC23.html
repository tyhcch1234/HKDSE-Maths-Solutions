The figure below consists of eight identical squares. The number of folds of rotational symmetry of the figure is

\(\mathrm{A.}\ 2\quad \mathrm{B.}\ 4\quad \mathrm{C.}\ 6\quad \mathrm{D.}\ 8\)

aFind the Order of Rotational Symmetry

We need to find the smallest positive angle \(\theta\) that maps the figure onto itself, then the order \(n = \dfrac{360^\circ}{\theta}\).

Step 1
Mark the centre of rotation as \(O\), the intersection point of the diagonals of the central square. The figure is symmetric about \(O\).
Hint: A figure made of a pinwheel of squares has its centre of rotation at the exact middle.
Step 2
Rotate the figure by \(90^\circ\) counter-clockwise about \(O\). The 8 squares land exactly on the original positions.
Hint: Try \(90^\circ, 180^\circ, 270^\circ\) on the slider above — all three map the figure on itself.
Smallest angle of rotational symmetry: \(\theta = 90^\circ\)
Step 3
Order of rotational symmetry: \[ n = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta} = \frac{360^\circ}{90^\circ} = 4 \] So the figure has 4 folds of rotational symmetry.
Hint: The answer is option B.
Number of folds of rotational symmetry \(= 4\)  (Option B)

Summary of Answers

  • Centre of rotation \(O\) = intersection of the diagonals of the central square.
  • Smallest rotation angle that maps the figure onto itself: \(90^\circ\)

  • Correct option: B
