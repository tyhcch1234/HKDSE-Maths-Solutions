HKDSE 2014 · Paper 2 · Q32 Laws of Indices, Exponential & Log Functions

Exponential Graphs & Logarithmic Identities

The figure shows the graphs of \(y = b^x\) and \(y = c^x\) on the same rectangular coordinate system, where \(b\) and \(c\) are positive constants. A horizontal line \(L\) cuts the y-axis, the graph of \(y=b^x\) and the graph of \(y=c^x\) at \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\) respectively. Which of the following are true?

Statements:

  • I. \(b < c\)
  • II. \(bc > 1\)
  • III. \(\dfrac{AB}{AC} = \log_b c\)

Options:

  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III

Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Analyse the graph shapes

Both curves are upward sloping (increasing), so \(b > 1\) and \(c > 1\). Since \(y=b^x\) rises faster than \(y=c^x\), the base \(b\) is larger than \(c\).

Hint: For \(x>0\), the graph of \(y=b^x\) lies above that of \(y=c^x\). This indicates \(b > c\).

Therefore, statement I (\(b < c\)) is false.

Step 2: Evaluate statement II

Since both bases are greater than 1, \(b > 1\) and \(c > 1\). Their product must also be greater than 1.

\(b c > 1\) is true.

Step 3: Coordinate setup for A, B, C

Let the horizontal line \(L\) have equation \(y = k\) where \(k > 0\).

  • Point \(A\) lies on the y-axis: \(A = (0, k)\).
  • Point \(B\) lies on \(y = b^x\). Let its x-coordinate be \(x_B\). Then \(k = b^{x_B}\) so \(x_B = \log_b k\).
  • Point \(C\) lies on \(y = c^x\). Let its x-coordinate be \(x_C\). Then \(k = c^{x_C}\) so \(x_C = \log_c k\).

Hint: The horizontal distances \(AB\) and \(AC\) are simply the x-coordinates of B and C (since A is on y-axis).

Step 4: Express AB and AC

From the coordinates:

\(AB = x_B = \log_b k\)

\(AC = x_C = \log_c k\)

Step 5: Compute the ratio AB/AC

We have:

\(\dfrac{AB}{AC} = \dfrac{\log_b k}{\log_c k}\)

Using the change of base formula, \(\log_c k = \dfrac{\log_b k}{\log_b c}\). Therefore:

\(\dfrac{AB}{AC} = \dfrac{\log_b k}{\log_b k / \log_b c} = \log_b c\)

Thus statement III is true.

Conclusion

Statements II and III are true, while statement I is false.

Answer: C (II and III only)
