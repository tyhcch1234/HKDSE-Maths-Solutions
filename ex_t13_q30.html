Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Sine/Cosine Law

T13 Q30

Find the area of an equilateral triangle with side length $6$.

Solution

An equilateral triangle with side length $6$ has all sides equal to $6$ and each included angle equal to $60^\circ$.
Using the area formula $\text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$, take $a=b=6$ and $C=60^\circ$.
$\text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}(6)(6)\sin 60^\circ$.
Since $\sin 60^\circ=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, we get $\text{Area}=18\cdot\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}=9\sqrt{3}$.
The area of the equilateral triangle is $9\sqrt{3}$ square units.
