HKDSE 2025 · PAPER 2 · Q45 · STATISTICS

Question

It is given that $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$ are four distinct real numbers. Let $m_1$, $r_1$ and $v_1$ be the mean, the range and the variance of the group of numbers $\{a,b,c,d\}$ respectively, while $m_2$, $r_2$ and $v_2$ be the mean, the range and the variance of the group of numbers $\{2a,2b,2c,2d\}$ respectively. Denote the mean, the range and the variance of the group of numbers $\{a+3,b+3,c+3,d+3\}$ by $m_3$, $r_3$ and $v_3$ respectively. Which of the following are true?

I.  $m_1+m_3>m_2$
II.  $r_1+r_3=r_2$
III.  $v_1+v_3
A. I and II only B. I and III only C. II and III only D. I, II and III

Step-by-step Solution

SETUP Order the numbers without loss of generality: $d>c>b>a$, so $r_1=d-a$. The other sets keep the same order.
Hint: range = largest − smallest; ordering lets us write it with one symbol.
STEP 1 — MEANS $m_1=\dfrac{a+b+c+d}{4}$, i.e. $a+b+c+d=4m_1$.
$m_2=\dfrac{2a+2b+2c+2d}{4}=2\cdot\dfrac{a+b+c+d}{4}=2m_1$.
$m_3=\dfrac{(a+3)+(b+3)+(c+3)+(d+3)}{4}=m_1+3$.
Hint: adding a constant shifts the mean; multiplying by $k$ scales it.
CHECK I $m_1+m_3=m_1+(m_1+3)=2m_1+3>2m_1=m_2$. So I is true.
Hint: $3>0$ makes the left side strictly larger.
STEP 2 — RANGES $r_1=d-a$.
$r_3=(d+3)-(a+3)=d-a=r_1$.
$r_2=2d-2a=2(d-a)=2r_1$.
Hint: shifting both ends by the same constant leaves the gap unchanged.
CHECK II $r_1+r_3=r_1+r_1=2r_1=r_2$. So II is true.
Hint: doubling every value doubles the spread.
STEP 3 — VARIANCES $v_1=v_3$ (a $+3$ shift does not change spread).
$v_2=2^2v_1=4v_1$ (multiplying data by $2$ multiplies variance by $2^2$).
Hint: variance scales with the square of the multiplier.
CHECK III $v_1+v_3=v_1+v_1=2v_1<4v_1=v_2$. So III is true.
Hint: $2v_1<4v_1$ since $v_1>0$ (numbers are distinct).
CONCLUSION All three statements I, II and III are true.
Answer: D  (I, II and III)
