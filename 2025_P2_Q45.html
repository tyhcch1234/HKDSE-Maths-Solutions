Mean Range Variance Linear Transformation MC Reasoning

Question

It is given that \(a, b, c\) and \(d\) are four distinct real numbers. Let \(m_1, r_1\) and \(v_1\) be the mean, the range and the variance of the group of numbers \(\{a, b, c, d\}\) respectively while \(m_2, r_2\) and \(v_2\) be the mean, the range and the variance of the group of numbers \(\{2a, 2b, 2c, 2d\}\) respectively. Denote the mean, the range and the variance of the group of numbers \(\{a+3, b+3, c+3, d+3\}\) by \(m_3, r_3\) and \(v_3\) respectively.

Which of the following are true?

I. \(m_1+m_3>m_2\)
II. \(r_1+r_3=r_2\)
III. \(v_1+v_3

A. I and II only
B. I and III only

D. I, II and III

Hint: The mean is the sum divided by 4. Rearranging gives the total sum in terms of \(m_1\).
STEP 2
\[ m_2=\frac{2a+2b+2c+2d}{4}=\frac{2(a+b+c+d)}{4}=2m_1 \]
Hint: Multiplying every data value by \(2\) multiplies the mean by \(2\).
\(m_2 = 2m_1\)
STEP 3
\[ m_3=\frac{(a+3)+(b+3)+(c+3)+(d+3)}{4}=m_1+3 \]
Hint: Adding \(3\) to every value adds \(3\) to the mean.
\(m_3 = m_1 + 3\)
STEP 4
\[ m_1+m_3=m_1+m_1+3=2m_1+3 \] \[ 2m_1+3>2m_1=m_2 \] \[ \therefore\; m_1+m_3>m_2 \quad\checkmark\; \textbf{I is true.} \]
Hint: Since \(3>0\), \(m_1+m_3\) always exceeds \(m_2\). The statement is true for every choice of \(a,b,c,d\).
\[ \text{WLOG let } d>c>b>a \quad\Rightarrow\quad r_1=d-a \]
\[ r_2=2d-2a=2(d-a)=2r_1 \qquad r_3=(d+3)-(a+3)=d-a=r_1 \]
\[ r_1+r_3=r_1+r_1=2r_1=r_2 \] \[ \therefore\; r_1+r_3=r_2 \quad\checkmark\; \textbf{II is true.} \]
Hint: Range depends only on the largest and smallest. Scaling by \(2\) doubles it; shifting by \(3\) leaves it unchanged.
Statements I and II are both true.

Summary of Results
