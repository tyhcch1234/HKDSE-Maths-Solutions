Geometry of a Regular 8-sided Polygon — Equal Arcs, Equal Chords & Angles at the Circumference
Regular Polygon Circumcircle Equal Chords / Equal Arcs Angles at Circumference Parallel Lines

Question

\(ABCDEFGH\) is a regular 8-sided polygon (octagon). Which of the following are true?
  1. \(AG /\!/ BF\)
  2. \(BD = EG\)
  3. \(\angle CAG = 2\,\angle BDH\)
A. I and II only    B. I and III only    C. II and III only    D. I, II and III
(Answer: D) — 1 mark

Move the sliders and toggles in the diagrams below to explore why each statement holds. The regular octagon has 8 equal sides, 8 equal arcs on its circumcircle, and each arc subtends \(\dfrac{360^\circ}{8}=45^\circ\) at the centre.

Statement I — Is \(AG /\!/ BF\)?

arc \(\widehat{FG}\) = 45°
arc \(\widehat{AB}\) = 45°
\(\angle FAG\) = 22.5°
\(\angle AFB\) = 22.5°
\(\angle FAG-\angle AFB\) = 0° ⇒ parallel ✓
1 All vertices lie on one circle
Since \(ABCDEFGH\) is a regular polygon, there is a circle passing through all its vertices (the circumcircle). The whole circumference is split into 8 equal arcs:
\[ \widehat{AB}=\widehat{BC}=\widehat{CD}=\widehat{DE}=\widehat{EF}=\widehat{FG}=\widehat{GH}=\widehat{HA}=\frac{360^\circ}{8}=45^\circ \]
Hint: "Regular" means equal sides and equal interior angles \(\Rightarrow\) the 8 arcs on the circumcircle are equal. Every fact below comes from this.
2 Equal arcs \(\Rightarrow\) equal angles at the circumference
Equal chords cut off equal arcs: \(\widehat{FG}=\widehat{AB}=45^\circ\).
Angles subtended by equal arcs at the circumference are equal:
\[ \angle FAG = \angle AFB = \tfrac{1}{2}\times 45^\circ = 22.5^\circ \]
Hint (arc prop. to \(\angle\)s at circumference): an angle at the circumference equals half the arc it stands on. Both angles stand on a \(45^\circ\) arc.
3 Alternate angles equal \(\Rightarrow\) parallel
\( \angle FAG \) and \( \angle AFB \) are alternate angles formed by the transversal \(AF\) cutting lines \(AG\) and \(BF\). Since they are equal, the lines are parallel.
\[ \boxed{AG /\!/ BF} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{Statement I is TRUE} \]
Answer: I is true.

Statement II — Is \(BD = EG\)?

arc \(\widehat{BCD}\) = 135°
arc \(\widehat{EFG}\) = 135°
\(BD\) = 0.000
\(EG\) = 0.000
\(BD-EG\) = 0.000 ⇒ equal ✓
1 Every side cuts off an equal arc
From the regular octagon (see Statement I):
\[ \widehat{BC}=\widehat{CD}=\widehat{EF}=\widehat{FG}=45^\circ \]
Hint: each side of a regular octagon subtends \(45^\circ\) at the centre — this is the "equal chords, equal arcs" fact.
2 Add the arcs to compare
\[ \widehat{BCD}=\widehat{BC}+\widehat{CD}=45^\circ+45^\circ=90^\circ \] But wait — we must compare the arcs standing on the two chords: \[ \widehat{BCD}=\widehat{BC}+\widehat{CD}=90^\circ,\qquad \widehat{EFG}=\widehat{EF}+\widehat{FG}=90^\circ \] \[ \therefore \widehat{BCD}=\widehat{EFG} \]
Hint: chord \(BD\) is subtended by the arc from \(B\) to \(D\) through \(C\); chord \(EG\) by the arc from \(E\) to \(G\) through \(F\). Both arcs consist of exactly 2 sides of the octagon.
3 Equal arcs \(\Rightarrow\) equal chords
Equal arcs in the same circle are subtended by equal chords:
\[ \widehat{BCD}=\widehat{EFG} \;\Rightarrow\; \boxed{BD=EG} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{Statement II is TRUE} \]
Answer: II is true.

Statement III — Is \(\angle CAG = 2\,\angle BDH\)?

arc \(\widehat{CDEFG}\) = 225°
arc \(\widehat{BAH}\) = 112.5°
\(\angle CAG\) = 112.5°
\(\angle BDH\) = 56.25°
ratio = 2.00
1 Count the arcs on each side
The arc standing on chord/angle \(CAG\) runs from \(C\) to \(G\) through \(D,E,F\): \[ \widehat{CDEFG}=\widehat{CD}+\widehat{DE}+\widehat{EF}+\widehat{FG}=4\times 45^\circ = 180^\circ \] The arc standing on \(\angle BDH\) runs from \(B\) to \(H\) through \(C,D,E,F,G\): \[ \widehat{BAH}=\widehat{AB}+\widehat{GH}=2\times45^\circ=90^\circ \]
Hint: "arc prop. to \(\angle\)s at circumference" — an angle at the circumference equals half the arc standing on it. (Careful: the arc must be the one not containing the vertex.)
2 Apply the arc–angle relation
\[ \angle CAG = \tfrac12 \times \widehat{CDEFG} = \tfrac12 \times 180^\circ = 90^\circ \] \[ \angle BDH = \tfrac12 \times \widehat{BAH} = \tfrac12 \times 90^\circ = 45^\circ \]
Hint: check numerically in the diagram — the pink angle is twice the blue angle in every configuration.
3 Compare
\[ \frac{\angle CAG}{\angle BDH}=\frac{90^\circ}{45^\circ}=2 \;\Rightarrow\; \angle CAG = 2\,\angle BDH \]
\[ \boxed{\angle CAG = 2\,\angle BDH} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{Statement III is TRUE} \]
Answer: III is true.

Summary of Answers

  • I. \(AG /\!/ BF\) — TRUE (equal arcs \(\widehat{FG}=\widehat{AB}\) give equal alternate angles \(\angle FAG=\angle AFB=22.5^\circ\)).
  • II. \(BD = EG\) — TRUE (equal arcs \(\widehat{BCD}=\widehat{EFG}=90^\circ\) subtend equal chords).
  • III. \(\angle CAG = 2\,\angle BDH\) — TRUE (\(\angle CAG=90^\circ\) stands on the semicircular arc; \(\angle BDH=45^\circ\)).
  • Therefore the correct choice is D. I, II and III.
Answer: D
