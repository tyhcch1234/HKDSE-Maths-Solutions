bits above $2^{10}$ (block A) bits at $2^{10}$ and below (block B) final combined value
HKDSE 2019 · Paper 2 · Q33

Evaluate / express in index form:

$100110000010110_2 = $

  • A. $19\times 2^{10}+22$
  • B. $19\times 2^{10}+44$
  • C. $19\times 2^{11}+22$
  • D. $19\times 2^{11}+44$

Step-by-step Solution

1Write each bit with its power of 2

The binary string $100110000010110_2$ has 15 digits. Reading from the left, the leading digit is the $2^{14}$ place:

$$100110000010110_2 = 2^{14}+2^{11}+2^{10}+2^{4}+2^{2}+2^{1}$$

Hint: only the positions where a "1" appears contribute.
2Split off the high part ($\ge 2^{11}$)

Group the terms above $2^{10}$ together and factor out $2^{10}$:

$$2^{14}+2^{11}+2^{10} = \left(2^{4}+2^{1}+2^{0}\right)\times 2^{10}$$

Check: $2^{4}+2+1 = 16+2+1 = 19$, so this block equals $19\times 2^{10}$.

Hint: $2^{11}=2\cdot 2^{10}$ and $2^{10}=1\cdot 2^{10}$.
3Evaluate the low part ($< 2^{10}$)

The remaining bits are $2^{4}+2^{2}+2^{1}$:

$$2^{4}+2^{2}+2^{1} = 16+4+2 = 22$$

Hint: these correspond to the last three "1"s at the right of the string.
4Combine both blocks

$$\begin{aligned}100110000010110_2 &= \left(2^{4}+2^{1}+2^{0}\right)\times 2^{10}+\left(2^{4}+2^{2}+2^{1}\right)\\ &= 19\times 2^{10}+22\end{aligned}$$

Hint: this matches option A exactly.
5Check the numerical value

$$19\times 2^{10}+22 = 19\times 1024+22 = 19456+22 = 19478$$

Verify directly: $2^{14}=16384$, then $+2^{11}=2048 \to 18432$, $+2^{10}=1024 \to 19456$, $+2^{4}=16 \to 19472$, $+2^{2}=4 \to 19476$, $+2^{1}=2 \to 19478$. ✓

Hint: a quick decimal check confirms the algebra.
Answer: A  —  $100110000010110_2 = 19\times 2^{10}+22$
