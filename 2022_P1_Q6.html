Solution of (*)  (the or union) Branch 1: $x<-2$ Branch 2: $x\le-4$ Integer points

HKDSE 2022 Paper 1 — Question 6 [4 marks]

Consider the compound inequality \[ -2(3x+2) > x+10 \quad \text{or} \quad 2x \le -8 \qquad \cdots (*) \]
(a) Solve $(*)$.
(b) Write down the greatest integer satisfying $(*)$.
STEP 1 · Expand branch 1
\[ -2(3x+2) > x+10 \]
\[ -6x-4 > x+10 \]
Distribute $-2$ over the bracket: $-2\cdot 3x=-6x$ and $-2\cdot 2=-4$. Keep the inequality sign unchanged.
STEP 2 · Collect like terms (branch 1)
\[ -6x-x > 10+4 \]
\[ -7x > 14 \]
Move the $x$-terms to the left and the numbers to the right. This step earns the M mark.
STEP 3 · Divide by the negative coefficient
\[ -7x > 14 \;\Longrightarrow\; x < -2 \]
Dividing (or multiplying) by a negative number reverses the inequality sign: $>$ becomes $<$.
STEP 4 · Branch 2
\[ 2x \le -8 \]
\[ x \le -4 \]
Divide both sides by the positive $2$ — the sign stays the same.
STEP 5 · Combine with “or”
\[ x < -2 \quad \text{or} \quad x \le -4 \]
Every $x$ with $x \le -4$ already satisfies $x < -2$, so the union is simply
\[ x < -2 \]
For an “or” inequality, take the union of the two sets. Since $(-\infty,-4]\subset(-\infty,-2)$, the shorter branch is swallowed up.
STEP 6 · (b) Greatest integer
Integers just below $-2$ are $\dots,-5,-4,-3$. The open circle at $-2$ means $x\ne -2$.
Greatest integer $= -3$
$-3<-2$ ✓, but $-2$ itself does not satisfy $x<-2$ ✗.
Answer:
(a) $x<-2$   (i.e. the composite inequality reduces to $x<-2$)
(b) The greatest integer satisfying $(*)$ is $\mathbf{-3}$.
