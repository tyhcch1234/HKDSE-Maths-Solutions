Compound Inequality

Consider the compound inequality

\[ -2(3x+2) > x+10 \quad \text{or} \quad 2x \le -8 \quad \cdots (*) \]

(a) Solve \((*)\).

(b) Write down the greatest integer satisfying \((*)\).

Visualizing the solution

Drag the slider to see integer values and how they relate to the solution set.

⚠️ x = -2 does not satisfy x < -2, but it satisfies the second inequality (2x ≤ -8).

The compound inequality uses OR, so a number is a solution if it satisfies at least one part.

Step-by-step solution

1

Solve the first inequality: \(-2(3x+2) > x+10\)

\[ -2(3x+2) > x+10 \] \[ -6x - 4 > x + 10 \]
💡 Hint: Expand the left-hand side: \(-2 \cdot 3x = -6x\) and \(-2 \cdot 2 = -4\).
2

Rearrange terms to isolate \(x\)

\[ -6x - x > 10 + 4 \] \[ -7x > 14 \]
💡 Hint: Move all \(x\)-terms to one side and constants to the other. Here: subtract \(x\) from both sides, add \(4\) to both sides.
3

Divide by \(-7\) — remember to reverse the inequality sign

\[ \frac{-7x}{-7} < \frac{14}{-7} \] \[ x < -2 \]
💡 Hint: When you multiply or divide an inequality by a negative number, the inequality sign flips. Here: \(>\) becomes \(<\).
4

Solve the second inequality: \(2x \le -8\)

\[ 2x \le -8 \] \[ x \le -4 \]
💡 Hint: Divide both sides by \(2\) (positive), so the inequality sign stays the same.
5

Combine using “OR”

\[ x < -2 \quad \text{or} \quad x \le -4 \]

But \(x \le -4\) is a subset of \(x < -2\). So the combined solution is simply:

\[ x < -2 \]
💡 Hint: For “OR”, a number satisfies the compound inequality if it satisfies at least one of the inequalities. Since every number less than or equal to \(-4\) is also less than \(-2\), the condition \(x < -2\) alone already includes all those values.
b

Greatest integer satisfying \((*)\)

The solution is \(x < -2\). The integers less than \(-2\) are \(-3, -4, -5, \dots\).

The greatest among them is \(-3\).

Answer: \(-3\)

Key takeaway

  • Solve each inequality separately.
  • Remember to reverse the inequality sign when multiplying/dividing by a negative number.
  • For “OR” compound inequalities, take the union of solution sets.
  • The greatest integer strictly less than \(-2\) is \(-3\).
