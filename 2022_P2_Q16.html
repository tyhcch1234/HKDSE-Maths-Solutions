[2022MC#16] The diameter of a circle is 10 cm. The circle is divided into a major segment and a minor segment by a chord of length 8 cm. Find the area of the major segment correct to the nearest cm².

  • A. 11 cm²
  • B. 23 cm²
  • C. 55 cm²
  • D. 67 cm² ✓
Step 1 · Set up
Diameter \(=10\text{ cm}\Rightarrow\) radius \(OA=OB=\dfrac{10}{2}=5\text{ cm}\).
Chord \(AB=8\text{ cm}\). Let \(M\) be the mid-point of \(AB\).
The major segment lies on the far side of the chord from the centre O.
Step 2 · Perpendicular from centre
\(OM\perp AB\) and \(OM\) bisects \(AB\) (property of isos. \(\triangle OAB\)).
\[AM=\frac{AB}{2}=\frac{8}{2}=4\text{ cm}\] \[OM=\sqrt{OA^{2}-AM^{2}}=\sqrt{5^{2}-4^{2}}=3\text{ cm}\]
Step 3 · Half-angle at centre
\[\sin\angle AOM=\frac{AM}{OA}=\frac{4}{5}\] \[\angle AOM=\sin^{-1}\frac{4}{5}\quad(\approx 53.13^\circ)\]
\(OM\) bisects the angle \(AOB\), so \(\angle AOB=2\angle AOM\).
Step 4 · Reflex angle for the major sector
\[\angle AOB=2\sin^{-1}\frac{4}{5}\] \[\text{reflex }\angle AOB=360^\circ-2\sin^{-1}\frac{4}{5}\approx 253.74^\circ\]
Step 5 · Area of major segment
Major segment \(=\) Major sector \(OAB\) \(+\) \(\triangle OAB\) \[\begin{aligned} &=\pi OA^{2}\times\frac{360^\circ-2\sin^{-1}\frac45}{360^\circ}+\frac12\,AB\cdot OM\\[4pt] &=\pi(5)^{2}\times\frac{360^\circ-2\sin^{-1}\frac45}{360^\circ}+\frac12(8)(3) \end{aligned}\]
Step 6 · Evaluate
\[\begin{aligned} &=\pi(25)\times\frac{360^\circ-2\sin^{-1}(0.8)}{360^\circ}+12\\[3pt] &\approx 55.35743589+12\\[3pt] &\approx 67.35743589\\[3pt] &\approx 67\text{ cm}^{2} \end{aligned}\]
Answer: D — 67 cm² (correct to the nearest cm²)
