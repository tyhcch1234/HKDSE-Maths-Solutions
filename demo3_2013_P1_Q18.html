Question & solution

(a) Figure 3(a) shows a triangular paper card \(ABC\) with \(AB=28\text{ cm}\), \(BC=21\text{ cm}\), \(AC=35\text{ cm}\). \(M\) lies on \(AC\) such that \(\angle BMC=75^\circ\). Find
(i) \(\angle BCM\),
(ii) \(CM\).

(b) The card is folded along \(BM\) so that \(AB\) and \(BC\) lie on the horizontal ground, with \(\angle AMC=107^\circ\).
(i) Find the distance between \(A\) and \(C\).
(ii) \(N\) lies on \(BC\) with \(MN\perp BC\). Is the angle between face \(BCM\) and the ground equal to \(\angle ANM\)?

STEP 1 · ∠BCM (a)(i)
\[ 28^2+21^2=784+441=1225=35^2 \;\Rightarrow\; \angle ABC=90^\circ \]
\[ \sin\angle BCM=\frac{AB}{AC}=\frac{28}{35}=\frac45 \;\Rightarrow\; \angle BCM \approx 53.1^\circ \]
STEP 2 · CM (a)(ii)

In \(\triangle BCM\): \(BC=21\), \(\angle BCM=53.13^\circ\), \(\angle BMC=75^\circ\), so \(\angle CBM=51.87^\circ\).

\[ \frac{CM}{\sin 51.87^\circ}=\frac{21}{\sin 75^\circ}\;\Rightarrow\; CM=\frac{21\sin 51.87^\circ}{\sin 75^\circ}\approx 17.1\text{ cm} \]
Answer (a): \(\angle BCM\approx 53.1^\circ,\ CM\approx 17.1\text{ cm}\)
STEP 3 · Distance AC on ground (b)(i)

After folding, \(AM=35-17.1=17.9\text{ cm}\) and \(CM=17.1\text{ cm}\) stay fixed, with \(\angle AMC=107^\circ\).

\[ AC^2=17.9^2+17.1^2-2(17.9)(17.1)\cos107^\circ \]
\[ AC\approx 28.1\text{ cm} \]
Answer (b)(i): \(AC\approx 28.1\text{ cm}\)
STEP 4 · Angle between faces (b)(ii)

The angle between face \(BCM\) and the ground is measured along the line of intersection \(BC\), using two lines both \(\perp BC\).

\(MN\perp BC\) is the line in face \(BCM\), but in the ground the perpendicular to \(BC\) through \(N\) does not pass through \(A\).

So \(\angle ANM\) is not the required angle — Peter is wrong.
Answer (b)(ii): Disagree — the ground-side perpendicular to \(BC\) does not pass through \(A\).
