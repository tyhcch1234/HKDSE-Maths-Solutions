HKDSE 2018 · Paper 2 · Q8 Polynomials — Remainder Theorem

Step 1 — Given: $g(x)=x^8+ax^7+b$ and $g(x)$ divisible by $(x-1)$

Polynomial $g(x)$
Constraint $g(1)=0$
Required value $g(-1)$
Result
2018MC#8
Let $g(x)=x^8+ax^7+b$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants. If $g(x)$ is divisible by $x-1$, find the remainder when $g(x)$ is divided by $x+1$.
  • A. $0$
  • B. $2a$
  • C. $-2a$
  • D. $-2a+2$
1

Set up the given condition

$g(x)$ is divisible by $x-1$, i.e. $x-1$ is a factor of $g(x)$.

By the Factor Theorem, $g(1)=0$.

Hint: “divisible by $x-1$” means the remainder is $0$ when $x=1$.

2

Substituting $x=1$ into $g(x)$

$$g(1)=(1)^8+a(1)^7+b=1+a+b$$

Since $g(1)=0$:   $1+a+b=0$

$$\Rightarrow\ b=-a-1$$

This relation between $a$ and $b$ is the key — keep it for the final step.

3

Apply the Remainder Theorem for $x+1$

The remainder when $g(x)$ is divided by $x+1$ is the value of $g$ at the root of $x+1=0$, i.e. $x=-1$.

$$\text{Remainder}=g(-1)$$

Remainder Theorem: dividing $g(x)$ by $x-c$ gives remainder $g(c)$.

4

Evaluate $g(-1)$

$$g(-1)=(-1)^8+a(-1)^7+b$$

$$=1-a+b$$

Note that $8$ is even and $7$ is odd, so $(-1)^8=1$ and $(-1)^7=-1$.

Careful with signs — the exponents determine the signs.

5

Substitute $b=-a-1$ and simplify

$$g(-1)=1-a+(-a-1)$$

$$=1-a-a-1$$

$$=-2a$$

Here $b$ is eliminated, giving an answer purely in terms of $a$.

ANSWER The remainder is $-2a$  →  choice C.
