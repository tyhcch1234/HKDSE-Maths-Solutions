The graph in the figure shows the linear relation between \(y^3\) and \(\sqrt{x}\). If \(x=36\), then \(y=\)

A.  \(-64\)
B.  \(-16\)
C.  \(-8\)
D.  \(-4\)  ✓

Step 11M

The graph is a straight line of \(y^3\) against \(\sqrt{x}\). Read two clear points from the graph:

\[ (0,\;32) \quad\text{and}\quad (2,\;0) \]
Hint: The vertical intercept is \(32\), and the line cuts the horizontal axis at \(\sqrt{x}=2\).
Step 21M

Compute the slope between these two points:

\[ m=\frac{0-32}{2-0}=\frac{-32}{2}=-16 \]
Hint: Slope \(=\dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}\). The graph falls, so the slope is negative.
Slope of the graph \(= -16\)
Step 31A

Using the slope-intercept form of a straight line equation (i.e. \(Y=mX+c\)), with \(Y=y^3\) and \(X=\sqrt{x}\):

\[ y^3 = -16\sqrt{x} + 32 \]
Hint: This is the relation connecting \(y^3\) and \(\sqrt{x}\) — it is exactly what the graph shows.
Relation: \(y^3 = -16\sqrt{x} + 32\)

Step 11M

When \(x=36\), substitute into \(y^3 = -16\sqrt{x} + 32\):

\[ y^3 = -16\sqrt{36} + 32 \]
Hint: \(\sqrt{36}=6\) since \(6\times 6 = 36\).
Step 21M

Evaluate:

\[ y^3 = -16(6)+32 = -96+32 = -64 \]
Hint: A negative right-hand side means \(y^3\) is negative, so \(y\) must be negative.
Step 31A

Take the cube root of both sides:

\[ y = \sqrt[3]{-64} = -4 \]
Hint: \((-4)^3 = -64\), so the cube root of \(-64\) is \(-4\).
Answer: \(y = -4\)  →  Option D

Summary of Answers

  • Relation   \(y^3 = -16\sqrt{x} + 32\)
  • Slope   \(m = -16\),   \(y^3\)-intercept \(= 32\)
  • At \(x=36\)   \(y^3 = -16\sqrt{36}+32 = -64\)
  • Cube root   \(y = \sqrt[3]{-64} = -4\)
  • Answer   D. \(-4\)
