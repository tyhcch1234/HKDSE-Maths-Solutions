T1 Q04

\$P is deposited at 5% p.a. compound interest, compounded yearly. After 2 years the amount is \$1,102.5. Find P.

Solution

The compound interest formula is $A = P(1+r)^t$.
Substitute $A = 1102.5$, $r = 0.05$ and $t = 2$: $1102.5 = P(1+0.05)^2$.
Simplify: $1102.5 = P(1.05)^2 = P(1.1025)$.
Solve for $P$: $P = \frac{1102.5}{1.1025} = 1000$.
Check: $1000(1.05)^2 = 1000(1.1025) = 1102.5$, so the value is correct.
$P = 1000$.
