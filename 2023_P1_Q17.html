Question 17

(a) It is given that $WXY$ is a triangle, where $WX = 6$ cm, $XY = 5$ cm and $\angle WYX = 70^\circ$. Find $\angle XWY$. [2 marks]

(b) Figure 3 shows the pyramid $WXYZ$, where $WZ = XZ = YZ$. The base of this pyramid is the triangle $WXY$ described in (a).

It is given that the angle between $WZ$ and the triangle $WXY$ is $30^\circ$. Does the angle between the triangles $WXY$ and $XYZ$ exceed $45^\circ$? Explain your answer. [4 marks]

STEP 1 · (a) Sine rule

$$\frac{\sin\angle XWY}{XY}=\frac{\sin\angle WYX}{WX}$$

$$\frac{\sin\angle XWY}{5}=\frac{\sin 70^\circ}{6}$$

$$\angle XWY \approx 51.54318937^\circ\ \text{(or }128.4568106^\circ\text{ rejected)}$$

$$\boxed{\angle XWY \approx 51.5^\circ}$$

Reject $128.5^\circ$ since the other angle ($128.46^\circ + 70^\circ > 180^\circ$).
STEP 2 · (b) Locate the foot & centre

Let $P$ be the projection of $Z$ on the plane $WXY$. Since $WZ=XZ=YZ$:

$$PW=PX=PY$$

So $P$ is the circumcentre of $\triangle WXY$.

Denote the mid-point of $XY$ by $M$. Then $PM\perp XY$ and $ZM\perp XY$.

The angle between planes $WXY$ and $XYZ$ is $\angle PMZ$.

STEP 3 · Relate $MP$ to $PW$

Since $P$ is on the perpendicular bisector of $XY$ through $M$:

$$MP = PX\cos\angle MPX = PW\cos\angle XWY$$

because $\angle MPX=\tfrac12\angle XPY=\angle XWY$.

STEP 4 · Height $PZ$ from the $30^\circ$ angle

The angle between line $WZ$ and the plane $WXY$ is $\angle PWZ = 30^\circ$:

$$\tan 30^\circ=\frac{PZ}{PW}\ \Rightarrow\ PZ = PW\tan 30^\circ$$

STEP 5 · Compute $\tan\angle PMZ$

$$\tan\angle PMZ=\frac{PZ}{MP}=\frac{PW\tan 30^\circ}{PW\cos\angle XWY}=\frac{\tan 30^\circ}{\cos\angle XWY}$$

$$\approx \frac{\tan 30^\circ}{\cos 51.54318937^\circ}\approx 0.928328501$$

STEP 6 · Conclusion

Since $\tan\angle PMZ \approx 0.928 < 1$, we have $\angle PMZ < 45^\circ$.

The angle between the triangles $WXY$ and $XYZ$ does NOT exceed $45^\circ$. ✔
