Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Equation of Straight Line

T12 Q28

Rewrite the equation \(2x+3y=6\) in slope-intercept form.

Solution

Start with $2x+3y=6$.
Subtract $2x$ from both sides: $3y=-2x+6$.
Divide every term by $3$: $y=-\frac{2}{3}x+2$.
Check: substituting $y=-\frac{2}{3}x+2$ into $3y$ gives $3\left(-\frac{2}{3}x+2\right)=-2x+6$, so $2x+3y=6$ is recovered.
The slope-intercept form is $y=-\frac{2}{3}x+2$.
