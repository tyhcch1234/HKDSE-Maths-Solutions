Laws of Indices Logarithmic Functions Quadratic Equation Change of Variable

Q Question

If   $$\frac{3}{3\log x - 2} + 7 \;=\; \frac{2}{2\log x + 1}\,,$$ then   $\log\dfrac{1}{x} =$

💡 Step-by-Step Guided Solution

The expression repeats $\log x$ many times. Let a new variable carry it:

$$t = \log x$$
Hint: Substitution turns a messy log‑fraction equation into an ordinary algebraic one. Once we solve for $t$, we convert back with $\log x = t$.

Replacing every $\log x$ by $t$:

$$\frac{3}{3t-2} + 7 \;=\; \frac{2}{2t+1}$$
Hint: Denominators $3t-2$ and $2t+1$ cannot be zero, so $t \ne \frac{2}{3}$ and $t \ne -\frac{1}{2}$.

Multiply both sides by the common denominator $(3t-2)(2t+1)$:

$$3(2t+1) + 7(3t-2)(2t+1) = 2(3t-2)$$
Hint: Expand $(3t-2)(2t+1) = 6t^2 - t - 2$. Then every term becomes a polynomial in $t$.

Expand the middle term:

$$3(2t+1) + 7(6t^2 - t - 2) = 2(3t-2)$$

Distribute:

$$6t + 3 + 42t^2 - 7t - 14 = 6t - 4$$

Collect like terms and bring everything to one side:

$$42t^2 - 7t - 7 = 0$$

Divide throughout by $7$:

$$6t^2 - t - 1 = 0$$

Factorise $6t^2 - t - 1 = 0$ by splitting the middle term:

$$6t^2 - 3t + 2t - 1 = 0$$ $$3t(2t - 1) + 1(2t - 1) = 0$$ $$(2t - 1)(3t + 1) = 0$$

So:

$$t = \frac{1}{2} \quad \text{or} \quad t = -\frac{1}{3}$$
Hint: These are the two candidate values of $\log x$. Now we must convert them to $\log\frac{1}{x}$.

Use the quotient / negative‑exponent law:

$$\log\frac{1}{x} = \log x^{-1} = -\log x = -t$$
Hint: So whenever we find $t = \log x$, the answer is simply $-t$. No need to find $x$ itself.

If $t = \tfrac{1}{2}$:   $\log\frac{1}{x} = -\tfrac{1}{2}$

If $t = -\tfrac{1}{3}$:   $\log\frac{1}{x} = \tfrac{1}{3}$

$$\log\frac{1}{x} = -\frac{1}{2} \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{1}{3}$$

∴ Correct option: D

🔑 Key Take‑aways

  • Substituting $t=\log x$ converts a log‑fraction equation into a plain quadratic equation.
  • Always clear denominators by multiplying by the common denominator, then expand and simplify.
  • Remember the log law: $\log\frac{1}{x} = -\log x$ — this is the final step that distinguishes the answer choices.
