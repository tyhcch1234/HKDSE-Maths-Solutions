[2018 MC #41]
In the figure, \(ABCDEFGH\) is a rectangular block. Let \(X\) be a point lying on \(DE\) such that \(DX=9\text{ cm}\) and \(EX=4\text{ cm}\). Denote the angle between \(BX\) and the plane \(ABGF\) by \(\theta\). Find \(\cos\theta\).
Step 1 · Set up the block

The block measures \(AB=12\text{ cm}\) (along the plane), \(BC=8\text{ cm}\) (depth) and the vertical edge \(AD=13\text{ cm}\). Point \(X\) lies on \(DE\) with \(DX=9\text{ cm}\) and \(EX=4\text{ cm}\), so \(DE=13\text{ cm}\) matches the height.

Key idea: the angle between a line and a plane is measured between the line and its perpendicular projection onto the plane.

Step 2 · Drop the perpendicular

Let \(Y\) be the foot of the perpendicular from \(X\) to the plane \(ABGF\).

Since \(AD\) is perpendicular to the plane, \(AY = DX = 9\text{ cm}\) and \(XY = BC = 8\text{ cm}\) (the depth of the block). Then \(ADXY\) is a rectangle, so \(XY\perp BY\).

Visual check: the purple segment \(XY\) is vertical, exactly the depth \(8\text{ cm}\) of the block.
Step 3 · Find \(BY\) in \(\triangle ABY\)

\(\angle BAY=90^\circ\), \(AB=12\) and \(AY=9\):

\[BY=\sqrt{AB^2+AY^2}=\sqrt{12^2+9^2}=\sqrt{225}=15\text{ cm}\]

The angle between \(BX\) and the plane is \(\theta=\angle XBY\).

Step 4 · Find \(BX\) in \(\triangle BXY\)

Since \(XY\perp BY\), triangle \(BXY\) is right-angled at \(Y\):

\[BX=\sqrt{BY^2+XY^2}=\sqrt{15^2+8^2}=\sqrt{289}=17\text{ cm}\]

Step 5 · Evaluate \(\cos\theta\)

In right triangle \(BXY\), with \(\theta=\angle XBY\):

\[\cos\theta=\frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}=\frac{BY}{BX}=\frac{15}{17}\]

A\(\tfrac35\)   B\(\tfrac45\)   C\(\tfrac{8}{17}\)   D\(\tfrac{15}{17}\)

FINAL ANSWER
\(\cos\theta = \dfrac{15}{17}\)   (Option D)
