Similar Triangles

Question

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a rectangle. Let \(E\) be a point on \(BC\). The extension of \(AB\) and the extension of \(DE\) meet at point \(F\). If \(AB=3\) cm, \(BE=12\) cm and \(CE=4\) cm, then \(EF=\)

  • A. \(13\) cm
  • B. \(15\) cm
  • C. \(16\) cm
  • D. \(20\) cm

In the figure, \(AB\) is the right-hand short side, \(BC\) the long top side with \(E\) on it, and \(F\) lies above \(B\) where \(AB\) and \(DE\) are extended.

Solution

Find \(AD\)

Opposite sides of a rectangle are equal, so

\[ AD=BC=BE+CE=12+4=16\text{ cm} \]
Similar triangles

Since \(BE\parallel AD\), \(\triangle FBE\sim\triangle FAD\). Hence

\[ \frac{FB}{FA}=\frac{BE}{AD}=\frac{12}{16}=\frac{3}{4} \]

With \(FA=FB+AB=FB+3\):

\[ \frac{FB}{FB+3}=\frac{3}{4}\ \Rightarrow\ 4FB=3FB+9\ \Rightarrow\ FB=9\text{ cm} \]
Apply Pythagoras in \(\triangle FBE\)

\(\angle FBE=90^\circ\), so

\[ EF=\sqrt{BE^2+FB^2}=\sqrt{12^2+9^2}=\sqrt{144+81}=\sqrt{225}=15\text{ cm} \]
Final Answer: B
