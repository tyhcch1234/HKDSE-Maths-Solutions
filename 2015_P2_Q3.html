Step 1 — the two given equations.

HKDSE 2015 · Paper 2 · Q3

Simultaneous linear equations in $p$ and $q$

If \[ \begin{cases} p+3q=4 \\ 5p+9q=2 \end{cases} \] then $p=$

  • A $-5$
  • B $-3$
  • C $3$
  • D $5$

Step-by-step solution

1

Read off the given information

Label the two equations:

\[p+3q=4 \qquad \cdots (1)\]
\[5p+9q=2 \qquad \cdots (2)\]

Two equations, two unknowns — a standard simultaneous-equation problem. Only $p$ is asked for, so eliminate $q$.

2

Match the coefficient of $q$

Multiply equation (1) by $3$ so that the $q$-terms in both equations have the same coefficient $9$:

\[(1)\times 3:\quad 3p+9q=12 \qquad \cdots (1')\]

Now both equations contain $9q$, so subtracting removes $q$ completely.

3

Eliminate $q$: compute $(2)-(1)\times 3$

Subtract $(1')$ from $(2)$, term by term:

\[(5p-3p)+(9q-9q)=2-12\]
\[2p=-10\]

Why it works: the $9q$ terms cancel, leaving a single linear equation in $p$ alone.

4

Solve for $p$

\[2p=-10\]
\[p=\frac{-10}{2}=-5\]

Divide both sides by the coefficient of $p$, i.e. by $2$.

5

Check and conclude

Substitute $p=-5$ into $(1)$ to find $q$, then verify $(2)$:

\[(-5)+3q=4 \;\Rightarrow\; 3q=9 \;\Rightarrow\; q=3\]
\[\text{Check in }(2):\; 5(-5)+9(3)=-25+27=2 \;\checkmark\]

Both equations are satisfied, so $p=-5$ is correct.

Answer
$p = -5$  →  Option A
