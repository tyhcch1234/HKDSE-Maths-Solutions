HKDSE 2026 · Paper 2 · MC29

Box-and-Whisker Diagram

Question

Which of the following can be obtained from any box-and-whisker diagram?

I. Mean
II. Lower quartile
III. Inter-quartile range

  • A. I only
  • B. II only
  • C. I and III only
  • D. II and III only

Solution

What it shows A box-and-whisker diagram gives the minimum, lower quartile \(Q_1\), median, upper quartile \(Q_3\) and maximum.
Read off The lower quartile (II) is shown directly, and IQR \(=Q_3-Q_1\) (III) follows.
Mean The mean (I) cannot be determined from the diagram.
Conclusion Hence II and III only.
Answer: D — II and III only
