Question [2020MC#9]

Q. The cost of a toy is $x\%$ lower than its selling price. After selling the toy, the percentage profit is $25\%$. Find $x$.

A. 20
B. 25
C. 75
D. 80

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1Let the selling price be $S$.

Then "$x\%$ lower than its selling price" means the cost is $S\,(1-x\%)$.

Always introduce a symbol for the unknown base value.
Percentage Decrease\(\text{cost}=S\,(1-x\%)\)The cost is \(x\%\) lower than the selling price \(S\), so it equals \(S(1-x\%)\).
STEP 2Write the profit in money terms.

Percentage profit $= \dfrac{\text{Profit}}{\text{Cost}}\times 100\%$.

A profit of $25\%$ means the selling price equals the cost multiplied by $1+25\%$:

$$S = S\,(1-x\%)\times(1+25\%).$$

Profit% is measured on the cost, not on the selling price.
Percentage Profit\(S=\text{cost}\times(1+25\%)\)A \(25\%\) profit is measured on the cost, so the selling price is \(1.25\) times the cost.
STEP 3Cancel $S$ (assuming $S\ne 0$) and solve.

$$1 = (1-x\%)\times 1.25$$

$$1-x\% = \frac{1}{1.25} = 0.8$$

$$x\% = 1-0.8 = 0.2$$

$$x = 20.$$

Divide both sides by the factor $1.25$ to isolate $1-x\%$.
Reverse Percentage\(1-x\%=\dfrac{1}{1.25}=0.8\)Dividing by the multiplier \(1.25\) undoes the \(25\%\) profit and gives \(x\%=20\%\).
STEP 4Check.

If $S=100$, cost $=100\times(1-20\%)=80$, and $80\times1.25=100$ ✓. So $x=20$.

Substituting a convenient value of $S$ verifies the answer.
Answer: $x = 20$  →  Option A

Question. The cost of an item is \(x\%\) lower than its selling price, and the percentage profit is \(60\%\). Find \(x\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let the selling price be \(S\). Then the cost is \(S(1-x\%)\).
Step 2. Profit measured on the cost: \(S=S(1-x\%)\times(1+60\%)\).
Step 3. Cancel \(S\neq0\): \(1=(1-x\%)\times1.6\), so \(1-x\%=0.625\).
Step 4. Hence \(x\%=0.375\), i.e. \(x=37.5\). Check: cost \(=62.5\), and \(62.5\times1.6=100\) ✓.
Answer: \(x=37.5\)
