T11 Q22

In the figure, \(PQ\) is a tangent to the circle with centre \(O\) at the point \(T\). If \(\angle OPT = 40^\circ\), find \(\angle POT\).

Solution

Since $PQ$ is tangent to the circle at $T$, $OT \perp PT$.
Thus $\angle OTP = 90^\circ$.
In $\triangle OPT$, the angles sum to $180^\circ$: $\angle POT + 90^\circ + 40^\circ = 180^\circ$.
Therefore $\angle POT = 50^\circ$.
Thus, $\angle POT = 50^\circ$.
