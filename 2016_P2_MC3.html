HKDSE Math 2016 Paper 2 Q3 Polynomials

Factorise the following expression:

\(16 - (2x - 3y)^2 =\)

🔍 Visual Check: Difference of Squares

Move the slider to change the value of \( x \) (with \( y = 1 \)). See how the original expression and the factored form give the same value.

✅ Both forms match!

📘 Step-by-Step Solution

1

Recognise the structure

The expression is of the form \( A^2 - B^2 \) with:
  • \( A^2 = 16 \), so \( A = 4 \)
  • \( B^2 = (2x - 3y)^2 \), so \( B = 2x - 3y \)
Hint: Difference of Squares: \( \color{indigo}{A^2 - B^2 = (A-B)(A+B)} \)
2

Apply the difference of squares formula

Substitute \( A = 4 \) and \( B = 2x - 3y \) into the formula: \[ 16 - (2x - 3y)^2 = [4 - (2x - 3y)] [4 + (2x - 3y)] \]
Hint: Think of \( (2x - 3y) \) as a single block. Keep the brackets to avoid sign mistakes.
3

Simplify each bracket

Remove the inner brackets carefully: \[ = (4 - 2x + 3y)(4 + 2x - 3y) \]
Hint: Distributing the minus sign: \( - (2x - 3y) = -2x + 3y \)
4

Match with the options

The final factored form is: \[ (4 - 2x + 3y)(4 + 2x - 3y) \] This matches option D.
