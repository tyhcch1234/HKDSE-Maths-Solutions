HKDSE 2019 · Paper 2 · Q15
The base of a solid right pyramid is a square of side 18 cm. If the height of the pyramid is 12 cm, then the total surface area of the pyramid is
A. 432 cm²
B. 540 cm²
C. 756 cm²
D. 864 cm²
Step-by-step solution

Step 1 · Identify the parts of the pyramid

The square base has side $18\text{ cm}$;
the vertical height is $12\text{ cm}$.

The total surface area = base area + areas of the 4 congruent triangular lateral faces.

We need the slant height (height of a triangular face), not the vertical height.

Step 2 · Find the slant height

Pythagoras' Theorem \(VM^{2}=VO^{2}+OM^{2}\) \(VO=12\) and \(OM=9\) give the slant height \(VM=\sqrt{9^{2}+12^{2}}=15\text{ cm}\).

Drop the height from the apex $V$ to the centre $O$ of the base,
then go from $O$ to the mid-point $M$ of a base edge.

Since $OM$ is half the base side,

$$OM=\frac{18}{2}=9\text{ cm}$$

Triangle $VOM$ is right-angled at $O$,
so with $VO=12$:

$$VM=\sqrt{OM^{2}+VO^{2}}=\sqrt{9^{2}+12^{2}}=\sqrt{225}=15\text{ cm}$$

Step 3 · Area of one lateral face

Area of a Triangle \(\text{Area}=\dfrac{1}{2}\times\text{base}\times\text{height}\) Each lateral face has base \(18\text{ cm}\) and slant height \(15\text{ cm}\), giving \(135\text{ cm}^{2}\).

Each lateral face is a triangle with base $18\text{ cm}$ and height $VM=15\text{ cm}$:

$$\text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}\times 18\times 15 = 135\text{ cm}^{2}$$

Step 4 · Total surface area

Total Surface Area of a Pyramid \(A=\text{base area}+4\times\text{lateral face area}\) Adding the square base \(324\) to four faces of \(135\) gives \(864\text{ cm}^{2}\).

Base area $=18^{2}=324\text{ cm}^{2}$.

Four lateral faces $=4\times 135 = 540\text{ cm}^{2}$.

$$\text{Total S.A.}=324+540=864\text{ cm}^{2}$$

Check: $18^{2}+4\cdot\frac{1}{2}(18)(15)=864$ ✓
Answer: D. 864 cm²

Question. The base of a solid right pyramid is a square of side \(16\text{ cm}\), and its height is \(15\text{ cm}\). Find the total surface area of the pyramid.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \(V\) be the apex, \(O\) the centre of the base and \(M\) the mid-point of a base edge.
Then \(OM=\dfrac{16}{2}=8\text{ cm}\).
Step 2. By Pythagoras' Theorem, the slant height is \(VM=\sqrt{8^{2}+15^{2}}=\sqrt{289}=17\text{ cm}\).
Step 3. Area of one lateral face \(=\dfrac{1}{2}\times 16\times 17=136\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Step 4. Base area \(=16^{2}=256\text{ cm}^{2}\), so total surface area \(=256+4(136)=800\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Answer: \(800\text{ cm}^{2}\)
