Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Sine/Cosine Law

T13 Q27

Find the area of a triangle with two sides of lengths \(10\) and \(12\) and an included angle of \(30^\circ\).

Solution

Use the area formula with two sides and the included angle: $A=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$.
Substitute $a=10$, $b=12$, and $C=30^\circ$: $A=\frac{1}{2}(10)(12)\sin 30^\circ$.
Since $\sin 30^\circ=\frac{1}{2}$, we get $A=\frac{1}{2}(10)(12)\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=30$.
The area of the triangle is $30$ square units.
