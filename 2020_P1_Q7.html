HKDSE Math 2020 Paper 1 Q7

Quadratic Equations Discriminant Equal Roots x-intercepts
Question

Let \( p(x) = 4x^2 + 12x + c \), where \( c \) is a constant. The equation \( p(x) = 0 \) has equal roots.

Find

  1. \( c \),
  2. the \( x \)-intercept(s) of the graph of \( y = p(x) - 169 \). [5 marks]

Step-by-step Solution

Since \( p(x) = 0 \) has equal roots, the discriminant \( \Delta = 0 \).

\[ \Delta = b^2 - 4ac = 0 \]
💡 Hint For \( ax^2 + bx + c = 0 \):
\( a = 4,\; b = 12,\; c = \text{?} \)
\[ \begin{aligned} 12^2 - 4(4)(c) &= 0 \\ 144 - 16c &= 0 \\ 16c &= 144 \\ c &= 9 \end{aligned} \]

So the answer to part (a) is \( c = 9 \).

✔ Check: \( p(x) = 4x^2 + 12x + 9 = (2x + 3)^2 \). Equal root \( x = -1.5 \).

Set \( y = 0 \) for \( x \)-intercepts:

\[ \begin{aligned} p(x) - 169 &= 0 \\ 4x^2 + 12x + 9 - 169 &= 0 \\ 4x^2 + 12x - 160 &= 0 \end{aligned} \]
💡 Hint Divide by 4 to simplify: \( x^2 + 3x - 40 = 0 \)
\[ \begin{aligned} 4x^2 + 12x - 160 &= 0 \\ 4(x^2 + 3x - 40) &= 0 \\ 4(x + 8)(x - 5) &= 0 \end{aligned} \]

Therefore,

\( x = -8 \) or \( x = 5 \)

So the \( x \)-intercepts are \( -8 \) and \( 5 \).

Visual Check: Graph of \( y = p(x) - 169 \)

The curve crosses the \(x\)-axis at \( x = -8 \) and \( x = 5 \).

Final Answers

(a) \( c = 9 \)
(b) \( x = -8 \) and \( x = 5 \)
