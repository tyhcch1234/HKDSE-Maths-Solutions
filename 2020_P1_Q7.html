HKDSE 2020 · Paper 1 · Q7 [5 marks]

Question

Let \(p(x)=4x^{2}+12x+c\), where \(c\) is a constant. The equation \(p(x)=0\) has equal roots.

Find
(a) \(c\),
(b) the \(x\)-intercept(s) of the graph of \(y=p(x)-169\).

1(a) Condition for equal roots

The equation \(p(x)=0\), i.e. \(4x^{2}+12x+c=0\), has equal roots, so its discriminant is zero:

\[ \Delta = 0 \]

Hint: For \(ax^{2}+bx+c=0\), equal roots \(\iff \Delta=b^{2}-4ac=0\).
Discriminant \(\text{equal roots}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Delta=b^{2}-4ac=0\) The equation \(4x^{2}+12x+c=0\) has equal roots, so \(12^{2}-4(4)c=0\).
2(a) Solve for c

\[ 12^{2}-4(4)c = 0 \]

\[ 144-16c = 0 \]

\[ c = 9 \]

Hint: Substitute \(a=4,\;b=12\) into \(\Delta=b^{2}-4ac\) and solve for \(c\).
Solving a Linear Equation \(144-16c=0\ \Longrightarrow\ c=9\) The discriminant condition is linear in the unknown \(c\).
3(b) Form the new function

Using \(c=9\) from part (a):

\[ y = p(x)-169 = 4x^{2}+12x+9-169 \]

\[ y = 4x^{2}+12x-160 \]

Hint: Subtract 169 from the whole of \(p(x)\).
4(b) Factorise

\[ y = 4(x^{2}+3x-40) \]

\[ y = 4(x+8)(x-5) \]

Hint: Factor out \(4\), then factorise \(x^{2}+3x-40\): two numbers with product \(-40\) and sum \(3\) are \(8\) and \(-5\).
Cross Method \(x^{2}+(p+q)x+pq=(x+p)(x+q)\) For \(x^{2}+3x-40\) the pair \(8\) and \(-5\) has sum \(3\) and product \(-40\).
5(b) Find the x-intercepts

Set \(y=0\):

\[ 4(x+8)(x-5)=0 \]

\[ x=-8 \quad\text{or}\quad x=5 \]

Hence the \(x\)-intercepts of the graph of \(y=p(x)-169\) are \(-8\) and \(5\).

Hint: An \(x\)-intercept occurs where \(y=0\).
Answer: (a) \(c=9\)   |   (b) \(x=-8\) and \(x=5\)

Question. It is given that \(p(x)=3x^{2}+18x+c\) and the equation \(p(x)=0\) has equal roots. (a) Find \(c\). (b) Find the \(x\)-intercepts of the graph of \(y=p(x)-75\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. (a) Equal roots means \(\Delta=0\): \(18^{2}-4(3)c=0\), i.e. \(324-12c=0\).
Step 2. (a) Hence \(c=27\), so \(p(x)=3x^{2}+18x+27\).
Step 3. (b) \(y=p(x)-75=3x^{2}+18x+27-75=3x^{2}+18x-48=3\left(x^{2}+6x-16\right)\).
Step 4. (b) \(x^{2}+6x-16=(x+8)(x-2)\), so \(y=3(x+8)(x-2)\) and the \(x\)-intercepts are \(x=-8\) and \(x=2\).
Answer: (a) \(c=27\) (b) \(x=-8\) and \(x=2\)
