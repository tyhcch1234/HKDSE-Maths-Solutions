T2 Q05

Convert $72\ \text{km/h}$ to $\text{m/s}$.

Solution

Use $1\ \text{km}=1000\ \text{m}$ and $1\ \text{h}=3600\ \text{s}$.
Then $72\ \text{km/h}=72\times\frac{1000\ \text{m}}{3600\ \text{s}}$.
Simplify: $72\times\frac{1000}{3600}=72\times\frac{5}{18}=4\times 5=20$.
Check: $20\ \text{m/s}\times 3600\ \text{s/h}\div 1000\ \text{m/km}=72\ \text{km/h}$, so the conversion is correct.
The final answer is $20\ \text{m/s}$.
