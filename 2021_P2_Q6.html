HKDSE 2021 · Paper 2 · Q6

2. Rate, Ratio and Variations

[2021MC#6]   If $a$, $b$ and $c$ are non-zero constants such that $$a(x+3)+b(3x+1)\equiv c(x+2),$$ then $a:b=$

  • A.  $1:3$
  • B.  $1:5$
  • C.  $3:1$
  • D.  $5:1$  
Key idea: An identity holds for every value of $x$. Either substitute a clever value of $x$, or expand and compare coefficients term by term.
1Write down the given identity.

$$a(x+3)+b(3x+1)\equiv c(x+2)$$

Both sides are linear in $x$,
and “$\equiv$” means they are equal for all real $x$.

2Method 1 — Substitute $x=-2$ (kill the right side).
Identity (Substitution Method) \(f(x)\equiv g(x)\ \Rightarrow\ f(a)=g(a)\ \text{for every }a\) Taking \(x=-2\) kills the right-hand side, so \(a-5b=0\) at once.

At $x=-2$ the factor $(x+2)$ becomes $0$, so the RHS vanishes:

\begin{aligned} a(-2+3)+b(3(-2)+1)&
=c(-2+2)\\ a(1)+b(-5)&
=0\end{aligned}

\[ a-5b=0 \]

Choosing $x=-2$ makes $c$ disappear — that is the trick of substitution.

3Solve for the ratio.

\[ a=5b \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{a}{b}=5 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a:b=5:1 \]

Since $b\neq 0$, dividing is valid.
Note $5:1$, not $1:5$.

4Method 2 — Expand and compare coefficients.
Equating Coefficients \(Ax+B\equiv Cx+D\ \Rightarrow\ A=C,\ B=D\) Matching \((a+3b)x+(3a+b)\) with \(cx+2c\) gives \(a+3b=c\) and \(3a+b=2c\).

\[ a(x+3)+b(3x+1)=(a+3b)x+(3a+b) \]

Matching with $cx+2c$:

\begin{cases} a+3b = c &\quad(1)\\[2pt] 3a+b = 2c &\quad(2)\end{cases}

Coefficients of $x$ must agree; constant terms must agree — for an identity.

5Eliminate $c$.

Compute $(2)-(1)\times 2$:

\begin{aligned}(3a+b)-2(a+3b)&
=2c-2c\\ 3a+b-2a-6b&
=0\\ a-5b&
=0\end{aligned}

\[ a=5b \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{a}{b}=5 \]

Same result from a completely different route — a good consistency check.

6Read off the requested ratio.

\[ \frac{a}{b}=5 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a:b=5:1 \]

“$a:b$” means the value of $a$ first.
So the answer is option D.

Answer:  $a:b = 5:1$  →  Option D

Question. If \(a\), \(b\) and \(c\) are non-zero constants such that \(a(x+5)+b(3x+2)\equiv c(x+1)\), then \(a:b=\)
A. \(1:3\)    B. \(1:4\)    C. \(4:1\)    D. \(5:1\)  (1 mark)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Put \(x=-1\) so that the right-hand side vanishes: \(a(4)+b(-1)=0\),
i.e. \(4a=b\).
Step 2. Since \(b\ne0\), \(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{1}{4}\), so \(a:b=1:4\).
Step 3. Check by comparing coefficients: LHS \(=(a+3b)x+(5a+2b)\), so \(c=a+3b\) and \(c=5a+2b\);
these agree exactly when \(4a=b\). Option B.
Answer: B. \(1:4\)
