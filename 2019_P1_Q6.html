HKDSE Math 2019 Paper 1 Q6

Inequalities & Integer Counting

#inequalities #integer solutions #linear
Q

(a) Solve the inequality \(\dfrac{7x+26}{4} \le 2(3x-1)\).

(b) Find the number of integers satisfying both inequalities \(\dfrac{7x+26}{4} \le 2(3x-1)\) and \(45 - 5x \ge 0\).

[4 marks]

🔍 Step-by-step solution

Step 1

Multiply both sides by 4 to clear the fraction.

\[ \frac{7x+26}{4} \le 2(3x-1) \quad \xrightarrow{\times 4} \quad 7x+26 \le 8(3x-1) \]

✨ Multiplying by a positive number keeps the inequality sign unchanged.

Hint: \(2(3x-1) \times 4 = 8(3x-1)\)
Step 2

Expand the right side.

\[ 7x+26 \le 24x - 8 \]

Expand: \(8(3x-1) = 24x - 8\).

Step 3

Collect like terms.

\[ \begin{aligned} 7x + 26 &\le 24x - 8 \\ 7x - 24x &\le -8 - 26 \\ -17x &\le -34 \end{aligned} \]

Move \(24x\) to the left, constants to the right.

⚠️ Remember: Subtracting \(24x\) from both sides is allowed — no sign flip yet.
Step 4

Divide by \(-17\) and reverse the inequality sign.

\[ \begin{aligned} -17x &\le -34 \\ x &\ge 2 \end{aligned} \]

Dividing by a negative number flips the inequality.

🚨 Critical: \(\frac{-34}{-17} = 2\), and the inequality becomes \(\ge\).

🧮 Part (b) — Interactive number line

From (a), we have \(x \ge 2\). The second inequality gives:

\[ 45 - 5x \ge 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad -5x \ge -45 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x \le 9 \]

So \(x\) must satisfy: \(\;2 \le x \le 9\).

Integer solutions: \(2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9\) → 8 integers.
