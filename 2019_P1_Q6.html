Question 6 [4 marks]

(a) Solve the inequality $\dfrac{7x+26}{4} \le 2(3x-1)$.

(b) Find the number of integers satisfying both inequalities $\dfrac{7x+26}{4}\le 2(3x-1)$ and $45-5x \ge 0$.

1(a) Clear the fraction / expand
Multiplication Property of Inequalities \(a\le b \;\Longrightarrow\; ac\le bc\quad(c>0)\) Multiplying \(\dfrac{7x+26}{4}\le2(3x-1)\) by the positive number \(4\) keeps the sign.
$\dfrac{7x+26}{4} \le 2(3x-1)$
Multiply both sides by $4$ (positive,
so the sign keeps):
$7x+26 \le 8(3x-1) = 24x-8$
Multiplying by a positive number never flips an inequality sign.
2Collect like terms
$7x+26 \le 24x-8$
$7x-24x \le -8-26$
$-17x \le -34$
Bring $x$-terms to one side, constants to the other.
3Divide by $-17$ (reverse the sign!
)
Reversing the Inequality Sign \(a-b\) Dividing \(-17x\le-34\) by \(-17\) reverses the sign, giving \(x\ge2\).
$-17x \le -34$
$x \ge \dfrac{-34}{-17}$
$x \ge 2$
Dividing by a negative number reverses the inequality sign. Key step!
4(b) Solve the second inequality
$45-5x \ge 0$
$45 \ge 5x$
$x \le 9$
Isolate $x$; here the coefficient of $x$ is negative, so watch the sign again.
5Combine both conditions
From (a): $x \ge 2$  
 
From (b): $x \le 9$
Common solution: $2 \le x \le 9$
Intersection of the two shaded rays on the number line.
6Count the integers
Counting Integers in an Interval \(a\le x\le b \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{number}=b-a+1\) For \(2\le x\le9\) the count is \(9-2+1=8\) integers.
Integers: $2,\,3,\,4,\,5,\,6,\,7,\,8,\,9$
Number $= 9 - 2 + 1 = 8$
For inclusive bounds $a\le x\le b$, count $=b-a+1$.
Answer: (a) $x \ge 2$     (b) $8$ integers.

Question.
(a) Solve \(\dfrac{5x+9}{2}\le3(x+1)\).
(b) Solve \(40-4x\ge0\).
(c) How many integers satisfy both inequalities? [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Multiply the first inequality by \(2\) (positive): \(5x+9\le6x+6\),
so \(9-6\le6x-5x\) and \(x\ge3\).
Step 2. The second inequality: \(40\ge4x\), so \(x\le10\).
Step 3. Combining gives \(3\le x\le10\).
Step 4. The integers are \(3,4,\dots,10\), numbering \(10-3+1=8\).
Answer: (a) \(x\ge3\)   (b) \(x\le10\)   (c) \(8\) integers
