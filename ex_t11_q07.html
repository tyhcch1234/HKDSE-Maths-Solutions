Geometry (106) · Sub-Topic: Angles in Triangles/Polygons

T11 Q07

The sum of the interior angles of a polygon is $720^\circ$. Find the size of each interior angle if the polygon is regular.

Solution

Let the polygon have $n$ sides. The sum of its interior angles is $(n-2)180^\circ$.
Since the sum is $720^\circ$, solve $(n-2)180^\circ=720^\circ$.
Divide both sides by $180^\circ$: $n-2=4$, so $n=6$.
For a regular polygon, each interior angle is the total sum divided by $n$: $720^\circ/6=120^\circ$.
Each interior angle is $120^\circ$.
