HKDSE 2024 · Paper 2 · Q25 — Coordinate Geometry

Line \(x-y+13=0\) \(A(-3,1)\) \(B(-7,-5)\) \(P(x,y)\) \(AP=PB\) Perp. bisector
[2024MC#25]
The coordinates of the points \(A\) and \(B\) are \((-3,1)\) and \((-7,-5)\) respectively. If \(P\) is a point lying on the straight line \(x-y+13=0\) such that \(AP=PB\), then the \(y\)-coordinate of \(P\) is
A. \(-11\)
B. \(-2\)
C. \(2\)
D. \(11\)
STEP 1 · Set up \(P\)
Let \(P=(x,y)\). Since \(P\) lies on the straight line \(x-y+13=0\), \[x-y+13=0 \quad\cdots(1)\]
Hint: the constraint gives one equation linking \(x\) and \(y\). We need one more.
STEP 2 · Use \(AP=PB\)
Perpendicular Bisector \(AP=PB\ \Rightarrow\ P\text{ lies on the perpendicular bisector of }AB\) Squaring \(AP=PB\) gives one linear equation in \(x\) and \(y\).
Equal distances from \(P\) to \(A\) and \(B\) means \(P\) lies on the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\). \[AP=PB\] Square both sides using the distance formula: \[[x-(-3)]^{2}+(y-1)^{2}=[x-(-7)]^{2}+[y-(-5)]^{2}\]
STEP 3 · Expand & simplify
Distance Formula \(AP^{2}=(x-x_1)^{2}+(y-y_1)^{2}\) Expanding both squared distances cancels \(x^{2}\) and \(y^{2}\), leaving \(2x+3y+16=0\).
Expand and cancel the \(x^{2}\) and \(y^{2}\) terms: \[(x+3)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}=(x+7)^{2}+(y+5)^{2}\] \[x^{2}+6x+9+y^{2}-2y+1=x^{2}+14x+49+y^{2}+10y+25\] \[6x-2y+10=14x+10y+74\] \[-8x-12y-64=0\] \[\Rightarrow\;2x+3y+16=0\quad\cdots(2)\]
STEP 4 · Solve the system
From (1): \(x=y-13\). Substitute into (2): \[2(y-13)+3y+16=0\] \[2y-26+3y+16=0\] \[5y-10=0\] \[y=2\]
✔ Final answer: the \(y\)-coordinate of \(P\) is \(2\) — option C.
Check: then \(x=y-13=-11\), so \(P=(-11,2)\), and indeed \(AP=PB=\sqrt{65}\).

Question. \(P\) lies on the line \(x+y-4=0\) and is equidistant from \(A(1,2)\) and \(B(5,4)\). Find the \(y\)-coordinate of \(P\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(AP=PB\); by the distance formula \((x-1)^{2}+(y-2)^{2}=(x-5)^{2}+(y-4)^{2}\).
Step 2. Expand and cancel squares: \(-2x-4y+5=-10x-8y+41\Rightarrow 8x+4y=36\Rightarrow 2x+y=9\).
Step 3. With \(x+y-4=0\), subtract: \(x=5\), then \(y=-1\).
Answer: \(y=-1\)
