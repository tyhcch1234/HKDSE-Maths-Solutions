HKDSE 2020 Paper 1 — Q19 (Trigonometry)

PQRS is a quadrilateral paper card, where $PQ = 60$ cm, $PS = 40$ cm, $\angle PQR = 30^\circ$, $\angle PRQ = 55^\circ$ and $\angle QPS = 120^\circ$. The paper card is held with $QR$ lying on the horizontal ground as shown in Figure 3.

(a) Find the length of $RS$.   [3 marks]
(b) Find the area of the paper card.   [2 marks]
(c) It is given that the angle between the paper card and the horizontal ground is $32^\circ$.
  (i) Find the shortest distance from $P$ to the horizontal ground.
  (ii) A student claims that the angle between $RS$ and the horizontal ground is at most $20^\circ$. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer.   [7 marks]
STEP 1 · Set up triangle PQR
Sine Formula\(\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}\)\(\dfrac{PR}{\sin30^\circ}=\dfrac{60}{\sin55^\circ}\) gives \(PR\approx36.62\ \text{cm}\).

We know $PQ = 60$ and the two angles in $\triangle PQR$:

$\angle QPR = 180^\circ - 30^\circ - 55^\circ = 95^\circ$

Use the sine formula in $\triangle PQR$ to find $PR$ and later $QR$:

$\dfrac{PR}{\sin \angle PQR} = \dfrac{PQ}{\sin \angle PRQ}$

$\dfrac{PR}{\sin 30^\circ} = \dfrac{60}{\sin 55^\circ}$

$PR = \dfrac{60\sin 30^\circ}{\sin 55^\circ} \approx 36.62323766\ \text{cm}$

STEP 2 · Find RS in triangle PRS
Cosine Formula\(c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}-2ab\cos C\)\(RS^{2}=40^{2}+36.62^{2}-2(40)(36.62)\cos25^\circ\approx285.9\), so \(RS\approx16.9\ \text{cm}\).

In $\triangle PRS$ we know $PS = 40$, $PR \approx 36.62323766$ and

$\angle RPS = \angle QPS - \angle QPR = 120^\circ - 95^\circ = 25^\circ$.

Apply the cosine formula:

$RS^2 = PS^2 + PR^2 - 2(PS)(PR)\cos\angle RPS$

$RS^2 = 40^2 + (36.62323766)^2 - 2(40)(36.62323766)\cos 25^\circ$

$RS^2 \approx 285.890411$

$RS \approx 16.9\ \text{cm}$
STEP 3 · Area of the paper card
Area of a Triangle\(\text{Area}=\dfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C\)Diagonal \(PR\) splits the card into two triangles using the angles \(95^\circ\) and \(25^\circ\) at \(P\).

The card is split by diagonal $PR$ into $\triangle PQR$ and $\triangle PRS$:

$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}(PQ)(PR)\sin\angle QPR + \tfrac{1}{2}(PR)(PS)\sin\angle RPS$

$= \tfrac{1}{2}(60)(36.62323766)\sin 95^\circ + \tfrac{1}{2}(36.62323766)(40)\sin 25^\circ$

$\approx 1094.738813 + 309.353423 \approx 1404.092236\ \text{cm}^2$

Area $\approx 1400\ \text{cm}^2$ (3 s.f.)
STEP 4 · (c)(i) Shortest distance from P to ground

Let $H$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $P$ to $QR$. In the plane of the card,

$PH = PQ\sin\angle PQH = 60\sin 30^\circ = 30\ \text{cm}$.

Let $G$ be the projection of $P$ on the horizontal ground.
Then $\angle PHG = 32^\circ$ and $PG = PH\sin 32^\circ$:

$PG = 30\sin 32^\circ \approx 15.89757793\ \text{cm}$

Shortest distance $\approx 15.9\ \text{cm}$
STEP 5 · (c)(ii) Analyse angle of RS

Let $K$ be the projection of $S$ on the ground, and $T$ the intersection of $PS$ produced and $QR$ produced.
Then $\triangle SKT \sim \triangle PGT$ and $PT = PQ$ (isosceles $\triangle PQT$ since $\angle PQT = \angle PTQ = 30^\circ$).

$SK = \left(\dfrac{PT-PS}{PT}\right)PG = \left(\dfrac{60-40}{60}\right)PG = \dfrac{1}{3}PG = \dfrac{1}{3}(30\sin 32^\circ) = 10\sin 32^\circ$

If $\theta$ is the angle between $RS$ and the ground,
then in right triangle $SRK$:

$\sin\theta = \dfrac{SK}{RS} = \dfrac{10\sin 32^\circ}{16.90879944}$

$\theta \approx 18.26416068^\circ \le 20^\circ$

ANSWER · Conclusion

The angle between $RS$ and the horizontal ground is approximately $18.3^\circ$,
which is less than 20°.

The student's claim is correct.
CHECK · Key results

$PR \approx 36.62$ cm  
|  $RS \approx 16.9$ cm  
| 
Area $\approx 1400$ cm²  
| 
Shortest distance $\approx 15.9$ cm  
| 
Angle of $RS \approx 18.3^\circ \le 20^\circ$

Question. \(PQRS\) is a quadrilateral paper card, where \(PQ=50\ \text{cm}\), \(PS=30\ \text{cm}\), \(\angle PQR=35^\circ\), \(\angle PRQ=60^\circ\) and \(\angle QPS=110^\circ\).
(a) Find the length of \(RS\).
(b) Find the area of the paper card.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. In \(\triangle PQR\): \(\angle QPR=180^\circ-35^\circ-60^\circ=85^\circ\).
Step 2. Sine formula: \(PR=\dfrac{50\sin35^\circ}{\sin60^\circ}\approx33.115\ \text{cm}\).
Step 3. \(\angle RPS=110^\circ-85^\circ=25^\circ\);
by the cosine formula \(RS^{2}=30^{2}+33.115^{2}-2(30)(33.115)\cos25^\circ\approx195.9\), so \(RS\approx14.0\ \text{cm}\).
Step 4. Area \(=\dfrac{1}{2}(50)(33.115)\sin85^\circ+\dfrac{1}{2}(33.115)(30)\sin25^\circ\approx824.9+210.0\approx1035\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Answer: (a) \(RS\approx14.0\ \text{cm}\) (b) area \(\approx1035\ \text{cm}^{2}\)
