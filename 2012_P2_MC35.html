#Complex Numbers #Powers of i #Algebraic Simplification
Q35

Simplify \( i^3(\beta i - 3) \).

A. \(\beta + 3i\)
B. \(-\beta - 3i\)
C. \(\beta - 3i\)
D. \(-\beta + 3i\)

📝 Step-by-Step Solution

1

Expand the expression

\( i^3(\beta i - 3) = i^3 \cdot \beta i - i^3 \cdot 3 \)

Distribute \(i^3\) to both terms inside the parentheses.

💡 Hint: Use the distributive law: \(a(b+c) = ab + ac\).
2

Simplify the powers of \(i\)

\( i^3 \cdot \beta i = \beta i^4 \)
\( i^3 \cdot 3 = 3i^3 \)

Multiply the coefficients and add the exponents for the same base \(i\).

💡 Hint: Recall \(i^m \cdot i^n = i^{m+n}\).
3

Evaluate \(i^4\) and \(i^3\)

\( i^4 = (i^2)^2 = (-1)^2 = 1 \)
\( i^3 = i^2 \cdot i = (-1) \cdot i = -i \)

Substitute these values back into the expression.

💡 Hint: Remember \(i^2 = -1\). This gives \(i^4 = 1\) and \(i^3 = -i\).
4

Substitute and simplify

\( \beta \cdot 1 - 3 \cdot (-i) \)
\( = \beta + 3i \)

The final simplified form is \(\beta + 3i\).

✅ Correct answer: A. \(\beta + 3i\)

📌 Key Takeaway

  • The powers of \(i\) cycle every 4: \(i^0=1,\; i^1=i,\; i^2=-1,\; i^3=-i,\; i^4=1\).
  • Always expand and simplify using \(i^2 = -1\).
  • Watch for signs when multiplying negative terms.
