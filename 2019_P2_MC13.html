Combined variation with percentage change

Direct Variation Inverse Variation Percentage Change Combined Variation

It is given that \(z\) varies directly as the square of \(x\) and inversely as the square root of \(y\).

If \(x\) is decreased by 40% and \(y\) is increased by 44%, then \(z\)

A. is decreased by 70%.
B. is increased by 70%.
C. is decreased by 76%.
D. is increased by 76%.

Original \(z\) New \(z\)
Key insight from the original question: When \(x\) drops by 40%, \(x^2\) becomes \(0.36\) times the original. When \(y\) rises by 44%, \(\sqrt{y}\) becomes \(1.2\) times the original. So \(z\) becomes \(\frac{0.36}{1.2} = 0.3\) times the original → a 70% decrease.
