Question

The figure shows the graph of \( y = mx^2 + n \), where \(m\) and \(n\) are constants. Which of the following is true?

A. \( m<0 \) and \( n<0 \) B. \( m<0 \) and \( n>0 \) C. \( m>0 \) and \( n<0 \) D. \( m>0 \) and \( n>0 \)
Step 1

Understand the equation. The given graph is a parabola of the form \(y = mx^2 + n\). The coefficient \(m\) controls the direction of opening, and \(n\) gives the \(y\)-intercept (since \(y(0)=n\)).

💡 Hint: Look at the shape and where it cuts the \(y\)-axis.
Step 2
Leading Coefficient and Direction of Opening \(y=ax^{2}+bx+c:\ a>0\ \Rightarrow\ \text{opens upwards},\quad a<0\ \Rightarrow\ \text{opens downwards}\) The graph of \(y=mx^{2}+n\) opens upwards, so its coefficient of \(x^{2}\) must satisfy \(m>0\).

Determine the sign of \(m\). The graph opens upwards, which means the coefficient of \(x^2\) is positive. Therefore,

\[ m > 0. \]

💡 Hint: “Opens upwards” → \(m>0\); “opens downwards” → \(m<0\).
Step 3
y-intercept of a Quadratic Graph \(y=ax^{2}+bx+c\ \Rightarrow\ y\text{-intercept}=c\) Putting \(x=0\) in \(y=mx^{2}+n\) gives \(y=n\), and the graph cuts the \(y\)-axis below \(O\), so \(n<0\).

Determine the sign of \(n\). The graph cuts the \(y\)-axis below the origin, so the \(y\)-intercept is negative. Since the \(y\)-intercept is exactly \(n\), we have

\[ n < 0. \]

💡 Hint: \(y(0) = n\). If the point \((0,n)\) is below the \(x\)-axis, then \(n<0\).
Step 4

Combine the results. We have found

\[ m > 0 \quad \text{and} \quad n < 0. \]

Comparing with the options:

  • A: \(m<0\), \(n<0\) ❌
  • B: \(m<0\), \(n>0\) ❌
  • C: \(m>0\), \(n<0\) ✅
  • D: \(m>0\), \(n>0\) ❌
💡 Hint: Match the signs to the correct option.
Answer: C — \(m > 0\) and \(n < 0\)

Question. The figure shows the graph of \(y=ax^{2}+b\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are constants. The graph opens downwards and cuts the \(y\)-axis above the origin. Which of the following is true?
A. \(a<0\) and \(b<0\)    B. \(a<0\) and \(b>0\)    C. \(a>0\) and \(b<0\)    D. \(a>0\) and \(b>0\)  (1 mark)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The graph opens downwards, so the coefficient of \(x^{2}\) is negative: \(a<0\).
Step 2. Putting \(x=0\) gives \(y=b\), so the \(y\)-intercept is \(b\); it lies above the origin, hence \(b>0\).
Step 3. Therefore \(a<0\) and \(b>0\) — option B.
Answer: B. \(a<0\) and \(b>0\)
