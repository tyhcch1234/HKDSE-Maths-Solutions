Coordinate Geometry — the median of a triangle and the equation of a straight line.
Mid-point Formula Two-point Form Median of a Triangle Equation of a Line

Question Multiple Choice

The coordinates of the points \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\) are \((3, 3)\), \((5, 8)\) and \((9, 2)\) respectively. Let \(P\) be a point such that \(AP\) is a median of \(\triangle ABC\). Find the equation of the straight line which passes through \(A\) and \(P\).

  • A. \(x - 2y + 3 = 0\)
  • B. \(2x - 3y + 1 = 0\)
  • C. \(2x - 3y + 3 = 0\)
  • D. \(3x + 2y - 15 = 0\)

Part (a) Locate the mid-point of BC

Since \(AP\) is a median of \(\triangle ABC\), the point \(P\) must be the mid-point of \(BC\). Drag the slider to see the mid-point \(M\) move along \(BC\) — the median from \(A\) always passes through \(M\).

Step 1

Since \(AP\) is a median of \(\triangle ABC\), \(P\) is the mid-point of the side \(BC\).

Hint: A median joins a vertex to the mid-point of the opposite side.
Step 2

By the mid-point formula, the coordinates of \(P\) are

\[ P = \left( \frac{5+9}{2}, \frac{8+2}{2} \right) = (7, 5) \]
Hint: Mid-point \(= \left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \dfrac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)\).
Step 3
\(P = (7, 5)\) — the mid-point of \(BC\).

Part (b) Equation of the line through A and P

Step 1

Slope of the line through \(A(3, 3)\) and \(P(7, 5)\):

\[ m = \frac{5 - 3}{7 - 3} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2} \]
Hint: Slope \(m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\).
Step 2

Using the point-slope form with point \(A(3, 3)\):

\[ y - 3 = \frac{1}{2}(x - 3) \]
Hint: \(y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)\).
Step 3

Multiply both sides by 2 and rearrange:

\[ 2(y - 3) = x - 3 \;\Rightarrow\; 2y - 6 = x - 3 \] \[ x - 2y + 3 = 0 \]
Hint: Bring all terms to one side to match the standard form \(Ax + By + C = 0\).
Step 4
The equation of the line is \(x - 2y + 3 = 0\), which is option A.

Summary of Answers

  • P is the mid-point of \(BC\): \(P = (7, 5)\).
  • Slope of \(AP\): \(m = \dfrac{1}{2}\).
  • Equation: \(x - 2y + 3 = 0\) → Option A.
