Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Applications

T13 Q41

A ladder of length $5$ m leans against a vertical wall, making an angle of $60°$ with the horizontal ground. Find the height reached by the ladder on the wall.

Solution

Let $h$ be the height reached by the ladder on the wall. The ladder forms the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle, with angle $60^\circ$ at the ground.
Using the sine ratio: $\sin 60^\circ=\frac{h}{5}$.
Thus $h=5\sin 60^\circ=5\times\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}=\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
So the height is $\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}$ m, which is approximately $4.33$ m.
The ladder reaches a height of $\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}$ m (approximately $4.33$ m) on the wall.
