HKDSE 2025 · Paper 2 · Q3 · Polynomials

If $a$ and $b$ are constants such that

$$(x+8)(x+a)+b \equiv x^2+5a(x+3),$$

then $b =$

  • A. $-14$
  • B. $-2$
  • C. $2$
  • D. $14$

1Expand the left-hand side

$$(x+8)(x+a)+b$$

$$= x^2+ax+8x+8a+b$$

$$= x^2+(8+a)x+8a+b$$

Multiply out the brackets first, then collect like terms. The $+b$ just adds onto the constant term.
Expanding a Product of Binomials \((x+p)(x+q)=x^{2}+(p+q)x+pq\) The left side becomes \(x^{2}+(8+a)x+8a+b\), so the \(x\)-coefficient is \(8+a\).

2Expand the right-hand side

$$x^2+5a(x+3)$$

$$= x^2+5ax+15a$$

Only the bracket $5a(x+3)$ needs expanding — keep the coefficient $5a$ outside.

3Compare the coefficients of $x$

$$8+a = 5a$$

$$8 = 4a$$

$$a = 2$$

Since the two expressions are identically equal, the $x$-term coefficients must match. This gives $a$ directly.
Equating Coefficients in an Identity \(\text{coefficient of }x\text{ on the left}=\text{coefficient of }x\text{ on the right}\) Matching gives \(8+a=5a\), so \(4a=8\) and \(a=2\).

4Compare the constant terms to find $b$

$$8a+b = 15a$$

Substitute $a = 2$:

$$8(2)+b = 15(2)$$

$$16+b = 30$$

$$b = 14$$

Now compare the constant terms using the value of $a$ you just found.
Solving a Linear Equation \(ax+b=0\ \to\ x=-\dfrac{b}{a}\) With \(a=2\) the constant equation \(16+b=30\) gives \(b=14\).
Answer: D. $14$
$a = 2$ and $b = 14$.

Question. If \(a\) and \(b\) are constants such that \((x+6)(x+c)+d\equiv x^{2}+2c(x+5)\), find \(c\) and \(d\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Left side: \(x^{2}+(6+c)x+6c+d\).
Step 2. Right side: \(x^{2}+2cx+10c\).
Step 3. Comparing the \(x\)-coefficients: \(6+c=2c\), so \(c=6\).
Step 4. Comparing the constants: \(6(6)+d=10(6)\), so \(36+d=60\) and \(d=24\).
Answer: \(c=6\), \(d=24\)
