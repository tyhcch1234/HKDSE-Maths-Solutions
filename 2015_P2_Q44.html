HKDSE 2015 Paper 2 · Q44 · 14. Probability

Probability of Drawing a Green Ball

Bag $P$ contains 2 red balls and 4 green balls while bag $Q$ contains 1 red ball and 3 green balls. If a bag is randomly chosen and then a ball is randomly drawn from the bag, find the probability that a green ball is drawn.

A. $\dfrac{3}{10}$ B. $\dfrac{7}{10}$ C. $\dfrac{7}{24}$ D. $\dfrac{17}{24}$

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1Identify the two stages.
Stage 1: choose a bag — $P(\text{bag }P)=\dfrac{1}{2}$, $P(\text{bag }Q)=\dfrac{1}{2}$ (equally likely).
Randomly chosen bag ⇒ each bag has probability $\tfrac12$.
STEP 2Green ball probabilities inside each bag.
Bag $P$: $2$ red, $4$ green ⇒ $P(\text{green}\mid P)=\dfrac{4}{2+4}=\dfrac{4}{6}$.
Bag $Q$: $1$ red, $3$ green ⇒ $P(\text{green}\mid Q)=\dfrac{3}{1+3}=\dfrac{3}{4}$.
Conditional probability = green count ÷ total count in that bag.
STEP 3Multiply along each branch (Law of Total Probability).
$P(\text{green} \cap P)=P(\text{green}\mid P)\times P(P)=\dfrac{4}{6}\times\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
$P(\text{green} \cap Q)=P(\text{green}\mid Q)\times P(Q)=\dfrac{3}{4}\times\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{3}{8}$.
Tree diagram: probability of a path = product of the branch probabilities.
STEP 4Add the two disjoint outcomes.
$$P(\text{green})=\frac{4}{6}\cdot\frac{1}{2}+\frac{3}{4}\cdot\frac{1}{2} =\frac{1}{3}+\frac{3}{8}$$
The paths are mutually exclusive, so add their probabilities.
STEP 5Simplify over a common denominator.
$$\frac{1}{3}+\frac{3}{8}=\frac{8}{24}+\frac{9}{24}=\frac{17}{24}$$
$24$ is the LCM of $3$ and $8$.
Answer: D. $\dfrac{17}{24}$
