Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Distance/Slope

T12 Q06

Find the midpoint of the line segment joining $(-3,5)$ and $(3,-1)$.

Solution

Use the midpoint formula: $\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$ for points $(x_1,y_1)=(-3,5)$ and $(x_2,y_2)=(3,-1)$.
Substitute the coordinates: $\left(\frac{-3+3}{2},\frac{5+(-1)}{2}\right)$.
Simplify: $\left(\frac{0}{2},\frac{4}{2}\right)=(0,2)$.
The midpoint of the line segment is $(0,2)$.
