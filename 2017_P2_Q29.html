Statistics — Reading a Box-and-Whisker Diagram
Box-and-Whisker Quartiles Five-Number Summary

Q Question

The box-and-whisker diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of online hours spent by a class of students in a certain week. Find the lower quartile of the distribution.
A. 5
B. 15
C. 25
D. 40

1 Interactive Diagram — The Box-and-Whisker Plot

Drag the sliders to move the five key values and see how the box plot changes. Use the checkboxes to highlight each part of the five-number summary.

Min = 5 Q1 = 15 Median = 22 Q3 = 30 Max = 56 IQR = 15

2 Solution — Reading the Lower Quartile

STEP 1

Recall the structure of a box-and-whisker diagram. The box runs from the lower quartile \(Q_1\) to the upper quartile \(Q_3\), with the median marked inside the box by a vertical line.

Hint: The left edge of the rectangular box is always the lower quartile \(Q_1\). Do not confuse it with the left end of the whisker (the minimum).
STEP 2

Locate the left edge of the box on the horizontal axis. Reading from the diagram, the left edge of the box lies at:

\[ Q_1 = 15 \]
Hint: Align the left edge of the box vertically with the scale: it sits exactly halfway between \(10\) and \(20\).
STEP 3

Therefore the lower quartile of the distribution is \(15\).

Lower quartile \(Q_1 = 15\)

Checking the options — this matches B.

0 / 3

3 Compare the Three Quartiles

Use the sliders to set \(Q_1\), the median, and \(Q_3\). The diagram highlights the interquartile range (IQR) and how the four quarters of the class are distributed.

Q1 = 15 Median = 22 Q3 = 30 IQR = Q3 − Q1 = 15
Hint: In any box-and-whisker diagram, the left edge of the box is \(Q_1\), the line inside the box is the median, and the right edge of the box is \(Q_3\). Each whisker covers \(25\%\) of the data, and each half-box (between \(Q_1\) and the median, or the median and \(Q_3\)) also covers \(25\%\).

★ Summary of Answers

  • From the box-and-whisker diagram, the left edge of the box gives the lower quartile: \[ Q_1 = \mathbf{15} \]
  • Median (line inside the box) = \(22\), upper quartile (right edge of box) = \(30\).
  • Interquartile range \(= Q_3 - Q_1 = 30 - 15 = 15\).
  • Correct option: B (15)
