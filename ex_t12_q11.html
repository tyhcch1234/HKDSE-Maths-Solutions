Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Distance/Slope

T12 Q11

Find the distance between the points $(1, 2)$ and $(4, -2)$.

Solution

Use the distance formula: $d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$.
Substitute $(x_1,y_1)=(1,2)$ and $(x_2,y_2)=(4,-2)$: $d=\sqrt{(4-1)^2+(-2-2)^2}$.
Simplify: $d=\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2}=\sqrt{9+16}=\sqrt{25}$.
Therefore, $d=5$.
The distance between $(1,2)$ and $(4,-2)$ is $5$.
