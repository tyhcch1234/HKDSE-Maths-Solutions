Change of Subject: Making \(a\) the Subject

Topic 5 — Formulas and Sequences

Change of Subject Algebraic Fractions Cross-multiplication Factorisation

QThe Question

If \(\;\dfrac{a+5b}{7a+2b}=\dfrac{1}{b+3}\;\), then \(a=\)
A \(\dfrac{4-b}{5b^{2}+13b}\)
B \(\dfrac{4+b}{5b^{2}+13b}\)
C \(\dfrac{5b^{2}+13b}{4-b}\)
D \(\dfrac{5b^{2}+13b}{4+b}\)
Tap an option to test your answer.
Tip: you may use the interactive checker below to test any expression numerically.

1Step-by-Step Solution

1 Clear the fractions (cross-multiply) ▼
\[ \frac{a+5b}{7a+2b}=\frac{1}{b+3} \] Multiply both sides by \(\,(7a+2b)(b+3)\,\): \[ (a+5b)(b+3)=(7a+2b)(1) \] \[ \boxed{\,ab+3a+5b^{2}+15b=7a+2b\,} \]
Hint — Cross-multiplication: for \(\dfrac{P}{Q}=\dfrac{R}{S}\), we get \(P\times S=Q\times R\).
Here \(P=a+5b,\;Q=7a+2b,\;R=1,\;S=b+3\).
2 Collect every term containing \(a\) on one side ▼
Move all \(a\)-terms to the right and all non-\(a\) terms to the left: \[ ab+3a+5b^{2}+15b=7a+2b \] \[ 5b^{2}+15b-2b=7a-ab-3a \] \[ \boxed{\,5b^{2}+13b=4a-ab\,} \]
Hint: The terms \(7a-ab-3a\) still contain \(a\) — leave them on the right for factorising in Step 3.
3 Factorise out the common factor \(a\) ▼
Take \(a\) out of \(\,4a-ab\): \[ 4a-ab=a(4-b) \] So the equation becomes \[ \boxed{\,5b^{2}+13b=a(4-b)\,} \]
Hint: \(4a-ab=a\cdot 4-a\cdot b=a(4-b)\).
Factorising is the key trick that lets us divide to isolate \(a\).
4 Divide both sides by \((4-b)\) ▼
\[ \frac{5b^{2}+13b}{4-b}=\frac{a(4-b)}{4-b} \] \[ \boxed{\,a=\frac{5b^{2}+13b}{4-b}\,} \]
Hint: Dividing by an expression is only valid when it is not zero — here \(b\neq 4\).
5 Check the options → Answer is C ▼
Our result matches option C: \(\;a=\dfrac{5b^{2}+13b}{4-b}\).
Numerical verification (a smart MC technique): pick \(b=1\). LHS: \(\frac{a+5}{7a+2}\), RHS: \(\frac{1}{4}\). Solving gives \(4a+20=7a+2\Rightarrow a=6\). Substituting \(b=1\) into option C: \(\frac{5+13}{3}=\frac{18}{3}=6\) ✓.

3Quick Challenge — Spot the Mistake

A student wrote \(a=\dfrac{4-b}{5b^{2}+13b}\). Which option is that, and what did they get wrong?

Final Answer

\[ a=\frac{5b^{2}+13b}{4-b} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{Option C} \]
