Quadratic equations Sum & product of roots

Question:

If \( m \neq n \) and \( 2m^2 + 5m = 2n^2 + 5n = 14 \), then \( (m+2)(n+2) = \)

A. –8
B. 2
C. 6
D. 16

📘 Step-by-step solution

Given \(2m^2 + 5m = 14\) and \(2n^2 + 5n = 14\), both \(m\) and \(n\) satisfy the same equation:

\(2x^2 + 5x = 14\)

Rearranging into standard form:

\(2x^2 + 5x - 14 = 0\)

💡 Hint: Since \(m \neq n\), they are the two distinct roots of this quadratic equation.

For a quadratic \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) with roots \(m\) and \(n\):

\(m + n = -\frac{b}{a}, \quad mn = \frac{c}{a}\)

Here \(a = 2, b = 5, c = -14\).

So:

\(m + n = -\frac{5}{2}, \quad mn = \frac{-14}{2} = -7\)

💡 Hint: These formulas are derived directly from expanding \(a(x-m)(x-n)=0\).

We need \((m+2)(n+2)\). Expand:

\((m+2)(n+2) = mn + 2m + 2n + 4\)

Group the terms involving \(m+n\):

\(= mn + 2(m+n) + 4\)

💡 Hint: Now substitute the values of \(m+n\) and \(mn\) from Step 2.

Substitute \(m+n = -\frac{5}{2}\) and \(mn = -7\):

\((m+2)(n+2) = (-7) + 2\left(-\frac{5}{2}\right) + 4\)

\(= -7 - 5 + 4 = -8\)

✅ Final answer: –8 (Option A)

Answer

A. –8

Key: Use sum & product of roots to avoid solving for m and n individually.
