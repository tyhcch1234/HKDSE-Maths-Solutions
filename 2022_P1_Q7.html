HKDSE 2022 · Paper 1 · Q7 — Coordinate Geometry

axes / grid \(S' T'\) segment \(T \to T'\) reflection \(S \to S'\) rotation points
[2022 #7]

The coordinates of the points \(S\) and \(T\) are \((12,-5)\) and \((-3,-7)\) respectively. \(S\) is rotated anticlockwise about \(O\) through \(90^\circ\) to \(S'\), where \(O\) is the origin. \(T'\) is the reflection image of \(T\) with respect to the \(x\)-axis.

(a) Write down the coordinates of \(S'\) and \(T'\).

(b) Find the slope of \(S'T'\).

[4 marks]
Step 1 · Rotation rule (anticlockwise about O by 90°)

Under an anticlockwise rotation of \(90^\circ\) about the origin, the rule is

\[ (x,y) \;\longrightarrow\; (-y,\; x). \]

Applying it to \(S(12,-5)\):

\[ S' = (-(-5),\; 12) = (5,\,12). \]
Step 2 · Reflection in the x-axis

Reflection in the \(x\)-axis keeps \(x\) and changes the sign of \(y\):

\[ (x,y) \;\longrightarrow\; (x,\,-y). \]

Applying it to \(T(-3,-7)\):

\[ T' = (-3,\;-(-7)) = (-3,\,7). \]
Part (a) answer: \(S'(5,12)\) and \(T'(-3,7)\) — 1A each.
Step 3 · Slope formula

The slope of a line through \(A(x_1,y_1)\) and \(B(x_2,y_2)\) is

\[ m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}. \]

Using \(S'(5,12)\) and \(T'(-3,7)\):

\[ m_{S'T'} = \frac{12 - 7}{5 - (-3)}. \]
Step 4 · Simplify
\[ m_{S'T'} = \frac{12-7}{5+3} = \frac{5}{8}. \]

So the slope of \(S'T'\) is \(\dfrac{5}{8}\) (positive, since the line rises from left to right — consistent with the diagram).

Answer (a): \(S'(5,12)\), \(T'(-3,7)\)

Answer (b): Slope of \(S'T' = \dfrac{5}{8}\)
