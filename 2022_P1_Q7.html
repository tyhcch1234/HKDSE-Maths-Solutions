Coordinate Geometry — Rotation & Reflection of Points
Rotation about Origin Reflection in x-axis Slope Formula Transformations

Question 4 marks

The coordinates of the points \(S\) and \(T\) are \((12,-5)\) and \((-3,-7)\) respectively. \(S\) is rotated anticlockwise about \(O\) through \(90^\circ\) to \(S'\), where \(O\) is the origin. \(T'\) is the reflection image of \(T\) with respect to the \(x\)-axis.

(a) Write down the coordinates of \(S'\) and \(T'\). 2A

(b) Find the slope of \(S'T'\). 1M 1A

Part (a) · Finding \(S'\) and \(T'\)

90°
S'(5, 12)  |  T'(-3, 7)  |  OT = OT' = √58
0 / 3
1 1A

Rotate \(S(12,-5)\) anticlockwise about \(O\) through \(90^\circ\).

Under a \(90^\circ\) anticlockwise rotation about the origin, \((x,y) \to (-y,x)\).

\[ S(12,-5) \;\longrightarrow\; S'(-(-5),\,12) = S'(5,\,12) \]

💡 Hint: Anticlockwise \(90^\circ\) about \(O\): swap coordinates and negate the new \(x\)-coordinate. Equivalently \((x,y)\mapsto(-y,x)\).
\(S' = (5,\,12)\)
2 1A

Reflect \(T(-3,-7)\) in the \(x\)-axis.

Reflection in the \(x\)-axis changes the sign of the \(y\)-coordinate: \((x,y) \to (x,-y)\).

\[ T(-3,-7) \;\longrightarrow\; T'(-3,\,7) \]

💡 Hint: The \(x\)-axis acts as a mirror. The \(x\)-coordinate stays the same; the \(y\)-coordinate flips sign.
\(T' = (-3,\,7)\)
3 ✓

Geometric check.

\(OS = \sqrt{12^2+(-5)^2} = \sqrt{144+25} = 13\).

\(OS' = \sqrt{5^2+12^2} = \sqrt{25+144} = 13\). ✓ Rotation preserves distance from the origin.

\(OT = \sqrt{(-3)^2+(-7)^2} = \sqrt{9+49}=\sqrt{58}\). \(OT' = \sqrt{(-3)^2+7^2} = \sqrt{58}\). ✓ Reflection preserves distance from origin.

Both transformations preserve distances from \(O\).

Part (b) · Finding the slope of \(S'T'\)

Slope \(= \dfrac{12-7}{5-(-3)} = \dfrac{5}{8}\)
0 / 3
1 1M

Use the slope formula.

For two points \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\), the slope is

\[ m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} \]

Taking \(S'(5,12)\) and \(T'(-3,7)\):

\[ m = \frac{12 - 7}{5 - (-3)} \]

💡 Hint: The order of points does not matter as long as you subtract in the same order for both numerator and denominator.
2 1A

Simplify.

\[ \frac{12 - 7}{5 - (-3)} = \frac{5}{5+3} = \frac{5}{8} \]

💡 Hint: Remember \(5 - (-3) = 5 + 3 = 8\). A positive slope means the line rises from left to right.
Slope of \(S'T' = \dfrac{5}{8}\)
3 ✓

Geometric interpretation.

From \(T'(-3,7)\) to \(S'(5,12)\): run \(= 5-(-3)=8\) and rise \(= 12-7=5\).

So the slope is \(\dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \dfrac{5}{8}\). The line rises gently to the right.

The line \(S'T'\) has a positive slope of \(\dfrac{5}{8}\).

📝 Summary of Final Answers

  • (a) \(S'\) \((5,\,12)\)
  • (a) \(T'\) \((-3,\,7)\)
  • (b) Slope of \(S'T'\) \(\dfrac{5}{8}\)
