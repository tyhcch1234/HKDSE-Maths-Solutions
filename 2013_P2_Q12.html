HKDSE 2013 · Paper 2 · Q12
The actual area of a playground is $900\text{ m}^2$. If the area of the playground on a map is $36\text{ cm}^2$, then the scale of the map is
  • A. $1:25$
  • B. $1:50$
  • C. $1:500$
  • D. $1:250\,000$
1
Given information.
Map area $=36\text{ cm}^2$,  
Actual area $=900\text{ m}^2$. A scale compares lengths — but here we are given areas,
so we must be careful.
2
Convert to the same unit.
Since $1\text{ m}=100\text{ cm}$,   $1\text{ m}^2 = 100^2 = 10\,000\text{ cm}^2$.
Area Unit Conversion\(1\ \text{m}=100\ \text{cm}\ \Rightarrow\ 1\ \text{m}^{2}=100^{2}\ \text{cm}^{2}=10\,000\ \text{cm}^{2}\)The playground of \(900\ \text{m}^{2}\) becomes \(900\times10\,000=9\,000\,000\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
So the actual area is \[900\text{ m}^2 = 900 \times 10\,000\text{ cm}^2 = 9\,000\,000\text{ cm}^2.\] Always square the unit conversion factor when converting areas!
3
Ratio of areas.
\[\frac{\text{map area}}{\text{actual area}}=\frac{36\text{ cm}^2}{9\,000\,000\text{ cm}^2} =\frac{36}{9\,000\,000}=\frac{1}{250\,000}.\] This is the ratio of areas, not of lengths.
4
From area ratio to length scale.
Area Ratio of Similar Figures\(\dfrac{A_{1}}{A_{2}}=\left(\dfrac{\ell_{1}}{\ell_{2}}\right)^{2}\)The map and the playground are similar, so the length scale is the square root of the area ratio \(\dfrac{1}{250\,000}\).
The map and the real region are similar figures.
For similar figures, \[\frac{\text{area on map}}{\text{actual area}}=\left(\frac{\text{length on map}}{\text{actual length}}\right)^{2}.\] Therefore \[\frac{\text{map length}}{\text{actual length}}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{250\,000}}=\frac{1}{500}.\] Take the square root — this is the key step!
5
Conclusion.
Square Root of a Quotient\(\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{b}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}}\)Taking \(\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{250\,000}}\) gives \(\dfrac{1}{500}\), so the scale is \(1:500\).
\[\text{Scale of the map}=1:500.\] Checking the options: $(1:25)^2=1:625$, $(1:50)^2=1:2500$, $(1:500)^2=1:250\,000$ ✓, $(1:250\,000)^2=1:6.25\times10^{10}$ ✗. Square each option to test it quickly against the area ratio $1:250\,000$.
Answer: C  (scale = $1:500$)

Question. The actual area of a reservoir is \(1\,200\ \text{m}^{2}\). On a map its area is \(75\ \text{cm}^{2}\). Find the scale of the map.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Convert the actual area to \(\text{cm}^{2}\): \(1\,200\times10\,000=12\,000\,000\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Step 2. Ratio of areas \(=\dfrac{75}{12\,000\,000}=\dfrac{1}{160\,000}\).
Step 3. The scale compares lengths,
so take the square root: \(\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{160\,000}}=\dfrac{1}{400}\).
Step 4. Check: \((1:400)^{2}=1:160\,000\), which matches the area ratio ✓. The scale is \(1:400\).
Answer: \(1:400\)
