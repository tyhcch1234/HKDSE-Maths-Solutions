HKDSE 2023 Paper 2 · Q44 · 15. Statistics

The table below shows the scores (in marks) and the corresponding standard scores of three students in an examination.

Score (marks) 46 x 86
Standard score −3 1 2

Find x.

  • A. 64
  • B. 66
  • C. 70
  • D. 78

Step-by-step Solution

1
Standard Score \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\) Each row of the table gives one equation, e.g. score \(46\) with \(z=-3\) means \(\mu-3\sigma=46\).
Set up the standard-score definition

Let $\mu$ be the mean score and $\sigma$ the standard deviation.
The standard score is \[ z = \frac{\text{score}-\mu}{\sigma}. \]

Known data: score $46 \leftrightarrow z=-3$,
score $x \leftrightarrow z=1$, score $86 \leftrightarrow z=2$.

Every row of the table gives one linear equation in the two unknowns $\mu$ and $\sigma$.
2
Clearing Fractions in an Equation \(\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}=z\iff x-\mu=z\sigma\) Multiplying by \(\sigma\) turns the two rows into \(\mu-3\sigma=46\) and \(\mu+2\sigma=86\).
Form two equations from the known scores

From score 46 with $z=-3$: \[ \frac{46-\mu}{\sigma} = -3 \;\Longrightarrow\; \mu - 3\sigma = 46 \quad \text{… (1)} \]

From score 86 with $z=2$: \[ \frac{86-\mu}{\sigma} = 2 \;\Longrightarrow\; \mu + 2\sigma = 86 \quad \text{… (3)} \]

Rearranging avoids fractions — multiply through by $\sigma$.
3Solve for $\mu$ and $\sigma$

Subtract (1) from (3): \[ (\mu+2\sigma)-(\mu-3\sigma)=86-46 \;\Rightarrow\; 5\sigma=40 \;\Rightarrow\; \sigma=8. \]

Substitute into (3): \[ \mu + 2(8)=86 \;\Rightarrow\; \mu = 70. \]

The gap $86-46=40$ spans $2-(-3)=5$ standard deviations, so each standard deviation is $40/5=8$ marks.
4
Score from a Standard Score \(x=\mu+z\sigma\) With \(\mu=70\) and \(\sigma=8\), the score with \(z=1\) is \(70+8=78\).
Find $x$ using $z=1$

Score $x$ has standard score 1,
i.e. \[ \frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; x=\mu+\sigma. \]

Put $\mu=70$ and $\sigma=8$: \[ x = 70 + 8 = \boxed{78}. \]

Check: $x=78$ lies exactly one standard deviation (8 marks) above the mean 70.
Answer: D.   $x = 78$ (with $\mu = 70$, $\sigma = 8$)
