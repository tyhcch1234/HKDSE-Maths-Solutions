HKDSE Math 2024 Paper 1 Q16

Probability with Combinations

Red & White Cups – Draw 5 without replacement

Combinations \(C^n_r\) Probability Complementary Events

📋 Question

In a bag, there are 16 red cups and 4 white cups. If 5 cups are randomly drawn from the bag at the same time, find

  1. the probability that exactly 1 white cup is drawn; (2 marks)
  2. the probability that at most 3 red cups are drawn. (2 marks)

🎨 Visualise the Bag

Red (16) White (4)

Total 20 cups in the bag. 5 will be drawn at random.

Hint: "Drawn at the same time" means order does not matter → use combinations \(C^n_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}\).

Total cups = \(16 + 4 = 20\). We choose 5 from 20.

\[ \text{Total outcomes} = C^{20}_5 = 15504 \]

Hint: Choosing exactly 1 white cup means the other 4 cups must be red.

Choose \(1\) white from \(4\): \(C^4_1\)

Choose \(4\) red from \(16\): \(C^{16}_4\)

Favourable outcomes = \(C^4_1 \times C^{16}_4\)

\[ P(\text{exactly 1 white}) = \frac{C^{16}_4 \, C^4_1}{C^{20}_5} \]

\[ = \frac{1820 \times 4}{15504} = \frac{7280}{15504} = \frac{455}{969} \]

✅ Answer for (a): \(\dfrac{455}{969}\)

Hint: Using the complement is easier! "At most 3 red" means \(0,1,2,\) or \(3\) red. The complement is \(4\) or \(5\) red.

Since we draw 5 cups, the only way to have more than 3 red cups is to draw 4 red or 5 red.

Complement event: \(P(\text{at least 4 red}) = P(4R) + P(5R)\)

Remember: total red = 16, so 4R means 1 white; 5R means 0 white.

\(P(4\text{ red}) = \dfrac{C^{16}_4 C^4_1}{C^{20}_5} = \dfrac{455}{969}\) (same as part a)

\(P(5\text{ red}) = \dfrac{C^{16}_5}{C^{20}_5} = \dfrac{4368}{15504} = \dfrac{91}{323} = \dfrac{273}{969}\)

\[ P(\text{at least 4 red}) = \frac{455}{969} + \frac{273}{969} = \frac{728}{969} \]

So, \(P(\text{at most 3 red}) = 1 - \dfrac{728}{969} = \dfrac{241}{969}\)

✅ Answer for (b): \(\dfrac{241}{969}\)

  • Use combinations \(C^n_r\) when order doesn't matter.
  • Favourable outcomes = product of combinations for each colour.
  • For "at most" problems, consider the complement event to simplify.

Final answers:

(a) \(\dfrac{455}{969}\)  (b) \(\dfrac{241}{969}\)
