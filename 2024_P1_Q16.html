Question 16

In a bag, there are 16 red cups and 4 white cups. If 5 cups are randomly drawn from the bag at the same time, find
(a) the probability that exactly 1 white cup is drawn; (2 marks)
(b) the probability that at most 3 red cups are drawn. (2 marks)
0Set up the counting model
The bag contains $16$ red and $4$ white cups, so $N = 16+4 = 20$ cups in total. We draw $5$ cups at the same time, so the order does not matter — use combinations
Combination \({}^{n}C_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}\) Five cups are drawn at the same time, so order is irrelevant and the sample space has \({}^{20}C_{5}=15\,504\) outcomes.
.
"At the same time" ⇒ unordered sample ⇒ denominator $C_5^{20}$.
1(a) Identify the favourable case
Exactly $1$ white cup drawn means we draw 1 white and 4 red. Number of ways: $C_1^{4} \times C_4^{16}$.
Split the draw into "white part" × "red part".
2(a) Compute the probability
\[ \frac{C_1^{4}\,C_4^{16}}{C_5^{20}} = \frac{4 \times 1820}{15504} = \frac{7280}{15504} = \frac{455}{969} \]
Cancel the common factor $16$: $\dfrac{7280}{15504}=\dfrac{455}{969}$.
3(b) Use the complement
Complement Rule \(P(A^{\prime})=1-P(A)\) The complement of "at most \(3\) red" is "\(4\) red or \(5\) red", which is much shorter to count.
"At most 3 red cups" is hard to list directly, so use the complement: \[ P(\text{at most }3 \text{ red}) = 1 - P(4 \text{ red or } 5 \text{ red}) \]
Complement of "at most 3 red" is "4 red or 5 red".
4(b) Evaluate the complement
$P(4 \text{ red}) = \dfrac{C_4^{16}}{C_5^{20}} = \dfrac{1820}{15504} = \dfrac{91}{323}$ (draw 1 white + 4 red)
Probability of Equally Likely Outcomes \(P(E)=\dfrac{n(E)}{n(S)}\) Each of the \({}^{20}C_{5}\) draws is equally likely, so \(P(4\text{ red})=\frac{1820}{15\,504}\) and \(P(5\text{ red})=\frac{4368}{15\,504}\).
$P(5 \text{ red}) = \dfrac{C_5^{16}}{C_5^{20}} = \dfrac{4368}{15504} = \dfrac{455}{969}$
Note $P(4\text{ red})=\frac{91}{323}$ and $P(5\text{ red})=\frac{455}{969}$ have common denominator $969$.
5(b) Finish the calculation
\[ 1 - \frac{91}{323} - \frac{455}{969} = 1 - \frac{273}{969} - \frac{455}{969} = 1 - \frac{728}{969} = \frac{241}{969} \]
Rewrite $\frac{91}{323}=\frac{273}{969}$ so all fractions share denominator $969$.
✓ Answer: (a) $\dfrac{455}{969}$     (b) $\dfrac{241}{969}$

Question. In a bag there are \(12\) red cups and \(8\) white cups. If \(4\) cups are randomly drawn at the same time, find (a) the probability that exactly \(1\) white cup is drawn,
(b) the probability that at most \(3\) red cups are drawn.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. There are \(20\) cups, and order does not matter, so the number of possible draws is \({}^{20}C_{4}=4845\).
Step 2. (a) Exactly \(1\) white means \(1\) white and \(3\) red: \({}^{8}C_{1}\times{}^{12}C_{3}=8\times220=1760\).
Step 3. So \(P(1\text{ white})=\dfrac{1760}{4845}=\dfrac{352}{969}\).
Step 4. (b) The complement of "at most \(3\) red" is "\(4\) red", i.e. \({}^{12}C_{4}=495\) draws.
Step 5. Hence \(P(\text{at most }3\text{ red})=1-\dfrac{495}{4845}=\dfrac{4350}{4845}=\dfrac{290}{323}\).
Answer: (a) \(\dfrac{352}{969}\); (b) \(\dfrac{290}{323}\)
