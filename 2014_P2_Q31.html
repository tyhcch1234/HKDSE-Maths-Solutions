HKDSE 2014 Paper 2 · Q31 · Polynomials

The H.C.F. of $3x^4y^2z$,  $4xy^5z$  and  $6x^2y^3$  is

A.  $xy^2$
B.  $xy^2z$
C.  $12x^4y^5z$
D.  $12x^7y^9z^2$

1Rewrite each expression with every power explicit

Zero Index Law \(a^{0}=1\quad(a\neq0)\) The third expression \(6x^{2}y^{3}\) has no \(z\), so it is written \(6x^{2}y^{3}z^{0}\) before powers are compared.

The H.C.F. trick: a missing variable is really a power of 0.

$3x^4y^2z = 3^{\,1}x^{\,4}y^{\,2}z^{\,1}$

$4xy^5z = 4^{\,1}x^{\,1}y^{\,5}z^{\,1}$

$6x^2y^3 = 6^{\,1}x^{\,2}y^{\,3}\color{#dc2626}{z^{\,0}}$

Hint: writing $z^0$ makes the third expression "comparable" with the other two.

2Compare powers of each variable

H.C.F. of Algebraic Terms \(\text{H.C.F.}\left(x^{a}y^{b},\ x^{c}y^{d}\right)=x^{\min(a,c)}y^{\min(b,d)}\) The smallest powers are \(x^{1}\), \(y^{2}\) and \(z^{0}\), so the variable part of the H.C.F. is \(xy^{2}\).
VariableExp 1Exp 2Exp 3Minimum
$x$4121
$y$2532
$z$1100
Hint: The H.C.F. takes the smallest power of each factor (the L.C.M. would take the largest).

3Deal with the numerical coefficients

The coefficients are $3$, $4$ and $6$.

$\mathrm{H.C.F.}(3,4,6) = 1$

Since $1$ contributes nothing,
it is simply omitted from the answer.

Hint: Only the variable parts matter here — the numerical H.C.F. is 1.

4Combine to form the H.C.F.

Quotient Law of Indices \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\) Dividing each expression by \(xy^{2}\) gives \(3x^{3}z\), \(4y^{3}z\) and \(6x\), confirming the common factor.

$\mathrm{H.C.F.} = x^{1}\,y^{2}\,z^{0} = x\,y^{2}$

Check: $xy^2$ divides each expression exactly:

$3x^4y^2z \div xy^2 = 3x^3z$  
·  $4xy^5z \div xy^2 = 4y^3z$  
·  $6x^2y^3 \div xy^2 = 6x$

Hint: A quick division check confirms the factor really is common to all three.
Answer:  A   $\boxed{xy^2}$

Question. Find the H.C.F. of \(8a^{3}b^{2}c\), \(12a^{2}b^{4}\) and \(20a^{5}bc^{2}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Write every power explicitly: \(8a^{3}b^{2}c^{1}\), \(12a^{2}b^{4}c^{0}\), \(20a^{5}b^{1}c^{2}\).
Step 2. Take the smallest power of each variable: \(a^{2}\), \(b^{1}\) and \(c^{0}=1\).
Step 3. H.C.F.\((8,\ 12,\ 20)=4\).
Step 4. Hence the H.C.F. is \(4a^{2}b\). Check: \(8a^{3}b^{2}c\div4a^{2}b=2abc\), \(12a^{2}b^{4}\div4a^{2}b=3b^{3}\) and \(20a^{5}bc^{2}\div4a^{2}b=5a^{3}c^{2}\) ✓.
Answer: \(4a^{2}b\)
