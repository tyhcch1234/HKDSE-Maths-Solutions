15. It is given that $\log_9 y$ is a linear function of $\log_3 x$. Denote the graph of the linear function by $L$. The slope of $L$ is $4$ and $L$ passes through the point $(5,\,22)$. Express $y$ in terms of $x$.

1 Set up the line equation
Slope $m=4$, passing through $(5,22)$ with variables $X=\log_3 x$ and $Y=\log_9 y$:
$$\log_9 y - 22 = 4(\log_3 x - 5)$$
Hint: point–slope form $Y-y_1=m(X-x_1)$ with $Y=\log_9 y$.
Point-Slope Form of a Line \(Y-y_{1}=m(X-x_{1})\) With \(m=4\) through \((5,22)\): \(\log_{9}y-22=4(\log_{3}x-5)\).
2 Use index laws to combine constants
$$\log_9 y = 4\log_3 x - 20 + 22 = 4\log_3 x + 2$$ Since $4\log_3 x = \log_3 x^4$ and $2 = \log_3 9$:
$$\log_9 y = \log_3 x^4 + \log_3 9 = \log_3 9x^4$$
Hint: turn every term into a single log (base 3).
3 Change base of $\log_9 y$ to base 3
Using $\log_b a = \dfrac{\log_c a}{\log_c b}$: $$\frac{\log_3 y}{\log_3 9} = \log_3 9x^4 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{\log_3 y}{2} = \log_3 9x^4$$
Hint: $\log_3 9 = \log_3 3^2 = 2$.
Change of Base Formula \(\log_{b}a=\dfrac{\log_{c}a}{\log_{c}b}\) \(\log_{9}y=\dfrac{\log_{3}y}{\log_{3}9}=\dfrac{\log_{3}y}{2}\) because \(\log_{3}9=2\).
4 Remove logs and simplify
Multiply both sides by $2$: $$\log_3 y = 2\log_3 9x^4 = \log_3 (9x^4)^2$$ $$\therefore\; y = 81x^{8}$$
Hint: $(9x^4)^2 = 9^2 x^{8} = 81x^8$.
Power Law of Logarithms \(\log_{b}a^{n}=n\log_{b}a\) Multiplying by \(2\) gives \(\log_{3}y=\log_{3}(9x^{4})^{2}\), so \(y=81x^{8}\).
Answer: $\quad \boxed{y = 81x^{8}}$

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Point-slope form: \(\log_{4}y-10=3(\log_{2}x-2)\).
Step 2. Hence \(\log_{4}y=3\log_{2}x-6+10=3\log_{2}x+4\).
Step 3. Changing base, \(\log_{4}y=\dfrac{\log_{2}y}{\log_{2}4}=\dfrac{\log_{2}y}{2}\).
Step 4. So \(\dfrac{\log_{2}y}{2}=3\log_{2}x+4\), giving \(\log_{2}y=6\log_{2}x+8=\log_{2}x^{6}+\log_{2}256\).
Step 5. Therefore \(y=256x^{6}\).
Answer: \(y=256x^{6}\)
