HKDSE Math 2024 Paper 1 Q15 Logarithms Linear Functions Change of Base

Express \(y\) in terms of \(x\)

It is given that \(\log_9 y\) is a linear function of \(\log_3 x\). Denote the graph of the linear function by \(L\). The slope of \(L\) is \(4\) and \(L\) passes through the point \((5,22)\). Express \(y\) in terms of \(x\). (3 marks)

The graph is a straight line when plotting \(\log_9 y\) against \(\log_3 x\). Use the point, slope, and log laws to eliminate the logs.
1 Set up the linear equation ›

Let \(u = \log_3 x\) and \(v = \log_9 y\). The graph of \(v\) against \(u\) is a straight line \(L\) with slope \(4\) passing through \((5,22)\).

\[ v - 22 = 4(u - 5) \]
Hint: Slope‐point form: \(y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)\). Here the variables are \(v\) (on vertical axis) and \(u\) (on horizontal axis).
2 Substitute back \(u\) and \(v\) ›

Replace \(u = \log_3 x\) and \(v = \log_9 y\) into the linear equation:

\[ \log_9 y - 22 = 4(\log_3 x - 5) \]

This equation relates \(\log_9 y\) and \(\log_3 x\) directly. To solve for \(y\), we need to consolidate the logs. Since the bases are different (9 and 3), we will first rearrange the terms.

3 Simplify the right-hand side ›

Expand and simplify:

\[ \begin{aligned} \log_9 y - 22 &= 4\log_3 x - 20 \\ \log_9 y &= 4\log_3 x + 2 \end{aligned} \]
Hint: Add \(22\) to both sides. Then use the power law: \(a\log_b x = \log_b x^a\). So \(4\log_3 x = \log_3 x^4\).
4 Change of base & combine logs ›

Use the change-of-base formula to express \(\log_9 y\) in base \(3\):

\[ \log_9 y = \frac{\log_3 y}{\log_3 9} = \frac{\log_3 y}{2} \]

Substitute into the equation from Step 3:

\[ \frac{\log_3 y}{2} = 4\log_3 x + 2 \]

Multiply both sides by \(2\):

\[ \log_3 y = 8\log_3 x + 4 \]
5 Express \(y\) in terms of \(x\) ›

Write \(8\log_3 x + 4\) as a single logarithm:

\[ \begin{aligned} \log_3 y &= \log_3 x^8 + \log_3 3^4 \\ &= \log_3 (81 x^8) \end{aligned} \]

Since the logarithms are equal and one-to-one, we have:

\[ \boxed{y = 81x^8} \]
Hint: \(4 = \log_3 3^4 = \log_3 81\). Then \(\log_3 y = \log_3 (81x^8)\) implies \(y = 81x^8\).
