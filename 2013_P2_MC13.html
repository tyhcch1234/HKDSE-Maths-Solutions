Variation Direct & Inverse Proportion Percentage Change
Q

It is given that \(z\) varies directly as \(x\) and inversely as \(\sqrt{y}\). If \(y\) is decreased by \(64\%\) and \(z\) is increased by \(25\%\), then \(x\)

  1. A. is increased by \(20\%\).
  2. B. is increased by \(80\%\).
  3. C. is decreased by \(25\%\).
  4. D. is decreased by \(75\%\).

🧩 Step-by-Step Solution

Since \(z\) varies directly as \(x\) and inversely as \(\sqrt{y}\), we can write:

\[ z = k\,\frac{x}{\sqrt{y}} \]

where \(k\) is a non-zero constant. This means \(z\) is proportional to \(x\) and inversely proportional to the square root of \(y\).

Let \(p\) be the percentage change in \(x\) (as a decimal). Then:

  • \(y\) is decreased by \(64\%\) → new \(y = (1 - 0.64)y\)
  • \(z\) is increased by \(25\%\) → new \(z = (1 + 0.25)z\)
  • \(x\) becomes \((1 + p)x\)

💡 Hint

A \(25\%\) increase means multiplying by \(1.25\). A \(64\%\) decrease means multiplying by \(0.36\) (\(1 - 0.64\)).

The constant \(k\) remains unchanged, so we can equate the two expressions for \(k\):

\[ \frac{(1+0.25)z\,\sqrt{(1-0.64)y}}{(1+p)x} = \frac{z\sqrt{y}}{x} \]

This equates the new \(k\) (left) with the original \(k\) (right).

Cancel \(z\) and \(\sqrt{y}\) from both sides:

\[ \frac{1.25 \times \sqrt{0.36}}{1+p} = 1 \]

Since \(\sqrt{0.36} = 0.6\):

\[ \frac{1.25 \times 0.6}{1+p} = 1 \]

\[ 0.75 = 1 + p \]

\[ p = -0.25 \]

Since \(p = -0.25\), \(x\) is changed by \(-25\%\).

Therefore, \(x\) is decreased by \(25\%\).

✅ Correct answer: C

🔑 Key Insight

When a quantity varies directly or inversely, changes can be handled by ratio rather than substitution. Here, \(\sqrt{y}\) decreases by \(40\%\) (since \(\sqrt{0.36} = 0.6\)), which increases the overall expression. To keep \(k\) constant, \(x\) must decrease to compensate.
