Question 10. Mensuration

[2020 #12]

The height and the base radius of a solid right circular cone are \(36\text{ cm}\) and \(15\text{ cm}\) respectively. The circular cone is divided into three parts by two planes which are parallel to its base. The heights of the three parts are equal. Express, in terms of \(\pi\),

  1. the volume of the middle part of the circular cone; 3 marks
  2. the curved surface area of the middle part of the circular cone. 3 marks

Part (a) — Volume of the Middle Part 3 marks

show 3 / 3
Step 11M

By similar triangles, when a cone of height \(h\) is cut from the apex, its radius is proportional to its height. The full cone has height \(36\) and base radius \(15\), so the radius at height \(h\) from the apex is

\[ r(h)=\frac{15}{36}\,h=\frac{5}{12}h. \]
Hint: The three parts are equal in height, so each has height \(36\div 3=12\text{ cm}\).
Step 21M

Volume of a cone of height \(h\) and radius \(\frac{5}{12}h\) is

\[ V(h)=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}h=\frac{1}{3}\pi\left(\frac{5h}{12}\right)^{2}h. \]

Hence

\[ V(h)=\frac{1}{3}\pi\cdot\frac{25h^{2}}{144}\cdot h=\frac{25\pi}{432}h^{3}. \]
Hint: The middle part = cone of height \(24\) minus cone of height \(12\).
Step 31A

Volume of the middle part:

\[ V(24)-V(12)=\frac{25\pi}{432}\left(24^{3}-12^{3}\right). \] \[ =\frac{25\pi}{432}\left(13824-1728\right)=\frac{25\pi}{432}\times 12096. \] \[ =\frac{25\pi}{432}\times 12096=700\pi. \]
Volume of the middle part \(=700\pi\text{ cm}^{3}\)

Part (b) — Curved Surface Area of the Middle Part 3 marks

show 3 / 3
Step 11M

The slant height of the full cone is

\[ l=\sqrt{15^{2}+36^{2}}=\sqrt{225+1296}=\sqrt{1521}=39\text{ cm}. \]
Hint: The slant height of any similar sub-cone with height \(h\) is \(\frac{39}{36}h=\frac{13}{12}h\).
Step 21M+1M

The curved surface area of a cone of height \(h\) (and hence radius \(\frac{5}{12}h\), slant \(\frac{13}{12}h\)) is

\[ S(h)=\pi r l=\pi\left(\frac{5}{12}h\right)\left(\frac{13}{12}h\right)=\frac{65\pi}{144}h^{2}. \]

The middle part's curved surface is the lateral surface of the cone of height \(24\) minus the lateral surface of height \(12\):

\[ S(24)-S(12)=\frac{65\pi}{144}\left(24^{2}-12^{2}\right). \]
Hint: Only the curved surface of the middle part counts—the top and bottom circular faces are cut faces, not part of the curved surface area.
Step 31A

Compute:

\[ \frac{65\pi}{144}\left(576-144\right)=\frac{65\pi}{144}\times 432=195\pi. \]
Curved surface area \(=195\pi\text{ cm}^{2}\)

Summary of Answers

  • Height of each of the three equal parts: \(36\div 3=12\text{ cm}\).
  • Radius–height relation: \(r(h)=\frac{5}{12}h\).
  • (a) Volume of the middle part: \(700\pi\text{ cm}^{3}\)
  • Slant height of the full cone: \(l=\sqrt{15^{2}+36^{2}}=39\text{ cm}\).
  • Lateral area of the sub-cone: \(S(h)=\frac{65\pi}{144}h^{2}\).
  • (b) Curved surface area of the middle part: \(195\pi\text{ cm}^{2}\)
  • Key idea: the sub-cone from the apex is similar to the whole cone, so lengths scale by the same factor and volumes scale by its cube.
