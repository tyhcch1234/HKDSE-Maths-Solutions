2020 #12Mensuration — Circular Cone

The height and the base radius of a solid right circular cone are 36 cm and 15 cm respectively. The circular cone is divided into three parts by two planes which are parallel to its base. The heights of the three parts are equal.

Express, in terms of $\pi$,

(a) the volume of the middle part of the circular cone; [3 marks]

(b) the curved surface area of the middle part of the circular cone. [3 marks]

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1 · Set up similar figures
Similar Figures (Ratio of Lengths)\(r_{1}:r_{2}:r_{3}=12:24:36=1:2:3\)The two cutting planes give similar cones of height \(12\), \(24\), \(36\), so the radii are \(5\), \(10\), \(15\).

The full cone: height $H=36$, base radius $R=15$. Two cutting planes parallel to the base divide the height into three equal parts of $12$ cm each.

From the apex,
the small similar cones have heights $12$, $24$ and $36$ cm.
The radii are proportional:

$$r_1 = 15\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}=5,\quad r_2=15\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}=10,\quad r_3=15.$$
STEP 2 · Volume of middle part — method

The middle solid is the difference between the cone of height $24$ and the cone of height $12$ (subtracting volumes with the same apex):

$$V_{\text{middle}}=\pi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{3}\right)(15^2)(36)\left(\frac{2^3-1^3}{3^3}\right).$$

Check the general formula $V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi R^2H\!\left(\dfrac{2^3-1^3}{3^3}\right)$.

STEP 3 · Volume — compute
Volume of a Cone\(V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}h\)The middle part is \(\dfrac{1}{3}\pi(10)^{2}(24)-\dfrac{1}{3}\pi(5)^{2}(12)=800\pi-100\pi=700\pi\).
$$V=\tfrac{1}{3}\pi(225)(36)\cdot\frac{7}{27}=\pi\cdot(225)(12)\cdot\frac{7}{27}.$$ $$=2700\pi\cdot\frac{7}{27}=700\pi\ \text{cm}^3.$$
STEP 4 · Slant heights for part (b)

Slant height of the full cone: $l=\sqrt{15^2+36^2}=\sqrt{1521}=39$ cm.

Similar cones: $l_1=39\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}=13$, $l_2=39\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}=26$.

STEP 5 · Curved surface area of middle part
Curved Surface Area of a Cone\(\text{CSA}=\pi r l\)Subtracting gives \(\pi(10)(26)-\pi(5)(13)=260\pi-65\pi=195\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\).

Curved surface area of middle frustum = (CSA of cone $l_2$) − (CSA of cone $l_1$):

$$A=\pi(15)\sqrt{15^2+36^2}\left(\frac{2^2-1^2}{3^2}\right)=\pi(15)(39)\cdot\frac{3}{9}.$$ $$=585\pi\cdot\frac{1}{3}=195\pi\ \text{cm}^2.$$
Answers:
(a) Volume of middle part $=700\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
(b) Curved surface area of middle part $=195\pi\ \text{cm}^2$

Question. The height and the base radius of a solid right circular cone are \(12\ \text{cm}\) and \(9\ \text{cm}\) respectively. The cone is divided into three parts by two planes parallel to its base, and the heights of the three parts are equal. Express, in terms of \(\pi\),
(a) the volume of the middle part,
(b) the curved surface area of the middle part.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The three parts each have height \(4\ \text{cm}\), so from the apex the similar cones have heights \(4\), \(8\), \(12\) and radii \(3\), \(6\), \(9\) (ratio \(1:2:3\)).
Step 2. Volume of middle part \(=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi(6)^{2}(8)-\dfrac{1}{3}\pi(3)^{2}(4)=96\pi-12\pi=84\pi\ \text{cm}^{3}\).
Step 3. Slant height of the full cone: \(l=\sqrt{9^{2}+12^{2}}=15\ \text{cm}\), so \(l_{1}=5\) and \(l_{2}=10\).
Step 4. Curved surface area \(=\pi(6)(10)-\pi(3)(5)=60\pi-15\pi=45\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Answer: (a) \(84\pi\ \text{cm}^{3}\) (b) \(45\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\)
💡 Key idea: cutting planes parallel to the base give similar cones, so all lengths (radii, slant heights) scale with the linear ratio, and areas scale with its square.
