Question
Let $f(x)=2x^{2}+4mx+8x+2m^{2}+8m+n$, where $m$ and $n$ are real constants such that $mn<0$. Denote the vertex of the graph of $y=f(x)$ by $P$.
  1. Using the method of completing the square, express the coordinates of $P$ in terms of $m$ and $n$. (2 marks)
  2. Describe the geometric meaning represented by transforming $f(x)$ to $f\!\left(\dfrac{x}{5}\right)+7$. (2 marks)
  3. Denote the vertex of the graph of $y=f\!\left(\dfrac{x}{5}\right)+7$ by $Q$. Let $(a_1,b_1)$ and $(a_2,b_2)$ be the coordinates of $P$ and $Q$ respectively. It is given that $a_1,\,1+n,\,a_2$ is an arithmetic sequence and $b_1,\,4-m,\,b_2$ is a geometric sequence.
    1. Find the coordinates of $P$ and $Q$.
    2. The coordinates of the points $R$ and $S$ are $(3t+27,\,t)$ and $(3t+3,\,2t-3)$ respectively, where $t$ is a real number. Is it possible that $PQRS$ is a rhombus? Explain your answer.
    (8 marks)
STEP 1 · Completing the square

Group the $x$-terms and complete the square:

$$f(x)=2x^{2}+4mx+8x+2m^{2}+8m+n$$

$$=2\bigl(x^{2}+2mx+4x\bigr)+2m^{2}+8m+n$$

$$=2\bigl[x^{2}+2(m+2)x+(m+2)^{2}-(m+2)^{2}\bigr]+2m^{2}+8m+n$$

$$=2\bigl[x+(m+2)\bigr]^{2}-2(m+2)^{2}+2m^{2}+8m+n$$

$$=2(x+m+2)^{2}+n-8$$

Check: $-2(m^{2}+4m+4)+2m^{2}+8m+n=-2m^{2}-8m-8+2m^{2}+8m+n=n-8$ ✓
Completing the Square \(ax^{2}+bx+c=a\left(x+\dfrac{b}{2a}\right)^{2}+c-\dfrac{b^{2}}{4a}\) Taking out \(2\) from the \(x\)-terms and adding back \(2(m+2)^{2}\) gives \(f(x)=2(x+m+2)^{2}+n-8\), so the vertex is \(P(-m-2,\,n-8)\).
STEP 2 · Coordinates of $P$

The vertex form $y=2(x+m+2)^{2}+(n-8)$ gives the vertex at

$$P=(-m-2,\;n-8).$$

Answer (a): $\;P\left(-m-2,\;n-8\right)$
STEP 3 · Geometric meaning of $f(x/5)+7$

Replacing $x$ by $\dfrac{x}{5}$ (i.e. $x\mapsto 5x$) stretches the graph horizontally by a factor of $5$ about the $y$-axis, and adding $7$ translates the graph upwards by $7$ units.

Answer (b): The graph is enlarged $5$ times along the $x$-axis, then translated $7$ units upwards.
Horizontal Stretch and Vertical Translation \(y=f\left(\dfrac{x}{k}\right)+c\ \text{stretches by }k\text{ along the }x\text{-axis, then shifts up }c\) Replacing \(x\) by \(\dfrac{x}{5}\) widens the graph \(5\) times horizontally and the \(+7\) lifts it \(7\) units.
STEP 4 · Vertex $Q$

For $y=f\!\left(\dfrac{x}{5}\right)+7$, the vertex is obtained from $P$ by $(x,y)\mapsto(5x,\,y+7)$:

$$Q=\bigl(5(-m-2),\,(n-8)+7\bigr)=(-5m-10,\;n-1).$$

STEP 5 · Solve for $m$ and $n$

Here $a_1=-m-2$, $a_2=-5m-10$ and $b_1=n-8$, $b_2=n-1$.

Arithmetic sequence $a_1,\,1+n,\,a_2$: $\;2(1+n)=a_1+a_2$

$$2+2n=(-m-2)+(-5m-10)=-6m-12$$

$$2n=-6m-14 \;\Rightarrow\; n=-3m-7. \tag{1}$$

Geometric sequence $b_1,\,4-m,\,b_2$: $\;(4-m)^{2}=b_1b_2$

$$(4-m)^{2}=(n-8)(n-1).$$

Substitute (1): $n-8=-3m-15$, $\;n-1=-3m-8$:

$$m^{2}-8m+16=9m^{2}+69m+120$$

$$8m^{2}+77m+104=0 \;\Rightarrow\;(8m+13)(m+8)=0.$$

So $m=-\dfrac{13}{8}$ or $m=-8$. Since $mn<0$ and $n=-3m-7$:

$m=-8 \Rightarrow n=17$ (product $-136<0$ ✓); $\;m=-\frac{13}{8}\Rightarrow n=\frac{39}{8}-7=-\frac{17}{8}$ (product $\frac{221}{64}>0$ ✗).

Hence $\;m=-8,\;n=17$.

STEP 6 · Coordinates of $P$ and $Q$

$$P=(-m-2,\;n-8)=(8-2,\;17-8)=(6,\,9)$$

$$Q=(-5m-10,\;n-1)=(40-10,\;16)=(30,\,16)$$

Answer (c)(i): $\;P(6,\,9),\;Q(30,\,16)$
STEP 7 · Slope condition $PQ \parallel RS$

Slope of $PQ$: $\;\dfrac{16-9}{30-6}=\dfrac{7}{24}$.

$R(3t+27,\,t)$, $S(3t+3,\,2t-3)$. Slope of $RS$:

$$\frac{t-(2t-3)}{(3t+27)-(3t+3)}=\frac{-t+3}{24}.$$

For $PQ\parallel RS$: $\dfrac{-t+3}{24}=\dfrac{7}{24}\;\Rightarrow\;-t+3=7\;\Rightarrow\;t=-4.$

Parallel Lines Have Equal Slopes \(PQ\parallel RS\ \Longleftrightarrow\ m_{PQ}=m_{RS}\) \(\dfrac{-t+3}{24}=\dfrac{7}{24}\) gives \(t=-4\), which is then tested for equal side lengths.
STEP 8 · Check rhombus $PQRS$

When $t=-4$: $R(15,-4)$, $S(-9,-11)$.

$$PQ=\sqrt{(30-6)^{2}+(16-9)^{2}}=\sqrt{24^{2}+7^{2}}=25$$

$$RS=\sqrt{(15-(-9))^{2}+(-4-(-11))^{2}}=\sqrt{24^{2}+7^{2}}=25$$

$$QR=\sqrt{(30-15)^{2}+(16-(-4))^{2}}=\sqrt{15^{2}+20^{2}}=25$$

So $PQ=QR=RS=25$ and $PQ\parallel RS$.

Answer (c)(ii): Yes. When $t=-4$, $PQ=QR=RS$ and $PQ\parallel RS$, so $PQRS$ is a rhombus.

Question. Let \(f(x)=2x^{2}+4mx+12x+2m^{2}+12m+n\), where \(m\) and \(n\) are real constants. (a) Using the method of completing the square, express the coordinates of the vertex \(P\) of the graph of \(y=f(x)\) in terms of \(m\) and \(n\). (b) Describe the geometric meaning of transforming \(f(x)\) to \(f\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)+5\). (c) Denote the vertex of the graph of \(y=f\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)+5\) by \(Q\). Express the coordinates of \(Q\) in terms of \(m\) and \(n\). (d) Let \((a_{1},b_{1})\) and \((a_{2},b_{2})\) be the coordinates of \(P\) and \(Q\). Given that \(a_{1},\ 4+n,\ a_{2}\) is an arithmetic sequence, find \(n\) in terms of \(m\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(f(x)=2\left(x^{2}+2mx+6x\right)+2m^{2}+12m+n=2\left[x^{2}+2(m+3)x+(m+3)^{2}\right]-2(m+3)^{2}+2m^{2}+12m+n\).
Step 2. Simplify the constant: \(-2(m^{2}+6m+9)+2m^{2}+12m+n=n-18\). Hence \(f(x)=2(x+m+3)^{2}+n-18\) and \(P(-m-3,\ n-18)\).
Step 3. (b) Replacing \(x\) by \(\dfrac{x}{3}\) enlarges the graph \(3\) times along the \(x\)-axis about the \(y\)-axis, and adding \(5\) translates it \(5\) units upwards.
Step 4. (c) The map \((x,y)\mapsto(3x,\ y+5)\) sends \(P\) to \(Q(-3m-9,\ n-13)\).
Step 5. (d) Here \(a_{1}=-m-3\) and \(a_{2}=-3m-9\). Since the three terms are in AP, \(2(4+n)=a_{1}+a_{2}=-4m-12\).
Step 6. So \(8+2n=-4m-12\), giving \(n=-2m-10\).
Answer: (a) \(P(-m-3,\ n-18)\); (b) enlarged \(3\) times along the \(x\)-axis, then shifted \(5\) units up; (c) \(Q(-3m-9,\ n-13)\); (d) \(n=-2m-10\)
