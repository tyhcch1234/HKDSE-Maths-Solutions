HKDSE 2018 · Paper 2 · Q3

Factorise $h^2 - 6h - 4k^2 - 12k$.

A. $(h-2k)(h-2k+6)$
B. $(h-2k)(h+2k+6)$
C. $(h+2k)(h-2k-6)$
D. $(h+2k)(h+2k-6)$

Step-by-step Solution

1Group the terms in pairs

Look for a difference of two squares and a shared factor.

$h^2 - 6h - 4k^2 - 12k \;=\; \underbrace{(h^2 - 4k^2)}_{\text{D.O.S.}} \;-\; \underbrace{(6h + 12k)}_{\text{common }6}$

Hint: rearranging $h^2 - 4k^2$ is natural because $h^2$ and $4k^2$ are both perfect squares.

2Factorise each group
Common Factor Law\(6h+12k=6(h+2k)\)Taking out \(6\) from the second group produces the same bracket \((h+2k)\) as the first group.
Difference of Two Squares\(a^{2}-b^{2}=(a-b)(a+b)\)With \(a=h\) and \(b=2k\), the group \(h^{2}-4k^{2}\) becomes \((h+2k)(h-2k)\).

$h^2 - 4k^2 = (h+2k)(h-2k)$  (difference of squares)

$6h + 12k = 6(h+2k)$  (take out the common factor $6$)

So:   $h^2 - 6h - 4k^2 - 12k = (h+2k)(h-2k) - 6(h+2k)$

Hint: watch for the new common bracket $(h+2k)$ appearing in both parts.

3Take out the common factor $(h+2k)$
Common Binomial Factor\(A\cdot B-6A=A(B-6)\)Both terms contain the bracket \((h+2k)\), so it is taken out to give \((h+2k)\big[(h-2k)-6\big]\).

Both terms contain the bracket $(h+2k)$:

$(h+2k)(h-2k) - 6(h+2k) = (h+2k)\big[(h-2k) - 6\big]$

Hint: this is the key move — a common binomial factor, not just a common number.

4Simplify the remaining bracket

$(h+2k)\big[(h-2k) - 6\big] = (h+2k)(h - 2k - 6)$

Check by expanding:

$(h+2k)(h-2k-6) = h^2 - 2hk - 6h + 2kh - 4k^2 - 12k$

$= h^2 - 6h - 4k^2 - 12k$ ✓   (the $-2hk$ and $+2kh$ terms cancel)

Hint: the answer must match the original expression exactly — this confirms the factorisation.

✓Answer: C  —  $(h+2k)(h-2k-6)$

Question. Factorize \(p^{2}-3p-9q^{2}-9q\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Group as a difference of squares and a common factor: \((p^{2}-9q^{2})-(3p+9q)\).
Step 2. \(p^{2}-9q^{2}=(p+3q)(p-3q)\) and \(3p+9q=3(p+3q)\).
Step 3. So the expression is \((p+3q)(p-3q)-3(p+3q)=(p+3q)(p-3q-3)\).
Step 4. Check by expanding: \(p^{2}-3pq-3p+3pq-9q^{2}-9q=p^{2}-3p-9q^{2}-9q\) ✓.
Answer: \((p+3q)(p-3q-3)\)
