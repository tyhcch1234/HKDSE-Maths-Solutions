HKDSE Math 2014 Paper 2 Q31 Polynomials H.C.F.

Highest Common Factor of Polynomials

The H.C.F. of \(3x^4y^2z\), \(4xy^5z\) and \(6x^2y^3\) is

A. \(xy^2\)
B. \(xy^2z\)
C. \(12x^4y^5z\)
D. \(12x^7y^9z^2\)
1

What is H.C.F.?

H.C.F. (Highest Common Factor) of algebraic expressions is the product of the lowest powers of each common variable, and the H.C.F. of the numerical coefficients.

💡 Hint: For numbers, H.C.F. = largest number that divides all coefficients. For variables, take the smallest exponent for each variable.
2

Rewrite each term with visible exponents

Make all powers explicit (including exponent 0 and 1):

\(3x^4y^2z = 3x^4y^2z^1\)
\(4xy^5z = 4x^1y^5z^1\)
\(6x^2y^3 = 6x^2y^3z^0\)
💡 Hint: Any variable not written has exponent 0. For example, \(6x^2y^3\) has no \(z\), so its \(z\)-power is \(0\). Also, \(x = x^1\).
3

Find the H.C.F. of coefficients

Coefficients are \(3,\ 4,\ 6\).

\(\text{H.C.F.}(3,4,6) = 1\)

💡 Hint: 3 and 4 are coprime, so their H.C.F. is 1. Therefore the overall numerical H.C.F. is 1.
4

Find the lowest power of each variable

Compare exponents across all three expressions:

Expression \(x\) power \(y\) power \(z\) power
\(3x^4y^2z^1\)421
\(4x^1y^5z^1\)151
\(6x^2y^3z^0\)230
Lowest power 1 2 0
💡 Hint: For H.C.F., we take the smallest exponent of each variable. For \(z\), the smallest exponent is \(0\) (since one term has no \(z\)).
5

Construct the H.C.F.

Multiply the H.C.F. of coefficients with the variables raised to the lowest powers:

\(\text{H.C.F.} = 1 \cdot x^{1} \cdot y^{2} \cdot z^{0} = xy^2\)

(Recall \(z^0 = 1\), so \(z\) does not appear in the answer.)

6

Final Answer

\(\therefore \text{The H.C.F. is } xy^2\)

Correct choice: A
