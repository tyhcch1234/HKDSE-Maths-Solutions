In the figure, \(AB = BC\) and \(AB \parallel CD\). Let \(E\) be the point of intersection of \(AD\) and \(BC\). If \(\angle ADC = 28^\circ\) and \(\angle AEB = 94^\circ\), then \(\angle CAD =\)
  1. \(30^\circ\)
  2. \(33^\circ\)
  3. \(36^\circ\)
  4. \(39^\circ\)
∠BAD = 28° (alt. ∠s, AB ∥ CD) ∠ACB = 61° ∠CAB = 61° ∠CAD = 33°

Drag the sliders to change \(\angle ADC\) and \(\angle AEB\). The figure is redrawn with the constraints \(AB=BC\) and \(AB \parallel CD\) always satisfied — notice how \(\angle CAD = \angle AEB - 2\angle ADC\) stays true.

Part (a) Use parallel lines to relate the angles 1M

∠BAD = 28° ∠ADC = 28° Because AB ∥ CD, the alternate angles are equal.
Step 11M

\(AD\) is a transversal cutting the parallel lines \(AB\) and \(CD\).

By alternate angles,

\[ \angle BAD = \angle ADC = 28^\circ \]
Identify the "Z-shape" formed by \(AB\), \(CD\) and the transversal \(AD\).
\(\angle BAD = 28^\circ\)

Part (b) Use \(AB = BC\) — the isosceles triangle 1M

∠CAB = 61° ∠ACB = 61° ∠ACD = 33° ∠CAD = ∠CAB − ∠BAD = 33°
Step 11M

Let \(x = \angle CAD\). From part (a), \(\angle BAD = 28^\circ\), so

\[ \angle CAB = \angle CAD + \angle DAB = x + 28^\circ \]
Split the angle at \(A\) into the two pieces we know or named.
Step 21M

In \(\triangle ABC\), \(AB = BC\) (given), so \(\triangle ABC\) is isosceles with the equal sides meeting at \(B\).

Hence the two base angles are equal:

\[ \angle ACB = \angle CAB = x + 28^\circ \]
In an isosceles triangle, angles opposite equal sides are equal (base ∠s, isos. Δ).
\(\angle ACB = \angle CAB = x + 28^\circ\)

Part (c) Use the exterior angle of \(\triangle ACE\) 1M+1A

∠CAE = x ∠ACE = x+28° ∠AEB = 94° x + (x + 28°) = 94°
Step 11M

Consider \(\triangle ACE\). Its two interior angles at \(A\) and \(C\) are

\[ \angle CAE = x \qquad\text{and}\qquad \angle ACE = \angle ACB = x + 28^\circ \]
\(\angle ACE\) is the same angle as \(\angle ACB\) since \(E\) lies on \(BC\).
Step 21M

By the exterior angle of a triangle, the exterior angle \(\angle AEB\) equals the sum of the two remote interior angles:

\[ \angle CAE + \angle ACE = \angle AEB \] \[ x + (x + 28^\circ) = 94^\circ \]
The exterior angle at \(E\) of \(\triangle ACE\) equals the sum of the opposite interior angles \(A\) and \(C\).
Step 31A

Solve for \(x\):

\[ 2x + 28^\circ = 94^\circ \] \[ 2x = 66^\circ \] \[ x = 33^\circ \]
\(\angle CAD = x = 33^\circ\) — Answer: B

Summary of Solution

  • Let \(x = \angle CAD\).
  • Alternate angles (AB ∥ CD): ∠BAD = ∠ADC = 28°
  • Isosceles ΔABC with AB = BC: ∠ACB = ∠CAB = x + 28°
  • Exterior angle of ΔACE: x + (x + 28°) = 94°
  • Solving: 2x = 66° ⇒ x = 33°
  • Final answer: ∠CAD = 33° (Option B)
