QUESTION

In the figure, $O$ is the centre of the circle $ABCD$. If $\angle BAO = 28^\circ$, $\angle BCD = 114^\circ$ and $\angle CDO = 42^\circ$, then $\angle ABC =$

  • A. $96^\circ$
  • B. $90^\circ$
  • C. $100^\circ$
  • D. $104^\circ$
STEP 1 · Isosceles △OAB

Join $OB$. In $\triangle OAB$,

$OA = OB$  (radii)

$\therefore \angle OAB = \angle OBA = 28^\circ$  (base $\angle$s, isos. $\triangle$)

STEP 2 · Isosceles △OCD

Join $OC$. In $\triangle OCD$,

$OC = OD$  (radii)

$\therefore \angle OCD = \angle ODC = 42^\circ$  (base $\angle$s, isos. $\triangle$)

Base Angles of an Isosceles Triangle \(OA=OB \Rightarrow \angle OAB=\angle OBA\) \(OA\) and \(OB\) are radii, so \( riangle OAB\) is isosceles.
STEP 3 · Split ∠BCD

$\angle BCD$ is split by the chord $OC$:

$\angle BCO = \angle BCD - \angle OCD = 114^\circ - 42^\circ = 72^\circ$

STEP 4 · Isosceles △OBC

In $\triangle OBC$,

$OB = OC$  (radii)

$\therefore \angle OBC = \angle BCO = 72^\circ$  (base $\angle$s, isos. $\triangle$)

Angle Sum of a Triangle \(\angle A+\angle B+\angle C=180^{\circ}\) In \( riangle OBC\), \(180^{\circ}-36^{\circ}=144^{\circ}\) is shared between two equal base angles.
STEP 5 · Add up ∠ABC

$\angle ABC = \angle OBA + \angle OBC = 28^\circ + 72^\circ$

Angle Sum at a Point \(\angle ABC=\angle OBA+\angle OBC\) The two base angles \(28^{\circ}\) and \(72^{\circ}\) make up \(ngle ABC\).
$\angle ABC = 100^\circ$  → Answer: C
Hint: The key move is drawing the two extra radii $OB$ and $OC$. Every radius is equal, so each of $\triangle OAB$, $\triangle OCD$ and $\triangle OBC$ is isosceles — equal base angles follow immediately.

Question. In the figure, \(O\) is the centre of the circle and \(A\), \(B\), \(C\) lie on it. \(OA=OB=OC\). If \(\angle AOB=40^{\circ}\) and \(\angle BOC=100^{\circ}\), find \(\angle ABC\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(\triangle OAB\) is isosceles with \(OA=OB\), so \(\angle OBA=\dfrac{180^{\circ}-40^{\circ}}{2}=70^{\circ}\).
Step 2. \(\triangle OBC\) is isosceles with \(OB=OC\), so \(\angle OBC=\dfrac{180^{\circ}-100^{\circ}}{2}=40^{\circ}\).
Step 3. \(\angle ABC=\angle OBA+\angle OBC=70^{\circ}+40^{\circ}=110^{\circ}\).
Step 4. Check with the angle at the centre: reflex \(\angle AOC=360^{\circ}-140^{\circ}=220^{\circ}\), and \(\angle ABC=\dfrac{220^{\circ}}{2}=110^{\circ}\). \(\checkmark\)
Answer: \(110^{\circ}\)
