T6 Q30

Solve the quadratic equation \(6x^2-x-2=0\).

Solution

Write the equation as $6x^2-x-2=0$.
Find two numbers whose product is $6\times(-2)=-12$ and whose sum is $-1$: these are $-4$ and $3$.
Split the middle term: $6x^2+3x-4x-2=0$.
Factor by grouping: $3x(2x+1)-2(2x+1)=0$, so $(3x-2)(2x+1)=0$.
Set each factor to zero: $3x-2=0$ or $2x+1=0$.
Solve: $x=\frac{2}{3}$ or $x=-\frac{1}{2}$.
Check by substitution: both values satisfy $6x^2-x-2=0$.
The solutions are $x=\frac{2}{3}$ and $x=-\frac{1}{2}$.
