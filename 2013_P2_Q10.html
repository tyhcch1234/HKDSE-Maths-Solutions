HKDSE 2013 Paper 2 — Q10

Topic: Percentages (profit % & loss %)

Susan sells two cars for $\$80\,080$ each. She gains $30\%$ on one and loses $30\%$ on the other. After the two transactions, Susan

  • A. loses $\$15\,840$.
  • B. gains $\$5\,544$.
  • C. gains $\$10\,296$.
  • D. has no gain and no loss. ( correct option is A in fact; see note)
Step 1Identify what is given

Two cars, each sold for $\$80\,080$.

Car A: gain $30\%$ $\Rightarrow$ selling price $= 130\%$ of cost.
Car B: loss $30\%$ $\Rightarrow$ selling price $= 70\%$ of cost.

A gain/loss % is always calculated on the cost, not the selling price.

Percentage Change\(\text{new value}=(1\pm r\%)\times\text{original value}\)A \(30\%\) gain makes the selling price \(\$80\,080\) equal to \(130\%\) of the cost.
Step 2Find the cost of the car with a $30\%$ gain

Selling price $=\$80\,080 = (1+30\%)\times C_A = 1.3\,C_A$

$$C_A=\frac{80\,080}{1.3}= \$61\,600$$

Divide by $1.3$, not multiply by $0.7$.

Reverse Percentage (finding the cost)\(C=\dfrac{\text{selling price}}{1+r\%}\)Dividing by \(1.3\) undoes the \(30\%\) mark-up and gives \(C_A=\$61\,600\).
Step 3Find the cost of the car with a $30\%$ loss

Selling price $=\$80\,080 = (1-30\%)\times C_B = 0.7\,C_B$

$$C_B=\frac{80\,080}{0.7}= \$114\,400$$

A $30\%$ loss means the $80\,080$ is only $70\%$ of the original cost.

Step 4Compare total selling price with total cost

Total selling price $= 2\times 80\,080 = \$160\,160$

Total cost $= 61\,600 + 114\,400 = \$176\,000$

$$\text{Profit} = 160\,160 - 176\,000 = -\$15\,840$$

A negative profit means an overall loss.

Profit and Loss\(\text{profit}=\text{total selling price}-\text{total cost}\)Subtracting the combined cost \(\$176\,000\) from the combined income \(\$160\,160\) gives \(-\$15\,840\).
Step 5Interpret the result

Since the answer is negative, Susan loses $\$15\,840$ overall.

Common trap: assuming a $30\%$ gain and a $30\%$ loss cancel out. They do not, because the percentages are taken on different base amounts.

Answer: A — Susan loses $\$15\,840$.
PRACTICESimilar question — try it yourself

Question. Two tablet computers are each sold for \(\$5\,280\). One is sold at a profit of \(20\%\) and the other at a loss of \(20\%\). Find the overall profit or loss.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. A \(20\%\) gain means \(\$5\,280=1.2\,C_1\), so \(C_1=\dfrac{5\,280}{1.2}=\$4\,400\).
Step 2. A \(20\%\) loss means \(\$5\,280=0.8\,C_2\), so \(C_2=\dfrac{5\,280}{0.8}=\$6\,600\).
Step 3. Total cost \(=4\,400+6\,600=\$11\,000\); total selling price \(=2\times5\,280=\$10\,560\).
Step 4. Profit \(=10\,560-11\,000=-\$440\), so there is an overall loss of \(\$440\). The \(20\%\) is taken on two different costs, so the two results do not cancel.
Answer: a loss of \(\$440\)
