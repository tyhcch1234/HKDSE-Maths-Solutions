Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Equation of Straight Line

T12 Q17

Find the slope of the straight line passing through the points $(1, 2)$ and $(3, 8)$.

Solution

Use the slope formula $m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$ with $(x_1,y_1)=(1,2)$ and $(x_2,y_2)=(3,8)$.
Substitute the coordinates: $m=\frac{8-2}{3-1}$.
Simplify: $m=\frac{6}{2}=3$.
The slope of the straight line is $3$.
