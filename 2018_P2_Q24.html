HKDSE 2018 · Paper 2 · MC24

Perimeter from Polar Coordinates

Question

The polar coordinates of the points \(C\), \(D\) and \(E\) are \((16,\,127^\circ)\), \((12,\,217^\circ)\) and \((5,\,307^\circ)\) respectively. Find the perimeter of \(\triangle CDE\).

  1. \(54\)
  2. \(78\)
  3. \(126\)
  4. \(130\)

Solution

Step 1 · Angle between \(OC\) and \(OD\) \[ \angle COD = 217^\circ - 127^\circ = 90^\circ, \]

so \(\triangle COD\) is right-angled at \(O\), and by the Pythagorean theorem,

\[ CD = \sqrt{16^{2} + 12^{2}} = \sqrt{256 + 144} = \sqrt{400} = 20. \]
Step 2 · Angle between \(OD\) and \(OE\) \[ \angle DOE = 307^\circ - 217^\circ = 90^\circ, \]

so \(\triangle DOE\) is right-angled at \(O\), and

\[ DE = \sqrt{12^{2} + 5^{2}} = \sqrt{144 + 25} = \sqrt{169} = 13. \]
Step 3 · Angle between \(OC\) and \(OE\) \[ \angle COE = 307^\circ - 127^\circ = 180^\circ, \]

so \(C\), \(O\), \(E\) are collinear with \(O\) between \(C\) and \(E\). Therefore

\[ CE = OC + OE = 16 + 5 = 21. \]
Step 4 · Perimeter \[ \text{Perimeter} = CD + DE + CE = 20 + 13 + 21 = 54. \]
Final Answer: A. \(54\)
