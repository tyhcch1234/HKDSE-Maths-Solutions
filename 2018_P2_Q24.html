Question
The polar coordinates of the points \(C\), \(D\) and \(E\) are \((16,\,127^\circ)\), \((12,\,217^\circ)\) and \((5,\,307^\circ)\) respectively. Find the perimeter of \(\triangle CDE\).
A 54
B 78
C 126
D 130
Solution
Step 1 · Angle between OC and OD

\[ \angle COD = 217^\circ - 127^\circ = 90^\circ \]

So \(\triangle COD\) is right-angled at \(O\). By the Pythagorean theorem,

\[ CD = \sqrt{16^{2} + 12^{2}} = \sqrt{256+144} = \sqrt{400} = 20 \]

Angle between Two Polar Coordinates \(\angle POQ=\theta_{\text{large}}-\theta_{\text{small}}\) \\(\angle COD=217^\circ-127^\circ=90^\circ\\), so \\(\triangle COD\\) is right-angled at the pole \\(O\\).
Step 2 · Angle between OD and OE

\[ \angle DOE = 307^\circ - 217^\circ = 90^\circ \]

So \(\triangle DOE\) is right-angled at \(O\), and

\[ DE = \sqrt{12^{2} + 5^{2}} = \sqrt{144+25} = \sqrt{169} = 13 \]

Pythagoras’ Theorem \(c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}\) With \\(\angle DOE=90^\circ\\), \\(DE=\sqrt{12^{2}+5^{2}}=13\\).
Step 3 · Angle between OC and OE

\[ \angle COE = 307^\circ - 127^\circ = 180^\circ \]

Hence \(C\), \(O\), \(E\) are collinear with \(O\) between \(C\) and \(E\). Therefore

\[ CE = OC + OE = 16 + 5 = 21 \]

Sum of Radii on Opposite Rays \(\angle COE=180^\circ\ \Longrightarrow\ CE=OC+OE\) \\(307^\circ-127^\circ=180^\circ\\), so \\(C,O,E\\) are collinear and \\(CE=16+5=21\\).
Step 4 · Perimeter

\[ \text{Perimeter} = CD + DE + CE = 20 + 13 + 21 = 54 \]

Tip: convert each polar point to a bearing difference — a \(90^\circ\) gap gives a right-angled triangle at the pole, and a \(180^\circ\) gap gives collinear points.
