4. Polynomials

Question

Let \( f(x) = x^3 + kx^2 + 5x + 10 \), where \( k \) is a constant. If \( f(x) \) is divisible by \( x + k \), find the remainder when \( f(x) \) is divided by \( x + 1 \).

A. −2
B. 2
C. 6
D. 18

✏️ Tap an option to check your answer.

Factor Theorem Remainder Theorem Polynomial Evaluation Solving for constant

Step-by-step Solution

1 Understand divisibility condition ▼

If a polynomial \( f(x) \) is divisible by \( x + k \), then \( x + k \) is a factor of \( f(x) \).

By the Factor Theorem, substituting \( x = -k \) gives zero:

\( f(-k) = 0 \)
Hint: What does Factor Theorem say?

Factor Theorem: \((x - a)\) is a factor of \( f(x) \) \(\iff\) \( f(a) = 0 \).

Here the factor is \( x + k = x - (-k) \), so we substitute \( x = -k \).

2 Substitute \( x = -k \) into \( f(x) \) ▼

Start with \( f(x) = x^3 + kx^2 + 5x + 10 \).

Replace every \( x \) with \( -k \):

\( f(-k) = (-k)^3 + k(-k)^2 + 5(-k) + 10 = 0 \)
(-k)^3 = \(-k^3\)
k(-k)^2 = \(k \cdot k^2 = k^3\)
5(-k) = \(-5k\)
+10 constant
Hint: Simplify \( k(-k)^2 \)

\( (-k)^2 = k^2 \). Then \( k \cdot k^2 = k^3 \).

3 Solve for \( k \) ▼

Combine like terms:

\( -k^3 + k^3 - 5k + 10 = 0 \)
\( -5k + 10 = 0 \)

Solving gives:

\( -5k = -10 \implies k = 2 \)
Hint: Why did \(-k^3\) and \(k^3\) cancel?

They are exact opposites: \(-k^3 + k^3 = 0\). This leaves a simple linear equation in \( k \).

4 Write the polynomial \( f(x) \) ▼

Now that \( k = 2 \), substitute back:

Resulting polynomial

\( f(x) = x^3 + 2x^2 + 5x + 10 \)
Hint: Check divisibility

We can verify that \( f(-2) = (-2)^3 + 2(-2)^2 + 5(-2) + 10 = -8 + 8 - 10 + 10 = 0 \).

So \( x + 2 \) is indeed a factor. ✅

5 Find remainder using Remainder Theorem ▼

When \( f(x) \) is divided by \( x + 1 \), the remainder is \( f(-1) \).

\( f(-1) = (-1)^3 + 2(-1)^2 + 5(-1) + 10 \)
\( = -1 + 2 - 5 + 10 \)
\( = 6 \)
Hint: Remainder Theorem

When a polynomial \( f(x) \) is divided by \( x - a \), the remainder is \( f(a) \).

Here \( x + 1 = x - (-1) \), so remainder = \( f(-1) \).

✅

Remainder = 6

Correct option: C
