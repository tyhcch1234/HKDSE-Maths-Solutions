Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Identities

T13 Q38

Simplify the expression $\cos\theta\tan\theta$.

Solution

Use the identity $\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$.
Substitute: $\cos\theta\tan\theta=\cos\theta\cdot\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$.
Cancel the common factor $\cos\theta$ (where $\cos\theta\ne0$): $\cos\theta\tan\theta=\sin\theta$.
The simplified expression is $\sin\theta$ (for $\cos\theta\ne0$).
