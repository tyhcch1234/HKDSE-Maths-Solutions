HKDSE Math 2016 Paper 2 Q2 Formulas & Sequences

Rearranging a Formula

Given \(\frac{a}{x} + \frac{b}{y} = 3\). Express \(x\) in terms of \(a\), \(b\), and \(y\).

Question

If \(\displaystyle \frac{a}{x} + \frac{b}{y} = 3\), then \(x =\)

A. \(\displaystyle \frac{ay}{3y - b}\)
B. \(\displaystyle \frac{ay}{b - 3y}\)
C. \(\displaystyle \frac{by}{3y - a}\)
D. \(\displaystyle \frac{by}{a - 3y}\)

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Start with the given equation: \[ \frac{a}{x} + \frac{b}{y} = 3 \]

Hint: We need to isolate \(x\). Move the \(\frac{b}{y}\) term to the right side.
2

Subtract \(\frac{b}{y}\) from both sides: \[ \frac{a}{x} = 3 - \frac{b}{y} \]

Hint: Keep the right side as a single fraction by finding a common denominator.
3

Combine the right side into one fraction: \[ 3 - \frac{b}{y} = \frac{3y}{y} - \frac{b}{y} = \frac{3y - b}{y} \] So, \[ \frac{a}{x} = \frac{3y - b}{y} \]

Hint: Now we have a proportion. Cross-multiply to solve for \(x\).
4

Cross-multiply: \[ a \cdot y = x \cdot (3y - b) \] \[ ay = x(3y - b) \]

Hint: Divide both sides by \((3y - b)\) to make \(x\) the subject.

Therefore, \[ x = \frac{ay}{3y - b} \]

Answer: A

Key Concepts

  • Isolating the variable: Move all terms not containing \(x\) to the other side.
  • Common denominator: Combine \(3 - \frac{b}{y}\) into \(\frac{3y - b}{y}\).
  • Cross-multiplication: If \(\frac{a}{x} = \frac{c}{d}\), then \(ad = cx\).
  • Division caution: Ensure the denominator \(3y - b \neq 0\).
