HKDSE 2024 · Paper 2 · Q11

In a school, $40\%$ of the students are girls and $\beta\%$ of the girls are foreign students. It is given that $30\%$ of the boys in the school are foreign students. In the school, the number of foreign students and the number of girls are equal. Find $\beta$.

  • A. 20
  • B. 45
  • C. 55
  • D. 80
1

Let $x$ be the total number of students

Introduce a single unknown for the whole school so every group can be written as a percentage of $x$.

Hint: we never need the actual value of $x$ — it cancels out at the end.

2

Write down each group

Percentage of a Quantity \(p\%\ \text{of }N=\dfrac{p}{100}\times N\) Foreign girls \(=0.4x\cdot\dfrac{\beta}{100}\), and foreign boys \(=(0.6x)(0.28)=0.168x\).

Girls $= 40\%$ of $x = 0.4x$, so Boys $= (1-40\%)\,x = 0.6x$.

Foreign girls $= 40\% \times \beta\%$ of $x = 0.4x \cdot \dfrac{\beta}{100}$.

Foreign boys $= (1-40\%) \times 30\%$ of $x = 0.6x \times 0.3 = 0.18x$.

Hint: convert every percentage into a decimal fraction of $x$.

3

Total number of foreign students

$$\text{Foreign} = 0.4x \cdot \frac{\beta}{100} + 0.6x \times 0.3$$

$$= 0.004\beta x + 0.18x$$

Hint: add the foreign girls and the foreign boys together.

4

Set up the equation (foreign = girls)

Forming an Equation from a Condition \(\text{foreign students}=\text{girls}\) Adding the two foreign groups and setting the total equal to \(0.4x\) produces an equation in \(\beta\).

The question says the number of foreign students equals the number of girls,
and girls $= 0.4x$:

$$0.004\beta x + 0.18x = 0.4x$$

Hint: this is the key condition given in the question.

5

Solve for $\beta$

Divide both sides by $x$ (as $x>0$):

$$0.004\beta + 0.18 = 0.4$$

$$0.004\beta = 0.22$$

$$\beta = \frac{0.22}{0.004} = 55$$

Hint: $x$ cancels out — the answer is independent of the school size.

Answer: C  ( $\beta = 55$ )
