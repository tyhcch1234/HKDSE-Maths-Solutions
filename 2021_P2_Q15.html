Question

The base of a solid right prism is a regular 6-sided polygon of side $8\ \text{cm}$. If the volume of the prism is $288\ \text{cm}^3$, find the total surface area of the prism correct to the nearest $\text{cm}^2$.

A. $166\ \text{cm}^2$
B. $249\ \text{cm}^2$
C. $416\ \text{cm}^2$
D. $748\ \text{cm}^2$
STEP 1 · Area of the regular hexagonal base
Area of a Regular Hexagon \(\text{Area}=\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}a^{2}\) A regular hexagon of side 8 is six equilateral triangles, giving \(96\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
A regular hexagon of side $a$ consists of $6$ equilateral triangles: \[ \text{Base area} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}a^{2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}(8^{2}) = 96\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^{2}. \]
STEP 2 · Find the height from the volume
Volume of a Prism \(V=\text{base area}\times h\) With \(V=288\) and base area \(96\sqrt{3}\), the height is \(\dfrac{288}{96\sqrt{3}}=\sqrt{3}\).
\[ V = \text{base area}\times h \implies 288 = 96\sqrt{3}\,h \implies h = \frac{288}{96\sqrt{3}} = \frac{3}{\sqrt{3}} = \sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}. \]
STEP 3 · Curved (lateral) surface area
\[ \text{Curved area} = \text{perimeter}\times h = (6\times 8)\times\sqrt{3} = 48\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^{2}. \]
STEP 4 · Total surface area
Total Surface Area of a Prism \(\text{TSA}=2\times\text{base area}+\text{perimeter}\times h\) Two hexagonal faces plus six rectangular lateral faces give \(192\sqrt{3}+48\sqrt{3}=240\sqrt{3}\).
\[ \text{TSA} = 2\times 96\sqrt{3} + 48\sqrt{3} = 240\sqrt{3} \approx 240\times 1.73205 \approx 415.69 \approx 416\ \text{cm}^{2}. \]
💡 Key idea: TSA = 2 × (base area) + perimeter × height. For a right prism the lateral faces are always rectangles.
ANSWER C. $416\ \text{cm}^2$

Question. A right prism has a regular hexagonal base of side \(6\ \text{cm}\) and volume \(216\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^{3}\). Find its total surface area correct to the nearest \(\text{cm}^{2}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Base area \(=\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}(6)^{2}=\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}(36)=54\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Step 2. \(V=\text{base area}\times h\): \(216\sqrt{3}=54\sqrt{3}\,h\), so \(h=4\ \text{cm}\).
Step 3. Curved surface area \(=\text{perimeter}\times h=(6\times6)(4)=144\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Step 4. \(\text{TSA}=2(54\sqrt{3})+144=108\sqrt{3}+144\approx187.06+144=331.06\approx331\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Answer: \(331\ \text{cm}^{2}\)
