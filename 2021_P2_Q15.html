Total Surface Area of a Right Prism (Hexagonal Base)

Question

The base of a solid right prism is a regular 6-sided polygon of side 8 cm. If the volume of the prism is 288 cm³, find the total surface area of the prism correct to the nearest cm².

  1. 166 cm²
  2. 249 cm²
  3. 416 cm²
  4. 748 cm²

Solution

STEP 1 · Area of the regular hexagonal base A regular hexagon of side \( a \) consists of 6 equilateral triangles: \[ \text{Base area} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}a^{2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}(8^{2}) = 96\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^{2}. \]
STEP 2 · Find the height from the volume \[ V = \text{base area} \times h \implies 288 = 96\sqrt{3}\,h \implies h = \frac{288}{96\sqrt{3}} = \frac{3}{\sqrt{3}} = \sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}. \]
STEP 3 · Curved (lateral) surface area \[ \text{Curved area} = \text{perimeter} \times h = (6\times8)\times\sqrt{3} = 48\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^{2}. \]
STEP 4 · Total surface area \[ \text{TSA} = 2\times96\sqrt{3} + 48\sqrt{3} = 240\sqrt{3} \approx 240\times1.73205 \approx 415.69 \approx 416\ \text{cm}^{2}. \]
Final Answer: C. 416 cm²
