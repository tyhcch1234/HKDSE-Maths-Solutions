Area ratio = (Scale)² → Scale = √(Area ratio)
HKDSE 2020 Paper 2 · Q10  [Rate, Ratio and Variations]
The actual area of a golf course is $0.75\ \text{km}^2$. If the area of the course on a map is $300\ \text{cm}^2$, then the scale of the map is
  • $1 : 250$
  • $1 : 5\,000$
  • $1 : 62\,500$
  • $1 : 25\,000\,000$

Step-by-step Solution

Step 1 · Set up the area ratio

Let the scale of the map be $1:n$ (i.e. $1\ \text{cm}$ on the map = $n\ \text{cm}$ actual).

Write the ratio of map area to actual area:

$$\frac{\text{Area on map}}{\text{Actual area}}=\frac{300\ \text{cm}^2}{0.75\ \text{km}^2}$$

Divide the given map area by the actual area — keep the units in mind.
Step 2 · Convert km² to cm²

Convert the actual area to $\text{cm}^2$ using $1\ \text{km}=1000\ \text{m}=1000\times100\ \text{cm}$:

$$0.75\ \text{km}^2 = 0.75\times(1000\times100)^2\ \text{cm}^2$$

$$=0.75\times(10^5)^2=0.75\times10^{10}\ \text{cm}^2$$

For area, square the length conversion factor.
Area Unit Conversion\(1\ \text{km}=10^{5}\ \text{cm}\ \Rightarrow\ 1\ \text{km}^{2}=10^{10}\ \text{cm}^{2}\)\(0.75\ \text{km}^{2}=0.75\times10^{10}\ \text{cm}^{2}\), squaring the length conversion.
Step 3 · Simplify the area ratio

$$\frac{300\ \text{cm}^2}{0.75\times10^{10}\ \text{cm}^2}=\frac{300}{0.75\times10^{10}}=\frac{1}{2.5\times10^{7}}$$

So the ratio of areas is

$$\frac{\text{Area on map}}{\text{Actual area}}=\frac{1}{25\,000\,000}$$

$0.75\times10^{10}\div300=2.5\times10^{7}$.
Simplifying an Area Ratio\(\dfrac{300}{0.75\times10^{10}}=\dfrac{1}{2.5\times10^{7}}\)Dividing the map area by the actual area gives the area ratio \(1:25\,000\,000\).
Step 4 · Take the square root

The map region and the actual region are similar. If the scale is $1:n$, then the ratio of areas is $1:n^2$.

$$n^2=\frac{25\,000\,000}{1}=25\,000\,000$$

$$n=\sqrt{25\,000\,000}=5\,000$$

Scale = √(Area ratio).
Area Ratio of Similar Figures\(A_{1}:A_{2}=1:n^{2}\)The map is similar to the actual region, so \(n=\sqrt{25\,000\,000}=5\,000\).
Step 5 · State the scale

Therefore the scale of the map is

$$1:5\,000$$

Check: $5000^2=25\,000\,000$ ✓ matches Step 3.
Answer: B   $1:5\,000$

Question. On a map, the area of a lake is \(200\ \text{cm}^{2}\). The actual area of the lake is \(0.32\ \text{km}^{2}\). Find the scale of the map.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Convert the actual area: \(0.32\ \text{km}^{2}=0.32\times10^{10}=3.2\times10^{9}\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Step 2. Area ratio \(=\dfrac{200}{3.2\times10^{9}}=\dfrac{1}{1.6\times10^{7}}\).
Step 3. For a scale \(1:n\), the area ratio is \(1:n^{2}\), so \(n^{2}=1.6\times10^{7}=16\,000\,000\).
Step 4. \(n=\sqrt{16\,000\,000}=4\,000\). Check: \(4000^{2}=1.6\times10^{7}\) ✓. The scale is \(1:4\,000\).
Answer: \(1:4\,000\)
