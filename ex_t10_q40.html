Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Surface Area

T10 Q40

A cone has a base radius of $3$ cm and a slant height of $5$ cm. Find its curved surface area, taking $\pi = 3.14$.

Solution

Use the formula for the curved surface area of a cone: $A = \pi r l$, where $r$ is the base radius and $l$ is the slant height.
Substitute $r = 3$ cm, $l = 5$ cm, and $\pi = 3.14$: $A = 3.14 \times 3 \times 5$.
Calculate: $3.14 \times 15 = 47.1$.
The curved surface area of the cone is $47.1\text{ cm}^2$.
