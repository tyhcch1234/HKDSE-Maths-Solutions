Mensuration — Areas of similar triangles in a parallelogram

Area of Triangle Similar Triangles (AAA) Corresponding Sides Equal Height → Area Ratio

QQuestion Multiple Choice

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram. \(E\) is a point lying on \(BC\) such that \(BE : EC = 5 : 3\). \(AE\) and \(BD\) intersect at the point \(F\). If the area of \(\triangle ABF\) is \(120\text{ cm}^2\), then the area of the quadrilateral \(CDFE\) is

A. \(237\text{ cm}^2\)
B. \(307\text{ cm}^2\)
C. \(312\text{ cm}^2\)
D. \(429\text{ cm}^2\)

1Part (a) — Understand the figure & the key ratio Setup

Drag the slider to change the position of \(E\) on \(BC\) (keeping \(BE:EC = 5:3\) fixed is the given case). Observe how the intersecting lines \(AE\) and \(BD\) create similar triangles.

Parallelogram \(ABCD\) Lines \(AE\), \(BD\) \(\triangle ABF\) (area 120) \(\triangle EBF\)
\(BE/EC = \) 5 : 3
\(EF/AF = \) 5 : 8
Step 1Given

Since \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram, \(AD /\!/ BC\) and \(AD = BC\).

Given \(BE : EC = 5 : 3\), take \(BE = 5k\), \(EC = 3k\). Then

\[ BC = 5k + 3k = 8k, \qquad AD = BC = 8k. \]
Hint: A parallelogram's opposite sides are equal and parallel — this is the bridge between \(\triangle EBF\) and \(\triangle ADF\).
Step 21A

In \(\triangle EBF\) and \(\triangle ADF\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \angle EFB &= \angle AFD \quad (\text{vert. opp. } \angle\text{s})\\ \angle EBF &= \angle ADF \quad (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s},\ BC /\!/ AD)\\ \angle BEF &= \angle DAF \quad (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s},\ BC /\!/ AD) \end{aligned} \] \[\therefore \triangle EBF \sim \triangle ADF \quad (\text{AAA})\]
Hint: Two pairs of alternate angles plus one pair of vertically opposite angles give AAA similarity.
0 / 2

2Part (b) — Find the area of \(\triangle EBF\) 2M

\(\triangle ABF = 120\) \(\triangle EBF = x_1\)
\(EF/AF = \) 5/8
Area of \(\triangle EBF = \) 75 cm²
Step 11M

From the similarity \(\triangle EBF \sim \triangle ADF\) with corresponding sides \(BE \leftrightarrow AD\):

\[ \frac{EF}{AF} = \frac{BE}{AD} = \frac{BE}{BC} = \frac{5}{8}. \]
Hint: Corresponding sides of similar triangles are in the same ratio. \(AD = BC = 8k\).
Step 21M

\(\triangle EBF\) and \(\triangle ABF\) share the same height from \(B\) to line \(AE\). Therefore their areas are in the ratio of their bases on that line:

\[ \frac{\text{Area of } \triangle EBF}{\text{Area of } \triangle ABF} = \frac{EF}{AF} = \frac{5}{8}. \] Hence \[ \text{Area of } \triangle EBF = 120 \times \frac{5}{8} = 75\text{ cm}^2. \]
Area of \(\triangle EBF = 75\text{ cm}^2\)
0 / 2

3Part (c) — Areas of \(\triangle ABE\) and \(ABCD\) 2M

\(\triangle ABF = 120\) \(\triangle EBF = 75\) \(\triangle ABE = 195\) Parallelogram \(ABCD\)
Step 11M

\(\triangle ABE\) is made up of \(\triangle ABF\) and \(\triangle EBF\):

\[ \text{Area of } \triangle ABE = 120 + 75 = 195\text{ cm}^2. \]
Hint: \(F\) lies inside \(\triangle ABE\), so the two triangles \(ABF\) and \(EBF\) partition it exactly.
Area of \(\triangle ABE = 195\text{ cm}^2\)
Step 21M

\(\triangle ABE\) and parallelogram \(ABCD\) share the same height relative to the line \(BC\) (both built on base \(BC\)). So

\[ \frac{\text{Area of } ABCD}{\text{Area of } \triangle ABE} = \frac{BC}{BE} = \frac{8}{5}. \] \[ \therefore \text{Area of } ABCD = 195 \times \frac{8}{5} = 312\text{ cm}^2. \]
Hint: The area of a parallelogram equals base × height, while a triangle is ½ × base × height. With the same height, the parallelogram has twice the area of a triangle on the same base — here the bases differ by \(BC/BE = 8/5\).
Area of \(ABCD = 312\text{ cm}^2\)
0 / 2

4Part (d) — Area of quadrilateral \(CDFE\) 1A

\(\triangle EBF = 75\) Quadrilateral \(CDFE = x\)
Step 11A

The parallelogram \(ABCD\) is partitioned into the quadrilateral \(CDFE\) and \(\triangle EBF\). Let the area of \(CDFE\) be \(x\text{ cm}^2\). Then

\[ \text{Area of }ABCD = x + 75. \]

But from Part (c), \(\text{Area of }ABCD = 312\). So

\[ x + 75 = 312 \implies x = 312 - 75 = 237. \]
Hint: Count the pieces! The parallelogram = quadrilateral \(CDFE\) + triangle \(EBF\) (plus nothing else, since \(F\) lies inside the figure).
Area of \(CDFE = 237\text{ cm}^2\) → Answer: A
0 / 1

Summary of Answers

  • \(\triangle EBF \sim \triangle ADF\) (AAA) \(\Rightarrow \dfrac{EF}{AF} = \dfrac{BE}{AD} = \dfrac{5}{8}\)
  • Area of \(\triangle EBF = 120 \times \dfrac{5}{8} = \mathbf{75\text{ cm}^2}\)
  • Area of \(\triangle ABE = 120 + 75 = \mathbf{195\text{ cm}^2}\)
  • Area of \(ABCD = 195 \times \dfrac{8}{5} = \mathbf{312\text{ cm}^2}\)
  • Area of \(CDFE = 312 - 75 = \mathbf{237\text{ cm}^2}\)
  • Correct option: A
