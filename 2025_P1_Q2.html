Step 1 — the two given fractions and their denominators.

HKDSE 2025 Paper 1 — Q2 3 marks

Simplify   $\dfrac{1}{\,3d-4\,} - \dfrac{2}{\,6d+5\,}$.
(3 marks)
1Find the common denominator
L.C.D. of Algebraic Fractions \(\frac{a}{b}\pm\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad\pm bc}{bd}\) The lowest common denominator here is the product of the two denominators, since they share no factor.

The two denominators are $3d-4$ and $6d+5$. They share no common factor,
so the least common denominator (L.C.M.) is their product:

$\text{L.C.D.} = (3d-4)(6d+5)$

Do not cross-multiply — for addition/subtraction of algebraic fractions we must first make the denominators the same.
2Rewrite each fraction over the L.C.D.
Distributive Law \(a(b+c)=ab+ac\) Expand the numerator carefully after writing over the common denominator.

Multiply the numerator and denominator of each term by the missing factor:

$\dfrac{1}{3d-4}-\dfrac{2}{6d+5} =\dfrac{1\cdot(6d+5)}{(3d-4)(6d+5)}-\dfrac{2\cdot(3d-4)}{(3d-4)(6d+5)}$

$=\dfrac{(6d+5)-2(3d-4)}{(3d-4)(6d+5)}$

Keep the bracket around $2(3d-4)$ — the minus sign applies to the whole numerator.
3Expand and simplify the numerator
Simplifying Algebraic Fractions \(\frac{ad-bc}{bd}\ \text{ is simplified when no common factor remains}\) Collect like terms in the numerator and check that nothing cancels with the denominator.

Expand $-2(3d-4)=-6d+8$:

$\dfrac{6d+5-6d+8}{(3d-4)(6d+5)} =\dfrac{\,13\,}{(3d-4)(6d+5)}$

The $6d$ terms cancel ($6d-6d=0$), leaving only the constants $5+8=13$.
Answer

$\dfrac{1}{3d-4}-\dfrac{2}{6d+5} = \boxed{\dfrac{13}{(3d-4)(6d+5)}}$

Question. Simplify \(\dfrac{2}{5e+2}-\dfrac{3}{10e-1}\).

Show the worked solution
1. L.C.D. \(=(5e+2)(10e-1)\), so the expression becomes \(\dfrac{2(10e-1)-3(5e+2)}{(5e+2)(10e-1)}\).
2. Expand: \(2(10e-1)=20e-2\) and \(3(5e+2)=15e+6\).
3. Numerator \(=20e-2-15e-6=5e-8\).
4. Hence the answer is \(\dfrac{5e-8}{(5e+2)(10e-1)}\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{5e-8}{(5e+2)(10e-1)}\)
Marking: 1M for a correct common denominator, 1M for correct expansion of the numerator, 1A for the final answer. (Accepted equivalently as $\dfrac{13}{18d^{2}-9d-20}$.)
