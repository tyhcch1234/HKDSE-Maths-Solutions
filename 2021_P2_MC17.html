Mensuration — sector area, perimeter and the circle through O, A and B.

Arc Length Sector Area Perimeter of a Sector Angle in Semicircle Circle through 3 Points

QUESTION Multiple Choice

The area of the sector \(OAB\) is \(\pi\text{ cm}^2\), where \(O\) is the centre of the sector \(OAB\). If \(\angle AOB = 90^\circ\), which of the following are true?

  1. The radius of the sector \(OAB\) is \(2\text{ cm}\).
  2. The perimeter of the sector \(OAB\) is \(\pi\text{ cm}\).
  3. The area of the circle passing through \(O\), \(A\) and \(B\) is \(2\pi\text{ cm}^2\).

A. I and II only    B. I and III only    C. II and III only    D. I, II and III

STATEMENT I Radius = 2 cm ?

Find the radius \(r\) of the sector from its area, using \(\angle AOB = 90^\circ\).

Area of sector
π cm²
Target area
π cm²
Matches target?
✓ Yes
1Write the sector-area formula
\[ \text{Area of sector} = \pi r^2 \times \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \] Here \(\theta = \angle AOB = 90^\circ\) and the area is given as \(\pi\text{ cm}^2\).
Hint: A sector is simply a fraction \(\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\) of the whole circle.
2Substitute the given values
\[ \pi r^2 \times \frac{90^\circ}{360^\circ} = \pi \] \[ \pi r^2 \times \frac{1}{4} = \pi \]
Hint: \(\frac{90}{360}=\frac14\), so the sector is exactly one quarter of the circle.
3Solve for \(r\)
\[ \frac{\pi r^2}{4} = \pi \] \[ r^2 = 4 \] \[ r = 2 \quad \text{or} \quad r = -2 \ (\text{rejected, } r>0) \]
Hint: Length must be positive, so the negative root must be rejected.
The radius of the sector \(OAB\) is \(2\text{ cm}\) \(\Rightarrow\) Statement I is TRUE.

STATEMENT II Perimeter = π cm ?

Now that \(r = 2\), build the perimeter from the two radii and the arc.

Arc length \(AB\)
π cm
Two radii \(OA+OB\)
4 cm
Perimeter
(4 + π) cm
1Recall what the perimeter of a sector is
\[ \text{Perimeter} = OA + OB + \text{(arc }AB) \] A common mistake is to forget the two straight radii. A sector is a closed region: any closed region's perimeter has 3 parts (two radii + one arc).
Hint: The arc alone is not the perimeter — you must add the two straight edges \(OA\) and \(OB\).
2Compute the arc length
\[ \text{Arc }AB = 2\pi r \times \frac{90^\circ}{360^\circ} = 2\pi(2) \times \frac14 = \pi\text{ cm} \]
Hint: Arc length is the same fraction \(\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\) of the circumference \(2\pi r\).
3Add all three parts
\[ OA + OB + \text{arc }AB = 2 + 2 + \pi = (4 + \pi)\text{ cm} \] \[ 4 + \pi \approx 7.14 \ne \pi \approx 3.14 \]
Hint: \(\pi \approx 3.14\), so \(\pi\text{ cm}\) is far too small for a perimeter of a \(2\text{ cm}\) radius sector.
The perimeter is \((4+\pi)\text{ cm}\), not \(\pi\text{ cm}\) \(\Rightarrow\) Statement II is FALSE. ✗

STATEMENT III Circle through O, A, B area = 2π cm² ?

A circle through \(O\), \(A\) and \(B\). Use the angle in a semicircle to find its diameter.

\(r\) (sector)
2 cm
\(AB\) (diameter)
√8 cm
Radius of circle
√8⁄2 cm
Area of circle
2π cm²
1Identify the diameter of the circle
In \(\triangle OAB\), the angle at \(O\) is the right angle: \[ \angle AOB = 90^\circ \] By the converse of the angle in a semicircle, \(AB\) must be a diameter of the circle passing through \(O\), \(A\) and \(B\).
Hint: If a chord subtends a right angle at a point on the circle, that chord is a diameter.
2Find \(AB\) using Pythagoras
\[ AB = \sqrt{OA^2 + OB^2} = \sqrt{2^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}\text{ cm} \] So the diameter is \(\sqrt{8}\text{ cm}\), and the radius is \(\dfrac{\sqrt{8}}{2}\text{ cm}\).
Hint: \(\triangle OAB\) is right-angled and isosceles (both legs \(= 2\)), so \(AB = 2\sqrt2\).
3Compute the area of the circle
\[ \text{Area} = \pi \left(\frac{\sqrt{8}}{2}\right)^2 = \pi \cdot \frac{8}{4} = 2\pi\text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: Square the radius before multiplying by \(\pi\): \(\left(\frac{\sqrt8}{2}\right)^2 = \frac84 = 2\).
The area of the circle through \(O\), \(A\) and \(B\) is \(2\pi\text{ cm}^2\) \(\Rightarrow\) Statement III is TRUE.

📋 Summary of Answers

  • Statement I — TRUE. \(r^2\cdot\frac{90}{360}=\pi \Rightarrow r=2\text{ cm}\).
  • Statement II — FALSE. Perimeter \(= 2+2+2\pi(2)\cdot\frac{90}{360}=(4+\pi)\text{ cm} \ne \pi\text{ cm}\).
  • Statement III — TRUE. \(AB=\sqrt{2^2+2^2}=\sqrt8\); circle radius \(=\frac{\sqrt8}{2}\), area \(=\pi\left(\frac{\sqrt8}{2}\right)^2=2\pi\text{ cm}^2\).
∴ Only I and III are true → the answer is B. I and III only.
