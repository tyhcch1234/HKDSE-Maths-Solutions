QUESTION2017 Paper 2 · Q23

In the figure, the equations of the straight lines \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) are \[x+my=n \quad\text{and}\quad x+py=q\] respectively, where the slopes of both lines are positive. Which of the following are true?

I.  \(m < p\)
II.  \(n > q\)
III.  \(n+m < p+q\)

A. I and II only
B. I and III only
C. II and III only
D. I, II and III

(Tick the correct option — the figure shows the two lines crossing the line \(y=-1\) at different points.)

Step 1
Make \(y\) the subject of each equation: \[x+my=n \;\Rightarrow\; my=n-x \;\Rightarrow\; y=\frac{n}{m}-\frac{1}{m}x\] \[x+py=q \;\Rightarrow\; py=q-x \;\Rightarrow\; y=\frac{q}{p}-\frac{1}{p}x\]
Hint: The coefficient of \(x\) in slope-intercept form is the gradient. Don't forget the minus sign!
Step 2
The slopes are therefore \[m_1=-\frac{1}{m}, \qquad m_2=-\frac{1}{p}.\] The question states both slopes are positive, so \(-\dfrac1m>0\) and \(-\dfrac1p>0\), giving \[m<0 \quad\text{and}\quad p<0 .\]
Hint: A fraction is positive only when numerator and denominator have the same sign. Here the numerator \((-1)\) is negative, so the denominators must be negative — move the m and p sliders to see only negative values keep the lines rising.
So \(m<0\) and \(p<0\): both \(m\) and \(p\) are negative numbers.
Step 3
From the figure, the slope of \(L_1\) is smaller than the slope of \(L_2\): \[-\frac1m < -\frac1p .\] Since \(m,p<0\), the product \(-pm<0\), so multiplying both sides by \(mp\) (negative) reverses... — more carefully, cross-multiply by \(mp<0\) and flip: \[-\frac1m < -\frac1p \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac1m>\frac1p \;\Longrightarrow\; p>m \;\Longrightarrow\; m<p .\]
Hint: Try it numerically on the canvas: with \(m=-3,\;p=-1.5\), we get \(\text{slope}_1=0.333<0.667=\text{slope}_2\) and \(m=-3<p=-1.5\). Statement I is TRUE.
Statement I is TRUE: \(m<p\).

Hint: Drag the \(n\) and \(q\) sliders. When \(n>q\) the point \((n,0)\) is always to the right of \((q,0)\), matching the given figure.

Summary — Final Answer

  • I. TRUE — slope of \(L_1\) smaller than slope of \(L_2\) \(\Rightarrow -\frac1m<-\frac1p \Rightarrow m<p\).
  • II. TRUE — \(x\)-intercept of \(L_1\) is to the right of that of \(L_2\) \(\Rightarrow n>q\).
  • III. FALSE — at \(y=-1\), \(L_1\) gives \(x=n+m\), \(L_2\) gives \(x=p+q\), and the figure shows \(n+m>p+q\).
  • Only I and II are true.
Correct option: D — I, II and III… no! Only I and II → Option A
