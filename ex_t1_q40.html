Percentages (30) · Sub-Topic: Profit/Loss & Discount

T1 Q40

A shop sells a microwave for \$1,050 at a profit of 5% after giving a 25% discount. Find the marked price.

Solution

Let the marked price be $M$ and the cost price be $C$.
Since a $25\%$ discount is given, the selling price is $0.75M$. Thus, $0.75M=1050$.
Solving gives $M=\frac{1050}{0.75}=1400$.
Check the profit: the cost price satisfies $1.05C=1050$, so $C=1000$. The profit is $1050-1000=50$, which is $5\%$ of $1000$.
The marked price is $\$1400$.
