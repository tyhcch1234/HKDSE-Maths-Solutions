[2023MC#33] — Laws of Logs / Linear Function

It is given that $\log_8 y$ is a linear function of $\log_4 x$. The intercepts on the vertical axis and on the horizontal axis of the graph of the linear function are $5$ and $3$ respectively. Which of the following must be true?
  • A. $x^{5}y^{2}=8^{10}$
  • B. $x^{6}y^{5}=8^{20}$
  • C. $x^{10}y^{3}=8^{20}$
  • D. $x^{9}y^{10}=8^{30}$

Step-by-step solution

Step 1Read the two intercepts.

Let $X=\log_4 x$ and $Y=\log_8 y$. The graph of $Y$ against $X$ is a straight line with: vertical-axis intercept $=5$, i.e. through $(0,5)$; horizontal-axis intercept $=3$, i.e. through $(3,0)$.

Tip: the "vertical axis" carries $Y=\log_8 y$, the "horizontal axis" carries $X=\log_4 x$.
Step 2Find the slope.

\[ \text{slope}=\frac{0-5}{3-0}=-\frac{5}{3}. \]

Rise over run between the two intercepts gives the gradient.
Slope Formula \(m=\dfrac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}\) The two intercepts give two points, and the gradient follows from rise over run.
Step 3Write the equation of the line.

\[ \log_8 y - 5 = -\tfrac{5}{3}\,(\log_4 x - 0) \;\Longrightarrow\; \log_8 y = -\tfrac{5}{3}\log_4 x + 5. \]

Point–slope form using the point $(0,5)$.
Step 4Convert $\log_4 x$ to base $8$.

Since $4=8^{2/3}$, \[ \log_4 x=\frac{\log_8 x}{\log_8 4}=\frac{\log_8 x}{2/3}=\tfrac{3}{2}\log_8 x. \] Substituting: \[ \log_8 y=-\frac{5}{3}\cdot\frac{3}{2}\log_8 x+5 =-\frac{5}{2}\log_8 x+5. \]

Change of base: $\log_b a=\dfrac{\log_c a}{\log_c b}$.
Change of Base Formula \(\log_{b}a=\dfrac{\log_{c}a}{\log_{c}b}\) Converting \(\log_{4}x\) to base 8 puts both variables in the same logarithm base.
Step 5Clear logs and match the options.

Multiply by $2$: \[ 2\log_8 y=-5\log_8 x+10 \;\Longrightarrow\; 5\log_8 x+2\log_8 y=10. \] Raise $8$ to both sides: \[ 8^{\,5\log_8 x+2\log_8 y}=8^{10} \;\Longrightarrow\; x^{5}y^{2}=8^{10}. \]

$8^{k\log_8 t}=t^{k}$ by the laws of indices and logarithms.
Laws of Logarithms \(k\log_{8}t=\log_{8}t^{k}\) Raising 8 to both sides turns the log equation into a single relation between powers of \(x\) and \(y\).
Answer: A  —  $x^{5}y^{2}=8^{10}$

Question. It is given that \(\log_{8}y\) is a linear function of \(\log_{4}x\). The intercepts on the vertical axis and on the horizontal axis of its graph are \(4\) and \(2\) respectively. Find the relation between \(x\) and \(y\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \(X=\log_{4}x\) and \(Y=\log_{8}y\). The graph passes through \((0,4)\) and \((2,0)\).
Step 2. Slope \(=\dfrac{0-4}{2-0}=-2\), so \(Y=-2X+4\), i.e. \(\log_{8}y=-2\log_{4}x+4\).
Step 3. Since \(4=8^{2/3}\), \(\log_{4}x=\dfrac{\log_{8}x}{\log_{8}4}=\dfrac{3}{2}\log_{8}x\).
Step 4. Substitute: \(\log_{8}y=-2\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}\log_{8}x+4=-3\log_{8}x+4\), so \(3\log_{8}x+\log_{8}y=4\).
Step 5. Thus \(\log_{8}\left(x^{3}y\right)=4\) and therefore \(x^{3}y=8^{4}\).
Answer: \(x^{3}y=8^{4}\)
