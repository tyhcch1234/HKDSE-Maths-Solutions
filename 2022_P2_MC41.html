Coordinate Geometry — Circumcentre of a Right Triangle on a Given Straight Line

Coordinate Geometry Right Triangle Circumcentre Angle in Semicircle Mid-point Formula

QQuestion Multiple Choice

Let \(O\) be the origin. The coordinates of the points \(A\) and \(B\) are \((a,\,0)\) and \((0,\,b)\) respectively, where \(a\) and \(b\) are positive numbers. If the circumcentre of \(\triangle OAB\) lies on the straight line \(4x+16y=17a\), then \(a:b=\)

  • A. \(8:15\)
  • B. \(15:8\)
  • C. \(16:47\)
  • D. \(47:16\)

Step 11M

\(A=(a,0)\) lies on the \(x\)-axis and \(B=(0,b)\) lies on the \(y\)-axis. Since the \(x\)-axis and \(y\)-axis are perpendicular at the origin \(O\),

\[ \angle AOB = 90^\circ \]
Hint: any point on the \(x\)-axis together with any point on the \(y\)-axis always forms a right angle at \(O\). Use the slider to stretch the triangle — the angle stays \(90^\circ\).
Answer: \(\angle AOB = 90^\circ\), so \(\triangle OAB\) is a right-angled triangle.
Step 21M

By the converse of the angle in a semicircle: if a triangle is right-angled, then the hypotenuse is a diameter of its circumcircle.

\[ \therefore\; AB \text{ is a diameter of the circumcircle of } \triangle OAB \]
Hint: tick the checkbox "Show circumcircle" and watch the circle pass through \(O\), \(A\) and \(B\) with centre exactly at the mid-point of \(AB\).
Answer: \(AB\) is a diameter, so the circumcentre is the mid-point of \(AB\).

Step 11M

The circumcentre \(M\) is the mid-point of the diameter \(AB\). Using the mid-point formula on \(A(a,0)\) and \(B(0,b)\):

\[ M=\left(\frac{a+0}{2},\ \frac{0+b}{2}\right)=\left(\frac{a}{2},\ \frac{b}{2}\right) \]
Hint: drag the ratio slider — the centre \(M\) always stays exactly halfway between \(A\) and \(B\), and \(MO=MA=MB\).
Answer: \(M\left(\dfrac{a}{2},\ \dfrac{b}{2}\right)\)

Step 11M

Since the circumcentre \(M\) lies on the straight line \(4x+16y=17a\), substitute \(x=\dfrac{a}{2}\) and \(y=\dfrac{b}{2}\):

\[ 4\left(\frac{a}{2}\right)+16\left(\frac{b}{2}\right)=17a \]
Hint: only the point \(M\) must satisfy the line equation. Slide to a non-\(8\!:\!15\) ratio and the green dot leaves the line.
Equation obtained: \(2a+8b=17a\)
Step 21A

Simplify and rearrange to isolate the ratio:

\[ 2a+8b=17a \] \[ 8b = 15a \] \[ \frac{a}{b}=\frac{8}{15} \]
Hint: divide both sides by \(15b\) — this gives the required ratio directly in the form \(a:b\).
Answer: \(a:b = 8:15\) — choice A.

✓Summary of Answers

  • \(\angle AOB = 90^\circ\) because \(OA\perp OB\) (axes are perpendicular).
  • By the converse of the angle in a semicircle, the hypotenuse \(AB\) is a diameter of the circumcircle of \(\triangle OAB\).
  • Circumcentre \(M\) = mid-point of \(AB=\left(\dfrac{a}{2},\ \dfrac{b}{2}\right)\).
  • Substituting into \(4x+16y=17a\): \(2a+8b=17a\Rightarrow 8b=15a\).
  • Final answer: \(a:b = 8:15\), i.e. choice A.
