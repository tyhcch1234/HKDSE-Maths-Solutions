Polynomials — Expanding a Product

Topic 4: Polynomials

Identities Difference of Squares Distributive Law

📝 Question

Simplify:

\[ (u+v)(u-v)(u-1) \;=\; ? \]
A. \(u^3 + u^2 + uv^2 + v^2\)
B. \(u^3 + u^2 - uv^2 + v^2\)
C. \(u^3 - u^2 + uv^2 + v^2\)
D. \(u^3 - u^2 - uv^2 + v^2\)

Tap an option to check your guess.

🧠 Step-by-Step Solution

1
Identify the structure — group the first two factors
▼

The product has three factors. Notice that the first two share the pattern \((u+v)(u-v)\) — a classic difference of squares.

Difference of squares identity: \((a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2\).
Here \(a = u\) and \(b = v\), so \((u+v)(u-v) = u^2 - v^2\).
2
Apply the identity to simplify
▼

Replace the first two factors with the simplified result:

\[ (u+v)(u-v)(u-1) = (u^2 - v^2)(u-1) \]

Now we only need to expand a product of two binomials.

3
Expand using the distributive law
▼

Multiply each term of \((u^2 - v^2)\) by each term of \((u-1)\):

\[ (u^2 - v^2)(u-1) = u^2(u-1) - v^2(u-1) \]

Then distribute further:

\[ = u^3 - u^2 - uv^2 + v^2 \]
Be careful with signs: \(-\,v^2 \cdot (-1) = +\,v^2\).
4
Compare with the options
▼

The expanded form is:

\[ u^3 - u^2 - uv^2 + v^2 \]

This matches Option D.

Answer: D

\[ u^3 - u^2 - uv^2 + v^2 \]
