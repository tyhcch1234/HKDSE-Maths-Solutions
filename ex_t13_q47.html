Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Applications

T13 Q47

A vertical tower casts a horizontal shadow of 25 m on level ground when the angle of elevation of the sun is $45^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.

Solution

Height $= 25\tan45° = 25$ m.
