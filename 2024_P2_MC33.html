Question

If \( x = \log_2 y - 2 \) and \( (\log_2 y)^2 = 5\log_2 y + x - 7 \), then \( y = ? \)

A. 1    B. 8    C. 1 or 3    D. 3 or 8

Laws of Logarithms Substitution Quadratic Equations Change of Base

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Identify the System of Equations

Hint: We have two equations involving \( \log_2 y \) and \( x \). Our goal is to find \( y \).

Given:

\[ \begin{cases} x = \log_2 y - 2 \quad \cdots (1) \\ (\log_2 y)^2 = 5\log_2 y + x - 7 \quad \cdots (2) \end{cases} \]
2

Substitute (1) into (2)

Hint: Replace every \( x \) in equation (2) with \( \log_2 y - 2 \).

Substituting \( x = \log_2 y - 2 \) into (2):

\[ (\log_2 y)^2 = 5\log_2 y + (\log_2 y - 2) - 7 \]
3

Simplify the Equation

Combine like terms on the right-hand side:

\[ (\log_2 y)^2 = 6\log_2 y - 9 \]

Bring all terms to one side:

\[ (\log_2 y)^2 - 6\log_2 y + 9 = 0 \]
4

Recognise a Perfect Square

Hint: The quadratic is a perfect square trinomial: \( a^2 - 2ab + b^2 = (a-b)^2 \).

Let \( a = \log_2 y \) and \( b = 3 \):

\[ (\log_2 y - 3)^2 = 0 \]
5

Solve for \( \log_2 y \)

Taking the square root of both sides:

\[ \log_2 y - 3 = 0 \implies \log_2 y = 3 \]
6

Convert to Exponential Form

Hint: If \( \log_a b = c \), then \( b = a^c \).

Using \( \log_2 y = 3 \):

\[ y = 2^3 = 8 \]
Answer: \( y = 8 \)   (Option B)
