Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Identities

T13 Q33

Simplify the expression $\tan\theta\cos\theta$.

Solution

Use the identity $\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$.
Substitute into the expression: $\tan\theta\cos\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\cos\theta$.
Cancel $\cos\theta$, where $\cos\theta \neq 0$: $\tan\theta\cos\theta = \sin\theta$.
The simplified expression is $\sin\theta$ for $\cos\theta \neq 0$.
