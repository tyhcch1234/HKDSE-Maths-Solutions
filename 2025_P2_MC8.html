Functions and Graphs — evaluating a quadratic function and solving for an unknown constant

Quadratic Function Substitution Symmetry of \(x^2\) Linear Equation in \(k\) Multiple Choice

Question The Problem

Let \( f(x) = x^{2} + 7x + k \), where \( k \) is a constant. If \( f(4) + f(-4) = 38 \), find \( k \).

A. \(-6\)     B. \(-3\)     C. \(3\)     D. \(6\)

Part (a) Substitute \(x=4\) and \(x=-4\) into \(f\)

The function is \( f(x) = x^{2} + 7x + k \). Move the slider to change \(k\) and watch the two points \( \bigl(4, f(4)\bigr) \) and \( \bigl(-4, f(-4)\bigr) \) on the parabola.

1 Write down the function Given

\[ f(x) = x^{2} + 7x + k \]

Hint: \(k\) is an unknown constant — keep it as a letter until the last step.
2 Evaluate \(f(4)\) 1M

\[ f(4) = (4)^{2} + 7(4) + k = 16 + 28 + k = 44 + k \]

Hint: Substitute \(x=4\) everywhere \(x\) appears. Remember \((4)^2 = 16\).
3 Evaluate \(f(-4)\) 1M

\[ f(-4) = (-4)^{2} + 7(-4) + k = 16 - 28 + k = -12 + k \]

Hint: \((-4)^2 = +16\) (a square is never negative) but \(7(-4) = -28\). Be careful with signs!
\( f(4) = 44 + k \)   and   \( f(-4) = -12 + k \)

Part (b) Use the condition \( f(4) + f(-4) = 38 \) to find \(k\)

The equation \( f(4)+f(-4)=38 \) defines a unique value of \(k\). The graph below plots \( g(k)=f(4)+f(-4)=32+2k \) against \(k\); the intersection with the horizontal line \(g=38\) gives the answer.

Gap from target: 10.0

1 Form the equation 1M

Substitute the two values from Part (a):

\[ f(4) + f(-4) = (44 + k) + (-12 + k) = 38 \]

Hint: The condition "\( f(4)+f(-4)=38 \)" simply means: add the two expressions and set the total equal to \(38\).
2 Simplify the left-hand side 1A

\[ 44 + k - 12 + k = 38 \]

\[ 32 + 2k = 38 \]

Hint: Collect constants: \(44-12=32\). Collect \(k\)-terms: \(k+k=2k\). Notice the \(x^2\)-terms \(16+16=32\) and the \(7x\)-terms \(+28-28=0\) cancel — this is why \(k\) is easy to isolate.
\( 32 + 2k = 38 \)
3 Solve for \(k\) 1A

\[ 2k = 38 - 32 \]

\[ 2k = 6 \]

\[ k = 3 \]

Hint: Divide both sides by 2. Check: \(f(4)=47\), \(f(-4)=-9\), and \(47+(-9)=38\). ✓
\[ \boxed{k = 3} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \textbf{Answer: C} \]

Summary of Answers

  • ▸\( f(4) = 4^{2} + 7(4) + k = \mathbf{44 + k} \)
  • ▸\( f(-4) = (-4)^{2} + 7(-4) + k = \mathbf{-12 + k} \)
  • ▸Condition: \( (44+k) + (-12+k) = 38 \;\Rightarrow\; 32 + 2k = 38 \)
  • ▸Final answer: \( k = 3 \)  (Option C)
  • ▸Key insight: the \(7x\) terms \( +28 \) and \( -28 \) cancel, so \( f(4)+f(-4) = 2x^{2}+2k \) at \( x=4 \) simplifies to \( 32+2k \).
