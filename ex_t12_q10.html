Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Distance/Slope

T12 Q10

A vertical line passes through the points $(3, 2)$ and $(3, 7)$. What is the slope of this line?

Solution

Identify the two points: $(x_1,y_1)=(3,2)$ and $(x_2,y_2)=(3,7)$.
Use the slope formula: $m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$.
Substitute the coordinates: $m=\frac{7-2}{3-3}=\frac{5}{0}$.
Since division by $0$ is undefined, the line is vertical and has no finite slope.
The slope of the vertical line is undefined.
