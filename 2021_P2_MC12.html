Q12 · Rate, Ratio and Variations

Variation (Direct & Inverse) Algebraic Manipulation Constant Term

Question

If \(w\) varies directly as the square of \(x\) and inversely as the cube of \(y\), which of the following must be constant?

A. \(\displaystyle \frac{x}{w^2 y^6}\)
B. \(\displaystyle \frac{x^2}{w y^3}\)
C. \(\displaystyle \frac{w}{x^2 y^3}\)
D. \(\displaystyle \frac{w^2}{x y^2}\)

🧩 Step-by-Step Guide

“varies directly as the square of \(x\)” ⇒ \(w \propto x^2\)

“inversely as the cube of \(y\)” ⇒ \(w \propto \dfrac{1}{y^3}\)

Combining both: \[ w = k \cdot \frac{x^2}{y^3} \] where \(k\) is a non‑zero constant.

💡 Hint
Direct variation with \(x^2\): multiply by \(x^2\).
Inverse variation with \(y^3\): divide by \(y^3\).
The combined variation is \(w = \dfrac{k x^2}{y^3}\).

We look for an expression that simplifies to a constant (independent of \(x\) and \(y\)).

Option A: \(\displaystyle \frac{x}{w^2 y^6}\)

\[ \frac{x}{\left(\frac{k x^2}{y^3}\right)^2 y^6} = \frac{x}{\frac{k^2 x^4}{y^6} \cdot y^6} = \frac{x}{k^2 x^4} = \frac{1}{k^2 x^3} \]

Depends on \(x\) → not constant.

Option B: \(\displaystyle \frac{x^2}{w y^3}\)

\[ \frac{x^2}{\left(\frac{k x^2}{y^3}\right) y^3} = \frac{x^2}{k x^2} = \frac{1}{k} \]

✅ Constant! (only depends on \(k\))

Option C: \(\displaystyle \frac{w}{x^2 y^3}\)

\[ \frac{\frac{k x^2}{y^3}}{x^2 y^3} = \frac{k}{y^6} \]

Depends on \(y\) → not constant.

Option D: \(\displaystyle \frac{w^2}{x y^2}\)

\[ \frac{\left(\frac{k x^2}{y^3}\right)^2}{x y^2} = \frac{k^2 x^4}{y^6} \cdot \frac{1}{x y^2} = \frac{k^2 x^3}{y^8} \]

Depends on \(x\) and \(y\) → not constant.

💡 Hint
Replace every \(w\) with \(k x^2 / y^3\) and simplify.
Only Option B gives \(\dfrac{1}{k}\), which is a constant.

Since \(\dfrac{1}{k}\) is a constant (as \(k\) is a fixed non‑zero constant), the expression that must be constant is:

\[ \boxed{\frac{x^2}{w y^3}} \]

Answer: B
