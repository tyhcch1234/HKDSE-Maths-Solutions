Mensuration — Areas in a Parallelogram

Q Question MC

In the figure, \(PQRS\) is a parallelogram. Let \(X\) be a point lying on \(PQ\). Denote the point of intersection of \(PR\) and \(SX\) by \(Y\). If the area of \(\Delta PXY\) and the area of the quadrilateral \(QRYX\) are \(32\,\text{cm}^2\) and \(58\,\text{cm}^2\) respectively, then the area of \(\Delta RSY\) is \[ \text{A. }40\,\text{cm}^2 \qquad \text{B. }50\,\text{cm}^2 \qquad \text{C. }58\,\text{cm}^2 \qquad \text{D. }72\,\text{cm}^2 \]

Step 1
Split the parallelogram along diagonal \(PR\). Since \(PQRS\) is a parallelogram, opposite sides are parallel and equal in length, so triangles \(PQR\) and \(RSP\) share the same base and height — they are congruent. \[ \text{Area of } \Delta PQR = \text{Area of } \Delta RSP \]
Hint: The diagonal \(PR\) of a parallelogram bisects it into two congruent triangles of equal area.
Step 2
The area of \(\Delta PQR\) equals the sum of the areas of \(\Delta PXY\) and the quadrilateral \(QRYX\): \[ \text{Area of } \Delta PQR = 32 + 58 = 90\,\text{cm}^2 \] Therefore, by the congruence in Step 1, \[ \text{Area of } \Delta RSP = 90\,\text{cm}^2 \]
Hint: \(\Delta PQR\) is partitioned into \(\Delta PXY\) and \(QRYX\) by the segment \(SX\).
Area of \(\Delta RSP = 90\,\text{cm}^2\)

b Using Similar Triangles to Find \(x\)

Step 1
Let \(x\,\text{cm}^2\) be the area of \(\Delta RSY\). Then, \[ \text{Area of } \Delta PSY = \text{Area of } \Delta RSP - \text{Area of } \Delta RSY = 90 - x \]
Hint: \(\Delta RSP\) is made up of \(\Delta RSY\) and \(\Delta PSY\).
Step 2
Triangles \(\Delta PXY\) and \(\Delta PSY\) share the same height (from \(P\) to line \(SX\)), so their areas are proportional to their bases \(XY\) and \(SY\): \[ \frac{XY}{SY} = \frac{\text{Area of } \Delta PXY}{\text{Area of } \Delta PSY} = \frac{32}{90 - x} \]
Hint: When two triangles share the same altitude, the ratio of their areas equals the ratio of their bases.
Step 3
Similarly, triangles \(\Delta PSY\) and \(\Delta RSY\) share the same height (from \(S\) to line \(PR\)), so: \[ \frac{PY}{RY} = \frac{\text{Area of } \Delta PSY}{\text{Area of } \Delta RSY} = \frac{90 - x}{x} \]
Hint: Again, equal altitudes give a base ratio equal to the area ratio.
Step 4
Now consider \(\Delta PXY\) and \(\Delta RSY\):
  • \(\angle PYX = \angle RYS\) (vertically opposite angles)
  • \(\angle PXY = \angle RSY\) (alternate angles, since \(PQ /\!/ SR\))
  • \(\angle XPY = \angle SRY\) (alternate angles, since \(PQ /\!/ SR\))
Therefore \(\Delta PXY \sim \Delta RSY\) (AAA). \[ \frac{XY}{SY} = \frac{PY}{RY} \quad \text{(corresponding sides of similar triangles)} \] Substituting from Steps 2 and 3: \[ \frac{32}{90-x} = \frac{90-x}{x} \]
Hint: Match the ratios carefully — both are "small triangle side / big triangle side" for the two similar triangles.
Step 5
Cross-multiply and solve: \[ 32x = (90-x)^2 = 8100 - 180x + x^2 \] \[ x^2 - 212x + 8100 = 0 \] \[ (x-50)(x-162) = 0 \] So \(x = 50\) or \(x = 162\).

Since \(\Delta RSY\) is inside \(\Delta RSP\) which has area \(90\,\text{cm}^2\), we must have \(x < 90\). Hence \(x = 162\) is rejected.
Hint: The area of a part cannot exceed the area of the whole triangle \(\Delta RSP = 90\,\text{cm}^2\).
Area of \(\Delta RSY = x = 50\,\text{cm}^2\) — Answer: B

Summary of Answers

  • Area of ΔPQR \(= 32 + 58 = 90\,\text{cm}^2\)
  • Area of ΔRSP \(= 90\,\text{cm}^2\) (congruent to ΔPQR)
  • Similar triangles \(\Delta PXY \sim \Delta RSY\) (AAA)
  • Equation \(\dfrac{32}{90-x} = \dfrac{90-x}{x}\)
  • Quadratic \(x^2 - 212x + 8100 = 0 \Rightarrow (x-50)(x-162)=0\)
  • Rejection \(x=162\) rejected since \(x < 90\)
  • Final answer Area of \(\Delta RSY = 50\,\text{cm}^2\) → Option B
