HKDSE 2024 Paper 2 — Q44

In an examination, the scores (in marks) of the candidates are as follows:

1013161719 2526283032 39

Which of the following is/are true?

I. The median of the examination scores of the candidates is 22 marks.

II. The standard score of each candidate in the examination is lower than 2.

III. The standard deviation of the examination scores of the candidates exceeds 8 marks.

  • A. I only
  • B. II only
  • C. I and III only
  • D. II and III only
1

Step 1 — Order the data

Rearrange the 11 scores in ascending order and label them by position:

\(10,\;13,\;16,\;17,\;19,\;25,\;26,\;28,\;30,\;32,\;39\)

There are \(n = 11\) scores (an odd number), so the median is the \(\frac{11+1}{2} = 6^{\text{th}}\) datum.

2

Step 2 — Check statement I (median)

The \(6^\text{th}\) score is the middle one:

\(\displaystyle \text{Median} = 25 \ne 22\)

So statement I is NOT true.

A common error is to average the 5th and 6th values (19 and 25) — that is only correct when \(n\) is even.

3

Step 3 — Compute the mean

\(\displaystyle \bar{x} = \frac{10+13+16+17+19+25+26+28+30+32+39}{11} = \frac{255}{11} \approx 23.18\)

Sum the 11 scores first, then divide by 11.

4

Step 4 — Compute the standard deviation

Using the deviation formula \(\displaystyle \sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n}}\):

\(\sigma \approx 8.35 \text{ marks}\)

Since \(8.35 > 8\), statement III is true.

This is a population standard deviation (all 11 candidates are the whole population).

5

Step 5 — Check statement II (standard score)

The standard score of the largest datum \(39\) is

\(\displaystyle z = \frac{39 - 23.18}{8.35} \approx 1.89 < 2\)

Since the largest score gives the largest standard score, every candidate has a standard score below 2. So statement II is true.

Only the maximum value needs checking — if it passes, all others automatically pass.

Statements II and III are true → Answer: D
