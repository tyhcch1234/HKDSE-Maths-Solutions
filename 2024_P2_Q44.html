Statistics — Median, Mean, Standard Deviation & Standard Score
Median Mean Standard Deviation Standard Score (z-score) Data Comparison

QQuestion

In an examination, the scores (in marks) of the candidates are as follows:

\[10\;\;13\;\;16\;\;17\;\;19\;\;25\;\;26\;\;28\;\;30\;\;30\;\;32\;\;39\]

Which of the following is/are true?

  1. The median of the examination scores of the candidates is \(22\) marks.
  2. The standard score of each candidate in the examination is lower than \(2\).
  3. The standard deviation of the examination scores of the candidates exceeds \(8\) marks.

A. I only
B. II only
C. I and III only
D. II and III only

APart (a) — Check Statement I: the MedianStatement I

Is the median of the \(12\) scores equal to \(22\) marks?

0 / 3
Step 1Arrange the data in ascending order

Each score appears with the following frequency:

\[10,\;13,\;16,\;17,\;19,\;25,\;26,\;28,\;30,\;30,\;32,\;39\]
Hint: Always sort the data before finding the median. Here there are \(12\) values (an even number).
Step 2Locate the middle positions

With \(n = 12\) (even), the median is the average of the \(\tfrac{n}{2}\)-th and \(\left(\tfrac{n}{2}+1\right)\)-th values:

\[\frac{12}{2}=6 \quad\text{and}\quad \frac{12}{2}+1=7\]

The \(6^{\text{th}}\) score is \(25\) and the \(7^{\text{th}}\) score is \(26\).

Hint: Use the slider above to highlight the 6th and 7th scores and see where the median is situated on the number line.
Step 3Compute the median
\[\text{Median}=\frac{25+26}{2}=25.5 \neq 22\]
Hint: A common trap is choosing the higher of the two middle scores (\(26\)) instead of averaging them.
∴ Statement I is NOT true. Median \(=25.5\) marks.

0 / 3
Step 1Find the mean \(\bar{x}\)
\[\bar{x}=\frac{10+13+16+17+19+25+26+28+30+30+32+39}{12}=\frac{285}{12}=23.75\]
Hint: Double-check the sum: \(10+13=23,\;23+16=39,\dots\) total \(285\). Divide by \(12\).
Step 2Compute the deviations and their squares

Using \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum(x-\bar{x})^2}{n}}\), first find each \((x-\bar{x})^2\):

\[ \begin{aligned} &(-13.75)^2+(-10.75)^2+(-7.75)^2+(-6.75)^2+(-4.75)^2\\ &+(1.25)^2+(2.25)^2+(4.25)^2+(6.25)^2+(6.25)^2\\ &+(8.25)^2+(15.25)^2 \end{aligned} \]

Sum of squares \(=189.0625+115.5625+60.0625+45.5625+22.5625+1.5625+5.0625+18.0625+39.0625+39.0625+68.0625+232.5625=836.25\)

Hint: The deviations must sum to \(0\) — use this as a quick check of your arithmetic.
Step 3Compute \(\sigma\)
\[\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{836.25}{12}}=\sqrt{69.6875}\approx 8.348\]

Since \(8.348 > 8\), Statement III holds.

Hint: Move the sliders left/right: shrinking the extremes pulls \(\sigma\) below \(8\); the given data keeps it above \(8\).
∴ Statement III is true. \(\sigma \approx 8.35\) marks \(> 8\).

CPart (c) — Check Statement II: Standard ScoreStatement II

Is the standard score of every candidate less than \(2\)? The largest score gives the largest \(z\)-score, so it suffices to check the maximum.

z = — Max z = —
0 / 3
Step 1Recall the standard-score formula
\[z=\frac{x-\bar{x}}{\sigma}\]

The largest standard score occurs at the largest score, \(x=39\).

Hint: \(z\) measures "how many standard deviations above the mean". Only the maximum \(x\) can produce the maximum \(z\).
Step 2Substitute the maximum score
\[z_{\max}=\frac{39-23.75}{8.348}\approx\frac{15.25}{8.348}\approx 1.827\]
Hint: Use \(\sigma \approx 8.348\) (not \(8.3479\ldots\)) — the difference is negligible for the comparison with \(2\).
Step 3Compare with 2
\[1.827 < 2\]

Since the largest standard score is below \(2\), every candidate's standard score is below \(2\).

Hint: Check the number line: \(1.827\) lies to the left of \(2\), so Statement II holds.
∴ Statement II is true. \(\max z \approx 1.83 < 2\).

Summary of Answers

  • Median \(=\dfrac{25+26}{2}=25.5\) marks \(\Rightarrow\) Statement I is false.
  • Mean \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{285}{12}=23.75\) marks.
  • Standard deviation \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{836.25}{12}}\approx 8.348\) marks \(>8\) \(\Rightarrow\) Statement III is true.
  • Largest standard score \(=\dfrac{39-23.75}{8.348}\approx 1.827 < 2\) \(\Rightarrow\) Statement II is true.
  • Only Statements II and III are true.
  • Answer: D
