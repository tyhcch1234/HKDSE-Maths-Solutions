HKDSE 2012 · Paper 1 · Q16 · Probability

There are $8$ departments in a company. To form a task group of $16$ members, $2$ representatives are nominated by each department. From the task group, $4$ members are randomly selected.

(a) Find the probability that the $4$ selected members are nominated by $4$ different departments. [2 marks]

(b) Find the probability that the $4$ selected members are nominated by at most $3$ different departments. [2 marks]

1Total number of outcomes
Combination \({}^{n}C_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}\) Order does not matter when a group is selected, so count combinations, not permutations.

There are $8$ departments $\times$ $2$ representatives $=16$ members.
Choose any $4$ of them:

$$\text{Total}={}^{16}C_{4}=\frac{16\cdot15\cdot14\cdot13}{4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1}=1820$$

💡 Use combinations — the order in which members are picked does not matter.
2Part (a): Count 4 different departments
Multiplication Principle \(N=n_{1}\times n_{2}\times\cdots\times n_{k}\) Choose the 4 departments \(\left({}^{8}C_{4}\right)\) and then one of 2 representatives from each \(\left(2^{4}\right)\); multiply the independent choices.

Choose $4$ departments out of $8$, then pick $1$ of the $2$ representatives from each:

$${}^{8}C_{4}\left({}^{2}C_{1}\right)^{4}=70\times2^{4}=70\times16=1120$$

💡 ${}^{8}C_{4}$ selects which departments appear; the factor $2^4$ selects one representative per department.
3Part (a): Probability

$$P=\frac{{}^{8}C_{4}\left({}^{2}C_{1}\right)^{4}}{{}^{16}C_{4}}=\frac{1120}{1820}=\frac{8}{13}$$

Alternatively, using ordered selection:

$$P=\left(\frac{16}{16}\right)\!\left(\frac{14}{15}\right)\!\left(\frac{12}{14}\right)\!\left(\frac{10}{13}\right)=\frac{8}{13}$$

💡 After picking the first member, $2$ of the remaining $15$ share his department, so only $14$ are "safe", and so on.
4Part (b): Complement method
Complement Rule \(P(A^{\prime})=1-P(A)\) "At most 3 departments" is the complement of "4 different departments", so subtract from 1.

"At most $3$ different departments" is the complement of part (a) ("$4$ different departments"):

$$P=1-\frac{8}{13}=\frac{5}{13}$$

💡 Whenever the event can be split into many messy cases, the complement is usually faster.
Answer: (a) $\dfrac{8}{13}$   ·   (b) $\dfrac{5}{13}$

Question. A company has \(6\) departments, and each department nominates \(3\) representatives to a committee of \(18\) members. From the committee, \(3\) members are chosen at random.
(a) Find the probability that the 3 members come from 3 different departments.
(b) Find the probability that they come from at most 2 different departments.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Total number of ways: \({}^{18}C_{3}=\dfrac{18\cdot17\cdot16}{3\cdot2\cdot1}=816\).
Step 2. Favourable: \({}^{6}C_{3}\times\left({}^{3}C_{1}\right)^{3}=20\times3^{3}=540\).
Step 3. (a) \(P=\dfrac{540}{816}=\dfrac{45}{68}\).
Step 4. (b) "At most 2 departments" is the complement: \(P=1-\dfrac{45}{68}=\dfrac{23}{68}\).
Answer: (a) \(\dfrac{45}{68}\) (b) \(\dfrac{23}{68}\)
