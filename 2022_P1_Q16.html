Step 1 — Identify the given quadratic y = g(x) and complete the square.

HKDSE 2022 Paper 1 · Q16

Let $g(x)=3x^{2}+12kx+16k^{2}+8$, where $k$ is a non-zero real constant.

(a) [2 marks]

Using the method of completing the square, express, in terms of $k$, the coordinates of the vertex of the graph of $y=g(x)$.

(b) [3 marks]

On the same rectangular coordinate system, denote the vertex of the graph of $y=g(x)$ and the vertex of the graph of $y=2g(-x)$ by $A$ and $B$ respectively. Let $M$ be a point lying on $AB$ such that the area of $\triangle OBM$ is the triple of the area of $\triangle OAM$, where $O$ is the origin. Express, in terms of $k$, the coordinates of $M$.

1Complete the square of $g(x)$  ·  Part (a)

\[ \begin{aligned} g(x)&=3x^{2}+12kx+16k^{2}+8\\[2pt] &=3\bigl(x^{2}+4kx\bigr)+16k^{2}+8\\[2pt] &=3\bigl(x^{2}+4kx+4k^{2}\bigr)+4k^{2}+8\\[2pt] &=3(x+2k)^{2}+4k^{2}+8 \end{aligned} \]

Hint: Factor the coefficient of $x^{2}$ out of the first two terms; you must add back $3\cdot(2k)^2=12k^2$, leaving a net change of $+4k^2$.
2Read off vertex $A$  ·  Part (a) answer

From the completed-square form $3(x+2k)^{2}+4k^{2}+8$ (a minimum at $x=-2k$):

\[ A=\bigl(-2k,\;4k^{2}+8\bigr) \]

Hint: $y=3(x-h)^2+c$ has vertex $(h,\,c)$, so the vertex is at $(-2k,\;4k^2+8)$.  [1A]
3Find vertex $B$ of $y=2g(-x)$  ·  Part (b)

\[ 2g(-x)=2\Bigl[3(-x+2k)^{2}+4k^{2}+8\Bigr]=6(x-2k)^{2}+8k^{2}+16 \]

So the vertex of $y=2g(-x)$ is

\[ B=\bigl(2k,\;8k^{2}+16\bigr) \]

Hint: $g(-x)$ reflects the parabola in the $y$-axis, moving the vertex from $x=-2k$ to $x=2k$; multiplying by $2$ doubles the vertical offset.  [1M]
4Use the area condition to get the ratio $AM:MB$

Both triangles have the same height from $O$ to the line $AB$, so their areas are in the ratio of their bases:

\[ \frac{[\triangle OBM]}{[\triangle OAM]}=\frac{MB}{AM}=3 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad AM:MB=1:3 \]

Hint: $M$ lies on $AB$, so $\triangle OBM$ and $\triangle OAM$ share the perpendicular height from $O$; the area ratio equals the base ratio $MB\!:\!AM$.  [1M]
5Section formula for $M$

$M$ divides $AB$ internally in the ratio $AM:MB=1:3$:

\[ \begin{aligned} M&=\left(\frac{3(-2k)+1(2k)}{1+3},\;\frac{3(4k^{2}+8)+1(8k^{2}+16)}{1+3}\right)\\[4pt] &=\left(\frac{-6k+2k}{4},\;\frac{12k^{2}+24+8k^{2}+16}{4}\right)\\[4pt] &=\left(\frac{-4k}{4},\;\frac{20k^{2}+40}{4}\right)\\[4pt] &=\bigl(-k,\;5k^{2}+10\bigr) \end{aligned} \]

Hint: For internal division with $AM:MB=1:3$, the point is $\dfrac{3A+1B}{1+3}$ — the weight on each vertex is the opposite segment length.  [1M 1A]
Answers
(a)  Vertex of $y=g(x)$: $\;A\bigl(-2k,\;4k^{2}+8\bigr)$
(b)  $M=\bigl(-k,\;5k^{2}+10\bigr)$
