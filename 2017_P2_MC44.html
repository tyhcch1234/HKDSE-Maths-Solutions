Statistics — Standard Score & Standard Deviation

Standard Score Mean Standard Deviation z-score Multiple Choice

QuestionMultiple Choice · 1 mark

The standard score of Tom in a Mathematics examination is \(-2\). If the score of Tom in the Mathematics examination is \(33\) marks and the mean of the scores of the Mathematics examination is \(45\) marks, then the standard deviation of the scores of the Mathematics examination is

  • A. \(3\) marks
  • B. \(6\) marks ✓
  • C. \(12\) marks
  • D. \(36\) marks

Part (a) — Set up the standard score equation Translate the words into algebra

The standard score (z-score) measures how many standard deviations a value lies from the mean:

\[ z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma} \]

We are given \(z = -2\), \(x = 33\), \(\mu = 45\), and we must find \(\sigma\).

\(x\) = 33
\(\mu\) = 45
\(z = (x-\mu)/\sigma\) = -2.00
\(\sigma\) = 6
Step 11M

Write down the standard score formula and substitute the known values:

\[ -2 = \frac{33 - 45}{\sigma} = \frac{-12}{\sigma} \]
Hint: Standard score \(z = \dfrac{x - \mu}{\sigma}\), where \(x\) is Tom's score, \(\mu\) the mean, and \(\sigma\) the standard deviation.
Step 21A

Solve for \(\sigma\):

\[ -2\sigma = -12 \implies \sigma = 6 \]
Standard deviation \(\sigma = 6\) marks  →  Answer: B

Part (b) — Interpret the z-score on the normal curve Why is the answer 6, not 3 or 12?

A standard score of \(-2\) means Tom's score is 2 standard deviations below the mean. The gap between the mean \(45\) and Tom's score \(33\) is \(12\) marks — that gap equals \(2\sigma\).

gap \(= 45-33 =\) 12
\(2\sigma\) = 12.0
\(\sigma\) = 6.0
✓ Matches
Step 11M

A z-score of \(-2\) means the raw score lies exactly \(2\sigma\) below the mean:

\[ x = \mu - 2\sigma \]
Hint: The sign of \(z\) tells direction (below/above the mean); the magnitude tells how many standard deviations.
Step 21A

Substitute \(x = 33\) and \(\mu = 45\):

\[ 33 = 45 - 2\sigma \implies 2\sigma = 12 \implies \sigma = 6 \]
\(\sigma = 6\) marks  →  the correct choice is B.
0 / 2

Summary of Answers

  • Given: \(z = -2\), \(x = 33\), \(\mu = 45\)
  • Formula: \(z = \dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\)
  • Working: \(-2 = \dfrac{33-45}{\sigma} = \dfrac{-12}{\sigma}\)
  • Solve: \(\sigma = \dfrac{-12}{-2} = 6\)
  • Check: \(45 - 2(6) = 45 - 12 = 33\) ✓
Standard deviation = 6 marks  ·  Answer: B
