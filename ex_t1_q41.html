T1 Q41

The annual salary of Mr Lee is \$360,000. A tax of 15% is charged on the amount above \$132,000. Find his tax payable.

Solution

Calculate the amount above $132000$: $360000 - 132000 = 228000$.
Apply the $15\%$ tax rate: $228000 \times 15\% = 228000 \times 0.15$.
Compute the tax payable: $228000 \times 0.15 = 34200$.
His tax payable is $34200$ dollars.
