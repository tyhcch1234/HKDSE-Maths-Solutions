T1 Q49

The marked price of a meal is \$180. With a 10% service charge and a 12% discount, find the final amount.

Solution

Convert the service charge and discount to decimals: $10\% = 0.10$ and $12\% = 0.12$.
Apply the 10% service charge: $180(1+0.10)=180(1.10)=198$.
Apply the 12% discount: $198(1-0.12)=198(0.88)=174.24$.
Check using one expression: $180 \times 1.10 \times 0.88 = 174.24$, so the calculation is correct.
The final amount is $\$174.24$.
