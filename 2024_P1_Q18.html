QUESTION

(a) $PQRS$ is a thin quadrilateral metal sheet, where $PQ = 12\text{ cm}$, $PS = 10\text{ cm}$, $QR = 13\text{ cm}$, $\angle QPS = 82^\circ$ and $\angle QRS = 65^\circ$. Find

(i) the length of $QS$,
(ii) $\angle RQS$.  (4 marks)

(b) The metal sheet $PQRS$ described in (a) is now folded along $QS$ (see Figure 2). It is given that the angle between the plane $PQS$ and the plane $QRS$ is $80^\circ$.

(i) Find the shortest distance from $R$ to the plane $PQS$.
(ii) Let $X$ be any point lying on the plane $QRS$. Someone claims that the distance between $P$ and $X$ exceeds $8\text{ cm}$. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer.  (4 marks)

📐 Figure 2 — the folded sheet along $QS$ (reconstructed accurately in the panel on the left).
STEP 1 · Part (a)(i) — find QS by cosine rule
Cosine Formula \(c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}-2ab\cos C\) In \(\triangle PQS\): \(QS^{2}=12^{2}+10^{2}-2(12)(10)\cos82^{\circ}\approx210.6\), so \(QS\approx14.5\) cm.

In triangle $PQS$ we know two sides and the included angle $\angle QPS = 82^\circ$.

$$QS^2 = PQ^2 + PS^2 - 2(PQ)(PS)\cos\angle QPS$$ $$QS^2 = 12^2 + 10^2 - 2(12)(10)\cos 82^\circ$$ $$QS^2 = 144 + 100 - 240\cos 82^\circ \approx 210.6019$$ $$QS \approx 14.5120 \text{ cm}$$
QS ≈ 14.5 cm
STEP 2 · Part (a)(ii) — find ∠RQS by sine rule
Sine Formula \(\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}\) \(\dfrac{\sin\angle QSR}{QR}=\dfrac{\sin\angle QRS}{QS}\) gives \(\angle QSR\approx54.3^{\circ}\); the obtuse value \(125.7^{\circ}\) is rejected by the angle sum.

In triangle $QRS$ we know $QR = 13$, $QS \approx 14.5120$ and $\angle QRS = 65^\circ$.

$$\frac{\sin\angle QSR}{QR} = \frac{\sin\angle QRS}{QS}$$ $$\sin\angle QSR \approx \frac{13\sin 65^\circ}{14.5120} \approx 0.81147$$

So $\angle QSR \approx 54.28^\circ$ or $125.72^\circ$ (rejected — angle sum would exceed $180^\circ$).

$$\angle RQS \approx 180^\circ - 65^\circ - 54.28^\circ \approx 60.72^\circ$$
∠RQS ≈ 60.7°
Hint: Always test the ambiguous case of the sine rule against the triangle angle sum.
STEP 3 · Part (b)(i) — shortest distance from R to plane PQS
Angle between Two Planes \(\text{angle between planes}=\angle(\text{perpendiculars to the common line})\) \(RT\perp QS\) in plane \(QRS\), so the perpendicular from \(R\) to plane \(PQS\) is \(RT\sin80^{\circ}\approx11.2\) cm.

Let $T$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $R$ to $QS$ (so $T$ lies on the fold line).

Drop $RT$ in triangle $QRS$:

$$RT = QR \sin\angle RQS = 13\sin 60.7200^\circ \approx 11.337 \text{ cm}$$

The angle between the two planes ($PQS$) and ($QRS$) is $80^\circ$,
and the perpendicular from $R$ to $QS$ makes the same angle with the folded plane $PQS$.

Let $h$ be the shortest distance from $R$ to plane $PQS$:

$$h = RT \sin 80^\circ \approx 11.337 \times \sin 80^\circ \approx 11.17 \text{ cm}$$
h ≈ 11.2 cm
STEP 4 · Part (b)(ii) — compare d with 8 cm using volumes

Let $d$ be the shortest distance from $P$ to plane $QRS$ (i.e. the perpendicular height of the tetrahedron $PQRS$ over base $QRS$).

Equate the volume of tetrahedron $PQRS$ computed in two ways:

$$\tfrac{1}{3}\,(\text{area of } \triangle QRS)\,h = \tfrac{1}{3}\,(\text{area of } \triangle PQS)\,d$$ $$\frac{d}{h} = \frac{(\text{area of } \triangle PQS)}{(\text{area of } \triangle QRS)} = \frac{\tfrac{1}{2}(12)(10)\sin 82^\circ}{\tfrac{1}{2}(13)(14.5120)\sin 60.7200^\circ}$$ $$d \approx 11.1689 \times \frac{59.4162}{82.1365} \approx 8.0641 \text{ cm}$$

Since $P$ itself lies in the plane $PQS$ but not in $QRS$,
and its perpendicular distance to $QRS$ is $d \approx 8.064 > 8$, every point $X$ on plane $QRS$ satisfies

$$PX \ge d > 8 \text{ cm}$$
d ≈ 8.06 cm > 8 cm → the claim is CORRECT.

Question. \(PQRS\) is a thin quadrilateral metal sheet with \(PQ=15\text{ cm}\), \(PS=12\text{ cm}\), \(QR=16\text{ cm}\), \(\angle QPS=75^{\circ}\) and \(\angle QRS=70^{\circ}\).
(a) Find \(QS\) and \(\angle RQS\).
(b) The sheet is folded along \(QS\) so that the angle between the planes \(PQS\) and \(QRS\) is \(70^{\circ}\).
(i) Find the shortest distance from \(R\) to the plane \(PQS\).
(ii) A student claims that the distance between \(P\) and any point \(X\) on the plane \(QRS\) exceeds \(9\text{ cm}\). Is the claim correct? Explain.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Cosine formula in \(\triangle PQS\): \(QS^{2}=15^{2}+12^{2}-2(15)(12)\cos75^{\circ}=225+144-93.2\approx275.8\), so \(QS\approx16.61\text{ cm}\).
Step 2. Sine formula in \(\triangle QRS\): \(\sin\angle QSR=\dfrac{16\sin70^{\circ}}{16.61}\approx0.9053\), so \(\angle QSR\approx64.9^{\circ}\) (the obtuse value is rejected because \(64.9^{\circ}+70^{\circ}<180^{\circ}\)).
Step 3. Hence \(\angle RQS\approx180^{\circ}-70^{\circ}-64.9^{\circ}=45.1^{\circ}\).
Step 4. (b)(i) Let \(T\) be the foot of the perpendicular from \(R\) to \(QS\): \(RT=16\sin45.1^{\circ}\approx11.34\text{ cm}\). The shortest distance to plane \(PQS\) is \(h=RT\sin70^{\circ}\approx10.66\approx10.7\text{ cm}\).
Step 5. (b)(ii) Let \(d\) be the distance from \(P\) to plane \(QRS\). Equating volumes of the tetrahedron: \(\dfrac{d}{h}=\dfrac{\text{area of }\triangle PQS}{\text{area of }\triangle QRS}=\dfrac{86.93}{94.17}\approx0.923\),
so \(d\approx10.66\times0.923\approx9.84\text{ cm}\).
Step 6. Since \(PX\ge d\approx9.84>9\) for every \(X\) on plane \(QRS\), the claim is correct.
Answer: (a) \(QS\approx16.6\) cm, \(\angle RQS\approx45.1^{\circ}\); (b)(i) \(\approx10.7\) cm; (ii) correct, since \(PX\ge9.84>9\) cm
