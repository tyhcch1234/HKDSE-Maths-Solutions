HKDSE 2018 Paper 1 Question 6

6.
(a) Find the range of values of $x$ which satisfy $\dfrac{3 - x}{2} > 2x + 7$ and $x + 8 \ge 0$.

(b) Write down the greatest integer satisfying both inequalities in (a). [4 marks]

Step-by-step solution

1 Solve the first inequality
Multiplication Property of Inequalities \(a0)\) Multiplying by the positive number 2 does not change the direction of the inequality.
\[ \frac{3 - x}{2} > 2x + 7 \] Multiply both sides by $2$ (positive,
so the inequality sign is unchanged): \[ 3 - x > 2(2x + 7) = 4x + 14 \]
Multiply out the bracket carefully.
Keep the inequality direction.
2 Rearrange and solve
Reversing the Inequality Sign \(a-b\) Dividing \(-5x>11\) by the negative number \(-5\) turns \(>\) into \(<\) .
\[ 3 - x > 4x + 14 \] Bring $x$ terms to one side and constants to the other: \[ -x - 4x > 14 - 3 \quad\Rightarrow\quad -5x > 11 \] Divide by $-5$ (negative,
so reverse the sign): \[ x < -\frac{11}{5} = -2.2 \]
Dividing by a negative flips the inequality sign.
3 Solve the second inequality
\[ x + 8 \ge 0 \] \[ x \ge -8 \]
Subtract 8 from both sides.
No sign flip here.
4 Combine the ranges
Both conditions must hold: \[ x \ge -8 \quad\text{and}\quad x < -\frac{11}{5} \] \[ \therefore\; -8 \le x < -\frac{11}{5} \]
Intersection of the two sets.
Left endpoint included, right endpoint excluded.
5 Greatest integer (part b)
The integers satisfying $-8 \le x < -2.2$ are: \[ -8,\,-7,\,-6,\,-5,\,-4,\,-3 \] The greatest of these is $\mathbf{-3}$.
List the integers in the interval;
the largest one is the answer.
Answer:
(a) $\displaystyle -8 \le x < -\frac{11}{5}$
(b) The greatest integer is $-3$.

Question. Solve the inequalities \(\dfrac{5-x}{3}>2x+4\) and \(x+5\ge0\). Hence write down the greatest integer satisfying both inequalities.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Multiply both sides by the positive number \(3\): \(5-x>6x+12\).
Step 2. Collect terms: \(5-12>6x+x\), i.e. \(-7>7x\), so \(x<-1\) (dividing by 7 keeps the sign).
Step 3. For \(x+5\ge0\): \(x\ge-5\).
Step 4. Combining: \(-5\le x<-1\). The integers are \(-5,-4,-3,-2\), so the greatest is \(-2\).
Answer: \(-5\le x<-1\); the greatest integer is \(-2\).
