HKDSE 2025 · Paper 2 · Q8 · Functions and Graphs

Let $f(x)=x^{2}+7x+k$, where $k$ is a constant. If $f(4)+f(-4)=38$, find $k$.

A. $-6$
B. $-3$
C. $3$
D. $6$
Step 1Write down what is given

$f(x)=x^{2}+7x+k$, with $k$ a constant, and $$f(4)+f(-4)=38.$$

Substituting $x=4$ and $x=-4$ both give $k$ the same coefficient, so the $k$'s will add up to $2k$.
Step 2Evaluate $f(4)$ and $f(-4)$

$f(4)=4^{2}+7(4)+k=16+28+k=44+k$

$f(-4)=(-4)^{2}+7(-4)+k=16-28+k=-12+k$

Careful with the sign: $7(-4)=-28$, and $(-4)^2=+16$.
Step 3Add the two values

$$f(4)+f(-4)=(44+k)+(-12+k)=32+2k.$$

Only the $k$-terms merge into $2k$; the numbers give $44-12=32$.
Step 4Form and solve the equation

$$32+2k=38$$

$$2k=38-32=6$$

$$k=\frac{6}{2}=3$$

Subtract $32$ from both sides, then divide by $2$.
Step 5Check

With $k=3$: $f(4)=44+3=47$ and $f(-4)=-12+3=-9$, so $f(4)+f(-4)=47-9=38.$ ✓

The answer matches the required sum, so $k=3$ and the answer is C.
ANSWER$k=3$  →  Option C
