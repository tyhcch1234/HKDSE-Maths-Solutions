QThe Question

The bar chart below shows the distribution of the numbers of books read by a group of students in a year.

Distribution of the numbers of books read by the group of students in the year

If a student is randomly selected from the group, then the probability that the selected student reads fewer than \(26\) books in the year is \(\dfrac{7}{10}\).

(a) Find \(k\).

(b) Write down the range, the inter-quartile range and the standard deviation of the distribution.

[5 marks]

aPart (a) — Find \(k\)

Drag the slider to change \(k\) and watch the probability \(P(\text{reads fewer than }26)\) update live. The correct \(k\) is the value that makes this probability equal to \(\dfrac{7}{10}\).

12
Step 11M

Count the students who read fewer than 26 books: these are those who read \(23\), \(24\) or \(25\) books.

\[ 12 + k + 16 \]

Count the total number of students:

\[ 12 + k + 16 + 9 + 11 + 4 = 52 + k \]
Hint: The probability is \(\dfrac{\text{favourable}}{\text{total}}\). Set the favourable count over the total count equal to \(\dfrac{7}{10}\).
Step 21M

Set up the equation:

\[ \frac{12+k+16}{12+k+16+9+11+4} = \frac{7}{10} \] \[ \frac{28+k}{52+k} = \frac{7}{10} \]
Hint: Cross-multiply to clear the fractions. Both sides are linear in \(k\).
Step 31A

Solve for \(k\):

\[ 10(28+k) = 7(52+k) \] \[ 280 + 10k = 364 + 7k \] \[ 3k = 84 \] \[ k = 28 \]
\(\boxed{k = 28}\)
Check: Total \(= 52 + 28 = 80\), favourable \(= 28 + 28 = 56\), and \(\dfrac{56}{80} = \dfrac{7}{10}\). ✓

Step 11A

Range = largest value − smallest value.

\[ \text{Range} = 28 - 23 = 5 \]
Hint: The range only depends on the two extreme values of the data, not on how many students read each number.
\(\boxed{\text{Range} = 5}\)
Step 21A

Total number of students \(= 12 + 28 + 16 + 9 + 11 + 4 = 80\).

Locate the quartiles. The lower quartile \(Q_1\) is at the \(\tfrac{80}{4}=20\)th position and the upper quartile \(Q_3\) is at the \(\tfrac{3(80)}{4}=60\)th position (counting cumulatively).

Cumulative counts: \(12,\;40,\;56,\;65,\;76,\;80\).

  • The 20th value lies in the runs of \(24\) books \(\Rightarrow Q_1 = 24\).
  • The 60th value lies in the runs of \(26\) books \(\Rightarrow Q_3 = 26\).
\[ \text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 = 26 - 24 = 2 \]
Hint: Count students from the smallest number of books upward until you pass the required position. The corresponding "books read" value is the quartile.
\(\boxed{\text{Inter-quartile range} = 2}\)
Step 31A

First compute the mean:

\[ \bar{x} = \frac{23(12)+24(28)+25(16)+26(9)+27(11)+28(4)}{80} = \frac{2000}{80} = 25 \]

Then the standard deviation is

\[ \sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (x-\bar{x})^2 \cdot f}{80}} \approx 1.43 \]
Hint: Subtract the mean from each value, square it, multiply by the frequency, add everything up, divide by the total, then take the square root. The HKDSE answer booklet accepts \(\approx 1.43\).
\(\boxed{\text{Standard deviation} \approx 1.43}\)

✓Summary of Answers

  • (a)   \(k = \mathbf{28}\)
  • (b)   Range = 5
  • (b)   Inter-quartile range = 2
  • (b)   Standard deviation \(\approx\) 1.43

Key idea: use total students \(= 52 + k\) and favourable students \(= 28 + k\), solve the probability equation, then the full recovered data set is \(23\!:\!12,\;24\!:\!28,\;25\!:\!16,\;26\!:\!9,\;27\!:\!11,\;28\!:\!4\).
