Distribution of books read (bar chart)

Readout: step 1 — given counts & the probability condition.

HKDSE 2021 Paper 1 · Q9 · Statistics

The bar chart below shows the distribution of the numbers of books read by a group of students in a year.

If a student is randomly selected from the group, then the probability that the selected student reads fewer than $26$ books in the year is $\dfrac{7}{10}$.

(a) Find $k$.

(b) Write down the range, the inter-quartile range and the standard deviation of the distribution.

[5 marks]

1

Set up the probability condition

Probability from a Frequency Table \(P(E)=\dfrac{\text{frequency of }E}{\text{total frequency}}\) \(12+k+16\) students read fewer than \(26\) books out of \(68+k\) students in total.

"Fewer than $26$ books" means $23$, $24$ or $25$ books.
The total number of students is $12+k+16+9+11+4$, so

\[ \frac{12+k+16}{12+k+16+9+11+4}=\frac{7}{10}. \]
Identify the numerator (counts less than 26) and the denominator (all students).
2

Solve for $k$

\[ \frac{28+k}{68+k}=\frac{7}{10} \] \[ 10(28+k)=7(68+k) \] \[ 280+10k=476+7k \] \[ 3k=196 \;\Rightarrow\; k=28 \]
Cross-multiply, then collect the $k$ terms on one side.
3

Ordered data and the range

With $k=28$, the frequencies are:

Books read232425262728
No. of students1228169114

Total students $=80$. Smallest value $=23$,
largest value $=28$, hence

\[ \text{range}=28-23=5. \]
Range only depends on the smallest and largest readings.
4

Inter-quartile range

Inter-Quartile Range \(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) The \(20\)th value is \(24\) and the \(60\)th value is \(26\), so the IQR is \(26-24=2\).

Cumulative frequencies: $12,\,40,\,56,\,65,\,76,\,80$.

$Q_1$: the $20$th value falls in the "$24$" group ($13$–$40$),
so $Q_1=24$.
$Q_3$: the $60$th value falls in the "$26$" group ($57$–$65$),
so $Q_3=26$.

\[ \text{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1=26-24=2. \]
Use $\frac{1}{4}(80)=20$ and $\frac{3}{4}(80)=60$ with cumulative frequencies.
5

Mean and standard deviation

Standard Deviation from a Frequency Table \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum fx^{2}}{N}-\bar{x}^{2}}\) With \(\sum fx^{2}=48\,096\), \(N=80\) and \(\bar{x}=24.475\), \(\sigma=\sqrt{601.2-599.0}\approx1.43\).
\[ \bar{x}=\frac{\sum fx}{80}=\frac{12(23)+28(24)+16(25)+9(26)+11(27)+4(28)}{80}=\frac{1958}{80}=24.475 \] \[ \sum fx^{2}=12(23^2)+28(24^2)+16(25^2)+9(26^2)+11(27^2)+4(28^2)=48096 \] \[ \sigma=\sqrt{\frac{\sum fx^{2}}{80}-\bar{x}^{2}}=\sqrt{\frac{48096}{80}-24.475^{2}}=\sqrt{2.2244}\approx 1.43 \]
For a whole population, divide by $N=80$ (not $79$). Round to $2$ d.p.
Answer:
(a) $k=28$
(b) Range $=5$, inter-quartile range $=2$, standard deviation $\approx 1.43$

Question. The bar chart shows the numbers of books read by a group of students in a year. The numbers of students reading \(23,24,25,26,27,28\) books are \(10,k,14,8,6,2\) respectively. If a student is chosen at random, the probability that the student reads fewer than \(26\) books is \(\dfrac{3}{4}\).  [5 marks]
(a) Find \(k\).
(b) Write down the range, the inter-quartile range and the standard deviation of the distribution.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Students reading fewer than \(26\) books: \(10+k+14=24+k\);
total students \(=40+k\). Hence \(\dfrac{24+k}{40+k}=\dfrac{3}{4}\).
Step 2. Cross-multiply: \(4(24+k)=3(40+k)\), so \(96+4k=120+3k\) and \(k=24\). The total is \(64\) students.
Step 3. Smallest value \(=23\), largest \(=28\), so range \(=28-23=5\).
Step 4. Cumulative frequencies: \(10,34,48,56,62,64\). The \(16\)th value is \(24\) so \(Q_{1}=24\);
the \(48\)th value is \(25\) so \(Q_{3}=25\). IQR \(=25-24=1\).
Step 5. \(\sum fx=1582\), so \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{1582}{64}\approx24.72\); \(\sum fx^{2}=39\,214\), so \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{39\,214}{64}-24.72^{2}}\approx1.30\).
Answer: (a) \(k=24\)  (b) range \(=5\), IQR \(=1\), standard deviation \(\approx1.30\)
