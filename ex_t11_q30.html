T11 Q30

In the figure, a radius of a circle bisects a chord. If the radius and the chord meet at an angle of $(3x + 6)^\circ$, find $x$.

Solution

A radius that bisects a chord is perpendicular to the chord.
Thus, the angle between the radius and the chord is $90^\circ$.
Set up the equation: $3x+6=90$.
Subtract $6$: $3x=84$.
Divide by $3$: $x=28$.
$x=28$.
