A box contains $n$ white balls, 5 black balls and 8 red balls. If a ball is randomly drawn from the box, then the probability of drawing a red ball is $\dfrac{2}{5}$.

Find the value of $n$.   [3 marks]

Step 1 · Set up Count the total number of balls in the box. $$\text{Total} = n + 5 + 8 = n + 13$$ Hint: the total is the sum of all colours — remember to include the unknown $n$.
Step 2 · Form the equation
Probability of an Event \(P(E)=\dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total number of outcomes}}\) There are 8 red balls out of \(n+13\) balls in total.
Number of favourable outcomes (red balls) $= 8$. $$P(\text{red}) = \frac{8}{n+5+8} = \frac{8}{n+13}$$ Given $P(\text{red}) = \dfrac{2}{5}$: $$\frac{8}{n+13} = \frac{2}{5}$$ Hint: probability $=\dfrac{\text{number of red balls}}{\text{total number of balls}}$. (1M for a correct expression.)
Step 3 · Solve for n
Cross-Multiplication \(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d}\ \Longrightarrow\ ad=bc\) Applying it to \(\dfrac{8}{n+13}=\dfrac{2}{5}\) gives \(8\times5=2(n+13)\).
Cross-multiply: $$8 \times 5 = 2(n+13)$$ $$40 = 2n + 26$$ $$2n = 14$$ $$n = 7$$ Hint: cross-multiplication is valid since the denominators are non-zero. (1A for correct answer.)
Step 4 · Check If $n=7$, total $=7+5+8=20$ and $P(\text{red})=\dfrac{8}{20}=\dfrac{2}{5}$ ✓ Hint: always verify by substituting back into the original probability.
Answer: $n = 7$

Question. A box contains \(n\) white balls, 6 black balls and 9 red balls. If a ball is drawn at random, the probability of drawing a red ball is \(\dfrac{3}{7}\). Find \(n\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Total number of balls \(=n+6+9=n+15\).
Step 2. \(P(\text{red})=\dfrac{9}{n+15}=\dfrac{3}{7}\).
Step 3. Cross-multiply: \(9\times7=3(n+15)\), i.e. \(63=3n+45\).
Step 4. So \(3n=18\) and \(n=6\). Check: \(\dfrac{9}{21}=\dfrac{3}{7}\) ✓
Answer: \(n=6\)
