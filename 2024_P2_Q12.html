[2024MC#12]  Rate, Ratio and Variations

A car travels at an average speed of $60$ km/h for $18$ minutes and then the car travels at an average speed of $40$ km/h for $27$ minutes. The average speed of the car for the whole journey is

  • A. $48$ km/h.
  • B. $50$ km/h.
  • C. $52$ km/h.
  • D. $54$ km/h.

1Identify the two legs of the journey

Leg 1: speed $=60$ km/h, time $=18$ min.
Leg 2: speed $=40$ km/h, time $=27$ min.

These are two different speeds,
so we cannot simply take $\dfrac{60+40}{2}$.

Hint: "Average speed" means total distance ÷ total time, not the mean of the two speeds.

2Convert minutes to hours

$$18\text{ min}=\frac{18}{60}\text{ h},\qquad 27\text{ min}=\frac{27}{60}\text{ h}$$

Total time $=\dfrac{18}{60}+\dfrac{27}{60}=\dfrac{45}{60}=0.75$ h $=45$ min.

Hint: Keep the same unit (hours) for speed and time, or work directly in minutes — both give the same answer.

3Find the distance of each leg

Distance, Speed and Time \(\text{distance}=\text{speed}\times\text{time}\) Each leg is converted separately: \(45\times\dfrac{1}{3}=15\) km and \(30\times\dfrac{2}{3}=20\) km.

$$\text{Distance}=\text{Speed}\times\text{Time}$$

Leg 1: $60\times\dfrac{18}{60}=60\times 18\text{ min-units}=18$ km (using the 60-based minute shortcut).

Leg 2: $40\times\dfrac{27}{60}=40\times 27\text{ min-units}=18$ km.

Total distance $=18+18=36$ km.

Hint: Working in "minute units" ($\text{km/h}\times\text{min}$) is safe because the $60$ cancels at the end.

4Apply the average-speed formula

Average Speed \(\text{average speed}=\dfrac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}\) It is not the mean of the two speeds; here it equals \(\dfrac{15+20}{1}=35\) km/h.

$$\text{Average speed}=\frac{\text{Total distance}}{\text{Total time}}=\frac{60\times 18+40\times 27}{18+27}$$

$$=\frac{1080+1080}{45}=\frac{2160}{45}$$

Hint: This is exactly the weighted average with the times as weights — faster leg weighted more heavily.

5Evaluate and conclude

$$\frac{2160}{45}=48$$

So the average speed for the whole journey is $48$ km/h,
which is option A.

Check: $48$ lies between $40$ and $60$, and is closer to $40$ because the car spent more time (27 min) at $40$ km/h. ✓
Answer: A. 48 km/h  $\left(\dfrac{60\times18+40\times27}{18+27}=48\right)$

Question. A car travels at \(45\) km/h for \(20\) minutes, then at \(30\) km/h for \(40\) minutes. Find the average speed for the whole journey.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Convert the times to hours: \(20\) min \(=\dfrac{1}{3}\) h and \(40\) min \(=\dfrac{2}{3}\) h,
so the total time is \(1\) h.
Step 2. First leg: distance \(=45\times\dfrac{1}{3}=15\) km.
Step 3. Second leg: distance \(=30\times\dfrac{2}{3}=20\) km, so the total distance is \(35\) km.
Step 4. Average speed \(=\dfrac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}=\dfrac{35}{1}=35\) km/h.
Step 5. Check: \(35\) lies between \(30\) and \(45\), and is closer to \(30\) because more time was spent at the slower speed ✓.
Answer: \(35\) km/h
