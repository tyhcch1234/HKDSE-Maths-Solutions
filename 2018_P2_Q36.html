Step 1 — Identify the given information

HKDSE 2018 Paper 2 Q36

Equations — Relations of Roots

If $m \ne n$ and $2m^2 + 5m = 2n^2 + 5n = 14$, then $(m+2)(n+2) =$

A. $-8$ B. $2$ C. $6$ D. $16$
Step 1
Roots of a Quadratic Equation \(\text{if } p(a)=0,\ \text{then } a \text{ is a root of } p(x)=0\) Both \\(m\\) and \\(n\\) satisfy \\(2x^{2}+5x-14=0\\) and are different, so they are its two roots.

Interpret the given information.

Since $2m^2+5m=14$ and $2n^2+5n=14$ with $m \ne n$,
both $m$ and $n$ satisfy the same quadratic equation $$2x^2+5x-14=0.$$

Hence $m$ and $n$ are the two distinct roots of this equation.

Substitute $x=m$ and $x=n$ into one quadratic — this reveals that $m,n$ are its roots.
Step 2
Sum and Product of Roots of a Quadratic \(m+n=-\dfrac{b}{a},\qquad mn=\dfrac{c}{a}\) With \\(a=2\\), \\(b=5\\), \\(c=-14\\): \\(m+n=-\\dfrac{5}{2}\\) and \\(mn=-7\\).

Sum and product of roots.

For $ax^2+bx+c=0$ with roots $m,n$: $$m+n=-\frac{b}{a},\qquad mn=\frac{c}{a}.$$

Here $a=2,\;b=5,\;c=-14$,
so $$m+n=-\frac{5}{2},\qquad mn=\frac{-14}{2}=-7.$$

Watch the sign of $c$: $-14 \div 2 = -7$.
Step 3
Expansion of a Product of Binomials \((m+2)(n+2)=mn+2(m+n)+4\) The target expression is rewritten using only \\(m+n\\) and \\(mn\\), which are already known.

Expand the target expression.

$$(m+2)(n+2)=mn+2m+2n+4=mn+2(m+n)+4.$$

Rewrite the expression using the sum $m+n$ and product $mn$ already found.
Step 4

Substitute and evaluate.

$$(m+2)(n+2)=(-7)+2\left(-\frac{5}{2}\right)+4$$

$$=-7-5+4=-8.$$

$2 \times \left(-\tfrac{5}{2}\right) = -5$.
Answer: A.  $-8$
