Question

Let \(k\) be a constant. The straight line \(3x-y-2=0\) and the circle \(5x^2+5y^2+kx+4y-20=0\) intersect at the points \(P\) and \(Q\). If the \(x\)-coordinate of the mid-point of \(PQ\) is \(2\), find \(k\).

  • A. \(-152\)
  • B. \(-52\)
  • C. \(148\)
  • D. \(248\)

Solution

STEP 1 · Substitute the line into the circle

From the line, make \(y\) the subject:

$$y = 3x-2$$

Substitute into \(5x^2+5y^2+kx+4y-20=0\):

$$5x^2+5(3x-2)^2+kx+4(3x-2)-20=0$$ $$5x^2+5(9x^2-12x+4)+kx+12x-8-20=0$$ $$50x^2+(k-48)x-8=0 \quad \cdots (*)$$
The \(x\)-coordinates of \(P\) and \(Q\) are the two roots \(p\) and \(q\) of \((*)\).
Substitution Method \(y=mx+c\ \text{into the circle}\ \Longrightarrow\ ax^{2}+bx+c=0\) Replacing \(y\) by \(3x-2\) leaves one quadratic in \(x\) whose roots are the \(x\)-coordinates of \(P\) and \(Q\).
STEP 2 · Sum of roots = twice the mid-point \(x\)

By the relations of roots of \((*)\):

$$p+q = -\frac{k-48}{50} = \frac{48-k}{50}$$

The \(x\)-coordinate of the mid-point of \(PQ\) is \(2\):

$$\frac{p+q}{2}=2 \;\Longrightarrow\; p+q=4$$ $$\frac{48-k}{50}=4 \;\Longrightarrow\; 48-k=200$$ $$k = -152$$
Sum of Roots of a Quadratic \(p+q=-\dfrac{b}{a}\) Here \(p+q=-\dfrac{k-48}{50}\), and the mid-point condition gives \(p+q=2\times2=4\).
STEP 3 · Locate the mid-point \(M\)

With the \(x\)-coordinate \(2\) on the line \(3x-y-2=0\):

$$3(2)-y-2=0 \;\Longrightarrow\; y=4$$

So the mid-point of the chord is \(M(2,4)\).

Check with \(k=-152\): \(p+q=4\) ✓
STEP 4 · Perpendicular check (line ⋯ radius)

Write the circle in centre–radius form. Divide by \(5\):

$$x^2+y^2+\frac{k}{5}x+\frac{4}{5}y-4=0$$

Centre \(O\left(-\dfrac{k}{10},-\dfrac{2}{5}\right)\), i.e. \(O\left(\dfrac{76}{5},-\dfrac{2}{5}\right)\) when \(k=-152\).

Since the line joining the centre to the mid-point of a chord is perpendicular to the chord:

$$\text{slope of }OM \times \text{slope of }PQ = -1$$ $$\frac{4-\left(-\frac{2}{5}\right)}{2-\left(-\frac{k}{10}\right)}\times 3 = -1$$

Solving gives \(k=-152\), confirming our answer. ✓

Perpendicular from the Centre to a Chord \(OM\perp PQ\ \text{when } M\ \text{is the mid-point of }PQ\) The centre \(O\), the mid-point \(M(2,4)\) and the chord \(PQ\) of slope 3 must satisfy \(m_{OM}\times3=-1\).
Answer: \(k = -152\)  (Option A)

Show the worked solution

Step 2. Expanding: \(5x^{2}-4x+1+kx+6x-3-5=0\), i.e. \(5x^{2}+(k+2)x-7=0\).
Step 3. The sum of the roots is \(-\dfrac{k+2}{5}\); the mid-point has \(x=1\), so the sum is \(2\): \(-\dfrac{k+2}{5}=2\).
Step 4. Hence \(k+2=-10\) and \(k=-12\). The mid-point is \(M(1,\,1)\), since \(y=2(1)-1=1\).
