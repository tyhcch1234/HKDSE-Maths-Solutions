Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Sine/Cosine Law

T13 Q25

In triangle \(ABC\), \(BC = 5\) and \(AC = 7\), and angle \(B = 50^\circ\). Write down an equation, using the sine law, relating angle \(A\) to the given values.

Solution

$\tfrac{\sin A}{5} = \tfrac{\sin50°}{7}$.
$\sin A = \tfrac{5\sin50°}{7} \approx 0.547$, $A \approx 33.2°$.
