Regular polygon — interior angles, diagonals & rotational symmetry

Angle Sum of Polygon Regular n-gon Diagonals Rotational Symmetry

Question MC

If an interior angle of a regular \(n\)-sided polygon is \(4\) times an exterior angle of the polygon, which of the following is/are true?

  • I. The value of \(n\) is \(10\).
  • II. The number of diagonals of the polygon is \(10\).
  • III. The number of folds of rotational symmetry of the polygon is \(10\).

A. I only    B. II only    C. I and II only    D. II and III only

Statement I — Finding \(n\) Angle relations

Interior \(=\frac{(n-2)180°}{n}\) = 120.0°
Exterior \(=\frac{360°}{n}\) = 60.0°
Ratio int : ext = 2.00
Step 1

For a regular \(n\)-sided polygon, each interior angle and each exterior angle are related by the fact they form a straight line (they are supplementary). Let the exterior angle be \(x\).

\[\text{Interior} = 4x, \qquad \text{Interior} + \text{Exterior} = 180^\circ\]
Hint: An interior angle and its adjacent exterior angle always add up to \(180^\circ\).
Step 2

Substitute the interior angle:

\[4x + x = 180^\circ \;\Rightarrow\; 5x = 180^\circ \;\Rightarrow\; x = 36^\circ\]
Hint: The exterior angle is one-fifth of \(180^\circ\), i.e. \(36^\circ\).
Step 3

Since the exterior angles of a polygon sum to \(360^\circ\):

\[n \times 36^\circ = 360^\circ \;\Rightarrow\; n = 10\]

Equivalently, the interior angle is \(4 \times 36^\circ = 144^\circ\), and

\[\frac{(n-2)180^\circ}{n}=144^\circ \Rightarrow (n-2)180 = 144n \Rightarrow 36n = 360 \Rightarrow n = 10.\]
Answer: Statement I is TRUE. \(n = 10\).
Hint: Drag the slider to \(n=10\) and check that the ratio becomes exactly \(4.00\).
0 / 3

Statement II — Number of diagonals Combinatorics

From one vertex: \(n-3\) = 7
Total end-points: \(n(n-3)\) = 70
Diagonals: \(\frac{n(n-3)}{2}\) = 35
Step 1

Take one vertex \(P\) of the decagon. A diagonal from \(P\) joins \(P\) to a vertex that is not next to it. Since \(P\) has \(2\) neighbours, the number of diagonals from \(P\) is

\[n - 3 = 10 - 3 = 7\]
Hint: From each vertex you can draw diagonals to all vertices except itself and its two neighbours.
Step 2

Counting over all \(10\) vertices gives \(10 \times 7 = 70\) end-points of diagonals. But each diagonal has \(2\) end-points, so every diagonal has been counted twice.

Hint: Be careful about double counting — the segment \(AB\) is the same diagonal as \(BA\).
Step 3

Divide by \(2\):

\[\text{Number of diagonals} = \frac{n(n-3)}{2} = \frac{10 \times 7}{2} = 35\]
Answer: The number of diagonals is \(35\), not \(10\). Statement II is FALSE.
Hint: Toggle “Show all diagonals” in the diagram to see there are far more than 10.

Statement III — Rotational symmetry Symmetry

Order of rotational symmetry = \(n\) = 10
Angle per fold = \(360°/n\) = 36°
Step 1

A regular \(n\)-sided polygon looks unchanged after a rotation of \(\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}\) about its centre. We already found \(n = 10\).

\[\frac{360^\circ}{10} = 36^\circ\]
Hint: Rotate the decagon by \(36^\circ\) in the diagram — it lands exactly on itself.
Step 2

A figure has order of rotational symmetry \(k\) if it maps onto itself under rotations of \(\dfrac{360^\circ}{k}, \dfrac{2\cdot360^\circ}{k},\dots,\dfrac{(k-1)\cdot360^\circ}{k}\) and \(360^\circ\).

For a regular \(n\)-gon these are exactly the rotations \(\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}, \dfrac{2\cdot360^\circ}{n}, \dots, 360^\circ\), i.e. \(n\) positions in total — this is the “number of folds of rotational symmetry”.

Hint: The word “folds” here means the number of rotational positions that make the polygon coincide with itself.
Step 3

Therefore the number of folds of rotational symmetry is \(n\), which is

\[n = 10\]
Answer: The number of folds of rotational symmetry is \(10\). Statement III is TRUE.
Hint: Count the highlighted positions while dragging the rotation slider — there are \(10\) of them.
0 / 3

Summary of Answers

  • I. \(n = 10\) — TRUE  (\(4 \times 36^\circ = 144^\circ\), and \(\frac{(10-2)180^\circ}{10}=144^\circ\))
  • II. Number of diagonals \(= \frac{10 \times (10-3)}{2} = 35\) — FALSE
  • III. Number of folds of rotational symmetry \(= n = 10\) — TRUE

Correct choice:  C. I and III only  (as given: I and III only)

Note: Statements I and III are true; II is false, so the answer is “I and III only”.
