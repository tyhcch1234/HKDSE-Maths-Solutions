HKDSE Mathematics 2024 · Paper 2 · Q24

The coordinates of the point \(U\) are \((-3,-8)\). \(U\) is rotated anticlockwise about the origin through \(90^\circ\) to the point \(V\). \(V\) is then reflected with respect to the straight line \(x=2\) to the point \(W\). Find the \(x\)-coordinate of \(W\).
A. \(-4\)
B. \(-3\)
C. \(7\)
D. \(12\)
STEP 1 · Understand the transformations
We have three points to find in order:
\(U(-3,-8)\ \xrightarrow{\text{rotate }90^\circ\text{ anticlockwise about }O}\ V\) \(\ \xrightarrow{\text{reflect in }x=2}\ W\).
Hint: rotation changes both coordinates; reflection only flips the \(x\)-coordinate.
STEP 2 · Rotate \(U\) \(90^\circ\) anticlockwise
A \(90^\circ\) anticlockwise rotation about the origin maps \[(x,y)\ \longrightarrow\ (-y,\ x).\] So for \(U(-3,-8)\): \[V=(-(-8),\,-3)=(8,\,-3).\]
Check: rotating the positive \(x\)-axis \(90^\circ\) anticlockwise gives the positive \(y\)-axis — the rule works.
Rotation about the Origin \((x,y)\to(-y,\,x)\quad(90^\circ\text{ anticlockwise})\) \(U(-3,-8)\to V(8,-3)\).
STEP 3 · Reflection in the line \(x=2\)
Reflection in a vertical line \(x=k\) sends \[(x,y)\ \longrightarrow\ (2k-x,\ y).\] With \(k=2\) and \(V(8,-3)\): \[W=(2(2)-8,\ -3)=(-4,\ -3).\]
The line \(x=2\) is the perpendicular bisector of segment \(VW\).
Reflection in a Vertical Line \((x,y)\to(2k-x,\,y)\quad(\text{in }x=k)\) With \(k=2\) and \(V(8,-3)\): \(W=(4-8,-3)=(-4,-3)\).
STEP 4 · Read off the \(x\)-coordinate of \(W\)
From Step 3, \(W=(-4,-3)\), so \[x\text{-coordinate of }W=-4.\] This matches option A.
ANSWER
\(x\)-coordinate of \(W=-4\)  →  Option A

Show the worked solution
