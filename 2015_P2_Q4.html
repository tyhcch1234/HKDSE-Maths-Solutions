2015 MC Q4

Which of the following is true?

$0.0023456789 = ?$
  • A. $0.0023456789 = 0.00235$  (correct to 6 decimal places)
  • B. $0.0023456789 = 0.002345$  (correct to 6 decimal places)
  • C. $0.0023456789 = 0.002346$  (correct to 6 significant figures)
  • D. $0.0023456789 = 0.00234568$  (correct to 6 significant figures)
1Find the first significant figure.
Significant Figures \(0.00\,2\,3\,4\,5\,6\,7\,8\,9\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{counting starts at }2\) Leading zeros are not significant, so the first significant figure of \(0.0023456789\) is the \(2\).
Ignore all leading zeros.
The digits of $0.0023456789$ after the leading zeros are $$2\;\underline{3}\;\underline{4}\;\underline{5}\;\underline{6}\;\underline{7}\;\underline{8}\;\underline{9}$$ so the first significant figure is 2 (in the 3rd decimal place). Significant figures start at the first non-zero digit.
2Round to 6 significant figures.
Count 6 sig. figs from the 2: $$2,\;3,\;4,\;5,\;6,\;\underbrace{7}_{\text{6th s.f.}},\;\underbrace{8}_{\text{decides}}\;9$$ The 7th digit is $8 \ge 5$,
so round the 6th digit up
Rounding Rule \(d\ge5\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{round up},\qquad d<5\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{round down}\) The digit after the 6th significant figure is \(8\ge5\), so \(0.00234567\,|\,89\) rounds up to \(0.00234568\).
: $$0.0023456789 \;\to\; 0.00234568 \quad (6\text{ s.f.})$$ Look at the digit just after the 6th sig. fig. to decide up or down.
3Check the "6 decimal places" claim.
6 decimal places means keep 6 digits after the decimal point: $0.002345\,|\,6789$ → the 7th decimal is $6 \ge 5$,
so $$0.0023456789 \to 0.002346 \quad (6\text{ d.p.})$$ This is not
Decimal Places \(6\ \text{d.p.}=\text{keep 6 digits after the decimal point}\) Here \(0.002345\,|\,6789\) has 7th decimal \(6\ge5\), so it becomes \(0.002346\) — a different answer from 6 s.f.
$0.002346$ (option C) — and it is definitely not $0.00235$ or $0.002345$. Options A and B use decimal places,
but their values are wrong anyway.
4Compare with the options.
Option Claim Verdict
A $0.00235$ (6 d.p.) ✗ value wrong
B $0.002345$ (6 d.p.) ✗ value wrong
C $0.002346$ (6 s.f.) ✗ only 2 s.f.,
not 6
D $0.00234568$ (6 s.f.) ✓ correct
Counting sig. figs from the first non-zero digit gives 6: 2,
3,4,5,6,8.
AnswerD  —  $0.0023456789 = 0.00234568$ (correct to 6 significant figures)

Question. Round \(0.00047362581\) correct to (a) \(4\) significant figures,
(b) \(5\) decimal places.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Ignore the leading zeros: the first significant figure is \(4\),
followed by \(7,3,6,2,\dots\)
Step 2. (a) The first four significant figures are \(4,7,3,6\), and the next digit is \(2<5\),
so the 4th figure stays: \(0.0004736\).
Step 3. (b) Five decimal places means 5 digits after the point: \(0.00047\,|\,362581\). The next digit is \(3<5\),
so the answer is \(0.00047\).
Answer: (a) \(0.0004736\) (b) \(0.00047\)
