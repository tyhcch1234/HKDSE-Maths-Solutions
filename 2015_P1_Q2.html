Question.

Make b the subject of the formula \[ \frac{4a+5b-7}{b} = 8. \quad [3 \text{ marks}] \]

Step-by-step Solution

1Clear the denominator
Multiply both sides by \(b\) (note \(b \neq 0\)): \[ \frac{4a+5b-7}{b}\times b = 8 \times b \] \[ 4a + 5b - 7 = 8b \]
Hint: multiply through by the denominator \(b\) to remove the fraction. (1M)
2Collect the \(b\)-terms on one side
Subtract \(5b\) from both sides: \[ 4a - 7 = 8b - 5b \] \[ 4a - 7 = 3b \]
Hint: move every term containing \(b\) to the same side, keeping \(a\) on the other. (1M)
3Divide by the coefficient of \(b\)
Divide both sides by \(3\): \[ b = \frac{4a - 7}{3} \]
Hint: \(b\) is now the subject with coefficient \(1\). (1A)
Answer: \(\displaystyle b = \frac{4a - 7}{3}\)
