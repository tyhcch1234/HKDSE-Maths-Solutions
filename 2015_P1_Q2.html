HKDSE Math 2015 Paper 1 Q2

Make b the subject of the formula

Formulas Sequences Algebra

Make \(b\) the subject of the formula

\[ \frac{4a+5b-7}{b}=8 \]

[3 marks]

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Multiply both sides by \(b\)

Hint: Clear the fraction

To eliminate the denominator, multiply every term by \(b\) (assuming \(b \neq 0\)).

\[ \frac{4a+5b-7}{b} \times b = 8 \times b \] \[ 4a + 5b - 7 = 8b \]
2

Group \(b\) terms on one side

Hint: Collect like terms

Move all terms containing \(b\) to the right side and constants/other terms to the left.

\[ 4a - 7 = 8b - 5b \] \[ 4a - 7 = 3b \]
3

Divide by the coefficient of \(b\)

Hint: Isolate \(b\)

Divide both sides by \(3\) to make \(b\) the subject.

\[ \frac{4a - 7}{3} = b \] \[ \boxed{b = \frac{4a - 7}{3}} \]
