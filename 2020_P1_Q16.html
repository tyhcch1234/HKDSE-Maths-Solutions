HKDSE 2020 · Paper 1 · Q16

Geometric Sequence

Question

The 3rd term and the 6th term of a geometric sequence are 144 and 486 respectively.

  1. Find the 1st term of the sequence. (2 marks)
  2. Find the least value of \( n \) such that the sum of the first \( n \) terms of the sequence is greater than \( 8\times10^{18} \). (3 marks)

Solution

STEP 1 · Set up Let the first term be \( a \) and the common ratio be \( r \). Then \[ ar^{2}=144 \qquad\text{and}\qquad ar^{5}=486. \]
STEP 2 · Find the ratio \( r \) \[ r^{3}=\frac{ar^{5}}{ar^{2}}=\frac{486}{144}=\frac{27}{8} \implies r=\frac{3}{2}. \]
STEP 3 · Part (a) — the first term \[ a=\frac{144}{r^{2}}=\frac{144}{\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{2}}=\frac{144}{\frac{9}{4}}=144\times\frac{4}{9}=64. \]
STEP 4 · Part (b) — set up the sum condition \[ S_{n}=\frac{a(r^{n}-1)}{r-1}=\frac{64\left[\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{n}-1\right]}{\frac{1}{2}}=128\left[\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{n}-1\right]. \] We require \[ 128\left[\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{n}-1\right] > 8\times10^{18} \implies \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{n} > 6.25\times10^{16}+1. \]
STEP 5 · Solve for \( n \) with common logarithms \[ n\,\log_{10}\!\left(\tfrac{3}{2}\right) > \log_{10}\!\left(6.25\times10^{16}+1\right) \] \[ n > \frac{16.79588\ldots}{0.17609\ldots} \approx 95.38. \] Since \( n \) is a positive integer, the least value is \( n=96 \).
STEP 6 · Check \[ S_{95}\approx 128(5.354\times10^{16}-1)\approx 6.85\times10^{18} < 8\times10^{18}, \] \[ S_{96}\approx 128(8.029\times10^{16}-1)\approx 1.03\times10^{19} > 8\times10^{18}. \]
Final Answer: (a) \( a = 64 \)  |  (b) \( n = 96 \)
