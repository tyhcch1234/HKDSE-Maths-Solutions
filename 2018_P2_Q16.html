Area of a quadrilateral inside a parallelogram · scroll the solution to highlight the diagram

Parallelogram ABCD Triangle ABF (120 cm²) Triangle EBF Quadrilateral CDFE = ? Segment BE : EC = 5 : 3
Figure not drawn to scale proportions? — it is: BE:EC = 5:3, AB∥DC, AD∥BC.

Question

In the figure, $ABCD$ is a parallelogram. $E$ is a point lying on $BC$ such that $BE:EC = 5:3$. $AE$ and $BD$ intersect at the point $F$. If the area of $\triangle ABF$ is $120\text{ cm}^2$, then the area of the quadrilateral $CDFE$ is
A. $237\text{ cm}^2$
B. $307\text{ cm}^2$
C. $312\text{ cm}^2$
D. $429\text{ cm}^2$
💡 Key idea: find similar triangles $\triangle EBF \sim \triangle ADF$, then use the common-height area ratio for triangles $BEF$/$ABF$ and $ABE$/$BCD$.
STEP 1 · SIMILAR TRIANGLES
In $\triangle EBF$ and $\triangle ADF$: $$\angle EFB=\angle AFD \ (\text{vert. opp. } \angle\text{s})$$ $$\angle EBF=\angle ADF \ (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s},\ BC\parallel AD)$$ $$\angle BEF=\angle DAF \ (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s},\ BC\parallel AD)$$ $$\therefore \triangle EBF \sim \triangle ADF \quad (\text{AAA})$$
STEP 2 · SIDE RATIO
$DA=BC$ (prop. of //gram), so $$BE:DA = BE:BC = 5:(5+3)=5:8$$ By corresponding sides of similar triangles: $$EF:AF = BE:DA = 5:8$$
STEP 3 · AREA OF $\triangle EBF$
$\triangle EBF$ and $\triangle ABF$ share the same height (from vertex $B$ to line $AE$), so $$\frac{[\triangle EBF]}{[\triangle ABF]}=\frac{EF}{AF}=\frac{5}{8}$$ $$[\triangle EBF]=120\times\frac{5}{8}=75\text{ cm}^2$$
STEP 4 · AREA OF $\triangle ABE$
$$[\triangle ABE]=[\triangle ABF]+[\triangle EBF]=120+75=195\text{ cm}^2$$
STEP 5 · LINK $\triangle ABE$ TO PARALLELOGRAM
Let $x\text{ cm}^2$ be the area of quadrilateral $CDFE$. Since $[\triangle ABE]$ and parallelogram $ABCD$ share the same height (with bases $BE$ and $BC$ along the same line): $$\frac{[ABCD]}{[\triangle ABE]}=\frac{BC}{BE}=\frac{8}{5}$$ Also $[ABCD]=[\triangle ABF]+[\triangle EBF]+[\text{quad. }CDFE]=(120+75)+x=(x+75)\text{ cm}^2$ $$\therefore \frac{x+75}{195}=\frac{8}{5}$$
STEP 6 · SOLVE
$$x+75=\frac{8}{5}\times 195=312$$ $$x=312-75=237$$
Answer: A.   $237\text{ cm}^2$
