HKDSE Math 2021 Paper 2 Q11 Rate, Ratio & Variations Algebraic Manipulation

Proportional Relation Challenge

If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are non-zero numbers such that $$\frac{2\alpha + 3\beta}{3\alpha + 2\beta} = \frac{7}{10},$$ then $\displaystyle \frac{2\alpha + \beta}{\alpha + 2\beta} =$

A. $\frac{1}{2}$
B. $\frac{3}{2}$
C. $\frac{11}{6}$
D. $\frac{13}{8}$

💡 Key concept: Cross‑multiplication to find a linear relation between $\alpha$ and $\beta$, then substitute to evaluate the required expression.

1 Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Cross‑multiply the given equation ＋

Multiply both sides by $10(3\alpha + 2\beta)$ to eliminate the denominators:

$$10(2\alpha + 3\beta) = 7(3\alpha + 2\beta)$$

Hint: Cross‑multiplication is valid because $\alpha, \beta \neq 0$ and $3\alpha+2\beta \neq 0$.
Step 2: Expand and simplify ＋

Expand both sides:

$$20\alpha + 30\beta = 21\alpha + 14\beta$$

Collect like terms:

$$30\beta - 14\beta = 21\alpha - 20\alpha$$

$$16\beta = \alpha$$

Key result: $\alpha = 16\beta$. This shows $\alpha$ is 16 times $\beta$.
Step 3: Substitute into the target expression ＋

We want to evaluate $\displaystyle \frac{2\alpha + \beta}{\alpha + 2\beta}$. Replace $\alpha$ by $16\beta$:

$$\frac{2(16\beta) + \beta}{16\beta + 2\beta} = \frac{32\beta + \beta}{16\beta + 2\beta} = \frac{33\beta}{18\beta}$$

Cancel $\beta$: Since $\beta \neq 0$, we can divide numerator and denominator by $\beta$.
Step 4: Simplify the fraction ＋

Simplify $\displaystyle \frac{33}{18}$ by dividing numerator and denominator by $3$:

$$\frac{33}{18} = \frac{11}{6}$$

Therefore, the answer is C. $\frac{11}{6}$.

✅ Final Answer: C
