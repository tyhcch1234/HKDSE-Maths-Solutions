HKDSE 2013 · Paper 1 · Q6

Polar Coordinates and Symmetry

Question

In a polar coordinate system, \(O\) is the pole. The polar coordinates of the points \(A\) and \(B\) are \((26,\,10^\circ)\) and \((26,\,130^\circ)\) respectively. Let \(L\) be the axis of reflectional symmetry of \(\triangle OAB\).

  1. Describe the geometric relationship between \(L\) and \(\angle AOB\). (2 marks)
  2. Find the polar coordinates of the point of intersection of \(L\) and \(AB\). (2 marks)

Solution

Step 1 · Shape of \(\triangle OAB\)

Since \(OA = OB = 26\), \(\triangle OAB\) is isosceles with vertex \(O\), and

\[ \angle AOB = 130^\circ - 10^\circ = 120^\circ. \]
Step 2 · Part (a): relationship between \(L\) and \(\angle AOB\)

In an isosceles triangle, the axis of symmetry through the vertex bisects the vertex angle (and meets the base perpendicularly at its midpoint).

Hence \(L\) bisects \(\angle AOB\), i.e. \(\angle AOL = \angle BOL = 60^\circ\), and \(L\) is also the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\).

Step 3 · Direction of \(L\)

The axis \(L\) lies halfway between the directions of \(OA\) and \(OB\):

\[ \frac{10^\circ + 130^\circ}{2} = 70^\circ. \]
Step 4 · Part (b): the intersection point

Let \(M\) be the intersection of \(L\) and \(AB\). Then \(OM \perp AB\), and in right triangle \(OMA\),

\[ \angle AOM = 60^\circ, \qquad OM = OA\cos 60^\circ = 26 \times \tfrac{1}{2} = 13. \]

So \(M\) lies \(13\) units from \(O\) in the direction \(70^\circ\).

Check: \(AM = OA\sin 60^\circ = 26\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2} = 13\sqrt3\), so \(AB = 26\sqrt3\), matching the chord \(2\cdot26\sin 60^\circ = 26\sqrt3\). ✓
Final Answer: (a) \(L\) bisects \(\angle AOB\) (and is the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\)).  (b) The intersection of \(L\) and \(AB\) has polar coordinates \((13,\,70^\circ)\).
