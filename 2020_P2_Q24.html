Translation, Reflection & Polar Coordinates

Question

The point \( P \) is translated leftwards by 4 units to the point \( Q \). If the coordinates of the reflection image of \( Q \) with respect to the \( y \)-axis are \( (5,-1) \), then the polar coordinates of \( P \) are

  1. \( \left(1,45^{\circ}\right) \)
  2. \( \left(1,225^{\circ}\right) \)
  3. \( \left(\sqrt{2},45^{\circ}\right) \)
  4. \( \left(\sqrt{2},225^{\circ}\right) \)

Solution

STEP 1 · Undo the reflection Reflection in the \( y \)-axis sends \( (x,y)\mapsto(-x,y) \). Since the image of \( Q \) is \( (5,-1) \), \[ Q = (-5,-1). \]
STEP 2 · Undo the translation \( P \) moved 4 units left to become \( Q \), so \( Q = P + (-4,0) \), hence \[ P = Q + (4,0) = (-5+4,\,-1) = (-1,-1). \]
STEP 3 · Convert to polar coordinates \[ r = \sqrt{(-1)^{2}+(-1)^{2}} = \sqrt{2}. \] \( P=(-1,-1) \) lies in the third quadrant with reference angle \( 45^{\circ} \), so \[ \theta = 180^{\circ}+45^{\circ} = 225^{\circ}. \]
Final Answer: D. \( \left(\sqrt{2},\,225^{\circ}\right) \)
