[2024MC#27]
Denote the circle $3x^2+3y^2-6x+12y-4=0$ by $C$.
Which of the following are true?
I. The origin lies inside $C$.
II. The circumference of $C$ is less than $16$.
III. The perpendicular distance from the centre of $C$ to the $x$-axis is $2$.
A. I and II only
B. I and III only
C. II and III only
D. I, II and III
Step 1 · Reduce the circle to standard form

Divide the whole equation by $3$ so that the coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ become $1$:

$$3x^2+3y^2-6x+12y-4=0 \;\Rightarrow\; x^2+y^2-2x+4y-\frac{4}{3}=0$$

Step 2 · Find the centre $G$
Centre of a Circle \(\left(-\dfrac{D}{2},\,-\dfrac{E}{2}\right)\) \(D=-2\), \(E=4\), so the centre is \(G(1,-2)\).

For $x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey+F=0$, the centre is $\left(-\dfrac{D}{2},-\dfrac{E}{2}\right)$.

Here $D=-2,\;E=4$, so $$G=\left(\frac{2}{2},\frac{-4}{2}\right)=(1,\,-2).$$

Step 3 · Find the radius $r$
Radius of a Circle \(r=\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{D}{2}\right)^{2}+\left(\dfrac{E}{2}\right)^{2}-F}\) \(F=-\dfrac43\), so \(r=\sqrt{1+4+\dfrac43}=\sqrt{\dfrac{19}{3}}\approx2.52\).

$$r=\sqrt{\left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{E}{2}\right)^2-F} =\sqrt{(1)^2+(-2)^2-\left(-\frac{4}{3}\right)} =\sqrt{1+4+\frac{4}{3}}=\sqrt{\frac{19}{3}}$$

Numerically, $r=\sqrt{19/3}\approx 2.517$.

Remember: subtract $F$, and $F=-\frac43$ here, so it becomes $+\frac43$.
Step 4 · Test statement I — is $O$ inside $C$?

Compare $OG$ with $r$:

$$OG=\sqrt{(1-0)^2+(-2-0)^2}=\sqrt{1+4}=\sqrt{5}$$

$$\sqrt{5}\;\approx 2.236 \;<\; \sqrt{\frac{19}{3}}\approx 2.517$$

$\therefore$ $O$ lies inside $C$.  
I is TRUE.

Step 5 · Test statement II — circumference <
16?

$$\text{Circumference}=2\pi r=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{19}{3}}.$$

Since $\sqrt{19/3}<2.52$ and $\pi<3.15$: $$2\pi\sqrt{\frac{19}{3}}<2(3.15)(2.52)=15.876<16.$$

$\therefore$ the circumference is less than $16$.  
II is TRUE.

Step 6 · Test statement III — distance to the $x$-axis
Distance from a Point to the x-axis \(d=-y_1\quad(y_1<0)\) The centre is \(G(1,-2)\), so its distance to the \(x\)-axis is \(2\).

The perpendicular distance from $G(1,-2)$ to the $x$-axis ($y=0$) is the opposite of $y_G$,
because $y_G=-2$ is below the axis.

$$d=0-(-2)=2$$

$\therefore$ the distance is $2$.  
III is TRUE.

Step 7 · Conclusion

I, II and III are all true.

Answer: D  (I, II and III)
