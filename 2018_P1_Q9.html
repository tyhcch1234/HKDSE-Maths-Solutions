Equations — linear equation in one unknown from a "speed × time = distance" setting

Step 1 — the whole journey: 210 km in 161 minutes.

P → Q (72 km/h) Q → R (90 km/h) Unknown / highlighted quantity
2018 P1 Q9  ·  5 marks

A car travels from city P to city Q at an average speed of $72\text{ km/h}$ and then travels from city Q to city R at an average speed of $90\text{ km/h}$. It is given that the car travels $210\text{ km}$ in $161$ minutes for the whole journey. How long does the car take to travel from city P to city Q?

1Define the unknown

Let $x$ minutes be the time required for the car to travel from city $P$ to city $Q$.

Then the time for the journey from city $Q$ to city $R$ is $(161 - x)$ minutes.

The two times add up to the whole journey time, 161 minutes.
2Change the units (minutes → hours)

Time is given in minutes but speed is in km/h, so convert:

\[ \frac{x}{60}\ \text{h} \qquad\text{and}\qquad \frac{161-x}{60}\ \text{h} \]

1 minute = $\dfrac{1}{60}$ hour. Ratio of times is unchanged, so the equation could also be written in minutes directly.
Unit Conversion \(1\ \text{minute}=\dfrac{1}{60}\ \text{hour}\) The speeds are in km/h, so the times \(x\) and \(170-x\) minutes must be divided by 60.
3Form the equation: distance = speed × time

Total distance $= 210$ km,

\[ 72\left(\frac{x}{60}\right) + 90\left(\frac{161-x}{60}\right) = 210 \]

1M for the unit change, 1M for obtaining a linear equation in one unknown.
Distance, Speed and Time \(\text{distance}=\text{speed}\times\text{time}\) Each leg contributes speed \(\times\) time: \(60\cdot\dfrac{x}{60}\) and \(84\cdot\dfrac{170-x}{60}\).
4Solve the linear equation

Multiply the whole equation by $60$:

\[ 72x + 90(161 - x) = 210 \times 60 \]

\[ 72x + 14490 - 90x = 12600 \]

\[ -18x = 12600 - 14490 \]

\[ -18x = -1890 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x = 105 \]

Combine like terms first, then divide by the coefficient of $x$.
Solving a Linear Equation \(ax+b=c\ \Longrightarrow\ x=\dfrac{c-b}{a}\) After collecting terms the equation is \(-24x=-1920\).
5Conclusion

Thus, the car takes $105$ minutes to travel from city $P$ to city $Q$.

Check: $72 \times \frac{105}{60} = 126$ km, $90 \times \frac{56}{60} = 84$ km, and $126 + 84 = 210$ km. ✓

1A for the correct answer with proper justification.
Answer: The car takes 105 minutes (i.e. 1 hour 45 minutes) to travel from city $P$ to city $Q$.

Question. A car travels from town P to town Q at \(60\ \text{km/h}\) and then from town Q to town R at \(84\ \text{km/h}\). The whole journey of \(206\ \text{km}\) takes \(170\) minutes. How long does the car take to travel from P to Q?

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \(x\) minutes be the time from P to Q; the time from Q to R is \(170-x\) minutes.
Step 2. Total distance: \(60\left(\dfrac{x}{60}\right)+84\left(\dfrac{170-x}{60}\right)=206\).
Step 3. Multiply by 60: \(60x+14280-84x=12360\), so \(-24x=-1920\) and \(x=80\).
Step 4. Check: \(60\times\dfrac{80}{60}=80\) km and \(84\times\dfrac{90}{60}=126\) km, and \(80+126=206\) km ✓
Answer: \(80\) minutes (1 hour 20 minutes)
