HKDSE Math 2018 Paper 1 Q9 Algebra Equations Speed & Distance

Car Journey: P → Q → R

Find the time taken from city P to city Q.

A car travels from city P to city Q at an average speed of 72 km/h, and then from city Q to city R at an average speed of 90 km/h. The car travels a total distance of 210 km in 161 minutes for the whole journey. How long does the car take to travel from city P to city Q?

⏱️ Total time: 161 min 📏 Total distance: 210 km 🚗 Speeds: 72 km/h & 90 km/h

📘 Step-by-Step Solution

1

Define the variable

Let \( x \) minutes be the time required for the car to travel from city \( P \) to city \( Q \).

2

Express the time for Q → R

The total time for the whole journey is \( 161 \) minutes. Therefore, the time for \( Q \to R \) is \((161 - x)\) minutes.

3

Set up the distance equation

Use the formula \(\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}\).
Important: Convert time from minutes to hours by dividing by \(60\).

💡 Hint: Unit Conversion

\( x \) minutes \(= \frac{x}{60} \) hours, and \( (161 - x) \) minutes \(= \frac{161 - x}{60} \) hours.

\[ 72\left(\frac{x}{60}\right) + 90\left(\frac{161 - x}{60}\right) = 210 \]

First term: distance \(P \to Q\). Second term: distance \(Q \to R\). Sum = total distance 210 km.

4

Simplify and solve the linear equation

Multiply through by \(60\) to clear the denominator:

\(72x + 90(161 - x) = 210 \times 60\)

\(72x + 14490 - 90x = 12600\)

\(-18x + 14490 = 12600\)

\(-18x = 12600 - 14490\)

\(-18x = -1890\)

\(x = 105\)

5

Final Answer

Thus, the car takes 105 minutes to travel from city \( P \) to city \( Q \).
