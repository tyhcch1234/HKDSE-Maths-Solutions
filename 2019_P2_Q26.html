Coordinate Geometry — Locus of a Moving Point at a Fixed Distance from a Line

Equation of a Straight Line Perpendicular Distance Locus Parallel Lines

Q26 The Question

The equation of the straight line \(L\) is \[5x-7y-14=0.\]

If \(P\) is a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that the perpendicular distance from \(P\) to \(L\) is equal to \(3\), then the locus of \(P\) is

  • A. a sector.
  • B. a square.
  • C. a parabola.
  • D. a pair of straight lines.

The key fact: points at a fixed perpendicular distance from a given straight line sit on two straight lines parallel to it.

Step 1

Write down the standard perpendicular-distance formula from a point \(P(x_0,y_0)\) to the line \(ax+by+c=0\):

\[ \text{distance} = \frac{|a x_0 + b y_0 + c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}} \]
Hint: For \(L:5x-7y-14=0\), we have \(a=5,\;b=-7,\;c=-14\).
Step 2

Set the distance equal to 3 and substitute the coefficients:

\[ \frac{|5x-7y-14|}{\sqrt{5^2+(-7)^2}} = 3 \] \[ \frac{|5x-7y-14|}{\sqrt{74}} = 3 \]
Hint: \(\sqrt{5^2+7^2}=\sqrt{25+49}=\sqrt{74}\). The absolute value is the crucial feature — it produces two equations.
Step 3

Remove the absolute value, giving two separate linear equations:

\[ 5x-7y-14 = 3\sqrt{74} \quad \text{or} \quad 5x-7y-14 = -3\sqrt{74} \]
Hint: Each equation is a straight line. Both have the same left-hand side, so they are parallel to \(L\).
Locus: a pair of straight lines, both parallel to \(L\), one on each side at distance 3.

Step 1

Recall the definition of a locus: the set of all points satisfying a given geometric condition.

Here the condition is: "the perpendicular distance from \(P\) to \(L\) equals \(3\)".

Hint: The condition uses perpendicular distance to a straight line — it involves one line, not two fixed points and not a fixed point with a fixed radius.
Step 2

Compare with other loci:

  • Circle: constant distance from a fixed point.
  • Parabola: equal distance from a fixed point (focus) and a fixed line (directrix) — the distances change together.
  • Pair of parallel lines: constant perpendicular distance from a fixed line.
Hint: In the parabola, the distance to the line is not constant — only equal to the distance to the focus, which changes.
Step 3

Therefore the correct choice is:

Answer: D — a pair of straight lines.
Key idea (memorise): Fixed perpendicular distance from a line ⇒ two parallel lines.

Summary at a Glance

  • Line: \(L: 5x-7y-14=0\)  ⟹  \(a=5,\;b=-7\)
  • Distance formula: \(\displaystyle \frac{|5x-7y-14|}{\sqrt{74}} = 3\)
  • Split the absolute value into two equations:
    \(5x-7y-14 = \pm\,3\sqrt{74}\)
  • Both resulting equations are straight lines, and both have slope \(\frac{5}{7}\) — so they are parallel to \(L\).
  • Condition type: fixed perpendicular distance from a line ⇒ pair of parallel straight lines.
  • Final answer: D — a pair of straight lines.
