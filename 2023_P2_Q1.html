HKDSE 2023 Paper 2 · Q1 (Formulas and Sequences) If $\dfrac{a+5b}{7a+2b}=\dfrac{1}{b+3}$, then $a=$
A. $\dfrac{4-b}{5b^{2}+13b}$
B. $\dfrac{4+b}{5b^{2}+13b}$
C. $\dfrac{5b^{2}+13b}{4-b}$
D. $\dfrac{5b^{2}+13b}{4+b}$
Step 1 · Given Start with the equation with both denominators non-zero: \[\frac{a+5b}{7a+2b}=\frac{1}{b+3},\qquad b\neq-3,\;7a+2b\neq0.\]
Hint: the goal is to make $a$ the subject — so isolate every term containing $a$.
Step 2 · Cross-multiply Multiply both sides by $(7a+2b)(b+3)$: \[(a+5b)(b+3)=1\cdot(7a+2b).\]
Hint: cross-multiplication clears both fractions in one move.
Cross-Multiplication \(\dfrac{A}{B}=\dfrac{C}{D}\ \Longrightarrow\ AD=BC\) Multiplying by \((7a+2b)(b+3)\) clears both fractions in one move.
Step 3 · Expand Expand the left-hand side: \[ab+3a+5b^{2}+15b=7a+2b.\]
Hint: $(a+5b)(b+3)=ab+3a+5b^2+15b$ — four terms.
Step 4 · Move non-$a$ terms Bring $7a+2b$ to the left and collect like terms: \[ab+3a-7a+5b^{2}+15b-2b=0\] \[\Rightarrow\; 5b^{2}+13b-4a+ab=0.\]
Hint: gather all $a$-terms on one side, all $b$-only terms on the other.
Step 5 · Take $a$ out Rearrange and factor $a$ from the $a$-terms: \[5b^{2}+13b=4a-ab=a(4-b).\]
Hint: $4a-ab=a(4-b)$ — factoring is the key trick in this question.
Common Factor Law \(4a-ab=a(4-b)\) Factoring \(a\) out of the two \(a\)-terms makes \(a\) the subject of a single product.
Step 6 · Solve for $a$ Divide both sides by $(4-b)$, where $b\neq4$: \[a=\frac{5b^{2}+13b}{4-b}.\]
Hint: the denominator is $(4-b)$, not $(4+b)$ — read the options carefully.
Answer: $\;a=\dfrac{5b^{2}+13b}{4-b}\;$ — option C.

Question. It is given that \(\dfrac{a+3b}{5a+2b}=\dfrac{1}{b+4}\), where \(b\neq-4\) and \(5a+2b\neq0\). Make \(a\) the subject of the formula.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Cross-multiply: \((a+3b)(b+4)=5a+2b\).
Step 2. Expand the left-hand side: \(ab+4a+3b^{2}+12b=5a+2b\).
Step 3. Collect the \(a\)-terms: \(ab+4a-5a=2b-3b^{2}-12b\), i.e. \(a(b-1)=-3b^{2}-10b\).
Step 4. Divide by \(b-1\): \(a=\dfrac{-3b^{2}-10b}{b-1}=\dfrac{3b^{2}+10b}{1-b}\).
Step 5. Check with \(b=2\): the formula gives \(a=-32\), and then \(\dfrac{-32+6}{-160+4}=\dfrac{-26}{-156}=\dfrac{1}{6}\) ✓
Answer: \(a=\dfrac{3b^{2}+10b}{1-b}\)
