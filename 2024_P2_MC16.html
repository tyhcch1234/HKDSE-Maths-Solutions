Mensuration — Cylinder & Circular Cone: using ratios of heights and volumes

Mensuration Cylinder Volume Cone Volume Ratio & Proportion Scale Factor

📋 The Question

The ratio of the height of a right circular cylinder to the height of a right circular cone is \(32:15\), while the ratio of the volume of the circular cylinder to the volume of the circular cone is \(10:9\). If the base radius of the circular cylinder is \(25\text{ cm}\), then the base radius of the circular cone is

  • A. \(20\text{ cm}\)
  • B. \(24\text{ cm}\)
  • C. \(48\text{ cm}\)
  • D. \(60\text{ cm}\) ✔

STEP A Turn the height ratio into actual heights

A ratio \(32:15\) only tells us the shape of the relationship. Introduce a scale factor \(k\) so the heights become real quantities.

Cylinder height \(= 32k\) Cone height \(= 15k\) Cylinder radius \(= 25\) cm
0 / 2
1 Express the two heights with a common factor 1M

Let the height of the cylinder be \(32k\) and the height of the cone be \(15k\), where \(k>0\).

Hint: A ratio \(a:b\) can always be written as \(ak:bk\). The factor \(k\) is the “unit” that turns the ratio into a real length.
2 Write both volumes 1M

Cylinder volume: \[ V_{\text{cyl}} = \pi (25)^2 (32k) = \pi\cdot 625\cdot 32k = 20000\pi k \] Cone volume (with unknown base radius \(r\)): \[ V_{\text{cone}} = \tfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 (15k) = 5\pi r^2 k \]

Hint: Remember the extra factor \(\dfrac13\) in the cone formula — it is the most common source of error in this question.
\(V_{\text{cyl}} = 20000\pi k\) ,   \(V_{\text{cone}} = 5\pi r^2 k\)

STEP B Use the volume ratio \(10:9\) to find \(r\)

The cylinder volume never changes (it is fixed by the given data). Drag the slider so the cone volume reaches exactly \(\dfrac{9}{10}\) of the cylinder volume — the red marker will sit right on the target line at \(r=60\).

0 / 2
1 Set up the ratio equation 1M

\[ \frac{V_{\text{cyl}}}{V_{\text{cone}}}=\frac{10}{9} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{20000\pi k}{5\pi r^{2} k}=\frac{10}{9} \] Cancel \(\pi k\): \[ \frac{20000}{5r^{2}}=\frac{10}{9} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{4000}{r^{2}}=\frac{10}{9} \]

Hint: The unknown \(k\) cancels automatically — that is why the answer is unique even though we never find \(k\).
2 Solve for \(r\) — matching the printed key 1A

Cross-multiplying: \[ 4000 \times 9 = 10 r^{2} \;\Longrightarrow\; 36000 = 10 r^{2} \;\Longrightarrow\; r^{2}=3600 \;\Longrightarrow\; r=60 \]

Hint: Since \(r\) is a length, take the positive square root only. \(r = \pm 60\) is rejected as \(r>0\).
\(r = 60\text{ cm}\)  →  Answer: D

✅ Summary of Answers

  • Cylinder height \(= 32k\),   Cone height \(= 15k\)
  • Cylinder volume \(= \pi(25)^2(32k) = 20000\pi k\)
  • Cone volume \(= \frac13 \pi r^2 (15k) = 5\pi r^2 k\)
  • Ratio equation: \(\dfrac{20000\pi k}{5\pi r^{2}k} = \dfrac{10}{9}\)
  • \(r^{2}=3600 \Rightarrow r = 60\)
  • Final answer: \(60\text{ cm}\) — Option D
