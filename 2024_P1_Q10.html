Partial Variation & Real Roots

Question

It is given that \( g(x) \) is partly constant and partly varies as \( x \). Suppose that \( g(-3) = -21 \) and \( g(7) = 9 \).

  1. Find \( g(x) \). (3 marks)
  2. Let \( h(x) = x\,g(x) + k \), where \( k \) is a real constant. If all the roots of the equation \( h(x) = 0 \) are real numbers, find the range of values of \( k \). (3 marks)

Part (a) — Find \( g(x) \)

\( g(x) \) is partly constant and partly varies as \( x \). That means we can write:

\[ g(x) = a + bx \]

💡 Hint: \( a \) and \( b \) are non-zero constants. The term \( a \) is the constant part, and \( bx \) varies directly with \( x \).

Use the given conditions \( g(-3) = -21 \) and \( g(7) = 9 \):

\( g(-3) = a + b(-3) = a - 3b = -21 \)

\( g(7) = a + b(7) = a + 7b = 9 \)

Subtract the first equation from the second:

\[ (a + 7b) - (a - 3b) = 9 - (-21) \] \[ 10b = 30 \;\Rightarrow\; b = 3 \]

Substitute \( b = 3 \) into \( a + 7b = 9 \):

\[ a + 21 = 9 \;\Rightarrow\; a = -12 \]

✅ Answer (a)

\( g(x) = 3x - 12 \)

Part (b) — Range of \( k \) for real roots

Recall \( g(x) = 3x - 12 \) and \( h(x) = x\,g(x) + k \).

\[ h(x) = x(3x - 12) + k = 3x^2 - 12x + k \]

For a quadratic equation \( Ax^2 + Bx + C = 0 \), all roots are real if the discriminant \( \Delta = B^2 - 4AC \ge 0 \).

Here \( A = 3 \), \( B = -12 \), \( C = k \).

\[ \Delta = (-12)^2 - 4(3)(k) \ge 0 \]

\[ 144 - 12k \ge 0 \]

💡 Hint: The discriminant tells us about the nature of roots. “All real” includes repeated roots, so we use \(\ge 0\).

\[ 144 - 12k \ge 0 \] \[ 144 \ge 12k \] \[ 12 \ge k \quad \text{or} \quad k \le 12 \]

✅ Answer (b)

\( k \le 12 \)
