HKDSE 2026 · Paper 2 · MC35

Logarithmic Graph

Question

Which of the following statements about the graph of \(y=2-\log_3(x+4)\) is/are true?

I. \(y\) increases when \(x\) increases.
II. The \(x\)-intercept of the graph is \(5\).
III. The \(y\)-intercept of the graph is \(2\).

  • A. I only
  • B. II only
  • C. I and III only
  • D. II and III only

Solution

Monotonicity For \(x>-4\), as \(x\) increases, \(x+4\) increases, so \(\log_3(x+4)\) increases (base \(3>1\)). Subtracting it from \(2\) therefore makes \(y=2-\log_3(x+4)\) decrease.
Monotonicity of Logarithmic Functions \(a>1\ \Rightarrow\ y=\log_{a}x\ \text{is increasing for } x>0\) Since \(3>1\), \(\log_{3}(x+4)\) increases with \(x\); the minus sign in \(y=2-\log_{3}(x+4)\) therefore makes \(y\) decrease, so statement I is false.
Equivalently, the base graph \(y=\log_3 x\) is increasing, and \(y=2-\log_3(x+4)\) is its reflection in a horizontal line followed by a shift — still decreasing.
(I false)
x-intercept
Logarithmic to Exponential Form \(\log_{a}X=b\iff X=a^{b}\) Setting \(y=0\) gives \(\log_{3}(x+4)=2\), so \(x+4=3^{2}=9\) and \(x=5\): statement II is true.
\(0=2-\log_3(x+4)\Rightarrow x+4=9\Rightarrow x=5\). (II true)
y-intercept
y-intercept of a Graph \(x=0\ \Rightarrow\ y=2-\log_{3}4\) \(\log_{3}4\) lies between \(1\) and \(2\), so the \(y\)-intercept is about \(0.74\), not \(2\) — statement III is false.
\(x=0:\ y=2-\log_3 4\neq 2\). (III false)
Conclusion II only.
Answer: B — II only

Question. Let \(y=3-\log_{2}(x+5)\). Which of the following statements are true? I. \(y\) increases as \(x\) increases. II. The \(x\)-intercept of the graph is \(3\). III. The \(y\)-intercept of the graph is \(3\). (3 marks)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Because \(2>1\), \(\log_{2}(x+5)\) increases with \(x\), so \(y=3-\log_{2}(x+5)\) decreases.
Statement I is false.
Step 2. Put \(y=0\): \(3-\log_{2}(x+5)=0\), so \(\log_{2}(x+5)=3\) and \(x+5=8\), giving \(x=3\). Statement II is true.
Step 3. Put \(x=0\): \(y=3-\log_{2}5\approx 3-2.32=0.68\ne 3\). Statement III is false.
Step 4. Only statement II is true.
Answer: II only
