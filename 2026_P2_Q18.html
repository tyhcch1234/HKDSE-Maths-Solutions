HKDSE 2026 · Paper 2 · MC18

Areas of Trapezia

Question

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a trapezium with \(AB\parallel DC\) and \(AB:CD=3:5\). Let \(E\) be a point on \(BD\) such that \(BE:ED=2:3\). It is given that \(AB\parallel EF\) and \(AD\parallel BF\). If the area of \(\triangle ABD\) is \(225\) cm\(^2\), then the area of the trapezium \(CDEF\) is

  • A. \(222\) cm\(^2\)
  • B. \(260\) cm\(^2\)
  • C. \(279\) cm\(^2\)
  • D. \(285\) cm\(^2\)

In the figure, \(AB\) is the shorter top base and \(DC\) the longer bottom base; diagonal \(BD\) is drawn with \(E\) on it; \(F\) lies inside the trapezium so that \(EF\) is horizontal and \(BF\parallel AD\); the shaded region is trapezium \(CDEF\).

Original Figure

Solution

Set up variables

Let \(AB=3k\), \(CD=5k\), and let \(h\) be the height of the trapezium. From the area of \(\triangle ABD\) (base \(AB\), height \(h\)):

\[ \frac{1}{2}(3k)(h)=225\ \Rightarrow\ kh=150 \]
Locate \(E\) on \(BD\)

Place \(A(0,0)\), \(B(3k,0)\), \(D(d,-h)\), \(C(d+5k,-h)\). Since \(BE:ED=2:3\),

\[ E=\frac{3}{5}B+\frac{2}{5}D=\left(\frac{9k}{5}+\frac{2d}{5},\ -\frac{2h}{5}\right) \]
Locate \(F\) using the parallel conditions

Since \(EF\parallel AB\), \(F\) has the same height \(y=-\dfrac{2h}{5}\). Since \(BF\parallel AD\), the vector \(BF\) is parallel to \(AD=(d,-h)\); with the \(y\)-component \(-\dfrac{2h}{5}=\dfrac{2}{5}(-h)\), the \(x\)-component must be \(\dfrac{2}{5}d\). Hence

\[ F=\left(3k+\frac{2d}{5},\ -\frac{2h}{5}\right) \]

Therefore

\[ EF=\left(3k+\frac{2d}{5}\right)-\left(\frac{9k}{5}+\frac{2d}{5}\right)=\frac{6k}{5}, \qquad \text{height between }EF\text{ and }DC=h-\frac{2h}{5}=\frac{3h}{5} \]
Area of trapezium \(CDEF\) \[ \text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}\left(5k+\frac{6k}{5}\right)\left(\frac{3h}{5}\right) =\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{31k}{5}\cdot\frac{3h}{5} =\frac{93}{50}kh \] \[ =\frac{93}{50}\times150=279\ \text{cm}^2 \]
Final Answer: C
