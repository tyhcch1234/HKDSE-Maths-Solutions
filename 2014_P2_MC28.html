Q The Question

The mean height of \(25\) teachers and \(140\) students is \(150\text{ cm}\). If the mean height of the students is \(145\text{ cm}\), then the mean height of the teachers is
  • A. \(151\text{ cm}\)
  • B. \(155\text{ cm}\)
  • C. \(176\text{ cm}\)
  • D. \(178\text{ cm}\) ✓

Step 1 1M

Let \(x\) cm be the mean height of the teachers.

Hint: Whenever a "combined mean" is involved, the key idea is that the total sum = (mean) × (number of people).
Step 2 1A

Total height of teachers \(= 25x\).

Total height of students \(= 140 \times 145 = 20300\).

Total height of all \(165\) people \(= 150 \times 165 = 24750\).

Total sum equation: \(25x + 20300 = 24750\)

b Part (b) — Solve for the teachers' mean

Step 1 1M

Form the weighted-mean equation (sum of heights is conserved):

\[ x\cdot 25 + 145\cdot 140 = 150\cdot(25+140) \]
Hint: This is exactly the "combined mean" formula: \[ \bar{x}_{\text{combined}} = \frac{n_1\bar{x}_1 + n_2\bar{x}_2}{n_1+n_2} \] Multiply both sides by \(n_1+n_2\) to clear the fraction.
Step 2 1M

Simplify each side:

\[ 25x + 20300 = 150 \times 165 \] \[ 25x + 20300 = 24750 \]
Hint: Be careful with the arithmetic \(150 \times 165 = 24750\) — split it as \(150\times100 + 150\times65 = 15000 + 9750\).
Step 3 1A

Solve for \(x\):

\[ 25x = 24750 - 20300 = 4450 \] \[ x = \frac{4450}{25} = 178 \]
The mean height of the teachers is \(178\text{ cm}\).   Answer: D

📋 Summary — Answer at a Glance

  • Let \(x\) = mean height of teachers. Total height of teachers \(= 25x\).
  • Total height of students \(= 140 \times 145 = 20300\).
  • Overall total \(= 150 \times (25+140) = 150 \times 165 = 24750\).
  • Solve \(25x + 20300 = 24750 \;\Rightarrow\; 25x = 4450\).
  • Teachers' mean height \(= \dfrac{4450}{25} = \mathbf{178\text{ cm}}\) → Option D
