📐 Laws of Indices 📈 Exponential & Log Functions 🔍 Comparison

📌 Question

Which of the following is the greatest?

A. \(124^{241}\)
B. \(241^{214}\)
C. \(412^{142}\)
D. \(421^{124}\)

Tap an option to see if it's the greatest!

🧭 Guided Solution

1

Why compare directly is hard

All four numbers have different bases and different exponents. Their values are enormous (e.g. \(124^{241}\) has ~505 digits), so direct computation is impossible.

💡 Hint: When bases and powers differ, take logarithms to turn exponents into multipliers.

2

Apply logarithms

We use the law: \(\log(a^b) = b \cdot \log a\).

Since \(\log x\) is an increasing function, comparing \(\log(\text{value})\) is the same as comparing the original values.

\[ \begin{aligned} \log(124^{241}) &= 241 \log 124 \\ \log(241^{214}) &= 214 \log 241 \\ \log(412^{142}) &= 142 \log 412 \\ \log(421^{124}) &= 124 \log 421 \end{aligned} \]

Now we just need to compare these four numerical values.

3

Compute the log values

Using a calculator (base 10 log):

\( \log 124^{241} = 241 \log 124 \approx \mathbf{504.51} \)
\( \log 241^{214} = 214 \log 241 \approx \mathbf{509.75} \)
\( \log 412^{142} = 142 \log 412 \approx \mathbf{371.32} \)
\( \log 421^{124} = 124 \log 421 \approx \mathbf{325.41} \)

💡 Hint: The largest log value gives the largest original number.

4

Conclusion

Since \(\log x\) is increasing and \(509.75\) is the largest, \(241^{214}\) is the greatest.

✅ Answer: B (\(241^{214}\))

🧠 Key Takeaway

When comparing powers with different bases and exponents, take logarithms. The law \(\log(a^b) = b\log a\) turns exponents into multipliers, and since \(\log\) is increasing, the largest log value corresponds to the largest original number.
