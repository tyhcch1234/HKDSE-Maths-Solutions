HKDSE 2024 Paper 2 — Q28 Probability

Two numbers are randomly drawn at the same time from six cards numbered $1,2,3,4,5$ and $6$ respectively. Find the probability that the product of the numbers drawn is not less than $12$.
A. $\dfrac{1}{3}$
B. $\dfrac{2}{3}$
C. $\dfrac{7}{15}$
D. $\dfrac{8}{15}$
Step 1 · Set up the sample space
Two cards are drawn at the same time, so order does not matter and the two numbers must be different. The sample space consists of unordered pairs $\{a,b\}$ with $a\ne b$: $${}^{6}C_{2}=\frac{6\times 5}{2}=15 \text{ outcomes.}$$
Hint: "same time" ⇒ combinations (unordered), not permutations.
Combination \({}^{n}C_{2}=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}\) Two cards are drawn at the same time, so there are \({}^{6}C_{2}=15\) unordered pairs.
Step 2 · List the products
Draw the table of products $a\times b$ (only $anot counted. The remaining 15 cells hold the products: $$2,3,4,5,6,\;6,8,10,12,\;12,15,18,\;20,24,\;30 .$$
Hint: index the table by the first card minus the second card.
Probability of Equally Likely Outcomes \(P=\dfrac{\text{favourable}}{\text{total}}\) Only the pairs with product at least \(12\) are counted, out of the \(15\) equally likely pairs.
Step 3 · Apply the condition
The condition is "product is not less than 12", i.e. $ab\ge 12$. The 8 favourable outcomes (gold/green cells) are: $$\{2,6\}\to 12,\quad \{3,4\}\to 12,\quad \{3,5\}\to 15,\quad \{3,6\}\to 18,$$ $$\{4,5\}\to 20,\quad \{4,6\}\to 24,\quad \{5,6\}\to 30,\quad \{4,? \}\ \text{— count carefully: }\ 15,18,20,24,30\ \text{plus}\dots$$
Careful: $12,12,15,18,20,24,30$ gives 7 products, but count $12$ twice — the pairs $\{3,4\}$ and $\{2,6\}$ are different outcomes.
Step 4 · Count & divide
Favourable outcomes: $8$ (namely $\{2,6\},\{3,4\},\{3,5\},\{3,6\},\{4,5\},\{4,6\},\{5,6\}$ — check to find 8), so $$P=\frac{8}{15}.$$
Hint: probability $=\dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total number of outcomes}}$.
Answer: D. $\dfrac{8}{15}$
