13. The base of a solid right pyramid is a square of side 64 cm. The height of the pyramid is 24 cm. The pyramid is divided into a frustum X and a pyramid Y by a plane which is parallel to its base. It is given that the height of Y is 18 cm.

(a) Find the volume of X. (4 marks)

(b) The base of another solid right pyramid is a square. This pyramid is divided into a frustum Z and a pyramid by a plane which is parallel to its base. The height and the total surface area of Z are 3 cm and 960 cm² respectively. Are X and Z similar? Explain your answer. (4 marks)
STEP 1 · Understand the figure

The whole pyramid has base $64\times 64$ cm and height $24$ cm. A plane parallel to the base cuts off the top small pyramid $Y$ with height $18$ cm.

By similar figures, $Y$ is similar to the whole pyramid. Scale factor $k=\dfrac{18}{24}=\dfrac{3}{4}$.

💡 Key idea: a plane parallel to the base always creates a pyramid similar to the original — all corresponding lengths share the same ratio $k$.
STEP 2 · Base side of Y

Since linear ratio $k=\dfrac{3}{4}$:

$$\text{base side of }Y = 64\times\tfrac{3}{4}=48\text{ cm}$$

So the frustum $X$ has bottom side $64$ cm and top side $48$ cm, with height

$$24-18=6\text{ cm}.$$

STEP 3 · Volume of Y

$$V_Y=\tfrac{1}{3}(48^2)(18)=\tfrac{1}{3}(2304)(18)=13824\text{ cm}^3$$

Or directly using the scale factor $k^3$:

$$V_Y = \left(\tfrac{3}{4}\right)^3 V_{\text{whole}} = \tfrac{27}{64}\Big(\tfrac{1}{3}(64^2)(24)\Big)=\tfrac{27}{64}(32768)=13824.$$

STEP 4 (a) · Volume of frustum X

$$V_X = V_{\text{whole}} - V_Y = \tfrac{1}{3}(64^2)(24) - 13824$$

$$=32768 - 13824 = 18944\text{ cm}^3$$

(a)   $\boxed{18944\text{ cm}^3}$

Answer (a): The volume of $X$ is $18\,944\text{ cm}^3$.
STEP 5 (b) · Lateral face of X

Slant height of the whole pyramid: base half-side $32$, height $24$, so

$$l=\sqrt{32^2+24^2}=\sqrt{1024+576}=\sqrt{1600}=40\text{ cm}.$$

Slant height of $Y$ (ratio $\tfrac{3}{4}$): $l_Y=40\times\tfrac{3}{4}=30$ cm, so slant height of the frustum face is $40-30=10$ cm.

Each lateral trapezium of $X$ (bases $64$ and $48$, height $10$):

$$\tfrac{1}{2}(64+48)(10)=560\text{ cm}^2.$$

Four faces: $4\times 560 = 2240\text{ cm}^2$.

STEP 6 (b) · Total surface area of X

Top square: $48^2 = 2304$; bottom square: $64^2 = 4096$.

$$S_X = 2240 + 2304 + 4096 = 8640\text{ cm}^2.$$

STEP 7 (b) · Compare X and Z

For $Z$: height $3$ cm, total surface area $960\text{ cm}^2$.

If $X\sim Z$, corresponding lengths would satisfy $\dfrac{3}{24}=k'$, so areas would scale by $k'^2=\left(\tfrac{3}{24}\right)^2=\tfrac{1}{64}$ and

$$S_X = 8640 \neq 960\times 64 = 61440.$$

Equivalently comparing the actual values gives

$$\frac{S_X}{S_Z}=\frac{8640}{960}=9 \neq \left(\tfrac{6}{3}\right)^2=4$$

(or using heights $\dfrac{24}{3}=8$, we would need $\dfrac{S_X}{S_Z}=64\neq 9$).

STEP 8 (b) · Conclusion

For similar solids the ratio of total surface areas must equal the square of the ratio of corresponding lengths. Here

$$\frac{S_X}{S_Z} = 9 \neq \left(\frac{h_X}{h_Z}\right)^2 = 4,$$

so $X$ and $Z$ are not similar.

Answer (b): $X$ and $Z$ are not similar, because $\dfrac{\text{T.S.A. of }X}{\text{T.S.A. of }Z}=9$ is not equal to $\left(\dfrac{\text{height of }X}{\text{height of }Z}\right)^2=4$.
💡 Always check: for similar figures, area ratio = (length ratio)². If that fails, the figures are not similar.
