Mensuration · Frustum, Pyramids & Similarity

Frustum Volume Total Surface Area Similar Solids Test Square Pyramid

📄 Question 8 marks

The base of a solid right pyramid is a square of side \(64\text{ cm}\). The height of the pyramid is \(24\text{ cm}\). The pyramid is divided into a frustum \(X\) and a pyramid \(Y\) by a plane which is parallel to its base. It is given that the height of \(Y\) is \(18\text{ cm}\).

(a) Find the volume of \(X\). (4 marks)

(b) The base of another solid right pyramid is a square. This pyramid is divided into a frustum \(Z\) and a pyramid by a plane which is parallel to its base. The height and the total surface area of \(Z\) are \(3\text{ cm}\) and \(960\text{ cm}^2\) respectively. Are \(X\) and \(Z\) similar? Explain your answer. (4 marks)

Part (a) Volume of frustum \(X\) 4 marks

Interactive: The Right Pyramid Cut by a Plane
Step 1 1M

Here \(X\) is the frustum. The best route is: \[ V_X = V_{\text{whole}} - V_Y \]

For a right pyramid, \(V = \frac{1}{3}(\text{base area})\times(\text{height})\).

Hint A frustum is always "big solid minus small solid". Identify the two pyramids first.
Step 2 1M

Volume of the whole pyramid:

\[ V_{\text{whole}} = \frac{1}{3}(64^2)(24) = \frac{1}{3}(4096)(24) = 32768\text{ cm}^3. \]
Hint Base is a square of side \(64\), so base area \(= 64^2\).
Step 3 1M

The small pyramid \(Y\) is similar to the whole pyramid with linear ratio

\[ \frac{\text{height of }Y}{\text{height of whole}} = \frac{18}{24} = \frac{3}{4}. \]

Hence \[ V_Y = \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^3 V_{\text{whole}} \] \[ V_X = V_{\text{whole}} - V_Y = \frac{1}{3}(64^2)(24)\left[1 - \left(\frac{18}{24}\right)^3\right]. \]

Hint For similar solids, \(\dfrac{V_1}{V_2} = \left(\dfrac{\ell_1}{\ell_2}\right)^3\). Cube the linear ratio.
Step 4 1A

Compute: \[ \left(\frac{18}{24}\right)^3 = \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^3 = \frac{27}{64}. \] \[ V_X = \frac{1}{3}(4096)(24)\left(1 - \frac{27}{64}\right) = 32768 \times \frac{37}{64} = 18944. \]

Answer \[ \boxed{V_X = 18944\text{ cm}^3} \]
0 / 4

Part (b)(i) Area of one lateral face of \(X\) 1M

Interactive: A Lateral Face (Isosceles Trapezium)
Step 1 1M

A lateral face of the frustum \(X\) is an isosceles trapezium. Its two parallel sides are:

• the base edge \(= 64\) (bottom),
• the top edge \(= \frac{3}{4}\times 64 = 48\) (because \(Y\) is similar with ratio \(3:4\)).

Its slant height is the slant height \(\ell\) of one face of the whole pyramid:

\[ \ell = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10 \]

(since half the base is \(32\), and \(24:32 = 6:8\), scaled version).

Hint The frustum's slant height equals the full pyramid's slant height, because the cut plane removes only a similar smaller pyramid from the top.
Step 2 1A

Area of the trapezium:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2}\left(64 + 48\right)\times 10 \] \[ = \frac{1}{2}(112)(10) = 560\text{ cm}^2. \]
Answer Each lateral face of \(X\) has area \(560\text{ cm}^2\).

Part (b)(ii) Total surface area of \(X\) 1M

Interactive: Unfolded Net of Frustum \(X\)
Step 1 1M

The total surface area of \(X\) consists of:

• 4 lateral trapezium faces (each \(560\text{ cm}^2\)),
• 1 bottom square (\(64^2\)),
• 1 top square \(\left(\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^2 \times 64^2\right)\).

So \[ \text{TSA}_X = 4(560) + 64^2\left[1 + \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^2\right]. \]

Hint Top edge is \(48 = \frac34\times 64\), so top square area is \(\left(\frac34\right)^2 64^2\).
Step 2 1A

Evaluate: \[ 4(560) = 2240, \qquad \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{16}. \] \[ 64^2\left(1 + \frac{9}{16}\right) = 4096 \times \frac{25}{16} = 6400. \] \[ \text{TSA}_X = 2240 + 6400 = 8640. \]

Answer \[ \boxed{\text{Total surface area of }X = 8640\text{ cm}^2} \]

Part (b)(iii) Are \(X\) and \(Z\) similar? 2 marks

Interactive: Similar-Solid Test (Height Ratio vs Area Ratio)
Step 1 1M

If \(X\) and \(Z\) were similar, then the squares of their linear ratios would equal the ratio of their total surface areas. Here we don't know the height of \(X\) as a frustum — but we can compare the volumes or use the given data.

The key insight from the official answer: the volume of \(X\) was obtained in part (a). But actually the simplest route is to use:

\[ \left(\frac{\text{height of }X}{\text{height of }Z}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{6}{3}\right)^2 = 4. \]

Wait — the height of the frustum \(X\) is \(24-18 = 6\text{ cm}\), and the height of \(Z\) is given as \(3\text{ cm}\).

For similar frustums, the ratio of total surface areas should equal the square of the ratio of corresponding heights.

Hint Height of \(X = 24 - 18 = 6\text{ cm}\). Height of \(Z = 3\text{ cm}\). Linear ratio \(= 6:3 = 2:1\).
Step 2 1A

Predicted TSA ratio if similar: \[ \left(\frac{6}{3}\right)^2 = 4. \]

Actual TSA ratio: \[ \frac{\text{TSA}_X}{\text{TSA}_Z} = \frac{8640}{960} = 9. \]

Since \(9 \neq 4\), the two frustums are not similar.

Answer \[ \frac{\text{TSA}_X}{\text{TSA}_Z} = 9 \neq \left(\frac{\text{height of }X}{\text{height of }Z}\right)^2 = 4 \] Therefore \(X\) and \(Z\) are not similar.
0 / 2

📌 Final Answers at a Glance

  • (a) \(V_X = 18944\text{ cm}^3\) (volume of the frustum).
  • (b)(i) Area of each lateral face of \(X\) \(= 560\text{ cm}^2\).
  • (b)(ii) Total surface area of \(X\) \(= 8640\text{ cm}^2\).
  • (b)(iii) \(X\) and \(Z\) are not similar because \(\displaystyle\frac{\text{TSA}_X}{\text{TSA}_Z} = 9 \neq \left(\frac{\text{height of }X}{\text{height of }Z}\right)^2 = 4\).
