Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Equation of Straight Line

T12 Q18

Find the equation of the straight line with slope $-1$ and $y$-intercept $5$.

Solution

The slope-intercept form of a straight line is $y=mx+c$, where $m$ is the slope and $c$ is the $y$-intercept.
Substitute $m=-1$ and $c=5$ into $y=mx+c$.
This gives $y=-x+5$.
Rearrange if desired: $x+y=5$.
The equation of the straight line is $y=-x+5$ (or equivalently $x+y=5$).
