Quadratic Graphs — reading the signs of the coefficients

Quadratic Function Graph of \(y=mx^2+x+n\) Opening Direction \(y\)-intercept MC · 1 mark

Question 5 Multiple Choice

The figure shows the graph of \(y=mx^2+x+n\), where \(m\) and \(n\) are constants. Which of the following is true?

\[ y = mx^2 + x + n \]

The graph is a parabola that opens upwards and its \(y\)-intercept is below the \(x\)-axis.

  • A. \(\;m<0\) and \(n<0\)
  • B. \(\;m<0\) and \(n>0\)
  • C. \(\;m>0\) and \(n<0\)
  • D. \(\;m>0\) and \(n>0\)
m = ? n = ? shape: ?

Part (a) — Reading the opening direction Shape of the parabola

The coefficient of \(x^2\) decides whether the parabola opens upwards or downwards.

sign of \(m\): ? opens: ?
Step 11M

Look at the shape of the curve in the given figure.

\[ \text{The parabola opens \textbf{upwards}.} \]

Hint: For \(y=ax^2+bx+c\), the graph opens upwards when \(a>0\) and downwards when \(a<0\). Here the coefficient of \(x^2\) is \(m\).
Step 21A

Since the graph opens upwards, the coefficient of \(x^2\) must be positive:

\[ m > 0. \]

Result: \(\;m>0\)  ⟹  options A and B are eliminated.

Part (b) — Reading the \(y\)-intercept Constant term

Put \(x=0\) to find where the curve cuts the \(y\)-axis.

sign of \(n\): ? y-intercept = ?
Step 11M

Substitute \(x=0\) into \(y=mx^2+x+n\):

\[ y = m(0)^2 + 0 + n = n. \]

So the \(y\)-intercept of the graph is exactly \(n\).

Hint: The \(y\)-intercept of \(y=ax^2+bx+c\) is always the constant term \(c\).
Step 21A

From the figure, the curve cuts the \(y\)-axis below the origin, so the \(y\)-intercept is negative:

\[ \text{intercept} = n < 0. \]

Result: \(\;n<0\)  ⟹  option D is eliminated.

Part (c) — Combining both clues Choose the answer

Put the two signs together and match with the four options.

m = ? n = ? verdict: ?
Step 11M

Collect the two facts obtained:

\[ \text{opens upwards} \Rightarrow m>0, \qquad \text{y-intercept below } O \Rightarrow n<0. \]

Hint: The coefficient of \(x\) (which is \(1\)) only shifts the vertex horizontally — it does not change the opening direction or the \(y\)-intercept.
Step 21A

Compare with the options:

A. \(m<0,\;n<0\) — ✗ (wrong sign of \(m\))
B. \(m<0,\;n>0\) — ✗
C. \(m>0,\;n<0\) — ✓
D. \(m>0,\;n>0\) — ✗ (wrong sign of \(n\))

Answer: \(\;C\;\)  (\(m>0\) and \(n<0\))
0 / 2

Summary — Answers at a glance

  • Curve opens upwards \(\Rightarrow\) coefficient of \(x^2\) is positive: \(m>0\).
  • \(y\)-intercept of \(y=mx^2+x+n\) is \(n\); it lies below the \(x\)-axis \(\Rightarrow\) \(n<0\).
  • Therefore \(m>0\) and \(n<0\).
  • Correct option: C.
  • Quick tip: The middle term \(x\) (coefficient \(1\)) does not affect the opening direction nor the \(y\)-intercept, so it can be ignored when reading \(m\) and \(n\) from a graph.
