[2014MC#10]

If the angle and the radius of a sector are decreased by $x\%$ and $50\%$ respectively so that its area is decreased by $90\%$, then $x = $

  • A. 20
  • B. 40
  • C. 60
  • D. 80

1Write down the sector-area formula

\[ A = \pi r^{2}\times\frac{\theta^\circ}{360^\circ} \]

A sector is a fraction of the full circle; the fraction is $\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}$.

Hint: Identify what each symbol means — $\theta$ = angle, $r$ = radius, $A$ = area.
Area of a Sector \(A=\pi r^{2}\times\dfrac{\theta^{\circ}}{360^{\circ}}\) The sector is the fraction \(\dfrac{\theta}{360}\) of the circle of radius \(r\), so its area depends on \(r^{2}\) and \(\theta\).

2Form the constant ratio $\dfrac{r^{2}\theta}{A}$

\[ \frac{r^{2}\theta}{A} = \frac{r^{2}\theta}{\pi r^{2}\cdot\frac{\theta}{360}} = \frac{360}{\pi} \;=\; \text{constant} \]

Rearranging shows that $r^{2}\theta \propto A$ — i.e. scaling $r$ and $\theta$ scales $A$ by the product of the two factors.

Hint: A constant ratio is the key to handling percentage changes.

3Translate each percentage change into a factor

\[ r \to (1-50\%)r = 0.5r \] \[ \theta \to (1-x\%)\theta \] \[ A \to (1-90\%)A = 0.1A \]

"Decreased by $p\%$" means multiplied by $(1-p\%)$.

Hint: Keep everything as decimals to avoid mixing fractions and percentages.
Percentage Change Multiplier \(\text{value after }p\%\text{ decrease}=(1-p\%)\times\text{original value}\) A \(50\%\) decrease gives the factor \(0.5\) for \(r\), \(x\%\) gives \((1-x\%)\) for \(\theta\), and \(90\%\) gives \(0.1\) for \(A\).

4Substitute into the constant ratio

\[ \frac{[(1-50\%)r]^{2}\,[(1-x\%)\theta]}{(1-90\%)A} \;=\; \frac{r^{2}\theta}{A} \] \[ \frac{(0.5)^{2}(1-x\%)\,r^{2}\theta}{0.1\,A} \;=\; \frac{r^{2}\theta}{A} \]

Both sides use the same original $r^{2}\theta/A$, so the $r$, $\theta$ and $A$ symbols cancel.

Hint: Cancel $r^{2}\theta/A$ on both sides first, then solve for $x$.

5Solve for $(1-x\%)$

\[ \frac{0.25\,(1-x\%)}{0.1} = 1 \] \[ 1-x\% = \frac{0.1}{0.25} = 0.4 \] \[ x\% = 0.6 \]

So the angle must be decreased by $60\%$.

Hint: $0.25 = \frac14$ and $0.1 = \frac{1}{10}$, so $\frac{1/10}{1/4} = 0.4$.
Solving a Linear Equation in One Unknown \(a(1-x\%)=b\ \Rightarrow\ 1-x\%=\dfrac{b}{a}\) After cancelling, \(\dfrac{0.25\,(1-x\%)}{0.1}=1\), so \(1-x\%=\dfrac{0.1}{0.25}=0.4\).

6State the value of $x$

\[ x\% = 60\% \;\Longrightarrow\; x = 60 \]

Check: $(0.5)^{2}\times(1-0.6) = 0.25\times 0.4 = 0.1 = 10\%$ of the original area, i.e. a $90\%$ decrease. ✓

Hint: Match with option C.
Answer: C. 60

Question. If the radius of a sector is decreased by \(25\%\) and its angle is decreased by \(20\%\), then its area is decreased by
A. \(40\%\)    B. \(45\%\)    C. \(55\%\)    D. \(60\%\)  (1 mark)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Area of a sector: \(A=\pi r^{2}\times\dfrac{\theta^{\circ}}{360^{\circ}}\), so \(A\propto r^{2}\theta\).
Step 2. New radius \(=0.75r\) and new angle \(=0.8\theta\).
Step 3. New area \(=0.75^{2}\times 0.8\) times the old area \(=0.5625\times 0.8=0.45\), i.e. \(45\%\) of the original.
Step 4. So the area is decreased by \(100\%-45\%=55\%\) — option C.
Answer: C. \(55\%\)
