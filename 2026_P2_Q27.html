Circle: Circumference

The \(x\)-coordinate of the centre of the circle \(C\) is \(5\). If \(C\) passes through the points \((5,0)\) and \((11,4)\), then the circumference of \(C\) is

A. \(13\pi\)
B. \(14\pi\)
C. \(22\pi\)
D. \(42\pi\)
STEP 1 · CENTRE
Equation of a Circle \((x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=r^{2}\) Centre \((5,k)\) and the point \((5,0)\) give \(r^{2}=(5-5)^{2}+k^{2}=k^{2}\).

The \(x\)-coordinate of the centre is \(5\), so write the centre as \((5,k)\).

Using \(P(5,0)\): \(\;r^2=(5-5)^2+(k-0)^2=k^2\).

STEP 2 · SECOND POINT
Equating Two Expressions for r squared \(36+(4-k)^{2}=k^{2}\) Expanding and cancelling \(k^{2}\) leaves \(52-8k=0\).

Using \(Q(11,4)\): \(\;(11-5)^2+(4-k)^2=r^2=k^2\).

So \(\;36+16-8k+k^2=k^2\).

STEP 3 · SOLVE FOR k

\(52-8k=0\Rightarrow k=6.5\).

Hence the centre is \((5,\,6.5)\) and the radius is \(\;r=k=6.5\).

STEP 4 · CIRCUMFERENCE
Circumference of a Circle \(C=2\pi r\) With \(r=6.5\), \(C=2\pi(6.5)=13\pi\).

Circumference \(=2\pi r = 2\pi(6.5) = 13\pi\).

💡 Hint: since the centre has \(x=5\) and the circle passes through \((5,0)\), the vertical segment from the centre down to \((5,0)\) is a radius — so \(r\) equals the \(y\)-coordinate \(k\) of the centre.
Answer: A — \(13\pi\)

Step 2. Using \((9,6)\): \((9-3)^{2}+(6-k)^{2}=k^{2}\), i.e. \(36+36-12k+k^{2}=k^{2}\).

the centre is \((3,6)\) and \(r=6\).
Step 4. Circumference \(=2\pi(6)=12\pi\).
