Circle: Circumference

Question

The \(x\)-coordinate of the centre of the circle \(C\) is \(5\). If \(C\) passes through the points \((5,0)\) and \((11,4)\), then the circumference of \(C\) is

  • A. \(13\pi\)
  • B. \(14\pi\)
  • C. \(22\pi\)
  • D. \(42\pi\)

Solution

Centre Let the centre be \((5,k)\). Through \((5,0)\): \(r^2=(5-5)^2+(k-0)^2=k^2\).
Second point Through \((11,4)\): \((11-5)^2+(4-k)^2=k^2\Rightarrow 36+16-8k+k^2=k^2\).
Solve \(52-8k=0\Rightarrow k=6.5\), so \(r=6.5\).
Circumference \(2\pi r=2\pi(6.5)=13\pi\).
Answer: A — 13π
