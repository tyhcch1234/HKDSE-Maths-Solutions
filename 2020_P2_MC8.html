Summary of Answers

  • I. TRUE — \(L_2\) has a positive \(y\)-intercept \(-c>0\), so \(c<0\).
  • II. TRUE — \(m_1>m_2>0\) gives \(a<0\) and \(-\tfrac1a>-b\), hence \(ab<1\).
  • III. FALSE — the figure gives \(-\tfrac{b}{a}<-c\), which leads to \(ac>b\), not \(ac<b\).

Therefore only I and II are true.  Answer: A
