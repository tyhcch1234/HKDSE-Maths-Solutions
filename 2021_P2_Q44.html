[HKDSE 2021 Paper 2 · Q44] · Topic 15 — Statistics

In an examination, the mean of the examination scores is $45$ marks. A boy gets $25$ marks in the examination and his standard score is $-5$. If the standard score of a girl in the examination is $7$, then her examination score is

  • A. $4$ marks.
  • B. $53$ marks.
  • C. $73$ marks.
  • D. $80$ marks.
1 Define the unknowns

Let $\sigma$ marks be the standard deviation of the examination scores, and let $y$ marks be the examination score of the girl.

Recall: standard score $z=\dfrac{\text{score}-\text{mean}}{\text{standard deviation}}$.
2 Use the boy's data to find $\sigma$

The boy's score is $25$, mean is $45$, and his standard score is $-5$:

$$\frac{25-45}{\sigma}=-5$$

$$\frac{-20}{\sigma}=-5 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \sigma = \frac{-20}{-5}=4$$

So: the standard deviation is $\sigma = 4$ marks (the boy scores $20$ marks below the mean, i.e. $5$ standard deviations below).
3 Form the equation for the girl

The girl's standard score is $7$, so:

$$\frac{y-45}{4}=7 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y-45=28$$

Check: her score is $7$ standard deviations above the mean, with each standard deviation being $4$ marks.
4 Solve for $y$

$$y = 45 + 7(4) = 45 + 28 = 73$$

Alternative (as in the official solution): combining $(1)\times 7+(2)\times 5$: $540 = 175 + 5y$, giving the same $y=73$.
Answer:  C.  $73$ marks
