Statistics — Standard Scores & the Mean / Standard Deviation Relationship

Standard Score Mean & SD Normalisation Multiple Choice

Question [2021MC#44]

In an examination, the mean of the examination scores is \(45\) marks. A boy gets \(25\) marks in the examination and his standard score is \(-5\). If the standard score of a girl in the examination is \(7\), then her examination score is

  1. \(4\) marks.
  2. \(53\) marks.
  3. \(73\) marks.
  4. \(80\) marks.

(Multiple-choice, 1 mark each. Working shown as a full solution.)

Part (a) — Find the standard deviation \(\sigma\) Key Idea

The standard score (z-score) of a raw score \(x\) is defined as \[ z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}, \] where \(\mu\) is the mean and \(\sigma\) is the standard deviation.

\(x_b=Boy marks..\) \(\\sigma=..\)
1 Write the z-score formula for the boy

The boy's score is \(25\) and the mean is \(45\). Using \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\):

\[ -5=\frac{25-45}{\sigma}=\frac{-20}{\sigma}. \]
Hint: A negative standard score means the raw score is below the mean. Here \(25<45\), so the z-score must be negative — consistent with \(-5\).
2 Solve for \(\sigma\)

Multiply both sides by \(\sigma\):

\[ -5\sigma = -20 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \sigma = 4. \]
Answer: \(\;\sigma = 4\) marks.
Hint: A common trap is forgetting the negative sign. Since the z-score is \(-5\) and \(x-\mu=-20\), the sign works out — \(\sigma\) must be positive, so \(\sigma=4\), not \(-4\).

Part (b) — Find the girl's examination score Solve

Now we apply the same z-score formula to the girl, whose standard score is \(7\).

\(x_g=..\) \(x_g=\mu+z_g\sigma\)
1 Set up the equation for the girl

Let the girl's score be \(y\) marks. With \(\mu=45\), \(\sigma=4\) and \(z=7\):

\[ 7=\frac{y-45}{4}. \]
Hint: Rearranging \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\) gives the useful formula \[ x=\mu+z\sigma, \] so the girl's score = mean + (z-score)×(SD).
2 Solve for \(y\)

Multiply both sides by \(4\):

\[ y-45 = 7\times 4 = 28 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y = 73. \]
Answer: \(\;y = 73\) marks, i.e. option C.
Hint: Because the girl's z-score is \(7\), she scores \(7\) standard deviations above the mean. Since \(\sigma=4\), that is \(7\times 4 = 28\) marks above \(45\), giving \(45+28=73\).

Summary of Answers

  • Standard score definition: \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\)
  • Mean: \(\mu = 45\) marks
  • From the boy: \(\sigma = \dfrac{25-45}{-5} = 4\) marks
  • Girl's score: \(y = \mu + z\sigma = 45 + 7\times 4 = \mathbf{73}\) marks
  • Correct option: C. 73 marks.
