Mensuration — sectors, arc length and the area of a shaded “annular” region

Sector Area Arc Length Annulus / Shaded Ring Multiple-Choice Reasoning

Question Full text

In the figure, \(OAB\) and \(OCD\) are sectors with centre \(O\), where \(OA = 33\text{ cm}\) and \(OC = 39\text{ cm}\).

The area of the shaded region \(ABDC\) is \(72\pi\text{ cm}^2\).

Which of the following is/are true?

I. The angle of the sector \(OAB\) is \(60^\circ\).
II. The area of the sector \(OAB\) is \(11\pi\text{ cm}^2\).
III. The perimeter of the sector \(OCD\) is \(13\pi\text{ cm}\).

Options:

A.I only
B.II only
C.I and III only
D.II and III only

(Correct choice shown in green when revealed by the reasoning below.)

Part I — The angle of the sector \(OAB\) Statement I

120°
loading…

Drag the slider to change the common central angle \(\theta\) of both sectors and watch how the shaded area changes. The shaded area equals \(72\pi\text{ cm}^2\) only when \(\theta = 60^\circ\).

Step 1

Let the angle of the sector \(OAB\) be \(\theta\). The two sectors share the same centre \(O\), so the sector \(OCD\) also has central angle \(\theta\).

Hint: The shaded region \(ABDC\) is the sector \(OCD\) with the sector \(OAB\) removed, so
\[ A_{ABDC} = A_{OCD} - A_{OAB} \]
Step 2

Use the sector-area formula \(A=\pi r^2 \times \dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\):

\[ \pi(39)^2 \times \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} - \pi(33)^2 \times \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} = 72\pi \]
Hint: Factor out \(\dfrac{\pi\theta}{360^\circ}\) and use \(39^2-33^2=(39-33)(39+33)\).
Step 3
\[ \frac{\pi\theta}{360^\circ}\bigl(39^2-33^2\bigr)=72\pi \] \[ \frac{\theta}{360^\circ}(1521-1089)=72 \] \[ \frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times 432 = 72 \] \[ \theta = \frac{72\times 360^\circ}{432} = 60^\circ \]
Result: The angle of sector \(OAB\) is \(60^\circ\).

Statement I is TRUE ✓

Part II — The area of the sector \(OAB\) Statement II

33 cm
loading…
Step 1

From Part I, \(\theta = 60^\circ\). The area of sector \(OAB\) with radius \(33\text{ cm}\):

\[ A_{OAB}=\pi(33)^2 \times \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ} \]
Hint: \(\dfrac{60}{360}=\dfrac16\).
Step 2
\[ A_{OAB}=\pi(1089)\times \frac16 = \frac{1089\pi}{6} = \frac{363\pi}{2} = 181.5\pi\ \text{cm}^2 \]
Result: Area of sector \(OAB\) \(=\dfrac{363\pi}{2}\text{ cm}^2 \approx 570.2\text{ cm}^2\).

Statement II says \(11\pi\text{ cm}^2\), so Statement II is FALSE ✗

Part III — The perimeter of the sector \(OCD\) Statement III

60°
loading…
Step 1

The perimeter of sector \(OCD\) consists of the two radii \(OC\), \(OD\) plus the arc \(CD\):

\[ P = OC + OD + \widehat{CD} = 2(39) + 2\pi(39)\times\frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ} \]
Hint: Arc length \(=2\pi r \times \dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\).
Step 2
\[ P = 78 + 2\pi(39)\times\frac16 = 78 + 13\pi = (78+13\pi)\ \text{cm} \]
Result: Perimeter \(=(78+13\pi)\text{ cm} \approx 118.8\text{ cm}\).

Statement III says \(13\pi\text{ cm}\), so Statement III is FALSE ✗

Summary Answers at a glance

  • Given: \(OA=33\), \(OC=39\), shaded area \(ABDC = 72\pi\text{ cm}^2\).
  • The angle of sector \(OAB\) is \(\theta = 60^\circ\). Statement I TRUE
  • Area of sector \(OAB = \dfrac{363\pi}{2}\text{ cm}^2 \approx 570.2\text{ cm}^2\). Statement II FALSE
  • Perimeter of sector \(OCD = (78+13\pi)\text{ cm} \approx 118.8\text{ cm}\). Statement III FALSE
  • Conclusion: Only Statement I is true → Answer: A (I only)
