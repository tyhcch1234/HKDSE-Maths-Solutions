Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Ratios

T13 Q08

In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite angle $\theta$ is $3$ and the hypotenuse is $5$. Find $\sin\theta$.

Solution

Use the definition: $\sin\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$.
Substitute the given values: $\sin\theta = \frac{3}{5}$.
$\sin\theta = \frac{3}{5}$.
