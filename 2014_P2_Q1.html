HKDSE 2014 Paper 2 · Q1

MCTopic: Laws of Indices, Exponential & Logarithmic Functions

$(2n^{3})^{-5}=$

  • A $\dfrac{1}{32n^{2}}$
  • B $\dfrac{1}{32n^{15}}$
  • C $\dfrac{1}{10n^{125}}$
  • D $\dfrac{1}{10n^{243}}$
1Read the expression

The expression is a product raised to a power:

$$(2n^{3})^{-5}=(2\cdot n^{3})^{-5}$$

Hint: notice there are two factors inside the bracket — the number $2$ and the power $n^{3}$. The outer power $-5$ must act on both.
2Power of a product

Apply $(ab)^{m}=a^{m}b^{m}$:

$$(2n^{3})^{-5}=2^{-5}\cdot (n^{3})^{-5}$$

Hint: a very common error is to apply $-5$ only to $n^{3}$ and leave $2$ unchanged. The exponent distributes over every factor.
Power of a Product \((ab)^{m}=a^{m}b^{m}\) The bracket holds two factors, \(2\) and \(n^{3}\), so the outer power \(-5\) must be applied to each of them.
3Power of a power

Apply $(a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn}$ to the second factor:

$$(n^{3})^{-5}=n^{3\times(-5)}=n^{-15}$$

So the expression becomes

$$2^{-5}\cdot n^{-15}$$

Hint: multiply the exponents: $3\times(-5)=-15$. Do not add them (that would give $n^{-2}$, which is option A's mistake).
Power of a Power \((a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn}\) \(\left(n^{3}\right)^{-5}=n^{3\times(-5)}=n^{-15}\) — multiply the exponents, do not add them.
4Evaluate the number

$$2^{-5}=\frac{1}{2^{5}}=\frac{1}{32}$$

Hint: a negative exponent means reciprocal: $a^{-m}=\dfrac{1}{a^{m}}$. Here $2^{5}=32$, so $2^{-5}=\dfrac{1}{32}$.
5Write with positive exponents

$$n^{-15}=\frac{1}{n^{15}}$$

Combining the two factors:

$$(2n^{3})^{-5}=\frac{1}{32}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{15}}=\frac{1}{32n^{15}}$$

Hint: negative exponents move across the fraction bar and become positive. Note $10n^{125}$ and $10n^{243}$ (options C, D) come from wrongly using base $10$ or multiplying exponents incorrectly.
Negative Index Law \(a^{-m}=\dfrac{1}{a^{m}}\) Both \(2^{-5}\) and \(n^{-15}\) are negative powers, so they move to the denominator as \(32n^{15}\).
6Check the options
Option A$\dfrac{1}{32n^{2}}$ — exponent should be $-5$, not $-2$ ✗
Option B$\dfrac{1}{32n^{15}}$ — matches $2^{-5}n^{-15}$ ✓
Option C$\dfrac{1}{10n^{125}}$ — base $2^{5}\ne 10$ ✗
Option D$\dfrac{1}{10n^{243}}$ — wrong base and exponent ✗
Hint: always check the two bases ($2$ and $n$) and the two exponents ($-5$ on the number, $-15$ on $n$) separately.
Answer
B  ·  $(2n^{3})^{-5}=\dfrac{1}{32n^{15}}$

Question. If \(n\ne 0\), \(\left(3m^{4}\right)^{-3}=\)
A. \(\dfrac{1}{27m^{12}}\)    B. \(\dfrac{1}{9m^{12}}\)    C. \(\dfrac{1}{27m^{7}}\)    D. \(\dfrac{3}{m^{12}}\)  (1 mark)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Power of a product: \(\left(3m^{4}\right)^{-3}=3^{-3}\left(m^{4}\right)^{-3}\).
Step 2. Power of a power: \(\left(m^{4}\right)^{-3}=m^{-12}\).
Step 3. Negative index: \(3^{-3}=\dfrac{1}{3^{3}}=\dfrac{1}{27}\) and \(m^{-12}=\dfrac{1}{m^{12}}\).
Step 4. Hence \(\left(3m^{4}\right)^{-3}=\dfrac{1}{27m^{12}}\), which is option A.
Answer: A. \(\dfrac{1}{27m^{12}}\)
