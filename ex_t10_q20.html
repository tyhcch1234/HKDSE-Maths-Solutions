Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q20

Find the volume of a cube with side length $5$ cm.

Solution

The volume of a cube is given by $V = s^3$, where $s$ is the side length.
Substitute $s = 5$ cm into the formula: $V = 5^3$.
Calculate $5^3 = 125$.
The volume of the cube is $125\text{ cm}^3$.
