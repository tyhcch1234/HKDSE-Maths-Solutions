Arithmetic Sequence Recursive Pattern

In the figure, the 1st pattern consists of 9 dots. For any positive integer \(n\), the \((n+1)\)th pattern is formed by adding 5 dots to the \(n\)th pattern. Find the number of dots in the 7th pattern.

1st: 9
→
2nd: 14
→
3rd: 19
⋯
A. 29
B. 34
C. 39
D. 44

Correct answer: C (39)

🔍 Explore the pattern

Drag the slider to see how the pattern grows. Orange dots are newly added.

\(n =\) 1

Number of dots: 9

Formula: \(T(n) = 9 + 5(n-1)\)

🧠 Step-by-Step Solution

From the problem, the 1st pattern has 9 dots.

Define \(T(1) = 9\).
💡 Hint: This is the starting value of the sequence.
Each new pattern is formed by adding 5 dots to the previous pattern.

So for any positive integer \(n\): \[ T(n+1) = T(n) + 5 \]
💡 Hint: This is an arithmetic sequence with common difference \(d = 5\).
Applying the rule repeatedly:
\(T(2) = T(1) + 5 = 9 + 5 = 14\)
\(T(3) = T(2) + 5 = 14 + 5 = 19\)
\(T(4) = T(3) + 5 = 19 + 5 = 24\)
\(T(5) = T(4) + 5 = 24 + 5 = 29\)
\(T(6) = T(5) + 5 = 29 + 5 = 34\)
\(T(7) = T(6) + 5 = 34 + 5 = 39\)
💡 Hint: You can also use the general formula: \(T(n) = T(1) + (n-1)d\)
For an arithmetic sequence: \[ T(n) = a + (n-1)d \] where \(a = T(1) = 9\) and \(d = 5\).

Substitute \(n = 7\): \[ T(7) = 9 + (7-1) \times 5 = 9 + 6 \times 5 = 9 + 30 = 39 \]
✅ Answer: 39 (Option C)

📌 Key takeaway

When a quantity changes by a constant amount at each step, it forms an arithmetic sequence. Use \(T(n) = a + (n-1)d\) to jump directly to any term.
