Mensuration — Areas in Proportion (Common-Height Triangles)
Area Ratios Common Height Quadrilateral Area Multiple Choice

Question5 marks

In the figure, \(M\) and \(N\) are points lying on \(PQ\) and \(QR\) respectively such that \(PM:MQ = 5:6\) and \(QN:NR = 3:4\). If the area of the quadrilateral \(MNRP\) is \(59\text{ cm}^2\), then the area of \(\Delta MNQ\) is

  1. \(17\text{ cm}^2.\)
  2. \(18\text{ cm}^2.\)
  3. \(19\text{ cm}^2.\)
  4. \(20\text{ cm}^2.\)

Answer: B

\(\dfrac{PM}{MQ}=\) 0.833 \(\Rightarrow \dfrac{PQ}{MQ}=\) 1.833
\(\dfrac{QN}{NR}=\) 0.750 \(\Rightarrow \dfrac{QR}{QN}=\) 2.333
\(\Delta MNQ =\) 18.0 cm²
Quadrilateral \(MNRP =\) 59.0 cm²

Drag the sliders: the two blue segments change their cut points, and the green quadrilateral area on the right is re-computed from the same ratios. Only \(k=5/6,\;m=3/4\) reproduces the given \(59\text{ cm}^2\) with \(MNQ=18\text{ cm}^2\).

Part (a) — Two triangles sharing the same height2 marks

When two triangles share the same height, their areas are in the ratio of their bases.

Bases \(MQ:QN =\) 1 : 1
Areas \(\Delta MPQ : \Delta NPQ =\) 1 : 1
Step 11M
Since \(\Delta NPQ\) and \(\Delta MNQ\) have a common height (the perpendicular distance from \(N\) to \(PQ\)): \[\frac{\text{Area of }\Delta NPQ}{\text{Area of }\Delta MNQ}=\frac{PQ}{MQ}\]
Hint: Triangles with the same apex and bases lying on one straight line have equal heights, so area \(\propto\) base.
Step 21A
\(PM:MQ=5:6\), so \(PQ=5+6=11\) parts. Hence \[\frac{\text{Area of }\Delta NPQ}{\text{Area of }\Delta MNQ}=\frac{11}{6}\]
\(\therefore\ \text{Area of }\Delta NPQ=\dfrac{11}{6}\times\text{Area of }\Delta MNQ\)
0 / 2

Part (b)(i) — Building up to \(\Delta PQR\)2 marks

Repeat the same height argument along the side \(QR\), with apex \(P\).

\(QN:NR =\) 1 : 1
\(\Delta PQN : \Delta PNR =\) 1 : 1
So \(\dfrac{\Delta PQR}{\Delta PQN}=\) 2.00
Step 11M
\(\Delta PQR\) and \(\Delta PQN\) have a common height (from \(P\) to \(QR\)): \[\frac{\text{Area of }\Delta PQR}{\text{Area of }\Delta PQN}=\frac{QR}{QN}\]
Hint: \(N\) divides \(QR\), and both triangles use the full height from \(P\).
Step 21A
\(QN:NR=3:4\Rightarrow QR=7\) parts, so \[\frac{\text{Area of }\Delta PQR}{\text{Area of }\Delta PQN}=\frac{7}{3}\]
\(\therefore\ \text{Area of }\Delta PQR=\dfrac{7}{3}\times\text{Area of }\Delta PQN\)
0 / 2

Part (b)(ii) — Express everything in terms of \(x\)2 marks

Let \(x\text{ cm}^2\) be the area of \(\Delta MNQ\). Chain the two relations together to get the area of \(MNRP\).

\(\Delta MNQ =\) 18.0 cm²
\(\Delta NPQ = \frac{11}{6}x =\) 33.0 cm²
\(\Delta PQR = \frac{77}{18}x =\) 77.0 cm²
\(MNRP = \frac{59}{18}x =\) 59.0 cm²
Step 11M
Substitute the result of (a) into (b): \[\text{Area of }\Delta PQR=\frac{7}{3}\times\frac{11}{6}x=\frac{77}{18}x\ \text{cm}^2\]
Hint: \(\Delta PQN\) is the middle triangle; multiply the two ratio factors in order.
Step 21A
The quadrilateral is the big triangle minus the small one: \[\text{Area of }MNRP=\frac{77}{18}x-x=\frac{59}{18}x\ \text{cm}^2\]
Area of \(MNRP=\dfrac{59}{18}x\ \text{cm}^2\)
0 / 2

Part (b)(iii) — Solve for \(x\)1 mark

Use the given condition: the area of \(MNRP\) is \(59\text{ cm}^2\).

Predicted \(MNRP=\frac{59}{18}x =\) 59.0 cm²
Target \(=59\) cm² → match ✔
Step 11A
\[\frac{59}{18}x=59\ \Rightarrow\ x=59\times\frac{18}{59}=18\]
Hint: The two \(59\)'s cancel immediately — no need to expand decimals.
Area of \(\Delta MNQ = 18\text{ cm}^2\) → Answer B
0 / 1

Answers at a glance

  • (a) \(\dfrac{\text{Area of }\Delta NPQ}{\text{Area of }\Delta MNQ}=\dfrac{PQ}{MQ}=\dfrac{11}{6}\)
  • (b)(i) \(\dfrac{\text{Area of }\Delta PQR}{\text{Area of }\Delta PQN}=\dfrac{QR}{QN}=\dfrac{7}{3}\)
  • (b)(ii) \(\text{Area of }MNRP=\dfrac{77}{18}x-x=\dfrac{59}{18}x\ \text{cm}^2\)
  • (b)(iii) \(\dfrac{59}{18}x=59\ \Rightarrow\ x=18\)
Answer: B — \(18\text{ cm}^2\)
