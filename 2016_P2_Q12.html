Variation Model

Step 1 — Given: $z$ varies directly as $\sqrt{x}$ and inversely as $y$.

$\sqrt{x}$ (numerator) $y$ (denominator) $z$ (result) Percentage changes
HKDSE 2016 · Paper 2 · Q12

It is given that $z$ varies directly as $\sqrt{x}$ and inversely as $y$.

If $x$ is decreased by $36\%$ and $y$ is increased by $60\%$, then $z$

  • A. is increased by $24\%$.
  • B. is increased by $28\%$.
  • C. is decreased by $40\%$.
  • D. is decreased by $50\%$

Step-by-step Solution

1
Translate the statement into an equation "$z$ varies directly as $\sqrt{x}$ and inversely as $y$" gives: $$z = k\,\frac{\sqrt{x}}{y},$$ where $k$ is a non-zero constant. Remember: "directly as $\sqrt{x}$" → $\sqrt{x}$ on top; "inversely as $y$" → $y$ at the bottom.
2
Write the original relation as a ratio Rearranging to keep $k$ alone: $$k=\frac{zy}{\sqrt{x}}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{z\,y}{\sqrt{x}}=\text{constant}.$$ Because $k$ is fixed, the quantity $\dfrac{zy}{\sqrt{x}}$ never changes when $x$ and $y$ are altered.
3
Apply the percentage changes to $x$ and $y$ A $36\%$ decrease in $x$: $\;x \to (1-0.36)x = 0.64x$.
A $60\%$ increase in $y$: $\;y \to (1+0.60)y = 1.6y$. Decrease → subtract from $1$; increase → add to $1$.
4
Let $p$ be the percentage change in $z$ If $z$ changes by $p$, the new value is $(1+p)z$. Equating the constant $\dfrac{zy}{\sqrt{x}}$ before and after: $$\frac{(1+p)z\,(1.6y)}{\sqrt{0.64x}}=\frac{zy}{\sqrt{x}}.$$ New value on the left, original value on the right — the constant $k$ is the same on both sides.
5
Simplify using $\sqrt{0.64}=0.8$ $$\frac{1.6\,(1+p)}{0.8}=1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2(1+p)=1.$$ $\sqrt{0.64x}=0.8\sqrt{x}$, and $0.8\sqrt{x}$ cancels with $\sqrt{x}$.
6
Solve for $p$ and interpret $$1+p=\frac{1}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p=-\frac{1}{2}=-50\%.$$ A negative $p$ means a decrease, so $z$ is decreased by $50\%$. Check quickly: $z \propto \dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{y} \to \dfrac{0.8}{1.6}=0.5$, i.e. exactly half.
Answer:  D. is decreased by $50\%$ $z_{\text{new}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{0.64x}}{1.6y}=\dfrac{0.8}{1.6}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{y}=0.5\,z_{\text{old}}$
