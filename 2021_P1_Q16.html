2021 P1 Q16The straight lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ are perpendicular to each other.

The $y$-intercept of $L_1$ is $3$. It is given that $L_1$ and $L_2$ intersect at the point $(2,6)$. Let $R$ be the region (including the boundary) bounded by $L_1$, $L_2$ and the $x$-axis.

(a) It is given that $R$ represents the solution of a system of inequalities. Find the system of inequalities.  [3 marks]

(b) Find the least value of $8x-5y$, where $(x,y)$ is a point lying in $R$.  [2 marks]

1Find the equation of $L_1$

$L_1$ cuts the $y$-axis at $(0,3)$ and passes through $(2,6)$, so its slope is \[\text{slope}=\frac{6-3}{2-0}=\frac{3}{2}.\] Hence \[y-3=\tfrac{3}{2}(x-0)\ \Longrightarrow\ 2y-6=3x\ \Longrightarrow\ \boxed{3x-2y+6=0.}\]

Hint: $y=mx+c$ with $c=3$; find $m$ from the two points. (1M, 1A)

Slope-Intercept Form \(y=mx+c\) \(L_1\) cuts the \(y\)-axis at \(3\) and has slope \(\dfrac{6-3}{2-0}=\dfrac{3}{2}\), giving \(3x-2y+6=0\).

2Find the equation of $L_2$ (perpendicular to $L_1$)

Since $L_1\perp L_2$, the slope of $L_2$ is the negative reciprocal of $\tfrac32$, i.e. $-\tfrac{2}{3}$. Through $(2,6)$: \[y-6=-\tfrac{2}{3}(x-2)\ \Longrightarrow\ 3y-18=-2x+4\ \Longrightarrow\ \boxed{2x+3y-22=0.}\]

Hint: $m_1m_2=-1$. Check: $3(2)+2(-2)=2\neq 0$… and $3\cdot2+2\cdot3=12$, product $-1$ ✓

Condition for Perpendicular Lines \(m_{1}m_{2}=-1\) \(L_1\) has slope \(\dfrac{3}{2}\), so \(L_2\) has slope \(-\dfrac{2}{3}\), giving \(2x+3y-22=0\).

3Try the intersection point $(2,6)$ in each line

Test a point of $R$ (the vertex $(2,6)$) against each line: \[3(2)-2(6)+6=0,\qquad 2(2)+3(6)-22=0.\] The region lies above $L_1$ so $3x-2y+6\ge 0$; it lies below $L_2$ so $2x+3y-22\le 0$; and it is above the $x$-axis so $y\ge 0$.

Hint: pick a clear interior point, e.g. $(2,1)$: $3(2)-2(1)+6=10>0$ ✓, $2(2)+3(1)-22=-15<0$ ✓.

4Write the system of inequalities (part a)

\[\begin{cases} 3x-2y+6\ \ge\ 0\\[2pt] 2x+3y-22\ \le\ 0\\[2pt] y\ \ge\ 0 \end{cases}\]

(1A) — three correct inequalities give the region $R$ (boundary included).

5Find the vertices of $R$ (part b)

$x$-intercepts: $L_1$: $3x+6=0\Rightarrow x=-2$; $L_2$: $2x-22=0\Rightarrow x=11$. So the vertices are \[A(-2,0),\qquad B(2,6)\ (\text{given}),\qquad C(11,0).\]

Hint: the objective function takes its extreme values at a vertex of the feasible region. (1M)

Vertex Test (Linear Programming) \(f(x,y)=ax+by\ \text{is extremal at a vertex}\) \(8x-5y\) on the triangle with vertices \((-2,0)\), \((2,6)\), \((11,0)\) only needs these three substitutions.

6Evaluate $8x-5y$ at each vertex

At $A(-2,0)$: $\;8(-2)-5(0)=-16$
At $B(2,6)$: $\;8(2)-5(6)=16-30=-14$
At $C(11,0)$: $\;8(11)-5(0)=88$

Hint: substitute all three vertices — the smallest value is the answer. (1A)

7Conclusion

(a) \(\begin{cases}3x-2y+6\ge 0\\ 2x+3y-22\le 0\\ y\ge 0\end{cases}\)
(b) The least value of $8x-5y$ is $-16$ (attained at $(-2,0)$).
