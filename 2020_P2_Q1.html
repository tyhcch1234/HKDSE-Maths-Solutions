HKDSE 2020 · Paper 2 · Q1
Simplify $\dfrac{6x}{(3x^{-5})^{-2}} = \ ?$
  • A. $54x^{8}$
  • B. $\dfrac{2x^{8}}{3}$
  • C. $\dfrac{54}{x^{9}}$
  • D. $\dfrac{2}{3x^{9}}$
STEP 1Look at the denominator: a negative index outside a bracket. $(3x^{-5})^{-2}$ Recall the law $(ab)^{n}=a^{n}b^{n}$ and $(x^{m})^{n}=x^{mn}$.
STEP 2Apply the outer power $-2$ to each factor inside. $(3x^{-5})^{-2}=3^{-2}\cdot(x^{-5})^{-2}$ Multiply the inner exponent by the outer exponent: $-5\times(-2)=+10$.
Power of a Product\((ab)^{n}=a^{n}b^{n}\)\((3x^{-5})^{-2}=3^{-2}(x^{-5})^{-2}\), applying the outer power to each factor.
STEP 3Simplify the powers. $3^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{9},\qquad (x^{-5})^{-2}=x^{10}$ $\Rightarrow\ (3x^{-5})^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{9}\,x^{10}$ A negative index moves the base to the other side of the fraction bar.
Power of a Power\((x^{m})^{n}=x^{mn}\)\(-5\times(-2)=+10\), so \((x^{-5})^{-2}=x^{10}\) and the denominator is \(\dfrac{1}{9}x^{10}\).
STEP 4Write the whole expression and divide. $\dfrac{6x}{(3x^{-5})^{-2}}=\dfrac{6x}{\tfrac{1}{9}x^{10}}$ Dividing by $\tfrac{1}{9}x^{10}$ multiplies by its reciprocal: $\dfrac{9}{x^{10}}$.
STEP 5Combine numbers and indices. $=6x\cdot\dfrac{9}{x^{10}}=\dfrac{54x}{x^{10}}$ Use $\dfrac{x^{a}}{x^{b}}=x^{a-b}$ with $a=1,\ b=10$.
STEP 6Final simplification. $\dfrac{54x}{x^{10}}=\dfrac{54}{x^{9}}$ $x^{1-10}=x^{-9}=\dfrac{1}{x^{9}}$. So option C is correct.
Quotient Law of Indices\(\dfrac{x^{a}}{x^{b}}=x^{a-b}\)\(\dfrac{54x}{x^{10}}=54x^{1-10}=54x^{-9}=\dfrac{54}{x^{9}}\).
Answer: $\dfrac{54}{x^{9}}$ C

Question. Simplify \(\dfrac{8x}{(2x^{-3})^{-2}}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Apply the outer power to each factor: \((2x^{-3})^{-2}=2^{-2}(x^{-3})^{-2}\).
Step 2. \(2^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{4}\) and \((x^{-3})^{-2}=x^{6}\), so the denominator is \(\dfrac{1}{4}x^{6}\).
Step 3. \(\dfrac{8x}{\frac14 x^{6}}=8x\times\dfrac{4}{x^{6}}=\dfrac{32x}{x^{6}}\).
Step 4. Quotient law: \(32x^{1-6}=32x^{-5}=\dfrac{32}{x^{5}}\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{32}{x^{5}}\)
