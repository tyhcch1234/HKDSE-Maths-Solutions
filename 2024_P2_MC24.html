QQuestion

The coordinates of the point \(U\) are \((-3,\,-8)\). \(U\) is rotated anticlockwise about the origin through \(90^\circ\) to the point \(V\). \(V\) is then reflected with respect to the straight line \(x=2\) to the point \(W\). Find the \(x\)-coordinate of \(W\).

A. \(-4\)
B. \(-3\)
C. \(7\)
D. \(12\) ✓

aStep 1 — Rotate \(U\) about the origin by \(90^\circ\) anticlockwise

Apply the rotation rule for a \(90^\circ\) anticlockwise turn about the origin:

\[ (x,\,y)\;\xrightarrow{\;90^\circ\text{ ACW}\;}\;(-y,\,x) \]

Drag the slider to see how the image \(V\) moves as the rotation angle changes. When the angle reaches \(90^\circ\) the dashed red square is formed — a visual proof of the rule.

90°
U = (-3, -8)
V(θ) = (-8, -3)
|OU| = 8.544
Step 11M

Rotation of \(90^\circ\) anticlockwise about the origin maps \((x,y)\mapsto(-y,x)\).

Hint: Anticlockwise 90° swaps the coordinates and changes the sign of the new \(x\)-coordinate.
Step 21A

Substitute \(U(-3,-8)\) into the rule:

\[ V = \bigl(-(-8),\;-3\bigr) = (8,\,-3) \]
Hint: \(x_V = -y_U = -(-8) = 8\), and \(y_V = x_U = -3\).
Therefore \(V = (8,\,-3)\).

bStep 2 — Reflect \(V\) in the line \(x = 2\) to get \(W\)

The line \(x=2\) is a vertical mirror. The reflection leaves the \(y\)-coordinate unchanged and sends the \(x\)-coordinate \(x'\\) to \(x''\\) such that the midpoint lies on the mirror:

\[ \frac{x'+x''}{2}=2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x'' = 4 - x'. \]

Drag the slider to move the mirror line \(x=c\) and watch how \(W\) tracks the reflection. The dashed vertical segment shows the perpendicular distance to the line.

c = 2
V = (8, -3)
W = (-4, -3)
Midpoint M = (2, -3)
Distance V–M = 6
Step 11M

Reflection in the vertical line \(x=2\) keeps \(y\) unchanged, while the midpoint of \(VW\) lies on the mirror:

\[ \frac{x_V + x_W}{2} = 2 \]
Hint: The mirror is the perpendicular bisector of \(VW\).
Step 21M

Let \(x_W = x'\\). Then

\[ 2 - x' = 8 - 2 \] (equal distances from the mirror line on either side, or equivalently \(\frac{8+x'}{2}=2\)).
Hint: Both distances from the mirror must be equal: \(|x_W - 2| = |8 - 2| = 6\).
Step 31A

Solving:

\[ x' = 2 - 6 = -4 \] \[ \therefore W = (-4,\,-3) \]
Hint: \(V\) is \(6\) units to the right of the mirror, so \(W\) is \(6\) units to the left: \(2-6=-4\).
The \(x\)-coordinate of \(W\) is \(-4\). Answer: A

★Summary of Answers

  • Rotation rule (90° ACW about origin): \[(x,y)\mapsto(-y,x)\]
  • \(U(-3,-8) \;\longrightarrow\; V = (8,\,-3)\)
  • Reflection in \(x=2\): \(\;\dfrac{x_V+x_W}{2}=2 \;\Rightarrow\; x_W = 4 - x_V\)
  • \(x_W = 4 - 8 = -4\)
  • \(x\)-coordinate of \(W\) = \(-4\) → Option A
