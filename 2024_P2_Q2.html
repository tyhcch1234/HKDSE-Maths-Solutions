Simplify:

$$\frac{(2a)^3}{\left(4a^{-5}\right)^{-1}}=?$$

  1. $2a^{8}$
  2. $32a^{8}$
  3. $\dfrac{2}{a^{2}}$
  4. $\dfrac{32}{a^{2}}$
1Expand the numerator and the denominator.

$$(2a)^3 = 2^3 a^3 = 8a^3$$

$$\left(4a^{-5}\right)^{-1} = 4^{-1}a^{5}$$

Hint: \((xy)^n=x^n y^n\), and \((a^m)^{-1}=a^{-m}\).
2Rewrite the whole expression.

$$\frac{(2a)^3}{\left(4a^{-5}\right)^{-1}} = \frac{8a^3}{4^{-1}a^{5}}$$

Hint: treat power laws separately for the number and for \(a\).
3Simplify the numerical part.

$$\frac{8}{4^{-1}} = 8 \times 4 = 32$$

Hint: dividing by \(4^{-1}\) is the same as multiplying by \(4\).
4Simplify the index part of \(a\).

$$\frac{a^{3}}{a^{5}} = a^{3-5} = a^{-2} = \frac{1}{a^{2}}$$

Hint: \(\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}\).
5Combine to get the final answer.

$$\frac{(2a)^3}{\left(4a^{-5}\right)^{-1}} = \frac{32}{a^{2}}$$

Hint: match against the options — the numerical coefficient and the index of \(a\) must both be correct.
Answer: D  —  $\dfrac{32}{a^{2}}$
