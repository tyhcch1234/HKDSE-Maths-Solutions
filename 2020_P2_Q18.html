Parallelogram & Similar Triangles — Area Ratio

📐 Interactive Figure (not to scale in artwork, angles preserved)

Parallelogram ABCD Line EF & AB (G) Diagonal BD (H) Line CG G / H Segment E–D

📝 Question

In the figure, $ABCD$ is a parallelogram. Let $E$ be a point lying on $AD$ such that $AE:ED=2:5$. $CB$ is produced to the point $F$ such that $BF=DE$. Denote the point of intersection of $AB$ and $EF$ by $G$. It is given that $BD$ and $CG$ intersect at the point $H$. If the area of $\Delta AEG$ is $48\ \text{cm}^2$, then the area of $\Delta CDH$ is

  • A. $98\ \text{cm}^2$
  • B. $343\ \text{cm}^2$
  • C. $420\ \text{cm}^2$
  • D. $588\ \text{cm}^2$
💡 Plan: Because $AE:ED=2:5$ and $BF=DE$, you can fix every length. Then chain similar triangles → equal altitude area ratios to travel from $\Delta AEG$ to $\Delta CDH$.

🔎 Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1 · Set up the length ratios

In parallelogram $ABCD$, opposite sides are equal, so $AB=CD$ and $AD=BC$.

Let $AE=2k$ and $ED=5k$. Then $$AD=7k,\qquad BF=DE=5k,\qquad CD=AB.$$

Also $AE:ED=2:5$ is the key "seed" ratio for the whole question.

STEP 2 · Find BG : AE from ΔAEG ~ ΔBFG

Since $AE \parallel BF$ (both lie on $AD$ and $CB$ directions of a parallelogram), $$\angle EAG=\angle FBG,\quad \angle AEG=\angle BFG,\quad \angle AGE=\angle BGF\ (\text{vert. opp.}).$$ $$\therefore \Delta AEG \sim \Delta BFG \quad (\text{AAA}).$$

Corresponding sides: $$\frac{AG}{BG}=\frac{AE}{BF}=\frac{AE}{DE}=\frac{2k}{5k}=\frac{2}{5}.$$

Hence $AG:GB=2:5$, so $$AB:GB=(2+5):5=7:5.$$

STEP 3 · Find DH : BH from ΔCDH ~ ΔGBH

Since $CD \parallel GB$, $$\angle DCH=\angle BGH,\quad \angle CDH=\angle GBH,\quad \angle CHD=\angle GHB\ (\text{vert. opp.}).$$ $$\therefore \Delta CDH \sim \Delta GBH \quad (\text{AAA}).$$

Corresponding sides: $$\frac{CD}{GB}=\frac{DH}{BH}.$$ And $CD=AB$ (opposite sides of parallelogram), therefore $$\frac{DH}{BH}=\frac{AB}{GB}=\frac{7}{5}.$$

So along the diagonal, $DH:BH=7:5$, and $BD=DH+BH \Rightarrow DH=\dfrac{7}{12}BD$.

STEP 4 · Area of ΔADG relative to ΔAEG (common height)

$\Delta ADG$ and $\Delta AEG$ share the same height from $D$ (or $G$) onto the line $AD$. So areas are in the ratio of their bases on that line:

$$\frac{[ADG]}{[AEG]}=\frac{AD}{AE}=\frac{7k}{2k}=\frac{7}{2}.$$ $$[ADG]=48\times\frac{7}{2}=168\ \text{cm}^2.$$
STEP 5 · Area of ΔCBD relative to ΔADG (equal heights)

$BD$ splits the parallelogram into two equal halves, so $[CBD]=\tfrac12[ABCD]$ and $[ADG]$ shares the same altitude from $A$ onto line $CD$ / from $G$ onto line $CD$. Comparing $\Delta CBD$ and $\Delta ADG$ with a common height from $B$ / from $G$ onto $CD$:

$$\frac{[CBD]}{[ADG]}=\frac{CD}{AG}=\frac{7}{2}.$$ $$[CBD]=168\times\frac{7}{2}=588\ \text{cm}^2.$$
STEP 6 · Area of ΔCDH (common height)

$\Delta CDH$ and $\Delta CBD$ share the height from $C$ onto line $BD$, so their areas are in the ratio of the bases on $BD$:

$$\frac{[CDH]}{[CBD]}=\frac{DH}{BD}=\frac{7}{12}.$$ $$[CDH]=588\times\frac{7}{12}=343\ \text{cm}^2.$$

Shortcut chain: $48\times\dfrac{7}{2}\times\dfrac{7}{2}\times\dfrac{7}{12}=48\times\dfrac{343}{48}=343$.

Answer: B.   The area of $\Delta CDH$ is $$\boxed{343\ \text{cm}^2}$$
