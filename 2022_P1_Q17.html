Formulas & Sequences
Quadratic roots Sum & product of roots Arithmetic sequence Sum of AP

Let \(c\) be a real constant. The roots of \(x^2 + cx - 9 = 0\) are \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\).

(a) Express \(\alpha^2 + \beta^2\) in terms of \(c\).

[3 marks]

(b) The 1st, 2nd and 3rd terms of an arithmetic sequence are \(c^2\), \(\alpha^2+\beta^2\), and \(85\). Find the least \(n\) such that the sum of the first \(n\) terms > \(2 \times 10^6\).

[4 marks]

1 Step-by-step solution

For the quadratic \(x^2 + c x - 9 = 0\), the sum and product of roots are:

\[ \alpha + \beta = -c, \qquad \alpha \beta = -9 \]
💡 Identity to remember: \(\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\alpha+\beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta\)

Substitute the sum and product into the identity:

\[ \begin{aligned} \alpha^2 + \beta^2 &= (\alpha+\beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta \\ &= (-c)^2 - 2(-9) \\ &= c^2 + 18 \end{aligned} \]

✅ So \(\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = c^2 + 18\).

The 1st term is \(T_1 = c^2\).

The 2nd term is \(T_2 = \alpha^2+\beta^2 = c^2+18\).

The 3rd term is \(T_3 = 85\).

Since they form an arithmetic sequence, the common difference \(d\) is constant:

\[ T_2 - T_1 = T_3 - T_2 \] \[ (c^2+18) - c^2 = 85 - (c^2+18) \] \[ 18 = 67 - c^2 \quad \Rightarrow \quad c^2 = 49 \]

✅ Thus \(c^2 = 49\).

Since \(c^2 = 49\), the first term is \(a = c^2 = 49\).

Common difference \(d = T_2 - T_1 = (49+18) - 49 = 18\).

So the AP is: \(49, 67, 85, \dots\) with \(a = 49,\; d = 18\).

The sum of the first \(n\) terms of an AP is:

\[ S_n = \frac{n}{2}\big[2a + (n-1)d\big] \]

With \(a=49\), \(d=18\), we want \(S_n > 2\times 10^6\):

\[ \frac{n}{2}\big[2(49) + 18(n-1)\big] > 2\times 10^6 \] \[ 9n^2 + 40n - 2\times 10^6 > 0 \]
💡 Discriminant: \(\Delta = 40^2 - 4(9)(-2\times10^6) = 1600 + 72\times10^6 = 72\,001\,600\). \( \sqrt{\Delta} = \sqrt{40^2 + 72\times10^6} = 40\sqrt{1 + 45000} = 40\sqrt{45001} \approx 8486.0\)

Solve \(9n^2 + 40n - 2\times10^6 = 0\) using the quadratic formula:

\[ n = \frac{-40 \pm \sqrt{40^2 - 4(9)(-2\times10^6)}}{2(9)} \] \[ n = \frac{-40 \pm \sqrt{72\,001\,600}}{18} \] \[ n \approx \frac{-40 \pm 8485.99}{18} \]

So \(n < -473.63\) or \(n > 469.187\).

Since \(n\) is a positive integer, the least value is \(\lceil 469.187 \rceil = 470\).

✅ The least value of \(n\) is \(470\).

📌 Key takeaways

  • Use \(\alpha+\beta=-b/a\) and \(\alpha\beta=c/a\) to find symmetric expressions.
  • Identity: \(\alpha^2+\beta^2 = (\alpha+\beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta\).
  • For an arithmetic sequence, \(T_2 - T_1 = T_3 - T_2\) gives the unknown constant.
  • Sum formula \(S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a+(n-1)d]\) and solve quadratic inequality; always take the least integer.
