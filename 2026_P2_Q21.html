Similar Triangles and Perimeter

WX = 9 WY = 40 (diagonal) XY = 41 WZ, YZ (found) Vertices

Question

It is given that \(WXYZ\) is a quadrilateral, where \(\angle WXY=\angle YWZ\) and \(\angle WYX=\angle WZY\). If \(WX=9\) cm, \(WY=40\) cm and \(XY=41\) cm, find the perimeter of the quadrilateral \(WXYZ\).
A. \(100\) cm
B. \(180\) cm
C. \(410\) cm
D. \(490\) cm
Step 1 · Establish the similarity
In \(\triangle XYW\) and \(\triangle WZY\): \[ \angle WXY=\angle YWZ \quad(\text{given}),\qquad \angle WYX=\angle WZY \quad(\text{given}). \] The third angles must then also be equal: \(\angle XWY=\angle WYZ\). Hence \[ \triangle XYW \sim \triangle WZY \quad(\text{A.A.A.}) \]
A.A.A. Similarity \(\angle XWY=\angle WYZ\) Two given pairs of equal angles force the third pair equal, so \(\triangle XYW\sim\triangle WZY\).
Step 2 · Ratio of corresponding sides
Match vertices \(X\leftrightarrow W,\; Y\leftrightarrow Z,\; W\leftrightarrow Y\): \[ \frac{WX}{WY}=\frac{XY}{WZ}=\frac{WY}{YZ}=\frac{9}{40} \]
The ratio is fixed by the known sides \(WX=9\) and \(WY=40\).
Corresponding Sides of Similar Triangles \(\dfrac{WX}{WY}=\dfrac{XY}{WZ}=\dfrac{WY}{YZ}\) Matching \(X\) with \(W\), \(Y\) with \(Z\), \(W\) with \(Y\) fixes the ratio at \(\dfrac{9}{40}\).
Step 3 · Find \(WZ\) and \(YZ\)
\[ WZ=\frac{40\times XY}{9}=\frac{40\times 41}{9}=\frac{1640}{9}\text{ cm} \] \[ YZ=\frac{40\times WY}{9}=\frac{40\times 40}{9}=\frac{1600}{9}\text{ cm} \]
Step 4 · Compute the perimeter
\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Perimeter} &=WX+XY+YZ+WZ\\ &=9+41+\frac{1640}{9}+\frac{1600}{9}\\ &=50+\frac{3240}{9}=50+360=410\text{ cm} \end{aligned} \]
Perimeter of a Quadrilateral \(WX+XY+YZ+WZ\) \(9+41+\dfrac{1640}{9}+\dfrac{1600}{9}=50+360=410\text{ cm}\).
FINAL ANSWER C. \(410\) cm

Show the worked solution
Step 1. In \(\triangle XYW\) and \(\triangle WZY\), the two given pairs of angles are equal, so the third pair is equal too and \(\triangle XYW\sim\triangle WZY\).
Step 2. Corresponding sides give \(\dfrac{WX}{WY}=\dfrac{XY}{WZ}=\dfrac{WY}{YZ}=\dfrac{8}{15}\).
Step 3. \(WZ=\dfrac{15\times 17}{8}=\dfrac{255}{8}\text{ cm}\) and \(YZ=\dfrac{15\times 15}{8}=\dfrac{225}{8}\text{ cm}\).
Step 4. Perimeter \(=8+17+\dfrac{255}{8}+\dfrac{225}{8}=25+60=85\text{ cm}\).
Answer: \(85\text{ cm}\)
