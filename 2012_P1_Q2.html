Formulas & Sequences
[3 marks] Make $a$ the subject of the formula \[ \frac{3a+b}{8}=b-1. \]
1Clear the denominator
Multiplication Property of Equality \(\dfrac{A}{k}=B \;\Longrightarrow\; A=kB\quad (k\neq 0)\) Multiplying both sides by the same non-zero number keeps the equation balanced.

Multiply both sides of the equation by $8$:

\[ \frac{3a+b}{8}=b-1 \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; 3a+b=8(b-1) \]
Hint: whatever you do to the left side, do to the right side. Multiplying by 8 removes the fraction. (1M)
2Expand the bracket &
collect like terms
Distributive Law \(a(b+c)=ab+ac\) Used in reverse to expand \(8(b-1)=8b-8\).

Expand the right-hand side, then move the terms containing $a$ to one side and everything else to the other:

\[ 3a+b=8b-8 \] \[ 3a = 8b-8-b = 7b-8 \]
Hint: subtracting $b$ from both sides gives $3a = 8b - 8 - b$, which simplifies to $7b-8$. (1M)
3Divide by the coefficient of $a$
Division Property of Equality \(kA=B \;\Longrightarrow\; A=\dfrac{B}{k}\quad (k\neq 0)\) Divide both sides by the coefficient of the subject.

Divide both sides by $3$ to isolate $a$:

\[ a=\frac{7b-8}{3} \]
Hint: the coefficient of $a$ is 3, so divide through by 3. (1A)
✓Check by substitution

Take $b=2$: then $a=\dfrac{14-8}{3}=2$.

Original formula: $\dfrac{3(2)+2}{8}=\dfrac{8}{8}=1$ and $b-1=2-1=1$. ✓ Both sides agree.

Hint: a quick numerical check catches sign and arithmetic slips — worth 10 seconds in the exam.
Answer \[ a=\frac{7b-8}{3} \]

Question. Make \(p\) the subject of the formula \(\dfrac{4p+q}{3}=q-2\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Multiply both sides by \(3\): \(4p+q=3(q-2)\).
Step 2. Expand the bracket: \(4p+q=3q-6\).
Step 3. Subtract \(q\) from both sides: \(4p=2q-6\).
Step 4. Divide by \(4\): \(p=\dfrac{2q-6}{4}=\dfrac{q-3}{2}\).
Answer: \(p=\dfrac{q-3}{2}\)
