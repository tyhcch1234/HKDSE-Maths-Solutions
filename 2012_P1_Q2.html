Question:

Make \(a\) the subject of the formula

\[ \frac{3a+b}{8} = b - 1 \]

[3 marks]

The bars show that \(3a = 7b - 8\).

📝 Step-by-Step Solution

Hint: Multiply both sides by the denominator \(8\).

\[ \frac{3a+b}{8} \times 8 = (b-1) \times 8 \] \[ 3a + b = 8(b-1) \]

Hint: Distribute \(8\) to both \(b\) and \(-1\).

\[ 3a + b = 8b - 8 \]

This step gives you 1 mark (M).

Hint: Subtract \(b\) from both sides to move it to the right.

\[ 3a + b - b = 8b - 8 - b \] \[ 3a = 7b - 8 \]

This step gives you 1 mark (M).

Hint: Divide both sides by \(3\).

\[ \frac{3a}{3} = \frac{7b - 8}{3} \] \[ a = \frac{7b - 8}{3} \]

This step gives you 1 mark (A).

✅ Final Answer

\[ a = \frac{7b - 8}{3} \]
