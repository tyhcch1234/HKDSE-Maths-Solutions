[2021 MC #20] In the figure, $ABCD$ is a square. Let $E$ and $F$ be points lying on $AB$ and $BC$ respectively such that $AE = 3\,BE$ and $\angle DEF = 90^\circ$. If the area of $\Delta DEF$ is $25 \text{ cm}^2$, then the area of $\Delta CDF$ is

A. $48 \text{ cm}^2$
B. $50 \text{ cm}^2$
C. $52 \text{ cm}^2$
D. $75 \text{ cm}^2$
STEP 1 · Set up the variable

Let $BE = a$ cm. Since $AE = 3\,BE$, we get $AE = 3a$ cm.

The side of the square is therefore

$$AB = AE + EB = 3a + a = 4a \text{ cm}$$

so $AD = BC = CD = AB = 4a$ cm.

STEP 2 · Use the right angle at E

In $\Delta DEF$, $DE \perp EF$, so $\angle DEF = 90^\circ$.

Since $A$, $E$, $B$ are collinear, $\angle AED + \angle DEF + \angle BEF = 180^\circ$:

$$\angle AED + \angle BEF = 180^\circ - 90^\circ = 90^\circ$$

Also $\angle ADE + \angle AED = 90^\circ$ (right triangle $ADE$), so $\angle ADE = \angle BEF$.

STEP 3 · Solve for BF

Triangles $ADE$ and $BEF$ have equal angles and a right angle each, so $\Delta ADE \sim \Delta BEF$. Hence

$$\frac{AE}{AD} = \frac{BF}{BE}$$

$$\frac{3a}{4a} = \frac{BF}{a} \;\Rightarrow\; BF = \frac{3a}{4} \text{ cm}$$

Thus $CF = 4a - \dfrac{3a}{4} = \dfrac{13a}{4}$ cm.

STEP 4 · Lengths of the three sides of $\Delta DEF$

$$DE = \sqrt{AD^2 + AE^2} = \sqrt{(4a)^2+(3a)^2} = 5a$$

$$EF = \sqrt{BE^2 + BF^2} = \sqrt{a^2 + \left(\tfrac{3a}{4}\right)^2} = \tfrac{5a}{4}$$

STEP 5 · Relate the areas

Area of $\Delta DEF$:

$$\tfrac12 \cdot DE \cdot EF = \tfrac12 \cdot 5a \cdot \tfrac{5a}{4} = \tfrac{25a^2}{8} = 25$$

$$\Rightarrow a^2 = 8$$

Area of $\Delta CDF$ (base $CD=4a$, height $CF=\tfrac{13a}{4}$):

$$\tfrac12 \cdot CD \cdot CF = \tfrac12 \cdot 4a \cdot \tfrac{13a}{4} = \tfrac{13a^2}{2}$$

Substituting $a^2 = 8$:

$$\tfrac{13 \times 8}{2} = 52 \text{ cm}^2$$

FINAL ANSWER
C. $52 \text{ cm}^2$
