HKDSE Math 2019 Paper 1 Q10 Rate, Ratio & Variations

Partial Variation & Quadratic Equation

📌

It is given that \(h(x)\) is partly constant and partly varies as \(x\). Suppose that \(h(-2) = -96\) and \(h(5) = 72\).

(a) Find \(h(x)\). [3 marks]

(b) Solve the equation \(h(x) = 3x^2\). [2 marks]

Partial Variation Simultaneous Equations Quadratic Equation

🧩 Step-by-step Solution

“Partly constant and partly varies as \(x\)” means:

\( h(x) = r + s\,x \)

where \(r\) and \(s\) are non-zero constants.

Substitute \(x = -2\) and \(h(-2) = -96\):

\( r - 2s = -96 \) … (1)

Substitute \(x = 5\) and \(h(5) = 72\):

\( r + 5s = 72 \) … (2)

Subtract (1) from (2):

\( (r + 5s) - (r - 2s) = 72 - (-96) \)

\( 7s = 168 \)

\( s = 24 \)

Substitute \(s = 24\) into (2):

\( r + 5(24) = 72 \)

\( r + 120 = 72 \)

\( r = -48 \)

✅ \(h(x) = 24x - 48\)

We need \(h(x) = 3x^2\). Since \(h(x) = 24x - 48\):

\( 24x - 48 = 3x^2 \)

Rearrange:

\( 3x^2 - 24x + 48 = 0 \)

Divide by 3:

\( x^2 - 8x + 16 = 0 \)

\( (x - 4)^2 = 0 \)

\( x = 4 \)

✅ \( x = 4 \)
