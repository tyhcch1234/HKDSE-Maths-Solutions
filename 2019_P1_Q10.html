[2019 #10]

It is given that $h(x)$ is partly constant and partly varies as $x$. Suppose that $h(-2)=-96$ and $h(5)=72$.

(a) Find $h(x)$.  [3 marks]

(b) Solve the equation $h(x)=3x^{2}$.  [2 marks]

1(a) Set up the variation model
Partial Variation \(y=k_{1}+k_{2}x \quad(k_{1}\neq0,\ k_{2}\neq0)\) Partly constant and partly varying as \(x\) means \(h(x)=r+sx\) with both \(r\) and \(s\) non-zero.

“Partly constant and partly varies as $x$” means $h$ is a linear function of $x$ with a non-zero constant term and a non-zero coefficient of $x$. Let

$$h(x)=r+sx \quad (r\neq 0,\; s\neq 0)$$
Hint: “partly constant” → constant term $r$; “partly varies as $x$” → direct-variation term $sx$.
2Substitute the two given data points
Elimination Method \(\text{subtract one equation from the other to eliminate } r\) Subtracting (1) from (2) removes \(r\) and gives \(7s=168\), so \(s=24\) and \(r=-48\).

Using $h(-2)=-96$:

$$r+s(-2)=-96 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad r-2s=-96 \tag{1}$$

Using $h(5)=72$:

$$r+s(5)=72 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad r+5s=72 \tag{2}$$
Hint: each given pair $(x,h(x))$ gives one linear equation in $r$ and $s$.  (1 M)
3Solve the simultaneous equations
Perfect Square Identity \(a^{2}-2ab+b^{2}=(a-b)^{2}\) \(x^{2}-8x+16=(x-4)^{2}=0\) gives the repeated root \(x=4\).

$(2)-(1)$:

$$(r+5s)-(r-2s)=72-(-96)$$
$$7s=168 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad s=24$$

Substitute $s=24$ into (1):

$$r-2(24)=-96 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad r-48=-96 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad r=-48$$

Therefore

$$h(x)=-48+24x \;=\; 24x-48$$
Hint: eliminate $r$ by subtracting; both $r$ and $s$ are non-zero, consistent with the question.  (1A + 1A)
4(b) Form the quadratic equation

Solve $h(x)=3x^{2}$, i.e.

$$24x-48=3x^{2}$$

Bring everything to one side:

$$3x^{2}-24x+48=0 \tag{3}$$
Hint: rearrange so the $x^2$ term is positive — easier to factorise.  (1 M)
5Solve and check

Divide (3) by 3:

$$x^{2}-8x+16=0$$

Factorise as a perfect square:

$$(x-4)^{2}=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=4$$

Check: $h(4)=24(4)-48=48$ and $3(4)^{2}=48$. ✓

Hint: a repeated root means the line $y=h(x)$ is tangent to the parabola $y=3x^2$ at $x=4$.  (1A)
Answer:  (a) $h(x)=24x-48$   |   (b) $x=4$

Question. It is given that \(h(x)\) is partly constant and partly varies as \(x\). Suppose that \(h(-1)=-144\) and \(h(2)=-36\). (a) Find \(h(x)\). [3 marks] (b) Solve the equation \(h(x)=3x^{2}\). [2 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Write \(h(x)=r+sx\) with \(r\neq0\) and \(s\neq0\).
Step 2. \(h(-1)=-144\) gives \(r-s=-144\) and \(h(2)=-36\) gives \(r+2s=-36\).
Step 3. Subtracting: \(3s=108\), so \(s=36\); then \(r=-36-72=-108\). Hence \(h(x)=36x-108\).
Step 4. (b) \(36x-108=3x^{2}\), i.e. \(3x^{2}-36x+108=0\), so \(x^{2}-12x+36=0\).
Step 5. \((x-6)^{2}=0\), so \(x=6\). Check: \(h(6)=108\) and \(3(6)^{2}=108\) ✓.
Answer: (a) \(h(x)=36x-108\)   (b) \(x=6\)
