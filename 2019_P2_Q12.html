Step 1: Given — brand A costs \$140/kg, brand B costs \$315/kg, mixture costs \$210/kg.

Brand A — \$140/kg Brand B — \$315/kg Mixture — \$210/kg
HKDSE 2019 · Paper 2 · Q12

The costs of tea of brand A and brand B are $\$140$ / kg and $\$315$ / kg respectively. If $x$ kg of tea of brand A and $y$ kg of tea of brand B are mixed so that the cost of the mixture is $\$210$ / kg, then $x : y =$

  • A. $2:3$
  • B. $3:2$
  • C. $4:9$
  • D. $9:4$
STEP 1
Interpret the given information.
Brand A: $x$ kg at $\$140$ per kg · Brand B: $y$ kg at $\$315$ per kg · Mixture: $(x+y)$ kg at $\$210$ per kg.
Total cost of the mixture = total cost of A + total cost of B.
STEP 2
Set up the cost equation.
\[ \frac{140x+315y}{x+y}=210 \]
The weighted average cost equals the mixture price.
Weighted Average \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{p_{1}q_{1}+p_{2}q_{2}}{q_{1}+q_{2}}\) The mixture price \(210\) is the weighted average of \(140\) and \(315\) with weights \(x\) and \(y\).
STEP 3
Clear the denominator and expand.
\[ 140x+315y=210(x+y) \] \[ 140x+315y=210x+210y \]
Multiply both sides by $(x+y)$.
Clearing the Denominator \(\dfrac{A}{B}=C\Rightarrow A=BC\) Multiplying by \((x+y)\) gives \(140x+315y=210x+210y\).
STEP 4
Group like terms and solve for $x:y$.
\[ 315y-210y=210x-140x \] \[ 105y=70x \] \[ \frac{x}{y}=\frac{105}{70}=\frac{3}{2} \]
Collect $x$ terms on one side, $y$ terms on the other.
STEP 5
State the ratio and check with the alligation diagram.
\[ x:y=105:70=3:2 \] Cross-check: $3$ kg of A and $2$ kg of B cost $3(140)+2(315)=420+630=1050$, and $5$ kg of mixture cost $5(210)=1050$. ✓
Ratio must be simplified to lowest terms.

\[ \boxed{\,x:y=3:2\,} \]

Answer: B.  $3:2$

Question. Brand \(A\) tea costs \(\$150\) per kg and brand \(B\) tea costs \(\$280\) per kg. They are mixed to give a blend costing \(\$200\) per kg. Find \(x:y\), where \(x\) kg of \(A\) is mixed with \(y\) kg of \(B\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Total cost of the blend equals the sum of the costs of the two brands: \(\dfrac{150x+280y}{x+y}=200\).
Step 2. Clear the denominator: \(150x+280y=200x+200y\).
Step 3. Group like terms: \(280y-200y=200x-150x\), so \(80y=50x\).
Step 4. Hence \(\dfrac{x}{y}=\dfrac{80}{50}=\dfrac{8}{5}\), i.e. \(x:y=8:5\). Check: \(8(150)+5(280)=2600=13(200)\). ✓
Answer: \(8:5\)
