Coordinate Geometry — Incentre, Circumcentre & Tangents to a Circle

Coordinate Geometry Circle Equation Tangent Condition Discriminant = 0 Trapezium Area

QUESTION Full Question

[2016 #20] \( \Delta OPQ \) is an obtuse-angled triangle. Denote the incentre and the circumcentre of \( \Delta OPQ \) by \( I \) and \( J \) respectively. It is given that \( P \), \( I \) and \( J \) are collinear.

(a) Prove that \( OP = PQ \). [3 marks]

(b) A rectangular coordinate system is introduced so that the coordinates of \( O \) and \( Q \) are \( (0,0) \) and \( (40,30) \) respectively while the \( y \)-coordinate of \( P \) is \( 19 \). Let \( C \) be the circle which passes through \( O \), \( P \) and \( Q \).

(i) Find the equation of \( C \). [3 marks]

(ii) Let \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) be two tangents to \( C \) such that the slope of each tangent is \( \dfrac{3}{4} \) and the \( y \)-intercept of \( L_1 \) is greater than that of \( L_2 \). \( L_1 \) cuts the \( x \)-axis and the \( y \)-axis at \( S \) and \( T \) respectively while \( L_2 \) cuts the \( x \)-axis and the \( y \)-axis at \( U \) and \( V \) respectively.
Someone claims that the area of the trapezium \( STUV \) exceeds \( 17\,000 \). Is the claim correct? Explain your answer. [9 marks]

PART (a) Prove that \( OP = PQ \) 3 marks

125°
Triangle \(OPQ\) Incentre / incircle Circumcentre \(J\) \(P,I,J\) collinear
Drag the slider — notice \(PIJ\) stays collinear only when the marked sides are equal.
Step 11M
Note that \( J \) is the circumcentre of \( \Delta OPQ \), so \( JO = JP = JQ \) (radii of the circumcircle). Because \( P,\ I,\ J \) are collinear, \( \angle IPO = \angle JPQ \).
Hint: A collinearity condition lets us transfer an angle from \(I\) to \(J\).
Step 21M
\( \angle IPO = \angle IPQ \) and \( \angle JPQ = \angle JQP \) (incentre bisects angles; base angles of isosceles triangle \(JPQ\)). Hence \( \angle IPO = \angle JPQ \) and \( \angle JQP = \angle IPQ \). Also \( IP = IP \) (common side).
Hint: Identify the two triangles \( \Delta IPO \) and \( \Delta JPQ \) and check for a pair of equal angles and an included side.
Step 31A
\[ \angle IPO = \angle JPQ,\quad \angle IOP = \angle JQP,\quad IP = JP \] Thus \( \Delta IPO \cong \Delta JPQ \) (AAS). Therefore \( OP = PQ \) (corresponding sides, congruent triangles).
Hint: AAS congruence gives the required equal sides.
\( OP = PQ \) (proved).

PART (b)(i) Find the equation of \( C \) 3 marks

19
Fixed points \(O(0,0), Q(40,30)\) \(P(h,19)\) Circle \(C\)
h = 17, centre = (56, 33), r = 65
Step 11M
By (a), \( OP = PQ \). Let \( P = (h,\,19) \). Then \[ h^2 + 19^2 = (40-h)^2 + (30-19)^2 \] \[ h^2 + 361 = 1600 - 80h + h^2 + 121 \] \[ 80h = 1360 \Rightarrow h = 17 \]
Hint: \( OP = PQ \) gives an equation in \(h\) using the distance formula.
\( P = (17,\,19) \)
Step 21M
Let the equation of \( C \) be \( x^2 + y^2 + Dx + Ey + F = 0 \). Since \( C \) passes through the origin \( O(0,0) \), we get \( F = 0 \). Substitute \( P(17,19) \): \( 17D + 19E + 650 = 0 \). Substitute \( Q(40,30) \): \( 40D + 30E + 2500 = 0 \).
Hint: Three points give three equations; the origin instantly gives \(F=0\).
Step 31A
Solving the two linear equations: \[ D = -112,\qquad E = 66 \] Therefore the equation of \( C \) is \[ x^2 + y^2 - 112x + 66y = 0 \]
Hint: Verify with \(P\): \(17^2+19^2-112(17)+66(19)=650-1904+1254=0\). ✓
\( C:\ x^2 + y^2 - 112x + 66y = 0 \)

PART (b)(ii) Tangents with slope \( \tfrac{3}{4} \) & area of \( STUV \) 9 marks

c = 0
\(L_1: y=\frac34 x+\frac{25}{4}\) \(L_2: y=\frac34 x-\frac{625}{4}\) Circle \(C\) Trapezium \(STUV\)
Choose \(c\) and observe: tangency occurs when the discriminant is 0.
Step 11M
A line with slope \( \tfrac34 \) has the form \( y = \tfrac34 x + c \), where \( c \) is a constant. Substituting into the circle equation: \[ x^2 + y^2 - 112x + 66y = 0 \] \[ x^2 + \left(\tfrac34 x + c\right)^2 - 112x + 66\left(\tfrac34 x + c\right) = 0 \] \[ 25x^2 + (24c - 1000)x + 16c^2 + 1056c = 0 \]
Hint: Substitute the line into the circle and collect powers of \(x\).
Step 21M
Since \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) are tangents to \( C \), the quadratic in \( x \) must have a repeated root, so its discriminant is \( 0 \): \[ (24c - 1000)^2 - 4(25)(16c^2 + 1056c) = 0 \] \[ 16c^2 + 2400c - 15625 = 0 \] \[ (4c - 25)(4c + 625) = 0 \]
Hint: Tangency ⇔ discriminant \(=0\) ⇔ exactly one intersection point.
Step 31A
\[ c = \frac{25}{4} \quad \text{or} \quad c = -\frac{625}{4} \] Therefore \[ L_1:\ y = \frac34 x + \frac{25}{4}, \qquad L_2:\ y = \frac34 x - \frac{625}{4} \]
Hint: The larger intercept belongs to \(L_1\).
\( L_1:\ y=\tfrac34x+\tfrac{25}{4} \), \( L_2:\ y=\tfrac34x-\tfrac{625}{4} \)
Step 41M
Intercepts: \[ S\left(-\frac{25}{3},0\right),\quad T\left(0,\frac{25}{4}\right) \] \[ U\left(\frac{625}{3},0\right),\quad V\left(0,-\frac{625}{4}\right) \]
Hint: Set \(y=0\) for \(x\)-intercepts and \(x=0\) for \(y\)-intercepts.
Step 51M
\( L_1 /\!/ L_2 \) (same slope), so \( STUV \) is a trapezium with parallel sides \(ST\) and \(UV\). Split it along the \(y\)-axis: \[ \text{Area}(STUV) = \text{Area}(\Delta STO) + \text{Area}(\Delta UVO) \] \[ = \frac12\left(\frac{625}{3}\right)\left(\frac{625}{4}\right) + \frac12\left(\frac{625}{3}\right)\left(\frac{25}{4}\right) \] \[ = \frac{105\,625}{6} \approx 17\,604.1667 \]
Hint: The two triangles share vertex \(O\) and have bases on the \(x\)-axis; their heights are \(y\)-intercepts.
Step 61A
\[ \frac{105\,625}{6} = 17\,604.1\overline{6} > 17\,000 \] Thus the claim is correct.
Hint: Compare the exact fraction with \(17\,000 = \frac{102\,000}{6}\).
Area \(= \dfrac{105\,625}{6} \approx 17\,604.17 > 17\,000\) — the claim is correct.

SUMMARY All Answers at a Glance

  • (a) \( P,I,J \) collinear \( \Rightarrow \angle IPO=\angle JPQ \). With \( JO=JP=JQ \) and \( IP=JP \), \( \Delta IPO \cong \Delta JPQ \) (AAS), hence \( OP = PQ \).
  • (b)(i) \( h=17 \), so \( P=(17,19) \). The equation of \( C \) is \( x^2 + y^2 - 112x + 66y = 0 \).
  • (b)(ii) Tangency \( \Rightarrow (24c-1000)^2 - 4(25)(16c^2+1056c)=0 \Rightarrow c=\tfrac{25}{4} \) or \( c=-\tfrac{625}{4} \).
  • Equations: \( L_1:\ y=\tfrac34x+\tfrac{25}{4} \) and \( L_2:\ y=\tfrac34x-\tfrac{625}{4} \).
  • Intercepts: \( S\left(-\tfrac{25}{3},0\right) \), \( T\left(0,\tfrac{25}{4}\right) \), \( U\left(\tfrac{625}{3},0\right) \), \( V\left(0,-\tfrac{625}{4}\right) \).
  • Area of trapezium \( STUV = \dfrac{105\,625}{6} \approx 17\,604.17 > 17\,000 \) — the claim is correct.
