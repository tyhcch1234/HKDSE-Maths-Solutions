HKDSE 2023 · Paper 2 · Q25

12. Coordinate GeometryPerpendicular lines

Find the constant \(a\) such that the straight lines \(2x+(a+3)y-5=0\) and \(ax-4y+1=0\) are perpendicular to each other.

A. \(-6\)
B. \(-5\)
C. \(-2\)
D. \(4\)

STEP 1 · Name the lines & find slopes

Slope of a Straight Line \(ax+by+c=0\ \Longrightarrow\ m=-\dfrac{a}{b}\) Rearranging each line into \(y=mx+c\) reads off the two slopes.

Denote the lines \(L_1:2x+(a+3)y-5=0\) and \(L_2:ax-4y+1=0\).

For \(L_1\), making \(y\) the subject:

$$(a+3)y=-2x+5 \;\Rightarrow\; y=-\frac{2}{a+3}x+\frac{5}{a+3}$$

so the slope of \(L_1\) is \(m_1=-\dfrac{2}{a+3}\) (requires \(a\neq-3\)).

For \(L_2\): \(4y=ax+1\Rightarrow y=\dfrac{a}{4}x+\dfrac14\),
so \(m_2=\dfrac{a}{4}\).

When \(a=-3\), \(L_1\) becomes \(2x-5=0\), a vertical line; \(L_2\) would be \(-3x-4y+1=0\), not horizontal. So they would not be perpendicular.

STEP 2 · Perpendicular condition

Condition for Perpendicular Lines \(m_{1}\times m_{2}=-1\) Perpendicular lines have slopes whose product is \(-1\); this is the equation to solve.

Two lines are perpendicular when the product of their slopes equals \(-1\):

$$m_1\times m_2=-1$$

STEP 3 · Substitute and solve

$$\left(-\frac{2}{a+3}\right)\times\left(\frac{a}{4}\right)=-1$$ $$-\frac{2a}{4(a+3)}=-1 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{2a}{4(a+3)}=1$$ $$2a=4(a+3)$$ $$2a=4a+12 \;\Rightarrow\; -2a=12 \;\Rightarrow\; a=-6$$

STEP 4 · Check & conclude

With \(a=-6\): \(m_1=-\dfrac{2}{-3}=\dfrac{2}{3}\) and \(m_2=\dfrac{-6}{4}=-\dfrac{3}{2}\).

Indeed \(m_1 m_2=\dfrac{2}{3}\times\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)=-1\), so the lines are perpendicular. ✓

FINAL ANSWER\(a=-6\)  (option A)

Question. If the straight lines \((a+1)x+3y-4=0\) and \(2x-ay+5=0\) are perpendicular, find \(a\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Slopes: \(m_{1}=-\dfrac{a+1}{3}\) and \(m_{2}=\dfrac{2}{a}\) (with \(a\neq 0\)).
Step 2. Perpendicular condition \(m_{1}m_{2}=-1\): \(-\dfrac{2(a+1)}{3a}=-1\).
Step 3. Multiply by \(3a\): \(2(a+1)=3a\), so \(2a+2=3a\) and \(a=2\).
Step 4. Check with \(a=2\): \(m_{1}=-1\) and \(m_{2}=1\), and \((-1)(1)=-1\) ✓
Answer: \(a=2\)
