Question. The centre of the circle $C$ is the point $G(83,112)$. It is found that the point $A(158,12)$ lies outside $C$. $AP$ and $AQ$ are the tangents to $C$ at the points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. It is given that $C$ passes through the point $(23,67)$.

(a) Find the equation of the straight line passing through $A$ and $G$.  [2 marks]
(b) Find the coordinates of the point of intersection of $AG$ and $PQ$.  [3 marks]
(c) Find the equation of the inscribed circle of $\Delta APQ$.  [4 marks]
(d) Someone claims that the ratio of the area of the inscribed circle to the area of the circumcircle of $\Delta APQ$ is $1:4$. Do you agree? Explain your answer.  [3 marks]
STEP 1 · Part (a) Slope of $AG$ and its equation. $$m_{AG}=\frac{112-12}{83-158}=\frac{100}{-75}=-\frac{4}{3}$$ Line through $A(158,12)$: $$y-12=-\tfrac{4}{3}(x-158)$$ $$\Rightarrow\; 4x+3y-668=0$$
(a) $\;4x+3y-668=0$
Point-Slope Form \(y-y_{1}=m(x-x_{1})\) With \(m_{AG}=-\dfrac{4}{3}\) and \(A(158,12)\) the line is \(y-12=-\tfrac{4}{3}(x-158)\), i.e. \(4x+3y-668=0\).
STEP 2 · Radius and tangent points Radius of $C$. $$\text{radius}=\sqrt{(83-23)^2+(112-67)^2}=\sqrt{60^2+45^2}=75$$ Tangent points $P$ and $Q$.
$$AG=\sqrt{(158-83)^2+(12-112)^2}=\sqrt{75^2+100^2}=125$$ The chord of contact $PQ$ is perpendicular to $AG$ and meets it at $M$ with $$GM=\frac{r^2}{AG}=\frac{75^2}{125}=45,\qquad AM=125-45=80$$ Going $45$ units from $G$ towards $A$ in the direction $\big(\tfrac35,-\tfrac45\big)$ (a step of $1$ unit): $$M=\Big(83+45\cdot\tfrac35,\;112-45\cdot\tfrac45\Big)=(110,\,76)$$ The half-chord is $\sqrt{75^2-45^2}=60$, along $\big(\tfrac45,\tfrac35\big)$: $$P=\Big(110+60\cdot\tfrac45,\;76+60\cdot\tfrac35\Big)=(158,\,112),\qquad Q=(62,\,40)$$
Check: $AP=112-12=100$ and $AQ=\sqrt{96^2+28^2}=100$ — the two tangents from $A$ are equal.
$r=75$, $\;AP=AQ=100$, $\;P(158,112)$, $\;Q(62,40)$
Distance Formula \(r=\sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}+(y_{2}-y_{1})^{2}}\) Using \(G(83,112)\) and the point \((23,67)\) on \(C\) gives \(r=\sqrt{60^{2}+45^{2}}=75\).
STEP 3 · Part (b) The chord of contact $PQ$ is perpendicular to $AG$, so $AG$ meets $PQ$ at the midpoint $M$ of $PQ$.
Line $PQ$ through $M(110,76)$ with slope $\tfrac34$ (negative reciprocal of $m_{AG}=-\tfrac43$): $$y-76=\tfrac34(x-110)\;\Rightarrow\;3x-4y=26$$ Together with $4x+3y=668$: $$12x-16y=104,\qquad 12x+9y=2004\;\Rightarrow\;25y=1900,\;y=76,\;x=110$$
(b) The point of intersection is $(110,\,76)$.
Chord of Contact \(GM=\dfrac{r^{2}}{AG},\qquad PQ\perp AG\) From \(G\) the chord \(PQ\) is \(45\) units towards \(A\), because \(\dfrac{75^{2}}{125}=45\).
STEP 4 · Part (c) — the triangle $APQ$ Sides and area of $\triangle APQ$. $$AP=AQ=100,\qquad PQ=2\times60=120$$ The height from $A$ to $PQ$ is $AM=80$, so $$\text{Area}=\tfrac12(120)(80)=4800,\qquad s=\frac{100+100+120}{2}=160$$
STEP 5 · Part (c) — inscribed circle $$r_{\text{in}}=\frac{\text{Area}}{s}=\frac{4800}{160}=30$$ The centre $I$ lies on $AM$ (the angle bisector of $\angle PAQ$) at distance $30$ from $PQ$, so $$I=\Big(110+30\cdot\tfrac{48}{80},\;76-30\cdot\tfrac{64}{80}\Big)=(128,\,52)$$
Check: $IM=\sqrt{18^2+24^2}=30$ and $4(128)+3(52)-668=0$, so the centre lies on $AG$.
(c) Inscribed circle: $(x-128)^2+(y-52)^2=30^2$
Inradius from Area \(r=\dfrac{\Delta}{s}\) The area is \(4800\) and the semi-perimeter is \(160\), so the inscribed radius is \(\dfrac{4800}{160}=30\).
STEP 6 · Part (d) The circumcircle of $\triangle APQ$ is $C$ itself, since $P$ and $Q$ lie on $C$ and $\angle APG=\angle AQG=90^\circ$ (tangent and radius). Its radius is $75$, while the inscribed circle has radius $30$. $$\frac{\text{Area}_{\text{in}}}{\text{Area}_{\text{circ}}}=\left(\frac{30}{75}\right)^2=\frac{900}{5625}=\frac{4}{25}\neq\frac14$$
(d) Disagree — the ratio of the areas is $4:25$, not $1:4$.
Ratio of Areas of Similar Figures \(\dfrac{A_{1}}{A_{2}}=\left(\dfrac{r_{1}}{r_{2}}\right)^{2}\) With radii \(30\) and \(75\) the area ratio is \(\left(\dfrac{30}{75}\right)^{2}=\dfrac{4}{25}\), not \(\dfrac{1}{4}\).

Question. The centre of the circle \(C\) is \(G(14,\,0)\) and its radius is \(15\). The point \(A(-11,\,0)\) lies outside \(C\), and \(AP\) and \(AQ\) are the tangents to \(C\) at \(P\) and \(Q\) respectively. (a) Find the length of a tangent from \(A\) to \(C\). (b) Find the coordinates of the point of intersection of \(AG\) and \(PQ\). (c) Find the equation of the inscribed circle of \(\triangle APQ\). (d) A student claims that the ratio of the area of the inscribed circle to the area of the circumcircle of \(\triangle APQ\) is \(1:4\). Do you agree? Explain.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. (a) \(AG=14-(-11)=25\). Since a tangent is perpendicular to the radius, \(AP=\sqrt{AG^{2}-r^{2}}=\sqrt{625-225}=20\).
Step 2. (b) By symmetry \(PQ\perp AG\), and the chord of contact is at distance \(\dfrac{r^{2}}{AG}=\dfrac{225}{25}=9\) from \(G\), so \(PQ\) is the line \(x=14-9=5\). Hence \(AG\) meets \(PQ\) at \((5,\,0)\).
Step 3. Check: \(P(5,12)\) lies on \(C\) since \((5-14)^{2}+12^{2}=81+144=225\), and \(AP=\sqrt{16^{2}+12^{2}}=20\).
Step 4. (c) The inscribed circle touches \(PQ\) at \((5,0)\) with radius \(6\), so its centre is \(I(-1,0)\): check the distance to \(AP\) (\(3x-4y+33=0\)) is \(\dfrac{|-3+33|}{5}=6\). Hence \((x+1)^{2}+y^{2}=36\).
Step 5. (d) The circumcircle of \(\triangle APQ\) is \(C\) itself, of radius \(15\), so the area ratio is \(\dfrac{6^{2}}{15^{2}}=\dfrac{36}{225}=\dfrac{4}{25}\).
Step 6. Since \(\dfrac{4}{25}\neq\dfrac{1}{4}\), the claim is not correct.
Answer: (a) \(20\); (b) \((5,0)\); (c) \((x+1)^{2}+y^{2}=36\); (d) No, the ratio is \(4:25\)
