HKDSE 2022 · Paper 1 · Q19 — Coordinate Geometry (Tangents, Inscribed Circle)

Circle C (centre G) Tangents AP, AQ Chord of contact PQ Inscribed circle Centre I
Question. The centre of the circle $C$ is the point $G(83,112)$. It is found that the point $A(158,12)$ lies outside $C$. $AP$ and $AQ$ are the tangents to $C$ at the points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. It is given that $C$ passes through the point $(23,67)$.

(a) Find the equation of the straight line passing through $A$ and $G$.  [2 marks]
(b) Find the coordinates of the point of intersection of $AG$ and $PQ$.  [3 marks]
(c) Find the equation of the inscribed circle of $\Delta APQ$.  [4 marks]
(d) Someone claims that the ratio of the area of the inscribed circle to the area of the circumcircle of $\Delta APQ$ is $1:4$. Do you agree? Explain your answer.  [3 marks]
STEP 1 · Part (a) Slope of $AG$ and its equation. $$m_{AG}=\frac{112-12}{83-158}=\frac{100}{-75}=-\frac{4}{3}$$ Line through $A(158,12)$: $$y-12=-\tfrac{4}{3}(x-158)$$ $$\Rightarrow\; 4x+3y-668=0$$
(a) $\;4x+3y-668=0$
STEP 2 · Radius of C Radius via the given point $(23,67)$. $$\text{radius}= \sqrt{(83-23)^2+(112-67)^2}=\sqrt{60^2+45^2}=75$$ Since $AG=75+? $ — actually $G$ to $A$ along slope gives $P,\,Q$: with radius $75$, $\;83+75=158$ so $P=(158,112)$, and $112-75=37$ … but check $AQ$ vertical: $Q=(158,112)$? No — $AP$ vertical at $x=158$, $AQ$ vertical at $y=112$ (light-radius reasoning from the mark scheme):
Since $83+75=158$, the point $P$ has $x=158$; since $112-75=37$ … the tangent points are $P(158,112)$ and $Q=(158,112)$? Use the symmetric property: $AG\perp PQ$.
In fact, by symmetry about line $AG$: $P$ and $Q$ lie on the circle of radius $75$ centred at $G$, and the chord $PQ$ is perpendicular to $AG$.
STEP 3 · Part (b) Intersection of $AG$ and $PQ$.
$\Delta AGP\cong\Delta AGQ$ and $AG\perp PQ$ (tangent symmetry), so the slope of $PQ$ is the negative reciprocal of $-\tfrac43$: $$m_{PQ}=\tfrac{3}{4}$$ Through $P(158,112)$: $$y-112=\tfrac{3}{4}(x-158)\;\Rightarrow\; 3x-4y+… $$ Actually $P=(158,112)$ lies at top of circle; the chord of contact is: $$y-112=\tfrac34(x-158)$$ Solving with $4x+3y-668=0$ gives $$x=110,\quad y=76$$
(b) Intersection of $AG$ and $PQ$ is $(110,\,76)$
STEP 4 · Part (c) — centre of incircle Let $I$ and $r$ be the centre and radius of the inscribed circle of $\Delta APQ$.
$AP=AQ$ (tangent lengths) so $I$ lies on $AG$. The $x$-coordinate: $$x_I=158-r$$ Since $I$ lies on line $AG$ ($4x+3y-668=0$): $$y_I=\frac{-4}{3}(158-r)+\frac{668}{3}=\frac{4r}{3}+12$$ So $$I=\Big(158-r,\;\frac{4r}{3}+12\Big)$$
STEP 5 · Part (c) — radius equation Distance from $I$ to the point of intersection $(110,76)$ equals $r$. $$\big[(158-r)-110\big]^2+\Big[\Big(\frac{4r}{3}+12\Big)-76\Big]^2=r^2$$ $$\frac{16}{9}r^2-\frac{800}{3}r+6400=0$$ $$r^2-150r+3600=0$$ $$r=30 \quad\text{or}\quad r=120\;(\text{rejected})$$ Hence $r=30$ and $$I=\big(158-30,\;\tfrac{4(30)}{3}+12\big)=(128,\,52)$$
(c) Inscribed circle: $(x-128)^2+(y-52)^2=30^2$
STEP 6 · Part (d) Ratio of areas: inscribed circle vs circumcircle of $\Delta APQ$.
The circumcircle of $\Delta APQ$ has centre $G$ and radius $75$ (the original circle $C$).
Inscribed circle radius $r=30$. $$\frac{\text{Area}_{\text{in}}}{\text{Area}_{\text{circ}}}=\frac{\pi(30)^2}{\pi(75)^2}=\frac{900}{5625}=\frac{4}{25}\neq\frac14$$
(d) Disagree — the ratio is $4:25$, not $1:4$.
Reason: the claim $r:R=1:2$ is false; here $r:R=30:75=2:5$, so areas are $4:25$.
