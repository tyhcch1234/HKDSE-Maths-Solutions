HKDSE 2015 Paper 2 · Q2 Laws of Indices Exponential Functions

Simplify the expression

Which of the following is equal to the given expression?

\[ \frac{(3y^6)^4}{3y^2} \]
A. \[ 4y^5 \]
B. \[ 4y^8 \]
C. \[ 27y^{12} \]
D. \[ 27y^{22} \]

Step-by-Step Solution

Hint: \((ab)^n = a^n \cdot b^n\)

Here, the base \(3y^6\) is a product of \(3\) and \(y^6\).

\[ (3y^6)^4 = 3^4 \cdot (y^6)^4 \]

Hint: \((a^m)^n = a^{m \times n}\)

For \(y^6\) raised to the 4th power, multiply the exponents: \(6 \times 4 = 24\).

\[ (y^6)^4 = y^{6 \times 4} = y^{24} \]

Hint: Calculate \(3^4 = 81\).

Combine the constant and the variable part.

\[ 3^4 \cdot y^{24} = 81y^{24} \]

Hint: \(\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}\)

Divide the constants: \(\frac{81}{3} = 27\).

Subtract the exponents of \(y\): \(24 - 2 = 22\).

\[ \frac{81y^{24}}{3y^2} = \frac{81}{3} \cdot y^{24-2} = 27y^{22} \]
✓

Correct Answer: D

\[ \frac{(3y^6)^4}{3y^2} = 27y^{22} \]
