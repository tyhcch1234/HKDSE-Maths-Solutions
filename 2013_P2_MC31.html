HKDSE Math 2013 Paper 2 Q31

Topic 4 · Polynomials
L.C.M. Factorization Sum / Difference of Cubes Perfect Square

Question

The L.C.M. of $a^2 + 4a + 4$, $a^2 - 4$ and $a^3 + 8$ is
A. $a + 2$
B. $(a-2)(a+2)^2(a^2 - 2a + 4)$
C. $(a-2)(a+2)^2(a^2 + 2a + 4)$
D. $(a-2)(a+2)^4(a^2 - 2a + 4)$

Step-by-Step Solution

💡 Hint: Look for common patterns: perfect squares, difference of squares, sum of cubes.

Factorise each polynomial into irreducible factors:

$a^2 + 4a + 4 = (a+2)^2$
$a^2 - 4 = (a-2)(a+2)$
$a^3 + 8 = (a+2)(a^2 - 2a + 4)$
💡 Hint: The L.C.M. must contain the highest power of every factor that appears in any expression.
Expression Factorisation
$a^2 + 4a + 4$ $(a+2)^2$
$a^2 - 4$ $(a-2)^1(a+2)^1$
$a^3 + 8$ $(a+2)^1(a^2 - 2a + 4)^1$

Distinct factors: $(a-2)$, $(a+2)$, $(a^2 - 2a + 4)$.

💡 Hint: Compare the powers of each factor across all expressions and pick the maximum.

$(a-2)$

Powers: $0, 1, 0$

Max = $1$

$(a+2)$

Powers: $2, 1, 1$

Max = $2$

$(a^2 - 2a + 4)$

Powers: $0, 0, 1$

Max = $1$

💡 Hint: Multiply the factors with their highest powers together.

Multiply the factors with the maximum powers found:

$\text{L.C.M.} = (a-2)^1 (a+2)^2 (a^2 - 2a + 4)^1$

This matches option B.

Final Answer

$\text{L.C.M.} = (a-2)(a+2)^2(a^2 - 2a + 4)$

Correct option: B
