Marked price $255 Discount 40% Selling price Cost price $150 Profit 2%
HKDSE 2014 · Paper 1 · Q6 · Percentages

The marked price of a toy is $\$255$. The toy is now sold at a discount of $40\%$ on its marked price.

(a) Find the selling price of the toy.

(b) If the percentage profit is $2\%$, find the cost of the toy. [4 marks]

Step 1 · Identify the given information
\[ \text{Marked price} = \$255,\qquad \text{Discount} = 40\% \]
Discount is always taken on the marked price, so the discount amount is $40\%$ of $\$255$.
Step 2 · Selling price (discount method)
\[ \text{Selling price} = 255 \times (1 - 40\%) = 255 \times 0.6 = 153 \]
Paying $100\% - 40\% = 60\%$ of the marked price. (1M + 1A)
Step 3 · Set up the profit equation
\[ \text{Let } x \text{ be the cost of the toy.} \] \[ (1 + 2\%)x = 153 \]
Selling price $=$ cost $\times (1 + \text{profit}\%)$. Here profit is $2\%$ on the cost. (1M)
Step 4 · Solve for the cost
\[ 1.02x = 153 \] \[ x = \frac{153}{1.02} = 150 \]
Divide both sides by $1.02$. (1A)
Answer:  (a) Selling price $= \$$153  ·  (b) Cost of the toy $= \$$150
