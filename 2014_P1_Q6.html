Percentages: Marked Price, Discount & Profit

📊 Percentages 💰 Discount 📈 Profit % 🔢 Algebra

QQuestion

The marked price of a toy is $255. The toy is now sold at a discount of 40% on its marked price.

(a) Find the selling price of the toy.

(b) If the percentage profit is 2%, find the cost of the toy.

[4 marks]

📝Guided Solution

Click each step to reveal the reasoning. Try to predict before opening!

1
Understand the meaning of “discount”
▼

A discount of 40% means you pay only 60% of the marked price.

Selling price = Marked price × (1 − discount%)
Hint: “Discount” is the amount taken off.
Original price (marked price) → 100%.
Pay = 100% − 40% = 60%.
2
(a) Calculate the selling price
▼

Substitute marked price = $255 and discount = 40%:

Selling price = 255 × (1 − 40%)
= 255 × 0.6
= $153
✅ (a) Selling price = $153
3
(b) Set up an equation for profit
▼

Let the cost of the toy be $x.

The percentage profit is 2%. This means:

Profit = Selling price − Cost = 2% × Cost

Equivalently, the selling price is 102% of the cost:

Selling price = Cost × (1 + 2%)
(1 + 2%) x = 153
Hint: Profit percentage is calculated on the cost.
Marked price = 100%  →  Cost = 100%  →  Selling price = 102% of cost.
4
(b) Solve for the cost
▼

Solve the equation:

(1 + 2%) x = 153
1.02 x = 153
x = 153 ÷ 1.02
x = 150
✅ (b) Cost of the toy = $150

Check: Profit = $153 − $150 = $3. Percentage profit = 3/150 × 100% = 2%. ✔
