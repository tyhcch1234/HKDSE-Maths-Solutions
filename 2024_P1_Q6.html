Percentages — Marked Price of a Calculator

The marked price of a calculator is 40% higher than its cost. The calculator is sold at a discount of 25% on its marked price and the profit is \$13. Find the marked price of the calculator. (4 marks)
1Set up Let the cost of the calculator be $\$c$.
The marked price is $40\%$ higher than the cost:
$\text{Marked price}=(1+40\%)\,c=\$1.4c$
Hint: “40% higher than cost” means cost × 1.4.
Percentage Increase \(\text{new value}=\text{old value}\times(1+r\%)\) A marked price \(40\%\) above cost is \(1.4c\) when the cost is \(\$c\).
2Selling price A 25% discount is taken on the marked price, so the customer pays 75% of it:
$\text{Selling price}=(75\%)(1.4c)=\$1.05c$
Hint: discount 25% ⇒ pay 100% − 25% = 75%.
Discount on the Marked Price \(\text{selling price}=\text{marked price}\times(1-d\%)\) A \(25\%\) discount means the customer pays \(75\%\) of \(1.4c\), i.e. \(1.05c\).
3Profit equation Profit = selling price − cost:
$1.05c-c=13$
$0.05c=13 \quad\Rightarrow\quad c=260$
Hint: solving gives the cost first.
Profit \(\text{profit}=\text{selling price}-\text{cost}\) \(1.05c-c=0.05c=13\), so the cost is \(\$260\).
4Marked price Marked price $=1.4c=1.4\times 260=\$364$
Hint: the question asks for the marked price, not the cost — finish the job!
Answer: The marked price of the calculator is $\boxed{\$364}$.

Question. The marked price of a calculator is \(30\%\) higher than its cost. The calculator is sold at a discount of \(20\%\) on its marked price and the profit is \(\$16\). Find the marked price of the calculator.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let the cost be \(\$c\). The marked price is \((1+30\%)c=1.3c\).
Step 2. The selling price is \(80\%\) of the marked price: \((0.8)(1.3c)=1.04c\).
Step 3. Profit \(=\) selling price \(−\) cost: \(1.04c-c=16\), so \(0.04c=16\) and \(c=400\).
Step 4. Marked price \(=1.3(400)=\$520\).
Answer: The marked price is \(\$520\)
