HKDSE 2020 · Paper 2 · Q13

The solution of \(5-4x<9\)  and  \(\dfrac{2x-3}{7}>1\) is

A. \(x>-1\)
B. \(x<5\)
C. \(x>5\)
D. \(x>5\)  (the required combined solution)
1Read the conditions

We must satisfy both inequalities at the same time:

\[5-4x<9 \qquad\text{and}\qquad \frac{2x-3}{7}>1\]

Hint: “and” means we take the overlap (intersection) of the two solution sets.
2Solve the first inequality
Reversing the Inequality Sign \(a-kb\quad(k>0)\) Dividing \(5-4x<9\) by \(-4\) turns \(<\) into \(>\), giving \(x>-1\).

\[5-4x<9\] \[-4x<4\] \[x>-1 \quad\text{(divide by } -4,\ \text{reverse the sign)}\]

Hint: dividing (or multiplying) an inequality by a negative number flips the inequality sign.
3Solve the second inequality
Multiplying an Inequality by a Positive Number \(a0)\) Multiplying \(\dfrac{2x-3}{7}>1\) by the positive \(7\) keeps the sign, giving \(2x-3>7\).

\[\frac{2x-3}{7}>1\] \[2x-3>7\] \[2x>10\] \[x>5\]

Hint: multiply both sides by the positive number \(7\) — the sign is unchanged.
4Take the intersection
Intersection of Solution Sets \(x>a\ \text{ and }\ x>b\ \Rightarrow\ x>\max(a,b)\) With \(x>-1\) and \(x>5\) the overlap is \(x>5\), the stricter lower bound.

We need \(x>-1\) and \(x>5\).
On the number line, \(-1\) lies to the left of \(5\). The overlap of the two rays is everything strictly to the right of \(5\):

\[\therefore\quad x>5\]

Hint: the stricter (larger) lower bound wins — \(x>5\) automatically makes \(x>-1\) true as well.
Answer: \(\;x>5\;\)  →  Option C / D-marked choice

Question. Solve \(3-5x<18\) and \(\dfrac{3x-2}{5}>2\) simultaneously.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(3-5x<18\Rightarrow -5x<15\);
divide by \(-5\) and reverse the sign: \(x>-3\).
Step 2. Multiply \(\dfrac{3x-2}{5}>2\) by \(5\): \(3x-2>10\Rightarrow 3x>12\Rightarrow x>4\).
Step 3. We need \(x>-3\) and \(x>4\);
the overlap of the two rays is everything to the right of \(4\).
Answer: \(x>4\)
