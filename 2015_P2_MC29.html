Statistics — Reading a Box-and-Whisker Diagram

Box-and-Whisker Diagram Quartiles Inter-quartile Range Five-Number Summary

Question

The box-and-whisker diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of books read by some teachers in a term. Find the inter-quartile range of the distribution.

  1. 20
  2. 35
  3. 40
  4. 45

Part (a) — Read the quartiles from the diagram

\(Q_1\) = 25
\(Q_3\) = 45
IQR = 20
0 / 3
Step 11M

In a box-and-whisker diagram the box is bounded by the lower quartile \(Q_1\) on the left and the upper quartile \(Q_3\) on the right. Locate the left and right edges of the box and read off their values on the horizontal axis.

Hint: The whiskers reach the minimum and maximum; the line inside the box is the median \(Q_2\).
Step 21A

From the diagram:

\[ Q_1 = 25 \qquad Q_3 = 45 \]
Hint: Check the scale — each small division on the axis is 5 units, and the 4th and 8th vertical guide lines sit at 25 and 45.
\(Q_1 = 25,\quad Q_3 = 45\)
Step 31A

Compute the inter-quartile range:

\[ \text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 = 45 - 25 = 20 \]
Hint: IQR measures the spread of the middle 50% of the data. It is never negative, so always take \(Q_3 - Q_1\).
IQR \(= 20\) → Answer: A

Part (b) — Why the other options are wrong

Range = 50
IQR = 20
Median \(Q_2\) = 40
0 / 3
Step 11M

Option A (20) — correct. This is exactly \(Q_3 - Q_1\). Toggle the “Highlight IQR” box to see the shaded region which spans from 25 to 45.

Hint: The IQR is the width of the box, not the length of the whole diagram.
Step 21M

Option B (35) — wrong. This is \(60 - 25\) or another mis-read; it mixes a whisker end with a quartile. The whisker ends are the minimum and maximum, not quartiles.

Hint: Don’t confuse the ends of the whiskers (min / max) with the edges of the box (\(Q_1\) / \(Q_3\)).
Step 31M

Options C (40) and D (45) — wrong. \(40\) is the median \(Q_2\); \(45\) is simply \(Q_3\). Neither is a difference of two quartiles.

Hint: IQR is always a subtraction. Tick “Show median” to see the vertical line at 40 — that is the median, not the IQR.
Correct option: A (20)

Summary of Answers

  • Lower quartile: \(Q_1 = 25\)
  • Upper quartile: \(Q_3 = 45\)
  • Inter-quartile range: \(\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 = 45 - 25 = 20\)
  • Correct option: A
  • Reminder: IQR \(=\) width of the box \(=\) spread of the middle 50% of the data.
