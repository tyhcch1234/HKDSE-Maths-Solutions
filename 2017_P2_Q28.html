Q The Question

The bar chart below shows the distribution of the numbers of tokens got by a group of children in a game. If a child is randomly selected from the group, find the probability that the selected child gets fewer than 5 tokens in the game.

Bar chart: number of children against the number of tokens got.

1 Read the chart: how many children in total? ▶

Each bar tells us how many children got that number of tokens.

Tokens34567
Children520151010
Hint — Formula: Total number of outcomes $=$ (number of children) $=$ sum of all heights.
We add every bar height to obtain the size of the whole group.
$5 + 20 + 15 + 10 + 10 = \mathbf{60}$

So there are 60 children in the group — this is the total number of possible outcomes when one child is picked at random.

2 Decode "fewer than 5 tokens" ▶

The key words are "fewer than 5".

  • "Fewer than 5" means strictly less than 5, i.e. $< 5$.
  • 5 itself is NOT included.
  • The numbers of tokens satisfying this are: $3$ and $4$ only.
Common trap: If the question said "5 or fewer" ($\le 5$), then 5 would be included as well. Read the inequality carefully! In a discrete bar chart, "fewer than 5", "at most 4" and "$<5$" all mean $\{3,4\}$ here.
3 Count the favourable outcomes ▶

We need the number of children who got 3 or 4 tokens.

Hint — Formula: Number of favourable outcomes $=$ sum of the bar heights belonging to the accepted tokens (here: 3 and 4).
$5 + 20 = \mathbf{25}$ children
4 Apply the probability formula ▶
Hint — Formula: $P(E)=\dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total number of possible outcomes}}$
Numerator: outcomes that satisfy the condition. Denominator: all equally likely outcomes.
$P = \dfrac{25}{60}$

Now reduce the fraction: divide top and bottom by 5.

$\dfrac{25}{60} = \dfrac{25\div 5}{60\div 5} = \dfrac{5}{12}$

The denominator 60 counts children, not token values — every child is equally likely to be chosen, so this is a uniform sample space.

Correct Option
C  ·  $\dfrac{5}{12}$
$\dfrac{5+20}{5+20+15+10+10}=\dfrac{25}{60}=\dfrac{5}{12}$
  • Denominator $=$ total children $= 60$
  • Numerator $=$ children with 3 or 4 tokens $= 25$
  • "Fewer than 5" excludes the 15 children with 5 tokens
  • Fraction simplified by dividing by 5
