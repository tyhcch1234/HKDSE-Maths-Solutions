HKDSE Math 2018 Paper 2 Q11

Variation: Direct & Inverse Proportion

Variation Exponents Algebra

If \( w \) varies directly as the square root of \( u \) and inversely as the square of \( v \), which of the following must be constant?

Try the interactive steps below to see why the answer is D.

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Translate the variation statement into an equation

"\( w \) varies directly as the square root of \( u \)" means \( w \propto \sqrt{u} \).
"and inversely as the square of \( v \)" means \( w \propto \dfrac{1}{v^2} \).

Combining both, we introduce a non‑zero constant \( k \):

\[ w = k \cdot \frac{\sqrt{u}}{v^2} \]
Hint
Hint: Direct variation → multiplication by the variable (or its power).
Inverse variation → division by the variable (or its power).
The constant of proportionality \( k \) absorbs the "variation".
2

Rearrange to isolate \( k \)

The question asks which expression must be constant. Since \( k \) is constant, any expression equal to \( k \) (or a power of \( k \)) will be constant. Rearrange:

\[ k = \frac{w \, v^2}{\sqrt{u}} \]

But the options are not in this exact form. We need to match the powers of \( u \) and \( v \).

3

Square both sides to get integer powers

Squaring eliminates the square root and gives a clean relationship:

\[ k^2 = \frac{w^2 v^4}{u} \]

This is exactly option D: \( \dfrac{v^4 w^2}{u} \).

✅ Option D must be constant (it equals \( k^2 \)).
4

Check why the others are not necessarily constant

Substitute \( w = k \dfrac{\sqrt{u}}{v^2} \) into each option:

  • A. \( u^4 v w^2 = u^4 v \left( k \dfrac{\sqrt{u}}{v^2} \right)^2 = k^2 \dfrac{u^5}{v^3} \) — depends on \( u,v \).
  • B. \( u v^4 w^2 = u v^4 \left( k \dfrac{\sqrt{u}}{v^2} \right)^2 = k^2 u^2 \) — depends on \( u \).
  • C. \( \dfrac{v w^2}{u^4} = \dfrac{v}{u^4} \left( k \dfrac{\sqrt{u}}{v^2} \right)^2 = \dfrac{k^2}{u^3 v^3} \) — depends on \( u,v \).
  • D. \( \dfrac{v^4 w^2}{u} = \dfrac{v^4}{u} \left( k \dfrac{\sqrt{u}}{v^2} \right)^2 = k^2 \) — constant! ✅

🔑 Key takeaway

When a quantity varies directly / inversely with several variables, write it as an equation with a constant \( k \). To find which expression is constant, rearrange the equation so that \( k \) (or a power of \( k \)) is isolated on one side. The other side is the constant expression.
