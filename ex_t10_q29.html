Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q29

A cuboid has a base area of $15$ cm$^2$ and a height of $6$ cm. Find its volume.

Solution

The volume of a cuboid is given by $V=Ah$, where $A$ is the base area and $h$ is the height.
Here, $A=15$ cm$^2$ and $h=6$ cm.
Substitute into the formula: $V=15\times6=90$.
Thus the volume is $90$ cm$^3$.
This is mathematically correct.
The volume of the cuboid is $90$ cm$^3$.
