Question

19. According to the figure, which of the following must be true?

  • I.   $u-v+w=0^\circ$
  • II.   $u+v-w=180^\circ$
  • III. $u+v+w=450^\circ$

A. I only    B. II only    C. I and III only    D. II and III only

Solution

Step 1 · Understand the figure

$A\!B$ and $E\!D$ are two parallel lines (arrow marks). A broken line $B\!-\!C\!-\!D$ joins them.

  • $u$ = angle between ray $BA$ and segment $BC$ (at $B$)
  • $w$ = angle between ray $DE$ and segment $DC$ (at $D$)
  • $v$ = the reflex angle at $C$
Trick: two of the three angles are measured on the same side as the "opening" of the broken line, so they are alternate-looking angles — the reflex angle is the key.
Step 2 · Test Statement I

Move $B$ along line $A\!B$ so that $AB$ gets longer.

Then $u$ decreases, $v$ increases,
and $w$ stays unchanged.

So the L.H.S. $u-v+w$ changes while the R.H.S.
($0^\circ$) stays fixed.

Statement I is NOT always true. FALSE

Step 3 · Statement II — Method 1
Interior and Alternate Angles \(AB // CF // DE\) With \(CF\) drawn parallel to both lines, \(\angle BCF=180^\circ-u\) and \(\angle DCF=w\), so \(v=180^\circ-u+w\).

Draw $CF // AB // DE$ through $C$.

$\angle BCF = 180^\circ - u$  
(int. $\angle$s, $BC$ transversal to $FC // AB$)

$\angle DCF = w$  
(alt. $\angle$s, $FC // DE$)

So the reflex angle at $C$ is $$v = \angle BCF + \angle DCF = (180^\circ-u)+w$$ $$u+v-w = 180^\circ \quad \checkmark$$

Statement II is always true. TRUE

Step 4 · Statement II — Method 2 (check)
Angle Sum of a Triangle \(\angle A+\angle B+\angle C=180^\circ\) In \(\triangle GBC\) the angles add to \(180^\circ\), which again gives \(u+v-w=180^\circ\).

Produce $BA$ to $G$ so that $GCD$ is a straight line.

$\angle BGD = 180^\circ - w$  
(int. $\angle$s, $GB // DE$)

$\angle BCG = v - 180^\circ$  
(since $v$ is the reflex angle at $C$)

In $\triangle GBC$: $$(180^\circ - w)+u+(v-180^\circ)=180^\circ$$ $$u+v-w = 180^\circ \quad \checkmark$$ Both methods agree,
so Statement II holds.

Step 5 · Test Statement III

Move $D$ alone so that $DE$ gets longer.

Then $u$ stays unchanged,
but $v$ decreases and $w$ decreases.

So $u+v+w$ changes while the R.H.S.
($450^\circ$) remains fixed.

Statement III is NOT always true. FALSE

Step 6 · Choose the answer

Only Statement II must be true: $u+v-w=180^\circ$.

Answer:  
B (II only)

Key identity:  $u+v-w=180^\circ$

Reason: the reflex angle $v$ at $C$ equals the sum of the two exterior turns, so $v=180^\circ-u+w$. Hence $u+v-w=180^\circ$.

Question. In the figure, \(AB // ED\) and the broken line \(B-C-D\) joins them. The angle at \(B\) is \(u=40^\circ\), the angle at \(D\) is \(w=25^\circ\) and the reflex angle at \(C\) is \(v\). Find \(v\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Draw \(CF // AB // DE\) through \(C\).
Step 2. \(\angle BCF=180^\circ-u=180^\circ-40^\circ=140^\circ\) (interior angles, \(AB // CF\)).
Step 3. \(\angle DCF=w=25^\circ\) (alternate angles, \(CF // DE\)).
Step 4. \(v=\angle BCF+\angle DCF=140^\circ+25^\circ=165^\circ\).
Answer: \(165^\circ\)
