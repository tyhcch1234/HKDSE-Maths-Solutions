Step 1 — the two given conditions are set up.

2022 #2[3 marks]

Let $x$ and $y$ be two numbers. The sum of $x$ and $y$ is $456$ while the product of $7$ and $x$ is $y$. Find $x$.

Step-by-step Solution

1Translate both conditions into equations

“The sum of $x$ and $y$ is $456$”:   $x+y=456$   (1)

“The product of $7$ and $x$ is $y$”:   $7x=y$   (2)

Hint: write one equation for each sentence before doing any algebra.  1A
2Substitute (2) into (1)

Replace $y$ in equation (1) by $7x$:

$x+7x=456$

Hint: since (2) already gives $y$ in terms of $x$, substitution is the fastest method.  1M
3Solve the resulting linear equation

$x+7x=456$

$8x=456$

$x=\dfrac{456}{8}$

$x=57$

Hint: combine like terms first, then divide by the coefficient of $x$.  1A
4Check (optional but quick)

If $x=57$, then $y=7x=7\times 57=399$.

Sum check: $57+399=456$ ✓

Hint: a 5-second check protects your last mark.
ANSWER $x = 57$
