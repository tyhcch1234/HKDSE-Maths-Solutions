Let \(x\) and \(y\) be two numbers. The sum of \(x\) and \(y\) is 456 while the product of 7 and \(x\) is \(y\). Find \(x\). [3 marks]

Forming equations from words Substitution method Solving linear equations

Step-by-step Solution

1 Translate the word problem into equations
💡 Hint: The sum of \(x\) and \(y\) is 456 → \(x + y = 456\).
The product of 7 and \(x\) is \(y\) → \(7x = y\).

Equation (1): \(x + y = 456\)

Equation (2): \(7x = y\)

2 Substitute equation (2) into equation (1)
💡 Hint: Since \(y = 7x\), replace \(y\) in \(x + y = 456\) with \(7x\).
\[ \begin{aligned} x + \color{red}{y} &= 456 \\ x + \color{red}{7x} &= 456 \end{aligned} \]
3 Simplify and solve for \(x\)
💡 Hint: Combine like terms: \(x + 7x = 8x\). Then divide both sides by 8.

\[ x + 7x = 456 \]

\[ 8x = 456 \]

\[ x = \frac{456}{8} \]

\[ x = 57 \]

✅ Final answer: \(x = 57\)
