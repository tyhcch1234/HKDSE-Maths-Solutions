HKDSE 2026 · Paper 1 · Q13

Similar Solids: Volume & Surface Area

Question

A solid metal sphere of radius \(10\) cm is melted and recast into three similar solid right circular cylinders \(X\), \(Y\) and \(Z\). Let \(r_1\) cm, \(r_2\) cm and \(r_3\) cm be the base radii of \(X\), \(Y\) and \(Z\) respectively. It is given that \(r_1:r_2:r_3=2:3:5\).

(a)   Express the volume of \(Y\) in terms of \(\pi\). (3 marks)

(b)   A craftsman finds that the height of \(Y\) is \(9\) cm.

(i)  Find \(r_2\).

(ii)  The craftsman claims that the total surface area of \(Z\) is greater than the surface area of the sphere. Do you agree? Explain your answer. (5 marks)

Solution

STEP 1 — volume of the sphere
\[ V_{\text{sphere}}=\frac{4}{3}\pi(10)^3=\frac{4000}{3}\pi \text{ cm}^3 \]
PART (a) — split by similar solids
For similar solids, volumes are in the cube of the ratio of corresponding lengths: \[ V_X:V_Y:V_Z=2^3:3^3:5^3=8:27:125 \] Total parts \(=8+27+125=160\), so \[ V_Y=\frac{27}{160}\times\frac{4000}{3}\pi=225\pi \text{ cm}^3 \]
PART (b)(i) — use the cylinder volume
\[ V_Y=\pi r_2^2 h = \pi r_2^2(9)=225\pi \implies r_2^2=25 \implies r_2=5 \text{ cm} \]
PART (b)(ii) — dimensions of Z
The cylinders are similar with length ratio \(2:3:5\), so relative to Y: \[ r_3=r_2\times\frac{5}{3}=\frac{25}{3}\text{ cm}, \qquad h_Z=9\times\frac{5}{3}=15\text{ cm} \]
PART (b)(ii) — compare surface areas
Total surface area of \(Z\): \[ 2\pi r_3(r_3+h_Z)=2\pi\cdot\frac{25}{3}\left(\frac{25}{3}+15\right)=2\pi\cdot\frac{25}{3}\cdot\frac{70}{3}=\frac{3500\pi}{9}\approx 388.9\pi \] Surface area of the sphere: \[ 4\pi(10)^2=400\pi \] Since \(\dfrac{3500\pi}{9} < 400\pi\), the total surface area of \(Z\) is actually smaller.
(a) \(V_Y=225\pi\) cm³    (b)(i) \(r_2=5\) cm    (b)(ii) Disagree — \(TSA_Z=\frac{3500\pi}{9}\approx388.9\pi < 400\pi = S_{\text{sphere}}\).
