13. A solid metal sphere of radius \(10\) cm is melted and recast into three similar solid right circular cylinders \(X\), \(Y\) and \(Z\). Let \(r_1\) cm, \(r_2\) cm and \(r_3\) cm be the base radii of \(X\), \(Y\) and \(Z\) respectively. It is given that \(r_1:r_2:r_3=2:3:5\).

(a) Express the volume of \(Y\) in terms of \(\pi\). 3 marks

(b) A craftsman finds that the height of \(Y\) is \(9\) cm.

(i) Find \(r_2\). 2 marks

(ii) The craftsman claims that the total surface area of \(Z\) is greater than the surface area of the sphere. Do you agree? Explain your answer. 3 marks

Step 1 · Volume of the sphere
Volume of a Sphere \(V=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}\) With \(r=10\) the sphere holds \(\dfrac{4000}{3}\pi\text{ cm}^{3}\), and melting keeps this volume.
\[ V_{\text{sphere}}=\frac{4}{3}\pi(10)^3=\frac{4000}{3}\pi\ \text{cm}^3 \]

The whole sphere is recast, so the three cylinders' total volume equals the sphere's volume.

Step 2 · Split by similar solids (a)
Volume Ratio of Similar Solids \(\dfrac{V_{1}}{V_{2}}=k^{3}\) Heights in the ratio \(2:3:5\) give volumes \(8:27:125\), and \(Y\) takes \(27\) of the \(160\) parts.

For similar solids, volumes scale as the cube of the length ratio:

\[ V_X:V_Y:V_Z=2^3:3^3:5^3=8:27:125 \]

Total parts \(=8+27+125=160\), so

\[ V_Y=\frac{27}{160}\times\frac{4000}{3}\pi=225\pi\ \text{cm}^3 \]
Step 3 · Radius of Y (b)(i)
\[ V_Y=\pi r_2^2 h=\pi r_2^2(9)=225\pi \ \Rightarrow\ r_2^2=25 \ \Rightarrow\ r_2=5\ \text{cm} \]
Step 4 · Dimensions of Z (b)(ii)

Length ratio \(2:3:5\), so relative to \(Y\):

\[ r_3=r_2\times\frac{5}{3}=\frac{25}{3}\ \text{cm},\qquad h_Z=9\times\frac{5}{3}=15\ \text{cm} \]
Step 5 · Compare surface areas (b)(ii)
Total Surface Area of a Cylinder \(A=2\pi r(r+h)\) With \(r=\dfrac{25}{3}\) and \(h=15\), the total is \(\dfrac{3500\pi}{9}<400\pi\).
\[ \text{TSA}_Z=2\pi r_3(r_3+h_Z)=2\pi\cdot\frac{25}{3}\left(\frac{25}{3}+15\right)=\frac{3500\pi}{9}\approx 388.9\pi \]
\[ S_{\text{sphere}}=4\pi(10)^2=400\pi \]

Since \(\frac{3500\pi}{9} < 400\pi\), the surface area of \(Z\) is smaller.

(a) \(V_Y=225\pi\) cm³    (b)(i) \(r_2=5\) cm    (b)(ii) Disagree — \(\text{TSA}_Z=\frac{3500\pi}{9}\approx 388.9\pi < 400\pi\).

Question. A solid metal sphere of radius \(6\text{ cm}\) is melted and recast into three similar solid cylinders \(X\), \(Y\) and \(Z\) whose heights are in the ratio \(1:2:3\). (a) Find the volume of \(Y\) in terms of \(\pi\). (b) The height of \(Y\) is \(4\text{ cm}\); find its base radius. (c) Find the total surface area of \(Z\) and state whether it is smaller than the surface area of the sphere.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(V_{\text{sphere}}=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(6)^{3}=288\pi\text{ cm}^{3}\).
Step 2. Volumes are in the ratio \(1^{3}:2^{3}:3^{3}=1:8:27\), with \(36\) parts in total, so \(V_{Y}=\dfrac{8}{36}\times288\pi=64\pi\text{ cm}^{3}\).
Step 3. \(\pi r^{2}(4)=64\pi\), so \(r^{2}=16\) and \(r=4\text{ cm}\).
Step 4. From \(Y\) to \(Z\) the length factor is \(\dfrac{3}{2}\): \(r_{Z}=6\text{ cm}\) and \(h_{Z}=6\text{ cm}\).
Step 5. \(A_{Z}=2\pi(6)(6+6)=144\pi\), while \(4\pi(6)^{2}=144\pi\), so the two surface areas are equal, not smaller.
Answer: (a) \(64\pi\text{ cm}^{3}\) (b) \(4\text{ cm}\) (c) \(144\pi\), equal to the sphere
