Question

In Figure 1, \(OPQR\) is a quadrilateral such that \(OP = OQ = OR\). \(OQ\) and \(PR\) intersect at the point \(S\).

It is given that \(S\) is the mid-point of \(PR\).
  1. Prove that \(\Delta OPS \cong \Delta ORS\). [2 marks]
  2. It is given that \(O\) is the centre of the circle which passes through \(P\), \(Q\) and \(R\). If \(OQ = 6\text{ cm}\) and \(\angle PRQ = 10^\circ\), find the area of the sector \(OPQR\) in terms of \(\pi\). [4 marks]
STEP 1 · Set up the congruent triangles

In \(\Delta OPS\) and \(\Delta ORS\), list the three matching facts:

StatementReason
\(OP = OR\)given
\(PS = RS\)given (\(S\) is mid-point of \(PR\))
\(OS = OS\)common side
Mark the equal radii (blue) and the equal halves of \(PR\) (green) on the diagram.
STEP 2 · Conclude the proof (a)

Three pairs of corresponding sides are equal, so

\[\Delta OPS \cong \Delta ORS \quad (\text{SSS})\]

Since the triangles are congruent, \(\angle POQ = \angle QOR\) (i.e. \(OQ\) bisects \(\angle POR\)). This will be used in (b).

STEP 3 · Use the centre–chord angle property

\(O\) is the centre and \(Q\) lies on the circle between \(P\) and \(R\). The angle at the centre \(\angle POQ\) is twice the angle at the circumference \(\angle PRQ\):

\[\angle POQ = 2\angle PRQ = 2(10^\circ) = 20^\circ\]

The angle subtended at the centre is twice the angle subtended at the circumference on the same arc.
STEP 4 · Find the sector angle \(\angle POR\)

From (a), \(\angle QOR = \angle POQ = 20^\circ\), so

\[\angle POR = \angle POQ + \angle QOR = 20^\circ + 20^\circ = 40^\circ\]

So the sector \(OPQR\) spans \(40^\circ\) at the centre.

STEP 5 · Area of the sector

Radius \(= OQ = 6\) cm and sector angle \(= 40^\circ\):

\[\text{Area} = \frac{40}{360}\times \pi (6)^2\]

\[= \frac{1}{9}\times 36\pi = 4\pi \text{ cm}^2\]

Answer (b): \(4\pi \text{ cm}^2\)
