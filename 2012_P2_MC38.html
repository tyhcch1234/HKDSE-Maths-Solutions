Step 11M

Inside the bracket: \(x\mapsto x-2\)

Replacing \(x\) by \(x-2\) affects the graph horizontally.

\[ y=f(x-2) \]

A point \((a,\,f(a))\) on \(y=f(x)\) moves to \((a+2,\,f(a))\) on \(y=f(x-2)\).

Hint: A change inside the bracket works in the “opposite” direction — the graph moves right by 2 units (not left).
Step 21M

Outside the bracket: \(+1\)

Adding \(1\) outside the function affects the graph vertically.

\[ y=f(x-2)+1 \]

Every \(y\)-value is increased by \(1\), so the curve moves up by 1 unit.

Hint: A change outside the bracket works in the “natural” direction — up means up. Use the sliders to confirm \(h=2,\;k=1\).
Step 31A

Combine both translations

Do them one after another (order does not matter here):

\[ y=f(x) \;\xrightarrow{\text{right }2}\; y=f(x-2) \;\xrightarrow{\text{up }1}\; y=f(x-2)+1 \]

Answer: The graph of \(y=f(x-2)+1\) is the graph of \(y=f(x)\) translated right by 2 units and then up by 1 unit.

Step 11M

Shape is preserved

Translation never changes the shape or orientation of a curve — only its position.

Hint: Rule out any option where the two curves have different widths, or where one is flipped upside-down.
Step 21M

The image must be right and up

If \(V_0=(a,b)\) is a point on \(y=f(x)\), then on \(y=f(x-2)+1\) the corresponding point is

\[ V_1=(a+2,\;b+1). \]

So the whole image curve sits 2 units to the right and 1 unit above the original.

Check: the image curve is always shifted right & up relative to \(y=f(x)\).

★Summary of Final Answers

  • Transformation rule: \(y=f(x)\to y=f(x-2)+1\) means right 2 and up 1.
  • Point mapping: \((a,b)\to(a+2,\;b+1)\) for every point on the graph.
  • Shape: unchanged (translation is rigid).
  • Correct option: C — the image curve lies to the right of and above \(y=f(x)\).
