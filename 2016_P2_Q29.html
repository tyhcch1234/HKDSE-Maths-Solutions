HKDSE Math 2016 Paper 2 Q29 Probability Expected Value Multiple Choice

Expected Number of Tokens

Question

A bag contains 1 red ball, 3 yellow balls and 6 white balls. In a lucky draw, a ball is randomly drawn from the bag and a certain number of tokens will be got according to the following table:

Colour of the ball drawn Red Yellow White
Number of tokens got 90 20 10

Find the expected number of tokens got in the lucky draw.

A 10
B 21
C 40
D 61

Key Concept: Expected Value

The expected value (weighted average) is calculated by multiplying each outcome by its probability and summing the results: \[ E[X] = \sum ( \text{value} \times P(\text{value}) ) \] Here, the "value" is the number of tokens, and the probability comes from the proportion of balls of each colour.

Step-by-Step Solution

Add up all the balls to find the denominator for the probabilities:

Total balls = \(1 + 3 + 6 = 10\)

The probability of drawing a colour is the number of that colour divided by the total (10).

Red \( \frac{1}{10} \)
Yellow \( \frac{3}{10} \)
White \( \frac{6}{10} \)

Multiply each token value by its probability, then add them up:

\( E = 90 \times P(\text{Red}) + 20 \times P(\text{Yellow}) + 10 \times P(\text{White}) \)

Hint: \(P(\text{Red}) = \frac{1}{10}\), \(P(\text{Yellow}) = \frac{3}{10}\), \(P(\text{White}) = \frac{6}{10}\).

\( E = 90 \times \frac{1}{10} + 20 \times \frac{3}{10} + 10 \times \frac{6}{10} \)

\( E = 9 + 6 + 6 \)

\( E = 21 \)

The expected number of tokens is 21.

Therefore, the correct option is B.
