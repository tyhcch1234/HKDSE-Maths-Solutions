HKDSE 2026 · Paper 2 · MC23

Regular Hexagon

Question

It is given that \(ABCDEF\) is a regular hexagon. If \(AC\) and \(BF\) intersect at point \(G\), which of the following are true?

I. \(BG\parallel CE\)
II. \(\triangle ABG\sim\triangle BDC\)
III. \(\triangle AGF\cong\triangle BGC\)

  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III

Solution

Statement I — \(BG\parallel CE\)

In \(\triangle ABF\), \(AB=AF\) and \(\angle BAF=120^\circ\) (interior angle of a regular hexagon), so \(\angle ABF=30^\circ\). Hence

\[ \angle FBC=\angle ABC-\angle ABF=120^\circ-30^\circ=90^\circ \]

Similarly, in \(\triangle CDE\), \(CD=DE\) and \(\angle CDE=120^\circ\), so \(\angle DCE=30^\circ\) and \(\angle BCE=120^\circ-30^\circ=90^\circ\).

Both \(BF\) (hence \(BG\)) and \(CE\) are perpendicular to \(BC\), so \(BG\parallel CE\). I is true.

Statement II — \(\triangle ABG\sim\triangle BDC\)

\(\angle ABG=\angle ABF=30^\circ\). In \(\triangle ABC\), \(AB=BC\) and \(\angle ABC=120^\circ\), so \(\angle BAC=30^\circ\); since \(G\) lies on \(AC\), \(\angle BAG=30^\circ\). Thus \(\triangle ABG\) has angles \(30^\circ,30^\circ,120^\circ\).

In \(\triangle BDC\), \(BC=CD\) and \(\angle BCD=120^\circ\), so it also has angles \(30^\circ,30^\circ,120^\circ\).

Both triangles are isosceles with the same set of angles, hence similar. II is true.

Statement III — \(\triangle AGF\cong\triangle BGC\)

Let the side length be \(s\). Then \(AC=BF=\sqrt3\,s\) (short diagonals), and from \(\triangle ABG\)

\[ AG=BG=\frac{s}{\sqrt3} \]

so

\[ GF=BF-BG=\sqrt3\,s-\frac{s}{\sqrt3}=\frac{2s}{\sqrt3}, \qquad GC=AC-AG=\sqrt3\,s-\frac{s}{\sqrt3}=\frac{2s}{\sqrt3} \]

The sides of \(\triangle AGF\) are \(\left\{AG=\dfrac{s}{\sqrt3},\ GF=\dfrac{2s}{\sqrt3},\ AF=s\right\}\), and the sides of \(\triangle BGC\) are \(\left\{BG=\dfrac{s}{\sqrt3},\ GC=\dfrac{2s}{\sqrt3},\ BC=s\right\}\). All three pairs of sides are equal, so the triangles are congruent (SSS). III is true.

Final Answer: D
