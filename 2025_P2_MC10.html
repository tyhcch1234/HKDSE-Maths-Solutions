HKDSE Math 2025 Paper 2 · Q10

Compound Interest

Percentages · Compounded half‑yearly
#CompoundInterest #Percentage #Half-yearly #MC
A sum of $40 000 is deposited at an interest rate of 3% per annum for 5 years, compounded half‑yearly. Find the amount correct to the nearest dollar.
A $46 000
B $46 371
C $46 422
D $46 465

Step-by-step Solution

1
Identify the compound interest formula
▼

For compound interest, the amount \(A\) is given by:

\[ A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} \]

where \(P\) is the principal, \(r\) the annual interest rate (as a decimal), \(n\) the number of compounding periods per year, and \(t\) the time in years.

💡 Hint: Since the interest is compounded half‑yearly, \(n = 2\).
2
Substitute the values
▼

Principal \(P = 40\,000\), annual rate \(r = 3\% = 0.03\), time \(t = 5\) years, and \(n = 2\).

\[ A = 40\,000\left(1 + \frac{0.03}{2}\right)^{2 \times 5} \]
💡 Hint: The exponent is \(nt = 2 \times 5 = 10\) compounding periods.
3
Simplify the expression
▼

First evaluate the bracket and the exponent:

\[ \frac{0.03}{2} = 0.015 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 1 + 0.015 = 1.015 \]

So the expression becomes:

\[ A = 40\,000 \times (1.015)^{10} \]
💡 Hint: You may also write the rate as a fraction: \(\displaystyle \frac{3\%}{2} = \frac{1.5}{100} = 0.015\).
4
Compute the final amount
▼

Using a calculator:

\[ (1.015)^{10} \approx 1.160540825 \]
\[ A \approx 40\,000 \times 1.160540825 = 46\,421.633 \]

Rounding to the nearest dollar gives:

\[ A \approx \$46\,422 \]
💡 Hint: Make sure you do not round intermediate steps; keep full precision until the final rounding.
5
Select the correct option
▼

The calculated amount is $46 422.

Comparing with the given options:

A. $46 000   ✗
B. $46 371   ✗
C. $46 422   ✓
D. $46 465   ✗

Therefore the answer is C.
