2020MC#38

In the figure, \(ABCDEF\) is a right triangular prism. \(P\) is a point lying on \(AE\). If \(AB = AC = 12\) cm, \(AP = 9\) cm, \(EP = 5\) cm and \(BD = 2k\) cm, find the area of \(\triangle BDP\).

A. \(\sqrt{(k^2-1)(196-k^2)}\)
B. \(\sqrt{(k^2-1)(196+k^2)}\)
C. \(\sqrt{(k^2+1)(196-k^2)}\)
D. \(\sqrt{(k^2+1)(196+k^2)}\)
STEP 1 · Work inside the right-angled triangular face ABC
Pythagoras Theorem \(c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}\) In the right-angled face \(ABC\): \(BC^{2}=12^{2}+12^{2}=288\), so \(BC=12\sqrt{2}\).

The prism is right, so \(AB \perp AC\) in the base triangle \(ABC\).

\[BC^2 = AB^2 + AC^2 = 12^2+12^2 = 288\]

\[\Rightarrow BC = 12\sqrt{2}\ \text{cm}\]

STEP 2 · Find BP in \(\triangle ABP\)

The right prism gives \(AB \perp AE\),
so \(\angle BAP = 90^\circ\).

With \(AB=12\), \(AP=9\): \[BP = \sqrt{12^2+9^2} = \sqrt{144+81} = 15\ \text{cm}\]

STEP 3 · Find DP in \(\triangle DEP\)

Also \(DE \perp AE\) (right face) and \(DE = BC = 12\sqrt2\) … actually \(DE // BC\),
and more directly \(DE = AB = 12\) cm (corresponding edge), \(EP = 5\) cm.

\[DP = \sqrt{12^2+5^2} = \sqrt{144+25} = 13\ \text{cm}\]

STEP 4 · Sides of \(\triangle BDP\)
Semi-perimeter of a Triangle \(s=\dfrac{a+b+c}{2}\) With \(BD=2k\), \(BP=15\) and \(DP=13\), \(s=\dfrac{2k+28}{2}=k+14\).

Given \(BD = 2k\) cm, and from Steps 2–3: \[BP = 15,\quad DP = 13,\quad BD = 2k\]

Semi-perimeter: \[s = \frac{BD+BP+DP}{2} = \frac{2k+15+13}{2} = k+14\]

STEP 5 · Apply Heron's formula
Heron’s Formula \(A=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}\) Substituting \(s=k+14\) and the three sides gives \(\sqrt{(k^{2}-1)(196-k^{2})}\).

\[\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s-BD)(s-BP)(s-DP)}\]

\[= \sqrt{(k+14)\big[(k+14)-2k\big]\big[(k+14)-15\big]\big[(k+14)-13\big]}\]

\[= \sqrt{(k+14)(14-k)(k-1)(k+1)}\]

\[= \sqrt{(k^2-1)(196-k^2)}\ \text{cm}^2\]

✅ Correct option: A   \(\sqrt{(k^2-1)(196-k^2)}\ \text{cm}^2\)

Question. In the figure, \(ABCDEFGH\) is a right prism with \(AB=AC=24\text{ cm}\), \(AP=18\text{ cm}\) and \(EP=10\text{ cm}\). It is given that \(BD=2k\text{ cm}\). Find the area of \(\triangle BDP\) in terms of \(k\). A. \(\sqrt{(k^{2}-4)(784-k^{2})}\ \text{cm}^{2}\) B. \(\sqrt{(k^{2}-2)(784-k^{2})}\ \text{cm}^{2}\) C. \(\sqrt{(k^{2}-4)(676-k^{2})}\ \text{cm}^{2}\) D. \(\sqrt{(k^{2}-16)(784-k^{2})}\ \text{cm}^{2}\) [1 mark]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(BP\): the right prism gives \(\angle BAP=90^{\circ}\), so \(BP=\sqrt{24^{2}+18^{2}}=\sqrt{576+324}=30\text{ cm}\).
Step 2. \(DP\): in the right face with \(DE=AB=24\) and \(EP=10\), \(DP=\sqrt{24^{2}+10^{2}}=\sqrt{576+100}=26\text{ cm}\).
Step 3. The sides of \(\triangle BDP\) are \(2k\), \(30\), \(26\), so \(s=\dfrac{2k+56}{2}=k+28\).
Step 4. Heron: area \(=\sqrt{(k+28)(28-k)(k-2)(k+2)}=\sqrt{(784-k^{2})(k^{2}-4)}\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Answer: A. \(\sqrt{(k^{2}-4)(784-k^{2})}\ \text{cm}^{2}\)
💡 Hints: Right prism ⇒ lateral edges ⟂ base, and \(AB \perp AC\) at the base. Use Pythagoras twice (Steps 2–3) to get \(BP\) and \(DP\), then a single Heron's formula. Remember \(s-BD = 14-k\) and \(s-DP = k+1\), which multiply to give the \((k^2-1)(196-k^2)\) pattern.
