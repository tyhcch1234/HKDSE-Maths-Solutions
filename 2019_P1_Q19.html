Functions and Graphs

Functions and GraphsPassing rate 10%Difficulty · <50%12 marks

Question

Let \(f(x)=\dfrac{1}{1+k}\big(x^2+(6k-2)x+(9k+25)\big)\), where \(k\) is a positive constant. Denote the point \((4,33)\) by \(F\).

(a) Prove that the graph of \(y=f(x)\) passes through \(F\). 1 mark

(b) The graph of \(y=g(x)\) is obtained by reflecting the graph of \(y=f(x)\) with respect to the \(y\)-axis and then translating the resulting graph upwards by 4 units. Let \(U\) be the vertex of the graph of \(y=g(x)\). Denote the origin by \(O\). (i) Using the method of completing the square, express the coordinates of \(U\) in terms of \(k\). (ii) Find \(k\) such that the area of the circle passing through \(F,O\) and \(U\) is the least. (iii) For any positive constant \(k\), the graph of \(y=g(x)\) passes through the same point \(G\). Let \(V\) be the vertex of the graph of \(y=g(x)\) such that the area of the circle passing through \(F,O\) and \(V\) is the least. Are \(F,G,O\) and \(V\) concyclic? Explain your answer. 11 marks

Solution

a(a) Point on graph
\[f(4)=\frac{1}{1+k}\big(4^2+4(6k-2)+(9k+25)\big)=\frac{1}{1+k}(33+33k)=33\]

1A correct.

Thus the graph of \(y=f(x)\) passes through \(F\).

b(b)(i) Vertex U
\[g(x)=f(-x)+4\]

1M g(x).

\[=\frac{1}{1+k}\big(x^2-(6k-2)x+(9k+25)\big)+4\]
\[=\frac{1}{1+k}\Big((x-(3k-1))^2-(3k-1)^2+(9k+25)\Big)+4\]

1M completing square.

\[=\frac{1}{k+1}\Big((x-(3k-1))^2-(k+1)(9k-24)\Big)+4\]

1M simplifying.

\[=\frac{1}{k+1}(x-(3k-1))^2+(28-9k)\]

The coordinates of \(U\) are \((3k-1,\ 28-9k)\).

1A correct.

b(b)(ii) Least area

The area is least when \(FO\) is a diameter of the circle, so \(\angle FUO=90^\circ\).

1M FO is diameter.

\[\frac{(28-9k)-0}{(3k-1)-0}\cdot\frac{33-(28-9k)}{4-(3k-1)}=-1\]

1M product = −1.1A correct.

\[\frac{(28-9k)(5+9k)}{(3k-1)(5-3k)}=-1\ \Rightarrow\ 2k^2-5k-3=0\]
\[k=3\ \text{or}\ k=-\tfrac12\ (\text{rejected})\]

1A k=3.

b(b)(iii) Concyclic?

Taking \(x=-4\) gives \(g(-4)=37\) for all \(k\), so \(G=(-4,37)\).

1A coordinates of G.

\[\frac{37-33}{-4-4}\cdot\frac{37-0}{-4-0}=\frac{37}{8}\neq-1\]

1M product of slopes.

So \(\angle FGO\neq90^\circ\). Since \(\angle FVO=90^\circ\), \(G\) does not lie on the circle through \(F,O\) and \(V\).

Hence \(F,G,O\) and \(V\) are not concyclic.

1A correct.

Answer

Answer(b)(i) \((3k-1,28-9k)\) · (ii) \(k=3\) · (iii) not concyclic
