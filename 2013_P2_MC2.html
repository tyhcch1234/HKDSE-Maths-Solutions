Answer: D

The correct expression is \[ y = \frac{c+d}{\,d-c\,}\,. \] This matches option D.

Common trap! Students often stop at \(dy - cy = c + d\) and forget to factorise the \(y\). Remember: \(dy - cy = (d-c)\,y\), not \(d - c\) alone. Also watch the sign: the denominator is \(d - c\), not \(c - d\).
