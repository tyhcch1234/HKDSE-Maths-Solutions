On the same rectangular coordinate system, the graph of \(y=\log_a x\) cuts the graph of \(y=a^x\) and the \(x\)-axis at the points \(P\) and \(Q\) respectively, where \(a\) is a positive constant. Denote the origin by \(O\). Which of the following are true?

I. \(a < 1\)

II. \(OQ > a\)

III. \(\angle POQ = 45^\circ\)

A. I and II only
B. I and III only
C. II and III only
D. I, II and III

Correct Answer

D. I, II and III

Key Concepts Recap

  • • For \(a>1\), \(y=\log_a x\) and \(y=a^x\) do not intersect.
  • • For \(0<a<1\), they intersect once.
  • • \(y=\log_a x\) and \(y=a^x\) are reflections of each other across \(y=x\).
  • • \(\log_a x = 0 \implies x = 1\) (since \(x>0\)).
  • • The line \(y=x\) makes a \(45^\circ\) angle with the \(x\)-axis.
