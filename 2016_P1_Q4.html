Factorize
  1. $5m-10n$,
  2. $m^{2}+mn-6n^{2}$,
  3. $m^{2}+mn-6n^{2}-5m+10n$.  [4 marks]
1(a) TAKE OUT THE COMMON FACTOR
Common Factor Law\(AB+AC=A(B+C)\)\(5m-10n\) has HCF \(5\), giving \(5(m-2n)\).
$5m-10n$
Both terms share the factor $5$, and the variables $m,n$ differ:
$=5(m-2n)$  1A
Hint: look for the HCF of the numerical coefficients first, then the common variable parts.
2(b) TEST THE CROSS TERM
Cross Method of Factorisation\((x+p)(x+q)=x^{2}+(p+q)x+pq\)With \(p+q=1\) and \(pq=-6\), the pair \(3\) and \(-2\) gives \(m^{2}+mn-6n^{2}\).
Try $(m+an)(m+bn)$ with $a+b=1$ and $ab=-6$.
$a=3,\;b=-2$ gives $3+(-2)=1$ ✓ and $3\times(-2)=-6$ ✓
Check: $(m+3n)(m-2n)=m^{2}-2mn+3mn-6n^{2}=m^{2}+mn-6n^{2}$ ✓
$m^{2}+mn-6n^{2}=$ $(m+3n)(m-2n)$  1A
Hint: split the middle term $+mn$ into $+3mn-2mn$ (factor by grouping).
3(c) SPOT THE HIDDEN FACTOR
Factorisation by Grouping\(A\cdot B-5A=A(B-5)\)The bracket \((m-2n)\) is common to \((m+3n)(m-2n)\) and \(-5(m-2n)\).
$m^{2}+mn-6n^{2}-5m+10n$
Group the last two terms:
$-5m+10n=-(5m-10n)=\text{—}$ see (a)
$=m^{2}+mn-6n^{2}-(5m-10n)$
$=\underbrace{(m+3n)}_{\text{(b)}}(m-2n)-5(m-2n)$  1M
Hint: part (c) is built from parts (a) and (b) — the bracket $(m-2n)$ is common to both.
4FINAL FACTORIZATION
$=(m-2n)(m+3n)-5(m-2n)$
Take out the common factor $(m-2n)$ from each term:
$=(m-2n)(m+3n-5)$  1A
Hint: $A\cdot B-5A=A(B-5)$ with $A=(m-2n)$.
Answer: (a)$\;5(m-2n)$  ·  (b)$\;(m+3n)(m-2n)$  ·  (c)$\;(m-2n)(m+3n-5)$

Question. Factorize (a) \(4p+12q\), (b) \(p^{2}-2pq-15q^{2}\), (c) \(p^{2}-2pq-15q^{2}-4p-12q\). [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. (a) The HCF of \(4p\) and \(12q\) is \(4\): \(4p+12q=4(p+3q)\).
Step 2. (b) Try \((p+aq)(p+bq)\) with \(a+b=-2\) and \(ab=-15\). \(a=-5\), \(b=3\) gives \(-5+3=-2\) ✓ and \((-5)(3)=-15\) ✓, so \(p^{2}-2pq-15q^{2}=(p-5q)(p+3q)\).
Step 3. (c) Group the last two terms: \(-4p-12q=-4(p+3q)=-(4p+12q)\) from part (a), so \(p^{2}-2pq-15q^{2}-4p-12q=(p-5q)(p+3q)-4(p+3q)\).
Step 4. Take out the common factor \((p+3q)\): \((p+3q)(p-5q-4)\).
Answer: (a) \(4(p+3q)\) (b) \((p-5q)(p+3q)\) (c) \((p+3q)(p-5q-4)\)
