Discount & Profit Percentage

Question:

A wallet is sold at a discount of 25% on its marked price. The selling price of the wallet is $690.

(a) Find the marked price of the wallet.

(b) After selling the wallet, the percentage profit is 15%. Find the cost of the wallet. [4 marks]

🧩 Step-by-Step Solution

Let \(m\) be the marked price of the wallet.

A discount of 25% means the selling price is 75% of the marked price.

\[ (1 - 25\%)\,m = 690 \]
💡 Hint: \(1 - 25\% = 0.75\). So the equation is \(0.75m = 690\).

Divide both sides by \(0.75\):

\[ m = \frac{690}{0.75} = 920 \]

✅ \(\therefore\) The marked price is $920.

Let \(c\) be the cost of the wallet.

A profit of 15% means the selling price is 115% of the cost.

\[ (1 + 15\%)\,c = 690 \]
💡 Hint: \(1 + 15\% = 1.15\). So the equation is \(1.15c = 690\).

Divide both sides by \(1.15\):

\[ c = \frac{690}{1.15} = 600 \]

✅ \(\therefore\) The cost of the wallet is $600.

🎯 Final Answers

Part (a) – Marked price

$920

Part (b) – Cost price

$600

Always define variables clearly and write the correct percentage multiplier:
discount 25% → multiply by 0.75; profit 15% → multiply by 1.15.
