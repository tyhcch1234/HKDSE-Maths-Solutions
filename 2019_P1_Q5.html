[2019#5] Wallet Discount & Profit

A wallet is sold at a discount of $25\%$ on its marked price. The selling price of the wallet is $\$690$.

(a) Find the marked price of the wallet.

(b) After selling the wallet, the percentage profit is $15\%$. Find the cost of the wallet. [4 marks]

1(a) Set up the equation
Discount \(\text{selling price}=(1-d)\times\text{marked price}\) A \(25\%\) discount means the wallet is sold at \(75\%\) of the marked price, so \(0.75m=690\).

Let $\$m$ be the marked price of the wallet.

The selling price equals the marked price reduced by $25\%$:

$$(1-25\%)\,m = 690$$

Key idea: a $25\%$ discount means you pay $75\%$ of the marked price.

2Solve for $m$

$$0.75\,m = 690$$

$$m=\frac{690}{0.75}$$

$$m = 920$$

Divide both sides by $0.75$. Since $0.75<1$, the marked price is larger than $\$690$ — makes sense for a discount.

3Conclusion for (a)

Thus, the marked price of the wallet is $\$920$.

Check: $25\%$ of $920 = 230$, and $920-230 = 690$. ✔

4(b) Set up the profit equation
Percentage Profit \(\text{selling price}=(1+p)\times\text{cost}\) A \(15\%\) profit makes the selling price \(115\%\) of the cost, so \(1.15c=690\).

Let $\$c$ be the cost of the wallet.

A $15\%$ profit means the selling price is $115\%$ of the cost:

$$(1+15\%)\,c = 690$$

Selling price $=$ cost $\times (1+$ profit $\%)$.

5Solve for $c$

$$1.15\,c = 690$$

$$c=\frac{690}{1.15}=600$$

Here $1.15>1$, so the cost is less than the selling price — there is a profit.

6Conclusion for (b)

Thus, the cost of the wallet is $\$600$.

Check: profit $=690-600=90$, and $\dfrac{90}{600}=15\%$. ✔

Answer: (a) marked price $=\$920$  ·  (b) cost $=\$600$

Question. A wallet is sold at a discount of \(20\%\) on its marked price. The selling price of the wallet is \(\$720\).
(a) Find the marked price of the wallet.
(b) After selling the wallet, the percentage profit is \(20\%\). Find the cost of the wallet. [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. (a) Let \(\$m\) be the marked price. A \(20\%\) discount means the selling price is \(80\%\) of it: \(0.8m=720\).
Step 2. \(m=\dfrac{720}{0.8}=900\), so the marked price is \(\$900\).
Step 3. (b) A \(20\%\) profit means the selling price is \(120\%\) of the cost: \(1.2c=720\).
Step 4. \(c=\dfrac{720}{1.2}=600\), so the cost is \(\$600\). Check: profit \(=720-600=120\) and \(\dfrac{120}{600}=20\%\) ✓.
Answer: (a) \(\$900\)   (b) \(\$600\)
