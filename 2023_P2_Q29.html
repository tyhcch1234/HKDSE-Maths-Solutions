The box-and-whisker diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of training hours of some engineers in a year. Find the upper quartile of the distribution.

A.20
B.40
C.60
D.70
1

Read the five-number summary from the diagram

A box-and-whisker diagram displays five key values: the minimum, the lower quartile $Q_1$, the median $Q_2$, the upper quartile $Q_3$, and the maximum.

From the given diagram:

Minimum $=10$,   $Q_1=20$,   $Q_2=30$,   $Q_3=60$,   Maximum $=80$.

💡 Hint: the right edge of the box is the upper quartile $Q_3$; the left edge is the lower quartile $Q_1$.
Five-Number Summary \(\text{min},\ Q_{1},\ Q_{2},\ Q_{3},\ \text{max}\) A box-and-whisker diagram marks the minimum, the three quartiles and the maximum.
2

Identify the upper quartile

The upper quartile $Q_3$ corresponds to the vertical line forming the right side of the rectangular box.

Looking at the diagram, this right edge aligns with the mark for $60$ on the number line.

Therefore, the upper quartile is:

$$Q_3 = 60$$

💡 Hint: the whiskers extend from the box to the minimum (left) and maximum (right); the box itself represents the interquartile range $Q_1$ to $Q_3$.
3

Check the interquartile range (optional verification)

Interquartile range $= Q_3 - Q_1 = 60 - 20 = 40$.

The median $Q_2 = 30$ lies within the box $[20,60]$, which is consistent with a valid box-and-whisker diagram.

💡 Hint: a quick consistency check — the median must lie between $Q_1$ and $Q_3$.
Interquartile Range \(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) The width of the box is the interquartile range, a quick check that the reading is sensible.
4

Select the correct option

The upper quartile is $60$, which matches option C.

💡 Hint: don't confuse the upper quartile (60) with the maximum (80) or the lower quartile (20).
Answer: C  (upper quartile $= 60$)

Question. The box-and-whisker diagram of a set of data shows the minimum \(12\), the lower quartile \(24\), the median \(35\), the upper quartile \(48\) and the maximum \(70\). Find the interquartile range and the range of the data.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Read the five-number summary: minimum \(=12\), \(Q_{1}=24\), \(Q_{2}=35\), \(Q_{3}=48\), maximum \(=70\).
Step 2. Interquartile range \(=Q_{3}-Q_{1}=48-24=24\).
Step 3. Range \(=\text{maximum}-\text{minimum}=70-12=58\).
Step 4. Check: the median \(35\) lies between \(Q_{1}=24\) and \(Q_{3}=48\), so the diagram is consistent ✓
Answer: Interquartile range \(=24\); range \(=58\)
