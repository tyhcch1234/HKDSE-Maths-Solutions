HKDSE 2019 Paper 2 · Q11

1. Percentages

[2019MC#11] A sum of $\$65000$ is deposited at an interest rate of $7\%$ per annum for $8$ years, compounded quarterly. Find the amount correct to the nearest dollar.

  • A. $\$101\,400$
  • B. $\$111\,682$
  • C. $\$113\,244$
  • D. $\$113\,609$

Step-by-step solution

1Identify the given values

Principal \(P = 65000\),
nominal annual interest rate \(r = 7\% = 0.07\),
compounding frequency \(m = 4\) (quarterly),
time \(t = 8\) years.

Hint: “Compounded quarterly” means interest is added 4 times each year.
2Find the rate per period and the number of periods

Rate per quarter \(= \dfrac{7\%}{4} = 1.75\%\),
Number of periods \(n = 8 \times 4 = 32\).

Hint: Always divide the annual rate and multiply the years by the compounding frequency.
3Apply the compound interest formula

\[ \text{Amount} = 65000 \times \left(1 + 7\% \div 4\right)^{8 \times 4} \] \[ = 65000 \times (1.0175)^{32} \] \[ = 113243.8770\ldots \]

Hint: \(\text{Amount} = P\left(1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right)^{mt}\).
Compound Interest Formula \(A=P\left(1+\dfrac{r}{m}\right)^{mt}\) \(65000(1.0175)^{32}\approx 113243.88\) for \(7\%\) per annum compounded quarterly over \(8\) years.
4Round to the nearest dollar

\[ \text{Amount} \approx \$113244 \quad (\text{cor. to the nearest dollar}) \]

Hint: Compare with the options — C matches.
Rounding to the Nearest Dollar \(113243.877\ldots \to 113244\) The digit after the units place is \(8\), so \(\$113243.88\) rounds up to \(\$113244\).
Answer: C · $\$113\,244$

Question. A sum of \(\$48\,000\) is deposited at \(6\%\) per annum for \(5\) years, compounded half-yearly. Find the amount correct to the nearest dollar.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Rate per half-year \(=\dfrac{6\%}{2}=3\%\), and the number of periods is \(5\times 2=10\).
Step 2. Amount \(=48000\left(1+\dfrac{6\%}{2}\right)^{5\times 2}=48000(1.03)^{10}\).
Step 3. \(48000(1.03)^{10}=64507.99\ldots\)
Step 4. Correct to the nearest dollar, the amount is \(\$64\,508\).
Answer: \(\$64\,508\)
