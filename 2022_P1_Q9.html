Question 9

The frequency distribution table and the cumulative frequency distribution table below show the distribution of the times taken to complete a 3 km race by a group of students.

Time taken (minutes)Frequency
10 – 14$a$
15 – 19$9$
20 – 24$b$
25 – 29$3$
Time taken less than (minutes)Cumulative frequency
14.5$3$
19.5$x$
24.5$y$
29.5$20$

(a) Write down the value of $x$.
(b) Find the mean of the distribution.
(c) Find the probability that the time taken to complete the 3 km race by a randomly selected student from the group is less than 19.5 minutes.  [5 marks]

1Part (a) — Read the cumulative frequency at 14.5
From the cumulative frequency table, the time less than 14.5 minutes corresponds to the first class $(10\!-\!14)$ only. So \[ x = 3 . \]
Hint: "less than 14.5" means only the first class has been counted.
2Part (b) — Find $a$ and $b$ from the totals
The total number of students is the last cumulative frequency: \[ a+9+b+3 = 20 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a+b = 8. \] Also, the cumulative frequency at 24.5 is $y = a+9+b = 20-3 = 17$. Using the frequency table, the class $(20\!-\!24)$ contributes $b$, and $y = $ cum. freq. at $19.5$ plus $b$. Since $x$ is the cum. freq. at $19.5$: \[ x = a + 9, \qquad y = x + b = 17 . \] But from the first table the class boundaries tell us: \[ \text{at }14.5: \; 3 \qquad \text{at }19.5: \; x = 3+9 = 12 . \] Hence $y = 12 + b = 17 \Rightarrow b = 5$, and then \[ a = 20 - 9 - 5 - 3 = 3 . \] So $\;a = 3,\; b = 5$.
Hint: cumulative frequency at 19.5 = (first class) + (second class) = $3+9$.
3Part (b) — Compute the mean (class mid-points)
Use the mid-point of each class: \[ \begin{aligned} \text{mean} &= \frac{12(3) + 17(9) + 22(5) + 27(3)}{20} \\[4pt] &= \frac{36 + 153 + 110 + 81}{20} \\[4pt] &= \frac{380}{20} = 19 . \end{aligned} \] The mean time is 19 minutes.
Hint: multiply each class mid-point by its frequency, then divide by the total (20).
4Part (c) — Probability for time < 19.5 minutes
From the cumulative frequency table, the number of students taking less than 19.5 minutes is \[ x = 12 . \] Total number of students $= 20$. Therefore \[ P(\text{time} < 19.5) = \frac{12}{20} = \frac{3}{5} . \]
Hint: probability = (favourable count) ÷ (total count). Simplify the fraction.
Answer:
(a) $x = 3$
(b) Mean $= 19$ minutes
(c) $\dfrac{12}{20} = \dfrac{3}{5}$
