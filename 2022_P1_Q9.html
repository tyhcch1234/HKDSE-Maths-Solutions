(a) Write down the value of \(x\).

(b) Find the mean of the distribution.

(c) Find the probability that the time taken to complete the 3 km race by a randomly selected student from the group is less than 19.5 minutes.

a Part (a) — Finding \(x\) 1A

Step 11M

In a cumulative frequency table, the entry at \(19.5\) minutes counts all students whose time is less than \(19.5\) minutes.

The class \(10-14\) has frequency \(3\) and the class \(15-19\) has frequency \(9\).

Reading the second table directly: the cumulative frequency at \(19.5\) is exactly the "less than \(19.5\)" count, which equals the sum of the first two class frequencies.
Step 21A

So

\[ x = 3 + 9 = 12 \]
\(x = 12\)
0 / 2

b Part (b) — Finding the mean 2 marks

Step 11M

From part (a), \(x=12\). Also the total number of students is \(20\), so the cumulative frequency at \(24.5\) is

\[ y = 12 + b \quad\text{and}\quad y + 3 = 20 \implies y = 17 \implies b = 5. \]

Also \(a+b+9+3 = 20\) gives \(a+5+9+3=20\), so \(a=3\). Hence

\[ a=3,\qquad b=5. \]
The last cumulative frequency is the total number of students = \(20\). Use it to recover the missing frequencies \(a\) and \(b\).
Step 21M

Use the class mid-points: \(12\), \(17\), \(22\), \(27\).

\[ \text{Mean} = \frac{12(3)+17(9)+22(5)+27(3)}{20} \]
For grouped data, the sum of all times is approximated by (mid-point) × (frequency) summed over all classes, then divided by the total frequency.
Step 31A
\[ \text{Mean} = \frac{36+153+110+81}{20} = \frac{380}{20} = 19 \]
The mean is \(19\) minutes.
0 / 3

c Part (c) — Required probability 2 marks

Step 11M

Favourable cases: students who took less than \(19.5\) minutes. From the cumulative frequency table (or part (a)), this count is \(12\).

\[ \text{Required probability} = \frac{12}{20} \]
Probability = (number of favourable outcomes) ÷ (total number of students). The total is \(20\).
Step 21A
\[ \frac{12}{20} = \frac{3}{5} \]
The required probability is \(\dfrac{3}{5}\).
0 / 2

📋 Summary of Answers

  • (a) \(x = 12\)
  • (b) Mean \(= 19\) minutes (with \(a=3,\; b=5\))
  • (c) Required probability \(= \dfrac{3}{5}\)
