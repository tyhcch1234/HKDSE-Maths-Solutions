2019MC#1

Simplify  $(a-b)(a^2+ab-b^2)$.

  • A. $(a-b)^3$
  • B. $a^3-b^3$
  • C. $a^3-2ab^2+b^3$
  • D. $a^3-2a^2b+2ab^2+b^3$
Step 1 — Expand the product
Multiply the first factor $a$ and $-b$ into the second bracket separately: $$(a-b)(a^2+ab-b^2)=a(a^2+ab-b^2)-b(a^2+ab-b^2)$$
Distributive law: split the two-term factor across the bracket.
Step 2 — Distribute term by term
$$= a^3+a^2b-ab^2 \;-\; a^2b-ab^2+b^3$$
Watch signs: $-b\cdot(-b^2)=+b^3$.
Step 3 — Combine like terms
The $+a^2b$ and $-a^2b$ cancel. The two $-ab^2$ terms add: $$a^3 - 2ab^2 + b^3$$
Only $a^2b$ cancels completely — no $a^2b$ remains in the answer.
Step 4 — Check against the options
The result $a^3-2ab^2+b^3$ matches option C.
Option D keeps $-2a^2b$ and $+2ab^2$: wrong — those terms cancel.
Answer: C   —   $a^3-2ab^2+b^3$
