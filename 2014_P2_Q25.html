HKDSE 2014 · Paper 2 · Q25 · Coordinate Geometry

In the figure, the two straight lines intersect at a point on the positive \(y\)-axis. Which of the following are true?

  • I. \(a<0\)
  • II. \(c>0\)
  • III. \(b=d\)
  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III
Step 0 · Read the figure

Line \(ax+by=1\) rises from lower-left to upper-right. Line \(cx+dy=1\) falls from upper-left to lower-right. They meet at one point \(P\) on the positive \(y\)-axis.

\[ \text{intercepts: } x=\tfrac{1}{a},\ \tfrac{1}{c}\quad\text{and}\quad y=\tfrac{1}{b},\ \tfrac{1}{d}. \]

Step 1 · Statement I · \(a<0\)

The line \(ax+by=1\) cuts the \(x\)-axis on the negative side, so its \(x\)-intercept is negative:

\[ \frac{1}{a}<0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a<0 \quad(\text{since }a^2>0). \]

I is TRUE

Step 2 · Statement II · \(c>0\)

The line \(cx+dy=1\) cuts the \(x\)-axis on the positive side, so its \(x\)-intercept is positive:

\[ \frac{1}{c}>0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad c>0 \quad(\text{since }c^2>0). \]

II is TRUE

Step 3 · Statement III · \(b=d\)

Both lines pass through the same point on the \(y\)-axis, so their \(y\)-intercepts are equal:

\[ \frac{1}{b}=\frac{1}{d}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad b=d. \]

III is TRUE

Step 4 · Conclusion

All three statements I, II and III are true.

\[ a<0,\quad c>0,\quad b=d. \]

Answer

D — I, II and III.
