HKDSE Math 2022 Paper 2 Q4

Solve the Equation

Topic: Equations & Factorization

QUESTION
Let \(c\) be a constant. Solve the equation \[ (x - c)(x - 4c) = (3c - x)(x - 4c) \]
A. \(x = c\)
B. \(x = 2c\)
C. \(x = c\) or \(x = 2c\)
D. \(x = 2c\) or \(x = 4c\)
Factorization Quadratic Equations Zero Product Property Common Factor

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Write down the given equation

\[ (x - c)(x - 4c) = (3c - x)(x - 4c) \]
Concept: This is a quadratic equation in terms of \(x\) with a constant \(c\). Notice that \((x - 4c)\) appears on both sides — this is a common factor.
2

Bring all terms to one side

Move the right-hand side to the left: \[ (x - c)(x - 4c) - (3c - x)(x - 4c) = 0 \]
Why? To solve a quadratic, we want the equation in the form ... = 0 so we can factorize and use the Zero Product Property.
3

Factor out the common factor \((x - 4c)\)

\[ [(x - c) - (3c - x)](x - 4c) = 0 \]
Factorisation: Both terms have \((x - 4c)\) as a factor. We take it out. The remaining factor is \([(x-c) - (3c - x)]\).
4

Simplify the expression inside brackets

\[ (x - c - 3c + x)(x - 4c) = 0 \] \[ (2x - 4c)(x - 4c) = 0 \]
Careful with signs: \(-(3c - x) = -3c + x\). So \(x - c - 3c + x = 2x - 4c\).
5

Factor out 2 and apply Zero Product Property

\[ 2(x - 2c)(x - 4c) = 0 \] For the product to be zero, one of the factors must be zero: \[ x - 2c = 0 \quad \text{or} \quad x - 4c = 0 \]
Zero Product Property: If \(A \times B = 0\), then \(A = 0\) or \(B = 0\). Here, \(2\) is a non-zero constant, so we ignore it.
6

Solve for \(x\)

\[ x = 2c \quad \text{or} \quad x = 4c \] Therefore, the correct answer is D.

Key Takeaway

When both sides of an equation share a common algebraic factor, bring everything to one side and factor out the common term. Then use the Zero Product Property to find the solutions. In this problem, the common factor was \((x - 4c)\), giving solutions \(x = 2c\) and \(x = 4c\).
