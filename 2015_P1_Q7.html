Question: The number of apples owned by Ada is 4 times that owned by Billy. If Ada gives 12 of her apples to Billy, they will have the same number of apples. Find the total number of apples owned by Ada and Billy.

Step-by-step solution

0 / 5
1 Let x be the number of apples owned by Billy

We introduce a variable to represent the unknown.

Let \( x \) = number of apples owned by Billy.

Since Ada owns 4 times as many apples as Billy, Ada has:

Number of apples owned by Ada = \( 4x \).
Hint: “4 times” means multiply by 4. Billy is the reference quantity, so we call it \(x\).
2 Ada gives 12 apples to Billy

After giving 12 apples:

  • Ada's new amount = \(4x - 12\)
  • Billy's new amount = \(x + 12\)
Hint: When Ada gives away apples, subtract 12 from her amount. Billy receives 12, so add 12 to his amount.
3 Set up the equation (they now have equal amounts)

After the transfer, the number of apples is the same:

\( 4x - 12 = x + 12 \)
Key idea: “they will have the same number” → we set the two expressions equal.

This is the 1A + 1M step in the marking scheme.

4 Solve the equation
\( 4x - 12 = x + 12 \)
\( 4x - x = 12 + 12 \)
\( 3x = 24 \)
\( x = 8 \)
Hint: Collect like terms: subtract \(x\) from both sides, add 12 to both sides. Then divide both sides by 3.

So Billy has 8 apples, Ada has \(4 \times 8 = 32\) apples.

5 Find the total number of apples

Total apples = Ada's + Billy's

Total = \( 32 + 8 = 40 \)
Answer: 40 apples
Check: After transfer: Ada \(32 - 12 = 20\), Billy \(8 + 12 = 20\). They match ✓

This final answer receives the last 1A mark.
