HKDSE 2015 Paper 1 — Question 7 [4 marks]

The number of apples owned by Ada is 4 times that owned by Billy. If Ada gives 12 of her apples to Billy, they will have the same number of apples. Find the total number of apples owned by Ada and Billy.

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1Define the variable.

Let $x$ be the number of apples owned by Billy.
Then Ada owns $4x$ apples (since Ada has 4 times Billy's).

Hint: always name the smaller/unknown quantity as \(x\) first, then express the other in terms of \(x\).

Representing Change Algebraically \(4x-12 \quad\text{and}\quad x+12\) Ada gives \(12\) away and Billy receives \(12\), so both totals change at the same time.
STEP 2Translate "Ada gives 12 to Billy".

Ada after giving away 12: $\;4x-12$
Billy after receiving 12: $\;x+12$

Hint: giving 12 away subtracts, receiving 12 adds — and both totals change together.

Forming a Linear Equation \(4x-12=x+12\) "They will have the same number" means the two expressions are equal.
STEP 3Set up the equation (they are equal).

$$4x-12 = x+12$$

Hint: "they will have the same number" means the two expressions are equal.

Solving a Linear Equation in One Unknown \(ax+b=cx+d \Rightarrow (a-c)x=d-b\) With \(4x-12=x+12\), subtracting \(x\) and adding \(12\) gives \(3x=24\).
STEP 4Solve for $x$.

\(4x - 12 = x + 12\)
\(4x - x = 12 + 12\)
\(3x = 24\)
\(x = 8\)

Hint: collect \(x\)-terms on one side, numbers on the other. So Billy has 8 apples.

STEP 5Find the total.

Ada: $4x = 4(8) = 32$ apples.
Total: $32 + 8 = 40$ apples.

Hint: check — after the swap: $32-12=20$ and $8+12=20$. ✓ They match.

Answer: 40 apples
(Ada has 32, Billy has 8.)

Question. Ada has \(5\) times as many stamps as Billy. If Ada gives \(16\) stamps to Billy, they will have the same number of stamps. How many stamps do they have altogether? [3 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let Billy have \(x\) stamps;
then Ada has \(5x\) stamps.
Step 2. After the transfer Ada has \(5x-16\) and Billy has \(x+16\).
Step 3. They are equal: \(5x-16=x+16\), so \(4x=32\) and \(x=8\).
Step 4. Ada has \(5(8)=40\) stamps, so altogether they have \(40+8=48\) stamps.
Step 5. Check: \(40-16=24\) and \(8+16=24\). ✓
Answer: \(48\) stamps
