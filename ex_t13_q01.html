Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Ratios

T13 Q01

In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite angle $\theta$ has length 3 and the hypotenuse has length 5. Find $\sin\theta$.

Solution

Identify the side opposite angle $\theta$: it has length $3$.
Identify the hypotenuse: it has length $5$.
Use the trigonometric ratio $\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$.
Substitute the given lengths: $\sin\theta=\frac{3}{5}$.
$\sin\theta=\frac{3}{5}$.
