TOPIC 6 · EQUATIONS · COMPLEX NUMBERS

Let $\;z=(a-5)i+\dfrac{(a+2)i}{2+i}\;$, where $i^2=-1$. If $a$ and $z$ are real numbers, then $a-z=$

A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. (numeric answer)
1Identify the given information

$$z=(a-5)i+\frac{(a+2)i}{2+i}$$ Here $a$ is the unknown real parameter, and we are told $z$ is real. That means its imaginary part must be $0$.

Hint: a complex number is real ⇔ its imaginary part equals zero.

2Rationalise the denominator

Multiply top and bottom by the conjugate $2-i$:

$$z=(a-5)i+\frac{(a+2)i}{2+i}\times\frac{2-i}{2-i}$$

Hint: multiply by $\dfrac{\text{conjugate}}{2-i}$ to make the denominator $2^2+1^2$.

Rationalising the Denominator \(\dfrac{1}{a+bi}=\dfrac{a-bi}{a^{2}+b^{2}}\) Multiplying by \(\dfrac{2-i}{2-i}\) makes the denominator \(2^{2}+1^{2}=5\).
3Expand the numerator

$$z=(a-5)i+\frac{2ai+4i-ai^2-2i^2}{2^2+1^2}$$

Using $i^2=-1$:

$$z=(a-5)i+\frac{2ai+4i+a+2}{5}=\frac{a+2}{5}+\frac{5a-25+2a+4}{5}i$$

Hint: $4i-25i$ comes from distributing $(a-5)i$ over the common denominator.

4Collect real and imaginary parts

$$z=\underbrace{\frac{a+2}{5}}_{\text{real}}+\underbrace{\frac{7a-21}{5}}_{\text{imaginary}}i$$

Hint: group all terms without $i$ as the real part.

5Apply "$z$ is real"

Since $z$ is a real number, its imaginary part is $0$:

$$\frac{7a-21}{5}=0\;\Rightarrow\;7a-21=0\;\Rightarrow\;a=3$$

Hint: set the coefficient of $i$ equal to zero.

Condition for a Complex Number to be Real \(z\text{ is real}\iff \operatorname{Im}(z)=0\) Setting the coefficient of \(i\), \(\dfrac{7a-21}{5}\), to zero gives \(a=3\).
6Find the value of $z$

Substitute $a=3$ into the real part:

$$z=\frac{3+2}{5}=1$$

Hint: after $a=3$, the imaginary part vanishes and $z$ lies on the real axis.

7Compute $a-z$

$$a-z=3-1=2$$

Hint: $a-z$ is just the horizontal distance between the point $z=1$ and the marker $a=3$.

Answer:  A. 2

Question. It is given that \(z=(a-2)i+\dfrac{(a+4)i}{1+2i}\), where \(a\) is a real number. If \(z\) is real, find \(a\) and \(z\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Rationalise: \(\dfrac{(a+4)i(1-2i)}{5}=\dfrac{2(a+4)+(a+4)i}{5}\).
Step 2. Collect parts: \(z=\dfrac{2a+8}{5}+\left[(a-2)+\dfrac{a+4}{5}\right]i\).
Step 3. Imaginary part \(=\dfrac{5a-10+a+4}{5}=\dfrac{6a-6}{5}=0\Rightarrow a=1\).
Step 4. Then \(z=\dfrac{2+8}{5}=2\).
Answer: \(a=1,\ z=2\)
