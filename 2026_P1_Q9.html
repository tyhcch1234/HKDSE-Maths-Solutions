Frequency Distribution & Mean

Question

The frequency distribution table and the cumulative frequency distribution table below show the distribution of the weights of \(20\) apples.

Weight (grams)Frequency
\(91-100\)\(2\)
\(101-110\)\(a\)
\(111-120\)\(6\)
\(121-130\)\(7\)
\(131-140\)\(h\)

Weight less than (grams)Cumulative frequency
\(100.5\)\(2\)
\(110.5\)\(6\)
\(120.5\)\(12\)
\(130.5\)\(b\)
\(140.5\)\(k\)

(a) Find \(a\) and \(b\).
(b) Find the mean of the distribution. (5 marks)

Solution

PART (a) — find a
The cumulative frequency at 110.5 equals the frequencies of the first two classes: \[ 2+a=6 \implies a=4 \]
PART (a) — find b
The cumulative frequency at 130.5 equals the sum of the first four frequencies: \[ b=2+4+6+7=19 \] (Consistency: since there are 20 apples, \(k=20\) and \(h=20-19=1\), which matches the last row of the cumulative table.)
PART (b) — mean using class midpoints
Midpoints: 95, 105, 115, 125, 135. \[ \text{Mean}=\frac{2(95)+4(105)+6(115)+7(125)+1(135)}{20} \] \[ =\frac{190+420+690+875+135}{20}=\frac{2310}{20} \]
(a) \(a=4,\; b=19\)    (b) Mean \(=115.5\) grams
