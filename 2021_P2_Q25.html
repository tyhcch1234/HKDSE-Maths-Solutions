HKDSE 2021 · Paper 2 · Q25 — Locus of a Point Equidistant from Two Points

Coordinate Geometry · Circle / Perpendicular Bisector
Locus \(x^2+y^2-10x-14y+72=0\) Segment \(MN\) \(M(5,7)\) & \(N(6,8)\) Moving point \(P\) Centre \((5,7)\)
[2021MC#25]
The coordinates of the points \(M\) and \(N\) are \((5,7)\) and \((6,8)\) respectively. Let \(P\) be a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that \(PM = MN\). Find the equation of the locus of \(P\).
A. \(x-y+2=0\)
B. \(x+y-13=0\)
C. \(x^2+y^2-10x-14y+72=0\)
D. \(x^2+y^2-12x-16y+98=0\)
Step 1 · Let the coordinates of P

Let \(P=(u,v)\). The condition is a distance condition: \(PM=MN\).

Since distances are positive, squaring both sides is valid:

$$PM^2 = MN^2$$
Hint: when a locus condition only involves lengths, square both sides to avoid square roots.
Step 2 · Compute MN²

First find the fixed length \(MN\) using the distance formula:

$$MN^2=(6-5)^2+(8-7)^2=1^2+1^2=2$$
\(MN=\sqrt{2}\) — a useful scale reference for the diagram.
Distance Formula \(d^{2}=(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}+(y_{2}-y_{1})^{2}\) \(MN^{2}=(7-4)^{2}+(7-3)^{2}=9+16=25\).
Step 3 · Apply the condition PM² = MN²

Now expand \(PM^2\) with \(P=(u,v)\), \(M=(5,7)\):

$$(u-5)^2+(v-7)^2=2$$

Expand both squares:

$$(u^2-10u+25)+(v^2-14v+49)=2$$ $$u^2+v^2-10u-14v+74=2$$
Squaring Both Sides \(PM=MN\ \Longrightarrow\ PM^{2}=MN^{2}\) Both lengths are positive, so the condition can be squared to avoid square roots.
Step 4 · Simplify the locus equation

Subtract \(2\) from both sides:

$$u^2+v^2-10u-14v+72=0$$

Replacing the general point \((u,v)\) by \((x,y)\), the locus is a circle:

$$x^2+y^2-10x-14y+72=0$$
Step 5 · Interpret geometrically & answer

Completing the square:

$$(x-5)^2+(y-7)^2=2$$

So the locus is the circle centred at \(M(5,7)\) with radius \(MN=\sqrt{2}\). Since \(MN\) already equals \(\sqrt2\), this is consistent.

Answer: C  →  \(x^2+y^2-10x-14y+72=0\)

Question. The point \(P(x,y)\) moves so that \(PM=MN\), where \(M=(4,3)\) and \(N=(7,7)\). Find the equation of the locus of \(P\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(PM=MN\) gives \(PM^{2}=MN^{2}\).
Step 2. \(MN^{2}=(7-4)^{2}+(7-3)^{2}=9+16=25\).
Step 3. \((x-4)^{2}+(y-3)^{2}=25\).
Step 4. Expanding: \(x^{2}-8x+16+y^{2}-6y+9=25\), so \(x^{2}+y^{2}-8x-6y=0\).
Answer: \(x^{2}+y^{2}-8x-6y=0\)
Equation of a Circle \((x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=r^{2}\) The locus is the circle with centre \(M\) and radius \(MN\).
