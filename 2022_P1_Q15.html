HKDSE Math 2022 Paper 1 Q15

Probability
Combination Probability Complement

There are 10 boys and 12 girls in a class. If 4 students are randomly selected from the class to form a committee,

(a) Find the probability that there are 2 boys and 2 girls in the committee. [2 marks]

(b) Find the probability that the number of boys and the number of girls in the committee are different. [2 marks]

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Part (a) – Identify the total number of ways to choose 4 students

There are \(10 + 12 = 22\) students in total. We choose any 4 of them.

Hint: Use combinations

The total number of ways to choose 4 students from 22 is \(C^{22}_{4}\).

\(C^{22}_{4} = \frac{22 \times 21 \times 20 \times 19}{4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1} = 7315\)

2

Part (a) – Count favorable outcomes (2 boys and 2 girls)

Choose 2 boys from 10 and 2 girls from 12 independently.

Multiplication principle

Number of ways = \(C^{10}_{2} \times C^{12}_{2}\)

\(C^{10}_{2} = 45,\quad C^{12}_{2} = 66\)

Total favorable = \(45 \times 66 = 2970\)

3

Part (a) – Compute the probability

Divide favorable outcomes by total outcomes.

Probability formula

\(P = \frac{\text{favorable}}{\text{total}}\)

\(P = \frac{2970}{7315} = \frac{54}{133}\)

4

Part (b) – Use the complement rule

The event "number of boys and girls are different" is the complement of "equal numbers of boys and girls".

Complement rule

\(P(\text{different}) = 1 - P(\text{equal})\)

Equal numbers means 2 boys & 2 girls (since total is 4).

5

Part (b) – Calculate the final probability

Subtract the result from part (a) from 1.

Final calculation

\(1 - \frac{54}{133} = \frac{79}{133}\)

So the required probability is \(\frac{79}{133}\).
