Let \(g(x)=3x^{2}-6kx+24x+3k^{2}-24k+55\), where \(k\) is a positive constant. Denote the vertex of the graph of \(y=g(x)\) by \(R\).

(a) Using the method of completing the square, express the coordinates of \(R\) in terms of \(k\).  (2 marks)

(b) The graph of \(y=h(x)\) is obtained by translating the graph of \(y=g(x)\) rightwards by 6 units and then downwards by 10 units. Denote the vertex of the graph of \(y=h(x)\) by \(S\). Let \(T\) be a point such that the orthocentre of \(\triangle RST\) is the origin.

(i) Express the coordinates of \(T\) in terms of \(k\).

(ii) The coordinates of the point \(U\) are \((-5,5)\). Let \(V\) be a point lying on \(RS\) such that \(TV\) is perpendicular to \(RS\). It is given that \(S\), \(T\), \(U\) and \(V\) are concyclic. Find \(k\).

Step 1 · Complete the square (Part a)
Completing the Square \(y=a\left(x-h\right)^{2}+c\ \to\ \text{vertex }(h,c)\) The \(k\)-terms cancel, leaving \(g(x)=3\left(x-(k-4)\right)^{2}+7\), so \(R\) is \((k-4,7)\).

\[ g(x)=3x^{2}-6kx+24x+3k^{2}-24k+55 \]

\[ =3(x^{2}-2kx+8x)+3k^{2}-24k+55 \]

\[ =3\bigl[x^{2}-2(k-4)x\bigr]+3k^{2}-24k+55 \]

\[ =3\Bigl[\bigl(x-(k-4)\bigr)^{2}-(k-4)^{2}\Bigr]+3k^{2}-24k+55 \]

Expand the \(k\)-terms:

\[ -3(k^{2}-8k+16)+3k^{2}-24k+55 = 7 \]

\[ \therefore\ g(x)=3\bigl(x-(k-4)\bigr)^{2}+7 \]

Hence the vertex is \(\boxed{R\,(k-4,\;7)}\).

Hint: for \(y=3(x-h)^2+c\), the vertex is \((h,c)\) — the \(+7\) is independent of \(k\).
Step 2 · Translate to get S (Part b)
Translation of a Graph \(y=f(x)\ \to\ y=f(x-h)+k\) Moving right \(6\) and down \(10\) sends \((k-4,7)\) to \(S(k+2,-3)\).

Right 6 units, then down 10 units:

\[ S=\bigl((k-4)+6,\;7-10\bigr)=\bigl(k+2,\;-3\bigr) \]

\[ RS:\ \text{from }(k-4,7)\ \text{to }(k+2,-3) \]

Slope of \(RS\):

\[ m_{RS}=\frac{-3-7}{(k+2)-(k-4)}=\frac{-10}{6}=-\frac{5}{3} \]

Step 3 · Use the orthocentre O (Part b i)
Product of Slopes of Perpendicular Lines \(m_{1}m_{2}=-1\) \(RS\) has slope \(-\dfrac{5}{3}\), so \(OT\) has slope \(\dfrac{3}{5}\) because \(O\) is the orthocentre.

Since the orthocentre of \(\triangle RST\) is the origin \(O\), we have \(RO\perp ST\) and \(SO\perp RT\).

Because \(RS\perp OT\):

\[ m_{OT}=-\frac{1}{m_{RS}}=\frac{3}{5} \]

Also \(RT\perp OS\),
so with \(OS\) slope \(\dfrac{-3-0}{k+2-0}=\dfrac{-3}{k+2}\):

\[ m_{RT}=\frac{k+2}{3} \]

Let \(T=(5t,\,3t)\) (since \(m_{OT}=\tfrac35\)). Then

\[ m_{RT}=\frac{3t-7}{5t-(k-4)}=\frac{k+2}{3} \]

\[ 3(3t-7)=(k+2)\bigl(5t-(k-4)\bigr) \]

\[ 9t-21=(k+2)(5t-k+4) \]

Solving for \(t\):

\[ t=\frac{k^{2}-2k-29}{5k+1} \]

\[ \therefore\ T\left(\frac{5(k^{2}-2k-29)}{5k+1},\;\frac{3(k^{2}-2k-29)}{5k+1}\right) \]

You only need the ratio \(3:5\) for the \(OT\) slope, then one perpendicular condition fixes \(T\).
Step 4 · Trigonometry / cyclic condition (Part b ii)

\(TV\perp RS\) with \(V\) on \(RS\) gives \(\angle TVS = 90^\circ\).

Since \(S,T,U,V\) are concyclic and \(\angle TVS=90^\circ\),
the point \(U\) subtends chord \(ST\):

\[ \angle SUT=90^\circ \]

Therefore \(US\perp UT\).

\[ m_{US}=\frac{-3-5}{(k+2)-(-5)}=\frac{-8}{k+7} \]

\[ m_{UT}=\frac{\frac{3(k^{2}-2k-29)}{5k+1}-5}{\frac{5(k^{2}-2k-29)}{5k+1}-(-5)} \]

Simplifying,

\[ m_{UT}=\frac{3k^{2}-31k-92}{5k^{2}+15k-140} \]

\[ \left(\frac{-8}{k+7}\right)\left(\frac{3k^{2}-31k-92}{5k^{2}+15k-140}\right)=-1 \]

\[ 8(3k^{2}-31k-92)=(k+7)(5k^{2}+15k-140) \]

\[ 24k^{2}-248k-736=5k^{3}+50k^{2}-35k-980 \]

\[ 5k^{3}+26k^{2}+213k-244=0 \]

Step 5 · Solve for k

Test \(k=1\):

\[ 5+26+213-244=0 \quad \checkmark \]

So \((k-1)\) is a factor:

\[ (k-1)(5k^{2}+31k+244)=0 \]

The quadratic has discriminant

\[ 31^{2}-4(5)(244)=961-4880<0 \]

hence no real roots.
Since \(k>0\),

\[ \boxed{k=1} \]

The question states \(k\) is a positive constant; the only admissible root is \(k=1\).
Final answers
(a) \(R\,(k-4,\;7)\)
(b)(i) \(T\left(\dfrac{5(k^{2}-2k-29)}{5k+1},\;\dfrac{3(k^{2}-2k-29)}{5k+1}\right)\)
(b)(ii) \(k=1\)

Question. Let \(g(x)=2x^{2}-4kx+12x+2k^{2}-12k+30\), where \(k\) is a positive constant.
(a) Express \(g(x)\) in the form \(a(x-h)^{2}+c\) and write down the vertex \(R\).
(b) The graph of \(y=h(x)\) is obtained by translating the graph of \(y=g(x)\) rightwards by \(5\) units and downwards by \(7\) units. Find the vertex \(S\) of \(y=h(x)\).
(c) Find the slope of \(RS\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(g(x)=2\left[x^{2}-2(k-3)x\right]+2k^{2}-12k+30\).
Step 2. \(=2\left[\left(x-(k-3)\right)^{2}-(k-3)^{2}\right]+2k^{2}-12k+30\), and the \(k\)-terms cancel, so \(g(x)=2\left(x-k+3\right)^{2}+12\).
Step 3. Hence \(R(k-3,12)\).
Step 4. Right \(5\) and down \(7\): \(S(k+2,5)\).
Step 5. Slope of \(RS=\dfrac{5-12}{(k+2)-(k-3)}=-\dfrac{7}{5}\).
Answer: (a) \(R(k-3,12)\) (b) \(S(k+2,5)\) (c) \(-\dfrac{7}{5}\)
