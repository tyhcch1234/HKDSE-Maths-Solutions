Compound Interest

Question

A sum of $\$P$ is deposited at an interest rate of \(4\%\) per annum for 5 years, compounded quarterly. If the amount is $\$80\,000$, find \(P\) correct to the nearest integer.

  • A. \(65\,520\)
  • B. \(65\,564\)
  • C. \(65\,754\)
  • D. \(66\,667\)

Solution

Compound interest formula
Compound Interest Formula \(A=P\left(1+\dfrac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}\) Quarterly at \(4\%\) for \(5\) years means \(1\%\) per quarter and \(20\) quarters, so \(80000=P(1.01)^{20}\).

Quarterly rate \(=\dfrac{4\%}{4}=1\%=0.01\);
number of quarters \(=5\times4=20\). Hence

\[ 80000=P(1.01)^{20} \]
Evaluate \((1.01)^{20}\)
Evaluating a Power \((1.01)^{20}\approx 1.22019\) Twenty compoundings at \(1\%\) multiply the principal by about \(1.22019\).

\((1.01)^{20}\approx 1.22019\).

Solve for \(P\)
Making the Principal the Subject \(P=\dfrac{A}{(1.01)^{20}}\) Dividing \(80000\) by \(1.22019\) gives \(65563.6\), i.e. \(\$65564\) to the nearest dollar.
\[ P=\frac{80000}{(1.01)^{20}}\approx\frac{80000}{1.22019}\approx 65\,563.6 \]

Rounded to the nearest integer, \(P=65\,564\).

Final Answer: B
