Geometry of a Circle — Angles in Triangles & Radii
Circle Geometry Isosceles Triangle Angles in a Triangle Radii

Q Question

In the figure, \(O\) is the centre of the circle \(ABCD\). If \(\angle BAO = 28^\circ\), \(\angle BCD = 114^\circ\) and \(\angle CDO = 42^\circ\), then \(\angle ABC =\)
Multiple Choice
A. \(96^\circ\)    B. \(90^\circ\)    C. \(100^\circ\)    D. \(128^\circ\)

1 Part (a) — Find \(\angle OBA\) and \(\angle OCD\)

Use the fact that \(OA, OB, OC, OD\) are all radii of the same circle, so each triangle formed with \(O\) is isosceles.

Step 11M
In \(\triangle OAB\), \(OA = OB\) (radii).
Hint: A triangle with two equal sides is isosceles, so its base angles are equal.
\[ \therefore \angle OBA = \angle OAB = 28^\circ \]
Step 21M
In \(\triangle OCD\), \(OC = OD\) (radii). \[ \therefore \angle OCD = \angle ODC = 42^\circ \]
Hint: Again, the two base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal.
Step 31A
Therefore: \[ \angle OBA = 28^\circ, \qquad \angle OCD = 42^\circ \]
Answers: \(\angle OBA = 28^\circ\), \(\angle OCD = 42^\circ\)
0 / 3

2 Part (b) — Find \(\angle OCB\) and \(\angle OBC\)

Use the given \(\angle BCD = 114^\circ\) and the result \(\angle OCD = 42^\circ\) to find \(\angle OCB\). Then use the isosceles triangle \(OBC\).

Step 11M
Since \(OC\) lies inside \(\angle BCD\): \[ \angle OCB = \angle BCD - \angle OCD = 114^\circ - 42^\circ = 72^\circ \]
Hint: The angle \(BCD\) is split into \(\angle BCO\) and \(\angle OCD\).
Step 21M
In \(\triangle OBC\), \(OB = OC\) (radii). \[ \therefore \angle OBC = \angle OCB = 72^\circ \]
Hint: Triangle \(OBC\) is isosceles with equal sides \(OB\) and \(OC\).
Step 31A
Therefore: \[ \angle OCB = 72^\circ, \qquad \angle OBC = 72^\circ \]
Answers: \(\angle OCB = 72^\circ\), \(\angle OBC = 72^\circ\)
0 / 3

3 Part (c) — Find \(\angle ABC\)

Combine the two angles at \(B\): \(\angle ABC = \angle OBA + \angle OBC\).

Step 11M
\(\angle ABC\) is the sum of the two angles at \(B\): \[ \angle ABC = \angle OBA + \angle OBC \]
Hint: \(BO\) divides \(\angle ABC\) into \(\angle ABO\) and \(\angle OBC\).
Step 21A
Substituting the values: \[ \angle ABC = 28^\circ + 72^\circ = 100^\circ \]
Answer: \(\angle ABC = 100^\circ\) — Option C
0 / 2

📌 Summary of Answers

  • \(\angle OBA = 28^\circ\) (base \(\angle\)s, isos. \(\triangle OAB\))
  • \(\angle OCD = 42^\circ\) (base \(\angle\)s, isos. \(\triangle OCD\))
  • \(\angle OCB = 114^\circ - 42^\circ = 72^\circ\)
  • \(\angle OBC = 72^\circ\) (base \(\angle\)s, isos. \(\triangle OBC\))
  • \(\angle ABC = 28^\circ + 72^\circ = 100^\circ\)

Correct option: C. \(100^\circ\)
