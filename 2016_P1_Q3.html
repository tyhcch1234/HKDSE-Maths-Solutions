Simplifying algebraic fractions using a common denominator

Polynomials Algebraic Fractions Common Denominator
Question
Simplify $$ \frac{2}{4x-5} + \frac{3}{1-6x} \,. $$
3 marks
Progress 0 / 5 steps
1
Identify the denominators and find a common denominator
▼

The two denominators are:

\( \text{Denominator 1: } \; 4x - 5 \)
\( \text{Denominator 2: } \; 1 - 6x \)

Since they have no common factor, the lowest common denominator (L.C.D.) is their product:

\[ \text{L.C.D.} = (4x-5)(1-6x) \]
💡 Hint: The common denominator is found by multiplying the two denominators together when they share no common factor. Here, \(4x-5\) and \(1-6x\) are not multiples of each other.
2
Rewrite each fraction with the common denominator
▼

Multiply numerator and denominator of each fraction by the missing factor:

\[ \frac{2}{4x-5} = \frac{2(1-6x)}{(4x-5)(1-6x)} \]
\[ \frac{3}{1-6x} = \frac{3(4x-5)}{(4x-5)(1-6x)} \]
💡 Hint: For the first fraction, the missing factor is \((1-6x)\). For the second fraction, the missing factor is \((4x-5)\). Multiply both numerator and denominator by that missing factor.
3
Combine into a single fraction and expand the numerator
▼

Now add the two fractions:

\[ \frac{2(1-6x) + 3(4x-5)}{(4x-5)(1-6x)} \]

Expand the numerator carefully:

\( 2(1-6x) = 2 - 12x \)
\( 3(4x-5) = 12x - 15 \)
💡 Hint: Distribute the \(2\) and the \(3\) across the brackets. Be careful with signs — especially the \( -12x \) term.
4
Simplify the numerator
▼

Add the expanded terms in the numerator:

\[ \frac{2 - 12x + 12x - 15}{(4x-5)(1-6x)} \]

Combine like terms:

\[ \frac{-13}{(4x-5)(1-6x)} \]
💡 Hint: The \(x\)-terms cancel out! \( -12x \) and \( +12x \) add to zero, leaving only the constant terms: \(2 - 15 = -13\).
5
Final form — handle the negative sign in the denominator
▼

The answer can be left as:

\[ \frac{-13}{(4x-5)(1-6x)} \]

Or written in an equivalent, often preferred form by absorbing the negative sign:

\[ \frac{13}{(4x-5)(6x-1)} \]

Note: \(1-6x = -(6x-1)\), so the negative sign in the numerator and the negative sign from swapping \(1-6x\) to \(6x-1\) cancel each other out.

💡 Hint: Both forms are mathematically correct. The form with \((6x-1)\) avoids a leading negative sign, which is generally considered neater.

Final Answer

\[ \frac{13}{(4x-5)(6x-1)} \]
or equivalently \( \dfrac{-13}{(4x-5)(1-6x)} \)
