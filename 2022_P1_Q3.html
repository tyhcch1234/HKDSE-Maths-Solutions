HKDSE Math 2022 Paper 1 Q3

Polynomials Algebraic Fractions LCM of Denominators

Question

Simplify \(\displaystyle \frac{3}{k-9} + \frac{2}{5k+6}\).

[3 marks]

Step-by-step solution

1

Identify the denominators & find the LCM

We need to combine two fractions:

\[ \frac{3}{k-9} + \frac{2}{5k+6} \]

Denominators: \((k-9)\) and \((5k+6)\). They are linear and distinct, so the Least Common Multiple (LCM) is simply their product.

\[ \text{LCM} = (k-9)(5k+6) \]
Hint If two denominators have no common factor, the LCM is their product.
2

Rewrite each fraction with the common denominator

Adjust each fraction so that the denominator becomes \((k-9)(5k+6)\):

\[ \frac{3}{k-9} \cdot \frac{5k+6}{5k+6} + \frac{2}{5k+6} \cdot \frac{k-9}{k-9} \]
\[ = \frac{3(5k+6)}{(k-9)(5k+6)} + \frac{2(k-9)}{(k-9)(5k+6)} \]
Hint Multiply numerator and denominator of the first fraction by \((5k+6)\), and of the second by \((k-9)\).
3

Combine into a single fraction

Now both fractions have the same denominator, so we can add the numerators:

\[ \frac{3(5k+6) + 2(k-9)}{(k-9)(5k+6)} \]
4

Expand and simplify the numerator

\[ \begin{aligned} 3(5k+6) + 2(k-9) &= 15k + 18 + 2k - 18 \\ &= 17k + 0 \\ &= 17k \end{aligned} \]

Notice that \(18 - 18 = 0\), so the constant term cancels out.

Hint Distribute carefully: \(3(5k+6)=15k+18\) and \(2(k-9)=2k-18\).
5

Final answer

\[ \frac{17k}{(k-9)(5k+6)} \]

The numerator \(17k\) and denominator \((k-9)(5k+6)\) have no common factors, so this is fully simplified.

Check with a numerical value

Choose a value of \(k\) (avoid \(k=9\) and \(k=-1.2\) where denominators are zero) and see both the original expression and simplified result match.
