Equations (44) · Sub-Topic: Simultaneous Equations

T6 Q42

The solution begins by adding two equations to get \(6x = 12\). Write a question involving a pair of simultaneous equations whose sum gives \(6x = 12\), leading to \(x = 2\).

Solution

Question: Solve the simultaneous equations $2x+y=5$ and $4x-y=7$.
Add the two equations: $(2x+y)+(4x-y)=5+7$.
Simplify: $6x=12$.
Divide both sides by $6$: $x=2$.
Substitute $x=2$ into $2x+y=5$: $2(2)+y=5$, so $4+y=5$.
Subtract $4$ from both sides: $y=1$.
Check in the second equation: $4(2)-1=8-1=7$, which matches the right-hand side.
Question: Solve $2x+y=5$ and $4x-y=7$; adding gives $6x=12$, so the solution is $x=2$ and $y=1$.
