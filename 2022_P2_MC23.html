Trigonometry — The Rhombus Diagonal Ratio
Rhombus Properties Right-Angled Trigonometry Perpendicular Diagonals

QThe Question

It is given that \(PQRS\) is a rhombus. Let \(T\) be the point of intersection of \(PR\) and \(QS\). If \(\angle QRT=\theta\), then \(\dfrac{PQ}{ST}=\)
  • A. \(\sin\theta\)
  • B. \(\cos\theta\)
  • C. \(\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}\)
  • D. \(\dfrac{1}{\cos\theta}\)

1Part (a) — Diagonals of a Rhombus

Drag the slider to change \(\theta\) and observe the shape of the rhombus. Notice that the diagonals are always perpendicular and each diagonal is bisected at \(T\).

QT = ST = – PT = TR = – PQ = – PR ⟂ QS
Step 1

In a rhombus, the diagonals bisect each other at right angles. So \(T\) is the midpoint of both \(PR\) and \(QS\), and \(PR \perp QS\).

Hint: A rhombus is a parallelogram with equal sides; its diagonals are perpendicular.
Step 2

Hence \(QT = ST\) and \(PT = TR\). Also in \(\triangle QRT\), \(\angle QTR = 90^\circ\), so it is a right-angled triangle.

Key fact: \(ST = QT\) (bisected diagonal).

2Part (b)(i) — The Triangle \(\triangle QRT\)

Isolate \(\triangle QRT\). Move the slider to see how \(QT\) and \(QR\) change as \(\theta\) changes, while \(QR\) (the side of the rhombus) stays fixed.

QR (hyp) = 1.000 QT (opp) = – RT (adj) = – sin θ = QT/QR = –
Step 1

Consider the right-angled triangle \(\triangle QRT\). The angle at \(R\) is \(\angle QRT = \theta\). The side opposite \(\theta\) is \(QT\) and the hypotenuse is \(QR\).

Hint: Use SOH CAH TOA — here we need the ratio involving the opposite side and the hypotenuse.
Step 2

\[ \sin\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}} = \frac{QT}{QR} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad QT = QR\sin\theta. \]

Hint: Since \(ST = QT\), we also get \(ST = QR\sin\theta\).
\(QT = QR\sin\theta\) and \(ST = QR\sin\theta\).

3Part (b)(ii) — The Ratio \(\dfrac{PQ}{ST}\)

The bar chart below compares \(PQ = QR\) with \(ST\). Slide \(\theta\) and watch the ratio \(\dfrac{PQ}{ST}\) — the answer is \(\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}\).

PQ = 1.000 ST = sin θ = – PQ / ST = 1 / sin θ = –
Step 1

Since \(PQRS\) is a rhombus, all sides are equal: \[ PQ = QR = RS = SP. \] From Part (b)(i) we have \(ST = QR\sin\theta\).

Hint: Replace \(QR\) by \(PQ\) because they are equal sides of the rhombus.
Step 2

Therefore \[ \frac{PQ}{ST} = \frac{QR}{QR\sin\theta} = \frac{1}{\sin\theta}. \]

Hint: The \(QR\) cancels because it is the common factor in the numerator and denominator.
Step 3

Hence the correct option is C.

Answer: \(\displaystyle \frac{PQ}{ST} = \frac{1}{\sin\theta}\)  →  Option C
0 / 3

✓Summary of Answers

  • Diagonals of rhombus: \(PR \perp QS\) and \(QT = ST\), \(PT = TR\).
  • In \(\triangle QRT\): \(\sin\theta = \dfrac{QT}{QR} \Rightarrow QT = QR\sin\theta\).
  • Since \(ST = QT\): \(ST = QR\sin\theta\).
  • Since \(PQ = QR\) (rhombus sides equal): \[ \frac{PQ}{ST} = \frac{QR}{QR\sin\theta} = \frac{1}{\sin\theta}. \]
  • Answer: Option C — \(\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}\)
