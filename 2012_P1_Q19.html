📝 Question

In a city, the air cargo terminal \(X\) of an airport handles goods of weight \(A(n)\) tonnes in the \(n\)th year since the start of its operation, where \(n\) is a positive integer. It is given that \[ A(n) = ab^{2n}, \] where \(a\) and \(b\) are positive constants. It is found that the weights of the goods handled by \(X\) in the 1st year and the 2nd year since the start of its operation are \(254\,100\) tonnes and \(307\,461\) tonnes respectively.

(a)(i) Find \(a\) and \(b\). Hence find the weight of the goods handled by \(X\) in the 4th year since the start of its operation.

(a)(ii) Express, in terms of \(n\), the total weight of the goods handled by \(X\) in the first \(n\) years since the start of its operation. [6 marks]

(b) The air cargo terminal \(Y\) starts to operate since \(X\) has been operated for 4 years. Let \(B(m)\) tonnes be the weight of the goods handled by \(Y\) in the \(m\)th year since the start of its operation, where \(m\) is a positive integer. It is given that \[ B(m) = 2ab^m. \]

(b)(i) The manager of the airport claims that after \(Y\) has been operated, the weight of the goods handled by \(Y\) is less than that handled by \(X\) in each year. Do you agree? Explain your answer.

(b)(ii) The supervisor of the airport thinks that when the total weight of the goods handled by \(X\) and \(Y\) since the start of the operation of \(X\) exceeds \(20\,000\,000\) tonnes, new facilities should be installed to maintain the efficiency of the air cargo terminals. According to the supervisor, in which year since the start of the operation of \(X\) should the new facilities be installed? [7 marks]

🧩 Step-by-Step Guided Solution

Tap each step to reveal the reasoning. Try to solve it yourself first!

1
Find \(b\) using the ratio of \(A(2)\) to \(A(1)\)
▼
💡 Hint: \(A(1)=ab^2\) and \(A(2)=ab^4\). Dividing eliminates \(a\) and gives \(b^2\).

Given:

\[ A(1)=ab^2=254\,100,\qquad A(2)=ab^4=307\,461. \]

Divide \(A(2)\) by \(A(1)\):

\[ \frac{A(2)}{A(1)} = \frac{ab^4}{ab^2} = b^2 = \frac{307\,461}{254\,100}. \]

Compute:

\[ b^2 = 1.21 \quad\Rightarrow\quad b = \sqrt{1.21} = 1.1. \]
✅ \(b = 1.1\)
2
Find \(a\) using \(A(1)=254\,100\)
▼
💡 Hint: Substitute \(b=1.1\) into \(A(1)=ab^2\).
\[ a (1.1)^2 = 254\,100 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a = \frac{254\,100}{1.21} = 210\,000. \]
✅ \(a = 210\,000\)
3
Weight in the 4th year \(A(4)\)
▼
💡 Hint: \(A(4) = ab^{8} = 210\,000 (1.1)^8\). Use a calculator.
\[ A(4) = 210\,000 (1.1)^{8} = 450\,153.6501 \text{ tonnes} \]

(Round to 4 decimal places as per marking scheme.)

✅ \(A(4) \approx 450\,153.6501\) tonnes
4
Total weight in first \(n\) years: sum of a GP
▼
💡 Hint: \(A(1), A(2), \dots, A(n)\) form a geometric sequence. Use \(S_n = \frac{a(r^n-1)}{r-1}\). Here the first term is \(A(1)=ab^2\) and common ratio is \(b^2\).

Total weight:

\[ \sum_{k=1}^{n} ab^{2k} = ab^2 + ab^4 + \cdots + ab^{2n}. \]

First term \(=ab^2\), common ratio \(=b^2\). So:

\[ S_n = \frac{ab^2\big((b^2)^n - 1\big)}{b^2 - 1}. \]

Substitute \(a=210\,000,\; b=1.1\):

\[ S_n = \frac{210\,000(1.1)^2\big((1.1)^{2n} - 1\big)}{(1.1)^2 - 1} = \frac{254\,100\big((1.1)^{2n} - 1\big)}{1.21 - 1}. \]

Since \(1.21 - 1 = 0.21\) and \(\frac{254\,100}{0.21} = 1\,210\,000\):

\[ S_n = 1\,210\,000\big((1.1)^{2n} - 1\big) \text{ tonnes}. \]
✅ Total weight \(= 1\,210\,000\big((1.1)^{2n} - 1\big)\) tonnes
5
(b)(i) Compare \(A(m+4)\) and \(B(m)\)
▼
💡 Hint: After \(m\) years of \(Y\), \(X\) has operated for \(m+4\) years. Show \(A(m+4) > B(m)\) for all \(m\).

\(X\) starts 4 years earlier, so when \(Y\) has operated for \(m\) years, \(X\) has operated for \(m+4\) years.

\[ A(m+4) = ab^{2(m+4)} = ab^{2m+8} = ab^{2m} \cdot b^{8}. \]

Also \(B(m) = 2ab^{m}\).

To compare, consider the ratio \(\frac{A(m+4)}{B(m)}\):

\[ \frac{A(m+4)}{B(m)} = \frac{ab^{2m} \cdot b^{8}}{2ab^{m}} = \frac{b^{m+8}}{2}. \]

Since \(b=1.1 > 1\) and \(m \ge 1\), \(b^{m+8} > 1.1^9 \approx 2.358 > 2\).

Thus \(\frac{A(m+4)}{B(m)} > 1\), so \(A(m+4) > B(m)\).

Therefore, the manager's claim is incorrect. \(Y\) handles less than \(X\) in each year.

✅ Disagree — \(A(m+4) > B(m)\) for all \(m\).
6
(b)(ii) Set up total weight inequality
▼
💡 Hint: After \(n\) years, total = sum of \(A(1),\dots,A(n)\) plus sum of \(B(1),\dots,B(n-4)\). Need > \(20\,000\,000\).

Let \(n\) be the number of years since \(X\) started. \(Y\) operates from year 5 to year \(n\), i.e. \(m=1\) to \(n-4\).

Total weight:

\[ \underbrace{1\,210\,000\big((1.1)^{2n}-1\big)}_{\text{terminal } X} + \underbrace{\sum_{m=1}^{n-4} 2ab^{m}}_{\text{terminal } Y}. \]

For \(Y\): \(2ab = 462\,000\), so sum \(= \frac{462\,000(1.1^{n-4}-1)}{1.1-1} = 4\,620\,000(1.1^{n-4}-1)\).

Inequality:

\[ 1\,210\,000(1.1^{2n}-1) + 4\,620\,000(1.1^{n-4}-1) > 20\,000\,000. \]
7
Solve for \(n\) using logarithms
▼
💡 Hint: Let \(x = (1.1)^n\). Then \(1.1^{2n}=x^2\) and \(1.1^{n-4} = x \cdot 1.1^{-4}\). Solve quadratic in \(x\) and use \(\log\).

Let \(x = (1.1)^n\). Then \(1.1^{2n}=x^2\) and \(1.1^{n-4}=x \cdot 1.1^{-4} = \frac{x}{1.1^4}\).

Inequality becomes:

\[ 1\,210\,000(x^2-1)+4\,620\,000\left(\frac{x}{1.4641}-1\right) > 20\,000\,000. \]

Simplify (divide by \(10\,000\)):

\[ 121(x^2-1) + 462\left(\frac{x}{1.4641}-1\right) > 2000. \]

Further simplification leads to:

\[ 121x^2 + 315.55x - 2583 > 0. \]

Solving the quadratic (positive root):

\[ x > \frac{-315.55 + \sqrt{315.55^2 + 4\cdot121\cdot2583}}{2\cdot121} \approx 3.496831134. \]

So \((1.1)^n > 3.496831134\). Take log:

\[ n \log(1.1) > \log(3.496831134) \quad\Rightarrow\quad n > 13.13455888. \]

Since \(n\) is an integer, \(n \ge 14\).

✅ New facilities should be installed in the 14th year since \(X\) started operation.
