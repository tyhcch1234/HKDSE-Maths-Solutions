QUESTION

In a city, the air cargo terminal $X$ of an airport handles goods of weight $A(n)$ tonnes in the $n$th year since the start of its operation, where $n$ is a positive integer. It is given that \[ A(n)=ab^{\,2n}, \] where $a$ and $b$ are positive constants. It is found that the weights of the goods handled by $X$ in the 1st year and the 2nd year since the start of its operation are $254\,100$ tonnes and $307\,461$ tonnes respectively.

(a) (i) Find $a$ and $b$. Hence find the weight of the goods handled by $X$ in the 4th year since the start of its operation.

    (ii) Express, in terms of $n$, the total weight of the goods handled by $X$ in the first $n$ years since the start of its operation.  [6 marks]

(b) The air cargo terminal $Y$ starts to operate since $X$ has been operated for 4 years. Let $B(m)$ tonnes be the weight of the goods handled by $Y$ in the $m$th year since the start of its operation, where $m$ is a positive integer. It is given that $B(m)=2ab^{\,m}$.

    (i) The manager of the airport claims that after $Y$ has been operated, the weight of the goods handled by $Y$ is less than that handled by $X$ in each year. Do you agree? Explain your answer.

    (ii) The supervisor of the airport thinks that when the total weight of the goods handled by $X$ and $Y$ since the start of the operation of $X$ exceeds $20\,000\,000$ tonnes, new facilities should be installed. According to the supervisor, in which year since the start of the operation of $X$ should the new facilities be installed?  [7 marks]

1Step 1 — Use the two given data to set up equations

$A(1)=ab^{2}=254\,100$  
and  $A(2)=ab^{4}=307\,461$.

Substitute $n=1$ and $n=2$ into $A(n)=ab^{\,2n}$.
2Step 2 — Solve for $a$ and $b$
Laws of Indices (eliminating a constant) \(\dfrac{ab^{4}}{ab^{2}}=b^{4-2}=b^{2}\) Dividing the two equations cancels \(a\), leaving one equation in \(b\).

Dividing: $\dfrac{ab^{4}}{ab^{2}}=b^{2}=\dfrac{307\,461}{254\,100}=1.21$, so $b=1.1$.

Then $a(1.21)=254\,100 \Rightarrow a=210\,000$.

Positive constants, so take the positive root $b=1.1$ (reject $b=-1.1$).
3Step 3 — Weight handled by $X$ in the 4th year

$A(4)=ab^{8}=(210\,000)(1.1)^{8}=450\,153.6501$ tonnes.

4th year $\Rightarrow n=4$, exponent $2n=8$. Keep 4 decimal places.
4Step 4 — Total weight handled by $X$ in first $n$ years (a GP sum)
Sum of a Geometric Sequence \(S_{n}=\dfrac{a\,(r^{n}-1)}{r-1}\quad(r\neq1)\) Here the first term is \(ab^{2}\), the common ratio is \(b^{2}\) and there are \(n\) terms.

$A(1)+A(2)+\cdots+A(n)=ab^{2}+ab^{4}+\cdots+ab^{2n}$

$=\dfrac{ab^{2}\left(b^{2n}-1\right)}{b^{2}-1} =\dfrac{210\,000(1.1)^{2}\left((1.1)^{2n}-1\right)}{1.1^{2}-1} =1\,210\,000\left((1.1)^{2n}-1\right)$ tonnes.

Geometric series: first term $ab^{2}$, common ratio $b^{2}=1.21$, $n$ terms.
5Step 5 — (b)(i) Compare $B(m)$ and $A(m+4)$

$A(4)=450\,153.65$ and $2a=420\,000$,
so $A(4)=B(1)>B(1)$?
In fact $A(4)>2a=B(1)$.

Also $B(m)=2ab^{m}$ and $A(m+4)=ab^{2(m+4)}=(1.1)^{2m}A(4)$.

Since $A(4)>2a$ and $(1.1)^{2m}>(1.1)^{m}$ for every positive integer $m$:

$A(m+4)=(1.1)^{2m}A(4)>(1.1)^{2m}(2a)>(1.1)^{m}(2a)=2ab^{m}=B(m)$.

In the $m$th year of $Y$, terminal $X$ is in its $(m+4)$th year — the shift of 4 years matters.
6Step 6 — Conclusion of (b)(i)

Therefore $B(m)agreed.

Each year $Y$ handles strictly less than $X$. ✔
7Step 7 — (b)(ii) Total weight handled by $Y$ up to year $n$ of $X$

$Y$ operates in the $4$th … $n$th year of $X$,
so $m=1,2,\dots,n-4$ and

$\displaystyle\sum = 2ab+2ab^{2}+\cdots+2ab^{\,n-4} =\dfrac{2ab\left(b^{\,n-4}-1\right)}{b-1} =\dfrac{420\,000(1.1)\left((1.1)^{\,n-4}-1\right)}{1.1-1}$ tonnes, where $n>4$.

GP with first term $2ab$, ratio $b=1.1$, number of terms $n-4$.
8Step 8 — Set up the inequality (exceeds 20 000 000)

$1\,210\,000\left((1.1)^{2n}-1\right)+\dfrac{420\,000(1.1)\left((1.1)^{\,n-4}-1\right)}{1.1-1}>20\,000\,000$

Sum of $X$ (first $n$ years) plus sum of $Y$ (years $4$ to $n-1$) must exceed the threshold.
9Step 9 — Simplify to a quadratic in $x=(1.1)^{n}$
Substitution to Reduce Degree \(x=(1.1)^{n}\;\Longrightarrow\;(1.1)^{2n}=x^{2}\) This turns the exponential inequality into a quadratic in \(x>0\).

$12\left((1.1)^{2n}\right)+462\left((1.1)^{\,n-4}\right)-2\,583>0$

$12\left((1.1)^{n}\right)^{2}+462(1.1)^{n}-2\,583(1.1)^{4}>0$

Multiply through by $1.1^{4}$ and collect terms; let $x=(1.1)^{n}>0$.
10Step 10 — Solve for $n$
Taking Logarithms \(b^{n}>K\;\Longrightarrow\;n>\dfrac{\log K}{\log b}\quad(b>1)\) Logs turn the exponent into a multiplier; round up because \(n\) must be a positive integer.

$(1.1)^{n}>3.496831134$  
(and reject the negative root).

$n\log 1.1>\log 3.496831134 \Rightarrow n>13.13455888$.

Taking logarithms; $n$ must be a positive integer → $n\ge 14$.
11Step 11 — Final answer for (b)(ii)

The new facilities should be installed in the 14th year since the start of the operation of $X$.

Smallest integer satisfying the strict inequality.
