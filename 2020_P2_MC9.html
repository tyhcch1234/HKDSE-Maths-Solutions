2020 Paper 2 — Question 9

Topic: Percentages

Percentage Profit Percentage Change Unknown Percentage

Question

The cost of a toy is \(x\%\) lower than its selling price. After selling the toy, the percentage profit is \(25\%\). Find \(x\).

  • A. 20
  • B. 25
  • C. 75
  • D. 80

Visualise the Situation

Drag the slider to see how the cost compares with the selling price. The gap between them is the profit.

Selling Price= 100% of \(S\)
Cost= 80% of \(S\)

Profit \(= S - \text{Cost} = S - S(1-x\%) = S \times x\%\).

Percentage profit \(= \dfrac{\text{Profit}}{\text{Cost}} \times 100\% = \dfrac{x\%}{1-x\%} \times 100\%\).

We need: \(\dfrac{x\%}{1-x\%} = 25\% = 0.25\) ⇒ \(x = 20\).

Step-by-Step Solution

Let \(S\) be the selling price of the toy.

💡 Hint — Why choose the selling price?

The question says the cost is “lower than its selling price”, and the profit is measured against the cost. Taking the selling price as the base \(S\) makes the algebra simple: the cost is written as a fraction of \(S\).

“The cost is \(x\%\) lower than the selling price” means:

\(\text{Cost} = S \times (1 - x\%)\)

💡 Hint — Percentage decrease formula

For a percentage decrease of \(x\%\): New value = Original value × \((1 - x\%)\).

Here the “original value” is the selling price \(S\). So if \(x = 20\), the cost is \(0.8S\) (80% of the selling price).

Percentage profit is calculated based on the cost:

\(\text{Percentage profit} = \dfrac{\text{Selling price} - \text{Cost}}{\text{Cost}} \times 100\%\)

Given that the percentage profit is \(25\%\):

\(\dfrac{S - S(1-x\%)}{S(1-x\%)} = 25\%\)

💡 Hint — Profit percentage denominator

A very common mistake is dividing the profit by the selling price. The percentage profit is always divided by the cost (the money invested).

Simplify the numerator:

\(\dfrac{S - S(1-x\%)}{S(1-x\%)} = \dfrac{S\bigl[1-(1-x\%)\bigr]}{S(1-x\%)} = \dfrac{x\%}{1-x\%}\)

So the equation becomes:

\(\dfrac{x\%}{1-x\%} = 25\% = 0.25\)

Cross-multiply:

\(x\% = 0.25\,(1 - x\%)\)

\(x\% + 0.25\,x\% = 0.25\)

\(1.25\,x\% = 0.25\)

\(x\% = 0.2 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 20\)

💡 Hint — Careful with the symbol \(x\%\)

Remember that \(x\%\) means \(\dfrac{x}{100}\). If you prefer, replace every \(x\%\) by \(0.01x\) and solve; the answer is the same: \(x = 20\).

If the selling price is \(S = 100\):

  • Cost \(= 100 \times (1 - 20\%) = 80\)
  • Profit \(= 100 - 80 = 20\)
  • Percentage profit \(= \dfrac{20}{80} \times 100\% = 25\%\) ✓

Therefore \(x = 20\), which is option A.

Key Formulas Used

Percentage decrease

\(\text{New} = \text{Original} \times (1 - r\%)\)

Percentage profit

\(\dfrac{\text{Selling price} - \text{Cost}}{\text{Cost}} \times 100\%\)
