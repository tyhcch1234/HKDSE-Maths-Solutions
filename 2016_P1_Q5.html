Recreation Club Members

In a recreation club, there are $180$ members and the number of male members is $40\%$ more than the number of female members. Find the difference of the number of male members and the number of female members.
[4 marks]
Step 1Define the unknowns

Let $x$ be the number of male members and $y$ be the number of female members.

Hint: Always name the two unknown quantities first. Choose letters and state clearly what each stands for.
Step 2Set up the two equations
Simultaneous Linear Equations in Two Unknowns\(\begin{cases}x+y=180\\ x=1.4y\end{cases}\)The total number of members and the percentage relation supply two equations for \(x\) and \(y\).
Percentage Increase\(\text{new value}=(1+r\%)\times\text{original value}\)“\(40\%\) more than \(y\)” is the multiplier \(1.4\), so \(x=1.4y\).

Total members:   $x + y = 180$

“$40\%$ more than” means $\;x = (1 + 40\%)\,y$

$$ \begin{cases} x+y = 180 \\[4pt] x = (1+40\%)y \end{cases} $$
Hint: “$40\%$ more than $y$” is $1.4y$, not $0.4y$. The multiplier is $1 + 40\% = 1.4$.
Step 3Substitute to get one equation in $y$
Method of Substitution\(x=1.4y\ \Rightarrow\ 1.4y+y=180\)Replacing \(x\) by \(1.4y\) leaves a single equation in \(y\), giving \(2.4y=180\).

Put $x = 1.4y$ into $x + y = 180$:

$$ 1.4y + y = 180 $$ $$ 2.4y = 180 $$
Hint: Substitution removes one unknown, leaving a single linear equation to solve.
Step 4Solve for $x$ and $y$ $$ y = \frac{180}{2.4} = 75 $$ $$ x = 1.4 \times 75 = 105 $$
Hint: Check: $105 + 75 = 180$ ✓ and $105 = 1.4 \times 75$ ✓
Step 5Find the required difference

Difference $= x - y = 105 - 75 = 30$.

Hint: The question asks for the difference, so subtract the two values (a positive value is expected).
Answer: $\;30$  (i.e. there are $105$ male and $75$ female members, so the difference is $30$).

Question. In a recreation club there are \(225\) members and the number of female members is \(20\%\) less than the number of male members. Find the difference between the number of female members and the number of male members. [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \(x\) be the number of male members and \(y\) the number of female members.
Step 2. Total: \(x+y=225\). “\(20\%\) less than” means the multiplier \(1-0.2=0.8\),
so \(y=0.8x\).
Step 3. Substitute: \(x+0.8x=1.8x=225\), so \(x=125\).
Step 4. Then \(y=0.8\times125=100\). Check: \(125+100=225\) ✓ and \(100=0.8\times125\) ✓.
Step 5. Difference \(=x-y=125-100=25\).
Answer: \(25\) \ (125 male, 100 female)
