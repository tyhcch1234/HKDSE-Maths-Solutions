Interactive Figure — \(y=f(x)\) and \(G:y=f(x)+3\)

\(y=f(x)=3(x+4)^2-3\) \(G:y=f(x)+3\) key points

Step 1 — Given: \(f(-3)=0\) and \(f(2)=105\).

HKDSE 2021 Paper 1 — Q10

Partial Variation

It is given that \(f(x)\) is partly constant and partly varies as \((x+4)^2\). Suppose that \(f(-3)=0\) and \(f(2)=105\).

(a) Find \(f(0)\).  [3 marks]

(b) Denote the graph of \(y=f(x)+3\) by \(G\).
   (i) Write down the \(y\)-intercept of \(G\).
   (ii) Find the \(x\)-intercept(s) of \(G\).  [3 marks]

1Set up the model (part (a))

“Partly constant and partly varies as \((x+4)^2\)” means

\[f(x)=m(x+4)^2+n,\]

where \(m\) and \(n\) are non-zero constants.

Marks 1M — state the correct form of the function.
2Use the two given conditions

Substitute \(f(-3)=0\):

\[m(-3+4)^2+n = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; m+n=0 \quad (1)\]

Substitute \(f(2)=105\):

\[m(2+4)^2+n = 105 \;\Rightarrow\; 36m+n=105 \quad (2)\]

Marks 1M — form a pair of simultaneous equations.
3Solve for \(m\) and \(n\)

From (1): \(n=-m\). Substitute into (2):

\[36m-m=105 \;\Rightarrow\; 35m=105 \;\Rightarrow\; m=3\]

Then \(n=-3\). So

\[f(x)=3(x+4)^2-3.\]

Careful arithmetic: \(36-1=35\).
4Find \(f(0)\) — answer to (a)

\[f(0)=3(0+4)^2-3 = 3(16)-3 = 48-3 = 45\]

1A — correct value of \(f(0)\).
5Part (b)(i) — \(y\)-intercept of \(G\)

The graph \(G\) is \(y=f(x)+3\), so its \(y\)-intercept is

\[f(0)+3 = 45+3 = 48.\]

1M — recognising the vertical shift of \(+3\).
6Part (b)(ii) — \(x\)-intercept(s) of \(G\)

Set \(y=0\): \(f(x)+3=0 \;\Rightarrow\; f(x)=-3\).

\[3(x+4)^2-3 = -3 \;\Rightarrow\; 3(x+4)^2=0\]

\[(x+4)^2=0 \;\Rightarrow\; x=-4.\]

So \(G\) has exactly one \(x\)-intercept at \((-4,0)\).

1M — solve \(f(x)+3=0\); 1A — \(x=-4\).
Answer:
(a) \(f(0)=45\)
(b)(i) \(y\)-intercept of \(G\) is \(48\) (i.e. \((0,48)\))
(b)(ii) \(x\)-intercept of \(G\) is \(-4\) (i.e. \((-4,0)\))
