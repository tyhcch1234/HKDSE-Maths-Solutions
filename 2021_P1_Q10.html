HKDSE Math 2021 Paper 1 Q10

Rate, Ratio & Variations

Question Overview

It is given that \( f(x) \) is partly constant and partly varies as \( (x+4)^2 \). Suppose \( f(-3) = 0 \) and \( f(2) = 105 \).

(a) Find \( f(0) \). [3 marks]

(b) Denote the graph of \( y = f(x) + 3 \) by \( G \).

(i) Write down the \( y \)-intercept of \( G \).

(ii) Find the \( x \)-intercept(s) of \( G \). [3 marks]

Step-by-Step Guided Solution

1 Model the variation

“Partly constant and partly varies as \( (x+4)^2 \)” translates to:

\[ f(x) = m(x+4)^2 + n \]

where \( m \) and \( n \) are non-zero constants.

💡 Hint

The coefficient of variation is \( m \), and the constant part is \( n \).

2 Set up simultaneous equations

Use the given conditions \( f(-3) = 0 \) and \( f(2) = 105 \):

\( f(-3) = m(-3+4)^2 + n = m(1)^2 + n = m + n = 0 \)

\( f(2) = m(2+4)^2 + n = m(6)^2 + n = 36m + n = 105 \)

💡 Hint

Substitute the \( x \) values into the template from Step 1.

3 Solve for \( m \) and \( n \)

From \( m+n=0 \), we get \( n = -m \).

Substitute into \( 36m + n = 105 \):

\( 36m + (-m) = 105 \Rightarrow 35m = 105 \Rightarrow m = 3 \)

Then \( n = -3 \).

Thus, \( f(x) = 3(x+4)^2 - 3 \).

4 Find \( f(0) \) (Part a)

Substitute \( x = 0 \) into \( f(x) = 3(x+4)^2 - 3 \):

\( f(0) = 3(0+4)^2 - 3 = 3(16) - 3 = 48 - 3 = 45 \)

Answer for (a): \( 45 \)

5 \( y \)-intercept of \( G \) (Part b(i))

\( G \) is the graph of \( y = f(x) + 3 \).

The \( y \)-intercept is the value of \( y \) when \( x = 0 \):

\( y = f(0) + 3 = 45 + 3 = 48 \)

Answer for (b)(i): \( 48 \)

💡 Hint

Adding 3 to the function shifts the whole graph vertically upwards by 3 units.

6 \( x \)-intercept(s) of \( G \) (Part b(ii))

The \( x \)-intercept(s) occur when \( y = 0 \):

\( f(x) + 3 = 0 \)

\( 3(x+4)^2 - 3 + 3 = 0 \)

\( 3(x+4)^2 = 0 \)

\( (x+4)^2 = 0 \)

\( x = -4 \)

Answer for (b)(ii): \( x = -4 \)

Final Answers

  • (a) \( f(0) = 45 \)
  • (b)(i) The \( y \)-intercept of \( G \) is \( 48 \).
  • (b)(ii) The \( x \)-intercept of \( G \) is \( -4 \).
