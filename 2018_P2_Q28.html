HKDSE 2018 · Paper 2 · Q28
Two numbers are randomly drawn at the same time from seven cards numbered $1$, $1$, $1$, $2$, $2$, $3$ and $4$ respectively. Find the probability that the sum of the numbers drawn is $5$.
A. $\dfrac{5}{21}$
B. $\dfrac{5}{42}$
C. $\dfrac{5}{49}$
D. $\dfrac{10}{49}$

Step-by-step solution

1Identify the cards
The seven cards are: three “$1$”s, two “$2$”s, one “$3$” and one “$4$”. Label the cards as $1_1, 1_2, 1_3$ (the “1”s), $2_1, 2_2$ (the “2”s), then $3$ and $4$ — even identical-looking cards are distinguishable physical cards.
Hint: label repeated cards so that every physical card can be counted separately.
2Count all possible outcomes
Two cards are drawn at the same time, so order does not matter — we choose $2$ cards out of $7$: \[ {}^{7}C_{2}=\frac{7\times 6}{2}=21 . \] So the total number of possible outcomes is $21$.
Hint: “at the same time” ⇒ a combination, not a permutation.
3Find the favourable outcomes
A sum of $5$ can be made in exactly two ways: \[ \{1,4\}\quad\text{or}\quad\{2,3\}. \] Count the pairs on the cards:
  • $\{1,4\}$: $3$ cards of “$1$” $\times$ $1$ card of “$4$” $=3$ pairs  $\{1_1,4\},\{1_2,4\},\{1_3,4\}$.
  • $\{2,3\}$: $2$ cards of “$2$” $\times$ $1$ card of “$3$” $=2$ pairs  $\{2_1,3\},\{2_2,3\}$.
Hence the number of favourable outcomes is $3+2=5$.
Hint: “$1+4=5$” and “$2+3=5$” — check every card combination for each.
4Compute the probability
\[ P=\frac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}} =\frac{5}{21}. \] This matches option A.
Hint: keep the denominator $21$ (a combination) — using $42$ would wrongly count order.
✓Answer: A  $\dfrac{5}{21}$
