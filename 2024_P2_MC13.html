HKDSE Math 2024 Paper 2 Q13 Percentages Variation Percentage Change

It is given that \( z \) varies directly as the square of \( x \) and inversely as \( y \).

If \( x \) is increased by \( 20\% \) and \( y \) is decreased by \( 20\% \), then \( z \)

A. is increased by 20%.
B. is decreased by 20%.
C. is increased by 80%.
D. is decreased by 80%.

Correct answer: C

🧩 Step-by-step solution

1

Express the variation as an equation.

Given: \(z\) varies directly as \(x^2\) and inversely as \(y\). So

\[ z = \frac{kx^2}{y} \]

where \(k\) is a non-zero constant.

2

Write the new value \(z'\\) after changes.

\(x\) is increased by \(20\%\), so new \(x = (1+20\%)x = 1.2x\). \(y\) is decreased by \(20\%\), so new \(y = (1-20\%)y = 0.8y\). Then

\[ z' = \frac{k(1.2x)^2}{0.8y} \]

3

Simplify the expression for \(z'\\).

Remember \((1.2x)^2 = 1.44x^2\). So

\[ z' = \frac{1.44kx^2}{0.8y} = 1.8 \cdot \frac{kx^2}{y} = 1.8z \]

4

Calculate percentage change.

Percentage change in \(z\) =

\[ \frac{z' - z}{z} \times 100\% = \frac{1.8z - z}{z} \times 100\% = 0.8 \times 100\% = 80\% \]

Since the change is positive, \(z\) is increased by \(80\%\).

Answer: C

💡 Hint: Variation formulas

• Direct variation with \(x^2\): \(z \propto x^2\) (as \(x\) increases, \(z\) increases).

• Inverse variation with \(y\): \(z \propto \frac{1}{y}\) (as \(y\) increases, \(z\) decreases).

• Combined: \(z = \frac{kx^2}{y}\), where \(k\) is the constant of variation.

• Percentage change = \(\frac{\text{new} - \text{original}}{\text{original}} \times 100\%\).
