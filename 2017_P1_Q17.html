HKDSE Math 2017 Paper 1 Q17 Probability Combinations

Probability of Drawing Pens

In a bag, there are 4 green pens, 7 blue pens and 8 black pens. If 5 pens are randomly drawn from the bag at the same time,

  1. find the probability that exactly 4 green pens are drawn; [2 marks]
  2. find the probability that exactly 3 green pens are drawn; [2 marks]
  3. find the probability that not more than 2 green pens are drawn. [2 marks]
#combinations #probability #without replacement #complementary counting

🎯 Key Concept: Combinations

We draw 5 pens simultaneously. The total number of ways to choose 5 pens from 19 is \( C^{19}_{5} \). For each case, we count how many ways to choose the required green pens and the remaining pens from the other colours.

Visual representation: 4 green, 7 blue, 8 black pens in a bag.

Step 1 — Total number of ways to choose 5 pens

From 19 pens, choose any 5: \[ C^{19}_{5} \]

Step 2 — Number of ways to choose exactly 4 green pens

Choose 4 green from 4 green: \[ C^{4}_{4} \]

Choose the remaining 1 pen from the 15 non-green pens (7 blue + 8 black): \[ C^{15}_{1} \]

Step 3 — Compute the probability

\[ P(\text{exactly 4 green}) = \frac{C^{4}_{4}C^{15}_{1}}{C^{19}_{5}} \]

\[ = \frac{1 \times 15}{11628} = \frac{5}{3876} \]

✅ Final Answer

\[ \boxed{\frac{5}{3876}} \]

Step 1 — Total ways remains the same

\[ C^{19}_{5} \]

Step 2 — Choose exactly 3 green pens

Choose 3 green from 4: \[ C^{4}_{3} \]

Choose remaining 2 pens from the 15 non-green: \[ C^{15}_{2} \]

Step 3 — Compute the probability

\[ P(\text{exactly 3 green}) = \frac{C^{4}_{3}C^{15}_{2}}{C^{19}_{5}} \]

\[ = \frac{4 \times 105}{11628} = \frac{420}{11628} = \frac{35}{969} \]

✅ Final Answer

\[ \boxed{\frac{35}{969}} \]

Step 1 — Use complementary counting

"Not more than 2 green pens" means 0, 1, or 2 green pens.

Easier to subtract the complementary events: exactly 3 and exactly 4 green pens.

\[ P(\text{not more than 2}) = 1 - P(3\text{ green}) - P(4\text{ green}) \]

Step 2 — Substitute the values from parts (a) and (b)

\[ = 1 - \frac{35}{969} - \frac{5}{3876} \]

Note: \(\frac{35}{969} = \frac{140}{3876}\)

Step 3 — Compute the final probability

\[ = 1 - \frac{140}{3876} - \frac{5}{3876} \]

\[ = 1 - \frac{145}{3876} = \frac{3876 - 145}{3876} = \frac{3731}{3876} \]

✅ Final Answer

\[ \boxed{\frac{3731}{3876}} \]
