HKDSE 2017 Paper 1 — Q17 · Probability
In a bag, there are 4 green pens, 7 blue pens and 8 black pens. If 5 pens are randomly drawn from the bag at the same time,
  1. find the probability that exactly 4 green pens are drawn; [2 marks]
  2. find the probability that exactly 3 green pens are drawn; [2 marks]
  3. find the probability that not more than 2 green pens are drawn. [2 marks]
Step 1Count the total number of pens.
Total pens $=4+7+8=19$.
Since all 5 pens are drawn at the same time, the order does not matter — we use combinations.
Total ways of choosing 5 pens from 19: \[ \binom{19}{5} \]
Hint: "at the same time" ⇒ unordered selection ⇒ $\binom{n}{r}$ (not permutations).
Step 2(a) Exactly 4 green pens.
Choose 4 green pens from the 4 green pens, and the remaining 1 pen from the $7+8=15$ non-green pens:
\[ P(\text{exactly 4 green}) = \frac{\binom{4}{4}\binom{15}{1}}{\binom{19}{5}} \] \[ = \frac{1\times 15}{11\,628} = \frac{5}{3\,876} \]
Hint: the 5 drawn pens = 4 green + 1 other. "Other" means blue or black ⇒ 15 pens to choose from.
Step 3(b) Exactly 3 green pens.
Choose 3 green pens from 4, and the remaining 2 pens from the 15 non-green pens:
\[ P(\text{exactly 3 green}) = \frac{\binom{4}{3}\binom{15}{2}}{\binom{19}{5}} \] \[ = \frac{4\times 105}{11\,628} = \frac{420}{11\,628} = \frac{35}{969} \]
Hint: split the selection into "green part" and "non-green part", then multiply the combinations.
Step 4(c) Not more than 2 green pens — use the complement.
"Not more than 2 green" $= 1 - P(3\text{ green or more})$.
From (a) and (b): $P(4\text{ green}) = \dfrac{5}{3\,876}$ and $P(3\text{ green}) = \dfrac{35}{969}$.
\[ P(\text{not more than 2 green}) = 1 - \frac{5}{3\,876} - \frac{35}{969} \]
Hint: $\dfrac{35}{969} = \dfrac{140}{3\,876}$, so the fractions share the same denominator.
Step 5Simplify. \[ 1 - \frac{5}{3\,876} - \frac{140}{3\,876} = \frac{3\,876 - 5 - 140}{3\,876} = \frac{3\,731}{3\,876} \]
Hint: the complement method avoids listing the many cases 0, 1, 2 green pens.
(a) $\dfrac{5}{3\,876}$
(b) $\dfrac{35}{969}$
(c) $\dfrac{3\,731}{3\,876}$
Answer: exactly 4 green  =  5/3876  ·  exactly 3 green  =  35/969  ·  not more than 2 green  =  3731/3876
