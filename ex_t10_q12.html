Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Area

T10 Q12

Find the area of a trapezium with parallel sides $6\text{ cm}$ and $10\text{ cm}$ and height $4\text{ cm}$.

Solution

Use the trapezium area formula: $A=\frac{1}{2}(a+b)h$.
Substitute $a=6$, $b=10$, and $h=4$: $A=\frac{1}{2}(6+10)(4)$.
Simplify: $A=\frac{1}{2}(16)(4)=32$.
The area of the trapezium is $32\text{ cm}^2$.
