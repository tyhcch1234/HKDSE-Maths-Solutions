Mensuration — area of a sector built from a chord & perpendicular distances from the centre.

Question Full text

Read the figure carefully — the trick is to find the radius and the sector angle.

In the figure, \(O\) is the centre of the sector \(OABCD\). \(AD\) and \(OC\) are perpendicular to each other and intersect at the point \(E\). \(F\) is a point lying on \(AD\) such that \(BF\) is perpendicular to \(AD\). If \(AF = 9\text{ cm}\), \(DF = 39\text{ cm}\) and \(OE = 18\text{ cm}\), then the area of the sector \(OBC\) is

A. \(\;48\pi\text{ cm}^2\)
B. \(\;75\pi\text{ cm}^2\)
C. \(\;96\pi\text{ cm}^2\)
D. \(\;150\pi\text{ cm}^2\)

Find the radius \(OA=OB\) and the angle \(\angle BOG\), then apply \(A=\pi r^2\times\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\).

Part (a) — Find the radius \(OA\) Pythagoras

Use the fact that the perpendicular from the centre \(O\) to the chord \(AD\) bisects \(AD\).

Step 11M
\[AD=AF+DF=9+39=48\text{ cm}\] \[AE=DE=\frac{AD}{2}=\frac{48}{2}=24\text{ cm}\]
Hint: \(OE\perp AD\), and the perpendicular from the centre of a circle to a chord bisects the chord. So \(E\) is the midpoint of \(AD\).
Step 21M
In the right-angled triangle \(OAE\): \[OA^2=AE^2+OE^2=24^2+18^2\] \[OA^2=576+324=900\]
Hint: \(OE\perp AD\) makes \(\angle OEA=90^\circ\), so Pythagoras' theorem applies directly.
Step 31A
\[OA=\sqrt{900}=30\text{ cm}\]
Hint: The radius is exactly \(30\) cm — a nice number, which is a good sign.
Radius of the sector: \(OA=OB=OC=OD=30\text{ cm}\).

Step 11M
Let \(G\) be the point on \(OC\) such that \(BG\perp OC\). Then \(BGEF\) is a rectangle, so \[BG=EF=AE-AF=24-9=15\text{ cm}\]
Hint: \(BF\perp AD\) and \(OE\perp AD\), so \(BF \parallel OE\). Also \(BG\perp OC\) and \(EF\perp OC\). Thus \(BGEF\) has four right angles — a rectangle — giving \(BG=EF\).
Step 21M
In the right-angled triangle \(OBG\): \[\sin\angle BOG=\frac{BG}{OB}=\frac{15}{30}=\frac12\]
Hint: \(OB=30\) cm is the radius found in part (a). The side opposite \(\angle BOG\) is \(BG=15\) cm, so use sine.
Step 31A
\[\angle BOG=30^\circ\]
Hint: \(\sin 30^\circ=\tfrac12\), so the sector angle of the green sector \(OBC\) is \(30^\circ\).
Sector angle: \(\angle BOC=\angle BOG=30^\circ\).

Part (c) — Area of sector \(OBC\) Final answer

Combine the radius and angle: \(A=\pi r^2\times\dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\).

Step 11M
\[\text{Area of sector }OBC=\pi r^2\times\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} =\pi(30)^2\times\frac{30^\circ}{360^\circ}\]
Hint: A full circle is \(360^\circ\), so a \(30^\circ\) sector is \(\frac{30}{360}=\frac{1}{12}\) of the circle.
Step 21A
\[=\pi(900)\times\frac{1}{12}=75\pi\text{ cm}^2\]
Hint: \(900\div 12 = 75\), so the area is \(75\pi\text{ cm}^2\).
Area of sector \(OBC = 75\pi\text{ cm}^2\) → the answer is B.

Summary of the solution

Every quantity needed is linked by the radius \(OA\) and the sector angle.

  • 1\(AD = AF+DF = 9+39 = 48\); the perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord, so \(AE = 24\).
  • 2Pythagoras in \(\triangle OAE\): \(OA=\sqrt{24^2+18^2}=\sqrt{900}=\mathbf{30\text{ cm}}\) — the radius.
  • 3Rectangle \(BGEF\) gives \(BG=EF=24-9=\mathbf{15\text{ cm}}\).
  • 4\(\sin\angle BOG=\dfrac{15}{30}=\dfrac12 \Rightarrow \angle BOC=\mathbf{30^\circ}\).
  • 5Area \(=\pi(30)^2\times\dfrac{30}{360}=\mathbf{75\pi\text{ cm}^2}\).
Correct option: B  ·  \(75\pi\text{ cm}^2\)
