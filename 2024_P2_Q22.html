Question

In the figure, ABCDE is a circle. AC and BE are diameters of the circle. Let P be the point of intersection of AC and BD. If $\angle ABE = 46^\circ$ and $\angle DBE = 16^\circ$, then $\angle APD =$

A. $108^\circ$    B. $110^\circ$    C. $112^\circ$    D. $114^\circ$

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1 · Locate the centre O

Since AC and BE are both diameters of the circle, they must meet at the centre $O$ of the circle — and they bisect each other there.

So $OA = OB = OC = OE = r$ (all radii).

💡 Every diameter passes through the centre — so the intersection of two diameters is the centre.
STEP 2 · Isosceles triangle OAB
Base Angles of an Isosceles Triangle \(OA=OB\ \Rightarrow\ \angle OAB=\angle OBA\) Both are radii, so \(\angle OAB=\angle OBA=46^\circ\).

In $\triangle OAB$, $OA = OB$ (radii), so the base angles are equal:

$$\angle OAB = \angle OBA = 46^\circ$$

(base $\angle$s,
isos. $\triangle$)

STEP 3 · Exterior angle of △OAB
Exterior Angle of a Triangle \(\angle POB=\angle OAB+\angle OBA\) The exterior angle at \(O\) equals the sum of the two remote interior angles: \(46^\circ+46^\circ=92^\circ\).

$P$ lies on the line $OB$, so $\angle POB$ is the exterior angle of $\triangle OAB$ at vertex $O$:

$$\angle POB = \angle OBA + \angle OAB \quad (\text{ext. } \angle \text{ of } \triangle)$$ $$= 46^\circ + 46^\circ = 92^\circ$$
STEP 4 · Exterior angle of △PBD

In $\triangle PBD$, the angle $\angle APD$ (at $P$) is the exterior angle opposite the two remote interior angles $\angle POB$ and $\angle DBE$:

$$\angle APD = \angle POB + \angle DBE \quad (\text{ext. } \angle \text{ of } \triangle)$$ $$= 92^\circ + 16^\circ = 108^\circ$$
💡 $P$ lies on $AC$ and on $BD$; the line $OP$ extended is the same line $OB$. So the exterior angle at $P$ equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles of $\triangle PBD$.
✅ Answer:   $\angle APD = 108^\circ$   →   Option A

Question. \(ABCDE\) is a circle whose diameters \(AC\) and \(BE\) meet at the centre \(O\). \(P\) is the intersection of \(AC\) and \(BD\). If \(\angle ABE=52^\circ\) and \(\angle DBE=20^\circ\), find \(\angle APD\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(OA=OB\), so \(\angle OAB=\angle OBA=52^\circ\).
Step 2. Exterior angle of \(\triangle OAB\): \(\angle POB=52^\circ+52^\circ=104^\circ\).
Step 3. Exterior angle of \(\triangle PBD\): \(\angle APD=\angle POB+\angle DBE=104^\circ+20^\circ=124^\circ\).
Answer: \(124^\circ\)
