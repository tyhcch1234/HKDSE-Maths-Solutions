The Question

A bag contains 7 red balls, 3 yellow balls and 5 black balls. A child repeats drawing one ball at a time randomly from the bag without replacement until a black ball is drawn. Find the probability that the child needs at most three draws.

Bag (15 balls):

💡 Core Idea — "At Most" → Think Complement

"Needs at most 3 draws" means the first black ball appears on draw 1, 2 or 3. Instead of adding three separate cases, it is much cleaner to use the complement rule:

\(P(\text{at most 3 draws}) = 1 - P(\text{more than 3 draws})\)

"More than 3 draws" means the first three balls are all non-black (red or yellow).

STEP BY STEP Guided Solution

Total balls: \(7 + 3 + 5 = 15\).

A draw is a "success" the moment a black ball is drawn.

We want at most 3 draws: the first black ball appears on draw \(1\), \(2\) or \(3\).

Hint · Complement Formula

\(P(\text{at most 3}) = 1 - P(\text{more than 3 draws})\)

The child needs more than 3 draws ⟺ the first three balls drawn are all non-black.

Non-black balls = red + yellow = \(7 + 3 = 10\).

RRY … any 3 of the 10 non-black balls.

Consider choosing the first 3 balls from the 15 balls.

Total ways: \(\displaystyle C^{15}_{3}\)

Favourable ways (all non-black, chosen from the \(10\) non-black balls): \(\displaystyle C^{10}_{3}\)

\(\displaystyle P(>3)= \frac{C^{10}_{3}}{C^{15}_{3}} = \frac{120}{455} = \frac{24}{91}\)

Hint · Combination Formula

\(C^{n}_{r} = \dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}\) — ways of choosing \(r\) objects from \(n\) without order.

Because we draw without replacement, each numerator and denominator decreases by 1.

  1. Draw 1 non-black: \(\dfrac{10}{15}\)
  2. Draw 2 non-black: \(\dfrac{9}{14}\)
  3. Draw 3 non-black: \(\dfrac{8}{13}\)

\(\displaystyle P(>3)= \frac{10}{15}\cdot\frac{9}{14}\cdot\frac{8}{13} = \frac{24}{91}\)

Hint

Independent events multiply: \(P(A\cap B\cap C)=P(A)\,P(B|A)\,P(C|A\cap B)\).

Required probability:

\(\displaystyle 1 - \frac{24}{91} = \frac{91-24}{91} = \frac{67}{91}\)

✅ Answer: Option D   \(\dfrac{67}{91}\)

Key Takeaway

When a question says "at most \(k\)" or "at least \(k\)", first check whether the complement gives a shorter calculation. Here "more than 3 draws" collapses three cases into one: the first three balls are all non-black.

\(P = 1 - \dfrac{10}{15}\cdot\dfrac{9}{14}\cdot\dfrac{8}{13} = 1 - \dfrac{24}{91} = \dfrac{67}{91}\)
