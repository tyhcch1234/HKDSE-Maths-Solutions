HKDSE 2021 Paper 2 — Q43

Topic 14 · Probability (drawing without replacement)

A bag contains $7$ red balls, $3$ yellow balls and $5$ black balls. A child repeats drawing one ball at a time randomly from the bag without replacement until a black ball is drawn. Find the probability that the child needs at most three draws.

A. $\dfrac{4}{7}$
B. $\dfrac{5}{9}$
C. $\dfrac{19}{27}$
D. $\dfrac{67}{91}$

Step-by-step solution

Step 1Read off the bag. Total balls $=7+3+5=15$. Only the $5$ black balls stop the process,
so the non-black balls are $10$ in number. Hint: “needs at most 3 draws” = a black ball appears in draw 1,
2 or 3.
Step 2Use the complement. Counting the complement is much easier: the child needs more than $3$ draws exactly when the first three balls drawn are all non-black. $$P(>3\text{ draws}) = ?$$ Hint: “more than 3 draws” happens iff the first 3 draws are red or yellow.
Step 3Method 1.1 — count combinations.
Combination Formula \(C_{r}^{n}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}\) The first three balls are any \(3\) of the \(15\), so \(C_{3}^{15}=455\); all non-black gives \(C_{3}^{10}=120\).
Total ways of choosing the first $3$ balls: $C_3^{15}$. Favourable ways (all non-black): $C_3^{10}$. $$P(>3\text{ draws})=\frac{C_3^{10}}{C_3^{15}}=\frac{120}{455}=\frac{24}{91}.$$ Hint: $C_3^{10}=\frac{10\cdot9\cdot8}{6}=120$, $C_3^{15}=\frac{15\cdot14\cdot13}{6}=455$.
Step 4Method 1.2 — multiply the fractions.
Multiplication Rule for Drawing without Replacement \(P=\dfrac{10}{15}\times\dfrac{9}{14}\times\dfrac{8}{13}\) Each non-black ball removed lowers both the favourable and the total count, giving the same \(\dfrac{24}{91}\).
$$\frac{10}{15}\times\frac{9}{14}\times\frac{8}{13}=\frac{24}{91}.$$ Hint: the numerators 10, 9, 8 decrease because each non-black ball removed is not replaced.
Step 5Take the complement.
Complement Rule \(P(E)=1-P(E')\) 'At most \(3\) draws' is the complement of 'more than \(3\) draws', so \(1-\dfrac{24}{91}=\dfrac{67}{91}\).
$$P(\le 3\text{ draws}) = 1 - P(>3\text{ draws}) = 1-\frac{24}{91}=\frac{67}{91}.$$ Hint: subtract from 1 — the complement of “at most 3” is “more than 3”.
Answer: D  $\dfrac{67}{91}$

Question. A bag contains \(5\) red balls, \(4\) yellow balls and \(6\) black balls. A child draws one ball at a time at random without replacement until a black ball is drawn. Find the probability that the child needs at most three draws.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Total balls \(=5+4+6=15\), of which \(15-6=9\) are not black.
Step 2. The child needs more than \(3\) draws exactly when the first three balls are all non-black.
Step 3. \(P(>3\text{ draws})=\dfrac{C_{3}^{9}}{C_{3}^{15}}=\dfrac{84}{455}=\dfrac{12}{65}\).
Step 4. By the complement rule, \(P(\le3\text{ draws})=1-\dfrac{12}{65}=\dfrac{53}{65}\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{53}{65}\)
