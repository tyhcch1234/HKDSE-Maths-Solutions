Geometry (106) · Sub-Topic: Circle Properties

T11 Q27

The angle at the circumference of a circle subtended by an arc is half the angle at the centre subtended by the same arc. If the angle at the centre is $140°$, find the angle at the circumference.

Solution

The angle at the circumference is half the angle at the centre subtended by the same arc.
Angle at circumference $=\frac{1}{2}\times 140^\circ$.
Angle at circumference $=70^\circ$.
The angle at the circumference is $70^\circ$.
