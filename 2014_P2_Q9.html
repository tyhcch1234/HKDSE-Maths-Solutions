2014 MC #9 1. Percentages

There are 792 workers in a factory. If the number of male workers is 20% less than that of female workers, then the number of male workers is

  • A. 352.
  • B. 360.
  • C. 432.
  • D. 440.

1Set up the unknown

Let the number of female workers be $F$ (the larger group).
Male workers $= F \times (1-20\%) = 0.8F$
Hint: “$x$ is 20% less than $y$” means $x = y(1-20\%)$. Anchor the unknown on the reference amount (the female count).

2Write the total equation

The two groups together give 792 workers:
$F + 0.8F = 792$
So male $= 0.8F$, female $= F$, and their sum is the whole factory.
Hint: Always form one equation linking the parts to the given total.

3Solve for $F$

Combine like terms:
$1.8F = 792 \;\Longrightarrow\; F = \dfrac{792}{1.8} = 440$
So there are 440 female workers.
Hint: $1+0.8 = 1.8$ — a quick mental divide: $792 \div 1.8 = 7920 \div 18 = 440$.

4Find the male workers

Male $= 0.8 \times 440 = 352$
Hint: The question asks for the male count — do not stop at $F = 440$ (that is the distractor D).

5Check

$352 + 440 = 792$ ✓  and  $\dfrac{440-352}{440} = \dfrac{88}{440} = 20\%$ ✓
The male number is indeed 20% less than the female number.
Hint: A one-line check guards against picking the wrong unknown.
ANSWER A. 352
