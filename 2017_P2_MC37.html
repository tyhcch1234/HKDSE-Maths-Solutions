HKDSE Math 2017 Paper 2 Q37 Linear Programming Inequalities

Greatest Value in a Feasible Region

Question

Consider the following system of inequalities:

\[ \begin{cases} y \le 9 \\ x - y - 9 \le 0 \\ x + y - 9 \ge 0 \end{cases} \]

Let \(R\) be the region which represents the solution of the above system of inequalities. If \((x, y)\) is a point lying in \(R\), then the greatest value of \(x - 2y + 43\) is

A. 25
B. 43
C. 52
D. 61

Step-by-Step Solution

The system of inequalities gives three boundary lines:

  • \(y = 9\) (horizontal line)
  • \(x - y - 9 = 0\) (rearranged: \(y = x - 9\))
  • \(x + y - 9 = 0\) (rearranged: \(y = -x + 9\))
💡 Hint: The inequalities \(y \le 9\), \(x - y - 9 \le 0\), and \(x + y - 9 \ge 0\) define the half-planes.

Solve the boundary lines in pairs to find the vertices:

\(y=9\) and \(x-y-9=0\)
\(x - 9 - 9 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 18\)
\(\Rightarrow (18, 9)\)
\(x-y-9=0\) and \(x+y-9=0\)
Adding: \(2x - 18 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 9\)
Then \(9 - y - 9 = 0 \Rightarrow y = 0\)
\(\Rightarrow (9, 0)\)
\(x+y-9=0\) and \(y=9\)
\(x + 9 - 9 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 0\)
\(\Rightarrow (0, 9)\)
💡 Hint: These are the three vertices of the triangular region \(R\).

The greatest value of a linear expression on a closed polygonal region is attained at a corner point.

Point \((x, y)\) Value of \(x - 2y + 43\)
\((18, 9)\) \(18 - 2(9) + 43 = 43\)
\((9, 0)\) \(9 - 2(0) + 43 = 52\)
\((0, 9)\) \(0 - 2(9) + 43 = 25\)
💡 Hint: Compare the three values: \(43\), \(52\), and \(25\).

The greatest value is \(\mathbf{52}\), attained at the point \((9, 0)\).

Correct Option C. 52

Key Takeaway

  • For a linear objective function over a polygonal feasible region, the maximum (or minimum) occurs at a corner point.
  • Always test all corner points to compare values.
  • The feasible region is the intersection of all half-planes defined by the inequalities.
