HKDSE Math 2024 · Paper 1 · Question 4

Inequalities & Integer Solutions

📐 Linear Inequalities 🔢 Integer Solutions 📊 Number Line ✏️ 4 marks

📋 Question

(a) Find the range of values of \(x\) which satisfy both \(\dfrac{5x+7}{4}-1 < 2x\) and \(3x+9 \ge 0\). 3 marks
(b) Write down the least integer satisfying both inequalities in (a). 1 mark

🧩 Step-by-step Solution

1
Solve the first inequality
▼

Start with the first inequality and clear the fraction:

\[ \frac{5x+7}{4} - 1 < 2x \]

Multiply both sides by \(4\) (positive, so no flip):

\[ 5x + 7 - 4 < 8x \]
\[ 5x + 3 < 8x \]

Subtract \(5x\) from both sides:

\[ 3 < 3x \]

Divide by \(3\):

\[ 1 < x \quad \text{i.e. } x > 1 \]
💡 Hint Why multiply by 4? It removes the denominator, making the inequality easier to solve. Since \(4 > 0\), the inequality sign stays the same.
2
Solve the second inequality
▼

The second inequality is:

\[ 3x + 9 \ge 0 \]

Subtract \(9\) from both sides:

\[ 3x \ge -9 \]

Divide by \(3\) (positive, so no flip):

\[ x \ge -3 \]
💡 Hint Remember: When dividing by a negative number, the inequality sign reverses. Here we divide by \(+3\), so the sign stays \(\ge\).
3
Combine the two inequalities
▼

We need values of \(x\) that satisfy both conditions:

\[ x > 1 \quad \text{and} \quad x \ge -3 \]

The stricter condition is \(x > 1\). Any \(x > 1\) automatically satisfies \(x \ge -3\).

So the required range is:

\[ \boxed{x > 1} \]
💡 Hint Intersection: On a number line, the overlap of \(x > 1\) (open circle at 1, arrow right) and \(x \ge -3\) (closed circle at -3, arrow right) is just the region starting just after 1.
4
Find the least integer (Part b)
▼

From part (a), the solution is \(x > 1\).

The integers greater than \(1\) are \(2, 3, 4, \dots\)

The least (smallest) integer satisfying this is:

\[ \boxed{2} \]
💡 Hint The open circle at \(x = 1\) means \(1\) itself is not included. So the first integer we can take is \(2\).
✅ Answer: \(2\)

📝 Key Takeaway

  • Solve each inequality separately first.
  • When multiplying/dividing by a negative number, flip the inequality sign.
  • The combined solution is the intersection of the two solution sets.
  • For integer solutions, check the boundary carefully (open vs closed circle).
Final range: \(x > 1\) · Least integer: \(2\)
