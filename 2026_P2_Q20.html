QUESTION
In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a rectangle. Let \(E\) be a point on \(BC\). The extension of \(AB\) and the extension of \(DE\) meet at point \(F\). If \(AB=3\text{ cm}\), \(BE=12\text{ cm}\) and \(CE=4\text{ cm}\), then \(EF=\)
A. \(13\) cm
B. \(15\) cm
C. \(16\) cm
D. \(20\) cm
STEP 1 · Find AD
Opposite sides of a rectangle are equal, so
\[ AD=BC=BE+CE=12+4=16\text{ cm} \]
STEP 2 · Similar triangles
Since \(BE\parallel AD\), \(\triangle FBE\sim\triangle FAD\). Hence
\[ \frac{FB}{FA}=\frac{BE}{AD}=\frac{12}{16}=\frac{3}{4} \]
With \(FA=FB+AB=FB+3\):
\[ \frac{FB}{FB+3}=\frac{3}{4}\;\Rightarrow\;4FB=3FB+9\;\Rightarrow\;FB=9\text{ cm} \]
STEP 3 · Pythagoras in △FBE
Since \(\angle FBE=90^\circ\),
\[ EF=\sqrt{BE^2+FB^2}=\sqrt{12^2+9^2}=\sqrt{144+81}=\sqrt{225}=15\text{ cm} \]
Tip: recognise the \(9\text{-}12\text{-}15\) (i.e. \(3\text{-}4\text{-}5\)) right triangle.
Final Answer: B  (\(EF=15\) cm)
