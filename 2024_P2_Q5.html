Question. The price of 2 apples and 3 lemons is \$38. If the price of 3 apples and 2 lemons is \$47, then the price of 4 apples and 7 lemons is

A. \$70    B. \$80    C. \$90    D. \$100

STEP 1Set up the variables and the two equations.
Let \(x\) = price of an apple, \(y\) = price of a lemon (in dollars). \[ 2x+3y = 38 \quad \cdots (1) \] \[ 3x+2y = 47 \quad \cdots (2) \] Hint: each "bundle" of fruit gives one linear equation.
STEP 2Eliminate one variable.
\((1)\times 3:\; 6x+9y = 114\)
\((2)\times 2:\; 6x+4y = 94\)
Subtract: \(\;5y = 20\;\Rightarrow\; y = 4.\) Hint: match the coefficients of \(x\), then subtract to remove \(x\).
Elimination Method \(\text{scale the equations, then subtract to remove one unknown}\) \((1)\times5\) and \((2)\times2\) make the \(y\)-coefficients equal, so subtracting leaves an equation in \(x\) only.
STEP 3Back-substitute to find the other price.
Put \(y=4\) into \((1):\; 2x+12=38\;\Rightarrow\;2x=26\;\Rightarrow\;x=13.\) Hint: check in (2): \(3(13)+2(4)=39+8=47\) ✔
Back-Substitution \(\text{put the known value into the simpler original equation}\) Substituting \(y=6\) into \(3x+2y=39\) gives \(3x=27\), so \(x=9\).
STEP 4Find the required cost \(4x+7y\).
\[ 4x+7y = 4(13)+7(4) = 52+28 = 80 \] Hint: substitute both prices only at the last step to avoid rounding errors.
Answer: B. \$80

Question. Three apples and two lemons cost \(\$39\), while two apples and five lemons cost \(\$48\). Find the cost of five apples and three lemons.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let an apple cost \(\$x\) and a lemon cost \(\$y\). Then \(3x+2y=39\) and \(2x+5y=48\).
Step 2. Eliminate \(y\): multiply the first equation by \(5\) and the second by \(2\), giving \(15x+10y=195\) and \(4x+10y=96\).
Step 3. Subtracting: \(11x=99\), so \(x=9\).
Step 4. Back-substitute into \(3x+2y=39\): \(27+2y=39\), so \(y=6\). Check: \(2(9)+5(6)=48\) ✓.
Step 5. The required cost is \(5x+3y=45+18=\$63\).
Answer: \(\$63\)
