HKDSE Math 2025 Paper 1 Q1

Laws of Indices Positive Indices Simplification
Q

Simplify \[ \frac{(x^4 y^{-5})^3}{x y^2} \] and express your answer with positive indices.

(3 marks)

1 Step-by-Step Guided Solution

Click each step to reveal the next part of the reasoning.

1 Remove the outer exponent on the numerator ▼

Apply the power rule: \((a^m)^n = a^{mn}\) to each factor inside the bracket.

\[ \frac{(x^4 y^{-5})^3}{x y^2} = \frac{x^{4 \times 3} \, y^{-5 \times 3}}{x y^2} = \frac{x^{12} y^{-15}}{x y^2} \]
💡 Hint Remember: \((x^a y^b)^n = x^{a n} y^{b n}\). Here \(4 \times 3 = 12\) and \(-5 \times 3 = -15\).
2 Apply the quotient rule to the \(x\) terms ▼

Use \(\frac{x^a}{x^b} = x^{a-b}\) for the \(x\) part:

\[ \frac{x^{12}}{x} = x^{12 - 1} = x^{11} \]
💡 Hint Recall that \(x = x^1\). So \(x^{12} \div x^1 = x^{12-1} = x^{11}\).
3 Apply the quotient rule to the \(y\) terms ▼

Simplify \(\frac{y^{-15}}{y^2}\) using \(\frac{y^a}{y^b} = y^{a-b}\):

\[ \frac{y^{-15}}{y^{2}} = y^{-15 - 2} = y^{-17} \]
💡 Hint Subtract the exponent in the denominator from the exponent in the numerator: \(-15 - 2 = -17\).
4 Express with positive indices ▼

Use the negative index rule: \(a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}\).

\[ x^{11} y^{-17} = \frac{x^{11}}{y^{17}} \]
💡 Hint Move the \(y^{-17}\) to the denominator to make the index positive.
Final Answer
\[ \boxed{\frac{x^{11}}{y^{17}}} \]

(All indices are positive)
