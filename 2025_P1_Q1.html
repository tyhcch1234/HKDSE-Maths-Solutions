Step 1 · Given expression with negative index \(y^{-5}\)

HKDSE 2025 · Paper 1 · Q1

Laws of Indices — Simplify with Positive Indices

Question. Simplify $\;\dfrac{\left(x^{4}y^{-5}\right)^{3}}{xy^{2}}\;$ and express your answer with positive indices. (3 marks)

Step-by-step solution

1Apply the power of a product

Raise every factor inside the bracket to the power 3:

\[ \left(x^{4}y^{-5}\right)^{3}=x^{4\times 3}\,y^{-5\times 3}=x^{12}y^{-15} \]

Hint: \(\left(a^{m}b^{n}\right)^{k}=a^{mk}b^{nk}\) — multiply the indices.
2Combine using the quotient rule

The expression becomes

\[ \frac{x^{12}y^{-15}}{xy^{2}} = x^{12-1}\,y^{-15-2} = \frac{x^{11}}{y^{17}} \]

Hint: \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\); subtract the “bottom” index from the “top” index.
3Rewrite with positive indices

Any negative index moves the base across the fraction bar:

\[ x^{11}y^{-17}=\frac{x^{11}}{y^{17}} \]

Hint: \(a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^{n}}\). The final answer must contain no negative indices.
Answer: \(\;\dfrac{x^{11}}{y^{17}}\;\) (all indices positive)
