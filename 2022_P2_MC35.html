HKDSE 2022 Paper 2 Q35

Complex Numbers & Equating Real & Imaginary Parts

Complex Numbers Real & Imaginary Parts Powers of i Solving Linear Equations

Question: Let \( z = 4 + 5i^{10} - ki^{15} + 6i^{21} + 2ki^{28} \), where \( k \) is a real number. If the real part and the imaginary part of \( z \) are equal, then the real part of \( z \) is

A. 7
B. 13
C. 17
D. 25

Step-by-step Solution

Step 1 of 5
1 Simplify powers of \(i\)

Recall the cyclic pattern of powers of \(i\):

\[ i^1 = i,\quad i^2 = -1,\quad i^3 = -i,\quad i^4 = 1,\quad i^5 = i,\ \ldots \]

Any power \(i^n\) can be reduced modulo 4. Let's simplify each term:

\[ \begin{aligned} i^{10} &= i^{4\cdot2 + 2} = (i^4)^2 \cdot i^2 = 1^2 \cdot (-1) = -1, \\[4pt] i^{15} &= i^{4\cdot3 + 3} = (i^4)^3 \cdot i^3 = 1^3 \cdot (-i) = -i, \\[4pt] i^{21} &= i^{4\cdot5 + 1} = (i^4)^5 \cdot i^1 = 1^5 \cdot i = i, \\[4pt] i^{28} &= i^{4\cdot7} = (i^4)^7 = 1^7 = 1. \end{aligned} \]

Hint: Divide the exponent by 4 and take the remainder: \(n \bmod 4\) gives the effective power.
2 Substitute and group real / imaginary parts

Substitute the simplified powers into \(z\):

\[ \begin{aligned} z &= 4 + 5(-1) - k(-i) + 6(i) + 2k(1) \\ &= 4 - 5 + ki + 6i + 2k \\ &= (2k - 1) + (k + 6)i. \end{aligned} \]

Thus:

\[ \text{Real part} = 2k - 1, \qquad \text{Imaginary part} = k + 6. \]

Hint: Collect all real terms (numbers) together, and all \(i\) terms together. The coefficient of \(i\) is the imaginary part.
3 Set real part = imaginary part

The condition says the real part and the imaginary part are equal:

\[ 2k - 1 = k + 6. \]

Solve for \(k\):

\[ \begin{aligned} 2k - k &= 6 + 1 \\ k &= 7. \end{aligned} \]

Hint: Simply equate the two expressions and solve the resulting linear equation.
4 Find the real part

We need the real part of \(z\), which is \(2k - 1\). Substitute \(k = 7\):

\[ \text{Real part} = 2(7) - 1 = 14 - 1 = 13. \]

Therefore, the real part is \(13\).

Check: The imaginary part would be \(7 + 6 = 13\) as well, confirming they are equal.
5 Conclusion

Since the real part equals the imaginary part and we found \(k = 7\), the real part is \(13\).

Answer: B. 13

Well done! You have solved the problem by simplifying powers of \(i\), grouping real and imaginary parts, and solving a linear equation.
