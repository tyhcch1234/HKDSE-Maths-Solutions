Mensuration — Cylinder & Cone

If the volume of a right circular cylinder of base radius $5a$ cm and height $7b$ cm is $525\ \text{cm}^3$, then the volume of a right circular cone of base radius $7a$ cm and height $5b$ cm is

  • A. $175\ \text{cm}^3$
  • B. $245\ \text{cm}^3$
  • C. $490\ \text{cm}^3$
  • D. $735\ \text{cm}^3$
Step 1 · Use the cylinder to find $a^2b$
Volume of a Cylinder \(V=\pi r^{2}h\) The cylinder has radius \(5a\) and height \(7b\), so \(175\pi a^{2}b=525\).

$$\text{Volume of cylinder}=\pi r^2h=\pi(5a)^2(7b)=525$$

$$25a^2\cdot 7b\cdot\pi=525 \;\Rightarrow\; 175\,\pi a^2b = 525$$

$$\boxed{\,\pi a^2b = \dfrac{525}{175}=3\,}$$

Step 2 · Write the cone volume formula
Volume of a Cone \(V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}h\) The cone has radius \(7a\) and height \(5b\), giving \(V=\dfrac{245}{3}\pi a^{2}b\).

$$\text{Volume of cone}=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2h =\frac{1}{3}\pi(7a)^2(5b)$$

$$=\frac{1}{3}\pi\cdot 49a^2\cdot 5b =\frac{245}{3}\,\pi a^2b$$

Tip: the cone radius is $7a$ (not $5a$), and remember the factor $\tfrac13$ for a cone.
Step 3 · Substitute $\pi a^2b=3$

$$V_{\text{cone}}=\frac{245}{3}\,(\pi a^2b) =\frac{245}{3}\times 3 = 245$$

Answer: B  ·  $245\ \text{cm}^3$

Question. A cylinder of radius \(3a\) and height \(4b\) has volume \(288\text{ cm}^{3}\). Find the volume of a cone of radius \(4a\) and height \(3b\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Cylinder: \(\pi(3a)^{2}(4b)=36\pi a^{2}b=288\Rightarrow \pi a^{2}b=8\).
Step 2. Cone: \(V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi(4a)^{2}(3b)=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi\cdot16a^{2}\cdot3b=16\pi a^{2}b\).
Step 3. Substitute \(\pi a^{2}b=8\): \(V=16\times8=128\text{ cm}^{3}\).
Answer: \(128\ \text{cm}^{3}\)
