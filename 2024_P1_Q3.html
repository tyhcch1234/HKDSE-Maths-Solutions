Question

3. Factorize

(a) $\; 6r^2 - 13rs - 28s^2$,
(b) $\; 4r - 14s + 6r^2 - 13rs - 28s^2$. (3 marks)
1
Part (a) — set up the factorization

We want to write \[6r^2 - 13rs - 28s^2 = (\,?\,r \;?\;\; ?\,s)(\,?\,r \;?\;\; ?\,s)\] Since $6 = \mathbf{2 \times 3}$ and $28 = \mathbf{4 \times 7}$,
try coefficients $2,3$ for $r$ and $4,7$ for $s$.

Hint: the two brackets share the pattern $(2r - 7s)$ and $(3r + 4s)$.
2
Part (a) — cross-check the middle term
Cross-multiplication Check \((2r-7s)(3r+4s)=6r^{2}+8rs-21rs-28s^{2}\) The middle terms \(8rs-21rs\) give \(-13rs\), matching the given expression.

Expand $(2r - 7s)(3r + 4s)$:

\[ \begin{aligned} (2r)(3r) &= 6r^2 \\ (2r)(4s) + (-7s)(3r) &= 8rs - 21rs = -13rs \\ (-7s)(4s) &= -28s^2 \end{aligned} \]

So the product is exactly $6r^2 - 13rs - 28s^2$.

Hint: the $rs$ terms combine to $-13rs$ — matches the given expression.
3
Part (a) — answer

\[ \boxed{\,6r^2 - 13rs - 28s^2 = (2r - 7s)(3r + 4s)\,} \]

Hint: this is worth 1 mark (1A).
4
Part (b) — take out the common factor $2$
Factorising by Grouping \(4r-14s+6r^{2}-13rs-28s^{2}=2(2r-7s)+(2r-7s)(3r+4s)\) Taking \(2\) out of the first two terms creates the repeated bracket \((2r-7s)\).

Group the four terms and factor out $2$ from the first two:

\[ 4r - 14s + 6r^2 - 13rs - 28s^2 = 2(2r - 7s) + \bigl(6r^2 - 13rs - 28s^2\bigr) \]

By part (a),
the bracket factorizes as $(2r - 7s)(3r + 4s)$:

\[ = 2(2r - 7s) + (2r - 7s)(3r + 4s) \]
Hint: the factor $(2r - 7s)$ now appears in both terms.
1M for this grouping/factorization step.
5
Part (b) — extract the common bracket
Common Bracket as a Factor \(2(2r-7s)+(2r-7s)(3r+4s)=(2r-7s)(2+3r+4s)\) The bracket \((2r-7s)\) appears in both terms, so it is taken out as the common factor.

Take $(2r - 7s)$ out as a common factor:

\[ = (2r - 7s)\bigl[\,2 + (3r + 4s)\,\bigr] = (2r - 7s)(2 + 3r + 4s) \]
Hint: simplify the bracket — 1A for the final answer.
Answer

(a) $\;6r^2 - 13rs - 28s^2 = \mathbf{(2r - 7s)(3r + 4s)}$

(b) $\;4r - 14s + 6r^2 - 13rs - 28s^2 = \mathbf{(2r - 7s)(2 + 3r + 4s)}$

Question. Factorize
(a) \(8r^{2}+2rs-15s^{2}\),
(b) \(8r-10s+8r^{2}+2rs-15s^{2}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. (a) Try the coefficients \(8=4\times 2\) and \(15=5\times 3\) with opposite signs: \((4r-5s)(2r+3s)\).
Step 2. Check the middle term: \(12rs-10rs=2rs\) ✓, and the ends are \(8r^{2}\) and \(-15s^{2}\). So \(8r^{2}+2rs-15s^{2}=(4r-5s)(2r+3s)\).
Step 3. (b) Group the first two terms: \(8r-10s=2(4r-5s)\), and by (a) the rest is \((4r-5s)(2r+3s)\).
Step 4. Hence \(8r-10s+8r^{2}+2rs-15s^{2}=(4r-5s)\bigl[2+(2r+3s)\bigr]=(4r-5s)(2+2r+3s)\).
Answer: (a) \((4r-5s)(2r+3s)\); (b) \((4r-5s)(2+2r+3s)\)

Marks:
(a) 1A  ·  (b) 1M + 1A  =  total 3 marks.
