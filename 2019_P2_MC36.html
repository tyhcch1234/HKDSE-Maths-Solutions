HKDSE Math 2019 Paper 2 — Question 36

Formulas and Sequences

Geometric Sequence Common Ratio System of Equations
📘

Question

The sum of the 2nd term and the 5th term of a geometric sequence is 9 while the sum of the 7th term and the 10th term of the sequence is 288. Find the 20th term of the sequence.

A

65 536

B

131 072

C

262 144

D

524 288

🧩 Step-by-Step Solution

1

Define the terms of the geometric sequence

Use the first term $a$ and common ratio $r$.

▼

In a geometric sequence, the $n$-th term is:

$T_n = a r^{n-1}$

So the required terms are:

  • $T_2 = a r$
  • $T_5 = a r^4$
  • $T_7 = a r^6$
  • $T_{10} = a r^9$
2

Form two equations from the given sums

Translate the word problem into algebra.

▼

Sum of 2nd and 5th term is 9:

$ar + ar^4 = 9$ … (1)

Sum of 7th and 10th term is 288:

$ar^6 + ar^9 = 288$ … (2)

3

Eliminate $a$ by dividing the equations

Factorise and divide (2) by (1).

▼

Factorise the left-hand sides:

$ar(1 + r^3) = 9$ … (1)

$ar^6(1 + r^3) = 288$ … (2)

Divide (2) by (1):

$\dfrac{ar^6(1 + r^3)}{ar(1 + r^3)} = \dfrac{288}{9}$

$r^5 = 32$

4

Find the common ratio $r$

Solve $r^5 = 32$.

▼

Since $32 = 2^5$, we have:

$r = 2$

(For a geometric sequence with positive terms, we take the positive real root.)

5

Find the first term $a$

Substitute $r = 2$ into equation (1).

▼

Substitute $r = 2$ into $ar + ar^4 = 9$:

$a(2) + a(2^4) = 9$

$2a + 16a = 9$

$18a = 9$

$a = \dfrac{1}{2}$

6

Calculate the 20th term

Use $T_n = a r^{n-1}$ with $n = 20$.

▼

Substitute $a = \frac{1}{2}$, $r = 2$, $n = 20$:

$T_{20} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 2^{20-1} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 2^{19}$

$= 2^{18}$

$= 262\,144$

Therefore, the answer is C.

Final Answer

C. 262 144
