Mensuration — Area Ratios in a Parallelogram Diagram
Parallelogram Similar Triangles (AAA) Ratio of Areas Common Height Congruent Triangles

QQuestionMultiple Choice

In the figure, \(ABCD\) and \(BEDF\) are parallelograms. \(E\) is a point lying on \(BC\) such that \(BE:EC=2:3\). \(AC\) cuts \(BF\) and \(DE\) at \(G\) and \(H\) respectively. If the area of \(\Delta ABG\) is \(135\text{ cm}^2\), then the area of the quadrilateral \(DFGH\) is

A. \(60\text{ cm}^2\)
B. \(81\text{ cm}^2\)
C. \(90\text{ cm}^2\)
D. \(144\text{ cm}^2\ \checkmark\)

Big idea: reduce the whole figure to a scale factor. The given \(BE:EC=2:3\) forces \(AG:GC=3:5\), which lets us grow \(\Delta ABG\) into \(\Delta ABC\), then into the whole parallelogram, and finally into the target quadrilateral.

Step 11M Angle chase for similarity

In \(\Delta AFG\) and \(\Delta CBG\):

\[ \angle AGF=\angle CGB \quad(\text{vert. opp. }\angle\text{s}) \] \[ \angle GAF=\angle GCB \quad(\text{alt. }\angle\text{s},\ AD/\!/ BC) \] \[ \angle GFA=\angle GBC \quad(\text{alt. }\angle\text{s},\ AF/\!/ BC) \]
Hint: Because \(AD/\!/ BC\), the line \(AC\) acts as a transversal — that gives the alternate angles. Two pairs of equal angles are already enough; the third confirms it.
\(\therefore \Delta AFG \sim \Delta CBG\) (AAA) \(\;\Rightarrow\; AG:GC=AF:CB\).
Step 21M Replace \(AF\) using the parallelograms

In parallelogram \(ABCD\): \(AD=CB\). In parallelogram \(BEDF\): \(FD=EB\).

\[ AF=AD-FD=CB-EB=CE \] Since \(BE:EC=2:3\), we get \(CE=\frac{3}{5}CB\). \[ AF:CB=CE:CB=3:5 \]
Hint: \(BEDF\) being a parallelogram is exactly what makes \(FD=EB\) — it lets you convert the segment \(AF\) into something on \(BC\).
\(AG:GC=AF:CB=3:5\) (corr. sides, \(\sim\Delta\)s)
Step 31A Same height ⇒ areas in the ratio \(AG:GC\)

\(\Delta ABG\) and \(\Delta CBG\) share the same altitude from \(B\) to the line \(ACG\). Hence

\[ \frac{[ABG]}{[CBG]}=\frac{AG}{GC}=\frac{3}{5} \]
Hint: Triangles with the same height have areas proportional to their bases. Always hunt for a "common height" pair — this is the standard HKDSE trick.
\([ABG]:[CBG]=3:5\), and \([ABC]=[ABG]+[CBG]\).

Step 11M Area of \(\Delta ABC\)

\(\Delta ABC\) and \(\Delta ABG\) have the common height from \(B\) to the line \(ACG\), so

\[ \frac{[ABC]}{[ABG]}=\frac{AC}{AG}=\frac{3+5}{3}=\frac{8}{3} \] \[ \therefore [ABC]=135\times\frac{8}{3}=360\text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: \(AG:GC=3:5 \Rightarrow AG:AC=3:8\), so \(AC/AG=8/3\). Fractions of a base become multipliers on the area.
\([ABC]=360\text{ cm}^2\)
Step 21M Area of parallelogram \(ABCD\)

Diagonal \(AC\) splits \(ABCD\) into the two congruent triangles \(\Delta ABC\) and \(\Delta CDA\).

\[ [ABCD]=2[ABC]=2\times 360=720\text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: A diagonal of any parallelogram halves it. So the parallelogram is always exactly twice the triangle on the same base with the same height.
\([ABCD]=720\text{ cm}^2\)
Step 31M Area of \(BEDF\) via common height on \(BC\)

\(BEDF\) and \(ABCD\) share the same height relative to the line \(BC\) (both rest on \(BC\), and \(DF/\!/ BC\)). Hence areas are in the ratio of the bases:

\[ \frac{[BEDF]}{[ABCD]}=\frac{BE}{BC}=\frac{2}{2+3}=\frac{2}{5} \] \[ \therefore [BEDF]=720\times\frac{2}{5}=288\text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: Base of \(BEDF\) is \(BE\), base of \(ABCD\) is \(BC\). Do not use \(BE:EC\) directly — the denominator must be \(BC=BE+EC\).
\([BEDF]=288\text{ cm}^2\)
Step 41A Halve \(BEDF\) to get \(DFGH\)

In parallelogram \(BEDF\), the quadrilaterals \(DFGH\) and \(BEHG\) are congruent (the diagonal \(EF\) of the parallelogram structure halves it through \(H\) and \(G\)). Therefore

\[ [BEDF]=2[DFGH] \] \[ [DFGH]=\frac{288}{2}=144\text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: Let \(x=[DFGH]\) cm². Then \([BEDF]=2x\), and the earlier ratio gave \(\dfrac{2x}{720}=\dfrac{2}{5}\), i.e. \(x=144\).
\([DFGH]=144\text{ cm}^2 \Rightarrow\) Answer D

Summary of Answers

  • Step 6\([BEDF]=720\times\dfrac{2}{5}=288\text{ cm}^2\)

Answer: D — \(144\text{ cm}^2\)
