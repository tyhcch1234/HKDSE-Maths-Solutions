Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Circle Equation

T12 Q38

Find the centre and radius of the circle with equation \(x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 16 = 0\).

Solution

Rewrite the equation by grouping the $x$ terms: $x^2+6x+y^2-16=0$.
Complete the square for $x$: $x^2+6x=(x+3)^2-9$.
Substitute back: $(x+3)^2-9+y^2-16=0$.
Simplify: $(x+3)^2+y^2=25$.
Compare with the standard form $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$, giving $h=-3$, $k=0$, and $r^2=25$.
Thus the radius is $r=5$.
The centre of the circle is $(-3,0)$ and its radius is $5$.
