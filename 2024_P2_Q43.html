2024MC#43

There are three questions in a test. The probabilities that a student answers the first question correctly, the second question correctly and the third question correctly are $0.6$, $0.7$ and $0.8$ respectively. Find the probability that the student answers at least $1$ question correctly in this test.

  • A. $0.024$
  • B. $0.188$
  • C. $0.812$
  • D. $0.976$

Solution — step by step

1List the given data (independent events)
Multiplication Law for Independent Events \(P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)\) The three attempts are independent, so their probabilities are multiplied.

$$P(Q_1)=0.6,\qquad P(Q_2)=0.7,\qquad P(Q_3)=0.8$$ Since the three answers are independent, $$P(Q_1')=1-0.6=0.4,\quad P(Q_2')=1-0.7=0.3,\quad P(Q_3')=1-0.8=0.2.$$

Hint: “$Q_i'$” means answering question $i$ wrongly.
Use the complement rule $P(E')=1-P(E)$.
2Use the complement: “at least 1 correct” = NOT “all wrong”
Complement Rule \(P(\text{not }A)=1-P(A)\) Turn each success into the probability of failing on that attempt.

Multiplying along the single branch “all wrong”: $$P(\text{all wrong})=P(Q_1')\times P(Q_2')\times P(Q_3')=0.4\times 0.3\times 0.2.$$

Hint: for “all wrong” only one path of the tree works,
so multiply the three failure probabilities together.
3Compute $P(\text{all wrong})$
At Least One \(P(\text{at least one})=1-P(\text{none})\) It is much shorter to find the probability of no success and subtract it from 1.

$$0.4\times 0.3=0.12,\qquad 0.12\times 0.2=0.024.$$ So $P(\text{all wrong})=0.024$.

Hint: this value is answer A — it is a trap!
It is only the “all wrong” case.
4Take the complement to get the final answer

$$\text{Required probability}=1-P(\text{all wrong})=1-(1-0.6)(1-0.7)(1-0.8)$$ $$=1-0.024=0.976.$$

Hint: subtract from 1 — the answer must be larger than any single given probability.
Answer: D. $0.976$

Question. Three archers shoot independently at a target. The probabilities that they hit it are \(0.5\), \(0.6\) and \(0.9\) respectively. Find the probability that at least one of them hits the target.

Show the worked solution
1. The probabilities of all three missing are \(0.5\), \(0.4\) and \(0.1\).
2. By independence, \(P(\text{none hits})=0.5\times0.4\times0.1=0.02\).
3. By the complement rule, \(P(\text{at least one hits})=1-0.02\).
4. Hence the probability is \(0.98\).
Answer: \(0.98\)
