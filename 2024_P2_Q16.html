HKDSE 2024 · PAPER 2 · Q16

10. Mensuration

The ratio of the height of a right circular cylinder to the height of a right circular cone is 32 : 15, while the ratio of the volume of the circular cylinder to the volume of the circular cone is 10 : 9. If the base radius of the circular cylinder is 25 cm, then the base radius of the circular cone is

  • A. 20 cm
  • B. 24 cm
  • C. 48 cm
  • D. 60 cm
Step 1 · Name the Unknown Height
Let the heights be \(32k\) (cylinder) and \(15k\) (cone), so their ratio is exactly \(32:15\). Let \(r\) cm be the base radius of the cone. \[h_{cyl}=32k,\qquad h_{cone}=15k,\qquad r_{cyl}=25,\qquad r_{cone}=r\]
Using a common multiplier \(k\) locks in the height ratio automatically.
Step 2 · Write Down the Two Volumes
\[V_{cyl}= \pi (25)^2 (32k)=20000\pi k\] \[V_{cone}= \tfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 (15k)=5\pi r^2 k\]
Remember: cone volume \(=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h\) — the \(\frac13\) is the key difference!
Step 3 · Apply the Volume Ratio 10 : 9
\[\frac{V_{cyl}}{V_{cone}}=\frac{10}{9}\] \[\frac{20000\pi k}{5\pi r^2 k}=\frac{10}{9}\] The factors \(\pi k\) cancel: \[\frac{4000}{r^2}=\frac{10}{9}\]
Step 4 · Solve for \(r\)
Cross-multiply: \[10\,r^2 = 4000 \times 9 = 36000\] \[r^2 = 3600\] \[r = 60 \quad (\text{length is positive})\]
Check: \(V_{cyl}:V_{cone}=20000\pi k : 5\pi(60)^2 k = 20000 : 18000 = 10:9\) ✓
Base radius of the circular cone = 60 cm  →  Option D Heights 32k : 15k combined with volumes 10 : 9 force r² = 3600.
