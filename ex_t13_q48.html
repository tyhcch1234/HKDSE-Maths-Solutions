Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Applications

T13 Q48

A man stands 80 m from the base of a vertical tower. The angle of elevation of the top of the tower from his position is $20°$. Find the distance from the man to the top of the tower.

Solution

Let $d$ be the distance from the man to the top of the tower, and let $h$ be the height of the tower.
In the right triangle formed, the horizontal distance from the man to the base is adjacent to the $20^\circ$ angle. Thus, $\cos 20^\circ = \frac{80}{d}$.
Rearranging gives $d = \frac{80}{\cos 20^\circ}$.
Using $\cos 20^\circ \approx 0.9397$, we get $d \approx \frac{80}{0.9397} \approx 85.1$ m.
The distance from the man to the top of the tower is approximately $85.1$ m.
