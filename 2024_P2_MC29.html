Statistics — Range & Inter-quartile Range of a Box-and-Whisker Diagram
Box-and-Whisker Diagram Range Inter-quartile Range Linear Equation

Q Question

The box-and-whisker diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of tokens got by a group of children in a game. If the range of the distribution is the triple of its inter-quartile range, find \(m\).

  • A. \(248\)
  • B. \(275\)
  • C. \(336\)
  • D. \(360\)

Diagram values: minimum \(=136\), lower quartile \(Q_1=163\), median \(=224\), upper quartile \(Q_3=m\), maximum \(=472\).

a Reading the Range and the Inter-quartile Range 1M + 1A

Express the range and the inter-quartile range of the distribution in terms of \(m\).
Drag the slider to move \(Q_3 = m\). Watch how the range and the inter-quartile range change.
275
Step 11M

From the box-and-whisker diagram, the smallest value is the left whisker end and the largest value is the right whisker end:

\[ \text{Range} = \text{Maximum} - \text{Minimum} = 472 - 136 = 336 \]
The range depends only on the two whisker ends. It does not involve \(m\) — the upper quartile is inside the box, not at the end.
\(\text{Range} = 336\)
Step 21M

The inter-quartile range is the width of the box, i.e. from \(Q_1\) to \(Q_3\):

\[ \text{Inter-quartile range} = Q_3 - Q_1 = m - 163 \]
\(Q_1 = 163\) is the left edge of the box and \(Q_3 = m\) is the right edge. The IQR is the "middle half" of the data.
\(\text{Inter-quartile range} = m - 163\)
0 / 2

b Setting up the Equation to Find \(m\) 1M + 1A

It is given that the range is the triple of the inter-quartile range. Solve for \(m\).
Adjust \(m\): the range (blue) is fixed at \(336\); the triple of the IQR (amber) grows with \(m\). They match exactly at \(m = 275\).
275
Step 11M

Translate the word condition into an equation. "The range is the triple of the inter-quartile range" means:

\[ \text{Range} = 3 \times \text{Inter-quartile range} \] Substitute the expressions found in part (a): \[ 336 = 3\,(m - 163) \]
Careful — "the triple of the IQR" means multiply the IQR by \(3\), not multiply the range by \(3\). Write \(3(m-163)\), not \(3 \times 336\).
\(3\,(m - 163) = 336\)
Step 21M

Divide both sides by \(3\):

\[ m - 163 = \frac{336}{3} = 112 \]
\(336 \div 3 = 112\) since \(3 \times 112 = 336\). This gives the IQR directly — a useful mental check.
\(\text{Inter-quartile range} = 112\)
Step 31A

Add \(163\) to both sides:

\[ m = 112 + 163 = 275 \]
Check: \(Q_3 = 275\) lies between the median \(224\) and the maximum \(472\) — consistent with a valid box-and-whisker diagram (quartiles must be in increasing order).
\(m = 275\)  ⟹  Answer: B
0 / 3

Σ Summary of Answers

  • Range \(= 472 - 136 = 336\) (depends only on the whisker ends)
  • Inter-quartile range \(= Q_3 - Q_1 = m - 163\) (the width of the box)
  • Condition given: Range \(= 3 \times \text{IQR}\)
  • Equation: \(336 = 3(m - 163)\)
  • Solution: \(m - 163 = 112 \;\Rightarrow\; m = 275\)
Therefore \(m = 275\), i.e. the answer is B.
