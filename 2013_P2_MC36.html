HKDSE Math 2013 Paper 2 Q36

Complex Numbers

The real part of $i+2i^2+3i^3+4i^4$ is

A. $2$
B. $-2$
C. $6$
D. $-6$

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — Recall the powers of $i$

$i^2=-1,\qquad i^3=-i,\qquad i^4=1$

Step 2 — Substitute into the expression

$i+2i^2+3i^3+4i^4=i+2(-1)+3(-i)+4(1)$

$=i-2-3i+4$

Step 3 — Collect the real and imaginary parts

$=(4-2)+(1-3)i=2-2i$

Step 4 — Read off the real part

For $2-2i$, the real part is $2$.

Answer: A. $2$
