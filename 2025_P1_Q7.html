HKDSE Math 2025 Paper 1 Q7

📌 Question

The selling price of a souvenir is $378. The souvenir is sold at a discount of 60% on its marked price. The marked price of the souvenir is 75% above its cost.
(a) Find the marked price of the souvenir.
(b) Find the cost of the souvenir.
(c) Determine whether there is a gain or a loss after selling the souvenir. Explain your answer.
(5 marks)

🧩 Step-by-Step Solution

Click each step to reveal the explanation. Use hints if you get stuck.

1 Understand the discount on marked price ▶

The selling price is $378. This price is after a 60% discount on the marked price.

That means the selling price is 40% of the marked price (since 100% − 60% = 40%).

💡 Hint: Discount percentage is always applied to the marked price. So:
\(\text{Selling Price} = \text{Marked Price} \times (1 - \text{discount}\%)\)
2 Find the marked price (Part a) ▶

Let \(M\) be the marked price. We have:

\(378 = M \times 40\%\)

Solve for \(M\):

\(M = \dfrac{378}{40\%} = \dfrac{378}{0.4} = \dfrac{378 \times 10}{4} = 945\)
✅ Marked price = $945
📘 Note: 40% is the same as \(\frac{2}{5}\). So \(378 \div \frac{2}{5} = 378 \times \frac{5}{2} = 945\).
3 Relate marked price to cost ▶

The marked price is 75% above the cost. So:

\(\text{Marked Price} = \text{Cost} \times (1 + 75\%) = \text{Cost} \times 1.75\)

Equivalently, the marked price is 175% of the cost.

💡 Hint: "75% above cost" means if cost = 100, marked price = 175. So marked price = \(1.75 \times\) cost.
4 Find the cost (Part b) ▶

We know marked price = $945, and marked price = \(1.75 \times\) cost.

\(945 = \text{Cost} \times 1.75\)

So,

\(\text{Cost} = \dfrac{945}{1.75} = \dfrac{945}{\frac{7}{4}} = \dfrac{945 \times 4}{7} = 135 \times 4 = 540\)
✅ Cost = $540
📘 Shortcut: \(1.75 = \frac{7}{4}\), so dividing by 1.75 is the same as multiplying by \(\frac{4}{7}\).
5 Determine gain or loss (Part c) ▶

Compare the selling price and the cost:

  • Selling price = $378
  • Cost = $540

Since \(540 > 378\), the cost is greater than the selling price. Therefore, the shop sells at a loss.

📉 There is a loss after selling the souvenir.
💡 Explanation: Loss occurs when Selling Price < Cost. Here $378 < $540, so it is a loss.
📝

Examiner's tip

Always identify the base (100%) for each percentage. Here, discount is on marked price, and "75% above cost" means cost is the base. Be careful not to confuse marked price with cost.
