HKDSE 2025 · Paper 1 · Q7

The selling price of a souvenir is $\$378$. The souvenir is sold at a discount of $60\%$ on its marked price. The marked price of the souvenir is $75\%$ above its cost.

(a) Find the marked price of the souvenir.

(b) Find the cost of the souvenir.

(c) Determine whether there is a gain or a loss after selling the souvenir. Explain your answer.

(5 marks)

1Set up the variables & the key relationship

Let the marked price be $M$ and the cost be $C$.

Selling price $= 378$, discount $= 60\%$ on $M$:

\[ \text{Selling price} = (1-60\%)\times M = 40\%\times M \]
Paying 60% less means you pay 40% of the marked price.

2(a) Marked price of the souvenir

\[ 378 = 40\%\times M \] \[ M = \frac{378}{40\%} \] \[ M = \frac{378}{0.4} = 945 \]
Divide the selling price by 40% (i.e. multiply by 2.5).

3(b) Cost of the souvenir

The marked price is $75\%$ above the cost, so:

\[ M = (1+75\%)\times C = 175\%\times C \] \[ C = \frac{945}{1+75\%} = \frac{945}{1.75} \] \[ C = 540 \]
"75% above the cost" → marked price is 175% of the cost.

4(c) Gain or loss?

Compare the cost with the selling price:

\[ C = 540 \quad\text{and}\quad \text{Selling price} = 378 \] \[ 540 > 378 \]

So the amount received is less than the cost:

\[ \text{Loss} = 540 - 378 = 162 \]
Selling price < cost ⟹ a loss.
Answer: (a) Marked price $= \$945$  ·  (b) Cost $= \$540$  ·  (c) Since $540 > 378$, there is a loss of $\$162$ after selling the souvenir.
