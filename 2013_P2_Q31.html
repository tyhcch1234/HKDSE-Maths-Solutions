HKDSE 2013 · Paper 2 · Q31 · Polynomials

The L.C.M. of  $a^2+4a+4$ ,  $a^2-4$  and  $a^3+8$  is

  • A. $a+2$
  • B. $(a-2)(a+2)^2(a^2-2a+4)$
  • C. $(a-2)(a+2)^2(a^2+2a+4)$
  • D. $(a-2)(a+2)^4(a^2-2a+4)$
1Factorise each expression
Difference of Two Squares \(a^{2}-b^{2}=(a+b)(a-b)\) \\(a^{2}-4=a^{2}-2^{2}=(a-2)(a+2)\\), which is where the factor \\(a-2\\) comes from.
Sum of Two Cubes \(a^{3}+b^{3}=(a+b)(a^{2}-ab+b^{2})\) With \\(a=a\\) and \\(b=2\\), \\(a^{3}+8=(a+2)(a^{2}-2a+4)\\), so \\((a+2)\\) occurs once here and twice in \\(a^{2}+4a+4\\).

$a^2+4a+4=(a+2)^2$  
— a perfect square (identity $(x+y)^2$).

$a^2-4=(a-2)(a+2)$  
— difference of two squares.

$a^3+8=a^3+2^3=(a+2)(a^2-2a+4)$  
— sum of two cubes.

Hint: recognise the three standard identities — perfect square,
difference of squares, sum of cubes.

2List the distinct factors and their powers
L.C.M. of Algebraic Expressions \(\text{L.C.M.}=f_{1}^{\,m_{1}}f_{2}^{\,m_{2}}\cdots f_{k}^{\,m_{k}}\) Each distinct factor is taken to its highest power in any one expression, so \\((a+2)^{2}\\) and not \\((a+2)^{4}\\).
Factor$a^2+4a+4$$a^2-4$$a^3+8$max power
$a-2$0101
$a+2$2112
$a^2-2a+4$0011

Hint: for an L.C.M. you take the highest power of every common factor.

3Multiply the highest powers together

$\text{L.C.M.}=(a-2)^{1}(a+2)^{2}(a^2-2a+4)^{1}$

$=(a-2)(a+2)^2(a^2-2a+4)$

Hint: check the power of $(a+2)$ carefully — it is 2,
not 4, so option D is out.

4Check the options

A is only one factor — far too small.
C wrongly uses $(a^2+2a+4)$, the factor of $a^3-8$, not $a^3+8$. D uses $(a+2)^4$,
but the largest power is only 2.

The L.C.M. is $(a-2)(a+2)^2(a^2-2a+4)$.

Answer:  B  — $(a-2)(a+2)^2(a^2-2a+4)$

Question. Find the L.C.M. of \\(b^{2}-9\\), \\(b^{2}+6b+9\\) and \\(b^{3}-27\\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \\(b^{2}-9=(b-3)(b+3)\\) by the difference of two squares.
Step 2. \\(b^{2}+6b+9=(b+3)^{2}\\) \u2014 a perfect square.
Step 3. \\(b^{3}-27=b^{3}-3^{3}=(b-3)(b^{2}+3b+9)\\) by the difference of two cubes.
Step 4. The distinct factors are \\(b-3\\), \\(b+3\\) and \\(b^{2}+3b+9\\), with highest powers \\(1\\),
\\(2\\) and \\(1\\).
Step 5. Hence the L.C.M. is \\((b-3)(b+3)^{2}(b^{2}+3b+9)\\).
Answer: \((b-3)(b+3)^{2}(b^{2}+3b+9)\)
