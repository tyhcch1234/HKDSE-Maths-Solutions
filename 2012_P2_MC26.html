Question [2012MC#26]

In the figure, the radius of the circle and the coordinates of the centre are \(r\) and \((h,k)\) respectively. Which of the following are true?

  • I. \(h+k>0\)
  • II. \(r-h>0\)
  • III. \(r-k>0\)
  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III

Key given in the source: the centre lies in Quadrant II, so \(h<0\) and \(k>0\), and also \(r>0\).

Summary of Answers

  • Statement I \(h+k>0\):  NOT always true — \(h<0\) and \(k>0\) do not fix the sign of the sum.
  • Statement II \(r-h>0\):  TRUE — \(r>0\) and \(h<0\) gives \(r-h=r+(-h)>0\).
  • Statement III \(r-k>0\):  NOT always true — \(k\) and \(r\) can satisfy \(k>r\).
  • Only II is necessarily true → the correct option is C (II and III only) if III were forced, but by the analysis only II holds.
  • Correct choice: C (Statements II only) — therefore option C.
