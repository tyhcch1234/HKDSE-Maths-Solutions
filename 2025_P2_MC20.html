Trapezium geometry — using perpendicular feet and trigonometry to find a side length.

Trapezium Perpendicular Feet Trigonometry Angle of Inclination Multiple Choice

Q20 The Question

Paper 2 · Multiple Choice · 1 mark

It is given that \(PQRS\) is a trapezium with \(PS=41\text{ cm}\), \(RS=53\text{ cm}\), \(\angle PSR = 120^\circ\) and \(\angle QRS = 150^\circ\). Then \(PQ=\)

  1. \(94\text{ cm}\)
  2. \(100\text{ cm}\)
  3. \(118\text{ cm}\)
  4. \(135\text{ cm}\)

Key idea: Drop perpendiculars from \(S\) and \(R\) onto \(PQ\). This splits \(PQ\) into three parts: \(PT\), \(TU\) (the “top” base offset) and \(UQ\).

a Drop the two perpendiculars

Setting up the diagram · 1M

Let \(T\) and \(U\) be the feet of perpendiculars from \(S\) and \(R\) to \(PQ\) respectively. This turns the trapezium into two right triangles and a rectangle.

PS = 41 cm  |  RS = 53 cm
Step 1

Add the two perpendicular feet

Draw \(ST \perp PQ\) and \(RU \perp PQ\), with feet \(T\) and \(U\) on \(PQ\).

Since \(PS\) makes \(120^\circ\) with the base \(PS\) direction, the angle between \(PS\) and the base \(PQ\) is \(180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ\).

\[ \angle SPT = 60^\circ \]

Similarly, the angle between \(RQ\) and base \(PQ\) is \(180^\circ - 150^\circ = 30^\circ\), so

\[ \angle RQU = 30^\circ \]
Hint: The given angles \(120^\circ\) and \(150^\circ\) are measured inside the trapezium (between \(PS\) and \(RS\), and between \(QR\) and \(RS\)). The angles with the base \(PQ\) are their supplements.
Result: \(\angle SPT = 60^\circ,\quad \angle RQU = 30^\circ\)
Step 2

Get \(PT\) and \(RU\)

In right triangle \(SPT\) (right angle at \(T\)):

\[ PT = PS\cos 60^\circ = 41 \times \tfrac{1}{2} = 20.5 \text{ cm} \] \[ ST = PS\sin 60^\circ = 41 \times \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{41\sqrt{3}}{2} \text{ cm} \]

Since \(RTUS\) is a rectangle, \(RU = ST = \dfrac{41\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{ cm}\).

Hint: \(RTUS\) is a rectangle because a trapezium has \(PS /\!/ QR\), and both \(ST\) and \(RU\) are perpendicular to \(PQ\). Opposite sides of a rectangle are equal, so \(RU = ST\).
Result: \(PT = 20.5\text{ cm},\quad RU = \dfrac{41\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{ cm}\)
2 steps

b Use the \(30^\circ\) angle to get \(UQ\)

Right triangle \(RQU\) · 1M 1A

In the right triangle \(RQU\), we know the opposite side \(RU\) and need the adjacent side \(UQ\).

RU / tan 30° = 35.48 cm
Step 1

Write the tangent ratio

In right triangle \(RQU\), right angle at \(U\):

\[ \tan\angle RQU = \frac{RU}{UQ} \]
Hint: \(\tan\theta = \dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}\). Here the side opposite to \(\angle RQU\) is \(RU\), and the adjacent side is \(UQ\).
Equation: \(\dfrac{RU}{UQ} = \tan 30^\circ\)
Step 2

Solve for \(UQ\)

\[ UQ = \frac{RU}{\tan 30^\circ} = \frac{\dfrac{41\sqrt{3}}{2}}{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}} = \frac{41\sqrt{3}}{2} \times \sqrt{3} = \frac{41 \times 3}{2} = \frac{123}{2} \text{ cm} \]

As a decimal, \(UQ = 61.5\text{ cm}\).

Hint: \(\tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\). Multiplying by \(\sqrt{3}\) clears the fraction neatly, giving an exact answer.
Result: \(UQ = 61.5\text{ cm}\)
Step 3

Recognise \(TU = RS\)

Because \(RTUS\) is a rectangle (top side \(RS\) is parallel to the base \(PQ\), and \(ST\), \(RU\) are both perpendicular to \(PQ\)):

\[ TU = RS = 53\text{ cm} \]
Hint: A trapezium has only one pair of parallel sides. Since \(RS /\!/ PQ\), the segment \(RS\) is horizontal between the two perpendiculars, so \(TU\) equals \(RS\).
Result: \(TU = 53\text{ cm}\)
3 steps

c Add the three parts of \(PQ\)

Final computation · 1A

The base \(PQ\) is made of three pieces: \(PT\), \(TU\) and \(UQ\).

PT = 20.5   TU = 53   UQ = 61.5   total = 135 cm
Step 1

Decompose \(PQ\)

Along the base, we have:

\[ PQ = PT + TU + UQ \]
Hint: The order along \(PQ\) from \(P\) to \(Q\) is \(P \to T \to U \to Q\). Check that the three segments don't overlap.
Equation: \(PQ = PT + TU + UQ\)
Step 2

Substitute and evaluate

\[ \begin{aligned} PQ &= 41\cos 60^\circ + 53 + \frac{41\sin 60^\circ}{\tan 30^\circ} \\[6pt] &= 20.5 + 53 + 61.5 \\[6pt] &= 135 \text{ cm} \end{aligned} \]
Hint: Use exact values: \(41\cos 60^\circ = 20.5\), and \(\dfrac{41\sin 60^\circ}{\tan 30^\circ} = \dfrac{41 \times (\sqrt{3}/2)}{1/\sqrt{3}} = 61.5\).
Answer: \(PQ = 135\text{ cm}\)  — which is option D.
2 steps

Summary of results

  • PS = 41 cm (given)
  • RS = 53 cm (given)
  • \(\angle SPT\) = \(180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ\)
  • \(\angle RQU\) = \(180^\circ - 150^\circ = 30^\circ\)
  • \(PT\) = \(41\cos 60^\circ = 20.5\) cm
  • \(RU\) = \(41\sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{41\sqrt{3}}{2}\) cm
  • \(UQ\) = \(\dfrac{RU}{\tan 30^\circ} = 61.5\) cm
  • \(TU\) = \(RS = 53\) cm
\(PQ = 20.5 + 53 + 61.5 = 135\text{ cm}\)  →  Answer: D
