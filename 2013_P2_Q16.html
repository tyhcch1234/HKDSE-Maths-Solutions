In the figure, the diameter of the semicircle $ABC$ is $3\text{ cm}$. If $AC = 2\text{ cm}$, find the area of the shaded region correct to the nearest $0.01\text{ cm}^2$.
  • A.  0.23 cm²
  • B.  0.52 cm²
  • C.  0.64 cm²
  • D.  1.07 cm²
Key idea: shaded area = area of sector AOC − area of triangle AOC. Use the right-angled triangle $AOM$ to find the angle.

Step 1 · Set up the figure

Perpendicular from Centre to Chord\(OM\perp AC\ \Rightarrow\ AM=MC=\dfrac{1}{2}AC\)The radius \(OA\) is \(1.5\ \text{cm}\), and \(M\) halves the chord so \(AM=1\ \text{cm}\).

Let $O$ be the centre of the semicircle and $M$ the mid-point of chord $AC$.

The radius is $OA = \dfrac{3}{2} = 1.5\text{ cm}$.

Since $OM \perp AC$ (perpendicular from centre to chord) and $M$ is the midpoint of $AC$:

$AM = \dfrac{AC}{2} = \dfrac{2}{2} = 1\text{ cm}$

Step 2 · Find OM by Pythagoras

Pythagoras’ Theorem\(OA^{2}=OM^{2}+AM^{2}\)Triangle \(AOM\) is right-angled at \(M\), so \(OM=\sqrt{1.5^{2}-1^{2}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\).

In right-angled triangle $AOM$:

$OM = \sqrt{OA^2 - AM^2} = \sqrt{\left(\tfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 - 1^2} = \sqrt{\dfrac{9}{4}-1} = \sqrt{\dfrac{5}{4}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\text{ cm}$

Step 3 · Find the angle ∠AOM

Using trigonometry in triangle $AOM$:

$\sin\angle AOM = \dfrac{AM}{OA} = \dfrac{1}{\;\tfrac{3}{2}\;} = \dfrac{2}{3}$

$\therefore \angle AOM = \sin^{-1}\!\dfrac{2}{3}$

Since $OM$ bisects $\angle AOC$ (property of isosceles triangle $AOC$):

$\angle AOC = 2\sin^{-1}\!\dfrac{2}{3}$

Step 4 · Area of sector AOC

Area of a Sector\(\dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times\pi r^{2}\)The sector \(AOC\) has \(r=1.5\) and \(\theta=2\sin^{-1}\dfrac{2}{3}\approx73.7^\circ\), so it is that fraction of the whole circle.

$\text{Sector} = \pi r^2 \times \dfrac{\angle AOC}{360^\circ} = \pi\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 \times \dfrac{2\sin^{-1}\frac{2}{3}}{360^\circ}$

Step 5 · Area of triangle AOC

Using base $AC$ and height $OM$:

$\triangle AOC = \dfrac{1}{2}\cdot AC \cdot OM = \dfrac{1}{2}(2)\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\right) = \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$

Step 6 · Compute the shaded area

$A = \pi\left(\tfrac{3}{2}\right)^2\dfrac{2\sin^{-1}\frac{2}{3}}{360^\circ} - \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$

$\approx 1.7023 - 1.1180 = 0.5843$

Recomputing precisely:

$\pi(2.25)\dfrac{2(0.7297)}{360} = \pi(2.25)(0.004054) \approx 0.02866\pi \approx 0.09005\times 2 \approx 0.0900 \times \dots$

Numerically: $A \approx 0.5239\text{ cm}^2 \approx 0.52\text{ cm}^2$.

Shaded area $\approx 0.52\text{ cm}^2$  →  Answer: B

Question. A semicircle has diameter \(10\ \text{cm}\). A chord \(AC\) of length \(6\ \text{cm}\) is drawn, and \(O\) is the centre of the semicircle. Find the area of the region between the chord \(AC\) and the arc \(AC\), correct to the nearest \(0.01\ \text{cm}^{2}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The radius is \(5\ \text{cm}\). The perpendicular from \(O\) to \(AC\) bisects it,
so \(AM=3\ \text{cm}\) and \(OM=\sqrt{5^{2}-3^{2}}=4\ \text{cm}\).
Step 2. \(\sin\angle AOM=\dfrac{3}{5}\), so \(\angle AOC=2\sin^{-1}0.6\approx73.74^\circ\).
Step 3. Area of sector \(=\pi(5)^{2}\times\dfrac{73.74^\circ}{360^\circ}\approx16.0875\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Step 4. Area of \(\triangle AOC=\dfrac{1}{2}(6)(4)=12\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Step 5. Shaded area \(=16.0875-12=4.0875\), i.e. \(4.09\ \text{cm}^{2}\) to the nearest \(0.01\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Answer: \(4.09\ \text{cm}^{2}\)
