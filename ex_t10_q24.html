Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q24

Find the volume of a sphere with radius $21$ cm, taking $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.

Solution

Use the volume formula for a sphere: $V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$.
Substitute $r=21$ and $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$: $V=\frac{4}{3}\times\frac{22}{7}\times 21^3$.
Compute $21^3=9261$, so $V=\frac{4}{3}\times\frac{22}{7}\times 9261$.
Simplify $\frac{9261}{7}=1323$, giving $V=\frac{4}{3}\times 22\times 1323$.
Calculate $22\times 1323=29106$, so $V=\frac{4}{3}\times 29106=4\times 9702=38808$.
The volume of the sphere is $38808\text{ cm}^3$.
