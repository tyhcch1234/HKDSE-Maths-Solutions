Tangent–Secant Theorem, Similar Triangles & the Pythagorean Triple 5 : 12 : 13
∠ in alt. segment AAA Similarity Angle in Semicircle Tangent–Secant

Question Full text

In Figure 2, \(U\), \(V\) and \(W\) are points lying on a circle. Denote the circle by \(C\). \(TU\) is the tangent to \(C\) at \(U\) such that \(TVW\) is a straight line.

(a) Prove that \(\triangle UTV \sim \triangle WTU\). 2 marks

(b) It is given that \(VW\) is a diameter of \(C\). Suppose that \(TU = 780\text{ cm}\) and \(TV = 325\text{ cm}\).

(i) Express the circumference of \(C\) in terms of \(\pi\). 2 marks

(ii) Someone claims that the perimeter of \(\triangle UVW\) exceeds \(35\text{ m}\). Do you agree? Explain your answer. 3 marks

Original Figure

Part (a) — Proving \(\triangle UTV \sim \triangle WTU\) 2 marks

Step 11M

Consider \(\angle TUV\) and \(\angle TWU\).

\(TU\) is a tangent to the circle at \(U\), and \(UW\) is a chord. By the angle in the alternate segment theorem (tangent–chord angle):

\[ \angle TUV = \angle TWU \]
Hint: Sketch the tangent at \(U\) and the chord \(UW\) — the angle between them equals the angle the chord \(UW\) subtends in the alternate segment, i.e. \(\angle TWU\).
Step 21M

Consider \(\angle UTV\) and \(\angle WTU\).<\/p>

\(T\), \(V\), \(W\) are collinear, and the ray \(TU\) is shared. Hence \(\angle UTV\) and \(\angle WTU\) are the same angle measured from the common ray \(TU\):

\[ \angle UTV = \angle WTU \quad(\text{common angle}) \]
Hint: Both angles sit at the vertex \(T\) between the line \(TW\) and the line \(TU\).
Step 31A

The remaining angles of the two triangles must then be equal (angle sum of a triangle):

\[ \angle TVU = \angle TUW \]

Since two pairs of corresponding angles are equal, \(\triangle UTV\) and \(\triangle WTU\) are similar (AAA):

\[ \boxed{\triangle UTV \sim \triangle WTU} \quad (\text{AAA}) \]
Hint: Write the vertices in corresponding order: \(U \leftrightarrow W\), \(T \leftrightarrow T\), \(V \leftrightarrow U\).

Part (b)(i) — Circumference of \(C\) 2 marks

Step 11M

By the similarity proved in (a), corresponding sides are in the same ratio. The side \(TU\) corresponds to \(TW\), and \(TV\) corresponds to \(TU\):

\[ \frac{TW}{TU} = \frac{TU}{TV} \]
Hint: Use the matching order \(\triangle UTV \sim \triangle WTU\) — pair up the sides carefully.
Step 21M

Since \(TVW\) is a straight line, \(TW = TV + VW\). Substitute the given lengths \(TU = 780\), \(TV = 325\):

\[ \frac{TV+VW}{TU} = \frac{TU}{TV} \Rightarrow \frac{325 + VW}{780} = \frac{780}{325} \] \[ 325 + VW = \frac{780^2}{325} = 1872 \] \[ VW = 1547\text{ cm} \]
Hint: \(780^2 / 325 = 608400/325 = 1872\).
Step 31A

\(VW\) is a diameter of \(C\), so the radius is \(r = \dfrac{1547}{2}\text{ cm}\). The circumference is

\[ 2\pi r = \pi \cdot VW = 1547\pi\text{ cm} \]
Circumference of \(C = \boxed{1547\pi\text{ cm}}\)
Hint: Since \(VW\) is a diameter, circumference \(=\pi d = \pi \cdot VW\). No need to halve and double again.

Part (b)(ii) — Does the perimeter exceed \(35\text{ m}\)? 3 marks

Step 11M

From (a), \(\dfrac{UV}{UW} = \dfrac{TV}{TU} = \dfrac{325}{780} = \dfrac{5}{12}\).

Also, since \(VW\) is a diameter, the angle in a semicircle gives

\[ \angle VUW = 90^\circ \]

So \(\triangle UVW\) is right-angled at \(U\). With legs in the ratio \(5:12\), the hypotenuse follows the \(5:12:13\) pattern:

\[ UV : UW : VW = 5 : 12 : 13 \]
Hint: \(5^2+12^2=25+144=169=13^2\) — the classic Pythagorean triple.
Step 21M

With \(VW = 1547\text{ cm}\) as the \(13\)-part:

\[ UV = 1547 \times \frac{5}{13} = 595\text{ cm} \] \[ UW = 1547 \times \frac{12}{13} = 1428\text{ cm} \]
Hint: One part \(= 1547/13 = 119\text{ cm}\), so \(5\) parts \(=595\) and \(12\) parts \(=1428\).
Step 31A

Perimeter of \(\triangle UVW\):

\[ 595 + 1428 + 1547 = 3570\text{ cm} = 35.7\text{ m} \]

Since \(35.7\text{ m} > 35\text{ m}\), the claim is correct.

Perimeter \(= \boxed{35.7\text{ m}} > 35\text{ m}\) — Yes, agree with the claim.
Hint: Remember the unit conversion: \(1\text{ m} = 100\text{ cm}\), so divide the centimetre total by \(100\).

Summary of Answers

  • (a)\(\triangle UTV \sim \triangle WTU\)  (AAA: \(\angle TUV=\angle TWU\) by ∠ in alt. segment; \(\angle UTV=\angle WTU\) common angle)
  • (b)(i)\(VW = 1547\text{ cm}\); Circumference of \(C = 1547\pi\text{ cm}\)
  • (b)(ii)\(UV:UW:VW = 5:12:13\) (right angle at \(U\)); \(UV=595\text{ cm}\), \(UW=1428\text{ cm}\)
  • (b)(ii)Perimeter \(= 595+1428+1547 = 3570\text{ cm} = 35.7\text{ m} > 35\text{ m}\) → Yes, agree with the claim
