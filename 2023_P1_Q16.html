Question & Step-by-step Solution

  1. Let $a$ and $b$ be real constants. If the roots of the equation $x^2+ax+b=0$ are $p$ and $5p$, prove that $5a^2=36b$. [2 marks]
  2. Denote the circle $x^2+y^2-6x-12y+20=0$ by $C$. Find the constant $m$ such that the straight line $y=mx$ cuts $C$ at the points $Q$ and $R$ with $OQ:QR=1:4$, where $O$ is the origin. [3 marks]
Step 1 · Part (a) — Vieta's formulas The roots of $x^2+ax+b=0$ are $p$ and $5p$. Comparing with $x^2+ax+b=0$: $$\text{sum of roots}=p+5p=-a,\qquad \text{product of roots}=p(5p)=b.$$ So $6p=-a$ and $5p^2=b$.
Hint: for $x^2+Ax+B=0$, the sum of roots is $-A$ and the product is $B$.
Step 2 · Part (a) — Eliminate $p$ From $6p=-a$ we get $p=-\dfrac{a}{6}$. Substitute into $5p^2=b$: $$5\left(\frac{-a}{6}\right)^{2}=b \;\Longrightarrow\; 5\cdot\frac{a^2}{36}=b \;\Longrightarrow\; \boxed{5a^{2}=36b}.$$
Take care with the square of a negative: $\left(-\frac a6\right)^2=\frac{a^2}{36}$.
Step 3 · Part (b) — Circle centre & radius Complete the square for the circle $C$: $$x^2-6x+y^2-12y+20=0$$ $$(x-3)^2-9+(y-6)^2-36+20=0$$ $$(x-3)^2+(y-6)^2=25.$$ So the centre is $(3,6)$ and the radius is $5$. Note that $OQ:QR=1:4$, so with $QR=4\,OQ$ we have $OR=OQ+QR=5\,OQ$, i.e. $OR=5\cdot OQ$.
Step 4 · Part (b) — Intersect line with circle Let $t$ be the $x$-coordinate of $Q$; since $OR=5\,OQ$, the $x$-coordinate of $R$ is $5t$. Put $y=mx$ into the equation of $C$: $$x^2+(mx)^2-6x-12(mx)+20=0$$ $$(m^2+1)x^2-(12m+6)x+20=0.$$ Dividing by $(m^2+1)$, the roots are $t$ and $5t$ of $$x^2-\frac{6(2m+1)}{m^2+1}x+\frac{20}{m^2+1}=0 .$$
Step 5 · Part (b) — Apply the result of (a) The roots are $t$ and $5t$ (with $a=-\dfrac{6(2m+1)}{m^2+1}$, $b=\dfrac{20}{m^2+1}$). Using $5a^2=36b$ from (a): $$5\left(\frac{-6(2m+1)}{m^2+1}\right)^{2}=36\left(\frac{20}{m^2+1}\right)$$ $$5\cdot\frac{36(2m+1)^2}{(m^2+1)^2}=\frac{720}{m^2+1}$$ $$\frac{180(2m+1)^2}{(m^2+1)^2}=\frac{720}{m^2+1}$$ $$(2m+1)^2=4(m^2+1).$$
Step 6 · Part (b) — Solve for $m$ Expand and simplify: $$4m^2+4m+1=4m^2+4$$ $$4m=3 \;\Longrightarrow\; \boxed{m=\frac{3}{4}}.$$
Answer: (a) $5a^2=36b$  ·  (b) $m=\dfrac{3}{4}$
