HKDSE 2021 Paper 2 Q42 Probability Combination

Committee & Choir Selection

A committee is formed by 20 students and 10 teachers. If 7 members are selected from the committee to form a choir consisting of at least 4 students, how many different choirs can be formed?

A. 581 400
B. 873 120
C. 1 162 800
D. 1 744 200

1

Understand the condition "at least 4 students"

What does this mean for the possible compositions?

+

The choir has exactly 7 members total.

If there are at least 4 students, the number of students can be:

4 students 5 students 6 students 7 students

For each student count, the remaining members must be teachers (since only students and teachers are available).

💡 Hint: The phrase "at least" means we must consider all cases that satisfy the minimum requirement. We cannot simply subtract from the total, because the composition matters.

2

Apply the Combination Formula

How do we count selections without order?

+

Since the order of selection does not matter, we use the combination formula:

\( C^n_r = \binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!} \)

For example:

  • Choosing 4 students from 20: \( \binom{20}{4} \)
  • Choosing 3 teachers from 10: \( \binom{10}{3} \)

💡 Hint: "Committee" and "choir" selections are combinations because the order of members does not affect the group.

3

Compute each case individually

Calculate the number of choirs for 4, 5, 6, and 7 students.

+

Case 1: Exactly 4 students (and 3 teachers)

\( \binom{20}{4} \times \binom{10}{3} = 4\,845 \times 120 = 581\,400 \)

Case 2: Exactly 5 students (and 2 teachers)

\( \binom{20}{5} \times \binom{10}{2} = 15\,504 \times 45 = 697\,680 \)

Case 3: Exactly 6 students (and 1 teacher)

\( \binom{20}{6} \times \binom{10}{1} = 38\,760 \times 10 = 387\,600 \)

Case 4: Exactly 7 students (and 0 teachers)

\( \binom{20}{7} \times \binom{10}{0} = 77\,520 \times 1 = 77\,520 \)

💡 Hint: Remember \( \binom{10}{0} = 1 \) because there is exactly one way to choose zero teachers.

4

Sum all cases for the final answer

Add the results of all valid cases.

+

Since the cases are mutually exclusive, we add them together:

\( \binom{20}{4}\binom{10}{3} + \binom{20}{5}\binom{10}{2} + \binom{20}{6}\binom{10}{1} + \binom{20}{7}\binom{10}{0} \)

\( = 581\,400 + 697\,680 + 387\,600 + 77\,520 \)

\( = 1\,744\,200 \)

✅ Correct Answer: D. 1 744 200

Final Answer

D. 1 744 200
