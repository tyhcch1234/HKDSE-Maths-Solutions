Rounding and Significant Figures

Question

\(230.045678=\)

  • A. \(230.0\) (correct to 4 significant figures)
  • B. \(230.046\) (correct to 4 significant figures)
  • C. \(230.0456\) (correct to 4 decimal places)
  • D. \(230.04568\) (correct to 4 decimal places)

Solution

Test option A (4 s.f.)

The first 4 significant figures of \(230.045678\) are \(2, 3, 0, 0\). The next digit is \(4<5\), so rounding gives \(230.0\). Option A is a correct statement.

Rounding to Significant Figures \(\text{keep }n\text{ significant figures, then check the next digit}\) For \(230.045678\) the first four significant figures are \(2,3,0,0\) and the next digit is \(4\).
Check option B

\(230.046\) has 6 significant figures, not 4, so B is false.

Check options C and D (4 d.p.)

Correct to 4 decimal places, look at the 5th decimal digit, which is \(7\ge 5\), so

\[ 230.045678\approx 230.0457 \]

Thus C (\(230.0456\), which only truncates) is false, and D (\(230.04568\), which has 5 decimal places) is also false.

Rounding to Decimal Places \(230.045678\approx230.0457\ \text{(4 d.p.)}\) The fifth decimal digit is \(7\), so the fourth decimal place rounds up from \(6\) to \(7\).
Final Answer: A
