Polar Coordinates and the Equilateral Triangle

Polar axis / radius 12 Segment AB Points A, B Pole O / centre Angle 60°
Question

In a polar coordinate system, \(O\) is the pole. The polar coordinates of the points \(A\) and \(B\) are \((12,\,75^\circ)\) and \((12,\,135^\circ)\) respectively.

  1. Find \(\angle AOB\). (1 mark)
  2. Find the perimeter of \(\triangle AOB\). (2 marks)
  3. Write down the number of folds of rotational symmetry of \(\triangle AOB\). (1 mark)
Step 1 · Part (a): angle at the pole \[ \angle AOB = 135^\circ - 75^\circ = 60^\circ. \]
Step 2 · Identify the type of triangle Since \(OA = OB = 12\), \(\triangle AOB\) is isosceles with vertex angle \(\angle AOB = 60^\circ\). The base angles are equal: \[ \angle OAB = \angle OBA = \frac{180^\circ - 60^\circ}{2} = 60^\circ. \] All three angles are \(60^\circ\), so \(\triangle AOB\) is equilateral, and therefore \(AB = OA = OB = 12\).
Hint: An isosceles triangle with a \(60^\circ\) vertex angle must be equilateral.
Step 3 · Part (b): the perimeter \[ \text{Perimeter} = OA + OB + AB = 12 + 12 + 12 = 36. \]
Step 4 · Part (c): rotational symmetry An equilateral triangle is mapped onto itself by rotations of \(120^\circ\), \(240^\circ\) and \(360^\circ\) about its centre, so it has 3 folds of rotational symmetry.
Final Answer: (a) \(\angle AOB = 60^\circ\)   (b) perimeter \(= 36\)   (c) \(3\) folds of rotational symmetry.
