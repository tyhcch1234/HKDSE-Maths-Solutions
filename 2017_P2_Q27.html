HKDSE 2017 · Paper 2 · Q27 — Locus & Centre of a Circle

Circle $x^2+y^2-6x-4y-87=0$ Centre $C(3,2)$ Locus $x+2y+k=0$ Points $A,\ B$ Moving point $P$
2017 MC #27

It is given that $A$ and $B$ are two distinct points lying on the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}-6x-4y-87=0$. Let $P$ be a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that $AP=BP$. The equation of the locus of $P$ is $x+2y+k=0$, where $k$ is a constant. Find $k$.

A. $-8$
B. $-7$
C. $7$
D. $8$
Key idea: the locus of points equidistant from $A$ and $B$ is the perpendicular bisector of $AB$ — and since $A,B$ lie on the circle, this locus always passes through the circle's centre.
Step 1 · Rewrite the circle in centre–radius form

Complete the square in $x$ and in $y$:

$$x^{2}-6x+y^{2}-4y-87=0$$

$$(x^{2}-6x+9)+(y^{2}-4y+4)=87+9+4$$

$$(x-3)^{2}+(y-2)^{2}=100$$

So the centre is $C(3,2)$ and the radius is $r=10$.

Step 2 · Describe the locus of $P$

$AP=BP$ means $P$ is always equidistant from $A$ and $B$.

Therefore the locus of $P$ is the perpendicular bisector of $AB$, drawn in red.

Step 3 · Use the centre of the circle

Since $CA=CB=r=10$ (both are radii), the centre $C$ is also equidistant from $A$ and $B$.

Hence $C(3,2)$ must lie on the locus $x+2y+k=0$.

Step 4 · Substitute and solve for $k$

Put $(x,y)=(3,2)$ into $x+2y+k=0$:

$$(3)+2(2)+k=0$$

$$3+4+k=0 \;\Rightarrow\; k=-7$$

ANSWER
$k=-7$  →  Option B
