Coordinate Geometry — Locus & the Centre of a Circle

Equation of a Circle Locus Perpendicular Bisector Centre of a Circle Multiple Choice

Question Multiple Choice

It is given that \(A\) and \(B\) are two distinct points lying on the circle \[x^{2}+y^{2}-6x-4y-87=0.\]

Let \(P\) be a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that \(AP=BP\). The equation of the locus of \(P\) is \[x+2y+k=0,\] where \(k\) is a constant. Find \(k\).

A. \(-8\)    B. \(-7\)    C. \(7\)    D. \(8\)

Step 1

Complete the square in \(x\) and in \(y\):

\[x^{2}-6x+y^{2}-4y=87\] \[(x-3)^{2}-9+(y-2)^{2}-4=87\]
Hint: The centre can be read directly from the linear coefficients: for \(x^{2}+y^{2}+Dx+Ey+F=0\), the centre is \(\left(-\tfrac{D}{2},-\tfrac{E}{2}\right)=\left(3,2\right)\).

Centre \(=(3,\,2)\),   radius \(=\sqrt{100}=10\).

Part (b) — The locus is the perpendicular bisector Key idea

The set of points \(P\) with \(AP=BP\) is the perpendicular bisector of segment \(AB\). Drag the slider to move the chord \(AB\) (changing \(A\) and \(B\) on the circle) and watch how the perpendicular bisector always passes through the centre.

Step 1

\(AP=BP\) means \(P\) is equidistant from \(A\) and \(B\), so \(P\) lies on the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\).

Hint: The locus of all points equidistant from two fixed points is a straight line — the perpendicular bisector. So the given locus \(x+2y+k=0\) is this line.
Step 2

Since \(A\) and \(B\) both lie on the circle, the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\) must pass through the centre of the circle, \(C(3,2)\).

The locus line passes through \((3,2)\).
Hint: This is the crucial observation — the centre of a circle is equidistant from every point on the circle, so it is certainly equidistant from \(A\) and \(B\).

Summary of Answers

  • Circle: \(x^{2}+y^{2}-6x-4y-87=0\;\Rightarrow\;(x-3)^{2}+(y-2)^{2}=100\)
  • Centre of circle: \((3,\,2)\),   radius \(=10\)
  • Locus \(AP=BP\) is the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\)
  • This bisector passes through the centre of the circle, \((3,2)\)
  • Substitute: \(3+2(2)+k=0\;\Rightarrow\;\) \(k=-7\)
  • Correct option: B
