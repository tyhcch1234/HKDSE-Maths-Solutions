Question

Let $x$, $y$ and $z$ be non-zero numbers. If

$$x:y = 8:5 \quad\text{and}\quad 2x = 4z - 3y,$$

then $y:z =$

  1. A $16:17$
  2. B $17:16$
  3. C $20:31$
  4. D $31:20$
