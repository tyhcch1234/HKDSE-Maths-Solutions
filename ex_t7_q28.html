Functions and Graphs (40) · Sub-Topic: Graph Transformation

T7 Q28

The graph of \(y=f(x)\) is transformed to \(y=-f(x+1)\). Describe the transformation.

Solution

Compare $y=f(x)$ with $y=-f(x+1)$: the transformation has a minus sign outside $f$ and $x$ is replaced by $x+1$.
The minus sign outside $f$ reflects the graph about the $x$-axis.
Replacing $x$ by $x+1$ shifts the graph $1$ unit to the left, since $x+1=x-(-1)$.
Thus a point $(a,f(a))$ maps to $(a-1,-f(a))$.
The graph of $y=f(x)$ is reflected about the $x$-axis and translated $1$ unit to the left to obtain $y=-f(x+1)$.
