[2022MC#15] · MCQ

The radius of a solid hemisphere and the base radius of a solid right circular cylinder are equal. If the height of the circular cylinder is equal to its base diameter, then the ratio of the total surface area of the hemisphere to the total surface area of the circular cylinder is

A. 1 : 2
B. 1 : 3
C. 2 : 3
D. 2 : 5

Step 1 · Define the common radius

Let the common radius be $x$ cm.

Hemisphere radius $= x$ cm.

Cylinder base radius $= x$ cm.

Write one letter for the unknown — then everything else follows from the question.

Step 2 · Convert the height condition

Height = base diameter $= 2\times x = 2x$ cm.

So the cylinder has base radius $x$ and height $2x$.

Base diameter means twice the base radius — do not confuse it with the height of the hemisphere.

Step 3 · Total surface area of the hemisphere

Curved surface $=\dfrac{1}{2}(4\pi x^{2}) = 2\pi x^{2}$

Flat circular base $=\pi x^{2}$

$$\text{TSA}_{\text{hemi}} = 2\pi x^{2} + \pi x^{2} = 3\pi x^{2}$$

Total Surface Area of a Hemisphere \(3\pi r^{2}\) Curved surface \(2\pi x^{2}\) plus the flat base \(\pi x^{2}\) gives \(3\pi x^{2}\).

Step 4 · Total surface area of the cylinder

Curved surface $= 2\pi x\,(2x) = 4\pi x^{2}$

Two circular ends $= 2(\pi x^{2}) = 2\pi x^{2}$

$$\text{TSA}_{\text{cyl}} = 4\pi x^{2} + 2\pi x^{2} = 6\pi x^{2}$$

Total Surface Area of a Cylinder \(2\pi rh+2\pi r^{2}\) With radius \(x\) and height \(2x\): \(2\pi x(2x)+2\pi x^{2}=6\pi x^{2}\).

Step 5 · Form the ratio

$$\frac{\text{TSA}_{\text{hemi}}}{\text{TSA}_{\text{cyl}}} = \frac{3\pi x^{2}}{6\pi x^{2}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

So the ratio is $1:2$.

Answer: A  (1 : 2)

Question. A hemisphere and a cylinder have the same base radius \(x\). The height of the cylinder is three times its base radius. Find the ratio of the total surface area of the hemisphere to that of the cylinder.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Hemisphere: \(2\pi x^{2}+\pi x^{2}=3\pi x^{2}\).
Step 2. Cylinder: curved surface \(2\pi x(3x)=6\pi x^{2}\), plus two ends \(2\pi x^{2}\).
Step 3. \(\text{TSA}_{\text{cyl}}=6\pi x^{2}+2\pi x^{2}=8\pi x^{2}\).
Step 4. Ratio \(=3\pi x^{2}:8\pi x^{2}=3:8\).
Answer: \(3:8\)
