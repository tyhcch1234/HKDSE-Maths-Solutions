Solve the simultaneous inequalities

$-5x>21-2x \quad$ and $\quad 6x-18<0$

  • A. $x<-7$
  • B. $x<3$
  • C. $-7
  • D. $x<-7$   (the intersection)
1
Step 1 — Read the two conditions We must satisfy both inequalities at once: \[ -5x > 21-2x \qquad\text{and}\qquad 6x-18 < 0. \]
"And" means we take the overlap (intersection) of the two solution sets.
2
Step 2 — Solve the first inequality \[ -5x > 21-2x \] Add $2x$ to both sides: \[ -3x > 21 \] Divide both sides by $-3$ — remember to reverse the sign: \[ x < -7. \]
Dividing (or multiplying) by a negative number flips the inequality sign.
Reversing the Inequality Sign \(ax>b,\ a<0\ \Longrightarrow\ x<\dfrac{b}{a}\) Dividing \\(-3x>21\\) by \\(-3\\) turns \\(>\\) into \\(<\\), giving \\(x<-7\\).
3
Step 3 — Solve the second inequality \[ 6x-18 < 0 \] Add $18$ to both sides: \[ 6x < 18 \] Divide both sides by $+6$ (positive, sign unchanged): \[ x < 3. \]
Divide by a positive number: no sign change.
Solving a Linear Inequality (positive coefficient) \(ax0\ \Longrightarrow\ x<\dfrac{b}{a}\) The coefficient \\(6\\) is positive, so \\(6x<18\\) gives \\(x<3\\) without changing the sign.
4
Step 4 — Combine with "and" (intersection) We need $x<-7$ and $x<3$ simultaneously. On a number line, the overlap of the two rays is the smaller one: \[ x < -7 . \]
Since $-7<3$, everything left of $-7$ is automatically left of $3$.
Intersection of Solution Sets \(\{x Both conditions must hold at once, and since \\(-7<3\\) the overlap is simply \\(x<-7\\).
5
Step 5 — Check a test point Try $x=-8$: $-5(-8)=40>21-2(-8)=37$ ✔ and $6(-8)-18=-66<0$ ✔. Try $x=0$: $-5(0)=0$ is not $>21$ �’ so $x=0$ fails the first inequality.
A quick numerical check confirms the overlap.
Answer:   $x<-7$  →  Option D
