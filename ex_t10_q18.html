Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q18

A cone has a base radius of $3$ cm and a height of $7$ cm. Find its volume, taking $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Solution

Use the formula for the volume of a cone: $V=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$.
Substitute $r=3$, $h=7$, and $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$: $V=\frac{1}{3}\times \frac{22}{7}\times 3^2\times 7$.
Simplify: $V=\frac{1}{3}\times \frac{22}{7}\times 9\times 7=66$.
The volume of the cone is $66\text{ cm}^3$.
