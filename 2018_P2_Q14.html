Question

In the figure, \(ABCDEFGH\) is an octagon, where all the measurements are correct to the nearest cm. Let \(x\) cm\(^2\) be the actual area. Find the range of values of \(x\).

  • A.\(13<x<23\)
  • B.\(13<x<27\)
  • C.\(17<x<23\)
  • D.\(17<x<27\)

Solution

1Bounds of measurements
\[5.5\le BC<6.5,\quad 3.5\le h<4.5\quad(\text{outer});\qquad 1.5\le w<2.5,\quad 1.5\le h'<2.5\quad(\text{cutout})\]
2Area = outer rectangle − cutout
\[5.5\times3.5-2.5\times2.5<x<6.5\times4.5-1.5\times1.5\]
\[13<x<27\]
Answer\(\;\mathbf{B}.\;\;\(13<x<27\)\)
◆Distractor analysis

Bcorrect.

D\(17<x<27\) — lower bound error.
