Rate, Ratio and Variations (50) · Sub-Topic: Direct & Inverse Variation

T2 Q33

If 6 workers take 8 days to complete a job, and the number of workers varies inversely with the number of days, write an equation relating the number of workers and days, given that 4 workers take \(t\) days.

Solution

Let $W$ be the number of workers and $D$ be the number of days. Since $W$ and $D$ vary inversely, $WD=k$ for some constant $k$.
Using $6$ workers and $8$ days: $k=6\times 8=48$.
Therefore the equation is $WD=48$, or equivalently $W=\frac{48}{D}$.
For $4$ workers taking $t$ days: $4t=48$.
Solving gives $t=12$.
The inverse variation equation is $WD=48$ (or $W=\frac{48}{D}$), and $4$ workers take $t=12$ days.
