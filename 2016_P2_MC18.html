Mensuration — Volume of a right prism with a trapezoidal base
Right Prism Trapezoidal Base Pythagoras' Theorem Volume = Base Area × Length

Question MC

The figure shows a right prism. Find the volume of the prism.

The prism stands on a trapezium base. From the given figure:

  • the height of the trapezium is \(5\text{ cm}\),
  • the shorter parallel side of the trapezium is \(4\text{ cm}\),
  • a slanted edge of the trapezium is \(6\text{ cm}\) and the other is \(13\text{ cm}\),
  • the two parallel sides differ by \(12\text{ cm}\).
A. \(216\text{ cm}^3\)
B. \(240\text{ cm}^3\)
C. \(300\text{ cm}^3\)
D. \(328\text{ cm}^3\)

Part 2 — Finding \(x\) by Pythagoras' Theorem Key Step

The trapezium splits into a rectangle and a right triangle of height \(5\) and slant \(13\).

Part 3 — Base Area and Volume Finish

With \(x=16\), the trapezium base has parallel sides \(16\) and \(4\) with height \(5\). Multiply the base area by the prism length \(6\) cm.

Base area \(=\frac{1}{2}(16+4)\times 5\) = 50 cm²
Volume \(=50\times\) depth = 300 cm³
0 / 3

Summary of Answers

  • Trapezium lower base: \(x = 16\) cm ( \(x=-8\) rejected ).
  • Base area: \(\dfrac{1}{2}(16+4)(5) = 50\text{ cm}^2\).
  • Prism length: \(6\) cm.
  • Volume: \(50 \times 6 = 300\text{ cm}^3\).
  • Answer: Option C.
