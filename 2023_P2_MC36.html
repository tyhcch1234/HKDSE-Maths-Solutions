Arithmetic Sequence & Sum Inequality

Let \(T(n)\) be the \(n\)th term of an arithmetic sequence.

It is given that \(T(11) = 83\) and \(T(25) + T(30) = 463\).

Find the least value of \(k\) such that \[ T(1) + T(2) + T(3) + \cdots + T(k) > 4 \times 10^5. \]

Step-by-Step Guided Solution

1 Set up the general term

Let the first term be \(a\) and the common difference be \(d\).

The \(n\)th term of an arithmetic sequence is:

\[ T(n) = a + (n-1)d \]
💡 Hint: We need two equations to find the two unknowns \(a\) and \(d\).
2 Form two equations

From \(T(11) = 83\):

\[ a + 10d = 83 \quad \text{......(1)} \]

From \(T(25) + T(30) = 463\):

\[ (a + 24d) + (a + 29d) = 463 \] \[ 2a + 53d = 463 \quad \text{......(2)} \]
💡 Hint: \(T(25) = a+24d\) and \(T(30) = a+29d\). Combine like terms.
3 Solve for \(a\) and \(d\)

From (1): \(a = 83 - 10d\). Substitute into (2):

\[ 2(83 - 10d) + 53d = 463 \] \[ 166 - 20d + 53d = 463 \] \[ 33d = 297 \] \[ d = 9 \]

Then:

\[ a = 83 - 10(9) = -7 \]
💡 Hint: Always check: \(T(11) = -7 + 10(9) = 83\) ✓
4 Write the sum of the first \(k\) terms

The sum of an arithmetic sequence is:

\[ S(k) = \frac{k}{2}\big[\,2a + (k-1)d\,\big] \]

Substitute \(a = -7\) and \(d = 9\):

\[ S(k) = \frac{k}{2}\big[2(-7) + (k-1)9\big] = \frac{k}{2}(9k - 23) \] \[ S(k) = \frac{9k^2 - 23k}{2} \]
💡 Hint: This is a quadratic in \(k\). We need \(S(k) > 4 \times 10^5\).
5 Form and solve the quadratic inequality
\[ \frac{9k^2 - 23k}{2} > 4 \times 10^5 \] \[ 9k^2 - 23k - 8 \times 10^5 > 0 \]

Solve \(9k^2 - 23k - 8 \times 10^5 = 0\) using the quadratic formula:

\[ k = \frac{23 \pm \sqrt{(-23)^2 - 4(9)(-8 \times 10^5)}}{2(9)} \] \[ k = \frac{23 \pm \sqrt{529 + 28\,800\,000}}{18} \] \[ k \approx \frac{23 \pm 5367.38}{18} \]

\(k \approx -296.87\) or \(k \approx 299.42\)

💡 Hint: For \(9k^2 - 23k - 8 \times 10^5 > 0\), we need \(k < -296.87\) or \(k > 299.42\).
6 Choose the least integer \(k\)

Since \(k\) is a positive integer (number of terms):

\[ k > 299.42 \]

The least integer satisfying this is:

\[ k = 300 \]
Answer: B
⚠️ Common trap: Don't round to 299 (too small, sum < 400,000) or 945 (far too large). Always check the inequality direction!

Key Takeaways

  • 1. General term: \(T(n) = a + (n-1)d\)
  • 2. Sum of first \(k\) terms: \(S(k) = \frac{k}{2}[2a + (k-1)d]\)
  • 3. Solving two equations gives \(a = -7\), \(d = 9\)
  • 4. Quadratic inequality: \(9k^2 - 23k - 8 \times 10^5 > 0\)
  • 5. Least integer \(k > 299.42\) is \(300\)
