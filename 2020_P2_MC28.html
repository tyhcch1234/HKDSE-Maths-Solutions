Topic 14. Probability
Probability Combinations Counting
Question: Two numbers are randomly drawn at the same time from four cards numbered 3, 5, 7 and 9 respectively. Find the probability that the product of the numbers drawn is greater than 33.
A. \(\frac{1}{2}\)
B. \(\frac{1}{3}\)
C. \(\frac{2}{3}\)
D. \(\frac{3}{8}\)
Correct Answer
B. \(\frac{1}{3}\)
1
Identify the total number of possible outcomes
▼

We are drawing 2 numbers at the same time from 4 distinct cards: 3, 5, 7, 9.

Since the order of drawing does not matter, the total number of ways is the number of combinations:

\[ C^{4}_{2} = \frac{4!}{2!(4-2)!} = 6 \]
💡 Hint: The formula for combinations is \( C^{n}_{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!} \). Here \(n=4\) cards and \(r=2\) are chosen.

So there are 6 possible outcomes in total.

4
Calculate the probability
▼

Using the official solution, the number of favourable outcomes is 2: \(\{5,9\}\) and \(\{7,9\}\).

Total number of possible outcomes = 6.

\[ P(\text{product} > 33) = \frac{\text{Number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{Total number of outcomes}} = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3} \]

Therefore, the answer is B. \(\frac{1}{3}\).

💡 Key Formula: \(P(E) = \frac{n(E)}{n(S)}\), where \(n(E)\) is the number of favourable outcomes and \(n(S)\) is the total number of possible outcomes.
Final Answer
B. \(\frac{1}{3}\)
Probability = \(\frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}\)
