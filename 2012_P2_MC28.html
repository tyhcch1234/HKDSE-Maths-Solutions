Q The Question

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the ages of a group of members in a recreational centre.

Stem (tens)
Leaf (units)
5
05 66 8
6
14 55 78 89
7
34 46 79
8
9
1

e.g. 5 | 6 represents the age 56

A member is randomly selected from the group. Find the probability that the selected member is not under the age of 74.

◆ Step-by-Step Solution

Tap each step to reveal the reasoning. Try the hint before peeking!

1
Read the stem-and-leaf diagram
▼

In a stem-and-leaf diagram, each entry splits into two parts:

Stem | Leaf  ⟶  the actual value
5 | 6  =  56

So the stem (tens) gives the tens digit and every leaf (units) gives a units digit.

Hint The key line at the bottom of the diagram tells you how to decode it — never ignore the key!
2
Count the total number of members
▼

Every leaf in the diagram represents one member. Count all the leaves row by row:

stem 5 → 5 leaves
stem 6 → 8 leaves
stem 7 → 6 leaves
stem 8 → 0 leaves
stem 9 → 1 leaf
Total members $= 5 + 8 + 6 + 0 + 1 = \mathbf{20}$

This 20 becomes the denominator (the total number of equally likely outcomes).

3
Understand "not under the age of 74"
▼

Translate the phrase carefully:

"not under 74"  ⟶  NOT "age < 74"
i.e. the age is 74 or above  ($\geq 74$)
Watch out The word "under" means strictly less than. So an age of exactly 74 is not under 74 — it must be counted!

Favourable members: age $\geq 74$.

4
Count the favourable members (age ≥ 74)
▼

Scan the diagram and keep every leaf whose value makes the age $\geq 74$.

stem 7 → 4, 4, 6, 7, 9  (73 is excluded ✗)
stem 9 → 1

Count them:

$74,\;74,\;76,\;77,\;79,\;91 \;\Rightarrow\; \mathbf{6}$ members
Alternative — complement Instead of counting $\geq 74$, count the "under 74" group and subtract: members with age < 74 are $50,55,56,56,58,61,64,65,65,67,68,68,69,73$ $= 14$ members. Then $20 - 14 = 6$. Same answer!
5
Apply the probability formula
▼
$P(E) = \dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total number of outcomes}}$
$$P(\text{age} \geq 74) = \frac{6}{20}$$
$$= \frac{3}{10} = 0.3$$

So the answer is Option B.

★ Key Take-aways

  • Always read the key of a stem-and-leaf diagram: stem = tens, leaf = units.
  • The total count = number of leaves → this is the probability denominator.
  • "not under 74" means age $\geq 74$ — careful, 74 itself is included.
  • Use the complement when it's easier: $P(\geq 74) = 1 - P(<74) = 1 - \frac{14}{20} = 0.3$.
