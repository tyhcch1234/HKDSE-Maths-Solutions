HKDSE 2026 · Paper 1 · Q11

Partial Variation & Quadratic Graph

Question

It is given that \(f(x)\) partly varies as \(x^2\) and is partly constant. Suppose that \(f(4)=0\) and \(f(8)=-9\). Denote the graph of \(y=f(x)\) by \(\Gamma\).

(a)   Find the \(y\)-intercept of \(\Gamma\). (3 marks)

(b)   \(\Gamma\) cuts the \(x\)-axis at the points \(U\) and \(V\) while \(\Gamma\) cuts the \(y\)-axis at the point \(W\). Find the perimeter of \(\triangle UWV\). (3 marks)

Solution

STEP 1 — write the general form
Partial Variation \(y=ax^{2}+b\) One part varies as \(x^{2}\) and one part is constant, so \(f(x)=ax^{2}+b\) with \(b=3\).
"Partly varies as \(x^2\) and partly constant" means \[ f(x)=ax^2+b \] for constants \(a\) and \(b\).
STEP 2 — use the two given values
\(f(4)=0:\; 16a+b=0\)  
 
  \(f(8)=-9:\; 64a+b=-9\).
Method of Elimination \(16a+b=0,\quad 64a+b=-9\ \to\ 48a=-9\) Subtracting the two data equations leaves \(48a=-9\), so \(a=-\dfrac{3}{16}\).
Subtracting the first from the second: \[ 48a=-9 \implies a=-\frac{3}{16}, \qquad b=-16a=3 \] \[ f(x)=-\frac{3}{16}x^2+3 \]
PART (a) — y-intercept
Set \(x=0\): \(f(0)=3\). The \(y\)-intercept is \(\mathbf{3}\) (point \((0,3)\)).
PART (b) — find U, V, W
\(x\)-intercepts: \(-\frac{3}{16}x^2+3=0 \implies x^2=16 \implies x=\pm4\).
So \(U=(-4,0)\), \(V=(4,0)\);
and \(W=(0,3)\).
PART (b) — perimeter
Distance Formula \(d=\sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}+(y_{2}-y_{1})^{2}}\) \(UW=WV=\sqrt{4^{2}+3^{2}}=5\), and with \(UV=8\) the perimeter is \(18\).
\[ UV=8, \qquad UW=WV=\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5 \] \[ \text{Perimeter}=8+5+5=18 \]
(a) \(y\)-intercept \(=3\)    (b) Perimeter of \(\triangle UWV = 18\)

Question. It is given that \(f(x)=ax^{2}+b\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are constants, and that \(f(0)=6\) and \(f(8)=0\). The curve \(y=f(x)\) cuts the \(x\)-axis at \(U\) and \(V\) and the \(y\)-axis at \(W\).
(a) Find the \(y\)-intercept of the curve.
(b) Find the perimeter of \(\triangle UWV\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(f(0)=b\), so the \(y\)-intercept is \(6\) and \(W=(0,6)\).
Step 2. \(f(8)=64a+6=0\), so \(a=-\dfrac{3}{32}\) and \(f(x)=6-\dfrac{3}{32}x^{2}\).
Step 3. Setting \(f(x)=0\) gives \(x^{2}=64\), so \(U=(-8,0)\) and \(V=(8,0)\).
Step 4. \(UV=16\) and \(UW=WV=\sqrt{8^{2}+6^{2}}=10\).
Step 5. Perimeter \(=16+10+10=36\).
Answer: (a) \(6\) (b) \(36\)
