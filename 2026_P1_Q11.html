HKDSE 2026 · Paper 1 · Q11

Partial Variation & Quadratic Graph

Question

It is given that \(f(x)\) partly varies as \(x^2\) and is partly constant. Suppose that \(f(4)=0\) and \(f(8)=-9\). Denote the graph of \(y=f(x)\) by \(\Gamma\).

(a)   Find the \(y\)-intercept of \(\Gamma\). (3 marks)

(b)   \(\Gamma\) cuts the \(x\)-axis at the points \(U\) and \(V\) while \(\Gamma\) cuts the \(y\)-axis at the point \(W\). Find the perimeter of \(\triangle UWV\). (3 marks)

Solution

STEP 1 — write the general form
"Partly varies as \(x^2\) and partly constant" means \[ f(x)=ax^2+b \] for constants \(a\) and \(b\).
STEP 2 — use the two given values
\(f(4)=0:\; 16a+b=0\)     \(f(8)=-9:\; 64a+b=-9\).
Subtracting the first from the second: \[ 48a=-9 \implies a=-\frac{3}{16}, \qquad b=-16a=3 \] \[ f(x)=-\frac{3}{16}x^2+3 \]
PART (a) — y-intercept
Set \(x=0\): \(f(0)=3\). The \(y\)-intercept is \(\mathbf{3}\) (point \((0,3)\)).
PART (b) — find U, V, W
\(x\)-intercepts: \(-\frac{3}{16}x^2+3=0 \implies x^2=16 \implies x=\pm4\).
So \(U=(-4,0)\), \(V=(4,0)\); and \(W=(0,3)\).
PART (b) — perimeter
\[ UV=8, \qquad UW=WV=\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5 \] \[ \text{Perimeter}=8+5+5=18 \]
(a) \(y\)-intercept \(=3\)    (b) Perimeter of \(\triangle UWV = 18\)
