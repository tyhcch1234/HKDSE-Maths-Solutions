HKDSE 2022 · Paper 2 · Q35
Question. Let \[ z = 4 + 5i^{10} - ki^{15} + 6i^{21} + 2ki^{28}, \] where \(k\) is a real number. If the real part and the imaginary part of \(z\) are equal, then the real part of \(z\) is
  1. 7.
  2. 13.
  3. 17.
  4. 25.
Step 1Recall the cycle of powers of \(i\). \[ i^1=i,\quad i^2=-1,\quad i^3=-i,\quad i^4=1, \] and the pattern repeats every 4. Hence reduce each exponent modulo 4: \[ i^{10}=i^{2}=-1,\quad i^{15}=i^{3}=-i,\quad i^{21}=i^{1}=i,\quad i^{28}=i^{0}=1. \] Hint: divide the index by 4 and keep the remainder.
Step 2Substitute the simplified powers. \[ z = 4 + 5(-1) - k(-i) + 6(i) + 2k(1) \] \[ z = 4 - 5 + ki + 6i + 2k. \] Watch the signs: \(-k(-i)=+ki\), and \(2k(1)=2k\).
Step 3Collect real and imaginary parts. \[ z = \underbrace{(2k-1)}_{\text{Re}(z)} + \underbrace{(k+6)}_{\text{Im}(z)}i. \] Group the terms without \(i\) together, and those with \(i\) together.
Step 4Apply the condition \(\mathrm{Re}(z)=\mathrm{Im}(z)\). \[ 2k-1 = k+6 \] \[ 2k-k = 6+1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad k = 7. \] A single linear equation in \(k\).
Step 5Find the real part of \(z\). \[ \mathrm{Re}(z) = 2k-1 = 2(7)-1 = 13. \] (Check: \(\mathrm{Im}(z)=k+6=13\) — equal, as required.) \[ z = 13 + 13i. \] Substitute \(k=7\) back into \(2k-1\).
Answer:  B  (the real part of \(z\) is \(13\))
