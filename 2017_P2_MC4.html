Significant Figures Decimal Places Rounding

Which of the following expressions gives the value of 1 / π4 correct to the stated number of significant figures or decimal places?

1 π4

Evaluate

A. 0.0102 (3 s.f.)
B. 0.01025 (4 s.f.)
C. 0.01026 (5 d.p.)
D. 0.010266 (6 d.p.)

🔍 Step-by-Step Solution

Click each step to reveal the guidance and working.

Use a calculator to find the decimal value of \( \pi^4 \), then take the reciprocal.

\( \pi \approx 3.1415926535\ldots \)
\( \pi^4 \approx 97.409091034\ldots \)
\( \frac{1}{\pi^4} \approx 0.01026598225\ldots \)

We keep extra digits for now to avoid rounding errors in intermediate steps.

💡 Hint: Use the π button on your calculator, not 3.14

Significant figures count from the first non-zero digit.

For \( 0.01026598225\ldots \):

  • First non-zero digit: the 1 after the decimal point and leading zeros.
  • The leading zeros (0.0) are not significant.
  • So the first three significant figures are: 1, 0, 2 → giving \(0.0102\).
0.01026598225...

Wait, let's check the next digit after 2. It's 6, so we round up? Let's see in the next step.

💡 Hint: Leading zeros are not significant

Answer: D — \( \frac{1}{\pi^4} = 0.010266 \) (correct to 6 decimal places)

📌 Key Takeaways

  • ✦ Significant figures start from the first non-zero digit. Leading zeros are not significant.
  • ✦ Decimal places count digits after the decimal point, including zeros.
  • ✦ When rounding, look at the digit immediately to the right of the last kept digit. If it is 5 or more, round up.
  • ✦ Always use the full calculator value (or keep extra digits) until the final rounding step.
