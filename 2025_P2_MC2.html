2025MC#2 Polynomials Factorization Difference of Squares

Factorize:  \(36 - (3m + 4n)^2 = ?\)

Guided Solution

Click each step to reveal the reasoning.

The expression is a difference of two squares:

\(a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)\)

Here, \(36 = 6^2\) and the second term is already a square: \((3m + 4n)^2\).

So we can let \(a = 6\) and \(b = (3m + 4n)\).

Substitute \(a = 6\) and \(b = (3m + 4n)\) into the difference of squares formula:

\[ 36 - (3m + 4n)^2 = [6 + (3m + 4n)]\,[6 - (3m + 4n)] \]

💡 Hint: Notice the subtraction distributes over the entire binomial \((3m + 4n)\).

Remove parentheses carefully. Pay attention to the negative sign in the second factor:

\[ = (6 + 3m + 4n)(6 - 3m - 4n) \]

💡 Remember: \( - (3m + 4n) = -3m - 4n \). A common mistake is to only negate the first term.

Our simplified expression matches Option B:

\((6 + 3m + 4n)(6 - 3m - 4n)\)

✅ Correct answer: B
