[2017 MC #22]  ·  13. Trigonometry
In the figure, $ABCD$ is a rectangle with $AB = 3$ cm and $BC = 2$ cm. $E$ is a point lying on $CD$ such that $\angle CBE = 40^\circ$. Find $\angle AED$ correct to the nearest degree.
A. 33°
B. 43°
C. 47°
D. 57°
Sides and Angles of a Rectangle \(AB=DC,\ AD=BC,\ \angle A=\angle B=\angle C=\angle D=90^{\circ}\) Both \(\triangle BCE\) and \(\triangle ADE\) are right-angled, because \(\angle BCE=90^{\circ}\) and \(\angle ADE=90^{\circ}\).
STEP 1 · Read the rectangle

$ABCD$ is a rectangle, so opposite sides are equal and every angle is $90^\circ$:

$$AB = DC = 3\ \text{cm},\qquad AD = BC = 2\ \text{cm}$$

$E$ lies on $CD$, so the side $DC$ splits into

$$DE + EC = DC = 3\ \text{cm}.$$

Tangent Ratio \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\text{opposite side}}{\text{adjacent side}}\) With \(\angle CBE=40^{\circ}\), \(BC=2\) cm is adjacent and \(EC\) is opposite, so \(EC=2\tan 40^{\circ}=1.6782\) cm.
STEP 2 · Find $EC$ in the right-angled $\triangle BCE$

In $\triangle BCE$ the angle at $C$ is $90^\circ$, the side opposite $\angle CBE$ is $EC$, and the side adjacent to it is $BC = 2$ cm. Hence

$$\tan 40^\circ = \frac{EC}{2} \;\Longrightarrow\; EC = 2\tan 40^\circ = 1.6782\ \text{cm}.$$

STEP 3 · Subtract to get $DE$

$$DE = DC - EC = 3 - 1.6782 = 1.3218\ \text{cm}.$$

This is positive and smaller than $DC$, so $E$ does lie between $D$ and $C$.

Inverse Tangent \(\theta=\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}\right)\) \(\tan\angle AED=1.5131\) gives \(\angle AED=56.54^{\circ}\), i.e. \(57^{\circ}\) correct to the nearest degree.
STEP 4 · Find $\angle AED$ in the right-angled $\triangle ADE$

In $\triangle ADE$ the angle at $D$ is $90^\circ$, the side opposite $\angle AED$ is $AD = 2$ cm, and the side adjacent to it is $DE = 1.3218$ cm:

$$\tan \angle AED = \frac{AD}{DE} = \frac{2}{1.3218} = 1.5131$$

$$\angle AED = \tan^{-1} 1.5131 = 56.54^\circ \approx 57^\circ \text{ (to the nearest degree)}.$$

The answer is $57^\circ$, which is option D.

ANSWER
D. 57°

Question. In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a rectangle with \(AB=5\text{ cm}\) and \(BC=3\text{ cm}\). \(E\) is a point lying on \(CD\) such that \(\angle CBE=30^{\circ}\). Find \(\angle AED\) correct to the nearest degree.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(ABCD\) is a rectangle with \(DC=AB=5\text{ cm}\) and \(BC=3\text{ cm}\), so \(\triangle BCE\) is right-angled at \(C\).
Step 2. \(\tan 30^{\circ}=\dfrac{EC}{3}\), so \(EC=3\tan 30^{\circ}=1.7321\text{ cm}\).
Step 3. \(DE=DC-EC=5-1.7321=3.2679\text{ cm}\).
Step 4. In right-angled \(\triangle ADE\), \(\tan\angle AED=\dfrac{AD}{DE}=\dfrac{3}{3.2679}=0.9180\), so \(\angle AED=42.55^{\circ}\approx 43^{\circ}\).
Answer: \(\angle AED\approx 43^{\circ}\)
