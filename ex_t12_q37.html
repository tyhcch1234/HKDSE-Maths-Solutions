Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Circle Equation

T12 Q37

Find the equation of the circle with centre \((2,0)\) and radius \(2\).

Solution

The standard equation of a circle with centre $(h,k)$ and radius $r$ is $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$.
Substitute $(h,k)=(2,0)$ and $r=2$: $(x-2)^2+(y-0)^2=2^2$.
Simplify: $(x-2)^2+y^2=4$.
Expand if required: $x^2-4x+4+y^2=4$, so $x^2+y^2-4x=0$.
Check: the centre is $(2,0)$ and the radius is $\sqrt{4}=2$, so the equation is correct.
The equation of the circle is $(x-2)^2+y^2=4$, or equivalently $x^2+y^2-4x=0$.
