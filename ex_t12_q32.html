Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Circle Equation

T12 Q32

Find the equation of the circle with centre $(0,0)$ and radius $4$.

Solution

Use the standard equation of a circle: $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$.
Substitute the centre $(h,k)=(0,0)$ and radius $r=4$: $(x-0)^2+(y-0)^2=4^2$.
Simplify: $x^2+y^2=16$.
The equation of the circle is $x^2+y^2=16$.
