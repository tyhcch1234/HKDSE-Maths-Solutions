Formulas and Sequences (65) · Sub-Topic: Geometric Sequence

T5 Q38

Find the sum of the first 8 terms of the geometric sequence $1, 3, 9, 27, \ldots$.

Solution

Identify the first term and common ratio: $a=1$ and $r=\frac{3}{1}=3$.
Use the sum formula for the first $n$ terms of a geometric sequence: $S_n=\frac{a(r^n-1)}{r-1}$.
Substitute $n=8$, $a=1$, and $r=3$: $S_8=\frac{1(3^8-1)}{3-1}$.
Compute $3^8=6561$, so $S_8=\frac{6561-1}{2}=\frac{6560}{2}=3280$.
The sum of the first 8 terms is $3280$.
