Solving a Linear Inequality

Find the solution set of the given inequality.

Question

The solution of

\(18 + 7x > 4\)

is

A \(x > -2\)
B \(-2 < x < 1\)
C \(x < 1\)
D \(x > 1\)

Step-by-step solution

Tap each step to expand.

Hint: Subtract 18 from both sides.

\(18 + 7x > 4\)

\(18 + 7x \color{red}{-18} > 4 \color{red}{-18}\)

\(7x > -14\)

We subtract 18 from both sides to keep the inequality balanced.

Hint: Divide both sides by 7 (a positive number).

\(\dfrac{7x}{7} > \dfrac{-14}{7}\)

\(x > -2\)

Since 7 > 0, the inequality sign remains unchanged.

Hint: This means any number greater than \(-2\) satisfies the inequality.

\(x > -2\)

Graph on a number line:

-2
x > -2

The correct choice is A.

✖ Some students flip the inequality when dividing by a positive number — that's wrong!

✔ Only flip the sign when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.

\(7x > -14 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x > -2\)   (correct)
