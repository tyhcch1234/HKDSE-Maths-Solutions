HKDSE 2020 P1 Q10 Rate, Ratio and Variations

10. The price of a brand X souvenir of height h cm is $P. P is partly constant and partly varies as h³.  When h = 3, P = 59 and when h = 7, P = 691.

(a) Find the price of a brand X souvenir of height 4 cm.   [4 marks]

(b) Someone claims that the price of a brand X souvenir of height 5 cm is higher than the total price of two brand X souvenirs of height 4 cm. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer.   [2 marks]

Partial Variation \(P=a+bh^{3}\) "Partly constant and partly varies as \(h^{3}\)" means a constant term plus a term in \(h^{3}\).
1Set up the variation model

“Partly constant and partly varies as \(h^3\)” means: \[ P = a + b h^3 \] where \(a\) and \(b\) are non-zero constants.

Hint: Identify the form of the variation from the words “partly constant” and “partly varies”.

2Substitute the given data

When \(h = 3\), \(P = 59\): \[ a + b(3^3) = 59 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a + 27b = 59 \quad \text{… (1)} \] When \(h = 7\), \(P = 691\): \[ a + b(7^3) = 691 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a + 343b = 691 \quad \text{… (2)} \]

Hint: Plug in the two given pairs \((h, P)\) to form two equations.

Elimination Method \((2)-(1):\ 56b=168\ \Longrightarrow\ b=3\) The two constants \(a\) are already matched, so subtracting removes \(a\) immediately.
3Solve for \(a\) and \(b\)

Subtract (1) from (2): \[ (a + 343b) - (a + 27b) = 691 - 59 \] \[ 316b = 632 \quad\Rightarrow\quad b = 2 \] Substitute \(b = 2\) into (1): \[ a + 27(2) = 59 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a + 54 = 59 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a = 5 \] Hence \[ P = 5 + 2h^3 \]

Hint: Use elimination to find \(b\) first,
then back-substitute to find \(a\).

4Part (a): Find the price when \(h = 4\)

Substitute \(h = 4\) into \(P = 5 + 2h^3\): \[ P = 5 + 2(4^3) = 5 + 2(64) = 5 + 128 = 133 \]

Hint: The question asks for the price of a 4 cm souvenir,
so use \(h = 4\).

5Part (b): Compare the two prices

Price of one 5 cm souvenir: \[ h = 5:\quad P = 5 + 2(5^3) = 5 + 2(125) = 255 \] Total price of two 4 cm souvenirs: \[ 2 \times 133 = 266 \] Comparing: \[ 255 < 266 \] Therefore the claim is not correct.

Hint: Compute both quantities and compare them directly.
The claim says the 5 cm price is higher.

Answer:
(a) \( \$133 \)    (b) Not correct, since \(255 < 266\).

Question. The price \(\$P\) of a souvenir of height \(h\ \text{cm}\) is partly constant and partly varies as \(h^{3}\). When \(h=2\), \(P=39\); when \(h=4\), \(P=207\).
(a) Find the price of a souvenir of height \(3\ \text{cm}\).
(b) A shopkeeper claims that the price of a \(5\ \text{cm}\) souvenir is less than the total price of two \(3\ \text{cm}\) souvenirs. Do you agree?

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \(P=a+bh^{3}\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are non-zero constants.
Step 2. \(h=2\): \(a+8b=39\quad\cdots(1)\); \(h=4\): \(a+64b=207\quad\cdots(2)\).
Step 3. \((2)-(1)\): \(56b=168\), so \(b=3\);
then \(a+24=39\), so \(a=15\). Hence \(P=15+3h^{3}\).
Step 4. (a) \(h=3\): \(P=15+3(27)=96\).
Step 5. (b) \(h=5\): \(P=15+3(125)=390\), while two \(3\ \text{cm}\) souvenirs cost \(2\times96=192\). Since \(390>192\),
the claim is incorrect.
Answer: (a) \(\$96\) (b) No — \(\$390>\$192\).
