HKDSE Math 2015 Paper 2 Q36

Inequalities & Linear Programming

Inequalities Linear Programming Shaded Region Coordinate Geometry

The figure shows a shaded region (including the boundary). If $(a, b)$ is a point lying in the shaded region, which of the following are true?

  • I. $a \le 4$
  • II. $a \ge b - 5$
  • III. $a \ge 10 - 2b$
A I and II only
B I and III only
C II and III only
D I, II and III
📐 The shaded region bounded by three lines

🧭 Step-by-step guided solution

1
Identify the boundaries of the shaded region
⌄

The diagram shows a triangular region bounded by three straight lines. From the labels, the boundaries are:

$$x = 4,\quad y = x + 5,\quad x + 2y = 10$$

Each inequality that defines the region comes from one of these lines.

💡 Hint: The region is “closed” by a vertical line on the right, a line with positive slope on top, and a line with negative slope on the bottom. The inequality signs depend on which side of each line is shaded.
2
Find the inequality for the vertical boundary $x = 4$
⌄

The shaded region lies entirely to the left of the vertical line $x = 4$ (including the boundary). Therefore, every point $(x, y)$ in the region satisfies:

$$x \le 4$$

Since a point in the region is written as $(a, b)$, we have $a \le 4$. So statement I is true.

💡 A vertical line $x = k$ divides the plane into $x \le k$ (left) and $x \ge k$ (right). Always test a point from the region (e.g. $(0,5)$) to confirm the sign.
3
Find the inequality for the upper boundary $y = x + 5$
⌄

The shaded region is below the line $y = x + 5$, so the inequality is:

$$y \le x + 5$$

Substitute $x = a$ and $y = b$:

$$b \le a + 5$$

Rearranging gives:

$$a \ge b - 5$$

This matches statement II. So statement II is true.

💡 Rearrangement: $b \le a + 5$ becomes $a \ge b - 5$ by subtracting $5$ and then swapping sides (the inequality sign does not change when you add/subtract).
4
Find the inequality for the lower boundary $x + 2y = 10$
⌄

The shaded region is above the line $x + 2y = 10$ (including the boundary). Therefore:

$$x + 2y \ge 10$$

Substitute $x = a$, $y = b$:

$$a + 2b \ge 10$$

Rearranging yields:

$$a \ge 10 - 2b$$

This is exactly statement III. So statement III is true.

💡 Test with a point inside the region, e.g. $(0,5)$: $0 + 2(5) = 10 \ge 10$ ✓. The sign is indeed $\ge$.
5
Conclusion: which statements are true?
⌄

From the three steps, the shaded region is described by the system:

$$\begin{cases} x \le 4 \\ y \le x + 5 \\ x + 2y \ge 10 \end{cases}$$

Translating to $(a, b)$:

$$a \le 4, \quad a \ge b - 5, \quad a \ge 10 - 2b$$

All three statements I, II and III are true. The correct choice is D.

✅ Correct Answer: D (I, II and III)
