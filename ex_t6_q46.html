T6 Q46

If the quadratic equation \(ax^2+bx+c=0\) has \(a=2\) and \(b=-10\), find the sum of its roots.

Solution

For a quadratic equation $ax^2+bx+c=0$, the sum of its roots is given by $-\frac{b}{a}$.
Substitute $a=2$ and $b=-10$: sum of roots $=-\frac{-10}{2}$.
Simplify: $-\frac{-10}{2}=\frac{10}{2}=5$.
The sum of the roots is $5$.
