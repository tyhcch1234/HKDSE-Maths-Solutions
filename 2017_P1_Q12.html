Question [2017 #12]

A solid metal right prism of base area 84 cm² and height 20 cm is melted and recast into two similar solid right pyramids. The bases of the two pyramids are squares. The ratio of the base area of the smaller pyramid to the base area of the larger pyramid is 4 : 9.

(a) Find the volume of the larger pyramid. [3 marks]

(b) If the height of the larger pyramid is 12 cm, find the total surface area of the smaller pyramid. [4 marks]

💡 Key idea: volume is conserved when the metal is melted and recast, and for similar solids the ratio of volumes equals the cube of the ratio of corresponding lengths.
Step 1 · Set up the ratio of volumes

From base areas 4 : 9 to a linear scale factor

The bases are squares, so base area $\propto$ (side length)². Hence the linear scale factor is the square root of the area ratio:

$$\text{area ratio}=\frac{4}{9}\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{linear ratio}=\sqrt{\frac{4}{9}}=\frac{2}{3}$$

For similar solids, volume ratio = (linear ratio)³:

$$V_{\text{small}}:V_{\text{large}}=\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{3}=\frac{8}{27}$$

So $V_{\text{small}}=\dfrac{8}{27}V$ where $V$ cm³ is the volume of the larger pyramid.

Volume Ratio of Similar Solids \(V_{1}:V_{2}=L_{1}^{3}:L_{2}^{3}\) From the base-area ratio \(4:9\) the linear ratio is \(2:3\), so the volumes are in the ratio \(8:27\).
Step 2 · Conserve the volume (part a)

Melted metal keeps the same total volume

Volume of the original prism:

$$84\times 20=1680\ \text{cm}^{3}$$

This is divided between the two pyramids, so:

$$V+\frac{8}{27}V=1680$$

Step 3 · Solve for V (part a answer)

Evaluate the volume of the larger pyramid

$$\frac{35V}{27}=1680$$

$$V=\frac{1680\times 27}{35}=1296$$

$$\boxed{V=1296\ \text{cm}^{3}}$$

Step 4 · Side of the base of the larger pyramid

Use $V=\frac{1}{3}(\text{base area})\times\text{height}$

For the larger pyramid: $h=12$, so

$$1296=\frac{1}{3}\,a^{2}(12)=4a^{2}$$

$$a^{2}=324,\qquad a=18\ \text{cm}$$

i.e. the base of the larger pyramid is an $18\times18$ square.

Volume of a Pyramid \(V=\dfrac{1}{3}\times\text{base area}\times\text{height}\) For the larger pyramid \(1296=\dfrac{1}{3}a^{2}(12)\), which gives the base side \(a=18\) cm.
Step 5 · Slant height & TSA of larger pyramid

Total surface area of the larger pyramid

Slant height of one triangular face (apex above the centre of the base):

$$\ell=\sqrt{\left(\frac{18}{2}\right)^{2}+12^{2}}=\sqrt{81+144}=\sqrt{225}=15\ \text{cm}$$

TSA = base + 4 triangular faces with base $18$ and slant height $15$:

$$\text{TSA}=18(18)+4\times\frac{1}{2}(18)(15)=324+540=864\ \text{cm}^{2}$$

Step 6 · TSA of the smaller pyramid (part b)

Area ratio = (linear ratio)²

Areas of similar solids scale as the square of the linear ratio:

$$\frac{A_{\text{small}}}{A_{\text{large}}}=\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{2}=\frac{4}{9}$$

$$A_{\text{small}}=\frac{4}{9}(864)=384$$

Area Ratio of Similar Solids \(\dfrac{A_{1}}{A_{2}}=\left(\dfrac{L_{1}}{L_{2}}\right)^{2}\) The surface areas of the two similar pyramids are in the ratio \(\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{2}=\dfrac{4}{9}\).
Final answers

(a) The volume of the larger pyramid is $\ \boxed{1296\ \text{cm}^{3}}$.

(b) The total surface area of the smaller pyramid is $\ \boxed{384\ \text{cm}^{2}}$.

Check: smaller base side $=\frac{2}{3}(18)=12$ cm, smaller height $=\frac{2}{3}(12)=8$ cm, $\ell'=\frac{2}{3}(15)=10$ cm ⟹ TSA $=12(12)+4\cdot\frac12(12)(10)=144+240=384\ \checkmark$

Question. A solid metal right prism of base area \(144\text{ cm}^{2}\) and height \(24\text{ cm}\) is melted and recast into two similar solid right pyramids. The bases of the two pyramids are squares. The ratio of the base area of the smaller pyramid to the base area of the larger pyramid is \(1:4\). (a) Find the volume of the larger pyramid. [3 marks] (b) If the height of the larger pyramid is \(16\text{ cm}\), find the total surface area of the smaller pyramid. [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The volume of metal is \(144\times24=3456\text{ cm}^{3}\). The base-area ratio \(1:4\) gives a linear ratio \(1:2\), hence a volume ratio \(1:8\).
Step 2. Let the larger pyramid have volume \(V\); then the smaller has \(\dfrac{V}{8}\) and \(V+\dfrac{V}{8}=3456\), i.e. \(\dfrac{9V}{8}=3456\).
Step 3. \(V=\dfrac{3456\times8}{9}=3072\), so the larger pyramid has volume \(3072\text{ cm}^{3}\).
Step 4. \(3072=\dfrac{1}{3}a^{2}(16)\), so \(a^{2}=576\) and the base side of the larger pyramid is \(a=24\) cm.
Step 5. Its slant height is \(\ell=\sqrt{12^{2}+16^{2}}=20\) cm, so the total surface area is \(24(24)+4\times\dfrac{1}{2}(24)(20)=576+960=1536\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Step 6. Surface areas are in the ratio \(1:4\), so the smaller pyramid has total surface area \(\dfrac{1536}{4}=384\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Answer: (a) \(3072\text{ cm}^{3}\)   (b) \(384\text{ cm}^{2}\)
