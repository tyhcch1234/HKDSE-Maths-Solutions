Mensuration — Similar Solids, Volume & Total Surface Area

QQuestion

A solid metal right prism of base area \(84\text{ cm}^2\) and height \(20\text{ cm}\) is melted and recast into two similar solid right pyramids. The bases of the two pyramids are squares. The ratio of the base area of the smaller pyramid to the base area of the larger pyramid is \(4:9\).

(a) Find the volume of the larger pyramid. [3 marks]

(b) If the height of the larger pyramid is \(12\text{ cm}\), find the total surface area of the smaller pyramid. [4 marks]

Step 11M + 1M

Set up the similar-solid relationship. For similar solids, the ratio of volumes equals the cube of the ratio of corresponding lengths. Since areas scale as the square of lengths, \[k=\frac{A_s}{A_L}=\frac{4}{9}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{\ell_s}{\ell_L}=\sqrt{\frac{4}{9}}=\frac{2}{3} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{V_s}{V_L}=\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^3=\frac{8}{27}.\]

Let \(V\text{ cm}^3\) be the volume of the larger pyramid. Then the smaller pyramid has volume \(\left(\dfrac{4}{9}\right)^{3/2}V=\dfrac{8}{27}V=\left(\dfrac{4}{9}\right)^{1.5}V\).

Hint: The ratio of volumes of similar solids is the cube of the linear ratio, and the linear ratio is the square root of the area ratio.
Step 21M

Conserve volume (melted and recast → no volume lost): \[V + \left(\frac{4}{9}\right)^{3/2} V \;=\; (84)(20).\]

Since \(\left(\frac{4}{9}\right)^{3/2}=\frac{8}{27}\), this becomes \[\frac{35V}{27}=1680.\]

Hint: Always equate the total volume before with the total volume after. The prism volume \(=84\times 20=1680\) cm³.
Step 31A

Solve for \(V\): \[V=\frac{1680\times 27}{35}=1296.\]

Volume of the larger pyramid \(=1296\text{ cm}^3\).

0 / 4
Step 11M

Find the side of the larger square base. For a pyramid, \(V=\frac{1}{3}A h\), so \[1296=\frac{1}{3}a_L^2(12)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a_L^2=\frac{3(1296)}{12}=324 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a_L=18\text{ cm}.\]

Hint: Rearrange \(V=\frac13 A h\) to isolate the base area \(A=a_L^2\), then take the positive square root.
Step 21M

Total surface area of the larger pyramid. The slant height is \[s_L=\sqrt{h_L^2+\left(\frac{a_L}{2}\right)^2}=\sqrt{12^2+9^2}=15\text{ cm}.\] Each triangular face has area \(\frac12 a_L s_L\), and there are four of them: \[\text{TSA}_L=a_L^2+4\left(\tfrac12 a_L s_L\right)=18^2+4\left(\tfrac12\cdot 18\cdot\sqrt{9^2+12^2}\right)\] \[=(18)(18)+4\left(\tfrac12 (18)\sqrt{\left(\tfrac{18}{2}\right)^2+12^2}\right)=864\text{ cm}^2.\]

Hint: The slant height is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose legs are the pyramid height \(h_L\) and half the base side \(a_L/2\). Here \(9\)–\(12\)–\(15\) is a Pythagorean triple.
Step 31M

Use similarity for surface areas. Surface areas of similar solids scale as the square of the linear ratio. So \[\frac{\text{TSA}_s}{\text{TSA}_L}=\frac{A_s}{A_L}=\frac{4}{9} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{TSA}_s=\frac{4}{9}(864).\]

Hint: Since the two pyramids are similar, the ratio of their surface areas equals the ratio of corresponding areas, i.e. \(k=\frac49\).
Step 41A

Compute: \[\text{TSA}_s=\frac{4}{9}(864)=384.\]

Total surface area of the smaller pyramid \(=384\text{ cm}^2\).

✓Final Answers at a Glance

  • Part (a): Volume of the larger pyramid \(=1296\text{ cm}^3\)
  • Part (b): Total surface area of the smaller pyramid \(=384\text{ cm}^2\)
  • Key idea 1: For similar solids, \(\dfrac{V_s}{V_L}=\left(\dfrac{\ell_s}{\ell_L}\right)^3=\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{A_s}{A_L}}\right)^3\)
  • Key idea 2: For similar solids, \(\dfrac{\text{SA}_s}{\text{SA}_L}=\dfrac{A_s}{A_L}=\dfrac49\)
  • Key idea 3: Volume is conserved when a solid is melted and recast (no loss of material).
