HKDSE 2017 — Paper 1, Question 16

Formulas & Sequences

A city adopts a plan to import water from another city. It is given that the volume of water imported in the 1st year since the start of the plan is \(1.5\times10^{7}\ \mathrm{m^3}\) and in subsequent years, the volume of water imported each year is \(10\%\) less than the volume of water imported in the previous year.

(a) Find the total volume of water imported in the first 20 years since the start of the plan.  [2 marks]

(b) Someone claims that the total volume of water imported since the start of the plan will not exceed \(1.6\times10^{8}\ \mathrm{m^3}\). Do you agree? Explain your answer.  [2 marks]

Each year the volume is \(90\%\) of the previous year, so the volumes form a geometric sequence with

Question. A city adopts a plan to import water from another city. It is given that the volume of water imported in the 1st year since the start of the plan is \(2.4\times10^{7}\ \mathrm{m^3}\) and in subsequent years the volume imported each year is \(15\%\) less than the volume imported in the previous year. (a) Find the total volume of water imported in the first 20 years since the start of the plan. [2 marks] (b) Someone claims that the total volume of water imported since the start of the plan will not exceed \(1.7\times10^{8}\ \mathrm{m^3}\). Do you agree? Explain your answer. [2 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The volumes form a geometric sequence with \(a=2.4\times10^{7}\ \mathrm{m^3}\) and \(r=1-15\%=0.85\).
Step 2. \(S_{20}=\dfrac{2.4\times10^{7}\left(1-(0.85)^{20}\right)}{1-0.85}=\dfrac{2.4\times10^{7}\left(1-(0.85)^{20}\right)}{0.15}\).
Step 3. Since \((0.85)^{20}\approx0.0388\), \(S_{20}\approx153\,798\,475\ \mathrm{m^3}\approx1.54\times10^{8}\ \mathrm{m^3}\) (cor. to 3 sig. fig.).
Step 4. The total over all years is \(S_{\infty}=\dfrac{2.4\times10^{7}}{1-0.85}=1.6\times10^{8}\ \mathrm{m^3}\), and every partial sum is smaller.
Step 5. Since \(1.6\times10^{8}<1.7\times10^{8}\), the total never exceeds \(1.7\times10^{8}\ \mathrm{m^3}\). Agree.
Answer: (a) \(S_{20}\approx1.54\times10^{8}\ \mathrm{m^3}\)   (b) Agree, since \(S_{\infty}=1.6\times10^{8}<1.7\times10^{8}\)
