Water Import Plan — Geometric Sequences & Summation

Section A(2) · 4 marks total

Geometric Sequence Sum of a G.S. Sum to Infinity Estimation / Reasoning

Question

A city adopts a plan to import water from another city. It is given that the volume of water imported in the 1st year since the start of the plan is $1.5\times 10^{7}\ \text{m}^3$ and in subsequent years, the volume of water imported each year is $10\%$ less than the volume of water imported in the previous year.

(a) Find the total volume of water imported in the first 20 years since the start of the plan. [2 marks]
(b) Someone claims that the total volume of water imported since the start of the plan will not exceed $1.6\times 10^{8}\ \text{m}^3$. Do you agree? Explain your answer. [2 marks]

Step-by-Step Solution

Let $T_n$ be the volume of water imported in the $n$th year (in m³).

$$T_1 = 1.5\times 10^{7}$$

Each year the volume is $10\%$ less than the previous year, so it becomes $90\% = 0.9$ of it:

$$T_{n} = T_{n-1}\times 0.9$$

Hence $\{T_n\}$ is a geometric sequence with:

$$\text{first term } a = 1.5\times 10^{7}, \qquad \text{common ratio } r = 0.9$$
Hint: “$10\%$ less each year” always means multiply by $0.9$. The general term of a G.S. is $T_n = a\,r^{\,n-1}$.

The total imported in the first 20 years is $S_{20}=T_1+T_2+\cdots+T_{20}$:

$$S_{20}=1.5\times10^{7}+1.5\times10^{7}(0.9)+1.5\times10^{7}(0.9)^{2}+\cdots+1.5\times10^{7}(0.9)^{19}$$
Hint — Formula for the sum of the first $n$ terms of a G.S.
$$S_n=\frac{a(1-r^{\,n})}{1-r}\quad (r\neq 1)$$ where $a$ = first term, $r$ = common ratio, $n$ = number of terms.
Here $a=1.5\times10^{7}$, $r=0.9$, $n=20$.
$$S_{20}=\frac{1.5\times10^{7}\left(1-(0.9)^{20}\right)}{1-0.9}$$

Simplify the denominator ($1-0.9=0.1$) and evaluate:

$$S_{20}=\frac{1.5\times10^{7}(1-0.9^{20})}{0.1}$$
$$\phantom{S_{20}}= 131\,763\,501.8\ \text{m}^3$$

Rounding to 3 significant figures:

$S_{20}\approx 1.32\times10^{8}\ \text{m}^3$
Hint: Keep the exact value in your calculator until the last line, then round. “m³” must be stated in the final answer to earn the A mark.

“Since the start of the plan” means all years: $T_1+T_2+T_3+\cdots$ (infinitely many terms).

Because $0sum to infinity is less than $1.6\times10^{8}$:

$$T_1+T_2+T_3+\cdots < 1.6\times10^{8}$$
Hint — Sum to infinity of a G.S. $(|r|<1)$: $$S_\infty=\frac{a}{1-r}$$ Why: as $n\to\infty$, $r^{\,n}\to 0$, so $S_n=\dfrac{a(1-r^{\,n})}{1-r}\to\dfrac{a}{1-r}$.
$$S_\infty=\frac{1.5\times10^{7}}{1-0.9}=\frac{1.5\times10^{7}}{0.1}=1.5\times10^{8}\ \text{m}^3$$

Compare with the claimed limit:

$$1.5\times10^{8} < 1.6\times10^{8}$$

So the total will never exceed $1.6\times10^{8}\ \text{m}^3$:

The claim is agreed. ✓
Hint: The claim is about the grand total, so we use $S_\infty$ (not $S_{20}$). $S_\infty$ is the exact upper bound — the total can approach it but never reach it.
