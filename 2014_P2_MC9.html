HKDSE Math 2014 Paper 2 Q9 Topic: Percentages

Ratio & Percentage: Male vs Female Workers

#Percentage change #Linear equation #Word problem

There are 792 workers in a factory. If the number of male workers is 20% less than that of female workers, then the number of male workers is

A. 352 B. 360 C. 432 D. 440

Step-by-step Solution

Let \(x\) be the number of male workers.

💡 Hint

We choose the unknown as the quantity we want to find. Here the question asks for the number of male workers.

The number of male workers is 20% less than female workers.

So male workers = \(80\%\) of female workers.

\[\begin{aligned} \text{Male} &= \text{Female} \times (1 - 20\%) \\ x &= \text{Female} \times 0.8 \\ \text{Female} &= \frac{x}{0.8} = 1.25x \end{aligned}\]

Alternatively, female workers = \(x \div (1-20\%)\).

💡 Hint

“20% less” means the male count is \(1-20\%=80\%\) of the female count. To reverse, divide by \(0.8\).

Total workers = male + female = 792.

\[\begin{aligned} x + 1.25x &= 792 \\ 2.25x &= 792 \end{aligned}\]

💡 Hint

Combine like terms: \(1x + 1.25x = 2.25x\).

\[\begin{aligned} 2.25x &= 792 \\ x &= \frac{792}{2.25} \\ x &= 352 \end{aligned}\]

Thus the number of male workers is \(352\).

✔️ Check: Female workers \(= 1.25 \times 352 = 440\). Total \(= 352 + 440 = 792\).

Key Takeaway

  • “A is 20% less than B” → \(A = 0.8B\) → \(B = A / 0.8\).
  • Let the unknown be the quantity asked (male workers).
  • Use the total to form a linear equation in one variable.
