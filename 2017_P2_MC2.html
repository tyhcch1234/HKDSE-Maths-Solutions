HKDSE Math 2017 Paper 2 Q2

Laws of Indices

$\left(\dfrac{1}{9^{555}}\right)3^{444}=$

A. $0$
B. $\dfrac{1}{3^{111}}$
C. $\dfrac{1}{3^{222}}$
D. $\dfrac{1}{3^{666}}$

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — Express $9$ as a power of $3$

Since $9=3^2$,

$9^{555}=(3^2)^{555}=3^{2\times555}=3^{1110}$

Step 2 — Rewrite the reciprocal with a negative index

$\dfrac{1}{9^{555}}=\dfrac{1}{3^{1110}}=3^{-1110}$

Step 3 — Multiply the powers of $3$

When multiplying, add the indices:

$\left(\dfrac{1}{9^{555}}\right)3^{444}=3^{-1110}\times3^{444}=3^{-1110+444}=3^{-666}$

Step 4 — Express with a positive index

$3^{-666}=\dfrac{1}{3^{666}}$

Answer: D. $\dfrac{1}{3^{666}}$
