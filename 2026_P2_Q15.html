HKDSE 2026 · Paper 2 · MC15

Quadratic Graphs

Question

Let \(p\) and \(q\) be real constants such that \(pq<0\). Which of the following statements about the graph of \(y=(px+1)(qx+2)\) are true?

I. The graph opens downwards.
II. The graph has two \(x\)-intercepts.
III. The graph passes through the origin.

  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III

Solution

Statement I — direction of opening

Expanding, \(y=pqx^2+(2p+q)x+2\). The coefficient of \(x^2\) is \(pq<0\), so the parabola opens downwards. I is true.

Statement II — \(x\)-intercepts

The \(x\)-intercepts occur when \(px+1=0\) or \(qx+2=0\), i.e. at \(x=-\dfrac{1}{p}\) and \(x=-\dfrac{2}{q}\). Since \(pq<0\), \(p\) and \(q\) have opposite signs, so \(x=-\dfrac{1}{p}\) and \(x=-\dfrac{2}{q}\) also have opposite signs and are distinct. The graph has two \(x\)-intercepts. II is true.

Statement III — \(y\)-intercept

When \(x=0\), \(y=(1)(2)=2\neq 0\). The graph does not pass through the origin. III is false.

Final Answer: A
