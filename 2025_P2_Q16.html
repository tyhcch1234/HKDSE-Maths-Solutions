Question & Solution

[2025MC#16]

The height and the total surface area of a solid right circular cylinder are \(35\text{ cm}\) and \(492\pi\text{ cm}^2\) respectively. If the radius of a solid sphere is equal to the base radius of the circular cylinder, then the volume of the sphere is

  • A. \(144\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
  • B. \(288\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
  • C. \(576\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
  • D. \(864\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
Step 1 · Set up the model

Let \(r\) be the common radius of the cylinder (base radius) and of the sphere. The height of the cylinder is \(h = 35\text{ cm}\).

The total surface area of a cylinder includes the curved surface and the two circular ends.
Step 2 · Total surface area equation
Total Surface Area of a Cylinder \(2\pi rh+2\pi r^{2}\) The total surface area is the curved surface plus the two circular ends.

Total surface area:

\[ 2\pi r h + 2\pi r^2 = 492\pi \]

Substitute \(h=35\) and divide both sides by \(2\pi\):

\[ 35r + r^2 = 246 \]

Step 3 · Solve the quadratic
Factorisation of a Quadratic \(r^{2}+35r-246=(r-6)(r+41)\) Only the positive root \(r=6\) is a possible radius.

\[ r^2 + 35r - 246 = 0 \]

Factorising:

\[ (r-6)(r+41)=0 \]

So \(r=6\) or \(r=-41\). Since \(r>0\), we take \(r = 6\text{ cm}\).

Step 4 · Volume of the sphere
Volume of a Sphere \(V=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}\) The sphere has the same radius as the cylinder, so substitute \(r=6\) directly.

The sphere has radius \(r = 6\text{ cm}\).

\[ V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (6)^3 \]

\[ = \frac{4}{3}\pi (216) = 288\pi\text{ cm}^3 \]

Answer: B. \(288\pi\text{ cm}^3\)

Question. The height and the total surface area of a solid right circular cylinder are \(12\text{ cm}\) and \(378\pi\text{ cm}^{2}\) respectively. If the radius of a solid sphere equals the base radius of the cylinder, find the volume of the sphere.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let the base radius be \(r\). Total surface area: \(2\pi r(12)+2\pi r^{2}=378\pi\).
Step 2. Divide by \(2\pi\): \(12r+r^{2}=189\), i.e. \(r^{2}+12r-189=0\).
Step 3. Factorise: \((r-9)(r+21)=0\), so \(r=9\) (reject \(r=-21\)).
Step 4. Volume of the sphere \(=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(9)^{3}=972\pi\text{ cm}^{3}\).
Step 5. Check: \(2\pi(9)(12)+2\pi(81)=216\pi+162\pi=378\pi\) ✓
Answer: \(972\pi\text{ cm}^{3}\)
