Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Distance/Slope

T12 Q13

Find the slope of the line passing through the points $(-2, 4)$ and $(3, -1)$.

Solution

Use the slope formula: $m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$.
Let $(x_1,y_1)=(-2,4)$ and $(x_2,y_2)=(3,-1)$.
Substitute: $m=\frac{-1-4}{3-(-2)}$.
Simplify the numerator and denominator: $m=\frac{-5}{5}$.
Divide: $m=-1$.
The slope of the line passing through $(-2,4)$ and $(3,-1)$ is $-1$.
