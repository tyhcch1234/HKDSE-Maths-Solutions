Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Identities

T13 Q35

Express $\tan\theta$ in terms of $\sin\theta$ and $\cos\theta$.

Solution

Recall the definition of tangent in terms of sine and cosine: $\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$, provided the denominator is nonzero.
State the required restriction: this expression is valid when $\cos\theta\neq 0$.
$\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ for $\cos\theta\neq 0$.
