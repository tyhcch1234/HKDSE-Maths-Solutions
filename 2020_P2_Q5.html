Let \(f(x)=3x^{2}-x-2\). If \(\beta\) is a constant, then \(f(1+\beta)-f(1-\beta)=\)

  • A. \(\;2\beta\)
  • B. \(\;10\beta\)
  • C. \(\;6\beta^{2}-2\)
  • D. \(\;12\beta^{2}+4\beta-4\)
1 Write down the function and the two inputs \[ f(x)=3x^{2}-x-2,\qquad f(1+\beta)=3(1+\beta)^{2}-(1+\beta)-2 \] \[ f(1-\beta)=3(1-\beta)^{2}-(1-\beta)-2 \]
Substitute \(x=1+\beta\) and \(x=1-\beta\) separately first.
2 Expand the squares \[ (1+\beta)^{2}=1+2\beta+\beta^{2},\qquad (1-\beta)^{2}=1-2\beta+\beta^{2} \] \[ f(1+\beta)=3(1+2\beta+\beta^{2})-(1+\beta)-2 \] \[ f(1-\beta)=3(1-2\beta+\beta^{2})-(1-\beta)-2 \]
Use the identity \((a\pm b)^{2}=a^{2}\pm 2ab+b^{2}\).
3 Simplify each expression \[ f(1+\beta)=3+6\beta+3\beta^{2}-1-\beta-2=5\beta+3\beta^{2} \] \[ f(1-\beta)=3-6\beta+3\beta^{2}-1+\beta-2=-5\beta+3\beta^{2} \]
The constant terms \(3-1-2=0\) cancel nicely in both.
4 Subtract and collect like terms \[ \begin{aligned} f(1+\beta)-f(1-\beta) &=(5\beta+3\beta^{2})-(-5\beta+3\beta^{2})\\ &=5\beta+3\beta^{2}+5\beta-3\beta^{2}\\ &=10\beta \end{aligned} \]
The \(3\beta^{2}\) terms cancel, leaving a purely linear result.
Answer: B  —  \(f(1+\beta)-f(1-\beta)=10\beta\)
