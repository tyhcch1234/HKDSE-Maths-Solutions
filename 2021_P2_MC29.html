Box-and-Whisker Diagram Quartiles Inter-Quartile Range Multiple Choice

Question 1 mark · MC

The box-and-whisker diagram below shows the distribution of the ages of a group of researchers. Find the inter-quartile range of the distribution.

  • A. \(5\)
  • B. \(10\)
  • C. \(20\)
  • D. \(34\)

Part (a) — Read the Quartiles Analysis

The diagram gives us all five summary statistics directly. Drag the sliders below to explore what happens to the IQR as the lower quartile \(Q_1\) and upper quartile \(Q_3\) change.

26
36
\(Q_1\) = 26 \(Q_3\) = 36 \(Q_3 - Q_1\) = 10
1 1M Identify the quartiles from the box
In a box-and-whisker diagram:
  • the left edge of the box is the lower quartile \(Q_1\);
  • the right edge of the box is the upper quartile \(Q_3\);
  • the line inside the box is the median.
Reading off the diagram: \[ Q_1 = 26, \qquad Q_3 = 36 \]
Hint: Don't confuse the median with the quartiles. The IQR only uses the two ends of the box. The whiskers (ends at 20 and 44) are the minimum and maximum — they are not needed here.
2 1A Apply the IQR formula
\[ \text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 = 36 - 26 = 10 \]
Hint: The inter-quartile range measures the spread of the middle 50% of the data. It is unaffected by extreme values (the whisker extremes 20 and 44).
\(\text{Inter-quartile range} = 10\) → answer B

Part (b) — Why Not the Other Options? Distractors

Explore how the range and IQR behave for the same data. Move \(Q_1\) and \(Q_3\) and watch which statistics change.

26
36
Range = Max − Min = 24 IQR = 10 Median = 34
1 1A Check option A: \(5\)
Option A, \(5\), is half of the correct IQR. It might come from mis-reading one quartile (e.g. reading \(Q_1 = 31\) instead of \(26\)) or from dividing the IQR by 2: \[ \frac{36-26}{2}=5. \] There is no reason to halve the IQR.
Hint: The IQR is not divided by 2 — it is just \(Q_3 - Q_1\).
2 1A Check option C: \(20\)
Option C, \(20\), could arise from using the wrong pair of edges, e.g. \(Q_3 - \text{Min} = 36 - 20 = 16\) (not 20), or \(44 - 24 = 20\). The correct calculation uses only the two box edges \(26\) and \(36\).
Hint: Only the box edges give quartiles; the whisker ends are min/max.
3 1A Check option D: \(34\)
Option D, \(34\), is actually the median read from the diagram (the line inside the box). It is not the IQR. \[ \text{Median} = 34 \neq Q_3 - Q_1. \]
Hint: The median is the middle value; the IQR is a spread, not a centre.
Only \(36 - 26 = 10\) matches the definition → B

Summary of Answers

  • \(Q_1\) (lower quartile): \(26\)  — left edge of the box
  • \(Q_3\) (upper quartile): \(36\)  — right edge of the box
  • Median: \(34\)  — line inside the box (a distractor)
  • Minimum: \(20\), Maximum: \(44\)  — whisker ends
  • IQR: \(Q_3 - Q_1 = 36 - 26 = 10\)
  • Correct option: B
