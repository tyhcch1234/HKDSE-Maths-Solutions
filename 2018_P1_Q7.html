HKDSE Math 2018 Paper 1 Q7

Percentages Cost & Selling Price Discount
Q
The marked price of a vase is 30% above its cost. A loss of \$88 is made by selling the vase at a discount of 40% on its marked price. Find the marked price of the vase. [5 marks]
Progress
0%
1 Define the variable
Let $x$ be the marked price of the vase (in dollars).
💡 Hint: Start with the unknown you need to find.
2 Express the cost in terms of $x$
Marked price is 30% above cost, so: \[ x = \text{cost} \times (1 + 30\%) \] \[ \text{cost} = \frac{x}{1 + 30\%} = \frac{x}{1.3} = \frac{10x}{13} \]
📐 Remember: If marked price is 30% above cost, then \(\text{Marked Price} = \text{Cost} \times 1.3\). Rearranging: \(\text{Cost} = \dfrac{\text{Marked Price}}{1.3}\).
3 Express the selling price in terms of $x$
A 40% discount on marked price means the selling price is 60% of the marked price: \[ \text{Selling Price} = (1 - 40\%)x = 0.6x = \frac{3x}{5} \]
💡 Hint: Discount is always calculated on the marked price.
4 Set up the loss equation
Loss = Cost − Selling Price. The loss is \$88: \[ \frac{10x}{13} - \frac{3x}{5} = 88 \]
📐 Remember: Loss occurs when Cost > Selling Price.
5 Solve for $x$

Combine the fractions:

\[ \frac{10x}{13} - \frac{3x}{5} = \frac{50x - 39x}{65} = \frac{11x}{65} \]

So:

\[ \frac{11x}{65} = 88 \] \[ x = 88 \times \frac{65}{11} = 8 \times 65 = 520 \]

Marked price of the vase

\$520

📌 Key Takeaways

  • • Cost = Marked Price ÷ (1 + markup %)
  • • Selling Price = Marked Price × (1 − discount %)
  • • Loss = Cost − Selling Price  (if positive)
  • • Always define a variable for the unknown and express everything in terms of it.
