Price Ladder of the Vase

HKDSE 2018 · Paper 1 · Q7 · Percentages · [5 marks]

Question

The marked price of a vase is $30\%$ above its cost. A loss of $\$88$ is made by selling the vase at a discount of $40\%$ on its marked price. Find the marked price of the vase.

Step 1 · Define the unknown
Let $\$x$ be the marked price of the vase.
Hint: The question asks for the marked price, so let $x$ be exactly that. Everything else is written in terms of $x$.
Step 2 · Express the cost
Marked price is $30\%$ above cost, so \[ \text{cost}\times(1+30\%) = x \] \[ \text{cost} = \frac{x}{1+30\%} = \frac{x}{1.3} = \frac{10x}{13} \;\; (1\text{M}) \]
Hint: $+30\%$ means multiply the cost by $1.3$. To go backwards, divide by $1.3$.
Step 3 · Express the selling price
A $40\%$ discount is applied to the marked price: \[ \text{selling price} = (1-40\%)\,x = 0.6x = \frac{3x}{5} \;\; (1\text{M}) \]
Hint: A $40\%$ discount keeps $60\%$ of the marked price — that is the amount the customer actually pays.
Step 4 · Build the loss equation
Loss $=\text{cost}-\text{selling price}=88$: \[ \frac{10x}{13}-\frac{3x}{5}=88 \;\; (1\text{M}+1\text{A}) \] \[ \frac{50x-39x}{65}=88 \qquad\Rightarrow\qquad \frac{11x}{65}=88 \]
Hint: A loss means the cost is larger than the selling price, so subtract cost $-$ selling price (not the other way round).
Step 5 · Solve and conclude
\[ \frac{11x}{65}=88 \;\Rightarrow\; x=\frac{88\times 65}{11}=8\times 65=520 \;\; (1\text{A}) \] Thus, the marked price of the vase is $\$520$.
Check: cost $=\frac{10(520)}{13}=\$400$; selling price $=0.6(520)=\$312$; loss $=400-312=\$88$ ✓
Answer: The marked price of the vase is $\$520$.
