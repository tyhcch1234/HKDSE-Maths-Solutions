QQuestion

If the sum of the interior angles of a regular polygon is \(900^\circ\), which of the following is/are true?

I. The number of diagonals of the polygon is \(7\).
II. The number of folds of rotational symmetry of the polygon is \(7\).
III. The number of axes of reflectional symmetry of the polygon is \(7\).

A. I only    B. II only    C. I and III only    D. II and III only

(2 marks)

Step 11M
Let \(n\) be the number of sides. The sum of the interior angles of an \(n\)-sided polygon is \[ 180^\circ\times(n-2). \]
Hint: Split the polygon into \((n-2)\) triangles from one vertex — each triangle contributes \(180^\circ\).
Step 21A
Set this equal to the given sum and solve: \[ 180^\circ(n-2)=900^\circ \] \[ n-2=\frac{900}{180}=5 \] \[ n=7 \]
Answer: The polygon is a regular heptagon with \(n=7\) sides.

Step 11M
From \(n\) vertices, choose any 2 to form a segment: \[ C^{n}_{2}=\frac{n(n-1)}{2}=\frac{7\times 6}{2}=21. \] This counts both sides and diagonals together.
Hint: Of the 21 segments, exactly \(n=7\) of them are sides of the polygon.
Step 21A
Subtract the 7 sides: \[ \text{Number of diagonals}=\frac{7\times 6}{2}-7=21-7=14. \] Also check with the direct formula: \[ \frac{n(n-3)}{2}=\frac{7\times 4}{2}=14. \]
Answer: The number of diagonals is 14, not 7. Hence Statement I is NOT true.

Step 11M
For a regular \(n\)-gon, the number of folds of rotational symmetry equals \(n\) (rotating by \(\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}\) maps the figure onto itself).
Hint: There are \(n\) rotations, of angles \(0^\circ,\frac{360^\circ}{n},\frac{2\cdot360^\circ}{n},\dots,\frac{(n-1)\cdot360^\circ}{n}\).
Step 21A
With \(n=7\): \[ \text{Folds of rotational symmetry}=7. \]
Answer: Statement II is true (7 folds of rotational symmetry).
Step 31M
For a regular \(n\)-gon, the number of axes of reflectional symmetry also equals \(n\): one axis through each vertex and the midpoint of the opposite side (odd \(n\)), or through opposite vertices / opposite midpoints (even \(n\)).
Hint: A regular polygon is the "most symmetric" polygon — every axis passes through the centre and bisects the shape into congruent halves.
Step 41A
With \(n=7\): \[ \text{Axes of reflectional symmetry}=7. \]
Answer: Statement III is true (7 axes of reflectional symmetry).

✓Final Answer at a Glance

  • Interior angle sum: \(180^\circ(n-2)=900^\circ \Rightarrow n=7\).
  • Statement I — diagonals \(=\dfrac{7\times 6}{2}-7=14\)  ⇒ NOT true.
  • Statement II — rotational symmetry folds \(=n=7\)  ⇒ true.
  • Statement III — reflectional symmetry axes \(=n=7\)  ⇒ true.
Answer: D  (II and III only)
