Completing the Square & Optimisation

Completing the square Quadratic functions Vertex form Optimisation Area

Question

(a) Let \( f(x) = 36x - x^2 \). Using the method of completing the square, find the coordinates of the vertex of the graph of \( y = f(x) \). [2 marks]

(b) The length of a piece of string is \(108\text{ m}\). A guard cuts the string into two pieces. One piece is used to enclose a rectangular restricted zone of area \(A\text{ m}^2\). The other piece of length \(x\text{ m}\) is used to divide this restricted zone into two rectangular regions as shown in Figure 2.

(b)(i) Express \(A\) in terms of \(x\). [3 marks]

(b)(ii) The guard claims that the area of this restricted zone can be greater than \(500\text{ m}^2\). Do you agree? Explain your answer. [2 marks]

a Completing the square

\( f(x) = 36x - x^2 \)

Factor out \(-1\) to make the \(x^2\) coefficient positive for completing the square:

\( f(x) = -(x^2 - 36x) \)

💡 Hint: We want to write \(x^2 - 36x\) in the form \((x-h)^2 - h^2\). Here, half of \(36\) is \(18\).

Add and subtract \(18^2 = 324\) inside the bracket:

\( f(x) = -\big[ (x^2 - 36x + 324) - 324 \big] \)

\( f(x) = -\big[ (x - 18)^2 - 324 \big] \)

\( f(x) = -(x - 18)^2 + 324 \)

💡 Hint: The square term is \((x - 18)^2\). This is the vertex form \( y = a(x-h)^2 + k \) with \(a = -1\), \(h = 18\), \(k = 324\).

From \( f(x) = -(x - 18)^2 + 324 \), the vertex is at \((18, 324)\).

✅ Vertex = \((18, 324)\)

x = 18.0 f(x) = 324.0

The vertex is the highest point on the curve. Observe the symmetry.

b String & Restricted Zone

Total string: \(108\text{ m}\).

One piece of length \(x\text{ m}\) is used to divide the rectangle vertically. The other piece encloses the outer rectangle.

Outer perimeter = \(108 - x\) (since the dividing string is \(x\) m).

Let the rectangle have width \(w\) and height \(h\). The dividing string is vertical, so its length equals the height: \(h = x\).

💡 Hint: The perimeter comprises two widths and two heights. So \(2w + 2h = 108 - x\). Substitute \(h = x\) to find \(w\).

\( 2w + 2x = 108 - x \)

\( 2w = 108 - 3x \)

\( w = \dfrac{108 - 3x}{2} \)

💡 Hint: The area \(A\) of a rectangle is width × height. Here width is \(w\) and height is \(x\).

\( A = w \times h = \left( \dfrac{108 - 3x}{2} \right) \times x \)

\( A = x\left( \dfrac{108 - 3x}{2} \right) \)

\( A = \dfrac{108x - 3x^2}{2} = 54x - \dfrac{3}{2}x^2 \)

✅ \( A = 54x - \frac{3}{2}x^2 \) (or \( A = \frac{3}{2}(36x - x^2) \))

💡 Hint: Notice \( A = \frac{3}{2} f(x) \) where \( f(x) = 36x - x^2 \). The domain is \( 0 < x < 36 \) because width must be positive: \( 108 - 3x > 0 \Rightarrow x < 36 \).

From part (a), \( f(x) = 36x - x^2 \) has maximum value \(324\) at \(x = 18\).

Since \( A = \frac{3}{2} f(x) \), the maximum area is:

\( A_{\text{max}} = \frac{3}{2} \times 324 = 486\text{ m}^2\)

\(486 < 500\), so the area cannot exceed \(500\text{ m}^2\).

✅ The claim is disagreed.

📐 Adjust \(x\) and see the area

x = 18.0 Area = 486.0 m²

The maximum area is \(486\text{ m}^2\) at \(x = 18\). It never reaches \(500\text{ m}^2\).

🔑 Key Takeaway

  • Completing the square transforms \(y = ax^2 + bx + c\) into vertex form \(y = a(x-h)^2 + k\).
  • The vertex \((h, k)\) gives the maximum (if \(a < 0\)) or minimum (if \(a > 0\)) value.
  • For optimisation problems, express the quantity as a quadratic and find its vertex.
  • Always check the domain (e.g., \(0 < x < 36\)) to ensure the vertex lies within the valid range.
