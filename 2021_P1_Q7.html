Question

In a polar coordinate system, \(O\) is the pole. The polar coordinates of the points \(P\) and \(Q\) are \(\left(r,80^{\circ}\right)\) and \(\left(r,140^{\circ}\right)\) respectively, where \(r\) is a positive constant. It is given that the distance between \(P\) and \(Q\) is 21. Find

  1. \(\angle POQ\),
  2. \(r\),
  3. the perimeter of \(\triangle OPQ\).  (4 marks)
Step 1 · Part (a) — the angle at the pole
Angle between Two Polar Coordinates \(\angle POQ=\theta_{2}-\theta_{1}\ \text{ where }\ \theta_{2}>\theta_{1}\) The two points lie at \(80^{\circ}\) and \(140^{\circ}\); since \(140^{\circ}>80^{\circ}\), \(\angle POQ=140^{\circ}-80^{\circ}=60^{\circ}\).

The two points make angles \(80^{\circ}\) and \(140^{\circ}\) with the initial line;
the larger of the two is \(140^{\circ}\), so

\[ \angle POQ = 140^{\circ}-80^{\circ}=60^{\circ}. \]

Step 2 · Part (b) — find \(r\)
Cosine Rule \(c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}-2ab\cos C\) With \(a=b=r\) and \(C=60^{\circ}\): \(PQ^{2}=2r^{2}-2r^{2}\cos60^{\circ}=r^{2}\).

Since \(OP=OQ=r\), \(\triangle OPQ\) is isosceles.
By the cosine rule on \(\triangle OPQ\):

\[ PQ^{2}=r^{2}+r^{2}-2r^{2}\cos 60^{\circ}=2r^{2}-r^{2}=r^{2}, \]

so \(PQ=r\). Given \(PQ=21\),

\[ r=21. \]

Equivalently: an isosceles triangle with vertex angle \(60^{\circ}\) is equilateral.
Step 3 · Part (c) — the perimeter
Perimeter of a Triangle \(P=a+b+c\) All three sides equal \(21\), so the perimeter is \(63\).

All three sides of \(\triangle OPQ\) equal \(21\), so the perimeter is

\[ 21+21+21=63. \]

Final Answer

(a) \(\;60^{\circ}\)  |  (b) \(\;r=21\)  |  (c) \(\;63\)

Question. In a polar coordinate system, \(O\) is the pole. The polar coordinates of the points \(P\) and \(Q\) are \(\left(r,30^{\circ}\right)\) and \(\left(r,150^{\circ}\right)\) respectively, where \(r\) is a positive constant. It is given that the distance between \(P\) and \(Q\) is \(18\sqrt{3}\).
(a) Find \(\angle POQ\).
(b) Find \(r\).
(c) Find the perimeter of \(\triangle OPQ\). [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The two points lie at angles \(30^{\circ}\) and \(150^{\circ}\) with the initial line;
since \(150^{\circ}>30^{\circ}\), so \(\angle POQ=150^{\circ}-30^{\circ}=120^{\circ}\).
Step 2. Since \(OP=OQ=r\), the cosine rule gives \(PQ^{2}=2r^{2}-2r^{2}\cos120^{\circ}=2r^{2}+r^{2}=3r^{2}\).
Step 3. So \(PQ=r\sqrt{3}\). Given \(PQ=18\sqrt{3}\), we get \(r=18\).
Step 4. The perimeter is \(OP+OQ+PQ=18+18+18\sqrt{3}=36+18\sqrt{3}\).
Answer: (a) \(120^{\circ}\) (b) \(r=18\) (c) \(36+18\sqrt{3}\)
