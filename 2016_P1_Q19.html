HKDSE 2016 Paper 1 — Question 19

Question. Figure 2 shows a geometric model \(ABCD\) in the form of tetrahedron. It is given that \(\angle BAD = 86^\circ\), \(\angle CBD = 43^\circ\), \(AB = 10\text{ cm}\), \(AC = 6\text{ cm}\), \(BC = 8\text{ cm}\) and \(BD = 15\text{ cm}\).

(a) Find \(\angle ABD\) and \(CD\). [4 marks]
(b) A craftsman claims that the angle between \(AB\) and the face \(BCD\) is \(\angle ABC\). Do you agree? Explain your answer. [2 marks]
STEP 1 · Triangle ABD — Find ∠ABD (sine rule)

Applicable triangle \(ABD\): known \(AB=10\), \(BD=15\), \(\angle BAD=86^\circ\).

$$\frac{AB}{\sin\angle ADB}=\frac{BD}{\sin\angle BAD}$$ $$\frac{10}{\sin\angle ADB}=\frac{15}{\sin 86^\circ}$$ $$\sin\angle ADB = \frac{10\sin 86^\circ}{15}\approx 0.6650$$ $$\angle ADB \approx 41.6851^\circ \quad \text{or} \quad 138.3149^\circ\ (\text{rejected})$$

Rejecting the obtuse value (since \(\angle ABD\) must remain positive):

$$\angle ABD = 180^\circ - 86^\circ - 41.6851^\circ \approx 52.3149^\circ$$
\(\boxed{\angle ABD \approx 52.3^\circ}\)
STEP 2 · Triangle BCD — Find CD (cosine rule)

In \(\triangle BCD\): \(BC = 8\), \(BD = 15\), \(\angle CBD = 43^\circ\).

$$CD^{2}=BC^{2}+BD^{2}-2(BC)(BD)\cos\angle CBD$$ $$CD^{2}=8^{2}+15^{2}-2(8)(15)\cos 43^\circ$$ $$CD^{2}\approx 64 + 225 - 240(0.73135) \approx 113.476$$ $$CD \approx 10.65247$$
\(\boxed{CD \approx 10.7\text{ cm}}\)
STEP 3 · Is \(\angle ACB = 90^\circ\)? — Test with Pythagoras

Checking triangle \(ABC\) with sides \(AC=6\), \(BC=8\), \(AB=10\):

$$AC^{2}+BC^{2}=6^{2}+8^{2}=36+64=100=AB^{2}$$

Hence \(\triangle ABC\) is right-angled at \(C\):

$$\angle ACB = 90^\circ$$
STEP 4 · Find AD in triangle ABD (cosine rule)

With \(AB=10\), \(BD=15\), \(\angle ABD \approx 52.3149^\circ\):

$$AD^{2}=AB^{2}+BD^{2}-2(AB)(BD)\cos\angle ABD$$ $$AD^{2}\approx 10^{2}+15^{2}-2(10)(15)\cos 52.3149^\circ$$ $$AD^{2}\approx 100 + 225 - 300(0.61147) \approx 141.559$$ $$AD \approx 11.8990 \approx 11.8996$$
STEP 5 · Find ∠ACD in triangle ACD (cosine rule)

With \(AC=6\), \(CD\approx 10.65247\), \(AD\approx 11.8996\):

$$\cos\angle ACD = \frac{AC^{2}+CD^{2}-AD^{2}}{2(AC)(CD)}$$ $$\cos\angle ACD \approx \frac{6^{2} + 10.65247^{2} - 11.8996^{2}}{2(6)(10.65247)}$$ $$\cos\angle ACD \approx \frac{36 + 113.476 - 141.600}{127.830} \approx 0.0616$$ $$\angle ACD \approx 86.47^\circ$$

Since \(\angle ACD \neq 90^\circ\), \(AC\) is not perpendicular to \(CD\).

STEP 6 · Conclusion — Disagree with the claim

The angle between line \(AB\) and plane \(BCD\) is not simply \(\angle ABC\).

In fact, \(\angle ACB = 90^\circ\) only shows \(AC \perp BC\), but the foot of the perpendicular from \(A\) onto plane \(BCD\) is not \(C\) because \(AC\) is not perpendicular to \(CD\) (and hence not perpendicular to the plane \(BCD\)).

Therefore the angle between \(AB\) and face \(BCD\) is not \(\angle ABC\).

💡 The projection of \(A\) onto plane \(BCD\) lies somewhere else on line \(BC\) (extended), not at \(C\) itself. So the claimed angle is incorrect.
✅ DISAGREE — The angle between \(AB\) and face \(BCD\) is not \(\angle ABC\).
