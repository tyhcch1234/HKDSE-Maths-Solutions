[2025MC#42]  ·  14. Probability
A committee is formed by $2$ managers, $4$ officers and $12$ clerks. If $7$ members are selected from the committee to form a working group consisting of at least $1$ manager, how many different working groups can be formed?
A. 16016
B. 20384
C. 22880
D. 31824
Step-by-step solution
1
Read off the totals
Managers: $2$  
· 
Officers: $4$  
· 
Clerks: $12$.
Total committee size: \[ 2+4+12 = 18. \] We choose $7$ members,
and the group must contain at least $1$ manager.
Hint: the phrase “at least 1” is the classic signal for the complement (subtraction) method.
2
Count all possible groups of 7
Combination \({}^{n}C_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}\) Choosing \(7\) from \(18\) members gives \({}^{18}C_{7}=31\,824\) groups.
Order is irrelevant, so use combinations: \[ {}^{18}C_{7} = 31824. \]
Hint: “select 7 members” → $C^{18}_{7}$, not $P^{18}_{7}$.
3
Subtract the “no manager” groups
Complementary Counting \(\text{at least one}=(\text{total})-(\text{none})\) \({}^{18}C_{7}-{}^{16}C_{7}=31\,824-11\,440=20\,384\).
Groups with no manager must be formed from the $4+12=16$ non‑managers: \[ {}^{16}C_{7} = 11440. \] So the required number is \[ {}^{18}C_{7}-{}^{16}C_{7} = 31824-11440 = 20384. \]
Hint: “at least 1 manager” $=$ (all groups) $-$ (groups with 0 managers).
4
Check by direct counting
Split by the number of managers chosen ($1$ or $2$): \[ {}^{2}C_{1}{}^{16}C_{6}+{}^{2}C_{2}{}^{16}C_{5} = 2(8008)+1(4368) = 16016+4368 = 20384. \] Both methods agree.
Hint: a direct sum is a good verification, but the complement method is faster for MC.
Answer:  $\displaystyle {}^{18}C_{7}-{}^{16}C_{7}=20384$   →  B

Question. A committee consists of \(3\) managers, \(5\) officers and \(10\) clerks. In how many ways can \(6\) members be selected to form a working group containing at least \(1\) manager?

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Total members \(=3+5+10=18\), so there are \({}^{18}C_{6}=18\,564\) groups of \(6\).
Step 2. Groups with no manager use the \(15\) non-managers: \({}^{15}C_{6}=5005\).
Step 3. At least \(1\) manager: \(18\,564-5005=13\,559\).
Answer: \(13\,559\)
