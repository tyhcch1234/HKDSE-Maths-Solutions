[2012MC#31]

The H.C.F. and the L.C.M. of three expressions are $ab^2$ and $4a^4b^5c^6$ respectively. If the first expression and the second expression are $2a^2b^4c$ and $4a^4b^2c^6$ respectively, then the third expression is

  • A. $ab^2$.
  • B. $ab^5$.
  • C. $2ab^2c$.
  • D. $2ab^5c$.
1

Key idea — HCF and LCM work prime-factor by prime-factor

HCF and LCM of Expressions by Prime Powers \(\text{HCF}=\prod p^{\min(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3})},\qquad \text{LCM}=\prod p^{\max(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3})}\) Every prime can be handled on its own, so the given HCF and LCM give one min equation and one max equation per prime.

Write every expression in prime-factor form.
For each prime $p$ let the powers in the three expressions be $x_1,x_2,x_3$. Then

power in HCF $=\min(x_1,x_2,x_3)$,
 
 
power in LCM $=\max(x_1,x_2,x_3)$.

Hint: the HCF supplies the smallest power, the LCM the largest — so a number between them is forced.
2

List the known information

Let the third expression be $2^{p}a^{q}b^{r}c^{s}$, where $p,q,r,s$ are non-negative integers.

ExpressionsPower of 2Power of aPower of bPower of c
1st: $2a^2b^4c$1241
2nd: $4a^4b^2c^6$2426
3rd: $2^pa^qb^rc^s$$p$$q$$r$$s$
HCF $=ab^2$0120
LCM $=4a^4b^5c^6$2456
Hint: $4=2^2$, so the LCM carries $2^2$. Reading the table row by row gives four mini-equations.
3

Solve for each exponent with min / max

Solving Min-Max Conditions \(\min(4,2,r)=2\ \text{ and }\ \max(4,2,r)=5\ \Longrightarrow\ r=5\) For the factor \(b\) both conditions must hold together, so the largest power \(b^{5}\) has to come from the third expression.

Factor 2: $\min(1,2,p)=0$ forces $p=0$;
check $\max(1,2,p)=2$ ✓

Factor a: $\min(2,4,q)=1$ forces $q=1$;
check $\max(2,4,q)=4$ ✓

Factor b: $\min(4,2,r)=2$ forces $r\ge 2$, and $\max(4,2,r)=5$ forces $r=5$.

Factor c: $\min(1,6,s)=0$ forces $s=0$;
check $\max(1,6,s)=6$ ✓

Hint: for factor $b$ the two conditions must hold at the same time — take the overlapping value $r=5$.
4

Assemble the third expression

Zero Index \(a^{0}=1\quad(a\neq0)\) \(p=0\) and \(s=0\) mean the factors \(2\) and \(c\) are missing from the third expression.

The third expression $=2^{p}a^{q}b^{r}c^{s}=2^{0}a^{1}b^{5}c^{0}=ab^{5}$.

Hint: $2^0=1$ and $c^0=1$, so only $a$ and $b$ survive.
5

Check the answer

Expressions are $2a^2b^4c$, $4a^4b^2c^6$, $ab^5$.

HCF: $\min$ powers $=(2^0a^1b^2c^0)=ab^2$ ✓  
 
LCM: $\max$ powers $=2^2a^4b^5c^6=4a^4b^5c^6$ ✓

Hint: always substitute back — a single wrong exponent spoils one of the two given conditions.

Answer: B. $\;ab^{5}$

Question. The HCF and the LCM of the three expressions \(2a^{2}b^{3}\), \(4a^{4}b\) and a third expression are \(ab\) and \(4a^{4}b^{3}\) respectively. Find the third expression.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let the third expression be \(2^{p}a^{q}b^{r}\). Write the powers of each prime in a table: for \(2\) they are \(1,2,p\) with HCF power \(0\) and LCM power \(2\);
for \(a\) they are \(2,4,q\) with HCF \(1\) and LCM \(4\);
for \(b\) they are \(3,1,r\) with HCF \(1\) and LCM \(3\).
Step 2. Factor \(2\): \(\min(1,2,p)=0\) forces \(p=0\), and \(\max(1,2,0)=2\) agrees with the LCM \(4=2^{2}\) ✓.
Step 3. Factor \(a\): \(\min(2,4,q)=1\) forces \(q=1\), and \(\max(2,4,1)=4\) ✓. Factor \(b\): \(\min(3,1,r)=1\) gives \(r\ge1\),
while \(\max(3,1,r)=3\) forces \(r=3\).
Step 4. Hence the third expression is \(2^{0}a^{1}b^{3}=ab^{3}\).
Step 5. Check: the lowest powers are \(2^{0},a^{1},b^{1}\), giving \(ab\) ✓;
the highest powers are \(2^{2},a^{4},b^{3}\), giving \(4a^{4}b^{3}\) ✓.
Answer: \(ab^{3}\)
