▸Step-by-Step Guided Solution

1 Turn the ratio into an equation ▼

The notation $a:b = c:d$ is just a compact way of writing the fraction equation:

$$\frac{a}{b}=\frac{c}{d}$$

Here $a=3y-4x$, $b=2x+y$, $c=5$, $d=6$. So:

$$\frac{3y-4x}{2x+y}=\frac{5}{6}$$
💡
Concept: A ratio is a fraction. Writing it as a fraction lets you use all the normal algebraic tools (cross-multiplication, rearrangement).
2 Cross-multiply to clear the fractions ▼

Multiply both sides by $6$ and by $(2x+y)$:

$$6\,(3y-4x) \;=\; 5\,(2x+y)$$
💡
Why is this allowed? Because $y\neq 0$ and $x\neq 0$ are given, the denominator $2x+y$ could still be zero in principle — but the question guarantees the ratio is well defined, so we may multiply through safely.
3 Expand both brackets ▼
$$18y - 24x \;=\; 10x + 5y$$

Distribute carefully: $6\cdot 3y = 18y$, $6\cdot(-4x)=-24x$, $5\cdot 2x = 10x$, $5\cdot y = 5y$.

4 Collect like terms (get all $x$'s on one side) ▼

Move the $x$-terms to the left and the $y$-terms to the right:

$$-24x - 10x \;=\; 5y - 18y$$
$$-34x \;=\; -13y$$
💡
Common slip: Remember to flip the sign when a term "crosses the equals sign". $-11x$ and $-11y$ would be the wrong result if you forget.
5 Divide to isolate $\dfrac{x}{y}$ ▼

Both sides are negative, so divide by $-1$ first (the signs cancel):

$$34x = 13y$$

Now divide both sides by $34y$ (allowed because $y\neq 0$):

$$\frac{x}{y} \;=\; \frac{13}{34}$$
6 Convert the fraction back into ratio notation ▼

Since $\dfrac{x}{y}=\dfrac{13}{34}$, we have:

$$x:y \;=\; 13:34$$

Therefore the answer is D.

💡
Notation check: $\dfrac{x}{y}=\dfrac{a}{b}\;\Longleftrightarrow\;x:y=a:b$. Note the order matters — it is $x$ to $y$, not $y$ to $x$.

!Common Traps to Avoid

  • Trap 1 — Flipping the ratio: Writing $y:x=\dfrac{13}{34}$ and answering $34:13$. The question asks for $x:y$.
  • Trap 2 — Sign slip in cross-multiplication: Forgetting that $(3y-4x)$ has a minus in front of $4x$ gives $-24x$ on expansion, not $+24x$.
  • Trap 3 — Dividing by the wrong variable: Dividing both sides by $13x$ instead of $34y$ leads to an inverted result.
  • Trap 4 — Treating the ratio as an equation of the form $3y-4x=5$: The ratio $5:6$ means the fraction equals $\tfrac56$, not that each term equals $5$ and $6$.
