Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Sine/Cosine Law

T13 Q22

In triangle \(ABC\), \(A = 30^\circ\) and \(B = 60^\circ\). Find \(C\).

Solution

The angle sum of a triangle is $180^\circ$, so $A+B+C=180^\circ$.
Substitute $A=30^\circ$ and $B=60^\circ$: $30^\circ+60^\circ+C=180^\circ$.
Simplify: $90^\circ+C=180^\circ$.
Subtract $90^\circ$ from both sides: $C=90^\circ$.
Therefore, $C=90^\circ$.
