HKDSE 2025 · Paper 2 Q28 Probability

A box contains six cards numbered $2,2,2,3,4$ and $5$ respectively. In a game, a number is randomly drawn from the box and a certain number of tokens will be got according to the following table:

Number drawn $2$ $3$ $4$ $5$
Number of tokens got $10$ $15$ $25$ $50$

Find the expected number of tokens got in the game.

A. $3$
B. $4$
C. $20$
D. $25$

Step-by-step Solution

1Find the probability of drawing each number
Probability of an Event \(P(E)=\dfrac{n(E)}{n(S)}\) Each number is weighted by the number of cards showing it, so \(2\) carries probability \(\tfrac{3}{6}\).

The box has $6$ cards in total: three cards show $2$,
one card shows $3$, one card shows $4$, one card shows $5$.

$$P(2)=\frac{3}{6},\quad P(3)=\frac{1}{6},\quad P(4)=\frac{1}{6},\quad P(5)=\frac{1}{6}$$

Probability = (number of favourable cards) ÷ (total cards) = $\dfrac{n}{6}$.
2List each outcome with its token value

From the table:

$$\text{Number }2 \to 10 \text{ tokens},\quad \text{Number }3 \to 15 \text{ tokens},$$

$$\text{Number }4 \to 25 \text{ tokens},\quad \text{Number }5 \to 50 \text{ tokens}$$

The token values depend on which card is drawn.
3Apply the expectation formula
Expected Value \(E=\sum p_{i}x_{i}\) Multiply every token value by its probability, then add the four products.

Expected value is the sum of (probability × value) over all outcomes:

$$\begin{aligned} E &= P(2)\times 10 + P(3)\times 15 + P(4)\times 25 + P(5)\times 50 \\[4pt] &= \frac{3}{6}\times 10 + \frac{1}{6}\times 15 + \frac{1}{6}\times 25 + \frac{1}{6}\times 50 \end{aligned}$$

Each term = probability of that card × tokens for that card.
4Compute the sum

$$\begin{aligned} E &= \frac{30}{6} + \frac{15}{6} + \frac{25}{6} + \frac{50}{6} \\[4pt] &= \frac{30+15+25+50}{6} \\[4pt] &= \frac{120}{6} \\[4pt] &= 20 \end{aligned}$$

Combine the numerators over the common denominator $6$.
Answer: C. 20
