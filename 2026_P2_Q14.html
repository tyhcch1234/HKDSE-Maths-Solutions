Question
In the figure, the equations of the straight lines \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) are \(ax+y=b\) and \(cx+y=d\) respectively. It is given that \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) intersect at a point on the positive \(x\)-axis. Which of the following are true?
  • I. \(\; a<c\)
  • II. \(\; b>d\)
  • III. \(\; ad=bc\)
AI and II only
BI and III only
CII and III only
DI, II and III
Step-by-step Solution
STEP 1 · Read the \(y\)-intercepts (II)
y-intercept of a Line \(y=-ax+b\Rightarrow y\text{-intercept}=b\) At \(x=0\) the intercepts are \(b\) and \(d\), and \(L_{1}\) meets the axis higher, so \(b>d\).

The \(y\)-intercepts of \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) are \(b\) and \(d\). From the figure, \(L_1\) meets the \(y\)-axis higher,
so \(b>d\).

✓ Statement II is TRUE

STEP 2 · Common \(x\)-intercept (III)
Common Point on the x-axis \(t=\dfrac{b}{a}=\dfrac{d}{c}\Rightarrow ad=bc\) The shared point \((t,0)\) lies on both lines, so \(b/a=d/c\) and hence \(ad=bc\).

Both lines pass through the same point \((t,0)\) with \(t>0\),
so

$$t=\frac{b}{a}=\frac{d}{c}\;\Rightarrow\;bc=da\;\Rightarrow\;ad=bc$$

✓ Statement III is TRUE

STEP 3 · Compare the slopes (I)
Slope of a Line in General Form \(ax+y=b\Rightarrow\text{slope}=-a\) \(L_{1}\) is steeper, so \(-a<-c\); both \(a\) and \(c\) are positive, hence \(a>c\) and I is false.

The slopes are \(-a\) and \(-c\), and both are negative since the lines slope downwards from positive \(y\)-intercepts,
so \(a>0\) and \(c>0\). From the figure \(L_1\) is steeper (falls faster),
so \(-a<-c\), i.e. \(a>c\).

✗ Statement I is FALSE

Hint: A steeper downward slope means the line falls faster, which corresponds to a larger magnitude of \(a\), not a smaller one.
