T2 Q21

A sum of money is divided among three people in the ratio $2:3:5$. If the smallest share is $\$30$, find the value of one part.

Solution

The ratio is $2:3:5$, so the smallest share corresponds to $2$ parts.
Given the smallest share is $\$30$, we have $2$ parts $= \$30$.
Divide both sides by $2$: $1$ part $= \frac{\$30}{2} = \$15$.
The value of one part is $\$15$.
