HKDSE 2025 · Paper 2 · Q34

Topic 8 — Laws of Indices, Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

On the same rectangular coordinate system, the graph of $y=\log_a x$ cuts the graph of $y=a^x$ and the $x$-axis at the points $P$ and $Q$ respectively, where $a$ is a positive constant. Denote the origin by $O$. Which of the following are true?

I.  $a<1$
II.  $OQ > a$
III.  $\angle POQ = 45^\circ$

  • A.  I and II only
  • B.  I and III only
  • C.  II and III only
  • D.  I, II and III
1

Decide on the size of $a$

If $a>1$, then $y=a^x$ is increasing and $y=\log_a x$ is also increasing; in fact $a^x>x>\log_a x$ for all $x>0$, so the two graphs never meet. But the question says they do cut at $P$, so this case is impossible.

Hence $0I is true.

Hint: an exponential and its own logarithm only cross when the base is smaller than 1.
2

Locate $Q$, the $x$-intercept of $y=\log_a x$

Put $y=0$: $\;\log_a x=0 \Rightarrow x=a^{0}=1$.

So $Q=(1,0)$, therefore

$$OQ=1.$$

Since $0a$. Hence II is true.

Hint: the log curve always crosses the $x$-axis at $(1,0)$, whatever the base.
3

Use the symmetry of the pair of curves

For $a>0,\;a\neq 1$, the graphs of $y=a^x$ and $y=\log_a x$ are reflections of each other in the line $y=x$.

Reason: $\log_a x$ is the inverse function of $a^x$. Indeed, if $P=(p,q)$ lies on $y=a^x$, then $q=a^{p}$, so $\log_a q=p$, i.e. $(q,p)$ lies on $y=\log_a x$.

So $P=(x_0,y_0)$ and its mirror image $(y_0,x_0)$ are the corresponding points on the two curves.

Hint: swapping $x$ and $y$ reflects a point in the line $y=x$.
4

Reflect about $y=x$ to get $\angle POQ$

Reflect the whole figure in the mirror line $y=x$. The $x$-axis maps to the $y$-axis, so $Q=(1,0)$ maps to $Q'=(0,1)$.

But $P$ lies on the curve $y=\log_a x$, so its mirror image $P'$ lies on $y=a^x$. Since $P$ is the only intersection of the two curves, its mirror image must be $P$ itself — meaning $P$ lies on the mirror line $y=x$, i.e. $P=(t,t)$.

Therefore $OP$ makes $45^\circ$ with the $x$-axis and $OQ$ lies along the $x$-axis, giving

$$\angle POQ=45^\circ.$$

Hence III is true.

Hint: a point fixed by reflection in $y=x$ must satisfy $y=x$.
5

Conclude

Statements I, II and III are all true, so the answer is D.

All three statements pass — pick the option listing I, II and III.
Answer: D  (I, II and III are all true)
