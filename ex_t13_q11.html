Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Ratios

T13 Q11

In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite an angle $\theta$ has length $6$, and $\sin\theta = \frac{3}{5}$. Find the length of the hypotenuse.

Solution

Hyp $= \tfrac{6}{3/5} = 10$.
