Circle Geometry

Question

In the figure, \(O\) is the centre of the circle \(PQRS\). \(PR\) and \(OQ\) intersect at the point \(T\). If \(\angle POT=82^\circ\) and \(\angle PTQ=104^\circ\), then \(\angle QSR=\)

  • A. \(22^\circ\)
  • B. \(27^\circ\)
  • C. \(30^\circ\)
  • D. \(49^\circ\)

Solution

Same line Since \(O,T,Q\) are collinear, \(\angle POT=\angle POQ=82^\circ\).
Triangle POT \(\angle PTO=180^\circ-104^\circ=76^\circ\), so \(\angle OPT=180^\circ-82^\circ-76^\circ=22^\circ\).
Isosceles OPR \(OP=OR\) (radii) \(\Rightarrow \angle ORP=\angle OPR=22^\circ \Rightarrow \angle POR=180^\circ-2(22^\circ)=136^\circ\).
Central angle \(\angle QOR=\angle POR-\angle POQ=136^\circ-82^\circ=54^\circ\).
Angle at circumference \(\angle QSR=\tfrac12\angle QOR=\tfrac12(54^\circ)=27^\circ\).
Answer: B — 27°
