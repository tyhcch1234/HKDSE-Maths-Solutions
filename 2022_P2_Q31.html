Question. Which of the following is the least?

  • A. $\;(-345)^{768}$
  • B. $\;453^{-786}$
  • C. $\;\left(\dfrac{1}{435}\right)^{867}$
  • D. $\;\left(\dfrac{2}{543}\right)^{876}$
STEP 1

Strategy: take logarithms to compare

Power Law of Logarithms \(\log a^{n}=n\log a\) Each option becomes one product, e.g. \(\log 345^{768}=768\log 345\).
Logarithm is an Increasing Function \(0 Comparing the four logarithms ranks the four powers, however huge they are.

All four numbers are huge powers. Instead of comparing the enormous values directly, take $\log$ of each — because $\log x$ is an increasing function, the number with the smallest logarithm is the smallest number.

We use the law $\log a^{n}=n\log a$ on each option.

Hint: turning powers into products makes the comparison a simple arithmetic one.
STEP 2

Option A: $\;(-345)^{768}$

Since $768$ is even, $(-345)^{768}=345^{768}$ (positive).

$$\log(-345)^{768}=\log 345^{768}=768\log 345\approx 768(2.5378)\approx 1949.0$$

This is a huge positive value → option A is the largest, not the least.

Even power kills the negative sign.
STEP 3

Option B: $\;453^{-786}$

Negative exponent means reciprocal: $453^{-786}=\dfrac{1}{453^{786}}$, a tiny positive number.

$$\log 453^{-786}=-786\log 453\approx -786(2.6562)\approx -2087.7$$

A negative logarithm means the value is less than $1$.
STEP 4

Option C: $\;\left(\dfrac{1}{435}\right)^{867}$

Logarithm of a Reciprocal \(\log\dfrac{1}{x}=-\log x\quad(x>0)\) \(\left(\dfrac{1}{435}\right)^{867}\) has logarithm \(-867\log 435\approx-2287.6\), the smallest of the four.

$$\log\left(\frac{1}{435}\right)^{867}=867\log\frac{1}{435}=867\log 435^{-1}=-\,867\log 435$$

$$\approx -867(2.6385)\approx -2287.6$$

$\log\frac{1}{x}=-\log x$ — the most negative so far.
STEP 5

Option D: $\;\left(\dfrac{2}{543}\right)^{876}$

$$\log\left(\frac{2}{543}\right)^{876}=876\log\frac{2}{543}=876\big(\log 2-\log 543\big)$$

$$\approx 876(0.30103-2.73480)\approx 876(-2.43377)\approx -2132.0$$

Again a negative value, but not as negative as option C.
STEP 6

Compare all four logarithms

OptionNumber$\log$(value)Rank
A$(-345)^{768}$$\approx +1949.0$largest
C$(1/435)^{867}$$\approx -2287.6$least
D$(2/543)^{876}$$\approx -2132.0$2nd least
B$453^{-786}$$\approx -2087.7$3rd least

Since $\log x$ is an increasing function of $x$, the smallest logarithm gives the smallest number:

$$-2287.6 \;<\; -2132.0 \;<\; -2087.7 \;<\; +1949.0$$

Therefore $\left(\dfrac{1}{435}\right)^{867}$ is the least.

Order the logarithms on a number line — the leftmost one is the answer.
Answer:  $\boxed{\text{C}}\;$  $\left(\dfrac{1}{435}\right)^{867}$ is the least.

Question. Which of the following is the least? A. \(\left(\dfrac{1}{6}\right)^{50}\) B. \(9^{-40}\) C. \(\left(\dfrac{2}{7}\right)^{45}\) D. \((-5)^{60}\) [1 mark]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Take logarithms: \(\log x\) is increasing, so the least number has the least logarithm.
Step 2. A: \(\log\left(\dfrac{1}{6}\right)^{50}=-50\log 6\approx-50(0.7782)=-38.91\).
Step 3. B: \(\log 9^{-40}=-40\log 9\approx-40(0.9542)=-38.17\).
Step 4. C: \(\log\left(\dfrac{2}{7}\right)^{45}=45\log\dfrac{2}{7}\approx45(-0.5441)=-24.48\).
Step 5. D: \((-5)^{60}=5^{60}\), so its logarithm is \(60\log 5\approx+41.91\), the largest of all.
Step 6. \(-38.91<-38.17<-24.48<41.91\), so A is the least.
Answer: A. \(\left(\dfrac{1}{6}\right)^{50}\)
