HKDSE 2023 Paper 2 Q34 Complex Numbers

Real part of a complex fraction

If \(k\) is a real number, then the real part of \[ \frac{i}{k-i} + \frac{2}{k+i} \] is

A. \(\displaystyle \frac{2k+1}{k^2-1}\)
B. \(\displaystyle \frac{2k-1}{k^2+1}\) Correct
C. \(\displaystyle \frac{2k+1}{k^2+1}\)
D. \(\displaystyle \frac{2k-1}{k^2-1}\)

📘 Step-by-step solution

1

Combine into a single fraction

The common denominator is \((k-i)(k+i) = k^2 - i^2 = k^2 + 1\).

\[ \frac{i}{k-i} + \frac{2}{k+i} = \frac{i(k+i) + 2(k-i)}{(k-i)(k+i)} \]

💡 Hint

Multiply numerator and denominator of each term by the conjugate of the denominator. Note \(i^2 = -1\).

2

Expand the numerator

Expand \(i(k+i) + 2(k-i)\):

\(= ik + i^2 + 2k - 2i\)

\(= ik - 1 + 2k - 2i\) (since \(i^2 = -1\))

\(= (2k - 1) + (k - 2)i\)

💡 Hint

Group real and imaginary terms together. Remember \(i^2 = -1\).

3

Simplify denominator & separate real part

Denominator: \((k-i)(k+i) = k^2 - i^2 = k^2 + 1\).

\[ \frac{(2k-1) + (k-2)i}{k^2 + 1} = \frac{2k-1}{k^2+1} + \frac{k-2}{k^2+1}i \]

Therefore the real part is \(\displaystyle \frac{2k-1}{k^2+1}\).

💡 Hint

Write the result in the form \(a + bi\). The real part is the term without \(i\).

4

Identify the correct option

Comparing with the choices, we see that

\(\displaystyle \frac{2k-1}{k^2+1}\) matches option B.
