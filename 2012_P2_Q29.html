2012MC#29 15. Statistics

The bar chart below shows the distribution of the numbers of rings owned by the girls in a group. Find the standard deviation of the distribution correct to 2 decimal places.

  • A. 1.04
  • B. 1.16  
  • C. 1.19
  • D. 2.09
1Read the data from the chart.
Number of rings $x$: $0,\ 1,\ 2,\ 3,\ 4$
Number of girls (frequency $f$): $2,\ 8,\ 4,\ 6,\ 2$
Hint: the bar height gives the frequency of each value of $x$.
2Find the total number of girls $n$.
\[ n = 2+8+4+6+2 = 22 \]
Hint: $n=\sum f$ is the size of the distribution.
3Compute the mean $\mu=\dfrac{\sum fx}{n}$.
Mean of a Frequency Distribution \(\mu=\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}\) Multiply each number of rings by its frequency, add the \(fx\) column to get \(42\), then divide by the \(22\) girls.
$x$01234
$f$28462
$fx$088188
\[ \sum fx = 0+8+8+18+8 = 42 \qquad \mu = \frac{42}{22} = \frac{21}{11} \approx 1.9091 \]
Hint: the mean is marked by the red dashed line on the chart.
4Compute the variance $\sigma^2=\dfrac{\sum f(x-\mu)^2}{n}$.
Variance of a Frequency Distribution \(\sigma^{2}=\dfrac{\sum f(x-\mu)^{2}}{\sum f}\) Each squared deviation is weighted by the number of girls owning that many rings.
\[ \sigma^2=\frac{\left(0-\frac{21}{11}\right)^{2}\!\!\times 2 +\left(1-\frac{21}{11}\right)^{2}\!\!\times 8 +\left(2-\frac{21}{11}\right)^{2}\!\!\times 4 +\left(3-\frac{21}{11}\right)^{2}\!\!\times 6 +\left(4-\frac{21}{11}\right)^{2}\!\!\times 2}{22} \] \[ =\frac{441+800+100+1536+1444}{121\times 22} =\frac{4321}{2662}=\frac{164}{121} \]
Hint: each bar's height gives the weight of its squared deviation from the mean.
5Take the square root: standard deviation.
Standard Deviation \(\sigma=\sqrt{\sigma^{2}}\) Taking the square root of \(\dfrac{164}{121}\) returns the spread to the original unit, number of rings.
\[ \sigma=\sqrt{\frac{164}{121}}=\frac{\sqrt{164}}{11}\approx 1.16420 \] \[ \sigma \approx 1.16 \ (\text{corr. to 2 d.p.}) \]
Hint: finish by square-rooting the variance, then round to 2 d.p.
Answer: B. 1.16

Question. The table below shows the distribution of the numbers of pets owned by the \(20\) members of a club. Number of pets \(x\): \(1,\ 2,\ 3,\ 4,\ 5\); frequency \(f\): \(3,\ 5,\ 6,\ 4,\ 2\). Find the standard deviation of the distribution correct to \(2\) decimal places.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(n=\sum f=3+5+6+4+2=20\).
Step 2. \(\sum fx=3(1)+5(2)+6(3)+4(4)+2(5)=3+10+18+16+10=57\), so \(\mu=\dfrac{57}{20}=2.85\).
Step 3. \(\sum f(x-\mu)^{2}=3(1.85)^{2}+5(0.85)^{2}+6(0.15)^{2}+4(1.15)^{2}+2(2.15)^{2}=10.2675+3.6125+0.135+5.29+9.245=28.55\).
Step 4. \(\sigma^{2}=\dfrac{28.55}{20}=1.4275\), so \(\sigma=\sqrt{1.4275}\approx1.1948\).
Step 5. \(\sigma\approx1.19\) (correct to \(2\) decimal places).
Answer: \(1.19\)
