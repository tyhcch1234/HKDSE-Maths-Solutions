Q12Stem-and-Leaf & Box Plot

Stems (×10) Given leaves Unknowns w Before / After box

Step 1 — Read the given data: stems are tens, leaves are units.

Question (HKDSE 2025 Paper 1 Q12)

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the weights (in kg) of some athletes before training.

Stem (tens)Leaf (units)
45 6 8
5w w 6 7
63 4 5 6 9 9
70 2
87

The difference of the range and the inter-quartile range of the above distribution is $25$ kg.

(a) Write down the range of the above distribution. Hence, find $w$.  (3 marks)

(b) The box-and-whisker diagram below shows the distribution of the weights (in kg) of the athletes after the training.

[ Box plot: left whisker to $46$; left edge of box $54$; median line $61$; right edge of box $64$; right whisker to $67$. ]

(b)(i) Find the change in the upper quartile of the distribution due to the training.
(b)(ii) Is the distribution of the weights of the athletes after the training less dispersed than that before the training? Explain your answer.  (4 marks)

Step 1Identify the data.

Stems are tens, leaves are units. The given (non-$w$) values are:

$45,46,48,\;56,57,\;63,64,65,66,69,69,\;70,72,\;87$ — that is $14$ known values plus the two unknowns $w$.

So the total number of athletes is $16$.

Step 2(a) Range.

Largest $=87$, smallest $=45$:

$$\text{Range}=87-45=42\text{ kg}$$

The range only uses the extreme values, which are known.

Range of a Data Set \(\text{range}=\text{largest value}-\text{smallest value}\) The largest mass is \(87\) and the smallest is \(45\), so the range is \(42\text{ kg}\).
Step 3Find the IQR.

Given: Range $-$ IQR $=25$, so

$$\text{IQR}=42-25=17\text{ kg}$$

This is the key given relation — use it to unlock the quartiles.

Inter-quartile Range \(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) Since range \(-\) IQR \(=25\), the IQR is \(42-25=17\text{ kg}\).
Step 4Locate the quartiles.

From the diagram the largest value is $87$, so upper quartile $=69$ kg (the upper quartile must be at least as large as the $n=16$ data's $12$th value; without $w$ the value $69$ is forced).

Hence the lower quartile:

$$Q_1 = 69-17 = 52\text{ kg}$$

A stem of $5$ with leaf $2$ gives $52$.

Quartiles of an Ordered Data Set \(Q_{1}=\text{median of the lower half},\quad Q_{3}=\text{median of the upper half}\) With \(16\) athletes the quartiles sit at the \(4\)th and \(12\)th values, so \(Q_{1}=Q_{3}-17\).
Step 5Solve for $w$.

The $4$ leaves in stem $5$ are $w,\,w,\,6,\,7$. Arranged in increasing order, the $4$th value of the whole set is $Q_1$.

The stem-$5$ group is the $4$th to $7$th values of the $16$ values, so $Q_1=52$ must be the smallest leaf of stem $5$:

$$\boxed{w=2}$$

Then stem $5$ reads $52,\,52,\,56,\,57$ — consistent and ordered.

Step 6(b)(i) Change in upper quartile.

Before training: $Q_3=69$ kg. After training: from the box plot the right edge of the box is $64$ kg.

$$\text{Change}=64-69=-5\text{ kg}$$

Thus the upper quartile is decreased by $5$ kg.

A negative change means it went down.

Step 7(b)(ii) Compare the ranges.

After training, from the box plot: min $=46$, max $=67$.

$$\text{Range after}=67-46=21\text{ kg}$$

Range before $=42$ kg (from Step 2).

Since both sets use ranges as the dispersion measure, compare them directly.

Step 8Conclusion.

$21 < 42$, so the range after training is smaller.

Therefore the distribution after training is less dispersed than before training.

Answers:   (a) Range $=42$ kg, $\;w=2$;   (b)(i) $-5$ kg (upper quartile decreased by $5$ kg);   (b)(ii) Yes — range after ($21$ kg) $<$ range before ($42$ kg), so it is less dispersed.
PRACTICESimilar question — try it yourself

Question. The masses, in kg, of \(16\) students are
\(21, 23, 25, 27, 28, 30, 31, 33, 34, 36, 38, 40, 41, 43, 45, 47\).
(a) Find the range. (b) Find the inter-quartile range.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Range \(=47-21=26\text{ kg}\).
Step 2. The lower half is the first \(8\) values, so \(Q_{1}\) is the average of the \(4\)th and \(5\)th values: \(Q_{1}=\dfrac{27+28}{2}=27.5\text{ kg}\).
Step 3. The upper half is the last \(8\) values, so \(Q_{3}\) is the average of the \(12\)th and \(13\)th values: \(Q_{3}=\dfrac{40+41}{2}=40.5\text{ kg}\).
Step 4. IQR \(=40.5-27.5=13\text{ kg}\).
Answer: (a) \(26\text{ kg}\) (b) \(13\text{ kg}\)
