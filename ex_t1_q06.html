Percentages (30) · Sub-Topic: Interest

T1 Q06

A loan of \$20,000 is charged simple interest at 6% p.a. Find the total interest payable over 30 months.

Solution

30 months $= 2.5$ years.
$I = 20000 \times 0.06 \times 2.5$
$I = 3000$
Total interest is $\$3000$.
