[2013MC#25] The equation of the circle \(C\) is \[ 2x^2+2y^2-4x+8y-5=0 .\] The coordinates of the points \(P\) and \(Q\) are \((-1,2)\) and \((4,0)\) respectively. Which of the following is/are true?
  1. The radius of \(C\) is 5.
  2. The mid-point of \(PQ\) lies outside \(C\).
  3. If \(G\) is the centre of \(C\), then \(\angle PGQ\) is an acute angle.
A. I only    B. II only    C. I and III only    D. II and III only
STEP 1 · Rewrite C in standard form

Divide throughout by 2 and complete the squares.

\[ x^2+y^2-2x+4y-\tfrac{5}{2}=0 \]

\[ (x^2-2x+1)+(y^2+4y+4)=1+4+\tfrac{5}{2} \]

\[ (x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=\tfrac{15}{2} \]

So the centre is \(G=(1,-2)\) and \(r^2=\dfrac{15}{2}\), \(r=\dfrac{\sqrt{30}}{2}\approx 2.74\).

Hence statement I is FALSE (radius is \(\tfrac{\sqrt{30}}{2}\), not 5).

General Form of the Equation of a Circle \(x^{2}+y^{2}+Dx+Ey+F=0\ \text{has centre}\ \left(-\dfrac{D}{2},-\dfrac{E}{2}\right)\) Dividing \\(2x^{2}+2y^{2}-4x+8y-5=0\\) by \\(2\\) first makes the coefficients of \\(x^{2}\\) and \\(y^{2}\\) equal to \\(1\\), then completing the square gives \\(G(1,-2)\\).
STEP 2 · Mid-point M of PQ

\[ M=\left(\frac{-1+4}{2},\ \frac{2+0}{2}\right)=\left(\frac{3}{2},\,1\right) \]

Distance from centre \(G=(1,-2)\) to \(M\):

\[ GM=\sqrt{\left(\tfrac{3}{2}-1\right)^2+\left(1-(-2)\right)^2}=\sqrt{\tfrac14+9}=\frac{\sqrt{37}}{2}\approx 3.04 \]

Compare with \(r=\dfrac{\sqrt{30}}{2}\approx 2.74\). Since \(GM>r\), \(M\) lies outside \(C\).

Statement II is TRUE.

Distance Formula \(d=\sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}+(y_{2}-y_{1})^{2}}\) Bare \\(GM\\) is only useful once compared with \\(r=\dfrac{\sqrt{30}}{2}\\); here \\(GM=\dfrac{\sqrt{37}}{2}\\) is larger.
STEP 3 · Angle PGQ

Consider circle \(D\) with diameter \(PQ\). Its centre is \(M\), the mid-point of \(PQ\).

\[ \text{Radius of } D=\tfrac12 PQ=\tfrac12\sqrt{(4-(-1))^2+(0-2)^2}=\tfrac{\sqrt{29}}{2}\approx 2.69 \]

Since \(GM=\dfrac{\sqrt{37}}{2}>\dfrac{\sqrt{29}}{2}\), the point \(G\) lies outside circle \(D\).

By the angle-in-a-semicircle rule: if a variable point \(X\) lies outside the circle with diameter \(AB\), then \(\angle AXB\) is acute.

Statement III is TRUE.

Angle in a Semicircle \(\angle AXB=90^\circ\ \Longleftrightarrow\ X\ \text{lies on the circle with diameter}\ AB\) Since \\(G\\) is farther from \\(M\\) than the radius \\(\tfrac{\sqrt{29}}{2}\\) of circle \\(D\\), \\(G\\) is outside it and \\(\angle PGQ\\) is acute.
STEP 4 · Conclusion

I: False  |  II: True  |  III: True

The correct choice is D (II and III only).

Answer: D
