Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q28

A sphere has a radius of $6$ cm. Find its volume.

Solution

Use the sphere volume formula: $V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$.
Substitute $r=6$: $V=\frac{4}{3}\pi(6)^3$.
Compute $6^3=216$, so $V=\frac{4}{3}\pi(216)$.
Simplify: $V=288\pi$.
The volume of the sphere is $288\pi\text{ cm}^3$ (approximately $904.78\text{ cm}^3$).
