[2025MC#23]

The figure shows the quadrilateral $ABCD$. If

$\angle ABC + \angle ADC = \angle ACD = \angle BAC = 90^\circ$,

which of the following must be true?

A. $\tan\angle ACB=\dfrac{AB}{AD}$
B. $\tan\angle ACB=\dfrac{AB}{CD}$
C. $\tan\angle ADC=\dfrac{BC}{AD}$
D. $\tan\angle ADC=\dfrac{BC}{CD}$
STEP 1 · Read the right angles

Given $\angle ACD = \angle BAC = 90^\circ$, triangle $ABC$ is right-angled at $A$ and triangle $ACD$ is right-angled at $C$.

Write the two acute angles of $\triangle ABC$ as $x$ and $y$: \[ \angle ABC = x,\qquad \angle ACB = y,\qquad x+y=90^\circ \] (angle sum of a triangle).

STEP 2 · Use $\angle ADC = y$

Let $\angle ADC = y$. In $\triangle ACD$ (right angle at $C$): \[ \angle CAD = 90^\circ - y = x \] So $\triangle ACD$ has angles $x,\;90^\circ,\;y$ — exactly the same three angles as $\triangle ABC$.

STEP 3 · Similar triangles (AAA)

Since both triangles have angles $x,\,90^\circ,\,y$: \[ \triangle ABC \sim \triangle CAD \quad (\text{AAA}) \] Matching the order — $A\leftrightarrow C$, $B\leftrightarrow A$, $C\leftrightarrow D$ — gives the ratio of corresponding sides: \[ \frac{AB}{CA}=\frac{BC}{AD}=\frac{AC}{CD}=k \]

Watch the vertex order: the right angle of $\triangle ABC$ (at $A$) matches the right angle of $\triangle ACD$ (at $C$).

STEP 4 · Find $\tan\angle ADC$

In right-angled $\triangle ACD$ with the right angle at $C$: \[ \tan\angle ADC=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}=\frac{AC}{CD} \] From Step 3, $\dfrac{AC}{CD}=\dfrac{BC}{AD}$ (corresponding sides of similar triangles).

Therefore \[ \tan\angle ADC=\frac{AC}{CD}=\frac{BC}{AD} \]

STEP 5 · Check the options

A. $\tan\angle ACB=\dfrac{AB}{AD}$? From $\triangle ABC$: $\tan\angle ACB=\dfrac{AB}{AC}\neq\dfrac{AB}{AD}$. ✗

B. $\tan\angle ACB=\dfrac{AB}{CD}$? $\tan\angle ACB=\dfrac{AB}{AC}\neq\dfrac{AB}{CD}$. ✗

C. $\tan\angle ADC=\dfrac{BC}{AD}$? Matches Step 4. ✓

D. $\tan\angle ADC=\dfrac{BC}{CD}$? $\tan\angle ADC=\dfrac{AC}{CD}\neq\dfrac{BC}{CD}$. ✗

Answer: C

$\triangle ABC\sim\triangle CAD$ (AAA), so $\dfrac{AC}{CD}=\dfrac{BC}{AD}$, hence $\tan\angle ADC=\dfrac{AC}{CD}=\dfrac{BC}{AD}$.
