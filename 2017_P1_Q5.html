Solving Compound Inequalities

This question tests your ability to solve linear inequalities, find the intersection of solution sets, and count integer values that satisfy both conditions.

Question

(a) Find the range of values of \(x\) which satisfy both

\(7(x - 2) \le \dfrac{11x + 8}{3}\) and \(6 - x < 5\)

(b) How many integers satisfy both inequalities in (a)?

[4 marks]

Visualizing the Solution Range

The shaded region represents values of \(x\) that satisfy both inequalities.

\(-\infty\) \(+\infty\)

Solution: \(1 < x \le 5\)

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Solve the first inequality

\(7(x - 2) \le \dfrac{11x + 8}{3}\)

Multiply both sides by 3 to eliminate the fraction:

\(21(x - 2) \le 11x + 8\)

Expand the left side:

\(21x - 42 \le 11x + 8\)

Subtract \(11x\) from both sides and add 42:

\(10x \le 50\)

\(\therefore x \le 5\)

💡 Hint: When multiplying or dividing by a negative number, reverse the inequality sign. Here we divide by 10 (positive), so the sign stays the same.
2

Solve the second inequality

\(6 - x < 5\)

Subtract 6 from both sides:

\(-x < -1\)

Multiply both sides by -1. Reverse the inequality sign!

\(x > 1\)

💡 Hint: When we multiply or divide by a negative number, the direction of the inequality changes. For example: \(-2 < 3\) becomes \(2 > -3\) when multiplied by -1.
3

Combine the two inequalities

From Step 1: \(x \le 5\)

From Step 2: \(x > 1\)

The solution must satisfy both conditions. This means \(x\) is between 1 and 5, with 1 not included and 5 included.

\(\therefore 1 < x \le 5\)

💡 Hint: The intersection of \(x > 1\) and \(x \le 5\) is written as a compound inequality. On the number line, this is the region between open circle at 1 and closed circle at 5.
4

Count the integers (Part b)

The integers satisfying \(1 < x \le 5\) are:

\(2, 3, 4, 5\)

Note: 1 is not included (strict inequality), 5 is included (non-strict).

There are 4 integers in total.

Answer: 4

💡 Hint: Always double-check which endpoints are included. Here \(x > 1\) means 1 is not included, while \(x \le 5\) means 5 is included.

Final Answers

Part (a)

\(1 < x \le 5\)

Part (b)

4 integers
