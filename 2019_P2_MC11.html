Percentages Compound Interest Compounded Quarterly MC Answer: C

Question

A sum of $65\,000 is deposited at an interest rate of 7% per annum for 8 years, compounded quarterly. Find the amount correct to the nearest dollar.

A. $101 400
B. $111 682
C. $113 244
D. $113 609

i Core Idea

When interest is compounded several times per year, the annual rate is split and the number of periods is multiplied. The formula becomes:

$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{n\,t}$$

$P$ = principal · $r$ = annual rate · $n$ = compounds per year · $t$ = years

Step-by-Step Solution

Tap each step to reveal the reasoning.

Principal $P = 65000$
Annual rate $r = 7\%$
Time $t = 8$ years
Compounds / yr $n = 4$
Hint: "Quarterly" means interest is added 4 times a year, so $n = 4$.

Split the annual rate across the four quarters:

$$\frac{r}{n} = \frac{7\%}{4} = 1.75\% = 0.0175$$
Hint: Each quarter earns $1.75\%$ interest, not the full $7\%$.

Over $8$ years with $4$ periods per year:

$$n \times t = 4 \times 8 = 32 \text{ periods}$$
Hint: The answer uses the exponent $8 \times 4 = 32$ — a common trap is to use $8$.

Formula in general form:

$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{n\,t}$$

Now substitute all values:

$$A = 65000\left(1 + \frac{7\%}{4}\right)^{8 \times 4}$$
Hint: Write $\dfrac{7\%}{4}$ as $\dfrac{0.07}{4}$ in your calculator, and the exponent as $(8 \times 4)$.
$$A = 65000\,(1.0175)^{32} = 113243.8770\ldots$$

Round to the nearest dollar:

$113 244 (correct to nearest dollar) → Answer: C

! Common Traps

  • ✕ Using the exponent $8$ instead of $8 \times 4 = 32$.
  • ✕ Using $7\%$ as the per-period rate instead of $\dfrac{7\%}{4} = 1.75\%$.
  • Remember: period rate = annual rate ÷ n, exponent = n × years.
