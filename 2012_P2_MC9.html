HKDSE Math 2012 · Paper 2 · Q9

Ratio Problem: Finding $x:y$

Topic 2 · Rate, Ratio and Variations

Ratio Algebraic Manipulation Cross-Multiplication Non-zero values

❓Question

If $x$ and $y$ are non-zero numbers such that $$\frac{6x+5y}{3y-2x}=7,$$ then $x:y=$
A $4:5$
B $4:13$
C $5:4$
D $13:4$

🧩Step-by-Step Guided Solution

Progress
0 / 4
1 Remove the fraction (Cross-multiply) ▾

We want to eliminate the denominator. Multiply both sides by $(3y-2x)$.

Hint: The denominator must be non-zero — this is guaranteed because $x$ and $y$ are non-zero and the equation is defined.
$$\frac{6x+5y}{3y-2x}=7 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 6x+5y = 7(3y-2x)$$
2 Expand the bracket ▾

Distribute the $7$ on the right-hand side:

$$6x+5y = 21y - 14x$$
Hint: $7(3y-2x) = 7\cdot3y - 7\cdot2x = 21y - 14x$.
3 Collect like terms ▾

Move all $x$-terms to one side and all $y$-terms to the other:

$$6x + 14x = 21y - 5y$$ $$20x = 16y$$
Hint: Adding $14x$ to both sides removes the $-14x$ on the right; subtracting $5y$ from both sides removes $5y$ on the left.
4 Divide and simplify to get $x:y$ ▾

Divide both sides by $y$ (since $y\neq0$) and then by $20$:

$$\frac{x}{y} = \frac{16}{20} = \frac{4}{5}$$

Hence $x:y = \boxed{4:5}$ — that is option A.

Hint: Always reduce the fraction to its simplest form — divide numerator and denominator by the HCF (here $4$).
✅ Correct Answer: A. $4:5$
