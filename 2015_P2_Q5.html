Step 1 — Given identity: compare like terms on both sides.

HKDSE 2015 · Paper 2 · Q5  |  Topic 4: Polynomials

If $m$ and $n$ are constants such that $$x^{2}+mx+n \equiv (x+4)(x-m)+6,$$ then $n=$

  • A. $-8$
  • B. $-2$
  • C. $2$
  • D. $6$
1Expand the right-hand side

Expand the product and collect like terms:

$$(x+4)(x-m)+6 = x^{2}-mx+4x-4m+6 = x^{2}+\bigl(\hl{-m+4}\bigr)x+\bigl(\hl{-4m+6}\bigr)$$

Hint: multiply each term of the first bracket by each term of the second, then add the $+6$ constant.
2Write the identity and compare coefficients

Since the two polynomials are identically equal, the coefficients of $x$ and the constant terms must match:

$$x^{2}+\hl{ m }x+\hl{ n } \;\equiv\; x^{2}+\hl{(-m+4)}x+\hl{(-4m+6)}$$

$$ \begin{cases} m = -m+4 & \text{……(1)}\\[4pt] n = -4m+6 & \text{……(2)} \end{cases} $$

Hint: the $x^{2}$ terms already agree, so only the $x$-term and the constant term give equations.
3Solve equation (1) for $m$

$$m=-m+4 \;\Rightarrow\; 2m=4 \;\Rightarrow\; \hl{ m=2 }$$

Hint: bring the $m$ terms together — add $m$ to both sides.
4Substitute $m=2$ into equation (2)

$$n=-4(\hl{2})+6 = -8+6 = \hl2{-2}$$

Check: $x^{2}+2x-2 \equiv x^{2}+(4-2)x+(-8+6)$ ✓

Hint: substitute the known value of $m$ back into the constant-term equation to get $n$.
Answer:   B.   $n = -2$
