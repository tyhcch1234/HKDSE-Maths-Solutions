★ Exam Tips

  • Look for four terms: When you see an expression with four terms, try grouping them into pairs.
  • Difference of squares first: If a pair is of the form \(a^2 - b^2\), factor it immediately.
  • Create a common bracket: The goal is to make the same bracket appear in both groups so you can factor it out.
  • Sign awareness: Be careful with negatives — e.g., \(-5u+5v = -5(u-v)\), not \(5(u-v)\).
  • Verify by expansion: In an MCQ, you can always multiply your answer back out to confirm it matches the original expression.
