T11 Q38

In $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle PQR$, $\angle B = \angle Q = 90^\circ$, $AC = PR$, and $AB = PQ$. Name the condition that proves $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle PQR$.

Solution
