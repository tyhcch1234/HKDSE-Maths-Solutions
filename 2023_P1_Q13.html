HKDSE Math 2023 Paper 1 Q13

Polynomials: Division & Rational Roots

Polynomial division Remainder theorem Quadratic formula Rational roots

Question

Define \( g(x) = x^3 + 5x^2 - 12x - 1 \). Let \( h(x) = 3x^4 + ax^3 - 16x^2 + bx + c \), where \( a, b \) and \( c \) are constants. When \( h(x) \) is divided by \( g(x) \), the quotient and the remainder are equal.

  1. (a) Find the quotient when \( h(x) \) is divided by \( g(x) \). [3 marks]
  2. (b) How many rational roots does the equation \( h(x) = 0 \) have? Explain your answer. [4 marks]

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Set up the division equation

Let the quotient be \( mx + n \) and the remainder be \( mx + n \) (since they are equal).

Since \( h(x) \) is degree 4 and \( g(x) \) is degree 3, the quotient must be of degree 1 (i.e., linear).

\[ h(x) = g(x) \cdot (mx + n) + (mx + n) \] \[ h(x) = (mx + n)(x^3 + 5x^2 - 12x - 1) + mx + n \]
💡 Hint: The division algorithm states: Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder. Here quotient = remainder.
2

Expand and compare coefficients

Expand the right-hand side and compare with \( h(x) = 3x^4 + ax^3 - 16x^2 + bx + c \).

\[ (mx+n)(x^3+5x^2-12x-1) + mx + n = mx^4 + (5m+n)x^3 + (-12m+5n)x^2 + (-m-12n)x - n + mx + n \]

Comparing the coefficient of \( x^4 \):

\[ m = 3 \]

Comparing the coefficient of \( x^3 \):

\[ 5m + n = a \quad \Rightarrow \quad 5(3) + n = a \quad \Rightarrow \quad a = 15 + n \]
💡 Hint: Match coefficients of corresponding powers of \( x \). Start with the highest power.
3

Find \( n \) using the \( x^2 \) coefficient

From the \( x^2 \) term on the RHS: \( -12m + 5n \). This must equal \( -16 \) from \( h(x) \).

\[ -12m + 5n = -16 \]

Substitute \( m = 3 \):

\[ -12(3) + 5n = -16 \quad \Rightarrow \quad -36 + 5n = -16 \quad \Rightarrow \quad 5n = 20 \quad \Rightarrow \quad n = 4 \]

The quotient is \( 3x + 4 \).

💡 Hint: We used the \( x^2 \) coefficient because it contains both \( m \) and \( n \), and we already know \( m = 3 \).
4

Find the remaining constants \( a, b, c \)

Now \( m = 3 \) and \( n = 4 \).

  • \( a = 5m + n = 5(3) + 4 = 19 \)
  • \( x \) coefficient: \( -m - 12n + m = -12n = -12(4) = -48 \). So \( b = -48 \).
  • Constant term: \( -n + n = 0 \). So \( c = 0 \).

Thus \( h(x) = 3x^4 + 19x^3 - 16x^2 - 48x \).

💡 Hint: Compare all coefficients to find \( a, b, c \). Note that the constant term cancels out.
5

Solve \( h(x) = 0 \) (Part b)

From part (a), \( h(x) = (3x + 4)(x^3 + 5x^2 - 12x - 1) + (3x + 4) \).

So \( h(x) = (3x + 4)(x^3 + 5x^2 - 12x) = x(3x + 4)(x^2 + 5x - 12) \).

Setting \( h(x) = 0 \):

\[ x(3x + 4)(x^2 + 5x - 12) = 0 \]

So \( x = 0 \), \( 3x + 4 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -\frac{4}{3} \), or \( x^2 + 5x - 12 = 0 \).

Solving \( x^2 + 5x - 12 = 0 \) using the quadratic formula:

\[ x = \frac{-5 \pm \sqrt{25 + 48}}{2} = \frac{-5 \pm \sqrt{73}}{2} \]
💡 Hint: Factorise \( h(x) \) completely. Use the fact that \( h(x) = (3x+4)(x^3+5x^2-12x) \) from the division.
6

Identify rational roots

The roots are: \( x = 0 \) (rational), \( x = -\frac{4}{3} \) (rational), \( x = \frac{-5 + \sqrt{73}}{2} \) (irrational), \( x = \frac{-5 - \sqrt{73}}{2} \) (irrational).

Therefore, the equation \( h(x) = 0 \) has 2 rational roots.

💡 Hint: A rational root is a root that can be expressed as a fraction of integers. \( \sqrt{73} \) is not an integer, so those two roots are irrational.

Key Takeaways

  • Use the division algorithm: Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder.
  • When quotient and remainder are equal, set them as the same expression.
  • Compare coefficients to solve for unknowns.
  • To find rational roots, fully factorise the polynomial and check which roots are rational.
