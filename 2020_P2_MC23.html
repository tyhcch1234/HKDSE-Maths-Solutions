Shortest distance from a ship to a lighthouse — bearings & right-angled trigonometry

Bearings S 60° E Right-angled Triangle Perpendicular Distance sin ratio

Question

A ship is \(50\text{ km}\) due west of a lighthouse. If the ship moves in the direction \(\text{S }60^\circ\text{E}\), find the shortest distance between the ship and the lighthouse.

  • A. \(20\text{ km}\)
  • B. \(25\text{ km}\)
  • C. \(43\text{ km}\)
  • D. \(87\text{ km}\)

Part (a) — Set up the diagram & find \(\angle BAC\)

Place the lighthouse at \(A\) and the ship's initial position at \(B\), \(50\text{ km}\) due east of \(A\). The ship then travels in the direction \(\text{S }60^\circ\text{E}\).

STEP 11M

Let \(A\) be the lighthouse and \(B\) the ship's starting point. Then \(AB = 50\text{ km}\) and \(AB\) runs east–west.

Hint: "Due west of the lighthouse" means the ship is on the same horizontal line, to the west of \(A\) if we let \(A\) be at the origin and the ship at \(B\).
STEP 21M

The direction of travel is \(\text{S }60^\circ\text{E}\), i.e. it makes \(60^\circ\) with the south direction. In the diagram, the north line through \(B\) is vertical, so the south direction is the downward vertical.

\[ \angle ABC \text{ (between the south direction and } BA) = 90^\circ. \]

At \(A\), the line \(AB\) points due east, so the angle between \(AB\) and the travel line \(BC\) at \(A\) is:

\[ \angle BAC = 90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ. \]
Hint: The angle between due east and \(\text{S }60^\circ\text{E}\) is the complement of \(60^\circ\), i.e. \(90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ\).
\(\angle BAC = 30^\circ\)

Part (b) — Find the shortest distance \(BC\)

The shortest distance from a point to a line is the perpendicular distance. Let \(C\) be the foot of the perpendicular from \(A\) to the ship's path \(BC\).

STEP 11M

In \(\triangle ABC\), \(C\) is the foot of the perpendicular from \(A\) to the line of travel, so \(\angle ACB = 90^\circ\). The shortest distance is therefore \(BC\).

Hint: The perpendicular from a point to a line gives the shortest distance from the point to the line.
STEP 21A

Using the right-angled triangle \(\triangle ABC\):

\[ \sin\angle BAC = \frac{BC}{AB}. \] \[ BC = AB \cdot \sin\angle BAC = 50 \sin 30^\circ = 50 \times \frac{1}{2} = 25\text{ km}. \]
Hint: Here \(AB\) is the hypotenuse and \(BC\) is the side opposite to \(\angle BAC = 30^\circ\). So use \(\sin\).
Shortest distance \(= 25\text{ km}\) — answer B

📌 Summary of Answers

  • Let \(A\) = lighthouse, \(B\) = ship's starting point, \(C\) = foot of perpendicular from \(A\) to the ship's route.
  • \(AB = 50\text{ km}\) (due east–west).
  • \(\angle BAC = 90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ\).
  • \(\angle ACB = 90^\circ\) (perpendicular distance).
  • Shortest distance: \(BC = 50\sin 30^\circ = 25\text{ km}\).
  • Correct option: B (25 km).
