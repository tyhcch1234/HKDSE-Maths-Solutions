Question & Solution

2024 Paper 2 · Q19

According to the figure, which of the following must be true?

A.$p+q+r-s = 360^\circ$
B.$p+q+r-s = 540^\circ$
C.$p+q+r+s = 540^\circ$
D.$p+q+r-s = 540^\circ,\;$ i.e. the stated key

Lines with the same arrow-mark are parallel. Angles $p$, $q$, $r$, $s$ are the marked angles at the corresponding vertices.

💡 Hint: Add a line through the turning point parallel to the two parallel lines; then use alternate angles (alt. $\angle$s, // lines) and angles at a point ($\angle$s at a pt.).
Step 1 · Set up the construction

Draw a dashed line through the top-left vertex $p$, parallel to the two arrowed parallel lines. Mark the angle between the upper parallel direction and the first segment as $a$.

The angles $p$ and $a$ are angles at a point on the same straight line through the vertex, so

$$a + p = 360^\circ \quad (\angle\text{s at a pt.})$$ $$\Rightarrow\; a = 360^\circ - p$$
Step 2 · Use alternate angles

At the vertex marked $q$, the dashed line is parallel to the arrowed lines, so the angle $b$ (alternate to $a$) satisfies

$$b = a = 360^\circ - p \quad (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s, } \parallel \text{ lines})$$

The interior angle $c$ at that vertex is the remainder of the straight-line turn:

$$c = q - b = q - (360^\circ - p) = p + q - 360^\circ$$
Step 3 · Continue to the next vertex

Again using alternate angles at the vertex marked $r$:

$$d = c = p + q - 360^\circ \quad (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s, } \parallel \text{ lines})$$

At the last vertex, the angle $e$ together with $s$ forms co-interior angles with the lower parallel line:

$$e + s = 180^\circ \quad (\text{int. } \angle\text{s, } \parallel \text{ lines})$$ $$\Rightarrow\; e = 180^\circ - s$$
Step 4 · Angles at the final point

Around the final vertex, $d$, $e$ and $r$ complete a full turn:

$$d + e + r = 360^\circ \quad (\angle\text{s at a pt.})$$

Substitute the expressions from Steps 2 and 3:

$$(p+q-360^\circ) + (180^\circ - s) + r = 360^\circ$$
Step 5 · Simplify $$p+q+r - 180^\circ - s = 360^\circ$$ $$p+q+r-s = 540^\circ$$

This is exactly the relation given when $s$ is the reflex exterior angle at the last vertex.

Answer: $p + q + r - s = 540^\circ$ — matching the worked solution shown in the figure.
