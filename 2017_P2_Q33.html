HKDSE 2017 Paper 2 · Q33 — Coordinate Geometry (linear relation between $x$ and $\sqrt{y}$)

Graph line $\sqrt{y}=-2x+8$ Intercepts $(0,8)$ & $(4,0)$ Step-3 formula line Parabola $y=4x^2-32x+64$

[2017MC#33]

The graph in the figure shows the linear relation between $x$ and $\sqrt{y}$. Which of the following must be true?

  1. $y=x^2-4x+8$
  2. $y=x^2+4x+8$
  3. $y=4x^2-32x+64$
  4. $y=4x^2+32x+64$
Step 1 · Read the two intercepts

The straight line in the figure is the graph of $\sqrt{y}$ plotted against $x$ (a straight line,
not a parabola).

From the axes it passes through

$$(0,\;8)\qquad\text{and}\qquad(4,\;0).$$

So the $\sqrt{y}$-intercept is $8$ and the $x$-intercept is $4$.

Step 2 · Find the slope of the line
Slope of a Straight Line \(m=\dfrac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}\) Using \((0,8)\) and \((4,0)\): \(m=\dfrac{0-8}{4-0}=-2\).

Slope $m=\dfrac{0-8}{4-0}=\dfrac{-8}{4}=-2$.

Hint: the line falls from left to right,
so the slope must be negative — a good check.

Step 3 · Equation of the line ($\sqrt{y}$ in terms of $x$)
Point-Slope Form \(y-y_{1}=m(x-x_{1})\) Through \((4,0)\) with slope \(-2\): \(\sqrt{y}-0=-2(x-4)\), i.e. \(\sqrt{y}=-2x+8\).

Using point-slope form with $(4,0)$:

$$\sqrt{y}-0=\frac{8-0}{0-4}\,(x-4)$$

$$\sqrt{y}=-2x+8$$

Step 4 · Square both sides to get $y$
Perfect Square Identity \((A-B)^{2}=A^{2}-2AB+B^{2}\) With \(A=8\) and \(B=2x\): \((8-2x)^{2}=4x^{2}-32x+64\).

$$y=\left(-2x+8\right)^2$$

$$y=(-2)^2x^2+2(-2x)(8)+8^2$$

$$y=4x^2-32x+64$$

Remember: $y\ge 0$ automatically,
since $y=(\sqrt{y})^2$ — consistent with a graph drawn only for $\sqrt{y}\ge 0$.

Answer: C  —  $y=4x^2-32x+64$

Question. The figure shows the graph of \(\sqrt{y}\) plotted against \(x\), which is a straight line passing through \((0,\,6)\) and \((3,\,0)\). Express \(y\) in terms of \(x\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Read the intercepts: the \(\sqrt{y}\)-intercept is \(6\) and the \(x\)-intercept is \(3\).
Step 2. Slope \(m=\dfrac{0-6}{3-0}=-2\).
Step 3. Through \((3,0)\): \(\sqrt{y}-0=-2(x-3)\), i.e. \(\sqrt{y}=-2x+6\).
Step 4. Square both sides: \(y=(6-2x)^{2}=36-24x+4x^{2}\), i.e. \(y=4x^{2}-24x+36\).
Step 5. Check \(x=0\): \(y=36\), so \(\sqrt{y}=6\) ✓;
and \(x=3\) gives \(y=36-72+36=0\) ✓.
Answer: \(y=4x^{2}-24x+36\)
