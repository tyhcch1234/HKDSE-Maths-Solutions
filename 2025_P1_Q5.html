Question [2025 #5]  (4 marks)

Factorize

(a)  $10pr - 6qr$,

(b)  $25p^{2} - 9q^{2}$,

(c)  $25p^{2} - 9q^{2} - 10pr + 6qr$.

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1Part (a): take out the common factor

Look at each term: $10pr = 2\cdot 5\cdot p\cdot r$ and $6qr = 2\cdot 3\cdot q\cdot r$.

The common factor is $2r$ (the HCF of $10$ and $6$ is $2$; both terms contain $r$).

$$10pr - 6qr = 2r\,(5p - 3q)$$

Hint: always check for an HCF first — it is the fastest route to marks in (a).
Common Factor Law \(ab+ac=a(b+c)\) Take out the highest common factor of the two terms first.
STEP 2Part (b): recognise the difference of squares

Write each term as a perfect square: $25p^{2} = (5p)^{2}$ and $9q^{2} = (3q)^{2}$.

Apply $x^{2} - y^{2} = (x+y)(x-y)$ with $x = 5p$, $y = 3q$:

$$25p^{2} - 9q^{2} = (5p + 3q)(5p - 3q)$$

Hint: a two-term expression with a minus sign is a strong signal for the difference of squares.
Difference of Squares \(a^2-b^2=(a+b)(a-b)\) Recognise the two squared terms and write the result as a sum times a difference.
STEP 3Part (c): group the four terms in pairs

Group the squares together, and the $r$-terms together — note the sign change when you bracket the last two terms:

$$25p^{2} - 9q^{2} - 10pr + 6qr = \bigl(25p^{2} - 9q^{2}\bigr) - \bigl(10pr - 6qr\bigr)$$

Hint: choose the grouping so that each bracket factors into something containing $(5p-3q)$.
Factorisation by Grouping \(ax+ay+bx+by=a(x+y)+b(x+y)=(a+b)(x+y)\) Group the terms in pairs so that a common bracket appears, then take that bracket out.
STEP 4Factorize each bracket (reuse (a) and (b))

First bracket — difference of squares from part (b): $\;25p^{2} - 9q^{2} = (5p+3q)(5p-3q)$.

Second bracket — common factor from part (a): $\;10pr - 6qr = 2r(5p-3q)$.

$$= (5p+3q)(5p-3q) - 2r(5p-3q)$$

Hint: this is exactly why part (a) and part (b) were asked first — you are meant to reuse them.
STEP 5Take out the common bracket $(5p-3q)$

Both terms share the factor $(5p-3q)$:

$$= (5p-3q)\bigl[(5p+3q) - 2r\bigr] = (5p-3q)(5p+3q-2r)$$

Hint: the shared bracket $(5p-3q)$ plays the role of a single "common factor" — factor it out like any HCF.
Answers:
(a) $10pr - 6qr = 2r(5p - 3q)$
(b) $25p^{2} - 9q^{2} = (5p + 3q)(5p - 3q)$
(c) $25p^{2} - 9q^{2} - 10pr + 6qr = (5p - 3q)(5p + 3q - 2r)$

Question. Factorise (a) \(12mn-8np\), (b) \(49m^2-16p^2\), (c) \(49m^2-16p^2-14mn+8np\).

Show the worked solution
1. (a) \(12mn-8np=4n(3m-2p)\).
2. (b) \(49m^2-16p^2=(7m)^2-(4p)^2=(7m+4p)(7m-4p)\).
3. (c) \(49m^2-16p^2-14mn+8np=(7m+4p)(7m-4p)-2n(7m-4p)\).
4. Take out the common bracket: \((7m-4p)(7m+4p-2n)\).
Answer: (a) \(4n(3m-2p)\) (b) \((7m+4p)(7m-4p)\) (c) \((7m-4p)(7m+4p-2n)\)
