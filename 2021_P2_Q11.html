2021MC#11

If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are non-zero numbers such that \[ \frac{2\alpha+3\beta}{3\alpha+2\beta}=\frac{7}{10}, \] then $\dfrac{2\alpha+\beta}{\alpha+2\beta}=$

  • A. $1$.
  • B. $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
  • C. $\dfrac{11}{6}$.
  • D. $\dfrac{13}{8}$.
1Write down the given equation

\[\frac{2\alpha+3\beta}{3\alpha+2\beta}=\frac{7}{10}\]

Collect the known relation between $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

2Cross-multiply
Cross-Multiplication \(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d}\ \Longrightarrow\ ad=bc\) \(\dfrac{2\alpha+3\beta}{3\alpha+2\beta}=\dfrac{7}{10}\) becomes \(10(2\alpha+3\beta)=7(3\alpha+2\beta)\).

\[10(2\alpha+3\beta)=7(3\alpha+2\beta)\]

\[20\alpha+30\beta=21\alpha+14\beta\]

This turns the ratio equation into a simple linear equation.

3Solve for $\alpha$ in terms of $\beta$
Collecting Like Terms \(20\alpha+30\beta=21\alpha+14\beta\ \Longrightarrow\ \alpha=16\beta\) Moving the \(\alpha\)-terms left and the \(\beta\)-terms right isolates the relation between them.

\[30\beta-14\beta=21\alpha-20\alpha\]

\[16\beta=\alpha \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \alpha=16\beta\]

Both are non-zero, so dividing by $\beta$ is allowed.

4Substitute into the required expression

\[\frac{2\alpha+\beta}{\alpha+2\beta} =\frac{2(16\beta)+\beta}{(16\beta)+2\beta}\]

Everything is now in terms of $\beta$ alone.

5Simplify and read the answer
Cancelling a Common Factor \(\dfrac{33\beta}{18\beta}=\dfrac{11}{6}\) Both numerator and denominator carry the factor \(\beta\), which cancels since \(\beta\neq0\).

\[=\frac{32\beta+\beta}{16\beta+2\beta} =\frac{33\beta}{18\beta} =\frac{11}{6}\]

The $\beta$ cancels;
reduce $\dfrac{33}{18}$ to lowest terms.

Answer: C  —  $\dfrac{11}{6}$

Question. If \(\dfrac{3\alpha+2\beta}{2\alpha+5\beta}=\dfrac{3}{4}\) and \(\alpha,\beta\) are non-zero, find \(\dfrac{2\alpha+\beta}{\alpha-\beta}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Cross-multiply: \(4(3\alpha+2\beta)=3(2\alpha+5\beta)\).
Step 2. Expand: \(12\alpha+8\beta=6\alpha+15\beta\).
Step 3. Collect terms: \(6\alpha=7\beta\), so \(\alpha=\dfrac{7\beta}{6}\).
Step 4. Substitute: \(\dfrac{2\alpha+\beta}{\alpha-\beta}=\dfrac{\frac{14\beta}{6}+\beta}{\frac{7\beta}{6}-\beta}=\dfrac{\frac{20\beta}{6}}{\frac{\beta}{6}}=20\).
Answer: \(20\)
