HKDSE 2022 · Paper 1 · Q1
[3 marks]

1. Simplify $\dfrac{(a^{3}b^{-2})^{4}}{a^{-5}b^{6}}$ and express your answer with positive indices.

1 Apply the power of a product
Power of a Product \((ab)^{n}=a^{n}b^{n},\qquad (a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn}\) Raising \(a^{3}b^{-2}\) to the power \(4\) multiplies both indices by \(4\), giving \(a^{12}b^{-8}\).

Raise each factor inside the bracket to the power $4$: $(a^{3}b^{-2})^{4}=a^{3\times4}\,b^{-2\times4}=a^{12}b^{-8}$.

$(xy)^n=x^n y^n$ and $(x^m)^n=x^{mn}$ — multiply the exponents.

2 Write as a single fraction

The expression becomes $\dfrac{a^{12}b^{-8}}{a^{-5}b^{6}}$. Group the $a$'s and the $b$'s separately.

Keep the fraction form so the division law can be applied factor by factor.

3 Divide powers with the same base
Quotient Law of Indices \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n}\) \(\dfrac{a^{12}}{a^{-5}}=a^{12+5}=a^{17}\) and \(\dfrac{b^{-8}}{b^{6}}=b^{-14}\).

$\dfrac{a^{12}}{a^{-5}}=a^{12-(-5)}=a^{12+5}=a^{17}$ and $\dfrac{b^{-8}}{b^{6}}=b^{-8-6}=b^{-8-6}$… wait carefully: $\dfrac{b^{-8}}{b^{6}}=b^{-8-6}=b^{-14}$.

$\dfrac{x^m}{x^n}=x^{m-n}$ — subtract the exponents.

4 Make all indices positive
Negative Index Law \(x^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{x^{n}}\) Writing \(b^{-14}\) as \(\dfrac{1}{b^{14}}\) leaves \(\dfrac{a^{17}}{b^{14}}\) with positive indices only.

$b^{-14}=\dfrac{1}{b^{14}}$, so combining the two parts gives $\dfrac{a^{17}}{b^{14}}$.

$x^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{x^{n}}$ — a negative index means a reciprocal.

Answer: $\dfrac{a^{17}}{b^{14}}$

Question. Simplify \(\dfrac{(x^{4}y^{-3})^{2}}{x^{-3}y^{5}}\) and express your answer with positive indices.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \((x^{4}y^{-3})^{2}=x^{8}y^{-6}\), so the expression is \(\dfrac{x^{8}y^{-6}}{x^{-3}y^{5}}\).
Step 2. \(\dfrac{x^{8}}{x^{-3}}=x^{8-(-3)}=x^{11}\).
Step 3. \(\dfrac{y^{-6}}{y^{5}}=y^{-6-5}=y^{-11}\).
Step 4. Write \(y^{-11}=\dfrac{1}{y^{11}}\), so the answer is \(\dfrac{x^{11}}{y^{11}}\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{x^{11}}{y^{11}}\)
