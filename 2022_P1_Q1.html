Simplify and express with positive indices:

$$\frac{(a^{3}b^{-2})^{4}}{a^{-5}b^{6}}$$

When the exponent of \(a\) is 17, the simplified form is \(a^{17} / b^{14}\).

Step-by-step Solution

tap to reveal

Step 1 · Expand the power of a product

Apply \((xy)^n = x^n y^n\) to the numerator:

\((a^{3}b^{-2})^{4} = (a^{3})^{4} \cdot (b^{-2})^{4}\)

Then use \((x^m)^n = x^{mn}\):

\(= a^{12} \cdot b^{-8}\)
💡 Hint: Multiply the exponents: \(3 \times 4 = 12\) and \((-2) \times 4 = -8\).

Step 2 · Combine using quotient rule

Now the expression becomes:

\(\frac{a^{12} b^{-8}}{a^{-5} b^{6}}\)

Use \(\frac{x^m}{x^n} = x^{m-n}\) for both \(a\) and \(b\):

\(= a^{12 - (-5)} \cdot b^{-8 - 6}\)
💡 Hint: Subtracting a negative is the same as adding: \(12-(-5)=12+5=17\). And \(-8-6 = -14\).

Step 3 · Simplify & make indices positive

We have:

\(a^{17} \cdot b^{-14}\)

Rewrite \(b^{-14}\) as \(\frac{1}{b^{14}}\) to express with positive indices:

\(\displaystyle \frac{a^{17}}{b^{14}}\)
💡 Hint: A negative index means reciprocal: \(x^{-n} = 1/x^n\). Final answer must have only positive indices.

Final answer: \(\displaystyle \frac{a^{17}}{b^{14}}\)

Key takeaway

Always apply the power rule to remove brackets, then combine like bases using the quotient rule. Finally, rewrite any negative exponents as positive by moving them to the denominator (or numerator).
