Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Similar Solids

T10 Q41

Two similar solid cones have heights $3\text{ cm}$ and $6\text{ cm}$. Find the ratio of their corresponding linear dimensions in simplest form.

Solution

Linear ratio $= 3:6 = 1:2$.
Volume ratio $= 1^3:2^3 = 1:8$.
