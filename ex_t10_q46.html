Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Similar Solids

T10 Q46

Two similar solid cones have base radii $2\text{ cm}$ and $4\text{ cm}$. Find the ratio of their corresponding linear dimensions in simplest form.

Solution

Linear ratio $= 2:4 = 1:2$.
Surface area ratio $= 1:4$.
