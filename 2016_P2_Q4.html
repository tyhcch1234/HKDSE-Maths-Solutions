HKDSE 2016 · Paper 2 · Q4  |  Estimation, Approximation and Error

$0.0765403 = $

  • A$0.076$ (correct to 2 significant figures)
  • B$0.0765$ (correct to 3 decimal places)
  • C$0.07654$ (correct to 4 significant figures)
  • D$0.076540$ (correct to 5 decimal places)

Solution

1Line up the digits and mark the first significant figure Write the number with each digit in its own place-value column, and place the decimal point clearly: $$0.0765403 = 0\;\underset{\downarrow}{.}\;0\;7\;6\;5\;4\;0\;3$$ The leading zeros after the decimal point are not significant — they merely fix the position of the decimal point. Hint: the first non-zero digit is $7$, so the 1st significant figure is the $7$ (the 2nd decimal place).
Significant Figures \(\text{the 1st significant figure is the 1st non-zero digit}\) In \\(0.0765403\\) the leading zeros only fix the decimal point, so the 1st significant figure is the \\(7\\).
2Count the required significant figures Starting at that $7$, count out the required number of significant figures:

digit765403
So to 4 significant figures we keep $0.0765\underline{4}$, and the digit immediately after is $0$. Hint: the cutoff digit here is $0 < 5$, so we round down (do not increase the 4th significant figure $4$).
Rounding Rule (half up) \(\text{next digit}\ \ge 5\ \Rightarrow\ \text{round up},\quad <5\ \Rightarrow\ \text{round down}\) Keeping four significant figures gives \\(0.07654\\) and the next digit is \\(0\\), so no carry is needed.
3Check option A — "2 significant figures" To 2 s.f. we keep $0.076$, and the next digit is $5$. Since $5 \ge 5$ we round up: $$0.0765403 \approx 0.077 \quad(\text{not } 0.076)$$ Option A is incorrect. Common trap: forgetting the half-up rule drops the carried digit.
4Check option B — "3 decimal places" To 3 d.p. we keep three digits after the decimal point: $0.076|5403$. The next digit is $5$, so round up: $$0.0765403 \approx 0.077 \quad(\text{not } 0.0765)$$ Option B is incorrect. Trap: mixing up "decimal places" (counted from the point) with "significant figures" (counted from the first non-zero digit).
Decimal Places versus Significant Figures \(n\ \text{d.p. counts digits after the decimal point}\) To \\(3\\) d.p. we keep \\(0.076\\) and look at the next digit \\(5\\), which rounds the value up to \\(0.077\\).
5Check option C — "4 significant figures" Following Steps 1–2, to 4 s.f. we keep $0.0765$4 and look at the next digit $0$: $$0 \not\ge 5 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{round down} \;\Rightarrow\; 0.0765403 \approx 0.07654$$ Option C is correct. This is the only option whose stated accuracy matches the digits shown.
6Check option D — "5 decimal places" To 5 d.p. we keep five digits after the point: $0.07654|03$. The next digit is $0$, so round down: $$0.0765403 \approx 0.07654 \quad(\text{not } 0.076540)$$ The trailing zero in option D is not justified — writing it would claim accuracy to 6 d.p., which is wrong. Option D is incorrect. Trap: keeping a meaningless trailing zero.
Answer: C  —  $0.0765403 = 0.07654$ (correct to 4 significant figures)
