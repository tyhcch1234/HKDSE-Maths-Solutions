Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Identities

T13 Q36

Simplify $\frac{\sin\theta}{\tan\theta}$.

Solution

Use the identity $\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$.
Then $\frac{\sin\theta}{\tan\theta}=\frac{\sin\theta}{\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}}$.
Multiply numerator and denominator by $\cos\theta$: $\frac{\sin\theta}{\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}}=\frac{\sin\theta\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$.
Cancel $\sin\theta$ where $\sin\theta\neq0$: $\frac{\sin\theta\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}=\cos\theta$.
Final simplified answer: $\cos\theta$ (for values of $\theta$ where the original expression is defined, i.e. $\sin\theta\neq0$ and $\cos\theta\neq0$).
