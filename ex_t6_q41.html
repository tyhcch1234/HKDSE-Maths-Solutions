T6 Q41

Substitute \(x=10-y\) into \(2x+3y=24\) to form an equation in \(y\).

Solution

Start with \(2x+3y=24\).
Substitute \(x=10-y\): \(2(10-y)+3y=24\).
Expand: \(20-2y+3y=24\).
Simplify: \(20+y=24\).
Subtract \(20\) from both sides: \(y=4\).
Check: if \(y=4\), then \(x=10-4=6\), and \(2(6)+3(4)=12+12=24\), which is correct.
The equation in \(y\) is \(2(10-y)+3y=24\), which simplifies to \(y+20=24\), giving \(y=4\) (and \(x=6\)).
