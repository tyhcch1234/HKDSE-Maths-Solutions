HKDSE 2020 · Paper 1 · Question 1

Topic 8 — Laws of Indices, Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

[3 marks] Simplify $\dfrac{(mn^{-2})^{5}}{m^{-4}}$ and express your answer with positive indices.
STEP 1Remove the bracket — power of a product.
Power of a Product Law \((a^{p}b^{q})^{k}=a^{pk}b^{qk}\) The outer index 5 multiplies both indices inside \((mn^{-2})^{5}\).
$$(mn^{-2})^{5}=m^{1\times 5}\,n^{-2\times 5}=m^{5}n^{-10}$$
Multiply every index inside the bracket by the outside index $5$.
STEP 2Apply the quotient law $\dfrac{a^{p}}{a^{q}}=a^{p-q}$.
Quotient Law of Indices \(\dfrac{a^{p}}{a^{q}}=a^{p-q}\) For the base \(m\) this gives \(5-(-4)=9\), while the \(n\)-index is unchanged.
$$\frac{m^{5}n^{-10}}{m^{-4}}=m^{5-(-4)}\,n^{-10}=m^{9}n^{-10}$$
Careful with the double negative: $5-(-4)=5+4=9$.
STEP 3Rewrite with positive indices.
Negative Index Law \(a^{-k}=\dfrac{1}{a^{k}}\) The negative index \(n^{-10}\) is written as \(\dfrac{1}{n^{10}}\).
$$m^{9}n^{-10}=\frac{m^{9}}{n^{10}}$$
$a^{-k}=\dfrac{1}{a^{k}}$ moves a negative index to the other side of the fraction bar.
Answer: $\dfrac{m^{9}}{n^{10}}$

Question. Simplify \(\dfrac{\left(p^{-3}q^{2}\right)^{4}}{p^{-5}}\) and express your answer with positive indices.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(\left(p^{-3}q^{2}\right)^{4}=p^{-12}q^{8}\).
Step 2. \(\dfrac{p^{-12}q^{8}}{p^{-5}}=p^{-12-(-5)}q^{8}=p^{-7}q^{8}\).
Step 3. \(p^{-7}q^{8}=\dfrac{q^{8}}{p^{7}}\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{q^{8}}{p^{7}}\)
