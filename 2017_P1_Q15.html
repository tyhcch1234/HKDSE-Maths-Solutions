HKDSE Math 2017 Paper 1 Q15 Logarithmic Functions
Laws of Indices Logarithms Inverse Function

Question (4 marks):

Let \(a\) and \(b\) be constants. Denote the graph of \(y = a + \log_b x\) by \(G\). The \(x\)-intercept of \(G\) is \(9\) and \(G\) passes through the point \((243, 3)\). Express \(x\) in terms of \(y\).

💡 Key idea: Use given points to solve for \(a\) and \(b\), then rewrite the equation to make \(x\) the subject.

Step-by-step Solution

1 Use the \(x\)-intercept \((9, 0)\) ▼

The graph passes through \((9, 0)\). Substitute \(x=9\), \(y=0\) into \(y = a + \log_b x\):

\[0 = a + \log_b 9\]
💡 Hint: The \(x\)-intercept means the point lies on the \(x\)-axis, so its \(y\)-coordinate is \(0\).
2 Use the point \((243, 3)\) ▼

Substitute \(x = 243\), \(y = 3\) into \(y = a + \log_b x\):

\[3 = a + \log_b 243\]
💡 Hint: Now we have two equations with two unknowns \(a\) and \(b\).
3 Eliminate \(a\) ▼

From Step 1: \(a = -\log_b 9\).
From Step 2: \(a = 3 - \log_b 243\).
Equating the two expressions for \(a\):

\[-\log_b 9 = 3 - \log_b 243\]

Rearrange:

\[\log_b 243 - \log_b 9 = 3\]
💡 Hint: Use the log law \(\log_b M - \log_b N = \log_b \left(\frac{M}{N}\right)\).
4 Solve for \(b\) ▼

Simplify the logarithm:

\[\log_b \left(\frac{243}{9}\right) = 3 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \log_b 27 = 3\]

Convert to exponential form:

\[b^3 = 27\]
\[b = 3\]
💡 Hint: Since \(b\) is the base of a logarithm, \(b > 0\) and \(b \neq 1\). So \(b=3\) is valid.
5 Solve for \(a\) ▼

Substitute \(b=3\) into \(a = -\log_b 9\) (from Step 1):

\[a = -\log_3 9 = -\log_3 3^2 = -2\]

Thus \(a = -2\), \(b = 3\).

💡 Hint: \(\log_3 9 = \log_3 3^2 = 2\log_3 3 = 2\).
6 Express \(x\) in terms of \(y\) ▼

The equation becomes:

\[y = -2 + \log_3 x\]

Make \(\log_3 x\) the subject:

\[y + 2 = \log_3 x\]

Convert to exponential form:

\[x = 3^{y+2}\]
✅ Final answer: \(x = 3^{y+2}\)
