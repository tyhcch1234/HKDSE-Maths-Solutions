HKDSE 2017 · Paper 1 · Q15

Laws of Indices, Exponential & Logarithmic Functions

[4 marks] Let $a$ and $b$ be constants. Denote the graph of $$y=a+\log_b x$$ by $G$. The $x$-intercept of $G$ is $9$ and $G$ passes through the point $(243,\,3)$. Express $x$ in terms of $y$.
1

Translate the given points into equations

$x$-intercept at $9$: $\;y=0$ when $x=9$
$\Rightarrow\; 0=a+\log_b 9$
Passes through $(243,3)$: $\;3=a+\log_b 243$
Both facts are read straight off the graph — the red point sits on the axis at $x=9$, and the other red point is $(243,3)$.
2

Subtract to eliminate $a$

$(a+\log_b 243)-(a+\log_b 9)=3-0$
$\log_b 243-\log_b 9=3$
$\log_b \dfrac{243}{9}=3 \;\Rightarrow\; \log_b 27=3$
Use the quotient law of logarithms $\log_b M-\log_b N=\log_b\frac{M}{N}$.
3

Rewrite in index form to find $b$

$\log_b 27=3 \;\Rightarrow\; b^{3}=27$
$b=\sqrt[3]{27}=3$
Definition of a logarithm: $\log_b M=k \iff b^{k}=M$ (with $b>0$, $b\neq 1$).
4

Substitute back to find $a$

From $0=a+\log_b 9$:
$a=-\log_3 9=-\log_3 3^{2}=-2$
So $\;G:\; y=-2+\log_3 x$
Now the curve is fully known: the green curve on the left is exactly this one.
5

Make $x$ the subject

$y+2=\log_3 x$
$x=3^{\,y+2}$
Convert the logarithmic form back to index form: $\log_3 x = y+2 \iff x=3^{y+2}$.
Answer: $\;x=3^{\,y+2}\;\;$ (with $\;a=-2,\;b=3$)
