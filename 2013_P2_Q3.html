HKDSE 2013 Paper 2 — Question 3

Topic 4 · Polynomials (Factorization by grouping)
QUESTION
$h\ell - k\ell + hm - km - hn + kn =$
  • A. $(h+k)(\ell - m + n)$
  • B. $(h+k)(\ell + m - n)$
  • C. $(h-k)(\ell - m + n)$
  • D. $(h-k)(\ell + m - n)$
Factorisation by Grouping\(ax+ay+bx+by=a(x+y)+b(x+y)=(a+b)(x+y)\)Group the six terms in pairs so that each pair contains the same letters.
1

Read the six terms & group in pairs

$$h\ell - k\ell + hm - km - hn + kn$$ $$= (h\ell - k\ell) + (hm - km) - (hn - kn)$$ Group the terms so that each bracket contains the same letters.
Hint: pairing $\ell$-terms, $m$-terms and $n$-terms together reveals a repeated bracket.
Common Factor Law\(AB+AC=A(B+C)\)Every pair has \(h-k\) as its common factor, for example \(h\ell-k\ell=(h-k)\ell\).
2

Factor each pair

$$(h\ell - k\ell) = (h-k)\ell$$ $$(hm - km) = (h-k)m$$ $$(hn - kn) = (h-k)n$$ So the expression becomes $$(h-k)\ell + (h-k)m - (h-k)n.$$
In each pair, the common factor is $(h-k)$. Watch the minus sign on the last group: $-hn+kn = -(h-k)n$.
3

Take out the common factor $(h-k)$

$$(h-k)\ell + (h-k)m - (h-k)n = (h-k)(\ell + m - n).$$
Factor $h-k$ out of all three terms — the remaining parts are $\ell$, $+m$ and $-n$.
Distributive Law\(A(B+C-D)=AB+AC-AD\)Expanding \((h-k)(\ell+m-n)\) must reproduce all six terms of the original expression.
4

Check by expanding

$$(h-k)(\ell + m - n) = h\ell + hm - hn - k\ell - km + kn$$ which matches the original expression. Hence the answer matches option D.
Always verify: expanding your factorised form must reproduce all six original terms.
Answer: D  $(h-k)(\ell + m - n)$ Common factor $(h-k)$ taken out of $(h-k)\ell + (h-k)m - (h-k)n$.

Question. Factorize \(xy-2y+3x-6-xz+2z\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Group in pairs that share a factor: \((xy-2y)+(3x-6)-(xz-2z)\).
Step 2. Factor each pair: \(y(x-2)+3(x-2)-z(x-2)\). Note \(-xz+2z=-(x-2)z\).
Step 3. Take out the common bracket \((x-2)\): \((x-2)(y+3-z)\).
Step 4. Check by expanding: \(xy-2y+3x-6-xz+2z\), which is the original expression.
Answer: \((x-2)(y+3-z)\)
