Mensuration — Volumes of a Right Circular Cylinder & Cone

Volume of Cylinder Volume of Cone Ratio & Scaling Multiple Choice

Question MC

If the volume of a right circular cylinder of base radius \(5a\text{ cm}\) and height \(7b\text{ cm}\) is \(525\text{ cm}^3\), then the volume of a right circular cone of base radius \(7a\text{ cm}\) and height \(5b\text{ cm}\) is

A\(175\text{ cm}^3\)
B\(245\text{ cm}^3\)
C\(490\text{ cm}^3\)
D\(735\text{ cm}^3\)

The correct choice is highlighted. Note: \(245\text{ cm}^3\) is the answer in the official marking scheme; see the discussion in Part (c).

Part (a) Set up the cylinder volume

Write an equation from the given volume of the cylinder in terms of \(a\) and \(b\).

Step 11M

Recall the volume formula for a right circular cylinder, with base radius \(5a\) and height \(7b\):

\[ V_{\text{cyl}} = \pi r^2 h = \pi(5a)^2(7b) \]
Hint: Square the radius first — \((5a)^2 = 25a^2\).
Step 21A

Simplify and set equal to the given volume \(525\text{ cm}^3\):

\[ \pi(25a^2)(7b) = 175\pi a^2 b = 525 \]
Hint: \(25 \times 7 = 175\).
Step 31A

Divide both sides by \(175\pi\) to isolate the key quantity \(\pi a^2 b\):

\[ \pi a^2 b = \frac{525}{175} = 3 \]
Result: \(\;\pi a^2 b = 3\)
0 / 3

Part (b) Volume of the cone

Now compute the volume of the right circular cone with base radius \(7a\) and height \(5b\).

Step 11M

Recall the volume formula for a right circular cone, with base radius \(7a\) and height \(5b\):

\[ V_{\text{cone}} = \tfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \tfrac{1}{3}\pi(7a)^2(5b) \]
Hint: A cone's volume is one-third of the cylinder with the same base and height.
Step 21A

Expand the squares and multiply the numbers:

\[ (7a)^2 = 49a^2,\qquad \tfrac{1}{3}\pi \cdot 49a^2 \cdot 5b = \tfrac{245}{3}\,\pi a^2 b \]
Hint: \(49 \times 5 = 245\); keep the factor \(\tfrac{1}{3}\) outside.
Step 31A

Substitute the value \(\pi a^2 b = 3\) found in Part (a):

\[ V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{245}{3}\cdot 3 = 245 \]
Answer: \(245\text{ cm}^3\) → choice B
0 / 3

Part (c) Compare & verify

Check the answer by comparing the cone with the cylinder directly — a useful sanity check in an MC question.

Step 11M

Write the cone volume as a multiple of the cylinder volume \(V_{\text{cyl}} = \pi(5a)^2(7b) = 175\pi a^2b\):

\[ \frac{V_{\text{cone}}}{V_{\text{cyl}}} = \frac{\tfrac13 \pi(7a)^2(5b)}{\pi(5a)^2(7b)} = \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{49\cdot 5}{25\cdot 7} \]
Hint: All the \(\pi a^2b\) cancels — the ratio depends only on the numbers.
Step 21A

Simplify the ratio:

\[ \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{245}{175} = \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{7}{5} = \frac{7}{15} \]
Hint: Both \(245\) and \(175\) are divisible by \(35\).
Step 31A

Apply the ratio to the given cylinder volume:

\[ V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{7}{15}\times 525 = 7 \times 35 = 245 \]
Confirmed: \(245\text{ cm}^3\), consistent with Part (b).
0 / 3

📌 Summary of Answers

  • Key quantity from the cylinder: \(\;\pi a^2 b = \dfrac{525}{175} = 3\)
  • Volume of the cone: \(\;V = \dfrac{1}{3}\pi(7a)^2(5b) = \dfrac{245}{3}\,\pi a^2 b\)
  • Substituting \(\pi a^2 b = 3\): \(\;V = \dfrac{245}{3}\times 3 = \class{ansval}{245\text{ cm}^3}\)
  • Correct option: \(\;\class{ansval}{\text{B}}\)
