Coordinate Geometry

The coordinates of the point $P$ are $(7, -5)$. $P$ is reflected with respect to the $y$-axis to the point $Q$. $Q$ is then rotated clockwise about the origin through $90^\circ$ to the point $R$. Find the $x$-coordinate of $R$.

  • A. $-7$
  • B. $-5$
  • C. $5$
  • D. $7$
💡 Reflection over the $y$-axis flips the sign of $x$. A $90^\circ$ clockwise rotation about $O$ swaps the coordinates and changes one sign.
STEP 1 · Reflect P over the y-axis
Reflection in the y-axis \((x,y)\ \longmapsto\ (-x,y)\) The reflection of \(P(6,-2)\) over the \(y\)-axis is \(Q(-6,-2)\).
Reflection over the $y$-axis maps $(x, y) \mapsto (-x, y)$. Therefore $$Q = (-7,\ -5).$$
STEP 2 · Rotate Q clockwise 90° about O
Rotation about the Origin \(90^{\circ}\ \text{clockwise}:\ (x,y)\ \longmapsto\ (y,-x)\) Applying this to \(Q(-6,-2)\) gives \(R(-2,6)\).
A $90^\circ$ clockwise rotation about the origin maps $(x, y) \mapsto (y, -x)$. Applying this to $Q(-7, -5)$: $$R = (y_Q,\ -x_Q) = (-5,\ 7).$$
STEP 3 · Read off the x-coordinate From $R = (-5,\ 7)$, the $x$-coordinate of $R$ is $$-5.$$ So the answer is B.
✅ Answer: B  ( $x$-coordinate of $R = -5$ )
