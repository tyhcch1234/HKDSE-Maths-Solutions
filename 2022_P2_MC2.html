HKDSE Math 2022 Paper 2 Q2 Topic 8: Laws of Indices

Simplify: \(\displaystyle \frac{81^{\,2n+3}}{(27^{\,n+1})^2}\)

Multiple Choice Options

A. \(3\)
B. \(3^{2n+6}\)
C. \(3^{4n+8}\)
D. \(3^{10n+14}\)

Step-by-Step Solution

Notice that both 81 and 27 are powers of 3:

\[81 = 3^4 \quad \text{and} \quad 27 = 3^3\]

💡 Hint: \(3^4 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 = 81\), \(3^3 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27\).

Replace 81 with \(3^4\) and 27 with \(3^3\):

\[\frac{(3^4)^{\,2n+3}}{((3^3)^{\,n+1})^2}\]

Use \((a^m)^n = a^{m \times n}\):

\[\frac{3^{\,4(2n+3)}}{3^{\,3(n+1) \times 2}}\]

Simplify the exponents:

\[\frac{3^{\,8n+12}}{3^{\,6n+6}}\]

Use \(\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}\):

\[3^{(8n+12) - (6n+6)} = 3^{\,2n+6}\]

✅ Answer: \(3^{2n+6}\) — which is option B.
