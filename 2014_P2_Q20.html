Question. In the figure, $AC$ is a diameter of the circle $ABCDE$. If $\angle ADE = 28^\circ$, then $\angle CBE =$
A. $72^\circ$
B. $62^\circ$
C. $76^\circ$
B. $62^\circ$
Step 1 · Read the figure

Points $A, B, C, D, E$ all lie on one circle,
and $AC$ is a diameter (it passes through the centre $O$).

The two angles we care about are $\angle ADE=28^\circ$ and $\angle CBE$.

Hint: $\angle ADE$ and $\angle ABE$ subtend the same arc $AE$, so they are "angles in the same segment".
Step 2 · Angles in the same segment
Angles in the Same Segment \(\angle AXB=\angle AYB\) \(\angle ADE\) and \(\angle ABE\) both stand on the chord \(AE\), with \(D\) and \(B\) on the same arc.

Both $\angle ADE$ and $\angle ABE$ stand on the chord $AE$ (i.e. they subtend arc $AE$),
with their vertices $D$ and $B$ on the same circle.

$$\angle ABE=\angle ADE=28^\circ\quad(\angle\text{s in the same segment})$$

Look at the diagram: the red arc $AE$ is highlighted — both amber/red angle marks open onto that same arc.
Step 3 · Angle in a semi-circle
Angle in a Semi-circle \(\angle ABC=90^\circ \text{ if } AC \text{ is a diameter}\) \(AC\) is a diameter and \(B\) lies on the circle, so \(\angle ABC\) is a right angle.

Since $AC$ is a diameter and $B$ lies on the circle,
the angle subtended by the diameter at $B$ is a right angle:

$$\angle ABC=90^\circ\quad(\angle\text{ in semi-circle})$$

The green right-angle mark at $B$ is the key — $AB\perp BC$.
Step 4 · Subtract to get $\angle CBE$

At the point $B$,
the ray $BA$ and the ray $BC$ form the right angle $\angle ABC$, and ray $BE$ lies inside it.
So

$$\angle CBE=\angle ABC-\angle ABE=90^\circ-28^\circ=62^\circ$$

Check: $\angle ABE+\angle CBE=\angle ABC$ — the purple angle plus the blue angle fills the green right angle.
FINAL ANSWER $\angle CBE = 62^\circ$ → Option B

Question. In the figure, \(AC\) is a diameter of the circle \(ABCDE\). If \(\angle ADE=34^\circ\), then \(\angle CBE=\)
A. \(34^\circ\)   B. \(46^\circ\)   C. \(56^\circ\)   D. \(66^\circ\)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Angles in the same segment: \(\angle ABE\) and \(\angle ADE\) stand on the chord \(AE\),
so \(\angle ABE=\angle ADE=34^\circ\).
Step 2. Angle in a semi-circle: since \(AC\) is a diameter, \(\angle ABC=90^\circ\).
Step 3. Ray \(BE\) lies inside \(\angle ABC\), so \(\angle CBE=\angle ABC-\angle ABE=90^\circ-34^\circ=56^\circ\).
Answer: C   \(56^\circ\)
