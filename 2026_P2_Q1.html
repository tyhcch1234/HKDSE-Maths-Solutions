Algebraic Fractions

Question

\(\dfrac{1}{k+2}+\dfrac{3}{5k-6}=\)

  • A. \(-\dfrac{8k}{(k+2)(5k-6)}\)
  • B. \(-\dfrac{2k}{(k+2)(5k-6)}\)
  • C. \(\dfrac{2k}{(k+2)(5k-6)}\)
  • D. \(\dfrac{8k}{(k+2)(5k-6)}\)

Solution

Common denominator

The LCM of \(k+2\) and \(5k-6\) is \((k+2)(5k-6)\), so

\[ \frac{1}{k+2}+\frac{3}{5k-6} =\frac{(5k-6)+3(k+2)}{(k+2)(5k-6)} \]
Simplify the numerator

Expanding gives

\[ (5k-6)+3(k+2)=5k-6+3k+6=8k \]

hence the expression equals \(\dfrac{8k}{(k+2)(5k-6)}\).

Match with the options

This is exactly option D (positive \(8k\) over \((k+2)(5k-6)\)).

Final Answer: D
