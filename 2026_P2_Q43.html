Permutations & Combinations

Question

There are \(5\) full-time employees and \(3\) part-time employees in a company. From the company, \(7\) employees are selected to form a queue. If no part-time employees are next to each other, how many different queues can be formed?

  • A. \(5040\)
  • B. \(14400\)
  • C. \(18000\)
  • D. \(32400\)

Solution

Select 7 from 8 Choosing 7 of the 8 employees splits into two cases: drop one full-time (4F+3P) or drop one part-time (5F+2P).
Case 1 · Drop a full-time
Permutations of n Distinct Objects \(n!\quad\text{arrangements of } n \text{ distinct objects in a row}\) The four full-time staff can be ordered among themselves in \(4!=24\) ways, which is the factor \(4!\) in \(5\times 4!\times 60\).
Insertion Method for Non-Adjacent Objects \(k\ \text{objects in }(n+1)\ \text{gaps}:\ P_{k}^{\,n+1}\) Once the \(4\) full-time staff are seated in a row they create \(5\) gaps, and the \(3\) part-time staff must occupy \(3\) different gaps: \(P_{3}^{5}=60\).
Choose 4 of 5 full-time: \(C_4^5=5\). Arrange the 4 full-time: \(4!\). Place 3 part-time in the 5 gaps: \(P_3^5=60\). Subtotal \(5\times4!\times60=7200\).
Case 2 · Drop a part-time Choose 2 of 3 part-time: \(C_2^3=3\). Arrange the 5 full-time: \(5!\). Place 2 part-time in the 6 gaps: \(P_2^6=30\). Subtotal \(3\times5!\times30=10800\).
Total
Addition Principle for Mutually Exclusive Cases \(\text{total}=\text{case }1+\text{case }2\) Dropping one full-time or one part-time cannot happen at the same time, so the two subtotals \(7200\) and \(10800\) simply add to \(18000\).
\(7200+10800=18000\).
Answer: C — 18000

Question. A firm has \(6\) full-time and \(3\) part-time employees. \(7\) of them are selected and seated in a row for a photograph, so that no two part-time employees sit next to each other. Find the number of possible arrangements. (4 marks)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Choosing \(7\) of the \(9\) employees gives two cases: \(5\) full-time with \(2\) part-time, or \(6\) full-time with \(1\) part-time.
Step 2. Case 1 (\(5\)F, \(2\)P): choose the full-time staff in \(C_{5}^{6}=6\) ways, seat them in \(5!=120\) ways, giving \(6\) gaps; place the \(2\) part-time staff in different gaps: \(P_{2}^{6}=30\). Subtotal \(6\times 120\times 30=21600\).
Step 3. Case 2 (\(6\)F, \(1\)P): choose the part-time employee in \(C_{1}^{3}=3\) ways, seat the \(6\) full-time in \(6!=720\) ways, giving \(7\) gaps; the part-time employee takes any gap. Subtotal \(3\times 720\times 7=15120\).
Step 4. Total: \(21600+15120=36720\).
Answer: \(36720\)
