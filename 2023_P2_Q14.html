Question

Let \(a_n\) be the \(n\)th term of a sequence. If \(a_6 = 23\), \(a_8 = 60\) and \(a_{n+2} = a_n + a_{n+1}\) for any positive integer \(n\), then \(a_3 =\)

  • A. \(4\)
  • B. \(5\)
  • C. \(9\)
  • D. \(14\)
1Step 1 — Read the recurrence relation
Recurrence Relation \(a_{n+2}=a_{n}+a_{n+1}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ a_{n}=a_{n+2}-a_{n+1}\) Rearranging lets us step backwards from the known terms to the earlier ones.

The given relation is \[ a_{n+2} = a_n + a_{n+1} \qquad \text{for every positive integer } n. \] Rearranging it so that each term is expressed in terms of the two terms on its right: \[ a_n = a_{n+2} - a_{n+1}. \] This lets us step backwards from \(a_8\) and \(a_6\) to \(a_3\).

Hint: Think of the recurrence as “next term = previous two added”, so “previous = next minus the one after”.
2Step 2 — Find \(a_7\)

Put \(n = 6\): \[ a_7 = a_8 - a_6 = 60 - 23 = 37. \]

Hint: Use the rearranged form with the two known values \(a_8\) and \(a_6\).
3Step 3 — Find \(a_5\)
Working Backwards \(a_{n}=a_{n+2}-a_{n+1}\) Each step subtracts the term immediately to the right of the unknown.

Put \(n = 5\): \[ a_5 = a_7 - a_6 = 37 - 23 = 14. \]

Hint: Always subtract the term immediately to the right of the unknown from the term two places to the right.
4Step 4 — Find \(a_4\)

Put \(n = 4\): \[ a_4 = a_6 - a_5 = 23 - 14 = 9. \]

Hint: Keep moving one step to the left along the number line.
5Step 5 — Find \(a_3\)

Put \(n = 3\): \[ a_3 = a_5 - a_4 = 14 - 9 = 5. \]

Hint: This matches option B. Check: \(a_3 + a_4 = 5 + 9 = 14 = a_5\) ✓
Answer \[ a_3 = 5 \qquad \text{(Option B)} \]

Question. It is given that \(a_{n+2}=a_{n}+a_{n+1}\) for every positive integer \(n\), where \(a_{9}=88\) and \(a_{7}=34\). Find \(a_{4}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Rearrange: \(a_{n}=a_{n+2}-a_{n+1}\).
Step 2. \(a_{8}=a_{9}-a_{7}=88-34=54\).
Step 3. \(a_{6}=a_{8}-a_{7}=54-34=20\) and \(a_{5}=a_{7}-a_{6}=34-20=14\).
Step 4. \(a_{4}=a_{6}-a_{5}=20-14=6\). Check: \(6+14=20=a_{6}\) ✓
Answer: \(a_{4}=6\)

Quick mental check: the sequence begins \(a_3 = 5,\; a_4 = 9,\; a_5 = 14,\; a_6 = 23,\; a_7 = 37,\; a_8 = 60\).
