Q44 The Question

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the scores (in marks) of a group of students in a test. Ada gets the highest score in the test.

Stem (tens)Leaf (units)
45  6  7  8
55  5  6  8
63  5  5  6  9  9
70  0  1
80  2  5

Which of the following is/are true?

  1. The upper quartile of the distribution is 55 marks.
  2. The standard score of Ada in the test is lower than 2.
  3. The standard deviation of the distribution is greater than 12 marks.
    4. >
    A.I only
    B.II only
    C.I and III only
    D.II and III only

0 First, Read the Diagram

Each entry is stem \(\times 10\) + leaf. For example, stem \(5\) leaf \(8\) means \(58\) marks. Count the leaves: there are \(4+4+6+3+3=20\) students.

Students: n = 20
Lowest: 45
Highest (Ada): 85

I Statement I — Upper Quartile Verify Q3

Statement I claims: “The upper quartile of the distribution is 55 marks.”

\(n = 20\)
Q3 position = \(\tfrac{3}{4}(20+1) = \) 15.75 (or \(\tfrac{3n}{4}=15\))
Using \(\tfrac{3(n+1)}{4}\) nearest data: —
0 / 3
Step 1

List all \(20\) data in ascending order:

\[45,\ 46,\ 47,\ 48,\ 55,\ 55,\ 56,\ 58,\ 63,\ 65,\ 65,\ 66,\ 69,\ 69,\ 70,\ 70,\ 71,\ 80,\ 82,\ 85\]
Hint: The stem-and-leaf diagram already sorts the data — read left to right, top to bottom.
Step 2

For \(n=20\), the upper quartile \(Q_3\) is taken as the mean of the \(15\)th and \(16\)th ordered data (i.e. \(\tfrac{3(n+1)}{4} = 15.75\), interpolating between these two).

\[\text{15th} = 70, \qquad \text{16th} = 70\] \[Q_3 = \frac{70 + 70}{2} = 70 \text{ marks}\]
Hint: The 15th and 16th values both sit in the stem \(7\) leaf \(0\) row — they are equal, so \(Q_3 = 70\).
Step 3
\(Q_3 = 70 \ne 55\) ⇒ Statement I is FALSE.

II Statement II — Ada's Standard Score Verify z

Statement II claims: “The standard score of Ada in the test is lower than 2.” Ada gets the highest score, \(85\).

Mean \(\bar x\) = —
Std dev \(\sigma\) = —
Standard score \(z=\dfrac{x-\bar x}{\sigma}\) = —
0 / 4
Step 1

Find the mean \(\bar x\) of all 20 scores:

\[\bar x=\frac{45+46+47+48+55+55+56+58+63+65+65+66+69+69+70+70+71+80+82+85}{20}\]
Hint: Group the data by stem to add faster — e.g. \((45+46+47+48)=186\), \((55+55+56+58)=224\), etc.
Step 2

Sum \(=1265\), so

\[\bar x=\frac{1265}{20}=63.25 \text{ marks}\]
Step 3

Compute the standard deviation

\[\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{\sum (x-\bar x)^2}{20}}\] \[(45-63.25)^2+(46-63.25)^2+(47-63.25)^2+(48-63.25)^2\] \[+(55-63.25)^2+(55-63.25)^2+(56-63.25)^2+(58-63.25)^2\] \[+(63-63.25)^2+(65-63.25)^2+(65-63.25)^2+(66-63.25)^2\] \[+(69-63.25)^2+(69-63.25)^2+(70-63.25)^2+(70-63.25)^2\] \[+(71-63.25)^2+(80-63.25)^2+(82-63.25)^2+(85-63.25)^2\]
Hint: A scientific calculator with built-in mean / SD functions handles this in one step. The sum of squared deviations is \(2679.75\).
Step 4
\[\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{2679.75}{20}}=\sqrt{133.9875}=11.575\ldots \text{ marks}\]

Ada's standard score with \(x=85\):

\[z=\frac{85-63.25}{11.5753}=\frac{21.75}{11.5753}\approx 1.879\]
\(z \approx 1.879 < 2\) ⇒ Statement II is TRUE.

III Statement III — Standard Deviation Compare σ

Statement III claims: “The standard deviation of the distribution is greater than 12 marks.”

\(\sigma\) = 11.58 marks
Threshold = 12 marks
0 / 2
Step 1

From Part II we already computed:

\[\sigma=\sqrt{133.9875}=11.57529697\ldots \text{ marks}\]
Hint: Don't forget to take the square root — \(133.9875\) is the variance \(\sigma^2\), not the standard deviation.
Step 2
\[\sigma \approx 11.575 < 12\]
⇒ Statement III is FALSE.

Decide the Answer

Collect the verdicts on the three statements:

Only Statement II is true  ⇒  Answer: B. II only

Summary at a Glance

  • Data: 20 scores, sorted — lowest \(45\), highest (Ada) \(85\).
  • Statement I — Upper quartile: \(Q_3 = \frac{70+70}{2} = 70\) ⇒ FALSE (it is not 55).
  • Statement II — Mean \(\bar x = 63.25\), \(\sigma \approx 11.575\); Ada's standard score \(z=\dfrac{85-63.25}{11.5753}\approx 1.879 < 2\) ⇒ TRUE.
  • Statement III — \(\sigma \approx 11.575 < 12\) ⇒ FALSE.
  • Final answer: B. II only
