[2016MC#44]  Statistics MC

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the scores (in marks) of a group of students in a test. Ada gets the highest score in the test.

Stem (tens)Leaf (units)
45   6   7   8
55   5   6   8
63   5   5   6   9   9
70   0   1
80   2   5

Which of the following is/are true?

I. The upper quartile of the distribution is 55 marks.
II. The standard score of Ada in the test is lower than 2.
III. The standard deviation of the distribution is greater than 12 marks.

A. I only    B. II only    C. I and III only    D. II and III only

Step 1 — Read the data (key idea)

Stem = tens, Leaf = units.
Reading every leaf gives $n = 20$ ordered scores:

$45,46,47,48,\;55,55,56,58,\;63,65,65,66,69,69,\;70,70,71,\;80,82,85$.

Ada has the highest score ⇒
Ada's score $= 85$.

Hint: always count the data points first — here $4+4+6+3+3 = 20$.

Step 2 — Test statement I (upper quartile)

Upper Quartile \(Q_{3}=\ \text{the}\ \dfrac{3(n+1)}{4}\text{th value}\) With \(n=20\) the position is \(15.75\), so \(Q_{3}\) is taken between the 15th and 16th values \(70\) and \(70\).

With $n = 20$, the upper quartile is the mean of the 15th and 16th ordered data.

15th data $= 70$,
 
16th data $= 70$.

$$Q_3 = \frac{70+70}{2} = 70 \text{ marks} \;\ne\; 55$$

∴
Statement I is false.

Hint: the "upper quartile" is the 75th percentile,
not the median of the lower half.

Step 3 — Compute the mean

$$\bar{x} = \frac{45+46+47+48+55+55+56+58+63+65+65+66+69+69+70+70+71+80+82+85}{20}$$

Sum $= 1265$, so

$$\bar{x} = \frac{1265}{20} = 63.25 \text{ marks}$$

Hint: use the built-in statistics functions on your calculator to save time.

Step 4 — Compute the standard deviation

Standard Deviation (Population) \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum \left(x-\bar{x}\right)^{2}}{n}}\) Dividing by \(n=20\) (not \(n-1\)) gives \(\sigma\approx 11.575\) marks for these scores.

$$\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{(45-63.25)^2+(46-63.25)^2+\cdots+(82-63.25)^2+(85-63.25)^2}{20}}$$

$$=\sqrt{133.9875} \approx 11.575 \text{ marks}$$

Since $11.575 < 12$, Statement III is false.

Hint: note the divisor is $n = 20$ (population standard deviation).

Step 5 — Test statement II (standard score of Ada)

Standard Score \(z=\dfrac{x-\bar{x}}{\sigma}\) Ada\u2019s score \(85\) with \(\bar{x}=63.25\) and \(\sigma=11.5753\) gives \(z\approx1.879<2\).

$$z=\frac{85-63.25}{11.5753} \approx 1.879 < 2$$

∴
Statement II is true.

Only II is true ⇒
answer B.

Hint: standard score $z=\dfrac{x-\bar{x}}{\sigma}$ measures how many SDs above the mean.
Answer: B (II only)

Which of the following is/are true?
I. The upper quartile of the distribution is \(71.5\) marks.
II. The standard score of the highest score is greater than \(2\).
III. The standard deviation of the distribution is greater than \(13\) marks.
A. I only    B. II only    C. I and III only    D. II and III only  (1 mark)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Reading every leaf gives \(n=20\) ordered scores: \(42,43,45,46,\ 52,52,54,57,\ 61,63,63,65,68,69,\ 71,72,74,\ 80,83,88\). The highest score is \(88\).
Step 2. The upper quartile is taken from the 15th and 16th values: \(Q_{3}=\dfrac{71+72}{2}=71.5\) marks,
so I is true.
Step 3. The total is \(1248\), so \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{1248}{20}=62.4\) marks.
Step 4. \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum (x-62.4)^{2}}{20}}=\sqrt{172.74}\approx 13.14\) marks, so III is true.
Step 5. \(z=\dfrac{88-62.4}{13.14}\approx 1.95<2\), so II is false.
Only I and III — option C.
Answer: C. I and III only
