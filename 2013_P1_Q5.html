HKDSE 2013 P1 Q5Inequalities — Compound Solution from Two Conditions

Number-line model

Step 1 — Start with the two given conditions.

Question

  1. (a) Solve the inequality $$\frac{19-7x}{3} > 23-5x.$$
  2. (b) Find all integers satisfying both the inequalities $$\frac{19-7x}{3} > 23-5x \quad\text{and}\quad 18-2x \ge 0.$$

[4 marks]

1Clear the fraction (multiply by 3)

$$\frac{19-7x}{3} > 23-5x$$

Multiply both sides by the positive number $3$ — the direction of the inequality sign stays the same:

$$19-7x > 3(23-5x) = 69-15x$$

Hint: only reverse the sign when you multiply/divide by a negative number. Here $3>0$, so no reversal.
2Collect the $x$-terms on one side

$$19-7x > 69-15x$$

Add $15x$ to both sides, then subtract $19$:

$$-7x+15x > 69-19$$

$$8x > 50$$

Hint: moving terms across the inequality only changes their sign, never the inequality direction.
3Divide by $8$ — part (a) answer

$$8x > 50 \;\Rightarrow\; x > \frac{50}{8} = \frac{25}{4}$$

So part (a): $\;x > \dfrac{25}{4}$  (i.e. $x>6.25$).

Hint: $8>0$, so the direction is unchanged. Leave the answer as an exact fraction.
4Solve the second inequality

$$18-2x \ge 0$$

$$18 \ge 2x \quad\Rightarrow\quad x \le 9$$

Hint: add $2x$ to both sides to avoid dividing by a negative — then $x\le 9$ comes straight out.
5Combine both conditions

From (a): $x > \dfrac{25}{4}$  and  from (b): $x \le 9$.

$$\frac{25}{4} < x \le 9$$

Since $\dfrac{25}{4}=6.25$, the interval is $\;6.25 < x \le 9$.

Hint: the answer is the overlap of the two solution sets on the number line.
6Pick out the integers

The integers strictly greater than $6.25$ and at most $9$ are:

$$7,\;8,\;9$$

Hint: $6$ is excluded (since $6<6.25$), while $9$ is included because of the $\le$ sign.
