5. (a) Solve the inequality \( \displaystyle \frac{19-7x}{3} > 23 - 5x \).
(b) Find all integers satisfying both the inequalities \( 18 - 2x \ge 0 \) and \( \displaystyle \frac{19-7x}{3} > 23 - 5x \). [4 marks]

Visualising the solution range

6
6.25
9

The solution set is \( \displaystyle \frac{25}{4} < x \le 9 \). Integer solutions: \( 7, 8, 9 \).

Step-by-step solution

1 Multiply both sides by 3

Multiply both sides by \(3\) (positive, so inequality sign unchanged):

\( \displaystyle \frac{19-7x}{3} > 23 - 5x \)

\( \Rightarrow 19 - 7x > 3(23 - 5x) \)

\( \Rightarrow 19 - 7x > 69 - 15x \)

2 Collect like terms

Move \(x\) terms to the left and constants to the right:

\( -7x + 15x > 69 - 19 \)

\( 8x > 50 \)

Hint: Adding \(15x\) and subtracting \(19\) from both sides.
3 Solve for \(x\)

Divide both sides by \(8\) (positive):

\( \displaystyle x > \frac{50}{8} \)

\( \displaystyle x > \frac{25}{4} \)

Answer for (a): \( \displaystyle x > \frac{25}{4} \)
4 Solve the second inequality

Given \( 18 - 2x \ge 0 \):

\( -2x \ge -18 \)

\( x \le 9 \) (dividing by \(-2\) flips the sign)

5 Combine and find integers

From (a) and step 4:

\( \displaystyle \frac{25}{4} < x \le 9 \)

\( \displaystyle 6.25 < x \le 9 \)

The integers in this range are:

\( 7, 8, 9 \)

Final answer for (b): \( 7, 8, 9 \)
