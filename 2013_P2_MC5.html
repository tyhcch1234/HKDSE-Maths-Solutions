Question 5

Inequalities Linear Programming Number Line

The solution of $x > 9$ or $x > 10$ is

A. $x > 9$
B. $x > 10$
C. $x > 11$
D. $x > 19$

1 Visualise the solution on a number line

Drag the slider to place the point $x = k$ on the number line. See if it satisfies $x>9$ or $x>10$. The green highlight shows the combined solution region.

• Testing 8: $x>9$ → False; $x>10$ → False. Not a solution.

2 Step-by-step reasoning

1

Understand the logical connector

The word “or” means union. A number is a solution if it satisfies at least one of the inequalities.

Hint

Formula: $A \cup B$ — union. A point belongs if it is in $A$ or in $B$ (or both).

2

Draw each inequality separately

$x>9$ means everything to the right of 9 (open circle). $x>10$ means everything to the right of 10 (open circle).

$x>9$
9
→
shaded to the right
$x>10$
10
→
shaded to the right
Hint

Open circle at the boundary because the inequality is strict ($>$).

3

Combine using “or” (union)

Take everything that is shaded in either diagram. Since $x>10$ already includes all numbers greater than 10, adding $x>9$ only adds the numbers between 9 and 10.

$x>9$ or $x>10$  ⟺  $x>9$

Hint

Logic: $x>10$ is a subset of $x>9$. So the union is just the larger set: $x>9$.

Final answer

The combined solution is simply $x>9$. This corresponds to option A.
