HKDSE Math 2023 Paper 1 Q1
5. Formulas and Sequences · [3 marks]

📘Question

Make \(h\) the subject of the formula

\[ \frac{5}{h+k}=\frac{k}{h-3} \]
Change of Subject Algebraic Fractions Cross-multiplication Factorisation by Grouping Rearranging Formulas

🧭Step-by-Step Guided Solution

Step 0 of 4 revealed

An equation with one fraction on each side can be cleared by cross-multiplication: multiply the numerator of the left side by the denominator of the right side, and vice versa.

\[ \frac{5}{h+k}=\frac{k}{h-3} \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; 5(h-3)=k(h+k) \]
💡 Hint — Cross-multiplication
If \(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d}\) then \(ad=bc\) (valid when \(b\neq 0\) and \(d\neq 0\)). Here \(a=5,\;b=h+k,\;c=k,\;d=h-3\). So we multiply \(5\times(h-3)\) and \(k\times(h+k)\).

Remove the brackets using the distributive law.

\[ 5(h-3)=k(h+k) \] \[ \Rightarrow\quad 5h-15 = hk + k^{2} \]
💡 Hint — Distributive law
\(5(h-3)=5\cdot h-5\cdot 3 = 5h-15\), and \(k(h+k)=k\cdot h + k\cdot k = hk+k^{2}\).

Our goal is to have every term containing \(h\) on one side, and everything without \(h\) on the other side.

\[ 5h-15 = hk+k^{2} \] \[ \Rightarrow\quad 5h - \underline{hk} = k^{2} + 15 \]
💡 Hint — Moving terms
Subtract \(hk\) from both sides to move it left, and add \(15\) to both sides to move the constant to the right. Remember: what you do to one side, do to the other.

The left side has a common factor \(h\):

\[ 5h - hk = h(5-k) \]

So the equation becomes:

\[ h(5-k) = 15 + k^{2} \]
💡 Hint — Why factorise?
\(h\) appears in two separate terms. To isolate a single \(h\), it must first be taken out as a common factor. This is the key move in any "make \(x\) the subject" question where \(x\) appears more than once.

Divide both sides by \((5-k)\) to leave \(h\) alone:

\[ h=\frac{15+k^{2}}{5-k} \]

Marking scheme: 1M for cross-multiplication, 1M for collecting terms, 1A for the correct answer.

💡 Hint — Domain condition
Since we divided by \((5-k)\), we need \(5-k\neq 0\), i.e. \(\boxed{k\neq 5}\). Also, the original fractions require \(h+k\neq 0\) and \(h-3\neq 0\).
