HKDSE 2022 Paper 1 — Question 5  |  Topic: Percentages

A fan is sold at a discount of $30\%$ on its marked price. After selling the fan, the profit is $\$78$ and the percentage profit is $26\%$. Find the marked price of the fan.

[4 marks]

1
Let $x$ be the cost price of the fan
Profit Percentage \(\text{profit}=\text{profit }\%\times\text{cost price}\) A profit of \(\$78\) is \(26\%\) of the cost price \(x\), which gives the equation \(0.26x=78\).
The profit of $\$78$ equals $26\%$ of the cost price: $$26\% \times x = 78$$
Profit amount = (profit %) × (cost price).
2
Solve for the cost price $x$ $$(0.26)\,x = 78$$ $$x = \frac{78}{0.26}$$ $$x = 300$$ So the cost price of the fan is $\$300$.
Divide both sides by the decimal form of $26\%$.
3
Find the selling price of the fan $$\text{Selling price} = 300 + 78 = \$378$$
Selling price = cost price + profit.
4
Let $y$ be the marked price of the fan
Percentage Discount \(\text{selling price}=\text{marked price}\times(1-\text{discount }\%\times0.01)\) A \(30\%\) discount means the customer pays \(70\%\) of the marked price, so \(0.7y=378\).
A $30\%$ discount means the selling price is $70\%$ of the marked price: $$(70\%)\,y = 378$$
Discount $30\%$ ⇒ pay only $100\%-30\%=70\%$.
5
Solve for the marked price $y$ $$y = \frac{378}{0.7}$$ $$y = 540$$
Divide the selling price by $0.7$.
Answer: The marked price of the fan is $\$540$.

Question. A toy is sold at a profit of \(\$96\), which is \(32\%\) of its cost price. The selling price is obtained by giving a \(25\%\) discount on the marked price. Find the marked price of the toy.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let the cost price be \(x\). The profit is \(32\%\) of the cost price: \(0.32x=96\).
Step 2. So \(x=\dfrac{96}{0.32}=300\), i.e. the cost price is \(\$300\).
Step 3. Selling price \(=\) cost price \(+\) profit \(=300+96=\$396\).
Step 4. Let the marked price be \(y\). A \(25\%\) discount means the selling price is \(75\%\) of \(y\): \(0.75y=396\).
Step 5. Hence \(y=\dfrac{396}{0.75}=528\), so the marked price is \(\$528\).
Answer: \(\$528\)
