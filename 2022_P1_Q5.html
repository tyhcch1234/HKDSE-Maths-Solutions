HKDSE Math 2022 Paper 1 Q5

[4 marks]
Percentages Profit & Loss Discount Marked Price

A fan is sold at a discount of 30% on its marked price. After selling the fan, the profit is $78 and the percentage profit is 26%. Find the marked price of the fan.

Guided Solution

1

Define the cost price

Let the cost price of the fan be $x$.

Hint: Cost price is what the seller pays to get the fan.

2

Use the profit information

Profit = $78, percentage profit = 26%.

Profit is a percentage of cost price, so:

\[ 26\% \times x = 78 \]

Hint: Percentage profit = \(\frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{Cost price}} \times 100\%\).

3

Find the cost price \(x\)

Solve the equation:

\[ \begin{aligned} 0.26\,x &= 78 \\ x &= \frac{78}{0.26} \\ x &= 300 \end{aligned} \]

So the cost price is $300.

4

Calculate the selling price

Selling price = Cost price + Profit

\[ \text{Selling price} = 300 + 78 = \$378 \]
5

Relate discount to marked price

Let the marked price be $y$.

The fan is sold at 30% discount, so selling price = 70% of marked price.

\[ 70\% \times y = 378 \]

Hint: Discount of 30% means you pay 70% of the marked price.

6

Solve for marked price \(y\)

\[ \begin{aligned} 0.70\,y &= 378 \\ y &= \frac{378}{0.7} \\ y &= 540 \end{aligned} \]

∴ The marked price of the fan is $540.
