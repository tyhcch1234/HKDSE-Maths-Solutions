2019 MC #23

In the figure, the equation of the straight line $L$ is $ax+by+15=0$. Which of the following are true?

I. $\;a > b$
II. $\;a > -3$
III. $\;b > -5$

  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III
Step 1 · Read the intercepts from the figure
Intercepts of a Line \(ax+by+c=0\ \Longrightarrow\ x\text{-intercept}=-\dfrac{c}{a},\quad y\text{-intercept}=-\dfrac{c}{b}\) With \(c=15\) the intercepts are \(-\dfrac{15}{a}\) and \(-\dfrac{15}{b}\), both positive in the figure.

The line $L$ cuts the positive $x$-axis at a point beyond $5$, and the positive $y$-axis at a point above $3$.

For $ax+by+15=0$:

$x$-intercept: $\;y=0 \Rightarrow ax=-15 \Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{15}{a}>0$.

$y$-intercept: $\;x=0 \Rightarrow by=-15 \Rightarrow y=-\dfrac{15}{b}>0$.

Step 2 · Determine the signs of $a$ and $b$

$-\dfrac{15}{a}>0$ and $-\dfrac{15}{b}>0$.

Since $-15<0$, dividing by a negative product reverses the sign: both $a<0$ and $b<0$.

Step 3 · Statement I — is $a > b$?
Multiplication Property of Inequality \(A>B\ \text{and}\ C>0\ \Longrightarrow\ AC>BC\) Multiplying \(-\dfrac{15}{a}>-\dfrac{15}{b}\) by \(\dfrac{ab}{15}>0\) (since \(a,b<0\)) keeps the sign and gives \(a>b\).

The $x$-intercept is larger than the $y$-intercept:

$-\dfrac{15}{a}>-\dfrac{15}{b}$.

Multiply both sides by $\dfrac{ab}{15}$ (positive since $a,b<0$):

$-b>-a \;\Rightarrow\; a>b$.

✓ Statement I is TRUE.

Step 4 · Statement II — is $a > -3$?

The $x$-intercept is larger than $5$:

$-\dfrac{15}{a}>5$.

Multiply both sides by $\dfrac{a}{5}$ (negative since $a<0$), reversing the inequality:

$-3<a \;\Rightarrow\; a>-3$.

✓ Statement II is TRUE.

Step 5 · Statement III — is $b > -5$?

The $y$-intercept is smaller than $3$:

$-\dfrac{15}{b}<3$.

Multiply both sides by $\dfrac{b}{3}$ (negative since $b<0$), reversing the inequality:

$-5>b \;\Rightarrow\; b<-5$.

This is the opposite of $b>-5$.

✗ Statement III is FALSE.

Step 6 · Conclusion

Only statements I and II are true.

Answer: A (I and II only)

Question. In the figure, the equation of the straight line \(L\) is \(ax+by+12=0\). The line cuts the positive \(x\)-axis at a point beyond \(6\), and the positive \(y\)-axis at a point below \(4\). Which of the following are true? I. \(a>b\) II. \(a>-2\) III. \(b>-3\)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The intercepts are \(x=-\dfrac{12}{a}\) and \(y=-\dfrac{12}{b}\). Both are positive and \(-12<0\), so \(a<0\) and \(b<0\).
Step 2. The \(x\)-intercept exceeds the \(y\)-intercept: \(-\dfrac{12}{a}>-\dfrac{12}{b}\). Multiplying by \(\dfrac{ab}{12}>0\) gives \(-b>-a\), i.e. \(a>b\): I is true.
Step 3. The \(x\)-intercept exceeds \(6\): \(-\dfrac{12}{a}>6\). Multiplying by \(\dfrac{a}{6}<0\) reverses the sign, giving \(-2-2\): II is true.
Step 4. The \(y\)-intercept is below \(4\): \(-\dfrac{12}{b}<4\). Multiplying by \(\dfrac{b}{4}<0\) reverses the sign, giving \(-3>b\), i.e. \(b<-3\), which is the opposite of \(b>-3\): III is false.
Step 5. Only I and II are true.
Answer: I and II only
