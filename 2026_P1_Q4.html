Factorization

Question

Factorize
(a) \(5x^2y+17xy-12y\),
(b) \(5x^2y+17xy-12y-10x+6\). (4 marks)

Solution

PART (a) — common factor
Common Factor \(AB+AC=A(B+C)\) Every term contains \(y\), so \(5x^{2}y+17xy-12y=y\left(5x^{2}+17x-12\right)\).
Every term contains y, so take it out: \[ 5x^2y+17xy-12y=y(5x^2+17x-12) \]
PART (a) — split the middle term
Splitting the Middle Term \(ac=-60,\quad a+c=17\) \(20\) and \(-3\) have product \(-60\) and sum \(17\), giving \((5x-3)(x+4)\).
For \(5x^2+17x-12\): find two numbers with product \(5\times(-12)=-60\) and sum \(17\), i.e. \(20\) and \(-3\): \[ 5x^2+20x-3x-12=5x(x+4)-3(x+4)=(5x-3)(x+4) \] \[ \therefore\; 5x^2y+17xy-12y=y(5x-3)(x+4) \]
PART (b) — group using part (a)
Group the first three terms (result of (a)) and the last two terms: \[ 5x^2y+17xy-12y-10x+6 = y(5x-3)(x+4)-2(5x-3) \]
PART (b) — common binomial factor
Both groups contain \((5x-3)\): \[ =(5x-3)\big[y(x+4)-2\big] \]
Check: \((5x-3)(yx+4y-2)=5x^2y+20xy-10x-3xy-12y+6=5x^2y+17xy-12y-10x+6\) ✓
(a) \(y(5x-3)(x+4)\)    (b) \((5x-3)\big[y(x+4)-2\big]\)

Question. Factorize
(a) \(3x^{2}y+10xy-8y\),
(b) \(3x^{2}y+10xy-8y-12x+8\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Take out \(y\): \(3x^{2}y+10xy-8y=y\left(3x^{2}+10x-8\right)\).
Step 2. For \(3x^{2}+10x-8\): \(12\) and \(-2\) have product \(-24\) and sum \(10\), so \(3x^{2}+12x-2x-8=(3x-2)(x+4)\).
Step 3. Hence (a) \(=y(3x-2)(x+4)\).
Step 4. For (b): \(y(3x-2)(x+4)-4(3x-2)=(3x-2)\left[y(x+4)-4\right]\).
Answer: (a) \(y(3x-2)(x+4)\) (b) \((3x-2)\left[y(x+4)-4\right]\)
