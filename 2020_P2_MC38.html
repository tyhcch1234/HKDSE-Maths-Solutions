In the figure, \(ABCDEF\) is a right triangular prism. \(P\) is a point lying on \(AE\). If \(AB = AC = 12\) cm, \(AP = 9\) cm, \(EP = 5\) cm and \(BD = 2k\) cm, find the area of \(\Delta BDP\).

A. \(\sqrt{(k^2-1)(196-k^2)}\)
B. \(\sqrt{(k^2-1)(196+k^2)}\)
C. \(\sqrt{(k^2+1)(196-k^2)}\)
D. \(\sqrt{(k^2+1)(196+k^2)}\)

1Length \(BP\) in \(\triangle ABP\)

Since \(ABCDEF\) is a right triangular prism, \(AB\) is perpendicular to \(AE\). Drag the slider to see how \(P\) moves along \(AE\).

\(AB = 12\) cm \(AE = AP+EP = 14\) cm \(BP = 15.00\) cm
0 / 3
Step 11M

In the right-angled triangle \(ABP\), since \(AB \perp AE\):

\[ BP = \sqrt{AB^2 + AP^2} = \sqrt{12^2 + 9^2} \]
Hint: A right prism has all lateral edges perpendicular to the base, so \(AB \perp AE\) and hence \(AB \perp AP\).
Step 21A
\[ BP = \sqrt{144 + 81} = \sqrt{225} = 15 \text{ cm} \]
Hint: \(12\text{–}9\text{–}15\) is a scaled \(4\text{–}3\text{–}5\) Pythagorean triple.
\(BP = 15\) cm
Step 31A

Also note \(AE = AP + EP = 9 + 5 = 14\) cm — this full edge length will be needed for \(DP\).

Hint: The lateral edge joins the two parallel triangular faces \(ABC\) and \(DEF\).
\(AE = 14\) cm

2Length \(DP\) in \(\triangle DEP\)

The apex \(D\) sits above \(C\). Here \(DE = 12\) cm (matching \(AB\)) and \(EP = AE - AP = 5\) cm.

\(DE = 12\) cm \(AE = 14\) cm \(DP = 13.00\) cm
0 / 2
Step 11M

In the right-angled triangle \(DEP\), \(DE \perp EP\):

\[ DP = \sqrt{DE^2 + EP^2} = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} \]
Hint: \(DE\) is a lateral edge parallel to \(AB\), and \(EP\) lies along \(AE\), so the angle at \(E\) is \(90^\circ\).
Step 21A
\[ DP = \sqrt{144 + 25} = \sqrt{169} = 13 \text{ cm} \]
Hint: \(12\text{–}5\text{–}13\) is a standard Pythagorean triple.
\(DP = 13\) cm

3Area of \(\triangle BDP\) via Heron's Formula

We now have all three sides of \(\triangle BDP\): \(BD = 2k\), \(BP = 15\), \(DP = 13\). Adjust \(k\) to see the triangle change shape.

\(BD = 2k\) \(BP = 15\) \(DP = 13\) \(s = k+14\) Area = 47.62 cm²
0 / 3
Step 11M

The semi-perimeter of \(\triangle BDP\) is:

\[ s = \frac{BD+BP+DP}{2} = \frac{2k+15+13}{2} = k+14 \text{ cm} \]
Hint: Add the three sides and halve. The \(k\) terms simplify neatly.
Step 21M

By Heron's formula, \(\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s-BD)(s-BP)(s-DP)}\):

\[ = \sqrt{(k+14)\big[(k+14)-2k\big]\big[(k+14)-15\big]\big[(k+14)-13\big]} \] \[ = \sqrt{(k+14)(14-k)(k-1)(k+1)} \]
Hint: \((k+14)-2k = 14-k\) and \((k+14)-13 = k+1\). This requires the triangle inequality, i.e. \(1 < k < 14\).
Step 31A

Group the factors:

\[ = \sqrt{\big[(k-1)(k+1)\big]\big[(14-k)(14+k)\big]} \] \[ = \sqrt{(k^2-1)(196-k^2)} \text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: Use the difference of squares: \((k-1)(k+1)=k^2-1\) and \((14-k)(14+k)=196-k^2\).
Area \(= \sqrt{(k^2-1)(196-k^2)}\) cm²  ⟹  Answer A
