Question. In the figure, $ABCD$ is a rectangle. It is given that $AE = 20\text{ cm}$ and the area of $\triangle ADE$ is $150\text{ cm}^2$. If the area of $\triangle ADE$ is $150\text{ cm}^2$, then the perpendicular distance from $E$ to $CD$ is
$\text{A. }7\text{ cm}\qquad\text{B. }8\text{ cm}\qquad\text{C. }9\text{ cm}\qquad\text{D. }10\text{ cm}$
STEP 0 · UNDERSTAND THE FIGURE

$ABCD$ is a rectangle, so $AB \parallel CD$, $BC \parallel AD$ and $\angle BAD = 90^\circ$.

Point $E$ lies inside the rectangle so that $\triangle ADE$ has $AE=20$ and area $150$.

We must find the perpendicular distance from $E$ to side $CD$ (the top edge).

STEP 1 · USE THE AREA OF △ADE (with DE as base)

Taking $AE$ as base and $DE$ as height (right angle at $E$ is used below):

$$\text{Area of }\triangle ADE=\tfrac12 \times AE \times DE = 150$$ $$\tfrac12 \times 20 \times DE = 150$$
The rectangle gives a right angle at $A$, and the triangle is right-angled at $E$ because $AB \parallel CD$ and $AD \perp CD$.
STEP 2 · FIND $DE$

From $\tfrac12 \times 20 \times DE = 150$:

$$10 \times DE = 150 \;\Rightarrow\; DE = 15\text{ cm}$$
STEP 3 · FIND $AD$ (PYTHAGORAS)

$AD^2 = AE^2 + DE^2$

$$AD^2 = 20^2 + 15^2 = 400 + 225 = 625$$ $$AD = 25\text{ cm}$$
STEP 4 · HEIGHT $EF$ FROM $E$ TO $AD$

Let $F$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $E$ to $AD$. Using area again with base $AD$:

$$\tfrac12 \times AD \times EF = 150$$ $$\tfrac12 \times 25 \times EF = 150 \;\Rightarrow\; EF = 12\text{ cm}$$
STEP 5 · PERPENDICULAR DISTANCE $x$ TO $CD$

Let $x$ be the perpendicular distance from $E$ to $CD$. In the right triangle formed by $DE$, $EF$ and this distance:

$$x^2 + EF^2 = DE^2$$ $$x^2 + 12^2 = 15^2$$ $$x^2 = 225 - 144 = 81 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 9$$
So the perpendicular distance from $E$ to $CD$ is $9\text{ cm}$ — answer C.
Final Answer
$x = 9\text{ cm}$  (Option C)
