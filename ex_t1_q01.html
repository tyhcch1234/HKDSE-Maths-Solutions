T1 Q01

A bank offers simple interest at 4% p.a. A customer deposits \$5,000 for 3 years. Find the interest earned.

Solution

Use the simple interest formula $I = P r t$.
Substitute $P = 5000$, $r = 4\% = 0.04$, and $t = 3$.
Compute $I = 5000 \times 0.04 \times 3 = 600$.
The interest earned is $600$ dollars.
