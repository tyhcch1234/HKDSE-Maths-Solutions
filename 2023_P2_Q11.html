HKDSE 2023 · Paper 2 · Q11 · 1. Percentages
The marked price of a jacket is \(60\%\) above its cost. A profit of \(\$104\) is made by selling the jacket at a discount of \(25\%\) on its marked price. Find the cost of the jacket.
  • A. \(\$416\)
  • B. \(\$520\)
  • C. \(\$728\)
  • D. \(\$832\)
Step 1 · Define the unknown
Let \(C\) be the cost of the jacket (in dollars).
💡 When everything is a percentage of the cost, the cost is the natural unknown.
Step 2 · Marked price
The marked price is \(60\%\) above the cost: \[ \text{Marked price} = C\,(1 + 60\%) = 1.6C \]
💡 "\(60\%\) above" means \(\times (1+0.6) = \times 1.6\), not \(\times 0.6\).
Step 3 · Selling price
A \(25\%\) discount is applied to the marked price: \[ \text{Selling price} = 1.6C \times (1 - 25\%) = 1.6C \times 0.75 = 1.2C \]
💡 Discount is taken on the marked price, so multiply the marked price by \(0.75\).
Step 4 · Set up the profit equation
Profit \(=\) Selling price \(-\) Cost: \[ 1.2C - C = 104 \]
💡 Profit is measured against the cost, so subtract \(C\) (not the marked price).
Step 5 · Solve for \(C\)
\[ 0.2C = 104 \quad\Rightarrow\quad C = \frac{104}{0.2} = 520 \]
💡 Dividing by \(0.2\) is the same as multiplying by \(5\).
Step 6 · Interpretation
The cost of the jacket is \(\$520\). (Check: marked \(=\$832\), sell \(=\$624\), profit \(=\$624-\$520=\$104\) ✔)
💡 A quick back-substitution catches careless percentage slips.
Answer: B — the cost of the jacket is \(\$520\). Cost \(C=\$520\), marked price \(=\$832\), selling price \(=\$624\), profit \(=\$104\).
