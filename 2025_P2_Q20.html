Step 1 · Set up the two right triangles

Drop perpendiculars from $S$ and $R$ onto $PQ$. Let the feet be $T$ and $U$ respectively, so $ST \perp PQ$ and $RU \perp PQ$.

Because $PS \parallel QR$, the distance between the parallel sides is constant: $ST = RU = h$. Also $STUR$ is a rectangle, so $TU = RS$.
Step 2 · Read the base angles from the figure

Interior angles at $S$ and $R$ are given as $120^\circ$ and $150^\circ$. Since $ST \perp PQ$ and $PQ \parallel SR$:

$$\angle SPT = 180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ, \qquad \angle RQU = 180^\circ - 150^\circ = 30^\circ$$
Co-interior Angles \(\angle SPT=180^{\circ}-120^{\circ}=60^{\circ}\) \(PS\) and the line \(PQ\) meet the parallel sides, so the angles on the same side are supplementary.
Step 3 · Height $ST$ and the foot $TU$

In right triangle $PST$ (right angle at $T$):

$$ST = PS\sin 60^\circ = 41\sin 60^\circ \text{ cm}, \qquad PT = PS\cos 60^\circ = 41\cos 60^\circ \text{ cm}$$

Since $STUR$ is a rectangle:

$$TU = RS = 53 \text{ cm}$$
Sine Ratio \(ST=PS\sin 60^{\circ}\) In the right-angled \(\triangle PST\), \(ST\) is opposite the \(60^{\circ}\) angle.
Step 4 · Find $UQ$ from triangle $RQU$

In right triangle $RQU$ (right angle at $U$), $RU = ST = 41\sin 60^\circ$:

$$\frac{RU}{UQ} = \tan 30^\circ \;\Longrightarrow\; UQ = \frac{RU}{\tan 30^\circ} = \frac{41\sin 60^\circ}{\tan 30^\circ} \text{ cm}$$
$\dfrac{\sin 60^\circ}{\tan 30^\circ} = \dfrac{\sqrt3/2}{1/\sqrt3} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, so $UQ = 41 \times \dfrac{3}{2} = 61.5$ cm.
Tangent Ratio \(\tan 30^{\circ}=\dfrac{RU}{UQ}\) In the right-angled \(\triangle RQU\), \(RU\) is opposite and \(UQ\) is adjacent to \(30^{\circ}\).
Step 5 · Add the three parts of $PQ$

$PQ$ is split by the two feet into $PT + TU + UQ$:

$$PQ = 41\cos 60^\circ + 53 + \frac{41\sin 60^\circ}{\tan 30^\circ} = 20.5 + 53 + 61.5 = 135 \text{ cm}$$
