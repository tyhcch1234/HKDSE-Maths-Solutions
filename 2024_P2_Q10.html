2024MC#10

The solution of  $\dfrac{1-x}{2} \ge 4$  or  $7+5x \le -3$  is

  • A.  $x \le -7$
  • B.  $x \le -2$
  • C.  $x \ge -2$
  • D.  $x \le -7$ or $x \le -2$
1

Solve the first inequality

$$\frac{1-x}{2} \;\ge\; 4$$ Multiply both sides by $2$ (positive, so the sign stays): $$1-x \;\ge\; 8$$ Subtract $1$: $\;-x \ge 7\;$ → multiply by $-1$ (reverse the sign): $$x \;\le\; -7$$
Hint: dividing/multiplying by a negative number flips $\ge$ into $\le$.
2

Solve the second inequality

$$7+5x \;\le\; -3$$ Subtract $7$: $\;5x \le -10$ Divide by $5$ (positive, sign unchanged): $$x \;\le\; -2$$
Hint: no sign flip here — the divisor $5$ is positive.
3

Combine with “or” (union of solution sets)

The word “or” means we take the union: $$\{x \le -7\} \;\cup\; \{x \le -2\}$$ Every number $\le -7$ is already $\le -2$, so the smaller set is contained inside the bigger one. The union is simply the larger set: $$x \;\le\; -2$$
Hint: for “or”, draw both rays; the shown (shaded) part is everything covered by at least one ray.
4

Check with a test value

Take $x=-3$ (which satisfies $x \le -2$).
First: $\dfrac{1-(-3)}{2}=\dfrac{4}{2}=2 \ge 4$? False.
Second: $7+5(-3) = 7-15 = -8 \le -3$? True. ✔
Since it satisfies at least one inequality, $x=-3$ is in the solution set.
Take $x=-1$: first gives $1 \ge 4$ false; second gives $2 \le -3$ false → not a solution. ✔
Hint: “or” only needs one branch to be true.
Answer: B  —  $x \le -2$

Common trap (option D): writing “$x \le -7$ or $x \le -2$” without simplifying. Since $\{x \le -7\}\subset\{x \le -2\}$, their union collapses to the single inequality $x \le -2$.
