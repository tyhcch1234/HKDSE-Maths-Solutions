Step 1 — Rearrange the four terms and pair them up.

Group 1: α² − β² Group 2: −α + β Common factor (α − β)
[2022 MC #1]

$\alpha^{2}-\alpha-\beta^{2}+\beta =$

  • A. $(\alpha+\beta)(\alpha-\beta+1)$
  • B. $(\alpha+\beta)(\alpha-\beta-1)$
  • C. $(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta+1)$
  • D. $(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta-1)$
1Reorder the four terms

Group the two ${\alpha}$-terms together and the two ${\beta}$-terms together so that the difference of two squares becomes visible:

$\alpha^{2}-\alpha-\beta^{2}+\beta=\alpha^{2}-\beta^{2}-\alpha+\beta$

Hint: the middle two terms are $-\alpha+\beta=-(\alpha-\beta)$ — keep the common bracket $(\alpha-\beta)$ in mind.
2Factorise the first pair by difference of squares

Since $\alpha^{2}-\beta^{2}=(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta)$,

$\alpha^{2}-\beta^{2}-\alpha+\beta=(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta)-(\alpha-\beta)$

Hint: $a^{2}-b^{2}=(a-b)(a+b)$ is the key identity here.
Difference of Two Squares \(\alpha^{2}-\beta^{2}=(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta)\) This factorises the first pair of terms so that the common bracket \(\alpha-\beta\) appears.
3Take out the common factor $(\alpha-\beta)$

Both terms now contain the factor $(\alpha-\beta)$:

$(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta)-(\alpha-\beta)\cdot 1$

$=(\alpha-\beta)\big[(\alpha+\beta)-1\big]$

Hint: remember the invisible "$-1$" in the second term.
Factorisation by Taking out a Common Factor \(AB-AC=A(B-C)\) Both terms contain \(\alpha-\beta\), and the second term carries the invisible factor \(1\).
4Simplify and choose the option

$(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta-1)$

This matches option D.

Hint: expand $( \alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta-1)$ mentally to verify you recover $\alpha^{2}-\alpha-\beta^{2}+\beta$.
✓ Answer: Option D  —  $(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta-1)$

Question. Factorise \(a^{2}-b^{2}+3a-3b\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Group the terms: \(a^{2}-b^{2}+3a-3b=(a^{2}-b^{2})+(3a-3b)\).
Step 2. Difference of two squares: \(a^{2}-b^{2}=(a-b)(a+b)\).
Step 3. Take out the common factor \(3\) from the second pair: \(3a-3b=3(a-b)\).
Step 4. Take out the common factor \((a-b)\): \((a-b)\big[(a+b)+3\big]=(a-b)(a+b+3)\).
Step 5. Check by expanding: \(a^{2}+ab+3a-ab-b^{2}-3b=a^{2}-b^{2}+3a-3b\).
Answer: \((a-b)(a+b+3)\)
