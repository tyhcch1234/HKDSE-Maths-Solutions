Mensuration — Sector: angle from area, perimeter in terms of π

Sector Area Arc Length Perimeter Angles in Degrees

Q9 The Question 5 marks

The radius and the area of a sector are \(12\text{ cm}\) and \(30\pi\text{ cm}^2\) respectively.

(a) Find the angle of the sector. 2 marks

(b) Express the perimeter of the sector in terms of \(\pi\). 3 marks

Part (a) Finding the angle θ 2 marks

120°
r = 12 cm
θ (from slider) = 120°
Area = 150.80 cm²
Required area = 94.25 cm² (=30π)

Drag the slider: the sector's area changes with θ. Push it until the area equals \(30\pi\approx 94.25\text{ cm}^2\) — that pins down the required angle.

Step 1 1M

Let \(\theta^\circ\) be the angle of the sector. The area of a sector of radius \(r\) and angle \(\theta^\circ\) is

\[ \text{Area}=\frac{\theta}{360}\times \pi r^{2} \]

Substitute \(r=12\) and Area \(=30\pi\):

\[ \frac{\theta}{360}\Big(\pi(12^{2})\Big)=30\pi \]
Hint: Both sides contain a factor \(\pi\). You may cancel \(\pi\) first — then you are solving a simple equation in \(\theta\).
Step 2 1A

Simplify and solve for \(\theta\):

\[ \frac{\theta}{360}(144\pi)=30\pi \] \[ \frac{144\theta}{360}=30 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{2\theta}{5}=30 \] \[ \theta=75 \]
Therefore, the angle of the sector is \(\;\mathbf{75^\circ}\).
Check: \(\dfrac{75}{360}\times\pi(12)^2 = \dfrac{5}{24}\times 144\pi = 30\pi\) ✓

Part (b) Perimeter in terms of π 3 marks

75°
Arc length = 15.71 cm (= 5π)
Two radii = 24 cm
Perimeter = 39.71 cm ≈ (5π+24) cm

Toggle the layers to see that the perimeter is the curved arc plus the two straight radii. At θ = 75°, Arc = 5π exactly.

Step 1 1M

The perimeter of a sector = arc length + 2 radii. The arc length of a sector is

\[ \text{Arc}=\frac{\theta}{360}\times 2\pi r \]

Substitute \(\theta = 75\) (from part (a)) and \(r=12\):

\[ \frac{75}{360}\Big(2\pi(12)\Big) \]
Hint: In part (a) you used \(\pi r^2\) for area. Here the curved edge uses the circumference formula \(2\pi r\) instead.
Step 2 1M

Add the two straight sides, each of length \(r=12\):

\[ \text{Perimeter}=\frac{75}{360}\Big(2\pi(12)\Big)+2(12) \]
Hint: Do not forget the two radii — the "crust" of a pizza slice plus its two straight cuts.
Step 3 1A

Simplify:

\[ =\frac{75}{360}(24\pi)+24 = \frac{5}{24}(24\pi)+24 = 5\pi+24 \]
The required perimeter is \(\;\mathbf{(5\pi+24)\ \text{cm}}\).
Note: "In terms of \(\pi\)" means keep the exact form — do not substitute \(\pi\approx 3.14\).

Summary of Answers

  • (a) The angle of the sector is 75°
  • (b) The perimeter of the sector is (5π + 24) cm  \((\approx 39.7\text{ cm})\)
  • Key relations used: Area \(=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\pi r^2\), Arc \(=\dfrac{\theta}{360}(2\pi r)\), Perimeter = Arc \(+ 2r\)
