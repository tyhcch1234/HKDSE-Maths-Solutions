Question 9.
The radius and the area of a sector are $12\text{ cm}$ and $30\pi\text{ cm}^2$ respectively.
(a) Find the angle of the sector.
(b) Express the perimeter of the sector in terms of $\pi$.
[5 marks]
STEP 1 · SET UP THE ANGLE

Let $\theta^{\circ}$ be the angle of the sector. The sector is a fraction $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$ of the whole circle.

The whole circle has area $\pi r^2 = \pi(12^2)$.
STEP 2 · AREA EQUATION

Area of sector $=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times \pi r^2$. With area $=30\pi$ and $r=12$:

$$\frac{\theta}{360}\left(\pi(12^2)\right)=30\pi$$ $$\frac{\theta}{360}\left(144\pi\right)=30\pi$$
Cancel $\\pi$ on both sides to simplify.
STEP 3 · SOLVE FOR θ $$\frac{144\,\theta}{360}=30 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{2\theta}{5}=30 \;\Rightarrow\; \theta=75$$
ANSWER (a) The angle of the sector is $\boxed{75^{\circ}}$.
STEP 4 · PERIMETER STRUCTURE

The perimeter of a sector = arc length + two radii.

$$\text{Arc}=\frac{\theta}{360}\times 2\pi r,\qquad \text{two radii}=2(12)$$
Careful: the two straight radii are part of the perimeter!
STEP 5 · SUBSTITUTE VALUES $$\text{Perimeter}=\frac{75}{360}\big(2\pi(12)\big)+2(12)$$ $$=\frac{5}{24}\left(24\pi\right)+24$$ $$=5\pi+24$$
ANSWER (b) The perimeter of the sector is $(5\pi+24)\text{ cm}$.
STEP 6 · CHECK

With $\theta=75^{\circ}$, the sector is about $20.8\%$ of the circle.

  • Area: $0.2083\times144\pi \approx 30\pi$ ✓
  • Arc: $0.2083\times24\pi = 5\pi \approx 15.7$ cm ✓
  • Perimeter: $5\pi+24 \approx 39.7$ cm ✓
Key formulas used: $A_{\text{sector}}=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\pi r^2$ and $\text{Perimeter}=\dfrac{\theta}{360}(2\pi r)+2r$.
