Question & solution

In Figure 2, the volume of the solid right prism \(ABCDEFGH\) is \(1020\text{ cm}^3\). The base \(ABCD\) is a trapezium with \(AD\parallel BC\). It is given that \(\angle BAD=90^\circ\), \(AB=12\text{ cm}\), \(BC=6\text{ cm}\) and \(DE=10\text{ cm}\).

(a) Find the length of \(AD\).

(b) Find the total surface area of the prism.

STEP 1 · Volume formula
\[ \text{Volume} = \text{base area} \times \text{height} \]

Base area of trapezium \(ABCD\):

\[ \frac{AD+BC}{2}\times AB = \frac{AD+6}{2}\times 12 = 6(AD+6) \]
STEP 2 · Find AD (a)
\[ 6(AD+6)\times 10 = 1020 \;\Rightarrow\; 60(AD+6)=1020 \]
\[ AD+6=17 \;\Rightarrow\; AD=11\text{ cm} \]
Answer (a): \(AD=11\text{ cm}\)
STEP 3 · Surface area (b)

First find \(CD\) (drop a perpendicular):

\[ CD=\sqrt{(11-6)^2+12^2}=\sqrt{25+144}=13\text{ cm} \]

Base area \(=102\), and the four rectangular faces:

\[ 12{\cdot}10+6{\cdot}10+13{\cdot}10+11{\cdot}10=120+60+130+110=420 \]
\[ \text{Total}=2(102)+420=624\text{ cm}^2 \]
Answer (b): total surface area \(=624\text{ cm}^2\)
