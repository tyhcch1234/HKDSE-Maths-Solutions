Geometry (106) · Sub-Topic: Congruence/Similarity

T11 Q33

Two similar triangles have areas in the ratio $16:25$. Find the ratio of their corresponding sides.

Solution

For similar triangles, the ratio of their areas equals the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.
Area ratio is $16:25$, so the side ratio is $\sqrt{16}:\sqrt{25}$.
Thus the ratio of corresponding sides is $4:5$.
The ratio of their corresponding sides is $4:5$.
