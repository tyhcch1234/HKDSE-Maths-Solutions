Mensuration — Volumes & Surface Areas of a Cube and a Right Circular Cylinder

Q15 The Question MC

The length of a side of a solid cube is \(60\text{ cm}\). The volume of a solid right circular cylinder is equal to the volume of the cube while the curved surface area of the circular cylinder is equal to the total surface area of the cube. Find the base radius of the circular cylinder.

A. \(20\text{ cm}\)
B. \(30\text{ cm}\)
C. \(76\text{ cm}\)
D. \(172\text{ cm}\)

(a) Translating the Two Conditions into Equations 1M 1A

Let \(r\text{ cm}\) be the base radius and \(h\text{ cm}\) be the height of the cylinder. Use the slider below to see how the cylinder's dimensions are linked to the \(60\text{ cm}\) cube. The readouts update live.

Step 11M

Volume of the cube with side \(60\):

\[ V_{\text{cube}} = 60^3 = 216000\text{ cm}^3 \]

Volume of the cylinder with base radius \(r\) and height \(h\):

\[ V_{\text{cyl}} = \pi r^2 h \]
Hint: "Volume is equal" gives one equation immediately — set the two volume expressions equal.
Equation (1): \(\pi r^2 h = 216000\)
Step 21M

Total surface area of the cube (6 square faces of side \(60\)):

\[ A_{\text{cube}} = 6 \times 60^2 = 21600\text{ cm}^2 \]

Curved surface area of the cylinder (a rectangle of width \(2\pi r\) and height \(h\) when unrolled):

\[ A_{\text{cyl}} = 2\pi r h \]
Hint: The curved surface of a cylinder unrolls into a rectangle of base \(2\pi r\) and height \(h\) — the ends are not included.
Equation (2): \(2\pi r h = 21600\), i.e. \(\pi r h = 10800\)
Step 31A

We now have a system:

\[ \pi r^2 h = 216000 \tag{1} \] \[ \pi r h = 10800 \tag{2} \]
Hint: Both equations contain the product \(\pi r h\) in some form — dividing one by the other is the cleanest route.
Set-up complete: two equations in \(r\) and \(h\).

(b) Solving for the Base Radius \(r\) 1M 1A

Divide equation (1) by equation (2). The \(\pi\) and one power of \(r\) cancel, isolating \(r\). Watch how the ratio \(\dfrac{V_{\text{cyl}}}{A_{\text{cyl}}}\) behaves as \(r\) changes.

20.0 cm
V/A (cylinder) = r / 2 V/A (cube) = 10 cm Solution: r = 20 cm
Step 11M

Divide (1) by (2):

\[ \frac{\pi r^2 h}{\pi r h} = \frac{216000}{10800} \]

The left side simplifies because \(\pi r h\) cancels:

\[ r = 20 \]
Hint: For the cube, \(\dfrac{V}{A} = \dfrac{60^3}{6\cdot 60^2} = \dfrac{60}{6} = 10\) cm. For the cylinder, \(\dfrac{V}{A_{\text{curved}}} = \dfrac{\pi r^2 h}{2\pi r h} = \dfrac{r}{2}\). Equating gives \(r = 20\).
Base radius: \(r = 20\text{ cm}\)
Step 21A

The answer is option A.

\[\boxed{r = 20\text{ cm}}\]
Check: With \(r=20\), \(h = \dfrac{10800}{\pi \cdot 20} \approx 171.9\) cm, and \(\pi (20)^2 (171.9) \approx 216000\) ✓.
Final answer: A (20 cm)

Summary of Answers

  • Volume of cube: \(60^3 = 216000\text{ cm}^3\)
  • Curved surface area of cylinder: \(2\pi r h\)
  • Total surface area of cube: \(6 \times 60^2 = 21600\text{ cm}^2\)
  • Equations: \(\pi r^2 h = 216000\) and \(2\pi r h = 21600\)
  • Dividing one by the other: \(r/2 = 10\), so \(r = 20\text{ cm}\)
  • Height: \(h = \dfrac{10800}{20\pi} \approx 171.9\text{ cm}\)
  • Correct option: A
