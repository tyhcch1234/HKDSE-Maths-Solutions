QUESTION Perimeter of sector \(OMN\)

The perimeter of the sector \(OMN\) is \(12\pi\text{ cm}\), where \(O\) is the centre of the sector \(OMN\). If the radius of the sector \(OMN\) is \(3\pi\text{ cm}\), which of the following are true?

  1. The area of the sector \(OMN\) is \(9\pi^2\text{ cm}^2\).
  2. The perimeter of \(\triangle OMN\) is less than \(35\text{ cm}\).
  3. The angle of the sector \(OMN\) is greater than \(100^\circ\).
  • A. I and II only
  • B. I and III only
  • C. II and III only
  • D. I, II and III

PART (a) Verify Statement I — Area of the sector

We first find the sector angle \(\theta\) from the given perimeter, then compute the area and compare with \(9\pi^2\).

Step 1
Write the perimeter of the sector as arc length plus two radii: \[ 2\pi r\cdot\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} + 2r = 12\pi \]
Hint: A sector's perimeter consists of the arc \(MN\) and the two radii \(OM\), \(ON\).
Step 2
Substitute \(r=3\pi\): \[ 2\pi(3\pi)\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} + 2(3\pi)=12\pi \] \[ \frac{6\pi^2\theta}{360^\circ} = 6\pi \;\Rightarrow\; \theta=\frac{360^\circ}{\pi}\approx 114.59^\circ \]
Hint: \(6\pi^2\theta/360^\circ = 6\pi\) gives \(\theta = 360^\circ/\pi\), which is just over \(114^\circ\).
Step 3
Area of sector: \[ A=\pi r^2\cdot\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} =\pi(3\pi)^2\cdot\frac{360^\circ/\pi}{360^\circ} =9\pi^2 \] So Statement I is true.
Hint: Notice \(\theta/360^\circ = 1/\pi\) — the π cancels beautifully, leaving exactly \(9\pi^2\).
✓ Statement I is true: area \(=9\pi^2\text{ cm}^2\).

Step 1
From Part (a): \[ \theta = \frac{360^\circ}{\pi} \] We compare with \(100^\circ\): \[ \frac{360^\circ}{\pi} \approx 114.591559^\circ \]
Hint: Use \(\pi\approx 3.14159\); dividing \(360\) by it gives a value just over \(114.5^\circ\).
Step 2
Since \(114.59^\circ > 100^\circ\), Statement III is true.
Hint: A common mistake is assuming the angle is \(90^\circ\); the exact value \(360^\circ/\pi\) is well above \(100^\circ\).
✓ Statement III is true: \(\theta \approx 114.59^\circ > 100^\circ\).

PART (c) Verify Statement II — Perimeter of \(\triangle OMN\)

Now we compute \(MN\) using the cosine formula and add the two radii.

114.6°
\(r=3\pi\) cm
\(MN\) = 15.86 cm
Perimeter \(\triangle OMN\) = 34.71 cm
34.71 < 35 ✓
0 / 4
Step 1
Use the cosine formula in \(\triangle OMN\) with \(OM=ON=3\pi\) and included angle \(\theta = 360^\circ/\pi\): \[ MN^2=(3\pi)^2+(3\pi)^2-2(3\pi)(3\pi)\cos\theta \]
Hint: The two equal sides are the radii. The included angle is the sector angle we already found.
Step 2
\[ MN^2=9\pi^2+9\pi^2-18\pi^2\cos\!\left(\frac{360^\circ}{\pi}\right) =18\pi^2\left(1-\cos\!\frac{360^\circ}{\pi}\right) \] Taking the square root: \[ MN \approx 15.86135438\text{ cm} \]
Hint: \(\cos(360^\circ/\pi)=\cos(114.59^\circ)\approx -0.416\). So \(1-\cos\theta \approx 1.416\).
Step 3
Perimeter of \(\triangle OMN\): \[ P = OM + ON + MN = 3\pi + 3\pi + 15.86135438 \] \[ P \approx 34.71091031\text{ cm} \]
Hint: \(3\pi \approx 9.4248\), so \(2(3\pi) \approx 18.8496\). Add \(MN\).
Step 4
Since \(34.71091031 < 35\), Statement II is true.
Hint: The value is only just below 35 — the exact threshold matters. Use full calculator precision.
✓ Statement II is true: perimeter \(\approx 34.71\text{ cm} < 35\text{ cm}\).

📌 Final Answers at a Glance

  • Sector angle: \(\theta = \dfrac{360^\circ}{\pi} \approx 114.591559^\circ\)
  • I. Area \(= \pi(3\pi)^2\cdot\dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ} = 9\pi^2\text{ cm}^2\) → True
  • II. \(MN^2 = 18\pi^2\left(1-\cos\frac{360^\circ}{\pi}\right)\), \(MN\approx15.861\), perimeter \(\approx 34.711\text{ cm} < 35\text{ cm}\) → True
  • III. \(114.59^\circ > 100^\circ\) → True
Answer: D — I, II and III
