Topic: Probability & Combinations

Question

14. Probability

There are 15 teachers in a group. If 5 teachers are selected from the group to form a committee consisting of 1 chairperson and 4 members, how many different committees can be formed?

A. 3 003
B. 15 015
C. 20 475
D. 360 360
Combinations Counting Chairperson vs Members Distinct Roles

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Understand the task

We need to form a committee of 5 people from 15 teachers. One of the 5 is the chairperson, and the remaining 4 are members.

2

Method 1: Choose chairperson first

1. Choose 1 chairperson from 15 teachers: \(\binom{15}{1}\) ways.

2. Then choose 4 members from the remaining 14 teachers: \(\binom{14}{4}\) ways.

Hint: Multiplication principle: \(\binom{15}{1} \times \binom{14}{4}\)

3

Compute Method 1

\(\binom{15}{1} = 15\)

\(\binom{14}{4} = \frac{14 \times 13 \times 12 \times 11}{4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1} = 1001\)

Total: \(15 \times 1001 = 15015\)

4

Method 2: Choose 5 first, then chairperson

1. Choose any 5 teachers from 15: \(\binom{15}{5}\) ways.

2. From these 5, choose 1 as chairperson: \(\binom{5}{1}\) ways.

Hint: \(\binom{15}{5} \times \binom{5}{1}\)

5

Compute Method 2 & Verify

\(\binom{15}{5} = 3003\), \(\binom{5}{1} = 5\)

Total: \(3003 \times 5 = 15015\)

Both methods give the same answer: 15 015

Therefore, the correct option is B.
