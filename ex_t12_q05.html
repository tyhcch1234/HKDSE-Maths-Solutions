Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Distance/Slope

T12 Q05

Find the slope of the line passing through the points $(1, 1)$ and $(4, 7)$.

Solution

Use the slope formula $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$.
Let $(x_1, y_1) = (1, 1)$ and $(x_2, y_2) = (4, 7)$.
Substitute the coordinates: $m = \frac{7 - 1}{4 - 1}$.
Simplify: $m = \frac{6}{3} = 2$.
The slope of the line passing through $(1, 1)$ and $(4, 7)$ is $2$.
