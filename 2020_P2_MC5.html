Question

Multiple Choice

Let \(f(x) = 3x^2 - x - 2\). If \(\beta\) is a constant, then \(f(1+\beta) - f(1-\beta) =\)

  • A. \(2\beta\)
  • B. \(10\beta\)
  • C. \(6\beta^2 - 2\)
  • D. \(6\beta^2 - 2\beta\)
✎
(a)

Compute \(f(1+\beta)\) and \(f(1-\beta)\)

Method
1 1M

Substitute \(x = 1+\beta\) into \(f(x)\)

\[ f(1+\beta) = 3(1+\beta)^2 - (1+\beta) - 2 \]
Hint: Replace every \(x\) in \(f(x)=3x^2-x-2\) by \((1+\beta)\). Keep the bracket carefully.
2 1A

Expand and simplify

\[ \begin{aligned} f(1+\beta) &= 3(1+2\beta+\beta^2) - 1 - \beta - 2 \\ &= 3+6\beta+3\beta^2 - 3 - \beta \\ &= 3\beta^2 + 5\beta \end{aligned} \]
Hint: Expand \((1+\beta)^2 = 1+2\beta+\beta^2\) first; then collect like terms.
\[ f(1+\beta) = 3\beta^2 + 5\beta \]
3 1M

Substitute \(x = 1-\beta\) into \(f(x)\)

\[ f(1-\beta) = 3(1-\beta)^2 - (1-\beta) - 2 \]
Hint: Same substitution — but every \(\beta\) becomes \(-\beta\).
4 1A

Expand and simplify

\[ \begin{aligned} f(1-\beta) &= 3(1-2\beta+\beta^2) - 1 + \beta - 2 \\ &= 3 - 6\beta + 3\beta^2 - 3 + \beta \\ &= 3\beta^2 - 5\beta \end{aligned} \]
Hint: Note the sign change in front of \(\beta\) — this is the key to the whole problem.
\[ f(1-\beta) = 3\beta^2 - 5\beta \]
0 / 4
(b)

Subtract: \(f(1+\beta) - f(1-\beta)\)

Answer
1 1M

Write the difference using the results from part (a)

\[ f(1+\beta) - f(1-\beta) = (3\beta^2 + 5\beta) - (3\beta^2 - 5\beta) \]
Hint: Distribute the minus sign over both terms of \(f(1-\beta)\).
2 1A

Simplify

\[ \begin{aligned} f(1+\beta) - f(1-\beta) &= 3\beta^2 + 5\beta - 3\beta^2 + 5\beta \\ &= 10\beta \end{aligned} \]
Hint: The \(3\beta^2\) terms cancel — a classic elimination. Only the linear terms remain.
\[ f(1+\beta) - f(1-\beta) = 10\beta \quad \Rightarrow \quad \boxed{\textbf{B}} \]
0 / 2
✓

Check with the Graph

Visual Proof

The parabola \(y = 3x^2 - x - 2\) is symmetric about \(x = 1\). The points \((1+\beta, f(1+\beta))\) and \((1-\beta, f(1-\beta))\) are mirror images about this axis, so one value has \(+5\beta\) and the other has \(-5\beta\) — their difference is \(10\beta\).
