HKDSE 2023 Paper 1 — Question 6

Topic 2 · Rate, Ratio and Variations

Let $a$, $b$ and $c$ be non-zero numbers such that $7a=6b$ and $\dfrac{4a-3c}{2b-c}=9$. Find

(a)  $a:b:c$,

(b)  $\dfrac{5a+8b}{7b+3c}$.

[4 marks]

Combining Two Ratios \(a:b=m:n\ \text{and}\ b:c=p:q\ \Longrightarrow\ a:b:c=mp:np:nq\) The term \(b\) must take the same value in both ratios before the three parts are written together.
1Find the ratio $a:b$
From $7a=6b$, divide both sides by $7b$:
$\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{6}{7}\ \Rightarrow\ a:b = 6:7$.
Hint: $7a=6b$ means $a$ is smaller, so $a:b=6:7$ (the coefficient of $b$ becomes $a$'s share).
Ratio of Two Quantities \(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{m}{n}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ a:b=m:n\) A ratio is just a fraction, so each given pair is written as a quotient of the two letters.
2Use the second condition to link $b$ and $c$
$\dfrac{4a-3c}{2b-c}=9\ \Rightarrow\ 4a-3c = 18b - 9c$.
Substitute $a=\dfrac{6}{7}b$ (from step 1):
$4\left(\dfrac{6}{7}b\right)-3c = 18b-9c$
$\dfrac{24}{7}b + 6c = 18b\ \Rightarrow\ 6c=\left(18-\dfrac{24}{7}\right)b=\dfrac{102}{7}b$
$c=\dfrac{17}{7}b$.
Hint: collect the $c$-terms on one side and the $b$-terms on the other.
3Combine to get $a:b:c$
From $c=\dfrac{17}{7}b$ we get $b:c = 7:17$.
With $a:b=6:7$,
match the common $b$-part:
$a:b:c = 6:7:17$.
Hint: the ratio $b:c=7:17$ already shares the same $b$ value, so the three-part ratio can be written directly.
4Introduce $k$ and substitute
Let $a=6k$, $\ b=7k$, $\ c=17k$, where $k$ is a non-zero constant.
$\dfrac{5a+8b}{7b+3c}=\dfrac{5(6k)+8(7k)}{7(7k)+3(17k)}$
Hint: any common factor $k$ cancels out in the fraction, so the answer is independent of $k$.
Substituting Ratio Values \(a=mp,\quad b=np,\quad c=nq\) From \(a:b:c=6:7:17\) write \(a=6k\), \(b=7k\), \(c=17k\) with the same \(k>0\).
5Simplify
$=\dfrac{30k+56k}{49k+51k}=\dfrac{86k}{100k}=\dfrac{86}{100}=\dfrac{43}{50}$.
Hint: cancel the common factor $k$, then reduce the fraction by $2$.
Answer:
(a) $a:b:c = 6:7:17$   ·   (b) $\dfrac{5a+8b}{7b+3c}=\dfrac{43}{50}$

Question. It is given that \(a:b=3:4\) and \(b:c=2:5\), where \(a\), \(b\), \(c\) are positive.
(a) Find \(a:b:c\).
(b) Hence find the value of \(\dfrac{2a+3b}{3b+2c}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Make the \(b\) terms agree: \(a:b=3:4\) and \(b:c=4:10\).
Step 2. Hence \(a:b:c=3:4:10\).
Step 3. Put \(a=3k\), \(b=4k\), \(c=10k\): \(\dfrac{2(3k)+3(4k)}{3(4k)+2(10k)}=\dfrac{18k}{32k}\).
Step 4. The common factor \(2k\) cancels, so the value is \(\dfrac{9}{16}\).
Answer: (a) \(3:4:10\) (b) \(\dfrac{9}{16}\)

Note: since $a,b,c\neq 0$, all divisions above are valid.
