HKDSE 2012 · Paper 2 · Q9

If $x$ and $y$ are non-zero numbers such that \[ \frac{6x+5y}{\,3y-2x\,}=7, \] then

  • A. $4:5$
  • B. $4:13$
  • C. $5:4$
  • D. $13:4$
1

Step 1 — Clear the denominator

Multiply both sides by $(3y-2x)$ (which is non-zero since $x,y\neq 0$ and it is the denominator): \[ \frac{6x+5y}{3y-2x}=7 \;\Longrightarrow\; 6x+5y = 7(3y-2x). \] Hint: cross-multiplying removes the fraction; keep the bracket on the right.
2

Step 2 — Expand the right-hand side

\[ 6x+5y = 21y-14x. \] Hint: distribute the $7$ into each term of the bracket.
3

Step 3 — Collect like terms

Bring all $x$-terms to the left and all $y$-terms to the right: \[ 6x+14x = 21y-5y \;\Longrightarrow\; 20x = 16y. \] Hint: moving a term across the $=$ sign changes its sign.
4

Step 4 — Form the ratio $x:y$

Divide both sides by $20y$ (valid because $y\neq 0$): \[ \frac{20x}{20y}=\frac{16y}{20y} \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{x}{y}=\frac{16}{20}=\frac{4}{5}. \] Hence \[ x:y = 4:5. \] Hint: simplifying $16:20$ gives $4:5$ (both divided by 4).
Answer: A.   $x:y = 4:5$
