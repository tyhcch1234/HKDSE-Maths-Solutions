Step 1 — Reduce each power of $i$ by its cycle value.

HKDSE Math 2018 · Paper 2 · Q37

The real part of $\dfrac{2i^{12}+3i^{13}+4i^{14}+5i^{15}+6i^{16}}{1-i}$ is

A. $-3$
B. $-1$
C. $1$
D. $3$
Step 1 · Simplify the powers of $i$
Cycle of Powers of i \(i^{4k}=1,\quad i^{4k+1}=i,\quad i^{4k+2}=-1,\quad i^{4k+3}=-i\) The exponents \\(12\\) to \\(16\\) reduce to \\(1,i,-1,-i,1\\) because the pattern repeats every four steps.

Using the cycle $i^1=i,\;i^2=-1,\;i^3=-i,\;i^4=1$ (and the pattern repeats every 4):

$i^{12}=(i^4)^3=1^3=1$
$i^{13}=i^{12}\cdot i=1\cdot i=i$
$i^{14}=i^{12}\cdot i^2=1\cdot(-1)=-1$
$i^{15}=i^{12}\cdot i^3=1\cdot(-i)=-i$
$i^{16}=i^{12}\cdot i^4=1\cdot 1=1$

Hint: Reduce the exponent modulo 4 — the remainder tells you which of $i,-1,-i,1$ you get.
Step 2 · Substitute into the numerator

$2i^{12}=2(1)=2$
$3i^{13}=3(i)=3i$
$4i^{14}=4(-1)=-4$
$5i^{15}=5(-i)=-5i$
$6i^{16}=6(1)=6$

Sum the real parts and the imaginary parts separately:

$\dfrac{(2-4+6)+(3-5)i}{1-i}=\dfrac{4-2i}{1-i}$

Hint: Collect like terms: real numbers together, multiples of $i$ together.
Step 3 · Rationalise the denominator
Rationalising the Denominator \(\dfrac{a+bi}{c+di}\times\dfrac{c-di}{c-di}\) Multiplying numerator and denominator of \\(\\dfrac{4-2i}{1-i}\\) by the conjugate \\(1+i\\) makes the denominator real.

Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate $1+i$:

$\dfrac{4-2i}{1-i}\times\dfrac{1+i}{1+i}$

Hint: The conjugate of $1-i$ is $1+i$. This makes the denominator real.
Step 4 · Expand
Difference of Two Squares with i²=-1 \((1-i)(1+i)=1-i^{2}=2\) The conjugate pair multiplies to the real number \\(2\\), and the numerator becomes \\(6+2i\\).

Numerator: $(4-2i)(1+i)=4+4i-2i-2i^2=4+2i-2(-1)=6+2i$

Denominator: $(1-i)(1+i)=1-i^2=1-(-1)=2$

So $\dfrac{6+2i}{2}=3+i$

Hint: Remember $i^2=-1$ when expanding.
Step 5 · Identify the real part

The expression equals $3+i$, where the real part is $3$ and the imaginary part is $1$.

Hint: In $a+bi$, $a$ is the real part and $b$ is the imaginary part.
Answer: D  ($3$) The real part of the given complex expression is $3$.

Question. Find the real part of \\(\dfrac{3i^{8}+2i^{9}+5i^{10}}{2+i}\\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Reduce the powers: \\(i^{8}=1\\), \\(i^{9}=i\\), \\(i^{10}=-1\\), so the numerator is \\(3(1)+2i+5(-1)=-2+2i\\).
Step 2. Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate \\(2-i\\): \\(\\dfrac{(-2+2i)(2-i)}{(2+i)(2-i)}\\).
Step 3. \\((-2+2i)(2-i)=-4+2i+4i-2i^{2}=-2+6i\\) and \\((2+i)(2-i)=4+1=5\\).
Step 4. So the value is \\(\\dfrac{-2+6i}{5}=-\\dfrac{2}{5}+\\dfrac{6}{5}i\\), and the real part is \\(-\\dfrac{2}{5}\\).
Answer: \(-\dfrac{2}{5}\)
