Question

$PQRS$ is a regular tetrahedron. Find the angle between $PQ$ and the plane $\triangle QRS$ correct to the nearest degree.

  • A. $35^\circ$
  • B. $55^\circ$
  • C. $60^\circ$
  • D. $71^\circ$

Solution

Step 1 · Set up & the key triangle

Let each edge of the regular tetrahedron be $2x$. Let $T$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $P$ to the plane $\triangle QRS$.

The angle between the edge $PQ$ and the plane is the angle between $PQ$ and its projection $QT$ on the plane:

$$\text{required angle}=\angle PQT$$

In the right-angled triangle $PQT$ we have $\angle PTQ = 90^\circ$,
so $\cos\angle PQT=\dfrac{QT}{PQ}$.

Key idea: base $QRS$ is equilateral with side $2x$, so $T$ is the centroid of $\triangle QRS$ and $TQ$ is the projection of $PQ$.
Step 2 · Find the projection QT
Centroid of an Equilateral Triangle \(QT=\dfrac{2}{3}\times\text{median}\) \(T\) is the centroid of the base \(QRS\), so \(QV=x\) and \(\cos30^\circ=\dfrac{x}{QT}\) give \(QT=\dfrac{2x}{\sqrt3}\).

Let $V$ be the midpoint of $QS$. By symmetry $T$ lies on the median $RV$,
and $\angle TQV = \angle TQR = 30^\circ$ (since $T$ is the centroid).

With $QV = \dfrac{QS}{2}=x$ and the right angle at $V$ (in the base plane):

$$\cos 30^\circ=\frac{QV}{QT}=\frac{x}{QT}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad QT=\frac{x}{\cos 30^\circ}=\frac{2x}{\sqrt{3}}$$
Step 3 · Form cos∠PQT
Cosine Ratio \(\cos\angle PQT=\dfrac{QT}{PQ}\) \(\cos\angle PQT=\dfrac{2x/\sqrt3}{2x}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\), so the angle is about \(54.7^\circ\).

Now use the right-angled triangle $PQT$ (edge $PQ=2x$, projection $QT=\dfrac{2x}{\sqrt3}$):

$$\cos\angle PQT=\frac{QT}{PQ}=\frac{\dfrac{2x}{\sqrt3}}{2x}=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}$$
Step 4 · The angle to the nearest degree

Since $\cos\angle PQT=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$ and the angle is acute:

$$\angle PQT=\cos^{-1}\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\right)\approx 54.7^\circ$$
Rounded to the nearest degree: $\angle PQT = 55^\circ$  →  Answer: B

Check: the angle between an edge and a face of a regular tetrahedron is always $\approx 54.7356^\circ$.

Question. Each edge of a regular tetrahedron \(PQRS\) is \(2x\). Find the sine of the angle between the edge \(PQ\) and the plane \(QRS\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. With \(T\) the centroid of \(QRS\), the projection of \(PQ\) on the base is \(QT=\dfrac{2x}{\sqrt3}\).
Step 2. \(\cos\angle PQT=\dfrac{QT}{PQ}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\).
Step 3. \(\sin\angle PQT=\sqrt{1-\dfrac13}=\sqrt{\dfrac23}=\dfrac{\sqrt6}{3}\).
Answer: \(\dfrac{\sqrt6}{3}\)
