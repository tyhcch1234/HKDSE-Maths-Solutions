HKDSE 2013 · Paper 2 Q11 · Percentages

Compound Interest

Interest compounded monthly · 8% p.a. · 1 year

Q

A sum of $50\,000 is deposited at an interest of 8% per annum for 1 year, compounded monthly. Find the interest correct to the nearest dollar.

A. $4 000
B. $4 122
C. $4 143
D. $4 150

Concept tags: Compound interest · Percentage · Rounding

Hint: Interest is compounded monthly, so the annual rate must be split into monthly periods.

For compound interest, the amount accumulated is:

\[ A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} \]
  • \(P\) = principal = \$50 000
  • \(r\) = annual interest rate = 8% = 0.08
  • \(n\) = number of compounding periods per year = 12 (monthly)
  • \(t\) = time in years = 1
Important: The monthly interest rate is \(8\% \div 12\), not \(8\% \times 12\).

Plug in \(P = 50000\), \(r = 0.08\), \(n = 12\), \(t = 1\):

\[ A = 50000\left(1 + \frac{0.08}{12}\right)^{12 \times 1} \]

Note: \(0.08 \div 12 \approx 0.0066667\) per month.

Tip: Keep full precision until the final rounding step. Do not round intermediate values.
\[ \begin{aligned} A &= 50000\left(1 + \frac{0.08}{12}\right)^{12} \\[4pt] &= 50000\,(1.0066667)^{12} \\[4pt] &\approx 54149.975340 \end{aligned} \]

The question asks for the interest, not the total amount:

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Interest} &= A - P \\[4pt] &= 54149.975340 - 50000 \\[4pt] &= 4149.975340 \\[4pt] &\approx \$4150 \quad \text{(nearest dollar)} \end{aligned} \]
✔ Correct answer: D. $4 150
