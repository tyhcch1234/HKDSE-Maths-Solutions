HKDSE 2019 · Paper 2 · Q31

It is given that $\log_3 y$ is a linear function of $\log_3 x$.

The intercepts on the vertical axis and on the horizontal axis of the graph of the linear function are $7$ and $8$ respectively. Which of the following must be true?

  • A. $\;x^{4}y^{7}=3^{56}$
  • B. $\;x^{7}y^{4}=3^{56}$
  • C. $\;x^{7}y^{8}=3^{56}$
  • D. $\;x^{8}y^{7}=3^{56}$
1
Read off the two intercepts.
On the graph, the horizontal axis is $\log_3 x$ and the vertical axis is $\log_3 y$.
Vertical-axis intercept $=7$  ⟹  the point $(0,\,7)$ lies on the line, i.e. $$\log_3 x=0,\ \log_3 y=7 .$$ Horizontal-axis intercept $=8$  ⟹  the point $(8,\,0)$ lies on the line, i.e. $$\log_3 x=8,\ \log_3 y=0 .$$
Hint: intercepts are the two “anchor” points — mark them on the graph.
Point-Slope Form \(Y-Y_{1}=m(X-X_{1})\) With intercepts \((0,7)\) and \((8,0)\) the slope is \(-\dfrac{7}{8}\), giving \(\log_{3}y=-\dfrac{7}{8}\log_{3}x+7\).
2
Find the equation of the straight line.
Slope $=\dfrac{0-7}{8-0}=-\dfrac{7}{8}$. Using point $(0,7)$: $$\log_3 y-7=-\frac{7}{8}\big(\log_3 x-0\big)$$ $$\log_3 y=-\frac{7}{8}\log_3 x+7 .$$
Hint: $y$-intercept $=7$, slope $=-\dfrac{7}{8}$ gives the linear relation directly.
3
Clear the fraction.
Multiply the whole equation by $8$: $$8\log_3 y=-7\log_3 x+56 .$$ Bring every term to one side: $$7\log_3 x+8\log_3 y=56 .$$
Hint: getting integer coefficients makes the index form tidy.
Laws of Logarithms \(k\log_{3}a=\log_{3}a^{k},\qquad \log_{3}M+\log_{3}N=\log_{3}(MN)\) \(7\log_{3}x+8\log_{3}y=56\) becomes \(\log_{3}\left(x^{7}y^{8}\right)=56\).
4
Convert to a single logarithm.
Using $k\log_3 a=\log_3 a^{k}$: $$\log_3 x^{7}+\log_3 y^{8}=56$$ $$\log_3\!\big(x^{7}y^{8}\big)=56 .$$
Hint: coefficients $7$ and $8$ become the powers of $x$ and $y$.
Logarithmic Form \(\log_{3}A=k\ \Longleftrightarrow\ A=3^{k}\) So \(\log_{3}\left(x^{7}y^{8}\right)=56\) gives \(x^{7}y^{8}=3^{56}\).
5
Remove the logarithm.
Since $\log_3\!\big(x^{7}y^{8}\big)=56$, $$x^{7}y^{8}=3^{56}.$$ This matches option C.
Hint: $\log_3 A=k \Longleftrightarrow A=3^{k}$.
Answer: C  —  $x^{7}y^{8}=3^{56}$

Question. The graph of \(\log_{2}y\) against \(\log_{2}x\) is a straight line with vertical-axis intercept \(6\) and horizontal-axis intercept \(4\). Express the relation between \(x\) and \(y\) in the form \(x^{m}y^{n}=2^{k}\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The line passes through \((0,\,6)\) and \((4,\,0)\), so its slope is \(\dfrac{0-6}{4-0}=-\dfrac{3}{2}\).
Step 2. Using the intercept and slope: \(\log_{2}y=-\dfrac{3}{2}\log_{2}x+6\).
Step 3. Multiply by \(2\): \(2\log_{2}y=-3\log_{2}x+12\), i.e. \(3\log_{2}x+2\log_{2}y=12\).
Step 4. Use the power and product laws: \(\log_{2}x^{3}+\log_{2}y^{2}=\log_{2}\left(x^{3}y^{2}\right)=12\).
Step 5. Remove the logarithm: \(x^{3}y^{2}=2^{12}=4096\).
Answer: \(x^{3}y^{2}=2^{12}\)
