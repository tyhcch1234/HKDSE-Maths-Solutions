Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Equation of Straight Line

T12 Q25

Find the equation of the straight line passing through the point $(1, 2)$ with slope $4$.

Solution

Use the point-slope form $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$ with $(x_1,y_1)=(1,2)$ and $m=4$.
Substitute the values: $y-2=4(x-1)$.
Expand the right side: $y-2=4x-4$.
Add $2$ to both sides: $y=4x-2$.
Rearrange to general form: $4x-y-2=0$.
Check: at $x=1$, $y=4(1)-2=2$, so the line passes through $(1,2)$, and its slope is $4$.
The equation of the straight line is $y=4x-2$, equivalently $4x-y-2=0$.
