Equilateral Triangle in Polar Coordinates

Question

The polar coordinates of the points \( X \), \( Y \) and \( Z \) are \( \left(1,20^{\circ}\right) \), \( \left(2,80^{\circ}\right) \) and \( \left(r,\theta\right) \) respectively, where \( 20^{\circ} < \theta < 80^{\circ} \). If \( \triangle XYZ \) is an equilateral triangle, find \( r \).

  1. \( \sqrt{3} \)
  2. \( \sqrt{5} \)
  3. \( \sqrt{7} \)
  4. \( \sqrt{10} \)

Solution

STEP 1 · Side length of the equilateral triangle \( \angle XOY = 80^{\circ}-20^{\circ} = 60^{\circ} \). By the cosine rule in \( \triangle XOY \): \[ XY^{2} = 1^{2}+2^{2}-2(1)(2)\cos 60^{\circ} = 1+4-2 = 3 \implies XY = \sqrt{3}. \] So \( XZ = YZ = \sqrt{3} \) as well.
STEP 2 · Set up equations for \( Z = (r,\theta) \) Let \( u = \theta - 20^{\circ} \); then \( \theta - 80^{\circ} = u - 60^{\circ} \). \[ XZ^{2}: \quad r^{2}+1-2r\cos u = 3 \implies r^{2}-2 = 2r\cos u \quad (1) \] \[ YZ^{2}: \quad r^{2}+4-4r\cos(u-60^{\circ}) = 3 \implies r^{2}+1 = 4r\cos(u-60^{\circ}) \quad (2) \]
STEP 3 · Eliminate \( \cos u \) Expand (2) using \( \cos(u-60^{\circ}) = \tfrac{1}{2}\cos u + \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin u \): \[ r^{2}+1 = 2r\cos u + 2\sqrt{3}\,r\sin u. \] Substitute (1) (\( 2r\cos u = r^{2}-2 \)): \[ r^{2}+1 = r^{2}-2 + 2\sqrt{3}\,r\sin u \implies 2\sqrt{3}\,r\sin u = 3 \implies \sin u = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2r}. \] From (1): \( \cos u = \dfrac{r^{2}-2}{2r} \).
STEP 4 · Use \( \sin^{2}u + \cos^{2}u = 1 \) \[ \frac{3}{4r^{2}} + \frac{(r^{2}-2)^{2}}{4r^{2}} = 1 \implies 3 + (r^{2}-2)^{2} = 4r^{2}. \] Expanding: \[ r^{4} - 4r^{2} + 4 + 3 - 4r^{2} = 0 \implies r^{4} - 8r^{2} + 7 = 0 \] \[ (r^{2}-1)(r^{2}-7) = 0 \implies r = 1 \ \text{or}\ r = \sqrt{7}. \]
STEP 5 · Apply the restriction \( 20^{\circ} < \theta < 80^{\circ} \) - If \( r = 1 \): \( \cos u = \frac{1-2}{2} = -\tfrac{1}{2} \), \( \sin u = \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \implies u = 120^{\circ} \implies \theta = 140^{\circ} \) — rejected. - If \( r = \sqrt{7} \): \( \cos u = \frac{5}{2\sqrt{7}} \), \( \sin u = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2\sqrt{7}} \implies u \approx 19.1^{\circ} \implies \theta \approx 39.1^{\circ} \) — accepted.
Final Answer: C. \( r = \sqrt{7} \)
