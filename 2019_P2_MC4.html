Polynomials – Identity

Polynomials Identities Comparing Coefficients Substitution

Q Question

If \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are constants such that \[ (x-8)(x+\alpha)-6 \equiv (x-9)^2 + \beta, \] then \(\beta =\)

1 Method 1: By Substitution

Since the identity holds for all \(x\), choose \(x = 8\) to make the left side \((x-8)(x+\alpha)\) vanish.

\(x = 8\)

LHS: \((8-8)(8+\alpha)-6 = 0 - 6 = -6\)

RHS: \((8-9)^2 + \beta = (-1)^2 + \beta = 1 + \beta\)

\(-6 = 1 + \beta\)

\(\beta = -6 - 1 = -7\)

✅ \(\beta = -7\) (Option C)

💡 Hint: In an identity a(x) ≡ b(x), both sides are equal for any value of \(x\). Choose a value that simplifies the expression — here \(x=8\) eliminates the factor \((x-8)\).

2 Method 2: By Comparing Coefficients

\[ (x-8)(x+\alpha) - 6 = x^2 + \alpha x - 8x - 8\alpha - 6 \]

\[ = x^2 + (\alpha - 8)x - (8\alpha + 6) \]

\[ (x-9)^2 + \beta = x^2 - 18x + 81 + \beta \]

\[ = x^2 - 18x + (\beta + 81) \]

Since the two polynomials are identical:

Coefficient of \(x\): \(\alpha - 8 = -18\) … (1)

Constant term: \(-(8\alpha + 6) = \beta + 81\) … (2)

From (1): \(\alpha = -10\).

Substitute into (2):

\(-8(-10) - 6 = \beta + 81\)

\(80 - 6 = \beta + 81\)

\(74 = \beta + 81\)

\(\beta = -7\) ✅ (Option C)

📌 Key Takeaway

  • Substitution is fastest when one side has a factor that can become zero.
  • Comparing coefficients is systematic and works for all identities.
  • Both methods are valid — choose based on the given expression.

Answer: C. \(-7\)
