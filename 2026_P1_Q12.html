HKDSE 2026 · Paper 1 · Q12

Straight Lines, Perpendicular Bisector & Rhombus Area

Question

The straight line \(L\) passes through the point \((-10,11)\). The slope of \(L\) is \(-2\).

(a)   Find the equation of \(L\). (2 marks)

(b)   The coordinates of the points \(H\) and \(K\) are \((2,5)\) and \((-4,-7)\) respectively. Let \(P\) be a point lying on \(L\) such that \(P\) is equidistant from \(H\) and \(K\).

(i)  Find the coordinates of \(P\).

(ii)  Let \(Q\) be a point such that \(HPKQ\) is a quadrilateral, where \(HP=HQ=KQ\). Find the area of the quadrilateral \(HPKQ\). (5 marks)

Solution

PART (a) — equation of L
Using the point–slope form with point \((-10,11)\) and slope \(-2\): \[ y-11=-2(x+10) \implies y=-2x-9 \]
PART (b)(i) — equidistance condition
Let \(P=(x,y)\). From \(PH^2=PK^2\): \[ (x-2)^2+(y-5)^2=(x+4)^2+(y+7)^2 \] Expanding and cancelling \(x^2+y^2\): \[ -4x-10y+29=8x+14y+65 \implies 12x+24y=-36 \implies x+2y=-3 \] (This is the perpendicular bisector of \(HK\).)
PART (b)(i) — intersect with L
Substitute \(y=-2x-9\) into \(x+2y=-3\): \[ x+2(-2x-9)=-3 \implies -3x-18=-3 \implies x=-5 \] \[ y=-2(-5)-9=1 \] So \(P=(-5,\,1)\).  Check: \(PH^2=49+16=65=PK^2\) ✓
PART (b)(ii) — locate Q
\(Q\) is also at distance \(HP=\sqrt{65}\) from both \(H\) and \(K\), so \(Q\) also lies on \(x+2y=-3\). Let \(Q=(x,y)\) with \(x=-3-2y\). From \(HQ^2=65\): \[ (-5-2y)^2+(y-5)^2=65 \] \[ 5y^2+10y+50=65 \implies y^2+2y-3=0 \implies (y+3)(y-1)=0 \] \(y=1\) gives \(Q=P\) (rejected); \(y=-3\) gives \(x=3\). So \(Q=(3,\,-3)\).
PART (b)(ii) — area (rhombus shortcut)
All four sides equal \(\sqrt{65}\), so \(HPKQ\) is a rhombus; its diagonals \(HK\) and \(PQ\) are perpendicular. \[ HK=\sqrt{6^2+12^2}=6\sqrt5, \qquad PQ=\sqrt{8^2+4^2}=4\sqrt5 \] \[ \text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}\times HK\times PQ=\frac{1}{2}(6\sqrt5)(4\sqrt5)=60 \] (Soelace check with \((2,5),(-5,1),(-4,-7),(3,-3)\): \(\frac12|64-(-56)|=60\) ✓)
(a) \(y=-2x-9\)    (b)(i) \(P=(-5,\,1)\)    (b)(ii) Area \(=60\)
