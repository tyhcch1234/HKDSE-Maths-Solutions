HKDSE Math 2021 Paper 2 Q31 Topic 4: Polynomials

H.C.F. and L.C.M. of Algebraic Expressions

The H.C.F. and the L.C.M. of three expressions are

\(x^2 y^2 z\)  and  \(x^3 y^4 z^5\) respectively.

If the first expression and the second expression are

\(x^3 y^2 z^2\)  and  \(x^3 y^3 z^5\) respectively,

then the third expression is

🔑 Core Concept

For each variable base, the power in the H.C.F. is the minimum of the powers in the expressions, and the power in the L.C.M. is the maximum of the powers.

📘 Step-by-Step Guided Solution

Let the three expressions be \(E_1 = x^{a_1} y^{b_1} z^{c_1}\), \(E_2 = x^{a_2} y^{b_2} z^{c_2}\), \(E_3 = x^p y^q z^r\).

Then for each base (e.g. \(x\)):

  • Power in H.C.F. = \(\min(a_1, a_2, p)\)
  • Power in L.C.M. = \(\max(a_1, a_2, p)\)

💡 Hint: Since \(a_1, a_2\) are known, the min/max constraints reveal \(p\) exactly.

Given: \(E_1\) has \(x^3\), \(E_2\) has \(x^3\), H.C.F. has \(x^2\), L.C.M. has \(x^3\).

Let the third expression have \(x^p\).

H.C.F. condition

\(\min(3, 3, p) = 2\)

\(\Rightarrow p = 2\)

L.C.M. condition

\(\max(3, 3, p) = 3\)

\(\Rightarrow p \le 3\)

Both conditions are satisfied when \(p = 2\).

Given: \(E_1\) has \(y^2\), \(E_2\) has \(y^3\), H.C.F. has \(y^2\), L.C.M. has \(y^4\).

Let the third expression have \(y^q\).

H.C.F. condition

\(\min(2, 3, q) = 2\)

\(\Rightarrow q \ge 2\)

L.C.M. condition

\(\max(2, 3, q) = 4\)

\(\Rightarrow q = 4\)

Both conditions are satisfied when \(q = 4\).

Given: \(E_1\) has \(z^2\), \(E_2\) has \(z^5\), H.C.F. has \(z^1\), L.C.M. has \(z^5\).

Let the third expression have \(z^r\).

H.C.F. condition

\(\min(2, 5, r) = 1\)

\(\Rightarrow r = 1\)

L.C.M. condition

\(\max(2, 5, r) = 5\)

\(\Rightarrow r \le 5\)

Both conditions are satisfied when \(r = 1\).

Combining all results: \(p = 2\), \(q = 4\), \(r = 1\).

The third expression is

\(x^2 y^4 z\)

This corresponds to Option A.
