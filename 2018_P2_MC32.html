HKDSE Math 2018 Paper 2 Q32 Laws of Indices, Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

Logarithmic Graphs & Comparisons

Logarithms Graphs Change of Base Inequalities

The figure shows the graph of \(y = \log_a x\) and the graph of \(y = \log_b x\) on the same rectangular coordinate system, where \(a\) and \(b\) are positive constants. If a vertical line cuts the graph of \(y = \log_a x\), the graph of \(y = \log_b x\) and the \(x\)-axis at the points \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\) respectively, which of the following is/are true?

I. \(a > 1\)
II. \(a > b\)
III. \(\frac{AB}{BC} = \log_a \frac{b}{a}\)
A. I only B. II only C. I and III only D. II and III only

Interactive Graph Explorer

Drag the vertical line to change \(x = c\).
Observe: \(a > b > 1\) and the vertical order of curves.

Step-by-Step Solution

1

Analyse the shape of the curves

Both graphs are upward sloping (increasing). For a logarithmic function \(y = \log_a x\) to be increasing, the base must be greater than \(1\). Hence \(a > 1\) and \(b > 1\).

✅ Statement I is true.
💡 Hint: The graph of \(y = \log_a x\) is increasing if and only if \(a > 1\).
2

Compare the bases \(a\) and \(b\)

For any \(x > 1\), the graph of \(y = \log_a x\) lies above the graph of \(y = \log_b x\), so \(\log_a x > \log_b x\).

Pick \(x = b\) (where \(b > 1\)): \(\log_a b > \log_b b = 1\).

Hence \(b > a\).

❌ Statement II ( \(a > b\) ) is false.
💡 Hint: A larger base gives a smaller logarithm for \(x > 1\). So the upper curve has the smaller base.
3

Express \(AC\) and \(BC\) using coordinates

Let the vertical line be \(x = c\). Then:

  • \(A = (c,\, \log_a c)\) on \(y = \log_a x\)
  • \(B = (c,\, \log_b c)\) on \(y = \log_b x\)
  • \(C = (c,\, 0)\) on the \(x\)-axis

Using the change-of-base formula \(\log_p q = \frac{\log_c q}{\log_c p}\):

\(AC = \log_a c = \frac{1}{\log_c a}\) (1)
\(BC = \log_b c = \frac{1}{\log_c b}\) (2)
💡 Hint: Change of base: \(\log_a x = \frac{\log_c x}{\log_c a}\).
4

Derive the ratio \(\frac{AB}{BC}\)

Dividing (1) by (2):

\(\displaystyle \frac{AC}{BC} = \frac{\frac{1}{\log_c a}}{\frac{1}{\log_c b}} = \frac{\log_c b}{\log_c a} = \log_a b\).

Now:

\(\displaystyle \frac{AB}{BC} = \frac{AC - BC}{BC} = \frac{AC}{BC} - 1 = \log_a b - 1 = \log_a b - \log_a a = \log_a \frac{b}{a}\).
✅ Statement III is true.
💡 Hint: Use \(\log_a a = 1\) and \(\log_a m - \log_a n = \log_a \frac{m}{n}\).

✅ Only statements I and III are true.

Therefore, the answer is C. I and III only.
