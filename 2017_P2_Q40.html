2017 MC #40
In the figure, $O$ is the centre of the circle $ABC$. $DE$ is the tangent to the circle at $A$. If $\angle BAD = 68^\circ$ and $\angle BCO = 26^\circ$, then $\angle ABC =$
A. $42^\circ$    B. $48^\circ$    C. $54^\circ$    D. $64^\circ$
💡 Key facts: a tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact, and radii of the same circle are equal — so $\triangle ABO$ and $\triangle BCO$ are isosceles.

Step 1 · Tangent ⊥ Radius at A

Tangent Perpendicular to Radius \(DE\ \text{tangent at }A\ \Longrightarrow\ \angle DAO=90^{\circ}\) \(OA\) is a radius to the point of contact \(A\), so the tangent \(DE\) is perpendicular to it.
Since $DE$ is tangent at $A$, it meets the radius $OA$ at a right angle: $$\angle DAO = 90^\circ \quad (\text{tangent} \perp \text{radius})$$

Step 2 · Find ∠BAO

Along the straight line $DA\!A\!E$, the right angle is split by chord $AB$: $$\angle BAO = \angle DAO - \angle BAD = 90^\circ - 68^\circ = 22^\circ$$

Step 3 · Isosceles △ABO

Base Angles of an Isosceles Triangle \(OA=OB\ \Longrightarrow\ \angle ABO=\angle BAO\) Both \(OA\) and \(OB\) are radii, so \(\angle ABO=\angle BAO=22^{\circ}\); likewise \(\angle CBO=\angle BCO=26^{\circ}\).
$OB=OA$ (radii), so the base angles are equal: $$\angle ABO = \angle BAO = 22^\circ \quad (\text{base } \angle\text{s, isos. } \triangle)$$

Step 4 · Isosceles △BCO

$OB=OC$ (radii), so with $\angle BCO = 26^\circ$: $$\angle CBO = \angle BCO = 26^\circ \quad (\text{base } \angle\text{s, isos. } \triangle)$$

Step 5 · Combine at vertex B

The angle at $B$ splits into the two isosceles base angles: $$\angle ABC = \angle ABO + \angle CBO = 22^\circ + 26^\circ = 48^\circ$$
Answer:   B. $48^\circ$

Question. In the figure, \(O\) is the centre of the circle \(ABC\) and \(DE\) is the tangent to the circle at \(A\). If \(\angle BAD=74^{\circ}\) and \(\angle BCO=21^{\circ}\), find \(\angle ABC\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Since \(DE\) is tangent at \(A\), \(\angle DAO=90^{\circ}\).
Step 2. Along the straight line \(DAE\): \(\angle BAO=\angle DAO-\angle BAD=90^{\circ}-74^{\circ}=16^{\circ}\).
Step 3. \(OA=OB\) (radii), so \(\triangle ABO\) is isosceles and \(\angle ABO=\angle BAO=16^{\circ}\).
Step 4. \(OB=OC\) (radii), so \(\angle CBO=\angle BCO=21^{\circ}\).
Step 5. \(\angle ABC=\angle ABO+\angle CBO=16^{\circ}+21^{\circ}=37^{\circ}\).
Answer: \(\angle ABC=37^{\circ}\)
