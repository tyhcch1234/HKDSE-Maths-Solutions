HKDSE 2014 Paper 1 · Q1 — Laws of Indices

[3 marks]

Simplify \[ \frac{(xy^{-2})^{3}}{y^{4}} \] and express your answer with positive indices.

1Remove the bracket — power of a product
\[(xy^{-2})^{3}=x^{1\times 3}\,y^{-2\times 3}=x^{3}y^{-6}\]
Multiply the outer exponent into every factor inside the bracket: $(ab)^n=a^nb^n$.
2Divide the powers of $y$ — quotient law
\[\frac{x^{3}y^{-6}}{y^{4}}=x^{3}\,y^{-6-4}=x^{3}y^{-10}\]
$\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}$: subtract the indices of the same base.
3Write with positive indices
\[x^{3}y^{-10}=x^{3}\cdot\frac{1}{y^{10}}=\frac{x^{3}}{y^{10}}\]
$a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^{n}}$, so move every negative index to the denominator.
Answer \[\frac{(xy^{-2})^{3}}{y^{4}}=\frac{x^{3}}{y^{10}}\]
