Base-16 (Hexadecimal) Arithmetic

Question

\( 14\times16^{15}+17\times16^{14}+16^{2}+17 = \)

  1. E1010000000000002116
  2. F1000000000000001116
  3. E11000000000000002116
  4. F10000000000000001116
Note: the option strings above are transcribed from the scanned paper; the exact number of internal zeros in each printed option could not be fully verified from the scan, so the matching letter is marked TBC below.

Solution

STEP 1 · Combine the two large terms Since \( 17\times16^{14} = 16^{15}+16^{14} \), \[ 14\times16^{15}+17\times16^{14} = 15\times16^{15}+1\times16^{14}. \] In hexadecimal, \( 15 = \texttt{F} \), so the leading digits are \( \texttt{F1} \), and the result is a 16-digit base-16 number.
STEP 2 · The small terms \[ 16^{2}+17 = 256+17 = 273 = 1\times16^{2}+1\times16+1, \] which occupies the last three base-16 places, i.e. the trailing digits \( \texttt{111} \).
STEP 3 · Assemble the answer \[ 14\times16^{15}+17\times16^{14}+16^{2}+17 = \texttt{F100000000000111}_{16}. \]
Computed value: \( \texttt{F100000000000111}_{16} \) — the 16-digit option beginning with \( \texttt{F1} \).
Correct option: TBC (option letters A–D as printed could not be fully distinguished from the scan; the computed value above is unambiguous).
