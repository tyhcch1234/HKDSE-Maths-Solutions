Base-16 (Hexadecimal) Arithmetic

Question

\( 14\times16^{15}+17\times16^{14}+16^{2}+17 = \)

  1. E1010000000000002116
  2. F1000000000000001116
  3. E11000000000000002116
  4. F10000000000000001116
Note: the option strings above are transcribed from the scanned paper; the exact number of internal zeros in each printed option could not be fully verified from the scan, so the matching letter is marked TBC below.

Solution

STEP 1 · Combine the two large terms
Place Value in Base 16 \(N=d_{k}16^{k}+d_{k-1}16^{k-1}+\cdots+d_{1}16+d_{0}\) Rewriting \(17\times16^{14}=16^{15}+16^{14}\) leaves \(14\) blocks of \(16^{15}\) and one of \(16^{14}\).
Since \( 17\times16^{14} = 16^{15}+16^{14} \), \[ 14\times16^{15}+17\times16^{14} = 15\times16^{15}+1\times16^{14}. \] In hexadecimal, \( 15 = \texttt{F} \), so the leading digits are \( \texttt{F1} \),
and the result is a 16-digit base-16 number.
STEP 2 · The small terms
Hexadecimal Digits \(10=\texttt{A},\ 11=\texttt{B},\ 12=\texttt{C},\ 13=\texttt{D},\ 14=\texttt{E},\ 15=\texttt{F}\) The remainder \(16^{2}+17=273=1\times16^{2}+1\times16+1\) occupies the last three places as \(\texttt{111}\).
\[ 16^{2}+17 = 256+17 = 273 = 1\times16^{2}+1\times16+1, \] which occupies the last three base-16 places,
i.e. the trailing digits \( \texttt{111} \).
STEP 3 · Assemble the answer \[ 14\times16^{15}+17\times16^{14}+16^{2}+17 = \texttt{F100000000000111}_{16}. \]
Computed value: \( \texttt{F100000000000111}_{16} \) — the 16-digit option beginning with \( \texttt{F1} \).
Correct option: TBC (option letters A–D as printed could not be fully distinguished from the scan; the computed value above is unambiguous).

Question. Express \(13\times16^{15}+18\times16^{14}+16^{2}+19\) as a hexadecimal number.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Since \(18\times16^{14}=16^{15}+2\times16^{14}\), the two large terms become \(14\times16^{15}+2\times16^{14}\).
Step 2. In base \(16\) the digit \(14\) is \(\texttt{E}\) and the digit \(2\) is \(2\),
so the number begins \(\texttt{E2}\) and has \(16\) digits in total.
Step 3. The small terms give \(16^{2}+19=256+19=275=1\times16^{2}+1\times16+3\), i.e. the trailing digits \(\texttt{113}\).
Step 4. Filling the eleven empty places with zeros: \(13\times16^{15}+18\times16^{14}+16^{2}+19=\texttt{E200000000000113}_{16}\).
Answer: \(\texttt{E200000000000113}_{16}\)
