Percentage Profit & Marked Price

Q6

The cost of a book is $250. The book is now sold and the percentage profit is 20%.

(a) Find the selling price of the book.

(b) If the book is sold at a discount of 25% on its marked price, find the marked price of the book.

[4 marks]

📘 Step-by-Step Guided Solution

Click each step to reveal the working. Try to think about the next move before you peek!

1 Understand the relationship between cost & profit
▶

The cost price is the base value. A 20% profit means the selling price is 120% of the cost price.

💡 Hint: Profit is always calculated on the cost price unless stated otherwise.
\(\text{Selling Price} = \text{Cost Price} \times (1 + \text{Profit}\%)\)
2 (a) Calculate the selling price
▶

Substitute the cost price and profit percentage:

\(\begin{aligned} \text{Selling Price} &= 250(1 + 20\%) \\ &= 250 \times 1.2 \\ &= 300 \end{aligned}\)

∴ The selling price of the book is $300.

💡 Hint: \(1 + 20\% = 1 + 0.2 = 1.2\)
3 (b) Set up the marked price equation
▶

Let the marked price be $x$.

A 25% discount on the marked price means the customer pays 75% of the marked price. This selling price is $300.

\((1 - 25\%)x = 300\)
💡 Hint: Discount reduces the price. After a 25% discount, you pay \(100\% - 25\% = 75\%\) of the marked price.
4 (b) Solve for the marked price
▶
\(\begin{aligned} 0.75x &= 300 \\ x &= \frac{300}{0.75} \\ x &= 400 \end{aligned}\)

∴ The marked price of the book is $400.

💡 Hint: Dividing by 0.75 is the same as multiplying by \(\frac{4}{3}\).
5 Quick verification
▶

Check part (b): 25% discount on $400 = $100 discount → selling price = $400 − $100 = $300 ✓

This matches the selling price from part (a).

💡 Hint: Always check that your final answer is consistent with all conditions.

📋 Key Takeaways

  • Profit % is based on cost price: \(\text{SP} = \text{CP}(1 + \text{profit}\%)\).
  • Discount % is based on marked price: \(\text{SP} = \text{MP}(1 - \text{discount}\%)\).
  • When solving, clearly define your variable (e.g., let \(x\) be the marked price).
  • Always state the final answer with the correct unit ($).
