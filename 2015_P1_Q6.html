HKDSE 2015 · Paper 1 · Q6 · Percentages

Question

The cost of a book is $\$250$. The book is now sold and the percentage profit is $20\%$.

(a) Find the selling price of the book.

(b) If the book is sold at a discount of $25\%$ on its marked price, find the marked price of the book.

[4 marks]

1

(a) Step 1 — Identify the given information

Percentage Profit \(\text{percentage profit}=\dfrac{\text{selling price}-\text{cost price}}{\text{cost price}}\times 100\%\) The \(20\%\) profit is measured on the cost price \(\$250\), not on the selling price.

Cost price $C = \$250$, percentage profit $= 20\%$.

Profit is calculated on the cost price, not on the selling price.

Hint: “Percentage profit” always means profit ÷ cost price × 100%.

2

(a) Step 2 — Compute the selling price

Selling Price after a Percentage Increase \(SP=CP\,(1+r\%)\) A \(20\%\) increase on \(\$250\) gives \(250 imes 1.2=\$300\).

Selling price $= C\,(1 + 20\%)$

$= 250 \times 1.2 = \boxed{\$300}$

Multiply by $1.2$ (a 20% increase) rather than adding 20% of 250 separately — this avoids rounding slips.

3

(b) Step 3 — Set up the discount equation

Discount on the Marked Price \(SP=MP\,(1-d\%)\) The \(25\%\) discount is taken on the marked price \(x\), so \(0.75x=300\).

Let $x$ be the marked price of the book.

The selling price equals the marked price reduced by $25\%$:

$(1 - 25\%)\,x = 300 \;\Rightarrow\; 0.75x = 300$

Discount is calculated on the marked price, so the discount factor is applied to $x$.

4

(b) Step 4 — Solve for the marked price

$$x = \frac{300}{75\%} = \frac{300}{0.75} = 400$$

Therefore the marked price of the book is $\$400$.

Check: 75% of $400 = $300 ✓ which matches the selling price found in (a).

Answer:  (a) Selling price $= \$300$  ·  (b) Marked price $= \$400$

Question. The cost of a toy is \(\$180\). It is sold at a percentage profit of \(25\%\). (a) Find the selling price of the toy. (b) If the toy is sold at a discount of \(10\%\) on its marked price, find the marked price. [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Selling price \(=CP(1+25\%)=180\times 1.25=\$225\).
Step 2. Let the marked price be \(x\). After a \(10\%\) discount the selling price is \(0.9x\).
Step 3. \(0.9x=225\), so \(x=\dfrac{225}{0.9}=250\).
Step 4. Check: \(90\%\) of \(\$250=\$225\), which matches the selling price.
Answer: (a) \(\$225\); (b) \(\$250\)
