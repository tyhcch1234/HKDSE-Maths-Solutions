Standard Score & Standard Deviation

Question

In a test, the difference of the test scores and the difference of the standard scores of two students are 30 marks and 6 respectively. In the test, the standard deviation of the test scores is

  1. 5 marks.
  2. 24 marks.
  3. 25 marks.
  4. 36 marks.

Solution

STEP 1 · Standard-score formula
Standard Score \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\) A standard score counts how many standard deviations a score lies from the mean.
If the test scores are \( x_{1},x_{2} \) with mean \( \mu \) and standard deviation \( \sigma \),
the standard scores are \[ z_{i}=\frac{x_{i}-\mu}{\sigma}. \]
STEP 2 · Take the difference
Difference of Standard Scores \(z_{1}-z_{2}=\dfrac{x_{1}-x_{2}}{\sigma}\) The mean cancels in the subtraction, so \(z_{1}-z_{2}=\dfrac{x_{1}-x_{2}}{\sigma}\).
\[ z_{1}-z_{2}=\frac{x_{1}-x_{2}}{\sigma}. \] Substituting the given differences (larger score taken first): \( z_{1}-z_{2}=6 \) and \( x_{1}-x_{2}=30 \): \[ 6=\frac{30}{\sigma}. \]
STEP 3 · Solve for \( \sigma \) \[ \sigma=\frac{30}{6}=5. \]
Final Answer: A. 5 marks

Question. The standard scores of two test scores \(x_{1}\) and \(x_{2}\) in a test are \(z_{1}\) and \(z_{2}\) respectively. The difference of their standard scores is \(4\) and the difference of their test scores is \(36\). The standard deviation of the test scores is A. \(4\) B. \(6\) C. \(9\) D. \(12\) [1 mark]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Standard score: \(z_{i}=\dfrac{x_{i}-\mu}{\sigma}\).
Step 2. Subtract: \(z_{1}-z_{2}=\dfrac{x_{1}-x_{2}}{\sigma}\), so the difference of the standard scores is the difference of the scores divided by \(\sigma\).
Step 3. \(4=\dfrac{36}{\sigma}\), hence \(\sigma=\dfrac{36}{4}=9\).
Step 4. Check: two scores \(36\) apart are \(\dfrac{36}{9}=4\) standard deviations apart \u2713.
Answer: C. \(9\)
