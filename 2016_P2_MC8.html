Q8 · Quadratic Equation with Equal Roots

MC
Quadratic Equations Discriminant Δ Equal Roots Factorization

? Question

If \( k \) is a constant such that the quadratic equation

\( x^2 + kx + (8k + 36) = 0 \)

has equal roots, then \( k = \)

A 6
B 12
C 4 or 36
D 18 or 2

✦ Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1 of 4
1

Recall the condition for equal roots

A quadratic equation \( ax^2 + bx + c = 0 \) has equal roots when its discriminant \( \Delta \) is zero.

\( \Delta = b^2 - 4ac = 0 \)
💡 Hint: Identify \( a \), \( b \), and \( c \) from the given equation: \( a = 1 \), \( b = k \), \( c = 8k + 36 \).
2

Substitute into the discriminant

Substitute \( a = 1 \), \( b = k \), and \( c = 8k + 36 \) into \( \Delta = 0 \):

\( k^2 - 4(1)(8k + 36) = 0 \)

↓

\( k^2 - 32k - 144 = 0 \)

💡 Hint: Be careful with signs: \( -4 \times 36 = -144 \).
3

Factorize the quadratic in \( k \)

We need two numbers that multiply to \( -144 \) and add to \( -32 \).

\( (k + 4)(k - 36) = 0 \)

💡 Hint: Check: \( 4 \times (-36) = -144 \) and \( 4 + (-36) = -32 \). ✔️
4

Solve for \( k \)

Set each factor to zero:

\( k + 4 = 0 \)   or   \( k - 36 = 0 \)

\( k = -4 \)   or   \( k = 36 \)

✔️ Final Answer: \( k = 4 \) or \( 36 \) — matches option C.

★ Key Takeaway

  • For equal roots, set the discriminant \( \Delta = b^2 - 4ac = 0 \).
  • Be careful when substituting signs — a small error changes the answer completely.
  • Always factorize or use the quadratic formula to solve for the unknown constant.
  • Check both values of \( k \) satisfy the original condition (both do here).
