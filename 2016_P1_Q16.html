Statistics — Standard Score, Mean & Range of a Distribution

Standard Score Mean Range Reasoning & Justification

Q The Question

Read carefully — the key words are “at most 59 marks.”

In a test, the mean of the distribution of the scores of a class of students is \(61\) marks. The standard scores of Albert and Mary are \(-2.6\) and \(1.4\) respectively. Albert gets \(22\) marks. A student claims that the range of the distribution is at most \(59\) marks. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer.

[3 marks]

★ Summary of Answers

  • The standard deviation of the distribution is \(\sigma = 15\) marks.
  • Mary’s score is \(61 + 1.4(15) = 82\) marks.
  • The difference between Mary’s score and Albert’s score is \(82 - 22 = 60\) marks.
  • Since the range \(\ge\) the difference between any two scores, the range is at least \(60\) marks \(> 59\) marks.
  • Hence the claim is incorrect.
