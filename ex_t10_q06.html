Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Area

T10 Q06

Find the area of a square with side length $11$ cm.

Solution

The area of a square is given by $A = s^2$, where $s$ is the side length.
Substitute $s = 11$ cm: $A = 11^2$.
Calculate: $A = 121$.
The area of the square is $121\text{ cm}^2$.
