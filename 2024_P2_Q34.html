Question

2024MC#34

The graph in the figure shows the linear relation between $y^3$ and $\sqrt{x}$. If $x=36$, then $y=$

A. $-64$
B. $-16$
C. $-8$
D. $-4$

Step-by-step Solution

STEP 1 · Identify the two intercepts

From the graph, the straight line crosses the vertical axis ($\sqrt{x}=0$) at $y^3=32$, and the horizontal axis ($y^3=0$) at $\sqrt{x}=2$.

So the two points on the line are: $(0,\,32)$ and $(2,\,0)$.

STEP 2 · Find the slope

Using the two intercepts,

$$\text{slope}=\frac{0-32}{2-0}=\frac{-32}{2}=-16.$$

This matches the stated slope of the graph, $-16$.

STEP 3 · Write the equation (slope-intercept form)

With slope $m=-16$ and vertical intercept $c=32$:

$$y^3 = -16\sqrt{x} + 32.$$
STEP 4 · Substitute $x=36$

When $x=36$, $\sqrt{x}=\sqrt{36}=6$. Hence

$$y^3 = -16(6)+32 = -96+32 = -64.$$
STEP 5 · Solve for $y$

Since $y^3=-64=(-4)^3$, we obtain $y=-4$.

HINT · Avoid the trap

A common mistake is to stop at $y^3=-64$ and answer $-64$ (choice A). Remember the question asks for $y$, not $y^3$ — you must take the cube root.

ANSWER D. $\;y=-4$
