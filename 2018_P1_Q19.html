Question

The coordinates of the centre of the circle \(C\) are \((8,2)\). Denote the radius of \(C\) by \(r\). Let \(L\) be the straight line \(kx-5y-21=0\), where \(k\) is a constant. It is given that \(L\) is a tangent to \(C\).

(a) Find the equation of \(C\) in terms of \(r\). Hence, express \(r^2\) in terms of \(k\). 4 marks

(b) \(L\) passes through the point \(D(18,39)\). (i) Find \(r\). (ii) It is given that \(L\) cuts the \(y\)-axis at the point \(E\). Let \(F\) be a point such that \(C\) is the inscribed circle of \(\triangle DEF\). Is \(\triangle DEF\) an obtuse-angled triangle? Explain your answer. 8 marks

Solution

a(a) Tangency
\[C:(x-8)^2+(y-2)^2=r^2\]

1A correct.

Putting \(y=\frac{kx-21}{5}\) into \(C\):

\[(x-8)^2+\left(\tfrac{kx-21}{5}-2\right)^2=r^2\]

1M substituting.

\[(k^2+25)x^2+(-62k-400)x+(2561-25r^2)=0\]

Since \(L\) is tangent, the discriminant is 0:

\[(-62k-400)^2-4(k^2+25)(2561-25r^2)=0\]

1M discriminant = 0.

\[r^2=\frac{64k^2-496k+961}{k^2+25}\]

1A correct.

b(b)(i) Find r

Since \(L\) passes through \(D\), \(18k-5(39)-21=0\), so \(k=12\).

1M k=12.

\[r^2=\frac{64(12)^2-496(12)+961}{12^2+25}=25\ \Rightarrow\ r=5\]

1M substituting.1A r=5.

b(b)(ii) Obtuse?

Let \(G=(8,2)\) be the centre (the in-centre of \(\triangle DEF\)). Also \(E=(0,-\frac{21}{5})\).

1M coordinates of E.

\[DG=\sqrt{10^2+37^2}=\sqrt{1469},\qquad \sin\angle EDG=\frac{r}{DG}=\frac{5}{\sqrt{1469}}\ \Rightarrow\ \angle EDG\approx7.50^\circ\]

1M DG.1M angle EDG.

\[EG=\sqrt{8^2+(2+\frac{21}{5})^2}=\frac{\sqrt{2561}}{5},\qquad \sin\angle DEG=\frac{25}{\sqrt{2561}}\ \Rightarrow\ \angle DEG\approx29.60^\circ\]

Since \(G\) is the in-centre, \(\angle EDG=\angle FDG\) and \(\angle DEG=\angle FEG\).

1M equal angles.

\[\angle DFE\approx180^\circ-2(7.50^\circ)-2(29.60^\circ)=105.80^\circ>90^\circ\]

Thus \(\triangle DEF\) is obtuse-angled.

1A correct.

Answer

Answer(a) \(r^2=\frac{64k^2-496k+961}{k^2+25}\) · (b)(i) 5 · (ii) obtuse
