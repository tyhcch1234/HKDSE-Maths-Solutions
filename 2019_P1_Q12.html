HKDSE 2019 · Paper 1 · Q12

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the results (in seconds) of some boys in a 400 m race.

Stem (tens) Leaf (units)
5$a$
6$0\ \ 0\ \ 3\ \ c\ \ c\ \ 8\ \ 9\ \ 9\ \ 9$
7$0\ \ 1\ \ 1\ \ 1\ \ 2\ \ 2\ \ 5\ \ 6\ \ 9$
8$b$

It is given that the inter-quartile range of the distribution is 8 seconds.

(a) Find $c$. [2 marks]

(b) It is given that the range of the distribution exceeds 34 seconds and the mean of the distribution is 69 seconds. Find

(i) $a$ and $b$, [6 marks]

(ii) the least possible standard deviation of the distribution.

STEP 1 · Read the data

List the results in order. Total number of boys:

Stem 5: $1$ value, Stem 6: $9$ values, Stem 7: $9$ values, Stem 8: $1$ value.

$$n = 1 + 3 + 2 + 3 + 9 + 1 = 20$$

Hint: counting the leaves gives the data size needed for the quartile positions.

STEP 2 · Locate the quartiles

For $n=20$, the median is the average of the $10$th and $11$th values; $Q_1$ is the $5$th value and $Q_3$ is the $16$th value (using $\tfrac14(n)$ position convention for continuous data — here we use the standard HKDSE positions $5$, $10/11$, $16$).

Ordered list (with placeholders):

$$a,\; 60,60,63,c,c,68,69,69,69,\;70,71,71,71,72,72,75,76,79,\;b$$

Hint: the two $c$'s are two separate leaves, so they occupy two positions.

STEP 3 · (a) Use the IQR

Inter-quartile range $=Q_3-Q_1=8$.

From the ordered list, $Q_1=60+c$ (the $5$th value) and $Q_3=72$ (the $16$th value).

$$72-(60+c)=8$$ $$12-c=8 \quad\Rightarrow\quad c=4$$

Hint: $Q_3$ lies in the stem-7 row; $Q_1$ is in the stem-6 row.

STEP 4 · (b)(i) Use the range

The range exceeds 34 seconds: largest $-$ smallest $>34$.

$$(80+b)-(50+a) > 34$$ $$30 + b - a > 34$$ $$b - a > 4 \quad\cdots(1)$$

Hint: the smallest value is $50+a$, the largest is $80+b$.

STEP 5 · Use the mean

Sum of all $20$ values, with $c=4$:

$50+a+60+60+63+64+64+68+69+69+69+70+71+71+71+72+72+75+76+79+80+b$

$$\Rightarrow \frac{1290+a+b}{20}=69 \quad(\text{check: } a+b=90)$$

Wait — recompute the numeric sum: $60+60+63+64+64+68+69+69+69 = 586$; $70+71+71+71+72+72+75+76+79 = 657$; so $586+657=1243$. Then $50+a+1243+80+b = 1373 + a + b$.

$$\frac{1373+a+b}{20}=69 \;\Rightarrow\; a+b=1380-1373=7$$

With $b-a>4$ and $a+b=7$: possible $a=0,b=7$ or $a=1,b=6$.

Hint: solve the two equations together, keeping $a,b$ as digits.

STEP 6 · (b)(ii) Least standard deviation

Two cases:

  • Case 1: $a=0,\;b=7$ → data $50,60,\dots,87$ — std dev $\approx 7.5829$ s.
  • Case 2: $a=1,\;b=6$ → data $51,60,\dots,86$ — std dev $\approx 7.3417$ s.

The smaller spread occurs in Case 2, so the least possible standard deviation is about 7.34 s.

Hint: a smaller range tends to give a smaller standard deviation here.

Answers:  (a) $c=4$;  (b)(i) $a=0,b=7$ or $a=1,b=6$;  (b)(ii) least standard deviation $\approx 7.34$ seconds.
