Functions and Graphs — Using equal function values to find an unknown constant
Quadratic Function f(a) = f(b) Axis of Symmetry Simultaneous Equations Multiple Choice

Question Full Question Text

Read carefully. This is a Paper 2 multiple-choice question — use the properties of the graph to save time.

Let \( f(x) = (x+h)(x-3) + k \), where \( h \) and \( k \) are constants.

If \( f(0) = f(8) = 1 \), find \( k \).

A \( -14 \)
B \( -5 \)
C \( 20 \)
D \( 31 \)
Key idea: \( f(0)=f(8) \) means the points \( (0,1) \) and \( (8,1) \) lie on the parabola and have the same height. By symmetry, the axis of symmetry is the vertical line half-way between them: \( x = \dfrac{0+8}{2} = 4 \).

Part 1 Interpreting \( f(0) = f(8) = 1 \)

Explore the parabola. Drag the slider to change the vertical shift \( k \) and watch how the curve moves. The two marked points and the axis of symmetry help you see the symmetry idea.

1M Substitute \( x = 0 \) into the function
\[ f(0) = (0+h)(0-3) + k = -3h + k \]
Hint: Replace every \( x \) by \( 0 \). The constants \( h \) and \( k \) stay as unknowns.
Equation (1): \( -3h + k = 1 \)
2
1M Substitute \( x = 8 \) into the function
\[ f(8) = (8+h)(8-3) + k = (8+h)(5) + k = 40 + 5h + k \]
Hint: Expand carefully: \( 5(8+h) = 40 + 5h \). Then set it equal to the given value \( 1 \).
Equation (2): \( 5h + k = -39 \)
3
1A Solve the simultaneous equations
\[ \begin{cases} -3h + k = 1 & \text{(1)}\\[4pt] 5h + k = -39 & \text{(2)} \end{cases} \] Subtract (1) from (2): \[ 8h = -40 \quad\Rightarrow\quad h = -5 \] Substitute \( h=-5 \) into (1): \[ -3(-5) + k = 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 15 + k = 1 \;\Rightarrow\; k = -14 \]
Hint: Since both equations contain \( k \), subtracting eliminates \( k \) immediately — very fast for an MCQ.
\( h = -5, \quad k = -14 \)
4
1A Check and choose the option
With \( h=-5, k=-14 \): \[ f(x) = (x-5)(x-3) - 14 \] \[ f(0) = (-5)(-3) - 14 = 15 - 14 = 1 \; \checkmark \] \[ f(8) = (3)(5) - 14 = 15 - 14 = 1 \; \checkmark \]
Option A  (\( k = -14 \))

Part 2 Faster Method — Axis of Symmetry

Because \( f(0) = f(8) \), the axis of symmetry must be the mid-line \( x = 4 \). For a parabola written as \( f(x) = (x+h)(x-3)+k \), the vertex (and hence the axis) lies midway between the roots \( x=-h \) and \( x=3 \).

-5
Axis of symmetry: x = 4
Midpoint of roots: 4
Distance \(f(0)\)–\(f(8)\): 8
Set \(k\) to make \( f(0)=f(8)=1\): k = -14
1
1M Use symmetry: axis is mid-way between \(x=0\) and \(x=8\)
Since \( f(0) = f(8) \), the axis of symmetry of the parabola is the vertical line \[ x = \frac{0+8}{2} = 4 \]
Hint: Equal function values at two points mean those points are mirror images of each other about the axis. The axis is always exactly midway between them.
2
1M Relate the axis to the form \( (x+h)(x-3) \)
The roots of \( (x+h)(x-3) = 0 \) are \( x = -h \) and \( x = 3 \). The axis of symmetry passes through their midpoint: \[ a = \frac{-h + 3}{2} \]
Hint: For a quadratic with roots \(r_1, r_2\), the axis of symmetry is \( x = \dfrac{r_1+r_2}{2} \). Here the roots are \(-h\) and \(3\).
3
1A Set the two expressions for the axis equal
From Step 1 the axis is \( x = 4 \); from Step 2 it is \( x = \dfrac{-h+3}{2} \). Equate: \[ \frac{-h+3}{2} = 4 \] \[ -h + 3 = 8 \] \[ -h = 5 \quad\Rightarrow\quad h = -5 \]
Hint: Now \( h \) is known, so substitute it back into \( f(0)=1 \) to get \( k \) in one line.
\( h = -5 \)
4
1A Find \( k \) using \( f(0)=1 \)
\[ f(0) = (0-5)(0-3) + k = 1 \] \[ 15 + k = 1 \] \[ k = -14 \]
Hint: This avoids solving simultaneous equations — often the quickest way in an MCQ.
\( k = -14 \)  ⇒  Option A
