T14 Q19

Two fair dice are thrown. Find the number of ways to get a sum of at least 10.

Solution

Treat the two dice as distinct, so each outcome is an ordered pair $(a,b)$ with $1\le a,b\le 6$.
Count outcomes with sum at least $10$, i.e. sums $10$, $11$, or $12$.
Sum $10$: $(4,6),(5,5),(6,4)$, so there are $3$ ways.
Sum $11$: $(5,6),(6,5)$, so there are $2$ ways.
Sum $12$: $(6,6)$, so there is $1$ way.
Total number of ways $=3+2+1=6$.
The number of ways to get a sum of at least $10$ is $6$.
