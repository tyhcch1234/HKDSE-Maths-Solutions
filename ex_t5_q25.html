Formulas and Sequences (65) · Sub-Topic: Arithmetic Sequence

T5 Q25

Write down the first five terms of an arithmetic sequence whose first term is $4$ and common difference is $4$.

Solution

For an arithmetic sequence, the $n$th term is $a_n = a + (n-1)d$, where the first term is $a=4$ and the common difference is $d=4$.
The first five terms are obtained by adding $4$ repeatedly starting from $4$.
$a_1=4$, $a_2=4+4=8$, $a_3=8+4=12$, $a_4=12+4=16$, $a_5=16+4=20$.
The first five terms are $4, 8, 12, 16, 20$.
