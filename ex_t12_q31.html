Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Circle Equation

T12 Q31

Find the equation of the circle with centre at the origin and radius 5.

Solution

A circle with centre $(0,0)$ and radius $r$ has equation $x^2+y^2=r^2$.
Substitute $r=5$: $x^2+y^2=5^2$.
Simplify: $x^2+y^2=25$.
The equation of the circle is $x^2+y^2=25$.
