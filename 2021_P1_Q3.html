Question

[2021#3] Factorize

(a) $\;6x^{2}+xy-2y^{2}$,

(b) $\;8x-4y-6x^{2}-xy+2y^{2}$

Step-by-step Solution
Part (a) — Factorize $6x^{2}+xy-2y^{2}$
Step 1 · Strategy This is a quadratic in two variables of the form $ax^{2}+bxy+cy^{2}$, with $a=6,\;b=1,\;c=-2$.
Try the cross method: we need two numbers multiplying to $a\cdot c = 6\times(-2) = -12$ and adding to $b = 1$.
Hint: the only usable pair is $+4$ and $-3$, since $4\times(-3)=-12$ and $4+(-3)=1$.
Splitting the Middle Term \(ax^{2}+bxy+cy^{2}:\ \text{find } p,q\ \text{with } pq=ac,\ p+q=b\) For \(6x^{2}+xy-2y^{2}\): \(ac=-12\) and \(b=1\), so \(p=4\), \(q=-3\).
Step 2 · Split the middle term Split $xy$ into $4xy-3xy$: $$6x^{2}+xy-2y^{2}=6x^{2}+4xy-3xy-2y^{2}$$
Hint: splitting the middle term lets us group and take out common factors.
Step 3 · Group & factor $$\begin{aligned} 6x^{2}+xy-2y^{2} &= \underbrace{6x^{2}+4xy}_{2x(3x+2y)} - \underbrace{3xy+2y^{2}}_{y(3x+2y)}\\[4pt] &= 2x(3x+2y) - y(3x+2y) \end{aligned}$$
Hint: both groups share the common bracket $(3x+2y)$.
Factorising by Grouping \(ab+ac=a(b+c)\) Both groups \(2x(3x+2y)\) and \(y(3x+2y)\) share the bracket \((3x+2y)\).
Step 4 · Take out the common bracket $$= (3x+2y)(2x-y)$$ $$\boxed{\,6x^{2}+xy-2y^{2}=(2x-y)(3x+2y)\,}\quad \text{1A}$$
Hint: $(3x+2y)(2x-y)$ and $(2x-y)(3x+2y)$ are the same by commutativity.
Part (b) — Factorize $8x-4y-6x^{2}-xy+2y^{2}$
Step 5 · Spot the link to (a) Rearrange and group so that the result of (a) appears: $$8x-4y-6x^{2}-xy+2y^{2} = \underbrace{(8x-4y)}_{4(2x-y)} - \underbrace{\left(6x^{2}+xy-2y^{2}\right)}_{(2x-y)(3x+2y)}$$
Hint: $6x^{2}+xy-2y^{2}$ is exactly part (a) — reuse its factorisation. (1M)
Step 6 · Factor each group $$= 4(2x-y) - (2x-y)(3x+2y)$$
Hint: the common factor $(2x-y)$ is now visible in both terms.
Step 7 · Pull out $(2x-y)$ $$\begin{aligned} &= (2x-y)\big[\,4-(3x+2y)\,\big]\\ &= (2x-y)(4-3x-2y) \end{aligned}$$ $$\boxed{\,8x-4y-6x^{2}-xy+2y^{2}=(2x-y)(4-3x-2y)\,}\quad \text{1A}$$
Hint: distribute the minus sign carefully: $-3x-2y$, not $-3x+2y$.
Common Factor Law \(AB+AC=A(B+C)\) The common factor \((2x-y)\) is taken out, leaving \(4-(3x+2y)\).
Answers:
(a) $6x^{2}+xy-2y^{2} = (2x-y)(3x+2y)$
(b) $8x-4y-6x^{2}-xy+2y^{2} = (2x-y)(4-3x-2y)$

Question. (a) Factorize \(4x^{2}+4xy-3y^{2}\). (b) Hence factorize \(8x-4y-4x^{2}-4xy+3y^{2}\). [3 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. For \(4x^{2}+4xy-3y^{2}\): \(ac=4\times(-3)=-12\) and \(b=4\), so split \(4xy\) as \(6xy-2xy\).
Step 2. \(4x^{2}+6xy-2xy-3y^{2}=2x(2x+3y)-y(2x+3y)=(2x-y)(2x+3y)\).
Step 3. For (b), group so that part (a) appears: \(8x-4y-4x^{2}-4xy+3y^{2}=4(2x-y)-\left(4x^{2}+4xy-3y^{2}\right)\).
Step 4. Substitute the result of (a): \(=4(2x-y)-(2x-y)(2x+3y)\).
Step 5. Take out \((2x-y)\): \(=(2x-y)\left[4-(2x+3y)\right]=(2x-y)(4-2x-3y)\).
Answer: (a) \((2x-y)(2x+3y)\) (b) \((2x-y)(4-2x-3y)\)
