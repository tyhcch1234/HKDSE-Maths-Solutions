HKDSE 2019 Paper 2 · Q38 · Mensuration

Arc / sector boundary Chord AC & OB Equilateral △OAB Point D (intersection) Shaded region BCD Radius = 12 cm
[2019 MC #38]

In the figure, \(O\) is the centre of the sector \(OABC\). It is given that \(\Delta OAB\) is an equilateral triangle.

\(AC\) and \(OB\) intersect at the point \(D\). If \(OA=12\text{ cm}\) and \(\angle AOC=90^\circ\), find the area of the shaded region \(BCD\) correct to the nearest cm\(^2\).

A. 11 cm\(^2\)
B. 16 cm\(^2\)
C. 26 cm\(^2\)
D. 38 cm\(^2\)
STEP 1 · Angles at O

\(\Delta OAB\) is equilateral, so

\[\angle AOB = 60^\circ\]

Since \(\angle AOC = 90^\circ\) and \(OA\) lies inside the angle \(COB\):

\[\angle BOC = \angle AOC - \angle AOB = 90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ\]

Also \(OC = OA = 12\) cm (radii of the same sector).
STEP 2 · Split △AOC by the line OD

\(D\) lies on \(OB\), so the segment \(OD\) splits \(\Delta AOC\) into \(\Delta AOD\) and \(\Delta COD\).

\[\text{Area of } \Delta AOC = \tfrac12 \cdot OA \cdot OC = \tfrac12 (12)(12) = 72 \text{ cm}^2\]

STEP 3 · Ratio of the two triangles

Both triangles share the same segment \(OD\). Using \(\tfrac12 ab\sin C\):

\[\frac{[\Delta COD]}{[\Delta AOD]}=\frac{\frac12 \cdot OC\cdot OD\sin\angle COD}{\frac12 \cdot OA\cdot OD\sin\angle AOD}=\frac{\sin 30^\circ}{\sin 60^\circ}=\frac{1/\sqrt3}{1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\]

So \([\Delta COD]=\dfrac{72}{1+\sqrt3}\text{ cm}^2\).

STEP 4 · Area of the sector OBC

Area of sector = \(\pi r^2 \times \dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\):

\[[\text{Sector } OBC]=\pi(12)^2\times\frac{30^\circ}{360^\circ}=12\pi \text{ cm}^2\]

STEP 5 · Shaded region BCD

Shaded area \(=[\text{Sector }OBC]-[\Delta COD]\):

\[=12\pi-\frac{72}{1+\sqrt3}=12\pi-26.3538\approx 11.3453 \text{ cm}^2\]

Correct to the nearest cm\(^2\): \(11\) cm\(^2\).

Answer: A  ·  \(11\text{ cm}^2\)

Note: \(\dfrac{72}{1+\sqrt3}=72\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{2}=36(\sqrt3-1)\approx 26.35\).
