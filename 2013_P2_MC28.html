Statistics — Interpreting a Scatter Diagram

Scatter Diagram Correlation Direct / Inverse Variation Multiple-Choice

QThe Question

The scatter diagram below shows the relation between \(x\) and \(y\). Which of the following may represent the relation between \(x\) and \(y\)?

Data points (×) Axes
  • A. \(y\) increases when \(x\) increases.  ✓ Correct
  • B. \(y\) decreases when \(x\) increases.
  • C. \(y\) varies inversely as \(x^{2}\).
  • D. \(y\) varies directly as \(x^{3}\).

(Multiple-choice — no marks breakdown, 1 correct answer.)

Step 11M

Start from the left of the scatter diagram and move to the right. As \(x\) (the horizontal coordinate) increases, the plotted points climb steadily upwards.

Hint: In a scatter diagram, always read the trend from left to right along the horizontal axis — that is exactly "as \(x\) increases".
Step 21M

Because the points rise from bottom-left to top-right, the overall trend is a positive association: bigger \(x\) comes with bigger \(y\). In symbols,

\[ \text{as } x \uparrow,\quad y \uparrow \]
Hint: The cloud of points is roughly a long, tilted "cigar" pointing up to the right — that tilt is the whole answer.
Step 31A

Now check each option against that observation:

  • A. \(y\) increases when \(x\) increases — ✔ matches the upward trend.
  • B. \(y\) decreases when \(x\) increases — ✘ the points clearly do not fall.
  • C. \(y\) varies inversely as \(x^{2}\), i.e. \(y \propto \dfrac{1}{x^{2}}\) — ✘ for positive \(x\) this decreases as \(x\) increases.
  • D. \(y\) varies directly as \(x^{3}\), i.e. \(y \propto x^{3}\) — ✘ the cubic must curve steeply upwards; the points lie roughly along a straight band, and for negative \(x\) a cubic would give negative \(y\), which never happens here.
Hint: Options C and D are curved relations. The plotted points form an approximately straight rising band, so the simplest descriptor is "\(y\) increases when \(x\) increases".
Answer: A — \(y\) increases when \(x\) increases.

Step 11M

Option C says \(y \propto x^{-2}\). For \(x>0\), that curve falls steeply and hugs the axes — it cannot rise to the top-right as the data does.

Hint: Set \(p=-2\) on the slider: the curve immediately plunges downwards and never matches the point cloud.
Step 21A

Option D says \(y \propto x^{3}\). The cubic rises far too steeply and has an S-shape; the middle of the data would have to bend sharply, which it does not.

Hint: Set \(p=3\) on the slider: the curve shoots up almost vertically and completely misses the points.

Summary of the Answer

  • Given: A scatter diagram of \(y\) against \(x\) whose points run from bottom-left to top-right.
  • Observation: As \(x\) increases, \(y\) tends to increase — a positive association.
  • Correct option: A — \(y\) increases when \(x\) increases.
  • Why B is wrong: The points do not fall; \(y\) does not decrease as \(x\) increases.
  • Why C is wrong: \(y \propto x^{-2}\) means \(y\) decreases as \(x\) increases (for positive \(x\)).
  • Why D is wrong: A direct cube relation \(y \propto x^{3}\) is far too steeply curved and would give downward-moving points on one side — inconsistent with the straight, rising band shown.
