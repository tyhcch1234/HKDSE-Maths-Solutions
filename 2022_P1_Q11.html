Question

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the ages of the players of a football team.

Stem (tens)  |  Leaf (units)
1  |  7 8 9
2  |  0 a a 8 8 9
3  |  b b 5 5 6 6 6 6 6 7 8
4  |  3

The inter-quartile range and the median of the distribution are 14 and 31 respectively.

(a) Find a and b. [3 marks]

(b) A player now leaves the football team.

(i) Is there any change in the mode of the distribution due to the leaving of the player? Explain your answer.

(ii) If the range of the distribution is decreased, find the greatest possible standard deviation of the distribution. [4 marks]

Step-by-Step Solution
STEP 1 Write down the ordered data from the stem-and-leaf diagram.
Median of an Odd Number of Data \(\text{median}=x_{\frac{n+1}{2}}\quad(n\ \text{odd})\) There are \(21\) values, so the median is the \(11\)th value in order, which lies in the stem-\(3\) row.

Leaves read in order give: $17,\;18,\;19,\;20,\;a\!a,\;a\!a,\;28,\;28,\;29,\;b\!b,\;b\!b,\;35,\;35,\;36\times5,\;37,\;38,\;43$

Total number of players: $3 + 6 + 11 + 1 = \mathbf{21}$ (odd).

Tip: The median of 21 ordered values is the 11th value.
STEP 2 Use the median = 31 to find $b$.

The 11th value in order is a stem-3 leaf. Counting: positions 10 and 11 come from the stem-3 row, and the smallest two stem-3 values are $b\!b$ (i.e. $30+b$).

Median $= 30 + b = 31 \;\Rightarrow\; \boxed{b = 1}$

Hint: the median must be the 11th of 21 values — it lies in the stem-3 row.
STEP 3 Use the inter-quartile range = 14 to find $a$.
Inter-Quartile Range \(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) With \(Q_{1}=20+a\) and \(Q_{3}=36\), the condition IQR \(=14\) gives \(36-(20+a)=14\) and hence \(a=2\).

With 21 values, $Q_1$ is at position $\tfrac{1}{4}(21+1)=5.5$ (average of 5th & 6th) and $Q_3$ is at position $16.5$ (average of 16th & 17th).

The smallest possible values are $a\!a$ (i.e. $20+a$), so the 5th and 6th values are both $20+a$:
$Q_1 = 20 + a$.

The 16th and 17th values both equal 36 (the 36-runs), so $Q_3 = 36$.

IQR $= 36 - (20+a) = 14 \;\Rightarrow\; \boxed{a = 2}$

Hint: treat the diagram's a-entries as $22,22$. Note $36-(20+a)=14$.
STEP 4 Part (b)(i) — Check the mode.

With $a=2,\;b=1$, the data are: $17,18,19,20,22,22,28,28,29,31,31,35,35,36,36,36,36,36,37,38,43$.

The most frequent value is $36$ (appears 5 times), so the original mode is $36$.

If a player leaves, only the counts change; the value 36 still appears at least 4 times and remains the most frequent.

Therefore there is no change in the mode.

Hint: removing one value cannot create a new value — the mode stays 36.
STEP 5 Part (b)(ii) — Range decreased: which player left?

Original range $= 43 - 17 = 26$.

For the range to decrease, one of the extreme values must leave: either the age 17 or the age 43.

Case 1: 17 leaves → new range $= 43-18=25$.
Case 2: 43 leaves → new range $= 38-17=21$.

Both cases reduce the range; we must test the standard deviation in each case.

Hint: the range decreases only when a minimum or maximum data value leaves.
STEP 6 Compute the standard deviations for both cases.
Standard Deviation from Raw Data \(\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum x^{2}}{n}-\bar{x}^{2}}\) Applying it to the \(20\) remaining values in each case gives \(7.1625\) and \(7.1323\), the larger being \(7.16\).

Original (20 values after removal) — use $\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum x^2}{n}-\bar{x}^2}$.

Case 1 (17 leaves): $\sigma \approx 7.1625$

Case 2 (43 leaves): $\sigma \approx 7.1323$

Comparing, the greatest possible standard deviation is from Case 1.

$\sigma \approx 7.162537194 \approx \boxed{7.16}$

Hint: choose the larger of the two standard deviations and round to 3 significant figures.
Answer: (a) $a = 2$, $b = 1$  |  (b)(i) No, the mode is still $36$  |  (b)(ii) $7.16$

Question. The following are the ages of \(11\) players in a team, in ascending order: \(12,\ 14,\ 15,\ 17,\ 17,\ 19,\ 21,\ 22,\ 24,\ 26,\ 28\).
(a) Find the median.
(b) Find the inter-quartile range.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. There are \(11\) values (odd), so the median is the \(\dfrac{11+1}{2}=6\)th value, i.e. \(19\).
Step 2. \(Q_{1}\) is at position \(\dfrac{11+1}{4}=3\), so \(Q_{1}=15\).
Step 3. \(Q_{3}\) is at position \(\dfrac{3(11+1)}{4}=9\), so \(Q_{3}=24\).
Step 4. IQR \(=Q_{3}-Q_{1}=24-15=9\).
Answer: (a) \(19\); (b) \(9\)
