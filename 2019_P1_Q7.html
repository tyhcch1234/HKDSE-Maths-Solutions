HKDSE 2019 · Paper 1 · Q7

In a playground, the ratio of the number of adults to the number of children is $13 : 6$. If $9$ adults and $24$ children enter the playground, then the ratio of the number of adults to the number of children is $8 : 7$. Find the original number of adults in the playground.

[4 marks]

Step-by-step solution

Step 1  ·  Let a variable do the work
Ratio Written with One Unknown \(a:b \;\Longrightarrow\; ak \text{ and } bk\) The original numbers are \(13k\) adults and \(6k\) children for some positive constant \(k\).

The ratio is $13 : 6$, so write the original numbers using one unknown $k$ (a positive constant):

\[ \text{Original adults} = 13k, \qquad \text{Original children} = 6k \]

Hint: a ratio $a:b$ means the actual numbers are $ak$ and $bk$ — never just $a$ and $b$.
Step 2  ·  Add the newcomers

$9$ adults and $24$ children enter, so the new numbers are:

\[ \text{Adults} = 13k + 9, \qquad \text{Children} = 6k + 24 \]

Hint: this is the "after" column of your ratio table — the constant $k$ stays in the expression.
Step 3  ·  Build the equation
Ratio Equation \(\dfrac{A}{B}=\dfrac{p}{q} \;\Longleftrightarrow\; A:B=p:q\) The new ratio \(8:7\) becomes \(\dfrac{13k+9}{6k+24}=\dfrac{8}{7}\).

The new ratio is $8 : 7$, so:

\[ \frac{13k+9}{6k+24} = \frac{8}{7} \]

Hint: "the ratio of A to B is $8:7$" translates directly into $\dfrac{A}{B} = \dfrac{8}{7}$.
Step 4  ·  Cross-multiply and solve
Cross-Multiplication \(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d} \;\Longrightarrow\; ad=bc\) Cross-multiplying \(7(13k+9)=8(6k+24)\) gives \(43k=129\), so \(k=3\).

\[ 7(13k+9) = 8(6k+24) \]

\[ 91k + 63 = 48k + 192 \]

\[ 91k - 48k = 192 - 63 \]

\[ 43k = 129 \]

\[ k = 3 \]

Hint: collect the $k$ terms on one side and the numbers on the other.
Check: $91(3)+63 = 336$ and $48(3)+192 = 336$. ✓
Step 5  ·  Answer the question asked

We want the original number of adults:

\[ 13k = 13 \times 3 = 39 \]

Check: children $= 6(3) = 18$;
after arrivals $39+9 = 48$ and $18+24 = 42$, and $48 : 42 = 8 : 7$. ✓
Hint: don't stop at $k$ — always convert back to the quantity the question asks for and verify it.
Original number of adults = 39

Question. The original ratio of the number of adults to the number of children in a park is \(9:5\). After \(8\) adults and \(20\) children enter the park, the ratio becomes \(7:5\). Find the original number of adults.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Write the original numbers as \(9k\) adults and \(5k\) children,
where \(k\) is a positive constant.
Step 2. After the arrivals the numbers are \(9k+8\) adults and \(5k+20\) children,
and their ratio is \(7:5\).
Step 3. \(\dfrac{9k+8}{5k+20}=\dfrac{7}{5}\), so \(5(9k+8)=7(5k+20)\), i.e. \(45k+40=35k+140\).
Step 4. \(10k=100\), so \(k=10\), and the original number of adults is \(9k=90\).
Step 5. Check: children \(=50\);
after arrivals \(98:70=7:5\) ✓.
Answer: \(90\) adults
