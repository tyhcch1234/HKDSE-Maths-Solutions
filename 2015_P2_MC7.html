Step-by-step solution

1

Start with the given equation

We know \( \beta \) satisfies:

\[ 4\beta^2 - 5\beta - 1 = 0 \]
Hint: This is a quadratic equation in \( \beta \). We can rearrange it to find useful identities.
2

Rearrange to isolate \( 4\beta^2 - 5\beta \)

Add 1 to both sides:

\[ 4\beta^2 - 5\beta = 1 \]

This will help us simplify the target expression.

3

Write down the target expression

\[ 7 + 10\beta - 8\beta^2 \]

Notice it contains \( \beta^2 \) and \( \beta \) terms. We want to rewrite it using \( 4\beta^2 - 5\beta \).

4

Factor out \( -2 \) from the quadratic part

Observe that:

\[ -8\beta^2 + 10\beta = -2(4\beta^2 - 5\beta) \]

Check: \( -2 \times 4\beta^2 = -8\beta^2 \) and \( -2 \times (-5\beta) = 10\beta \).

5

Rewrite the target expression

\[ 7 + 10\beta - 8\beta^2 = 7 - 2(4\beta^2 - 5\beta) \]
6

Substitute \( 4\beta^2 - 5\beta = 1 \)

Replace the bracket with 1:

\[ 7 - 2(1) \]
7

Evaluate

\[ 7 - 2 = 5 \]

Therefore the answer is A. 5.

Key takeaway

By manipulating \( 4\beta^2 - 5\beta - 1 = 0 \) into \( 4\beta^2 - 5\beta = 1 \), we can simplify the target expression without solving for \( \beta \) exactly. This is a classic DSE technique.

Final answer

5
