The base of the pyramid \(VABCD\) is the quadrilateral \(ABCD\). The face \(VAB\) lies on horizontal ground. Given \(AB=90\) cm, \(AD=70\) cm, \(CD=100\) cm, \(\angle ADC=55^\circ\), \(\angle BCD=130^\circ\).

(a) Find
(i) distance \(AC\);
(ii) \(\angle BAD\).  (4 marks)
(b) Given \(VD=80\) cm and the angle between plane \(ABCD\) and the ground is \(65^\circ\). The student claims the angle between \(VD\) and the ground is less than \(50^\circ\). Is the claim correct?  (2 marks)
STEP 1 · (a)(i) — triangle ACD
Cosine Formula \(c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}-2ab\cos C\) With \(AD=70\), \(CD=100\) and \(\angle ADC=55^{\circ}\), \(AC^{2}=14900-8030.1\).
By the cosine rule in \(\triangle ACD\) with \(AD=70\), \(CD=100\), \(\angle ADC=55^\circ\): \[ AC^2=70^2+100^2-2(70)(100)\cos55^\circ=14900-8030.1 \] \[ AC^2\approx6869.9 \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; AC\approx82.885\text{ cm}\approx82.9\text{ cm} \]
STEP 2 · (a)(ii) — angles in △ACD
Sine Rule \(\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}=\dfrac{c}{\sin C}\) Using \(\dfrac{100}{\sin\angle DAC}=\dfrac{82.885}{\sin55^{\circ}}\) gives \(\angle DAC\approx81.2^{\circ}\).
By the sine rule: \[ \sin\angle DAC=\frac{100\sin55^\circ}{82.885}\approx0.9883 \] \[ \angle DAC\approx81.2^\circ \] \[ \angle ACD=180^\circ-55^\circ-81.2^\circ\approx43.8^\circ \]
STEP 3 · (a)(ii) — angle ACB and triangle ABC
Since \(AC\) lies inside \(\angle BCD=130^\circ\): \[ \angle ACB=130^\circ-43.8^\circ\approx86.2^\circ \] Cosine rule in \(\triangle ABC\) with \(AB=90,\ AC=82.885\): \[ 90^2=BC^2+82.885^2-2(BC)(82.885)\cos86.2^\circ \] \[ BC^2-10.99\,BC-1230.1=0 \;\Rightarrow\; BC\approx41.0\text{ cm} \]
STEP 4 · (a)(ii) — ∠BAD
Sine rule in \(\triangle ABC\): \[ \sin\angle BAC=\frac{41.0\sin86.2^\circ}{90}\approx0.4545 \Rightarrow \angle BAC\approx27.0^\circ \] \[ \angle BAD=\angle DAC+\angle BAC\approx81.2^\circ+27.0^\circ \] \[ \boxed{\angle BAD\approx108.2^\circ} \]
STEP 5 · (b) — height of D above the ground
Perpendicular Height from a Distance and an Angle \(d=AD\sin\angle DAB\) \(d=70\sin108.2^{\circ}\approx66.5\text{ cm}\), then the tilt \(65^{\circ}\) reduces it to \(60.3\text{ cm}\).
In plane \(ABCD\), perpendicular distance from \(D\) to \(AB\): \[ d=AD\sin\angle DAB=70\sin108.2^\circ\approx66.5\text{ cm} \] With tilt angle \(65^\circ\) between the planes,
height of \(D\) above ground: \[ DD'=d\sin65^\circ\approx66.5\times0.9063\approx60.3\text{ cm} \]
STEP 6 · (b) — angle between VD and the ground
\(V\) is on the ground, so in right triangle \(VDD'\) (right angle at \(D'\)): \[ \sin\theta=\frac{DD'}{VD}=\frac{60.3}{80}\approx0.753 \] \[ \theta\approx48.9^\circ \] Since \(48.9^\circ<50^\circ\),
the student's claim is correct. ✔
Answers:
(a)(i) \(AC\approx82.9\) cm
(a)(ii) \(\angle BAD\approx108^\circ\) (108.2°)
(b) Yes — the angle between \(VD\) and the ground \(\approx48.9^\circ<50^\circ\).

Question. In the figure, \(AD=50\text{ cm}\), \(CD=80\text{ cm}\) and \(\angle ADC=60^{\circ}\).

(a) Find \(AC\).
(b) Find \(\angle DAC\).
(c) Hence find \(\angle ACD\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(AC^{2}=50^{2}+80^{2}-2(50)(80)\cos60^{\circ}=2500+6400-4000=4900\), so \(AC=70\text{ cm}\).
Step 2. Sine rule: \(\dfrac{\sin\angle DAC}{80}=\dfrac{\sin60^{\circ}}{70}\), so \(\sin\angle DAC=\dfrac{80\sin60^{\circ}}{70}\approx0.9897\) and \(\angle DAC\approx81.8^{\circ}\).
Step 3. \(\angle ACD=180^{\circ}-60^{\circ}-81.8^{\circ}\approx38.2^{\circ}\).
Answer: (a) \(70\text{ cm}\) (b) \(81.8^{\circ}\) (c) \(38.2^{\circ}\)
