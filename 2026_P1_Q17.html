HKDSE 2026 · Paper 1 · Q17

3D Geometry: Quadrilateral Base, Dihedral Angle

Question

In Figure 2, the base of the pyramid \(VABCD\) is the quadrilateral \(ABCD\). The face \(VAB\) of the pyramid lies on the horizontal ground. It is given that \(AB=90\) cm, \(AD=70\) cm, \(CD=100\) cm, \(\angle ADC=55^\circ\) and \(\angle BCD=130^\circ\).

[Figure 2: a pyramid with apex \(V\) on the horizontal ground (shown by the dashed parallelogram); the quadrilateral base \(ABCD\) is tilted above the ground with \(A\) and \(B\) on the ground line, \(D\) highest and \(C\) at the right.]

(a)   Find

(i)  the distance between \(A\) and \(C\),

(ii)  \(\angle BAD\). (4 marks)

(b)   It is given that \(VD=80\) cm. A student finds that the angle between the plane \(ABCD\) and the horizontal ground is \(65^\circ\). The student claims that the angle between \(VD\) and the horizontal ground is less than \(50^\circ\). Is the claim correct? Explain your answer. (2 marks)

Solution

PART (a)(i) — triangle ACD
By the cosine rule on \(\triangle ACD\) (\(AD=70,\ CD=100,\ \angle ADC=55^\circ\)): \[ AC^2=70^2+100^2-2(70)(100)\cos55^\circ=14900-8030.1=6869.9 \] \[ AC\approx 82.9\text{ cm} \]
PART (a)(ii) — angles around the diagonal AC
In \(\triangle ACD\), by the sine rule: \[ \sin\angle DAC=\frac{100\sin55^\circ}{82.885}\approx 0.9883 \implies \angle DAC\approx 81.2^\circ \] \[ \therefore\ \angle ACD=180^\circ-55^\circ-81.2^\circ\approx 43.8^\circ \] Since the diagonal \(AC\) lies inside \(\angle BCD=130^\circ\): \[ \angle ACB=130^\circ-43.8^\circ\approx 86.2^\circ \]
PART (a)(ii) — triangle ABC
In \(\triangle ABC\): \(AB=90\), \(AC=82.885\), \(\angle ACB\approx 86.2^\circ\). Cosine rule for \(BC\): \[ 90^2=BC^2+82.885^2-2(BC)(82.885)\cos86.2^\circ \implies BC^2-10.99\,BC-1230.1=0 \] \[ BC\approx 41.0\text{ cm} \] Sine rule: \[ \sin\angle BAC=\frac{BC\sin\angle ACB}{AB}\approx\frac{41.0\times\sin86.2^\circ}{90}\approx 0.4545 \implies \angle BAC\approx 27.0^\circ \] \[ \angle BAD=\angle DAC+\angle BAC\approx 81.2^\circ+27.0^\circ=108.2^\circ \]
PART (b) — height of D above the ground
The planes \(ABCD\) and the ground meet along \(AB\), at an angle of \(65^\circ\). The distance from \(D\) to \(AB\) in the plane \(ABCD\) is \[ d=AD\sin\angle DAB=70\sin108.2^\circ\approx 66.5\text{ cm} \] If \(D'\) is the foot of the perpendicular from \(D\) to the ground, then \[ DD'=d\sin65^\circ\approx 66.5\times 0.9063\approx 60.3\text{ cm} \]
PART (b) — angle between VD and the ground
\(V\) lies on the ground, so the angle \(\theta\) between \(VD\) and the ground satisfies (right triangle \(VDD'\), right angle at \(D'\)): \[ \sin\theta=\frac{DD'}{VD}=\frac{60.3}{80}\approx 0.753 \implies \theta\approx 48.9^\circ \] Since \(48.9^\circ < 50^\circ\), the student's claim is correct.
(a)(i) \(AC\approx82.9\) cm    (a)(ii) \(\angle BAD\approx108^\circ\)    (b) Yes — the angle between \(VD\) and the ground \(\approx48.9^\circ<50^\circ\), so the claim is correct.
