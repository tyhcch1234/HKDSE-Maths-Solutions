HKDSE Math 2015 Paper 1 Q1

Topic 8: Laws of Indices, Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

Laws of Indices Positive Indices Simplification

Simplify and express your answer with positive indices.

\[ \frac{m^9}{(m^3 n^{-7})^5} \]

[3 marks]

Step-by-Step Guided Solution

Apply the power of a product rule: \((ab)^n = a^n b^n\). Raise each factor inside the bracket to the power of 5.

\[ \frac{m^9}{m^{3\times5} n^{-7\times5}} \]
Hint: Remember that \((m^3)^5 = m^{3 \times 5} = m^{15}\) and \((n^{-7})^5 = n^{-35}\).

Perform the multiplication in the exponents.

\[ \frac{m^9}{m^{15} n^{-35}} \]

Split the fraction into \(m\) and \(n\) parts for easier handling.

\[ \frac{m^9}{m^{15}} \times \frac{1}{n^{-35}} \]

Use the quotient rule: \(\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}\).

\[ = m^{9-15} \times n^{35} \]

Wait, let's handle the negative exponent first!

\[ = m^{9-15} \times n^{35} \]
Hint: Remember that \(\frac{1}{n^{-35}} = n^{35}\) because a negative exponent in the denominator becomes positive in the numerator.

Combine and simplify to get the final expression with positive indices.

\[ = m^{-6} n^{35} \] \[ = \frac{n^{35}}{m^6} \]
