HKDSE 2012 · Paper 1 · Q1 3 marks
Simplify $\dfrac{m^{-12}\,n^{8}}{n^{3}}$ and express your answer with positive indices.
1Write the given expression
The expression is \[ \frac{m^{-12}\,n^{8}}{n^{3}}. \] Notice the base $m$ only appears in the numerator, and the base $n$ appears in both the numerator and the denominator.
Hint: identify which base needs an index law — here, $n$ appears twice, so apply the quotient law to it first.
2Apply the quotient law of indices to $n$
For the same base, $\dfrac{n^{a}}{n^{b}} = n^{a-b}$. So \[ \frac{n^{8}}{n^{3}} = n^{8-3} = n^{5}. \] The expression becomes \[ \frac{m^{-12}\,n^{8}}{n^{3}} = m^{-12}\, n^{8-3} = m^{-12}\, n^{5}. \]
Quotient Law of Indices \(\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}} = a^{m-n}\quad (a\neq 0)\) Same base, dividing: subtract the indices.
Hint: subtract the index in the denominator from the index in the numerator: $8 - 3 = 5$.
3Convert the negative index to a positive one
Using $m^{-k} = \dfrac{1}{m^{k}}$, \[ m^{-12}\, n^{5} = \frac{1}{m^{12}}\, n^{5} = \frac{n^{5}}{m^{12}}. \]
Negative Index Law \(a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^{n}},\qquad \dfrac{1}{a^{-n}} = a^{n}\) A negative index moves the base across the fraction bar.
Hint: a negative index just "flips" the base across the fraction bar — the index becomes positive.
4Final simplified answer
\[ \frac{m^{-12}\,n^{8}}{n^{3}} = \frac{n^{5}}{m^{12}}. \] All indices are now positive.
Answer $\dfrac{m^{-12}n^{8}}{n^{3}} = \dfrac{n^{5}}{m^{12}}$

Question. Simplify \(\dfrac{x^{-7}y^{9}}{y^{4}}\) and express your answer with positive indices.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Apply the quotient law to the base \(y\): \(\dfrac{y^{9}}{y^{4}}=y^{9-4}=y^{5}\), so the expression becomes \(x^{-7}y^{5}\).
Step 2. Apply the negative index law to \(x^{-7}\): \(x^{-7}=\dfrac{1}{x^{7}}\).
Step 3. Hence the expression equals \(\dfrac{y^{5}}{x^{7}}\), with all indices positive.
Answer: \(\dfrac{y^{5}}{x^{7}}\)
