T5 Q42

Find the sum of the first 100 positive integers.

Solution

Identify the arithmetic sequence: $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 100$, with first term $a=1$, last term $l=100$, and number of terms $n=100$.
Use the sum formula for an arithmetic sequence: $S_n=\frac{n}{2}(a+l)$.
Substitute the values: $S_{100}=\frac{100}{2}(1+100)$.
Simplify: $S_{100}=50 \times 101 = 5050$.
The sum of the first 100 positive integers is $5050$.
