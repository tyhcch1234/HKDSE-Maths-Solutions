Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Trigonometric Ratios

T13 Q10

Evaluate $\sin^2 30^\circ + \cos^2 30^\circ$.

Solution

Use the Pythagorean identity: $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$ for any angle $\theta$.
Substitute $\theta=30^\circ$: $\sin^2 30^\circ+\cos^2 30^\circ=1$.
Alternatively, compute directly: $\sin 30^\circ=\frac{1}{2}$ and $\cos 30^\circ=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
Then $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^2=\frac{1}{4}+\frac{3}{4}=1$.
$\sin^2 30^\circ+\cos^2 30^\circ=1$.
