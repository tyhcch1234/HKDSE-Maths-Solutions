HKDSE Math 2019 Paper 1 Q1

Topic 5 · Formulas and Sequences · Change of Subject
Change of Subject Algebra Expand & Rearrange 3 Marks

QQuestion

Make \(h\) the subject of the formula \[ 9(h + 6k) = 7h + 8. \]
[3 marks]

AStep-by-Step Solution

0 Understanding the Problem ▼

We need to rearrange the given equation so that \(h\) appears alone on one side.

Currently, \(h\) appears on both sides of the equation. Our strategy:

  1. Expand the brackets.
  2. Collect all \(h\) terms on one side.
  3. Collect all other terms on the other side.
  4. Factorise and divide to isolate \(h\).
💡 Hint
When a variable appears on both sides, move all terms containing that variable to one side and everything else to the other side.
1 Expand the Brackets ▼

Apply the distributive law: multiply \(9\) by each term inside the bracket.

\[ 9(h + 6k) = 9h + 54k \]

So the equation becomes:

\[ 9h + 54k = 7h + 8 \]
💡 Hint
Distributive law: \(a(b + c) = ab + ac\). Here \(a = 9\), \(b = h\), \(c = 6k\).

✓ 1M awarded for expansion

2 Collect \(h\)-terms on One Side ▼

Subtract \(7h\) from both sides to gather all \(h\) terms on the left:

\[ 9h - 7h + 54k = 8 \]

Simplify:

\[ 2h + 54k = 8 \]
💡 Hint
Whatever operation you apply to one side, apply the same to the other side to keep the equation balanced.
3 Isolate the \(h\)-term ▼

Subtract \(54k\) from both sides to move the non-\(h\) term:

\[ 2h = 8 - 54k \]
💡 Hint
We want \(h\) alone. The term \(54k\) does not contain \(h\), so we move it to the right-hand side.

✓ 1M awarded for collecting terms

4 Divide to Find \(h\) ▼

Divide every term on both sides by \(2\):

\[ h = \frac{8 - 54k}{2} = \frac{8}{2} - \frac{54k}{2} \]
\[ h = 4 - 27k \]
💡 Hint
Division distributes over subtraction: \(\dfrac{a - b}{c} = \dfrac{a}{c} - \dfrac{b}{c}\).

✓ 1A awarded for correct answer

Final Answer
\(h = 4 - 27k\)
