HKDSE 2019 P1 Q1Formulas and Sequences

Make $h$ the subject of the formula

$$9(h+6k)=7h+8$$

[3 marks]

Solution

Step 1Expand the bracket on the LHS.

\[ 9(h+6k)=7h+8 \]

\[ 9h + 54k = 7h + 8 \]

Multiply each term inside the bracket by 9. (1M)
Distributive Law \(a(b+c)=ab+ac\) Expanding \(9(h+6k)\) gives \(9h+54k\), which frees the \(h\) term.
Step 2Collect the $h$ terms on one side.

Subtract $7h$ from both sides:

\[ 9h - 7h = 8 - 54k \]

Keep the $h$-terms left, numbers and $k$-terms right. (1M)
Collecting Like Terms \(pa-qa=(p-q)a\) Subtracting \(7h\) from both sides puts all \(h\) terms together: \(9h-7h=2h\).
Step 3Simplify and divide by the coefficient of $h$.

\[ 2h = 8 - 54k \]

\[ h = \frac{8-54k}{2} = 4 - 27k \]

Divide every term by 2 to isolate $h$. (1A)
Division Property of Equations \(pa=q \;\Longrightarrow\; a=\dfrac{q}{p}\) Dividing \(2h=8-54k\) by \(2\) isolates \(h=4-27k\).
Answer: \(\;h = 4 - 27k\)

Question. Make \(h\) the subject of the formula \(6(h-4k)=5h+12\). [3 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Expand the bracket: \(6h-24k=5h+12\).
Step 2. Collect the \(h\) terms on one side: \(6h-5h=12+24k\), so \(h=12+24k\).
Step 3. Check with \(k=1\): \(h=36\), and \(6(36-4)=192\) while \(5(36)+12=192\) ✓.
Answer: \(h=12+24k\)
