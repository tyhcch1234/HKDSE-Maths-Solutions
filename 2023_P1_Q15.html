HKDSE Math 2023 Paper 1 Q15

Probability Combinations

In a box, there are 5 red balls and 4 black balls. From the box, 2 balls are randomly chosen at the same time.

(a) Find the probability that the 2 balls chosen are red. [2 marks]

(b) In a bag, there are 8 red balls. The 2 balls chosen from the box are put into the bag and then 3 balls are randomly chosen at the same time from the bag. Find the probability that the 3 balls chosen are of the same colour. [2 marks]

Box content (initial)

R R R R R B B B B

5 red (R), 4 black (B) — total 9 balls

We choose 2 balls at the same time — order does not matter. So we use combinations.

Hint: \( ^nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!} \) = number of ways to choose \(r\) objects from \(n\) distinct objects.

Total number of ways to pick 2 balls from 9: \( ^9C_2 \).

Number of ways to choose 2 red from 5 red: \( ^5C_2 \).

Hint: Favourable outcomes ÷ total outcomes.

Required probability:

\[ \frac{^5C_2}{^9C_2} = \frac{10}{36} = \frac{5}{18} \]

So \( P(\text{both red}) = \frac{5}{18} \).

Initially, the bag has 8 red balls. The 2 balls from the box are added. We need the total colour composition.

Two cases for the 2 transferred balls:

  • Case 1: Both red (probability \( \frac{5}{18} \) from part a). Then bag has \(8+2=10\) red, 0 black → total 10.
  • Case 2: 1 red, 1 black. Bag has \(8+1=9\) red, 1 black → total 10.
  • Case 3: Both black. Bag has \(8\) red, 2 black → total 10.

We need probability that 3 chosen from bag are same colour.

P(both transferred are red) = \( \frac{^5C_2}{^9C_2} = \frac{5}{18} \)

P(both transferred are black) = \( \frac{^4C_2}{^9C_2} = \frac{6}{36} = \frac{1}{6} \)

P(1 red & 1 black) = \( \frac{^5C_1 \cdot ^4C_1}{^9C_2} = \frac{20}{36} = \frac{5}{9} \)

Hint: These three probabilities sum to 1: \( \frac{5}{18}+\frac{1}{6}+\frac{5}{9} = \frac{5}{18}+\frac{3}{18}+\frac{10}{18}=1 \).

Use Law of Total Probability. For each transfer case, compute \(P(\text{3 same colour from bag})\).

Case 1 (both red): Bag: 10 red, 0 black. P(3 same) = P(3 red) = \( \frac{^{10}C_3}{^{10}C_3}=1 \).

Case 2 (1 red, 1 black): Bag: 9 red, 1 black. P(3 same) = P(3 red) + P(3 black). But only 1 black, so P(3 black)=0. P(3 red) = \( \frac{^9C_3}{^{10}C_3} = \frac{84}{120} = \frac{7}{10} \).

Case 3 (both black): Bag: 8 red, 2 black. P(3 same) = P(3 red) + P(3 black) = \( \frac{^8C_3}{^{10}C_3} + \frac{^2C_3}{^{10}C_3} \). Note \( ^2C_3 = 0 \). So P = \( \frac{56}{120} = \frac{7}{15} \).

Now combine:

\[ \begin{aligned} P &= \frac{5}{18} \cdot 1 + \frac{5}{9} \cdot \frac{7}{10} + \frac{1}{6} \cdot \frac{7}{15} \\ &= \frac{5}{18} + \frac{35}{90} + \frac{7}{90} \\ &= \frac{25}{90} + \frac{35}{90} + \frac{7}{90} = \frac{67}{90} \end{aligned} \]

Therefore the probability is \( \frac{67}{90} \).

Key formulas used

  • Combination: \( ^nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!} \)
  • Probability = favourable outcomes / total outcomes
  • Law of total probability: \( P(A) = \sum P(A|B_i)P(B_i) \)
