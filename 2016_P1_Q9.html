(a) Find $x$, $y$ and $z$.

(b) If a plant is randomly selected from the garden, find the probability that the height of the selected plant is less than $1.25$ m but not less than $0.65$ m.

[5 marks]
1 Reading the tables — match the class boundaries

The cumulative frequencies are cumulative at each upper class boundary. Compare the boundaries with the class intervals:

$0.35 = 0.3 + 0.05$, $0.65 = 0.6+0.05$, $0.95=0.9+0.05$, $1.25=1.2+0.05$, $1.55=1.5+0.05$, $1.85=1.8+0.05$.

So each entry in the lower table corresponds to “up to the end of that class”.

Hint: The 1st row of the cumulative table ($2$) means the first class ($0.1-0.3$) has frequency $a=2$. This is our anchor.
2 Find $x$ (cumulative frequency below $0.65$ m)
Cumulative Frequency \(F_{k}=f_{1}+f_{2}+\cdots+f_{k}\) \\(x\\) is the cumulative frequency at the boundary \\(0.65\\), i.e. the total of the first two classes \\(2+4\\).

$x$ = total frequency of the classes $0.1-0.3$ and $0.4-0.6$:

$$x = a + 4 = 2 + 4 = 6$$

Hint: “Less than $0.65$ m” includes the whole first class ($a$) and the whole second class ($4$).
3 Find $y$ (cumulative frequency below $1.25$ m)
Cumulative Frequency of One Class \(f_{k}=F_{k}-F_{k-1}\) Going backwards from \\(37\\) at \\(1.55\\) m, subtract the \\(1.3\\)\u2013\\(1.5\\) class of frequency \\(15\\) to get \\(y=22\\).

The figure $37$ is the cumulative frequency below $1.55$ m, which includes the class $1.3-1.5$ of frequency $15$:

$$y = 37 - 15 = 22$$

Hint: Go backwards from a known entry: subtract the last included class’s frequency.
4 Find $z$ (total number of plants)

$z$ is the cumulative frequency at the final boundary $1.85$ m — i.e. the total:

$$z = 37 + 3 = 40$$

Hint: The last class ($1.6-1.8$) has frequency $3$, so add it to $37$.
5 (b) Identify the required number of plants

“Less than $1.25$ m” counts $y = 22$ plants.
“Not less than $0.65$ m” means we exclude the plants shorter than $0.65$ m, i.e. exclude $x = 6$ plants.

Required number $= y - x = 22 - 6 = 16$.

Hint: “Not less than $0.65$” — be careful, the boundary $0.65$ itself belongs to the class $0.7-0.9$, so plants counted in $x$ are exactly those below $0.65$.
6 (b) Compute the probability
Probability from Frequency \(P(E)=\dfrac{\text{required frequency}}{\text{total frequency}}\) The \\(16\\) plants between \\(0.65\\) m and \\(1.25\\) m are counted out of the \\(z=40\\) plants measured.

$$\text{Required probability} = \frac{y-x}{z} = \frac{22-6}{40} = \frac{16}{40} = \frac{2}{5}$$

Hint: Divide the favourable frequency by the total frequency $z=40$, then reduce the fraction.
Answers:   $x = 6$,   $y = 22$,   $z = 40$;   probability $= \dfrac{2}{5}$
