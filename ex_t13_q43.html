T13 Q43

A vertical tower is $50$ m tall. The angle of elevation of the top of the tower from a point on the ground is $45^\circ$. Find the distance from the point to the foot of the tower.

Solution

Let $d$ be the distance from the point on the ground to the foot of the tower.
The tower is vertical, so the height $50$ m is opposite the angle of elevation $45^\circ$.
Using $\tan 45^\circ = \frac{50}{d}$.
Since $\tan 45^\circ = 1$, we have $1 = \frac{50}{d}$.
Thus $d = 50$.
The distance from the point to the foot of the tower is $50$ m.
