Q16 The Question MC

In the figure, \(OAB\) and \(OCD\) are sectors with centre \(O\). If \(\widehat{AB} = 12\pi\text{ cm}\), \(\widehat{CD} = 16\pi\text{ cm}\) and \(OA = 30\text{ cm}\), then \(AC =\)

A. \(5\text{ cm}\)
B. \(10\text{ cm}\)
C. \(20\text{ cm}\)
D. \(40\text{ cm}\)

Original Figure

a Part (a) — Same Angle at the Centre

The two sectors share the same central angle \(\theta\) at \(O\). Drag the slider to change the common angle and watch how both arc lengths change — they always stay in the same ratio.

Arc \(AB\): — Arc \(CD\): — Ratio \(\widehat{AB}:\widehat{CD}\): —
Step 1

Both sectors \(OAB\) and \(OCD\) are drawn from the same centre \(O\) with the same central angle \(\theta\).

Hint: Arc length formula: \(\widehat{AB} = 2\pi r \times \dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\), i.e. \(\widehat{AB} = r\theta\) when the radius is \(r\) and \(\theta\) is in radians. The factor \(\theta\) is identical for both arcs.
Same centre + same angle \(\Rightarrow\) the two sectors are similar.
Step 2

Since the sectors are similar, all corresponding lengths (radii, arc lengths, chords) are proportional:

\[ \frac{OA}{OC} = \frac{\widehat{AB}}{\widehat{CD}} \]
Hint: In similar figures, the ratio of any two corresponding lengths equals the scale factor.

b Part (b) — Solve for \(AC\)

Now substitute the known values. Use the slider to check: whatever value of \(AC\) you choose, only \(AC = 10\) makes the ratio \(\dfrac{30}{30+AC}\) equal to \(\dfrac{12\pi}{16\pi} = \dfrac{3}{4}\).

\(OC = OA + AC =\) — Ratio \(\dfrac{OA}{OC}\): — Target \(\dfrac{\widehat{AB}}{\widehat{CD}}\): 0.75
Step 1

Let \(AC = x\) cm. Then \(OC = OA + AC = 30 + x\) cm. From the similarity in part (a):

\[ \frac{OA}{OC} = \frac{\widehat{AB}}{\widehat{CD}} \]
Hint: \(\widehat{AB} = 12\pi\) and \(\widehat{CD} = 16\pi\), so \(\dfrac{\widehat{AB}}{\widehat{CD}} = \dfrac{12\pi}{16\pi} = \dfrac{3}{4}\).
Step 2

Substitute the numbers into the proportion:

\[ \frac{30}{30+x} = \frac{12\pi}{16\pi} = \frac{3}{4} \]
Hint: Cross-multiply to form a linear equation in \(x\).
Step 3

Cross-multiply and solve:

\[ 30 \times 4 = 3(30 + x) \] \[ 120 = 90 + 3x \] \[ 3x = 30 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 10 \]
Hint: Always check that \(OC = 30 + 10 = 40\) cm, and \(\dfrac{30}{40} = \dfrac{3}{4}\). ✓
\(AC = 10\text{ cm}\) — the answer is B.

Summary — Answers at a Glance

  • Key idea: \(OAB\) and \(OCD\) are similar sectors (same centre \(O\), same central angle \(\theta\)).
  • Similarity ratio: \(\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{\widehat{AB}}{\widehat{CD}}\)
  • Substitute: \(\dfrac{30}{30 + AC} = \dfrac{12\pi}{16\pi} = \dfrac{3}{4}\)
  • Solve: \(120 = 90 + 3\,AC \Rightarrow AC = 10\text{ cm}\)
  • Final answer: B (10 cm)
