HKDSE 2020 Paper 2 — Q45 · Statistics The variance of the six numbers $$20a+3,\quad 20a+5,\quad 20a+9,\quad 20a+11,\quad 20a+15,\quad 20a+17$$ is
  • A. $5$
  • B. $10$
  • C. $25$
  • D. $20a+25$
1Key idea — shifting does not change the variance
Subtracting the same constant $20a+10$ from every number translates all the data but does not change how far apart they are. So the variance stays the same.
Variance measures spread only — adding/subtracting a constant changes the mean, not the spread.
Variance is Unchanged by Adding a Constant \(\operatorname{Var}(X+c)=\operatorname{Var}(X)\) Subtracting \(20a+10\) from all six numbers moves the data but not their spread, so the variance is unchanged.
2Shift every number by $-20a-10$
\[ \begin{aligned} (20a+3)-(20a+10)&=-7\\ (20a+5)-(20a+10)&=-5\\ (20a+9)-(20a+10)&=-1\\ (20a+11)-(20a+10)&=\;\;1\\ (20a+15)-(20a+10)&=\;\;5\\ (20a+17)-(20a+10)&=\;\;7 \end{aligned} \] Now the required variance equals the variance of $-7,\,-5,\,-1,\,1,\,5,\,7$.
Choose the shift so the numbers become small and symmetric — this makes the arithmetic easy.
3Find the mean
\[ \bar x=\frac{(-7)+(-5)+(-1)+1+5+7}{6}=\frac{0}{6}=0 \]
The shifted data are symmetric about $0$, so the mean is $0$.
4Compute the variance
\[ \sigma^{2} =\frac{(-7)^{2}+(-5)^{2}+(-1)^{2}+1^{2}+5^{2}+7^{2}}{6} =\frac{49+25+1+1+25+49}{6} \] \[ =\frac{150}{6}=25 \]
Squared deviations from the mean, divided by the number of data $n=6$.
Variance of a Data Set \(\sigma^{2}=\dfrac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(x_{i}-\bar{x}\right)^{2}\) With mean \(0\) the variance is just the mean of the squares: \(\dfrac{150}{6}=25\).
5Conclusion
The variance of the six original numbers is $25$ — independent of $a$.
Any answer containing $a$ (like D) cannot be a variance after a constant shift.
Answer: C. $25$

Question. If the six numbers \(9a+3,\ 9a+5,\ 9a+6,\ 9a+8,\ 9a+9,\ 9a+11\) have variance \(V\), where \(a\) is a real number, then \(V=\) A. \(7\) B. \(9\) C. \(25\) D. \(49\) [1 mark]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Variance is unaffected by adding or subtracting a constant, so subtract \(9a+7\) from every number.
Step 2. The numbers become \(-4,\ -2,\ -1,\ 1,\ 2,\ 4\), which are symmetric about \(0\), so their mean is \(0\).
Step 3. \(V=\dfrac{(-4)^{2}+(-2)^{2}+(-1)^{2}+1^{2}+2^{2}+4^{2}}{6}=\dfrac{16+4+1+1+4+16}{6}=\dfrac{42}{6}=7\).
Step 4. The value \(7\) does not involve \(a\), as expected after a constant shift.
Answer: A. \(V=7\)
