T11 Q03

A regular pentagon has its interior angles equal. Find the sum of its interior angles and the size of each interior angle.

Solution

A pentagon has $n=5$ sides.
Sum of interior angles $=(n-2)\times 180^\circ=(5-2)\times 180^\circ=3\times 180^\circ=540^\circ$.
Since the pentagon is regular, all 5 interior angles are equal.
Each interior angle $=540^\circ\div 5=108^\circ$.
The sum of the interior angles is $540^\circ$, and each interior angle is $108^\circ$.
