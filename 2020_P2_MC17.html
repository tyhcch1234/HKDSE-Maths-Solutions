Mensuration — Similar Triangles & Ratio of Areas
Similar Triangles (AAA) Ratio of Areas Trapezium Corresponding Sides

QQuestion

Full question text — Multiple choice.

In the figure, \(P\) and \(Q\) are points lying on \(OR\) while \(U\) and \(T\) are points lying on \(OS\) such that \[ OP = PQ = QR \quad \text{and} \quad PU /\!/ QT /\!/ RS. \] The ratio of the area of the trapezium \(PQTU\) to the area of the trapezium \(QRST\) is

A. \(1:2\)     B. \(2:3\)     C. \(3:5\)     D. \(4:9\)

Original Figure

1Setting up: Three Similar Triangles

Drag the slider to change the apex angle or toggle layers to see the nested similar triangles.

\(\triangle OPU\) (small) \(\triangle OQT\) (medium) \(\triangle ORS\) (large) Trapezium \(PQTU\) Trapezium \(QRST\)
Side ratio \(OP:PQ:QR =\) 1 : 1 : 1
\(PU:QT:RS =\) 1 : 2 : 3
Area \(OPU:OQT:ORS =\) 1 : 4 : 9
0 / 4
Step 1
\(OP = PQ = QR\), so along the line \(OR\), \[ OP : OQ : OR = 1 : 2 : 3. \]
Hint: \(OQ = OP + PQ = 1 + 1 = 2\) units; \(OR = OP + PQ + QR = 3\) units.
Step 2
Since \(PU /\!/ QT /\!/ RS\), the three triangles are similar: \[ \triangle OPU \sim \triangle OQT \sim \triangle ORS \quad (\text{AAA}). \]
Hint: They share the apex angle \(\angle ROS\), and corresponding angles are equal because of parallel lines (corr. \(\angle\)s, \(PU /\!/ QT /\!/ RS\)).
Step 3
Corresponding sides are in the same ratio: \[ PU : QT : RS = OP : OQ : OR = 1 : 2 : 3. \]
Hint: Ratio of corresponding sides in similar triangles equals the ratio of linear measures (like heights, bases).
\(PU : QT : RS = 1 : 2 : 3\)
Step 4
For similar figures, \(\text{Area ratio} = (\text{linear ratio})^2\): \[ \text{Area of } \triangle OPU : \text{Area of } \triangle OQT : \text{Area of } \triangle ORS = 1^2 : 2^2 : 3^2 = 1 : 4 : 9. \]
Hint: Square the linear ratio! \(1:2:3 \to 1:4:9\).
Area ratio \(1 : 4 : 9\)

2Finding the Ratio of the Trapeziums

Trapezium = big triangle minus small triangle. Use the slider to visually verify the subtraction.

\(\triangle OPU\) Area of \(PQTU\) Area of \(QRST\)
Area \(PQTU\) \(=\) Area \(OQT - \) Area \(OPU\) \(=\) \(4-1 =\) 3
Area \(QRST\) \(=\) Area \(ORS - \) Area \(OQT\) \(=\) \(9-4 =\) 5
Ratio \(= 3 : 5\) → Option C
0 / 3
Step 1
The trapezium \(PQTU\) is the triangle \(OQT\) with triangle \(OPU\) removed: \[ \text{Area}_{PQTU} = \text{Area}_{\triangle OQT} - \text{Area}_{\triangle OPU}. \]
Hint: Think of \(PQTU\) as a "band" between the two parallel lines \(PU\) and \(QT\).
Step 2
Similarly, trapezium \(QRST\) = triangle \(ORS\) minus triangle \(OQT\): \[ \text{Area}_{QRST} = \text{Area}_{\triangle ORS} - \text{Area}_{\triangle OQT}. \]
Hint: The figure is a "stack" of three triangles: \(OPU\), \(OQT\), \(ORS\). Each band is the difference of two consecutive triangles.
Step 3
Substitute the values \(1:4:9\): \[ \frac{\text{Area}_{PQTU}}{\text{Area}_{QRST}} = \frac{4 - 1}{9 - 4} = \frac{3}{5}. \] So the ratio is \(3:5\), i.e. Option C.
Hint: Don't make the classic mistake of writing \(2:3\). You must subtract the small triangle from each part — the small triangle's area is not zero!
\(\text{Area}_{PQTU} : \text{Area}_{QRST} = 3 : 5\) ✅

📋 Summary of Solution

  • Given \(OP = PQ = QR\), we get \(OP:OQ:OR = 1:2:3\).
  • By AAA similarity, \(\triangle OPU \sim \triangle OQT \sim \triangle ORS\).
  • So \(PU:QT:RS = 1:2:3\) (corresponding sides).
  • Area ratio of similar triangles \(= (1)^2:(2)^2:(3)^2 = 1:4:9\).
  • \(\text{Area}_{PQTU} = 4 - 1 = 3\) (in area units).
  • \(\text{Area}_{QRST} = 9 - 4 = 5\) (in area units).
  • Answer: \(3:5\) → Option C ✅
