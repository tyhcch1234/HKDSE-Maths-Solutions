HKDSE 2025 Paper 2 · Q17

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram. Let \(E\) and \(G\) be points lying on \(AB\) and \(DC\) respectively such that \(AE:BE = 1:3\) and \(CG:DG = 1:2\). It is given that \(EG\) produced and \(BC\) produced meet at the point \(H\). If the area of \(\triangle CGH\) is \(16\text{ cm}^2\), then the area of the quadrilateral \(DFEG\) is
A. \(36\text{ cm}^2\).   B. \(49\text{ cm}^2\).   C. \(56\text{ cm}^2\).   D. \(72\text{ cm}^2\).
Hint: Use ratios of areas of triangles with a common height (they are in the ratio of their bases), and similarity of triangles.
STEP 1 · Set the lengths

\(ABCD\) is a parallelogram, so \(AB = DC\).
\(AE:BE = 1:3\) and \(CG:DG = 1:2\).
Let \(AE = 3k,\ BE = 9k,\ CG = 4k,\ DG = 8k\) (then \(DC = 12k = AB\), \(1:3\) and \(1:2\) hold).

STEP 2 · Similar triangles give the area ratio

Since \(DC \parallel AB\), \(\triangle HGC \sim \triangle HEB\).
\(\dfrac{CG}{BE} = \dfrac{4}{9}\)
\(\dfrac{[CGH]}{[BEH]} = \left(\dfrac{CG}{BE}\right)^2 = \left(\dfrac{4}{9}\right)^2 = \dfrac{16}{81}\)
With \([CGH]=16\), \([BEH]=81\text{ cm}^2\).

STEP 3 · Area of trapezium CGEB

\([BEH] = [CGEB] + [CGH]\)
\(81 = [CGEB] + 16 \Rightarrow [CGEB] = 65\text{ cm}^2\).

STEP 4 · Split the trapezium

\(\triangle BGC\) and \(\triangle GEB\) have the same height, so \([BGC]:[GEB] = CG:BE = 4:9\).
\([CGEB] = [BGC] + [GEB] = 65\)
\(\Rightarrow [BGC]=20\text{ cm}^2,\quad [GEB]=45\text{ cm}^2\).

STEP 5 · Areas of \(\triangle EDG\) and \(\triangle DAE\)

\([EDG]:[BGC] = DG:CG = 2:1 \Rightarrow [EDG] = 40\text{ cm}^2\).
\([DAE]:[GEB] = AE:BE = 1:3 \Rightarrow [DAE] = 15\text{ cm}^2\).

STEP 6 · Split \(\triangle DAE\) at \(F\)

\(\triangle EDF\) and \(\triangle EAF\) have the same height, so \([EDF]:[EAF] = DF:AF\).
\([DAE] = [EDF] + [EAF] = 15\) and \(DF:AF = 3:2\)
\(\Rightarrow [EDF] = 9\text{ cm}^2,\quad [EAF] = 6\text{ cm}^2\).

STEP 7 · Final area of quadrilateral DFEG

\([DFEG] = [EDF] + [EDG] = 9 + 40 = 49\text{ cm}^2\).

FINAL ANSWER Area of quadrilateral \(DFEG = 49\text{ cm}^2\) — option B.
