Regular polygons — interior & exterior angles, reflectional symmetry
Polygon Angles Interior + Exterior = 180° Exterior Angle = 360°/n Axes of Symmetry

QQuestion

If an interior angle of a regular \(n\)-sided polygon is greater than an exterior angle by \(100^\circ\), which of the following are true?

I.  The value of \(n\) is \(10\).
II.  Each exterior angle of the polygon is \(40^\circ\).
III. The number of axes of reflectional symmetry of the polygon is \(9\).

A. I and II only    B. I and III only    C. II and III only    D. I, II and III

1Setting Up the Angle Equation

For any regular polygon, an interior angle and its adjacent exterior angle are supplementary. Let the exterior angle be \(e\).

10
Exterior angle 36.0° Interior angle 144.0° Interior − Exterior 108.0°

Move the slider: the difference (Interior − Exterior) equals \(180^\circ - 2e\). It hits \(100^\circ\) only when \(n = 10\).

Step 1

Let each exterior angle be \(e\). Then each interior angle is \(180^\circ - e\).

Hint: interior and exterior angles on a straight line sum to \(180^\circ\).
Step 2

The interior angle exceeds the exterior angle by \(100^\circ\):

\[ (180^\circ - e) - e = 100^\circ \] \[ 180^\circ - 2e = 100^\circ \;\Rightarrow\; 2e = 80^\circ \]
Hint: "greater than … by 100" means subtract: interior − exterior = 100.
Step 3
\[ e = 40^\circ \]
Each exterior angle \(= 40^\circ\) ✔  (Statement II is TRUE)

2Finding the Number of Sides \(n\)

The exterior angles of any convex polygon sum to \(360^\circ\). For a regular polygon each exterior angle is \(\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}\).

10
Exterior angle \(360^\circ/n\) 36.0° Target \(40^\circ\) ⟹ \(n =\) 9

Only at \(n = 10\) does the exterior angle equal exactly \(40^\circ\).

Step 1

Use the exterior-angle sum formula:

\[ \frac{360^\circ}{n} = 40^\circ \]
Hint: every exterior angle of a regular polygon is \(360^\circ \div n\).
Step 2
\[ 360^\circ = 40^\circ \times n \;\Rightarrow\; n = 9 \]
Check: \(9 \times 40^\circ = 360^\circ\)
\(n = 9\) — so Statement I ("\(n=10\)") is FALSE.
0 / 2

3Axes of Reflectional Symmetry

For a regular polygon with \(n\) sides, the number of axes of reflectional symmetry equals \(n\).

9
Sides \(n\) 9 Axes of symmetry 9 Statement III says 9 ⟹ TRUE (since n = 9)

For an \(n\)-gon, there are exactly \(n\) axes of reflectional symmetry. With \(n = 9\), the polygon has 9 axes — but wait, Statement III is really about the polygon with the given angle condition.

Step 1

The polygon satisfying the condition has \(n = 9\) (from Part 2). A regular \(n\)-gon has exactly \(n\) axes of reflectional symmetry.

Hint: a regular polygon has one axis through each vertex and the midpoint of the opposite side (or opposite vertex, depending on parity), giving \(n\) axes in total.
Step 2

Number of axes \(= n = 9\).

9 axes — so Statement III is TRUE.
0 / 2

Summary & Answer

  • Let exterior angle \(= e\). Then interior \(= 180^\circ - e\).
  • Condition: \((180^\circ - e) - e = 100^\circ \Rightarrow e = 40^\circ\).  Statement II ✓ TRUE
  • \(n = \dfrac{360^\circ}{40^\circ} = 9\), so \(n \neq 10\).  Statement I ✗ FALSE
  • A regular \(9\)-gon has \(9\) axes of reflectional symmetry.  Statement III ✓ TRUE
  • Therefore only Statements II and III are true.
Answer: C (II and III only)
