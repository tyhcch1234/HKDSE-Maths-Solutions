Geometry (106) · Sub-Topic: Angles in Triangles/Polygons

T11 Q09

The four angles of a quadrilateral are \(x\), \(2x\), \(3x\) and \(4x\). Form an equation in \(x\).

Solution

The sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral is $360$.
Adding the four given angles and setting the sum equal to $360$ gives $x+2x+3x+4x=360$.
Combining like terms simplifies the equation to $10x=360$, so $x=36$.
The required equation is $x+2x+3x+4x=360$, which simplifies to $10x=360$, giving $x=36$.
