Recurrence Sequences

Question

Let \(a_n\) be the \(n\)th term of a sequence. If \(a_2=7\), \(a_5=35\) and \(a_{n+2}=3a_{n+1}-2a_n\) for any positive integer \(n\), then \(a_8=\)

  • A. \(67\)
  • B. \(131\)
  • C. \(259\)
  • D. \(515\)

Solution

General form of the sequence
Characteristic Equation of a Linear Recurrence \(r^{2}-3r+2=0\Rightarrow r=1\ \text{or}\ 2\) Trying \(a_n=r^{n}\) in \(a_{n+2}=3a_{n+1}-2a_{n}\) gives \(r^{2}-3r+2=0\).

The characteristic equation of \(a_{n+2}=3a_{n+1}-2a_n\) is

\[ r^2-3r+2=0\ \Rightarrow\ (r-1)(r-2)=0\ \Rightarrow\ r=1\ \text{or}\ 2 \]

So \(a_n=A+B2^n\) for constants \(A, B\).

Use the given terms
Solving Simultaneous Equations by Elimination \(A+4B=7,\qquad A+32B=35\) Subtracting the first from the second gives \(28B=28\), so \(B=1\) and \(A=3\).
\[ a_2=A+4B=7,\qquad a_5=A+32B=35 \]

Subtracting: \(28B=28\Rightarrow B=1\), and then \(A=7-4=3\).

Find \(a_8\)
Evaluating a General Term \(a_{8}=3+2^{8}=259\) With \(a_n=3+2^{n}\), \(a_{8}=3+256=259\), consistent with \(a_2=7\) and \(a_5=35\).

Thus \(a_n=3+2^n\), and

\[ a_8=3+2^8=3+256=259 \]

Check: \(a_2=7\) ✓, \(a_5=35\) ✓.

Final Answer: C
