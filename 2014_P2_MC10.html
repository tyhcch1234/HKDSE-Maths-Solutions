Sector Area Percentage Change

Question 10 · Multiple Choice

Question

If the angle and the radius of a sector are decreased by $x\%$ and $50\%$ respectively so that its area is decreased by $90\%$, then $x =$

θ r
60%
Original Area
100%
New Area
10%
Target
10%

Adjust $x$ until the new area equals the target (10% of original).

Step-by-Step Solution

Click each step to expand

A sector is a "slice of a circle". Its area depends on the angle $\theta$ and the radius $r$:

$$A = \pi r^2 \times \frac{\theta}{360^\circ}$$

Where $\theta$ is in degrees and $r$ is the radius.

Divide both sides by $r^2$ and $A$ to isolate the constant:

$$\frac{A}{r^2} = \frac{\pi \theta}{360^\circ}$$

Equivalently:

$$\frac{r^2 \theta}{A} = \frac{360^\circ}{\pi} = \text{constant}$$

This ratio stays the same before and after the change.

Let the original angle be $\theta$ and the original area be $A$. After the changes:

  • New angle: $\theta(1 - x\%)$
  • New radius: $r(1 - 50\%) = 0.5r$
  • New area: $A(1 - 90\%) = 0.1A$

The ratio remains constant, so:

$$\frac{[r(1 - 50\%)]^2 \cdot \theta(1 - x\%)}{A(1 - 90\%)} = \frac{r^2 \theta}{A}$$

Substitute the percentage values ($50\% = 0.5$, $90\% = 0.9$):

$$\frac{(0.5)^2 \cdot (1 - \frac{x}{100})}{0.1} = 1$$

Simplify:

$$0.25 \left(1 - \frac{x}{100}\right) = 0.1$$

Divide both sides by $0.25$:

$$1 - \frac{x}{100} = 0.4$$

Therefore:

$$\frac{x}{100} = 0.6 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 60$$

Answer: C ($x = 60$)

The angle of the sector must be decreased by 60% so that the area decreases by 90% when the radius is halved.
