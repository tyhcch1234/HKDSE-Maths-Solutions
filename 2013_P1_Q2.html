HKDSE 2013 · Paper 1 · Q2 5. Formulas and Sequences
[3 marks] Make $k$ the subject of the formula $$ \frac{3}{h} - \frac{1}{k} = 2 . $$
1Keep the term containing $k$ alone
Subtraction Property of Equality \(A-B=C\;\Longrightarrow\;A=C+B\) Add \(\dfrac{3}{h}\) to both sides so that only the term containing \(k\) stays on the left.
$$ \frac{3}{h} - \frac{1}{k} = 2 $$
Move $\dfrac{3}{h}$ to the right-hand side by subtracting it from both sides:
$$ -\frac{1}{k} = 2 - \frac{3}{h} $$
Hint: only the $k$-term should remain on the left. Watch the sign — the $\frac{1}{k}$ term becomes negative.
2Make the reciprocal $\dfrac{1}{k}$ positive
$$ \frac{1}{k} = \frac{3}{h} - 2 $$
Multiply both sides by $-1$ (equivalent: move everything to the other side).
Hint: this step just flips all the signs so that $\frac{1}{k}$ stands alone and positive.
3Combine the right-hand side into a single fraction
Common Denominator \(\dfrac{a}{b}-\dfrac{c}{b}=\dfrac{a-c}{b}\) Write \(2\) as \(\dfrac{2h}{h}\) so that both terms can be combined over \(h\).
$$ \frac{1}{k} = \frac{3}{h} - \frac{2h}{h} = \frac{3 - 2h}{h} $$
Write $2$ as $\dfrac{2h}{h}$ so both terms share the denominator $h$,
then subtract.
Hint: a single fraction makes the reciprocal step in Step 4 safe and clean.
4Take reciprocals of both sides
Reciprocal of a Fraction \(\dfrac{1}{k}=\dfrac{a}{b}\;\Longrightarrow\;k=\dfrac{b}{a}\) Taking reciprocals of both sides inverts the fraction, which isolates \(k\).
$$ k = \frac{h}{3 - 2h} $$
Hint: if $\dfrac{1}{k}=\dfrac{a}{b}$ then $k=\dfrac{b}{a}$ — just turn both fractions upside down.
Answer: $k = \dfrac{h}{3 - 2h}$

Question. Make \(m\) the subject of the formula \(\dfrac{5}{p}-\dfrac{1}{m}=3\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Subtract \(\dfrac{5}{p}\) from both sides: \(-\dfrac{1}{m}=3-\dfrac{5}{p}\).
Step 2. Multiply both sides by \(-1\): \(\dfrac{1}{m}=\dfrac{5}{p}-3\).
Step 3. Combine over a common denominator: \(\dfrac{1}{m}=\dfrac{5-3p}{p}\).
Step 4. Take reciprocals: \(m=\dfrac{p}{5-3p}\). Check with \(p=2\): the formula gives \(-2\),
and \(\dfrac{5}{2}-\dfrac{1}{-2}=3\) ✓.
Answer: \(m=\dfrac{p}{5-3p}\)
