Formulas and Sequences

Question

Make \(k\) the subject of the formula:

\[ \frac{3}{h} - \frac{1}{k} = 2 \]

[3 marks]

1

Isolate the term containing \(k\)

We want \(k\) alone. First, move the term \(\frac{3}{h}\) to the right-hand side by subtracting it from both sides.

\[ -\frac{1}{k} = 2 - \frac{3}{h} \]

Multiply both sides by \(-1\) to make the left side positive:

\[ \frac{1}{k} = \frac{3}{h} - 2 \]
💡 Hint: Remember the goal: get \(k\) out of the denominator. We first collect the term with \(k\) on one side.
2

Combine the right-hand side into one fraction

Write \(2\) as \(\frac{2h}{h}\) so both terms have the same denominator \(h\):

\[ \frac{1}{k} = \frac{3}{h} - \frac{2h}{h} = \frac{3 - 2h}{h} \]
💡 Hint: To combine fractions, find a common denominator. Here the common denominator is \(h\).
3

Take the reciprocal of both sides

Since \(\frac{1}{k} = \frac{3 - 2h}{h}\), we take the reciprocal of both sides to solve for \(k\):

\[ k = \frac{h}{3 - 2h} \]

Note: This is valid as long as \(h \neq 0\) and \(3 - 2h \neq 0\).

💡 Hint: If \(\frac{1}{k} = \frac{a}{b}\), then \(k = \frac{b}{a}\). This is called "taking the reciprocal".

Final Answer

\[ k = \frac{h}{3 - 2h} \]
