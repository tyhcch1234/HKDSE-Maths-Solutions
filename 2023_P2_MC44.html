Statistics — Standard Score (z-score)
Mean μ Standard Deviation σ Standard Score Simultaneous Equations Multiple Choice

QQuestion (2023MC#44)

The table below shows the scores (in marks) and the corresponding standard scores of three students in an examination.

Score (marks)46\(x\)86
Standard score\(-3\)\(1\)\(2\)

Find \(x\).

A. 64
B. 66
C. 70
D. 78

1Setting up the equations — find μ and σ

Let \(\mu\) be the mean and \(\sigma\) be the standard deviation of the scores. The standard score of a score \(s\) is defined as

\[ z = \frac{s-\mu}{\sigma}. \]

Using the first and third students, we can form two equations.

μ = 70
σ = 8
Line (1) for 46: z = (-3.00)
Line (3) for 86: z = (2.00)
Ideal z: (46−70)/8 = −3, (86−70)/8 = 2
Step 11M

Write the standard score formula for the first student (score 46, z-score −3):

\[ \frac{46-\mu}{\sigma} = -3 \;\Longrightarrow\; \mu - 3\sigma = 46 \quad \cdots (1) \]
Step 21M

Write the standard score formula for the third student (score 86, z-score 2):

\[ \frac{86-\mu}{\sigma} = 2 \;\Longrightarrow\; \mu + 2\sigma = 86 \quad \cdots (3) \]
Step 31A

Solve (1) and (3) simultaneously. Subtract (1) from (3):

\[ 5\sigma = 40 \;\Longrightarrow\; \sigma = 8 \]

Substitute back into (1):

\[ \mu = 46 + 3(8) = 70 \]
Subtracting the two equations cancels μ, leaving a simple equation in σ. Always solve for σ first.
\(\mu = 70\) and \(\sigma = 8\).
0 / 3

2Using the second student to find \(x\)

Now we know \(\mu = 70\) and \(\sigma = 8\). The second student has score \(x\) and standard score \(1\).

x = 78
Standard score = (x − μ)/σ = (78 − 70)/8 = 1.00
Target z-score = 1
✓ Matched!
Step 11M

Write the standard score formula for the second student (score \(x\), z-score \(1\)):

\[ \frac{x-\mu}{\sigma} = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = \mu + \sigma \quad \cdots (2) \]
Step 21A

Substitute \(\mu = 70\) and \(\sigma = 8\) into (2):

\[ x = 70 + 8 = 78 \]
A student with z-score \(1\) is exactly one standard deviation above the mean.
\(x = 78\) → option D.
0 / 2

✓Summary of Answers

  • Mean of the examination scores: \(\mu = \mathbf{70}\)
  • Standard deviation: \(\sigma = \mathbf{8}\)
  • The unknown score: \(x = \mu + \sigma = \mathbf{78}\)
  • Correct option: D
