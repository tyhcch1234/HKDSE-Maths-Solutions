Simplify: \[ (27 \cdot 9^{\,n+1})^3 \]
A. \(3^{6n+12}\)
B. \(3^{6n+15}\)
C. \(3^{9n+12}\)
D. \(3^{9n+18}\)

Step-by-step solution

1 Express 27 and 9 as powers of 3 ⌄

We rewrite every number with base \(3\):

\(27 = 3^3\)
\(9 = 3^2\)
💡 Hint: Identify the common base. Since \(3^3=27\) and \(3^2=9\), base \(3\) is the natural choice.
2 Substitute with base 3 ⌄

Replace \(27\) and \(9\) in the original expression:

\[ (27 \cdot 9^{\,n+1})^3 = (3^3 \cdot (3^2)^{\,n+1})^3 \]
💡 Hint: Use \(9 = 3^2\), so \(9^{\,n+1} = (3^2)^{\,n+1}\).
3 Apply power of a power rule ⌄

Simplify \((3^2)^{\,n+1}\) using \((a^m)^n = a^{m \cdot n}\):

\[ (3^2)^{\,n+1} = 3^{\,2(n+1)} = 3^{\,2n+2} \]

Now the inside becomes:

\[ 3^3 \cdot 3^{\,2n+2} \]
💡 Hint: Multiply the exponents: \(2 \times (n+1) = 2n+2\).
4 Combine the indices ⌄

Using \(a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}\):

\[ 3^3 \cdot 3^{\,2n+2} = 3^{\,3 + 2n+2} = 3^{\,2n+5} \]

So the expression inside the outer bracket is \(3^{\,2n+5}\).

💡 Hint: Add the exponents: \(3 + (2n+2) = 2n+5\).
5 Apply the outer exponent 3 ⌄

Recall the original expression had an outer power of \(3\):

\[ \bigl(3^{\,2n+5}\bigr)^3 = 3^{\,3(2n+5)} = 3^{\,6n+15} \]
💡 Hint: Multiply the exponents: \(3 \times (2n+5) = 6n+15\).
6 Conclusion & match with option ⌄

The simplified form is:

\[ 3^{6n+15} \]

Compare with the choices:

  • A. \(3^{6n+12}\) – incorrect
  • B. \(3^{6n+15}\) – correct ✓
  • C. \(3^{9n+12}\) – incorrect
  • D. \(3^{9n+18}\) – incorrect
✅ Answer: B
Answer: B (\(3^{6n+15}\))
