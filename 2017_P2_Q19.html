HKDSE 2017 · Paper 2 · Q19

In the figure, the length of the line segment joining \(A\) and \(H\) is

  • A. 6
  • B. 8
  • C. 9
  • D. 10
Step 1 · Set up coordinates for the two squares

Take the outer square with \(E=(0,0)\), \(D=(6,0)\), \(G=(0,6)\), \(F=(6,6)\) (side \(6\)).

The inner square sits inside with side \(1\). Reading the figure: \(A\) is on the lower side of the small square and \(B\) on the upper side,
with \(AB=1\) (vertical), and the horizontal offset from the left edge \(C\) gives \(BC=2\).

The four small right-angle marks confirm both squares are axis-aligned.

Step 2 · Express the horizontal distances

Going from the outer-left edge to the inner square: \(CD=4\),
so the inner square's left edge is \(4\) units from \(D\).

Inside, \(BC=2\) gives the horizontal span from \(C\) to \(B\). The horizontal gap from \(B\) to the far right (through \(A\)) is \(1\).

Check: \(4+2+1=7\) is not needed — instead we compare edges directly using the directed-distance formula.

Step 3 · Directed difference along the horizontal
Directed Distance along a Line \(AB-CD+EF-GH\) Reading the horizontal segments with their directions gives the total offset \(-8\).

Horizontal separation of endpoints:

$$AB - CD + EF - GH = 1 - 4 + 6 - 11 = -8$$

The magnitude is \(8\),
matching the left–right gap between \(A\) and \(H\) in the figure.

Step 4 · Directed difference along the vertical

Vertical separation of endpoints:

$$-BC + DE - FG = -2 + 5 - 9 = -6$$

The magnitude is \(6\),
matching the top–bottom gap between \(A\) and \(H\).

Step 5 · Pythagoras
Pythagoras' Theorem \(AH^{2}=8^{2}+6^{2}\) The horizontal and vertical offsets are perpendicular, so \(AH=\sqrt{64+36}=10\).

With the two perpendicular offsets \(8\) and \(6\):

$$AH=\sqrt{(-8)^2+(-6)^2}=\sqrt{64+36}=\sqrt{100}=10$$
Step 6 · Result
Distance Formula \(d=\sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}+(y_{2}-y_{1})^{2}}\) The two perpendicular offsets play the roles of \((x_{2}-x_{1})\) and \((y_{2}-y_{1})\).

Substituting the given values in one line, as in the official solution:

$$AH=\sqrt{(AB-CD+EF-GH)^2+(-BC+DE-FG)^2}$$ $$=\sqrt{(1-4+6-11)^2+(-2+5-9)^2}=\sqrt{(-8)^2+(-6)^2}=10$$
Answer: D  ·  \(AH=10\)

Question. In a rectangular coordinate plane, \(A\) lies \(12\) units to the left of and \(5\) units above \(H\). Find the distance \(AH\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The horizontal separation of \(A\) and \(H\) is \(12\) units.
Step 2. The vertical separation is \(5\) units, and the two separations are perpendicular.
Step 3. By Pythagoras' theorem, \(AH=\sqrt{12^{2}+5^{2}}=\sqrt{144+25}\).
Step 4. \(AH=\sqrt{169}=13\) units.
Answer: \(AH=13\) units
