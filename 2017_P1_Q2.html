HKDSE Math 2017 Paper 1 Q2 Laws of Indices Exponential & Logarithmic Functions 3 marks

Simplify the following expression and express your answer with positive indices.

\[ \frac{(m^{4}n^{-1})^{3}}{(m^{-2})^{5}} \]
1

Apply the power of a product rule to the numerator

▼

For the numerator \((m^{4}n^{-1})^{3}\), apply \((ab)^n = a^n b^n\) and \((a^m)^n = a^{mn}\):

\[ (m^{4}n^{-1})^{3} = m^{4 \times 3} \cdot n^{-1 \times 3} = m^{12} n^{-3} \]
💡
Hint: Power of a product: \((ab)^n = a^n b^n\).
Power of a power: \((a^m)^n = a^{mn}\). Multiply the exponents.
2

Simplify the denominator using the power of a power rule

▼

For the denominator \((m^{-2})^{5}\), multiply the exponents:

\[ (m^{-2})^{5} = m^{-2 \times 5} = m^{-10} \]
💡
Hint: The power rule \((a^m)^n = a^{mn}\) works for negative exponents too. Here \(-2 \times 5 = -10\).
3

Combine using the quotient rule for indices

▼

Now the expression becomes:

\[ \frac{m^{12} n^{-3}}{m^{-10}} \]

Apply the quotient rule \(\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}\) to the base \(m\):

\[ m^{12 - (-10)} \cdot n^{-3} = m^{12 + 10} n^{-3} = m^{22} n^{-3} \]
💡
Hint: Quotient rule: \(\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}\).
Subtracting a negative is the same as adding, so \(12 - (-10) = 22\).
4

Rewrite with positive indices

▼

To express with positive indices, use \(a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}\):

\[ n^{-3} = \frac{1}{n^{3}} \]

Therefore the final simplified expression is:

\[ \boxed{\frac{m^{22}}{n^{3}}} \]
💡
Hint: Negative index rule: \(a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}\). Move the base with a negative exponent to the denominator (or numerator) to make it positive.

Final Answer

\[ \dfrac{m^{22}}{n^{3}} \]
