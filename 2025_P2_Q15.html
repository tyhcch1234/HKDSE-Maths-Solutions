2025 MC #15 Mensuration — Sector & Triangle

The perimeter of the sector OMN is $12\pi$ cm, where $O$ is the centre of the sector OMN. If the radius of the sector OMN is $3\pi$ cm, which of the following are true?

I. The area of the sector OMN is $9\pi^2$ cm$^2$.
II. The perimeter of $\triangle OMN$ is less than $35$ cm.
III. The angle of the sector OMN is greater than $100^\circ$.
A. I and II only B. I and III only C. II and III only D. I, II and III
Step 1 · Find the angle θ

The perimeter of the sector = arc length $MN$ + two radii.

$$2\pi(3\pi)\times\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} + 3\pi + 3\pi = 12\pi$$

$$6\pi^2 \cdot \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} = 12\pi - 6\pi$$

$$6\pi^2 \cdot \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} = 6\pi$$

$$\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} = \frac{6\pi}{6\pi^2} = \frac{1}{\pi}$$

$$\theta = \frac{360^\circ}{\pi} \approx 114.59^\circ$$

💡 The arc length formula: $\text{arc} = 2\pi r \times \frac{\theta}{360^\circ}$. Here $r = 3\pi$.
Arc Length of a Sector \(\text{arc}=2\pi r\times\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}\) The perimeter is the arc plus the two radii, so the arc is isolated first.
Step 2 · Check Statement I (Area)

Area of sector $OMN$:

$$= \pi(3\pi)^2 \times \frac{\theta}{360^\circ}$$

Since $\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} = \frac{1}{\pi}$:

$$= \pi \cdot 9\pi^2 \cdot \frac{1}{\pi} = 9\pi^2 \text{ cm}^2$$

✓ TRUE Statement I is correct.

💡 The $\pi$ in the denominator cancels with one $\pi$ from $\pi(3\pi)^2 = 9\pi^3$.
Area of a Sector \(\pi r^{2}\times\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}\) Once \(\tfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}\) is known, the sector area follows in one line.
Step 3 · Check Statement III (Angle > 100°)

From Step 1, $\theta = \frac{360^\circ}{\pi}$.

Since $\pi \approx 3.14159 < 3.6$, we get:

$$\theta = \frac{360^\circ}{\pi} > \frac{360^\circ}{3.6} = 100^\circ$$

Numerically, $\theta \approx 114.59^\circ > 100^\circ$.

✓ TRUE Statement III is correct.

💡 Compare $\pi$ with $3.6$: since $\pi < 3.6$, dividing $360°$ by a smaller number gives a larger angle.
Step 4 · Check Statement II (Perimeter of △OMN < 35 cm)

Use the cosine formula in $\triangle OMN$ with $OM = ON = 3\pi$:

$$MN^2 = (3\pi)^2 + (3\pi)^2 - 2(3\pi)(3\pi)\cos\!\left(\frac{360^\circ}{\pi}\right)$$

$$MN^2 = 9\pi^2 + 9\pi^2 - 18\pi^2 \cos\!\left(\frac{360^\circ}{\pi}\right)$$

$$MN^2 = 18\pi^2\left[1 - \cos\!\left(\frac{360^\circ}{\pi}\right)\right]$$

$$MN \approx 15.86135438 \text{ cm}$$

Perimeter of $\triangle OMN$:

$$= MN + OM + ON \approx 15.86135438 + 3\pi + 3\pi$$

$$\approx 34.71091031 \text{ cm} < 35 \text{ cm}$$

✓ TRUE Statement II is correct.

💡 Note that $\frac{360^\circ}{\pi}$ is in degrees. If using a calculator, ensure degree mode for the cosine.
Cosine Formula \(MN^{2}=OM^{2}+ON^{2}-2(OM)(ON)\cos\theta\) The chord \(MN\) of the sector is the third side of the isosceles triangle \(OMN\).
✅ All three statements are true → D. I, II and III

Question. A sector \(OMN\) of radius \(6\text{ cm}\) has perimeter \(21\text{ cm}\). Find the angle \(\theta\) of the sector and the area of the sector.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Arc \(MN=21-2(6)=9\text{ cm}\).
Step 2. \(2\pi(6)\times\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=9\), so \(\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=\dfrac{9}{12\pi}=\dfrac{3}{4\pi}\) and \(\theta=\dfrac{270^{\circ}}{\pi}\approx 85.9^{\circ}\).
Step 3. Area \(=\pi(6)^{2}\times\dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}}=36\pi\times\dfrac{3}{4\pi}=27\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Step 4. Check: arc \(=2\pi(6)\times 27/(360)=9\) when the fraction is \(\tfrac{3}{4\pi}\) ✓
Answer: \(\theta=\dfrac{270^{\circ}}{\pi}\approx 85.9^{\circ}\); area \(=27\text{ cm}^{2}\)
