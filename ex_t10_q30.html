Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Volume

T10 Q30

Find the volume of a cube with side length $8$ cm.

Solution

The volume of a cube is given by $V=s^3$, where $s$ is the side length.
Substitute $s=8$ cm into the formula: $V=8^3$.
Calculate $8^3=512$.
Therefore, the volume is $512\text{ cm}^3$.
The volume of the cube is $512\text{ cm}^3$.
