Circle Theorems

Question

In the figure, \(ABCD\) are points on a circle. \(AC\) and \(BD\) intersect at the point \(E\). \(BD\) is produced to the point \(T\) such that \(TC\) is tangent to the circle. It is given that \(AB\parallel TC\). If \(\angle AED=85^\circ\) and \(\angle CTE=23^\circ\), then \(\angle ADE=\)

  • A. \(39^\circ\)
  • B. \(56^\circ\)
  • C. \(62^\circ\)
  • D. \(72^\circ\)

Solution

Parallel \(AB\parallel TC\), transversal \(BD\Rightarrow \angle ABD=\angle CTE=23^\circ\).
Triangle ABE \(\angle AEB=180^\circ-85^\circ=95^\circ\), \(\angle ABE=23^\circ\Rightarrow \angle BAE=62^\circ\), so \(\angle BAC=62^\circ\).
Parallel again \(AB\parallel TC\), transversal \(AC\Rightarrow \angle TCA=\angle BAC=62^\circ\).
Alternate segment Tangent–chord angle \(\angle TCA=\) angle in the alternate segment \(=\angle CBA\Rightarrow \angle ABC=62^\circ\).
Angle ACB In \(\triangle ABC\): \(\angle ACB=180^\circ-62^\circ-62^\circ=56^\circ\).
Same arc AB C and D lie on the same (major) arc \(AB\), so \(\angle ADB=\angle ACB=56^\circ\). Hence \(\angle ADE=56^\circ\).
Answer: B — 56°
