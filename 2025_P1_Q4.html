Axes / grid Point A (4,−2) Point B (−2,−4) Point C (−2,t−4) Rotation 90° CW
Question 4. The coordinates of the point A are $(4,-2)$. $A$ is rotated clockwise about the origin $O$ through $90^\circ$ to $B$.
(a) Write down the coordinates of $B$.
(b) Suppose that $B$ is translated upwards by $t$ units to the point $C$. If $A$, $O$ and $C$ are collinear, find $t$.
(3 marks)
STEP 1 · Understand the rotation

A clockwise rotation of $90^\circ$ about the origin maps a point $(x,y)$ to:

$$(x,\,y)\;\longrightarrow\;(y,\,-x)$$

Clockwise about $O$: $x' = x\cos(-90^\circ)-y\sin(-90^\circ)=y$,
  $y' = x\sin(-90^\circ)+y\cos(-90^\circ)=-x$.

STEP 2 · Find B
Rotation about the Origin \((x,y)\to(y,-x)\ \text{for }90^\circ\text{ clockwise}\) A quarter turn clockwise about the origin swaps the coordinates and changes the sign of the new \(y\)-coordinate.

Apply the rule to $A(4,-2)$:

$$B = (\,-2,\,-4\,)$$

(a) B(−2, −4)

STEP 3 · Translate to C
Translation of a Point \((x,y)\to(x+h,\ y+k)\) Moving a point vertically adds the same amount to its \(y\)-coordinate only.

Moving $B(-2,-4)$ upwards by $t$ units adds $t$ to the $y$-coordinate:

$$C=(-2,\;-4+t)=(-2,\;t-4)$$

STEP 4 · Collinearity condition
Slope Formula \(m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\) A rotation about the origin keeps \(O\), \(A\) and \(C\) on one line, so their slopes are equal.

$A$, $O$, $C$ are collinear.
The line through $O(0,0)$ and $A(4,-2)$ has slope

$$m_{OA}=\frac{-2-0}{4-0}=-\frac12$$

Since $C$ lies on the same line,
its slope from $O$ must equal $-\tfrac12$:

$$\frac{t-4-0}{-2-0}=-\frac12$$

STEP 5 · Solve for t
Collinear Points (Equal Slopes) \(m_{OA}=m_{OC}\ \Leftrightarrow\ O,A,C\text{ are collinear}\) Equate the two slopes to form an equation in the unknown.

$$\frac{t-4}{-2}=-\frac12$$

$$t-4=1$$

$$t=5$$

(b) t = 5

Check: $C(-2,\,5-4)=(-2,1)$. Slope $OC=\frac{1}{-2}=-\frac12$ ✓ — same line as $OA$.

Question. \(A(3,-1)\) is rotated clockwise through \(90^\circ\) about the origin \(O\) to \(B\), and then \(B\) is translated to the right by \(s\) units to \(C\). If \(O\), \(A\) and \(C\) are collinear, find \(s\). (3 marks)

Show the worked solution
1. Clockwise \(90^\circ\) about \(O\): \((x,y)\to(y,-x)\), so \(B=(-1,-3)\).
2. Translate to the right by \(s\): \(C=(-1+s,\ -3)\).
3. Slope of \(OA\) is \(\dfrac{-1-0}{3-0}=-\dfrac{1}{3}\).
4. Since \(O\), \(A\), \(C\) are collinear, \(\dfrac{-3}{-1+s}=-\dfrac{1}{3}\), so \(s-1=9\).
5. Hence \(s=10\).
Answer: \(s=10\)
