Q4 The Question

Total: 3 marks

The coordinates of the point \( A \) are \( (4,\,-2) \). \( A \) is rotated clockwise about the origin \( O \) through \( 90^\circ \) to \( B \).

(a) Write down the coordinates of \( B \). (1 mark)

(b) Suppose that \( B \) is translated upwards by \( t \) units to the point \( C \). If \( A \), \( O \) and \( C \) are collinear, find \( t \). (2 marks)

a Part (a) — Finding the coordinates of \( B \)

Rotate \( A(4,-2) \) clockwise about \( O \) through \( 90^\circ \).

90°
A = (4.00, −2.00) B = (−2.00, −4.00) |OA| = √(4²+2²) = √20 OA ⟂ OB
0 / 3
Step 1

A \( 90^\circ \) clockwise rotation about the origin maps a point by the rule

\[ (x,\,y)\;\longmapsto\;(y,\,-x). \]

(Swap the coordinates, then negate the new \(y\)-coordinate.)

Step 2 1A

Apply the rule to \( A(4,-2) \):

\[ B = (y,\,-x) = (-2,\,-4). \]
Answer: \( B = (-2,\,-4) \)
Step 3 Check

Quick sanity check with the dot product:

\[ \overrightarrow{OA}\cdot\overrightarrow{OB} = (4)(-2)+(-2)(-4) = -8+8 = 0. \]

So \( OA \perp OB \), and \( |OA|=|OB|=\sqrt{20} \) — exactly what a \(90^\circ\) rotation gives.

Hint: The rotation is clockwise, so \((x,y)\to(y,-x)\). For an anti-clockwise \(90^\circ\) rotation the rule would be \((x,y)\to(-y,x)\) — do not mix them up!

b Part (b) — Finding \( t \) when \( A,\,O,\,C \) are collinear

Translate \( B(-2,-4) \) upwards by \( t \) units: \( C = (-2,\; t-4) \).

0.0
C = (−2.00, −4.00) slope OA = −0.50 slope OC = 2.00 Collinear? No
0 / 3
Step 1 1M

Translate \( B(-2,-4) \) upwards by \( t \) units: only the \(y\)-coordinate increases.

\[ C = (-2,\; -4 + t) = (-2,\; t-4). \]
Step 2

Since \(A\), \(O\) and \(C\) are collinear, the slope of \(OC\) equals the slope of \(OA\).

\[ \text{slope of }OA = \frac{-2-0}{4-0} = -\frac{1}{2}, \] \[ \text{slope of }OC = \frac{(t-4)-0}{-2-0} = -\frac{t-4}{2}. \]

Set them equal:

\[ -\frac{t-4}{2} = -\frac{1}{2} \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; t-4 = 1. \]
Step 3 1A

Solve the linear equation:

\[ t = 1+4 = 5. \]

Check: \( C = (-2,\;5-4) = (-2,\,1) \). Its slope from \(O\) is

\[ \frac{1}{-2} = -\frac{1}{2} = \text{slope of }OA. \]
Answer: \( t = 5 \), and \( C = (-2,\,1) \).
Hint: "Collinear with the origin" means the two points lie on the same straight line through \(O\). Using equal slopes is the fastest route; alternatively use \(\dfrac{y_1}{x_1}=\dfrac{y_2}{x_2}\): \(\dfrac{-2}{4}=\dfrac{t-4}{-2}\), giving \(t=5\).

★ Summary of Answers

  • (a) \( B = (-2,\,-4) \)  (1 mark)
  • (b) \( C = (-2,\;t-4) \); using equal slopes gives \( t - 4 = 1 \),
  • so \( \boxed{t = 5} \)  (2 marks)
  • Key facts: \(90^\circ\) clockwise rotation \( (x,y)\mapsto(y,-x) \); collinear points through the origin share the same \( \dfrac{y}{x} \) ratio.
