Q11Mock 1HKDSE 2019 P1 Q116 marks

Let $p(x)$ be a cubic polynomial. When $p(x)$ is divided by $x-1$, the remainder is $51$. When $p(x)$ is divided by $x+3$, the remainder is $-85$. It is given that $p(x)$ is divisible by $2x^{2}+7x+8$.

(a)Find the quotient when $p(x)$ is divided by $2x^{2}+7x+8$.(3 marks)
(b)How many rational roots does the equation $p(x)=0$ have? Explain your answer.(3 marks)
Answer: (a) $5x-2$   (b) 1 rational root
Show solution
(a)

Let $ax+b$ be the required quotient, where $a$ and $b$ are constants.

$$p(x)=(ax+b)(2x^{2}+7x+8)$$1M

Note that $p(1)=51$ and $p(-3)=-85$.

$$[a(1)+b]\,[2(1)^{2}+7(1)+8]=51 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 17(a+b)=51$$
$$[a(-3)+b]\,[2(-3)^{2}+7(-3)+8]=-85 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 5(b-3a)=-85$$1M

So $a+b=3$ and $b-3a=-17$, giving $a=5$ and $b=-2$.

$\therefore$ The required quotient is $5x-2$.1A

(b)
$$p(x)=0 \;\Rightarrow\; (5x-2)(2x^{2}+7x+8)=0 \;\Rightarrow\; 5x-2=0 \ \text{ or }\ 2x^{2}+7x+8=0$$

For $2x^{2}+7x+8=0$, the discriminant is

$$\Delta=7^{2}-4(2)(8)=49-64=-15<0$$1M

$\therefore$ The equation $2x^{2}+7x+8=0$ has no real roots, so it contributes no rational root.1M

Note that $x=\dfrac{2}{5}$ is a rational root of $p(x)=0$.

$\therefore$ The equation $p(x)=0$ has $1$ rational root.1A
