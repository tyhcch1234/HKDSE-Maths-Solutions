Question Multiple Choice

If \( f(x) = 3x^{2} - 2x + 1 \), then \( f(2m-1) = \)

A. \(6m^{2}-4m+2\)
B. \(6m^{2}-4m+6\)
C. \(12m^{2}-16m+6\)
D. \(12m^{2}-16m+2\)

Choose the correct answer. (No marks are awarded in Paper 2; the solution is worked step by step below.)

Step A Substitute \(x = 2m-1\)

We replace every \(x\) in \(f(x)=3x^{2}-2x+1\) by the expression \((2m-1)\).

1.0
…
Step 1Substitution
\[ f(2m-1) = 3(2m-1)^{2} - 2(2m-1) + 1 \]
Hint: The whole bracket \((2m-1)\) plays the role of \(x\). Keep the bracket — do not expand \(x\) yet.
Step 2Expand \((2m-1)^2\)
\[ (2m-1)^{2} = 4m^{2} - 4m + 1 \]
Hint: Use the identity \((a-b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2\) with \(a=2m,\; b=1\).
Step 3Expand fully
\[ \begin{aligned} f(2m-1) &= 3(4m^{2}-4m+1) - 2(2m-1) + 1 \\ &= 12m^{2} - 12m + 3 - 4m + 2 + 1 \end{aligned} \]
Hint: Distribute the 3 and the \(-2\) carefully. Watch the signs: \(-2(2m-1) = -4m + 2\).
Simplified form: \(12m^{2} - 16m + 6\) — this matches option C.

Step B Verify with a numerical check

Pick a value of \(m\), compute \(f(2m-1)\) directly from \(f(x)\), and compare with \(12m^2-16m+6\).

Step 1Numerical check
Take \(m = 1\): \[ x = 2(1)-1 = 1,\qquad f(1) = 3(1)^2 - 2(1) + 1 = 2 \]
Hint: This is the value we expect from the answer.
Step 2Compare
\[ 12(1)^{2} - 16(1) + 6 = 12 - 16 + 6 = 2 \;\checkmark \]
Hint: Both sides give 2, so option C is consistent. Options A, B, D give 4, 8 and \(-2\) respectively — all wrong.

Summary of Answers

  • Target expression: \(f(2m-1) = 3(2m-1)^{2} - 2(2m-1) + 1\)
  • Expanded form: \(12m^{2} - 16m + 6\)
  • Correct option: C — \(12m^{2} - 16m + 6\)
  • Key technique: substitute the whole bracket, expand \((a-b)^2\), then collect like terms.
