Question

There are 8 Chinese books and 7 English books in a box. If 5 books are randomly chosen from the box at the same time, find the probability that at most 3 Chinese books are chosen.

Step-by-Step Solution

Total books = 8 C + 7 E = 15 books.

We choose 5 books. The total number of ways to choose any 5 books from 15 is:

\( C_5^{15} = C^{15}_{5} = 3003 \)

Hint: Combination formula \( C_r^n = C^{n}_{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!} \) counts groups where order does not matter.

"At most 3 Chinese books" means 0, 1, 2, or 3 Chinese books.

The complement is easier: 4 or 5 Chinese books (i.e., more than 3).

\( P(\text{at most 3 C}) = 1 - P(4\text{ C}) - P(5\text{ C}) \)

Hint: Since the maximum Chinese books is 8, but we only choose 5, the only impossible-to-ignore cases are exactly 4 or exactly 5 Chinese books.

To get exactly 4 Chinese books, we choose:

  • 4 Chinese from 8: \( C_4^8 \)
  • 1 English from 7: \( C_1^7 \)

Number of favourable outcomes:

\( C_4^8 \cdot C_1^7 = 70 \cdot 7 = 490 \)

Probability:

\( P(4\text{ C}) = \frac{C_4^8 \cdot C_1^7}{C_5^{15}} = \frac{490}{3003} = \frac{70}{429} \)

Hint: Simplify \( \frac{490}{3003} \) by dividing numerator and denominator by 7.

To get exactly 5 Chinese books, we choose:

  • 5 Chinese from 8: \( C_5^8 \)
  • 0 English from 7: \( C_0^7 = 1 \)

Number of favourable outcomes:

\( C_5^8 \cdot C_0^7 = 56 \cdot 1 = 56 \)

Probability:

\( P(5\text{ C}) = \frac{C_5^8}{C_5^{15}} = \frac{56}{3003} = \frac{8}{429} \)

Hint: Simplify \( \frac{56}{3003} \) by dividing by 7.

Required probability:

\( P(\le 3\text{ C}) = 1 - \frac{70}{429} - \frac{8}{429} \)

\( = 1 - \frac{78}{429} = \frac{429 - 78}{429} = \frac{351}{429} \)

Simplify \( \frac{351}{429} \): divide both by 39.

\( = \frac{9}{11} \)

Answer: B \(\displaystyle \frac{9}{11}\)
