Question

Factorize

(a)   $4m^2 - 25n^2$,

(b)   $4m^2 - 25n^2 + 6m - 15n$.

[3 marks]

🎯 Core Idea: Recognize a difference of two squares pattern, then spot a common factor in the remaining terms so the whole expression can be factorized.

Visual Insight — The Difference of Two Squares

The blue square has area $(2m)^2 = 4m^2$; the red square has area $(5n)^2 = 25n^2$. Adjust the sliders to see how $4m^2 - 25n^2$ equals the shaded L-shape, which factorizes as $(2m-5n)(2m+5n)$.

Step-by-Step Solution

✅ Final Answers

(a)   $4m^2 - 25n^2 = (2m-5n)(2m+5n)$

(b)   $4m^2 - 25n^2 + 6m - 15n = (2m-5n)(2m+5n+3)$
