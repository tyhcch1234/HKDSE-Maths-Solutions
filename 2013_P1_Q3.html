terms from $(2m)^2$ / $(2m)$ terms from $(5n)^2$ / $(5n)$ common factor $(2m-5n)$ final answer

Step 1 · Identify the difference of two squares

[2013 #3] · 3 marks

Factorize

(a) $4m^{2}-25n^{2}$,

(b) $4m^{2}-25n^{2}+6m-15n$.

1

Part (a) — Spot the difference of two squares

Difference of Two Squares \(A^{2}-B^{2}=(A-B)(A+B)\) Here \(4m^{2}-25n^{2}=(2m)^{2}-(5n)^{2}\), so \(A=2m\) and \(B=5n\).

Write each term as a perfect square:

$4m^{2}=(2m)^{2}$  and  $25n^{2}=(5n)^{2}$.

So $4m^{2}-25n^{2}=(2m)^{2}-(5n)^{2}$, which has the form $A^{2}-B^{2}$ with $A=2m$, $B=5n$.

Hint: $A^{2}-B^{2}=(A-B)(A+B)$ always. First force both terms into square form.
2

Part (a) — Apply $A^{2}-B^{2}=(A-B)(A+B)$

$=(2m-5n)(2m+5n)$

The two brackets are identical except for the sign in the middle — that is the signature of the difference of two squares.
3

Part (b) — Use the result of (a) on the first two terms

$4m^{2}-25n^{2}+6m-15n$

$=\underline{(2m-5n)}(2m+5n)+6m-15n$

Key trick: reuse part (a)'s answer. The aim is to make the remaining terms share the same bracket.
4

Part (b) — Factor out $3$ from the last two terms

$6m-15n=3(2m-5n)$

$=\underline{(2m-5n)}(2m+5n)+3\underline{(2m-5n)}$

HCF of $6m$ and $15n$ is $3$. Notice $6m-15n$ is exactly $3\times$(the common bracket).
5

Part (b) — Take out the common factor $(2m-5n)$

Common Factor Law \(AB+AC=A(B+C)\) Both groups contain the bracket \((2m-5n)\), so it can be taken out in front.

$=(2m-5n)\big[(2m+5n)+3\big]$

$=(2m-5n)(2m+5n+3)$

Grouping done: both groups share $(2m-5n)$, so factor it out in front of a single pair of brackets.
6

Check & Answer

Expand to verify: $(2m-5n)(2m+5n+3)=4m^{2}-25n^{2}+6m-15n$ ✓

Answer (a) $(2m-5n)(2m+5n)$
(b) $(2m-5n)(2m+5n+3)$

Question. Factorize (a) \(9p^{2}-16q^{2}\), (b) \(9p^{2}-16q^{2}+6p-8q\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(9p^{2}=(3p)^{2}\) and \(16q^{2}=(4q)^{2}\), so part (a) has the form \(A^{2}-B^{2}\).
Step 2. (a) \(9p^{2}-16q^{2}=(3p-4q)(3p+4q)\).
Step 3. (b) Using (a) and grouping: \((3p-4q)(3p+4q)+6p-8q\), and \(6p-8q=2(3p-4q)\).
Step 4. \(=(3p-4q)\big[(3p+4q)+2\big]=(3p-4q)(3p+4q+2)\).
Answer: (a) \((3p-4q)(3p+4q)\) (b) \((3p-4q)(3p+4q+2)\)
