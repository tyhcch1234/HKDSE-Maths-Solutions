[2012 #9] In Figure 2, the volume of the solid right prism ABCDEFGH is $1020\text{ cm}^3$. The base $ABCD$ of the prism is a trapezium, where $AD$ is parallel to $BC$. It is given that $\angle BAD = 90^\circ$, $AB = 12\text{ cm}$, $BC = 6\text{ cm}$ and $DE = 10\text{ cm}$.

Find
(a) the length of $AD$,
(b) the total surface area of the prism $ABCDEFGH$.

SET UP

The shape at a glance

The prism stands on the trapezium $ABCD$ with $AD \parallel BC$ and $\angle BAD = 90^\circ$. The parallel sides of the base are therefore $AD$ and $BC = 6$ cm, and the distance between them is $AB = 12$ cm.

The prism is right, so every lateral edge is perpendicular to the base and all lateral edges are equal: $DE = 10$ cm is the prism height.

Volume of a prism $=$ (base area) $\times$ height.

STEP 1 · Part (a)

Find $AD$ from the volume

Base ABCD is a trapezium with parallel sides $AD=x$ and $BC=6$, height $AB=12$:

$$\text{Area of }ABCD=\frac{(x+6)(12)}{2}$$

Volume of prism $=$ base area $\times DE$:

$$\frac{(x+6)(12)}{2}\times 10 = 1020$$ $$6(x+6)\times 10 = 1020 \;\Rightarrow\; 60(x+6)=1020$$ $$x+6=17 \;\Rightarrow\; x=11$$
$AD = 11\text{ cm}$
Area of a Trapezium \(A=\dfrac{(a+b)\,h}{2}\) \(a\) and \(b\) are the two parallel sides \(AD\) and \(BC\); \(h\) is the perpendicular distance \(AB\) between them.
Volume of a Prism \(V=(\text{area of base})\times(\text{height})\) The base is the trapezium \(ABCD\); the height is the lateral edge \(DE\).
STEP 2 · Part (b) helper

Find the slant side $CD$

Draw $C$ dropped vertically to meet $AD$; the horizontal difference is $AD - BC = 11 - 6 = 5$ cm and the vertical difference is $AB = 12$ cm. By Pythagoras:

$$CD=\sqrt{12^{2}+(11-6)^{2}}=\sqrt{144+25}=\sqrt{169}=13\text{ cm}$$
$CD = 13\text{ cm}$
Pythagoras' Theorem \(c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}\) Drop a perpendicular from \(C\) to \(AD\): the legs are \(12\) (vertical) and \(11-6=5\) (horizontal).
STEP 3 · Part (b)

Total surface area

Perimeter of the base $ABCD$:

$$12+11+13+6=42\text{ cm}$$

Lateral surface area $=$ perimeter $\times$ prism height:

$$42\times 10 = 420\text{ cm}^2$$

Two identical trapezoidal ends:

$$2\times\frac{(6+11)(12)}{2}=132\text{ cm}^2$$

Total surface area:

$$(12+11+13+6)(10)+2\times\frac{(6+11)(12)}{2}$$ $$=420+132=624\text{ cm}^2$$
Total surface area $= 624\text{ cm}^2$
Total Surface Area of a Prism \(A=(\text{perimeter of base})\times h+2\times(\text{area of base})\) The four rectangular lateral faces have total area (perimeter) × height; the two ends are identical trapeziums.
CHECK

Consistency & final answers

Base area $=\frac{(11+6)(12)}{2}=102$ cm², times height $10$ gives $1020\text{ cm}^3$ ✓ (matches the given volume).

All four lateral faces are rectangles: $AB\cdot10=120$, $BC\cdot10=60$, $CD\cdot10=130$, $AD\cdot10=110$, sum $=420$ cm² ✓.

(a) $AD = 11\text{ cm}$  ·  (b) $624\text{ cm}^2$

Question. \(ABCDEF\) is a right prism whose base \(ABCD\) is a trapezium with \(AD\parallel BC\), \(\angle BAD=90^{\circ}\), \(BC=8\text{ cm}\) and \(AB=9\text{ cm}\). The height of the prism is \(12\text{ cm}\) and its volume is \(1512\text{ cm}^{3}\). (a) Find \(AD\). (b) Find \(CD\) and the total surface area of the prism.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Volume \(=\) base area \(\times\) height: \(1512=(\text{area of }ABCD)\times12\), so the base area is \(126\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Step 2. Area of trapezium: \(\dfrac{(AD+8)(9)}{2}=126\), so \(AD+8=28\) and \(AD=20\text{ cm}\).
Step 3. Drop a perpendicular from \(C\) to \(AD\): the legs are \(9\) and \(20-8=12\), so \(CD=\sqrt{9^{2}+12^{2}}=\sqrt{225}=15\text{ cm}\).
Step 4. Perimeter of base \(=9+20+15+8=52\text{ cm}\); lateral area \(=52\times12=624\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Step 5. Two trapezoidal ends: \(2\times126=252\text{ cm}^{2}\). Total \(=624+252=876\text{ cm}^{2}\).
Answer: (a) \(AD=20\) cm. (b) \(CD=15\) cm, total surface area \(=876\text{ cm}^{2}\).
