Mensuration — volume & total surface area of a right prism with a trapezoidal base

Volume of a Prism Trapezoidal Base Pythagoras' Theorem Total Surface Area Right Prism

Q Question 5 marks

In Figure 2, the volume of the solid right prism \(ABCDEFGH\) is \(1020\text{ cm}^3\). The base \(ABCD\) of the prism is a trapezium, where \(AD\) is parallel to \(BC\). It is given that \(\angle BAD = 90^\circ\), \(AB = 12\text{ cm}\), \(BC = 6\text{ cm}\) and \(DE = 10\text{ cm}\).

Find

(a) the length of \(AD\),    2 marks

(b) the total surface area of the prism \(ABCDEFGH\).    3 marks

a Part (a) — Finding the length of \(AD\) 2 marks

Set \(AD = x\text{ cm}\). The prism stands on the trapezium, and every cross-section parallel to the base is that same trapezium. So volume \(=\) (area of base) \(\times\) height.

Interactive trapezium base \(ABCD\) (left) and the volume model \(V=\text{base area}\times DE\) (right).
base area = — cm² volume = — cm³ target = 1020 cm³ gap = — cm³
Step 11M

Let \(x\text{ cm}\) be the length of \(AD\). Since \(AD \parallel BC\) and \(AB \perp AD\), the base \(ABCD\) is a trapezium whose parallel sides are \(AD=x\) and \(BC=6\), with height \(AB=12\).

\[ \text{Area of } ABCD=\frac{(x+6)(12)}{2}=6(x+6)\ \text{cm}^2 \]
Hint: The height of the trapezium is the perpendicular distance between the parallel sides \(AD\) and \(BC\), which is exactly \(AB=12\) cm because \(\angle BAD=90^\circ\).
Volume of prism: \(V=\text{area of base}\times\text{height}=6(x+6)\times 10\).
Step 21M

Set the volume equal to the given value \(1020\text{ cm}^3\) and solve for \(x\):

\[ \frac{(6+x)(12)}{2}\,(10)=1020 \] \[ 60(6+x)=1020 \] \[ 6+x=17 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=11 \]
Check: base area \(=6(11+6)=102\ \text{cm}^2\), and \(102\times 10=1020\ \text{cm}^3\). ✓ Drag the slider to \(AD=11\) and watch gap become \(0\).
Step 31A

The solution is \(x=11\), therefore the length of \(AD\) is \(11\text{ cm}\).

\(\boxed{AD = 11\text{ cm}}\)

b Part (b) — Total surface area of \(ABCDEFGH\) 3 marks

The total surface area is the sum of the areas of the two trapezoidal ends and the four rectangular lateral faces.

The base trapezium showing \(CD\) as the slanted side, with the right triangle \(CD\) drop and the "unfolded" lateral faces.
\(CD\) = — cm perimeter = — cm LSA = — cm² base area = — cm² TSA = — cm²
Step 11M

Find \(CD\), the slanted side of the trapezium, using Pythagoras' theorem. Drop a perpendicular from \(C\) to \(AD\); the horizontal difference is \(AD-BC=11-6=5\) and the vertical difference is \(AB=12\).

\[ \begin{aligned} CD &= \sqrt{12^2+(11-6)^2} \\ &= \sqrt{144+25} \\ &= \sqrt{169} \\ &= 13\text{ cm} \end{aligned} \]
Hint: The "shift" between the two parallel sides is \(AD-BC=5\) cm, and the perpendicular gap is \(AB=12\) cm. These two legs give the hypotenuse \(CD\).
Step 21M

The lateral surface unfolds into one rectangle whose length is the perimeter of the base and whose width is the prism height \(DE=10\).

\[ \text{Perimeter of } ABCD = 12+11+13+6 = 42\text{ cm} \] \[ \text{Lateral surface area} = 42 \times 10 = 420\text{ cm}^2 \]
Why perimeter? Each lateral face is a rectangle of height 10 sitting on one base edge \(AB,\,BC,\,CD,\,DA\). Adding the four face areas \(=10(AB+BC+CD+DA)\).
Step 31A

Add the two trapezoidal end faces. One end has area \(\dfrac{(6+11)(12)}{2}=102\text{ cm}^2\), and there are two identical ends:

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Total S.A.} &= (12+11+13+6)(10) + \frac{(6+11)(12)}{2}\times 2 \\ &= 420 + 204 \\ &= 624\text{ cm}^2 \end{aligned} \]
Structure: \(\text{TSA}=(\text{perimeter}\times\text{height}) + 2\times(\text{base area})\). Moving the \(AD\) slider keeps this formula live above.
\(\boxed{\text{Total surface area} = 624\text{ cm}^2}\)
0 / 3

Summary of Answers

  • (a) \(AD = \dfrac{1020}{10\cdot\frac{12}{2}} - 6 = 11\text{ cm}\), i.e. \(AD = 11\text{ cm}\)
  • (b) \(CD=\sqrt{12^2+(11-6)^2} = \sqrt{169} = \sqrt{144+25} = 13\text{ cm}\)
  • (b) Perimeter of base \(= 12+11+13+6 = 42\text{ cm}\)
  • (b) Lateral surface area \(=42\times 10 = 420\text{ cm}^2\)
  • (b) Two ends \(=2\times\dfrac{(6+11)(12)}{2}=204\text{ cm}^2\)
  • (b) Total surface area \(=420+204 = 624\text{ cm}^2\)
