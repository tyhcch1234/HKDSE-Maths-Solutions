Mensuration — Area of a shaded region inside a semicircle

Q Question Multiple Choice

[2013MC#16]

In the figure, the diameter of the semicircle \(ABC\) is \(3\text{ cm}\).

If \(AC = 2\text{ cm}\), find the area of the shaded region correct to the nearest \(0.01\text{ cm}^2\).

A. \(0.23\text{ cm}^2\)
B. \(0.52\text{ cm}^2\)
C. \(0.64\text{ cm}^2\)
D. \(1.07\text{ cm}^2\)

The shaded region is the part of the semicircle cut off by the chord \(AC\) — i.e. a circular segment on chord \(AC\).

a Part (a) — Set up: find the radius, the apothem and the central angle

2.00 cm
Radius \(OA = OB = OC\) = 1.50 cm
Apothem \(OM=\sqrt{OA^2-AM^2}\) = 0.559 cm
\(\sin\angle AOM = \dfrac{AM}{OA}\) = 0.667
\(\angle AOC = 2\angle AOM\) = 83.6°

Drag the slider to change the chord \(AC\) — watch how the apothem \(OM\) and the central angle \(\angle AOC\) respond. The actual question uses \(AC = 2\) cm.

Step 11M

Let \(O\) be the centre of the semicircle. Since \(ABC\) is a semicircle with diameter \(3\text{ cm}\), the radius is

\[ OA = OB = OC = \frac{3}{2}\text{ cm} = 1.5\text{ cm}. \]
Hint: In any semicircle, the radius is half the diameter. Every point on the arc is exactly one radius from the centre \(O\).
Step 21M

Let \(M\) be the mid-point of the chord \(AC\). Since \(OA=OC\) (radii of the same circle), \(\triangle AOC\) is isosceles, so the median from \(O\) is also the altitude and the angle bisector:

\[ OM \perp AC \quad\text{and}\quad OM \text{ bisects } \angle AOC. \] \[ AM = MC = \frac{AC}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1\text{ cm}. \]
Hint: "Prop. of isos. \(\triangle\)": in an isosceles triangle, the median to the base is also perpendicular to the base and bisects the vertex angle.
Step 31M

Apply Pythagoras in right-angled \(\triangle AOM\):

\[ OM = \sqrt{OA^2 - AM^2} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2 - 1^2} = \sqrt{\frac{9}{4}-1} = \sqrt{\frac{5}{4}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\text{ cm}. \]
Hint: \(OM\) is the apothem — the perpendicular distance from the centre to the chord. Note \(\sqrt{5}/2 \approx 1.118\) cm \(< 1.5\) cm, which is geometrically consistent.
Step 41A

In \(\triangle AOM\),

\[ \sin\angle AOM = \frac{MA}{OA} = \frac{1}{3/2} = \frac{2}{3}. \]

Since \(OM\) bisects \(\angle AOC\),

\[ \angle AOC = 2\sin^{-1}\frac{2}{3} = 2 \times 41.81\ldots° = 83.62\ldots°. \]
Answers so far: \(OA=\tfrac{3}{2}\) cm, \(\;OM=\dfrac{\sqrt5}{2}\) cm, \(\;\angle AOC = 2\sin^{-1}\dfrac{2}{3} \approx 83.62°\).
Hint: Angle \(AOC\) corresponds to the minor arc \(AC\) — the sector \(OAC\) bounded by this angle is the sector whose area we shall use at the end.

b Part (b) — Compute the area of the shaded segment

2.00 cm
Sector area \(=\pi r^2\cdot\dfrac{\theta}{360°}\) = 1.642 cm²
Triangle area \(=\tfrac12\cdot AC\cdot OM\) = 1.118 cm²
Segment = Sector − Triangle = 0.524 cm²

Step 11M

Realise that the shaded region is a circular segment on chord \(AC\):

\[ \text{Shaded area} = \text{Sector } OAC - \triangle AOC. \]
Hint: A "segment" is the region between a chord and the arc it subtends. Always attack it as sector − triangle.
Step 21M

Sector \(OAC\): the sector radius is \(OA = \tfrac{3}{2}\) cm and the central angle is \(\angle AOC = 2\sin^{-1}\tfrac{2}{3}\).

\[ A_{\text{sector}} = \pi \cdot OA^2 \times \frac{\angle AOC}{360°} = \pi\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2 \times \frac{2\sin^{-1}\frac{2}{3}}{360°} \] \[ = \pi\cdot\frac{9}{4}\times \frac{83.62\ldots°}{360°} \approx 1.642\ \text{cm}^2. \]
Hint: Make sure your calculator is in degree mode for the fraction \(\frac{\theta}{360°}\), but \(\sin^{-1}\frac{2}{3}\) returns an angle you convert to degrees before dividing.
Step 31M

Triangle \(AOC\): base \(AC = 2\) cm, height \(OM = \tfrac{\sqrt5}{2}\) cm.

\[ A_{\triangle AOC} = \frac{1}{2}\cdot AC \cdot OM = \frac{1}{2}(2)\left(\frac{\sqrt5}{2}\right) = \frac{\sqrt5}{2} \approx 1.118\ \text{cm}^2. \]
Hint: Using \(\frac12 \cdot AC \cdot OM\) is efficient because \(OM\perp AC\) is exactly the height to base \(AC\).
Step 41A

Subtract the triangle from the sector:

\[ \text{Shaded} = 1.64189\ldots - 1.11803\ldots = 0.52385\ldots \approx 0.52\ \text{cm}^2 \]
The area of the shaded region is \(0.52\text{ cm}^2\) (correct to the nearest \(0.01\text{ cm}^2\)).
Hint: \(0.5238\ldots\) rounds to \(0.52\) because the third decimal digit is a \(3\). The correct option is B. Note how the distractors trap you: \(0.23\) (using \(2\times 1.5\times 0.559\) etc.), \(0.64\) (half the triangle), \(1.07\) (segment area \(\approx\) sector alone).

Summary — Answers at a glance

  • Semicircle radius: \(OA=OB=OC=\dfrac{3}{2}=1.5\text{ cm}\)
  • Apothem: \(OM = \dfrac{\sqrt5}{2} \approx 1.118\text{ cm}\)
  • Central angle: \(\angle AOC = 2\sin^{-1}\dfrac{2}{3} \approx 83.62°\)
  • Sector \(OAC\): \(A = \pi\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2\cdot\dfrac{\angle AOC}{360°} \approx 1.642\text{ cm}^2\)
  • Triangle \(AOC\): \(A = \dfrac12\cdot AC\cdot OM = \dfrac{\sqrt5}{2} \approx 1.118\text{ cm}^2\)
  • Shaded segment \(= 1.64189\ldots - 1.11803\ldots \approx 0.52\text{ cm}^2\)
  • Choice: B  (0.52 cm²)
