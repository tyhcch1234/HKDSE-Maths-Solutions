Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Distance/Slope

T12 Q01

Find the distance between the points $(1,2)$ and $(4,6)$.

Solution

Use the distance formula $d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$.
Substitute $(x_1,y_1)=(1,2)$ and $(x_2,y_2)=(4,6)$: $d=\sqrt{(4-1)^2+(6-2)^2}$.
Simplify: $d=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{9+16}=\sqrt{25}=5$.
The distance between $(1,2)$ and $(4,6)$ is $5$ units.
