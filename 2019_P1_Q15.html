Counting the possible committees

Total students: 21 boys + 11 girls = 32 students. Choose 5.

Boys (21) Girls (11) Chosen committee (5) Rejected case (all girls)
HKDSE 2019 · Paper 1 · Q15 · Probability
There are $21$ boys and $11$ girls in a class. If $5$ students are selected from the class to form a committee consisting of at least $1$ boy, how many different committees can be formed?
[3 marks]

1Find the total number of students

Boys $=21$, girls $=11$, so the class has \[21+11=32\ \text{students.}\] A committee is a selection of $5$ students from these $32$.
Hint: a committee is just a combination — order does not matter.

2Count all possible 5-person committees

Combinations \({}^{n}C_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}\) All committees of \(5\) from \(32\) students number \({}^{32}C_{5}=201\,376\).
With no restriction, choosing any $5$ from the $32$ students gives \[C_5^{32}={}^{32}C_{5}=\frac{32!}{5!\,27!}=201\,376.\]
Use ${}^{n}C_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$.

3Subtract the "no boy" committees

Complementary Counting \(n(A)=n(S)-n(A^{\prime})\) Excluding the all-girl committees \({}^{11}C_{5}=462\) from the total leaves the committees with at least \(1\) boy.
"At least $1$ boy" means we exclude the committees with no boys, i.e. all $5$ members chosen from the $11$ girls: \[C_5^{11}={}^{11}C_{5}=\frac{11!}{5!\,6!}=462.\] These are the only rejected cases.
Complementary counting: (all cases) − (bad cases).

4Apply the complement and simplify

\[ C_5^{32}-C_5^{11}=201\,376-462=200\,914. \]
Answer is between the two prior numbers, as expected.
Answer: $\;C_5^{32}-C_5^{11}=200\,914$   committees.
PRACTICESimilar question — try it yourself

Question. There are \(18\) boys and \(12\) girls in a class. If \(4\) students are selected from the class to form a committee consisting of at least \(1\) girl, how many different committees can be formed?

Show the worked solution
Step 1. The class has \(18+12=30\) students, so the total number of \(4\)-person committees is \({}^{30}C_{4}=27\,405\).
Step 2. The rejected committees contain no girl, i.e. all \(4\) members are boys: \({}^{18}C_{4}=3060\).
Step 3. Hence the number with at least \(1\) girl is \(27\,405-3060=24\,345\).
Answer: \(24\,345\) committees
