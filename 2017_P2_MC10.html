Compound Interest – Half‑yearly Compounding

Percentages Compound Interest Interest Calculation

Question

A sum of $2000 is deposited at an interest rate of 5% per annum for 4 years, compounded half‑yearly. Find the interest correct to the nearest dollar.

A. $400
B. $431
C. $437
D. $440

Step-by-Step Solution

The compound interest formula is:

$$ A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} $$

Where:

  • \(A\) = final amount
  • \(P\) = principal (initial deposit)
  • \(r\) = annual interest rate (as a decimal)
  • \(n\) = number of times interest is compounded per year
  • \(t\) = time in years
💡 Hint – What does each symbol represent?

P = the starting money ($2000).

r = the yearly interest rate written as a decimal (5% = 0.05).

n = how many times per year the bank adds interest (half‑yearly → 2 times).

t = the number of years the money stays in the bank (4 years).

From the question we have:

  • \(P = 2000\)
  • \(r = 5\% = 0.05\)
  • \(n = 2\) (half‑yearly means twice a year)
  • \(t = 4\)

Half‑yearly means interest is added twice a year, so each period earns half of the annual rate:

$$ \text{Periodic rate} = \frac{r}{n} = \frac{0.05}{2} = 0.025 $$

The total number of compounding periods over 4 years is:

$$ nt = 2 \times 4 = 8 $$

Substitute the values into \( A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt} \):

$$ A = 2000\left(1 + \frac{0.05}{2}\right)^{2 \times 4} $$
$$ A = 2000(1.025)^{8} $$
$$ A \approx 2000 \times 1.2184029 = 2436.8058 $$

The interest earned is:

$$ \text{Interest} = A - P = 2436.8058 - 2000 = 436.8058 $$

Correct to the nearest dollar:

$$ \text{Interest} \approx \$437 $$
✅ Therefore, the answer is C. $437
