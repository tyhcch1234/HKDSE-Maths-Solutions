Rounding & Approximation: Decimal Places vs Significant Figures

Choose the statement that correctly rounds the number \(0.0023456789\).

Estimation & Approximation Significant Figures Decimal Places Rounding Errors

QThe Question

A0.00235  (correct to 6 decimal places)

Key point: the words “decimal places” and “significant figures” count completely different things. Misreading which one is asked is the classic trap in this question.

Decimal places are counted after the decimal point: \(0.\underbrace{0\,0\,2\,3\,4\,5\,6\,7\,8\,9}_{\text{10 places}}\).

Hint: The decimal point itself is not a place. Every digit written to the right of the point — zeros included — occupies one decimal place.

The \(7^{\text{th}}\) decimal place is \(6 \ge 5\), so we round up the \(6^{\text{th}}\) decimal place:

\(0.002345\,\big|\,\mathbf{6}\ldots \;\longrightarrow\; 0.002346\)

So \(0.0023456789 \approx 0.002346\) (to 6 d.p.).

Rounding rule: look at the first digit you are dropping. If it is \(5,6,7,8,9\) → round up (add \(1\) to the last kept digit). If it is \(0,1,2,3,4\) → round down (leave the last kept digit unchanged).

The first digit to be dropped is the \(7^{\text{th}}\) significant figure, which is \(8 \ge 5\), so we round up:

\(0.0023456\,\big|\,\mathbf{7}\ldots \;\longrightarrow\; 0.0023457\)

So \(0.0023456789 \approx 0.0023457\) (to 6 s.f.)?

Hint: Leading zeros only act as place-holders — they are never counted as significant figures. Always locate the first non-zero digit first.

Wait! We must not confuse the digits. Let us list the number again carefully:

0 . 0 0 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9

The significant figures are: \(2,\;3,\;4,\;5,\;6,\;7\) — that is 6 significant figures.

The digit after the \(6^{\text{th}}\) significant figure is \(8\), so we round the last kept digit \(7\) up to \(8\):

\(0.0023456\,\big|\,\mathbf{7}\,\mathbf{8}\,\mathbf{9}\ldots \;\longrightarrow\; 0.00234568\)

\(0.0023456789 = 0.00234568\) (correct to 6 significant figures).

Careful: If the digit immediately after the last kept digit is \(\ge 5\), round the last kept digit up by \(1\). Always look at only the first dropped digit, not all of them.

3Concept Summary

Decimal places (d.p.)
Count digits after the decimal point, including zeros. For \(0.0023456789\): the \(6^{\text{th}}\) decimal place is \(5\), the next digit is \(6\), so round up → \(0.002346\).
Significant figures (s.f.)
Start counting from the first non-zero digit. For \(0.0023456789\): the 6 significant figures are \(2,3,4,5,6,7\); the next digit is \(8\), so round up → \(0.00234568\).

Exam tip: Read the words after the number very carefully — “d.p.” and “s.f.” give different answers for the same number. Underline the phrase in the question before you calculate.
