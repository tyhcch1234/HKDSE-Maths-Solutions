Q5 · Polynomial Identity

MC · Algebra
Polynomials Identity Comparing Coefficients Substitution

If \(m\) and \(n\) are constants such that

\[ x^2 + mx + n \equiv (x + 4)(x - m) + 6 \]

then \(n =\)

🔍 Step-by-step Solution

\[ \begin{aligned} (x+4)(x-m)+6 &= x^2 - mx + 4x - 4m + 6 \\ &= x^2 + (-m+4)x + (-4m+6) \end{aligned} \]

💡 Hint: Expand the product first, then add the constant 6.

Since the two polynomials are identical for all \(x\), their corresponding coefficients must be equal.

LHS coefficient of \(x\): \(m\)
RHS coefficient of \(x\): \(-m+4\)
LHS constant term: \(n\)
RHS constant term: \(-4m+6\)

This gives the system:

\[ \begin{cases} m = -m + 4 & \text{(1)} \\[4pt] n = -4m + 6 & \text{(2)} \end{cases} \]

💡 Hint: Equate the coefficients of like terms.

\[ \begin{aligned} m &= -m + 4 \\ 2m &= 4 \\ m &= 2 \end{aligned} \]

💡 Hint: Add \(m\) to both sides, then divide by 2.

\[ \begin{aligned} n &= -4(2) + 6 \\ &= -8 + 6 \\ &= -2 \end{aligned} \]

✅ Therefore \(n = -2\), which corresponds to Option B.
