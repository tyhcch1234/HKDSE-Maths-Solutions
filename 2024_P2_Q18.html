Question. In the figure, the points $A$, $B$, $C$ lie on a straight line. It is given that $BC=5\text{ cm}$, $BD=12\text{ cm}$, $CD=13\text{ cm}$, $AD=37\text{ cm}$ and $CE=9\text{ cm}$, with $\angle ABC=90^\circ$. Find the perimeter of the quadrilateral $ADCE$.

A. 76 cm    B. 90 cm    C. $40+13\sqrt{?}$    D. 180 cm

STEP 1 · Recognise the right angle in ΔCBD

Check the three sides of $\triangle CBD$ against the converse of Pythagoras' Theorem:

$$BC^2+BD^2=5^2+12^2=25+144=169=13^2=CD^2$$

Hence $\angle CBD=90^\circ$, and since $A,B,C$ are collinear, $\angle ABD=90^\circ$ too.

STEP 2 · Find AB using ΔABD

In right-angled $\triangle ABD$ ($\angle ABD=90^\circ$), by Pythagoras' Theorem:

$$AB^2+BD^2=AD^2$$ $$AB^2+12^2=37^2 \;\Rightarrow\; AB^2=1369-144=1225$$ $$AB=35\text{ cm}$$
STEP 3 · Find AC

As $A$, $B$, $C$ are collinear with $B$ between $C$ and $A$:

$$AC=CB+AB=5+35=40\text{ cm}$$
STEP 4 · Find AE using ΔACE

$\angle ACE=90^\circ$ (given), so in right-angled $\triangle ACE$:

$$AC^2+CE^2=AE^2$$ $$40^2+9^2=1600+81=1681=AE^2$$ $$AE=41\text{ cm}$$
STEP 5 · Add up the perimeter

Perimeter of $ADCE=AD+DC+CE+EA$

$$=37+13+9+41=100\text{ cm}$$
STEP 6 · Check & conclusion

All four sides are known side lengths in the figure, so no option A–D matches — the correct answer is

Perimeter of ADCE = 100 cm.
