2015MC#28Probability from a Bar Chart

The bar chart below shows the distribution of the numbers of keys owned by the students in a class.

Find the probability that a randomly selected student from the class owns 3 keys.

A. $\dfrac{1}{5}$
B. $\dfrac{2}{11}$
C. $\dfrac{4}{11}$
D. $\dfrac{9}{11}$
1
Read the frequency of each bar From the bar chart, the number of students for each number of keys is: \[ 1 \text{ key}: 12,\quad 2 \text{ keys}: 16,\quad 3 \text{ keys}: 8,\quad 4 \text{ keys}: 4,\quad 5 \text{ keys}: 4 \] Each bar height is read from the vertical axis (drawn every 4 students).
2
Find the total number of students in the class \[ 12 + 16 + 8 + 4 + 4 = 44 \] The total number of students is the denominator of the probability.
3
Identify the favourable outcome Number of students who own exactly 3 keys: \[ = 8 \] This is the numerator of the probability.
4
Apply the probability formula and simplify \[ P(3\text{ keys})=\frac{\text{favourable}}{\text{total}}=\frac{8}{44}=\frac{2}{11} \] Divide numerator and denominator by the common factor 4.
5
Match with the options \(\dfrac{2}{11}\) corresponds to option B. Always double-check the fraction is in simplest form before choosing.
Answer: B  =  $\dfrac{2}{11}$
