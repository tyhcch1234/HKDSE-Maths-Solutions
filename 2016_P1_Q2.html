HKDSE Math 2016 Paper 1 Q2

Topic 5 — Formulas and Sequences

Change of Subject Algebraic Manipulation Linear Equations

Make \(x\) the subject of the formula \[ Ax=(4x+B)C . \]

[3 marks]

1

Expand the bracket

Multiply \(C\) into the bracket \((4x+B)\):

\[ Ax = 4Cx + BC \]

Now \(x\) appears in two terms: \(Ax\) on the left and \(4Cx\) on the right.

💡 Hint Distributive law: \(a(b+c)=ab+ac\)
2

Collect \(x\)-terms on one side

Subtract \(4Cx\) from both sides to bring \(x\)-terms together:

\[ Ax - 4Cx = BC \]

The right side now contains no \(x\).

💡 Hint Subtracting the same quantity from both sides keeps the equation balanced.
3

Factorise the left side

Take \(x\) out as a common factor:

\[ (A - 4C)x = BC \]

Now \(x\) appears only once, multiplied by \((A-4C)\).

💡 Hint \(ax - bx = (a-b)x\)
4

Divide both sides

Divide by \((A - 4C)\) to isolate \(x\):

\[ x = \frac{BC}{A - 4C} \]

This is the required subject.

💡 Hint When dividing by an expression, assume it is not zero: \(A-4C \neq 0\).
Step 1 of 4

Final answer

\[ x = \frac{BC}{A - 4C} \]

Marks awarded: 1M (expand), 1M (collect & factorise), 1A (correct subject).
