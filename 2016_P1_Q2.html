HKDSE 2016 Paper 1 · Q2

Make \(x\) the subject of the formula \[ Ax=(4x+B)C. \] [3 marks]

1
Expand the bracket
Distributive Law\(C(4x+B)=4Cx+BC\)\(C\) multiplies both terms inside the bracket \(4x+B\).

Multiply the bracket \((4x+B)\) by \(C\) term by term:

\[ Ax=(4x+B)C \] \[ Ax=4Cx+BC \]
Hint: The \(1\)M mark is for expanding correctly — remember \(C\) multiplies both \(4x\) and \(B\).
2
Collect the \(x\)-terms on one side

Take \(4Cx\) onto the left-hand side:

\[ Ax-4Cx=BC \]
Hint: Whatever you do to one side, do to the other. The \(BC\) term stays on the right.
3
Factorise out \(x\)
Common Factor Law\(Ax-4Cx=(A-4C)x\)Both terms on the left contain \(x\), so it is taken outside the bracket.

Both terms on the left contain \(x\), so take it out as a common factor:

\[ (A-4C)x=BC \]
Hint: This factorising step is what lets us isolate \(x\) with a single division.
4
Divide by the coefficient of \(x\)
Division Property of Equality\(a=b\ \Rightarrow\ \dfrac{a}{c}=\dfrac{b}{c}\quad(c\neq0)\)Dividing by \(A-4C\) isolates \(x\) and gives \(\dfrac{BC}{A-4C}\).

Divide both sides by \((A-4C)\):

\[ x=\frac{BC}{A-4C} \]
Hint: The \(1\)A mark is for the fully simplified subject with the correct denominator.
Answer: \(\displaystyle x=\frac{BC}{A-4C}\)

Question. Make \(x\) the subject of the formula \(Px=(3x+Q)R\). [4 marks]

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Expand the right-hand side: \(Px=3Rx+QR\).
Step 2. Collect the \(x\)-terms on the left: \(Px-3Rx=QR\).
Step 3. Factorise out \(x\): \((P-3R)x=QR\).
Step 4. Divide by \(P-3R\): \(x=\dfrac{QR}{P-3R}\). Check with \(P=11\), \(Q=4\), \(R=2\): \(x=\dfrac{8}{5}=1.6\),
and both sides of \(11x=(3x+4)2\) equal \(17.6\) ✓.
Answer: \(x=\dfrac{QR}{P-3R}\)
