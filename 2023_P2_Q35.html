Readout: the parabola $y=-27(x+m)^2+5m^2$ (shown with $m=1$).

Parabola $y=-f(3x)$ Vertex / turning point Axis of symmetry $x=-m$

HKDSE 2023 · Paper 2 · Q35 (Functions and Graphs)

Let $f(x)=3x^{2}+18mx+22m^{2}$, where $m$ is a real constant. Which of the following statements about the graph of $y=-f(3x)$ must be true?

  • I.  The $x$-coordinate of the vertex of the graph is $m$.
  • II. The $y$-coordinate of the vertex of the graph is $5m^{2}$.
  • III. The equation of the axis of symmetry of the graph is $x+m=0$.
  • A.  I only
  • B.  II only
  • C.  I and III only
  • D.  II and III only
STEP 1Substitute $3x$ into $f$.

$$y=-f(3x)=-\bigl[\,3(3x)^{2}+18m(3x)+22m^{2}\,\bigr]$$ Since $3(3x)^2=27x^2$ and $18m(3x)=54mx$: $$y=-27x^{2}-54mx-22m^{2}$$

Replace every $x$ in $f(x)$ by $3x$, then take the negative of the whole expression.
STEP 2Factor out $-27$ and complete the square.
Completing the Square \(x^{2}+2mx=(x+m)^{2}-m^{2}\) Adding and subtracting \(m^{2}\) inside the bracket produces a perfect square.

$$y=-27\bigl(x^{2}+2mx\bigr)-22m^{2}$$ Add and subtract $m^{2}$ inside the bracket: $$y=-27\bigl(x^{2}+2mx+m^{2}-m^{2}\bigr)-22m^{2}$$ $$y=-27\bigl(x+m\bigr)^{2}+27m^{2}-22m^{2}$$ $$\boxed{\,y=-27(x+m)^{2}+5m^{2}\,}$$

$x^{2}+2mx=(x+m)^{2}-m^{2}$ — the perfect-square trick with half of the coefficient of $x$.
STEP 3Read off the vertex from $y=a(x-h)^{2}+k$.
Vertex Form \(y=a(x-h)^{2}+k\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{vertex }(h,k)\) Once the expression is in vertex form, the vertex and the axis of symmetry read off directly.

Here $a=-27$, $h=-m$, $k=5m^{2}$, so the vertex is $$\bigl(-m,\;5m^{2}\bigr)$$ Therefore the $x$-coordinate of the vertex is $-m$, not $m$ — so I is false.
The $y$-coordinate of the vertex is $5m^{2}$ — so II is true.

In $y=a(x-h)^2+k$ the vertex is $(h,k)$; beware of the minus sign inside the bracket.
STEP 4Axis of symmetry.

The axis of symmetry is the vertical line through the vertex: $$x=-m\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x+m=0$$ So III is true.

Axis of symmetry: $x=h$ where $h$ is the $x$-coordinate of the vertex.
STEP 5Check each statement.
I ✗$x$-coordinate of vertex $=-m$ (given as $m$) → false II ✓$y$-coordinate of vertex $=5m^{2}$ → true III ✓Axis of symmetry $x+m=0$ → true
Only II and III hold for every real $m$.
Answer: D  (II and III only) $y=-27(x+m)^{2}+5m^{2}$ ⇒ vertex $(-m,\,5m^{2})$, axis of symmetry $x=-m$.

Question. Let \(f(x)=2x^{2}-8mx+5m^{2}\), where \(m\) is a real constant. Find the vertex and the axis of symmetry of the graph of \(y=-f(2x)\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Replace \(x\) by \(2x\): \(f(2x)=2(2x)^{2}-8m(2x)+5m^{2}=8x^{2}-16mx+5m^{2}\).
Step 2. So \(y=-f(2x)=-8x^{2}+16mx-5m^{2}=-8\left(x^{2}-2mx\right)-5m^{2}\).
Step 3. Complete the square: \(x^{2}-2mx=(x-m)^{2}-m^{2}\), so \(y=-8\left[(x-m)^{2}-m^{2}\right]-5m^{2}=-8(x-m)^{2}+3m^{2}\).
Step 4. Hence the vertex is \(\left(m,\,3m^{2}\right)\) and the axis of symmetry is the line \(x=m\).
Step 5. Check with \(m=1\): \(f(x)=2x^{2}-8x+5\), \(y=-f(2x)=-8x^{2}+16x-5=-8(x-1)^{2}+3\), vertex \((1,3)\) ✓
Answer: Vertex \(\left(m,\,3m^{2}\right)\); axis of symmetry \(x=m\)
