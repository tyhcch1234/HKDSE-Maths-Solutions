[2013MC#11]  1. Percentages

A sum of $\$50\,000$ is deposited at an interest of $8\%$ per annum for 1 year, compounded monthly. Find the interest correct to the nearest dollar.

A. $\$4\,000$ B. $\$4\,122$ C. $\$4\,143$ D. $\$4\,150$
1Identify the given values

Principal $P = \$50\,000$
Annual interest rate $r = 8\% = 0.08$
Compounded monthly $\Rightarrow$ number of periods per year $n = 12$
Time $t = 1$ year

Since it is compounded monthly, the amount grows $12$ times in the year.

Hint: The nominal rate $8\%$ p.a. must be divided into $12$ monthly periods.
Interest Rate per Compounding Period\(i=\dfrac{r}{n}\)The nominal \(8\%\) per annum is shared among \(12\) monthly periods, so each period earns \(\dfrac{0.08}{12}\).
2Write the compound interest amount formula

$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$

Here the amount $A$ is the total value after $1$ year, so the interest is

$$\text{Interest} = A - P = P\left[\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} - 1\right]$$

Hint: The question asks for the interest, not the final balance.
Compound Interest Formula\(A=P\left(1+\dfrac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}\)There are \(nt=12\times1=12\) conversions at \(8\%\div12\) per month, starting from \(P=\$50\,000\).
3Substitute the values

$$\text{Interest} = 50\,000 \times \left[\left(1 + \frac{8\%}{12}\right)^{12 \times 1} - 1\right]$$

Notice this is exactly the official method:

$$\text{Interest} = 50\,000 \times \left[\left(1 + 8\% \div 12\right)^{12} - 1\right]$$

Hint: Keep full accuracy in the calculator — do not round the monthly rate.
4Evaluate with a calculator

Monthly rate $= \dfrac{0.08}{12} \approx 0.0066667$
$1 + 0.0066667 = 1.0066667$
$(1.0066667)^{12} \approx 1.0829999$
$1.0829999 - 1 = 0.0829999$

$$\text{Interest} = 50\,000 \times 0.0829999 \approx 4149.97534$$

Hint: Compounding monthly gives slightly more than the simple $8\%$, which would be only $\$4\,000$.
Interest Earned\(I=A-P\)The question asks for the interest, so subtract the principal \(\$50\,000\) from the final amount.
5Round to the nearest dollar

$$4149.97534 \;\longrightarrow\; \$4\,150$$

Comparing with the options: A $\$4\,000$, B $\$4\,122$, C $\$4\,143$, D $\$4\,150$.

The correct option is D.

Hint: $4149.975$ rounds up to $4150$ because the first decimal is $9$.
ANSWER Interest $= \$4\,150$  ⟹  Option D

Question. A sum of \(\$80\,000\) is deposited at \(6\%\) per annum for 2 years, compounded quarterly. Find the interest correct to the nearest dollar.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(P=80\,000\), \(r=0.06\), \(n=4\) (quarterly) and \(t=2\), so the quarterly rate is \(i=\dfrac{0.06}{4}=0.015\) and there are \(nt=8\) periods.
Step 2. \(A=P(1+i)^{8}=80\,000(1.015)^{8}\).
Step 3. \((1.015)^{8}\approx1.126493\), so \(A\approx\$90\,119.41\).
Step 4. Interest \(=A-P\approx90\,119.41-80\,000=\$10\,119.41\), i.e. \(\$10\,119\) to the nearest dollar.
Answer: \(\$10\,119\)
