HKDSE Math 2019 Paper 2 Q43

Probability · At Most 2 Correct

🎯 Probability 🧮 Complement Rule ✖️ Independent Events
📋

Question

There are three questions in a mathematics competition. The probabilities that Susan answers the first question correctly, the second question correctly and the third question correctly are \(\frac{1}{3}\), \(\frac{1}{5}\) and \(\frac{1}{7}\) respectively. The probability that Susan answers at most 2 questions correctly in the competition is

A. \(\frac{1}{105}\)
B. \(\frac{13}{105}\)
C. \(\frac{92}{105}\)
D. \(\frac{104}{105}\)

🧭 Step-by-Step Solution

1

Identify what "at most 2 correct" means

▼

"At most 2 correct" means Susan can get 0, 1, or 2 questions correct. It is the complement of getting all 3 questions correct.

💡 Hint: Using the complement is much faster than calculating 0, 1, and 2 correct separately.
2

Write the complement probability

▼

We use the formula:

\( P(\text{at most 2 correct}) = 1 - P(\text{all 3 correct}) \)

💡 Hint: The event "all 3 correct" is the only case that is not "at most 2 correct".
3

Calculate P(all 3 correct)

▼

Since the questions are answered independently:

\( P(\text{all 3 correct}) = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{5} \times \frac{1}{7} \)

💡 Hint: Multiply the individual probabilities for "and" events.
4

Simplify the product

▼

\( \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{5} \times \frac{1}{7} = \frac{1}{105} \)

💡 Hint: \(3 \times 5 \times 7 = 105\), so the product is \(\frac{1}{105}\).
5

Final answer

▼

\( P(\text{at most 2 correct}) = 1 - \frac{1}{105} = \frac{104}{105} \)

💡 Hint: The answer is D.
