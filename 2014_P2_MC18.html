Question Multiple Choice

In the figure, \(\angle ABD=\angle ADC=\angle BCD=90^\circ\). If \(AB=\ell\), then \(CD=\)

A. \(\ell\sin\theta\)
B. \(\ell\cos\theta\)
C. \(\ell\sin\theta\tan\theta\)
D. \(\dfrac{\ell\tan\theta}{\cos\theta}\)

Step 1 — Set up the diagram Model

The key is breaking the figure into two right triangles: \(\triangle ABD\) and \(\triangle BCD\). Drag the slider to change \(\theta\) and watch how the lengths change. Toggle the layers to highlight each triangle.

Step 1

Focus on the right-angled triangle \(\triangle ABD\) with the right angle at \(B\):

\[ \angle ABD = 90^\circ,\qquad \angle BAD=\theta \]
Hint: In a right-angled triangle, the tangent ratio links the two legs: \(\tan(\text{angle})=\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}\).
Step 2

Apply the tangent ratio in \(\triangle ABD\):

\[ \tan\theta=\frac{BD}{AB}=\frac{BD}{\ell}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad BD=\ell\tan\theta \]
\(BD=\ell\tan\theta\)

Step 2 — Angle chasing Reasoning

Now find \(\angle ADB\) and then \(\angle BDC\). The diagram below shows the angles; the arcs update live as you move the slider.

∠BAD = θ = 30°
∠ADB = 60°
∠ADC = 90°
∠BDC = 30°
Step 1

Use the angle sum of \(\triangle ABD\):

\[ \angle ADB = 180^\circ - \angle ABD - \angle BAD = 180^\circ - 90^\circ - \theta = 90^\circ - \theta \]
Hint: The three angles of any triangle add up to \(180^\circ\).
\(\angle ADB = 90^\circ - \theta\)
Step 2

Since \(\angle ADC=90^\circ\) and \(\angle ADB\) forms part of it:

\[ \angle BDC = \angle ADC - \angle ADB = 90^\circ - (90^\circ - \theta) = \theta \]
Hint: \(\angle BDC\) is the remaining part of the right angle at \(D\).
\(\angle BDC = \theta\)
0 / 2

Step 3 — Final length \(CD\) Solve

In \(\triangle BCD\), the right angle is at \(C\). Slide \(\theta\) and watch \(CD\) change.

BD = ℓ tanθ = 4.04
∠BDC = θ = 30°
CD = ℓ sinθ = 3.50
Step 1

In the right-angled triangle \(\triangle BCD\), the right angle is at \(C\), and \(\angle BDC=\theta\). Using the cosine ratio:

\[ \cos\angle BDC=\frac{CD}{BD}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad CD=BD\cos\angle BDC \]
Hint: \(\cos(\text{angle})=\dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}\).
Step 2

Substitute \(BD=\ell\tan\theta\) and \(\angle BDC=\theta\):

\[ CD=(\ell\tan\theta)\cos\theta=\ell\cdot\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\cdot\cos\theta=\ell\sin\theta \]
Hint: \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\), so the \(\cos\theta\) cancels.
\(CD=\ell\sin\theta\) — Answer: A
0 / 2

Summary of Answers

  • Key relation 1: \(BD=\ell\tan\theta\)  (from \(\triangle ABD\))
  • Key relation 2: \(\angle ADB=90^\circ-\theta\)  (angle sum of \(\triangle ABD\))
  • Key relation 3: \(\angle BDC=\theta\)  (complementary angles at \(D\))
  • Final answer: \(CD=\ell\sin\theta\)  → Option A
