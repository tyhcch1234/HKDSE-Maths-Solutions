HKDSE Math 2021 Paper 1 Q2 5. Formulas and Sequences 3 marks

Make \(a\) the subject of the formula

\[ \frac{4 - 3a}{b} = 5 \]
Subject of formula Rearranging equations Algebraic manipulation

🎯 Goal: Rearrange the formula so that \(a\) is alone on one side. Think of the equation as a balance — whatever you do to one side, you must also do to the other.

1

Eliminate the denominator

Multiply both sides by \(b\) to remove the fraction. This is the reverse of division.

\[ \frac{4 - 3a}{b} \times b = 5 \times b \]
\[ 4 - 3a = 5b \]

💡 Hint: The denominator is \(b\). Multiplying by \(b\) undoes the division. Remember: \(b \neq 0\).

2

Isolate the term with \(a\)

Subtract \(4\) from both sides to move the constant to the right.

\[ 4 - 3a - 4 = 5b - 4 \]
\[ -3a = 5b - 4 \]

💡 Hint: We want all terms with \(a\) on one side and everything else on the other. \(4\) becomes \(-4\) when it moves across.

3

Divide by the coefficient of \(a\)

Divide both sides by \(-3\) to make \(a\) the subject.

\[ \frac{-3a}{-3} = \frac{5b - 4}{-3} \]
\[ a = \frac{5b - 4}{-3} \]

💡 Hint: Multiplying by \(-\frac{1}{3}\) is the same as dividing by \(-3\). You can also write: \[ a = -\frac{5b - 4}{3} = \frac{4 - 5b}{3} \] Both forms are correct! The official answer uses \(\frac{4 - 5b}{3}\).

Final Answer

\[ a = \frac{4 - 5b}{3} \]

📝 Key Takeaway

  • To make a variable the subject, undo operations in reverse order (BODMAS in reverse).
  • Always do the same operation to both sides to keep the equation balanced.
  • When dividing by a negative number, remember to flip signs correctly: \(-3a = 5b - 4 \Rightarrow a = \frac{4 - 5b}{3}\).
