Mensuration — Areas of triangles in a square with a right angle at a vertex on the side
Area of Triangle Ratio of Sides Pythagoras' Theorem \(\tan\theta\) & \(\angle\) on straight line

QQuestion

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a square. Let \(E\) and \(F\) be points lying on \(AB\) and \(BC\) respectively such that \(AE = 3BE\) and \(\angle DEF = 90^\circ\). If the area of \(\Delta DEF\) is \(25\text{ cm}^2\), then the area of \(\Delta CDF\) is
  1. \(48\text{ cm}^2\)
  2. \(50\text{ cm}^2\)
  3. \(52\text{ cm}^2\)
  4. \(75\text{ cm}^2\)
Square sides \(\Delta DEF\) \(\Delta CDF\)

aSet up parameters & the square's geometry 1M

2.0
1Introduce a parameter1M
Let \(BE = a\text{ cm}\). Then, since \(AE = 3BE\), \[AE = 3a\text{ cm},\qquad AB = AE + EB = 4a\text{ cm}.\]
Hint: Choosing the smallest segment as the parameter keeps every length an integer-free multiple of \(a\).
2Every side of the square1A
Since \(ABCD\) is a square, \[AD = BC = CD = AB = 4a\text{ cm}.\] Also \(FC = BC - BF\) will be used later — we still need \(BF\) from the right angle.
Hint: A square gives all four sides equal — write the whole figure in terms of the single unknown \(a\).

bUse \(\angle DEF = 90^\circ\) to find \(BF\) 1M

2.0
1Angles on the straight line \(AB\)1M
Since \(A,E,B\) are collinear, \(\angle AED + \angle DEF + \angle FEB = 180^\circ\). With \(\angle DEF = 90^\circ\), \[\angle AED + \angle FEB = 90^\circ.\] Hence \(\angle FEB = 90^\circ - \angle AED\), i.e. \(\angle AED\) and \(\angle FEB\) are complementary.
Hint: Complementary angles give \(\tan\theta\cdot\tan(90^\circ-\theta)=1\) — a very quick route to a length ratio.
2Apply the tangent product rule1M
From the two right triangles \(\Delta ADE\) and \(\Delta BEF\): \[\tan\angle AED = \frac{AD}{AE}=\frac{4a}{3a}=\frac{4}{3},\qquad \tan\angle BEF = \frac{BF}{BE}=\frac{BF}{a}.\] Since \(\angle AED + \angle BEF = 90^\circ\), \[\frac{4}{3}\times\frac{BF}{a}=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; BF=\frac{3a}{4}\text{ cm}.\]
Hint: \(\tan\theta\cdot\tan(90^\circ-\theta)=1\) — the product of the two tangent values must equal \(1\).
Key result: \(\displaystyle BF=\frac{3a}{4}\text{ cm}\)

cThe two legs \(DE\) and \(EF\) 1M

2.0
1Leg \(DE\) in \(\Delta ADE\)1M
\[DE=\sqrt{AD^2+AE^2}=\sqrt{(4a)^2+(3a)^2}=\sqrt{25a^2}=5a\text{ cm}.\]
Hint: \(3a-4a-5a\) is just the \(3\text{-}4\text{-}5\) Pythagorean triple scaled by \(a\).
2Leg \(EF\) in \(\Delta BEF\)1M
\[EF=\sqrt{BE^2+BF^2}=\sqrt{a^2+\left(\tfrac{3a}{4}\right)^2} =\sqrt{a^2\left(1+\tfrac{9}{16}\right)}=\frac{5a}{4}\text{ cm}.\]
Hint: Again a \(3\text{-}4\text{-}5\) triple — here \(BE:BF:EF = 4:3:5\).
Key results: \(DE=5a\text{ cm},\quad EF=\dfrac{5a}{4}\text{ cm}\)
0 / 2

dFind \(\boldsymbol{a}\), then the area of \(\Delta CDF\) 1M+1A

2.00
1Area of \(\Delta DEF\) in terms of \(a\)1M
\[\text{Area of }\Delta DEF=\frac{1}{2}\cdot DE\cdot EF =\frac{1}{2}\cdot 5a\cdot\frac{5a}{4}=\frac{25a^2}{8}\text{ cm}^2.\] Given this equals \(25\text{ cm}^2\): \[\frac{25a^2}{8}=25 \;\Longrightarrow\; a^2=8 \;\Longrightarrow\; a=2\sqrt{2}.\]
Hint: Substitute the given area and solve for \(a^2\) first — you rarely need \(a\) itself.
2Area of \(\Delta CDF\) in terms of \(a\)1M
Since \(FC=BC-BF=4a-\dfrac{3a}{4}=\dfrac{13a}{4}\) and \(CD=4a\), \[\text{Area of }\Delta CDF=\frac{1}{2}\cdot CD\cdot FC =\frac{1}{2}\cdot 4a\cdot\frac{13a}{4}=\frac{13a^2}{2}\text{ cm}^2.\]
Hint: \(\Delta CDF\) is right-angled at \(C\) (a corner of the square), so area \(=\tfrac12(CD)(FC)\).
3Ratio of the two areas (shortcut)1M
\[\frac{\text{Area of }\Delta CDF}{\text{Area of }\Delta DEF} =\frac{13a^2/2}{25a^2/8}=\frac{13}{2}\times\frac{8}{25}=\frac{52}{25}.\]
Hint: Both areas are multiples of \(a^2\), so the ratio is independent of \(a\) — no need to use the given \(25\text{ cm}^2\) yet!
4Final answer1A
\[\text{Area of }\Delta CDF = 25\times\frac{52}{25}=52\text{ cm}^2.\]
Answer: \(\boxed{52\text{ cm}^2}\) — option C.

✓Summary of Answers

  • Let \(BE=a\) ⇒ \(AE=3a\), all square sides \(=4a\).
  • Complementary angles ⇒ \(\tan\angle AED\cdot\tan\angle BEF=1\) ⇒ \(\displaystyle BF=\frac{3a}{4}\).
  • Pythagoras: \(DE=5a\), \(\displaystyle EF=\frac{5a}{4}\).
  • \(\displaystyle \text{Area}(\Delta DEF)=\frac{25a^2}{8}=25 \Rightarrow a^2=8\).
  • \(\displaystyle \text{Area}(\Delta CDF)=\frac{13a^2}{2}=\frac{52}{25}\times 25\).
Final answer: Area of \(\Delta CDF = 52\text{ cm}^2\)  →  Option C
