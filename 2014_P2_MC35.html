Vertex of the Graph of a Quadratic Function

Completing the Square Vertex Form Functions & Graphs

Question

Multiple-choice · choose the best answer.

Let \( f(x)=3x^{2}-6x+k \), where \(k\) is a constant. If the \(y\)-coordinate of the vertex of the graph of \(y=f(x)\) is \(7\), then \(k=\)

A. \(1\)    B. \(3\)    C. \(4\)    D. \(10\)

A. \(k=1\)non-answer
B. \(k=3\)non-answer
C. \(k=4\)non-answer
D. \(k=10\)correct answer

Part (a) — Complete the square

Rewrite \(f(x)\) in vertex (completing-the-square) form.

10.0
STEP 1 Factor out the leading coefficient from the \(x\)-terms
\[ f(x)=3x^{2}-6x+k = 3\left(x^{2}-2x\right)+k \]
Hint: Only the \(3\) is taken out of the \(x^{2}\) and \(x\) terms — the constant \(k\) stays outside the bracket.
STEP 2 Complete the square inside the bracket
\[ x^{2}-2x=(x-1)^{2}-1 \] \[ \therefore\ f(x)=3\big[(x-1)^{2}-1\big]+k \]
Half of \(-2\) is \(-1\). Squaring gives \((x-1)^2=x^2-2x+1\), so subtract \(1\) to compensate. Notice \(3\times(-1)=-3\), which is exactly the vertical shift away from the vertex of \(y=3x^2\).
STEP 3 Expand the bracket to read off the vertex form
\[ f(x)=3(x-1)^{2}-3+k \]
Vertex form: \(\;f(x)=3(x-1)^{2}+(k-3)\)
Hint: In the form \(y=a(x-h)^{2}+t\), the vertex is \((h,\,t)\). Here \(a=3>0\), so the parabola opens upward and the vertex is a minimum point.

Part (b) — Read the vertex and solve for \(k\)

Use the given \(y\)-coordinate of the vertex to find the value of \(k\).

-1.00
Vertex \(y=k-3=\) -4.00 Difference from 7: 11.00
STEP 1 State the coordinates of the vertex

Comparing \(f(x)=3(x-1)^{2}+(k-3)\) with \(y=a(x-h)^{2}+t\):

\[ \text{Vertex}=\left(1,\ k-3\right) \]
Hint: The \(x\)-coordinate of the vertex is \(-\dfrac{b}{2a}=-\dfrac{-6}{2(3)}=1\), matching Step 3. The \(y\)-coordinate is then \(f(1)=k-3\).
STEP 2 Set the vertex \(y\)-coordinate equal to \(7\)

The question states the \(y\)-coordinate of the vertex is \(7\):

\[ k-3=7 \]
Hint: In the diagram, drag the slider until the red vertex lands on the dashed line \(y=7\) — the readout "Difference from 7" becomes \(0\).
STEP 3 Solve for \(k\)
\[ k=7+3=10 \]
\(\therefore\ k=10\)  ⟹  the answer is D.
Quick check: with \(k=10\), \(f(x)=3x^{2}-6x+10=3(x-1)^{2}+7\), so the vertex is \((1,\,7)\). ✔

Summary of Answers

  • Completed-square (vertex) form: \(\;f(x)=3(x-1)^{2}+(k-3)\)
  • Vertex of \(y=f(x)\): \(\;(1,\ k-3)\)
  • Given vertex \(y\)-coordinate \(=7\): \(\;k-3=7\)
  • Required value: \(\;\boxed{k=10}\)
  • Correct option: \(\;\textbf{D}\)
