Statistics — Using the Mean to Find an Unknown Sum

Arithmetic Mean Sum of Data Weighted Average Multiple-Choice

Question MC

The mean of the numbers of pages of \(10\) magazines is \(132\). If the mean of the numbers of pages of \(6\) of these \(10\) magazines is \(108\), then the mean of the numbers of pages of the remaining \(4\) magazines is

A. \(148\)    B. \(156\)    C. \(168\)    D. \(176\)

Key idea: mean \(\times\) number of items = total sum. Compare the total sum of the \(10\) magazines with the sum of the chosen \(6\).

Part (a) — Find the total number of pages of all 10 magazines Set up

Every statistical mean statement is really a statement about a sum. The slider below changes the overall mean so you can watch the total sum change, while the bar chart shows how the \(6\) chosen magazines and the remaining \(4\) must balance.

Total sum of 10 = 1320 Sum of 6 = 648 Remaining sum = 672 Mean of remaining 4 = 168
Step 1 Write the total sum from the overall mean

For the \(10\) magazines: \[ \text{Total pages} \;=\; \text{mean}\times \text{count} \;=\;132\times 10 \;=\;1320. \]

Hint: “Mean \(=\) sum \(\div\) number” rearranges to “sum \(=\) mean \(\times\) number”. Never average averages directly.
Step 2 Find the sum of the \(6\) chosen magazines

For the \(6\) magazines whose mean is \(108\): \[ \text{Sum of 6} \;=\;108\times 6 \;=\;648. \]

Hint: Keep the two groups separate — a very common mistake is to subtract the means \(132-108\) instead of the sums.

Part (b) — Find the mean of the remaining 4 magazines Answer

Drag the slider to see how the answer depends on the given data. When the overall mean is \(132\), the remaining group must have mean \(168\) to make the two groups combine correctly.

Mean of 6 = 108 Overall mean = 132 Required mean of 4 = 168
Step 3 Let \(x\) be the required mean

The remaining \(4\) magazines have total pages \[ 4x. \] Their sum together with the \(6\) magazines must equal the total of all \(10\):

\[ 108\times 6 + x\times 4 \;=\;132\times 10. \]

Hint: This is the exact equation from the official solution: \[108\cdot 6 + x\cdot 4 = 132\cdot 10.\] The left side is (sum of 6) \(+\) (sum of remaining 4); the right side is the total sum.
Step 4 Solve for \(x\)

Substitute the known sums: \[ 648 + 4x = 1320. \]

\[ 4x = 1320-648 = 672. \]

\[ x = 672\div 4 = 168. \]

Answer: \(x=168\) → the mean of the remaining \(4\) magazines is \(\mathbf{168}\) pages, i.e. option C.
Check: \(648 + 4(168) = 648+672 = 1320 = 132\times 10\).

Summary of Answers

  • Total pages of all \(10\) magazines: \(\mathbf{132\times 10 = 1320}\).
  • Total pages of the \(6\) chosen magazines: \(\mathbf{108\times 6 = 648}\).
  • Total pages of the remaining \(4\) magazines: \(\mathbf{1320-648 = 672}\).
  • Mean of the remaining \(4\) magazines: \(\mathbf{672\div 4 = 168}\).
  • Correct option: C. 168
