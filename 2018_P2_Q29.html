Question

The mean of the numbers of pages of 10 magazines is \(132\). If the mean of the numbers of pages of 6 of these 10 magazines is \(108\), then the mean of the numbers of pages of the remaining 4 magazines is

  • A. 148
  • B. 156
  • C. 168
  • D. 176

Step-by-step solution

Step 1Set up the unknown.

Let \(x\) be the mean of the numbers of pages of the remaining \(4\) magazines.

Then the total number of pages in those 4 magazines is \(4x\).

Hint: it is usually easier to work with the total than with the mean.
Step 2Total pages of all 10 magazines.
Total from the Mean \(\text{total}=\text{mean}\times\text{number of data}\) The ten magazines contain \\(132\\times10=1320\\) pages altogether.

\[ \text{Total} = \text{mean} \times \text{number} = 132 \times 10 = 1320. \]

Hint: mean × count gives the sum — the key idea behind all "mean" problems.
Step 3Total pages of the first 6 magazines.

\[ \text{Sum of 6} = 108 \times 6 = 648. \]

So the remaining 4 magazines contain

\[ 1320 - 648 = 672 \ \text{pages.} \]

Hint: subtract the known part from the whole.
Step 4Find the mean of the remaining 4 magazines.
Mean of a Data Set \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{\text{total}}{n}\) The remaining four magazines hold \\(672\\) pages, so their mean is \\(\\dfrac{672}{4}=168\\).

\[ x = \frac{672}{4} = 168. \]

As one single equation:

\[ 108 \cdot 6 + x \cdot 4 = 132 \cdot 10 \;\Rightarrow\; 648 + 4x = 1320 \;\Rightarrow\; 4x = 672 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 168. \]

Hint: always divide the total of the remaining items by the number of remaining items.
Answer: C  (\(168\))

Question. The mean number of pages of \\(12\\) magazines is \\(96\\). The mean number of pages of \\(5\\) of them is \\(89\\). Find the mean number of pages of the remaining \\(7\\) magazines.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \\(x\\) be the mean of the remaining \\(7\\) magazines; their total is \\(7x\\) pages.
Step 2. Total pages of all \\(12\\) magazines \\(=96\\times12=1152\\).
Step 3. Total pages of the \\(5\\) magazines \\(=89\\times5=445\\), so the remaining \\(7\\) hold \\(1152-445=707\\) pages.
Step 4. \\(x=\\dfrac{707}{7}=101\\).
Answer: \(101\)
