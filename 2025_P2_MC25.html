Question 1 mark

Denote the origin by \(O\). The coordinates of the point \(A\) are \((a,\,2a)\), where \(a>0\). Let \(P\) be a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that \(AP=OA\). The locus of \(P\) is a

  • A. point.
  • B. circle.
  • C. rhombus.
  • D. straight line.

(Answer: B. circle)

Part (a) — Translate the condition into an equation Setup

Let \(P=(x,y)\). The condition \(AP=OA\) says: the distance from \(P\) to \(A\) equals the distance from \(O\) to \(A\).

OA (radius) AP (equal length) Locus circle
Step 11M

Compute \(OA\), the distance from \(O(0,0)\) to \(A(a,2a)\):

\[ OA=\sqrt{a^2+(2a)^2}=\sqrt{a^2+4a^2}=\sqrt{5a^2}=\sqrt{5}\,a \]
Hint: Since \(a>0\), \(\sqrt{5a^2}=\sqrt{5}\,a\). The quantity \(OA\) is a fixed positive constant once \(a\) is fixed.
Step 21M

Write the distance \(AP\) using the distance formula:

\[ AP=\sqrt{(x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2} \]
Hint: Apply \(\sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2}\) directly. Square both sides next to remove the radicals.
Step 31A

Set \(AP=OA\) and square both sides:

\[ \sqrt{(x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2}=\sqrt{5}\,a \] \[ \Rightarrow\ (x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2=5a^2 \]
✓ Equation of the locus: \((x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2=5a^2\)
Hint: This is the standard form \((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2\) of a circle.

Part (b) — Identify the locus Conclusion

Compare the squared equation with the general circle equation \((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2\).

Step 11M

Read off the centre and the radius from \((x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2=5a^2\):

\[ \text{centre}=(a,\,2a)=A,\qquad r=\sqrt{5a^2}=\sqrt{5}\,a \]
Hint: The centre of this circle is exactly the fixed point \(A\). The radius equals \(OA\), which is fixed.
Step 21M

The locus of \(P\) consists of all points at the fixed distance \(\sqrt5 a\) from the fixed point \(A\). This is by definition a circle:

\[ \text{Locus of }P:\ \text{circle centred at }(a,2a)\text{ with radius }\sqrt5\,a \]
Hint: A "point" would require \(OA=0\) (impossible since \(a>0\)). A "straight line" requires a perpendicular-distance condition, not a fixed distance from a fixed point.
Step 31A

Therefore the locus of \(P\) is a circle, so the correct option is B.

✓ The locus of \(P\) is a circle (Option B).
Hint: Key idea — a locus defined by a constant distance from a fixed point is a circle.
0 / 3

Summary of Answers

  • \(OA=\sqrt{a^2+(2a)^2}=\sqrt5\,a\) — a fixed radius.
  • \(AP=\sqrt{(x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2}\) — distance from \(P\) to the fixed point \(A\).
  • Condition \(AP=OA\) gives \((x-a)^2+(y-2a)^2=5a^2\).
  • Centre \(=(a,2a)=A\), radius \(=\sqrt5\,a\).
  • The locus of \(P\) is a circle → Option B.
