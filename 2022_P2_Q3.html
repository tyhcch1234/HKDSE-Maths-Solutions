2022MC#3

If $m$ and $n$ are constants such that

\[ (x+3)^2+mx \equiv (x-n)(x+1)+2n, \]

then $m=$

A.  $-14$
B.  $-8$
C.  $4$
D.  $9$
Expansion of a Perfect Square \((x+a)^{2}=x^{2}+2ax+a^{2}\) \((x+3)^{2}=x^{2}+6x+9\), so after adding \(mx\) the left side has \(x\)-coefficient \(m+6\).
1Expand the left-hand side

\[ (x+3)^2+mx = x^2+6x+9+mx \]

Collect like terms: \[ x^2+\blue{(m+6)}x+9 \]

So the L.H.S. has $x$-coefficient $\blue{m+6}$ and constant term $9$.

Hint: $(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2$, then group the $x$-terms.
2Expand the right-hand side

\[ (x-n)(x+1)+2n = x^2+x-nx-n+2n \]

Collect like terms: \[ x^2+\green{(-n+1)}x+\green{n} \]

So the R.H.S. has $x$-coefficient $\green{-n+1}$ and constant term $\green{n}$.

Hint: expand the brackets first, then add the $2n$ to the constant.
Equating Coefficients in an Identity \(Ax^{2}+Bx+C\equiv A_{1}x^{2}+B_{1}x+C_{1}\ \Longrightarrow\ A=A_{1},\ B=B_{1},\ C=C_{1}\) Matching constant terms gives \(9=n\), then matching the \(x\)-terms gives \(m+6=-n+1\).
3Equate constant terms

Since the two expressions are identically equal, their constant terms match:

\[ 9 = n \]

So $\boxed{n=9}$ (this is equation (2)).

Hint: "$\equiv$" means the coefficients of every power of $x$ must be equal.
4Equate the $x$-terms

\[ m+6=-n+1 \quad \text{......(1)} \]

Substitute $n=9$: \[ m+6=-9+1=-8 \]

\[ m=-8-6=\hi{-14} \]

Alternatively add (1)+(2): $m+15=1$, giving $m=-14$.
5Check by substitution ($x=0$, then $x=1$)

Put $x=0$: L.H.S. $=(0+3)^2+0=9$; R.H.S. $=(0-n)(1)+2n=-n+2n=n$ ⟹ $n=9$. ✔

Put $x=1$: L.H.S. $=(1+3)^2+m(1)=16+m$.

R.H.S. $=(1-n)(1+1)+2n = 2-2n+2n = 2$.

So $16+m=2 \Rightarrow m=\hi{-14}$. ✔

Hint: substituting a convenient value of $x$ gives a fast independent check.
Answer: A.  $m=-14$

Question. If \((x+4)^{2}+mx\equiv(x-n)(x+2)+3n\), find the value of \(m\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Expand the left side: \((x+4)^{2}+mx=x^{2}+(8+m)x+16\).
Step 2. Expand the right side: \((x-n)(x+2)+3n=x^{2}+(2-n)x-2n+3n=x^{2}+(2-n)x+n\).
Step 3. Equate the constant terms: \(16=n\).
Step 4. Equate the \(x\)-coefficients: \(8+m=2-n=2-16=-14\), so \(m=-22\).
Step 5. Check with \(x=1\): LHS \(=25-22=3\);
RHS \(=(1-16)(3)+48=3\).
Answer: \(m=-22\)
