Mensuration — Right Circular Cylinder & Sphere
Surface Area of Cylinder Volume of Sphere Quadratic Equation Rejecting Invalid Roots

Question MC

The height and the total surface area of a solid right circular cylinder are \(35\text{ cm}\) and \(492\pi\text{ cm}^2\) respectively. If the radius of a solid sphere is equal to the base radius of the circular cylinder, then the volume of the sphere is

A. \(144\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
B. \(288\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
C. \(576\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
D. \(864\pi\text{ cm}^3\)

Part (a) — Set up the equation for the radius

Let \(r\) be the base radius. The cylinder's total surface area equals the sum of the two circular bases and the curved surface.

Radius \(r\) = 6.00 cm
Height \(h\) = 35.00 cm
Total S.A. = 492.0 π cm²
Step 1

Let \(r\) be the base radius and \(h=35\) cm. The total surface area of a closed right circular cylinder is

\[ \text{T.S.A.} = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h \]
Hint: Two circles (top & bottom) contribute \(2\pi r^2\), and the curved surface is a rectangle of width \(2\pi r\) and height \(h\).
Step 2

Substitute \(h=35\) and T.S.A. \(=492\pi\):

\[ 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r(35) = 492\pi \]
Hint: Divide the whole equation by \(2\pi\) to simplify before solving.
Equation: \(r^2 + 35r = 246\)
0 / 2

Part (b) — Solve for \(r\) and find the sphere's volume

Solve the quadratic, reject the invalid root, then apply the sphere volume formula.

Chosen \(r\) = 6.00 cm
Quadratic value \(r^2+35r-246\) = 0.00
Sphere \(V\) = 288 π cm³
Step 1

Rearrange into standard quadratic form:

\[ r^2 + 35r - 246 = 0 \]
Hint: Factorise or use the quadratic formula with \(a=1,\ b=35,\ c=-246\).
Step 2

Factorise:

\[ (r-6)(r+41) = 0 \] \[ r = 6 \quad\text{or}\quad r = -41 \]
Hint: Since \(r\) is a length, the negative root must be rejected: \(r > 0\).
Base radius: \(r = 6\text{ cm}\) ( \(-41\) rejected)
Step 3

The sphere has the same radius \(r=6\text{ cm}\). Its volume is

\[ V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (6)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (216) = 288\pi\text{ cm}^3 \]
Hint: \(6^3 = 216\), and \(\tfrac{4}{3}\times 216 = 288\).
Volume of sphere: \(288\pi\text{ cm}^3\) → Answer B
0 / 3

Summary of Answers

  • Cylinder radius: \(r = 6\text{ cm}\)
  • Quadratic: \(r^2 + 35r - 246 = 0 \Rightarrow (r-6)(r+41)=0\)
  • Rejected root: \(r = -41\) (length must be positive)
  • Sphere volume: \(V = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi (6)^3 = 288\pi\text{ cm}^3\)
  • Correct option: B
