[2016 MC #32]
The graph in the figure shows the linear relation between $x$ and $\log_9 y$. If $y = ab^{x}$, then $b =$
  • A. $-2$
  • B. $\dfrac{1}{81}$
  • C. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
  • D. $3$
1Read the given graph

The straight line passes through $(0,-2)$ on the $\log_9 y$-axis and $(4,0)$ on the $x$-axis.

These two points give everything: the intercept gives $\log_9 a$, the slope gives $\log_9 b$.
2Take logarithms of both sides
Product and Power Laws of Logarithms \(\log(MN)=\log M+\log N,\qquad \log M^{k}=k\log M\) They turn \(y=ab^{x}\) into the straight line \(\log_{9}y=\log_{9}a+x\log_{9}b\).

$$y = ab^{x} \;\Longrightarrow\; \log_9 y = \log_9\!\left(ab^{x}\right)$$

Using $\log(MN)=\log M+\log N$ and $\log M^{k}=k\log M$:

$$\log_9 y = \log_9 a + x\log_9 b$$

Converting the exponential model into a linear model: $Y = mX + c$ with $Y=\log_9 y$, $X=x$.
3Identify slope and intercept
Slope-Intercept Form of a Straight Line \(Y=mX+c\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=\text{slope},\ c=Y\text{-intercept}\) Matching \(Y=\log_{9}y\), \(X=x\) shows the slope is \(\log_{9}b\) and the intercept \(c=\log_{9}a=-2\).

Comparing with $Y = mX + c$:

$$\text{slope } m = \log_9 b, \qquad c = \log_9 a = -2$$

The slope of the $(\log_9 y)$–$x$ graph equals $\log_9 b$.
4Compute the slope

$$\log_9 b = \frac{0-(-2)}{4-0} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$$

Slope $=\dfrac{\Delta(\log_9 y)}{\Delta x}$ using $(0,-2)$ and $(4,0)$.
5Solve for $b$
Definition of Logarithm \(\log_{b}y=x\iff y=b^{x}\) \(\log_{9}b=\dfrac{1}{2}\) therefore means \(b=9^{1/2}=3\).

$$\log_9 b = \frac{1}{2} \;\Longrightarrow\; b = 9^{1/2} = 3$$

Definition of logarithm: $\log_9 b = k \iff b = 9^{k}$.
Answer: D. 3
