HKDSE Math 2014 Paper 2 Q8

Simultaneous Equations in Context

Algebra Simultaneous Equations Word Problem Elimination
The price of 2 bowls and 3 cups is $506. If the price of 5 bowls and the price of 4 cups are the same, then the price of a bowl is
A. $88 B. $92 C. $110 D. $115

Step-by-step Solution

Step 1 / 5
1 Define the variables

Let \(x\) be the price of a bowl (in dollars).
Let \(y\) be the price of a cup (in dollars).

💡 Hint
Always define variables clearly. The question asks for the price of a bowl, so \(x\) is our target.
2 Form the first equation

2 bowls and 3 cups cost $506:

\[2x + 3y = 506 \quad \text{......(1)}\]

💡 Hint
"2 bowls" means \(2x\), "3 cups" means \(3y\), total is 506.
3 Form the second equation

The price of 5 bowls equals the price of 4 cups:

\[5x = 4y \quad \text{......(2)}\]

💡 Hint
"5 bowls" → \(5x\), "4 cups" → \(4y\). Since they are the same price, we equate them.
4 Eliminate one variable

From (2), we have \(5x = 4y\)   ⇒   \(y = \frac{5}{4}x\).
Substitute into (1):

\[2x + 3\left(\frac{5}{4}x\right) = 506\]

💡 Hint
Alternatively, multiply (1) by 4 and (2) by 3, then add to eliminate \(y\):
\[4 \times (1): 8x + 12y = 2024\]
\[3 \times (2): 15x = 12y\]
Adding gives \(23x = 2024\).
5 Solve for \(x\)

Using the elimination method shown in the hint:

\[23x = 2024\]

\[x = \frac{2024}{23} = 88\]

Therefore, the price of a bowl is $88.

✓ Answer: A
