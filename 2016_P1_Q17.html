HKDSE Math 2016 Paper 1 Q17 5. Formulas and Sequences Arithmetic Sequence

Arithmetic Sequence

Let the 1st term and the 38th term of an arithmetic sequence be 666 and 555 respectively.

(a) Find the common difference. [2 marks]

(b) Find the greatest value of \(n\) such that the sum of the first \(n\) terms is positive. [3 marks]

🧩 Step-by-step solution

📌 Formula: General term of an arithmetic sequence

\[ T(n) = a + (n - 1)d \]

where \(a\) = first term, \(d\) = common difference, \(n\) = term number.

We are given:

  • First term \(a = 666\)
  • 38th term \(T(38) = 555\)

Substitute into the formula:

\[ 555 = 666 + (38 - 1)d \]

💡 Hint

Rearrange the linear equation to isolate \(d\).

\[ 555 = 666 + 37d \]

\[ 555 - 666 = 37d \]

\[ -111 = 37d \]

\[ d = -3 \]

✅ The common difference is \(-3\).

📌 Formula: Sum of first \(n\) terms

\[ S(n) = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d] \]

We need \(S(n) > 0\).

Substitute \(a = 666\) and \(d = -3\):

\[ S(n) = \frac{n}{2}[2(666) + (n - 1)(-3)] > 0 \]

💡 Hint

Since \(n > 0\), divide both sides by \(n/2\) and simplify the inequality.

\[ \frac{n}{2}[1332 - 3n + 3] > 0 \]

\[ \frac{n}{2}[1335 - 3n] > 0 \]

Multiply by 2: \(n(1335 - 3n) > 0\)

Divide by 3: \(n(445 - n) > 0\)

Rewrite: \(n(n - 445) < 0\)

💡 Hint

The product \(n(445 - n)\) is positive when \(n\) lies between the roots 0 and 445.

Roots are \(n = 0\) and \(n = 445\). Since \(n\) is a positive integer, the inequality holds for:

\[ 0 < n < 445 \]

The greatest integer \(n\) satisfying this is \(n = 444\).

✅ Final answer: The greatest value of \(n\) is 444.
