HKDSE 2024 · Paper 2 · Q7 · Topic 4: Polynomials

Step map: solve $x^2-3x=(m-1)^2-3(m-1)$.

Given equation Rearrange Factorise Roots
[2024MC#7]

Let $m$ be a constant. Solve the equation \[x^{2}-3x=(m-1)^{2}-3(m-1).\]

  • A.  $x=m-1$ or $x=m-4$
  • B.  $x=m-1$ or $x=4-m$
  • C.  $x=1-m$ or $x=m-4$
  • D.  $x=1-m$ or $x=4-m$
1Write down & compare both sides

Given: $\;x^{2}-3x=(m-1)^{2}-3(m-1)$.

Notice the same pattern "$(\;\;)^{2}-3(\;\;)$" appears on both sides — one with $x$, one with $m-1$.

Spotting the repeated shape is the key.
2Move everything to one side

\[x^{2}-(m-1)^{2}-3x+3(m-1)=0\]

Collect the $x$-terms and the constant terms separately — this prepares the difference of squares.
3Group & factorise

\[x^{2}-(m-1)^{2}-3\big[x-(m-1)\big]=0\]

Use $a^{2}-b^{2}=(a+b)(a-b)$ with $a=x,\;b=m-1$:

\[\big[x+(m-1)\big]\big[x-(m-1)\big]-3\big[x-(m-1)\big]=0\]

Take out the common factor $\big[x-(m-1)\big]$:

\[\big[x-(m-1)\big]\big[x+(m-1)-3\big]=0\]

Difference of squares first, then common-factor extraction.
4Simplify the second bracket

\[(m-1)-3=m-4,\quad\text{so}\quad \big[x-(m-1)\big]\big[x+(m-4)\big]=0\]

Just combine the constants: $(m-1)-3=m-4$.
5Zero-product rule → roots

\[x-(m-1)=0 \;\Rightarrow\; x=m-1\]

\[x+(m-4)=0 \;\Rightarrow\; x=-(m-4)=4-m\]

Each bracket set to zero gives one root.
6Check the options

Roots: $x=m-1$ or $x=4-m$ → matches option B.

Option A has $m-4$ (sign slip); options C, D use $1-m$ (wrong sign on $(m-1)$).
Answer: B  —  $x=m-1$ or $x=4-m$.
