Find the constant \(k\) such that the straight lines \[ 3x+2y+k=0 \quad\text{and}\quad kx+12y-6=0 \] are perpendicular to each other.
A. \(-8\)
B. \(-4\)
C. \(4\)
D. \(8\)

Part (a)Find the slope of each line

Rewrite each equation in slope-intercept form \(y=mx+c\) to read off the slope.

m₁ = −3/2 m₂ = −k/12 m₁ × m₂ = Perpendicular?
Step 1

Make \(y\) the subject of \(L_1:\;3x+2y+k=0\).

\[ 2y=-3x-k \quad\Rightarrow\quad y=-\tfrac{3}{2}x-\tfrac{k}{2} \]
Hint: The coefficient of \(x\) after rearranging is the slope. Watch the signs carefully!
Step 2

Make \(y\) the subject of \(L_2:\;kx+12y-6=0\).

\[ 12y=-kx+6 \quad\Rightarrow\quad y=-\tfrac{k}{12}x+\tfrac{1}{2} \]
Hint: Here the slope is \(-\dfrac{k}{12}\). Note the slope still involves the unknown \(k\).
Step 3
Slope of \(L_1:\; m_1=-\dfrac{3}{2}\)   |   Slope of \(L_2:\; m_2=-\dfrac{k}{12}\)

Part (b)Apply the perpendicular condition

The key idea: for two non-vertical perpendicular lines, the product of their slopes equals \(-1\).

m₁ = −1.5 m₂ = m₁ × m₂ = Verdict
Step 1

Two lines are perpendicular iff \(m_1\times m_2=-1\).

\[ \left(-\frac{3}{2}\right)\left(-\frac{k}{12}\right)=-1 \]
Hint: This is the standard perpendicular condition. It only fails for vertical lines (undefined slope) — not our case here.
Step 2

Simplify the product of the slopes.

\[ \frac{3k}{24}=-1 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{k}{8}=-1 \]
Hint: \((-)\times(-)=(+)\); then multiply the fractions: \(\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{k}{12}=\dfrac{3k}{24}\).
Step 3

Solve for \(k\) and check the options.

\[ k=-8 \]
Check: With \(k=-8\), \(L_2:\;-8x+12y-6=0\), so \(m_2=-\dfrac{-8}{12}=\dfrac{2}{3}\) and \(m_1 m_2=-\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=-1\). ✓
Answer: \(k=-8\;\Rightarrow\;\) Option A

Summary — Answers at a glance

  • Rewrite \(L_1\): \(3x+2y+k=0\Rightarrow y=-\dfrac{3}{2}x-\dfrac{k}{2}\), so \(m_1=-\dfrac{3}{2}\).
  • Rewrite \(L_2\): \(kx+12y-6=0\Rightarrow y=-\dfrac{k}{12}x+\dfrac{1}{2}\), so \(m_2=-\dfrac{k}{12}\).
  • Perpendicular condition: \(\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)\left(-\dfrac{k}{12}\right)=-1\).
  • \(\dfrac{k}{8}=-1\;\Rightarrow\;\) k = −8
  • Therefore the correct choice is A.
