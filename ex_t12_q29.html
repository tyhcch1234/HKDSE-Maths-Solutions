Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Equation of Straight Line

T12 Q29

Find the slope of the straight line that is parallel to the line $y = 2x + 3$.

Solution

The given line is $y = 2x + 3$, which is in the form $y = mx + c$.
The slope of the given line is $m = 2$.
Parallel lines have equal slopes, so the required slope is also $2$.
The slope of the straight line parallel to $y = 2x + 3$ is $2$.
