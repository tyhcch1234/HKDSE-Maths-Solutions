Question. The box-and-whisker diagram below shows the distribution of the ages of a group of researchers. Find the inter-quartile range of the distribution.

  • A. 5
  • B. 10
  • C. 20
  • D. 34

1Read the quartiles off the box

A box-and-whisker diagram marks five values along the axis. The left edge of the box is the lower quartile and the right edge of the box is the upper quartile.

From the diagram: the left edge of the box lies at 26 and the right edge of the box lies at 36.

\[ Q_1 = 26, \qquad Q_3 = 36 \]

Tip: the vertical line inside the box is the median (here 32); the whisker ends are the minimum (22) and the maximum (42).
Reading a Box-and-Whisker Diagram \(Q_{1}=\text{left edge of the box},\quad Q_{3}=\text{right edge of the box}\) On this diagram the box runs from \(26\) to \(36\), so \(Q_{1}=26\) and \(Q_{3}=36\).

2Recall the definition of inter-quartile range

The inter-quartile range (IQR) measures the spread of the middle 50 % of the data:

\[ \text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 \]

The IQR is the length of the box itself.
Inter-Quartile Range \(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) The middle \(50\%\) of the ages spans from \(Q_{1}=26\) to \(Q_{3}=36\), the width of the box.

3Substitute and compute

\[ \text{IQR} = 36 - 26 = 10 \]

So the ages of the middle 50 % of the researchers span a range of 10 years.

Check the choices: 5, 10, 20, 34 — only 10 matches.

4Check the other options

Option C, $20$, is $42-22$ (the range, not the IQR). Option D, $34$, is $42-8$ or similar mis-readings. Option A, $5$, is a mis-reading of the box edges.

Always verify which pair of values the question asks for: range uses min & max, IQR uses $Q_1$ & $Q_3$.
Answer: B. 10

Question. A box-and-whisker diagram shows a minimum of \(12\), a lower quartile of \(18\), a median of \(24\), an upper quartile of \(31\) and a maximum of \(45\). (a) Find the inter-quartile range. (b) Find the range, and state which of the two measures describes the middle \(50\%\) of the data.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. (a) The lower quartile is \(18\) and the upper quartile is \(31\), so \(\text{IQR}=31-18=13\).
Step 2. (b) The range uses the minimum and the maximum: \(45-12=33\).
Step 3. The IQR (\(13\)) uses \(Q_{1}\) and \(Q_{3}\), so it describes the spread of the middle \(50\%\) of the data; the range uses the whisker ends.
Answer: (a) \(\text{IQR}=13\); (b) range \(=33\), and the IQR describes the middle \(50\%\)
