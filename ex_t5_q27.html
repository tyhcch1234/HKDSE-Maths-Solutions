Formulas and Sequences (65) · Sub-Topic: Geometric Sequence

T5 Q27

Find the common ratio of the geometric sequence \(5, 10, 20, \dots\).

Solution

Identify two consecutive terms of the geometric sequence: $a_1=5$ and $a_2=10$.
Use the common ratio formula $r=\frac{a_2}{a_1}$.
Substitute the values: $r=\frac{10}{5}=2$.
Check with the next pair: $\frac{20}{10}=2$, so the common ratio is consistent.
The common ratio of the geometric sequence is $2$.
