HKDSE 2015 Paper 2 — Question 17

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram. \(E\) is a point lying on \(CD\) such that \(DE:EC = 2:3\). \(AD\) produced and \(BE\) produced meet at \(F\) while \(AE\) produced and \(BC\) produced meet at \(G\). If the area of \(\triangle DEF\) is \(8\text{ cm}^2\), then the area of \(\triangle CEG\) is
  • A. 12 cm²
  • B. 18 cm²
  • C. 20 cm²
  • D. 27 cm²
STEP 1 · Similar Triangles ΔDEA ~ ΔCEG
AAA Similarity Test \(\angle A=\angle A^{\prime},\ \angle B=\angle B^{\prime}\ \Longrightarrow\ \triangle ABC\sim\triangle A^{\prime}B^{\prime}C^{\prime}\) Vertically opposite angles at \(E\) and alternate angles with \(AD // CG\) give the three equal angles.
\[ \begin{aligned} &\angle DEA = \angle CEG &&\text{(vert. opp. }\angle\text{s)}\\ &\angle DAE = \angle CGE &&\text{(alt. }\angle\text{s, }AD // CG)\\ &\angle ADE = \angle GCE &&\text{(alt. }\angle\text{s, }AD // CG) \end{aligned} \] Hence \(\triangle DEA \sim \triangle CEG\) (A.A.A.).
STEP 2 · Area ratio from the similarity
Ratio of Areas of Similar Triangles \(\dfrac{\text{area}_{1}}{\text{area}_{2}}=\left(\dfrac{l_{1}}{l_{2}}\right)^{2}\) For \(\triangle DEA\sim\triangle CEG\) the ratio is \(\left(\dfrac{DE}{CE}\right)^{2}=\dfrac{4}{9}\).
\[ \frac{\text{area of }\triangle DEA}{\text{area of }\triangle CEG} = \left(\frac{DE}{CE}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9} \] Let \(x\text{ cm}^2\) be the area of \(\triangle CEG\). Then \[ \text{area of }\triangle DEA = \frac{4}{9}x \text{ cm}^2 . \]
STEP 3 · Similar Triangles ΔCEB ~ ΔDEF
\[ \begin{aligned} &\angle CEB = \angle DEF &&\text{(vert. opp. }\angle\text{s)}\\ &\angle CBE = \angle DFE &&\text{(alt. }\angle\text{s, }BC // DF)\\ &\angle BCE = \angle FDE &&\text{(alt. }\angle\text{s, }BC // DF) \end{aligned} \] Hence \(\triangle CEB \sim \triangle DEF\) (A.A.A.), and \[ \frac{\text{area of }\triangle CEB}{\text{area of }\triangle DEF} = \left(\frac{CE}{DE}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{4}. \] Given area of \(\triangle DEF = 8\text{ cm}^2\), \[ \text{area of }\triangle CEB = 8 \times \frac{9}{4} = 18\text{ cm}^2 . \]
STEP 4 · Common vertex — ratio of bases
Triangles with the Same Height \(\dfrac{\text{area}_{1}}{\text{area}_{2}}=\dfrac{b_{1}}{b_{2}}\) \(\triangle DEA\) and \(\triangle CEB\) share the same height to line \(DC\), so their areas are in the ratio \(DE:CE\).
\(\triangle DEA\) and \(\triangle CEB\) have the same height \(h\) from \(A\) / \(B\) to line \(DC\) produced. \[ \frac{\text{area of }\triangle DEA}{\text{area of }\triangle CEB} = \frac{DE}{CE} = \frac{2}{3} \] \[ \frac{\frac{4}{9}x}{18} = \frac{2}{3} \]
STEP 5 · Solve for x
\[ \frac{4}{9}x = 18 \times \frac{2}{3} = 12 \] \[ x = 12 \times \frac{9}{4} = 27 \]
Area of \(\triangle CEG = 27\text{ cm}^2\)  →  Answer: D

Question. In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram. \(E\) lies on \(CD\) such that \(DE:EC=3:2\). \(AD\) produced and \(BE\) produced meet at \(F\), while \(AE\) produced and \(BC\) produced meet at \(G\). If the area of \(\triangle DEF\) is \(27\ \text{cm}^{2}\), find the area of \(\triangle CEG\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(\triangle CEB\sim\triangle DEF\) (vertically opposite and alternate angles),
so \(\dfrac{\text{area of }CEB}{\text{area of }DEF}=\left(\dfrac{CE}{DE}\right)^{2}=\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{2}=\dfrac{4}{9}\).
Step 2. Hence area of \(\triangle CEB=27\times\dfrac{4}{9}=12\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Step 3. \(\triangle DEA\sim\triangle CEG\) with ratio \(\left(\dfrac{DE}{CE}\right)^{2}=\dfrac{9}{4}\);
let \(x=\text{area of }\triangle CEG\), so area of \(\triangle DEA=\dfrac{9}{4}x\).
Step 4. \(\triangle DEA\) and \(\triangle CEB\) have the same height to \(DC\), so \(\dfrac{\frac{9}{4}x}{12}=\dfrac{3}{2}\), giving \(\dfrac{9}{4}x=18\).
Step 5. \(x=18\times\dfrac{4}{9}=8\), so the area of \(\triangle CEG\) is \(8\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Answer: \(8\ \text{cm}^{2}\)
