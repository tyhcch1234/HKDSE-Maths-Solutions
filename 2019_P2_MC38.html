Mensuration — Area of a Shaded Region inside a Sector
Sector Area Equilateral Triangle Sine Ratio for Area Area Ratio MCQ

QQuestion

In the figure, \(O\) is the centre of the sector \(OABC\). It is given that \(\Delta OAB\) is an equilateral triangle. \(AC\) and \(OB\) intersect at the point \(D\). If \(OA = 12\) cm and \(\angle AOC = 90^\circ\), find the area of the shaded region \(BCD\) correct to the nearest \(\text{cm}^2\).

A. \(11\ \text{cm}^2\)
B. \(16\ \text{cm}^2\)
C. \(26\ \text{cm}^2\)
D. \(38\ \text{cm}^2\)

Original Figure

aPart (a) — Angles in the Figure

Because \(\Delta OAB\) is equilateral, \(\angle AOB = 60^\circ\). Then the sector angle is split:

\[ \angle BOC = \angle AOC - \angle AOB = 90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ. \]
60°
Step 11M

In an equilateral triangle all interior angles equal \(60^\circ\), so

\[ \angle AOB = 60^\circ. \]
Hint: "Equilateral" is the trigger word — three equal sides and three \(60^\circ\) angles.
Step 21A

Since \(OA\) and \(OC\) are radii and \(\angle AOC = 90^\circ\),

\[ \angle BOC = 90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ. \]
Answer: \(\angle AOB = 60^\circ\) and \(\angle BOC = 30^\circ\)
0 / 2

bPart (b) — Area of \(\Delta AOC\) and \(\Delta COD\)

Use the "two sides and included angle" area formula on \(\Delta AOC\), then split it with the area ratio at \(D\).

12
Step 11M

Area of \(\Delta AOC\) using two sides and the included angle:

\[ [\Delta AOC] = \tfrac12 \cdot OA \cdot OC \cdot \sin \angle AOC = \tfrac12 (12)(12)\sin 90^\circ = 72\ \text{cm}^2. \]
Hint: \(OA = OC = 12\) are radii of the same sector.
Step 21M

Triangles \(\Delta COD\) and \(\Delta AOD\) share the same altitude from \(O\) to the line \(AC\) (in fact \(OD\) is on both). Using the included angle at \(O\) in each:

\[ \frac{[\Delta COD]}{[\Delta AOD]} = \frac{\tfrac12 \cdot OC \cdot OD \cdot \sin 30^\circ}{\tfrac12 \cdot OA \cdot OD \cdot \sin 60^\circ} = \frac{\sin 30^\circ}{\sin 60^\circ} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}. \]
Hint: \(\frac{1/2}{\sqrt3/2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\). Write the ratio as a fraction of the whole.
Step 31A

Therefore \([\Delta COD] = 72 \times \dfrac{1}{1+\sqrt3}\).

Answer: \([\Delta AOC] = 72\ \text{cm}^2,\qquad [\Delta COD] = \dfrac{72}{1+\sqrt3}\ \text{cm}^2\)
0 / 3

cPart (c) — Sector \(OBC\) and the Final Answer

The sector between \(OB\) and \(OC\) has angle \(30^\circ\); subtract \(\Delta COD\) to get the shaded region.

30°
Step 11M

Area of sector \(OBC\) with angle \(30^\circ\):

\[ [\text{sector }OBC] = \pi r^2 \times \frac{30^\circ}{360^\circ} = \pi (12)^2 \times \frac{1}{12} = 12\pi\ \text{cm}^2. \]
Hint: A full circle is \(360^\circ\), so a \(30^\circ\) sector is \(\frac{1}{12}\) of the circle's area.
Step 21A

The shaded region \(BCD\) = sector \(BOC\) minus triangle \(\Delta COD\):

\[ [BCD] = 12\pi - \frac{72}{1+\sqrt3} = 12\pi - 72(\sqrt3 - 1)\cdot\frac{1}{(\sqrt3+1)(\sqrt3-1)} \approx 37.699 - 26.354 = 11.345\ \text{cm}^2. \]
Hint: Rationalise \(\frac{72}{1+\sqrt3} = 36(\sqrt3-1)\) if you want an exact form. Then round only at the end.
Answer: \([BCD] \approx 11.35\ \text{cm}^2 \approx 11\ \text{cm}^2\) (to the nearest \(\text{cm}^2\))
\(\Rightarrow\) Option A.
0 / 2

Summary of Answers

  • \(\angle AOB = \mathbf{60^\circ}\),   \(\angle BOC = \angle AOC - \angle AOB = \mathbf{30^\circ}\)
  • \([\Delta AOC] = \frac12 (12)(12)\sin 90^\circ = \mathbf{72\ \text{cm}^2}\)
  • \(\dfrac{[\Delta COD]}{[\Delta AOD]} = \dfrac{\sin 30^\circ}{\sin 60^\circ} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt3} \;\Rightarrow\; [\Delta COD] = \dfrac{72}{1+\sqrt3} \approx \mathbf{26.35\ \text{cm}^2}\)
  • \([\text{sector }OBC] = \pi(12)^2 \times \frac{30}{360} = \mathbf{12\pi \approx 37.70\ \text{cm}^2}\)
  • Shaded area \([BCD] = 12\pi - \dfrac{72}{1+\sqrt3} \approx 11.35 \approx \mathbf{11\ \text{cm}^2}\)
  • Correct option: A.  11 cm²
