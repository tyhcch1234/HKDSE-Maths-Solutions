Quadratic Functions

Question

Let \(f(x)=(x+d)(x-2)+9x\), where \(d\) is a constant. If \(f(1+d)=0\), then \(d=\)

  • A. \(-2\)
  • B. \(-1\)
  • C. \(1\)
  • D. \(2\)

Solution

Simplify \(f(x)\) \[ f(x)=x^2+(d-2)x-2d+9x=x^2+(d+7)x-2d \]
Evaluate \(f(1+d)\) \[ f(1+d)=(1+d)^2+(d+7)(1+d)-2d \] \[ =(1+d)\big[(1+d)+(d+7)\big]-2d =2(1+d)(d+4)-2d \] \[ =2(d^2+5d+4)-2d=2d^2+8d+8=2(d+2)^2 \]
Solve \(f(1+d)=0\)

Since a square is zero only when its base is zero,

\[ 2(d+2)^2=0\ \Rightarrow\ d=-2 \]
Final Answer: A
