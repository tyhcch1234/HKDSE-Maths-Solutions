[2018 MC #1]

$\dfrac{8^{\,2n+1}}{4^{\,3n+1}} = $

  • A. $1$
  • B. $2$
  • C. $2^{\,n}$
  • D. $2^{\,-n}$
Step 1Write both numbers with the same base $2$. $$8 = 2^3 \qquad 4 = 2^2$$ Hint: To combine powers, all bases must be identical.
Step 2Replace $8$ and $4$ in the expression. $$\frac{8^{\,2n+1}}{4^{\,3n+1}} = \frac{(2^{3})^{\,2n+1}}{(2^{2})^{\,3n+1}}$$ Hint: Keep the exponent structure untouched when substituting.
Step 3Apply the law $(a^{m})^{k}=a^{mk}$ to expand. $$\frac{2^{\,3(2n+1)}}{2^{\,2(3n+1)}} = \frac{2^{\,6n+3}}{2^{\,6n+2}}$$ Hint: Multiply the outer power into each bracket term.
Step 4Apply the quotient law $\dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}} = a^{\,m-n}$. $$2^{\,(6n+3)-(6n+2)} = 2^{\,1}$$ Hint: Subtract the indices — the $n$ terms cancel!
Step 5Evaluate and answer. $$2^{1} = 2$$ Hint: The result is independent of $n$.
Answer: B.   $2$
