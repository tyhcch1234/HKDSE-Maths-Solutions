Simultaneous Linear Equations

Question. The price of 2 bowls and 3 cups is \$506. If the price of 5 bowls and the price of 4 cups are the same, then the price of a bowl is

  • A. \$88
  • B. \$92
  • C. \$110
  • D. \$115
1 Define the unknowns Let \(b\) be the price of a bowl and \(c\) the price of a cup (in dollars). Name what you are looking for — the question asks for \(b\).
2 Form the two equations “2 bowls and 3 cups cost \$506”: \[2b + 3c = 506 \quad \text{…… (1)}\] “the price of 5 bowls = the price of 4 cups”: \[5b = 4c \quad \text{…… (2)}\] One equation for the total cost, one for the “same price” condition.
3 Express \(c\) in terms of \(b\) From (2): \(\;4c = 5b\;\), so \[c = \frac{5b}{4}\] Substitution method: make one variable the subject first.
Making a Variable the Subject \(4c=5b\ \Rightarrow\ c=\dfrac{5b}{4}\) This turns equation (2) into an expression for \(c\) in terms of \(b\), ready to substitute into (1).
4 Substitute into (1) \[2b + 3\left(\frac{5b}{4}\right) = 506\] \[2b + \frac{15b}{4} = 506\] \[\frac{8b}{4} + \frac{15b}{4} = 506\] \[\frac{23b}{4} = 506\] Write \(2b\) over the denominator 4 so the fractions combine.
5 Solve for \(b\) \[23b = 506 \times 4 = 2024\] \[b = \frac{2024}{23} = 88\] So the price of a bowl is \$88.
Solving a Linear Equation in One Unknown \(Ax=B\ \Rightarrow\ x=\dfrac{B}{A}\quad (A\ne 0)\) \(\dfrac{23b}{4}=506\) is of this form, so \(b=\dfrac{4\times 506}{23}=88\).
6 Check the answer Then \(c = \dfrac{5 \times 88}{4} = 110\). \[2(88) + 3(110) = 176 + 330 = 506 \;\checkmark\] \[5(88) = 440 = 4(110) \;\checkmark\] Both conditions hold — the solution is consistent.
Answer: A.  \$88

Question. The price of \(3\) files and \(2\) pens is \(\$91\). If the price of \(2\) files equals the price of \(3\) pens, then the price of a file is
A. \(\$14\)    B. \(\$18\)    C. \(\$21\)    D. \(\$24\)  (1 mark)

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \(\$f\) be the price of a file and \(\$p\) the price of a pen. Then \(3f+2p=91\) …… (1) and \(2f=3p\) …… (2).
Step 2. From (2), \(p=\dfrac{2f}{3}\).
Step 3. Substitute into (1): \(3f+2\left(\dfrac{2f}{3}\right)=91\), i.e. \(3f+\dfrac{4f}{3}=91\), so \(\dfrac{13f}{3}=91\).
Step 4. Hence \(13f=273\) and \(f=21\).
Step 5. Check: \(p=\dfrac{2(21)}{3}=14\), so \(3(21)+2(14)=63+28=91\) \u2713 and \(2(21)=42=3(14)\) \u2713. Option C.
Answer: C. \(\$21\)
