Direct and Inverse Variation

Question

It is given that \(w\) varies directly as the square root of \(u\) and inversely as \(v\). If \(u\) is increased by \(44\%\) and \(v\) is decreased by \(20\%\), then \(w\) is increased by

  • A. \(40\%\)
  • B. \(50\%\)
  • C. \(64\%\)
  • D. \(80\%\)

Solution

Write the variation formula
Joint Variation \(w=\dfrac{k\sqrt{u}}{v}\) \(w\) varies directly as \(\sqrt{u}\) and inversely as \(v\), linked by one constant \(k\).
\[ w=\frac{k\sqrt{u}}{v} \]

for some constant \(k\).

Apply the changes
Effect of a Percentage Change \(\sqrt{1.44u}=1.2\sqrt{u},\qquad (1-0.20)v=0.8v\) A \(44\%\) rise multiplies \(u\) by \(1.44\) and a \(20\%\) fall multiplies \(v\) by \(0.8\).
\[ u' =1.44u\ \Rightarrow\ \sqrt{u'}=\sqrt{1.44}\,\sqrt{u}=1.2\sqrt{u} \] \[ v'=(1-0.20)v=0.8v \]
Find the new value of \(w\)
Combining Proportional Factors \(w'=\dfrac{1.2}{0.8}w=1.5w\) The factors \(1.2\) and \(0.8\) combine to \(1.5\), so \(w\) increases by \(50\%\).
\[ w'=\frac{k(1.2\sqrt{u})}{0.8v}=\frac{1.2}{0.8}\,w=1.5w \]

So \(w\) increases by \(50\%\).

Final Answer: B

Question. It is given that \(w\) varies directly as \(u^{2}\) and inversely as \(\sqrt{v}\). If \(u\) increases by \(20\%\) and \(v\) decreases by \(36\%\), find the percentage change in \(w\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Write \(w=\dfrac{ku^{2}}{\sqrt{v}}\).
Step 2. The new value of \(u\) is \(1.2u\), so \(u^{2}\) becomes \(1.44u^{2}\).
Step 3. The new value of \(v\) is \(0.64v\), so \(\sqrt{v}\) becomes \(0.8\sqrt{v}\).
Step 4. Hence \(w'=\dfrac{1.44ku^{2}}{0.8\sqrt{v}}=1.8w\), so \(w\) increases by \(80\%\).
Answer: an increase of \(80\%\)
