Coordinate Geometry (117) · Sub-Topic: Equation of Straight Line

T12 Q22

Write down the equation of the horizontal line passing through the point $(2, 4)$.

Solution

A horizontal line has an equation of the form $y=c$, where $c$ is a constant.
Since the line passes through $(2,4)$, its $y$-coordinate is $4$, so $c=4$.
Therefore the equation of the horizontal line is $y=4$.
The equation of the horizontal line passing through $(2,4)$ is $y=4$.
