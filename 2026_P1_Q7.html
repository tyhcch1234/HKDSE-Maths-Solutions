Profit, Discount & Percentage

Question

A profit of \(\$162\) is made by selling a ring at a discount of \(15\%\) on its marked price. The marked price of the ring is \(\$270\) above its cost. Find the percentage profit. (4 marks)

Solution

STEP 1 — define variables
Let M = marked price and C = cost price. \[ M=C+270 \]
STEP 2 — express the selling price
Discount \(\text{selling price}=(1-d\%)\times\text{marked price}\) A \(15\%\) discount means the ring sells at \(0.85M\).
A 15% discount means the ring sells at 85% of the marked price: \[ SP=0.85M \]
STEP 3 — use the profit information
\[ SP-C=162 \implies 0.85M-C=162 \] Substitute \(M=C+270\): \[ 0.85(C+270)-C=162 \] \[ 0.85C+229.5-C=162 \implies -0.15C=-67.5 \implies C=450 \] Hence \(M=450+270=720\), and \(SP=0.85\times720=612\).
STEP 4 — percentage profit
Percentage Profit \(\text{profit}\%=\dfrac{\text{profit}}{\text{cost}}\times100\%\) Profit is \(\$162\) on a cost of \(\$450\), so the percentage profit is \(36\%\).
\[ \text{Percentage profit}=\frac{162}{450}\times100\%=36\% \]
Check: \(SP-C=612-450=162\) ✓.
The percentage profit is \(\mathbf{36\%}\).

Question. The marked price of a bag is \(\$300\) more than its cost price. It is sold at a \(20\%\) discount and the profit is \(\$140\). Find the percentage profit.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Let \(M=C+300\) and \(SP=0.8M\).
Step 2. \(SP-C=140\Rightarrow 0.8(C+300)-C=140\).
Step 3. \(0.8C+240-C=140\Rightarrow 0.2C=100\Rightarrow C=500\), so \(M=800\).
Step 4. Percentage profit \(=\dfrac{140}{500}\times100\%=28\%\).
Answer: \(28\%\)
