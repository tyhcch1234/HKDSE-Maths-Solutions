[2019 #4]   Factorize

(a) $\;4m^{2}-9$ ,

(b) $\;2m^{2}n+7mn-15n$ ,

(c) $\;4m^{2}-9-2m^{2}n-7mn+15n$ .   [4 marks]

a · difference of squares b · take out n, then cross method c · common factor grouping
STEP 1 · Part (a)
Difference of Squares \(A^{2}-B^{2}=(A+B)(A-B)\) \(4m^{2}=(2m)^{2}\) and \(9=3^{2}\), so \(4m^{2}-9=(2m+3)(2m-3)\).

Recognise a difference of two squares: $4m^{2}=(2m)^{2}$ and $9=3^{2}$.

$$4m^{2}-9=(2m)^{2}-3^{2}=(2m+3)(2m-3)$$

Hint: $A^{2}-B^{2}=(A+B)(A-B)$. Remember one bracket is “$+$”, the other is “$-$”.
STEP 2 · Part (b)

Every term contains the factor $n$, so take out $n$ first:

$$2m^{2}n+7mn-15n=n\,(2m^{2}+7m-15)$$

Hint: Always extract the common monomial factor before factorising the quadratic inside.
STEP 3 · Part (b) continued
Splitting the Middle Term \(acx^{2}+(ad+bc)x+bd=(ax+b)(cx+d)\) For \(2m^{2}+7m-15\) the pair \(10m\) and \(-3m\) splits \(7m\), giving \((2m-3)(m+5)\).

Factorise $2m^{2}+7m-15$ by the cross method: we need numbers whose product is $2\times(-15)=-30$ and sum is $7$.

$$\begin{array}{c} \underline{2m \quad -3} \\[2pt] \underline{\;\;m \quad +5} \end{array}\qquad 2m\cdot 5 + m\cdot(-3)=10m-3m=7m \;\checkmark$$

$$2m^{2}+7m-15=(2m-3)(m+5)$$

Therefore $\;2m^{2}n+7mn-15n = n(2m-3)(m+5)$.

STEP 4 · Part (c) — regroup

Write the expression as a difference so part (a) can be re-used:

$$4m^{2}-9-2m^{2}n-7mn+15n = \bigl(4m^{2}-9\bigr)-\bigl(2m^{2}n+7mn-15n\bigr)$$

Hint: Taking the minus sign outside the bracket flips every sign inside — check: $-2m^2n-7mn+15n$ ✓.
STEP 5 · Part (c) — substitute (a) & (b)
Common Factor Law \(AB-AC=A(B-C)\) The bracket \((2m-3)\) appears in both parts of part (c), so it is taken out as a common factor.

Using the results of Step 1 and Step 3:

$$=(2m+3)(2m-3)-n\,(2m-3)(m+5)$$

The bracket $(2m-3)$ is now a common factor.

STEP 6 · Part (c) — take out the common factor

$$=(2m-3)\Bigl[(2m+3)-n(m+5)\Bigr]$$

Expand the square bracket:

$$=(2m-3)\,(2m+3-nm-5n)$$

Hint: Distribute the minus sign carefully: $-(m+5)n = -mn-5n$.

Answer

(a) $4m^{2}-9=(2m+3)(2m-3)$

(b) $2m^{2}n+7mn-15n=n(2m-3)(m+5)$

(c) $4m^{2}-9-2m^{2}n-7mn+15n=(2m-3)(2m+3-mn-5n)$

Question. Factorize (a) \(4a^{2}-25\), (b) \(2a^{2}b+3ab-20b\), (c) \(4a^{2}-25-2a^{2}b-3ab+20b\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. (a) \(4a^{2}-25=(2a)^{2}-5^{2}=(2a+5)(2a-5)\).
Step 2. (b) Take out \(b\) first: \(2a^{2}b+3ab-20b=b(2a^{2}+3a-20)\).
Step 3. Split the middle term: \(2a^{2}+8a-5a-20=2a(a+4)-5(a+4)=(2a-5)(a+4)\), so (b) is \(b(2a-5)(a+4)\).
Step 4. (c) Regroup: \(4a^{2}-25-2a^{2}b-3ab+20b=(2a+5)(2a-5)-b(2a-5)(a+4)\).
Step 5. Take out the common factor \((2a-5)\): \((2a-5)\left[(2a+5)-b(a+4)\right]=(2a-5)(2a+5-ab-4b)\).
Answer: (a) \((2a+5)(2a-5)\)   (b) \(b(2a-5)(a+4)\)   (c) \((2a-5)(2a+5-ab-4b)\)
