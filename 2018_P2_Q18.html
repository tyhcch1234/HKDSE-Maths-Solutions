In the figure, $ABCD$ is a rhombus. $E$ and $F$ are points lying on $AB$ and $AD$ respectively such that $AE = AF$ and $\angle ECF = 42^\circ$. If $\angle BAD = 110^\circ$, then $\angle BEC =$

A. $70^\circ$ B. $76^\circ$
C. $80^\circ$ D. $84^\circ$

Step 1 · Use the properties of the rhombus

In a rhombus $AB = BC = CD = DA$ and the diagonal $AC$ is the axis of symmetry. Hence $AC$ bisects both $\angle BAD$ and $\angle BCD$, so

$$\angle CAE=\angle CAF=\tfrac{1}{2}\angle BAD=\tfrac{1}{2}\times110^\circ=55^\circ$$

Also $AC = AC$ (common side) and $AE = AF$ (given).

Property of a Rhombus\(\text{diagonal }AC\ \text{bisects}\ \angle BAD\ \text{and}\ \angle BCD\)\(AC\) is the axis of symmetry, so \(\angle CAE=\angle CAF=\dfrac{110^\circ}{2}=55^\circ\).

Step 2 · Prove $\triangle ACE \cong \triangle ACF$ (SAS)

$$AC=AC \quad(\text{common side}),\qquad \angle CAE=\angle CAF=55^\circ \quad(\text{prop. of rhombus}),$$ $$AE=AF \quad(\text{given})$$ $$\therefore\ \triangle ACE \cong \triangle ACF \quad (\text{S.A.S.})$$

Corresponding angles are equal, therefore $$\angle ACE=\angle ACF=\tfrac{1}{2}\angle ECF=\tfrac{1}{2}\times42^\circ=21^\circ.$$

HINT — the two coloured triangles $ACE$ and $ACF$ are mirror images in the diagonal $AC$.
SAS Congruence Test\(AB=DE,\ \angle A=\angle D,\ AC=DF\ \Rightarrow\ \triangle ABC\cong\triangle DEF\)\(AC\) common, \(AE=AF\) and the \(55^\circ\) included angles make \(\triangle ACE\cong\triangle ACF\), so \(\angle ACE=21^\circ\).

Step 3 · Exterior angle of $\triangle ACE$

In $\triangle ACE$, the side $AE$ is produced to $B$, so $\angle BEC$ is an exterior angle of the triangle. Hence

$$\angle BEC=\angle ACE+\angle CAE \quad(\text{ext. }\angle\text{ of }\triangle)$$

The exterior angle equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles.

Exterior Angle of a Triangle\(\angle BEC=\angle ACE+\angle CAE\)\(AE\) produced to \(B\) makes \(\angle BEC\) an exterior angle of \(\triangle ACE\), so it equals \(21^\circ+55^\circ\).

Step 4 · Substitute and conclude

$$\angle BEC=\angle ACE+\angle CAE = 21^\circ+55^\circ = 76^\circ$$

Answer: B  ·  $\angle BEC = 76^\circ$

Quick check: $\angle AEC = 180^\circ-76^\circ=104^\circ$, so in $\triangle ACE$, $104^\circ+21^\circ+55^\circ=180^\circ$ ✓

Show the worked solution

Step 2. \(AC\) common, \(AE=AF\) and equal \(65^\circ\) angles give \(\triangle ACE\cong\triangle ACF\) (SAS).
