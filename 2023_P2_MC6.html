HKDSE Math 2023 Paper 2 Q6

Inequalities

The solution of $x-3<-5$ or $\dfrac{6-x}{4}<2$ is

A. $x<-2$
B. $x>-2$
C. $x=-2$
D. $x\ne-2$

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — Solve $x-3<-5$

Add $3$ to both sides:

$x<-2$

Step 2 — Solve $\dfrac{6-x}{4}<2$

Multiply both sides by $4$: $6-x<8$, so $-x<2$.

Divide by $-1$ (reverse the inequality sign):

$x>-2$

Step 3 — Take the union (“or”)

The solution is $x<-2$ or $x>-2$, which together cover every real number except $-2$:

$x\ne-2$

Answer: D. $x\ne-2$
