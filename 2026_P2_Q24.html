Question

The point \( (3,1) \) is translated leftwards by \(5\) units to the point \(U\). \(U\) is then rotated anticlockwise about the origin through \(270^\circ\) to the point \(V\). Find the \(y\)-coordinate of \(V\).

  • A. \( -2 \)
  • B. \( -1 \)
  • C. \( 1 \)
  • D. \( 2 \)
Solution
Step 1 · Translation

Moving \( (3,1) \) leftwards by \(5\) units subtracts \(5\) from the \(x\)-coordinate:

$$ U=(3-5,\;1)=(-2,\;1) $$

Step 2 · Rotation rule

An anticlockwise rotation of \(270^\circ\) about the origin maps

$$ (x,y)\;\longrightarrow\;(y,\,-x) $$

Tip: \(270^\circ\) anticlockwise = \(90^\circ\) clockwise, so \((x,y)\to(y,-x)\).
Step 3 · Apply to U

With \(U=(-2,1)\):

$$ V=\big(1,\,-(-2)\big)=(1,\,2) $$

The \(y\)-coordinate of \(V\) is therefore \(2\).

Answer: D — 2
