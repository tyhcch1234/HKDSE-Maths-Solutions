Coordinate Geometry — Mid-point of a chord of a circle

Equation of a Circle Mid-point Formula Perpendicular Chords Slope & Gradient

Question MULTIPLE CHOICE

The slope of the straight line \(L\) is \(4\). It is given that \(L\) and the circle \(x^2+y^2-18x-20y+96=0\) intersect at the points \(P\) and \(Q\). If the coordinates of the mid-point of \(PQ\) are \((s,t)\), which of the following must be true?

  1. A. \(s-4t-49=0\)
  2. B. \(s-4t+31=0\)
  3. C. \(s+4t-49=0\)
  4. D. \(s+4t+31=0\)

Answer: C

Part (a) — Finding the Centre and Radius of the Circle KEY STEP

Rewrite the circle equation in centre–radius form so we know the centre \(O\) and radius \(r\).

7.0
Step 1

Complete the square in \(x\) and in \(y\):

\[x^2-18x+y^2-20y+96=0\] \[(x^2-18x+9^2)+(y^2-20y+10^2)+96=9^2+10^2\] \[(x-9)^2+(y-10)^2=81+100-96\]
Hint: The general form \(x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey+F=0\) has centre \(\left(-\tfrac{D}{2},-\tfrac{E}{2}\right)\) and radius \(r=\sqrt{\tfrac{D^2+E^2}{4}-F}\).
Result \[(x-9)^2+(y-10)^2=85\]
Step 2

Compare with \((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2\):

\[h=9,\qquad k=10,\qquad r^2=85\]
Hint: The centre is obtained by taking half of the coefficients of \(x\) and \(y\) with opposite signs. Here \(O=(9,10)\) and \(r=\sqrt{85}\approx 9.22\).
Answer Centre \(O=(9,10)\), radius \(r=\sqrt{85}\).

Part (b) — Using \(OM \perp PQ\) to Locate the Mid-point CORE IDEA

Since \(M\) is the mid-point of the chord \(PQ\), the line \(OM\) (joining the centre to the mid-point of a chord) is perpendicular to the chord \(PQ\).

12.0
Step 1

Let \(M=(s,t)\). Since \(PQ\) has slope \(4\), and \(OM \perp PQ\):

\[\text{slope of }OM \times \text{slope of }PQ = -1\] \[\frac{t-10}{s-9}\times 4=-1\]
Hint: Perpendicular lines have gradients whose product is \(-1\). The slope of \(PQ\) is given as \(4\), so the slope of \(OM\) must be \(-\tfrac14\).
Equation \[4(t-10)=-(s-9)\]
Step 2

Expand and rearrange:

\[4t-40=-s+9\] \[s+4t-49=0\]
Hint: Bring all terms to one side to match the form of the options. This is exactly option C.
Answer \[s+4t-49=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{\text{C}}\]

Part (c) — Verify with the Discriminant CHECK

Substitute a general line of slope \(4\) into the circle and use the mid-point of the roots to confirm the result.

-20.0
Step 1

Let \(L\) be \(y=4x+c\). Substitute into \(x^2+y^2-18x-20y+96=0\):

\[x^2+(4x+c)^2-18x-20(4x+c)+96=0\] \[17x^2+(8c-98)x+(c^2-20c+96)=0\]
Hint: The two roots \(x_P,x_Q\) correspond to the two intersection points. Their sum gives the \(x\)-coordinate of \(M\).
Step 2

The \(x\)-coordinate of the mid-point is the average of the roots:

\[s=\frac{x_P+x_Q}{2}=\frac{-\,(8c-98)}{2\cdot 17}=\frac{98-8c}{34}=\frac{49-4c}{17}\]

And substituting \(y=4x+c\):

\[t=4s+c=\frac{4(49-4c)}{17}+c=\frac{196-16c+17c}{17}=\frac{196+c}{17}\]
Hint: Now eliminate \(c\). From \(s=\frac{49-4c}{17}\) we get \(4c=49-17s\), so \(c=\frac{49-17s}{4}\).
Step 3

Substitute \(c\) into \(t\):

\[t=\frac{196+c}{17}=\frac{196+\frac{49-17s}{4}}{17}=\frac{784+49-17s}{68}=\frac{833-17s}{68}\] \[68t=833-17s\] \[s+4t=49\]
Hint: The locus of the mid-points of parallel chords is a diameter of the circle. This confirms \(s+4t-49=0\).
Conclusion \[s+4t-49=0\] Hence the answer is Option C.

Summary of Answers

  • Centre of circle: \(O=(9,10)\)
  • Radius: \(r=\sqrt{85}\approx 9.22\)
  • Key property: \(OM\perp PQ\) (line joining centre to mid-point of chord)
  • Slope condition: \(\dfrac{t-10}{s-9}\times 4=-1\)
  • Required relation: \(s+4t-49=0\)
  • Final answer: C
