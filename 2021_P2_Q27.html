HKDSE 2021 · P2 Q27 Coordinate Geometry — Circle & Chord

Circle $C$ Line $L$ (slope 4) Chord $PQ$ Centre $O$ / $OM\perp PQ$ Points $P,Q,M$
The slope of the straight line $L$ is $4$. It is given that $L$ and the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}-18x-20y+96=0$ intersect at the points $P$ and $Q$. If the coordinates of the mid-point of $PQ$ are $(s,t)$, which of the following must be true?
A.  $s-4t-49=0$
B.  $s-4t+31=0$
C.  $s+4t-49=0$
D.  $s+4t+31=0$
Step 1 · Find the centre and radius

Complete the squares for the given circle:

\[x^{2}-18x+y^{2}-20y+96=0\] \[(x-9)^{2}-81+(y-10)^{2}-100+96=0\] \[(x-9)^{2}+(y-10)^{2}=85\]

So the centre is $O=(9,10)$ and the radius is $r=\sqrt{85}$.

Step 2 · A key chord property

Since $P$ and $Q$ lie on the circle, $PQ$ is a chord. The perpendicular from the centre $O$ to a chord bisects the chord.

Therefore, if $M=(s,t)$ is the mid-point of $PQ$, then the line $OM$ is perpendicular to the chord $PQ$, i.e. $OM\perp PQ$.

Step 3 · Use the perpendicular condition

The slope of $PQ$ (which lies on line $L$) is $4$, so the slope of $OM$ satisfies:

\[\text{slope}(OM)\times 4=-1\quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{slope}(OM)=-\tfrac14\]

But using the two points $O(9,10)$ and $M(s,t)$:

\[\frac{t-10}{s-9}\times 4=-1\]
Step 4 · Simplify to the required form \[\frac{4(t-10)}{s-9}=-1\] \[4t-40=-(s-9)\] \[4t-40=-s+9\] \[s+4t-49=0\]

This matches option C.

Answer: C  —  $s+4t-49=0$ Centre $O(9,10)$; since $OM\perp PQ$ with slope$(PQ)=4$, we get $(t-10)/(s-9)\times 4=-1$, which simplifies to $s+4t-49=0$.
