Trigonometry (59) · Sub-Topic: Sine/Cosine Law

T13 Q17

In triangle \(ABC\), \(a = 10\), \(A = 30^\circ\), and \(B = 45^\circ\). Find \(b\) using the sine law.

Solution

$\tfrac{b}{\sin45°} = \tfrac{10}{\sin30°}$.
$b = \tfrac{10\sin45°}{\sin30°} = 10\sqrt2$.
