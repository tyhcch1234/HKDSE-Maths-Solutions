Similar Triangles & Congruence (SAS)

Question

In Figure 1, \(ABC\) is a triangle. \(D\) is a point lying on \(AB\). Let \(E\) and \(F\) be points lying on \(CB\) produced and \(CD\) produced respectively such that \(BD \;/\!/\; EF\) and \(BC=EF\). It is given that \(BD=8\text{ cm}\) and \(BE=6\text{ cm}\).
(a) By considering a pair of similar triangles, find the length of \(BC\).
(b) Suppose that \(AD=10\text{ cm}\). Prove that \(\triangle ABC \cong \triangle CEF\). (5 marks)

Original Figure

Solution

PART (a) — identify similar triangles
Let \(BC=x\). Since \(E\) lies on \(CB\) produced, \(C\), \(B\), \(E\) are collinear; since \(F\) lies on \(CD\) produced, \(C\), \(D\), \(F\) are collinear. In \(\triangle CFE\), the segment \(DB\) joins points on \(CF\) and \(CE\), and \(DB\parallel FE\), so \[ \triangle CDB \sim \triangle CFE \] (corresponding angles are equal; e.g. \(\angle CDB=\angle CFE\)).
PART (a) — use the ratio of sides
\[ \frac{CB}{CE}=\frac{DB}{FE} \implies \frac{x}{x+6}=\frac{8}{x} \] (because \(EF=BC=x\) and \(CE=CB+BE=x+6\)). Hence \[ x^2=8(x+6) \implies x^2-8x-48=0 \implies (x-12)(x+4)=0 \] Since \(x>0\): \[ BC=12\text{ cm} \]
PART (b) — gather the three SAS ingredients
Side 1: \(AB=AD+DB=10+8=18\) and \(CE=CB+BE=12+6=18\), so \[ AB=CE \] Side 2: given \(BC=EF\) (both \(=12\)). Angle: since \(BD\parallel EF\) and \(CBE\) is a straight line, corresponding angles give \[ \angle DBC=\angle FEC \] and since \(D\) lies on \(AB\), ray \(BD\) is ray \(BA\), so \(\angle ABC=\angle DBC\). Therefore \[ \angle ABC=\angle CEF \] which is the included angle between the two pairs of equal sides.
PART (b) — conclusion
In \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle CEF\): \[ AB=CE,\quad \angle ABC=\angle CEF,\quad BC=EF \] \[ \therefore\;\triangle ABC\cong\triangle CEF\ \ (\text{SAS})\ \qquad\blacksquare \]
(a) \(BC=12\) cm    (b) \(\triangle ABC\cong\triangle CEF\) proved by SAS (shown above).
