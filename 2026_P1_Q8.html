HKDSE 2026 · P1 · Q8

In Figure 1, $ABC$ is a triangle. $D$ is a point lying on $AB$. Let $E$ and $F$ be points lying on $CB$ produced and $CD$ produced respectively such that $BD // EF$ and $BC=EF$. It is given that $BD=8\text{ cm}$ and $BE=6\text{ cm}$.

(a) By considering a pair of similar triangles, find the length of $BC$.

(b) Suppose that $AD=10\text{ cm}$. Prove that $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle CEF$. (5 marks)

Step 1 · Identify similar triangles
Similar Triangles (AAA) \(DB // FE\ \Rightarrow\ \triangle CDB\sim\triangle CFE\) \(DB\) joins points on \(CF\) and \(CE\) and is parallel to \(FE\), so corresponding angles are equal.

Let $BC=x$. Since $E$ lies on $CB$ produced, $C,B,E$ are collinear;
since $F$ lies on $CD$ produced, $C,D,F$ are collinear.

In $\triangle CFE$,
the segment $DB$ joins points on $CF$ and $CE$, and $DB // FE$, so

\[ \triangle CDB \sim \triangle CFE \]

(corresponding angles equal,
e.g. $\angle CDB=\angle CFE$).

Step 2 · Use the ratio of sides
Ratio of Corresponding Sides \(\dfrac{CB}{CE}=\dfrac{DB}{FE}\) \(\dfrac{x}{x+6}=\dfrac{8}{x}\) gives \(x^{2}-8x-48=0\), so \(x=12\).

\[ \frac{CB}{CE}=\frac{DB}{FE} \implies \frac{x}{x+6}=\frac{8}{x} \]

(because $EF=BC=x$ and $CE=CB+BE=x+6$). Hence

\[ x^2=8(x+6) \implies x^2-8x-48=0 \implies (x-12)(x+4)=0 \]

Since $x>0$: \[ BC=12\text{ cm} \]

Step 3 · Part (b) — gather the SAS ingredients

Side 1: $AB=AD+DB=10+8=18$ and $CE=CB+BE=12+6=18$,
so

\[ AB=CE \]

Side 2: given $BC=EF$ (both $=12$).

Step 4 · The included angle

Since $BD // EF$ and $CBE$ is a straight line,
corresponding angles give

\[ \angle DBC=\angle FEC \]

and since $D$ lies on $AB$,
ray $BD$ is ray $BA$, so $\angle ABC=\angle DBC$. Therefore

\[ \angle ABC=\angle CEF \]

which is the included angle between the two pairs of equal sides.

Step 5 · Conclusion

In $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle CEF$:

\[ AB=CE,\quad \angle ABC=\angle CEF,\quad BC=EF \]

\[ \therefore\;\triangle ABC\cong\triangle CEF\ \ (\text{SAS})\ \qquad\blacksquare \]

(a) $BC=12$ cm    (b) $\triangle ABC\cong\triangle CEF$ proved by SAS.

Question. In the figure, \(D\) lies on \(AB\). \(E\) and \(F\) lie on \(CB\) produced and \(CD\) produced respectively such that \(BD // EF\) and \(BC=EF\). Given \(BD=9\text{ cm}\) and \(BE=10\text{ cm}\),
(a) find \(BC\);
(b) if \(AD=16\text{ cm}\), prove that \(\triangle ABC\cong\triangle CEF\).

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(DB // FE\) gives \(\triangle CDB\sim\triangle CFE\), so \(\dfrac{CB}{CE}=\dfrac{DB}{FE}\).
Step 2. With \(BC=x\), \(CE=x+10\) and \(FE=x\): \(\dfrac{x}{x+10}=\dfrac{9}{x}\).
Step 3. \(x^{2}=9x+90\Rightarrow x^{2}-9x-90=0\Rightarrow(x-15)(x+6)=0\), so \(BC=15\) cm.
Step 4. \(AB=16+9=25\) and \(CE=15+10=25\), so \(AB=CE\);
also \(BC=EF=15\) and \(\angle ABC=\angle CEF\) (corresponding angles).
Step 5. Hence \(\triangle ABC\cong\triangle CEF\) (SAS).
Answer: (a) \(BC=15\) cm (b) SAS, since \(AB=CE=25\), \(\angle ABC=\angle CEF\), \(BC=EF=15\)
💡 Tip: Parallel lines $BD // EF$ create the similar triangle pair $CDB\sim CFE$ — always look for the “bow-tie” shape first.
