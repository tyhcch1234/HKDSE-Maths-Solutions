Tea Mixture – Finding the Ratio $x : y$

2. Rate, Ratio and Variations · Multiple Choice

Ratio Weighted Average Mixture / Alligation Linear Equation

📄 The Question

The costs of tea of brand A and brand B are $140/kg and $315/kg respectively. If $x$ kg of tea of brand A and $y$ kg of tea of brand B are mixed so that the cost of the mixture is $210/kg, then $x : y =$ Key idea: cost of the mixture = total money spent ÷ total mass
  • A. $2 : 3$
  • B. $3 : 2$
  • C. $4 : 9$
  • D. $9 : 4$

🧭 Step-by-Step Solution

When two teas are mixed, the price of the mixture is not the simple average of $140$ and $315$. It is the total money spent divided by the total mass.

\[ \text{cost of mixture} = \frac{\text{total value of A} + \text{total value of B}} {\text{total mass}} \]
Hint (weighted average): the mixture price lies closer to the price of whichever tea you use more of. Since $210$ is closer to $140$ than to $315$, we expect more of brand A → the answer ratio $x : y$ should be greater than $1$. This already removes A and C.

Brand A costs $\$140$ per kg and we use $x$ kg, so:

\[ \text{Value of A} = 140x \]

Brand B costs $\$315$ per kg and we use $y$ kg, so:

\[ \text{Value of B} = 315y \]
Hint: "Value" here means the amount of money paid, i.e. $(\text{price per kg}) \times (\text{mass in kg})$.

The total mass of the mixture is $x + y$ kg, and the mixture is priced at $\$210$ per kg. Equating the two ways of computing the total value:

\[ \frac{140x + 315y}{x + y} = 210 \]
Hint: This is the weighted average formula. Numerator = total money, Denominator = total mass.

Multiply both sides by $(x + y)$:

\[ 140x + 315y = 210(x + y) \]
\[ 140x + 315y = 210x + 210y \]
Hint: There is no need to find $x$ and $y$ separately — the question only asks for the ratio $x : y$.

Move the $x$-terms to the left and the $y$-terms to the right:

\[ 140x - 210x = 210y - 315y \]
\[ -70x = -105y \]
Hint: Both sides are negative — you can safely multiply the whole equation by $-1$ to get $70x = 105y$.

Divide both sides by $70y$ (valid since $y > 0$):

\[ \frac{x}{y} = \frac{105}{70} = \frac{3}{2} \]

Therefore:

\[ \boxed{x : y = 3 : 2} \]
Hint: Simplify $\dfrac{105}{70}$ by dividing top and bottom by $35$: $\dfrac{105 \div 35}{70 \div 35} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.

✅ Verify the answer

If $x : y = 3 : 2$, take $x = 3$ kg and $y = 2$ kg. Then

\[ \text{cost of mixture} = \frac{140(3) + 315(2)}{3 + 2} = \frac{420 + 630}{5} = \frac{1050}{5} = 210 \]

which matches the given $210$ per kg.

ANSWER B  3 : 2
