Statistics — Stem-and-Leaf Diagram, Mean, Mode & Probability
Stem-and-Leaf Diagram Mean Mode Range Probability

Q9 Question 5 marks

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of working hours of a group of workers in a week.

Stem (tens)Leaf (units)

33  3  3  4  5  5  9  9
40  1  4  4  5  6  7  7  9

The range of the distribution is 27.

(a) Find the mean and the mode of the distribution. 3 marks

(b) If a worker is randomly selected from the group, find the probability that the number of working hours of the selected worker in the week exceeds the mode of the distribution. 2 marks

Find the unknown leaf \(a\) from the range
1M

The largest value is \(49\) (stem 4, leaf 9) and the smallest possible value with stem 2 is \(20 + a\) (since \(a\) is the smallest leaf in the stem 2 row).

Range \(=27\), so:

\[49-(20+a)=27\] \[49-20-a=27\] \[a=2\]
Hint: Range \(=\) largest value \(-\) smallest value. The smallest value is \(20+a\) because \(a\) is the only unknown and it sits in the leftmost position of the smallest stem.
Answer: \(a=2\)
2
List all data values and count them
—

With \(a=2\) the data are:

22, 25, 25, 26, 26, 28, 28,
33, 33, 33, 34, 35, 35, 39, 39,
40, 41, 44, 44, 45, 46, 47, 47, 49

Counting: \(7+8+9=24\) values in total.

Hint: A stem of \(3\) with leaf \(5\) means \(35\) hours, not \(3.5\) or \(305\). Stem \(=\) tens digit, leaf \(=\) units digit.
Number of workers: \(24\)
3
Compute the mean
1A

Sum all values by grouping by stem:

\[(22+25+25+26+26+28+28) = 180\] \[(33+33+33+34+35+35+39+39) = 281\] \[(40+41+44+44+45+46+47+47+49) = 403\]

Total sum \(=180+281+403=864\).

\[\text{Mean}=\frac{864}{24}=36\]
Hint: Double-check the sum — a common slip is to forget that there are two \(44\)s and two \(47\)s.
Mean \(=36\) hours
4
Identify the mode
1A

The mode is the value that occurs most frequently. In the list, \(33\) appears three times — more often than any other value.

Hint: Inspect the stem-and-leaf diagram row by row. The row with the longest repeated leaf is the mode.
Mode \(=33\) hours

Interpret "exceeds the mode"
1M

From part (a), the mode is \(33\) hours. "Exceeds the mode" means the working hours are strictly greater than \(33\), i.e. \(>33\).

Hint: "Exceeds" means \(>\), not \(\ge\). Workers with exactly \(33\) hours should be excluded.
Condition: hours \(>33\)
2
Count favourable outcomes
—

Count all values greater than \(33\):

34, 35, 35, 39, 39,
40, 41, 44, 44, 45, 46, 47, 47, 49

Number of favourable outcomes \(=14\). Total number of workers \(=24\).

Hint: Note that \(33\) itself (which occurs three times) is not favourable. Carefully count across the stem 3 and stem 4 rows.
Favourable count \(=14\),   Total \(=24\)
3
Compute the probability
1A

Since a worker is chosen at random, each of the \(24\) workers is equally likely:

\[\text{Probability}=\frac{14}{24}=\frac{7}{12}\]
Hint: Always reduce the fraction to lowest terms for the final answer — \(14/24\) simplifies by dividing numerator and denominator by \(2\).
Required probability \(=\dfrac{7}{12}\)

📋 Summary of Answers

  • Unknown leaf: \(a=2\)
  • Mean of the distribution: \(\dfrac{864}{24}=36\) hours
  • Mode of the distribution: \(33\) hours
  • Probability that working hours exceed the mode: \(\dfrac{14}{24}=\dfrac{7}{12}\)
