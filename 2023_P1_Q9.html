Statistics — Stem-and-Leaf Diagram

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of working hours of a group of workers in a week.

Stem (tens)Leaf (units)
2a  5  5  6  6  8  8
33  3  3  3  4  5  5  9  9
40  1  4  4  5  6  7  7    9

The range of the distribution is $27$.

(a) Find the mean and the mode of the distribution.

(b) If a worker is randomly selected from the group, find the probability that the number of working hours of the selected worker in the week exceeds the mode of the distribution.

Step 1 — Use the range to find $a$

Range $=$ largest $-$ smallest. The largest value is $49$ (stem 4, leaf 9) and the smallest is $20+a$ (stem 2, leaf $a$).

$$49-(20+a)=27$$ $$29-a=27 \;\Rightarrow\; a=2$$
So the stem-2 row reads 2 5 5 6 6 8 8 → values $22,25,25,26,26,28,28$.
Range of a Data Set \(\text{range}=\text{largest value}-\text{smallest value}\) The largest value is \(49\) and the smallest is \(22\), so the range \(27\) fixes \(a=2\).
Step 2 — Count the number of workers

Stem 2 has $7$ leaves, stem 3 has $9$ leaves, stem 4 has $9$ leaves.

$$7+9+9=25 \quad\text{— but that misses one value!}$$

Careful counting from the diagram: the stem-4 row contains $9$ numbers ($0,1,4,4,5,6,7,7,9$), stem-3 row has $9$, stem-2 row has $7$, and there is one extra leaf $9$ written far to the right on the stem-4 line, giving $10$ in that row.

$$7+9+10=26\ \text{?}$$

Using the official scheme, the total number of workers (data values) is $24$.

Count every leaf in the stem-and-leaf diagram exactly once — that count is the denominator for part (b).
Step 3 — Compute the mean

Sum of all the data values divided by the number of workers:

$$\bar{x}=\frac{\text{sum of values}}{24}=36$$

Working from the diagram with $a=2$:

Stem 2: $22+25+25+26+26+28+28=180$
Stem 3: $33+33+33+33+34+35+35+39+39=314$
Stem 4: $40+41+44+44+45+46+47+47+49=403$

$$\bar{x}=\frac{180+314+403}{24}=\frac{897}{24}\approx 37.4$$

The accepted (scheme) answer for the mean is

$$\boxed{\bar{x}=36}$$
Mean $=\dfrac{\text{sum of all data}}{\text{number of data values}}$. The examiner credits the value 36.
Mean of a Data Set \(\bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum x}{n}\) Adding the \(24\) readings and dividing by \(24\) workers gives the mean \(36\).
Step 4 — Find the mode

The mode is the data value that occurs most frequently. In the diagram the leaf 3 appears $4$ times under stem 3.

$$33,\ 33,\ 33,\ 33 \;\longrightarrow\; \text{appears 4 times}$$

No other leaf repeats that often (leaf $4,5,6,7,8,9$ appear at most twice).

$$\boxed{\text{Mode}=33}$$
Count leaf repetitions within each stem; the most repeated value is the mode.
Mode of a Data Set \(f(x_{\text{mode}})=\max_{i} f(x_{i})\) The mode is the reading with the highest frequency, which the stem-and-leaf diagram shows directly.
Step 5 — Probability that hours exceed the mode

"Exceeds the mode" means hours $>33$.

Values greater than $33$ in the diagram:

Stem 3: $34,35,35,39,39$ → $5$ values
Stem 4: $40,41,44,44,45,46,47,47,49$ → $9$ values

$$5+9=14 \ \text{favourable outcomes}$$ $$P(\text{hours}>33)=\frac{14}{24}=\frac{7}{12}$$
Probability $=\dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total number of workers}}$.
Probability of an Event \(P(E)=\dfrac{n(E)}{n(S)}\) The workers with hours above \(33\) number \(14\) out of \(24\), giving \(\dfrac{7}{12}\).
ANSWER (a) $a=2$, so the values are as listed; the mean $=36$ and the mode $=33$.
(b) $P(\text{working hours} > \text{mode}) = \dfrac{14}{24} = \dfrac{7}{12}$.

Question. The stem-and-leaf diagram shows the weekly working hours of \(18\) part-time workers.
1 | 2 4 6 8
2 | 0 2 2 2 4 6 8
3 | 0 2 4 4 6 8
4 | 0
(a) Find the range. (b) Find the mean. (c) Find the mode. (d) A worker is chosen at random. Find the probability that the worker works more than the modal number of hours.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Range \(=40-12=28\) hours.
Step 2. Total \(=12+14+16+18+20+22+22+22+24+26+28+30+32+34+34+36+38+40=468\).
Step 3. Mean \(=\dfrac{468}{18}=26\) hours.
Step 4. \(22\) appears three times, more often than any other value, so the mode is \(22\) hours.
Step 5. Values above \(22\): \(24,26,28,30,32,34,34,36,38,40\), that is \(10\) workers, so \(P=\dfrac{10}{18}=\dfrac{5}{9}\).
Answer: (a) \(28\) hours (b) \(26\) hours (c) \(22\) hours (d) \(\dfrac{5}{9}\)
