HKDSE 2013 P2 Q29 — 15. Statistics

[2013MC#29] The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the hourly wages (in dollars) of some workers.

Stem (tens)  |  Leaf (units)
  4 | 0  2  2  2  4  4  4  7
  5 | 0  0  1  2  2  6  8  9
  6 | 3  5  5  7
  7 | 0
  8 | 2  6
  9 | 5

Which of the following box-and-whisker diagrams may represent the distribution of their hourly wages?

  • AA box-and-whisker diagram with a very long right whisker and a small box near the left.
  • BA box-and-whisker diagram with a long left whisker and a long right whisker.
  • CA box-and-whisker diagram with a nearly symmetric long box and two medium whiskers.
  • DA short left whisker, a compact box close to the left, and a long right whisker — skew to the right.
1Read the data

Each row lists leaves (units) for a given stem (tens). Listing every datum in order:

\(40,\ 42,\ 42,\ 42,\ 44,\ 44,\ 44,\ 47,\ 50,\ 50,\ 51,\ 52,\ 52,\ 56,\ 58,\ 59,\ 63,\ 65,\ 65,\ 67,\ 70,\ 82,\ 86,\ 95\)

There are \(24\) data values in total.

Count the leaves: \(8+8+4+1+2+1 = 24\). Each leaf is one worker's wage.
Range of a Data Set \(\text{range}=\text{largest value}-\text{smallest value}\) The whiskers end at \\(40\\) and \\(95\\), so the range is \\(95-40=55\\).
2Find the extremes

Smallest datum \(= 40\) (first leaf of stem 4).

Largest datum \(= 95\) (leaf of stem 9).

Range \(= 95 - 40 = 55\).

The two extreme whisker ends sit at 40 and 95.
Median of a Data Set (even n) \(\text{median}=\dfrac{x_{\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)}+x_{\left(\frac{n}{2}+1\right)}}{2}\) With \\(n=24\\) the median averages the \\(12\\)th and \\(13\\)th values, both of which are \\(52\\).
3Find the median

With \(24\) data, the median is the average of the \(12\)th and \(13\)th values:

\(12\text{th} = 52,\quad 13\text{th} = 52\)

\(\text{Median} = \dfrac{52+52}{2} = 52\)

Median splits the 24 data into two groups of 12.
Inter-Quartile Range \(\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}\) The quartiles \\(Q_{1}=44\\) and \\(Q_{3}=65\\) give \\(\\text{IQR}=65-44=21\\) for the middle half of the data.
4Find the quartiles

Lower half (12 values): \(40,\dots,52\). Its median is the average of the \(6\)th and \(7\)th values:

\(Q_1 = \dfrac{44+44}{2} = 44\)

Upper half (12 values): \(52,\dots,95\). Its median is the average of the \(6\)th and \(7\)th values of the upper half:

\(Q_3 = \dfrac{65+65}{2} = 65\)

\(\mathrm{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 = 65 - 44 = 21\)

Each half contains 12 values, so the quartiles use positions 6–7 within each half.
5Match with a box-and-whisker diagram

Five-number summary: \(\min = 40,\ Q_1 = 44,\ \mathrm{med} = 52,\ Q_3 = 65,\ \max = 95\).

Segment lengths: \(40\to44 = 4,\ 44\to52 = 8,\ 52\to65 = 13,\ 65\to95 = 30\).

The gaps increase steadily towards the right, i.e. the distribution is skewed to the right (positively skewed).

Left whisker shortest, right whisker much longer, box shifted to the left — exactly option D.
6Check the other options

A: small box far left with an extremely long right whisker — box width unrealistic.

B: long left and long right whiskers — would give a wide left whisker, but here \(40\to44=4\) is the shortest.

C: nearly symmetric box with equal whiskers — our left whisker is far shorter than the right.

Only D has the shortest segment on the left and increasing gaps to the right.
Answer: D

Question. The daily sales of a shop on \\(20\\) days are \\(5,\ 7,\ 7,\ 8,\ 9,\ 11,\ 12,\ 13,\ 15,\ 16,\ 18,\ 20,\ 22,\ 25,\ 28,\ 32,\ 38,\ 45,\ 52,\ 60\\). (a) Find the range. (b) Find the median and the inter-quartile range. (c) Describe the shape of the distribution.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Range \\(=60-5=55\\).
Step 2. With \\(n=20\\) the median is the average of the \\(10\\)th and \\(11\\)th values: \\(\dfrac{16+18}{2}=17\\).
Step 3. The lower half has median \\(Q_{1}=\dfrac{9+11}{2}=10\\); the upper half has median \\(Q_{3}=\dfrac{28+32}{2}=30\\).
Step 4. \\(\\text{IQR}=Q_{3}-Q_{1}=30-10=20\\).
Step 5. The segment lengths \\(5\\), \\(7\\), \\(13\\), \\(30\\) grow towards the right, so the distribution is skewed to the right (positively skewed).
Answer: (a) \\(55\\) \quad (b) median \\(=17\\), IQR \\(=20\\) \quad (c) skewed to the right
