HKDSE 2023 · Paper 2 · Q6

Question

The solution of $\ \dfrac{6-x}{4} < 2\ $ is

  • A. $\ x < -2$
  • B. $\ x > -2$
  • C. $\ x = -2$
  • D. $\ x - 3 < -5$
Step 1

Start with the given inequality and clear the denominator. Multiply both sides by $4$ ($4>0$, so the inequality sign is unchanged):

$$\frac{6-x}{4} < 2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 6-x < 8$$

Hint: multiplying by a positive number never flips the inequality sign.
Multiplication Property of Inequalities \(a0)\) Multiplying by \(4>0\) clears the denominator without changing the sign.
Step 2

Isolate the term in $x$. Subtract $6$ from both sides:

$$6-x-6 < 8-6 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad -x < 2$$

Hint: keep the $x$-term on the left, bring the constant to the right.
Step 3

Divide both sides by $-1$. Dividing by a negative number flips the inequality sign:

$$\frac{-x}{-1} > \frac{2}{-1} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x > -2$$

Hint: this is the key trap in the question — the sign must reverse.
Reversing the Inequality Sign \(a-b\) Dividing \(-x<2\) by \(-1\) turns the inequality into \(x>-2\).
Step 4

Check with a test value. Take $x=0$: $\dfrac{6-0}{4}=1.5<2\ \checkmark$, and $0>-2$ holds. Take $x=-4$: $\dfrac{6-(-4)}{4}=2.5\not<2\ \times$, and $-4\ngtr-2$ — consistent.

Hint: always verify with one value inside and one outside the interval.
Step 5

Compare with the options. The solution set is $x>-2$, which is option B. (Option A has the wrong sign, C is a single value, and D simplifies to $x<-2$.)

Hint: option B matches the interval shown on the number line.
Answer: B  ($x>-2$)
