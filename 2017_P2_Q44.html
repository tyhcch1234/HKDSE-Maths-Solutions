Question

[2017 MC #44]

The standard score of Tom in a Mathematics examination is \(-2\). If the score of Tom in the Mathematics examination is \(33\) marks and the mean of the scores of the Mathematics examination is \(45\) marks, then the standard deviation of the scores of the Mathematics examination is

  • A. 3 marks
  • B. 6 marks
  • C. 12 marks
  • D. 36 marks

Step-by-step Solution

1Recall the definition of a standard score.
Standard Score \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\) Tom has \(z=-2\), \(x=33\) and \(\mu=45\), so the unknown is the standard deviation \(\sigma\).

For a score \(x\), with mean \(\mu\) and standard deviation \(\sigma\):

\[z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\]
Standard score (z-score) counts how many standard deviations a score is away from the mean.
2Write down the given values.

Standard score of Tom: \(z=-2\)
Tom's score: \(x=33\)
Mean of the examination: \(\mu=45\)

A negative standard score means Tom scored below the mean.
3Substitute into the formula.
Rearranging to Find the Standard Deviation \(\sigma=\dfrac{x-\mu}{z}\) Here \(\sigma=\dfrac{33-45}{-2}=\dfrac{-12}{-2}=6\) marks.
\[-2=\frac{33-45}{\sigma}=\frac{-12}{\sigma}\]
The gap below the mean is \(45-33=12\) marks, i.e. Tom is 2 standard deviations below the mean.
4Solve for \(\sigma\). \[-2\sigma=-12 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \sigma=\frac{12}{2}=6\]
Both sides negative — divide carefully: \(-12\div(-2)=6\).
5Check with a sanity check.

Score \(=45-2\times 6=45-12=33\). ✓ Matches Tom's given score.

Always re-substitute to confirm the answer satisfies all conditions.
Answer: B. 6 marks
(Standard deviation \(\sigma=6\) marks)

Question. In an examination the mean mark is \(51\). Tom scored \(36\) marks and his standard score is \(-1.5\). Find the standard deviation of the marks.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. Use the standard score formula \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\) with \(z=-1.5\), \(x=36\) and \(\mu=51\).
Step 2. Substitute: \(-1.5=\dfrac{36-51}{\sigma}=\dfrac{-15}{\sigma}\).
Step 3. Rearrange: \(\sigma=\dfrac{-15}{-1.5}=10\).
Step 4. Check: \(z=\dfrac{36-51}{10}=-1.5\) ✓.
Answer: \(\sigma=10\) marks
