QQuestion Multiple Choice 4 marks

According to the figure, which of the following must be true?

A. \(p+q+r+s=360^\circ\)
B. \(p+q+r-s=360^\circ\)
C. \(p+q+r-s=540^\circ\)
D. \(p+q+r-s=540^\circ\)

aPart (a) — Draw the parallel auxiliary lines 1A

Produce the upper solid ray and the middle dashed ray to be parallel to the lower solid ray. Use the alternate angles and interior angles formed by parallel lines to relate \(b,c,d,e\) to \(a\).

Upper ray ∥ middle ray ∥ lower ray a = — b = — c = b − q = —
Angle \(p\) = 120°
Angle \(q\) = 70°
Step 11M

At the top vertex the two rays of the polygonal line and the upper parallel ray are drawn. The angle between the upper ray and the upward ray of the polyline is the reflex angle at a point:

\[ a + p = 360^\circ \quad (\angle\text{s at a pt.})\]
Hint: the two angles \(a\) and \(p\) together sweep one complete revolution about the top vertex, so their sum is \(360^\circ\).
Answer: \(a = 360^\circ - p\)
Step 21M

Since the upper ray and the middle dashed ray are parallel, the angles \(a\) and \(b\) are alternate angles:

\[ b = a = 360^\circ - p \quad (\text{alt. }\angle\text{s, } /\!/ \text{ lines})\]
Hint: alternate angles formed by a transversal cutting two parallel lines are equal.
Answer: \(b = 360^\circ - p\)
Step 31A

At the middle vertex, the interior angle \(q\) together with \(c\) completes the straight angle from \(b\):

\[ c = b - q = (360^\circ - p) - q = 360^\circ - p - q\]
Hint: the angle \(q\) is the interior angle of the polyline; subtracting \(q\) from \(b\) leaves the angle between the middle dashed ray and the downward ray.
Answer: \(c = 360^\circ - p - q\)

bPart (b) — Relate \(d\), \(e\) and \(s\) 2M

Using the same parallel rays, express \(d\) and \(e\) in terms of \(p,q,s\), and hence find the required relation.

d = — e = — s = — d+e+r = —
Angle \(r\) = 130°
Angle \(s\) = 100°
Step 11M

Since the middle dashed ray is parallel to the lower solid ray, the angles \(c\) and \(d\) are alternate angles:

\[ d = c = 360^\circ - p - q \quad (\text{alt. }\angle\text{s, } /\!/ \text{ lines})\]
Hint: the transversal is the polyline segment between the middle and bottom vertices.
Answer: \(d = 360^\circ - p - q\)
Step 21M

The angles \(e\) and \(s\) are interior angles on the same side of the transversal, so they are supplementary:

\[ e + s = 180^\circ \quad (\text{int. }\angle\text{s, } /\!/ \text{ lines}) \quad \Longrightarrow \quad e = 180^\circ - s\]
Hint: interior angles on the same side of a transversal between parallel lines add up to \(180^\circ\).
Answer: \(e = 180^\circ - s\)
Step 31A

At the bottom vertex the three angles \(d\), \(e\) and \(r\) sweep a full revolution:

\[ d + e + r = 360^\circ \quad (\angle\text{s at a pt.})\]
Hint: this is the same "angles at a point" property used in Part (a).
Answer: \(d + e + r = 360^\circ\)

cPart (c) — Eliminate \(d,e\) and obtain the relation 2M

Substitute the expressions for \(d\) and \(e\) into \(d+e+r=360^\circ\) and simplify to find the required relation among \(p,q,r,s\).

p+q+r−s = — 540° match?

Step 11M

Substitute the two expressions found in Part (b) into the "angles at a point" equation at the bottom vertex:

\[ (p+q-360^\circ) + (180^\circ - s) + r = 360^\circ\]
Hint: replace \(d\) by \(360^\circ-p-q\) and \(e\) by \(180^\circ-s\). Equivalent to writing \(d = p+q-360^\circ\) in the figure (the reflexive form).
Equation: \(p+q-360^\circ + 180^\circ - s + r = 360^\circ\)
Step 21M

Collect the constant terms on the left:

\[ p + q + r - s - 180^\circ = 360^\circ\]
Hint: \(-360^\circ + 180^\circ = -180^\circ\). Be careful with signs when moving the constant to the right.
Equation: \(p+q+r-s = 540^\circ\)
Step 31A

Therefore the relation that must hold for any such figure is

\[ p + q + r - s = 540^\circ\]
Check: move any slider above and watch the left-hand side stay exactly at \(540^\circ\) — this is why the answer is C.
Final answer: \(p+q+r-s = 540^\circ\) ⇒ Option C

Summary of Answers

  • Part (a): \(a+p=360^\circ\), \(b=a=360^\circ-p\), \(c=b-q=360^\circ-p-q\).
  • Part (b): \(d=c=360^\circ-p-q\), and \(e+s=180^\circ\) so \(e=180^\circ-s\).
  • Bottom vertex: \(d+e+r=360^\circ\) (angles at a point).
  • Part (c) — Final result: \[ p+q+r-s = 540^\circ \]
  • Correct option: C  (A, B, D do not hold in general.)
