Q Question

It is given that \(y\) partly varies directly as \(x^2\) and partly varies inversely as \(x\). When \(x=1\), \(y=-4\) and when \(x=2\), \(y=5\). When \(x=-2\), \(y=\ ?\)

🔍 Step-by-Step Solution

💡 Key Takeaway

  • Partly varies means the sum of two variation terms.
  • "Directly as \(x^2\)" → term \(k_1 x^2\); "inversely as \(x\)" → term \(\frac{k_2}{x}\).
  • Substitute the two given pairs to build two equations for \(k_1, k_2\).
  • Eliminate one unknown by subtraction, then back-substitute.
