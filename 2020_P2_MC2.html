HKDSE Math 2020 Paper 2 · Q2

Formulas and Sequences

Algebra Change of Subject Formula Manipulation

Question

If \( a(a + b) = 2(b - a) \), then \( b = \)
A. \( \dfrac{a^2 + a}{2 + a} \)
B. \( \dfrac{a^2 - 2a}{2 + a} \)
C. \( \dfrac{a^2 + 2a}{2 - a} \)
D. \( \dfrac{a^2 - a}{2 - a} \)

The correct answer is Option C.

📘 Step-by-Step Solution

Click each step to reveal the reasoning. Follow along to master changing the subject of a formula.

1 Expand both sides ▼

Start with the given equation:

\[ a(a + b) = 2(b - a) \]

Expand the left-hand side by distributing \(a\):

\[ a^2 + ab = 2(b - a) \]

Expand the right-hand side by distributing \(2\):

\[ a^2 + ab = 2b - 2a \]
💡 Hint: Use the distributive law: \( a(b + c) = ab + ac \).
2 Collect all terms containing \(b\) on one side ▼

We want to isolate \(b\). Move the term \(ab\) from the left side to the right side by subtracting \(ab\) from both sides:

\[ a^2 = 2b - 2a - ab \]

Now, move the term \(-2a\) from the right side to the left side by adding \(2a\) to both sides:

\[ a^2 + 2a = 2b - ab \]
💡 Hint: Keep all \(b\) terms on one side, and everything else on the other.
3 Factor out \(b\) on the right side ▼

The right side has two terms with \(b\): \(2b\) and \(-ab\). Factor out \(b\):

\[ a^2 + 2a = b(2 - a) \]
💡 Hint: \(2b - ab = b(2 - a)\). This is the key step to isolate \(b\).
4 Divide both sides by \((2 - a)\) ▼

To make \(b\) the subject, divide both sides by \((2 - a)\) (assuming \(a \neq 2\)):

\[ b = \frac{a^2 + 2a}{2 - a} \]

This matches Option C.

💡 Hint: Division by \((2 - a)\) is valid as long as \(a \neq 2\). In multiple-choice questions, this is typically assumed.
