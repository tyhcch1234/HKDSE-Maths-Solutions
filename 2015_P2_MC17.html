Mensuration — Areas of Similar Triangles inside a Parallelogram

Parallelogram Similar Triangles (AAA) Ratio of Areas Common Height

QQuestion

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram. \(E\) is a point lying on \(CD\) such that \(DE:EC = 2:3\). \(AD\) produced and \(BE\) produced meet at \(F\) while \(AE\) produced and \(BC\) produced meet at \(G\). If the area of \(\Delta DEF\) is \(8\text{ cm}^2\), then the area of \(\Delta CEG\) is

A.\(12\text{ cm}^2\)
B.\(18\text{ cm}^2\)
C.\(20\text{ cm}^2\)
D.\(27\text{ cm}^2\)
DE : EC 2 : 3
Area of ΔDEF 8 cm²
Area of ΔCEG ?

1Step 1 — The Similar Pair ΔDEA ~ ΔCEG

DE / EC 2 / 3
Scale factor 0.667
Area ratio 4 : 9
0 / 3 steps
Step 11M
\[ \angle DEA = \angle CEG \quad (\text{vertically opposite } \angle\text{s}) \] \[ \angle DAE = \angle CGE \quad (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s},\ AD /\!/ CG) \]
Hint: \(AD/\!/ CG\) because \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram — \(AD /\!/ BC\) and \(CG\) is an extension of \(BC\).
Step 21M
\[ \angle ADE = \angle GCE \quad (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s},\ AD /\!/ CG) \] \[ \therefore \Delta DEA \sim \Delta CEG \quad (\text{AAA}) \]
Hint: \(DE\) and \(CE\) are corresponding sides lying opposite the equal angles at \(A\) and \(G\).
Step 31A
Corresponding sides are in the ratio \[ \frac{DE}{CE}=\frac{2}{3} \] For similar figures, areas are in the ratio of the squares of corresponding sides: \[ \frac{\text{Area of }\Delta DEA}{\text{Area of }\Delta CEG}=\left(\frac{DE}{CE}\right)^2=\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2=\frac{4}{9} \]
Hint: Moving the slider above changes the ratio; notice that the area ratio is always the square of the length ratio.
Key result: \(\dfrac{[DEA]}{[CEG]}=\dfrac{4}{9}\)

2Step 2 — The Similar Pair ΔCEB ~ ΔDEF

CE / DE 3 / 2
Area ratio 9 : 4
[CEB] from [DEF]=8 18 cm²
0 / 3 steps
Step 11M
\[ \angle CEB = \angle DEF \quad (\text{vert. opp. } \angle\text{s}) \] \[ \angle CBE = \angle DFE \quad (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s},\ BC /\!/ DF) \] \[ \angle BCE = \angle FDE \quad (\text{alt. } \angle\text{s},\ BC /\!/ DF) \] \[ \therefore \Delta CEB \sim \Delta DEF \quad (\text{AAA}) \]
Hint: \(DF\) is \(AD\) produced, and \(BC/\!/ AD\) in the parallelogram, so \(BC/\!/ DF\).
Step 21M
Corresponding sides: \(CE \leftrightarrow DE\) \[ \frac{\text{Area of }\Delta CEB}{\text{Area of }\Delta DEF}=\left(\frac{CE}{DE}\right)^2=\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2=\frac{9}{4} \] Given \([\Delta DEF]=8\text{ cm}^2\): \[ [\Delta CEB] = 8\times\frac{9}{4}=18\text{ cm}^2 \]
Hint: Be careful — the given triangle is the smaller one here, so the area ratio is greater than \(1\).
Result: \([\Delta CEB]=18\text{ cm}^2\)
Step 31M
The two triangles have a common height from \(D\) (and from \(B\)) onto the base line \(CDG\). \[ \frac{[\Delta DEA]}{[\Delta CEB]} = \frac{DE}{CE}=\frac{2}{3} \]
Hint: Triangles on the same straight line with the same apex have areas proportional to their bases. Here base \(DE\) for \(\Delta DEA\) and base \(CE\) for \(\Delta CEB\) both lie on line \(CD\).

3Step 3 — Solve for the Area of ΔCEG

[DEA] = 4/9 · [CEG] 8.89 cm²
[CEB] = 3/2 · [DEA] 13.33 cm²
Required: [CEB] 18 cm²
0 / 3 steps
Step 11M
Let \(x\text{ cm}^2\) be the area of \(\Delta CEG\). From Step 1: \[ [\Delta DEA] = x\times\frac{4}{9}=\frac{4}{9}x \]
Hint: Rearranging \([DEA]:[CEG]=4:9\) gives \([DEA]=\frac{4}{9}\times[CEG]\).
Step 21M
From Step 2: \(\dfrac{[\Delta DEA]}{[\Delta CEB]}=\dfrac{DE}{CE}=\dfrac{2}{3}\), and \([\Delta CEB]=18\text{ cm}^2\): \[ \frac{4}{9}x = 18\times\frac{2}{3} \]
Hint: Both expressions for \([\Delta DEA]\) refer to the same triangle, so we may equate them — that is the bridge linking \(\Delta CEG\) and \(\Delta CEB\).
Step 31A
\[ \frac{4x}{9}=12 \;\Longrightarrow\; 4x=108 \;\Longrightarrow\; x=27 \]
Hint: The answer \(x=27\) corresponds to option D. Notice \(8+18=26\) is close to \(27\) — but the correct link is the ratio argument, not simple addition.
Answer: The area of \(\Delta CEG\) is \(27\text{ cm}^2\) — Option D.

✓Summary of Results

  • First pair: \(\Delta DEA \sim \Delta CEG\) (AAA), with \(\dfrac{DE}{CE}=\dfrac{2}{3}\).
  • Area ratio: \(\dfrac{[\Delta DEA]}{[\Delta CEG]}=\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^2=\dfrac{4}{9}\).
  • Second pair: \(\Delta CEB \sim \Delta DEF\) (AAA), with \(\dfrac{CE}{DE}=\dfrac{3}{2}\).
  • \(\dfrac{[\Delta CEB]}{[\Delta DEF]}=\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{9}{4}\), so \([\Delta CEB]=8\times\dfrac{9}{4}=\text{}18\text{ cm}^2\)
  • Common height on base line \(CD\): \(\dfrac{[\Delta DEA]}{[\Delta CEB]}=\dfrac{DE}{CE}=\dfrac{2}{3}\).
  • Solve \(\dfrac{4}{9}x=18\times\dfrac{2}{3}=12\), giving \(x=\text{}27\)
  • Final answer: \(27\text{ cm}^2\) — Option D
