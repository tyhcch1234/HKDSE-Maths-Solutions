FIGURE · Sector OABCD

Radius / arc (r = 30) Perpendiculars Chord AD (48) Centre O Angle ∠BOG = 30°

QUESTION

In the figure, $O$ is the centre of the sector $OABCD$. $AD$ and $OC$ are perpendicular to each other and intersect at the point $E$. $F$ is a point lying on $AD$ such that $BF$ is perpendicular to $AD$. If $AF=9$ cm, $DF=39$ cm and $OE=18$ cm, then the area of the sector $OBC$ is

A. $48\pi$ cm²
B. $75\pi$ cm²
C. $96\pi$ cm²
D. $150\pi$ cm²
STEP 1 · Use the perpendicular from the centre

$OC \perp AD$ and $O$ is the centre, so the perpendicular from the centre to the chord $AD$ bisects $AD$:

$$AE = DE = \frac{AD}{2} = \frac{9+39}{2} = \frac{48}{2} = 24 \text{ cm}$$

Key idea: a perpendicular line drawn from the centre of a circle to a chord always bisects that chord.
Perpendicular from Centre to Chord\(OM\perp AB\ \Rightarrow\ AM=MB=\dfrac{1}{2}AB\)Since \(OC\perp AD\) through the centre, \(E\) is the mid-point of \(AD\), so \(AE=24\ \text{cm}\).
STEP 2 · Find the radius in △OAE

In right-angled $\triangle OAE$ (right angle at $E$): $AE=24$, $OE=18$.

$$OA = \sqrt{24^2+18^2} = \sqrt{576+324} = \sqrt{900} = 30 \text{ cm}$$

Since $OB = OA$ (radii of the same sector), $\;r = \k2{30}$ cm.

Pythagoras’ Theorem\(OA^{2}=OE^{2}+AE^{2}\)Triangle \(OAE\) is right-angled at \(E\), so \(OA=\sqrt{18^{2}+24^{2}}=30\ \text{cm}\).
STEP 3 · Locate $G$ and build rectangle $BGEF$

Let $G$ be the point on $OC$ such that $BG \perp OC$.

Since $BF \perp AD$, $OC \perp AD$ and $BG \perp OC$, the quadrilateral $BGEF$ is a rectangle, so $BG = EF$.

$$EF = AE - AF = 24 - 9 = 15 \text{ cm} \;\Rightarrow\; BG = \k3{15} \text{ cm}$$

STEP 4 · Find the sector angle ∠BOG

In right-angled $\triangle OBG$ (right angle at $G$), with $OB=30$ and $BG=15$:

$$\sin\angle BOG = \frac{BG}{OB} = \frac{15}{30} = \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\therefore \angle BOG = \k2{30^\circ}$$

This $\angle BOC$ (same as $\angle BOG$, since $G$ lies on $OC$) is the angle of the sector $OBC$.
STEP 5 · Area of the sector $OBC$

$$\text{Area} = \pi r^2 \times \frac{\angle BOC}{360^\circ} = \pi(30)^2 \times \frac{30^\circ}{360^\circ}$$

$$= 900\pi \times \frac{1}{12} = \k2{75\pi} \text{ cm}^2$$

Area of a Sector\(\text{Area}=\dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times\pi r^{2}\)With \(r=30\) and \(\angle BOC=30^\circ\), the sector is \(\dfrac{1}{12}\) of the circle: \(75\pi\ \text{cm}^{2}\).
Answer: B  ·  $75\pi$ cm²

Show the worked solution

Step 2. \(r=\sqrt{20^{2}+15^{2}}=\sqrt{625}=25\ \text{cm}\).
Step 3. \(\sin\angle AOE=\dfrac{20}{25}=0.8\), so \(\angle AOE=53.13^\circ\) and \(\angle AOD=106.26^\circ\approx106^\circ\).
