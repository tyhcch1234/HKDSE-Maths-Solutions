The figure shows the square \(ABCD\), the regular pentagon \(ADEFG\) and the regular hexagon \(AGHIJK\). Find \(\angle ABK\).

A. \(69^\circ\) B. \(72^\circ\) C. \(74^\circ\) D. \(75^\circ\)

Part (a) Interior Angles of the Pentagon and Hexagon

Pentagon \(ADEFG\): interior angle = 108° Hexagon \(AGHIJK\): interior angle = 120° Square \(ABCD\): interior angle = 90°
The figure is built from the shared vertex \(A\). Move the slider to rotate the whole configuration and check that the marked interior angles stay constant.
Step 11M

For a polygon with \(n\) sides, the sum of interior angles is \((n-2)\times 180^\circ\).

Pentagon \(ADEFG\) has \(n=5\):

\[(5-2)\times 180^\circ = 3\times 180^\circ = 540^\circ\]
Hint: A pentagon splits into \(3\) triangles, so its angle sum is \(3\times 180^\circ\).
Step 21A

Since \(ADEFG\) is a regular pentagon, each interior angle is equal:

\[\angle DAG = \frac{540^\circ}{5} = 108^\circ\]
Hint: "Regular" means all sides and all interior angles are equal.
Step 31M

Hexagon \(AGHIJK\) has \(n=6\):

\[(6-2)\times 180^\circ = 4\times 180^\circ = 720^\circ\]
Hint: A hexagon splits into \(4\) triangles.
Step 41A

Since \(AGHIJK\) is a regular hexagon:

\[\angle GAK = \frac{720^\circ}{6} = 120^\circ\]
Answers: \(\angle DAG = 108^\circ\), \(\angle GAK = 120^\circ\).

Part (b)(i) Finding \(\angle BAK\) at the Point \(A\)

\(\angle BAD = 90^\circ\) (square) \(\angle DAG = 108° \(\angle GAK = 120° \(\angle BAK = 42°
Drag the sliders to see how the three angles around \(A\) always fill the full turn of \(360^\circ\).
Step 11M

At the point \(A\), the rays \(AB\), \(AD\), \(AG\), \(AK\) divide the full angle into four parts:

\[\angle BAK + \angle BAD + \angle DAG + \angle GAK = 360^\circ \quad (\angle\text{s at a pt.})\]
Hint: Angles around a point add up to \(360^\circ\).
Step 21M

Substitute the known interior angles (square \(90^\circ\), pentagon \(108^\circ\), hexagon \(120^\circ\)):

\[\angle BAK = 360^\circ - 90^\circ - 108^\circ - 120^\circ\]
Hint: \(\angle BAD = 90^\circ\) because \(ABCD\) is a square.
Step 31A
\[\angle BAK = 360^\circ - 318^\circ = 42^\circ\]
Answer: \(\angle BAK = 42^\circ\).

Part (b)(ii) The Isosceles Triangle \(ABK\)

\(AB = AK\) (given) \(\angle ABK = \angle AKB = 69° \(\angle BAK = 42°
Because two sides are equal, the base angles are equal. Vary \(\angle BAK\) to see the base angles adjust automatically.
Step 11M

In \(\triangle ABK\), we are given \(AB = AK\). Hence \(\triangle ABK\) is an isosceles triangle, so its base angles are equal:

\[\angle ABK = \angle AKB \quad (\text{base } \angle\text{s, isos. } \triangle)\]
Hint: Equal sides ⇒ equal angles opposite them.
Step 21M

The angle sum of a triangle is \(180^\circ\):

\[\angle ABK + \angle AKB = 180^\circ - \angle BAK \quad (\angle\text{ sum of } \triangle)\]
\[\angle ABK + \angle AKB = 180^\circ - 42^\circ = 138^\circ\]
Hint: Subtract the apex angle from \(180^\circ\) to find the sum of the two base angles.
Step 31A

Since \(\angle ABK = \angle AKB\), each base angle is half of \(138^\circ\):

\[\angle AKB = \frac{138^\circ}{2} = 69^\circ\]
Answer: \(\angle ABK = 69^\circ\), which is option A.

Summary of Answers

  • Interior angle of regular pentagon \(ADEFG\): \(\angle DAG = \dfrac{(5-2)\times 180^\circ}{5} = \dfrac{540^\circ}{5} = \mathbf{108^\circ}\)
  • Interior angle of regular hexagon \(AGHIJK\): \(\angle GAK = \dfrac{(6-2)\times 180^\circ}{6} = \dfrac{720^\circ}{6} = \mathbf{120^\circ}\)
  • Angle at the point \(A\): \(\angle BAK = 360^\circ - 90^\circ - 108^\circ - 120^\circ = \mathbf{42^\circ}\)
  • Isosceles triangle \(ABK\) (since \(AB = AK\)): \(\angle ABK = \angle AKB = \dfrac{180^\circ - 42^\circ}{2} = \dfrac{138^\circ}{2} = \mathbf{69^\circ}\)
  • Final answer: \(\angle ABK = \mathbf{69^\circ}\), which is option A.
