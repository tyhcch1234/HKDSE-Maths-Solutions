HKDSE Math 2023 Paper 2 Q2

Topic 4 · Polynomials — Subtraction of Algebraic Fractions

Polynomials Algebraic Fractions Difference of Squares Common Denominator Multiple Choice

Question

$$\frac{2}{5-4x}-\frac{1}{5+4x}=$$
A $\dfrac{5+4x}{25-16x^{2}}$
B $\dfrac{5-4x}{25-16x^{2}}$
C $\dfrac{5+12x}{25-16x^{2}}$
D $\dfrac{5-12x}{25-16x^{2}}$

Step-by-Step Solution

Tap each step to reveal the reasoning. Use the Hint button when stuck.

1
Identify the goal & the obstacles

We must subtract two algebraic fractions:

$$\frac{2}{5-4x}-\frac{1}{5+4x}$$

The denominators are different, so we cannot simply subtract the numerators. We need a common denominator first.

Just like with numerical fractions such as $\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{4}$, we cannot subtract unless the bottoms match. Identify the two denominators: $(5-4x)$ and $(5+4x)$.
2
Build the common denominator (LCM)

The two denominators are $(5-4x)$ and $(5+4x)$. They are different linear factors, so the LCM is their product:

$$(5-4x)(5+4x)$$

This expands by the difference of squares:

$$(5-4x)(5+4x)=25+20x-20x-16x^{2}=25-16x^{2}$$
For a fraction $\frac{1}{a-b}$, multiply top and bottom by $(a+b)$. More generally, write each fraction over the LCD by multiplying numerator and denominator by the missing factor.
3
Rewrite each fraction over the LCD

Multiply numerator and denominator of each fraction by the missing factor:

$$\frac{2}{5-4x}=\frac{2\color{#2563eb}{(5+4x)}}{\color{#2563eb}{(5+4x)}(5-4x)} \qquad \frac{1}{5+4x}=\frac{1\color{#f59e0b}{(5-4x)}}{\color{#f59e0b}{(5-4x)}(5+4x)}$$

Now both fractions share the denominator $(5+4x)(5-4x)$.

Only multiply numerator and denominator — never change the value of the fraction, just its form.
4
Subtract and simplify the numerator

Combine into a single fraction and distribute the minus sign carefully:

$$\frac{2(5+4x)-1(5-4x)}{(5-4x)(5+4x)} =\frac{10+8x-5+4x}{(5-4x)(5+4x)}$$

Watch out! $-1\times(-4x)=+4x$, not $-4x$. Group like terms:

$$\frac{(10-5)+(8x+4x)}{(5-4x)(5+4x)}=\frac{5+12x}{(5-4x)(5+4x)}$$
The most common mistake is forgetting to distribute the minus sign across both terms of $(5-4x)$. Use brackets: $-1(5-4x)=-(5-4x)=-5+4x$.
5
Write the final factorised form & match the option

Expand the denominator using the difference of squares:

$$(5-4x)(5+4x)=5^{2}-(4x)^{2}=25-16x^{2}$$

Therefore:

$$\frac{2}{5-4x}-\frac{1}{5+4x}=\frac{5+12x}{25-16x^{2}}$$

Comparing with the options, the correct choice is C.

Options A–D differ only in the numerator: $5+4x$, $5-4x$, $5+12x$, $5-12x$. Our work must be precise in sign and in the $x$-coefficient.

Key Takeaways

To subtract algebraic fractions:

① Find a common denominator → ② Rewrite each fraction → ③ Subtract numerators with brackets → ④ Simplify

Remember the difference of squares: (a–b)(a+b) = a² – b²
