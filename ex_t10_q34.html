Mensuration (64) · Sub-Topic: Surface Area

T10 Q34

A rectangular box has dimensions $3$ cm, $4$ cm and $5$ cm. Find its total surface area.

Solution

Total surface area formula: $2(lw+lh+wh)$ for a rectangular box with length $l$, width $w$, and height $h$.
Substitute $l=3$, $w=4$, $h=5$: $2(3\times4+3\times5+4\times5)$.
Calculate inside the brackets: $3\times4=12$, $3\times5=15$, $4\times5=20$, so $12+15+20=47$.
Multiply by $2$: $2\times47=94$.
The total surface area of the rectangular box is $94$ cm$^2$.
