HKDSE 2018 Paper 2 Q9 · Percentages

A sum of $\$100\,000$ is deposited at an interest rate of $2\%$ per annum for $3$ years, compounded monthly. Find the interest correct to the nearest dollar.

  • A.  $\$6\,000$
  • B.  $\$6\,121$
  • C.  $\$6\,176$
  • D.  $\$6\,178$
1Identify the given information
Principal  $P = 100\,000$  dollars
Nominal rate per annum  $R = 2\%$   (so $r = 0.02$)
Time  $t = 3$  years, compounding frequency  $n = 12$  (monthly)
Careful: "compounded monthly" means $12$ conversions per year — not $2\%$ per month.
2Find the rate per conversion period
\[ i=\frac{R}{n}=\frac{2\%}{12}=\frac{0.02}{12}\approx 0.0016\overline{6} \] The money earns about $0.1667\%$ each month.
Rule: the annual rate is divided by the number of periods per year — it is not multiplied.
Interest Rate per Compounding Period\(i=\dfrac{R}{n}\)The nominal \(2\%\) per annum is shared among \(12\) monthly periods, giving \(i=\dfrac{0.02}{12}\).
3Count the number of conversion periods
\[ N=n\times t = 12\times 3 = 36 \text{ months} \]
Rule: periods = (periods per year) $\times$ (years).
Number of Conversion Periods\(N=n\times t\)\(12\) periods per year for \(3\) years gives \(N=36\) conversions.
4Apply the compound interest formula
\[ A=P(1+i)^{N}=100\,000\left(1+\frac{0.02}{12}\right)^{36} \] \[ \text{Interest}=A-P=P\left[\left(1+\frac{0.02}{12}\right)^{36}-1\right] \]
Why? Each month the balance is multiplied by the same factor $\left(1+\frac{0.02}{12}\right)$; over $36$ months that compounds to $36$ multiplications.
Compound Interest Formula\(A=P(1+i)^{N}\)Each month multiplies the balance by \(1+\dfrac{0.02}{12}\), so the \(36\) multiplications give \(A=P\left(1+\dfrac{0.02}{12}\right)^{36}\).
5Evaluate and round
\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Interest} &= 100\,000\times\left[\left(1+\frac{2\%}{12}\right)^{3\times 12}-1\right]\\ &= 6178.351496\ldots\\ &= \$6\,178 \quad(\text{cor. to the nearest dollar}) \end{aligned} \]
Common trap: simple interest gives $100\,000\times 2\%\times 3=\$6\,000$ (option A) — that ignores compounding.
6Check against the options
$6\,178$ is between $6\,176$ and $6\,121$, and is closest to $6\,178$.
Hence the answer is D.
Sanity check: $\$6\,178 > \$6\,000$ (simple interest), which makes sense because compounding earns extra interest on interest.
Answer: D  —  $\$6\,178$

Question. A sum of \(\$60\,000\) is deposited at \(3\%\) per annum for \(2\) years, compounded quarterly. Find the interest correct to the nearest dollar.

Show the worked solution
Step 1. \(P=60\,000\), \(R=0.03\), \(n=4\) (quarterly), \(t=2\).
Step 2. Rate per period: \(i=\dfrac{0.03}{4}=0.0075\); number of periods: \(N=4\times2=8\).
Step 3. \(A=60\,000(1.0075)^{8}\approx\$63\,695.93\).
Step 4. Interest \(=A-P\approx63\,695.93-60\,000=\$3\,695.93\), i.e. \(\$3\,696\) to the nearest dollar.
Answer: \(\$3\,696\)
