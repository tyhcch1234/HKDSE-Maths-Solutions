Question & Solution

[2025 #14] The height and the base radius of a solid metal right circular cone are $45\text{ cm}$ and $24\text{ cm}$ respectively. The circular cone is divided into a circular cone and a frustum $X$ by a plane parallel to its base. It is given that the height of $X$ is $30\text{ cm}$.

(a) Express the volume of $X$ in terms of $\pi$. (3 marks)
(b) Express the total surface area of $X$ in terms of $\pi$. (3 marks)
(c) $X$ is melted and recast into a solid cube. Does the total surface area of the cube exceed the total surface area of $X$? Explain your answer. (3 marks)
Setup idea: the plane cuts the cone at height $45-30=15\text{ cm}$ from the apex, creating a small similar cone. Similar figures have lengths in ratio $k$, areas in ratio $k^2$, volumes in ratio $k^3$.
STEP 1 · Similar-cone ratio Original cone: $H=45$, $R=24$. Small cone above the cut: height $h=45-30=15$. $$k=\frac{15}{45}=\frac13$$ So the small cone's base radius is $\dfrac13(24)=8\text{ cm}$.
STEP 2 (a) · Volume of $X$ Volume of $X$ = volume of big cone − volume of small cone. $$V=\frac13\pi(24)^2(45)-\frac13\pi(8)^2(15)$$ $$=\frac13\pi(24)^2(45)\left[1-\left(\frac{15}{45}\right)^3\right] =8640\pi\left[1-\left(\tfrac13\right)^3\right]$$ $$=8640\pi\left(1-\frac1{27}\right)=8640\pi\cdot\frac{26}{27}=8320\pi$$ $$\boxed{V_X=8320\pi\ \text{cm}^3}$$
STEP 3 (b) · Slant heights Big cone slant height: $\ell_1=\sqrt{24^2+45^2}=51\text{ cm}$. Small cone slant height: $\ell_2=\sqrt{8^2+15^2}=17\text{ cm}$. Slant height of the frustum $X$: $51-17=34\text{ cm}$.
STEP 4 (b) · Total surface area of $X$ $X$ has: curved (lateral) surface, big base circle, small top circle. $$A=\pi(24+8)(34)+\pi(24)^2+\pi(8)^2$$ $$=1088\pi+576\pi+64\pi=1728\pi$$ $$\boxed{A_X=1728\pi\ \text{cm}^2}$$ (Equivalently $\pi(24)\sqrt{24^2+45^2}\left[1-\left(\tfrac{15}{45}\right)^2\right]+\pi(24)^2+\pi\left(24\cdot\tfrac{15}{45}\right)^2$.)
STEP 5 (c) · Side of the cube Melting preserves volume, so cube side $x$ satisfies $$x^3=8320\pi \;\Rightarrow\; x=\sqrt[3]{8320\pi}\approx 29.6773\text{ cm}.$$
STEP 6 (c) · Compare surface areas Total surface area of the cube: $$6x^2\approx 6(29.6773)^2\approx 5284.45\ \text{cm}^2.$$ Total surface area of $X$: $$1728\pi\approx 5428.67\ \text{cm}^2.$$ Since $5284.45<5428.67$, the cube's surface area does not exceed that of $X$.
(a) $8320\pi\ \text{cm}^3$  ·  (b) $1728\pi\ \text{cm}^2$  ·  (c) No — $6x^2\approx5284.5<1728\pi\approx5428.7$.
