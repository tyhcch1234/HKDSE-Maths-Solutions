HKDSE 2013 · Paper 1 · Q7 — Geometry

Quadrilateral ABCD Diagonals AC, BD Equal segments BE, CE Equal angles BAC, BDC Intersection E
In Figure 1, $ABCD$ is a quadrilateral. The diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at $E$. It is given that $BE = CE$ and $\angle BAC = \angle BDC$.

(a) Prove that $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DCB$.
(b) Consider the triangles in Figure 1.
   (i) How many pairs of congruent triangles are there?
   (ii) How many pairs of similar triangles are there?
(4 marks)
STEP 1 · Understand the given information

We are told:

• $BE = CE$ — so $\triangle BCE$ is an isosceles triangle with base $BC$.

• $\angle BAC = \angle BDC$ — an equal pair of angles subtending the same segment $BC$.

• $BC$ is a common side shared by $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle DCB$.

STEP 2 · Equal base angles (Isosceles Triangle)

In $\triangle BCE$, since $BE = CE$:

$\angle BCE = \angle CBE$    (base $\angle$s, isos. $\triangle$)

Note $\angle CBE = \angle CBD$ and $\angle BCE = \angle BCA$, so:

$\angle CBD = \angle BCA$

STEP 3 · Prove (a): $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DCB$

In $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle DCB$:

$\angle BCA = \angle CBD$    (base $\angle$s, isos. $\triangle$)

$\angle BAC = \angle BDC$    (given)

$BC = CB$    (common side)

$\therefore \triangle ABC \cong \triangle DCB$    (AAS)

💡 Marking Scheme: correct proof with reasons = 2 marks; without reasons = 1 mark.
STEP 4 · (b)(i) Count congruent pairs

The diagonals split the quadrilateral into 4 small triangles around $E$:

$ABE,\; BCE,\; CDE,\; DAE$

We also have the two big triangles $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle DCB$.

By the congruence in (a) and the symmetry of the configuration, the congruent pairs are:

△ABC ≡ △DCB △ABE ≡ △DCE △BCE ≡ △CBE

$\Rightarrow$ There are 3 pairs of congruent triangles.

STEP 5 · (b)(ii) Count similar pairs

Vertically opposite angles at $E$ give equal angles: $\angle AEB = \angle DEC$ and $\angle AED = \angle BEC$.

Combining equal angles from the isosceles / congruent triangles, the similar pairs of triangles in Figure 1 are:

△ABE ~ △DCE △BCE ~ △DAE △ABC ~ △DCB △ABD ~ △DCA

$\Rightarrow$ There are 4 pairs of similar triangles.

FINAL ANSWER

(a) $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DCB$ (AAS)

(b)(i) $3$ pairs of congruent triangles

(b)(ii) $4$ pairs of similar triangles
